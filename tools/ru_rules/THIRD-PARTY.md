# Sources of the rules

Copied on 2026-11-21 from the Riding the Boar site
(`.vale/styles/RidingTheBoarRu/`, state of that day), where they read the
Russian translations of the essays; here `tools/ru_check.py` runs them over
the Russian listing and website through the machine's `prose-scan --rules`.
`Translationese.yml` and `Afterthought.yml` carry the owner's corrections
(`docs/translation-style.md`); `Yo.yml` is written there.

`ChatArtifacts.yml`, `MixedScript.yml`, `ChatVoice.yml`, `ModelHand.yml`,
`Calques.yml`, `Cliches.yml`, `Connectors.yml`, `Formulas.yml` and
`Inflation.yml` carry the token lists of the Scanport rule families of
