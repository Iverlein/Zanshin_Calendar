A meditation timer and a mindfulness bell: sessions of periods of any length, presets, bowls, a bell and a wood block. Up to ten saved people, each with a year of age and obstacle years by the White Beryl (བཻཌཱུར་དཀར་པོ, bai DUr dkar po). On the Tibetan page, in the order of strength: the times within the day, the date's letters, the twelve links (རྟེན་འབྲེལ, rten 'brel), the hundred feet (རྐང་བརྒྱ, rkang brgya) with the fangs of eight bodies, the month heading and eclipses.
