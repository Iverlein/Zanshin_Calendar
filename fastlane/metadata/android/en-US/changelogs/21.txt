Festivals stand in painted frames: the Tibetan ones as in a thangka, now with the Nyingma days of Guru Rinpoche's birth, Longchenpa, Jigme Lingpa, Mipham and Dudjom Rinpoche; the 旧暦 ones in a round window in the manner of an Edo colour print. New on the 旧暦 page are the great days of the deities, each with its reading and its picture: Inari's 初午, Daikoku's day, Fudō's 初不動, Kannon's 四万六千日, Jizō's 地蔵盆, Amida's 十夜, 夏越の祓 and more. The rest of the Russian reads more naturally.
