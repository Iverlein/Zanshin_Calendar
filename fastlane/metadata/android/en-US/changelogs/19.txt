Read a dream, in the Reckonings: choose the part of the night or give the clock time, and tick the dream's images. Only the dawn dream is read: each tradition gives its verdict on each image, with one remedy for a bad dream, and nothing is summed. "The dreamer is ill" brings in the medical readings and their rule of the night. The clan is no longer asked. Anurādhā's good list now has rites for long life.
