# P.3908 numbers the chapter of the 建除 days 廿一 and has no chapter of the ten stems; a modern reference list gives a chapter 十干日得夢章 as 21 and the 建除 as 22. Does another manuscript of the book (S.5900 holds only the preface and chapter 1) have a ten-stem chapter, or is that reference's list an error?

RD375.

Narrowed (inv-vv, 2026-11-14): the manuscript's own page numbers the officers' chapter 廿一 and goes straight to the bad-dream taboos as 廿二 (cdb:i13-16; claim p3908-chapters-1-23-and-the-end-of-the-booklet), so the other list's order (stems 21, officers 22) is not P.3908's. No other copy with a ten-stem chapter was found: the NDL Lab search 周公解夢書 returned only unrelated Japanese titles; zh.wikisource has no text of the Dunhuang book (search 十干日得夢: no hit); ctext.org, IDP and Gallica answered bot checks and were not worked around. Whether a copy other than P.3908 has the chapter stays open.
