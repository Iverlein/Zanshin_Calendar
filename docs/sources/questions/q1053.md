# P.3908 numbers the chapter of the 建除 days 廿一 and has no chapter of the ten stems; a modern reference list gives a chapter 十干日得夢章 as 21 and the 建除 as 22. Does another manuscript of the book (S.5900 holds only the preface and chapter 1) have a ten-stem chapter, or is that reference's list an error?

RD375.

Narrowed (inv-vv, 2026-11-14): the manuscript's own page numbers the officers' chapter 廿一 and goes straight to the bad-dream taboos as 廿二 (cdb:i13-16; claim p3908-chapters-1-23-and-the-end-of-the-booklet), so the other list's order (stems 21, officers 22) is not P.3908's. No other copy with a ten-stem chapter was found: the NDL Lab search 周公解夢書 returned only unrelated Japanese titles; zh.wikisource has no text of the Dunhuang book (search 十干日得夢: no hit); ctext.org, IDP and Gallica answered bot checks and were not worked around. Whether a copy other than P.3908 has the chapter stays open.

Searched 2026-11-16 (inv-j): a web search on 周公解夢書 with P.3908, S.5900, 十干日得夢章, 建除 gave only general papers (Yuasa 1995 on the 新集周公解夢書, Takata's Zinbun reports, the zh.wikisource 周公解夢 print), none naming a ten-stem chapter or the chapter numbers; not opened further. Unchanged.

**Narrowed 2026-11-21 (inv-h).** Yuasa Kunihiro's 1995 paper (open on Osaka University's repository) lists the 23 chapters of the 新集周公解夢書: 19 十二支日得夢, 20 十二時得夢, 21 建除満日得夢, 22 悪夢為無禁忌, 23 献撰悪夢; there is no ten-stem chapter. So for P.3908 the modern reference's list is wrong (十干 for 十二支, and the numbers shifted by one). Claim p3908-23-chapters-yuasa-no-ten-stem-chapter. Whether another manuscript has a ten-stem chapter is not told; Yuasa names none. Open for other copies only.

## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-o)

Web searches (zh) on the Dunhuang copies: a note on the Dunhuang 周公解夢書 group names P.3908, S.5900, S.5900v, Дх.10787 and Дх.10787v (the last two a separate «解梦书», said to be independent of the others) and, as other 周公解夢書 残卷, S.2222 and P.3281+P.3685 (a bibliography's list, not opened on the manuscripts); no search result names a ten-stem chapter in any of them, and the daoinfo entry gives only P.3908 with no chapter list. Baidu Baike (403) and the Baidu Wenku papers were not reachable. Whether S.2222 or P.3281+P.3685 (a different book from the 新集) has a ten-stem section is not told. Open.
