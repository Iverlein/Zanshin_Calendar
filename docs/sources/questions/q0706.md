# «ཀུན་མཁྱེན་རིན་ཆེན་མཚན» and «རྟ་ལྡང / རྟ་ལྗང» of p. 70's homage

Who are the omniscient Rin chen mtshan and Rta ldang (or ljang) of the homage on p. 70? (RD189, corpus/wb/ch10.md)

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-ppp)

«རྟ་ལྗང» (rta ljang) is a name of the sun: "a wind-horse of the sun's good house" in the Tshig mdzod chen mo, "the sun" in Ives Waldo and Rangjung Yeshe; «rta ljang can» is Indra or the sun (Negi: haridaśva, sūrya). So the second verse is a figure of the sun and its rays and the reading «རྟ་ལྗང» fits better than «རྟ་ལྡང» (which has no headword): "by the learned and diligent Rta ljang [the sun] this light drawn out dispels the darkness of the wise" matches the verse before it («པདྨའི་གཉེན» "the friend of the lotus", the sun) and the verse after it («ཉི་འདིས» "this sun of new good explanation"). Whether a person of that name stands behind the figure (the author's teacher or the author himself) is not shown: BDRC's person records give nothing for «rta ldang», «rta ljang», «rin chen mtshan» or «kun mkhyen rin chen mtshan» (the autocomplete search by prefix, prefLabel and altLabel), and the phrase search «ཀུན་མཁྱེན་རིན་ཆེན་མཚན» and «རྟ་ལྡང་གིས་དྲངས་འོད་ཟེར» finds nothing in any etext, not even WB's own copies (the spelling of the machine text is perhaps off). The «kun mkhyen» is an honorific title and the "omniscient Rin chen mtshan" is left unidentified. Source: tmz:box9, wb.1:p70.
