# What is «ཡོ» («ཁ་ཡོ») in the rat day's «ཐེམ་བུར་མི་འཚག་ཡོ་ནས་འདྲེན» and what is served or led from it

On p. 224 the rat day's «ཐེམ་བུར་མི་འཚག་ཡོ་ནས་འདྲེན» (WB vol. 1, p. 133's table: «ཁ་ཡོ་ནས་འདྲེན»): the beer of the dead is not strained at the threshold (the verb is «to strain»), but what is «ཡོ» («ཁ་ཡོ»), the mouth of the jar askew, and what is served or led from it?
