# On the Gser thang reprint p. 144 l. 13, the glyph after «མ་ཧེ» is ink-filled: is it «རྟ» (horse) or another animal?

The fourth riding animal of the dream list; compare the Taikhang and Zhol prints.

## Answered 2026-11-10

Answered by RD294: the animal is «རྟ», the horse, clear on the Taikhang (I5878 img. 222) and the Zhol (I1KG1710 img. 840) by eye and by Yigdzin-1; the 1996 print is too small to tell it from ཧ and the reprint's ink-filled glyph is the same syllable.
