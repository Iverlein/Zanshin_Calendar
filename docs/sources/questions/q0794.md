# Does the Śramaṇā text's «yar ngo'i dus bzang po» mean any day of the waxing half, or does the tradition name one?

The text gives no lunar day.


Narrowed (inv-i, 2026-11-13): the phrase is a stock one: BDRC's full-text search for «ཡར་ངོའི་དུས་བཟང་པོ» (6 hits, 2026-11-13) finds it, as «tshes brgyad lta bu yar ngo'i dus bzang po la 'go brtsams pa'o», in the Sakya Ngag dbang kun dga' bsod nams's explanation of the Medicine Buddha rite (MW29307; the snippet: «like the eighth day, a good time of the waxing half, one begins»), and as «zla ba yar ngo'i dus bzang po» for the time of medicine in the *Rgyud bzhi'i 'grel ba mes po'i zhal lung* (MW8LS19192). Where a day is named it is given as an example (the eighth) and the phrase means a good time within the waxing half; the Śramaṇā text names none. The snippets are in Wylie, state etext; no passage was recorded.

## Answered 2026-11-14

No day is fixed. «ཡར་ངོའི་དུས་བཟང་པོ» (yar ngo'i dus bzang po) is a stock phrase for a good time within the waxing half, and where a text wants a particular day it adds one as an example. BDRC's full-text search (2026-11-14, 6 hits) finds the phrase in the Sakya Ngag dbang kun dga' bsod nams's explanation of the Medicine Buddha rite (MW29307, «tshes brgyad lta bu yar ngo'i dus bzang po la 'go brtsams pa'o», "one begins at a good time of the waxing half, like the eighth day"), in four medical works for the time of compounding medicine (MW8LS19192, MW20141, MW21659 and one more: «zla ba yar ngo'i dus bzang po», glossed in MW20141 as the days when the planets and mansions agree, "bdud rtsi'i sbyor ba'i nyin sogs gza' skar 'phrod sbyor legs pa'i dus"), and, as «yar ngo'i dus bzang por», in Jamgon Kongtrul's Rin chen gter mdzod (MW1KG14) and two ritual texts of Mipham (MW1PD76231), each time a rite begun "at a good time of the waxing half" with no day. So the phrase asks for a good (auspicious, planet-and-mansion fortunate) day of the bright fortnight, chosen by the practitioner; the Śramaṇā text names none, and no source found ties the phrase to one lunar day. The «tshes brgyad lta bu» of MW29307 is an example, not a rule.

Reserve: all snippets are Wylie of etexts (state etext), MW29307 and MW20141 and MW21659 search-only or unaligned; no passage was opened beyond the snippet, so the wording is a summary of the snippets.
