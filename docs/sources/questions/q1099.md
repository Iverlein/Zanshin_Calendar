# In the history's sentence on the other three sheets, is the reading «they were given» (the Marpa volume, the Bka' brgyud sngags mdzod) or «they were not given» (མ་གནང, the Shechen print, the Dpal spungs volume) the right one, and does the history mean that Mtshur ston kept the three back from the brothers' line?

RD383.

## Answered 2026-11-14

«Not given» (མ་གནང་བ) is the reading the story and the independent record bear out; the print readings were not settled by a scan, but the sense is. The history itself says that Mtshur ston Dbang nge gave the Breakthrough transference to the two brothers (dds.19:p97-102), that Bde gshegs rin chen got it unsealed while of the other three «he did not even say that they existed», and that the complete four went by another line, to the disciple of Lo ras chung ba (Snyag sgom), who is asked by Dwags po Mdo rtse (dds.19:p99-100). Padma dkar po's record of the teachings received independently gives the same two lines: the Breakthrough alone to the brothers, then 'Jang phu rgya ras, Stod lung 'Gar ras, Nyi ma lung pa, Bde legs rin chen, Gling; «all four» to Myags sgom Dge chung and on (dds.42:p165-166). So the three sheets were kept back from the brothers' line, and the sentence «of the other three sheets it stands that they were not given» (Shechen and Dpal spungs prints) says that; «given» (Marpa volume, Bka' brgyud sngags mdzod) can be read only as «given (to the other line)», which the next clause (the disciple of Lo ras chung ba asking and receiving all four) makes the same fact.
