# «བཅག» or «བཅུག» in the nose rite, and the nose of «སྣ་ཆོག»

In WB's departure (vol. 1, p. 377) the nose is «བཅག» (cut) or «བཅུག» (put in) in «སོ་སོའི་གཤེད་ཀྱིས་སྣ་བཅག་གོ»: Yigdzin-1 on the Zhol print reads བཅག; the 1996 etext, བཅུག. What is the sna of the sna chog (the nose of an image, of a figure of the escort, of the bride's lead)?

RD175 (2026-11-06), narrowed: Yigdzin-1 reads «བཅག» on the Zhol (img. 494 line 1) and the Taikhang (img. 511 line 1); at 9× a curl under ཅ on the Zhol may be its own foot or a ུ. Taikhang img. 511 not looked at by eye.

RD175 (2026-11-07, second run): Taikhang img. 511 and Gser thang img. 372 read «སྣ་བཅག་གོ»; only the 1996 etext has «བཅུག»; Zhol undecided. Likely answered: བཅག.
