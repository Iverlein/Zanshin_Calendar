# Two pegs on the nine-red, none on the eight-white

P. 127 sets two pegs on the nine-red (a red spear guarding the *btsad po* and the patron; an arrow with a mirror guarding the elders) and none on the eight-white. Is the first a slip for the eight-white?

**Narrowed 2026-11-03 (sweep from RD137).** Both pegs stand on the nine-red in the Zhol print (img. 813, ll. 2-3), the Gser thang reprint (img. 133) and the 1996 print, and the eight-white has none (its line holds only «བརྒྱད་དཀར་སྟེང་བཟང་ཁྲོམ་འཚོག་བྱ», the good assembly); the counting (each other mewa has one peg line, the eight-white none) is the case for a slip, but it is the same on all three prints, so a slip would be older than all of them (reckonings.md, *The pegs on the nine-red*).
