# Is Zhe chen rgyal tshab's «མར་ངོའི་མཚན་མོ་དང་། ཡར་ངོའི་ཉིན་མོ» a rule that the waning tenth's rite is held at night and the waxing tenth's by day?

Raised by the practice-days investigation (pd-f, 2026-11-16); see the cited passages.

## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-w)

Context of «མར་ངོའི་མཚན་མོ་དང་། ཡར་ངོའི་ཉིན་མོ» (BDRC exact search: a single hit, Zhe chen rgyal tshab's works MW1PD133163): it follows «if one wants the practice to grow, the waxing tenth's offering should be taken as chief» and is followed by «is a special great occasion of swift accomplishment (དངོས་གྲུབ་མྱུར་བའི་དུས་ཆེན་ཁྱད་པར་ཅན)», on which the great vajra-holders kept both tenths. The sentence names no rite and no hour for either tenth; read plainly, «night of the waning half» and «day of the waxing half» are the two halves' own times, not a rule that the waning tenth's rite is held at night. The same pair, reversed, occurs in a Drukpa work on the length of day and night (MW3CN2232: «ཡར་ངོའི་མཚན་མོ་དང་། མར་ངོའི་ཉིན་མོ»), as a statement about the halves. Not answered: the author does not say it explicitly; narrowed to «a statement about the two halves, not an hour rule» on the sentence alone.
