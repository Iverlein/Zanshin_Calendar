# p. 185's «རིག་བྱེད་མཁའ་མེ» and «སྐར་དྲུག་ཞེ་ལྔ»

p. 185, «རིག་བྱེད་མཁའ་མེ» (the difference of the seven days given to it) and «སྐར་དྲུག་ཞེ་ལྔ» (taken from twice): what quantities are these, and how does the author's place differ from the *Lamp that Makes the Solstice Clear*'s? (RD186, corpus/wb/ch16.md)

## Narrowed 2026-11-13

«རིག་བྱེད་མཁའ་མེ» (4, 0, 3 by the number words) is 4 *chu tshod* 30 *srang*: one house-day (*khyim zhag*) of the sun, the thirtieth of a house of 135 *chu tshod* (p. 51's divisor), so that the seven days' difference given to it is 7 · 4;30 = 31;30, the 31 *chu tshod* 30 *srang* of the winter solstice's measure (q676). The second route, «twenty-three days' difference, subtract «སྐར་དྲུག་ཞེ་ལྔ» twice», is not placed: 135 − 23 · 4;30 = 31;30 gives the same figure without the subtraction, and «སྐར་དྲུག་ཞེ་ལྔ» (6;45 if «ཞེ་ལྔ» is 45) is 1½ house-days, but how twice that quantity is taken from the 23 days was not worked out. Left open for that half.

## Answered 2026-11-17

Answered as to the quantities (2026-11-17, inv-a). «རིག་བྱེད་མཁའ་མེ» is 4 *chu tshod* 30 *srang*, one house-day (as narrowed 2026-11-13). «སྐར་དྲུག་ཞེ་ལྔ» is 6 mansions 45 *chu tshod*: the *Lamp that Makes the Solstice Clear*'s summer-solstice measure at the middle Brahmā-line of the southern continent, as the Gser thang commentary *Yang dag lam gyi 'jug sgo* (BDRC I3CN4644, vol. 4, pp. 45-47) quotes it («སྐར་དྲུག་ཆུ་ཚོད་ཞེ་ལྔ»), beside «སྐར་མཚོ་དབྱུག་གུ་མཁའ་མེ» (4 mansions 30 *chu tshod*) for the eastern line; they are 2;15 = 135 *chu tshod* = thirty house-days apart. The author's place is the eastern figure plus 7 · 4;30 or the middle one minus 23 · 4;30, and both are 5 mansions 1 *chu tshod* 30 *srang*, which is what WB's number words «སྐར་དབང་དབྱུག་གཟུགས་མཁའ་མེ» say. So the 'difference' of the author's place from the *Lamp*'s is only that the *Lamp* gives the two Brahmā-lines' measures and WB the place seven days from the eastern one. Claim [wb-solstice-summer-two-routes](../claims/wb-solstice-summer-two-routes.md). The word «གཉིས» after «ཕྲི་བ» ('twice' or 'the second way') is not decided by the figures; the commentary is a machine reading (etext), whose digits agree with the check.
