# p. 185's «རིག་བྱེད་མཁའ་མེ» and «སྐར་དྲུག་ཞེ་ལྔ»

p. 185, «རིག་བྱེད་མཁའ་མེ» (the difference of the seven days given to it) and «སྐར་དྲུག་ཞེ་ལྔ» (taken from twice): what quantities are these, and how does the author's place differ from the *Lamp that Makes the Solstice Clear*'s? (RD186, corpus/wb/ch16.md)

## Narrowed 2026-11-13

«རིག་བྱེད་མཁའ་མེ» (4, 0, 3 by the number words) is 4 *chu tshod* 30 *srang*: one house-day (*khyim zhag*) of the sun, the thirtieth of a house of 135 *chu tshod* (p. 51's divisor), so that the seven days' difference given to it is 7 · 4;30 = 31;30, the 31 *chu tshod* 30 *srang* of the winter solstice's measure (q676). The second route, «twenty-three days' difference, subtract «སྐར་དྲུག་ཞེ་ལྔ» twice», is not placed: 135 − 23 · 4;30 = 31;30 gives the same figure without the subtraction, and «སྐར་དྲུག་ཞེ་ལྔ» (6;45 if «ཞེ་ལྔ» is 45) is 1½ house-days, but how twice that quantity is taken from the 23 days was not worked out. Left open for that half.
