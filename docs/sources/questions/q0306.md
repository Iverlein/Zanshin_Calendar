# Is the check order the order of the eight fighting pebbles

The readings do not always fit the board's grouping: the first two checks of points 1, 2 and 6 both speak of the forefathers' or the father's-and-uncles' place, though the notes make the second pebble one of the "lower three", oneself. Is the check order the order of the eight fighting pebbles at all?

**Supported 2026-10-23 (RD47, [claims/thirty-board-places-speckled.md](../claims/thirty-board-places-speckled.md)).** Reply 138 (ys:i496) reads the horse point's board by places, the forefathers, the last part of life and the demons in the middle, matching the notes' upper, lower and middle groups; stated for that point only. It also explains "all speckled is good": the board read by its base, not as four white and four black.
