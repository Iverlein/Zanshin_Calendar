# UT's keying of harms to the fifteen places: a restatement of WB p. 172's counts?

In UT (pp. 202–204 printed) the year-stores' pairs, ox and sheep, dog and dragon, bird and hare, pig and snake, bring the same harms as WB p. 172's counts three and eight up and down; is UT's keying of the harms to the fifteen places a restatement of those counts, and which place is which count? (RD179; the pig-snake pair has the slayer in UT, the earth door in WB.)

INV-1107 (2026-11-07), narrowed: set side by side (wb.2:p171-172, ut:p224-226) the four counts of WB fall on UT's places two, four, five, six: three down (ox or sheep, ruin and lawsuit) = the second place's ox store; three up (bird or monkey, separation) = the fourth place's hare store (UT has bird and hare); eight up (dog or dragon, sky door) = the fifth place; eight down (pig or snake, earth door) = the sixth place (UT: the slayer). UT's first (rat, horse) and third (tiger, monkey) places have no count in WB's four. UT names no count, so 'a restatement' is likely by the pairs but not said.
