# Does the machine's order of hours against animals on pp. 360–361 hold on the scan

Pp. 360–361's twelve double-hours (the Hare to the Tiger, "the hour is a sharp weapon") give works for each; the machine's order of hours against animals (Dragon at sunrise, Snake at noon, Horse half past noon) is unchecked; read it digit by name on the scan before any use.
