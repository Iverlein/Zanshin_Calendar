# Does the machine's order of hours against animals on pp. 360–361 hold on the scan

Pp. 360–361's twelve double-hours (the Hare to the Tiger, "the hour is a sharp weapon") give works for each; the machine's order of hours against animals (Dragon at sunrise, Snake at noon, Horse half past noon) is unchecked; read it digit by name on the scan before any use.

**2026-11-05 (RD158).** The text the corpus held under pp. 360–361 is really pp. 358–359, moved to wb.2:p358-359, still machine; nothing new read for this question.

RD183 (2026-11-06): the order of the hours is answered (the hours verse of p. 359 runs from the Hare in the usual twelve-animal order; «ཉི་ཕྱེད» is noon, «ཉི་དྲོས» the forenoon, so the machine gloss 'Snake at noon, Horse half past noon' is wrong). The works of each hour were not read syllable by syllable.
