# Ch. 3 p. 23 line 12: Nāgārjuna's year prints (༧༡༦༧) and (༢༩༤)

On p. 23 line 12 the reprint prints Nāgārjuna's year as (༧༡༦༧) and the next figure with a middle sign like a nine, (༢༩༤) for 204. Are these misprints of the reprint's own typesetting, or does the Beijing 1996 print have the same figures? (The eye read of 2026-11-08 takes (༧༡༦༧) as a misprint of the first digit for 2167, bearing on q731.) RD197c (2026-11-08).

## Answered 2026-11-12

RD213x, 2026-11-12: the 1996 print (I1KG12906 img. 33) has the same glyph shapes in both figures as the Gser thang reprint, so the figures are not a slip of the reprint's own typesetting; they stand in both prints (passage wb.1:p23-27).
