# Does the Bṛhaspati Svapnādhyāya (Penn Ms. Coll. 390 item 1871; Negelein's i, Leipzig 1178) read three fortnights and a month as Negelein prints it, or eight months, three months, half a month?

Penn images not read (server too slow).

## Answered 2026-11-10

RD357: Penn Ms. Coll. 390 item 1871 (read on Penn's IIIF images, f. 1-4) reads «द्वितीये चाष्टभिर्मासैस्त्रिभिर्मासैस्त्रियामके । चतुर्थे प्रहरे स्वप्नो मासैकेन विपच्यते»: eight months, three months and a month for the fourth watch, not three fortnights; it is not Negelein's i. The colophon (f. 4r) «इति बृहस्पत्युक्तस्वप्नाध्यायः समाप्तः» names no scribe and no date.
