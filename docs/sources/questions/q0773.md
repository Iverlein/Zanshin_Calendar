# The unit of the eclipse tables' figures on pp. 60–62 and the four courses

What is the unit of the table's figures on pp. 60–62 (the «cut surplus» of Rāhu's face and the moon multiplied by 8280): sixtieths of a chu tshod, of a day, or a count of asterism-sixtieths; and what are the «four courses» (slow, crooked, arising, fast) in the true reckoning that WB means? RD203 (2026-11-08).

Narrowed (inv-j, 2026-11-13): by the text's own steps on p. 60 (cut remainder in chu tshod × 60 to chu srang, divided by 15) the table's figures are counted in units of 15 chu srang, a quarter of a chu tshod; the 8280 belongs to the Abridged Tantra's long way, which WB does not use, and the four courses are named only in the table's limits and in p. 62's corrections, no definition found. Claim wb-ch9-eclipse-table-figures-in-quarter-chu-tshod-and-corrections-in-chu-srang.

## Answered 2026-11-14

The four courses are settled by WB's own text, and the unit by its own steps. (1) The four courses (འགྲོས་བཞི) are the moon's courses by foot, shown for each month, not a stretch of the eclipse reckoning: p. 59 says «ཟླ་རེར་འགྲོས་བཞིའི་མཚན་མ་སྟོན། དེ་ཕྱིར་རིལ་པོར་ཆེས ༡༥ བསྲེས་ལ། མ་ནུས ༡༤ བགོས་ལྷག་ཟླ་རྐང་བཟུང་། རྙེད་མཉམ་སྔ་རྐང་མྱུར་འགྲོས་ཤར། ཕྱི་རྐང་དལ་འགྲོས་ལྷོ་རུ་བྱེད། མི་མཉམ་སྔ་རྐང་འཁྱོག་འགྲོས་ནུབ། ཕྱི་རྐང་འབྱུང་འགྲོས་བྱེད་དེ་བྱང་»: the tithi count of the full moon plus 15, divided by 14, the remainder takes the moon's foot; with the quotient even the earlier foot is the fast course (east), the later foot the slow course (south); with it odd the earlier foot is the crooked course (west), the later foot the arising course (north). They are the four courses of the claim wb-planet-four-courses (p. 40, ch. 6) taken for the moon. The table of pp. 60-62 gives, for each size of the portion seized, the four courses' figures, p. 62 says all of them differ by the same as the total and the half («ཆ་འཛིན་ཀུན་ལ་འགྲོས་བཞི་ཡི། ཁྱད་པར་རིལ་ཕྱེད་གཉིས་དང་མཚུངས»), and the corrections of p. 62 belong to them. (2) The unit: p. 60 turns the cut remainder in chu tshod into chu srang («མཁའ་རོས ༦༠ བསྒྱུར») and divides by 15 («འབྱུང་ཟླས ༡༥»), so the table's figures count units of 15 chu srang, a quarter of a chu tshod of the cut remainder; they are not sixtieths of a day, and no count of asterism-sixtieths enters. The chu tshod here measure the moon's disc: the Vimalaprabhā passage WB quotes on p. 68 says the disc is sixty chu tshod, sixteen parts, and that the remainder when Rāhu's motion cuts the moon's is pure for a total seizing and thirty left for half (p. 68, «དཀྱིལ་འཁོར་ཆུ་ཚོད་དྲུག་ཅུ་སྟེ་ཟླ་བའི་ཆ་བཅུ་དྲུག»). (3) The 8280 is not part of the way WB gives: p. 60 calls the Abridged Tantra's way of the size «slightly difficult and not understood», and p. 66 (the sun) names the multiplication by 8280 together with "the eight key points" as the detailed way that the abbreviated one does without. What 8280 measures is still not stated. Not found: a published commentary (Henning's book, which would give the unit in modern terms, could not be opened: the dokumen.pub copy was down, Janson's paper answers a certificate error) that restates the table.
