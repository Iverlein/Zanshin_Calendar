# In ch. 91's closing causes, what is the failing the chapter treats, and what are ཕྱུ, དྲི་ཆེན and བ་ར in its remedies?

Read on the scan (ch. 91, p. 493); the terms of the remedies are not explained in the lexica consulted.

## Answered 2026-11-14

Answered from the pool's own readings (st:p493 on the scan, RD227, RD264, RD294, RD304, RD356).

The failing: the closing part of ch. 91 (the chapter on making the flavour, ro tsa) gives the failings of the matter by cause: cold wind of the kidneys (medicines), heat (pills with salt, with beer), poison (its outer, inner and secret signs identified, killed by its antidote, the body guarded) and a spirit (gdon), whose sign is a dream in which women lead the dreamer away (the commentaries add: he is attached to them, kisses and embraces them, and seed is drawn out). The purpose clause (dgos pa) is that one without illness may lie with women unhindered in every season: the chapter treats the failing of the matter (the loss of the vigour of union) by its causes.

The terms. ཕྱུ is a misreading of ཕྱྭ (phywa): the Serthang scan at 10x shows the wa-zur, and the Bhutan and Drathang prints agree. མོ་ཕྱྭ (mo phywa) is one compound, divination and its omen, the means of finding which spirit it is (the Blue Beryl: «མོ་ཕྱྭ་བྱས་ནས་གདོན་ངོས་བཟུང་»; the dictionaries list མོ་ཕྱྭ as the divination's omen); the rite of service (rim gro) and the dependent link (rten 'brel) follow once the spirit is known. དྲི་ཆེན (dri chen) is plain dung (the commentaries: the patient's own dung and urine in the Blue Beryl; the dung he voided in the Drikung commentary), not a drug name. བ་ར (ba ra) is "cattle and goats", so «བ་རའི་འོ་མ» is herd milk (Tshig mdzod chen mo, Ives Waldo); mixed with the dung it is rubbed on the soles. Both bracketed remedies, the milk with dung and the knotted thread, are notes of the commentary tradition worked into the Serthang text and the Derge and Chag-po-ri prints, not in the Drathang verse. What no commentary says is why a dream of being led away calls for these two substances.
