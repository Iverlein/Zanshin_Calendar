# The light-clearer's corner in the strip charts against the verse's grave, neighbour and slayer

The speckled banner and the garuda are placed by vol. 2 p. 219 and agree in 12 of 12 charts; the light-clearer (snang sel) is put on the grave, the downward neighbour or the upward slayer by the month's place in its season, and the charts set it in one corner per group of three months (tiger, horse, dog; hare, pig; dragon, monkey, rat; snake, bird, ox); the sheep chart has none. Which animal is each month's grave, neighbour and slayer, and does the corner agree? (RD199)
