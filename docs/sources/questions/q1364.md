# The light-clearer's corner in the strip charts against the verse's grave, neighbour and slayer

The speckled banner and the garuda are placed by vol. 2 p. 219 and agree in 12 of 12 charts; the light-clearer (snang sel) is put on the grave, the downward neighbour or the upward slayer by the month's place in its season, and the charts set it in one corner per group of three months (tiger, horse, dog; hare, pig; dragon, monkey, rat; snake, bird, ox); the sheep chart has none. Which animal is each month's grave, neighbour and slayer, and does the corner agree? (RD199)

## Narrowed 2026-11-24 (inv-l)

Checked against vol. 2 pp. 217 and 219 (the 1996 etext, quoted in year-month-earth-lords.md) and the ring of the strip charts (the corners are the places of dragon top-left, sheep top-right, dog bottom-right, ox bottom-left, KP's order of kp:i87c); claims wb1-ch13-strip-charts-grave-taker-in-the-grave-corner and wb1-ch13-strip-charts-ro-dgu-by-month.

What the corners are: the four corners are exactly the four grave animals (ox, sheep, dog, dragon, wb-dur-is-one-of-the-four-slayer-animals), so the light-clearer is set on a grave animal's place in every chart that has it. The corner by month, from wb.1:p142-147r: tiger, horse, dog sheep; hare, pig (and sheep, whose cell is empty) dragon; dragon, monkey, rat ox; snake, bird, ox dog.

First months (tiger, snake, monkey, pig; the verse's «དུར་གྱི་སྟེང»): the corner is sheep, dog, ox, dragon. That is the element order that q515 gives for Lha mo tshul khrims and the Utpala (wood sheep, fire dog, metal ox, water dragon) and not the order of WB's ch. 23 triads (dog, ox, dragon, sheep). It is also not where the grave-taker's head stands in the same months by p. 217 (tiger ox, snake dragon, monkey sheep, pig dog: the animal opposite, six places on).

Middle months (hare, horse, bird, rat; «མར་གྱི་ཁྱིམ་མཚེས», the lower neighbour): the corner is dragon, sheep, dog, ox, the same animal as the grave-taker's head by p. 217 in those months (hare dragon, horse sheep, bird dog, rat ox), 4 of 4, and the animal next after the month's own in the cycle (hare then dragon, horse then sheep, bird then dog, rat then ox).

Last months (dragon, sheep, dog, ox; «ཡར་གྱི་གཤེད», the upper slayer): the corner is ox, dragon, sheep, dog, in each case the animal three places before the month's own.

So the three kinds of month give one corner for the three months of a triad (tiger, horse, dog the sheep, and so on) because the three rules (five places on from the first month, one on from the middle, three back from the last) fall on one animal when the months are four apart; the charts do show this single place per triad, and the verse's threefold wording is consistent with it. This is arithmetic on the corners, not a statement of the text: the verse names no animal, and what «མར་གྱི་ཁྱིམ་མཚེས» and «ཡར་གྱི་གཤེད» are is not glossed (PLAN.md, dead ends of q28, q444, q515). Not settled: whether the cell stands for the grave or for the lord's own animal; the sheep chart's missing light-clearer; the corner of each chart rests on the machine-eye readings of wb.1:p142-147r (identical printed blocks matched, looked at on the crop).
