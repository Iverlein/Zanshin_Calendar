# Is «mtshan brtas» in «tho rangs mtshan brtas 'di ltar byung» (the third Karmapa) a spelling of «mtshan brtags/rtags», and does it mean the following dream or a separate omen?

RD384.


Narrowed (inv-aa, 2026-11-14): the string «མཚན་བརྟས» has 8 hits in BDRC's full text: the Karmapa's line and seven others, all «དགེ་མཚན་བརྟས» in verse (the Seventh Lamo's life by Bla kho 'Jigs med 'phrin las rgya mtsho, two copies of a modern poem, Kun dga' dpal ldan rgya mtsho's verses, and Rdo ston's commentary in a garbled copy) and «nyin mtshan brtas pa» in Jigs med gling pa's Bla ma dgongs 'dus rite (day and night grown strong); in these «brtas» is the participle «grown, flourished» (rgyas pa'am dar ba, Tshig mdzod chen mo), so the form is a real word and the Karmapa's line can be read as it stands, «at dawn the sign (that) flourished came thus». No dictionary has «mtshan brtas» or «mtshan brtags» as a headword. For «mtshan brtags/rtags» as the sign: the Nyingma Tantra of the Black Smith has the dawn line «ཐོ་རངས་མཚན་མའི་རྟགས་ལ་བརྟགས» (at dawn examine the mark of the sign; lgd.38:p7), the same dawn with the same two words «mtshan ma, rtags, brtags»: the Karmapa's «mtshan brtas» may be a spelling of that sense, but it is no proof, and the Karmapa's line is the one place where the line stands. Still one witness for the whole line (q1142).
