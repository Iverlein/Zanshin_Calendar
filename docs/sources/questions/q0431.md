# How long is one mtho of the digging rite's figure

How long is one *mtho* (མཐོ) of the digging figure on p. 387 on a plot: a fixed length, or the plot's own measure divided by ten? Left open by q403's answer (RD64).

## Answered 2026-10-28

One *mtho* is a tenth of the plot. Ch. 32 p. 281 (the same figure of the earth lord Lto-'phye) says the figure's extent equals the plot's; the plot is divided into ten great parts a side, each of nine small parts, and the body is ten *mtho*. The worked example (stake, face, heart) is 3 great parts, 27 small parts. Not settled: the navel's six finger-breadths on p. 387 against ch. 32's whole-*mtho* stake (new question). (RD66)
