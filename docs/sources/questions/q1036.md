# Which text and author is the death-sign teaching «ནམ་ཕྱེད་ཀྱི་རྨི་ལམ་བརྟག་པ» (the midnight dream, with the Gya skag mountain and the naked donkey rider)? BDRC puts it under the Gdams ngag mdzod, under Mar pa Chos kyi blo gros, and in a Lho brag pa collection.

RD367.

## Answered 2026-11-10

RD374: the midnight-dream teaching (ནམ་ཕྱེད་ཀྱི་རྨི་ལམ་བརྟག་པ) is one text found in six places, the companion method «'pho ba spyi rtol ma'i lhan thabs» ending the Lho brag pa transference text (Gdams ngag mdzod, Bka' brgyud sngags mdzod, Marpa's collected works), part of Marpa's four sheets of the whispered lineage; BDRC credits Marpa Chos kyi blo gros but the history speaks of Marpa in the third person and a later holder says «I»: anonymous in its words. It names the midnight dream with three images and no span of life. Etext only; the Gdams ngag mdzod page checked against the scan (img. 173-174).
