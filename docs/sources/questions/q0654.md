# The byi sker and the sa khyi of the rat day's wool-thread remedy

What is the byi sker of SY's «བལ་སྐུད་བྱི་སྐེར་བཏགས་པས་ཐུབ» (printed p. 345): the rat's neck, a house-mouse's, or a name; and is the sa khyi of the Drigung verse work's «བལ་གྱི་སྐུད་པ་ས་ཁྱི་སྐོར་བཏགས» the earth-dog of the year or a figure of dough? (RD162, etext only)

RD173 (2026-11-06), narrowed: both lines are WB's own (vol. 2, p. 359: «བལ་སྐུད་ཀྱི་སྐེར་བཏགས་པས་ཐུབ», a wool thread tied at the neck, «སྐེར»); SY's «བྱི་སྐེར» and the verse work's «ས་ཁྱི་སྐོར» are copyists' variants of it, so no earth-dog and no dough figure; whose neck the line leaves unsaid. ([word-hunts.md](../word-hunts.md), *The catalogue's questions, the second round*)

RD173 (2026-11-06), narrowed: WB's own line (p. 359) is «བལ་སྐུད་ཀྱི་སྐེར་བཏགས», a wool thread tied at the neck («སྐེར»); SY's «བྱི་སྐེར» and the verse work's «ས་ཁྱི་སྐོར» are copyists' variants of it, so the sa khyi is not the year's earth-dog nor a dough figure. Whose neck is open. The rat day's «ཆང་རྫས» is the versifier's wording (q655).
