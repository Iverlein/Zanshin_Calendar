# Are the «staff» (dbyu gu, kd.3:p140) and the «water-hour» (chu tshod, pp. 141-143) the same third place of the five places in the Mchog dga'?

RD369.

## Answered 2026-11-13

Yes. The Mchog dga' itself calls the third place both: «sixty water-srang are called a staff or one water-hour» (ch. 1, printed p. 32), and ch. 2 names the places below the part as «water-hour, water-srang, breaths» where p. 120 has «staff, water-srang, breath». One unit, 60 to the day-and-night, five to a junction, seven and a half to a watch. The question's page numbers are the etext's (printed p. 120 = etext p. 140). Claim mchog-dga-dbyu-gu-and-chu-tshod-one-unit-sixty-to-the-day-and-night; the book also says that counts with four «staffs» to the day-and-night exist elsewhere, which are not this unit.
