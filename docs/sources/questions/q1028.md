# What do the two groups of day branches in each month of the table of the days a dream is not told mean (the gap or slash before the last group: mdz:p11, mdz:i234427b, the 萬歳大雑書日用寳 image 121)? No print gives a legend.

RD368.

## Answered 2026-11-13

No answer in the sources reached; a dead end after the prints named. No print gives a legend: the only wording about the table is the closing line of the 萬歳大雑書日用寳, 右の日ミる夢ハ善悪とも語らず, "of the dreams seen on the days above, good and bad alike nothing is told", and it says what the table is for, not what the two groups of each month are (mdz:i183121, RD371 answer 2). The third copy read again, the 清明秘鈔夢判事's neighbours (Tohoku THKK-22631 and the Koizumi copy), has a different table (the charm by day branch, mdz:i65134) and no month columns. The ORMK, TOKY and 国文研 prints all show the gap, no printed slash (mdz:i234427b). A reader of the almanac tradition must say whether the second group is the days with a stronger ban.

## Searched 2026-11-14 (inv-yyy), no answer

The NDL catalogue (ndl.py) for 不語夢日 and 語夢日 has no record; for 夢を見て語らざる日 it has two, the 永代大雑書大成 (1883, pid 759978) and the 吉凶禍福夢判断 (1890, pid 760913), and the full-text API answers «This PID is not allowed» for both (as in the PLAN's earlier note), so neither can be read for a legend. The 拾芥抄 entry (mdz:i772010) stays the oldest form: a two-column small note with the note 件日語吉夢、滅成惡夢凶也 and no legend for the columns. The meaning of the two groups is stated in no text reached.

## Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-z4)

Two older witnesses, neither of which explains the second line of a cell. (1) The Heian poetess Akazome Emon's collection (book 3) speaks of «夢かたらぬ日といふもの», the days on which a dream is not told, copied out as a list she kept; the Ruijū meibutsukō (NDL 898153, image 47) quotes it under 夢不語日, with no table (mdz:i898153). So the days are in use by about 1000. (2) The kana calendar of Jōō 2 (1223), as printed in the Kojiruien (NDL 897744, image 13), marks «ゆめかたらず» twice within the first seven days of its sixth month, in the lower column of daily notes, with the commentary «ゆめかたらずノ類、今ト同ジカラズ»: so a mark on single days stands in a calendar before the table of months and branches is quoted in the 拾芥抄; the print shows nothing of a slash or two groups. The 拾芥抄's own table (故実叢書, NDL 898152, image 77, read again on the page: each month's cell has two lines in small type, e.g. 二月 巳申 / 亥酉, under the rule 件日語吉夢、滅成悪夢凶也) gives the rule but no legend for the lines. Still open: what the two groups of one cell are.
