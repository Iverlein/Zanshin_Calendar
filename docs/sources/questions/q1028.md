# What do the two groups of day branches in each month of the table of the days a dream is not told mean (the gap or slash before the last group: mdz:p11, mdz:i234427b, the 萬歳大雑書日用寳 image 121)? No print gives a legend.

RD368.

## Answered 2026-11-13

No answer in the sources reached; a dead end after the prints named. No print gives a legend: the only wording about the table is the closing line of the 萬歳大雑書日用寳, 右の日ミる夢ハ善悪とも語らず, "of the dreams seen on the days above, good and bad alike nothing is told", and it says what the table is for, not what the two groups of each month are (mdz:i183121, RD371 answer 2). The third copy read again, the 清明秘鈔夢判事's neighbours (Tohoku THKK-22631 and the Koizumi copy), has a different table (the charm by day branch, mdz:i65134) and no month columns. The ORMK, TOKY and 国文研 prints all show the gap, no printed slash (mdz:i234427b). A reader of the almanac tradition must say whether the second group is the days with a stronger ban.
