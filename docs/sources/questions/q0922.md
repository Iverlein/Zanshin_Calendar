# What does the Moonbeams' «མཚན་སྟོད་ཕྱེད་པ་ཡན་ཆད» mean: up to the half of the first part of the night, or up to the half (midnight) of the night?

RD313; Zur mkhar ba and the Blue Beryl read the second («ནམ་ཕྱེད་ཡན་ཆད»), the Moonbeams' own words allow both.
