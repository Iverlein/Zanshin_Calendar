# The Four Tantras' line about the spirit entering has the same «རྨི་ལམ་ཟ་ཟི་མང»; Khro ru adds a rite there (I5622 p. 341) and treats it as the spirit's sign, against the fever places (I5621 p. 120): is the dream the sick one's symptom or the spirit's sign, and does the Tantra mean one by both lines?

RD316.

## Answered 2026-11-10

Answered by RD321 (2026-11-10), the premise corrected: the Serthang verse of ch. 81 names the five ways of entering and has no dream line; the second way's 'many confused dreams' is the commentators' own clause (Blue Beryl, Khro ru, Drikung, Skyem pa), with the rite against the spirit and the gathering of merit. In ch. 15's fever and the fevers of ch. 23 and 27 the line is a root line and the dream one of the sick body's signs; in ch. 81 it is the spirit's entering, Skyem pa glossing «རྨི་ལམ་ཟ་ཟི་གདོན་རྨི་བ», 'dreaming of the spirit'. No commentary defines ཟ་ཟི, and none says the Tantra means one by both lines. The ch. 81 verse was read on the Serthang only. Claim st-dream-za-zi-fever-versus-ch81-commentaries.
