# Who wrote or compiled the three dream examinations of the third section (Śramaṇā, Śākyamuni, Tārā) in the 'Brong rtse be'u bum?

RD315; the print names no author for any of the three and the editor's words give only the book's owner, 'Brong rtse Lha btsun Rin chen rgya mtsho.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-w)

Not answered further; nothing in the published sources names a compiler of the set. The pool's claims stand: the print names none (tdr-dream-set-names-no-compiler), the Śramaṇā rite is the text that stands in the First Dalai Lama's collected works and is ascribed to him by BDRC on its place there (brong-rtse-dream-set-no-author-sramana-in-dalai-lama-works), the Śākyamuni and White Tārā rites have no other carrier. New searches on BDRC's full text: the homage verse's first line «མན་ངག་རྒྱ་མཚོའི་གླིང་ལ་དབང་བསྒྱུར་བའི་དེད་དཔོན» 0 hits as written (the etext spaces the words), the two lines «གནས་སྐབས་མཐར་ཐུག་ཚེ་འདིའི་དགོས་འདོད་ཀུན» 2 hits, the 'Brong rtse etext and the Dge 'dun grub pa miscellany (MW24769, the Śramaṇā text already in the pool), «ཤྲ་མ་ཎའི་གདམས་པ་འདི་འདྲ་བ་མེད» 1 hit, the 'Brong rtse etext; so the homage verse and the Śramaṇā rite have two carriers and no third, and no colophon in either names a compiler. Whether Lha btsun took the set from the Dalai Lama's collection or the other way is not told.
