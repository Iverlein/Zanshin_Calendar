# Who wrote or compiled the three dream examinations of the third section (Śramaṇā, Śākyamuni, Tārā) in the 'Brong rtse be'u bum?

RD315; the print names no author for any of the three and the editor's words give only the book's owner, 'Brong rtse Lha btsun Rin chen rgya mtsho.
