# «ར་བར», where the living's year pebble is laid

What is «ར་བར» (dz:i055-057, f. 26b, l. 1), where the living's year pebble is laid, two of middling size?

RD166 (2026-11-06): beyond the grammar: a place on the board, an enclosure; «ར་བར» as a placing of a pebble is found in no other etext. Dead end.

**Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-h).** The sentence is «དང་པོ་བརྩི་བྱའི་ལོ་རྡེལ་ནི། ར་བར་རྡེལ་གཉིས་འབྲིང་པོར་རེ། རང་ཕྱོགས་འབྱུང་བའི་ཁ་དོག་བཞིན།» after «བཟའ་སྐོར་ཕྱེ་ཡིས་བར་བཅད་ལ» (the households set apart by partitions of flour). The dictionaries have «ར་བ» as enclosure, fence, wall, courtyard and, in Ives Waldo, "place where certain activity is done" (also the *Tshig mdzod chen mo* and the *Bod yig tshig gter rgya mtsho*: the inside of an enclosure; Steinert's `locate.py dict`, «ra ba»), so «ར་བར» can be «ར་བ» with the terminative, "in the enclosure", the household's partitioned square on the cloth; this fits the flour partitions of the line before and takes no emendation. It is a reading from the grammar and the dictionaries, not a text that says so; BDRC phrase search «ར་བར་རྡེལ་གཉིས་འབྲིང་པོར་རེ» finds no copy of the line (3 hits, none the line: the Kun 'dus, a Khams stod anthology, the Bzo rtsis zlos gar). Open, narrowed.
