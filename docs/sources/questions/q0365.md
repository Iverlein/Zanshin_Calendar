# For whom the four reckonings of «བརྗེ» are

For whom are the four reckonings of «བརྗེ» counted from the five rats (WB vol. 2, p. 23, l. 22): monks (བཙུན, btsun, or a form of «བྲེ»), as their vajra, discipline and begging suggest?

**In part 2026-10-25 (RD51, [claims/ch27-brje-set-is-the-monk.md](../claims/ch27-brje-set-is-the-monk.md)).** «བརྗེའི» is read as printed; by content (vajra, discipline, begging) and its place between the king's set and the mantrin's set it is the monk's set, matching «སྔགས་བཙུན» in the close. That «བརྗེ» spells བཙུན is not shown.
