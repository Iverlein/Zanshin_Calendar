# Does MW20396 p. 164 (the Kaḥ thog history that enumerates Nam mkha' rdo rje's sems sde pra khrid, BDRC person P7962) date him or the Nyang-tradition instruction (q1145)?

Raised by the 2026-11-13 investigation n.

## Narrowed 2026-11-13 (inv-s)

MW20396 is «Kaḥ thog pa'i lo rgyus mdor bsdus» by 'Jam dbyangs rgyal mtshan (Chengdu, Si khron mi rigs dpe skrun khang, 1996, 240 p.), a modern short history; the page is not in an etext, so its words on Nam mkha' rdo rje were not read; BDRC's own note says only «enumerates his sems sde pra khrid». What the record chain does show goes against the 1393 inference of q1145. BDRC holds a second person, P6025 «nyin dgon bya bral ba nam mkha' rdo rje» (Kaḥ thog, Nyin dgon; associated century 18, event «17»), a student of P6013 (Dza sprul Bsod nams rnam rgyal, a rebirth of Klong gsal snying po's nephew) and of P2943 (born 1761, died 1829), and teacher of P6010 Mtsho phu sgrub chen (born 18th c.; his record, with MW20396 as source, lists «bya bral ba nam mkha' rdo rje» among his lamas). So the Kaḥ thog «bya bral ba Nam mkha' rdo rje» the history knows is the Nyin dgon man of the 17th-18th century, and P7962 (14th c., student of Yag sde Paṇ chen) is probably a doubled record of the same person under another century; the catalogue is not consistent. The Kaḥ thog history of Bya bral Sangs rgyas rdo rje (MW3CN5276, search snippet only) says «yang nyin dgon bya bral ba nam mkha' rdo rje ni dge paN sogs kyi slob ma rtogs pa mchog ldan zhig nyin dgon rang du tshe dang sgrub pa mnyam…» («also Nyin dgon Bya bral ba Nam mkha' rdo rje, a supreme realised one, pupil of a dge paṇ and others, who stayed at Nyin dgon itself, life and practice together…»). BDRC also lists, under P6025, MW8LS19380 «rdzogs pa chen po sems sde'i pra khrid kyi man ngag» (72 p., «khams lugs» from the teacher Dga' rab rdo rje), not the Nyang-tradition text of q1145. Water-female bird years of the 17th-18th centuries: 1693, 1753. None is stated by a source. So the history does not date the colophon of q1145; it makes the dating by the 1378 teacher doubtful.
Still open: the page of MW20396 itself and the colophon's year.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-vvv)

No change for p. 164 itself (in copyright, no etext, no scan served). New evidence on the question's real object, the dating: see q1145's note of 2026-11-14: the life of Padma 'gro 'dul gsang sngags gling pa names Bya bral ba Nam mkha' rdo rje as a lama of the 19th-century circle, which supports the later person.

## Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-v)

p. 164 of MW20396 still unread. The question's object, the dating, is changed by the Kaḥ thog history of the thirteen great instructions: The Kaḥ thog's own short history of the thirteen great instructions («Rgyal ba kaH thog pa'i grub mchog rnams kyi nyams bzhes khrid chen bcu gsum gyi lo rgyus mdor bsdus zung 'jug grub pa'i lam bzang», BDRC etext VEIE0OPI48005610_I5994, OCR with errors; etext pages 105-107 and 118; read 2026-11-16 by etext only) names two Nam mkha' rdo rje, not one. (1) Under the second great instruction (the sems sde pra khrid of «mkhas grub Nam mkha' rdo rje», written down from Sprul pa'i sku Dga' rab rdo rje) the lineage lists «rtogs ldan Nam mkha' rdo rje» as the one on whom the Yang tig / Mkha' 'gro snying thig transmission «fell» from Vajraśrī (Badzra shrī), after Rtsa sde Nam mkha' rdo rje, Drung Thugs rje dbang phyug and Ston pa dbang 'byor dpal; and the chief of the line, «dpal 'bar ba Nam mkha' rdo rje», is said to be the son of Grags pa dbang 'byor's relative («dbon po»), to have come young to Kaḥ thog, met Bla ma Gyan pa rin po che and Ston pa dbang 'byor, studied under Byang chub dpal and Gnam stong pa Rdo rje dpal ba, taught at Rgyal mo tsha ba rong, and newly written the sems sde instruction texts from Dga' rab rdo rje's line; his pupils are listed down through «dge paN» and «Nyin dgon Gzhan phan rdo rje». (2) The line of the Kun mkhyen Klong chen pa's Yang tig (p. 118) runs «Kun mkhyen chos kyi rgyal po (Klong chen pa) → Mkhas grub Bde legs rgya mtsho → Bya bral chos rje → Gzhan phan rdo rje → Kaḥ thog pa Mkhas grub Nam mkha' rdo rje → Sman shod Sangs rgyas rdo rje»: a Kaḥ thog Mkhas grub Nam mkha' rdo rje some generations after Klong chen pa and after a Gzhan phan rdo rje. So the Kaḥ thog tradition itself has an earlier Nam mkha' rdo rje (the teachers named are of the Kaḥ thog lineage of the thirteenth and fourteenth centuries, the shape of P7962's record) and a later one placed after Gzhan phan rdo rje (the 17th-18th century setting of P6025); no text reads «bya bral ba» with the first. The text gives no year for either; whether the history that BDRC cites for P7962 (MW20396 p. 164) means the earlier or the later was not read.
