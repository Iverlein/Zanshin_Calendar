# Does MW20396 p. 164 (the Kaḥ thog history that enumerates Nam mkha' rdo rje's sems sde pra khrid, BDRC person P7962) date him or the Nyang-tradition instruction (q1145)?

Raised by the 2026-11-13 investigation n.

## Narrowed 2026-11-13 (inv-s)

MW20396 is «Kaḥ thog pa'i lo rgyus mdor bsdus» by 'Jam dbyangs rgyal mtshan (Chengdu, Si khron mi rigs dpe skrun khang, 1996, 240 p.), a modern short history; the page is not in an etext, so its words on Nam mkha' rdo rje were not read; BDRC's own note says only «enumerates his sems sde pra khrid». What the record chain does show goes against the 1393 inference of q1145. BDRC holds a second person, P6025 «nyin dgon bya bral ba nam mkha' rdo rje» (Kaḥ thog, Nyin dgon; associated century 18, event «17»), a student of P6013 (Dza sprul Bsod nams rnam rgyal, a rebirth of Klong gsal snying po's nephew) and of P2943 (born 1761, died 1829), and teacher of P6010 Mtsho phu sgrub chen (born 18th c.; his record, with MW20396 as source, lists «bya bral ba nam mkha' rdo rje» among his lamas). So the Kaḥ thog «bya bral ba Nam mkha' rdo rje» the history knows is the Nyin dgon man of the 17th-18th century, and P7962 (14th c., student of Yag sde Paṇ chen) is probably a doubled record of the same person under another century; the catalogue is not consistent. The Kaḥ thog history of Bya bral Sangs rgyas rdo rje (MW3CN5276, search snippet only) says «yang nyin dgon bya bral ba nam mkha' rdo rje ni dge paN sogs kyi slob ma rtogs pa mchog ldan zhig nyin dgon rang du tshe dang sgrub pa mnyam…» («also Nyin dgon Bya bral ba Nam mkha' rdo rje, a supreme realised one, pupil of a dge paṇ and others, who stayed at Nyin dgon itself, life and practice together…»). BDRC also lists, under P6025, MW8LS19380 «rdzogs pa chen po sems sde'i pra khrid kyi man ngag» (72 p., «khams lugs» from the teacher Dga' rab rdo rje), not the Nyang-tradition text of q1145. Water-female bird years of the 17th-18th centuries: 1693, 1753. None is stated by a source. So the history does not date the colophon of q1145; it makes the dating by the 1378 teacher doubtful.
Still open: the page of MW20396 itself and the colophon's year.
