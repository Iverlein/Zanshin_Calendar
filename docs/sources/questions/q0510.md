# Which mewa does each class label of p. 70's verse take

Which of the nine mewas does each class label of p. 70's verse take (the two reds, the black, blue and green), and why does the label say «གསུམ» after the red-two class? Mipham's note p. 507 counts 3 deciding pebbles + 4 mewa classes = 7 identifying pebbles.

**Narrowed 2026-11-05 (RD164, [reckonings.md](../reckonings.md)).** The notes' line reads «ལྗང་དང་གསུམ» (printed p. 501). A 3+3+3 reading is arithmetic only, stated nowhere.

## Searched 2026-11-16 (inv-t)

The verse manual of q275 (I4949, etext pp. 85-86, read) does not stop at the four-line verse: it goes on through the nine mewas in order, each named by number and colour, «ཞིབ་པར་བབས་སྨེ་གཅིག་དཀར། … གཉིས་ནག … གསུམ་མཐིང་གླང་དང་གོང་མོར། … བཞི་ལྗང་ཁུ་བྱུག་ལུས་ལེན་པས། … ལྔ་སེར་སྤྲེའུ་བྱ་ཕོར་སྐྱེ། … དྲུག་དཀར་སྟེང་དུ་ཤི་བ། … བདུན་དམར་འདྲེ་དང་འཕར་སྤྱང་སྐྱེ། … བརྒྱད་དཀར་བུད་མེད་ལུས་ལེན། … དགུ་དམར་ཁྱི་དང་བོང་བུར་སྐྱེ»: 1, 6 and 8 white; 2 black; 3 blue («མཐིང»); 4 green; 5 yellow; 7 and 9 red. So the classes' colours are the colours of WB's own mewas, and the lower births agree with WB pp. 71-72 (claim dead-rebirth-by-mewa). The manual keeps the numeral beside the colour («གསུམ་མཐིང»), which allows the notes' «ལྗང་དང་གསུམ» ("the green and the three") to be read as mewa 4 and mewa 3 (blue) by its number, so that «ནག་གཉིས་ལྗང་གསུམ» would be black 2, green 4 and 3, the three lower-birth mewas 2, 3, 4. This is a reading of the manual's usage, not stated by it, and it does not explain «དམར་གཉིས་སེར་གསུམ» (the reds 7 and 9 are two; there is no yellow 3).
