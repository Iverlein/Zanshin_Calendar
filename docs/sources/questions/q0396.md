# A white enemy pebble in the karma reckoning: good or bad

UT's karma reckoning (ut:p153-155, etext only) reads the enemy pebbles white as good, where WB's general verdict (wb.1:p329-331) and KD's worked sheet read a white enemy pebble as the bad one. Does Mipham's «དགྲ་དར་ཞེས་སོ་སོའི་དགྲ་རྡེལ་དཀར་ནའོ» (wb.2:p483), "the enemy rising: when each one's enemy pebble is white", settle it for the karma reckoning over a match?

**Searched 2026-11-01 (RD104).** The YS replies read (img. 467–514, vol. 2 img. 14–18) speak of the white pebble only in the dead's reckoning, where white is mother and friend and black the enemy (reply 148, ys:i506-508); none speaks of an enemy pebble that is white in the karma reckoning.
