# A white enemy pebble in the karma reckoning: good or bad

UT's karma reckoning (ut:p153-155, etext only) reads the enemy pebbles white as good, where WB's general verdict (wb.1:p329-331) and KD's worked sheet read a white enemy pebble as the bad one. Does Mipham's «དགྲ་དར་ཞེས་སོ་སོའི་དགྲ་རྡེལ་དཀར་ནའོ» (wb.2:p483), "the enemy rising: when each one's enemy pebble is white", settle it for the karma reckoning over a match?

**Searched 2026-11-01 (RD104).** The YS replies read (img. 467–514, vol. 2 img. 14–18) speak of the white pebble only in the dead's reckoning, where white is mother and friend and black the enemy (reply 148, ys:i506-508); none speaks of an enemy pebble that is white in the karma reckoning.

## Answered 2026-11-14

Answered 2026-11-01 in [claims/wb-white-enemy-pebble-is-the-bad-one.md](../claims/wb-white-enemy-pebble-is-the-bad-one.md), recorded 2026-11-14 (inv-eee). Yes, Mipham's gloss settles it: «དགྲ་དར་ཞེས་སོ་སོའི་དགྲ་རྡེལ་དཀར་ནའོ» (wb.2:p483) makes the white enemy pebble the finding that, with others, makes an aspect bad, and it is the same as WB's general verdict (wb.1:p329-331: the life force's enemy pebble white, beware leprosy and old illness; the body's, illness and faulty senses; the black enemy is good). The karma reckoning over a match is each spouse's own birth reckoning of ch. 22 (q210, inv-tt), so the white enemy pebble is the bad one there. UT's «white enemy good» (ut:p153-155, etext) is a manual of a later hand and stands as the conflict. Reply 148 (the dead's reckoning, white as mother and friend, black as enemy) is not a witness to this, as the 2026-11-01 search found.
