# The 雑書夢合 / 三世相小鑑 (Koizumi DIG-ORMK-02344, image 27) and the 清明秘鈔夢判事 (image 34) each carry a table of day branches in seal-script heads with a cell of cursive under each; read the cells letter by letter at 3000 px: is each cell the charm for a bad dream seen on that day, the same in both books, and does either carry the days on which a dream is not told?

RD358.

## Answered 2026-11-14

Answered. No, the two tables are not the same, and only one of them is the table of the days a dream is not told. The 清明秘鈔夢判事 table (image 34, mdz:i65134) is the charm for a bad dream by day branch: ten cells under seal-script heads and a box of the vermilion charm. The 雑書夢合 / 三世相小鑑 table (ORMK-02344 image 27, mdz:i234427, mdz:i234427b) is the table 夢をみてかたらぬ日: twelve month columns with two or three day branches, cell for cell the same as the 万代大雑書古今大成's (mdz:p11); it is the older table of the 拾芥抄's 不語夢日, not a charm, and its cells carry no manner of sending a dream away. The charm table has an older printed source: the 夢相金鏡録 of 1790 (mdz:i15617, ORMK-00156 image 17) has it with twelve cells, 子 and 丑 included, so the ten cells of the 1862 book are a shortened form (see q1015).
