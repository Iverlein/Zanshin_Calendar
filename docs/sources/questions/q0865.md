# What do the watch lines and «ལ་མོ»/«བླ་མོ» of Advayavajra's Svapnekṣā read in the Co ne (vol. 49, 130a1–130b7, img. 263–264)?

Not read: img. 263 returns nothing from whole-image OCR.

## Answered 2026-11-10

RD354: the Co ne's Svapnekṣā (vol. 49, img. 263) has no watch lines of month and year as in the Svapnohana and no «ལ་མོ» or «བླ་མོ»; its only watch line is the three parts of the night. By eye the line is unreadable; Yigdzin-1 read «ཐ་མར་རྨི་ལམ་བརྟགས་པ་ཉལ» in 14 of 14 windows that reached it (པ for the Derge's ལ). Machine reading only.
