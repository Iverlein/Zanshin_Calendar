# «རྟོགས་དཀའ་གུད་རྡུལ་ཀུན» on p. 448: which word, and what is «གུད»

On p. 448 (img. 454), line 24, the White Beryl is praised as clearing away «རྟོགས་དཀའ་གུད་རྡུལ་ཀུན»; the stack read «རྡུལ» (dust) has a flat head letter and could be «ཧྲུལ» or «ཏྲུལ», and the machine read «སྔས»: which word stands there, and what does «གུད» mean in it? (RD84)

RD178 (2026-11-07): Narrowed: the reprint (img. 454, line 24, 7×) reads «དཀའ་གུད་ཧྲུལ་ཀུན་བསལ་བའི» with a flat-headed stack and final ལ near ཧྲུལ; Yigdzin-1 on the Zhol print reads «གུད་སྦས». Not «སྔས». Unsettled between the two.

RD178 (2026-11-08, fourth run): Zhol img. 1230, line 6, 5.5× and 14×: the stack has a closing stroke like ས or ལ, head and vowel blurred; not settled between «ཡས», «སྦས» and «ཧྲུལ».

## Answered 2026-11-24

«གུད་སྦས» is the word: the 1996 etext, Yigdzin-1 and MITRA read it, and the pair occurs ten more times in WB with the sense hidden; «གུད་ཧྲུལ», «གུད་ཡས», «གུད་རྡུལ» have no hit in either volume (the reprint «ཧྲུལ» was a blot). Claim wb-p448-gud-sbas-by-usage (RD178).
