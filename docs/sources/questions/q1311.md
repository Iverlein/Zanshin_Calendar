# WB vol. 2 p. 236 line 21: the day's number, (༡༣) or (༡༢)?

Is p. 236 line 21 «ཉིན་ཞག(༡༣)» (the day, 13) or «(༡༢)»? The second digit reads «༣» at 3.5x only, and the order of the numbers asks for it.

## Answered 2026-11-24

Thirteen, by the run. The verse lists Rāhu's thirteen ways of moving, each number in a bracket after its name: year «ལོ» 1, month «ཟླ» 2, general «སྤྱི་འདོམས» 3, outer «བྱི» 4, inner «ནང» 5, secret «གསང» 6, treasure «གཏེར» 7, father «ཡབ» 8, mother «ཡུམ» 9, emanation «སྤྲུལ་པ» 10, messenger «ཕོ་ཉ» 11, «ཕྱག་བརྙན» 12, and the day «ཉིན་ཞག» last (wb.2:p236d, the verse after «ལེགས་ཆ་འདོམས་པར་བརྗོད»). The numbers climb by one from 1 to 12 with no gap, so the day's is 13; the (༡༢) of the question would repeat the twelfth. The glyph was read as ༣ at 3.5x only, and the order of the run is what settles it.
