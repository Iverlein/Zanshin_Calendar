# WB vol. 2 p. 236 line 21: the day's number, (༡༣) or (༡༢)?

Is p. 236 line 21 «ཉིན་ཞག(༡༣)» (the day, 13) or «(༡༢)»? The second digit reads «༣» at 3.5x only, and the order of the numbers asks for it.

## Answered 2026-11-21

(༡༣), 13. The day's number is 13.

The brackets of Rāhu's reckoning on p. 236 (wb.2:p236d, lines 19-21) run one after the other without a gap or a repeat: year (1), month (2), general counsel (3), outer (4), inner (5), secret (6), the treasure to be hidden (7), father (8), mother (9), emanation (10), messenger (11), hand-seal «ཕྱག་བརྙན(༡༢)» (12), and the day «ཉིན་ཞག(༡༣)». The hand-seal already has the 12 on the line above, so the day's number cannot be the same; the scan shows its second digit differs from that 12's (a vertical stem against a swinging tail), as a 3 does against a 2.
