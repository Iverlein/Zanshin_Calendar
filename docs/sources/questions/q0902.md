# In the Blue Beryl's ch. 7 the first part of the night is up to midnight on the Eight Limbs' verse (etext p. 209) and dusk with midnight alike on the Four Tantras' verse (p. 216): is this one rule read twice, or two different spans?

RD301.

## Answered 2026-11-10

Answered by RD313 (2026-11-10): the Blue Beryl's two places are two different rules, not one read twice: p. 209 (up to midnight, fruit far and small) is the Eight Limbs' verse with the Moonbeams' gloss, p. 216 (dusk and midnight alike, no fruit) is Zur mkhar ba's sentence; neither it nor Zur mkhar ba reconciles the two.
