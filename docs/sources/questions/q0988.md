# What does the Blue Beryl's clause on Drukpa yogis and Khri srong lde btsan's communities say among those of like kind to the good messenger, broken in the etext, and does the Drikung dka' mchan comment on the verse?

RD336; the Drikung etext (VEIE0OPI7984ABB9_I1KG24731) was not fetched.

Narrowed (inv-e, 2026-11-13): the Drikung dka' mchan etext (VEIE0OPI7984ABB9_I1KG24731, p. 79) was read at this verse: it glosses the verse with the Moonbeams' words on ya mtshan can as an outsider and a Tantra's list of outsider sects, and says nothing of Drukpa yogis or of Khri srong lde btsan's communities (gt:i41c). The Blue Beryl's own clause stays broken: its volume 1 (image group I1PD411) answers only 41 images to the open manifest (of 980 images; the pages around printed pp. 170–171 are not among them), so the etext's «གས་[གོས]དཀར་ལྕང་ལའི་[ལོའི]» cannot be checked on a scan. A web search for the reading found nothing.

## Answered 2026-11-14

The clause, with its broken end restored. The Blue Beryl (ch. 7, printed pp. 170-171) says that ya mtshan can is the name of an outsider's vow-observance but does not occur so in this region, and lists those of a like kind: the Drukpa school's realised ones who bear the forehead fringe (rbad mgo), the net-bag or net-belt (drwa phad), the thighbone trumpet and bone ornaments (see q989); the monks who follow Shakyamuni; and «བོད་འདིར་མངའ་བདག་ཁྲི་སྲོང་ལྡེ་བཙན་གྱི་དུས་མཆོད་གནས་སྡེ་གཉིས་ཀྱི་གོས་དཀར་ལྕང་ལོའི་སྡེ་རྣམས་དང་», "in Tibet, the communities of the white-robed locked ones of the two seats of offering of the lord Khri srong lde btsan's periodic offering", and "if neither of these two, one of like kind who has made his dress venerable and draws faith to himself". The etext's broken «གས་[གོས]དཀར་ལྕང་ལའི་[ལོའི]» is read so by a second copy of the clause, in the Gso ba rig pa vol. 3 (MW1PD95727, a modern compilation; its Wylie snippet «bod 'dir mnga' bdag khri srong lde btsan gyi dus mchod gnas sde gnyis kyi gos dkar lcang lo'i sde rnams dang / gal te de gnyis min yang de dang rigs mthun pa cha lugs btsun par bcos pa'i rang nyid dad pa 'dren pa» is search-only), and Tshul khrims rgya mtsho's commentary (MW30522, 2001, search-only) repeats the Drukpa half of the clause. The two seats are the ordained in saffron and the white-robed mantra-holders (gt-drikung-skyem-pa-ya-mtshan-can-blue-beryl-clause).

The Drikung dka' mchan does comment on the verse, but not on this: it takes the Moonbeams' outsider and a Tantra's list of sects (etext p. 79; inv-e, 2026-11-13) and mentions neither Drukpa yogis nor Khri srong lde btsan's communities. The clause is the Blue Beryl's own.
