# «ཆུ་ཀ་ལེན» in Jyeṣṭhā's good list, p. 323

What is «ཆུ་ཀ་ལེན» in Jyeṣṭhā's good list on p. 323? Both prints have ཀ with no ར above it, where the machine had «ཆུ་རྐ», a water channel.

RD176 (2026-11-06), narrowed: the reprint (img. 329) has a plain ཀ in «ཟམ་འཛུགས་ཆུ་ཀ་ལེན» at 3×; the Zhol line (img. 1077, line 5) has a top-left mark that may be a ར, not told at 4.5×, and the Zhol etext's machine text has «རྐལེན». Sense: digging a water channel, beside setting up a bridge.

RD176 second part (2026-11-06), narrowed: SY has «ཟམ་འཛུགས་བ་ཆུ་ཀ་ལེན་བ» with a plain ཀ, so the reprint's plain ཀ is the tradition's own spelling and not a lost ར; the sense of «ཆུ་ཀ» is unknown, no lexicon consulted.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-qqq)

Dictionaries (Steinert's public set): no headword «ཆུ་ཀ»; «ཆུ་རྐ» is a channel for leading water, an irrigation ditch (Valby, Ives Waldo, Tshig mdzod: «ཞིང་གའི་བར་མཚམས་སུ་ཆུ་རྐ་བཟོས་པ»), and «ཆུ་ལེན» is to draw or take water (Ives Waldo; Tshig mdzod lists «ཆུ་ལེན་པ» under «ཆུ»). So «ཆུ་ཀ་ལེན» next to «ཟམ་འཛུགས» (setting up a bridge) reads best as taking water into a channel, «ཆུ་རྐ» with its ར dropped as in SY; not settled, since no text spells the phrase (BDRC phrase search «ཟམ་འཛུགས་ཆུ་ཀ་ལེན» 0 hits).

## Answered 2026-11-16

inv-o, 2026-11-16, from BDRC full-text search of WB's list and its parallel copies; nothing from memory.

**«ཆུ་ཀ་ལེན» is «ཆུ་རྐ་ལེན», taking a water channel (leading water) in, listed after setting up a bridge; the spelling with ར is the one of every typed copy of the list.** The same good-list line is typed «skye shing zam 'dzug sa chu rka len / gtar sreg rta phyug ba la gtong» in WB's 1996 etext (MW2CZ8040), «skye shing zam 'dzugs chu rka len / gtar sreg rta phyugs khyu la gtong» in the Gser thang etext (MW3CN5026) and «skye shing zam 'dzugs chu rka len / gtar srag rta phyugs khyu la gtong» in the *Do shal* (MW2PD17386_D19206; snippets). Two further texts carry the phrase outside WB's verse: the Reb kong mantrins' almanac collection (*Reb kong sngags mang gi skar yig phyogs bsgrigs*, MW1KG6014, search-only snippet), in a list of works: «nor blang shing zam 'dzugs pa chu rka len pa gtar sreg lho nub tu don gnyer…», and a prose collection (MW3CN22937, snippet): «khang 'dabs sogs su chu rka len par…» (taking a channel to the roofs of houses). BDRC phrase search «ཆུ་རྐ་ལེན» 6 hits, «ཟམ་འཛུགས་ཆུ་རྐ» 3 hits, none with a plain ཀ.

With the dictionary entries of the narrowing above («ཆུ་རྐ» a channel for leading water, an irrigation ditch; «ཆུ་ལེན» to take water), the phrase is "setting up a bridge, taking a water channel": works of building and watering. The plain ཀ of the reprint's and the Zhol's print, and of SY, is then a dropped or unwritten ར above the ཀ in the print (a letter for the eye only, and not read on the scan, which stays as it was); the sense and the word are not in doubt.
