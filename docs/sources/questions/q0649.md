# «ཆུ་ཀ་ལེན» in Jyeṣṭhā's good list, p. 323

What is «ཆུ་ཀ་ལེན» in Jyeṣṭhā's good list on p. 323? Both prints have ཀ with no ར above it, where the machine had «ཆུ་རྐ», a water channel.

RD176 (2026-11-06), narrowed: the reprint (img. 329) has a plain ཀ in «ཟམ་འཛུགས་ཆུ་ཀ་ལེན» at 3×; the Zhol line (img. 1077, line 5) has a top-left mark that may be a ར, not told at 4.5×, and the Zhol etext's machine text has «རྐལེན». Sense: digging a water channel, beside setting up a bridge.

RD176 second part (2026-11-06), narrowed: SY has «ཟམ་འཛུགས་བ་ཆུ་ཀ་ལེན་བ» with a plain ཀ, so the reprint's plain ཀ is the tradition's own spelling and not a lost ར; the sense of «ཆུ་ཀ» is unknown, no lexicon consulted.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-qqq)

Dictionaries (Steinert's public set): no headword «ཆུ་ཀ»; «ཆུ་རྐ» is a channel for leading water, an irrigation ditch (Valby, Ives Waldo, Tshig mdzod: «ཞིང་གའི་བར་མཚམས་སུ་ཆུ་རྐ་བཟོས་པ»), and «ཆུ་ལེན» is to draw or take water (Ives Waldo; Tshig mdzod lists «ཆུ་ལེན་པ» under «ཆུ»). So «ཆུ་ཀ་ལེན» next to «ཟམ་འཛུགས» (setting up a bridge) reads best as taking water into a channel, «ཆུ་རྐ» with its ར dropped as in SY; not settled, since no text spells the phrase (BDRC phrase search «ཟམ་འཛུགས་ཆུ་ཀ་ལེན» 0 hits).
