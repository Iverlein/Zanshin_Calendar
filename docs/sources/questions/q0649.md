# «ཆུ་ཀ་ལེན» in Jyeṣṭhā's good list, p. 323

What is «ཆུ་ཀ་ལེན» in Jyeṣṭhā's good list on p. 323? Both prints have ཀ with no ར above it, where the machine had «ཆུ་རྐ», a water channel.

RD176 (2026-11-06), narrowed: the reprint (img. 329) has a plain ཀ in «ཟམ་འཛུགས་ཆུ་ཀ་ལེན» at 3×; the Zhol line (img. 1077, line 5) has a top-left mark that may be a ར, not told at 4.5×, and the Zhol etext's machine text has «རྐལེན». Sense: digging a water channel, beside setting up a bridge.
