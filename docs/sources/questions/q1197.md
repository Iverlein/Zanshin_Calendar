# Which work of the Rgyud sde kun btus (MW2PD14777) carries the sentence «dbang bskur ba'i dus dpa' bo mkha' 'gro rnams kyis rgyud byin gyis brlabs nas lung ston par mdzad pas…», and is it a copy of Kun dga' rin chen's Phur pa dbang mtshams sbyor (VE1KG4326_009) or of an older Sakya initiation manual (q1070)?

Raised by the 2026-11-13 investigation t.

## Answered 2026-11-16 (inv-v)

The work is «rdo rje phur pa'i dbang gi cho ga blo gsal dga' ba skyed byed», BDRC part MW27883_DA3161 of the Glog klad print of the Rgyud sde kun btus (MW27883, vol. 16, pp. 669-745, 39 ff.; the work was already found in q1070's answer of 2026-11-14). New: its BDRC record (purl.bdrc.io/resource/MW27883_DA3161.ttl, read 2026-11-16) carries the authorship statement «written by 'jam dbyangs blo gter dbang po on the basis of a work by sngags 'chang chen po kun dga' rin chen», and its title («blo gsal dga' ba skyed byed») is the title of Kun dga' rin chen's junction rite (MW1KG4326_EEFCFC, «rdo rje phur pa'i dbang gi mtshams sbyor blo gsal dga' ba skyed byed»). So by the catalogue's own words the Kun btus work is Blo gter dbang po's recast of Kun dga' rin chen's text, not a copy of an older Sakya manual; the sentence is Kun dga' rin chen's (or his source's), carried over. The Rdzong sar print's part (MW2PD14777) was not opened for its own record, and the two texts were compared on the one sentence only (q1070), not line by line. That Kun dga' rin chen took the sentence from an earlier manual is not excluded by this, only not shown by anything read.
