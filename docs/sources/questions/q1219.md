# KD: which of the old or new Chinese reckoning do the trigram, mewa and earth-lord pages of the Phugpa almanac derive from?

KD calls the nag rtsis the old Chinese reckoning of trigrams, mewa and earth lords with no star reckoning and the rgya rtsis the later astronomical one (kd.1:p549, p555), but does not say which the Phugpa almanac's day factors come from.

## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-k)

KD's own definition puts the trigrams, the mewa and the earth lords in the old Chinese (black) reckoning and leaves the new one to the eclipse examination and the working of the Chinese almanac (kd.1:p549), so the day's trigram, mewa and earth-lord factors are old-reckoning matter by KD's classification; no passage says which of the two the Phugpa almanac's pages were drawn from. The White Beryl's verse on the circuits (wb.1:p242-243) gives the six groups of the element reckoning (trigrams, mewa, dar gud, year and month, planets and mansions, zangs kyam) to Kong tse 'phrul rgyal, the old Chinese teacher, and the Fifth Dalai Lama's reply on the circuits (BDRC IE0OPIEFA32E69, I1PD107961, etext pp. 502-503) lists the same reckonings under the Chinese circuits; neither speaks of an almanac page. BDRC phrase searches «འབྱུང་བའི་སྐོར་འགོའི་གྲངས» (5 etexts, all the same verse) and «རྒྱ་རྩིས་རྙིང་མ» found no text on the almanac's source. Open.
