# KD: which of the old or new Chinese reckoning do the trigram, mewa and earth-lord pages of the Phugpa almanac derive from?

KD calls the nag rtsis the old Chinese reckoning of trigrams, mewa and earth lords with no star reckoning and the rgya rtsis the later astronomical one (kd.1:p549, p555), but does not say which the Phugpa almanac's day factors come from.
