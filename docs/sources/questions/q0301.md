# How the thirty's count runs through the years

In the thirty (WB vol. 1, p. 453; the notes, vol. 2, p. 532, «བརྩི་ལོ་གྲངས་དུ་ཡིན་པར»), does the count from the pair's year run "as many as the years" of the one reckoned inclusively (the birth year counted as one, as the year of age is) or by full years lived, and through the sixty years with their elements or through the twelve animals only?

**In part 2026-10-23 (RD44, [corpus/ut/](../corpus/ut/)).** UT counts a person's years inclusively, 1 in the birth year, then 13, 25 … at the same animal (ut:p4-5, etext), and the worked examples (DZ, ZG) count through the sixty years with their elements; that the thirty and the circles use this count is an inference.

**In part 2026-10-23 (RD47, [claims/circles-upward-for-all.md](../claims/circles-upward-for-all.md)).** Reply 139 (ys:i496-497): the circles are counted upward by the years lived (དུ་ལོན, du lon) for man and woman alike; counting a man down and a woman up is rejected, with Darchen Thigle's annotation and the seventh circle, the women's, as evidence. Whether the birth year counts as one is not said.

**In part 2026-10-25 (RD50, [claims/thirty-count-years.md](../claims/thirty-count-years.md)).** WB p. 453 gives no number; the notes say «ལོ་གྲངས་དུ་ཡིན་པར» ("as many as the years"), the circles' notes «ལོ་གྲངས་དུ་ལོན་པར» ("lived"); UT p. 4 numbers a person's years 1, 13, 25 … 97, one in the birth year, named by element. The nominal age run through the sixty is an inference; whether the pair's own year is the first place no text says.
