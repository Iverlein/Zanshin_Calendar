# What are KP's «འགྲོ་བདེ་གཉིས», and does screening a bad planet-mansion meeting leave its result?

KP img. 14, ll. 2–3, «གཟའ་སྐར་ཕྲད་འབྲས་ངན་ན་གཟའ་སྐར་དེ་གཉིས་ཀྱི་སྒྲིབ་བཅོས་ཀྱང་འགྲོ་བདེ་གཉིས་ཀྱི་འབྲས་བུ་ལས་ལོག་འགྱུར་མེད་ཅིང་།» (the Bhutan reprint has the same): what are the «འགྲོ་བདེ་གཉིས», and does the clause say that the screening of a bad planet-and-mansion meeting does not turn its result away?

**Narrowed 2026-11-03 (RD146, [weighing.md](../weighing.md), *Four open words of the works and rites*).** KP img. 14 (file `img016`) read by eye and with Yigdzin-1: the clause is KP's own (WB's verse after the weighing has no screening), the Bhutan reprint is the same. The grammar gives two readings, the remedy "does not turn away from" the result of the «འགྲོ་བདེ་གཉིས» or "is not contrary to" it. What the two are no text found says: WB has «འགྲོ་བདེ» only as «སྐྱེ་འགྲོ་བདེ» and «འགྲོ་བ་བདེ་ལ་འགོད» (the beings and their ease), and BDRC's search finds the pair only in KP's Bhutan reprint and a Jonang manual (the going and the ease of a rebirth). A doubt, not a claim.
