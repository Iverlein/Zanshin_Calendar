# The middle cells of the strip charts' sides (WB ch. 13 pp. 142-147)

What stands in the middle cell of each side of the strip charts (the top row second, the right column second, the bottom row second, the left column second cell), the place of no animal? (RD199)

## Narrowed 2026-11-24 (inv-l)

The middle cells are not blank places. By the older readings of the reprint (wb.1:p142e to p146e, 1.5x to 2.2x, cells counted from the top of a column, doubtful words as marked there) the right column's third cell, which is the right side's middle place, holds in the pig chart the vase-demon (བུམ་བདུད) with a second word not settled, in the horse chart «སེ་བདུད་ཉི…» (the se demon of the day, p. 216-217), in the dragon chart «ཐབ་ལྷ», in the sheep chart the inner-demon, in the snake chart «ཀླུ…»; the tiger chart's is «བླ་མཁྱེན». So the middle cells carry lords of the pp. 216-219 sections like the animals' cells do. Which lord stands in which chart's middle cell is not settled: the older readings count the cells of a column in another way than wb.1:p142-147r, and the multi-line cells are not read. A compass reading (the ring has the rat at the bottom and the horse at the top, so the sides' middles would be north at the bottom, south at the top, east at the left, west at the right) would fit the lords that the verse sets by direction (the hearth god Seshar, the raven of the dead, the unchanging, the five gods of Grolbu, vol. 2 pp. 217-218) but was not tested cell by cell; it is a hypothesis, no text states it.
