# Who is «ཆུ་བདག» in «ཉི་མ་ཤར་དགེ་ཆུ་བདག་ངན» on p. 313 and which planet stands on which of the eight points

In WB vol. 2 p. 313, line 5, who is «ཆུ་བདག», the lord of water, in «ཉི་མ་ཤར་དགེ་ཆུ་བདག་ངན» (the Sun in the east good, the lord of water bad), and which planet stands on which of the eight points beyond the first two? (RD79)

## Answered 2026-11-05

Answered 2026-11-01 (RD112, the same claim): «ཆུ་བདག» is the west (Varuṇa), Saturn's point, opposite the Sun.
