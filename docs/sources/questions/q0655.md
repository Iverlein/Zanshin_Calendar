# The Drigung verse work's author and its twelve-day list

Who wrote the Drigung verse work Gtsug lag dkar nag rgyud kyi dgongs don (MW3KG218, etext pp. 14 and 57–58) and when, and does its twelve-day list draw on WB or on a common older source, since it keeps WB's list of hair dates but not WB's remedies? Its rat-day line says «ཆང་རྫས» (the beer things) where SY has «ཆང་རྫ» (the jar): which does the older source mean? (RD162)

RD173 (2026-11-06), answered as to the writer and the source: the colophon (VE1ER68 etext pp. 83–85) names Lho rje drung pa Dkon mchog bstan 'dzin phrin las, in his 49th year, a fire bird year, at Thub bstan bshad sgrub gling in Mdo khams, the scribe Orgyan bstan 'dzin (the year: 1897, 1957 or 2017, not decided). Its twelve-day remedies are WB's vol. 2 pp. 356–359 versified (only the pig's differs, and the wording of the rat's), contrary to RD162's "quite unlike"; the rat day's «ཆང་རྫས» is the versifier's wording, not shown to be older. ([word-hunts.md](../word-hunts.md), *The catalogue's questions, the second round*)

## Answered 2026-11-06

RD173 (2026-11-06), as to writer and source (etext only, VE1ER68 pp. 83–85): the Drigung verse work is signed in its colophon by Lho rje drung pa Dkon mchog bstan 'dzin phrin las, in his 49th year, a fire bird year, at Thub bstan bshad sgrub gling in Mdo khams; the scribe is Orgyan bstan 'dzin; BDRC's record gives no author. A fire bird year fits 1897, 1957 or 2017; his birth year is unknown. Its twelve remedies are WB vol. 2 pp. 356–359 versified; only the pig's remedy and the rat's wording differ (this corrects RD162's 'quite unlike WB's'). The colophon says the exemplar was very corrupt.
