# Where the do gcig (དོ་གཅིག) is defined

In which place do the trigram reckonings define the do gcig (དོ་གཅིག), the term that the birth reckoning (vol. 1, p. 317), the dead's (vol. 2, p. 89) and the moment's spirit speeches (vol. 2, p. 164) share? It occurs also on vol. 1 p. 390 and in Mipham's note on p. 480, as what a count of year and trigram lands on beside the mother's; no passage defines it (RD165).

RD177 (2026-11-06): repeats q350 and q385: Mipham's note p. 480 is the one gloss («སྐྱེས་སྤར་སྟེང་བབ་སྤར་ཕྱིན་པ»); no place defines it.

## Answered 2026-11-14

INV-1114 (2026-11-14): nowhere. Mipham's note on the birth reckoning's results (wb.2:p480, «སྐྱེས་སྤར་སྟེང་བབ་སྤར་ཕྱིན་པ») is the one gloss, and no passage of the trigram reckonings (vol. 1, pp. 317 and 390; vol. 2, pp. 89 and 164) defines the word; the regent's reply 152 only uses it. BDRC's exact search for «དོ་གཅིག་ནི» (8 hits), «དོ་གཅིག་གནམ་ལོ» (3, all prints of WB) and «དོ་གཅིག་ཅེས་པ», «དོ་གཅིག་ཞེས་པ» (0 hits) finds no definition either; the other «དོ་གཅིག་གི» hits (27) are ordinary prose, for instance the Ras chung snyan brgyud's (BDRC's snippet, not read on a scan) «ངས་ན་ནིང་གི་ལོ་ལ་དཀའ་བ་དཔྱད། །དོ་གཅིག་གི་ལོ་ལ་དགའ་མགུ་མཐོང་», 'last year I weighed hardship, in the do gcig year I saw joy', where the word stands beside «ན་ནིང་», last year. Dictionaries (Steinert's 64, 2026-11-14) have no headword «do gcig»; they have «do cig» (དོ་ཅིག): the Tshig mdzod chen mo «do chig = thengs cig», 'one time, once'; Dan Martin's glossary (from the Zhi byed collection) and the Brda dkrol gser gyi me long, which glosses it «da lo'am do zhag» (ད་ལོའམ་དོ་ཞག), 'this year or today', with a line of the Dunhuang Tibetan letters, «do cig du dgung lo nyi shu lags» (I am twenty years old now/this year). Valby and Ives Waldo's «do zhag» is 'today, presently'. So the spelling «do gcig» is probably the same word as the old «do cig», 'the present, this year/time'; that fits «do gcig gi lo» but is a lexical lead only: it does not say what the trigram reckonings count, which Mipham's note states. Claim [do-gcig-glossed-once-in-the-notes](../claims/do-gcig-glossed-once-in-the-notes.md) gets the dictionary line.
