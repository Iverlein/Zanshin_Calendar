# The third Karmapa's verses (after Marpa) call the dream of midnight (ནམ་ཕྱེད) the power of spirits, LGD's «གུང» the time of the afflictions: are the two the same part of the night under two characterisations, or do the traditions differ?

RD355.

## Answered 2026-11-10

RD384: in the third Karmapa's own usage gung and nam phyed name the same part of the night: his six sessions of the day run early morning, gro kha, afternoon, dusk, gung, dawn, so gung (the fifth) lies between dusk and dawn (rrd.5:p111-112), and the dream verses quote Marpa: dusk dream habit, midnight (nam phyed) the power of spirits, whatever comes at dawn true (rrd.5:p57-59). The equation rests on the order of the sessions; he never says «middle of night» in words. The characterisations differ: spirits' power (Marpa and the Karmapa), afflictions (LGD), demons' play (Klong rdol), bile (the commentators); no text read says one reads the other.
