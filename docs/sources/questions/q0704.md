# The planet names of pp. 76–77 and the Fierce three and Peaceful two

The planet names of pp. 76–77 («བློ་ལྡན», «ཉི་བུ», «བླ་བུ», «དཀར་པོ») in the sections on the slow and the foot-holder tables: which planets are meant, and which are the Fierce three and Peaceful two? The machine readings «ཉི་བུའི» and «བླ་བུའི» may be miscopied «སྤེན་པ» or «ཕུར་བུ». (RD189, corpus/wb/ch10.md)

## Answered 2026-11-14

The names are the dictionaries' synonyms of the five planets, and ch. 6 (read on the scan) names the same five in the same order, so the machine readings of p. 77 are a misreading of one letter and not other planets.
- «བཀྲ་ཤིས» (bkra shis, "the auspicious") is Mars: bkra shis is one of the names of mig dmar in the Tshig mdzod chen mo and Ives Waldo.
- «བློ་ལྡན» (blo ldan, "the wise") is Jupiter: blo ldan is a name of phur bu in the Tshig mdzod chen mo, and in WB the section of blo ldan closes «ཕུར་བུའི་དལ་བའི་རེའུ་མིག་འགྲུབ» "Jupiter's slow table is completed". Ch. 6 (p. 38, scan) calls the same planet «བླ་མ» (bla ma, the lama; the Tshig mdzod chen mo lists lha'i bla ma among Jupiter's names).
- «ཉི་བུ» (nyi bu) is Saturn: the short form of «ཉི་མའི་བུ» (nyi ma'i bu, "the sun's son"), which the Tshig mdzod chen mo and Ives Waldo give as the mngon-brjod name of spen pa; ch. 6 reads «ཉི་བུ» "the sun's son" fifth, and Saturn comes last in every list. It is not a miscopied «སྤེན་པ».
- «དཀར་པོ» (dkar po, "the white") is Venus: «khu ba dkar po» is the first of the names of pa sangs in the Tshig mdzod chen mo, and the same section ends "the foot-holder table of the poet is completed" («སྙན་ངག་མཁན», snyan ngag mkhan, whose second sense in the Tshig mdzod chen mo is "(mngon) the planet pa sangs", Venus).
- «བླ་བུ» of p. 77 is Mercury, and the letter is ཟ not བ: ch. 6 (p. 38, read on the scan) has «ཟླ་བུ» (zla bu, "the son of the moon") as the second planet, and the Tshig mdzod chen mo lists «zla ba'i bu» among the names of lhag pa. On the machine text of p. 77 the first letter is a ཟླ read as བླ, the confusion ch. 6 already corrected on the scan. p. 77 itself was not looked at again.
So the Fierce three (དྲག་གསུམ, drag gsum), whose slow-pure tables fill p. 76, are Mars, Jupiter and Saturn in the order bkra shis, blo ldan, nyi bu; the Peaceful two (ཞི་གཉིས, zhi gnyis) are Mercury and Venus, and p. 76's own line names them «ལྷག་སངས་གཉིས» "the Lhag and Sangs two" (lhag pa and pa sangs). The slow table of the Peaceful two is made first, that of the Fierce three after.

Source: tmz:box9 (read 2026-11-14), wb.1:p38 (scan), wb.1:p76, wb.1:p77. The ཟླ for བླ on p. 77 is the inference from ch. 6; the print of p. 77 is not read.
