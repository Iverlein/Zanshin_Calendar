# The planet names of pp. 76–77 and the Fierce three and Peaceful two

The planet names of pp. 76–77 («བློ་ལྡན», «ཉི་བུ», «བླ་བུ», «དཀར་པོ») in the sections on the slow and the foot-holder tables: which planets are meant, and which are the Fierce three and Peaceful two? The machine readings «ཉི་བུའི» and «བླ་བུའི» may be miscopied «སྤེན་པ» or «ཕུར་བུ». (RD189, corpus/wb/ch10.md)
