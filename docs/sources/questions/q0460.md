# What are the «གཉན»-kind last names ending each cell of the upper table of WB vol. 1 p. 133

In the upper table each cell ends with a name of the «གཉན» kind (rat «གཉན་ཁྲ», ox «གཉན་སྣང», pig «གཉན་བེ», and for the other animals a name without «གཉན»: «བྱ་ཁྱུང», «རུས་སྦྲུལ», «པ་དན», «ཙང་ཀུན», «བྱི་ལམ», «བེ་དབང», «གཅིག་མཇུག», «ཧེ་ཐོན», «ཇཻ་ཁྱིས»): what are these names, and is the earth lord named by them? (RD78)

## Answered 2026-11-14

Settled by [claims/wb-table-last-names-are-the-day-lords.md](../claims/wb-table-last-names-are-the-day-lords.md): the last name of each cell is the earth lord of that day's animal, the twelve hidden earth lords of WB's own p. 221 and the day-courses of pp. 224–225; the ox's name is «གཉན་ལྗང». The question was left open in its file; closed 2026-11-14 (inv-ggg). Independent confirmation from the Buddhist side: the Gser thang etext of the Vaidurya dkar po (I3CN5029) has the twelve in its verses on the hidden earth lords, in BDRC's Wylie: «byi ba'i steng na sa bdag ni / gnyan khra gnas shing… glang thog gnyan ldang gnas pa yin / stag la bya khyung ga ru byi lam… lug la be sna lag chen gnas / gzig mjug gle'u he thon bya / byi dur khyil gnyan khra ni» (BDRC full-text snippet, machine, numbers of a notes layer interleaved; not read on a scan).
