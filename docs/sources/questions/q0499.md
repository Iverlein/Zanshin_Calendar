# When the 金神's two 遊行 meet on one day, which wins

§7 (by the 寅 days) and §8 (by the seasons) can put the 金神 in two places on the same day (koyomi8's example, a 丙午 day in summer). Does the 簠簋内伝 or any old almanac say which wins? (RD93)

**Narrowed 2026-11-05 (RD163).** No old text rules. The 1578 copy has neither list; the 1804 book avoids both directions and keeps three parts in ten in the seat; the 1927 almanac merges the two lists by season.

**Narrowed further 2026-11-16 (inv-c).** The 1920 通俗観相易占大鑑 (frame 61) has one table by season from a 寅 day (spring 甲寅–戊午 S, summer 丙寅–庚午 W, autumn 庚寅–甲午 N, winter 壬寅–丙午 E); the 1924 高島易断所 book has no second list and no case of two meeting; the 方位細鑑 (c.1800, frame 54) says where 大將軍 and 金神 coincide it marks the 大將軍 and leaves the 金神 unsaid. No text rules between two 金神 遊行.

**Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-j).** The 方鑑精義大成 (c.1804, NDL pid 2565914, frames 6–7, machine text) prints both 金神 lists one after the other, by the 寅 days («両寅の日より五日… 戊寅 中央, 庚寅 北, 壬寅 東, 甲寅 南») and «一同四季遊行の方» by the seasons (春は乙卯の日より東に五日, 夏は丙午の日より南…, 秋は辛酉の日より西, 冬は壬子の日より北), then says the places an outing has left must not be offended either, and that in an outing seven parts in ten of the baleful force go and three stay in the seat; it names no case of the two lists meeting and gives no winner. The 方鑑弁説 (760835), 方鑑秘伝集 (760832), 方鑑秘訣 (760826) and 陰陽方位便覧 (759955) print the 金神 by year only (大金神, 姫金神, 巡金神), no 遊行. No text rules between two 遊行. Not advanced.
