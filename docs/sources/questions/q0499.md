# When the 金神's two 遊行 meet on one day, which wins

§7 (by the 寅 days) and §8 (by the seasons) can put the 金神 in two places on the same day (koyomi8's example, a 丙午 day in summer). Does the 簠簋内伝 or any old almanac say which wins? (RD93)

**Narrowed 2026-11-05 (RD163).** No old text rules. The 1578 copy has neither list; the 1804 book avoids both directions and keeps three parts in ten in the seat; the 1927 almanac merges the two lists by season.
