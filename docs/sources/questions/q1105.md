# Is the clear light of experience at the border of sleep (Karma chags med) the same experience as the Gdams ngag mdzod's, and in which order of the three clear lights does it stand

Karma chags med calls the light before the eyes at the border of sleep and not-sleep 'the clear light of experience', and the clear light of the sleeper who knows he sleeps also 'of experience'; the Gdams ngag mdzod text defines it as seeing the room as if the sun had risen out of sleep. Are these one experience, and in which tradition's order of the three clear lights (dream, experience, thin) does it stand?

## Answered 2026-11-14

Not one experience, and no text says they are. The two share a name and overlap in part. The Gdams ngag mdzod text defines the clear light of experience (ཉམས་ཀྱི་འོད་གསལ, nyams kyi 'od gsal) as: out of the state of sleep, with few thoughts, to see the body, the dwelling and the furnishings as when the sun has risen, a mere form of emptiness (rrd.21:p19). Karma chags med gives the name to two things in one song (rrd.19:p118-119): first the light like a crystal ball or the new moon's lamp before the eyes at the border of sleep and not-sleep, with far-seeing and hearing like clairvoyance, which is an obstacle if pride comes of it; second, when one lies down knowing the sleep and holding the clear light of sleep (གཉིད་ཀྱི་འོད་གསལ, gnyid kyi 'od gsal), the sleeper's own house is as clear as day even in a dark chamber, and others' thoughts are felt (his «these too are called the clear light of experience»). The Gdams ngag mdzod definition, a room seen as in daylight out of sleep, matches Karma chags med's second thing in content, and not the first (a light before the eyes and clairvoyance, which the Gdams ngag mdzod text does not mention). The Gdams ngag mdzod text has no clairvoyance, no pride, no border of sleep.

Order: in Karma chags med's own list of five (etext pp. 302-303) the light of experience is kept for the first thing alone (the light before the eyes), the second being the sleep or thin light; the order there is sleep, thick, thin, experience, dream, so thin, then experience, then dream. The Gdams ngag mdzod text divides the path light only into experience and realisation (རྟོགས་པ, rtogs pa) and gives no order of the three; see claims rrd-karma-chags-med-five-clear-lights-of-the-night and mph-sleep-with-light-and-the-clear-light-of-experience.
