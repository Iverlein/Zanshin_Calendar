# WB ch. 10 p. 74: is the last glyph of the figure after «བུག་མཁའ་མིག་བུག་ཡིད» ༩ or ༡ with a flick?

## Answered 2026-11-21

The last glyph is ༩, and the figure has six glyphs, not five: (༡༤༩༢༠༩), 149209. Three checks. (1) The words «བུག་མཁའ་མིག་བུག་ཡིད» read from the units up: hole 9, sky 0, eye 2, hole 9, «ཡིད» 14 (written 4 then 1, as in «མ་ནུ་ཡིད» 1414), which is 9, 0, 2, 9, 4, 1, written 149209. (2) The same words carry (༡༤༩༢༠༩) wherever ch. 17 prints them (wb.1:p192, p193, p196, read on the scan), and 149209 = 67 × 2227, the divisor «རི་རོ» 67 the line itself names next. (3) The scan (Gser thang reprint I3CN5028, img. 81, p. 74, line 10 of the print, at 7x, read again 2026-11-21, rd-s): after the ༡ comes a curl-left ༤ with a hook-topped ༩ touching its right side, so that the two run together as one wide glyph (the same merging q1317 records for the ༤ and ༩ of p. 77), then ༢, ༠ and a last ༩ of the same hooked form as the ༩ touching the ༤. The stored (༡༤༢༠༩) lost the merged ༩; corrected in wb.1:p74.
