# What does p. 164 of MW20396 say about the Nyin dgon pa Nam mkha' rdo rje, and does the colophon of q1145 belong to P6025 (17th-18th c., Nyin dgon bya bral ba) or P7962 (14th c.)?

Raised by the 2026-11-13 investigation s.

## Narrowed 2026-11-13 (inv-t)

MW20396 («Kaḥ thog pa'i lo rgyus mdor bsdus», 'Jam dbyangs rgyal mtshan, Khreng tu'u 1996, 240 p.) is a modern in-copyright book with no etext on BDRC (record read via purl.bdrc.io); p. 164 was not read. What the colophon is attributed to is settled by other records, see q1145 and q1195: the sems sde pra khrid is P6025's work in BDRC, and the Kaḥ thog chos 'byung (MW3CN5276, snippet) says the Nyin dgon Bya bral ba Nam mkha' rdo rje was a pupil of Dge paN, i.e. 18th-19th century. Still open: p. 164 itself.
