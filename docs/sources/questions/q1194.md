# What does p. 164 of MW20396 say about the Nyin dgon pa Nam mkha' rdo rje, and does the colophon of q1145 belong to P6025 (17th-18th c., Nyin dgon bya bral ba) or P7962 (14th c.)?

Raised by the 2026-11-13 investigation s.

## Narrowed 2026-11-13 (inv-t)

MW20396 («Kaḥ thog pa'i lo rgyus mdor bsdus», 'Jam dbyangs rgyal mtshan, Khreng tu'u 1996, 240 p.) is a modern in-copyright book with no etext on BDRC (record read via purl.bdrc.io); p. 164 was not read. What the colophon is attributed to is settled by other records, see q1145 and q1195: the sems sde pra khrid is P6025's work in BDRC, and the Kaḥ thog chos 'byung (MW3CN5276, snippet) says the Nyin dgon Bya bral ba Nam mkha' rdo rje was a pupil of Dge paN, i.e. 18th-19th century. Still open: p. 164 itself.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-vvv)

p. 164 still unread (no etext or scan). The 19th-century attestation in the life of Padma 'gro 'dul gsang sngags gling pa (q1145's note of 2026-11-14) points to P6025's period, not P7962's; the colophon's «sprang po» is not identified with either by any text.

## Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-v)

p. 164 still unread. New: The Kaḥ thog's own short history of the thirteen great instructions («Rgyal ba kaH thog pa'i grub mchog rnams kyi nyams bzhes khrid chen bcu gsum gyi lo rgyus mdor bsdus zung 'jug grub pa'i lam bzang», BDRC etext VEIE0OPI48005610_I5994, OCR with errors; etext pages 105-107 and 118; read 2026-11-16 by etext only) names two Nam mkha' rdo rje, not one. (1) Under the second great instruction (the sems sde pra khrid of «mkhas grub Nam mkha' rdo rje», written down from Sprul pa'i sku Dga' rab rdo rje) the lineage lists «rtogs ldan Nam mkha' rdo rje» as the one on whom the Yang tig / Mkha' 'gro snying thig transmission «fell» from Vajraśrī (Badzra shrī), after Rtsa sde Nam mkha' rdo rje, Drung Thugs rje dbang phyug and Ston pa dbang 'byor dpal; and the chief of the line, «dpal 'bar ba Nam mkha' rdo rje», is said to be the son of Grags pa dbang 'byor's relative («dbon po»), to have come young to Kaḥ thog, met Bla ma Gyan pa rin po che and Ston pa dbang 'byor, studied under Byang chub dpal and Gnam stong pa Rdo rje dpal ba, taught at Rgyal mo tsha ba rong, and newly written the sems sde instruction texts from Dga' rab rdo rje's line; his pupils are listed down through «dge paN» and «Nyin dgon Gzhan phan rdo rje». (2) The line of the Kun mkhyen Klong chen pa's Yang tig (p. 118) runs «Kun mkhyen chos kyi rgyal po (Klong chen pa) → Mkhas grub Bde legs rgya mtsho → Bya bral chos rje → Gzhan phan rdo rje → Kaḥ thog pa Mkhas grub Nam mkha' rdo rje → Sman shod Sangs rgyas rdo rje»: a Kaḥ thog Mkhas grub Nam mkha' rdo rje some generations after Klong chen pa and after a Gzhan phan rdo rje. So the Kaḥ thog tradition itself has an earlier Nam mkha' rdo rje (the teachers named are of the Kaḥ thog lineage of the thirteenth and fourteenth centuries, the shape of P7962's record) and a later one placed after Gzhan phan rdo rje (the 17th-18th century setting of P6025); no text reads «bya bral ba» with the first. The text gives no year for either; whether the history that BDRC cites for P7962 (MW20396 p. 164) means the earlier or the later was not read. So the colophon of q1145 cannot be assigned to P6025 on the Kaḥ thog's own books alone; the earlier note's inference from the 19th-century life of Padma 'gro 'dul gsang sngags gling pa remains the only dated witness to a Bya bral ba Nam mkha' rdo rje.
