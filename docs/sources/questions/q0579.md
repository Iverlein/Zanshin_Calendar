# The daughter's secret obstacle named last with no symptoms

P. 390, «མ་སྤར་ལི» for the son and «མ་སྤར་ཁམ» for the daughter (Zhol img. 512, l. 4): the line's close «ཚོར་བ་དཀའ», 'hard to feel', is the daughter's secret obstacle's own; is that why the daughter's is named last and with no symptoms? See year-of-life.md, RD126.

RD174 (2026-11-06), narrowed: the Moonbeams' version (KD vol. 3 markers 674–675, etext) lacks «ཚོར་བ་དཀའ»; an interlinear bracket note reads «མ་སྤར་ལི» as son's and «མ་སྤར་ཁམ» as daughter's secret obstacle, as Mipham does. LT, UT and the notes also lack the clause, so it is WB's own. No source gives a reason for the order.

**Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-m).** BDRC «མ་སྤར་ཁམ» 6 hits (WB vol. 1 etext at 779869, the Gser thang reprint, the Bon manual MW1KG13780 and the Moonbeams' copies in I4PD3932, I4PD3282 and I4717) and «ཚོར་བ་དཀའ» 12 (the WB hit at 779869 among Four Tantras and other medical uses): the Moonbeams' copies give the mother's rows as RD174 read, no copy has WB's clause or a reason for the order. Open.

**Narrowed 2026-11-06 (sweep from RD174).** The Moonbeams' version, LT and UT lack «ཚོར་བ་དཀའ», so the clause is WB's own; an interlinear note reads «མ་སྤར་ལི» as the son's and «མ་སྤར་ཁམ» as the daughter's secret obstacle. No source gives a reason for the order.
