# Are the «རྨི་ལམ་རྩུབ» and «རྨི་ལམ་ཟ་ཟི་མང་བ» of WB ch. 25 and 27 dreams of the images listed as bad in ch. 30 p. 144, or only restless sleep?

Ch. 25: the ox day's «རྨི་ལམ་རྩུབ» and the five-yellow mewa's many confused dreams; ch. 27 (vol. 2 p. 21) «རྨི་ལམ་རྩུབ» under the sri of arrow and knife: do they mean the images of ch. 30 (falling, nakedness, being struck), or the sick person's restless sleep with no image?

## Answered 2026-11-10

Answered by RD283: in all three places the dream words are the noun «རྨི་ལམ» with a predicate (rough, many), no verb of seeing and no image, each in a row of bodily signs or signs of a class of years; ch. 30 p. 144 heads its list «རྨི་ལྟས», never «རྨི་ལམ». WB means restless sleep as a sign of the illness or the year class, not the images of ch. 30.
