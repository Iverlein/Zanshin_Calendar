# Dwa's food on p. 130

Dwa's food (p. 130, l. 2) reads on the reprint «བྱ་ཤ་ཇུ་ཙེ་སྐྱུར་རྩིབ་གཡོན», the three middle syllables uncertain (Yigdzin-1 «ཇོ་ཇུ་ལྕེ་སྐུར», four syllables against the metre's three). What is the line?

## Read further 2026-10-23

In part 2026-10-23 (RD46): the Taikhang reads «བྱ་ཤ་ཇུ་རྩེ་[སྐྱུར/སྒུར]་རྩིབ་གཡོན», seven syllables as the metre wants; the fifth stack stays between ka and ga; the Zhol folio is worn there.

## Answered 2026-11-14

The line is «བྱ་ཤ་ཇུ་རྩེ་སྐྱུར་རྩིབ་གཡོན»: bird meat, the grain *ju rtse* (ཇུ་རྩེ), the *skyur* rib, the left one. The Bhutan digest's list of the dwa provisions (printed pp. 535 and 545, read by eye, [word-hunts.md](../word-hunts.md) q312) gives the same items as «ཇུ་རྩེ། ར་བོང་། གོས་ཁྲབ། སྐྱུར་རྩིབ་གཡོན་པ་བཅས་དུར་སར་རྫོངས», with the fifth syllable's stack spelled with ya-btags, «སྐྱུར»; the zin block's «བོང་བུའི་སྐྱུར་རྩིབ» (printed p. 541–542) has the same two words. So Yigdzin-1's four-syllable «ཇོ་ཇུ་ལྕེ་སྐུར» is the machine's misreading of «ཇུ་རྩེ་སྐྱུར». *Skyur* is "sour" or "cast away" (Rangjung Yeshe, *skyur ba*); which it is for a rib is not stated, and no dictionary has «སྐྱུར་རྩིབ» or *ju rtse* as a headword (Steinert's 64 sets, 2026-11-14).
