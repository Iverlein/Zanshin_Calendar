# What does ལྷས་ཚོར་དུས་ཏེ say in ST ch. 81 (img. 363 ll. 3-4)?

Is ལྷས a misprint for ལུས (as the sister clause «རང་ཚོར་དུས་ཡིན་གདོན་གྱིས་ཐེབས་པར་ངེས» a few lines later suggests), and does another print read it so?

## Answered 2026-11-09

No. Neither a scan nor an etext read has ལུས་ in the clause: the Serthang scan (img. 363, 5–8× again), the Bhutan print's scan (img. 267, 12×) and the 2005 Supplement's scan (p. 325) all read ལྷས་ཚོར་དུས་ཏེ, with ལྷས (ལྷ and ས) on each. So ལྷས is not a misprint of the Serthang print. What it means is still said by no text (the commentaries gloss the clause whole as the time of the feeling). The sister clause «རང་ཚོར་དུས་ཡིན» was not read on the new scans. See the claim st-lhas-tshor-lder-bzo.
