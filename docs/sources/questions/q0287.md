# Objects and rites of the third examination left unexplained

Several objects and rites of pp. 58–68 are named without explanation. Are they glossed in any reckoner's manual or dictionary? They are: «སེལ་གོག», «ཏིང་མཉྫི» and the ox-year «ཡུ་མོའི་གསོབ» (p. 59); «སླ་ང་རྩེས་བྱིན་བརྒྱ་བཞི» (p. 59); «སྟེང་འཁོར» (p. 60); «ཟོ་བ་ཞབས་བརྡོལ» (p. 61); «འོག་རྟ» (p. 61); «སིག་ལྐོགས», «ཕྱ་ར», «སྐྱར་མ», «ཟལ» (pp. 67–68); «བུས་པ» (pp. 64–65); «གླང་མའི་བྱང་བུ» (pp. 65–66; is glang ma a willow or a cow?).

**Narrowed 2026-11-05 (RD161, [word-hunts.md](../word-hunts.md)).** Answered: sla nga rtses byin, the pan given to the reckoner, then the four hundred (digest p. 488, by eye); yu mo'i gsob; bus pa, a boy; zo ba zhabs brdol, a pail with a holed bottom. Narrowed: sel gog, ting manydzi, sig lkogs, sten 'khor. Left: skyar ma, zal.

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-lll).** From Steinert's 64 dictionaries (sparse clone): «གླང་མ» (glang ma) is a willow, a medicinal alpine kind (Valby, Ives Waldo, Tshig mdzod «shing zhig», Dag tshig, Lokesh Chandra vetasa, Sgom sde, Tshig gter), not a cow, so «གླང་མའི་བྱང་བུ» (pp. 65-66) is a willow slip, a tablet to be written on. «འོག་རྟ» ('og rta, p. 61) is a riding horse (Tshig mdzod and Dag tshig «bzhon rta»; Yisun by Dan Martin the same; Ives Waldo "breeding stallion", Valby "racehorse"). «ཕྱ་ར» (phya ra) is a door curtain of yak-hair cloth (Valby, Ives Waldo, Rangjung Yeshe; Tshig mdzod, Negi «phyar ba»), which fits «རོ་སྟེང་ཕྱ་ར་ནག་པོས་གཡོགས» (the corpse covered with a black curtain). «ཟལ» (zal) is the colour or markings of hair (Valby, Ives Waldo; Dag tshig: of livestock, white and speckled hair; Mkhyen brtse in Dan Martin: «dkar zal» white stripe), so «ཟལ་ཅན་བུད་མེད་དཀར་མོ» is a white woman with such marked hair (an inference from the dictionaries, not a gloss of the line). Still without a headword: «སེལ་གོག», «ཏིང་མཉྫི», «སིག་ལྐོགས», «སྟེང་འཁོར», «སྐྱར་མ» (only «skyar ba»: again and again, a water bird; «skyar po»: snipe, heron, none fitting a vessel).
