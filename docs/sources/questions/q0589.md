# «སྐྱུག» or «སྒྱུག» after «གནས» on the Taikhang leaf

Is the stack after «གནས» on the Taikhang print's img. 490 (WB p. 362, the in-laws' trigrams), read at 8×, «སྐྱུག» or «སྒྱུག»; does a still sharper copy (the original blocks, a better scan) show the subjoined letter's shape?

RD167 (2026-11-06): the Taikhang scan (img. 490) was not re-read; the etext and the sense favour «སྒྱུག»; see q557.

inv-scan2 (2026-11-14), narrowed: the Taikhang leaf (I5877 img. 490, 8700×1800 px) was fetched with bdrc.py scans; it carries five text lines between y≈470 and y≈1440 (about 200 px each; the verse starts at x≈350); lines 1 and 2 were cut into 2000 px strips and eyed at 1×, but the stack after «གནས» was not found in them and no letter was compared at 4×; the glyph stays scan-bound, the sense («སྒྱུག»), the three etexts and the dictionaries stand as in question 557.
