## Read again 2026-11-21 (rd-w)

Checked by arithmetic on the stored table (corpus/wb/ch16.md p. 188), not by the blot. In every other column the sage's date rises by 11 (12 where the desired part wraps from 12 to 0) modulo 30 and the desired part by 1 modulo 13; the pig's rows obey this in all 51 steps without exception. The sage's four off-step places are columns 12, 15, 18 and 25, and each one is a single cell that, put to the rule, makes the cell after it fit: column 12's date printed 27 (༢༧) should be 29 (18+11; the next column's 10 then follows 29+11), column 15's date printed 8 (༨) should be 2 (21+11 = 32; column 16's 13 follows 2+11), column 18's desired part printed 7 (༧) should be 9 (the 8 before it and the 10 after), column 25's date printed 13 (༡༣) should be 23 (12+11; column 26's 4 follows 23+11). Each is one glyph away from the print's (༢༧/༢༩, ༨/༢, ༧/༩, ༡༣/༢༣), so the print, which earlier readers found cell for cell as stored, very probably carries four slips (or the author's table did). No second print or slip was looked at: the Taikhang and Zhol folios of p. 188 were not located in this session. Not answered: the table stays as printed, the computed values are what a witness would have to show; the sign after «མཁའ་མེ་སྲང» is not touched.
