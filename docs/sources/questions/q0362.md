# The time «མྱུར་སྨད» of sending the ransoms

Pp. 431–433 and 443–444 send ransoms at «མྱུར་སྨད» (mewa four green; Li's and Zon's ransoms): which time of day is it, set beside sunrise, «ཉི་དྲོས», noon, sunset and midnight in the same lists?

## Answered 2026-11-06

RD182 (2026-11-06): «མྱུར་སྨད» is the monkey's time, the ninth of the twelve times of the day, between the sheep's «ཕྱེད་ཡོལ» and sunset «ཉི་ནུབ». Vol. 2 p. 359 on the Gser thang scan (img. 365): «ཉི་ཕྱེད་རྟ་ཡི་དུས། ཕྱེད་ཡོལ་ལུག་དུས། མྱུར་སྨད་སྤྲེའུའི་དུས་ཀྱི་ཚེ»; vol. 2 p. 356 (etext): the twelve links on the twelve times put «ལེན་པ་མྱུར་སྨད» ninth; vol. 1 p. 443 (etext): the ransoms stand at «མྱུར་སྨད» beside «ཉི་ནུབ» and «ཉི་ཕྱེད», so a point in the afternoon, not sunset; p. 133's second table calls the same time «ཉི་མབར». The clock hour is not given.
