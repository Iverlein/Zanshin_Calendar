# The 360's fifth and sixth years and «ཕྱི་རྩིས»

WB vol. 2, pp. 42–43 (img. 50–51): in the 360 starting points the fifth year again "fights the life force" and the sixth "meets the life force", while the list of what is sought names the grave and the executioner's grave; should the fifth and sixth be the grave and the executioner's grave (as the notes' count of six aspects, p. 538, suggests)? And is the boasters' reckoning «ཕྱི་རྩིས» or «བྱི་རྩིས»?

## Read further 2026-10-23

In part 2026-10-23 (RD46): the blocks carve the fifth and sixth years both against the life force, as printed, and «ཕྱི་རྩིས» (with «འོ་བཞིན»); whether the fifth and sixth should be the grave stays open.
