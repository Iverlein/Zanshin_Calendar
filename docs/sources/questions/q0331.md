# The 360's fifth and sixth years and «ཕྱི་རྩིས»

WB vol. 2, pp. 42–43 (img. 50–51): in the 360 starting points the fifth year again "fights the life force" and the sixth "meets the life force", while the list of what is sought names the grave and the executioner's grave; should the fifth and sixth be the grave and the executioner's grave (as the notes' count of six aspects, p. 538, suggests)? And is the boasters' reckoning «ཕྱི་རྩིས» or «བྱི་རྩིས»?

## Read further 2026-10-23

In part 2026-10-23 (RD46): the blocks carve the fifth and sixth years both against the life force, as printed, and «ཕྱི་རྩིས» (with «འོ་བཞིན»); whether the fifth and sixth should be the grave stays open.

## Read further 2026-11-01

Narrowed in RD99: Mipham's notes (p. 538, etext) make the 360 six aspects ending in the grave and the executioner's grave, which supports the question's reading; «ཕྱི་རྩིས» or «སྤྱི་རྩིས» open (claims/ch28-360-six-aspects.md).

## Read further 2026-11-14

The first half is answered, from Mipham's own worked example (notes pp. 537-538, read on the scan 2026-10-23): the printed fifth and sixth lines need no emendation. In the example the five-th aspect (one's own grave, water dragon) is counted down and "fights the life force" («སྲོག་དང་འཐབ་པས་ཆུ་ནང་ཕྲད»), and the sixth (the executioner's grave, earth dragon) gives «སྲོག་ཆུ་ནང་ཕྲད», the life force again; the first four are fought against the life force, body, power and la. So WB's «life force, body, power, la, life force, life force» is the list of what is fought, and the grave and the executioner's grave are what is sought (the fifth and sixth of the list of seeking), as the verse lists them; the notes' six «འཐབ་ཟླ» are the same six. The earlier claim's reading of the repeats as slips is withdrawn (claims/ch28-360-six-aspects.md corrected). The second half, «ཕྱི་རྩིས» or «སྤྱི་རྩིས», stays open: the line was not found on the reprint's image 048 or 049 in a crop that settled the first letter.
