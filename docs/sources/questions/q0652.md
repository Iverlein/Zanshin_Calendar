# The kung of «ཐུན་ཀུང» in the hand reckoning

What is the kung (ཀུང) of «ཐུན་ཀུང», which the hand reckoning counts after the year and the watch? No passage found explaining the watch or the kung (RD165).

RD177 (2026-11-06): «ཐུན་ཀུང» occurs in no other etext (the 1996 etext reads «ཐུན་ཀཱང»); lead: kung = «གུང» (noon), as in the notes' «གུང་གཉིས་ཟེར་གཉིས».
