# What is བ་ར (ba ra) in «བ་རའི་འོ་མ», a milk of which animal, and why is the milk with dung rubbed on the soles for a spirit's dream?

RD304.

## Answered 2026-11-14

**What བ་ར is.** «བ་ར» is «ba» (cow) and «ra» (goat), the herd: Ives-Waldo, the Tshig mdzod chen mo and the Bod yig tshig gter rgya mtsho give «cattle and goats» (claim ba-ra-herd-milk). The medical commentary on the Four Tantras' own milk verse confirms it. Zur mkhar ba, in the chapter on foods, comments on the lines that sum up the white foods (the verse from «དཀར་རྣམས» to «སྙོམས་པ་ཡིན», etext VEIE0OPI412934D9_I8LS19191, p. 589): «ལུག་དང་འབྲིའི་འོ་མ་སོགས་དྲོ་ལ་བཅུད་ཆེ་བ་དང༌། བ་དང་རའི་འོ་མ་སོགས་བསིལ་ཡང་དང་ལྡན་པ། མཛོའི་འོ་མ་དང་ཞོ་ལ་སོགས་པ་ནི་བསིལ་དྲོད་སྙོམས་པས་མཆོག་ཏུ་བཟང་བ་ཡིན»: the milk of sheep and female yaks is warm and rich, the milk of cattle and goats is cool and light, the milk of the dzo and curd are balanced and best. The same sentence stands in a modern health manual (MW1AC256). So «བ་རའི་འོ་མ», the cold-phlegm cause in the Four Tantras' lists, is the cool, light milk of cows and goats together, no single animal. Searched: BDRC «བ་རའི་འོ་མ» 109 hits (the Four Tantras' prints, remedy manuals, ritual and Bon texts: «བ་རའི་འོ་མ» with butter, curd or boiled with butter) and none glosses the pair; «བ་ར་ཞེས་པ་ནི» has four hits, none this word.

**Why the milk with dung is rubbed on the soles.** No text gives a reason. The Blue Beryl (p. 550) and Skyem pa Tshe dbang (p. 1015) both put it among the «རྟེན་འབྲེལ», the auspicious-connection rite (རྟེན་འབྲེལ, rten 'brel) that follows the divination and the service rite, not among the medicines: Blue Beryl «དེ་མཐུན་གྱི་རིམ་གྲོ་དང་། རྟེན་འབྲེལ་ནི་བ་རའི་འོ་མར་དྲི་ཆེན་སྦྱར་བ་རྐང་མཐིལ་ལ་བྱུག», Skyem pa «དེ་མཐུན་གྱི་རིམ་གྲོ་དང་རྟེན་འབྲེལ་བྱ», and the Serthang text's own bracket runs «མོ་ཕྱྭ་རིམ་གྲོ་རྟེན་ ‖ འབྲེལ་བར་འོ་མ་…་རྐང་མཐིལ་ལ་བྱུག» (st:p493, st:p493b). So the milk and dung are a rten 'brel for the spirit that leads the sleeper away, a connection made in the body, and the texts say no more of why milk, why dung or why the soles.

Scope: the identification of the milk is answered; the reason for the dung and the soles is not in the sources read, and stays unknown.
