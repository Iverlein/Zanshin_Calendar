# The thirty's 241st pebble

WB counts the thirty's pebbles at 241 (p. 454), but thirty points of eight make 240. What is the one more pebble?

**Narrowed 2026-10-25 (RD50, [claims/thirty-241-pebbles.md](../claims/thirty-241-pebbles.md)).** «ཉིས་བརྒྱ་དང་བཞི་བཅུ་རྩ་གཅིག», 241, reads so on the reprint (by eye), the Zhol print (machine) and the 1996 etext; the *Moonbeams* settles at 240 and all thirty points have exactly eight checks. No text names a 241st pebble; the layable pebbles are 240.
