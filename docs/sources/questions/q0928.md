# Does the Sanskrit of Nīlakaṇṭha's Ācāramayūkha (Negelein's q, Benares 1876 with Rāmanātha's Hindi commentary) read the fourth watch «māsena phalada» and the dawn «daśāhena» in a printed Sanskrit edition, as the Romanian translation's text layer shows?

RD317; a page image of a public-domain print is needed.

## Searched 2026-11-13, not answered

Three scans of the Ācāramayūkha on the Internet Archive (Gharpure's Bombay 1921 text, two Bhagavantabhāskara copies of the Central Government Library's collection) carry a Devanagari text layer that is unreadable (Latin and Tamil-like junk), so the verses could not be searched; the 1876 Benares print with Rāmanātha's commentary and a Svapnādhyāya of the Ācāramayūkha in a printed edition were not found by the Internet Archive's title search.

Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-j): Internet Archive title searches for the Mayūkha family list Gharpure's Ācāra Mayūkha (dli 2015.282933, the same 1921 scan already found unreadable), the Śānti, Dāna, Saṃskāra, Vyavahāra, Prāyaścitta and Pratiṣṭhā Mayūkhas (separate parts of the Bhagavantabhāskara) and a Kashi 1879 Śānti Mayūkha lithograph; none is the Ācāramayūkha with Rāmanātha's Hindi commentary of 1876, and no Svapnādhyāya turned up. Stays open for a legible print of the Ācāra part.
