# Which work in the second volume of Kun dga' rin chen's collected works (BDRC MW1KG4326, etext volume VE1KG4326_009, hit at character 14273) holds his wording of the reason for the last watch, and is it earlier than Nam mkha' dpal bzang's manual of 1581 (a reader with the volume's scan)?

RD377.

## Narrowed 2026-11-13 (inv-s)

The hit is in MW1KG4326, «gsung 'bum / Kun dga' rin chen», the Sa skya khri chen 23 Kun dga' rin chen (1517-1584): Gene Smith's collection, Kathmandu, Sa skya rgyal yongs gsung rab slob gnyer khang, 2007, «1 v. (only v. 2)», 850 pages (so the «volume 2» is the only one BDRC has). The snippet is «brlabs nas lung ston par mdzad pas / de yang srod kyi cha nyin bar gyi snang ba'i bag chags dang 'dres pas cung mi gsal zhing / nam gung gnyid mthug po zhes rmi lam mi 'byung / de nas tho rangs kyi cha'i rmi lam brtag dgos pas / de la rmi lam bzang po'i…», a dream examined after a rite «blessed and gave prophecy». BDRC's 25 parts of the volume (purl.bdrc.io, MW1KG4326_*) are in page order: dkar chag pp. 5-8; 'Jam dpal a ra pa tsa na'i bsgrub pa byed tshul pp. 9-22; Mol gtam yid bzhin nor bu pp. 23-38; Rab gnas don gsal gyi gsal byed pp. 39-188; then Yang dag me dgu, Phur pa, Mgon po, gtor chog and the like to p. 850. The character offset 14273 is early in the volume (some 16 pages if a page holds nine hundred characters; my estimate, not measured) and so falls in the Arapacana sādhana (pp. 9-22) or the Mol gtam (pp. 23-38). A prophecy-giving rite fits the Arapacana Mañjuśrī sādhana better; the work is not dated by its title. The volume answers 403, so the page was not read, and nothing here dates the work against 1581.
Still open: the text at the offset; its colophon year.

## Narrowed 2026-11-13 (inv-t)

The hit is not in the Arapacana sādhana or the Mol gtam: BDRC's etext volume VE1KG4326_009 is the ninth work of the volume, the Phur pa dbang gi mtshams sbyor (printed pp. 427-484), and not the volume as a whole (see q1196). The hit is therefore in a Vajrakīla empowerment manual of Kun dga' rin chen, where a dream after the empowerment is read. The same sentence («dbang bskur ba'i dus dpa' bo mkha' 'gro rnams kyis rgyud byin gyis brlabs nas lung ston par mdzad pas / de yang srod kyi cha nyin par gyi snang ba'i bag chags dang 'dres pas cung mi gsal zhing») stands in the Rgyud sde kun btus (MW2PD14777, Rdzong sar print, etext volume I2PD14794 at character 794583; a Glog klad print I4664 at 804052), compiled by 'Jam dbyangs blo gter dbang po (1847-1914) from the Sakya initiation manuals, so it was copied or shared, not written in the Kun btus. Whether Kun dga' rin chen's text is the source, or he took it from an earlier manual, is not known; the part has no colophon year in BDRC, so the comparison with the manual of 1581 stays open. Still open: the part's colophon (a reader with the volume's scan), and the Kun btus work that carries the sentence.
