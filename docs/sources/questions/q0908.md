# LGD p. 970 names «ལྷན་ཅིག་སྐྱེས་པའི་ལྷ» and «མཉམ་པོར་འོང་བའི་བདུད» among the causes of a confused dream; the Chöd texts gloss the second as one's own co-born ignorance and self-clinging (ལས་གདོན, las gdon), the sermon on death as the keeper of the black pebbles: which does LGD's author mean, or does he not distinguish them?

RD307.

Narrowed (inv-y, 2026-11-16): the Chöd texts read «མཉམ་པོར་འོང་བའི་བདུད» as the self-clinging, one of three names of the karmic spirit (answer to q876, nine copies of the sentence «ལས་གདོན་ཞེས་པ་ནི་ལྷན་ཅིག་སྐྱེས་པའི་མ་རིག་པའམ། ངའོ་སྙམ་པའི་བདག་འཛིན་ནམ། མཉམ་པོར་འོང་བའི་བདུད་ཅེས་སམ»), and the treasury's rites set it as the pair of the co-born god, Kong sprul's own sentence calling it a companion that arises with one. Whether the Lama Gongdü's author means this or only the stock pair is not said: no text with the Gongdü's wording was found (the search for «མཉམ་པོར་འོང་བའི་བདུད» 68 hits, none from the Gongdü's own commentary).
