# KP month panels: what does «བབ» (bab) at the end of many cells mean?

«Falls on», a place mark, or a lord's name? It is not in WB's lists.

## Answered 2026-11-21

Answered in sense: «བབ» is the verb «falls on, descends upon, alights», not a lord's name. WB uses it so in the very verse that gives the twelve earth lords of the day's animal (vol. 2 ch. 31, p. 224: «ནྟ་ཉིར་ས་བདག་བྱི་ལམ་བབ» on the horse day the earth lord mouse-road falls (here); «བྱ་ཉིར་ཧེ་ཐོན་རྒྱལ་པོར་བབ» on the bird day He-thon falls on the king), and in vol. 2's notes (བབ་སྨེ, the mewa that falls). So a cell ending «… བབ» says that the named lord or sign falls on that place of the month's house; it is not an extra lord and not in WB's lists of lords because it is not a name. What stays open is which place each cell's «བབ» marks (q1225): the panels are machine-read. State: WB etext (ch31-gaps.md) lines 478-481; KP panels machine.
