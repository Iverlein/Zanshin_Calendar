# Does MW1LT2427 hold the Moonbeams

Does the Library of Tibet manuscript volume MW1LT2427 hold the Moonbeams, and where does the 24-pebble count stand in it? (RD168)

INV-1107 (2026-11-07), answered in part: MW1LT2427 (I1LT2427) does hold the Moonbeams: BDRC's outline part 25 is «'byung rtsis man ngag zla ba'i 'od zer» (the same title as the pool's), and the scan (Yigdzin-1, imgs. 569–598) shows chapter closings at img. 572, 575, 581, 588, 591, 593 and the colophon of the Moonbeams at img. 598; BDRC's page range 577–626 is about six leaves too late (the text starts before img. 569). The 24-pebble count stands at img. 577 l. 1–2 (machine: «རྡེའུ་ཉེར་བཞིའི་གྲངས་གཅོད་དོ»), with «གྲངས་གཅོད» for the woodblock's «སྟངས་གཅོད»; the stack was not settled by eye, so q537 is not decided. Claim [mw1lt2427-holds-the-moonbeams](../claims/mw1lt2427-holds-the-moonbeams.md). The where-question is answered; the spelling is left to q537.

## Answered 2026-11-14

INV-1114 (2026-11-14), closing the question on INV-1107's reading: yes, MW1LT2427 (I1LT2427) holds the Moonbeams (མན་ངག་ཟླ་བའི་འོད་ཟེར, man ngag zla ba'i 'od zer), BDRC's outline part 25 «'byung rtsis man ngag zla ba'i 'od zer»; the scan shows the chapter closings at imgs. 572, 575, 581, 588, 591 and 593 and the Moonbeams' colophon at img. 598, BDRC's page range 577–626 being about six leaves too late. The 24-pebble count (རྡེའུ་ཉེར་བཞི, rde'u nyer bzhi) stands at img. 577, l. 1–2. The stack's spelling (གྲངས་གཅོད, grangs gcod, against the woodblock's སྟངས་གཅོད, stangs gcod) is not settled by eye and stays with q537. Claim [mw1lt2427-holds-the-moonbeams](../claims/mw1lt2427-holds-the-moonbeams.md).
