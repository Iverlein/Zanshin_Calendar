# Is «ཐུན་གསུམ» the Tibetan of Sanskrit triyāmā, and is it in the Amarakośa?

RD314; no Tibetan Amarakośa found.

## Answered 2026-11-10

Answered by RD322 (2026-11-10), on lexica: yes, ཐུན་གསུམ is the Tibetan of Sanskrit triyāmā in Negi's Tibetan–Sanskrit dictionary (Steinert no. 50): 'the night, triyāmā, rātriḥ', with the Amarakośa 1.4.5 synonym verse and the gloss 'trayo yāmā yasyāḥ sā triyāmā'; Negi gives ཐུན་གསུམ་པ also as tṛtīyapraharaḥ (citing Mahāvyutpatti 8242); the Tshig mdzod chen mo, the Tshig gter rgya mtsho, Valby and Ives–Waldo list it among the night's names. The Mahāvyutpatti itself has five watches of the night, the third ཐུན་གསུམ་པའམ་ལྔ་ཚིགས = tṛtīyapraharaḥ. The Tibetan Amarakośa itself was not reached.
