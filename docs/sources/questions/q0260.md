# Which trigram of the dead picks the grave provisions

Which trigram of the dead selects the block of grave provisions (WB vol. 2, pp. 129–132)? The blocks open "if one died in li" («ལིར་ཤི་ན», «ཁོན་ལ་ཤི་ན»…) and Mipham's notes (p. 522) say "whichever is the dead's trigram" («གཤིན་སྤར་གང་ཡིན»): the dead's natal trigram, or the trigram of the year of death by age and sex?
