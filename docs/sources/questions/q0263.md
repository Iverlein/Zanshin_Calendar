# «སྒོམ» or «སྒོ» in the fifteenth's heading

The heading of the fifteenth (p. 128) reads «དུར་ལྔའི་སྨྲང་སྒོམ་དབྱེ» with the stack after sa between ma and ka on the reprint (the etext «སྨྲང», RD16 «སྐྲང»); the Zhol blocks carve «སྨྲང་སྒོ་དབྱེ» (question 68). Is the 1996 «སྒོམ» a slip for «སྒོ»?

## Answered 2026-10-23

Answered 2026-10-23 (RD46): the blocks carve «སྒོ་དབྱེ» with no ma; the 1996 «སྒོམ» is the setting's slip.
