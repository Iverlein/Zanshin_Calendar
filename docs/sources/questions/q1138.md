# Do རབ་ཚེས and ཟླ་ཚེས in Mipham's colophon stand for a rab byung number or a fixed phrase, and which fire-horse is meant

«རབ་ཚེས་མེ་རྟ་ཟླ་ཚེས»: 1846, 1906 or a day?

## Answered 2026-11-14

1906, the fire-horse year of the fifteenth rab byung, with no month or day given. «རབ་ཚེས» (rab tshes) is a name of the fifteenth rab byung: the Tshig mdzod chen mo and the Tshig gter rgya mtsho «རབ་བྱུང་བཅོ་ལྔ་པའི་མིང་», Ives Waldo "15th year of rabjung". «ཟླ་ཚེས» (zla tshes) is, in the same two dictionaries, «ཟླ་གྲངས་ཚེས་གྲངས་ཟེར་བའི་བསྡུས་ཚིག» (the short form of «zla grangs tshes grangs», month-number and date-number); Ives Waldo "month and date, date of the month" (tmz:box8). The colophon «རབ་ཚེས་མེ་རྟ་ཟླ་ཚེས་ལ་མི་ཕམ་པས་བྲིས» reads "written by Mi pham on the month and date of the fire-horse [year] of the fifteenth rab byung", a stock closing that names the cycle and the year and leaves the month and the day as the bare words. The same use of «rab tshes» before an animal-year name stands in other colophon-like places found by BDRC's phrase search of 2026-11-14 («rab tshes me rta ste 1906lo» in a Nga khul history, which gives the year as 1906; «rab tshes chu glang ste 1913lo» in the same), and Mipham's own pattern «shing 'brug lo'i zla tshes la mi pham pas bris» (wood-dragon year, «zla tshes») in the Jonang liturgical collections, search-only snippets (etext API 403), shows the closing «zla tshes la mi pham pas bris» after a year-name. The app's engine places the fifteenth rab byung's fire-horse year in 1906 (rabjung 15/40, Fire Horse year; the fire-horse of 1846 is rabjung 14/40, and 1966 is 16/40), so 1846, the year of his birth, is excluded by «rab tshes», and no day is meant. The date is of the writing: the work is from 1906. The colophon gives no night or hour for the bad dream it turns back.
