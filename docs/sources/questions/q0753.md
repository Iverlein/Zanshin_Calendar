# In the mother-goddesses table (WB vol. 1, p. 129), do the right block's row 5 and row 6's middle cell (དཔལ་མོ where its counterparts read དབང་མོ) hold misprints? What are the numerals under each name, and what is the lobed label's second line?

**Narrowed 2026-11-24 (sweep from I1KG12906 [p139], [p144]).** Searched 2026-11-24 (inv-t) in the 1996 etext of vol. 1 (I1KG12906 [p139], printed p. 129): the table's names are typed, the numerals are typed too but as one jumbled stream without the cells' order, so no numeral can be put under a name from the text. What the etext does settle: its third row group reads «ཙམུ་ཟླ་དབང་མོ་ ཁྱབ་འཇུག་མ་ཙཱམུ་ཏྟཱ་ དཔལ་མོ་ ཁྱབ་འཇུག་མ་», a left block with དབང་མོ and a right block with དཔལ་མོ in the middle cell, the same as the eye reading; the other seven blocks of the Tsa mu nda rows read དབང་མོ (the rows of three blocks «ཙཱམུ་ཙྪཱ་ དབང་མོ་ ཁྱབ་འཇུག་མ་ཙཱམུ་ཎཱ་དབང་མོ་…ཙཱ་མུ་ཏྟཱ་དབང་མོ་» and «ཙཱམུ་ཌཱ་ དབང་མོ་» twice). So the དཔལ་མོ of the right block's row 6 is what the 1996 print prints (an OCR independent of the eye), and it is the only deviation among eight blocks; whether it is a misprint is not told by a text. The etext also spells the third name four ways (ཙྪཱ, ཎཱ, ཏྟཱ, ཌཱ), print variants of one name. The right block's row 5 is garbled in the etext («དྲུག་མོ་ ཁྱབ་འཇུག་མ་ ཕག་མོ་ ཕག་»), no use. A way to hold the numerals: p. 134's verses (etext [p144]) give each goddess her tithis, Tshangs ma 1, 9, 16, 24; Drag mo 2, 10, 17, 25; Gzhon nu ma 3, 11, 18, 26; Khyab 'jug ma 4, 12, 19, 27; Phag mo 5, 13, 20, 28; Dbang mo 6, 14, 21, 29; Tsa mu nda 7, 15, 22 and half the full-moon day; Dpal mo 8, 23 and the other half (read from the etext, not on the scan): numerals under a name on a sharper scan can be held against these. The lobed label's second line is not in the etext.
