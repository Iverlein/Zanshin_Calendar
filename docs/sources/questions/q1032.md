# What do the lines «རྨི་ལམ་ཞེས་བྱ་ཡོད་པ་སྤངས། བྱ་བ་དུས་ཀྱི་རིམ་པ་ཉིད། དེ་ཉིད་གཟིགས་པས་བསྟན་པ་ཡིན» (Toh 543 pp. 400-401) mean: is the dream «given up» in every way, and what is «the order of the times of the act»?

## Narrowed 2026-11-14

Two facts from the Sanskrit (edition of Gaṇapati Śāstrī, GRETIL keying, mmk.2:p149b, verse 15.46: «svapnākhyaṃ sattvavarjitam / kriyākālasamaś caiva nirdiṣṭas tattvadarśibhiḥ»). The first line pairs with the Tibetan word for word («ཞེས་བྱ» ākhya, «ཡོད་པ» sattva, «སྤངས» varjita), so the dream is not "given up in every way": the line says the so-called dream is devoid of being (see q1048). The second line's word is sama ("equal, even"), not krama, so «རིམ་པ» (rim pa) in «བྱ་བ་དུས་ཀྱི་རིམ་པ» is the Tibetan translators' rendering of a word the edition has as sama, or their copy had krama; what the "order (or equality) of the times of the act" is, no commentary says: BDRC's exact search of 2026-11-14 for «བྱ་བ་དུས་ཀྱི་རིམ་པ» and for «རྨི་ལམ་ཞེས་བྱ་ཡོད་པ་སྤངས» adds no hit beyond the Derge and Padma rdo rje's book. Still open: the sense of the second line.

## Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-z4)

From the 84000 TEI of Toh 543 (github.com/84000/data-tei, translations/kangyur/translations/088-038_toh543-the_root_tantra_of_manjusri.xml, fetched 2026-11-16), which now carries chapter 15 with its Sanskrit and notes: the line after «རྨི་ལམ་ཞེས་བྱ་ཡོད་པ་སྤངས» closes the section on the dreams of the three humours (Skt. 15.40-53), and the verses that follow it describe, type by type, the person of the phlegm type (fair, soft-spoken, long-lived, successful in rites on earth, 15.54-59) and of the bile type (hateful, strong, rites of subduing succeed, 15.60-71): so the 84000 translator renders «kriyākāla…nirdiṣṭaḥ» "those who teach the truth taught the activities and lifespans particular to each category of beings" (Skt. kāla read as lifespan, kriyā as the activities, the Tibetan's «dus» left as times). This is a reading from the text that follows, not a commentary; it makes «བྱ་བ་དུས་ཀྱི་རིམ་པ» the teaching of each type's activities and lifespans, and not a sequence of the night's watches. The same compound kriyākāla recurs at 16.9 («karma karmaphalaṃ sarvaṃ kriyākālaṃ tathaiva ca … svapnaprasaṅge pracoditam», where it is listed beside the act, its fruit, the vessel, the place and the dress) and at 54.34 («kriyākālasamāyogāt», "through the conjunction of the rite and the time"), so «rite and time» is the compound's own use, which supports kāla as time of the rite in 16.9 and 54.34 and leaves 15.53's «sama» as "equal, alike": «the rite and the time alike». Not settled.
