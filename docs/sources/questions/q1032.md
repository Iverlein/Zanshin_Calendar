# What do the lines «རྨི་ལམ་ཞེས་བྱ་ཡོད་པ་སྤངས། བྱ་བ་དུས་ཀྱི་རིམ་པ་ཉིད། དེ་ཉིད་གཟིགས་པས་བསྟན་པ་ཡིན» (Toh 543 pp. 400-401) mean: is the dream «given up» in every way, and what is «the order of the times of the act»?

## Narrowed 2026-11-14

Two facts from the Sanskrit (edition of Gaṇapati Śāstrī, GRETIL keying, mmk.2:p149b, verse 15.46: «svapnākhyaṃ sattvavarjitam / kriyākālasamaś caiva nirdiṣṭas tattvadarśibhiḥ»). The first line pairs with the Tibetan word for word («ཞེས་བྱ» ākhya, «ཡོད་པ» sattva, «སྤངས» varjita), so the dream is not "given up in every way": the line says the so-called dream is devoid of being (see q1048). The second line's word is sama ("equal, even"), not krama, so «རིམ་པ» (rim pa) in «བྱ་བ་དུས་ཀྱི་རིམ་པ» is the Tibetan translators' rendering of a word the edition has as sama, or their copy had krama; what the "order (or equality) of the times of the act" is, no commentary says: BDRC's exact search of 2026-11-14 for «བྱ་བ་དུས་ཀྱི་རིམ་པ» and for «རྨི་ལམ་ཞེས་བྱ་ཡོད་པ་སྤངས» adds no hit beyond the Derge and Padma rdo rje's book. Still open: the sense of the second line.
