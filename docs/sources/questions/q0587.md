# The first stack of «ཞྭ་བཞིན» on p. 435

Is the first stack of «[ཉ/ཞ]ྭ་བཞིན་ལ་ཁ་ནད་ལྕེར» (p. 435, line 4) the hat «ཞྭ», and what does it say of the fever of fire four and earth one?

RD175 (2026-11-06): the wa-zur under the first stack of «ཉྭ/ཞྭ་བཞིན» (Zhol img. 577 line 3) is plain, but the head is ཉ or ཞ and not told at 8×; Yigdzin-1 reads ཉྭ. Left.

RD175 (2026-11-07, second run): Gser thang prints «ཉ་བཞི་ན་ལ…» (head ཉ, no wa-zur); Taikhang's first stack is ཉ with wa-zur; Zhol not re-read.

RD175 (2026-11-07, third run): Zhol img. 577 line 4 at 12×: the head has the shape of ཉ (stem and a limb ending in a loop), wa-zur below; so ཉྭ on the Zhol and the Taikhang, ཉ on the Gser thang; the hat ཞྭ is on no print. What ཉྭ་བཞིན means is open.
