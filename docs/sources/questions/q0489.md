# The 歳破 and livestock: forbidden or good

The 歳破 and livestock: the 暦林問答集 (§11) forbids seeking (MS: bringing) horses and cattle from its direction; the NDL's exhibition says acquiring livestock there is good. Which does the printed almanac say (the paper 高島暦, D12)? And the 暦林問答集's own §31 calls the 歳對, the same place, harmless; does any text reconcile them? (RD92)
