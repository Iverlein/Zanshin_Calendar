# The 歳破 and livestock: forbidden or good

The 歳破 and livestock: the 暦林問答集 (§11) forbids seeking (MS: bringing) horses and cattle from its direction; the NDL's exhibition says acquiring livestock there is good. Which does the printed almanac say (the paper 高島暦, D12)? And the 暦林問答集's own §31 calls the 歳對, the same place, harmless; does any text reconcile them? (RD92)

**Narrowed 2026-11-05 (RD163).** No text read calls livestock good at the 歳破. The almanac line keeps livestock under the 豹尾; the handbook line (暦林問答集 §11, 1804, 1886) also forbids it at the 歳破. The exhibition page shows no source. Today's paper not seen.

**Searched 2026-11-16 (inv-z1), unchanged.** Itō's transcription of *Mandai dai zassho kokon taisei* (vols. 8, 9, 10, 12, 13, 15 and the paper in no. 77, read as text): no 歳破 or 太歳破 anywhere in them; web search «歳破 牛馬» finds the NDL exhibition page only. Not advanced.

**Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-j).** NDL Lab full texts (lab.ndl.go.jp, through the JP exit) of the handbooks the search lists for 歳破: 方鑑弁説 (pid 760835), 方鑑秘伝集 (760832), 方鑑秘訣 (760826), 陰陽方位便覧 (759955), 方鑑精義大成 (760817, 912878, 2565914), 永代万暦大雑書大全 (759985): a regex for 歳破/歲破 with 牛馬, 馬牛, 六畜, 畜類 or 家畜 within 60 characters before or 120 after gives no hit in any of them (the one 歳破 and 馬 pair, 759955 frame 54, is OCR noise on 日破). No text calls livestock good at the 歳破 or reconciles it with the 歳對. Not advanced.
