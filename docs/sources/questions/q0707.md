# The year Aṅgiras: ཨདྒིར or ཨངྒིར (entry 9, p. 206)

ཨདྒིར or ཨངྒིར as the print's name of the year Aṅgiras (entry 9, p. 206, line 16)? The scan's second letter is a stack whose first member could be ད or ང; MITRA read ཨངྐིར; ch. 13 has ཨངྒི་ར. (RD190, corpus/wb/ch18.md)

## Looked at 2026-11-13 (inv-x)

BDRC full-text search (the White Beryl etexts): «ཨངྒིར་ཞེས་ཆུ་ཕོ» 5 hits, among them the Gser thang etext (MW3CN5026, VEIE0OPI3864355D_I3CN5028, the volume of the print, char 335972 of the volume), where the line reads «ཨངྒིར་ཞེས་ཆུ་ཕོ་སྤྲེལ»; «ཨདྒིར་ཞེས་ཆུ་ཕོ» 0 hits. Other texts write the year's name as «ཨངྒིར» (ang+gir) or «ཨངྐི་ར» (3 hits, other works). So the etext of the same print has ང over ག, as ch. 13 does; an OCR reading, not a scan: the question stays open until the stack is seen on img. 205 at 6×.
