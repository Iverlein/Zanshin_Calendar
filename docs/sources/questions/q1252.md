# WB p. 70: the name «རྟ་ལྡང» (rta ldang / ljang) in the homage

In the homage of WB vol. 1 p. 70 (reprint I3CN5028 img. 77, line 6) the name of the learned and diligent one who drew out the tables reads «རྟ་ལྡང» (rta ldang) in both machine readings. On the Gser thang reprint, at 4× in the half-line crop, the stack under «ལ» is a blot in which ྡ (da), ྗ (ja) and ྟ (ta) cannot be told apart. Which is the name: Rta ldang, Rta ljang or Rta ltang? A reader who knows the Phug school's table-makers, or a second impression of the blocks (Zhol, Taikhang), would settle it.
