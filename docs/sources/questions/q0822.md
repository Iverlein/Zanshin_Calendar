# ཕྱུ in ST ch. 91 «མོ་ཕྱུ་རིམ་གྲོ་རྟེན་འབྲེལ»: what is the second of the four remedies?

Drathang machine reads ཕྱྭ.

## Answered 2026-11-09

The pair མོ་ཕྱྭ (mo phywa) is one stock compound, divination and its omen or fortune (the Tshig mdzod chen mo lists «མོ་ཕྱྭ» under ཕྱྭ, omen; Rangjung Yeshe, Dan Martin and the Dag tshig give luck, lots, prophecy). So there is no separate second remedy: the remedies are divination with its omen, the rite of service, the dependent link and the mantra. See st-mo-phywa-divination-and-omen.
