# The Treasury's verse on the one-day vow reads «ནམ་ནི་ལངས་བར་དུ» in Lo chen's print and «ནམ་ནི་ནང་པར་དུ» in the Derge etext of the commentary: which is the Derge blocks' reading?

Raised by the practice-days investigation (pd-c, 2026-11-16); see the cited passages.

## Answered 2026-11-16

On the Derge reprint's page (I1456 img. 364, the reprint's p. 362) the verse's second line is broken over lines 2 and 3 of the block: line 2 ends «…བཟླས་པ་ཡིས། །མི་བརྒྱན་ནམ» and line 3 begins with the rest before «།བསྙེན་གནས་ཡན་ལག་ཚང་བར་ནི». Enlarged six times, the head of line 3 shows six letter columns, «ནི», «ན», «ང», «པ», «ར», «དུ»: no «ལ», and no «ས» before the fourth. The Derge blocks read «ནམ་ནི་ནང་པར་དུ», as the etext has it; «ལངས་བར་དུ» is the reading of the print of Lo chen's commentary (dpn:i58). The image is 2000 px wide and bitonal, a line about 46 px high: the count and shape of the letters are clear at this size, the tsheg between them is not, so the division into syllables follows the etext.
