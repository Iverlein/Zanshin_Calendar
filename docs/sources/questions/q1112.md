# Does ཟ་ཟི (za zi) in Rtsa gsum gling pa's verse life name the half-asleep condition or only the dimness of the sight

The sight after a brief doze is a hazy experience-appearance dream and ends when memory is caught. Does za zi name the half-asleep condition itself, or only the dimness of the sight, in the usage of the tradition?

## Answered 2026-11-13

Only the dimness of what is seen. The keyed dictionaries give za zi (ཟ་ཟི) as "not clear, out of order", and the Dag tshig gsar bsgrigs gives «rmi lam za zi» (a hazy, disordered dream) as its own example beside «a za zi work» and «to make the head za zi»; the Tshig mdzod chen mo has it for confusion and the mirage that confuses the head; Barron has nebulous, indistinct, hazy (tmz:box4). No dictionary gives it the sense "half asleep" or any name of a state of sleep. So in «ཉམས་སྣང་རྨི་ལམ་ཟ་ཟི» (tsl.30:i102-103) the word qualifies the dream: a hazy, indistinct experience-appearance dream; that the sleeper had just dozed is said by the line before («ཅུང་ཟད་གཉིད་ལ་སོང», he slept a little), not by za zi.
