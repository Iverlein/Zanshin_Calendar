# In Rtsa gsum gling pa's life (tsl.30:i87), is the «Monkey month» (སྤྲེལ་ཟླ) the ninth lunar month (monkey-year animal) or a month named by another reckoning? The page gives the tenth day but no year.

RD363.

Narrowed 2026-11-13: «སྤྲེལ་ཟླ» is not a number and not the year's animal: it is the almanac's month named by its animal. WB's model almanac heads Hor month 5 «ཧོར་ཟླ་ལྔ་པ་རྒྱ་རྩིས་སྟོན་ར། … སྤྲེལ་ཟླ་ཆེ་ཆུང་བལྟ།» (wb.1:p163), "by the Chinese reckoning the beginning of autumn, see the monkey month's great and small"; the ninth month is not the monkey month there. The colophons of the same volumes write the first autumn month as «སྟོན་ར» (i661, the tenth of it in the earth-pig year). That the dream speaks in this count is an inference; no year is given. Claim tsl-monkey-month-named-by-its-animal.
