# Does a reckoner's own gloss say whether Wangkhyi's «ཉི་མ་དུས་ཚོད» is met only when day and hour are both the animal's, or by either

Does a reckoner's own gloss (a living almanac maker's, the Moonbeams', the Sde-srid's replies) say whether Wangkhyi's «ཉི་མ་དུས་ཚོད» of p. 210 is met only when the day and the hour are both the animal's, or by either? No print read separates them (RD76); narrows q445.

RD182 (2026-11-06), narrowed: no reckoner's gloss of «ཉི་མ་དུས་ཚོད»; WB's own uses read it as two things, a day and an hour, that can differ (vol. 1 p. 376 «ཉི་མ་དུས་ཚོད་མི་འདྲ་ན། དུས་ཚོད་རྣོ་བས་གཙོ།»; notes p. 498); none says whether one suffices; the leaning is both.
