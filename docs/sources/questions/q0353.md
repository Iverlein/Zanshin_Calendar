# The watch and the kung of the hand reckoning, and mung, krung, kyis

In the hand reckoning (pp. 538–539), what are the watch (ཐུན, thun) and the *kung* (the scan «ཐུན་ཀུང», Yigdzin-1 «ཐུན་ཀླུང») counted after the year, and what do the results *mung*, *krung* and *kyis* mean?

RD165 (2026-11-06), narrowed: the typeset notes (by eye, I1PD34647 img. 685–687) and the Gser thang etext read the three result names «མུང», «ཀྲུང», «ཀྱིས»; Mipham's block print, by the machine and RD132's eye, gives «ཕུང», «ཀླུང». The two editions part on the first two names; which Mipham wrote is open, and claims/notes-hand-reckoning-three-results.md (which calls the typeset readings misreadings) awaits review. The sense of the watch and of the kung is not found anywhere (q652).
