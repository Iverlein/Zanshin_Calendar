# «ཕྱི་བསྐོར» in Glag bla's passage

Is ཕྱི་བསྐོར in Glag bla's passage (printed p. 442) the reverse of the Dharma circuit, or an outward turning of the roll? The Bon circuit it belongs to reads as the reverse by K of prayer-wheel.md.

RD171 (2026-11-06), narrowed: in Tshig mdzod chen mo «ཕྱི་སྐོར» first means circling with the object kept on the left (Ives Waldo: counter-clockwise); a Nyingma text ties «bon bskor» to the mantra head turned inward. The likeliest sense is the reverse of the Dharma circuit. No Bon text used for any sense.
