# The la places' cut-off and friend years for the other four clans

The notes chart the twelve la places only for a wood clan (WB vol. 2, p. 500), where the great and small cut-off (sheep, ox) and the great and small friend year (dragon, dog) share the four earth corners. How are the cut-off and friend places assigned for clans of the other four elements?

## Answered 2026-10-23

Answered 2026-10-23 (RD45, [claims/clan-twelve-years.md](../claims/clan-twelve-years.md)): LT gives the great and small cut-off, the power, mother, son, friend and enemy years for all five clans (lt:p26-28, read on the scan): the great cut-off is the element's grave year (wood sheep, fire dog, iron ox, earth and water dragon), the small cut-off its seventh (lt:p12); UT agrees for water, wood and iron (ut:p56, etext). That this table is the la board's is an inference; LT's fire and iron rows go against the notes' rule of which is great and which small (question 375).
