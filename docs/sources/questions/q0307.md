# Over which pebbles the sounding's black and white are counted

In the copper vessel's sounding (WB vol. 2, p. 91), the summary "two black: the twenty-five demons; two white: the twenty-five great gods; one black … one white …" — over which pebbles are the two or one black counted: a place's pair of fighting pebbles, a column of four aspects, or the twenty-five deciding pebbles together?

## Answered 2026-11-01

Over the twenty-five deciding pebbles together, wherever they fall (Mipham's note on the sounding, WB vol. 2, p. 506): claim dead-sounding-black-white-over-twenty-five.
