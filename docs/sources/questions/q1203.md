# KP label 118: «ཤིང་སྒོ» or «ཤིད་སྒོ» (funeral feast) for the nine-three sky door?

The machine reads the nine-three door as «ཤིང་སྒོ» (wood door); WB's feast door would be «ཤིད» (shid, funeral feast). Not settled at 1.6×. Wanted: BDRC label 118, about line 5, in a sharper crop.

## Answered 2026-11-14

It is «ཤིད་སྒོ» (funeral-feast door, ཤིད, shid), WB's feast door. On BDRC label 118, line 5 (pool img. 116), at 7× and 12×, after «༧» the first syllable is ཤ with the vowel ི and a final «ད» whose head is the flagged angular form, where the «ང» of «ཀྱང་» a few syllables earlier on the same line is rounder with a curved tail; the heading follows «སྒོ་སྒྲིབ་ན་ཝ་ཐོད་ལ། སྲིན་མོ་ནག་མོའི་…» as the machine read. The machine's «ཤིང» (wood) was a misreading; the pair nine-three is therefore WB's. Recorded in kp:i114-116 and the claim kp-sky-doors-same-pairs-as-wb. KP2 (hand copy) could not be read at this place.
