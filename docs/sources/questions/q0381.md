# The household round's «སྐྱེད་པུར» and «མ་ཏྲཾ»

The household round's remedies (WB vol. 1, p. 386) name «སྐྱེད་པུར» or «སྐྱེད་བུར» on the Zhol blocks (a square-headed letter, not ཕ) beside the white broth, and the demon ransom with «མ་ཏྲཾ» (read with doubt): what are the two offerings?

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-ddd).** «སྐྱེད་པུར» is the hearth: the Tshig mdzod chen mo and the Tshig gter rgya mtsho give «sgyed pu» as (1) the hearthstone (2) the iron three-legged hearth, Ives Waldo and Valby as hearth, hearthstones, Negi as Sanskrit *cullī*; BDRC finds «སྐྱེད་པུར» spelled so in a Tsolho history («བང་ཁའི་ཨོང་བ་སྐྱེད་པུར་དེད་ནས་མེ་འབུད» with the variant *sgyed pu*) and in a tantra of the Gsang thus thig le (*skyed pur (sgyed pu) btsugs so*, the hearth planted). So the remedy is the white broth offered at the hearth (the household round's red pebble names the hearth on the same page). «མ་ཏྲཾ» (the Zhol print; «མ་ཏྲི» elsewhere): no headword in the sixty-four dictionaries except Jaeschke's, Valby's and Ives Waldo's «ma tri ma tris la 'dzu», a Bon prayer, which is no source here; the ransom stays unglossed. Half answered: the hearth, yes; the ransom, open.
