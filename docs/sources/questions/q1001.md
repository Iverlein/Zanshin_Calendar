# Does the 東都歳事記 (1838) entry for the second day of the first month (the canvases after 16 of the Ochanomizu copy, DIG-OCHA-00706, or the Hiroshima, Tsukuba or Kokubunken copies) name the 初夢, and does the 歳時記栞草's source 紀事 fix the night at New Year's Eve?

RD358.

## Answered 2026-11-13

Narrowed, not answered. The 東都歳事記's (1838) entry for the second day of the first month has no 初夢 (Ochanomizu canvas 18, RD364, read in part; the right page from the third day on was not read in RD398). The 栞草's 初夢 entry (mdz:i7333115b) cites 紀事 for "the dream from the night of the last day of the year to the dawn of New Year's Day", so the eve is fixed by the 栞草 through its source; the 紀事 itself is not named further and not found. The Hiroshima, Tsukuba and Kokubunken copies of the 東都歳事記 were not opened.

## Answered 2026-11-14

Yes to the second part, no to the first. The 東都歳事記's entry for the second day has no 初夢: it was read on the 1838 Ochanomizu leaf (mdz:i70618) and now also on the Meiji reprint of 1893 (NDL Digital Collections pid 764254, image 18, mdz:i76425418), where it is the same list (the castle audience, the merchants' credit day, the Yoshiwara visit, the linked-verse meeting, the first sewing), and the NDL's text of the four reprint volumes 764254, 764255, 764259 and 764260 has no 初夢 or 宝船 anywhere. The 歳時記栞草 gives the 初夢 under its source label 紀事: "the first dream is the dream from the night of the last day of the year to the dawn of New Year's Day" (大晦日の夜より元日の暁に至るの夢, mdz:i87533211, read in the Meiji reprints of 1892 and 1894), so the 紀事, as the 栞草 quotes it, fixes the night at New Year's Eve; the 紀事 itself was not found. The Hiroshima, Tsukuba and Kokubunken copies were not opened.
