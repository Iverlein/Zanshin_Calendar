# ཐང་ཞིང and རི་ཞིང: plain and mountain field

ཐང་ཞིང (*thang zhing*) and རི་ཞིང (*ri zhing*), the plain field and the mountain field: which crops? (RD190, corpus/wb/ch18.md)

## Looked at 2026-11-13 (inv-x)

The Gser thang etext (VEIE0OPI3864355D_I3CN5028) has both words as the pool does, always as a pair against each other (e.g. «ཐང་ཞིང་ལེགས་ཤིང་སྐྱ་རྒྱལ་འོང»; «རི་ཞིང་བཟང་ལ་གཤོངས་ཞིང་ངན» ; «རི་ཞིང་རྒྱལ་ལ་གཤོངས་ཞིང་འཕམ»); the verses give no crop. No gloss found. Open.

## Answered 2026-11-14

They are not crops. ཐང་ཞིང (thang zhing) is "a field lying on the plain" (thang la yod pa'i zhing ga) and རི་ཞིང (ri zhing) "a field lying on the face of a hill" (ri'i ngos la yod pa'i zhing ga), Tshig mdzod chen mo; Valby "fields on level land" and "field on hill slope"; Ives Waldo "field in a flatland" and "mountain-side field". The notation sets them as a pair against each other (the plain fields good, the mountain fields harmed, or the reverse). Which crop does well on which is not said by the dictionaries and not by WB's verses.

Source: tmz:box6 (read 2026-11-14); the pairs in wb.1:p207 to wb.1:p212.
