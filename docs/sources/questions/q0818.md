# Does ST ch. 2's dream of a man mean the pregnant woman sees a man, or sees clearly?

The pregnancy line's «ཡང» and «དག» are unsettled.

## Answered 2026-11-10

RD356: the dream of a man in ch. 2 is of men seen many times (དག plural), not the woman seeing clearly; same letters on the Serthang, Drathang, Derge and Zhol.
