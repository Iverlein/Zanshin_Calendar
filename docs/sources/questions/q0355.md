# Which findings make an aspect bad in the karma reckoning

The karma reckoning that overrules a match (WB p. 364; the notes, p. 526) is each spouse's birth reckoning of ch. 22; which of its findings make the life force, body or luck "bad": the deciding pebbles of that aspect's block of the hundred (pp. 261, 329–331), its rise and decline at birth, or something else? The notes say so only of the power ("small in the karma reckoning", the hundred's power, p. 478).

**By analogy 2026-10-23 (RD48, [claims/match-two-bad-lives.md](../claims/match-two-bad-lives.md)).** Reply 111 (ys:i467-468, on the scan) bars a match when both lives are "utterly bad" (ངན་ཐ་ཆད), each spouse's life reckoning gathered into one verdict pebble by its white and black; the single bad findings are not each a bar. Which verdict that is, question 388.

**Narrowed 2026-10-25 ([claims/karma-reckoning-aspect-bad.md](../claims/karma-reckoning-aspect-bad.md)).** Mipham's notes on the birth (wb.2:p483, on the scan) call an aspect in decline (གུད, gud) when its verdict on its pebbles of the hundred is black, one such finding alone "no great fault"; WB's black readings of the life force's, body's, power's and luck's own pebbles (wb.1:p329-331, those lines on the scan) answer p. 364's "life exhausted", "vitality exhausted", "poor" and "ill speech", and p. 331's «ཟས་ནོར་དབང་ཐང་ཆུང་བ» is the notes' own "power small"; UT's karma reckoning (ut:p153-155, etext only) also ends in one verdict per aspect. So, as a reading: an aspect is bad when its block's verdict is black, and "the reckoning bad in general" is WB's verdict on the whole birth (wb.1:p349). No text states it of the match. Still open: the five doors' condition (question 393) and the white enemy pebble (question 394).
