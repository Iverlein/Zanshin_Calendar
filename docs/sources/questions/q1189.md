# Do the Sanskrit manuscripts of the Mañjuśrīmūlakalpa (not Śāstrī's print) read kramaś or samaś in verse 15.46, and which did the Tibetan translators have for «རིམ་པ»?

Raised by the 2026-11-13 investigation o.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-gg)

Narrowed, not settled. The print itself reads samaś: on the page image of Śāstrī's edition (TSS 70, p. 149, leaf n154) the line is «kriyākālasamaścaiva nirdiṣṭastattvadarśibhiḥ», with no footnote and no variant, so GRETIL's «samaś» (q1136) is not a keying error. Śāstrī's print is of a single palm-leaf manuscript (found near Padmanabhapuram in 1909) and Vaidya's Buddhist Sanskrit Texts 18 (1964) reprints it with small changes. The other manuscripts the 84000 translation's Sanskrit appendix lists (two National Archives Kathmandu copies, 5/814 and 3/303, the Thiruvananthapuram C-2388, and a Tokyo University copy, Matsunami no. 275) were not reachable: 84000's published text of Toh 543 does not reach chapter 15, and the Hamburg Early Tantra project's critical edition of some chapters is not published. Still open: krama against sama in the manuscripts, and the translators' Sanskrit for «རིམ་པ»; the NGMPP microfilms of the Kathmandu copies are the next witness. Passage mmk.2:p149c.
