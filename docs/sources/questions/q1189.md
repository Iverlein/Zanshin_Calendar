# Do the Sanskrit manuscripts of the Mañjuśrīmūlakalpa (not Śāstrī's print) read kramaś or samaś in verse 15.46, and which did the Tibetan translators have for «རིམ་པ»?

Raised by the 2026-11-13 investigation o.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-gg)

Narrowed, not settled. The print itself reads samaś: on the page image of Śāstrī's edition (TSS 70, p. 149, leaf n154) the line is «kriyākālasamaścaiva nirdiṣṭastattvadarśibhiḥ», with no footnote and no variant, so GRETIL's «samaś» (q1136) is not a keying error. Śāstrī's print is of a single palm-leaf manuscript (found near Padmanabhapuram in 1909) and Vaidya's Buddhist Sanskrit Texts 18 (1964) reprints it with small changes. The other manuscripts the 84000 translation's Sanskrit appendix lists (two National Archives Kathmandu copies, 5/814 and 3/303, the Thiruvananthapuram C-2388, and a Tokyo University copy, Matsunami no. 275) were not reachable: 84000's published text of Toh 543 does not reach chapter 15, and the Hamburg Early Tantra project's critical edition of some chapters is not published. Still open: krama against sama in the manuscripts, and the translators' Sanskrit for «རིམ་པ»; the NGMPP microfilms of the Kathmandu copies are the next witness. Passage mmk.2:p149c.

## Narrowed again 2026-11-16 (inv-w)

No manuscript reading reached. A web search for the two forms «kriyākālakramaś» and «kriyākālasamaś» with the pāda «nirdiṣṭastattvadarśibhiḥ» returned no page that holds either string (only the 84000 translation page, encyclopediaofbuddhism.org, Wikipedia and a McGill thesis on the kriyā classification of three chapters, M. Ellis, 2021, not opened); the critical text and the Nepal manuscripts remain the open witnesses.

## Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-z3)

The translators' Sanskrit for «རིམ་པ»: the Tibetan of the line is «བྱ་བ་དུས་ཀྱི་རིམ་པ་ཉིད» (mmk.1:p400-401), and «rim pa» is «kramaḥ» in the Mahāvyutpatti (Steinert's set 21), in Negi's Sanskrit dictionary (set 50: 1. kramaḥ) and in Hopkins' Sanskrit-equivalents («[LCh,MSA] krama»); a rendering of «sama» would be «mnyam pa» or «mtshungs pa». So the translators most probably had krama, the reading of the pool's question, against Śāstrī's single manuscript («samaś»); the dictionary shows what «rim pa» regularly renders, not a Sanskrit manuscript of the translators. The Sanskrit manuscripts themselves are still unseen: 84000's web pages of Toh 543 (chapter 15, appendix; WebFetch 2026-11-16) are cut off at chapter 1 and give neither the Sanskrit of the verse nor its apparatus; a web search of «kriyākālasamaś / kramaś» finds nothing.

## Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-z4)

A new text: the 84000 TEI of Toh 543 (github.com/84000/data-tei, translations/kangyur/translations/088-038_toh543-the_root_tantra_of_manjusri.xml, fetched 2026-11-16), which now carries chapter 15 with its Sanskrit and notes. Its Sanskrit of 15.53 reads «tasmāt sarvaprakāreṇa svapnākhyaṃ sattvavarjitam / kriyākālasamaś caiva nirdiṣṭas tattvadarśibhiḥ», samaś, with no apparatus entry on the word (emendations are marked there as «em.; … S», S being Śāstrī's print), and its note on the Tibetan of the verse (15.52 of the English) says only "The Tibetan translates as «…the sequence of activities and lifespans…»" without the remark «reflecting the Sanskrit *…» that the same notes make where the translators' text differed (e.g. 15.54 bahupānā, 15.55 kṣatriye). The siglum list names the manuscripts A (NAK 5/814), B (3/303), R (3/645), T (Tokyo, Matsunami 275) and Delhey's 2008 edition, but in chapter 15 the apparatus shows only readings of S and the Tibetan, so the chapter is not shown to be collated against A, B, R or T: the edition is no independent witness for the manuscripts' reading here. The Tokyo copy is a fragment of chapters 19, 49 and 50 (Samhitā catalogue record, not opened), so it cannot hold 15.53. Still open: krama against sama in A, B, R; the NGMPP films are the next witness.
