# WB p. 250: «ཁ་སྨྲས» or «ཁ་སྤྲས», and «གློ་གཅོང» or «བློ་གཙང» (print line 23)? The first letter and the ཅ/ཙ hook are not settled; and does «རྭ» (p. 250 l. 21, p. 252 l. 18) carry a wa-zur (Yigdzin-1) or not (MITRA «ར»)?

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-ppp)

Two of the three parts are settled by the dictionaries. «ཁ་སྨྲས» (kha smras) is a headword: "a man's talk, gossip" (Tshig mdzod chen mo, Ives Waldo); «ཁ་སྤྲས» has none; and the lines read as gossip: «གྲོང་དེར་ཁ་སྨྲས་གླང་གཞི་མང» "that village has much gossip and quarrel", «ཁ་སྨྲས་དགྲ་གྱོད་འཚེ་བ་མེད» "no gossip, enemies, lawsuits, harm". «གློ་གཅོང» is a headword (glo gcong: a chronic lung illness, one of the eight lung illnesses) and stands in the list of ills «འབྲས་དང་དམུ་ཆུ་གློ་གཅོང» (smallpox, dropsy, glo gcong), again in «གློ་གཅོང་དམུ་ཆུ་མཛེ་ནད» (p. 254); «བློ་གཙང» (blo gtsang, "wise, learned") has no place in a list of illnesses. The wa-zur of «རྭ» (p. 250 l. 21, p. 252 l. 18) is not decided: «རྭ་བ» (horn) and «ར་བ» (fence) are both words. Source: tmz:box9, wb.2:p250.
