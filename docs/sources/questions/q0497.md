# Which of the 簠簋内伝's two 金神 year tables did the Edo almanac follow

Which of the 簠簋内伝's two year tables (§5, or §6, "which appears in the calendars") did the printed almanac of the Edo period follow, and where does today's 乙庚 = 辰巳 alone come from, a row neither table has? (RD93)

**Searched 2026-11-16 (inv-z1), unchanged.** Itō Takayuki's transcription of *Mandai dai zassho kokon taisei* (Hokudai HUSCAP, vols. 8, 9, 10, 12, 13, 14, 15 read as text): 金神 occurs in vol. 12 (hdl 2115/85267, 「金神并七殺の事」: 金神 is Aizen myōō in origin, offence brings death to seven kin, 遊行日 exempt) and 「金神方角により年の吉凶を知る事」 (金神 at 午, at 子, at 辰巳 ... gives the year's weather and prices), a table by direction, not by year stem; no row 甲己, 乙庚 etc. was found in these volumes' text. Not advanced.

## Answered 2026-11-21

**Answered 2026-11-21 (inv-j; the finding was already in [kyureki.md](../kyureki.md), *金神 (Q497–Q501)*, and this note files it).** The printed almanac of the Edo period followed neither of the 簠簋内伝's two year tables but the table of the 拾芥抄 (Shugaisho; NDL pid 2543902, frame 26, machine text read again today): 甲己 午未申酉, 乙庚 辰巳, 丙辛 寅卯午未子丑, 丁壬 寅卯戌亥, 戊癸 申酉子丑, which the book itself ascribes to 呂才's 百忌暦 («大呂才百忌暦文也»). The 乙庚 = 辰巳 alone of today's almanac is that table's row: it is the two directions the 簠簋内伝's §5 (辰巳戌亥) and §6 (寅卯辰巳) share, and the 丙辛 row is the two put together. The Ise almanac of Enkyō 2 (1745, NDL pid 2534320, image 2) prints the 金神 on 辰 and 巳 (read by eye, in the kyureki notes), and the Edo handbook 方鑑弁説 (NDL pid 760835, frame 13, machine text) says the 金神 of the year is given «年々国字暦にも載せ記さる通り». Not a row found in the 簠簋内伝 itself, so the earlier phrase "a row neither table has" is right for the 簠簋内伝 and wrong for the 拾芥抄.

**Added 2026-11-21 (inv-j).** The 方鑑精義大成 (c.1804, NDL pid 2565914, frame 5, machine text) gives the same table by name («甲巳歳は四方午未申酉方、乙庚歳は二方辰巳方、丙辛歳は六方子丑寅卯午未方、丁壬歳は四方寅卯戌亥方、戊癸歳は四方子丑申酉方») and says the 金神 has been in the Japanese calendar for about three hundred years; it cites the 拾芥抄 for the 二白九紫 exemption.
