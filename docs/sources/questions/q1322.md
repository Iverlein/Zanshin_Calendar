# WB ch. 13 p. 154: do the 11th head's day and night stacks (36 and 33 in the upper tier by the page's own digit forms) not make a day-and-night of 60, and which figure is another digit?

The middle tiers make 30 + 30; the upper tiers 36 + 33 = 69, so one figure is misread, or the tiers are not chu tshod.

## Answered 2026-11-21

The tiers are chu tshod over chu srang over a third place, and the day's stack is 26 over 30 over 0, not 36: the first figure of the day's upper tier is a 2 read as a 3. The day and night then make 60.

WB's table of the day's length by house (wb.1:p51-52, the claim wb-ch8-day-length-by-house, figures read on the scan) gives the day as hours over *srang* for each sign: Sagittarius 26;30 for the day, so the night is 33;30; Gemini the reverse; the pairs of each sign sum to 60 hours (31;10 + 28;50, 32;20 + 27;40, 33;30 + 26;30). The 11th Hor month's head says «གཞུ་སླེབས», "the sun arrives at Sagittarius" (wb.1:p154g, line 5, and «གཞུའི་ཁྱིམ», the house of the bow, in line 1). Its day length is therefore 26;30 and its night 33;30. The middle tiers read on the page, 30 and 30, are those 30 srang; the lower tier 0 is the third place. 26 + 33 hours and 30 + 30 srang is 59 hours and 60 srang, that is 60 hours, a whole day and night: the upper tiers are 26 and 33, not 36 and 33 (36 + 33 would be 69).

So the misread figure is the day's first: the earlier pass of wb.1:p154b took it for a ring-headed hook (the page's 2 by the key of wb.1:p154g's claim), and the later pass of wb.1:p154g took it for a heavy 3; the table settles it as 2. The night's «33» and both middle tiers stand.
