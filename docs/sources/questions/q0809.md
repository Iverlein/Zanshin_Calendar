# What is the dream part of MDP (printed pp. 134–137), and who composed it: an old text or the collectors' own?

Only the contents line was reached.

## Answered 2026-11-10

Answered by RD284: MDP pp. 134–137 is a transmitted verse rite for seeing one's next birth, ending «རྫོགས་སོ», with a lineage (Vikhyātadeva, Śākyaśrī, Dānaśrī, 'Jam gsar, Rga lo, Zed dmar ba, Bu ston) and prose instructions, closing with the notes of a monk Sgra tshad pa; not the collectors' composition, no composer named. The item at p. 138 is the RST text of Klong rdol bla ma.
