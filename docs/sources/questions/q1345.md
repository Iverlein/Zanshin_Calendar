# In mdb:p165 (the Mindroling rule-book's monthly list) which day precedes gcig la after the Rgyal tshab Drung pa tea-day, where the OCR has a gap

From the 2026-11-21 practice-day readings (rd-f, rd-g).

## Answered 2026-11-21

No day is missing: «གཅིག་ལ» (gcig la) is part of the tea clause, not the end of a day word. Read on the scan (BDRC I2210, images 163-166, folios 79a-80b) and in the etext, mdb:p163-166.

The clause reads «བཅོ་ལྔ་ལ་བསང་མཆོད་སྔར་ལྟར་དང་། ཡུམ་གྱི་དུས་མཆོད་དུ་… བསྐང་བ་གསོལ་མཆོད་དང་། རྒྱལ་ཚབ་དྲུང་པའི་དགོངས་རྫོགས་ཀྱི་ཟླ་ཇ་གཅིག་ལ་བཟང་སྤྱོད་འདོན་ཞིང་། ཡར་ངོའི་གསོ་སྦྱོང་མཛད།» (bco lnga la bsang mchod snga ltar dang, … rgyal tshab drung pa'i dgongs rdzogs kyi zla ja gcig la bzang spyod 'don zhing, yar ngo'i gso sbyong mdzad): "on the fifteenth, the smoke offering as before, … and, on the one monthly tea (zla ja) for the memorial (dgongs rdzogs) of the Rgyal tshab Drung pa, the Prayer of Good Conduct is recited, and the waxing half's restoring and vow is kept." The word before «གཅིག་ལ» is «ཟླ་ཇ» (zla ja, the monthly tea), and the page break falls between them («ཟླ་ཇ་[165]གཅིག་ལ»); the OCR's «e» and shad there are noise. The same words stand after the 10th's clause, «སྨོན་གྲུབ་པའི་ཟླ་ཇ་གཅིག་ལ» (smon grub pa'i zla ja gcig la), the monthly tea of Smon grub pa, so «ཟླ་ཇ་གཅིག་ལ» is the house's set phrase for a monthly tea-day with its prayer; the tea-day of the Rgyal tshab Drung pa falls on the 15th with the rest of that clause. The scan confirms every day word in order: 3rd, 4th, 8th, 10th, 12th, 14th, 15th, 18th, 21st, 22nd, 24th, 25th, 29th.
