# Does the Blue Beryl's commentary on the Explanatory Tantra (Vaidūrya sngon po, part 2) say anything on the Four Tantras' own two night lines beyond the Moonbeams' gloss read at etext p. 209 (BDRC I1PD411)?

Only the window around that gloss was read (RD299).

## Answered 2026-11-10

Answered by RD313 (2026-11-10): the Blue Beryl speaks on the Four Tantras' night lines only at pp. 216–217 beyond p. 209, which is the Eight Limbs' verse with the Moonbeams' gloss as Zur mkhar ba p. 351 recasts it; p. 216 (dusk and midnight alike, no fruit) is Zur mkhar ba p. 356 word for word with one word different, so the Blue Beryl is a copy, not a second witness.
