# WB vol. 2 p. 237 line 13: «བཅོ» or «བཙོ»?

On p. 237 line 13, is the word «ཆང་བཅོ་བཟང» (brewing beer is good) or «ཆང་བཙོ་བཟང» (boiling beer)? Does the Zhol or Taikhang print read ཅ or ཙ?

## Answered 2026-11-18

Read on the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5029 img. 243, 2026-11-18): the syllable before «བཟང» in line 13 is «བཅོ». At 6x it is the same glyph pair as «བཅོ» of line 1 («བཅོ་ལྔ», fifteen), the «ཅ» with the vowel «ོ» as two bumps above; the «བཙ» of line 26 has a single flag on the «ཙ» and no vowel. The line reads «ཤིང་གཅོད་དཀར་ཆོས་ཆང་བཅོ་བཟང»: brewing beer is good.
