# KP box 16: is «ཟླ་ཀླུང» WB's «ཟླ་[ཆུང]་ཕྱོགས» (one name with ཆུང/ཀླུང/རླུང)?

WB's etext has a damaged «ཟླ་[ཆུང]་ཕྱོགས»; WB's reading should be checked on its scan.


## Read again 2026-11-21 (rd-v)

Checked: WB names «ཟླ་ཀླུངས» (the month's luck lord, no. 5 of the month's eighteen gods) in vol. 2 p. 97 (wb.2:p96-97) and ch. 13's table has «ཟླ་ཀླུང་གི་མིང་»; the verse «སེ་བྱི་ཧང་ཕན་ཟླ་[ཆུང]་ཕྱོགས» runs three earth lords of WB's lists with «ཕྱོགས», and a one-syllable ཀླུང/ཆུང fits the seven-syllable line, so KP's «ཟླ་ཀླུང» is very probably the same lord. Not settled: WB's own syllable was sought on the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5029 img. 401-402, p. 395, work 14) at 1x-1.1x and the line was not found in the crops; the etext's bracket stays unread on a scan.
