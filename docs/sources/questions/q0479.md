# Is the bare «ཁམས» of the circles and of p. 17 the power's element throughout

In ch. 27 the circles count from the enemy, mother, son or friend of «བརྩི་བྱའི་ཁམས», the element of the one reckoned (WB vol. 2, p. 3, l. 12–13 and the circles after it), while of the four parallel reckonings on p. 17 two name «དབང་ཁམས», the power's element (l. 16), and «དབང་མ», the power's mother (l. 21), and two say only «ཁམས» (l. 10, l. 24): is the bare «ཁམས» of the circles and of p. 17 the power's element throughout, or does WB name the power on p. 17 because the bare word means another of the person's elements?
