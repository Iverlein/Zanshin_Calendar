# Is the bare «ཁམས» of the circles and of p. 17 the power's element throughout

In ch. 27 the circles count from the enemy, mother, son or friend of «བརྩི་བྱའི་ཁམས», the element of the one reckoned (WB vol. 2, p. 3, l. 12–13 and the circles after it), while of the four parallel reckonings on p. 17 two name «དབང་ཁམས», the power's element (l. 16), and «དབང་མ», the power's mother (l. 21), and two say only «ཁམས» (l. 10, l. 24): is the bare «ཁམས» of the circles and of p. 17 the power's element throughout, or does WB name the power on p. 17 because the bare word means another of the person's elements? (RD90)

RD179 (2026-11-06), narrowed: Mipham's notes give two rules for the start: the thirty starts (p. 532) count from the sign whose element agrees with the year-horse of the one reckoned; the Balmo circles (p. 533) from the enemy of the power's element. p. 17's four reckonings are drawn from the limbs of the secret reckoning, not the circles, so the bare «ཁམས» on p. 17 is the year's element and «དབང་ཁམས»/«དབང་མ» name the power's. That p. 17 is one of Mipham's thirty is our step (q663). Qualifies claims/ch27-bare-khams-is-the-power-by-mipham: it stands for the circles but should not be extended to p. 17.

INV-g (2026-11-13): q663 is answered no: the notes' thirty are ch. 26's, not p. 17's, so the year-horse rule is not shown for p. 17's bare «ཁམས»; [notes-thirty-are-ch26-not-ch27-p17](../claims/notes-thirty-are-ch26-not-ch27-p17.md). The claim that read p. 17 as one of the thirty is marked corrected.
