# «རུས་ནག», the black bone of the tenth circle

In the tenth circle's fine way (WB vol. 2, p. 10, l. 25), what is «རུས་ནག», "the black bone", on which the pig, sheep and hare are counted, as against the turquoise dragon and the red bird of the other nails?

## Answered 2026-10-28

«རུས་ནག» is the northern guardian of the four-direction set, the black turtle (རུས་སྦལ་ནག་པོ) with its name cut short: the tenth circle's four unchanging nails are east tiger, south turquoise dragon, west red bird and north «རུས་ནག», the monkey at the centre. Ch. 29 pp. 133–135 has the same verse with «བྱང་གི་རུས་སྦལ་ནག་པོ» and p. 139 «བྱང་གི་རུས་ནག» (read on the scan). Why the turtle is called "bone" is not told.
