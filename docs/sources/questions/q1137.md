# Is the first syllable of the title of Bu ston's Examination of Dreams in the 2008 Dpal brtsegs print (img. 833) རྟག or བརྟག on a sharper image

BDRC's scan is 2230 px wide; no visible བ at 4x. Does the dropped prefix stand in the manuscript behind it?

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-vvv)

The scan was fetched again (I2DB62486 img. 833, 2230 × 158 px: the page image is only the title strip) and the title line enlarged 5× in a crop. The letters are about ten pixels high; the syllable after «རྨི་ལམ» is a ར་ཏ with the vowel-less ག and shows no separate བ to its left, but the blur of the tiny glyphs does not exclude a small prefix touching the next letter, so «རྟག» is the more likely reading and still not certain. BDRC serves no larger derivative (the width option returns an empty file). Sharper evidence must come from another copy of the 2008 Dpal brtsegs print or from the text's own lines: «བརྟག» is the word of the other two prints and of BDRC's title.

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (sweep from inv-vvv).** The title line of img. 833 (2230 x 158 px, the only image served) enlarged 5x shows a ར་ཏ with the vowel-less ག and no separate བ, so «རྟག» is the more likely reading and still not certain; a sharper copy of the print or the text's own lines is needed.

## Searched again 2026-11-24 (inv-s)

The other two prints and the body of this one (2026-11-24, inv-s): the Zhol par ma (Delhi) and Zhol par khang prints, whose title lines were checked on their scans (bsr:p617-621, bsr:p599-603), both read «རྨི་ལམ་བརྟག་པ་བཞུགས་སོ»; the 2008 print's own first line of the text (bsr:p825-828) writes «བརྟག་པར་འདོད་པས», with the prefix. BDRC's exact search finds the title string with «བརྟག» in five etexts (the Dpe bsdur ma Tengyur title of another work among them, MW1PD95844_0652, and the Sa skya collection's catalogue) and «རྨི་ལམ་རྟག་པ་བཞུགས་སོ» in none; the hits for «རྨི་ལམ་རྟག་པ» are four unrelated passages with «རྟག་པ» «permanent» or a garbled «brtag» in a ritual list (MW29307, MW1AC375, MW1KG11708), none a title. Sense: «རྨི་ལམ་རྟག་པ» "permanent dream" has no meaning in a text whose first line speaks of examining birth and death, so the title is «རྨི་ལམ་བརྟག་པ» in the two other prints and by the sense; whether the 2008 reproduction's title line really lacks the prefix (a slip of the manuscript behind it, as the doubtful 5x crop suggests) is not shown by any sharper image. Narrowed: the work's title is «བརྟག»; the 2008 title line's own letter stays unread.
