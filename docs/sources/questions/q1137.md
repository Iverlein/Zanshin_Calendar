# Is the first syllable of the title of Bu ston's Examination of Dreams in the 2008 Dpal brtsegs print (img. 833) རྟག or བརྟག on a sharper image

BDRC's scan is 2230 px wide; no visible བ at 4x. Does the dropped prefix stand in the manuscript behind it?

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-vvv)

The scan was fetched again (I2DB62486 img. 833, 2230 × 158 px: the page image is only the title strip) and the title line enlarged 5× in a crop. The letters are about ten pixels high; the syllable after «རྨི་ལམ» is a ར་ཏ with the vowel-less ག and shows no separate བ to its left, but the blur of the tiny glyphs does not exclude a small prefix touching the next letter, so «རྟག» is the more likely reading and still not certain. BDRC serves no larger derivative (the width option returns an empty file). Sharper evidence must come from another copy of the 2008 Dpal brtsegs print or from the text's own lines: «བརྟག» is the word of the other two prints and of BDRC's title.
