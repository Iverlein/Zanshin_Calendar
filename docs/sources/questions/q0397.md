# The son text that lays a trigram pebble in the household round

LT (lt:p20-25) says that with five pebbles a member the household round (རྒྱ་ནག་རྡེལ་སྐོར, rgya nag rdel skor) needs four members, and with six, a trigram pebble added as "the son text" (བུ་ཡིག, bu yig) has it, three. Which text is the son text?

**Narrowed 2026-11-01 (RD104, [claims/son-text-hidden-text.md](../claims/son-text-hidden-text.md)).** The regent names the son text (བུ་ཡིག) as one of a class: "the hidden text (ལྐོག་ཡིག), the son text and the oral transmission" are where some of these instructions are learned (reply 136, img. 493), and "texts such as the hidden text or the accompanying son text" the cut-and-drilled instructions come from (reply 145, img. 501); question 119 calls the place where the born child's luck is shown «མན་ངག་བུ་ཡིག». No single work is named; the pool cannot point to LT's «bu yig» as one book (both readings machine only).
