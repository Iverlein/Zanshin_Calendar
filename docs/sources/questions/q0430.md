# Is «སྐྱས» (work 24) gifts alone, or also the bride's dowry

Is «སྐྱས» (work 24 of ch. 34's list, WB vol. 2, pp. 378–384) gifts alone, or also the bride's dowry, as KP's wording «གཉེན» beside it suggests? No text read says "dowry" (RD64).

## Answered 2026-10-28

Gifts in general, not the bride's dowry. Both clear prints read «སྐྱས» at work 24 (the 1996 etext's «སྐྱེས» is an etext error). In the verses and manuals read (the 1996 etext pp. 300–470, LT, UT, the Bhutan digest) «སྐྱས» is a gift or offering: beside funerals, calling the life, transfer, and the king's and sri rites. No text ties it to the bride. (RD66; etext readings.)
