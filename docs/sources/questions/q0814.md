# What does the word before «a snake, a water creature seized» in SVO p. 270 («བླ་མོ») read, and is «བཅུ་ཡི་བར» in days?

Kept with the earlier doubts of RD207.


Narrowed (RD271, 2026-11-09): «རྟ་བླ་མོ» is the centipede (རྟ་བླ་མོ, rta bla mo, a stock word in tantras in Tibetan: «a body of many limbs, like the rta bla mo»; the dictionaries' རྟ་བླ), so the line names creatures, a centipede and a snake (ལྟོ་འཕྱེ, lto 'phye), and the Peking and Narthang «རྟ་ལ་མོ» is a shortening of the same word; the passage is a machine reading and was not checked on the scan here. Claim svo-rta-bla-mo-is-the-centipede.


Narrowed (inv-k, 2026-11-14): the word is the centipede (see q800). For «བཅུ་ཡི་བར»: in the Svapnohana's own frame the only ten is the dawn dream's ten days (ཞག་བཅུ), and the Agnipurāṇa has the same scheme (the dawn, daśāha, agp:p280-281), but no Sanskrit text found has the white snake line, so the unit of the snake's ten is not shown; BDRC's «ཞག་བཅུ་ཡི་བར» has two hits only (a terma practice and a Gesar episode, both of days, not dreams). See q797.
