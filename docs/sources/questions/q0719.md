# ད་བོར among the nine sme ba (the eight, white)

ད་བོར in "the eight, white" of the nine *sme ba* (children's danger of body): what is it? (RD194, corpus/wb/ch18.md)

## Looked at 2026-11-14 (inv-p)

The 1996 scan (BDRC I1KG12906, img. 222, p. 212) prints «བྱིས་པ་ད་བོར་ལུས་ཉེན་ཆེ» at the foot of the nine *sme ba* list; at 10× the syllable before བོར has a hooked foot under the ད (དུ or དྲ), so the print is not plain ད, and བོར, ལུས་ཉེན་ཆེ are clear. A second WB print is searchable on BDRC (MW2PD17386_D19206, in the collection *Bod lugs gso rig rtsa che'i dpe rnying kun btus*; the etext answers 403, the snippet is a Wylie conversion): «brgyad dkar rlung dmar chen po'ang / than pa dang bza ba mi 'ong / byas pa dwakhor lus rten che», that is a different wording again («dwa khor … lus rten che»); the phrase searches «བྱིས་པ་ད་བོར», «བྱིས་པ་དུར་བོར», «བྱིས་པ་ད་བོར་ལུས», «བྱིས་པ་དོར་ལུས» found nothing. The word is not identified; the clause means that the eighth patch bears on the child's body and its danger, which is all the row uses. Stays open.
