# Which animal is the rnga mo of line 3 (Byang pa and Rin spungs pa རྔ་མོང, Khro ru ཇ་མོ, glossed jo mo, the female dzo)?

Left as read by the machines.

## Answered 2026-11-09

The camel: རྔ་མོ (rnga mo) and རྔ་མོང (rnga mong) are one headword in the Tshig mdzod chen mo, Dag tshig gsar bsgrigs (a large long-necked animal, good for riding and loads), Jim Valby, Ives Waldo, Dan Martin and the Sanskrit lists (uṣṭra); the ridden animals of the line (buffalo, horse, pig, donkey) fit it, and the archaic sense "milk" does not. Khro ru's ཇ་མོ and "female dzo" has no dictionary support (ཇོ་མོ is a lady or nun, the dzo mo is མཛོ་མོ). See gt-rnga-mo-is-the-camel.
