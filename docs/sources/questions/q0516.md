# The la-knower's animals in the second table of p. 133: tiger, pig, monkey, snake

The la-knower's animals in the second table of p. 133 run tiger, pig, monkey, snake by the time's place: why those four, and what does the monkey's time (tiger expected, monkey read) and the dog's (illegible) hold on the scan? (narrows 459)

RD182 (2026-11-06): as q459; not advanced.

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-ggg).** As q459's narrowing: the four la-knowers «བླ་མཁྱེན་སྟག / ཕག / སྤྲེལ / སྦྲུལ» are named in other copies of the table by the time of the day (snippets in Wylie, not scanned); why these four and not others is said in no source seen.

## Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-o)

The table is in more copies than q459 saw, and they agree with WB's four. Phrase searches «བླ་མཁྱེན་ཕག» 3 hits, «བླ་མཁྱེན་སྦྲུལ» 4 hits (WB's Zhol print, the Nag rtsis *Snang srid me long* I1KG15279 and MW1KG13780, the Bhutan collection I1KG11905, MW26041, I4PD3282). The sheet «དུས་ཚོད་ཀྱི་ཤོག་ཁྲ» in I4PD3282 (etext p. 297) gives: «ཉི་ཤར བླ་མཁྱེན་སྟག / ཉི་དྲོས བླ་མཁྱེན་ཕག / ཉིན་ཕྱེད བླ་མཁྱེན་སྤྲེལ / ཕྱེད་ཡོལ བླ་མཁྱེན་སྦྲུལ / ནམ་ལངས … བླ་མཁྱེན་སྦྲུལ», and the second half of the day-night: «ཉི་མྱུར … སྤྲེལ / ཐོ་རངས སྤྲེལ / ཉི་རྒས … ཕག / ཕྱེད་ཡོལ ཕག / ནམ་ཕྱེད སྟག / སྲོད་འཁོར སྦྲུལ / མུན་སྲོད སྤྲེལ»; the Bhutan copy (I1KG11905, p. 452) has the same series (machine text, garbled in places). So in every copy seen the la-knower is one of these four animals only, on all twelve places of the day, and the series from dawn runs tiger, pig, monkey, snake as in WB. Why these four is stated in none of the texts. The monkey's and the dog's places on WB's scan are not touched by this (the dog is the «ས་རྒྱལ» row's animal in the sheet, not the la-knower's). Not answered.
