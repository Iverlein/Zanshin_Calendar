# The spud pa and rgyug of «རྒྱ་ཡི་སྤུད་པ་དགུ»

What are the *spud pa* and *rgyug* (སྤུད་པ, རྒྱུག) of «རྒྱ་ཡི་སྤུད་པ་དགུ» (WB vol. 2, p. 111; the notes, p. 517)?

## Answered 2026-11-05

Spud pa is sbud pa, bellows (the Bhutan digest p. 494, and p. 111's blown mouths). «རྒྱུག» stays open as a question of its own. ([word-hunts.md](../word-hunts.md), RD161)
