# Does «སྙིང་སྡུག» in the Svapnohana mean something dear to the heart or heartache?

RD314; seven BDRC hits, all the Svapnohana itself.

## Answered 2026-11-10

Answered by RD322 (2026-11-10), on lexica: སྙིང་སྡུག is 'a loved one', not heartache: Rangjung Yeshe 'a loved one', Valby 'darling, beloved', Ives–Waldo 'sweetheart', the Tshig gter a person who strongly appeals to the mind; Negi sends it to སྙིང་དུ་སྡུག་པ (priya, hṛdya), the Mahāvyutpatti gives snying du sdug pa = hṛdyam, and the Sgra bye brag tu rtogs byed chen mo 'pleasing to the heart'; no lexicon gives the compound as sorrow (the Tshig mdzod chen mo gives only the separate verb སྡུག་པ as sorrow). q799 (the whole clause) stays open: no commentary on the Svapnohana was found.
