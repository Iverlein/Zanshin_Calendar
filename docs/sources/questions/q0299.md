# The rites gzhob sel, gung phur, sha ri bhyo, sha ru, ma tram, ur mnan

What are the rites *gzhob sel* (p. 105), *gung phur* (pp. 105–106), *sha ri bhyo* (p. 107), *sha ru* (pp. 106, 108), *ma tram* (p. 108) and the *ur mnan*'s seed syllables on the spokes (pp. 112–113), and which of them are Bon rites rather than WB's own?

## Answered 2026-11-14

WB marks which rites are Bon in its own lines, and the others are the speckled (khra bo) rite's own or Nyingma ones.

- **gzhob sel** (p. 105): «བོན་དུ་མདེ་ཐུང་གཞོབ་སེལ་དང་» (as Bon, the short arrow and the gzhob sel): Bon by WB's word; the removing of the singe's taint (gzhob, the smell of burnt hair or bone, Tshig mdzod chen mo, Dag tshig); the same pair in the Bon list of the trigram-remedy verse of other prints (claims/gzhob-sel-gung-phur-bon-rites.md).
- **gung phur** (pp. 105-106): «ཁྲ་བོར་མདོས་ནི་ས་བདག་གི། འཁྲུགས་གཏོ་གུང་ཕུར་བཏང་བའམ།» and «ཁྲ་བོ་གཟའ་ཡི་གུང་ཕུར་གཏོ། བོན་པོས་རྒྱལ་པོ་གྲ་ཤེལ་བྱ།»: it is the speckled rite's own, the sending of the earth lords' disturbance rite (the White Beryl's outline and the Gser thang etext name it so); the Bon priest's part is the next line, the king rite (rgyal po gra shel). Not marked Bon.
- **sha ri bhyo** (p. 107): «ཁྲ་བོ་ཤ་རི་བྷྱོ་མདོས་དང་། བོན་པོས་རྒྱལ་པོ་སྐྱས་འདེབས་བྱ»: the mdos of the speckled rite; Bon is again the next line's king rite. BDRC's exact phrase «ཤ་རི་བྷྱོ་མདོས» gives 12 hits, all titles in the Nyingma and Gelug ritual collections of the NLM scans, among them «slob dpon padma mdzad pa'i sha ri bhyo mdos» (the ransom rite made by the master Padma) in five collections, and one beginning «sha ri bhyo mdos zhes bya ba … gnam khyi nag po'i mdos» (a black sky-dog ransom). So a Buddhist mdos, not Bon.
- **sha ru** (pp. 106, 108): the stag's antler. «བོན་དུ་ཤ་རུ་གཡས་པ་བཏང་» and «ཁྲ་བོ་བོན་གྱི་ཤ་རུ་གཡས» (the right one) are Bon by WB's word; the left one is, by Mipham (p. 517, «ཤ་རུ་གཡོན་ནི་བོན་གྱི་གཏོ་ཞིག་གོ»), a Bon rite as well; «ཤ་རུ་གཡོན་པ་མཆུ་གསུམ་འདོན» is its line in WB.
- **ma tram** (p. 108): «ཁྲ་བོ་བོན་གྱི་ཤ་རུ་གཡས། མདོས་ནི་མ་ཏྲཾ་ཁྲག་མདོས་བྱ།»: the blood mdos of the speckled rite, with the Bon sha ru beside it. «མ་ཏྲཾ་ཁྲག་མདོས» is in BDRC's catalogue as «ma traM khrag mdos bdud kyi zhags gcod» in two Nyingma terma collections (MW21974, MW4CZ1101, the Rtsa gsum yi dam dgongs 'dus and Stag sham nus ldan rdo rje's treasure): a Nyingma rite, not Bon.
- **ur mnan's spoke seeds** (pp. 112-113): hrī-like letters on the Taikhang, at the most one seed «hrī» repeated, as in claims/ur-mnan-spoke-seeds-on-taikhang.md; not read further.

Found 2026-11-14 (inv-lll) by BDRC phrase search «ཤ་རི་བྷྱོ་མདོས» (12 hits), «མ་ཏྲཾ་ཁྲག་མདོས» (2), «གུང་ཕུར་གཏོ» (2: the 1996 and Gser thang prints of WB), «བོན་གྱི་ཤ་རུ» (3: WB's three prints), «ཁྲག་མདོས་བྱ» (12, not opened beyond the first row of titles). What gung phur itself is, beyond its place in the earth-lord rites, no text says.
