# Does the Narthang Tengyur catalogue (Bstan pa chos 'gyur ro cog gi dkar chag tshangs pa'i dbyangs, vol. 225 of the Tibet House manuscript, I2KG212509; the etext is unreadable, the entry probably near etext pp. 168 and 200) list the Svapnohana, and with what words for its maker?

RD359.

## Answered 2026-11-13

Yes (machine reading, 2026-11-13 from the earlier readings, nothing new opened): the Narthang manuscript's catalogue (I2KG212509, vol. 225, img. 77, printed folio 75) lists the Examination of Dreams directly after the Deceiving Death in 360 verses, as «བི་ཌཱུ་ཏི་ཙནྡྲའི་རང་འགྱུར» (Vibhūticandra's own translation) with no maker named (svo:i77); the same words and order as the Derge and Jo nang catalogues and Phur lcog's Tshangs pa'i dbyangs. The entry was read by Yigdzin-1 on the whole image and looked at on the scan in two crops, not syllable by syllable. The question's own guess of etext pp. 168 and 200 was wrong: the entry is in the mother-tantra run.
