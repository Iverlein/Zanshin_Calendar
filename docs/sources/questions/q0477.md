# Is the feat of the body on p. 440 «ཟོར་ཚུགས» or «ཐོར་ཚུགས»

On p. 440 (img. 446) the list of feats of the body has «ཟོར་ཚུགས» (Yigdzin-1) or «ཐོར་ཚུགས» (MITRA) before «གོམ་སྟབས་»; which is the word, and what is the feat? (RD84)

**Answered in part 2026-10-31 (RD84, eye pass, [corpus/wb/ch35.md](../corpus/wb/ch35.md#wb-2-p440)).** The print has «ཐོར་ཚུགས» (MITRA's reading), not «ཟོར»: on the Gser thang scan (img. 446, line 21), enlarged three and four times beside the page's own «ཐོས» and «བཟོ», the letter has the open frame and inner stroke of ཐ. What feat the word names is still open.
