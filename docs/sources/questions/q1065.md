# The dream verse «gang zhig gi ni rmi lam mthar …» is in the Derge Kanjur's Saṃvarodaya, ch. 19 (dds.25:p580-582); is the Sanskrit of the Śrī Mahāsaṃvarodaya (the verses on «Time-Night», dus mtshan, and the six months) the same in the Sanskrit and the other Tibetan translations (Peking, Narthang, the Dpe bsdur ma), and is «dus mtshan» there «kālarātri»?

RD374.

## Answered 2026-11-10

RD382: Tsuda's Sanskrit of the Saṃvarodaya ch. 19 (thesis p. 134, read on the page image; his five Tibetan editions Derge, Narthang, Peking, Cone, Lhasa collated) gives the verses 21-25 with Time-Night as kālarātri and the Peking, Narthang and Derge agree in sense; the other Tibetan translations were seen only through his collation.
