# «དབོ» in p. 305's fifth meeting: the mansion or the vowel O

On p. 305 the fifth meeting reads «རྫོགས་པ་སྤེན་པ་བྲ་ཉེ་དབོ», where the other four end in the vowel (A, I, U, E): does «དབོ» name the mansion Uttaraphalgunī beside Bharaṇī, or is it a contraction of «དང་ཨོ», the vowel O?

RD176 second part (2026-11-06), narrowed, nearly answered (BDRC snippets and etexts, not a scan): five other works state the same five meetings with «སྤེན་པ་བྲ་ཉེ» (Pema Karpo MW10736, Karmapa III MW30541, Tshur phu MW3CN2232, Sherab Rinchen MW2DB4577, Rebkong MW1KG6014); Saturn's mansion is Bharaṇī alone and nothing follows it that could be a second mansion; in the three that give vowels a vowel stands where WB has «དབོ», and it is «ལྀ» (vocalic L), not O; so «དབོ» holds the vowel's slot and is not Uttaraphalgunī. The line was not found on Zhol img. 1054–1055.
