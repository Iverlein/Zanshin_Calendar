# «དབོ» in p. 305's fifth meeting: the mansion or the vowel O

On p. 305 the fifth meeting reads «རྫོགས་པ་སྤེན་པ་བྲ་ཉེ་དབོ», where the other four end in the vowel (A, I, U, E): does «དབོ» name the mansion Uttaraphalgunī beside Bharaṇī, or is it a contraction of «དང་ཨོ», the vowel O?

RD176 second part (2026-11-06), narrowed, nearly answered (BDRC snippets and etexts, not a scan): five other works state the same five meetings with «སྤེན་པ་བྲ་ཉེ» (Pema Karpo MW10736, Karmapa III MW30541, Tshur phu MW3CN2232, Sherab Rinchen MW2DB4577, Rebkong MW1KG6014); Saturn's mansion is Bharaṇī alone and nothing follows it that could be a second mansion; in the three that give vowels a vowel stands where WB has «དབོ», and it is «ལྀ» (vocalic L), not O; so «དབོ» holds the vowel's slot and is not Uttaraphalgunī. The line was not found on Zhol img. 1054–1055.

Viewed 2026-11-07 on the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5029, img. 311 = p. 305), the page cut into strips at 2-3x: the line sits about the 19th text line of the page (first line at y ≈ 230 px, pitch ≈ 90 px, line at y ≈ 1850-1950 px). It runs «…ཨེ་མིང་སྔར་བཞིན་ངན། རྫོགས་པ་སྤེན་པ་བྲ་ཉེ་དབོ། འཛོམ་ཚེ་ཨོ་ཡི་མིང་ཅན་འཛོམས། གཞན་…» across the page's middle; the four syllables «བྲ་ཉེ་དབོ» read as «བྲ་ཉེ་དབོ» by eye, the last one with the ད་ཏ and ོ plain enough to say the reprint has «དབོ», not a form of «དང་ཨོ» or a second mansion name. That confirms only what the etexts say; the question stays open. The Zhol print (I1KG1710, img. 1055) was not cut again in this pass.

ZHOL (2026-11-07): the Zhol print (I1KG1710, img. 1055, 3738x607) was cut by ink projection into six text lines (y about 50-140, 140-215, 212-285, 282-355, 352-425, 422-500) and each line into six slices at 3x. The page carries the same run of vowel verses, each closing «...ཡི་མིང་ཅན» (seen at the ends of verses on lines 2, 3 and 5, where the vowel before «ཡི» looks like ཨི and ཨཱ), so this is the right page; but the verse with «རྫོགས་པ་སྤེན་པ་བྲ་ཉེ» and the landmark «འཛོམ་ཚེ་ཨོ་ཡི་མིང་ཅན» was not made out on the slices viewed (lines 2-4 at slices 3-5 and a few others, not every slice), and the syllables «དབོ» were not seen. The Zhol therefore says nothing yet on «དབོ»; the question stays open.
