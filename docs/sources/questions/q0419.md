# What is «འཇུག་ཕོད» in Sādhuputra's ground rite?

what is «འཇུག་ཕོད» in Sādhuputra's ground rite, a time to be free of with the fierce planets (etext p. 238)? Viṣṭi, or another?

**Narrowed 2026-11-03 (RD146, [weighing.md](../weighing.md), *Four open words of the works and rites*).** The spelling «འཇུག་ཕོད» is the etext's and the other Tengyur copy's (Yigdzin-1's «ཡོད» on the 2004-px one-bit scan is a misreading). The list «…ཁྱིམ་དང་། དུས་འབྱོར་དང་། འཇུག་ཕོད་ལ་སོགས་པ» is the *Sarvadurgatipariśodhana*'s list of time-gods; its commentaries (Kun dga' snying po, Sangs rgyas gsang ba, VE23703_1383 p. 263, VE23703_1382 pp. 404, 651) gloss it as the gods that give good, bad or illness by one's birth time, as the god into which the maṇḍala is entered, and as «བཟང་པོའམ་སྒྲ་གཅན་ཉིད» (*bzang po*, or Rāhu). None says *viṣṭi*: the identification stays the dictionary's (Lokesh Chandra, RD114).

## Answered 2026-11-14

Viṣṭi, the karaṇa, as claim sdp-jug-phod-is-vishti (and q607) say; one more witness is now added, and it is in Sanskrit. Negi's Tibetan-Sanskrit dictionary (Steinert's set, no. 50) has the entry «mjug phod» (the same word spelled with an ma prefix): «pā. viṣṭiḥ, karaṇaviśeṣaḥ», with the Sanskrit of the Sarvadurgatipariśodhana list from which Sādhuputra's wording is taken: «tshes dang sbyor ba dang khyim dang dus sbyor dang mjug phod la sogs pa'i dkyil 'khor gyi lha rnams» = «tithiyogarāśilagnaviṣṭimaṇḍalādidevatāḥ» (sa.du. 205/204; Mahāvyutpatti 4397). So in the list the Tibetan commentaries read as gods of times the Sanskrit original has the date, yoga, sign, rising sign and Viṣṭi. The same dictionary has, under «biSh+Ti», a ground rite from the Vimalaprabhā (3.29): the ground is cleansed on the fifth, tenth or fifteenth of the bright half, "avoiding viṣṭi" («bco lnga pa biSh+Ti spangs nas sa sbyang bar bya», «viṣṭiṃ varjayitvā bhūmiṃ śodhayet»): the same demand as Sādhuputra's «time free of … 'jug phod». The reading of the SDP line stands: a time free of the fierce planets and of Viṣṭi and the like. Not seen on a scan (dictionary quotations); the glyph «ཕ» of the SDP scan stays as RD146 left it.
