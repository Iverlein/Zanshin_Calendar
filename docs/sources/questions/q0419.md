# What is «འཇུག་ཕོད» in Sādhuputra's ground rite?

what is «འཇུག་ཕོད» in Sādhuputra's ground rite, a time to be free of with the fierce planets (etext p. 238)? Viṣṭi, or another?

**Narrowed 2026-11-03 (RD146, [weighing.md](../weighing.md), *Four open words of the works and rites*).** The spelling «འཇུག་ཕོད» is the etext's and the other Tengyur copy's (Yigdzin-1's «ཡོད» on the 2004-px one-bit scan is a misreading). The list «…ཁྱིམ་དང་། དུས་འབྱོར་དང་། འཇུག་ཕོད་ལ་སོགས་པ» is the *Sarvadurgatipariśodhana*'s list of time-gods; its commentaries (Kun dga' snying po, Sangs rgyas gsang ba, VE23703_1383 p. 263, VE23703_1382 pp. 404, 651) gloss it as the gods that give good, bad or illness by one's birth time, as the god into which the maṇḍala is entered, and as «བཟང་པོའམ་སྒྲ་གཅན་ཉིད» (*bzang po*, or Rāhu). None says *viṣṭi*: the identification stays the dictionary's (Lokesh Chandra, RD114).
