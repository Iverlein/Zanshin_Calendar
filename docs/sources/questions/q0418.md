# Is the serpent's place for the first stroke reckoned by the day's mansion or by the season?

*A General Account of Tibet* (BSB) says the serpent's body stretches, curls and turns "with the course of the planets and mansions" (pp. 435–436): is the place of the first stroke reckoned by the day's mansion, as Chos kyi grags pa's serpent walks one cell a day, or only by the season, as Longchenpa and Sādhuputra give it?
