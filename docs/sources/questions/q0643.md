# The ko long of pp. 360 and 370

What is the ko long (ཀོ་ལོང, ko long) with which Pi ling 'phar ma binds all good and bad on p. 360, and Wang-ging and the Hal-dog on p. 370?

## Answered 2026-11-06

RD183 (2026-11-06): the pool's earlier work plus the verb and objects read on p. 360 (Gser thang reprint img. 366): «བཟང་ངན་ཀུན་ལ་ཀོ་ལོང་སྡོམ» and «གནམ་ཁྱི་བསྟན་མ་ཐེ་ཁྱིམ་དང་། སྐར་ངན་རྣམས་ལ་བག་མ་སྤང»; all three names are earth lords.
