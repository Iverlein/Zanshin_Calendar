# Does «དེ་ཡིས་དེ་དུས་སྙིང་སྡུག་འཐོབ» in the Svapnohana mean getting what is dear to the heart, or sorrow?

After the female vulture, house-bird and krauñca are obtained and the dreamer wakes (ཆུང་མ་ཉེ་དུ་སྨྲ་བ་ཅན…).


Narrowed (inv-k, 2026-11-14): the word is settled by q940 (a loved one). The verse's other verdicts all say what is obtained (property, glory, kingship, a son); the Agnipurāṇa's dream chapter (agp:p282) has no vulture, house-bird or krauñca, so no parallel decides the clause, and no commentary was found. The reading «gets what is dear to the heart» stands on the lexica and the verse run, sorrow on no text; still open for a commentary.
