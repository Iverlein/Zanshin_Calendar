# Does «དེ་ཡིས་དེ་དུས་སྙིང་སྡུག་འཐོབ» in the Svapnohana mean getting what is dear to the heart, or sorrow?

After the female vulture, house-bird and krauñca are obtained and the dreamer wakes (ཆུང་མ་ཉེ་དུ་སྨྲ་བ་ཅན…).
