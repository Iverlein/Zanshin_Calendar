# Aśvinī's numeral «༤» on p. 328

Aśvinī's numeral on p. 328 is printed «༤» in a series that counts from 0: is it a misprint for «༠» in the Gser thang print alone, or also in the 1996 edition?

RD176 (2026-11-06), narrowed: on the Zhol (img. 1084, line 4) a small open ring stands above ཐ of ཐ་སྐར, so the Zhol has the zero and «༤» is the reprint's misprint; the 1996 scan (687 px wide) was not legible.

## Answered 2026-11-08

RD176 (2026-11-08): the Zhol (img. 1084, line 4) and the Taikhang (I5878 img. 466) both carry an open ring over «ཐ» of «ཐ་སྐར», the zero; the 1996 print has a solid hooked sign there, shaped like its own «༤» for Mṛgaśiras on img. 338, and its etext reads «༤». The 1996 print itself prints the ring for Aśvinī on p. 330. So «༤» is a slip shared by the 1996 print and the Gser thang reprint; the number is 0. Claim: wb-p328-aswini-numeral-zero.
