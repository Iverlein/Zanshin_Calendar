# The Breakthrough's companion method on the death signs (the midnight dream, the donkey going south, the red flower on the Gya skag mountain): does any Kagyu or Gdams ngag text outside the four sheets' prints give it, with an author, under another title?

RD383.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-ds)

No other text found. BDRC exact phrases «རྒྱ་སྐག་གི་རི་ལ་མེ་ཏོག་དམར་པོ» (6 hits), «གཅེར་བུར་བོང་བུ་ལ་ཕྱི་ཕྱོགས་སུ་ཞོན» (5), «སྔར་འགྲོ་མ་མྱོང་བའི་ཡུལ་ཕྱོགས་གཅིག་པུར་འགྲོ་བ» (3), «ནམ་ཕྱེད་ཀྱི་རྨི་ལམ་བརྟག་པ» (6) all fall on the same six copies of the four sheets' set: the Shechen and Dpal spungs Gdams ngag mdzod, the Bka' brgyud sngags mdzod, Marpa's collected works (MW1KG12222), the 'Bri lugs bang mdzod skor lnga and the Nechu Lhakhang copy; the 'Brug pa'i chos mdzod (MW3CN2232_B09931) lists the same text under «bde mchog snyan rgyud las / spyod pa bzhi nyams su blang ba'i rim pa …», a collection title, not a different author or title. No Kagyu or Gdams ngag text outside these has the three images.
