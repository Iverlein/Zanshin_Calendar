# Must Wangkhyi's animal day and animal hour fall together

On p. 210, when Wangkhyi's «གླང་གི་ཉི་མ་དུས་ཚོད» is the ox's day and hour, must the ox day and the ox hour fall together (as the notes' «ལུག་གི་ཉི་མ་དང་དུས་ཚོད»), or is the ox day at any hour, or the ox hour on any day, enough? Narrows q442 and q29 (RD72).
