# Ch. 32 p. 241 l. 1: «སྤོར་ཐང» or «སྤྱོར་ཐང»

On WB vol. 2 p. 241, l. 1 (Gser thang reprint I3CN5029 img. 247, typeset, 3x), the first words read «སྤོར་ཐང་རྩིས་ཀུན་འཛིན་པའི་མཁྱེན་པའི་ཆ» in the 1996 etext. On the scan the first stack seems to carry a subjoined ya (སྤྱོར) and the third syllable's stack is not clear at 3x. Is it «སྤོར་ཐང» or «སྤྱོར་ཐང», and «རྩིས» as the etext has it? The heading (l. 1-2 above the verse) was read on the scan and agrees with the etext.

## Answered 2026-11-05

Answered (RD156, [ch32](../corpus/wb/ch32.md), wb.2:p241): «སྤྱོར» with a subjoined ya on both prints, «རྩིས» confirmed; the bracket in the passage is kept.
