# The bstan ma and the the khyim beside the Sky Dog on p. 360

On p. 360, who are the bstan ma (བསྟན་མ, bstan ma) and the the khyim (ཐེ་ཁྱིམ, the khyim) named beside the Sky Dog and the bad stars as shunning a bride?

## Answered 2026-11-06

RD183 (2026-11-06), on the scan (p. 360 and the pool): Bstan ma is the goddess Tenma, first of the nine earth lords under the ground; Khyim is the Thé-se's chief wife, the dog-day lord; the Sky Dog is the hidden dog lord.
