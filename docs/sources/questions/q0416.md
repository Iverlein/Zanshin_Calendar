# DZ «སྣ་ཚོགས་བྱ» or KD's «སྣ་ཆོག་བྱ», the nose rite: which is meant?

DZ img. 045 has «བདུན་ཟུར་དགྲ་ལོས་སྣ་ཚོགས་བྱ», KD's print «…སྣ་ཆོག་བྱ», the nose rite: which is meant?

## Answered 2026-11-03

«སྣ་ཆོག་བྱ», the nose rite (སྣ་ཆོག, sna chog), is meant. DZ's line is WB's vol. 1, p. 377 line cut short («ཀླུང་རྟ་དགྲ་ཡིས་སྣ་ཆོག་བྱ», read by eye on the Zhol print, img. 493), and WB's other places, Mipham's note and the other prints of the *Moonbeams*' line have ཆོག; the scan of DZ img. 045 has ཚོགས across the line break (by eye), a variant of the same line, not a second rite. See weighing.md, *Four open words of the works and rites (RD146)*.
