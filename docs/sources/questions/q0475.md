# Is «གོས་ལེགས་ཚོང་ལེགས» on p. 24 a slip for «གྲོས་ལེགས»

In the minister's set of the reckonings in four (WB vol. 2, p. 24, l. 16), is «གོས་ལེགས་ཚོང་ལེགས» (good clothes, good trade), printed without a ra on both scans, a slip for «གྲོས་ལེགས» (good counsel), as the same verse's «གྲོས་ཀྱི་མཚོ» and the black verse's «གྲོས་ཉེས» suggest? (RD89)

RD178 (2026-11-07): Narrowed: Yigdzin-1 on the Zhol print reads «གྲོས་ལེགས་ཚོང་ལེགས», and by eye at 5× the Zhol and Taikhang syllable has the foot of the ra like the neighbouring «གྲོས»; the slip is the 1996 setting's. Not proved letter by letter ([corpus/wb/ch27.md](../corpus/wb/ch27.md)).

**Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-q).** BDRC exact phrase «གྲོས་ལེགས་ཚོང་ལེགས» 1 hit, in the Zhol-block *Do shal* commentary (MW1KG1617_A9DE66, I1KG1710, offset 1038744; etext of poor quality, snippet): «དབང་དཀར་གྲོས་ཀྱི་མཚོ་སྐམས། །གྲོས་ལེགས་ཚོང་ལེགས་ཟས་ནོར་འདུ།», the commentary's copy of WB's white-verse line, with the ra. «གོས་ལེགས་ཚོང་ལེགས» 2 hits, WB's own etexts (1996 print I1KG12907, offset 52216; Gser thang I3CN5029, offset 54433), both without it (the black verse beside reads «ནག་ན་གྲོས་ཉེས་ཚོང་ཉེས་འབྱུང»). So the commentary on the Zhol block, which keeps the lines of its own base print, has «གྲོས», the two WB etexts «གོས»; the first witness to the ra besides the scan by eye. Etext snippet only, so not built on; the question stays narrowed.
