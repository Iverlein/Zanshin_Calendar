# Is «གོས་ལེགས་ཚོང་ལེགས» on p. 24 a slip for «གྲོས་ལེགས»

In the minister's set of the reckonings in four (WB vol. 2, p. 24, l. 16), is «གོས་ལེགས་ཚོང་ལེགས» (good clothes, good trade), printed without a ra on both scans, a slip for «གྲོས་ལེགས» (good counsel), as the same verse's «གྲོས་ཀྱི་མཚོ» and the black verse's «གྲོས་ཉེས» suggest? (RD89)

RD178 (2026-11-07): Narrowed: Yigdzin-1 on the Zhol print reads «གྲོས་ལེགས་ཚོང་ལེགས», and by eye at 5× the Zhol and Taikhang syllable has the foot of the ra like the neighbouring «གྲོས»; the slip is the 1996 setting's. Not proved letter by letter ([corpus/wb/ch27.md](../corpus/wb/ch27.md)).
