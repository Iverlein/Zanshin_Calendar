# KP's year ring puts four lords in other places than WB's rules for the mouse year: another reckoning, or a slip?

In the Rat panel of KP's year ring (§13, kp:i87b) four lords stand in cells other than the ones WB 2, ch. 31 gives for the year of the mouse (claim kp-year-ring-rat-panel-against-wb): Se-lo Sa-'phyag (སེ་ལོ་ས་འཕྱག, se lo sa 'phyag) in the Hare, WB's upward four-slayer, where WB 15 says the downward one (the Bird); Tsang-kun the dog-headed (ཙང་ཀུན་ཁྱི་མགོ, tsang kun khyi mgo) in the Snake with Hal-khyi's girl, where WB 22 says the upward four-slayer (the Hare); the life-spirit knower Phe'u (བླ་མཁྱེན་ཕེའུ, bla mkhyen phe'u) in the Dog, where WB 23 says the third upward in the middle years (the Tiger); Kungs-myul (ཀུངས་མྱུལ, kungs myul) in the Dog, where WB 14 says the king's upward and downward slayer years. Is the chart drawn on another reckoning of these four (the old Chinese against the new, as for the sky dog's body), is it a cut cell or a copyist's error, or are the four names misread? Wanted: a Tibetan astrologer who knows both the chart and ch. 31.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (rd-kp3)

The differences are the chart's rule and not one cell's slip. Se-lo Sa-'phyag stands on the upward four-slayer in four panels (the Rat year's Hare, the Tiger year's Snake, the Hare year's Horse, the Snake year's Monkey). Phe'u stands in the opposite direction from WB's in both groups (the middle years two places back, the corner years two places on) and on the seventh in the last years, as WB says. Kungs-myul has no fixed offset in six panels (the Dog, Horse, Ox, Dragon, Ox, Sheep for the Rat, Ox, Tiger, Hare, Dragon, Snake years), and Zin-phung is not at WB's sixth downward in the three panels where it was read (claim [kp-year-ring-offsets-against-wb](../claims/kp-year-ring-offsets-against-wb.md)). The question is now: does KP's chart follow another reckoning of «upward» and «downward» for these lords, and what is Kungs-myul's rule?

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (rd-kp4)

Read again with the Horse to Pig panels ([kp:i89b](../corpus/kp/kp.md#kp-i89b), [kp:i90b](../corpus/kp/kp.md#kp-i90b)), the four lords of the Rat panel are two exchanged and two loose. Se-lo stands three places on from the year (WB: three back), and Tsang-kun the dog-headed three places back (WB: three on), with the minister's group: the Rat panel's cell T2 shows Zin-phung, not the dog-headed, beside Hal-khyi's girl on the scan, so the dog-headed stands in R2 with the minister, three back from the Rat. Phe'u runs the other way from WB in the middle and corner years in all six new panels; Kungs-myul again has no fixed place. Zin-phung stands five places on from the year in the Rat panel and in five of the new ones (WB: five back). What remains to ask of a reader of the tradition: does KP's chart take «upward» and «downward» the other way round for Se-lo, the dog-headed, Phe'u and Zin-phung, and what is Kungs-myul's rule (claim [kp-year-ring-horse-to-pig-against-wb](../claims/kp-year-ring-horse-to-pig-against-wb.md)).
