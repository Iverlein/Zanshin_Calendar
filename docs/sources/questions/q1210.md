# KP's year ring puts four lords in other places than WB's rules for the mouse year: another reckoning, or a slip?

In the Rat panel of KP's year ring (§13, kp:i87b) four lords stand in cells other than the ones WB 2, ch. 31 gives for the year of the mouse (claim kp-year-ring-rat-panel-against-wb): Se-lo Sa-'phyag (སེ་ལོ་ས་འཕྱག, se lo sa 'phyag) in the Hare, WB's upward four-slayer, where WB 15 says the downward one (the Bird); Tsang-kun the dog-headed (ཙང་ཀུན་ཁྱི་མགོ, tsang kun khyi mgo) in the Snake with Hal-khyi's girl, where WB 22 says the upward four-slayer (the Hare); the life-spirit knower Phe'u (བླ་མཁྱེན་ཕེའུ, bla mkhyen phe'u) in the Dog, where WB 23 says the third upward in the middle years (the Tiger); Kungs-myul (ཀུངས་མྱུལ, kungs myul) in the Dog, where WB 14 says the king's upward and downward slayer years. Is the chart drawn on another reckoning of these four (the old Chinese against the new, as for the sky dog's body), is it a cut cell or a copyist's error, or are the four names misread? Wanted: a Tibetan astrologer who knows both the chart and ch. 31.
