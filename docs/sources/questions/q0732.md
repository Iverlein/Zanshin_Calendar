# The print's figure slips (2587, 1048, 278) in other editions of the Pad dkar zhal lung

the same slips (2587, 1048, 278 where the chain gives 2567, 1058, 178) are in the print's own figures; is the figure in the Pad dkar zhal lung's other editions the chain's, and which edition is the Gser thang reprint's source? (RD197, corpus/wb/ch03.md)

## Searched 2026-11-17 (inv-b)

Not answered; the field is smaller. The 1996 etext prints the two slips of the brackets: «ལོ(༡༠༤༨)» at the king's coming to the throne (p. 33 of the etext's markers, [p33]) and «བཏབ(༢༧༨)» for the founding of the Dga' ldan seat ([p35]); the Gser thang etext has «༢༧༨» too ([p32]); both OCRs of two prints agree, so the two prints share the figures, and the reprint is no independent witness (the Gser thang record, MW3CN5026, a typeset copy of «2 vol. (472+545 p.)» from the Gser thang Bla rung lnga rig nang bstan slob gling, names no source). The third text with the verse, the *Do shal* (MW2PD17386_D19206, search-only), shows in its snippet the lines «gangs can mgon gcig srong btsan rje / mi yi srid du pabs pa'i lo / de la nyi mas phra ba yi» without the bracketed figures, which the snippet's Wylie conversion drops; so it cannot be compared. Neither «༢༥༨༧» nor «༢༥༦༧» occurs in either etext (the OCR drops the figure of the first clause), so the slip of 2587 is not checked here. BDRC has no other print of the *Pad dkar zhal lung* whose etext is open (phrase search «མི་ཡི་སྲིད་དུ་ཕེབས་པའི་ལོ» 1 hit, «གངས་ཅན་མགོན་གཅིག་སྲོང་བཙན་རྗེ» 3, the two prints and the Do shal).
