# WB ch. 19 p. 214: is the number-word «རྩ» in «མིག་བུག་མདའ་རྩ་མེ» 9?

It must be 9 to give (༣༩༥༩༢).

## Answered 2026-11-21

Yes: in «མིག་བུག་མདའ་རྩ་མེ» the word «རྩ» is 9, and the figure is 39592.

Checked 2026-11-21 (rd-t) by arithmetic, not by a dictionary. Written from the lowest place the words are eye 2, hole 9, arrow 5, rtsa x, fire 3, so the figure is 3x592. 39592 is the divisor of ch. 4 (wb.1:p29–30, claim wb-ch4-mean-month-and-sun-divisors): 7 · 8657 / 39592 comes out as 1 day 31 dbyu gu 50 srang 0 breaths 480/707 with no remainder, which is the five places of the pure month's constant on p. 30. No other digit in the place of x leaves the remainder zero, so rtsa counts 9 here; with the print's «(༣༩༥༩༢)» read at 4× on p. 214 the three agree.
