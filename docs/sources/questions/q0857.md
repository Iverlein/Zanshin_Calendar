# What is the 'bro pa's tax in the Drathang line «བྲོ་པའི་ཁྲལ་འདེད་བག་མ་ལན་པ»?

At 10× not settled between ལན་པ and ལེན་པ ('taking a bride'), བྲོ་པའི and བྲོ་བའི, འདེད and འདོད.

## Answered 2026-11-09

The line is the Four Tantras' «བྲོ་བའི་ཁྲལ་འདེད་བག་མ་ལེན་པ» in nearly every witness (23 hits for «བྲོ་བའི་ཁྲལ»): the Drathang's བྲོ་པའི and ལན་པ are minority spellings (ལན་པ is attested for "taking a bride" in a Kālacakra commentary and an Abhidharma commentary). The words: a levy (ཁྲལ, khral) of the "bro" being demanded (འདེད, 'ded: collected), glossed in a medical dictionary as meat levy and the like, a fine, a tax imposed; བྲོ in the Tshig mdzod chen mo is, besides dance and oath, "provisions for a journey"; and taking a bride. Not a dancer's tax. Which "bro" stays open. See gt-bro-ba-khral-ded-a-levy and gt-bag-ma-lan-pa-is-bag-ma-len-pa.
