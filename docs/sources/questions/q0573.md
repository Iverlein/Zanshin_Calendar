# The Moonbeams' son line: a half, or the etext's «བྱེད་མ»

In the Moonbeams (KD vol. 3, etext p. 527) is the son's line «བུ་འཕྲད་ཕྱེད་མ་ནག་གཉིས་དགྲ» (a half, the enemy two black), as O18's scan reading of DZ f. 21b would have it, and not «བུ་འཕྲད་པ་བྱེད་མ…» as the etext prints, and does the leaf of KD itself (vol. 3 p. 507) show the half? See verdicts.md, RD123.

## Answered 2026-11-06

RD166 (2026-11-06): the scan (DZ img. 046, f. 21b l. 5) has «ཕྱེད་མ», the half; KD's own leaf (vol. 3, p. 507) is lent and was not seen.
