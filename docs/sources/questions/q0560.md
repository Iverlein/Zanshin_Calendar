# The day's lo for the rejected lords Pi ling, 'Khor chung and Byi lam

Does a reckoner's text say what "the day's *lo*" (ཞག་གི་ལོ) is for the lords Pi ling, 'Khor chung and Byi lam, whom p. 225 rejects?

RD169 (2026-11-07), final: dead end. Searches of «ཞག་གི་ལོ་སྟེང་གནས» give the Gser thang reprint and the Zhol print only; no text says what the day's lo is for the three lords.

**Searched again 2026-11-14 (inv-jjj).** BDRC's phrase search «པི་ལིང་འཁོར་ཆུང་» (2 hits): the Gser thang reprint of the White Beryl (MW3CN5026, WB's own line) and one new witness, the catalogue record of *Tibetan elemental divination paintings* (MW1KG6061, Eskenazi, 2001, search only), whose snippet says the set "comprises gTso-bo gser-mdzod ser-po, Zhag-gi bla-mkhyen, Thab-lha se-shar and Pi-ling 'khor-chung": the name Pi ling 'khor chung is that of one of the divination paintings (charts) of the White Beryl's own tradition, named beside a "Zhag gi bla mkhyen". So the rejected lords are, at least by that title, named charts of the paintings and not only earth lords; what the day's *lo* is for them is still not told (the book's text is not searchable here). «ཞག་གི་ལོ་» alone gives 50 hits, none read as a gloss (the snippets are the usual «ཞག་གི་ལོ་འབྱུང» of the golden reckoning). Still open.
