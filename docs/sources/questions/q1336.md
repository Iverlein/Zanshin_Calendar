# Who revealed the pure-vision life of the Guru in the anthology Bka' thang phyogs bsgrigs (BKT), and which Padma bka' thang redaction is it based on

From the 2026-11-21 practice-day readings.

Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-r): the pool holds no text on the anthology Bka' thang phyogs bsgrigs (བཀའ་ཐང་ཕྱོགས་བསྒྲིགས, bka' thang phyogs bsgrigs) or its pure-vision life; kb.py search finds only this question. Not searched further (no etext of the anthology's colophon or the BDRC record was opened in the time given). Still open.

Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-a): the anthology is «Bka' thang phyogs bsgrigs snyigs ma'i rgud 'joms rdo rje'i me char», compiled by Blo gros rgya mtsho, Bod ljongs mi dmangs dpe skrun khang, Lhasa 2012 (BDRC MW1AC2; the pool's sigla bkt, etext VEIE0OPIAF862726_I2PD18003). It holds several pure-vision (dag snang) lives; the one bkt:p272 reads from is «Dag snang rin chen gter mdzod las: O rgyan rnam thar mu tig phreng rgyan», printed p. 210-236 (etext pp. 250-276). Revealer: BDRC's separate record of the same title (MW4CZ355881, «dag snang rin chen gter mdzod las: o rgyan rnam thar mu tig phreng rgyan», 48 ff.) says "a short biography of Padmasambhava rediscovered by Khedrup Tenzin" (Mkhas grub Bstan 'dzin), and the colophon on etext p276 (OCR badly broken) names «མཁས་གྲུབ་བསྟན་འཛིན» in a passage about a dream of Orgyan Rinpoche, a treasure-revealer's name and a «Pad+ma lhan grub» of the east, the land of the Iron Female (lcags mo); the OCR does not let the speaker of the colophon be told from the person he names, and the anthology's contents page has the name lost in OCR. Redaction: the text calls itself a «short life» (rnam thar mdor bsdus) in 17-18 chapters, whose chapters (ch. 14, 16, 18) the anthology's etext quotes; it names no Padma bka' thang redaction, and no comparison with the Sde dge, Gser gling or Rdzong gter redactions was made. So the revealer is narrowed to Mkhas grub Bstan 'dzin on the catalogue's word, unconfirmed on the page, and the redaction is not answered. Still open.

**Narrowed 2026-11-24 (sweep from the notes of inv-a above (BDRC MW4CZ355881; etext colophon)).** The question's first half stands settled by the pool's own lines; the second half is not: see the notes above (the Rin chen gter mdzod preface says Mgon po rin chen's text of the twelve is not extant beyond a tradition of the Benefits of the Tenth Day).
