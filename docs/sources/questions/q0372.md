# Whose mewa and trigram among the burial reckoning's eleven roots

In the eleven root pebbles of the *Moonbeams*' burial reckoning (dz:i057-061), «གནམ་ལོ་ལོག་མེན་སྨེ་བ་དང་། །སྤར་ཁ་…»: are the mewa and the trigram those of the year of death as the almanac gives them, or the dead's descended ones in that year?

RD166 (2026-11-06), narrowed: repeats q283. The eleven roots write «སྨེ་བ» and «སྤར་ཁ» bare, while the living's pebbles say «བབས་སྨེ» and «སྐྱེས་སྨེ».
