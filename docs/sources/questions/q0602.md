# A print of WB p. 453 with the Moonbeams' order of the thirteenth and fourteenth pairs

Is there a print or manuscript of the White Beryl whose p. 453 lists the Tibetan thirteenth and fourteenth pairs in the order the Moonbeams give them (the enemy's bird and monkey before the friend's horse and sheep)? The Zhol blocks (I1KG1710 img. 602) were not read.

## Answered 2026-11-06

RD155 (2026-11-06): the Zhol blocks (I1KG1710 img. 602) and the Taikhang (I5877 img. 619) both list the Tibetan thirteenth as «བཅུ་གསུམ་གྲོགས་ཀྱི་རྟ་ལུག་གཉིས» (friend, horse and sheep) before the fourteenth «བཅུ་བཞི་དགྲ་ཡི་བྱ་སྤྲེ་གཉིས» (enemy, bird and monkey), the 1996 print's order; the Moonbeams' order is in none of the three prints. The Chinese section's pairs agree on both blocks, on machine readings only.
