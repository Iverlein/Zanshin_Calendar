# In Marpa's inner-heat manual, is the spirit of the horse, dog or woman dream a certain class, and is the cure tied to a watch

The dream of a horse, a dog or a woman that loses the drop is a spirit's work; the cure is done three times by day and three by night. Does the manual tie the spirit to a class or to a watch of the night?

## Answered 2026-11-13

Neither. The manual (dds.19:p187-189) calls the dream of a horse, a dog, an ugly old woman, or a woman with desire acted out, after which the drop is lost, the work of a spirit (གདོན, gdon), and names no class of spirit and no watch of the night: the «three times by day and three by night» are the repetitions of the cure (compassion and the gift of the body against hatred, the unlovely skeleton against desire, emptiness against dullness), not an hour of the dream. See claims/dds-marpa-inner-heat-manual-spirit-dreams-no-hour.md (RD387). The text was re-read for this question on the etext of 2026-11-11 as quoted in the passage; etext only.
