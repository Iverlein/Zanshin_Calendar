# Does a middling life reckoning bar the match

Reply 111 (ys:i467-468) bars a couple whose two life reckonings are "utterly bad", each gathered into one verdict pebble, read as WB's verdict on the whole birth (vol. 1, p. 349: the bad results greater and more, bad; even, middling; [life-reckoning-one-verdict](../claims/life-reckoning-one-verdict.md)). Does the bar fall on a couple whose life reckonings are judged middling, good and bad results even, or only when both verdicts are bad?
