# Does a middling life reckoning bar the match

Reply 111 (ys:i467-468) bars a couple whose two life reckonings are "utterly bad", each gathered into one verdict pebble, read as WB's verdict on the whole birth (vol. 1, p. 349: the bad results greater and more, bad; even, middling; [life-reckoning-one-verdict](../claims/life-reckoning-one-verdict.md)). Does the bar fall on a couple whose life reckonings are judged middling, good and bad results even, or only when both verdicts are bad?

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-fin)

Read again against the passages: reply 111 (ys:i467-468) names only «ངན་ཐ་ཆད་གཉིས་མི་སྡེབ་པ», not joining two utterly bad ones, and says whether a life reckoning is «ངན་ཐ་ཆད» is told by gathering its white and black pebbles into the one verdict pebble; it has no word on a middling verdict. WB's closing list of those bad "even if they agree" (vol. 1, p. 364) and Mipham's gloss of it (vol. 2, p. 526: life, body, power, luck, "the reckoning bad in general", ལས་འབྲས་ཏེ་རྩིས་སྤྱིར་ངན་པ) name only bad findings of the karma reckoning, never a middling one; WB's three grades of a verdict (p. 349: bad, even/middling, good) and the notes' nine of the match (p. 526: good, bad, middling each in three) set the middling apart from the bad. So by every wording in hand a middling (even) life reckoning is not in the bar. A new BDRC exact-phrase search («ངན་ཐ་ཆད་གཉིས», «ཚེ་རྩིས་ངན་པ་གཉིས») gave no hit, so no commentary states the point. Unanswered as a stated rule; the reading by the letter stands ([life-reckoning-middling-does-not-bar](../claims/life-reckoning-middling-does-not-bar.md)).
