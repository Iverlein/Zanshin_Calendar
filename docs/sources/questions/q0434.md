# Does Langging's list put the dragon year on the dog or the mouse

On p. 186 does Langging's list put the dragon year on the dog («ཁྱི») or on the mouse («བྱི»)? The stack is blotted on the scan; Yigdzin-1 reads «ཁྱི», the pool «བྱི» (RD63).

## Answered 2026-10-28

The dog, «ཁྱི». On the Gser thang reprint (img. 192, 10x) and the Taikhang print the stack has the long left stem of ka and the subjoined ya, unlike the round-headed «བྱི» of the hare's pair above; the 1996 etext, the Gser thang etext and a digest read «ཁྱི», and a search for the line with «བྱི» finds nothing. The Zhol crop is too small to tell. See claims/wb-langging-table-dragon-on-the-dog.md (RD69).
