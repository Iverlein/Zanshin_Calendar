# The figures 298 and 288 of p. 27 (Chos 'khor rgyal)

p. 27, the line of the Chos 'khor rgyal monastery reads «༢༩༨» and the next «༡༥༠» with a reduction «༢༨༨»; the chain from 207 gives 185 (Tshar chen) and then 178 and 150. Are the figures 298, 288 misreadings, and what are the true ones? (RD187, corpus/wb/ch03.md)

## Answered 2026-11-07

Read on the reprint (img. 34, p. 27, line 4, the reduction beside it in line 4 as well, and 185 in line 2) at 3×, RD197: the first figure prints «༢༧༨» (a ༢, the looped ༧ of 2647's last place, a ༨), not «༢༩༨»; it cannot follow 185, and the chain 185 − 7 gives 178: it is the print's slip, ༢ for ༡. The reduction beside it prints «༢༨», 28, with no third digit (the machines' 288 was the 28 run together with the next letter); 178 − 28 = 150 follows, and 150 is the print's next figure. The digit ༧ was told from ༩ by the shape: ༩ in «༥༩༥» (p. 24) is a small hook, not looped.
