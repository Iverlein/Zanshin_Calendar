# Did Sde srid build his gloss of the five ways of entering on the 'Bri gung dka' mchan, whose verse-lines carry the same clauses in the same order, or on a source both used?

RD321.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-w)

The two glosses were compared on the etexts (state etext; the Blue Beryl's page images are not served), clause by clause (claim st-ch81-five-ways-gloss-blue-beryl-and-drikung-compared). They share eight clauses in one order, with word-for-word stretches (the fourth way's «གནད་ལ་ཟིན་པ་ཡིན་པས་བསྙེན་སྒྲུབ་དང་སྨན་ལྟག་སྤྲོད», the fifth way's «གོང་དུ་སྤངས་སའི་རྟགས» / «གོང་བཤད་ཀྱི་སྤང་རྟགས») that the root verse does not have; but the Blue Beryl adds four clauses the Drikung lacks and ends the fifth way with another remedy (merit and purifying obscurations, where the Drikung has urging to virtue), and the later commentaries follow the Blue Beryl. So it is not a copy of the Drikung's printed gloss; whether Sde srid used the Drikung (older, 1595-1659) and rewrote it or both used an older gloss is not told by the etexts: neither names the other (no «འབྲི་གུང་» or «དཀའ་མཆན» in the Blue Beryl's second volume), and BDRC's full text (three phrases of the gloss) finds no older text with it. The Blue Beryl's closing pages (vol. 2's etext, pp. 935-937) list the works its author knew of the Four Tantras' commentary: the Yu thog pa's, Zur mkhar ba's («ཟུར་མཁར་དྷརྨ་སྭཱམི»), a «Skyem 'grel» (སྐྱེམ་འགྲེལ, presumably Skyem pa's), the Mes po'i zhal lung, Bhang ti's handbook and others, and name no Drikung work; if it is Skyem pa's, that commentary is older than 1688 (the pool had no date for it). Open: whether a scan of the Blue Beryl's ch. 81 or its first volume's preface says more.
## Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-z3)

A third, older witness. The «Skyem 'grel» that the Blue Beryl's closing pages list is the *Rgyud bzhi'i rnam bshad bde ba'i 'dod 'jo* of Skyem pa Tshe dbang (BDRC MW28843, WA28843, author P4109, whose birth BDRC gives as «14XX», from the Old TSD tables; etext VEIE0OPIF8756F5F_I4711, Mtsho sngon mi rigs 2000 print, 1263 pages, fetched whole; the identification of the work with the Blue Beryl's «Skyem 'grel» rests on the name and on modern medical authors citing «skyem 'grel las» for Four Tantras glosses, not on a statement). Its comment on the five ways of entering (etext [p818]-[p819], printed pp. 796-797) has the same clauses in the same order, in shorter words: «རྨི་ལམ་ཟ་ཟི་གདོན་རྨི་བ་ལོངས་སྤྱོད་རེ་འདོད་དེ་རིམ་གྲོ་ལ་འབད་དོ། །གདོན་བཅོས་བྱ། གློ་བུར་དུ་ལྡང་ཤེས་པ་བྱིང་བ། མི་གཙང་བ་མནོལ་ཏེ། ཁྲུས་དང་བསང་དང་བྱད་འགྲོལ་བྱ། གནད་ལ་ཟིན་པ་ནི་དོ་བསྡོ་བསད་པ་སྟེ་བསྙེན་སྨན་ལྟག་སྤྲད། སྤང་རྟགས་ཤར་བ་ནི་ལས་ཀྱི་རྣམ་སྨིན་ཏེ་ཚོགས་གསོག་སྒྲིབ་སྦྱང་ལ་འབད་དོ།» (the fifth way: the dream of eating and the like; the remedy against the spirit; the fourth's «gnad la zin pa» with approach, medicine and «lag» given; the rising «spang rtags» as ripened karma, to be met with merit and purifying obscurations). The fifth way's end «merit and purifying obscurations» is the Blue Beryl's, not the Drikung's «urging to virtue»; the stretches «གནད་ལ་ཟིན་པ», «བསྙེན་…སྨན་ལྟག་སྤྲད/སྤྲོད» and «སྤང་རྟགས» are common to Skyem pa and the Blue Beryl. So the Blue Beryl's gloss rests on a text at least as old as Skyem pa's (BDRC: 15th century or earlier), most likely this one, which it names, and not on the Drikung's printed gloss (1595-1659); that the Drikung also used Skyem pa is not shown. Dating still rests on BDRC's «14XX». BDRC phrases 2026-11-16: «སྐྱེམ་འགྲེལ» 29 hits (modern medical works quoting «skyem 'grel las» and the work itself), «སྐྱེམ་པ་གཞན་ཕན་» 0.
