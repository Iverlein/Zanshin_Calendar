# Did Sde srid build his gloss of the five ways of entering on the 'Bri gung dka' mchan, whose verse-lines carry the same clauses in the same order, or on a source both used?

RD321.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-w)

The two glosses were compared on the etexts (state etext; the Blue Beryl's page images are not served), clause by clause (claim st-ch81-five-ways-gloss-blue-beryl-and-drikung-compared). They share eight clauses in one order, with word-for-word stretches (the fourth way's «གནད་ལ་ཟིན་པ་ཡིན་པས་བསྙེན་སྒྲུབ་དང་སྨན་ལྟག་སྤྲོད», the fifth way's «གོང་དུ་སྤངས་སའི་རྟགས» / «གོང་བཤད་ཀྱི་སྤང་རྟགས») that the root verse does not have; but the Blue Beryl adds four clauses the Drikung lacks and ends the fifth way with another remedy (merit and purifying obscurations, where the Drikung has urging to virtue), and the later commentaries follow the Blue Beryl. So it is not a copy of the Drikung's printed gloss; whether Sde srid used the Drikung (older, 1595-1659) and rewrote it or both used an older gloss is not told by the etexts: neither names the other (no «འབྲི་གུང་» or «དཀའ་མཆན» in the Blue Beryl's second volume), and BDRC's full text (three phrases of the gloss) finds no older text with it. The Blue Beryl's closing pages (vol. 2's etext, pp. 935-937) list the works its author knew of the Four Tantras' commentary: the Yu thog pa's, Zur mkhar ba's («ཟུར་མཁར་དྷརྨ་སྭཱམི»), a «Skyem 'grel» (སྐྱེམ་འགྲེལ, presumably Skyem pa's), the Mes po'i zhal lung, Bhang ti's handbook and others, and name no Drikung work; if it is Skyem pa's, that commentary is older than 1688 (the pool had no date for it). Open: whether a scan of the Blue Beryl's ch. 81 or its first volume's preface says more.
