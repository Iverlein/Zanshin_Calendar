# «ཚེས་བསྲེས་མ་ནུས(༡༤)བགོ» on WB p. 30: is the word «མ་ནུས» or «ཡིད» (14, as the same verse has on p. 31)?

## Answered 2026-11-12

RD214x (2026-11-12), the 1996 print's own scan (I1KG12906 img. 40, p. 30, at 6x): the line «རིལ་པོར་ཚེས་བསྲེས་མ་ནུས(༡༤)བགོ» shows two syllables between «བསྲེས» and the bracket, the second with a clear vowel ུ and a final ས, then «བགོ»; the bracket holds «༡༤». «ཡིད» would be one syllable, so the word is «མ་ནུས» as the Gser thang reprint and the machines read. The same print's other lines of the page carry «ཡིད(༡༤)» in a separate place (the line «ཡིད» of the first verse), which is why the machine text confused the two; the figure 14 is the same in both words.
