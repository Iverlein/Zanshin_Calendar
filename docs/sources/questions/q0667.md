# Who dropped the 24th from the moxa dates of KL's six?

Who dropped the 24th from the moxa dates of the Blue Beryl in KL's six (KL p. 256), and does KL's 'in agreement with the Tibetan reckoning' mean that the 15th and 30th stand for the full and new moon? (RD179)

INV-g (2026-11-13), narrowed: the second half is answered by the word itself. A BDRC search snippet of a Tibetan commentary on the *Precious Jewel Heap* (Dpe chos rin chen spungs pa'i 'grel pa, MW16936_381C9F, search-only, in Blo bzang bshad sgrub rgya mtsho's collected works) glosses «ཉ་སྟོང་ཚེས་བརྒྱད་ཀྱི་སྡོམ་པ» (the vow of the eight, 'nya stong tshes brgyad'): «ཉ་ནི་ཚེས་བཅོ་ལྔ་དང༌། སྟོང་ནི་ཚེས་ཉེར་དགུའམ» ('nya' is the fifteenth, 'stong' the twenty-ninth or «གནམ་གང» (read so in the snippet's Wylie 'gnam gang', not identified); the gloss is cut off): so the Blue Beryl's «ཉ་སྟོང» are the 15th and the 29th or the last day, KL's 15 and 30 for full and new moon; KL's 'in agreement with the Tibetan reckoning' may mean just that. Who dropped the 24th: not found; BDRC's exact search for the six as numbers («ཚེས་གཅིག་དྲུག་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཅོ་བརྒྱད་ཉེར་གཉིས་སུམ་ཅུ», «གཏར་བསྲེག་ཚེས་གཅིག་དྲུག་བཅོ་ལྔ», «མེ་བསྲེག་ཚེས་གཅིག་དྲུག་བཅོ་བརྒྱད», «ཚེས་གཅིག་དྲུག་བཅོ་བརྒྱད་ཉེར་གཉིས་སུམ་ཅུ», «བཅོ་བརྒྱད་ཉེར་གཉིས་སུམ་ཅུ་ཉིན») finds KL alone again (I1KG21795, one hit); the list with the 24th stands in the Blue Beryl and its heirs only. Still open as to the dropping.

INV-c (2026-11-14), narrowed again: BDRC's exact search for «ཉ་སྟོང་རྣམས་ལ་གཏར་བསྲེག་གཉིས་ཀ་སྤང» finds the Blue Beryl's two chapters again (MW1PD3_254AC6, MW1PD3_BED6AF) with the 24th, and none without it; «བཅོ་བརྒྱད་ཉེར་གཉིས་ཉ་སྟོང», «ཉེར་གཉིས་ཉ་སྟོང་རྣམས་ལ་གཏར་བསྲེག», «མེ་བཙའ་མི་བྱ་བའི་ཚེས» and «གཏར་བསྲེག་མི་བྱ་བའི་ཚེས» 0 hits. The list's author is Acārya Phyag rdum (ཨ་ཙ་ར་ཕྱག་རྡུམ, a tsa ra phyag rdum, the Indian Fire-god physician, teacher of Yuthok's nine pupils: see open question 79); KL's author belongs to Yuthok's tradition, but no text of it with the dates was found, so the dropping is KL's (or its unnamed source's) and not traced.

## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-i)

Narrowed as to who held the 24th before KL. BDRC exact phrase «ཉེར་བཞི་ཉ་སྟོང་» (10 hits) shows the list with the 24th in works older than or beside the Blue Beryl and not named before: Gtsang sman Ye shes bzang po's Zhal gdams man ngag gnad 'gag do shal mdzes rgyan (MW7616 and MW1PD53272, good OCR), which quotes Phyag rdum («ཕྱག་སྡུམ་ཞལ་ནས་ཀྱང༌། ཁ་ཅར་བུ་ཡུག་འཚུབ་དུས་གཏར་མེ་སྤངས། ཚེས་གཅིག་དྲུག་དང་བཅོ་བརྒྱད་ཉེར་གཉིས་དང༌། ཉེར་བཞི་ཉ་སྟོང་རྣམས་ལ་གཏར་མེ་སྤངས།», snippet, Wylie of the etext), so the list reaches the physicians as Phyag rdum's own saying with the 24th; also in 'Bri gung Chos kyi grags pa's Dka' mchan on the Four Tantras (MW1KG24729, p. 918), a Mongol commentary on the Four Tantras' terms by Lung rigs bstan dar (MW00KG07597), the Arura textbook (MW8LS18556, 2019) and a Gso rig anthology (MW1KG982). So the dropping of the 24th is not in the line of the Tibetan commentators, who keep it; it is KL's (or its source's). The reason is not stated anywhere. BDRC exact «ཚེས་ཉེར་བཞི་གཏར་བསྲེག» and «ཉ་སྟོང་ཚེས་ཉེར་བཞི» 0 hits. Still open as to the dropping.
