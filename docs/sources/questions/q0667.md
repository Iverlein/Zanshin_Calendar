# Who dropped the 24th from the moxa dates of KL's six?

Who dropped the 24th from the moxa dates of the Blue Beryl in KL's six (KL p. 256), and does KL's 'in agreement with the Tibetan reckoning' mean that the 15th and 30th stand for the full and new moon? (RD179)

INV-g (2026-11-13), narrowed: the second half is answered by the word itself. A BDRC search snippet of a Tibetan commentary on the *Precious Jewel Heap* (Dpe chos rin chen spungs pa'i 'grel pa, MW16936_381C9F, search-only, in Blo bzang bshad sgrub rgya mtsho's collected works) glosses «ཉ་སྟོང་ཚེས་བརྒྱད་ཀྱི་སྡོམ་པ» (the vow of the eight, 'nya stong tshes brgyad'): «ཉ་ནི་ཚེས་བཅོ་ལྔ་དང༌། སྟོང་ནི་ཚེས་ཉེར་དགུའམ» ('nya' is the fifteenth, 'stong' the twenty-ninth or «གནམ་གང» (read so in the snippet's Wylie 'gnam gang', not identified); the gloss is cut off): so the Blue Beryl's «ཉ་སྟོང» are the 15th and the 29th or the last day, KL's 15 and 30 for full and new moon; KL's 'in agreement with the Tibetan reckoning' may mean just that. Who dropped the 24th: not found; BDRC's exact search for the six as numbers («ཚེས་གཅིག་དྲུག་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཅོ་བརྒྱད་ཉེར་གཉིས་སུམ་ཅུ», «གཏར་བསྲེག་ཚེས་གཅིག་དྲུག་བཅོ་ལྔ», «མེ་བསྲེག་ཚེས་གཅིག་དྲུག་བཅོ་བརྒྱད», «ཚེས་གཅིག་དྲུག་བཅོ་བརྒྱད་ཉེར་གཉིས་སུམ་ཅུ», «བཅོ་བརྒྱད་ཉེར་གཉིས་སུམ་ཅུ་ཉིན») finds KL alone again (I1KG21795, one hit); the list with the 24th stands in the Blue Beryl and its heirs only. Still open as to the dropping.
