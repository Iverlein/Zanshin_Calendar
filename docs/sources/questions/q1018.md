# In the Mchog dga's reckoning of when a dream comes (kd.3:p177-179), what are the thirty parts of the tattāla junction and the «five places» they stand in, and is «ten parts, a third of the night» a misprint, given that 6, 10, 15 and 30 are not one scale?

RD361.

## Answered 2026-11-10

Answered from the etext (RD369), not yet on a scan. The thirty parts are the thirty-fold division of the instant junction (tattāla) of the moment asked; the five places are the instant, its thirty parts, the water-hour (called the staff on one page), the water-srang and the breath, and the parts are 30 x the instant's water-srang / the house's own water-srang (the book's example: 200 x 30 / 400 = 15). 6, 10, 15 and 30 are one scale: the part n of thirty is n/30 of the night, a fifth, a third, a half (midnight) and the whole (dawn). «Ten parts, a third of the night» is no misprint: the Dbyangs 'char's own verse has ten, fifteen and thirty with a third, midnight and dawn; six and the early night are the commentary's example. A damaged clause in the Kun 'dus etext and one number in the Mongolian print (thirty for ten, looked at on the scan) are copy faults, the root deciding. Not said: from what the night is counted, why a part of a sign is a part of the night, and which night is meant.
