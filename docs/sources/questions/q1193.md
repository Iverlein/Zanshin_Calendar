# Do the Vajrāvalī empowerment manuals (Drukpa, Sangs rgyas rdo rje) take Mar me mdzad bzang po's bgegs mthar byed remedy (dkb:p163-164) from the Sngags rim chen mo, or is there an earlier witness (q1141)?

Raised by the 2026-11-13 investigation n.

## Answered 2026-11-14

For the Drukpa Vajrāvalī manual, from the Sngags rim chen mo, or from the source the Sngags rim chen mo itself drew on in the same words; no earlier witness of the Ender of Obstructors at a dream turned up. The manual's passage (dkb:p164) follows Tsong kha pa's (snr:p370) clause by clause: the Mañjuśrī root tantra's four-watch verse and the reason that the first three watches are not certain, then the remedies in the one order, the amṛta-whirling mantra of the Garland (the Vajrāvalī) first, Mar me mdzad bzang po's Ender of Obstructors second, Klu byang's meditation on selflessness third, the Common Tantra's pacifying fire offering fourth, Dārikapa's rites fifth. Only the wording is shortened or changed (the manual speaks of a prophecy for the future and adds «here too»). Such an order of five authorities, with the Garland's remedy placed with Mar me mdzad bzang po's, is Tsong kha pa's compilation, not a chance agreement; the manual (a later work) names Mar me mdzad bzang po as he does. The Sangs rgyas rdo rje manual was read only in BDRC's snippet (the same clause), not at length. What the Sngags rim chen mo rests on for the Ender (q1141) is not reached by this.
