# In which Edo source is the 初夢 night fixed at 2 January, and is it counted by the lunar months of the Edo calendar, the only one in use until 1872?

RD308; the Japanese Wikipedia's 初夢 (read 2026-11-10, a lead and not a source) names three nights in the Edo period, New Year's Eve to 1 January, 1 to 2 January and 2 to 3 January, the last the commonest in the late Edo period.
