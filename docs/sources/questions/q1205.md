# KP label 111: which animal for each of the east mansions (six mansions, five animals)?

The east group has six mansions (smin drug, snar ma, mgo, lag, nabs so, rgyal) but only five animals are named, and «བ་» and «པར» are doubtful. Wanted: the animal for each mansion on the scan, label 111.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (scan-g)

On BDRC label 111 (pool img. 109), line 2, read at 4×, 7× and 12×: the line is «༧དེ་ནས་རྒྱུ་སྐར་སྒྲིབ་ཐབས་བཤད། །སྨིན་སྣར་མགོ་ལག་ནབས་སོ་རྒྱལ། །རིམ་བཞིན་སྤྲེའུ་ཁྱི་དང་ཝ། །བ་དང་ཕག་པར་དྲུག་གི། །ཐོད་པ་ཤར་ཕྱོགས་འཕྱར་བས་སྒྲིབ།», exactly the machine's words: after the shad that follows «ཝ» come the single glyph «བ», «དང», «ཕག» and then two glyphs, a «པ» and a «ར», before «དྲུག་གི» ("of the six"). So the animals are six names for the six mansions in order, not five: the monkey (སྤྲེའུ, *sprel*) for the Pleiades (smin drug), the dog (ཁྱི, *khyi*) for snar ma, the fox (ཝ, *wa*) for mgo, then «བ» (*ba*), the pig (ཕག, *phag*) and «པར» (*par*), the last three for lag, nabs so and rgyal if the order holds. The machine had not lost a name; the doubt is only what «བ» and «པར» are (ba is the cow in common speech, but the text does not say so; «པར» is not a word the dictionaries read name, and the two glyphs could also be read as the «པ» ending «ཕག་པ» (pig) and a «ར» (goat), which would leave «བ» as the fifth animal; a slanted stroke between «ཕག» and the «པ» is taken as a tsheg, but it is not certain). No animal is therefore built on. The KP2 manuscript (I3CN12074 img. 330, a faint hand copy) shows the same passage on its first line but is unreadable at 4.5× at this place. Still open: the senses of «བ» and «པར» (a witness listing the animals of the mansions' earth lords, WB's or Mipham's list of mansions and their animals).
