# The Zhol print's son line: «དཀར་ནག་ཟུང་དུ» or «དཀར་ན་གཟུང་དུ»

Does the Zhol print (WBZ I1KG1710 img. 498, line 7) read «བུ་ཕྲད་དཀར་ནག་ཟུང་དུ་བོར», as its three machine readings do, where the 1996 and the Taikhang (img. 515) read «བུ་ཕྲད་དཀར་ན་གཟུང་དུ་བོར», and does a better crop of the Zhol or another print (the Sakya Centre I1KG12739–40, the Gser thang reprint I3CN5028 img. 375) settle which letters stand after དཀར? See verdicts.md, RD123.

RD175 (2026-11-06), narrowed: Taikhang img. 515 line 6 reads «དཀར་ན་གཟུང་དུ་བོར», as the 1996 print; on the Zhol (img. 498 line 7) Yigdzin-1 reads «དཀར་ནག་ཟུང་དུ་བོར»; the Zhol crop at 3.3× has the length of either reading and does not decide.
