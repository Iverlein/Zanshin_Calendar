# The Zhol's «ལོངས་པའི་ཉིན» against «ལངས་པའི་ཉིན» on p. 37

In the verse on p. 37 (img. 45), does the Zhol print's «ལོངས་པའི་ཉིན» («ཆུ་ཚོད་ལྔ་བཅུར་ལོངས་པའི་ཉིན») against the 1996 edition's «ལངས་པའི་ཉིན» change the sense of the day's mark, the sun having "reached" and not "risen to" the fiftieth water-hour?

RD180 (2026-11-06), narrowed: both etexts read «ལོངས་པའི་ཉིན» (1996 img. 45, Zhol img. 678); «ལངས་པའི་ཉིན» occurs in neither; the 1996 scan was not read.

RD180 second pass (2026-11-06), narrowed, premise confirmed: the 1996 scan (img. 45 line 21, 6×) reads «ལངས» with no vowel over the ང, and Yigdzin-1 agrees; the 1996 etext's «ལོངས» is the etext's spelling; the Zhol reads «ལོངས» only by its etext (scan not eyed); no source gives the difference a sense.
