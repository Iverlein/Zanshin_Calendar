# How does the end of the first line under the drawing on ch. 12 p. 95 («སྐྱིད་སྡུག་ནས་བརྩམས་ཞེས་བཤད་པོ») read: ཞེས་བཤད་པ and the next line's first syllable, and is སྡུག or དུག the right syllable?

## Answered 2026-11-12

On the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5028 img. 102) the first line under the drawing ends «ཞེས་བཤད་»: «བཤད» with a plain final ད and a tsheg, and the line closes there. The next line opens with «པོ» and then «། །དགུ་ཕྲག་…». The syllable after «སྐྱིད་» is the three-layered stack «སྡུག», the same stack as «སྡུག» in «གཡས་གཡོན་སྡུག་བསྔལ» nine lines lower, and not a plain «དུག». The line reads «སྐྱིད་སྡུག་ནས་བརྩམས་ཞེས་བཤད་པོ». (wb.1:p95b)
