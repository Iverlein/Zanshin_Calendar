# «བརྩིག» or «འཛིག» in Mūla's good list, p. 323

In Mūla's good list on p. 323, «སྒོ་འཛུགས་བང་བ་བརྩིག», does the reprint's syllable after «བང་བ» read «བརྩིག» or «འཛིག»? The reprint at 3× looks «འཛིག», and the Zhol print was not told.

RD176 (2026-11-06), narrowed toward «བརྩིག»: the reprint (img. 329) has a boxy བ, an angular prefix བ, and a stack with ི on ཙ over ག (5×), no round འ; the Zhol (img. 1078, line 3) shows the same sequence at 7×, but the glyphs are small, so not independent proof.

## Answered 2026-11-06

RD176 second part (2026-11-06): SY (Namkha Senge's Skar yig, I1PD30933) etext p. 384 has «སྒོ་འཛུགས་བང་ བརྩིག»; the reprint at 5× reads «བརྩིག»; the Zhol is consistent. The reprint's «འཛིག» is ruled out.
