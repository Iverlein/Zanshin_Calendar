## Answered 2026-11-24

Answered: the 29th. Dilgo Khyentse's life of Mipham (mrs:p117; BDRC MW8LS20744 and the copy MW3CN5285, which agree) says he stayed until the 29th of the Saga month and that his body came to rest on the 29th, a Friday under Snar ma, at sunset; the funeral was in the fifth month. Claim [mipham-passed-on-the-29th-of-the-fourth-month](../claims/mipham-passed-on-the-29th-of-the-fourth-month.md). Namdroling's 4/30 has no support in the life. Etext only; the scans are not served.
