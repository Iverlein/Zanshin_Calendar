# Item 15 of the forty of the birth wind: «ང་བཞི» (54) for fifty

Item 15 of the forty of the birth wind (p. 342) prints «ང་བཞི» (54) in every print; is it a slip for fifty, and does the Taikhang reproduction (I5877, about img. 462) read it so?

## Answered 2026-11-06

RD175 (2026-11-06), Taikhang img. 461–462: the run goes 58, 56, 54 (ང་བཞི), 52 (ང་གཉིས), then «བུ་གཅིག་མ་གསུམ་ང་བཞི» again, 54; the Taikhang carries the same «ང་བཞི» as the other prints, so the repeated 54 is in the block. q183 repeats q520.
