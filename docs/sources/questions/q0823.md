# ST ch. 2 «རྨི་ལམ་དག་ན»: is དག the plural marker or 'clear'?

What does a commentary on the Explanatory Tantra say? A 'clear' dream would restrict the verdict.

## Answered 2026-11-10

Answered in sense by RD300: four commentaries (Byang pa, Dri med kun gsal, an unsigned great commentary, the 'Bum chung gsal sgron) take the dream as of men seen many times or again and again; none reads དག as «clear». དག is the plural or repetition. The root line reads the same on all editions read.
