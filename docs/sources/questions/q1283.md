# Zhe chen rgyal tshab quotes «བཅུ་བཞི་དང་ནི་བཅོ་ལྔ་དང་། །ཚེས་བརྒྱད་དང་ནི་གནམ་སྟོང་ལ» from the Sūtra Requested by a God and the Avadāna beside his own three days: does any Nyingma author tell lay people to keep the fourteenth as a fourth fast day, or is the verse quoted for the benefit only?

**Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-b).** Narrowed, claim [fast-verse-with-the-fourteenth-quoted-for-benefit-by-nyingma-authors](../claims/fast-verse-with-the-fourteenth-quoted-for-benefit-by-nyingma-authors.md). BDRC's exact phrase «ཚེས་བརྒྱད་དང་ནི་གནམ་སྟོང་ལ» has 10 hits and «བཅུ་བཞི་དང་ནི་བཅོ་ལྔ་དང་» 81; the ten are Zhe chen's collected works, three fast-manual collections, the Kaḥ thog Rig 'dzin Tshe dbang nor bu's eleven-faced Avalokiteśvara fast liturgy (written 1753), Si tu Padma nyin byed, and others, each quoting the verse in its proofs of the benefit of the one-day vow. In none of the snippets, and in the one longer page read (a text that sets Maitreya's prophecy and the Avadāna beside the lay one-day vow), is the fourteenth given as a fourth day of the author's own rule. Open: the pages of the Kaḥ thog text and of the modern Nyingma manuals were not read beyond the snippet.
