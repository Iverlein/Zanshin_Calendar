# Does the Derge's «སྡིག་པ» at the eighth place of the list of houses (mmk.1:p403) render a Sanskrit word other than «vṛścika» (the lists in ch. 1 and ch. 18 have «vṛścika»), and is «སྦལ་པ» (frog) for «karkaṭa» (crab) a variant or a slip?

RD372.

## Answered 2026-11-13

«སྡིག་པ» renders vṛścika, the scorpion, and «སྦལ་པ» karkaṭaka, the crab, and neither is a slip. The first chapter of the same Derge volume lists the signs in the same words (mmk.1:p246: ལུག, གླང, སྦྱོར་བ, སྦལ་པ, སེང་གེ, བུ་མོ, སྲང, སྡིག་པ, གཞུ, ཆུ་སྲིན, བུམ་པ, ཉ, then མིའུ་ཐུང, ཉེ་བའི་བུམ་པ, རལ་གྲི, མ་ཧེ), and runs word for word with the Sanskrit list of mmk.2:p19 (meṣa ... karkaṭaka ... vṛścika ... mīna, vānara, upa-kumbha, khaḍga, mahiṣa); ch. 18's births call the fourth sign «the house of the frog» (mmk.1:p429). So the translation is consistent, and the frog for the crab is the translators' name for the sign (WB has a similar word for the same sign in its period names). Why a frog is not said.
