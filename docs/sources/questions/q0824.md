# Are the two bracketed remedies of ST ch. 91 (the thread; the milk of cattle and goats) interlinear notes that entered the Serthang text?

Drathang img. 647 (machine) has the root verse without them; needs the Drathang folio legible or another print.

**Narrowed 2026-11-17 (inv-d).** The question's premise is answered by the prints already read (claims st-ch91-remedies-prints-commentaries, st-ch91-notes-small-type-derge-chag-po-ri): both remedies stand in the verse's line of the Serthang, Zhol, Derge and Chag-po-ri prints, in smaller type on the Derge and the Chag-po-ri, and the Blue Beryl, the Drikung and the Khro ru commentaries have the same matter as glosses (Skyem pa the thread only), so they read as interlinear notes that entered the prints. Only the Drathang machine text lacks them; the Drathang folio is not legible enough to confirm, and no other print was found. Open for the Drathang only.
