# Is «གནམ་གང» in Mipham's Sarasvatī instruction the new moon or the full moon?

Raised by the practice-days investigation (pd-g, 2026-11-16); see the cited passages.

## Answered 2026-11-21

The new moon. Co ne Grags pa bshad sgrub's account of the times (dtn:i316, read on the scan) lists the three good days of each month as «ཚེས་བརྒྱད་བཅོ་ལྔ་གནམ་གང» (the eighth, the fifteenth, «gnam gang») and says of «གནམ་གང»: «ཟླ་བའི་དཀར་ཆ་ཐམས་ཅད་འགྲིབ་པའི་སྐབས» (the time when all the white part of the moon has waned), set against the fifteenth, when the moon is full. It is the Amitābha day in the same list. q1179 found «གནམ་གང» a fixed pair with «ཉེར་དགུ» (the 29th), the alternative last day of the month. So in Mipham's Sarasvatī instruction (mgz.33:p166) «གནམ་གང» is the day of the new moon, the month's end, not the full moon.
