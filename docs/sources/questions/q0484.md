# Which years put the 大將軍 in which 仲

Which years put the 大將軍 in which 仲? The 暦林問答集 (§8, §54) gives four 仲 and three years in each, with no anchor; the modern table (亥子丑 west, 寅卯辰 north, 巳午未 east, 申酉戌 south) is the digests'. Which old text gives the assignment? (RD92)

## Answered 2026-11-14

The old text that gives the assignment is the 拾芥抄 (洞院公賢, NDL pid 2543902, 巻下 方角禁忌, img. 21; the typeset 故実叢書 pid 772010, p. 57). After the 暦林問答集 §8's quotation of the 新撰陰陽書 («大將軍 … 不居四孟、常行四仲以正四方、三歳一移», "it does not stay in the four 孟 but always goes in the four 仲, moves once in three years") it adds: «仮令太歳在寅卯辰、大将軍常在子方、太歳在巳午未、大将軍在卯方、太歳在申酉戌、大将軍在午方、太歳在亥子丑、大将軍在酉方、訖南復始» ("if the year star is in 寅卯辰, the 大將軍 is always in the 子 direction; in 巳午未, in 卯; in 申酉戌, in 午; in 亥子丑, in 酉"). The 簠簋内伝's 八将神 table (巻一 §3, NDL pid 1911335, img. 9) gives the same by single years (大将軍方 酉酉子子子卯卯卯午午午酉 for 子 to 亥 years), the 子 year taken with 亥 and 丑 as in the 拾芥抄. So 亥子丑 = 酉 (west), 寅卯辰 = 子 (north), 巳午未 = 卯 (east), 申酉戌 = 午 (south): the modern digests' table is the old one. The 暦林問答集 stops before the assignment, so the gap there is the book's, not the tradition's.
