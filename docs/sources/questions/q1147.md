# Is the vidyā-king of the dusk session Nāgarakṣa, and is དཔའ་བོ་རྐྱང་སྒྲུབ in the life a name or a hero's single-deity practice

The short account says the nāga came in a dream at about nam gung after going to sleep; ku sā li rkyang sgrub there suggests a practice.

## Answered 2026-11-13

RD403 follow-up: yes to both, on the texts' own words. The short account (dds.50:p6-8) names the vidyā-king of the first dusk session Nāgarakṣa and the life (dds.49:p110-112) has the same sight, prayer and midnight nāga dream in the same session. «དཔའ་བོ་རྐྱང་སྒྲུབ» is a practice kind (a hero in his solitary form, BDRC titles of the Bla ma dgongs 'dus cycle), not a name, as the short account's «ཀུ་སཱ་ལི་རྐྱང་སྒྲུབ» is. Claim: dds-vidya-king-of-the-dusk-session-is-nagaraksa-and-dpa-bo-rkyang-sgrub-is-a-practice-kind.
