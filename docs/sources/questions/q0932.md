# Is the monk Sgra tshad pa of MDP p. 137 Rin chen rnam rgyal, Bu ston's pupil and biographer, whom BDRC's records call «dge slong sgra tshad pa»?

RD315.

## Answered 2026-11-13

Narrowed 2026-11-13: the epithet is Rin chen rnam rgyal's, the identity is not shown. BDRC's exact-phrase search for «དགེ་སློང་སྒྲ་ཚད་པ» (8 hits) gives, in the Sakya sādhana collection Sgrub thabs kun btus (MW23681, Gene Smith's collection; snippet only, no work opened), a colophon in the form «dge slong sgra tshad pa rin chen rnam rgyal gyis sbyar ba» (compiled by the monk Sgra tshad pa Rin chen rnam rgyal) in the Vajravārāhī (Rdo rje phag mo dkar mo) cycle, so the monk is named «dge slong sgra tshad pa» with his name in Sakya colophons, and BDRC's name suggestions list «sgra tshad pa rin chen rnam rgyal», his collected works and a short life. «སྒྲ་ཚད་པའི་ཟིན་བྲིས» has one hit only, the MDP's own line (the printed Sha bo Tshe ring's collection MW21880, p. 136-137); the exact words «ཟིན་བྲིས» are used by no other work under the epithet. The lineage of the MDP rite ends with Bu ston, whose pupil and biographer Rin chen rnam rgyal was, which fits a note taken down from Bu ston's teaching; nothing in the MDP names the monk's teacher or date, and no text says that Rin chen rnam rgyal took a note of this rite. Open: Rin chen rnam rgyal's collected works or the catalogue of them listing a «zin bris» on the rebirth-seeing rite.

## Answered 2026-11-14

Answered 2026-11-14, as very probable, not proven. BDRC's person record P154 is «Sgra tshad pa Rin chen rnam rgyal» (སྒྲ་ཚད་པ་རིན་ཆེན་རྣམ་རྒྱལ), 1318–1388, Bu ston's pupil, called his «thugs sras lo tsā ba», who held the abbot's seat of Zhwa lu for 32 years after Bu ston's death in 1364, "best known for his learning in the grammatical arts" (so «sgra tshad pa», one who knows the measure of words, is his title), a teacher in a Kālacakra transmission and in Bu ston's 'Od zer dri med lha drug. The only other person BDRC names «Sgra tshad pa» is P4284 Sgra tshad pa Rin chen rgyal mtshan, of the 13th century (flourished 1200–1299), who lived before Bu ston (1290–1364, P155) and cannot be the writer of notes on a rite whose lineage ends with Bu ston. The same volume of Bu ston's collected works (vol. 28 of the 2008 print, BDRC MW1PD45496), which carries the rite on pp. 825–828 with the note «དགེ་སློང་སྒྲ་ཚད་པའི་ཟིན་བྲིས», also holds the catalogue of that collection titled «Sgra tshad pa nyid kyi bka' 'bum dkar chag» (part MW1PD45496_3CBA25, printed pp. 415–420), so the pupil's name is tied to the compilation of the very collection in which the rite stands. Not shown: that the pupil wrote the note himself (no text says so), and the catalogue's own words were not read (the scans of that part are not served, the etext volumes fetched do not hold it). Searched in BDRC's titles of MW1PD45496 for «rmi lam»: only Bu ston's own text, no separate «zin bris» of Rin chen rnam rgyal.
