# What are ལྗོང, ལྕོལ, སྡིག་སྡོམ and ནིའུ་གསིང in ch. 81's dream list, and what does «ལྷས་ཚོར་དུས་ཏེ» say?

Read on the scan (ch. 81, pp. 350–351); the words are not in the lexica consulted.

## Answered 2026-11-10

RD356: the creatures are tadpole and frog, fish, snake, worm and bee-grub, scorpion and spider; ལྗོང is the Serthang, Derge, Chag-po-ri and Drikung spelling of ལྕོང «tadpole», the etext's ལྕོལ a slip; ནིའུ་གསིང is the meadow; ལྷས་ཚོར་དུས་ཏེ: see q885 (still open).
