# Are vol. 27's waking and sleeping instructions the compiler's prose or the treasure's own text

Vol. 27 (images 81-82, 131) gives instructions for waking and sleeping with awareness; the page does not say whether they are Mkhan chen Dpal ldan shes rab's prose or quote the treasure. The root verses of the six bardos quoted at image 282 are the treasure's.

## Answered 2026-11-11

RD402: tsl.27 images 81-82 and 131 lie in the Gzer bu section, a short commentary by its writer (Orgyan gsang sngags bstan 'dzin Nam sgo chen sprul) on the root vajra words of the treasure Sku gsum dgongs 'dus of 'Gro 'dul gling pa; the instructions are the writer's prose, the root words carry the ༔ mark. Whether the writer is the volume's compiler is not said.
