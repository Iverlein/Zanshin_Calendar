# Are vol. 27's waking and sleeping instructions the compiler's prose or the treasure's own text

Vol. 27 (images 81-82, 131) gives instructions for waking and sleeping with awareness; the page does not say whether they are Mkhan chen Dpal ldan shes rab's prose or quote the treasure. The root verses of the six bardos quoted at image 282 are the treasure's.
