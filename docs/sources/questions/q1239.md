Whether KP's «ཉི» abbreviates «ཉི་ཆེན་མོ» (as «དབུལ་ཉི་» in its box 57) and «ལུས» is the stage, is not settled. Claim: kp-dur-zhugs-stage-name-in-wb-work-9.
