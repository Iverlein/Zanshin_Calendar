# When the Sakya manual says that in the second and third watches «no dream comes», does it mean that no dream is dreamed, or that none is to be counted, given that the root tantra describes dreams of bile and wind for them?

RD377.

## Answered 2026-11-13

Answered on the manual's own words (rsk:p459-461): «ཐུན་གཉིས་པ་དང་གསུམ་པ་ནམ་གུང་གི་ཆ་གཉིད་འཐུག་པོ་ཞེས་རྨི་ལམ་མི་འབྱུང»: because the midnight part is thick sleep, no dream arises. It is not a rule that none is counted; the reason is the depth of sleep, given to explain why only the last watch is examined. The manual does not mention the root tantra's bile and wind dreams of the middle watches. Claim: rsk-second-and-third-watch-no-dream-comes-because-of-thick-sleep.
