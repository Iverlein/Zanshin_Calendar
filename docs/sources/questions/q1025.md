# In the opening of Mipham's notes on WB (wb.2:p474) «དུས་ཁམས» is printed where the sense wants «རུས་ཁམས» (clan element); is it a misprint?

RD362.

## Narrowed 2026-11-13

Narrowed (2026-11-13): not a misprint of one print. BDRC's search finds the opening sentence with «དུས་ཁམས» in both typeset editions of the White Beryl (Beijing 1996-1997 and Gser thang), and «རུས་ཁམས་ནི་ངོས་ཟིན་ན» nowhere; whether «དུས» is an error inherited by both from an earlier text or a term of the writer is not decided. Mipham's own print (I2029) and the Collected Works print (I1PD34647) were not read at this sentence. Claim wb-notes-opening-dus-khams-in-two-printings.

## Answered 2026-11-14

It is a misprint of the two typeset White Beryl editions, not Mipham's word. Mipham's notes as the Chengdu Collected Works print them (BDRC I1PD34647, img. 530, the opening folio under the homage «ན་མོ་མཉྫུ་ཤྲཱི་ཡེ») read «རུས་ཁམས་ནི་ངོས་འཛིན་ན་དེ་དང་མིན་ན་བླ་རྟགས་གཡག་སོགས་གང་ཡིན་ངག་རྒྱུན་དང་རྨི་ལམ་སོགས་ལས་བརྟག་པ་དང་། མི་གཤིས་སོགས་ཀྱིས་རུས་ཆེན་ལྔ་གང་ཡིན་ངོས་ཟིན་པར་བྱའོ», and again two lines on «རུས་ཁམས་དང་ཕ་མེས་བརྒྱུད…»: «རུས་ཁམས», the clan element, twice, with «ངོས་འཛིན» where the 1996 and Gser thang prints have «ངོས་ཟིན». So the sense the question guessed is the writer's, and «དུས་ཁམས» in both modern prints of the White Beryl is an error they share (their text of the notes is one text; the 1996 print also normalised «ངོས་འཛིན» to «ངོས་ཟིན»). Read by Yigdzin-1 on the scan of img. 530, and the first letter of the word, a ར with the vowel ུ, looked at on a 2× crop; machine reading, the crop not enlarged beyond that. Mipham's own block print (I2029, img. 621 on) was not opened at this sentence.
