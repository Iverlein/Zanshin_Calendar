# Bu ston's text ends with a lineage that names «Jam gsar» and a lo tsā ba whom the machine reads «རྒ་ལོ»; which masters are meant, and is the rite the one BDRC's title «རྨི་ལམ་བརྟག་པ» means, the first syllable of the title line on img. 833 «རྟག» or «བརྟག»?

RD329.

## Answered 2026-11-14

Answered 2026-11-14 for the masters, the title question being settled by the earlier claim (the Delhi and Zhol prints read «རྨི་ལམ་བརྟག་པ», the 2008 print's «རྟག» is a dropped prefix letter). «'Jam gsar» is 'Jam gsar ba Shes rab 'od zer (BDRC P2585, 12th century), a teacher in the 'Bro transmission of the Kālacakra; the lo tsā ba «Rga lo» is a Rgwa lo tsā ba, probably Rnam rgyal rdo rje (P1244), whom BDRC records as 'Jam gsar ba's pupil (details and what is not shown: q931). That they stand in a Kālacakra list agrees with the claim bsr-title-prefix-dropped-lineage-is-the-kalacakra-line.
