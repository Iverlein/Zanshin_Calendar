# Is the departure's «ཁྱད་པར་དམིགས་ཀྱིས་བསལ་བ» the weighing's dmigs bsal, leading over the day?

in the departure (WB vol. 1, p. 375) the earth lords that harm the father's line, the husband or the bride are introduced «ཁྱད་པར་དམིགས་ཀྱིས་བསལ་བ་ནི». Is this the *dmigs bsal* of the weighing (p. 376, «དམིགས་བསལ་བྱུང་ན་དེ་ཉིད་གཙོ»), so that such a lord leads over the departure's general good, or only "in particular, the following"?

**Narrowed 2026-11-03 (RD146, [weighing.md](../weighing.md), *Four open words of the works and rites*).** The clause is the commentators' idiom «X ཁྱད་པར་དམིགས་ཀྱིས་བསལ་བ», "X singled out in particular" (Śākya mchog ldan, the Abhidharma commentaries, the same verse in its copies), the same word as the weighing's *dmigs bsal* only in the plain sense of a named particular. The departure is seventeen checks with a remedy each and ranks none; Mipham's note (vol. 2, p. 531) takes the clause as "in particular, examine these as the text has them". No text says such a lord leads over the departure's general good; the weighing's rule speaks of the day's factors and works. Still open: which of the named lords bars a day outright.
