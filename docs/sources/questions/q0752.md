# What do columns 12 and 14 of the sixty-years table (WB vol. 1, pp. 105, 107, 109) count? Column 12's words (སེ་ཞིག, ཁ་རལ, ཁ་ཡག, འཆང་ཚོང, འདུག་ཁུར) and column 14's number-words with ལྡན (merged cells in years 2, 16, 25, 28) are not explained on these pages.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-ppp)

Column 12's words are the five «kha yan» year-kinds of the elemental reckoning ('byung rtsis): kha yan, khong nang, se zhig, kha ral, mdun khur, saying how the year's power-element stands to its life-element (same, mother, son, friend); see q726's note. Column 14 is still unexplained. Source: tmz:box9.

## Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-q)

Two facts from other prints of the same table. (1) Its columns 9–11 are headed there «སྨེ་ཕྲེང་དང་པོ / གཉིས་པ / གསུམ་པ» (the three rings of the sme ba, see q727) and column 12 «སེ་ཞིག་སོགས་ཀྱི་མིང» (the names of se zhig and the rest), then the moon's, Jupiter's, Saturn's «klung» names: BDRC's search snippets of the *Skar rtsis kyi lag len byed grub sgang dril ltar bkod pa* (MW3CN3480), the *Sgo bzhi mdzod lnga'i yang bcud* (MW3PD398) and the *Lnga sgra gza' lnga'i zhag gsum gyi 'khor grangs …* all give the row «sme phreng dang po, sme phreng gnyis pa, sme phreng gsum pa, se zhig sogs kyi ming: se zhig, kha yan, kha ral, mdun khur … klung gi ming: zla klung, phur klung, spen klung» in that order, so column 12 is confirmed as the kha yan kinds and their name there is «kha yan», not «kha yag». (2) Column 14 («n ldan», «phud») has no heading and no word in these snippets (the snippets carry no numbers of it); phrase searches «སྨེ་བ་གསུམ་ལྡན» (2 hits, both a Gter ma verse about hair "with three sme ba" i.e. moles), «སྨེ་བ་ལྔ་ལྡན» (1, moles again), «སྨེ་ཕྲེང་གར་གསུམ་ལྡན», «སྨེ་ཕྲེང་གསུམ་ལྡན», «སྨེ་བ་བཞི་ལྡན», «གསུམ་ག་བཞི་ཕུད», «སྨེ་ཕྲེང་གསུམ་པོ» (1: the *Chos rnam kun btus* table of q727) found nothing on it. Column 14 stays open.

## Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-z5, computed on the thirty rows read)

Tested on the thirty rows of columns 9 to 14 read in corpus/wb/ch13.md (pp. 105, 107, 109 and the left halves pp. 104, 106, 108; year 16's merged cell reads «ག་བཞི་ལྡན», four). (1) Columns 9 to 11 follow one law in all 90 cells: in year n the three rings are 7, 1, 4 each less n - 1 in the nine (checked cell by cell), so ring 2 is ring 1 plus three and ring 3 is ring 1 plus six in the nine in every year. (2) The merged cells stand exactly in the years whose three counts of column 14 are equal: 2 (3, 3, 3), 16 (4, 4, 4), 25 (3, 3, 3) and 28 (3, 3, 3), and no other of the thirty years has three equal counts; so the merge is a shorthand for "all three alike", not a different kind of entry. (3) The counts run from 2 to 5 (ring 1: 2 to 4, ring 2: 2 to 5, ring 3: 2 to 5); they are not a function of the ring's number alone (the same ring mewa, for instance ring 1 = 1, gives 2, 3 and 4 in different years), nor of the kinship of the ring's element (the mewa's element as `smeBaElement`) to any one or two of the year's elements (column 4's element, life, body, power or the wind-horse's element of column 8; no function of the kinships fits the 90 cells), nor the number of the year's five elements that equal the ring's element (at most 31 of 90 cells for the best subset, with the ring's own element added or not). So the counts are not derived here; the rule that makes them (the heading's «ཡི་གེས», "by letter") is open. Narrowed to the merge rule and the law of the rings; column 14's meaning stays open.
