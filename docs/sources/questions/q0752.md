# What do columns 12 and 14 of the sixty-years table (WB vol. 1, pp. 105, 107, 109) count? Column 12's words (སེ་ཞིག, ཁ་རལ, ཁ་ཡག, འཆང་ཚོང, འདུག་ཁུར) and column 14's number-words with ལྡན (merged cells in years 2, 16, 25, 28) are not explained on these pages.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-ppp)

Column 12's words are the five «kha yan» year-kinds of the elemental reckoning ('byung rtsis): kha yan, khong nang, se zhig, kha ral, mdun khur, saying how the year's power-element stands to its life-element (same, mother, son, friend); see q726's note. Column 14 is still unexplained. Source: tmz:box9.
