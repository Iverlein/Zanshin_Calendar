# The figure after «ཟླ་བ» before «རི་དབང་༥༧་དབྱུག» in the houses of WB p. 36: «(༡)» or «༡༡»?

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (scan-f).** Located on the Taikhang reproduction (I5877) img. 73 (1996 p. 36), line 4 near its left end: «…གཟའ་ལ་ཟླ་བ་རི་དབང་(༥༧)དབྱུག །ལུག་ཁྱིམ་དོན་དུ་བྱིན་པར་བྱ།». Over «ཟླ་བ» stands one hooked glyph, a single figure, not two (crop x 780–1100, y 800–910 at 6×); the figure over «རི་དབང» is the two-glyph ༥༧. So «(༡)», not «༡༡», is what the block shows, with the caveat that the hook is read as ༡ by its likeness to the ༡ of p. 30 («གཟུགས་༡») and not by a clear reference on this block.

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (scan-h).** Seen again on img. 73 line 4 (crop x 700–1500, y 780–1060 at 2.25×): over «ཟླ་བ» there is a small cluster of three tiny marks about 55 px wide, too small and too heavy to tell a bracket pair from a figure; the single hook is not told apart from ༡ by the references of img. 66 (the ༡ there is a thin hook, x 6663, y 820–860). «(༡)» stays the better reading, not settled.
