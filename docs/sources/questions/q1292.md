# Which scripture is «ལུང་སིལ་བུ» in the Fifth Dalai Lama's «ལུང་སིལ་བུ་དང་མྱང་འདས་སོགས་ལས་གསུངས», and does it or the Nirvāṇa sūtra read «ས་ཡ་ཕྲག་བརྒྱ» or «བྱེ་བ་ཕྲག་བརྒྱ» for the four great festivals?

**Narrowed 2026-11-24 (sweep from BDRC etexts I00EGS1017108, I2142, I3CN8749).** The two numbers are both attested, by different writers; the scripture is not identified. The Fifth Dalai Lama's wording «བྱེ་བ་ཕྲག་བརྒྱ» (bye ba phrag brgya) for the merit of the four great festivals, with «ལུང་སིལ་བུ་དང་མྱང་འདས་སོགས་ལས་གསུངས», is repeated in Ye shes rgya mtsho's life of Śākyamuni (BDRC etext I00EGS1017108, p. 174-175); the verses attributed to Atiśa (skl:i210), to Paṇchen Śākyaśrī (ckg.13:p19) and in the reckoners' manuals read «ས་ཡ་ཕྲག་བརྒྱ» (sa ya phrag brgya) «from the Myang 'das and the Sil bu». Ngag dbang chos kyi grags pa's Abhisamayālaṅkāra commentary quotes the same lines (sa ya) and gives the next verses (the sun and the moon) as from a «Dus bzang dge ba'i bsgyur khyad bstan pa», not from the Sil bu. No text read says what «ལུང་སིལ་བུ» (lung sil bu) is, or gives the scripture's own number.
