# The 簠簋内伝's §6–§9: added by the print, or cut by the short copies

The 簠簋内伝's copies of 1578 and the 寛永 years have neither 金神遊行 list and no 異説, and the 1632 print has both; are §6–§9 an addition of the printed text, or did the short copies cut them? A copy older than 1578 would decide.

RD172 (2026-11-06), narrowed: the 慶長16 (1611) copy is a third short copy, with no lists and no 異説; no copy older than 1578 found.


Narrowed (inv-sss, 2026-11-14): NDL Search (title 簠簋内傳, any 金烏玉兎集, 100 hits) lists no copy older than the 1578 (天正6) manuscript. Its records give a difference of make: the 1578 copy (3 vols., pids 2544991-2544993) is in 3 巻, the 1611 manuscript, the 1629 (寛永6), 1632 (寛永9), 1647, 1667, 1682 and 1710 prints in 5 巻. So the short copies are not a 5巻 text with a section cut out: the 1578 is a shorter three-volume form, and the later five-volume form (also the 1611 manuscript, which has no lists) is not tied to the lists by its volume count. Scholarship on the layers (馬場真理子 on 兵法書 and 簠簋内伝, 山下克明 2025 book) was found by title only and not read. Whether §6-§9 were added or cut stays undecided.
