# The 簠簋内伝's §6–§9: added by the print, or cut by the short copies

The 簠簋内伝's copies of 1578 and the 寛永 years have neither 金神遊行 list and no 異説, and the 1632 print has both; are §6–§9 an addition of the printed text, or did the short copies cut them? A copy older than 1578 would decide.

RD172 (2026-11-06), narrowed: the 慶長16 (1611) copy is a third short copy, with no lists and no 異説; no copy older than 1578 found.
