# The year «དོ་གཅིག» of the blue mewa, p. 164

On p. 164, what is the year «དོ་གཅིག» in the blue mewa's "in the year of «དོ་གཅིག» my share falls on you"?

RD165 (2026-11-06), narrowed: «དོ་གཅིག» occurs in four other places of WB (vol. 1 pp. 317, 390; vol. 2 p. 89; Mipham's note on p. 480): what a count of year and trigram lands on, beside the mother's. No passage defines it (q653).

## Answered 2026-11-14

Answered with q653 (2026-11-14), which this item repeats. The *do gcig* (དོ་གཅིག) of the blue mewa's «དོ་གཅིག་ལོ་ལ…» is glossed once, in Mipham's note on the birth reckoning's results (wb.2:p480, «སྐྱེས་སྤར་སྟེང་བབ་སྤར་ཕྱིན་པ»): the year on which the descended trigram has fallen on the born trigram and been reached, the second kind of result of the four planets beside the roof-cover. No passage of the trigram reckonings (vol. 1 pp. 317, 390; vol. 2 pp. 89, 164) defines it, and BDRC's phrase searches «དོ་གཅིག་ནི», «དོ་གཅིག་ཅེས་པ» find no definition. Lexical lead only: Steinert's dictionaries have «do cig» (the Tshig mdzod chen mo «thengs cig», one time; the Brda dkrol gser gyi me long «da lo'am do zhag», this year or today), which would make the spelling «do gcig» the present time. Claim do-gcig-glossed-once-in-the-notes. What remains unsaid (whether it is the dead's descended trigram in the year of death) is q385.
