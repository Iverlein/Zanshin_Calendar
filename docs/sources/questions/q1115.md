# In the preamble of Marpa's song is the speaker Marpa or Pen ta pa, and who is Pen ta pa

The Life says Lama Pe dha ba (Pen ta pa) offered as realisation the dream Marpa had had; who sings, and who is Pen ta pa?

## Answered 2026-11-11

RD402: the speaker is Marpa; Pen ta pa (Pe dha ba, Paṇḍa pa) is addressed and leads the fellow students; in the Life he is a lama, Marpa hears from him in the Rin chen tshul temple in Nepal and takes him as companion toward India (rrd.23:p292, rrd.23:p262); no dates, birthplace or lineage.
