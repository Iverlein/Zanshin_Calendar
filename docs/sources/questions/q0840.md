# What are «རམ་རུག» (ram rug) and «སྦང» (sbang) in the Lama Gongdü's goods of gossip, and is the dream phrase one three-word name or two items?

Palri, Kazi, the Shechen etext and the Zhe chen hand give «རམ་རུག» with «སྤང་གཡེང»/«སྤང་གཡེར»/«སྦང་གཡེར»; in a second text of the same volume the goods of gossip in a speckled woollen bag include «ཞབས་རམ་རུག» and «སྦང་མ».


Narrowed (RD271, 2026-11-09): the «རམ་རུག་སྦང་གཡེར» is a short list of goods of a rite (མི་ཁའི་རྫས, mi kha'i rdzas, the substances of a rite against gossip), not a three-word name or a place or persons: «རམ་རུག» holds the slot of the plant ram bu or ram pa, «སྦང» is སྦང་མ (the beer's mash) and «གཡེར» is གཡེར་མ (the Sichuan pepper), each written short; Kongtrul's treasury's lists name «རམ་བུ་སྦང་མ་གཡེར་མ» together as goods of gossip. What ram rug is exactly (the plant, by a spelling by ear) stays open; the spelling «རམ་ཏུག»/«རམ་བྲུག» occurs. Claims lgd-mi-kha-goods-of-gossip-are-rite-substances, lgd-ram-rug-is-the-ram-bu-spelling, lgd-sbang-g-yer-beer-mash-and-pepper.
