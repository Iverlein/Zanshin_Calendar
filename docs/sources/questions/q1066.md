# In the Hevajra rite of Marpa's collected works the dream verses (the donkey, monkey, ant-hill, the black girl «Time-Night», the months) stand as the bad signs of the empowerment's dream: are they the Hevajra Tantra's own words, the Sdom 'byung's, or the rite's compiler's, and what is its author's name?

RD374.

## Answered 2026-11-10

RD382: the verses of Marpa's Hevajra rite (dds.19:p53-55) are the verse block of the Deceiving Death ch. 1 with spellings, two dropped lines and a different opening, not the Hevajra Tantra's (Toh 417 and 418 have no death-dream verse) nor the Sdom 'byung's; the rite names no source (begun at Lho brag Chu khyer, found among Nāropa's pupils; a second note names «the venerable Rngog»).
