# KD vol. 1 p. 493: the Dpal ldan effect reckoning's skipped date «༢ ༡ཆད»: the 21st, or the 2nd and the 1st?

In the Lhasa almanac of a wood pig year, the 3rd month head, the Dpal ldan effect reckoning's skipped date is printed «༢ ༡ཆད» with a space (KD vol. 1 p. 493, etext p. 509). Only the lent scan (bdrc-W28845, leaf = printed page + 16) can show whether it is one number, 21, or two, the 2nd and the 1st.

## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-k)

Not settled; needs the lent scan. The year's month head prints the Phugpa skip and double as «༦ལྷག» and «༡༤ཚད» with no space between numeral and word, and the Dpal ldan skip as «༢ ༡ཆད» with one: so the etext's spacing is not the same practice as the Phugpa pair's and decides nothing. No other KD page printing a Dpal ldan skip was found (kb.py search «དཔལ་ལྡན་བྱེད་པ» in the KD corpus only this month head). Open.
