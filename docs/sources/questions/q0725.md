# The code-numbers of ch. 11 pp. 87–90 digit by digit

ch. 11 (pp. 87–90) rules are given in code-numbers in brackets (6270, 6900, 209, 440, 255, 317, 52, 220, 473, 484, 289, 3611, 583 and others) that the machine reads without their brackets; they are to be read digit by digit on the scan before any is used. (RD191, corpus/wb/ch12.md)
