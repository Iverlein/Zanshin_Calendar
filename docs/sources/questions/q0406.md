# «སྐྱས», «སྦ་ཕྱར», «ཤྭ་རགས» and «སེན» in ch. 34's list of works

What work does each of «སྐྱས», «སྦ་ཕྱར», «ཤྭ་རགས» and «སེན» name in the list of works (24, 17, 49, 50)? white-beryl-ch34.md reads 49 as dikes.

## Answered 2026-10-28

Works 24, 49 and 50: 24 is gifts (q430), 49 «ཤྭ་རགས» stays dikes (the 1996 etext's «ཤུ་རགས» is an etext error), 50 reads «བཟོ་སྐྲ/སྒྲ་སེན» with the head letter not shown, hair and nails in sense. Work 17 is most likely «སྦྲ་ཕྱར», tents and what flutters, the later verses and the Bhutan digest spelling «སྦྲ»; «ཕྱར» and «ཕྱ་ཐེར» are glossed nowhere read (new question). (RD66)
