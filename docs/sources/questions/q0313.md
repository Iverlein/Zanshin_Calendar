# The old one sri'u by the mewa, p. 80

The lines on the old one *sri'u* by the mewa (p. 80, ll. 3–11: «བཀྲ་སྲིའུ་དབུས་ནག་དང་། གཅིག་དཀར་བཞི་ལྗང་བབས་པའི་དུས། …») and on the old one's body that died black, yellow or red: what do "the centre black" and the power refer to, and where is the corpse laid?

**Narrowed 2026-11-05 (RD164, [reckonings.md](../reckonings.md)).** «དབུས་ནག» stands on the reprint and the Zhol, so no misprint. Mipham glosses the following lines' colours as the body mewa (p. 504).

**Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-d, [claims/bkra-sriu-mewa-corpse-way-other-manuals.md](../claims/bkra-sriu-mewa-corpse-way-other-manuals.md)).** BDRC phrase search «བཀྲ་སྲིའུ» 10 hits: besides WB's etexts, the Bon *Nag rtsis snang srid me long* (MW1KG13780), Si tu's *Bum bzang* (MW30023, MW1GS88389), the Gyalrong collection (MW1KG16672) and *Sgo bzhi mdzod lnga'i yang bcud* give a table of the corpse's way by the mewa: «གཅིག་དཀར་སྟེང་དུ་བཀྲ་སྲིའུ་སྤང་། གཉིས་ནག་ཀུན་ནས་སྤང་བར་བྱ། … བཞི་ལྗང་སྟེང་དུ་མཛེ་རོ་སྤང་», from «the *Clear Lamp*, practice of the Wise» (the Bon text names it). So the «one white» and «four green» of WB's line are mewa of that table, and «dbus» the centre in which a mewa is laid for the corpse's paces; that «dbus nag» is the two black is a reading, not stated. Where the corpse is laid is WB's own «the part from waist to limbs not sent, a place outside the house on the pleasant side, and the edge not dug».
