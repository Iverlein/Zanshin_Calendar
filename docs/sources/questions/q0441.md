# Why is a tent drawn at the door's right against the black times (p. 236)

On p. 236 the black times' remedy draws a tent («སྤྲ་ཁྱིམ»/«སྦྲ་ཁྱིམ») at the door's right, with the swastika and the sun and moon at the left: why a tent, and is it a picture of a tent or of the herdsman's black tent in particular? Follows q405's answer (RD65).

RD182 (2026-11-06), dead end with one lead: p. 236 on the scan (Gser thang img. 242) agrees with the etext; the line «བཅོས་ཐབས་སྦྲ་ཁྱིམ་སྒོ་གཡས་སུ། གཡུང་དྲུང་གཡོན་དུ་ཉི་ཟླ་བྲིས། དེ་ཡིས་ཐུབ་པར་བཤད་པ་ཡིན» gives no reason; lead: in the keg verse at vol. 1 p. 406 the tent is a place of death («བྱི་ཕག་བུ་འཆི་སྦྲ་ཁྱིམ་ལ»).
