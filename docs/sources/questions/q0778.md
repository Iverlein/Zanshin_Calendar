**Narrowed 2026-11-24 (sweep from inv-j).** Narrowed on 2026-11-24 with the 1996 etext (I1KG12906, etext page 50) and the page's other stacks; not answered.
- The etext leaves the bracket empty («གཉིས་གཉིས( )ཏེ»), the Gser thang etext has «(༢༣)»; neither is a reading of four glyphs in two rows.
- The way stacks behave on this page, checked on others that the etext types: a stack is the preceding word-numeral run broken into rows, the digits of the rows read in order being the digits of the run read last word first (p. 40 «མཚམས(༦༧ ༡༤༤༠༥)» = 6714405 against «ཡིད་རི་མཚམས»; the same on p. 43 and in ch. 17, e.g. «ཀླུ་མིག་ཀླུ་ལག(༢ ༨༢ ༨)» = 2828, «བླ་མ་སོ་མེ་རྒྱ་མཚོ(༤༣ ༣༢)» = 4332, «ཕྱོགས(༡༠ ༧༦༦)» = 10766). Four glyphs in two rows therefore hold a run of four digits: «ལག་མིག་གཉིས་གཉིས» alone spells 2222, i.e. ༢༢ over ༢༢, with «རྒྱལ་བ» (24) standing before as its own item (as «རྒྱལ་བ(༢༤)» does in the lines of p. 39). The words as a whole (222224) cannot be in four glyphs.
- Against 22 over 22: the glyphs were read by RD205s as the print's ༡ with its tail, not its ༢ (open hook, as in «ཟུང(༢)»). The hooked ༣ is read like ༡ on this print (p. 43, RD205w) but a ༡ for ༢ was not met elsewhere. So the sense is likely 2222 as 22 over 22, the glyphs are unresolved.
