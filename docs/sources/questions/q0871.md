# The Penn, Srinagar and Ramtek manuscripts of Bṛhaspati's Svapnādhyāya give the fourth watch a month (māsaikena), the Brahmavaivarta and the Svapnohana half a month, the Odia print a month and a half glossed fifteen days: which reading is older, and could ardhamāsena be a correction of māsena?

Penn 1871.

## Answered 2026-11-10

RD357: for the Penn copies (Items 1871, 2730, 687, 673) the fourth watch gives a month; the Brahmavaivarta has the half-month in both prints read (Jivananda 1888, Banaras 1935). Which is older is not decided: nothing read is older than the 18th century and the only dated copy is 1824 (Item 673, saṃvat 1881); the marginal repair in Item 687 restores the month, a weak hint only.
