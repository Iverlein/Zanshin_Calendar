# The sky-bird's parts and «སོ་གཉིས་བར་དུ»

WB vol. 2, p. 29 (img. 37): what are the sky-bird's parts «བྱ་མ», *khrums*, *shol po*, *smyung mo*, *she mo*, and is «སོ་གཉིས་བར་དུ» the 32nd day or 32 days?

## Read further 2026-11-01

Answered in part in RD99: the bird's days are forty (mother bird 6, wing corner 6, shoulder 3, neck 7, face 3, wing tip 5, chick 10), then khrums 12 days; «སོ་གཉིས་བར་དུ» the 32nd day of the count; shol po and smyung mo in no dictionary (claims/ch28-bird-days-forty.md).
