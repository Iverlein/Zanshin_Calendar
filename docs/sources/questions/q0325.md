# The sky-bird's parts and «སོ་གཉིས་བར་དུ»

WB vol. 2, p. 29 (img. 37): what are the sky-bird's parts «བྱ་མ», *khrums*, *shol po*, *smyung mo*, *she mo*, and is «སོ་གཉིས་བར་དུ» the 32nd day or 32 days?

## Read further 2026-11-01

Answered in part in RD99: the bird's days are forty (mother bird 6, wing corner 6, shoulder 3, neck 7, face 3, wing tip 5, chick 10), then khrums 12 days; «སོ་གཉིས་བར་དུ» the 32nd day of the count; shol po and smyung mo in no dictionary (claims/ch28-bird-days-forty.md).

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-mmm).** *Shol po* is a headword after all: «ཤིང་གི་བྱེ་བྲག་ཅིག་སྟེ་ཤོལ་པོའང་ཟེར» under *sbyar pa* (*Tshig mdzod chen mo*, *Bod yig tshig gter rgya mtsho*), "a kind of tree, also called shol po"; Valby "species of willow", Ives Waldo "sbyar pa / shol po tree". As a tree name it gives no span of days for the bird's count, so how WB's «ཤོལ་པོ» is a period (half a month) remains unexplained; the claim's "in no dictionary" is corrected (claims/ch28-bird-days-forty.md). *She mo*: «བྱ་འོལ་བ་སླེབས་པའི་དུས», the time the *'ol ba* bird arrives (as the claim has). *Smyung mo*: still in no dictionary.
