# The sky-bird's parts and «སོ་གཉིས་བར་དུ»

WB vol. 2, p. 29 (img. 37): what are the sky-bird's parts «བྱ་མ», *khrums*, *shol po*, *smyung mo*, *she mo*, and is «སོ་གཉིས་བར་དུ» the 32nd day or 32 days?

## Read further 2026-11-01

Answered in part in RD99: the bird's days are forty (mother bird 6, wing corner 6, shoulder 3, neck 7, face 3, wing tip 5, chick 10), then khrums 12 days; «སོ་གཉིས་བར་དུ» the 32nd day of the count; shol po and smyung mo in no dictionary (claims/ch28-bird-days-forty.md).

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-mmm).** *Shol po* is a headword after all: «ཤིང་གི་བྱེ་བྲག་ཅིག་སྟེ་ཤོལ་པོའང་ཟེར» under *sbyar pa* (*Tshig mdzod chen mo*, *Bod yig tshig gter rgya mtsho*), "a kind of tree, also called shol po"; Valby "species of willow", Ives Waldo "sbyar pa / shol po tree". As a tree name it gives no span of days for the bird's count, so how WB's «ཤོལ་པོ» is a period (half a month) remains unexplained; the claim's "in no dictionary" is corrected (claims/ch28-bird-days-forty.md). *She mo*: «བྱ་འོལ་བ་སླེབས་པའི་དུས», the time the *'ol ba* bird arrives (as the claim has). *Smyung mo*: still in no dictionary.

**Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-h).** A modern published text confirms the sums of the bird's days from the other side: Ngag dbang bstan 'phel's chapter on weather omens in the *Bod kyi rtsis rig kun 'dus chen mo* vol. 2 (MW28845, etext I4716, printed pp. 381–382) quotes the "Lho Mon bird-days reckoning" (ལྷོ་མོན་གྱི་བྱ་ཞག་རྩིས): «དགུན་ཉི་ལྡོག་ནས་ཞག་ཉི་ཤུ་རྩ་བཞི་སོང་རྗེས་བྱ་མ་ཞག་དྲུག་ཟིན་རིང་…» (after twenty-four days from the winter solstice, as long as the mother bird's six days) and «ཞག་བཞི་དགུ་སོང་རྗེས་གཤོག་རྩེ་ཞག་ལྔ་ཟིན་རིང་…» (after forty-nine days, the wing tip's five days): 24 + 6 + 6 + 3 + 7 + 3 = 49, the sum the claim's parts give, and the wing tip is spelt «གཤོག་རྩེ» as WB has it. The dictionaries' «ཁྲུམས» is the pair of mansions Pūrva- and Uttara-Bhādrapadā (*Tshig mdzod chen mo*; Negi: *bhādrapadāḥ*), also the 12 days after the bird days («བྱ་ཞག་བཞི་བཅུ་རྫོགས་རྗེས་སུ་ཁྲུམས་ཞག་བཅུ་གཉིས», a Tibetan monolingual dictionary in BDRC's etext VE1ALD50934_0001, entry «ཁྲུམས»). *Shol po* and *smyung mo* are named in no published bird-days text found: BDRC phrase search «ཤོལ་པོ་ཞག» 0, «སྨྱུང་མོ་ཞག» 0, «སྨྱུང་མོ་ཞག་དགུ» 0, «ཤོལ་པོ་ཟླ་བ་ཕྱེད» 6 (WB's copies, the Do shal, a history of Tibetan astronomy in the Kun 'dus, a Sde tshan volume), «ཤེ་མོ་ཞག་བདུན» 6 (the Kun 'dus, the Sman rtsis khang almanac, *Bod rgya skar rtsis rig pa'i tshig mdzod*, the Tibetan–Chinese astronomical dictionary: only she mo). The first half of the question (the parts' sums) is settled; shol po as a span and smyung mo stay open.
