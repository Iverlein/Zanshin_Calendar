# The Spor thang sheet of seven members: fold, leftovers and colours

Kun dga' rig 'dzin's worked household round of seven members from the *Spor thang* (kd.3:p726, seen in the etext only; KD vol. 3 lent scans, leaf 745) keeps a mewa row matching the zigzag rule ([household-round-zigzag](../claims/household-round-zigzag.md)). On the scan: does it fold by the notes' halves or by the manuals' quarter-runs, which of its 35 pebbles does it leave over as the fold's and the refold's (35 by four leaves three, by three two), and does any member bring a speckled pebble?

**In part 2026-10-25 (RD54, [claims/kd3-p726-household-round-not-read.md](../claims/kd3-p726-household-round-not-read.md)), KD vol. 3 leaf 745 at 3×.** The lower table has four rows of eight numbered cells, 1–8, 35 down to 28, 20–27, 16 down to 9: quarter-runs of eight, not halves; 17, 18 and 19 are not in it, so three are over (35 = 4×8 + 3). No member brings a speckled pebble: the drawn pebbles are white (1, 2 or a cluster of 3) and black (1 or 2), or one white with one black; the colour words belong to the fallen mewas. Not read: which of the three is the leftover, what «ཕྲེང་འདི་དག་དོར་བར་བྱ» casts away, the upper table's two-digit numbers and the seven members' sign names (1002 px is too small).
