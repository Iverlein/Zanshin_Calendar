# Does the Dbyangs 'char verse, the Gso rig gces btus piece and WB vol. 2 p. 403's «ཆུ་སྲང་བཞི་རེ» use the same 56¼-chu-srang unit as the Mchog dga'?

RD338.

## Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-k)

The 56¼ is Kālacakra's own measure, and Mipham states it: in the *'Grel chung zung 'jug nor bu'i sgron me* (etext p. 174, wb.2:p403d) the sixty-four dbyug gu of the day-and-night are 3600 chu srang divided by sixty-four, 56 chu srang with a quarter left to each, «ཆུ་སྲང་ང་དྲུག་རྐང་བཅས་ལ་ཆུ་ཚོད་དུ་བརྩི་བ་འདི་ཡིན་ལ། ... དུས་འཁོར་ལས་དེ་ལྟར་བཞེད་དོ», a chu tshod of 56¼ chu srang that the Kālacakra holds «at times», so that the sixty of it fall short of the common sixty. BDRC exact phrase «ཆུ་སྲང་ང་དྲུག་རྐང་བཅས» 13 hits (Karma pa gsum pa, the Mchog dga', two Mipham volumes, others not read). This does not say that the Dbyangs 'char verse, the Gso rig gces btus piece or p. 403's «ཆུ་སྲང་བཞི་རེ» (the other time's four chu srang each, wb.2:p403c) use it; «ཆུ་སྲང་བཞི་རེ» 7 hits in BDRC, none read. Open: no text found that ties p. 403's four chu srang to the 56¼ unit.

## Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-z5, arithmetic)

The unit of Mipham's text (3600 chu srang divided by 64 = 56¼) and the lengths the other passages use agree on one arithmetic. The day and night are 3600 chu srang; the sixteen half-watches (the eight great watches halved; Sgang dril and Mipham, wb.2:p403d) are 3600 / 16 = 225 chu srang each, which is exactly four of the 56¼ unit (4 x 56¼ = 225) and 3¾ chu tshod of sixty chu srang; and the Kun 'dus chen mo's «four chu tshod to a half-watch» for the Dbyangs 'char (wb.2:p403c, q968) is the rounding of that 3¾ to four. A «ཆུ་སྲང་བཞི་རེ» read as four chu srang of sixty to the chu tshod does not divide the half-watch (225 / 4 = 56¼, not whole) but divides the chu tshod fifteen times (60 / 4); read as four of the 56¼ unit it is the half-watch exactly and sixteen of them fill the day. WB's own line (p. 403: «four chu srang each, the fierce Rigs ldan arises», of the other time, six day rounds and five night rounds) does not say which unit; the arithmetic shows only that "four" of the 56¼ unit is the half-watch of the Dbyangs 'char and the Sgang dril, so that reading would make WB's line the same unit, which is an inference. Open on the line's own unit.
