# Is «gnam gang» in the BDRC snippet of MW16936 (a commentary in Blo bzang bshad sgrub rgya mtsho's works, glossing the stong day) a spelling of «nam gang» or «nam gong»? A look at its scan would settle it.

Raised by the 2026-11-13 investigation g.

## Answered 2026-11-14

The spelling is «གནམ་གང» (gnam gang), a fixed pair with «ཉེར་དགུ» (nyer dgu), the 29th. The scan of MW16936 was not available (lent on archive.org), so the answer rests on BDRC's etexts. BDRC's exact-phrase search (2026-11-14): «ཉེར་དགུའམ་གནམ་གང» 12 hits, «ཉེར་དགུ་དང་གནམ་གང» 18 hits (among them a Drukpa and a Kagyu monastery rule, a Vinaya summer-retreat ruling «drug pa rjes ma'i nyer dgu'am gnam gang yod na gnam gang la…» and a ritual calendar «nyer dgu dang gnam gang gi nyin yang…»); the alternatives «ཉེར་དགུའམ་ནམ་གང», «ཉེར་དགུའམ་ནམ་གོང» and «ཉེར་དགུའམ་གནམ་གོང» 0 hits. So «gnam gang» is the established word, not a misreading of «nam gang» or «nam gong»; in those contexts it is the alternative to the 29th as the last day of the month (the 30th), which fits the gloss of «stong» in the commentary of q667 as the 29th or the 30th.
