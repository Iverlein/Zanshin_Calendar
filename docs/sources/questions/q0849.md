# Does the Four Tantras' own text carry «mtshan stod rmi lam brjed nges 'bras bu med; tho rangs mthong dang gsal na 'bras bu 'byin», in which chapter, and does a Tibetan commentary read «mtshan stod» as the first part of the night?

By search the two lines are in Gso rig 'bum bzhi, Bdud rtsi'i chu rgyun and other Four Tantras etexts.

## Answered 2026-11-10

Answered by RD288: the two lines stand in ch. 7 on every print read, between the six kinds of dream and the good dreams.
