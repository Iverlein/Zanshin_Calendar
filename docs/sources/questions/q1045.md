# The 国文研 print (bid 200013909) and the Ebara volume bind the 夢人に咄さぬ日 table with a colophon that names a Kyoto bookseller; is the street line 京綾小路ふやゝ町西入 or 京松原通冨小路西入 (the cursive of the three glyphs after 綾小路 or 松原通)?

RD371.

## Answered 2026-11-13

Answered. Read again on image 9 of the 国文研 print (bid 200013909, 5616 px, crop of the lower left leaf, 2026-11-13): the bookseller's line is 京綾小路ふや丁西入, then 墨屋吉兵衛板, that is Kyoto, Ayanokoji, Fuya-cho (麩屋町), west of the street. Neither candidate stands there: the glyphs after 綾小路 are ふ, や, 丁, and 松原通冨小路 does not occur. The 京松原通冨小路東入 ...屋吉右衛門 of the Koizumi 雑書夢合 / 三世相小鑑 (mdz:i234427b) is another bookseller with another street line, so the two prints do not share a colophon. Reading as in mdz:i54254.
