# Which Vairocana-specific preparation text (the Vairocanābhisambodhi or its commentaries) names signs that a teacher reads in the pupils' dawn dreams?

RD360.

## Answered 2026-11-13

The tantra itself, in the Derge Kanjur (Toh 494): the pupils are told to repeat their dreams to the teacher at dawn after the tooth-sticks, with lists of good and bad dreams (vab:p329-330), and good or bad dreams are told to the lama to examine one's share (vab:p494-495).
