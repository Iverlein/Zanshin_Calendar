# In the Deceiving Death the verse of the black girl «bound» (bu mo nag mo rab khros nas / gang zhig bcings pas 'chi ba ni / de ni dus mtshan yin zhes bya / zhag bdun nas 'chi byed yin) has no match in the Sanskrit of the Saṃvarodaya 19; is there a Sanskrit of the Deceiving Death 1 (Schneider's edition, Vienna 2010) with this verse, and does it use kālarātri or another word for «dus mtshan» there?

RD382.

## Answered 2026-11-14

Yes. The Sanskrit of 'Deceiving Death' chapter 1 has the verse, in Schneider's edition (Vienna 2010, open access on the publisher's site), as strophe 127: «kālī kumārī saṃkruddhā yā tu badhnāti bandhanaiḥ / kālarātrī tu sā jñeyā saptāhān mṛtyukārikā». «Dus mtshan» is kālarātrī there, as in the Saṃvarodaya 19.23 (the claim mvup-sanskrit-parallels-sut19); «zhag bdun» is saptāha. Schneider notes the Saṃvarodaya's parallel for the third line and takes kālarātrī as the original reading there too.
