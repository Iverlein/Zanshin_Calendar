# In the Yu thog instruction are the kings, planets and nāgas classes of gdon tamed by Hayagrīva, Vajrapāṇi and Nāgarakṣa, and is the dream of spirits the dream where any shows as an animal

Are the rgyal po, gza' and nāgas the classes of gdon tamed by Hayagrīva, Vajrapāṇi and Nāgarakṣa, and is gdon gyi rmi lam the dream in which any of them shows as an animal?

## Answered 2026-11-14

Yes to the first half, not to the second, as far as the text goes (etext, 2026-11-14). The Yu thog sentence is «to tame spirits (གདོན་འདུལ་བ་ལ): for the kings Hayagrīva, for the planets Vajrapāṇi, for the nāgas Nāgarakṣa and the like, one meditates each one's own antidote and subdues them» (dds.47:p293-295): kings, planets and nāgas are the beings named under the taming of gdon, each with its antidote deity. The next sentence puts the animal chain (dog by wolf, wolf by tiger, tiger by lion) «in the dream of spirits too» and for «whichever other animal one sees»; so the dream of spirits is a dream in which animals are met and subdued. That the king, planet or nāga shows as the animal is not said, and no commentary on the Yu thog text was found (the same text in a second BDRC copy, the medical cycle's collected rituals, I4PD2077, has the same pages and no gloss). The animal reading stays with q1148. See claims/dds-yu-thog-gdon-classes-have-antidotes-and-the-animal-chain-is-in-the-dream-of-spirits.md (RD403).
