# On the 9th month head of the model almanac (WB vol. 1 p. 170, 1996 print img. 180), at the end of the dated avoidances the print has, before «west» (nub), a five and a syllable that looks like lha at 6x, in the order five, lha, west; the machine wrote lha, five, west. Is the syllable a stray of the printing or a word?

## Read again 2026-11-21 (rd-w)

Not advanced: the p. 170 leaf was not looked at in this session (time went to the code-number and table questions); a stray or a word is not decidable by number-words or arithmetic. Still open for a crop at 6x of the line end on the reprint (img. 180 of the 1996 print) beside the Zhol and Taikhang leaves.

## Answered 2026-11-21

The syllable is a word, and the order is «ལྷ་ལྔ་ནུབ» (lha lnga nub), not five, lha, west: the full form «lha lnga» (the five gods), as in the 11th head.

Checked 2026-11-21 (rd-z) by setting the print's glyphs beside a known reading, not by the blot alone. On the 1996 print (I1KG12906 img. 180, p. 170) the three syllables after «ནོར་ཕྱུགས་ཕྱིར་མི་གཏོང་།» were cut at 12x (line 5, x 325-385); the same three syllables in the 11th head, «ལྷ་ལྔ་ཤར།» on img. 164 (p. 154, line 7), cut at 12x, are the same two shapes in the same order: the first glyph with the hooked head and the descending tail of the subjoined ha, the second the squarer «ལྔ», then «ནུབ» in the 9th and «ཤར» in the 11th. The 11th head's reading is not the eye's alone: on the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5028 img. 161), whose print is bold and clean, the line reads «ལྷ་ལྔ་ཤར། དགུ་ནུབ།», and the Gser thang etext of the same head has it too; the 1996 etext of the 9th head (I1KG12906 [p180]) reads «ལྷ་ལྔ་ནུབ། དགུ་ཤར།» in the same order, an OCR independent of the eye. RD2's table already has the full form on exactly these two heads (img. 164 and 180), the other ten writing «ལྔ». So «ལྷ» is not a stray of the printing: it is the word of the name «ལྷ་ལྔ» that two of the twelve heads print in full. The first reading's «five, lha» came from taking the tail-hooked first glyph for a ལྔ; the passage wb.1:p170b is set right.
