# «བཙས་པ་སྲོག་རླུང» in question 134: the life-wind or the life force and wind-horse

In question 134 of the *Two Hundred and Eight Requests* (ys:i099, I5875 img. 99), is «བཙས་པ་སྲོག་རླུང» the birth's life-wind (སྲོག་རླུང, srog rlung), one word, or the birth's life force and wind-horse (srog, rlung rta), which would agree with the notes' gloss of the kind of person as the birth luck (བཙས་ཀླུང, btsas klung; wb.2:p497)?

**Narrowed 2026-11-01 (RD104, [claims/life-wind-srog-rlung-and-the-clan-stopgap.md](../claims/life-wind-srog-rlung-and-the-clan-stopgap.md)).** One word, the life-wind: the reply ends on «སྲོག་རླུང་ལ་བརྗོད་པ་ལས», "beyond what is said of the life-wind"; the question's next clause speaks of the parents' «རླུང་རྒྱུ» (wind-course); question 106 (ys:i092, machine) sets «སྲོག་རླུང» beside year, month, day and hour as the fifth factor; and the questioner writes the birth's luck elsewhere as «བཙས་ཀླུངས» (question 114, ys:i094b), a different word. So not the life force with the wind-horse; whether it also stands for the notes' «བཙས་ཀླུང» (wb.2:p497) is not said.
