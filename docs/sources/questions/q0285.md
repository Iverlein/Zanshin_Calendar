# The months of the day's-animal entries

The day's-animal entries (WB vol. 2, p. 63) give six days only (ox; tiger → bird month; snake → monkey; horse → bird; sheep → sheep; monkey → dog). Is the month named the month in which the la or the life was cut before the death, as in the hour entries' «བླ་བཅད»? Is there a rule behind these months?

**Narrowed 2026-11-03 (RD136, [claims/dead-day-animal-months-two-schemes.md](../claims/dead-day-animal-months-two-schemes.md)).** The first question is answered by the page: three entries say it in words («སྲོག་བཅད», «ཚེ་སྲོག་བཅད», «བཅད»), the month is the one in which the life (not the la, as the hour entries) was cut. No rule behind the months was found: they are not the opposite animal's month (as in the second examination's reason by year, month, day and hour, p. 57–58), they follow no count, and the hour entries' months (p. 66–68) agree with them only for the monkey (dog month) and the sheep. A dead end unless a commentary on the third examination is found.
