# The doors of sky and earth and the black days, p. 42

WB vol. 2, p. 42 (img. 50): which trigrams are the sky door's «སྤར་ཁ་མ། །ཁེན་ཐོག» and the earth door's «ཁོན་དང་བུ»; are the pig and snake years «ས་ཞིག» or «སེ་ཞིག»; which days are the black days «ཉི་ནག་དྲུག་གསུམ»?

## Read further 2026-11-01

Narrowed in RD99: trigrams deduced from the mewa (sky: khen and thunder; earth: khon and the wind trigram); «སེ་ཞིག» is the dictionary's term; the days are the great black day and the 6th, 16th, 26th (claims/ch28-doors-by-mewa.md).
