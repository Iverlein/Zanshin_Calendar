# Does the line-4 sgab of WB p. 104 (Taikhang img. 160) carry a subjoined ra, so that the word is sgrab

From RD180 (2026-11-21), on q561: the tail of the stack fits both at 2.5x on the Taikhang line 3.

## Answered 2026-11-24

No ra: the word is «སྒབ» (sgab). In the four WB etexts (1996 and Gser thang, both volumes) «སྒབ» stands 182 times and «སྒྲབ» (also «བསྒྲབ») not once; the line itself reads «ས་དཔྱད་བཅུ་གསུམ་ཤི་འགྲམས་སྒབ། །བཅད་པར་བྱ་བའི་ཐབས་གནད་དག» in the 1996 etext (vol. 2). So the tail of the stack fits ས over ག, and a subjoined ra has no usage. Settles q561's «སྒབ» as to the ra; its ག against ཀ is not tested by this count.
