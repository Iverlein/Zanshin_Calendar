# Do the Kālacakra seed letters on the joints and the Phugpa Khadbon list of p. 303–304 agree date by date

P. 303–304 give the Kālacakra seed letters on the joints for the dates 1–30 and the Phugpa Khadbon list after the Precious Garland; the machine reading of the sites is clean, but the two lists have not been set against each other date by date, nor against the sites in ch. 21 of WB or in Kālacakra's own text; do they agree?

**Narrowed 2026-11-05 (RD157, wb.2:p303-304).** The list is the Phug «ཁུ་དབོན», uncle and nephew, not «ཁ་དབོན», a machine error. Letters and places are now the print's; the vowel signs of dates 1, 3, 5, 9, 22, 26 and 28 stay in doubt. Ch. 21 and Kālacakra's own text were not compared.
