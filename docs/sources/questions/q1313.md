# WB vol. 2 p. 380: the bracketed numbers 0–9 after the mansions of the life-measure verses, what do they count?

In the life-measure verses of WB vol. 2 p. 380 the print puts a number in brackets after each mansion, running 0 to 9 from Dbyugpa (དབྱུག་པ). Does the verse number the mansions from Dbyugpa as 0, so that Bra nye (བྲ་ཉེར) is 1 and Snarma (སྣར་མ) 3, or is the series of the print's brackets another count? The Dbyugpa «(༠)» and Snarma «(༣)» glyphs were read on the reprint, the latter not beyond doubt.

## Answered 2026-11-21

Yes: the brackets of p. 380 count the mansions of this verse series from Dbyugpa (དབྱུག་པ) as 0, and the count goes on to 27, so the series has twenty-eight mansions. The pool's own passages show it twice over.

On p. 380 the ten brackets read on the reprint (wb.2:p378-384, the fifteenth pass) run «དབྱུག་པ(༠)», «བྲ་ཉེར(༡)», «སྨིན་དྲུག(༢)», «སྣར་མ(༣)», «མགོ(༤)», «ལག(༥)», «རྒྱལ(༦)སྟོད», «རྒྱལ(༧)སྨད», «ལ(༨)», «རྟ་ཆེན(༩)», and the same passage's pp. 381-382 carry on «རྟ་ཆུང(10)», «ཁྲ(11)», «བྱ་མ(12)», «བྱེའུ(13)», «ས་རི(14)», «ས་ག(15)», «ལག་སོར(16)», «ལྡེའུར(17)», «སོག་པ(18)», «བྲེ(19)», then Phul 20, Byasbor 21, Khrums-stod 24, Khrums-smad 25 and Shesang 27: one unbroken run from 0 to 27. Snarma's «༣» is the number the run asks for after Bra nye 1 and Smindrug 2, so the doubt over its glyph does not change the count.

The same numbers recur in the repayment list of p. 416 (wb.2:p415-416), which gives them again after the mansion names: «སྨིན་དྲུག ༢», «ཁྲ ༡༡», «བི་རྫི ༣», «རྒྱལ་སྟོད ༦», «མགོ ༤», «ཝ ༨», «བྱ་མ(༡༢)», «ས་ག(༡༥)», «ལག་སོར(༡༦)»: Smindrug 2, Khra 11, Mgo 4, Byama 12, Saga 15 and Lagsor 16 agree with p. 380-381, which is the same series in a second place of the print. (That list is machine text on the 1996 print; the check rests on those figures only, and «བི་རྫི» is the list's own name for the mansion numbered 3.)

So the bracket is the mansion's place in a series that starts at 0, not another count. Dbyugpa is 0 and Bra nye 1.

Audit 2026-11-21 (aud-1): confirmed for the question asked (the brackets number the mansions from Dbyugpa as 0, Bra nye 1, Snarma 3: the 0-9 run of p. 380 and the agreement of Smindrug 2, Mgo 4, Lag 5, Rgyal stod 6, Rta chen 9, Rta chung 10, Khra 11, Byama 12, Saga 15, Lagsor 16 on p. 415-416 carry it). Two details of the answer are corrected: the run does not end at 27 with Shesang, the print has «ཤེ་ས(༢༦)» (wb.1 ch. 34 pass, ch34.md, Shesa 26, no ང), so "twenty-eight mansions" is not shown by the brackets; and the machine text of pp. 381-382 repeats some numbers (21, 21, 22, 22) that the pool has not settled, and p. 415-416 gives «རྒྱལ་སྨད ༢» against p. 380's Rgyal smad 7 and «ཝ ༨» against «ལ(༨)», so the second list agrees with the first where named above and not everywhere.
