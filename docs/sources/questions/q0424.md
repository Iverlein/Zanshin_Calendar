# Whose aspects fight the landed year of the moment's counts, and what do the notes' «ཡར་གསུམ་སོགས» count?

The notes' «ཡར་གསུམ་སོགས་ལ་སྲོག་ལུས་དབང་ཀླུང་བཞིས་འཐབ། དུས་ཀྱིས་བརྩི་བྱའི་ལོ་ཚབ་བྱའོ» (vol. 2, p. 537): are «ཡར་གསུམ་སོགས» the three up, three down, eight up and eight down of p. 172's own counts (img. 180, l. 16–19), whose landed years the four aspects fight; and does «དུས་ཀྱིས་བརྩི་བྱའི་ལོ་ཚབ་བྱ» put the moment's year, with its four aspects, where the asker's is, so that the moment's aspects fight the landed year (the lines' «དུས་འཐབ»), or does the moment only give the element the starting sign is sought by, as the notes' worked example (p. 537, the water rat of fourteen and the wood ox's sky year) has it, the asker's own aspects fighting?
