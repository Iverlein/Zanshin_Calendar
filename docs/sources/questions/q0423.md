# How are the moment's small examinations of the asker combined?

How are the moment's small examinations of the asker combined (WB vol. 2, pp. 168–170)? Built (ROADMAP R2z) with these readings: wealth there when the asker's body and power both rise on the moment's animal and the moment's element cuts neither («དེ་གཉིས་བསྟུན་ལ་ཁ་དམར་གདགས»); beer there when both rise; beer given by the moment's power against the asker's power; trade by the power and disputes by the luck («བརྩི་བྱའི་དབང་ཀླུང་གཉིས»: WB gives both aspects for both); food eaten twice, once or not, with no reading where only the moment's power rises; the sick one's life «འབྲིང་པོ» as the breath-taking and the womb; the organs where the illness is as the five solid organs. Is each as the tradition reads it?
