# Which days are the six times (dus drug) of the Kun bzang bla ma'i zhal lung (kzl:i275) and of lcb:p568

From the 2026-11-21 practice-day readings (rd-f, rd-g).

## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-b)

lcb:p568: the six times are the note's days, the 8th, 10th and 15th of the waxing half and the 25th, 29th and the new moon of the waning half (the claim six-times-of-the-month-in-the-larung-note; scan). Kzl:i275: the Gser thang page names «ཉ་སྟོང་དུས་དྲུག» without listing; nothing in hand glosses it for Dpal sprul. Supporting only: Mkhan po Ngag dga', on the same commitment of Longchenpa's verse, writes «འབྲིང་ཉ་སྟོང་དུས་དྲུག་གམ་ཐ་ན་ལོ་ཟླར་མ་ཆགས་པར་བརྩོན་པ» (MW22946, Gsung 'bum vol. 8 etext p. 12, and p. 7: «འབྲིང་ཟླ་བ་གཅིག་གི་དུས་དྲུག»), so the six times are a month's, not the six of day and night, in the Nyingma commitment lists that use «nya stong dus drug» (the BDRC phrase search gives 77 hits, among them the same phrase in Karma chags med's, Penor's and Rin chen gter mdzod's confession lines, none listing days). That the Gser thang passage means the same six is an inference from the shared phrase. Open for the kzl half.

**Narrowed 2026-11-24 (sweep from notes of inv-b above).** Narrowed: lcb:p568 lists the six times (8th, 10th, 15th waxing; 25th, 29th, gnam stong waning); for kzl:i275 the shared phrase «ཉ་སྟོང་དུས་དྲུག» is a month's six, not day-and-night (Mkhan po Ngag dga'); that the Gser thang page means the same list is an inference.
