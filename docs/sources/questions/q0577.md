# The Taikhang leaf of the root verse «གནས་སྒྱུག་སྤར་ཁ་འཐབ་ན་ངན»

Which page of the Taikhang print (I5877/I5878) holds the root verse «གནས་སྒྱུག་སྤར་ཁ་འཐབ་ན་ངན» (WB vol. 1, p. 362, img. 372)? Ten images of I5877 (img. 464–486) were read by Yigdzin-1 and the verse is not among them, and the Zhol print's leaf (img. 473) is too grey to give the root of the stack. See verdicts.md, RD125.

## Answered 2026-11-03

The verse stands at the end of the last line of I5877 img. 490 (f. 480) and continues at the head of img. 491 (f. 481): «…བྱ་བ་བཟློག །གནས་སྐྱུག[…] / སྤར་ཁ་འཐབ་ན་ངན། །གཞན་ཡང་སྤར་ཁའི་དུར་བསྡེབས་ནི།» (RD133, marriage.md).
