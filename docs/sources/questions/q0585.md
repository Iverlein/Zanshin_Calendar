# The Zhol's «མི་ཆེན» and «བོན» on ch. 26's board

P. 472's board names «མི་ཆེན» and «བོན» on the Zhol print where the 1996 print and the Gser thang reprint have «མི་ཚན» and «ཁོན»: are the Zhol's the blocks' own readings (the others' a shared slip, as the sense of WB's point 30 and point 4 suggests), or an editor's mending of the Zhol? Does the Taikhang reproduction (I5877) or Mipham's print give a third reading?

**Narrowed 2026-11-03 (sweep from RD134).** The Taikhang reproduction (I5877 img. 645, p. 472) gives «མི་ཆེན» and «བོན» by Yigdzin-1 as the Zhol does, so they are the Lhasa blocks' reading and not an editor's mending of one copy; the 1996 print's «མི་ཚན» and «ཁོན» and the Gser thang reprint's stay unexplained (ROADMAP, RD134; machine reading, the Taikhang letters not told by eye).
