# Does the Dpe bsdur ma, in the Svapnohana's third watch line, leave «བར» against «བར་དུ» unrecorded because the Peking and the Narthang differ from the base there, or because the base reads «བར་དུ» too?

RD327.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-bb2)

Etext of the Dpe bsdur ma vol. 14 (VEIE0OPIA3E40644_I1PD95859) read again. The base text's third watch line reads «ཐུན་གསུམ་ཟླ་བ་གསུམ་གྱི་བར། །» without «དུ» in both OCR passes of the lines (etext p. 1364 and the foot of p. 1362, «བ་སུམ་ཀྱི་བར། །»), where the fourth watch's «དུ» is read (the OCR does not drop a དུ elsewhere in the lines); the Derge etext (MW23703_1749) reads the same, «གསུམ་གྱི་བར། །», and the Narthang etext's third watch was not quoted letter by letter. The Peking scan has «བར་དུ» (svo:p271w, by eye and Yigdzin-1). So the base most probably reads «བར» (not «བར་དུ»), the Peking differs from it, and the notes of printed p. 1301 hold no entry between entry 4 and the Peking's omission that could be this «དུ»: the notes leave a Peking variant unrecorded here. The alternative, that the base reads «བར་དུ» too, is against two OCR passes and the Derge etext. What would settle it is the restricted page image of printed p. 1301; the answer rests on the OCR text.
