# Rin spungs pa's ch. 7 verse reads «རྨི་ལམ་མི་ཟད་པ» where Byang pa, Khro ru and the 'Bri gung quote «རྨི་ལམ་མི་བཟང་བ»: which does the Serthang print have?

The Serthang pages of the bad-dream list of ch. 7 are not yet in the pool.

Narrowed (RD264, 2026-11-09): the Serthang scan (I3CN5038) has no image of the page that holds the verse (img. 43 ends in ch. 6, img. 45 begins in the near signs of ch. 7, img. 44 and img. 46 are the same page), and the Serthang etext has neither phrase. The Drathang print (I4CZ327131 img. 42, gt:i42) reads «རྨི་ལམ་མི་བཟང་བ» (by eye and by Yigdzin-1), as Byang pa, Khro ru and the Drikung quote; Rin spungs pa's «མི་ཟད་པ» is then his own text's reading. Whether the Serthang print itself has བཟང་བ stays open until its missing page is found.


Narrowed again (inv-n, 2026-11-14, from RD296 in `gt:` notes): the leaf of printed pp. 31–32 is absent from the Serthang digitization itself (the printed numbers on img. 43–47 run 30, 34, 33, 34, 35; the etext repeats p. 34), so no BDRC copy of this print can show the verse; BDRC lists no second instance of it (work WA3CN5036 has only MW3CN5036). The two typeset etexts of other editions that follow the same text, the Lhasa 1982 (MW16778, pp. 49–50) and the Beijing Explanatory Tantra (MW1KG25027, pp. 44–45), read «མི་བཟང་བ»/«མི་བཟང་པ», never «ཟད་». Scan-bound: it waits on a Serthang copy that has the leaf.
