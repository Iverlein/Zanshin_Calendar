# Do the Vimalaprabhā's «short vowels from noon to midnight, thirty long vowels from midnight to noon» (vol. 11 p. 409) and «the long vowel is the dream's nature» (p. 539) mean that the dream belongs to midnight-to-midday, or are they two different lists of vowels?

RD361.

## Answered 2026-11-13

Not said. The Vimalaprabhā gives the long vowels midnight to midday on p. 409 and calls the long vowel the dream's nature on pp. 538-539, in the same two words, but no sentence joins them to the dream's hours; the vowel list of p. 538 mixes in ཧ ཡ ར ཝ ལ. See the claims vimalaprabha-same-words-short-long-not-joined-to-hours and kcd-long-vowels-midnight-to-midday-and-dream-unjoined. The joining remains the reader's.
