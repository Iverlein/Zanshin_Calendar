# KP box 34: what are the two syllables before «མི་འཕྲོད་ཉི་མ» in the bad half's last line, and what follows ཉི་མ་?

WB work 32 has «དབུལ་ལག་ … དང་། ས་ཡི་ཕུང་བྱེད་རྒྱུ་དུས་ངན».

## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-a)

Looked at KP img. 43 (I4CZ65599) again, the upper right panel's last line at 3×: the line is short, begins with two syllables before «མི་འཕྲོད་» whose shapes do not settle (WB's wording is «དབུལ་ལག»), and after «ཉི་མ་» a run of a few syllables ending in «ངན» and a shad; not resolved at this size. WB's own line is in print: BDRC «དབུལ་ལག་མི་འཕྲོད་ཉི་མ་དང» 1 hit, the Vaiḍūrya dkar po alone («bdud rtsi thun mtshams 'char dus dang / gza' skar chu phrad bzang ba yin / dbul lag mi 'phrod nyi ma dang / sa yi phung byed rgyu dus ngan»), so the Vaiḍūrya dkar po reads as WB does; no other manual quotes it. Unchanged.
