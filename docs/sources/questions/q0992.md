# Do images 7–74 of the 夢合延寿袋大成 (NDL pid 2533938), the Kyoto, Kyushu and Tohoku copies, or the 夢合長寿鑑絵抄, 夢合長寿宝 and 夢合早占大成 set out the table of the days on which a dream is not told with a legend for the slash, or a 初夢 night and its calendar?

RD343.

Kyoto copy (RB00005533, 75 spreads) looked at 2026-11-10: the same edition as the NDL copy, nothing new (corpus/mdz/mdz.md, mdz:i2533938); its preface is dated 安永六年 (1777). Still open: the Kyushu, Tohoku and Osaka copies and the 夢合長寿鑑絵抄, 夢合長寿宝 and 夢合早占大成.

The 夢合長寿鑑絵抄 (Kyoto, Tanimura Bunko RB00012284, 1857) looked at 2026-11-10: it has no table of days on which a dream is not told and no slash legend on the leaves seen, but it does hold **a day-keyed dream table, 六甲の夢うらひ, a reading for each of the sixty days 甲子日 onward (canvases 26-29), and a table of the twelve day-officers 十二直 (canvases 29-30)** (corpus/mdz/mdz.md, mdz:kyoto-12284). Still open: the 夢合長寿宝 and 夢合早占大成 (Tohoku, Kano 22630).

The 夢合早占大成 (Tohoku, Kano 22630, 1857, 125 images) looked at 2026-11-10 on all 125 images: an iroha-ordered lexicon of dream objects with an introduction on the six kinds of dream and an appendix 禳夢秘法 whose charm and two methods are transcribed (images 121-122); no table of days not to tell, no slash legend, no sixty-day table, no 初夢 calendar (corpus/mdz/mdz.md, mdz:kano-22630). Still open: the 夢合長寿宝 (Hosa Bunko, Asia Museum) and the Kyushu and Osaka copies.

The six kinds of dream of the 夢合早占大成 (Tohoku, Kano 22630, images 7-19, read 2026-11-10 with their stories: Wu Ding and Fu Yue, a Heian prince, Confucius and the Duke of Zhou, Wang Jun's three knives, Go-Daigo's evergreen tree, Cao Cao's three horses) are recorded in corpus/mdz/mdz.md, mdz:kano-22630.

RD351: the sixty-day table of the 夢合長寿鑑絵抄 (canvases 26-29) and its 十二直夢占 read again at 3000 px: the grammar of a cell (direction, who or what comes, outcome) and thirteen cells transcribed, the others in part (corpus/mdz/mdz.md, mdz:kyoto-12284). What it does not do: it does not name the day on which a dream may not be told.

## Answered 2026-11-13

Narrowed. The images 7-74 of the NDL copy and the Kyoto, Kyushu, Osaka copies: no table, as in RD344 and RD364. The Tohoku copies THKK-22629 and 22653 and the Edo-Tokyo Museum copy 11383 were not opened. The related 夢合延寿袋絵抄 (Koizumi ORMK-00650, 1866) was looked at on contact sheets, 30 images: the same sections (夢現幻之傳 on image 19, 夢祭り供物上げやうの秘伝, 悪夢の伝, the contents leaf), no table of days at that size. The 夢合長寿鑑絵抄 (六甲の夢うらなひ, a day table of another kind), the 夢合早占大成 (no table) and the 夢合長寿宝 (no table, mdz:i446903) are recorded. Nothing carries a 初夢 calendar or the legend.
