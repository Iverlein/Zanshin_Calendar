# Is the verse of rrd.4:p13 Rang byung rdo rje's own song of experience, and what is its title

The heading of the verse text in collected works vol. 4 was not read.

## Answered 2026-11-11

RD402: rrd.4:p13 is p13 of the third text of vol. 4, the verse life of Rang byung rdo rje (ཐམས་ཅད་མཁྱེན་པ་རིན་པོ་ཆེ་རང་བྱུང་རྡོ་རྗེ་རྣམ་པར་ཐར་པ་ཚིགས་སུ་བཅད་པ), first person, the colophon at p22 (day 8 of month 2 of a hare year, at the palace of Tai tu, scribe Dkon mchog 'byung gnas); the verses have no title of their own.
