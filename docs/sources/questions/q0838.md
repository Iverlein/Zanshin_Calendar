# In the Palri print's mixed kind the last clause reads «སྤེལ་ཞིང་མ་བྱེད་ན་ཆོ་འཕྲུལ་དུ་ཤེས་པར་བྱའོ», where the Rin chen gter mdzod has «མ་ཤེས་ན»: does «མ་བྱེད་ན» mean the mixed dream is not to be followed, or is it a misspelling?

Read by eye on img. 542.

**Narrowed 2026-11-10 (sweep from RD318).** Palri img. 542 reads «མ་བྱེད» (plain ཡ-subjoined stack, e vowel, ད), the two etexts «མ་ཤེས», and Yigdzin-1 «མ་ཕྱེད» on Kazi, Zhe chen and Ngodrup; so «མ་ཕྱེད» is the best-supported wording, Palri's བྱེད is likely a slip for ཕྱེད, and the clause does not say that the mixed dream is not to be followed. Not told apart letter by letter.
