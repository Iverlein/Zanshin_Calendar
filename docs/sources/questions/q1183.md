# In the Do shal commentary and the Sgo bzhi mdzod lnga'i yang bcud skar rtsis, is the number-word «དོན» in «ཞག་པོ་སུམ་བརྒྱ་དོན་གཅིག» (WB ch. 2) glossed as 70? Is there a word-numeral list with «དོན» = 7?

Raised by the 2026-11-13 investigation j.

Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-ff): the number-word «དོན» is not glossed in any text that could be reached, but its value 70 is confirmed three other ways. Not found: a gloss in the Do shal (MW1KG1617, MW2PD17386) or the Sgo bzhi mdzod lnga'i yang bcud skar rtsis (MW3PD398): both are search-only and their snippets give only the verse (phrase search «ཞག་པོ་སུམ་བརྒྱ་དོན་གཅིག» finds WB's three prints and one Taikhang etext; with «དོན» the other hits are the common word "meaning"); no list of word-numerals with «དོན» was found («དོན་ནི་བདུན» 44 hits, all the ordinary «don ni bdun»; «དོན་ཞེས་བདུན» 0).

What does settle the number. (1) Plain figures in other texts: the year is «ཚེས་ཞག་སུམ་བརྒྱ་བདུན་ཅུ་རྩ་གཅིག» (371 tithi-days) in 16 hits (BDRC snippets): Padma dkar po's collected works («tshes zhag sum brgya bdun cu rtsa gcig go», after the division by 65 gives 11, put into the upper heap), Bu ston's skar rtsis, the Mkhas pa dga' byed, the Dus 'khor mchan 'grel, the Kun las btus chen po, the Sa lugs 'dul ba texts quoting «lo gcig la khyim zhag 360, nyin zhag 365, tshes zhag 371 with the following remainder». Pan chen Bsod nams grags pa writes «tshes zhag sum brgya don gcig dang zhag gcig gi bcu gnyis cha tsam» beside «nyin zhag 365 and a quarter», and Chone Lama's text «tshes zhag sum brgya don gcig / chu tshod bzhi / chu srang so drug / dbugs lnga / 'dod cha bdun» (the same fraction as WB's) so «don gcig» stands in the same sentence where the others write «bdun cu rtsa gcig» (71 after the hundreds). (2) Computed on Shes rab rin chen's tantra size (MW2DB4577, snippet): 1058 verses, 4232 pādas, letters «brgyad khri brgyad stong brgyad brgya don gnyis»: 4232 × 21 = 88,872, which is 8 khri 8 stong 8 brgya and «don gnyis» = 72 with don = 70, while 88,802 (don = 0) is not a whole number of pādas (the 21 is inferred from the text's «12 more to a pāda», not read). (3) WB's own ch. 27 p. 3: «སུམ་བརྒྱ་དོན་གཅིག» root pebbles, «ལྔ་བརྒྱ་ཉག་གཅིག» sign pebbles and their sum «བརྒྱད་བརྒྱ་དོན་གཉིས»: the sum fits don = 70 (371 + 501 = 872 = 800 + 70 + 2) as well as don = 0 (301 + 501 = 802), so it does not decide; but if don is 70 here too, the pool's reading of ch. 27's counts (301 root, 501 sign, 802 together, claim circles-count) is 371, 501 and 872 (not checked against a count of the chapter's pebbles).

Left open: that «དོན» (don) is a word-numeral for 7 (tens place) and not a place-marker is inferred from agreements, not glossed; a published word-numeral list (a «མངོན་བརྗོད» or Tshig mdzod chen mo entry) is still to be found.
