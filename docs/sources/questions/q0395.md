# Where the day of falling ill turns at night

Where does the day of falling ill turn when one falls ill at night: at midnight, at dawn, or by the Tibetan day (the date's span)?

**Searched 2026-10-25 (launcher).** The pool (kb.py search «ཉི་མ་ཤར», «ཉིན་མཚན», "day turns") holds no clause on where the day of falling ill turns at night; the only hits are the day-letter and earth-lord passages and the claim `illness-day-animal-of-onset`. The app's own rule applies meanwhile: the Tibetan day runs from daybreak, the civil date's span from its 05:00 to 05:00 the next morning (SPEC §5.13, §10.3; the dead's reckoning already turns at 05:00, SPEC §5.9.5), so an onset between midnight and 05:00 takes the previous Tibetan day. A convention for the build, not a gap a reading can fill.
