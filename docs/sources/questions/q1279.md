# What does «ར་བའི་དུས» mean in the Rngam glog's verse on reciting and offering without interruption, as Klong chen pa and Zhe chen rgyal tshab quote it?

Raised by the practice-days investigation (pd-b, 2026-11-16); see the cited passages.

## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-v)

No second occurrence or gloss of «ར་བའི་དུས» beyond lcb:p568 and zgt.11:p292 turned up in the pool; no published text read this round explains it. Stays open.

## Searched again 2026-11-24 (inv-n)

Narrowed. BDRC's exact phrase «ར་བའི་དུས» (135 hits) shows the word in other authors' own sentences as a season's first-month time: Kong sprul's Treasury of Precious Revelations (MW1KG14, Shechen 2007/8) «དབྱར་ཟླ་ར་བའི་དུས་སུ་ལོ་མ་དང་མེ་ཏོག་གཙོ་བོར་བྱ། སྟོན་ཟླ་ར་བའི་དུས་སུ་རྩ་བ་གཙོ་བོར་བྱས་ཏེ» (in the first summer month leaves and flowers are chief, in the first autumn month the root: instruction for gathering medicines), and Klong chen pa's Snying thig ya bzhi (MW2PD19078) «འབྲུག་ལོའི་དགུན་ཟླ་ར་བའི་དུས་ཚོད» (the first winter month of a Dragon year). So «ར་བ» is the first month of a season, and in the Rngam glog verse «ཟླ་རེ་འམ་ར་བའི་དུས་དག་ཏུ» ("every month, or at the ra ba times") the lowest rank of practitioner offers monthly or at the first months of the seasons (four times a year), after the middling's one month, six times, four times. The same verse is quoted in Klong chen pa (MW1KG4884), Zhe chen rgyal tshab (MW1PD133163) and the Ris med collection (MW3CN7099); none of the snippets glosses it. The reading from the season usage is an inference from the word's other uses, not a gloss by the quoting authors; the verse's own commentary was not found. Not answered.
