# WB's model almanac calls the days of months 1, 3 and 9 «དུས་ཆེན» and those of months 4 and 6 «དུས་སྟོན»: a difference of rank, or only of wording?

Raised by the practice-days investigation (pd-e, 2026-11-16); see the cited passages.

## Answered 2026-11-21

Only of wording. The White Beryl's own rule in ch. 14 (wb.1:p174-178, the line «ཐུབ་དབང་ལྷུམས་ཞུགས་བལྟམས་པ་དང༌། ཆོས་འཁོར་ཆོ་འཕྲུལ་མྱ་ངན་འདས། ལ་སོགས་དུས་ཆེན་བཅས་པ་རྣམས། རང་ལུགས་གང་བཤད་བཞིན་འདྲི་», read in almanac-page.md, the full almanac) says that the Sage's entry into the womb, birth, turning of the wheel, miracles and passing beyond sorrow "and the other great occasions" (དུས་ཆེན, dus chen) are written at the month head as one's own tradition explains them. These are the very events that the heads of the model almanac's months 4 and 6 call «དུས་སྟོན» (dus ston): the awakening and passing on the 15th of month 4 (wb.1:p162b), the turning of the wheel on the 4th and the entry into the womb on the 15th of month 6 (wb.1:p165c). So the chapter's rule puts the festival days of months 4 and 6 under the same name dus chen as those of months 1, 3 and 9; dus ston is the head's wording for a festival day, not a lower rank. The other works of the pool also call these days dus chen: «དུས་ཆེན་བཞི» (the four great festivals) of the Teacher are the miracles, the awakening, the wheel and the descent (dgk:i491, bdc:i76, rcz:p286-288). No text read ranks the two words against each other.
