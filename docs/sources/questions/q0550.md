# What reckoning brings the Black Moon and the lords after it to a given day

For the Black Moon and the lords after it (pp. 359–364), what is the reckoning that brings each to a given day, since the lines read give works, outcomes and remedies but no rule of arrival?

## Answered 2026-11-06

RD179 (2026-11-06): the lords after the Black Moon on WB pp. 360–364 are ch. 31's 'earth lords that move by date' (pp. 226–233), in the same order, from the great black day, Little Black, Pi ling 'phar ma and Zin phung (14) through Ki kang, Hal khyi, Gnam khyi, Gnam sbyor, Gza' rgod, Dbul lag, Gza' bdun, Ngams shing, Bar khyi, Ka khyung ki kang and Dra chen to Rāhu (27). Each has its course there, so the arrival rule sits a chapter earlier. The Black Moon is the black-month rule (vol. 1, p. 183) that p. 235 applies to days.
