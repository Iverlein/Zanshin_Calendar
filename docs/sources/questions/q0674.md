# Karkaṭa in the lifespan verses: Zhol «ཀ༷ཊ་ལ» and the reprint's «ཀཥྚ»

Is the Zhol print's «ཀ༷ཊ་ལ» (ka with a ring, ṭa, la; img. 1142 line 1, ch. 34 lifespan verses) a spelling of Karkaṭa, the crab's house, and is the reprint's «ཀཥྚ» the same word? (RD158, corpus/wb/ch34.md, fourth pass)

## Answered 2026-11-14

The crab's house of the lifespan verses is Karkaṭa in WB's own short spelling «ཀཊ» (ka, ṭa), followed by the case particle «ལ»: «ཀཊ་ལ་སྐྱེས་ལོ་ཟླ་ཞག». WB writes the crab as «ཀཊ» in its lists of the signs throughout ch. 34 (the banners, hunting, canals, supports, suppressing the sri, consecration, dikes, crafts and war lists of white-beryl-ch34.md: «ཀཊ་བུམ», «སྲང་ཀཊ», «ཀཊ་ལུག»), and the Zhol print's «ཀ༷ཊ་ལ» (img. 1142 line 1: ka with the print's ring, ṭa, la; no ན and no རྞ) is that word. The 1996 print's etext (I1KG12907, p. 391) has «ཀུ ཊ་ལ» (the first syllable misread, ཊ and ལ in place, no ན or རྞ); the reprint's etext (I3CN5029, p. 389) has «ཀན་ལ». So the passage's «ཀརྞ» (Karṇa) was a machine error, and the reprint's «ཀཥྚ» of both readers is a reading of the same ཀཊ stack at the print's size, not a different word (kaṣṭa, "hardship", and karṇa, "ear", make no house of the zodiac). Which letters the reprint's own stack above the ཊ holds was not settled on the image (img. 389; two 2×–3× looks, the line is small); the identity of the word does not depend on it. BDRC's phrase search for «ཀརྞ་ལ་སྐྱེས་ལོ་ཟླ་ཞག», «ཀཥྚ་ལ་སྐྱེས་ལོ་ཟླ་ཞག» and «ཀཊ་ལ་སྐྱེས་ལོ་ཟླ་ཞག» found 0 hits (the verse stands in WB only, with the figures in brackets between).
