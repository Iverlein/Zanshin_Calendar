# Is the writer of Rtsa gsum gling pa vol. 27's Gzer bu commentary (Orgyan gsang sngags bstan 'dzin Nam sgo chen sprul) the volume's compiler Mkhan chen Dpal ldan shes rab, and what does རབ་གནས before ཆུ་ལུག་ལོ mean

Colophon, images 132-134; Khenpo Ngag dbang dge legs mchog corrected it.

## Narrowed 2026-11-13

BDRC's exact-phrase search (2026-11-13) for «རབ་གནས་ཆུ་ལུག་ལོ» gives five hits: this colophon twice (MW4CZ1042_94F327 and MW4CZ42297, the Kathmandu print of the Gter chos, 30 vols, 2002/2014), and three colophons of recent writers with the same formula: the Lho lung dkar dgon history (MW00EGS1016678, by 'Brong sprul 07 Dkon mchog lhun grub snyan grags, born about 1965: «rab gnas chu lug lo zla tshes dge bar») and two in the Kun dga' thugs rje rnam thar (MW30066_332258, MW30066_C153D9; «'das rjes kyi rab gnas chu lug lo ste spyi lo 2002 lo'i dus mchod»), where the year is given in the same sentence as 2002. So «rab gnas» before a year-name is a dating formula found in recent colophons, not a part of the year's name; its sense is not defined in any text read. The colophon does not say which water-sheep year (1943 or 2003 by the print's date). No text read says that the writer is the compiler Mkhan chen Dpal ldan shes rab.

## Answered 2026-11-14

The writer is not the compiler: he is Gnam lcags Gsang sngags bstan 'dzin, the fifth Sgo chen tulku (born 1953), a treasure-revealer's incarnation of Rtsa gsum gling pa, not Mkhan chen Dpal ldan shes rab (1938-2010).
Evidence. (1) BDRC's person P8LS3511, «sgo chen sprul sku sku phreng 05 gsang sngags bstan 'dzin», born 1953, other names «gnam lcags gsang sngags bstan 'dzin» (a gter ston title), «ngag dbang klu sgrub» and «'jam dpal», an incarnation of Rtsa gsum gling pa (P680, «dri med gling pa'i sprul rabs drug pa»), with a note citing this very work (MW4CZ1042) pp. 268-270. The compiler is another man, P5640 «sgo chen mkhan po dpal ldan shes rab», 1938-2010, and the colophon's «Nam sgo chen sprul» is Gnam lcags Sgo chen sprul (tulku). (2) In the same volume the writer's other colophons give the same name and the age: «Gnam lcags Gsang sngags bstan 'dzin ... rang lo 51 son pas rab byung bcu bdun pa'i chu lug», aged 51 in the seventeenth cycle's water-sheep year (tsl.27:i591), which is 2003 and fits a birth in 1953; and «Orgyan Gsang sngags bstan 'dzin Nam, rab btags Sgo chen sprul ming du 'bod pas rab byung bcu bdun pa'i chu lug lo» (tsl.27:i40), the same year written out. (3) The Khenpo Ngag dbang dge legs mchog who corrected the text is his younger brother (གཅུང་མཁན་པོ་ངག་དབང་དགེ་ལེགས་མཆོག, the vol. 27 colophon at image 78: «gcung gnam lcags mkhan po Ngag dbang dge legs mchog»), and BDRC has no person under that spelling (autocomplete, 2026-11-14).
The year. The water-sheep year is 2003, not 1943 (the writer was born in 1953): the seventeenth cycle began in 1987.
What «rab gnas» means before «chu lug lo». The writer's own two other colophons in this volume have «rab byung bcu bdun pa'i» (the seventeenth cycle's) in that place, and one has «rab btags» ("so called") before «Sgo chen sprul ming». «Rab gnas» ("consecration") has no sense in the dating phrase; it is most likely a slip, or a short writing, for «rab byung» (the cycle's name) with the number lost. This is a reading of the three colophons side by side, not something a text says. The other recent colophons found by the phrase search (Lho lung dkar dgon history, Kun dga' thugs rje) are by other writers and do not decide it.
