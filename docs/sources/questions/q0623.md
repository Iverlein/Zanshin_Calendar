# Today's paper 高島暦: the 金神's 遊行 as two lists or merged

Does the paper 高島暦 of today print the 金神's 遊行 as two lists, or merged as the 御重寶 of 昭和3年 has them, and does it free the god's seat during a 遊行? (D12.)

RD172 (2026-11-06), narrowed: 家庭寶鑑 1928 prints the 金神's two lists apart; its rule is «金神遊行中は其の方凶とし本宮災なし。遊行せざる時は本宮凶とす», so the seat is free during an outing. RD163 found the 御重寶 1927 merged. Today's paper not seen.
