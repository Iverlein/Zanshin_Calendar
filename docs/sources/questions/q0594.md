# Five places of «ཤི་སྒབ» or «ཤི་སྐབ» on the Taikhang

At the five places of the run of deaths not read on a sharp print (p. 105, l. 15; p. 106, ll. 2, 15; p. 108, ll. 11, 24, 26), does the Taikhang show ག or ཀ under the ས?

RD150 (2026-11-06), by eye at 4–7× on img. 161, 162, 165, 166: at all six places the subjoined letter under the ས is the same closed box with a stem, none showing the hook or crossing stroke of ཀ; Yigdzin-1's one ཀ (img. 165) is the same box on the scan. A lean to ག, not settled: the scan is one-bit with filled counters and no certain ཀ-stack of the hand was set beside it. Line p. 108 l. 26 reads «བཤད་པའི་ཤི་སྒབ་བཅད», with བཅད, not ཆོད.

RD153 (2026-11-06), strips placed by Yigdzin-1, stacks cut at 5–8× on img. 162 and 166: img. 162 line 2 «ལོ་བཅུའི་ཤི་སྒབ» and line 5 «བཅུ་གཅིག་ཤི་སྒབ», img. 166 line 3 left «བཅུ་དྲུག་ཤི་སྒབ»: closed box under the ས, no crossing stroke, ག with doubt. Img. 166 line 3 right «བཤད་པའི་ཤི་སྒབ་བཅད»: a larger box with two holes divided by a diagonal; ཀ's crossing stroke cannot be told from the ས's foot at 5×, so a possible ཀ (RD150 saw a plain box here). Img. 165's place was not found by the strips. Needs a sharper print of the same page (the Zhol blocks) for the img. 166 right-hand place.
