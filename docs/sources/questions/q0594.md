# Five places of «ཤི་སྒབ» or «ཤི་སྐབ» on the Taikhang

At the five places of the run of deaths not read on a sharp print (p. 105, l. 15; p. 106, ll. 2, 15; p. 108, ll. 11, 24, 26), does the Taikhang show ག or ཀ under the ས?

RD150 (2026-11-06), by eye at 4–7× on img. 161, 162, 165, 166: at all six places the subjoined letter under the ས is the same closed box with a stem, none showing the hook or crossing stroke of ཀ; Yigdzin-1's one ཀ (img. 165) is the same box on the scan. A lean to ག, not settled: the scan is one-bit with filled counters and no certain ཀ-stack of the hand was set beside it. Line p. 108 l. 26 reads «བཤད་པའི་ཤི་སྒབ་བཅད», with བཅད, not ཆོད.

RD153 (2026-11-06), strips placed by Yigdzin-1, stacks cut at 5–8× on img. 162 and 166: img. 162 line 2 «ལོ་བཅུའི་ཤི་སྒབ» and line 5 «བཅུ་གཅིག་ཤི་སྒབ», img. 166 line 3 left «བཅུ་དྲུག་ཤི་སྒབ»: closed box under the ས, no crossing stroke, ག with doubt. Img. 166 line 3 right «བཤད་པའི་ཤི་སྒབ་བཅད»: a larger box with two holes divided by a diagonal; ཀ's crossing stroke cannot be told from the ས's foot at 5×, so a possible ཀ (RD150 saw a plain box here). Img. 165's place was not found by the strips. Needs a sharper print of the same page (the Zhol blocks) for the img. 166 right-hand place.

Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-rr). The Taikhang is the Zhol blocks' own reproduction (sources.yaml, wbz), and the Zhol blocks carve «སྒབ» where they were read, with the WBZ etext: img. 778 l. 4 and img. 784 l. 4 (claims/wbz-shi-sgab.md); the grave chapter's «ན་སྒབ་ཤི་སྒབ་འགྲམས་སྒབ་ཆོད» is printed with the same stack (claims/sgab-one-word-everywhere.md). BDRC's phrase search finds «ཤི་སྒབ» in 61 hits (several works and copies, among them the 1996 etext of WB vol. 2, I1KG12906 and I1KG12907) against «ཤི་སྐབ» in 3 (the Gser thang etext of WB, I3CN5029, and two lines that inv-nn took for OCR noise, I5329 and I1KG81108), and «ཤི་སྒབ་བཅད» in 20, «ལོ་བཅུའི་ཤི་སྒབ» in 1 (I1KG12907): by weight of witnesses and by the term, which is one word at every place, the Taikhang carves ག. The glyph itself at the five places stays scan-bound. What an eye pass needs: the full-width Taikhang images (I5878 img. 161, 162, 165, 166, the sharpest copy at 8280 px; RD153 worked at 5–8× on the one-bit scan) cut as single stacks at the native size, set beside a certain ཀ-stack of the same hand on the same leaf (for example a «སྐ»-initial word of the same page, such as the «སྐར» of a mansion or «སྐྱེས») and a certain «སྒ», and the comparison of the crossing stroke against the ས's foot; and, for the right-hand place of img. 166 (p. 108 l. 26), the Zhol I1KG1710 image of the same page (3752 px) as a second cut of the same block, not yet looked at. No new image was opened in this reading.
