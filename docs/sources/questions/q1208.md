# KP's year ring: are the life-spirit knowers 'inner' and 'secret' WB's three places of one lord?

KP's year ring (kp:i87b) names in its cells two life-spirit knowers that WB's twenty-six lords of the year do not list: «བླ་མཁྱེན་གསང» (bla mkhyen gsang, the secret one; the Rat panel's B3, with the time-holder) and «བླ་མཁྱེན་ནང» (bla mkhyen nang, the inner one; met in the Hare and later panels). WB 6 gives the life-spirit knower of the se three places, outer, inner and secret (the seventh from the year; the middle of the three in concord; in front of the corner years and behind the middle ones); are the two cells KP's way of drawing the inner and the secret place of that one lord, so that KP's three cells (the outer in the Horse's place, the inner and the secret elsewhere) are WB's three accounts set on the chart? In the Rat panel the cell of the secret one, B3, is the Pig's, which fits none of WB's secret places read plainly; the inner one's place in the Rat panel was not found among the cells read.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (rd-kp3)

Read cell by cell in six panels, «Gsang» stands behind the year by one place in the middle and last years (the Rat year's Pig, the Ox year's Rat, the Hare year's Tiger) and ahead by one in the corner years (the Tiger year's Hare, the Snake year's Horse), which is WB 6's secret place (behind the middle years, in front of the corner years); «Nang» stands in the Tiger year's Horse, the middle of the tiger, horse and dog triad, which is WB 6's inner place. In the Dragon panel «Nang» is in the Rat and in the Snake panel in the Bird, the second and the next place of the triad, not the middle one; the Ox and Hare panels' «Nang» (the Ox's Dog; the Hare's king cell, in part) is not settled. So KP's two cells are WB's inner and secret places of the one lord in the Tiger, Snake, Rat, Ox and Hare panels where read; the question left is the Dragon and Snake «Nang» and the Ox's (claim [kp-year-ring-offsets-against-wb](../claims/kp-year-ring-offsets-against-wb.md)).

## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-k)

A Bhutanese table manuscript (BDRC MW26041, I1KG11905 etext pp. 437-439) has year-ring panels naming «བླ་མཁྱེན་ནང» and «བླ་མཁྱེན་གསབ། ནང» among the year's lords, the same two cells as KP's; its etext is too broken (words and panels run together) to give any panel's cell, so the Dragon, Snake and Ox «Nang» places are still not settled. WB 6's three places of the one lord stand as in the narrowing above. Open.

## Answered 2026-11-21

Answered: yes. KP's «བླ་མཁྱེན་ནང» (bla mkhyen nang) and «བླ་མཁྱེན་གསང» (bla mkhyen gsang) are the inner and the secret place of the one life-spirit knower of the se whom WB lists sixth, drawn as cells of their own, and the inner cell follows WB's rule in every panel where it was read clearly. The earlier narrowing found the Dragon and Snake «Nang» off the rule because it took the rule's «middle» for the middle of the triad in the order of the animals; WB's «middle» is a class of years.

WB's verse (vol. 2, p. 180, in year-month-earth-lords.md, lord 6): «ནང་ལྟར་མཐུན་གསུམ་འབྲིང་པོའི་སྟེང་། །གསང་བ་ར་བཞིའི་མདུན་དང་ནི། །འབྲིང་བཞིའི་རྒྱབ་དང་ཐ་ཆུང་བཞིར། །ཕྱི་ཡི་གཤེད་ལ་འབྲང་བའོ།»: the inner on the middle («འབྲིང་པོ») of the three in concord; the secret in front of the four corner years («ར་བཞི»), behind the four middle years («འབྲིང་བཞི»), and in the four last years («ཐ་ཆུང་བཞི») following the outer slayer. The three classes are named by WB (vol. 1, p. 254, and again in the verse of Phe'u, no. 23): corner tiger, monkey, pig, snake; middle mouse, horse, bird, hare; last ox, sheep, dog, dragon. Each triad of three in concord holds one animal of each class (tiger, horse, dog; monkey, mouse, dragon; snake, bird, ox; pig, hare, sheep), so «the middle of the three in concord» is the triad's animal of the middle class, one place for every year: the horse for the tiger, horse and dog years; the mouse for the monkey, mouse and dragon years; the bird for the snake, bird and ox years; the hare for the pig, hare and sheep years.

KP's inner cells laid against that, panel by panel, from the cells already entered (kp:i87b, kp:i87c, kp:i88c, kp:i89b, kp:i90b): Rat panel, with the king in the Rat's own cell (the rat is its own middle); Tiger, the Horse (T3); Dragon, the Rat (B2); Snake, the Bird (R2); Horse, the Horse (T3, with the king); Sheep, the Hare (L1); Bird, the Bird (R2, with the king); Dog, the Horse (T3); Pig, the Hare (L1). That is nine panels, six of them (Tiger, Dragon, Snake, Sheep, Dog, Pig) with the cell not the year's own, so not a coincidence of place with the king. The Hare panel's king cell (L1) is read in part; the Monkey panel's B2 (the mouse's place) holds a «བླ་མཁྱེན་…» whose epithet is not resolved. The earlier note that the Dragon's «Nang» stands in the second place of its triad and the Snake's in the next is a misreading of «middle»: the Rat is the middle (class) animal of the Dragon's triad, the Bird of the Snake's. The secret cell was already found to be WB's («one place behind the middle years, ahead of the corner years», claim kp-year-ring-offsets-against-wb); with the inner now on WB's rule, KP's three cells for the lord are WB's three accounts, the outer on the seventh, the inner on the middle of the concord, the secret by the class of the year.

Not settled, and kept apart from the answer: in the Ox panel the one «Nang» read clearly stands in the Dog (B4, with the splitter), not in the Bird where the rule puts it (the Bird's R2 cell, two lines, is not resolved; a look at it on the scan at 4.5x (pool img. 87) reads its first line as ending «…མཁྱེན་ནང», but the stack before it is not told from «བླ», so it is not entered); and the Dog panel's B4 and the Pig panel's B2 (the Rat, with the eleven kings) carry a second «Nang» beside the one on the rule.
