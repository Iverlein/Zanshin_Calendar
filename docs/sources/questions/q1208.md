# KP's year ring: are the life-spirit knowers 'inner' and 'secret' WB's three places of one lord?

KP's year ring (kp:i87b) names in its cells two life-spirit knowers that WB's twenty-six lords of the year do not list: «བླ་མཁྱེན་གསང» (bla mkhyen gsang, the secret one; the Rat panel's B3, with the time-holder) and «བླ་མཁྱེན་ནང» (bla mkhyen nang, the inner one; met in the Hare and later panels). WB 6 gives the life-spirit knower of the se three places, outer, inner and secret (the seventh from the year; the middle of the three in concord; in front of the corner years and behind the middle ones); are the two cells KP's way of drawing the inner and the secret place of that one lord, so that KP's three cells (the outer in the Horse's place, the inner and the secret elsewhere) are WB's three accounts set on the chart? In the Rat panel the cell of the secret one, B3, is the Pig's, which fits none of WB's secret places read plainly; the inner one's place in the Rat panel was not found among the cells read.
