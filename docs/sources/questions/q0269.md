# The four executioners' pebble door among the la places

The specific verdict reads "the four executioners' pebble door empty" (WB vol. 2, p. 53, «གཤེད་བཞིའི་རྡེལ་སྒོ་སྟོངས»). Which of the twelve la places, or which pebbles, are the four executioners' door?

**A hint 2026-10-23 (RD45, [claims/earth-four-executioners.md](../claims/earth-four-executioners.md)).** UT's earth row gives the power year as «གཤེད་བཞི», the four executioners (ut:p56, etext only).

## Answered 2026-11-14

By inference, the door of the great cut-off place (ཆད་ཆེན). The line «གཤེད་བཞིའི་རྡེལ་སྒོ་སྟོངས་པ་ན། ཕ་མེས་ཡང་མེས་རིང་དག་ལ། མི་མགོ་ནག་པོ་ལྟོ་བར་བཅུག» (p. 53) has the same consequence in nearly the same words as the special verdict's great cut-off («ཕ་དང་མེས་པོའི་རིང་དག་ལ། མི་མགོ་ལྟོར་བཅུག»), and stands between the power year's place and the small cut-off's, where the great cut-off's entry would be ([claims/wb-four-executioners-door-follows-the-great-cut-off.md](../claims/wb-four-executioners-door-follows-the-great-cut-off.md)). No manual says it; BDRC's phrase search «གཤེད་བཞིའི་རྡེལ་སྒོ» finds only WB's two etexts. UT's «གཤེད་བཞི» as the earth's power year (ut:p56) is not taken.
