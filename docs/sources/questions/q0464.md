# What are «བུད་མེད་བུས་པ» on p. 283 and «ཁྱི་སྤྱང་བྲང་ཤུལ» on p. 294

On WB vol. 2 p. 283 what is «བུད་མེད་བུས་པ», and on p. 294 «ཁྱི་སྤྱང་བྲང་ཤུལ»? Both are read by the machine only. (RD81)

## Answered 2026-11-05

Answered (RD156, [ch32](../corpus/wb/ch32.md)): «བུད་མེད་བུས་པ» stands on the Zhol print too (img. 1023): women and small children. «ཁྱི་སྤྱང་བྲང་ཤུལ» as read on the Zhol print (img. 1040); dog and wolf or one wolf-like dog is not told by the text.
