# Which water-bird year does the colophon of the Nyang Sems sde pra khrid in Gdams ngag mdzod vol. 1 mean, and is the beggar Nam mkha' rdo rje the Nyang-tradition master

Printed pp. 361-362; at Dpal gnam steng ri khrod, water-female bird year.

## Narrowed 2026-11-13

BDRC's exact-phrase search (2026-11-13) for «དཔལ་གནམ་སྟེང་རི་ཁྲོད» finds the colophon twice: in the Gdams ngag mdzod (MW23605, Shechen 1999) and in a separate print of the same text, which BDRC titles «rdzogs chen sems sde'i khrid yig nyang lugs nam mkha' rdo rje'i gsung» (MW1PD100944_F1C9B5, in the Snga 'gyur bka' ma shin tu rgyas pa). So the text is catalogued as the Nyang-tradition (nyang lugs) mind-series instruction by Nam mkha' rdo rje; the title answers the second half. The colophon's snippet reads «chu mo bya lo zla ba lnga pa'i yar tshes la bla ma dam pa'i bzhugs gnas dpal gnam steng ri khrod du sbyar ba», the waxing half of the fifth month of a water-female bird year; the year itself is in no text read (BDRC's person record of Nam mkha' rdo rje was not opened).

## Narrowed 2026-11-13 (second pass)

BDRC's person record opened through its public API (purl.bdrc.io, 2026-11-13). The person whose name is Nam mkha' rdo rje and whose records fit the colophon is P7962, «kaH thog mkhas grub nam mkha' rdo rje», also «bya bral ba nam mkha' rdo rje» (བྱ་བྲལ་བ, bya bral ba, 'the one free of activity', which the colophon's «beggar» may render) and «rtogs ldan nam mkha' rdo rje» (རྟོགས་ལྡན་ནམ་མཁའ་རྡོ་རྗེ); Nyingma; a note of BDRC (source MW20396, p. 164) says «enumerates his sems sde pra khrid», i.e. a mind-series instruction of his is on record; flourished «14» (the century field says 15); student of P4312, Yag sde Paṇ chen Brtson 'grus dar rgyas (1299-1378). Water-female bird years in these centuries: 1333, 1393, 1453. A pupil of a master who died in 1378 is likelier to have written at Dpal gnam steng ri khrod in 1393 than in 1333; this is an inference from the record's dates, BDRC gives no year for the colophon. The identity of P7962 with the colophon's Nam mkha' rdo rje is likewise the catalogue's (the same text type under the same name), not a statement of the colophon. The record P7962 has no birth or death year, no birthplace and no list of works but the note.
Still open: a source that dates the colophon (the Kaḥ thog histories, MW20396 p. 164 not opened).
