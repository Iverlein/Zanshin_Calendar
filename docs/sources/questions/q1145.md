# Which water-bird year does the colophon of the Nyang Sems sde pra khrid in Gdams ngag mdzod vol. 1 mean, and is the beggar Nam mkha' rdo rje the Nyang-tradition master

Printed pp. 361-362; at Dpal gnam steng ri khrod, water-female bird year.

## Narrowed 2026-11-13

BDRC's exact-phrase search (2026-11-13) for «དཔལ་གནམ་སྟེང་རི་ཁྲོད» finds the colophon twice: in the Gdams ngag mdzod (MW23605, Shechen 1999) and in a separate print of the same text, which BDRC titles «rdzogs chen sems sde'i khrid yig nyang lugs nam mkha' rdo rje'i gsung» (MW1PD100944_F1C9B5, in the Snga 'gyur bka' ma shin tu rgyas pa). So the text is catalogued as the Nyang-tradition (nyang lugs) mind-series instruction by Nam mkha' rdo rje; the title answers the second half. The colophon's snippet reads «chu mo bya lo zla ba lnga pa'i yar tshes la bla ma dam pa'i bzhugs gnas dpal gnam steng ri khrod du sbyar ba», the waxing half of the fifth month of a water-female bird year; the year itself is in no text read (BDRC's person record of Nam mkha' rdo rje was not opened).

## Narrowed 2026-11-13 (second pass)

BDRC's person record opened through its public API (purl.bdrc.io, 2026-11-13). The person whose name is Nam mkha' rdo rje and whose records fit the colophon is P7962, «kaH thog mkhas grub nam mkha' rdo rje», also «bya bral ba nam mkha' rdo rje» (བྱ་བྲལ་བ, bya bral ba, 'the one free of activity', which the colophon's «beggar» may render) and «rtogs ldan nam mkha' rdo rje» (རྟོགས་ལྡན་ནམ་མཁའ་རྡོ་རྗེ); Nyingma; a note of BDRC (source MW20396, p. 164) says «enumerates his sems sde pra khrid», i.e. a mind-series instruction of his is on record; flourished «14» (the century field says 15); student of P4312, Yag sde Paṇ chen Brtson 'grus dar rgyas (1299-1378). Water-female bird years in these centuries: 1333, 1393, 1453. A pupil of a master who died in 1378 is likelier to have written at Dpal gnam steng ri khrod in 1393 than in 1333; this is an inference from the record's dates, BDRC gives no year for the colophon. The identity of P7962 with the colophon's Nam mkha' rdo rje is likewise the catalogue's (the same text type under the same name), not a statement of the colophon. The record P7962 has no birth or death year, no birthplace and no list of works but the note.
Still open: a source that dates the colophon (the Kaḥ thog histories, MW20396 p. 164 not opened).

## Narrowed 2026-11-13 (inv-t)

BDRC's search for «bya bral ba nam mkha' rdo rje» lists two person records with that name, P6025 and P7962 (see q1195), and under P6025 two works by «nyin dgon bya bral ba nam mkha' rdo rje»: MW8LS19380 «rdzogs pa chen po sems sde'i pra khrid kyi man ngag» (72 p.; BDRC's note: instruction on the Dzogchen mind series, by the Kathok master Namkha Dorje), and a reply to questions on the Zhi khro dgongs pa rang grol (MW8LS19473, MW3PD888_2D5AC8). So the mind-series instruction by this name is on BDRC under P6025, not P7962. The teachers and pupils on the records put P6025 later than the 14th century: P6025's teachers are P2943 (Dge rtse Paṇ chen 'Gyur med tshe dbang mchog grub, 1761-1829) and P6013 (Dza ka mchog sprul Bsod nams rnam rgyal, 17th c.); his pupils P5994 (Kaḥ thog Si tu 02 Chos kyi blo gros, b. 1820) and P6010 (Mtsho phu grub chen Chos dbyings rang grol, b. 18th c.), whose record names «bya bral ba nam mkha' rdo rje» among his lamas. The Kaḥ thog history (MW3CN5276, Bya bral Sangs rgyas rdo rje; search-only snippet) says «nyin dgon bya bral ba nam mkha' rdo rje ni dge paN sogs kyi slob ma rtogs pa mchog ldan zhig nyin dgon rang du tshe dang sgrub pa mnyam…», a pupil of Dge paN and others, a realised man living at Nyin dgon. Water-female bird years: 1753, 1813, 1873. Of these, 1813 is the one that fits a pupil of Dge rtse Paṇ chen (b. 1761) and teacher of a man born in 1820; this is an inference from the records, no text read dates the colophon. This replaces the 1393 guess of the second pass, which rested on P7962's tie to Yag sde Paṇ chen (1299-1378). Whether the Nyang-lugs print (MW1PD100944_F1C9B5) and MW8LS19380 are one text is not shown (different titles, the print's author field empty); the colophon's place (Dpal gnam steng ri khrod) was not found in the Kaḥ thog histories read.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-vvv)

The Nyang-tradition print (BDRC etext VEIE0OPI48DFFA46_I1PD106549, the Snga 'gyur bka' ma shin tu rgyas pa, text p. 618) was fetched whole through the etext API and its colophon read: the text was compiled by «ངེས་པའི་དོན་ལ་བློ་གཅིག་ཏུ་གཞོལ་བའི་སྤྲང་པོ་ནམ་མཁའ་རྡོ་རྗེ» (the beggar Nam mkha' rdo rje, single-minded on the definitive meaning) from the four-stage root verses, Dga' rab rdo rje's *Nyams sgron*, the *Rab 'byed* of Jam dpal bshes gnyen, the thirty-six «gol sgrib» of Dam pa bde gshegs pa, the *Bsam gtan spyi 'grel*, the *Don gsal* of Chos rje Gtsang pa and Rtogs ldan Shākya rdor pa's *Theg spyi*, and set down «chu mo bya lo zla ba lnga pa'i yar tshes» at Dpal gnam steng ri khrod; the text is the same in the Gdams ngag mdzod (the two prints share the colophon, so one text). So the author calls himself «sprang po» (beggar), which is not BDRC's «bya bral ba», and his latest named sources are Chos rje Gtsang pa and Rtogs ldan Shākya rdor pa (neither dated here, so no earliest date for the year). BDRC's phrase search «བྱ་བྲལ་བ་ནམ་མཁའ་རྡོ་རྗེ» (9 hits) adds one witness: the life of Padma 'gro 'dul gsang sngags gling pa (etext VE23945_001) names «bla ma Bya bral ba Nam mkha' rdo rje» among the teachers and disciples around the young gter ston, i.e. a 19th-century Kaḥ thog-Sgo chen figure, which agrees with q1145's 1813 and not with the 1333 or 1393 of the Yag sde pupil. The year is still not stated in any text read; no source identifies the colophon's «sprang po» with P6025 or with P7962.
