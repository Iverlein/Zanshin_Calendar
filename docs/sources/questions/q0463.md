# What are «མཇུག་མ་གླང་ཆེན་རྟ་མ་ཉེ» on p. 282 and the ninth part of Lto-'phye

On WB vol. 2 p. 282 what is «མཇུག་མ་གླང་ཆེན་རྟ་མ་ཉེ», and does digging at Lto-'phye's ninth part, back or tail harm the household as the machine reading has it (parents, sons and wife killed at the part, the digger killed or moved at the back)? The lines on the tail are doubtful. (RD81)

RD104b (2026-11-01): reply 170 (ys.2:i026, machine) says the elephant Lto-'phye's shrouding wood draws is crowned with the earth's own Rahu (see claims/lto-phye-shrouding-wood-rahu.md); it names no tail, horse-mother or part of the ground, so this question stays open.

## Answered 2026-11-05

Answered as far as a scan can (RD156, [ch32](../corpus/wb/ch32.md)): p. 282's tail reads «རྟ་མ་ཧེ» on the reprint (RD91) and has the same outline on the Zhol print (img. 1022); Padma dkar po and other etexts have «མ་ཧེ», the water buffalo. The harms stand as read: household at the part where he lies, the digger at the back, the beasts at the tail. What «དགུ་ཆ» means stays with q482.
