# Does the Narthang Tengyur catalogue (I2KG212509, vol. 225) list the Svapnohana anywhere, and where? Images 3-140 and the rest were not read; the entry is absent from the Black Lord run (images 202-206) where the Derge and Jo nang order would put it.

RD365.

## Answered 2026-11-13

Yes, once: img. 77 of the Narthang catalogue (vol. 225), in the run of the mother tantras, after the 360-verse Deceiving Death (svo:i77); no second entry in img. 3-206 and 208-240 (the unread even images and 241-288 excepted, see the claims narthang-catalogue-images-208-240-no-svapnohana and narthang-catalogue-entry-form-and-no-svapnohana-entry-found). Machine reading of the whole image; the entry is not in the Black Lord run (img. 202-206) as the question saw. The answer stands on the machine text and two scan crops, not on a read scan.
