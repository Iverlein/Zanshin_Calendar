# Is the east line's «དང་» of the door table on p. 287 the first place cut short, and from which side are the places counted

The east line of the door table on p. 287 (img. 293) opens «ཤར་གྱི་དང་གཉིས་གསུམ་པ་དང་»: is «དང་» there the first place cut short («དང་པོ», bad) or a conjunction, and from which side of the house are the eight places on each side counted? The page prints the places as ordinal words, not digits, and gives no direction. Narrows q462 (RD86).

**Narrowed 2026-11-05 (RD156, [ch32](../corpus/wb/ch32.md)).** «དང་» has no «པོ» on either print, and the line is seven syllables as it stands, as each line of the table is. The direction of counting is not stated on pp. 276–294.
