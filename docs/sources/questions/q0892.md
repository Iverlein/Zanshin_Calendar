# Is the afternoon for the bright beginning in the Aṅgavijjā ch. 41 a scribe's slip for the forenoon, as the lists of chs. 42 and 53 suggest, or a variant of the text itself?

The notes of the print are silent and no manuscript was seen (RD295).

**Narrowed 2026-11-10 (sweep from RD312).** The apparatus of Puṇyavijaya's print carries no note on the word for the bright beginning's time at chs. 41, 42 and 53, so the afternoon of ch. 41 is the print's reading, not a corrected slip; the lists differ among themselves, and which wording is original needs a collation at p. 185 line 28 (svo.md, q856 and q892).
