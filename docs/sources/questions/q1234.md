# KP box 10: is «ཀིང་ཟོར» one name or two (it follows «ཉི་མ་བྱ་ཡོས»)?

The existing answer is the contraction; not re-tested on the scan.

## Answered 2026-11-21

Answered: «ཀིང་ཟོར» is a contraction of two names, ki kang (ཀི་ཀང) and phung po'i zor (ཕུང་པོའི་ཟོར, phung zor), not a single name. WB's work 11, which box 10 digests, reads «བྱ་དང་ཡོས་ཀྱི་ཉི་མ་དང་། །འཆི་སྦྱོར་ཀི་ཀང་ཕུང་པོའི་ཟོར། །ནག་ཆུང་གཟའ་བདུན་ཟས་འཚོལ་བ།» (vol. 2, p. 391; white-beryl-ch34.md section 11), and KP's line follows the Bird and Hare days with «ཀིང་ཟོར་ནག་དབུལ»: ཀིང་ = ཀི་ཀང, ཟོར = (ཕུང་པོའི་)ཟོར, ནག = ནག་ཆུང. KP writes the same two names out in full elsewhere in the same order of words (box 17, kp:i32: «བིཥྚི་ ཀི་ཀང་ … ཕུང་ཟོར»), and WB's work 8 and 12 verses say «ཕུང་ཟོར» alone. So the old answer (a contraction) stands and is now laid against WB's verse word by word. State: WB verse read on the scan earlier; KP line from the scan.
