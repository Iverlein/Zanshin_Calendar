# Does the «middle watch» (བར་མའི་ཐུན) of the earthquake omens and of the births mean the second watch, or the middle of the night; and is the fourth watch of the dream verse named anywhere in the tantra?

RD378.

## Answered 2026-11-13

By the order of the lines, the «middle watch» of the earthquake omens stands between «midnight» and «the end of midnight»: it is neither the second watch (that has its own ordinal, beginning and middle) nor named «the middle of the night» in the text; the text never defines it, and three copies of the lines (the Derge, Kun 'dus vol. 2 and a metrical digest in an astrological handbook) give no gloss. The fourth watch of the dream verse is not named by ordinal anywhere else in the volume: the last watch of the night is «the last» (ཐུན་ཐ་མ) at [p408] and [p430]. Claims mmk-ch18-middle-watch-sits-between-midnight-and-its-end-no-fourth-watch and dds-mmk-middle-watch-has-no-gloss-in-the-copies-and-digests-found. The reading of the births' «middle watch» (mmk.1:p430-432) is the same word and is not settled by this.
