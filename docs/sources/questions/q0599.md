# Earth in the own place's lists, p. 168

In WB vol. 2, p. 168, the own place's lists give earth the south-west (with the east) in the sick's and the demon's lines; is that the own place's earth too, and who stands there?

## Answered 2026-11-14

The south-west is not the own place's earth. The own place (རང་ས, rang sa) of WB vol. 2, p. 167–168 ends with «ཤིང་མེ་ས། ལྕགས་ཆུའི་འབྲས་བུ་གོང་ལྟར་བཤད» (the results of wood, fire, earth, iron and water as above), and the very next passage (p. 168–169, img. 174–175) opens a new one, «ལོངས་སྤྱོད་ནོར་དང་ནད་པའི་རྩིས» (the reckoning of wealth and of the sick), with the demon's list after it. Those lists are by the moment's element (དུས་ཚོད), each line opening «ས་དུས», «ས་ལ»: earth's sick one gets the kinsman «ཤར་རམ་ལྷོ་ནུབ» from the east or south-west, an old woman kinswoman, a grandchild or a guest; earth's demon is the earth lord (ས་བདག), the dge bsnyen or the oath-breaking gongpo «ལྷོ་ནུབ་ཤར་ཕྱོགས་ནས» from the south-west or east. The own place names earth only among the "results as above", the earth results of the preceding house, field and grave passages (earth lords, sores, leprosy), none of which carries a direction. So the own place has no seat for earth; the south-west belongs to the wealth, sick and demon lists of the moment's earth, and the people standing there are the old woman of the kin (sick) and the earth lords and gongpo (demon).
