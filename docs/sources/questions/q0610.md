# The images of p. 336's Mara days and their mansions

On p. 336 the Mara days name their mansions by images (the iron speckled bird, goat-heads, the empty measure, the golden chain), and the table of p. 341 gives Uttaraphalgunī 11, Āśleṣā 8, Pūrvāṣāḍhā 19, Śatabhiṣaj 23 for them: what is the reading that ties each image to its mansion?

## Answered 2026-11-06

RD177 (2026-11-06), etext: KD vol. 3 (p. 752) names the pairs Moon–Uttaraphalgunī 11 (nun), Mars–Āśleṣā 8 (general), Mercury–Pūrvāṣāḍhā 19 (barley-measurer), Jupiter–Mgo 4, Venus–Puṣya 7, Saturn–Śatabhiṣaj 23, Sun–Kṛttikā 2; this matches the p. 341 table. The images are the day's persons and attributes, not mansion shapes; only Jupiter's 'mgo' and Saturn's 'sgrog' (WB's own name for Śatabhiṣaj) carry the mansion's name.
