# The clauses on water, wind and the frost of entries 14, 42, 50 and the sheep year

the clause མཚོན་དུ་ཆུ་དང་ས་རླུང་ཆེ (entry 14, p. 206), ཚན་ཆུ་ཆེ (the sheep year, p. 209), ལྕོག་ལ་ཝ་ཆུ་འཛུགས (entries 42 and 50, p. 208) and ཏི་སེ་མ་ཕམ་ངན (entry 42 of the *Little Tiger-Ear*, p. 211): what do they say? (RD190, corpus/wb/ch18.md)

## Looked at 2026-11-14 (inv-m)

BDRC phrase search: «མཚོན་དུ་ཆུ་དང་ས་རླུང་ཆེ» 3 hits, «ལྕོག་ལ་ཝ་ཆུ་འཛུགས» 3, «ལྕོག་ལ་ཝ་ཆུ» 6, «ཚན་ཆུ་ཆེ» 4, «ཏི་སེ་མ་ཕམ་ངན» 3: all are copies of the White Beryl and of its commentaries and handbooks (the Do shal, the 'Dzam gling skye dgu'i spyi nor, the Skar rtsis 'byung rtsis zung sdeb, the Sgo bzhi mdzod lnga'i yang bcud), with the same clauses, none with a gloss; the Sgo bzhi copy reads «lcog la wa chu 'jug pa 'ong» for «'dzugs par 'gyur». The words, from Steinert's dictionaries:

- ཝ་ཆུ (*wa chu*) is not frost: Ives-Waldo «water coming from a gutter or ditch», *Tshig mdzod chen mo* «khang thog gi wa kha'i chu» (water of the roof's gutter) and «chu'i rka'am yur ba nas 'bab pa'i chu» (water falling from a crack or channel); ཝ (*wa kha*) is the roof's drain-pipe. ལྕོག (*lcog*) is the roof's top, a turret or corner (Valby, *Tshig mdzod chen mo*, *Dag tshig gsar bsgrigs* «khang klad dang thog kheb»). So «ལྕོག་ལ་ཝ་ཆུ་འཛུགས/འཇུག» is, in the words, "gutter water is set on or comes onto the roof": the roof's water-spout runs, a wet year, in step with «ནམ་སྨད་ཞོད» in entry 42; the pool's "frost-water" has no support. The clause's own sense is a reading of the words, no text states it.
- ཚན་ཆུ (*tshan chu*) has no headword; «chu tshan» is hot water, a hot spring (Rangjung Yeshe, Valby, Ives-Waldo; *Tshig mdzod chen mo* «rang bzhin gyis byung ba'i tsha chu», water naturally hot), and ཚན་ is warm or hot (Ives-Waldo, Negi *uṣṇa*): the pool's "great cold water" is unsupported; "much warm water" is what the words give, its sense for the year not stated.
- ཏི་སེ་མ་ཕམ (*ti se ma pham*) are Kailash (Ti se, Rangjung Yeshe, Valby, 84000) and Lake Mapham (*mtsho ma pham*, near Ti se, *Dag tshig gsar bsgrigs*; 84000: Mānasarovara), so "Ti se and Ma pham are bad" is the words; what is bad for them is not said.
- མཚོན་དུ་ཆུ་དང་ས་རླུང་ཆེ: མཚོན (*mtshon*) is a weapon only as «mtshon cha», alone also "to show, indicate, such as" (Ives-Waldo; Rangjung Yeshe «mtshon pa»); in this list «mtshon gyis» and «mtshon dang gri» stand for weapons (entries of the pool), which does not fit «chu dang sa rlung». ས་རླུང (*sa rlung*) is only the medical earth-wind (Valby), no weather sense. Not settled: "as a sign, water and earth-wind are strong" and "as to weapons" both fit the words.

Stays open for the first and the last clause; the second and third are settled as to their words.
