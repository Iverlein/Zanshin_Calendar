# The thirty parts given to the planets, p. 55

p. 55 (the thirty parts) assigns stretches of the quantity found to planets: «from form (1) to arrow (5)» to the lama, «from sky-eye to ten» to Saturn, «from fierce (11) to fault (18)» to Jupiter, «from hole-form (19) to that itself (25)» to Mercury, «from time-eye (29) to sky-fire (30)» to Venus, and a second list with Mars, Venus and Mercury for the equal signs. Is the order of the planets in the two lists as printed, and is the mapping to the stretches as read here (the page was read from the machine text, the stretches' figures not checked on the scan)? (RD188, corpus/wb/ch06.md)

## Answered 2026-11-13

The scan (RD206) gives the order and the stretches: unequal signs Mars 1-5, Saturn 6-10, Jupiter 11-18, Mercury 19-25, Venus 26-30; equal signs Venus 1-5, Mercury 6-12, Jupiter 13-20, Saturn 21-25, Mars 26-30. They are the *triṃśāṃśa* of the Bṛhat Parāśara Horā Śāstra 6.27 (5, 5, 8, 7, 5 in the odd signs, reversed in the even), and each list sums to 30. The question's first stretch to «the lama» was the machine's misreading; Mars (*mig dmar*) has it. Claim `wb-ch8-thirty-parts-planets`.
