# The thirty parts given to the planets, p. 55

p. 55 (the thirty parts) assigns stretches of the quantity found to planets: «from form (1) to arrow (5)» to the lama, «from sky-eye to ten» to Saturn, «from fierce (11) to fault (18)» to Jupiter, «from hole-form (19) to that itself (25)» to Mercury, «from time-eye (29) to sky-fire (30)» to Venus, and a second list with Mars, Venus and Mercury for the equal signs. Is the order of the planets in the two lists as printed, and is the mapping to the stretches as read here (the page was read from the machine text, the stretches' figures not checked on the scan)?
