# On the Derge, Narthang and Peking prints, is the bird of Amoghasiddhi's signs «བྱ་ཤར་ཏེ་རུ», «བྱ་ཤིང་ཏེའུ» or «བྱ་ཤང་ཏེའུ», and is it a bird sent as messenger or something else?

RD306; the Peking bird line was not located on img. 311, the Derge scan I1365 img. 261 is 2003×322 px with letters of about 8 px.


Narrowed (inv-ooo, 2026-11-14): the Peking is «བྱ་ཤིང་ཏེའུ» (scan, svi:p310b) and the Dpe bsdur ma's note gives the Narthang as «ཤང་ཏེའུ» after the shared «བྱ» (q953); the Derge «བྱ་ཤར་ཏེ་རུ» rests on the etext and the machine, its 8 px scan not read by eye. It is a bird (all open with «བྱ»), sending a messenger; the dictionaries give «bya shang shang te'u» (jīvaṃjīvaka) as a headword and none for «shar te ru», so the three are best read as one bird's name (q913). What the Derge scan and the Narthang hand show letter by letter stays scan-bound.
