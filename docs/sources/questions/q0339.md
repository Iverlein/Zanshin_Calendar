# «ལུག་ལོ་ཁོ་ན་ཤི» among the belly strikes

The notes print «ལུག་ལོ་ཁོ་ན་ཤི» (WB vol. 2, p. 506, l. 11, I3CN5029 img. 512; wb.2:p504-512) among the demon's belly strikes, where the root text (p. 86) has a sheep dying on *khon*. Is «ཁོ་ན» a typesetting of «ཁོན་ཤི» ("a sheep-year one dying on *khon*"), or "the sheep year alone"?

## Answered 2026-11-01

A typesetting of «ཁོན་ཤི» (a sheep year dying on *khon*): the eight of the belly strike are each sign on its own trigram, which the root text's «ལུག་ལོ་ཁོན་ལ་ཤི་བ་སོགས» says and LT repeats (claim dead-belly-strike-khon-typeset).
