# Does the Tibetan tradition hold a dream seen on the night of the tenth or fifteenth lunar day to be better, truer or more worth telling than one on another night? Nine of the thirteen hits of «ཚེས་བཅུའི་ནུབ་མོ་རྨི་ལམ» and «ཚེས་བཅོ་ལྔའི་ནུབ་མོ་རྨི་ལམ» are visions on those nights, mostly of Orgyan or a dākinī; no sentence states a rule.

RD367.

Narrowed 2026-11-13: a sentence that does tie the tenth to visions exists, in the treasure of Rtsa gsum gling pa and not in the BDRC search's hits: «དངོས་སུ་ལུང་བསྟན་ཚེས་བཅུར་དག་སྣང་མཇལ», "on the tenth, meet the pure vision" (tsl.27:i221), and the editor's prose that the tenth is a time of special quality when the fortunate meet the deity in person, in experience or in dream (tsl.27:i225). It says visions come then, not that a dream of the tenth is truer than one of another night, and the editor's prose is a modern author's (facts only). The fifteenth has no such sentence in vols. 27, 29, 30 (the etexts' «ཚེས་བཅོ་ལྔ» hits are dates of drawing-out and writing). See claim tsl-tenth-day-is-the-series-of-the-dakini-gatherings.
