# The numeral in box 1 of the model almanac's 11th month

Box 1 of the 11th month of the model almanac carries the numeral 7 under the trigram li, while WB vol. 1 p. 178 begins the first month of a season on the white 1 and the tiger month on li. Is the numeral in the box the sme ba of the day, or another count? (RD196, corpus/wb/ch13.md)

## Answered 2026-11-14

Answered from KD vol. 1 (rd-kd1, 2026-11-14, etext): in Tshul khrims rgyal mtshan's explanation of a day's cell in a printed Lhasa almanac, the trigram letter (here li) is the date's spar kha of the eight and the numeral beneath it is the date's mewa (སྨེ་བ), «of the nine mewa one turns with each day» (kd.1:p495a, the 4 green of the 1st of the 3rd month of a wood pig year). So the 7 under li in box 1 of the model almanac's 11th month is the sme ba of that day, not a count of another kind; it need not agree with the mewa the first month of a season begins on (WB vol. 1 p. 178), which is the month's, not the day's. The identity is taken from the explanation of a modern descendant of the model almanac, whose entries follow the *Vaiḍūrya dkar po* (kd.1:p495); it is not stated in WB's own text. See the claim kd-almanac-day-entry-numeral-under-trigram-is-the-mewa.
