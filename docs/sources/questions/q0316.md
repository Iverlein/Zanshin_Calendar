# The mansions of the sounding's examination under other names

The mansions of the sounding's examination (p. 92) are given under names not in WB's list of 28 (རྟ་ཆེན, རྟ་ཆུང, བྱ་འུ, ལྡེ་འུ, ནོར་ཕྱུགས, བྱ་སྤོ, བྲ་ཉེ, with མགོ, ཁྲུམས་སྟོད, ཁྲུམས་སྨད, སྐག): which mansions are these?

**Narrowed 2026-11-05 (RD164, [reckonings.md](../reckonings.md)).** *Nor phyugs* is probably gro bzhin, by WB p. 121. *Bya 'u* undecided.

**Narrowed further 2026-11-14 (inv-mmm).** *Bya 'u* (བྱ་འུ) is a mansion name in three dictionaries: «སྐར་མ་ནག་པ» ("the star nag pa", *Tshig mdzod chen mo*, Ives Waldo, Valby: one of the 28), nag pa being Citrā, the thirteenth, which fits the verse's slot between 10 and 16 that the claim found for it. *Rta chung* is «སྐར་མ་གྲེ» (the same, so the claim's gre) and *lde'u* «སྐར་མ་སྒྲོན» (so the claim). The same dictionary says *bya sbo* is «སྐར་མ་གྲོ་བཞིན» (gro bzhin, Śravaṇa), not byi bzhin, which the dictionaries enter as a mansion of its own: then *nor phyugs* would stand at 22 or 23 (mon gre or mon gru), no longer gro bzhin. The dictionaries' synonym lists for mon gre and mon gru (*sgrog stod*, *thob ldan ma*, *dha ni shtha*, *nam mthong*; *sgrog smad*, *chu'i lha mo*, *thos mchog*) carry no "wealth". Left: *nor phyugs*, with the choice between byi bzhin and gro bzhin for *bya spo* (claims/dead-sounding-mansion-aliases.md). Dictionaries: Steinert's collection, sparse clone.
