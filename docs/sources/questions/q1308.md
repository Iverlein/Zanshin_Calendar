# WB vol. 2 p. 233 lines 24–25: the gnyan's moving times, 28th or 27th?

On the Gser thang reprint (img. 239, lines 24 and 25) the gnyan's moving times for the last month of summer and the last month of autumn read «ཉེར་བརྒྱད» (the 28th); the 1996 print's machine readings give «ཉེར་བདུན» (the 27th), and that scan (701 px) is too low to settle it. Which date does the printed WB give, and does the Zhol or Taikhang print settle it?
