# WB vol. 2 p. 233 lines 24–25: the gnyan's moving times, 28th or 27th?

On the Gser thang reprint (img. 239, lines 24 and 25) the gnyan's moving times for the last month of summer and the last month of autumn read «ཉེར་བརྒྱད» (the 28th); the 1996 print's machine readings give «ཉེར་བདུན» (the 27th), and that scan (701 px) is too low to settle it. Which date does the printed WB give, and does the Zhol or Taikhang print settle it?

## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-c)

The verse «དབྱར་གསུམ་བདུན་བཅུ་ཉེར་བརྒྱད/བདུན་རིམ། །སྟོན་གསུམ་དགུ་སྟོང་ཉེར་བརྒྱད/བདུན་ལ།» looked up in two other prints' etexts (BDRC OCR, tools/sources/locate.py). The Sakya Centre print (IE0OPI0BA3A0E0, image 338) reads «དབྱརགསུམབདུནཁསུཉརབདུནཡི།།སྟོནགསུམ་དགུ་སྟོཔཉེར་བདུནལ»: «བདུན» in both lines (27th, 27th). The Zhol print (IE0OPI7850CC9C, image 954) reads «དབྱརགསུམ་བདུན་བཅུཉེར་བདུན་པ ། ནགསུམ་དགུ་སྟོང་ཉེར་བདུལ»: «བདུན» in the summer line, «བདུལ» (a ན/ལ mis-OCR of བདུན, not བརྒྱད) in the autumn line. With the 1996 print's «ཉེར་བདུན» that makes three prints' texts for the 27th against the reprint's glyph «བརྒྱད» read by eye on a black bitmap; the Sde dge-derived Sakya Centre and the Zhol blocks being other printings than the reprint's, the 27th is the likelier date. It is an OCR witness, not a read page: the Zhol or Taikhang scan (BDRC I1KG1710, I5877-I5878) has to be looked at for «ཉེར་བརྒྱད/བདུན» before the question is answered. Left open.
