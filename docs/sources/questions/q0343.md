# Horse or bird in the thirteenth gate

The thirteenth gate is headed «འཕུང་རྟ་རྔ་ཞོན» but its result reads «འཕུང་བྱ་རྔ་ཞོན» (p. 171, read on the reprint): horse or bird?

## Answered 2026-10-23

Answered 2026-10-23 (RD46): the blocks themselves have the horse in the heading and the bird in the result line («འཕུང་བྱ» on the Taikhang); the disagreement is WB's own, not the setting's.
