# The wealth's places among the household's rows

The wealth's place (ནོར་ས, nor sa; p. 386, «ལྷ་ས་མི་སར་རེ་རེ་སྟེ། །ཁྱིམ་ས་དག་ལ་གཉིས་གཉིས་ཏེ།»; the notes, p. 491, «བཞི་ཁྱིམ་དང་ནོར་ས») is one place each in the god's and people's places and two in the house's; which pebbles of the rows are they, and what does a colour read there?
