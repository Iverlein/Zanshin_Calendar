# KD vol. 3 pp. 738-740: does the digest's order of the date's earth lords come from an earlier manual?

The digest groups the date's earth lords by kind of work, its only departure from WB's print order. Does an earlier manual (the Spor thang, the Moonbeams commentary) give that order? Related: q465, q550.

**Narrowed 2026-11-21 (sweep from inv-a).** Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-a). BDRC exact-phrase search of the digest's own lines: «ཁྲི་བརྩིག་བཤད་གསར་དམག་ལས་སྤང», «དབུལ་ལགོ་མཁར་ལས་མགོ་འཛུགས་དང», «ཉི་ནག་ངན་བྱེད་དྲག་ལས་ལ» and «ཟླ་བཤད་ནང་བཞིན་འཛེམ་པ་གཅེས» one hit each, KD (MW28845) alone. WB's lines «ངམས་ཤིང་དགེ་བཤེས་ཆེ་འདོན་གྱི … བཤད་གསར་མག་པ་བུ་སྲིང་ངན … བར་གནམ་ཧལ་ཁྱི་རྒྱུ་བའི་དུས» are in WB's prints, in the Vaiḍūrya dkar po's Do shal (three copies) and in 'Bro'i sbyin pa's 'Bras rtsis bar gsal li dwangs ma'i me long (MW1KG21827, 2006), all in WB's order (Ki kang, Ngams-shing, Bar-gnam). So no earlier manual with the digest's grouping was found: the grouping by works, and the Poor-hand's verse after Ngams-shing, appear only in KD. Not found is not proved absent (the Spor thang and the Moonbeams were not reached as etexts). Claim: kd-digest-date-lords-order-not-in-found-manuals.
