# Is Sprugtön's general verdict pebble outside the colophon's 75?

Is the verdict pebble that Sprugtön's last line lays 'as in general' over the sixteen limbs a pebble of its own, as the notes' «སྤྱིའི་ཁ་དམར་རྡེལ་གཅིག» is on p. 503, outside the colophon's 75? (RD179)

INV-1107 (2026-11-07), unchanged: nothing new settles it. WB's own total stands in the verse before the colophon (wb.2:p23-25, «དེ་སོགས་རྡེའུ་ཀུན་བསྡོམས་ན། བདུན་ཅུ་དོན་ལྔ་ཡོད་པ་སྟེ། ཀེག་རྩིས་རྡེའུ་དོན་ལྔར་བརྗོད»): seventy-five over 'these and the rest', no seventy-sixth pebble named. The Moonbeams (I1LT2427 img. 588, machine; the same verse as dz:i050-051) calls its verdicts' sum «ཁ་དམར་སྤྱི་སྡོམ་ལྔ», five totals, which are sums of verdicts and not extra pebbles: a parallel for reading Sprugtön's «ཁ་དམར་སྤྱི» as a general laying rather than a pebble of its own, not a proof. Open.
