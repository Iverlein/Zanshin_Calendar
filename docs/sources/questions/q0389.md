# The illness's first four pebbles: the regent's trigram elements or the notes' life force

For the illness's five pebbles (WB vol. 1, p. 435), the regent's reply 133 names the four times' trigram elements («དེ་བཞིའི་སྤར་ཁམས») and the notes (wb.2:p497) their life-force elements, each with the trigrams' commonest element as the fifth: which are the first four pebbles?

## Answered 2026-10-25

**Mostly answered 2026-10-25 ([claims/illness-first-four-pebbles-life-force.md](../claims/illness-first-four-pebbles-life-force.md), [claims/illness-trigram-of-own-month.md](../claims/illness-trigram-of-own-month.md)).** «སྲོག་ཁམས་བཞི» stands in every print of WB p. 435 (the 1996 print, the Gser thang reprint, the Zhol and Taikhang blocks on the scan, img. 577 and 594; the Sde dge print in the etext), and Mipham reads it as four life-force pebbles and the commonest trigram element as the fifth (wb.2:p497). Reply 133's question quotes WB's line, and the doubt the reply answers is only whether an inner reckoning's "illness of the four times" means medicine's seasons; its closing «དེ་བཞིའི་སྤར་ཁམས … བསྣན་པའི་ལྔ» sums up that inner reckoning, which the regent says does not conflict with WB. By rank WB's words and the notes stand: the first four pebbles are the life-force elements of the year, month, day and hour of falling ill. «རང་ཟླར་ཚེས་པའི་སྤར་ཁ» points to WB's almanac rule (p. 178; the notes, p. 535; LT; the *Moonbeams*): in tiger, horse and dog months the first date begins with li, the day's trigram is counted on from the trigram its own month begins with, and the hour's runs on from the day's. Whether tiger, horse and dog also give li for the year itself is question 390.
