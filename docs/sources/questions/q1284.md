# Which sūtras are the «གནས་འཇོག་གི་མདོ» and the «གདགས་པའི་མདོ» that Zhe chen rgyal tshab cites for the benefits of the one-day vow, and is the second the Lokaprajñapti?

Raised by the practice-days investigation (pd-i, 2026-11-16); see the cited passages.

## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-w)

BDRC exact-phrase search of «གནས་འཇོག་གི་མདོ་ལས» gives 99 hits, among them the Treasury of Precious Instructions (MW23605, Shechen 1999) and Padma dkar po's works (MW10736), which quote the sūtra for the benefits of the one-day vow ('gnas 'jog gi mdo las': a countless-rivers-and-oceans merit comparison); «གདགས་པའི་མདོ་ལས་» gives 21 hits, among them Klong chen pa's works (MW1KG4884: 'jig rten gdags pa'i mdo las, on the abodes of the asuras, which is the Lokaprajñapti's Tibetan title «འཇིག་རྟེན་གདགས་པ»). The identification of the second with the Lokaprajñapti is therefore plausible by the title's variant spelling, but the Gnas 'jog gi mdo's own Kangyur title was not found and the quoted passages were not compared with a Kangyur text. Narrowed, not answered.

**Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-d).** The first sūtra is identified. BDRC's Kangyur records (Derge MW4CZ5369_0333, also Urga, Lithang, Narthang, Peking, Phudrak, Dpe bsdur ma) carry «'phags pa gnas 'jog gi mdo zhes bya ba», other title «gnas mchog gi mdo», 20 ślokas, translated by the Indian preceptor Sarvajñādeva and the translator Bande Dpal brtsegs and others; Derge text no. 333 (BDRC's WA0RK0333), vol. 72, pp. 531-540. Its etext (VE4CZ5369_I1KG9198, read in full, etext only) is the Buddha's teaching to the Brahmin Gnas 'jog at Kapilavastu on the eight-limbed one-day vow (བསྙེན་གནས་ཡན་ལག་བརྒྱད་པ): its merit cannot be measured by a count, as the five great rivers (Ganges, Yamunā and the rest) and the great ocean cannot be measured by the pitcher; and the gods' lifespans are given for each heaven, from the Four Great Kings up to the Paranirmitavaśavartins, with the words «keeping it once» (ལན་ཅིག་གནས་པ) for each, so Zhe chen's lines on the rivers, the ocean and the gods match it. His arhat-and-jewels comparison and the one-to-six heavens by number of vows were not found in these pages (the sūtra gives each heaven's lifespan after «once»). The second, the «Gdags pa'i mdo», is not found by title in the Derge catalogue under this spelling; still unidentified beyond the earlier Lokaprajñapti guess. Narrowed: first answered, second open.
