# The Vaiḍūrya line on the moment's trigram: year or month, and the later dates

In reply 157 (ys.2:i016-017, I5876 img. 17, ll. 3–4) the *Vaiḍūrya* volume of the *Speckled Elements* has «སྟག་རྟ་ཁྱི་གསུམ་ལོ་ལ་སྤར་ཁ་ལི་ཡིན་པས་ཟླ་བ་ཡང་ལི། ཚེས་ཀྱང་གཅིག་ལ་ལི།»: are the tiger, horse and dog here the animals of the year (so that the first month's first date begins li only in those years), or of the month, as the notes (WB vol. 2, p. 535) have «སྟག་རྟ་ཁྱི་གསུམ་ཟླ་བ་ལི་ཡིས་ཚེས», and what does the trigram do on the dates after the first?

**Narrowed 2026-10-25 (RD52, [claims/moment-trigram-month-and-the-later-dates.md](../claims/moment-trigram-month-and-the-later-dates.md)).** The notes (wb.2:p535) and WB's almanac rule (vol. 1, p. 178) take the tiger, horse and dog triad as the month's; the trigram then turns forward date by date and the eight parts of the day in the same order. The *Vaiḍūrya*'s «ལོ་ལ» stays a doubt: read as the year it would make the first month li only in that triad's years, which no other text says. No print of the *Vaiḍūrya* volume or the *Speckled Sheet* with the clause was found.

**Re-read 2026-11-01 (RD104).** One point more from the reply's own sentence: the *Speckled Sheet* clause it quotes first, «སྟག་རྟ་ཁྱི་གསུམ་ལི་ཡིས་ཚེས་ཤིང་། ཚེས་པའི་ཞག་གི་ཐོག་མའི་དུས་ཀྱང་ལི་ཡིན», is built like the *General Tantra* clause the question restates, «དཔྱིད་གསུམ་དབྱར་གསུམ་ལི་ཡིས་ཚེས་ཤིང་། དུས་ཚོད་ནམ་ལངས་ལི» (the three spring and the three summer [months] begin their dates with li; the hour of dawn li): the triad stands where the tantra has the months, which agrees with the notes' month. The *Vaiḍūrya*'s «ལོ་ལ» is the one word against it and stays a doubt; the Yigdzin-1 reading of img. 17 agrees with the scan in every quoted clause (the scan's first letter of «སྤར་ཁ» still not told from «སྦར་ཁ»).
