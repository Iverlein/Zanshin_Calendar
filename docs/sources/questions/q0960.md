# Does the Drathang print of ch. 81 carry a clause on the dream in the second way's line, as the Serthang, Derge and Chag-po-ri do not?

RD325.

## Answered 2026-11-10

RD356: the Drathang's printed line of the second way («ལོངས་སྤྱོད་རེ་འདོད་ཚུལ་གྱིས་ཞུགས་པ་དང», GT I4CZ327131 img. 463) has no dream clause; the print carries many small hand notes that do not resolve at 2658 px, so a hand-written clause can be neither shown nor excluded (new question).
