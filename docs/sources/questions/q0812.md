# Are the four watches of the night in WB vol. 1 p. 101 equal, and what is the unit of their planets' stretches (ཆུ་ཚོད 5, 4, 6, 3, 7, 4, 1)?

A part of one watch or of the whole night.

## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-i)

Pool's own corpus read for the unit. Equal watches: two texts that count eight watches to the day-and-night make them equal eighths: the Mañjuśrī root tantra's 18th chapter, "four watches are the daytime, and the nights are shown likewise; being eight, day and night is reckoned" (claim mmk-watch-is-an-eighth-of-day-and-night, whose own unit, four chu tshod to a watch, does not fit its thirty to the day-and-night), and the Mchog dga' (kd.3:p32): sixty chu tshod to the day-and-night, five to each of twelve junctions, seven and a half to a watch (claim mchog-dga-dbyu-gu-and-chu-tshod-one-unit-sixty-to-the-day-and-night). On the Mchog dga's count four watches of the night are 30 chu tshod, the sum of WB's seven stretches (5+4+6+3+7+4+1, our arithmetic), so the stretches fit the night as a whole and not one watch (Jupiter's seven would not stand inside half a watch of 3.75); but WB p. 101 states no sixty to the day-and-night, so this is the Mchog dga's unit applied to WB, not WB's word.
