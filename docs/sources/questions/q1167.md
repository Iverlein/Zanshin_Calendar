# What is «མིང་པོའི་ཚོགས་འཁོར» in tsl.30:i287 (the gathering of the Ming po): a place, a lineage or a gathering name?

Raised by the 2026-11-13 investigation a; see the question's cited passages.

## Answered 2026-11-14

Neither a place nor a lineage: «མིང་པོ» (ming po) is "brother", and the gathering is the brother's feast gathering, the author's own.

In the same life the dākinīs call the writer «མིང་པོ» ("the brother", they being his «སྲིང་མོ», sisters): «མིང་པོ་བྱུང་ཟེར་དགའ་བ་བགྱིས་ནས» ("'the brother has come', they rejoiced", p. 68), «མིང་པོ་འགྲོའོ་ཟེར་ནས་ལག་པས་བཟུང་» (p. 224), «མིང་པོ་འདི་ཉིད་གདན་འདྲེན་བཏང་བ་སྟེ» (p. 231), and just before the line asked about, «ད་ལན་མིང་པོས་གཏང་རག་མ་གནང་ཀྱང་། མི་རིང་བ་རུ་ལན་གཅིག་འོང་དགོས་ཡོད།» ("though the brother gave no parting gift this time, we must come once again soon", p. 287); the dictionaries (Rangjung Yeshe, Jim Valby, the Tshig mdzod chen mo: «ཕ་མ་གཅིག་པའི་བུ་རྒན་གྲས», a brother of the same parents) give no other sense. «ཚོགས་འཁོར» is the feast gathering (gaṇacakra; the Tshig mdzod: short for «ཚོགས་ཀྱི་འཁོར་ལོ»). So «མིང་པོའི་ཚོགས་འཁོར་དུས་སུ་སླེབས་ནས་འོང་» (tsl.30:i287) is "we come, at the time of the brother's feast gathering": the dākinīs say that they come at the tenth and other good times to the writer's own gathering. The same scene's earlier uses of «ཚོགས་འཁོར» (pp. 68-69, 98) are the dākinīs' own feasts at which he is received as the brother. No text was found that names a «མིང་པོ» assembly as a place or a lineage; BDRC phrase «མིང་པོའི་ཚོགས་འཁོར» gave only the Gter chos itself in the etexts searched. Read in the etext; the passage is not read on the scan.
