# The length of the day sign by sign, p. 51

p. 51 gives the length of the day sign by sign in hours (*chu tshod*) and weights (*srang*) in number-words with bracketed figures (30 for the Fish and the Maiden, then 10, 32, 33, 35, 46 and 36 beside the other signs). How do the number-word strings and the figures fall on the twelve signs, and what is the table that the figures stand for? The figures are as machine-read and not checked on the scan. (RD188, corpus/wb/ch06.md)

## Answered 2026-11-13

The scan of p. 51 (RD206) gives the day-length as hours over *srang*: Pisces and Virgo 30; Aries and Leo 31;10; Taurus and Cancer 32;20; Gemini 33;30; Aquarius and Libra 28;50; Capricorn and Scorpio 27;40; Sagittarius 26;30. The machine's single figures 35, 46, 36 and the question's «10, 32, 33» were halves of these pairs. The signs fall by the symmetry about Gemini and Sagittarius, the steps being 1;10 and the pairs summing to 60; «འཁྲིག་པ» is Gemini and «སྲང» in the verse names Libra, but the 32;20 line's «སྲང» before the Crab stands for Taurus. Claim `wb-ch8-day-length-by-house`.
