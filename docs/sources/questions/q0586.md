# «སུམ་ཆ» in the notes' example and the spirits-and-conditions share

In the notes' example (vol. 2, p. 497) fire and wood each hold two of seven and iron three; is «སུམ་ཆ» there a rough share, and is the "spirits and conditions" of «གསུམ་ཆ་སུམ་གཉིས» a share's illness joined to a humoral one or a different cause?

RD167 (2026-11-06), first half answered: in p. 497's example (wood 2, fire 2, iron 3) «སུམ་ཆ» is a rough third, the iron 'a third and a little stronger'. The spirits-and-conditions half is not told by the notes.
