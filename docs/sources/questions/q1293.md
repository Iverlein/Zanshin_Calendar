# Is «འབུམ་ཚར་ཉི་ཤུ་བཏོན་པར་འགྱུར» twenty hundred-thousands of recitations of the Heart Sūtra or twenty recitations of the Hundred Thousand, and is the speaker «ཇོ་བོ་རྗེ་དཔོན་སློབ» (Rin chen gter mdzod) or «རྡོ་རྗེ་དཔོན་སློབ» (the Collected Works)?

Raised by the practice-days investigation (pd-h, 2026-11-16); see the cited passages.

## Answered 2026-11-24

Answered from BDRC etexts (snippets and full texts, no scan): the speaker is the Lord (ཇོ་བོ་རྗེ, jo bo rje), Atiśa, in the Rin chen gter mdzod and the Mdo khams gnas yig, and «དཔལ་ཨ་ཏི་ཤས» in two more; «རྡོ་རྗེ་དཔོན་སློབ» of the Collected Works stands alone. «འབུམ་ཚར» is a full round of the 'Bum, so the Heart Sūtra once recited equals twenty complete readings of the Hundred Thousand.
