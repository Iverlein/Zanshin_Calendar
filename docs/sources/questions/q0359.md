# The pillow-guard among the five people the sick need

P. 445's five people needed (nurse by the mother, physician by the son, messenger by the friend, the pillow-guard «སྔས་བསྲུང་པ»): by which relation of the sick person's life force is the pillow-guard chosen, and is «མཐུན་གསུམ་རང་དང་ལྔ» "the three in harmony and oneself", making five with the four named?

**In part 2026-10-25 (RD52, [claims/illness-five-people-pillow-guard.md](../claims/illness-five-people-pillow-guard.md)).** Reply 135 (ys:i492-493, machine) reckons the five people by the life force's element as the chief, the nurse the mother's attendant and the physician the son, "and so on", after Duhar Nagpo's plank-tree instruction. No text found names the pillow-guard's relation; «མཐུན་གསུམ་རང་དང་ལྔ» is glossed nowhere, and «མཐུན་གསུམ» elsewhere is the triad of three year animals (Dpal bsdus p. 43).

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-ccc).** The second claim, [claims/illness-five-people-life-force-gloss.md](../claims/illness-five-people-life-force-gloss.md) (RD, 2026-11-01), is the nearest answer and the question did not cite it: Khyentse Wangpo's and Takla Norbu's medical manuals quote the verse and add «ཞེས་ནད་པའི་སྲོག་གི་མ་བུ་དགྲ་གྲོགས་ལ་བྱའོ», "this is done with the mother, son, enemy and friend of the sick person's life force". WB names mother, son and friend for the nurse, physician and messenger, so the fourth relation, the enemy, falls to the pillow-guard by count, an inference since the gloss does not pair them. Searched again 2026-11-14 for the other spellings: BDRC «སྔས་བསྲུང་བ» 4 hits (the same Khyentse and Takla manual MW1KG8532; a house-blessing rite text in Mi pham's collected works, where «phyag mtshan sngas bsrung ba» stands in a list of protective things, sense not read; two lists of rites with «nad pa'i sngas bsrung ba», "the sick person's pillow-guarding", a rite and not a person), «སྔས་བསྲུང་ལ» 1 (the biography of A lce Rig stong rgyal mo: a great practitioner is set to guard a sick rich man at his pillow, in the Wylie snippet), «དགྲ་ལ་སྔས་བསྲུང» 0. So the word is attested for a person set to watch at a sick man's pillow and for a rite of the sick person's pillow-guarding; no text pairs him with a relation, and «མཐུན་གསུམ་རང་དང་ལྔ» is glossed nowhere (4 hits, all quoting the verse). Open: the pairing (inferred: the enemy) and the last line.
