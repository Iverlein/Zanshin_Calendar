# «ཡབ་སྐོར་ཡུམ་སྐོར་མེད་པར་སྐོར་བ» in the wheel text under Karma Chagme

What does «ཡབ་སྐོར་ཡུམ་སྐོར་མེད་པར་སྐོར་བ» mean in the wheel text catalogued under Karma Chagme, and what is «བར་སྐོར་ལ་བར་རྒྱུར་བྱེད་པ»?

RD171 (2026-11-06), narrowed: ཡབ་སྐོར་ཡུམ་སྐོར་ is the pair of q614, བར་སྐོར་ the middle wheel; a second sense, 'father cycle and mother cycle', is in the Bde mchog oral lineage. «བར་རྒྱུར་བྱེད་པ» occurs once (Karma Chagme text), in no dictionary.

**Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-x).** The first phrase is explained by q614's answer: the commentary on Karma Chagme's prayer (Glag bla Bsod nams chos 'grub, MW3CN4729) says that in a wheel with rims («འཁོར་ལོ་མུ་ཁྱུད་མ») there are a father cycle and a mother cycle, two ways of laying the written mantra (letters' face down, mantra head and letter head out, tail in; letters' face up, head in, tail out), and that a wheel laid wrongly goes in a backward going and brings fault instead of benefit. «ཡབ་སྐོར་ཡུམ་སྐོར་མེད་པར་སྐོར་བ» in Karma Chagme's own list of faults of one who turns a wheel (MW1KG11869, etext I1KG18520, snippet read at 1 hit each for «བར་རྒྱུར་བྱེད» and «བར་སྐོར་ལ་བར་རྒྱུར») stands beside «ཕྱིན་ཅི་ལོག་ཏུ་བསྐོར་བ» (turning backwards) and is then turning a wheel whose mantra is not laid in the father and mother cycle, so that the turning goes against the reading. «བར་སྐོར་ལ་བར་རྒྱུར་བྱེད་པ» is still open: «བར་སྐོར» stands as the middle wheel in the Ras chung snyan brgyud («ཡབ་འཁོར་ཡུམ་འཁོར། བར་འཁོར»), «བར་རྒྱུར» in no other text («བར་རྒྱུར་བྱེད» 1 hit, only this one); a guess (doing something in between, interrupting the middle wheel) is not a gloss. «ཡབ་སྐོར་ཡུམ་སྐོར» also stands in the title of a Bde mchog snyan brgyud praise («yab skor yum skor gyi bstod pa», 6 hits), the oral-lineage sense of q617's earlier note.
