# Where does earth stand in one's own place?

Where does earth stand in one's own place (WB vol. 2, p. 168; the notes, p. 536)? The notes' example of an iron snake moment places wood east, fire south, iron west and water north; no element of it is earth. Built (ROADMAP R2z) with earth in the centre, where the sme ba square sets the five yellow; the intermediate directions of the earth trigrams are the other reading.

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-fff, from RD145, [reckonings.md](../reckonings.md), *Earth in one's own place*; claim moment-earth-has-no-centre-in-lists).** WB's own-place lines (img. 174, ll. 5-6) put the father's side by the life force, the maternal uncle by the body, the maid by the friend, the lord by the luck, and name earth only among "the results of wood, fire, earth, iron and water as above"; no line seats a person on it. Where WB gives earth a direction, in the sick's and the demon's lists (img. 174-175), it is «ལྷོ་ནུབ» (south-west) with the east. No line puts earth in the centre; the build's centre is unsupported, the south-west has two lines.
