# What is «ཉེར་ཐམ» in Zhechen Gyaltsab's sums of the meadow's ornament

In Zhechen Gyaltsab's sums of the meadow's ornament (zg:p300-301, scan img. 344-345), what is «ཉེར་ཐམ» in «བརྒྱ་དང་ཉེར་ཐམ» ("a hundred and nyer tham") for the gold and the conch years, against «བརྒྱ་དང་ཉི་ཤུ» for the turquoise: is it twenty, so that each year comes to 120 and the three to 360?

## Answered 2026-10-28

Yes. The scan of Zhechen Gyaltsab prints «བརྒྱ་དང་ཉེར་ཐམ» for the gold year and the conch year and «བརྒྱ་དང་ཉི་ཤུའོ» for the turquoise year; «ཉེར་ཐམ» is "twenty exactly" in the verse's form, so each year comes to 120 and the three to 360. Witnesses in claims/zg-nyer-tham-is-twenty.md (RD70).
