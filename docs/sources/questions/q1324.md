# WB ch. 3 p. 22: is the third glyph of the figures printed 2619 and 2613 a ༡ set in the ༤ form, or a typesetting slip for 2649 and 2643?

The glyph is the same crescent shape as the ༤ of 2648, 2647 and of «(༢༤༠)» on p. 26; the chain wants ༡ in 2619 and 2613. The text keeps ༢༦༡༩ and ༢༦༡༣ by the chain.

## Answered 2026-11-21

The figures are 2619 and 2613, not 2649 and 2643.

Checked 2026-11-21 (rd-t), by the page's own chain and not by the glyph. The verse steps down by number-words: «དེ་ལ་གདེངས་མིག(༢༨)ཕྲི་བ» from 2647, «དེ་ལ་མཚམས(༦)སྦྱངས» and «དེ་ལ་གཟུགས་མིག(༢༡)ཕྲི་བ». The word-numerals are 28 (gdengs 8, mig 2), 6 (mtshams) and 21 (gzugs 1, mig 2; written lowest place first, as in ch. 19's «མཚམས་ཁྱིམ(༡༢༦)»). The ends of the chain are printed plainly: 2647 above and «(༢༥༩༢)» (2592) below, and 2647 − 28 − 6 − 21 = 2592 exactly. So the two figures between them are 2619 (2647 − 28) and 2613 (2619 − 6, and 2613 − 21 = 2592 again). With a ༤ in the third place they would be 2649 and 2643, which no step of the chain gives (2649 would be above 2647, and 2643 − 21 = 2622, not 2592). The year of the first teaching, 2613, and the 2567 two steps later (2592 − 25, «མདའ་མིག(༢༥)», p. 23) agree.

What the scan cannot say is whether the printer's glyph is a ༡ drawn in the ༤ form or a slip of type; that does not change the number, which is the chain's. The text of the passage stays «༢༦༡༩» and «༢༦༡༣».
