# Who is Thub bstan chos kyi grags pa, author of the Tshe thar srog bslu'i phan yon (BDRC: D+hAr+ma kIr+ti Kun bzang bsod nams, Mi nyag Kun bsod), and when did he live? Until then only its rule-carrying sentences are quoted.

Raised by the practice-days investigation (pd-i, 2026-11-16); see the cited passages.

## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-w)

The author is Kun bzang bsod nams of Go shul (མི་ཉག་གོ་ཤུལ་བླ་མ་ཀུན་བཟང་བསོད་ནམས, mi nyag go shul bla ma kun bzang bsod nams), who took the name Thub bstan chos kyi grags pa at his full ordination before Gam mtsho skyes dgyes pa Padma bde ba rtsal (མཚན་ཐུབ་བསྟན་ཆོས་ཀྱི་གྲགས་པ་ཞེས་གསོལ), studied the Bodhicaryāvatāra and the instructions with Dpal sprul Rdo rje 'chang, and was close to Mkhyen brtse and Mi pham. Born in the water-sheep year of the fourteenth sixty-year cycle, which the etext prints as 1823 (the digits are split «༡༨༢ ༣»), in Mi nyag. Source: Thub bstan klong chen rdo rje's «དཔེ་རྒྱུན་དཀོན་པའི་བླ་མ་འགའི་རྣམ་ཐར་དང་གནས་རི་ངོ་སྤྲོད་མདོར་བསྡུས་གསེར་གྱི་མེ་ལོང་» (BDRC MW1KG14784, etext VEIE0OPIFF4FB170_I1KG14883, pp. 9-15); the death year was not found in the pages read (to p. 15). He is the commentator of the Bodhicaryāvatāra commentary MW3CN4807 / MW15659 (author d+hAr+ma kir+ti kun bzang bsod nams). The Dharma kIrti form is the BDRC name; Go shul is his seat. Identity settled, death year open; a 20th-century compilation, not a primary source for the dates.

**Narrowed 2026-11-21 (sweep from inv-w).** The author's identity is settled in the note above (Kun bzang bsod nams of Go shul, born in the water-sheep year of the fourteenth cycle, printed as 1823); his death year was not found in the pages read.
