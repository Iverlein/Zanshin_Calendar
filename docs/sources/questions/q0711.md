# ནམ་གཞུག in entries 21, 31 and 51

ནམ་གཞུག (*nam gzhug*) in entries 21, 31 and 51 (pp. 207–208). (RD190, corpus/wb/ch18.md)

## Looked at 2026-11-13 (inv-x)

The Gser thang etext (VEIE0OPI3864355D_I3CN5028) has «ནམ་གཞུག» in «གནག་ལེགས་ནམ་གཞུག་ཐུང་བ་དང་» and «ནམ་གཞུག་ཆར་ཆེ་ཀུན་མ་རྒོད» (two of the three places); no other spelling and no gloss found. Open.

## Answered 2026-11-14

ནམ་གཞུག (nam gzhug) is "the end of a season of the year (nam dus), mostly said of autumn" (Tshig mdzod chen mo, nam gzhug 1; Ives Waldo has the same); the dictionary's second sense, «གཏན་གྱི་ཕུགས», is not glossed. In entries 21 and 51 of the Sūtra of the Secret's list the word stands as «ནམ་གཞུག་ཆར་ཆེ» "heavy rain at nam gzhug" and «ནམ་གཞུག་ཐུང་བ» "nam gzhug short", a stretch of time that can be rainy or short, which fits the closing part of a season. So the word is read as the close of the season, autumn in the usual use.

Source: tmz:box6 (read 2026-11-14), wb.1:p207 and wb.1:p208 for the entries. Entry 31 was not re-read; the sense is the dictionary's, not a commentary's on WB.
