# What is the critical text of the 山家集 poem on the first dream, and its headnote?

年くれぬ春来べしとは思ひ寝に／思ひ寝む まさしく見えてかなふ初夢; is its headnote 立つ春の朝よみける? Its number and edition are unknown.

Searched 2026-11-09 (RD224), partly answered, left open for the number: the headnote is たつ春の朝よみける in the 日本古典文学大系 text as quoted by a blog, 立春の朝よみける in the Utakura etext; the fifth line is かなふ初夢 in all; the fourth-line reading is 思ひ寝に / 思ひ寐に in every etext reached, and 思ひ寝む only in Wikipedia (see skf:p1). Not found: the poem's number in any edition (Shinpen Kokka Taikan, 大系, 和歌文学大系 21). Dead ends: ja.wikisource has no 山家集; 千人万首 (asahi-net) selects neither the poem nor a number; the 国歌大観 database and 国文学研究資料館 pages are not open to search. The position differs (first of the spring book in the 大系 as the blog counts, seventh in Utakura), so the order of the book differs by text.

## Answered 2026-11-10

Answered by RD285: the poem reads, in the common text (日本歌学全書 vol. 8, 1891, first poem of the spring book, headnote 立春の朝よみける), 年くれぬ春來べしとハ思ひねにまさしく見えてかなふ初夢; the old text (異本山家集, 1906) has the headnote 春たつこゝろを and おもはねど in the third line. Beginning-of-spring morning belongs to the common text only. No printed edition number found; the prints do not number their poems.
