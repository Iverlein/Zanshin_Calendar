# Printed year-name variants against ch. 13's list (entries 39, 43, 58–60)

ཐིལ་གནོན (printed, entry 43, p. 208) or ཟིལ་གནོན (ch. 13's list, Overpowering)? Also དགའ་བྱེད for དགེ་བྱེད (entry 39), གདང་ངན for བློ་ངན (58), ཇ་ཆེན for རྔ་ཆེན (59), ཁྲག་སྨྱུག་པ for ཁྲག་སྐྱུག (60): are these the print's own variants, and which does the tradition use? (RD190, corpus/wb/ch18.md)

## Looked at 2026-11-13 (inv-x)

BDRC full-text search. «ཟིལ་གནོན་ཞེས་བྱ་མེ» 9 hits, among them the Gser thang etext (MW3CN5026), other White Beryl etexts (MW1KG1617, MW28851, MW25151) and works quoting the verse; «ཐིལ་གནོན་ཞེས་བྱ» 0. In the list of ch. 13 (the verse «ཐིལ་གནོན་སྤྲེའུ་ཕུར་བུ»): 36 hits for «ཟིལ་གནོན», one for ཐིལ་གནོན, the Gser thang etext's own OCR. So ཟིལ་གནོན (zil gnon) is the tradition's spelling and the printed ཐིལ is a misreading or a one-off. «དགའ་བྱེད་དཔལ་ལྡན་»: 6 hits, in the Gser thang etext and in MW2CZ8040, MW4CZ74207, MW28851, MW25151 and MW2PD17386_D19206; «དགེ་བྱེད་དཔལ་ལྡན» 0: the Sūtra-of-the-Secret list has དགའ་བྱེད here in every print found, so it is that list's own name (the དགེ་བྱེད of ch. 13 is another list's). Entry 58: the Gser thang etext and three other prints read «གདོང་ངན་ལྕགས་བྱ» (*gdong ngan*, 4 hits, «གདང་ངན» 0), so the pool's «གདང་ངན» is a misreading of གདོང་ངན; «བློ་ངན» belongs to the calendar lists of years (22 hits, other works). Entry 59: the Gser thang etext alone has «ཇ་ཆེན་ཆུ་ཁྱི་དཀར་མོ» (1 hit); MW28851 and MW25151 have «རྔ་ཆེན་ཆུ་ཁྱི་དཀར་མོ» (2): ཇ་ཆེན is this print's variant, རྔ་ཆེན the other prints' and the calendars' (26 hits). Entry 60: «ཁྲག་སྐྱུག་པ་ཞེས་ཆུ་མོ་ཕག» in the Gser thang etext and MW28851 (2 hits), «ཁྲག་སྨྱུག་པ» 0: the pool's སྨྱུག is a misreading. All from etexts (OCR) of the same and other prints; the scan of img. 205–211 is not yet read for ཐིལ, གདང/གདོང and སྨྱུག.
