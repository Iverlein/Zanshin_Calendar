# Is the seventh house from the sun's house, where the Mchog dga' puts the moon for a clear dream, counted with the first house as one?

RD361.

## Answered 2026-11-10

Answered from the etext (RD369), by the book's own use and not by a statement at that line. The Mchog dga' counts houses with the first as one everywhere else (from Aries the fifth is Leo, the ninth Sagittarius, the tenth Capricorn; the first, fourth, seventh and tenth are the square), so the seventh from the sun's house is the opposite sign; the clear dream is sun and moon in opposite signs.
