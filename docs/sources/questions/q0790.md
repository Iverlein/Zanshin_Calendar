# What does ཟ་ཟི in «རྨི་ལམ་ཟ་ཟི་མང» mean in the fever and pestilence chapters?

Taken from context as turmoil.


Narrowed (RD290, 2026-11-10): the Drikung commentary never glosses ཟ་ཟི but uses it about thirty times with the mind, the eye, speech, sleep and the search for the bla; its range is a wandering or confusion of the mind that shows in dream, sight and speech.

## Answered 2026-11-10

Answered by RD316 (2026-11-10). ཟ་ཟི has a sense of its own: unclear, confused, troubled, not a doubled sound for 'many' (the 'many' is the separate word མང). Jäschke p. 485 (citing the medical line itself, 'troubled dreams') and Das p. 1089, both on the page images, and five of Steinert's lexica give «རྨི་ལམ་ཟ་ཟི» as 'troubled dream'; no lexicon gives 'restless' or 'repeated'. The commentaries (Drikung, Khro ru, Blo bzang dbang rgyal's mchan brgyab, the Phyi ma rgyud 'grel ba phyogs bsgrigs) use it for mind, eye, speech, sleep and the search for the bla without defining it; two physicians gloss it by partner words (Sog po Lung rigs bstan dar's Lhan thabs: ཟ་ཟི་ནི་ཉག་ཉོག; Karma ratna's sman yig: ཟ་ཟི་ནི་ཆལ་ཆོལ་མང་, adding a clouded awareness). Mipham's notes were search-only, a weak negative. Claim za-zi-confused-not-many.
