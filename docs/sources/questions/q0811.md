# Is «ཉིན་མཚན་ཐུན་བརྒྱད» one division of the day and night into eight watches, or the day's and night's lengths each divided by eight?

As on p. 101 (four by day, four by night), or as the notes p. 535 divide for the trigrams.


Narrowed (RD287, 2026-11-10): p. 135 reads «ཉིན་མཚན་ཚད་ནི་བརྒྱད་ཀྱིས་བགོས» (བརྒྱད, the 1996 etext's བརྒྱུད is OCR error); vol. 2 p. 403 cuts the day and night into eight watches, notes p. 535 cut the day's length in eight and the night likewise; whether p. 403's eight is four and four stays open.
