# The Guru's names for the monkey, hare, snake, horse, dog, pig and mouse months differ between Kong sprul's prayer of the twelve tenths and Zhe chen rgyal tshab's summary, though both rest on Mgon po rin chen's treasure: which names does that treasure give?

Raised by the practice-days investigation (pd-f, 2026-11-16); see the cited passages.

## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-w)

Searched for the treasure's own list of the Guru's names for the twelve tenths: no published text beyond the two already cited was reached in this pass; stays open.

**Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-d).** New route: BDRC phrases «མགོན་པོ་རིན་ཆེན་གྱི་གཏེར་ཆོས» 0, «གཏེར་སྟོན་མགོན་པོ་རིན་ཆེན» 6, «མཚན་མཚོ་སྐྱེས་རྡོ་རྗེར་གྲགས་པའི» 1 (only Zhe chen's own collected works, MW1PD133163), «ཚེས་བཅུའི་ཕན་ཡོན་གྱི་གཞུང» 1 (the same). New fact: the Rin chen gter mdzod (MW1KG14, vol. 3, etext VE1KG14_003 near char. 1268467, in the preface to Padma 'od gsal mdo sngags gling pa's Bla ma sku bzhi cycle, which says its treasure was given him by the guardian Gnyan chen thang lha) states: «འབྲིང་པོ་ལྷོ་བྲག་གཏེར་སྟོན་མགོན་པོ་རིན་ཆེན་གྱིས་བཏོན་པ་དེང་སང་ཚེས་བཅུའི་ཕན་ཡོན་ཙམ་གྱི་དཔེ་རྒྱུན་ལས་མི་བཞུགས་ཤིང་» ('the middle one, taken out by the Lho brag treasure-revealer Mgon po rin chen, does not survive today beyond a tradition of the Benefits of the Tenth Day'). So the treasure's own text of the twelve names is not extant by that account; what is left is the Benefits text that Zhe chen summarises, and Kong sprul's prayer rests on it only through that. The same preface says another text of Padma 'od gsal's joins «the twelve changes of the body and the distinctions of the names» to the twelve months' tenths: a possible third list, not found. Narrowed; the question cannot be answered from the treasure itself.
