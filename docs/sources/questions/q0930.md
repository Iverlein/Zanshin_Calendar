# Is «ལེགས་པར་བཤད་པ་པདྨ་དཀར» at the end of SKP's second text a title or the signature of a person, and does the second text close so in the Mongolian copy NLM9419 and in MDP's copy (p. 138 ff.), whose end was not read?

RD315.

Narrowed (inv-kkk, 2026-11-14): the closing couplet has no carrier in BDRC's full-text search (the six phrases listed at q883, all 0). The other copies' ends differ: RST (MW1ALS06558E) closes with Klong rdol bla ma's colophon on p. 20, not this verse, and GDM breaks off with its last page lost (gdm.md); the end of MDP's copy (p. 138 ff., search-only etext) and of NLM9419 (GDR, img. 151-158, clear hand) was not compared with «པདྨ་དཀར»: no snippet of either end is reachable. Whether the verse is a title or a signature is therefore undecided; no second copy carries it, which tells slightly against a name the verse's own tradition knew.
