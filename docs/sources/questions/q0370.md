# «བསྒྲུབ» or «བསྣུབ» among the five graves

WB vol. 2 carves «བསྒྲུབ» in the list of the five graves, «སྲེག་སྐྱེལ་སྦ་ཞིང་བསྒྲུབ» (p. 124, l. 23), and again in «བསྒྲུབ་ཏུ་མི་རུང་བསྒྲུབ་པ་ན» (p. 125, l. 12), where the five graves of p. 74 want sinking (བསྣུབ). Is «བསྒྲུབ» a misprint for བསྣུབ, or another name for that grave (wb.2:p124-125)?
