# Which year the sixty fixed signs count

The fixed sixty-year signs of pp. 171–172 (iron male dog, earth female sheep and the rest): which year is counted from them, and to how many years (the asker's years lived, as in the thirteen gates)? Why do iron male dog and earth female sheep each appear twice with different harms?

**By analogy 2026-10-23 (RD45, [claims/illness-heads-years-lived.md](../claims/illness-heads-years-lived.md)).** The illness plank-tree's heads in the *Moonbeams* and LT count the person's years lived from fixed sixty-year signs (dz:i052-055), which suggests the moment's fixed signs count the asker's years lived; the doubled iron dog and earth sheep have no parallel there.
