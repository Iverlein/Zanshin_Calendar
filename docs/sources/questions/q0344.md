# Which year the sixty fixed signs count

The fixed sixty-year signs of pp. 171–172 (iron male dog, earth female sheep and the rest): which year is counted from them, and to how many years (the asker's years lived, as in the thirteen gates)? Why do iron male dog and earth female sheep each appear twice with different harms?

**By analogy 2026-10-23 (RD45, [claims/illness-heads-years-lived.md](../claims/illness-heads-years-lived.md)).** The illness plank-tree's heads in the *Moonbeams* and LT count the person's years lived from fixed sixty-year signs (dz:i052-055), which suggests the moment's fixed signs count the asker's years lived; the doubled iron dog and earth sheep have no parallel there.

**Narrowed 2026-10-23 (RD48, [claims/moment-gates-small-reckonings.md](../claims/moment-gates-small-reckonings.md)).** Reply 160 (ys.2:i018) makes everything after the thirteenth gate, from the sixty-year signs to the sky and earth doors, the gates' small reckonings, so the signs are counted as the gates are, by the asker's years lived; why two signs stand twice stays open.

**Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-d).** The pooled scan reading of pp. 171-172 (wb.2:p171-172) shows the doubled signs as different entries, told apart by the part of the sign named: the iron male dog «ལྕགས་ཕོ་ཁྱི་ཡི་ཐོག་ཉིད་ནས» (from its very top: the demon's door breaks) and «ལྕགས་ཕོ་ཁྱི་ནས་ཕོ་མོ་གཉིས» (from the iron male dog, male and female both: the white crystal man); the earth female sheep «ས་མོ་ལུག་གི་སྟེང་ཉིད་ནས» (from its very top: the demon-man will not sell) and «ས་མོ་ལུག་གི་ཁ་ནས» (from its mouth: the stone sheep), and a third, «ས་ལུག་ཕོ་མོ» (the eldest son's grudge). The other lines use the same «top» and «mouth» (earth pig «སྟེང་ནས», bird «ཁ་ནས», dragon «ཁ་ལས»). So the doubling is a different place in the sign, not a repeated entry; what the «top» and the «mouth» of a year are, and so which year the count reaches, no source read says. The notes' own account (notes-8, Q344: one entry belongs to an earlier gate) does not fit this reading, in which both iron-dog entries stand in the one list after the demon's reckoning. The BDRC etext of the *Vaidurya dkar po* (MW2CZ8040) has the preceding gates' numbered lines «pho sbub mo bken dus dang 'thab / tshur bcad …»; «ཕོ་སྦུབ་མོ་བཀེན་དུས་དང་འཐབ» 1 hit (that passage), «ཐོག་ཉིད་ནས་ཕོ་སྦུབ» 0.
