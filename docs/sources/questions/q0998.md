# Do Svapnādhyāya copies older than the 18th century, if any exist, read a month, half a month or three fortnights for the fourth watch?

RD357.

Narrowed (inv-rrr, 2026-11-14): the dated Svapnādhyāya copies found are all late: Leipzig (Aufrecht 1901) no. 1177 a manuscript of 1766 in 78 ślokas with a Hindi rendering by Gaṅgādhara, a different recension that begins «dhanvantari praṇamyādau svapnajñānaprahelikā» and of which the catalogue quotes only the first and last verses; no. 1178 about 1830 (88 ślokas); no. 1179 (the Brahmavaivarta's chapter) 18??; Penn Ms. Coll. 390 items are dated 1700-1850. None is older than the 18th century, and the catalogue quotes no watch verse from any, so no copy gives the span of the fourth watch. No older copy was found.
