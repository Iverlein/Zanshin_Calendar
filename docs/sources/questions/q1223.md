# KP box 56: is the order Lady, Khrag sgor, Tsa mundi, Vishnu a difference from WB, and the spellings «ཁྲག་སྒོར»/«ཙ་མུནྡི» against WB's «ཁྲག་ཟོར»/«ཙཱ་མུཀྟྲི»?

WB places the Lady, Khrag sgor and Tsa mundi after Vishnu's course times; KP puts them before (box 56 on the scan). WB's own order rests on the machine reading of ch. 34.

## Answered 2026-11-21

Yes, on both counts, and the difference is in WB's print, not only in its etext. WB vol. 2 p. 422 (scan I1KG12907 img. 430, the line read at 6×) has «ཁྱབ་འཇུག་རྒྱུ་དུས་བྱོལ་མདོས་དང་། །དབང་མོར་ཁྲག་ཟོར་ཙཱ་མུ…» (Vishnu's course times and the bird-ransom mdos, then the Lady, Khrag zor, Tsa mu[kti]), agreeing with the etext «ཙཱ་མུཀྟྲི»: the Lady's group follows Vishnu. KP's box 56 has the Lady, «ཁྲག་སྒོར», «ཙ་མུནྡི» before Vishnu (kp:box56; claim kp-box56-is-wb-work53-condensed), and a Bhutanese table manuscript prints the same order and «ཁྲག་སྒོར» (claim bhutan-table-book-parallels-kp-boxes), so it is the table tradition's own order and spelling; «ཙ་མུནྡི» is the regular rendering of Cāmuṇḍī, WB's «ཙཱ་མུཀྟྲི» is not. WB's ch. 34 stands above KP: nothing in the app changes.
