# KP boxes 37 and 38 name no earth lord; do WB's works 34 (bathing) and 35 (divination) name any?

Does KP drop them, or are they in the part of the page not read?

## Answered 2026-11-14

Neither. WB's work 34 (bathing) ends on the links of dependent arising with no earth lord, and work 35 (divination) ends «གཞན་མ་རྣམས་ལ་ཞི་དྲག་བསྟན» and the bad dates «དགུ་གསུམ་ཉ་སྟོང་ཚེས་བརྒྱད་ངན» (white-beryl-ch34.md, vol. 2 pp. 404-405). KP's box 38 ends «གཞན་རྣམ་ཞི་དྲག་བསྟུན» with no earth lord, so KP drops nothing; the earth lords come with work 36 (enthronement), whose run (the Se, Se ba, Pi ling, Tsang kun, Se byi, the earth's destroyer, the Black Woman's course) KP's box 39 digests. The bad half of box 37 on the scan (img. 46, 6×) is a faint impression with its lines of dates; nothing was read in it that is an earth lord.
