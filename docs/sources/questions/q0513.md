# The secret obstacle's «མ་སྤར»: which own trigrams take li and which kham

The secret obstacle (WB vol. 1, p. 390): the manuals' relatives make «མ་སྤར» the mother's trigram of one's own, kham for a tiger or hare's; which own trigrams have li and which kham for their «མ་སྤར», and what makes li the son's secret obstacle and kham the daughter's?

RD177 (2026-11-06), narrowed: by the generating cycle li is the mother of khon/gin (earth) and kham of zin/zon (wood). The p. 390 line reads three clauses: thief obstacle of one's own birth takes li, the son's kham, the daughter's 'hard to feel'; why, no text says.

## Answered 2026-11-14

By the generating cycle, as read from the manuals (claims/secret-obstacle-ma-spar-by-cycle.md; inv-yy found «མ་སྤར» to be the mother in the set of four relatives of one's own trigram in three manuals): li (fire) is the mother's trigram of an own khon or gin (earth), kham (water) of an own zin or zon (wood); the other four own trigrams have another mother (wood for li, earth for dwa and khen, metal for kham), so WB's two names fit an own trigram of earth or wood only. The question's second half has a wrong premise: p. 390's three clauses give li to the thief obstacle of the own birth («སྤར་སྟེང་སླེབ་དང་མ་སྤར་ལི»), kham to the son's («བུ་ལ་གསང་ཀེག་མ་སྤར་ཁམ») and «ཚོར་བ་དཀའ» to the daughter's («བུ་མོར་གསང་ཀེག»); why li for the first and kham for the son no text says (BDRC's search of 2026-11-14 for «མ་སྤར་ལི» finds only the manual I4717 again). The mother's trigram of the natal one rests on the manuals and the generating cycle, not on WB's own words; it is an inference.
