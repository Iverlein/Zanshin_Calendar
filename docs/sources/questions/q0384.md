# «དཀར་ནག་བསྔོ་མེད», «རྦད་ཁམ», «སྤང་གནས་ཀྱི་རྩིས་ལོག་མིན་པ»

What do «དཀར་ནག་བསྔོ་མེད» (reply 138, the speckled in the *thang shing*), «རྦད་ཁམ» (reply 140) and «སྤང་གནས་ཀྱི་རྩིས་ལོག་མིན་པ» (reply 141) mean?

**Narrowed 2026-11-01 (RD104, [claims/regent-words-bsngo-med-rbad-khams-spang-gnas.md](../claims/regent-words-bsngo-med-rbad-khams-spang-gnas.md)).** «དཀར་ནག་བསྔོ་མེད» is the regent's short form of WB's «དཀར་ནག་བསྔོ་རུ་མ་བཏུབ» (white and black that cannot be apportioned): the plank-tree's speckled result, which he calls bad. «རྦད་ཁམ» (read «རྦད་ཁམས» in the questioner's own request 94, ys:i087, «ང་མཁས་ང་བཙུན་གྱི་རྦད་ཁམས་སྒྲོག», "proclaim a swagger of 'I am learned, I am venerable'") is a boast or bluster: reply 140 says that none is needed since the matter is cleared. «སྤང་གནས་ཀྱི་རྩིས་ལོག་མིན་པ» is "not a wrong reckoning of what is to be rejected" (the reply goes on to «སྤང་བྱ་དངོས་མ་ཡིན་པ»): the small reckonings are not false ones. No dictionary was opened (the clone is not on the machine). Answered in this reading's sense, apart from the dictionary.

## Answered 2026-11-14

«རྦད་ཁམ» is boasting. Ives Waldo has «rbad kham: kha ham dang 'ud shob», boasting; the Tshig mdzod chen mo and the Tshig gter rgya mtsho give the same two words with the example «མི་ཤེས་ཤེས་རློམ་གྱི་རྦད་ཁམ་ཆེ་བའི་ཁ་རྗེས་སུ་འབྲང་མི་ཉན» (do not follow the talk of one who boasts of knowing what he does not know); Dan Martin (Btsan lha) and the Brda dkrol gser gyi me long (quoting the Blo sbyong brda don) have «rbad kham che ba» as «skad cha kha shom che», talk full of swagger. So the reply 140 sense of RD104 stands: a boast or swagger is not needed. This is a word in its own right, as the note of inv-w says: «རྦད་མགོ», the forehead fringe, is another. The other two phrases stay as RD104 read them («དཀར་ནག་བསྔོ་མེད» the regent's short form of WB's «བསྔོ་རུ་མ་བཏུབ», white and black that cannot be apportioned; «སྤང་གནས་ཀྱི་རྩིས་ལོག་མིན་པ» not a wrong reckoning of what is to be rejected).
