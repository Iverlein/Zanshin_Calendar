# The circle of the great thousand on p. 213, its full value

In ch. 19 (p. 213) the circle of the great thousand is given first in word-numerals and then in a long figure: «ནམ་མཁའ་མཁའ་རོ་བུག་རི་གཟུགས། དབང་མཚོ་བུག་དུས་མཚམས་སྐར་མ། འདོད་རོ་ཐུབ་པ་འཁོར་ལོ་ཀླུ»; what is its full value, and is the «འཁོར་ལོ» in it the digit 2 or part of the number? (RD185, corpus/wb/ch17.md)

## Narrowed 2026-11-13

The number-words give the digits from the lowest up, with «འདོད» 13 (the print's «འདོད(༡༣)» on p. 192), «སྐར་མ» 27 and «དུས» 6: 0, 0, 6, 9, 7, 1 | 5, 4, 9, 6, 6, 7, 2 | 3, 1, 6, 7, 2, 8 (with «དུས» as 6 and «འཁོར་ལོ» as the digit 2, as on p. 30 «འཁོར་ལོས(༢)»). That is the 19-digit number 8276132766945179600: it begins 82 and ends 179600 as the machine readings say, and has 18 or 19 digits as they do. If «དུས» were 4 the 13th-from-last digit would be 4 (…6945…→…4945…, 8276132764945179600), and if «འཁོར་ལོ» were 12 the number would be 20 digits and begin 81 (not the 82 both machines show). The number is not a multiple of 65 (the year-juncture), which the passage does not require. Not settled: «དུས» 6 or 4 and the true printed figure, which was not read on the scan; the number is therefore not given as a claim. Left open.

## Narrowed further 2026-11-14 (inv-e)

The word-numbers are in the *Tshig mdzod chen mo* (Steinert's public set, where it lists a word's number sense as «grangs … mtshon»): «དུས» (dus) and «མཚམས» (mtshams) are six, for the six seasons of the year (dpyid, so ga, dbyar, ston, dgun stod, dgun smad); «རོ» (ro) six, the six tastes; «གཟུགས» (gzugs) one; «མཚོ» (mtsho) four, the four colours of the ocean; «ཐུབ་པ» (thub pa) seven, the seven Munis; «ཀླུ» (klu) eight, the eight great nāgas; «སྐར་མ» (skar ma) twenty-seven, the mansions; «དབང་པོ» (dbang po) five, the senses; «མཁའ» (mkha') the number line's zero and «ནམ་མཁའ» (nam mkha') zero («klad skor»); «འདོད་པ» ('dod pa) thirteen, the thirteen divine courtesans, which agrees with the print's «འདོད(༡༣)» on p. 192. So «དུས» is six, not four, and the earlier doubt on it is closed. New doubt on the other side: the same dictionary gives «འཁོར་ལོ» ('khor lo) the number twenty-seven, the wheel of the mansions (sense 6), and WB's own chapters use it so («འཁོར་ལོ ༢༧ བསྣན» on pp. 34 and 48); then the digits of the last group read from the lowest are 8, 2, 7, 7, 6, 1, 3, giving the twenty-digit 82776132766945179600 instead of 8276132766945179600. Both begin 82 and end 179600, as the machines read; the count of digits (the machines show 18 or 19) favours the nineteen-digit value with the digit 2, but the print's figure was not read on the scan. «བུག» (bug, nine) and «རི» (ri, seven) have no number sense in the dictionaries, and stand as the pool's. Open on the scan.
