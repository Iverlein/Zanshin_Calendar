# «སྟོངས», an empty aspect, in reply 148

In reply 148 (ys:i506-508, img. 506, l. 4), the regent restates WB's "died with the enemy rising on the life force" as «སྲོག་ལ་དགྲ་དར་སྟོངས[?]་སམ་ནང་ཕྲད་ན»: is the doubtful word «སྟོངས», "empty", a third black state of an aspect beside the enemy's rise and the inner meeting, and what is an empty aspect in the reckoning for the dead?

**Narrowed 2026-11-01 (RD104, [claims/regent-dgra-dar-black-and-white-in-reply-148.md](../claims/regent-dgra-dar-black-and-white-in-reply-148.md)).** Within the black verdict the same reply names three, the enemy meeting, the son meeting and the inner meeting, and the white goes no further than mother and friend; so the clause «དགྲ་དར་སྟོངས[?]་སམ་ནང་ཕྲད» lists the enemy's rise, a middle word and the inner meeting, and the middle slot is the son meeting's in the reply's own list. «སྟོངས» is not shown to be a fourth state. The letters are read «སྟོངས» by eye on the crop and by Yigdzin-1 alike (img. 506, l. 4); their sense is still a doubt.

RD166 (2026-11-06), etext only: WB vol. 1 p. 333 uses «ཀླུང་རྟ་སྟོངས་པ» as a named state of an aspect beside «དགྲ་དར», making «སྟོངས» a state of its own; this contradicts RD104's reading of the slot as the son meeting. To be checked on a scan.

## Answered 2026-11-14

Read on the scan and in the dictionaries, 2026-11-14 (inv-eee). Yes: «སྟོངས» is a state of an aspect of its own, "emptied", beside the enemy's rise.

(1) On the scan WB vol. 1, p. 333 (Gser thang reprint I3CN5028 img. 328, lines of the luck's results, at native size) reads «ཀླུང་རྟ་སྟོངས་པ་བྱ་བ་ནི། །དུར་ཞུགས་དབུགས་ལེན་མངལ་ནས་སྐྱེས། །གོད་ཁ་ཆེ་ཞིང་བདག་གིས་ཀྱང་། །འབད་ནས་བྱས་ཀྱང་མགུ་བ་མེད། །ཀླུང་རྟ་དགྲ་དར་བ་ལ་སྐྱེས།», so that the luck "emptied" and the luck "born under the enemy rising" are two entries of the one list; BDRC's phrase search «ཀླུང་རྟ་སྟོངས་པ» finds the line in three prints of WB (I3CN5028, I1KG12906, I1KG1710). (2) The word is the past of «སྟོང་པ»: Rangjung Yeshe «stongs: emptied; the treasury has been emptied», Ives Waldo «emptied/neglected/desolate», and the *Dag tshig gsar bsgrigs* «exhausted or come to nothing» with the examples «zog la gor nad byung na nor ra stongs» (the herd emptied when an epidemic strikes) and «khyim gzhi stongs» (the household emptied), the same sense as WB's «ནོར་ཕྱུགས་གང་སྟོངས» for the power (wb.1:p329-331). (3) So the regent's clause «སྲོག་ལ་དགྲ་དར་སྟོངས་སམ་ནང་ཕྲད་ན» reads "if on the life force the enemy rises, or it is emptied, or the inner meeting", with the middle word a named bad state, not the son meeting that the earlier narrowing put in its slot ([claims/regent-dgra-dar-black-and-white-in-reply-148.md](../claims/regent-dgra-dar-black-and-white-in-reply-148.md)); the reply's own list of three within the black (enemy, son, inner meeting) names meetings of pebbles, and this one names the state of the aspect itself, so the two lists do not clash. What the "emptied" life force is in the dead's reckoning beyond WB's sense of the luck (great harm, no contentment) is not said by any text found.
