# Who wrote the companion method on the death signs (the midnight dream, «'pho ba spyi rtol ma'i lhan thabs») that ends the Lho brag pa transference text in the Gdams ngag mdzod, the Bka' brgyud sngags mdzod and Marpa's collected works, and the history of the four sheets before it, which says «I» (བདག) of a holder who received them alone: is the «I» a named disciple of the lineage, and does any Kagyu catalogue give the author of the history and of the companion methods?

RD374.

## Narrowed 2026-11-13

The history of the four sheets (Gdams ngag mdzod vol. 8, VE23605_008, dds.40:p207-209) names the line of the Breakthrough transference itself: Mtshur dbang nge, to the brothers, 'Dza' phu in India, Stod lung 'Gas ras, the teacher Nyi ma lung ba, Bde gshegs rin po che. The sentence that says «I» (བདག) follows the account of how Bde gshegs rin chen asked Nyi ma lung ba on a journey, and tells that the dharma-brothers, among them a monk practitioner (dge slong sgrub pa po), asked three times and the text was given to the last, and that «I» was given it alone with the oral instructions; it names no one, and the history has no colophon (dds.40:p234). So the «I» is a holder after Bde gshegs rin chen in the line, or Bde gshegs rin chen himself telling in the narrator's turn; the lines do not decide. No catalogue record found names an author of the history or the companion methods: BDRC's catalogue lists the history under Marpa (the entry the claim dds-midnight-dream-teaching-belongs-to-marpas-four-sheets mentions) and the contents page of the volume calls it «Rje btsun Lho brag pa'i khyad par gyi gdams pa snyan brgyud kyi shog dril bzhi'i lo rgyus gzhung lhan thabs bcas» without an author. BDRC's title search for «lhan thabs» and «spyi rtol» returned no catalogue part for the companion method of this name (the ES title index, 2026-11-13), so no catalogue author was reached.
Still open: a Kagyu catalogue (a dkar chag of the Bka' brgyud sngags mdzod or of the Lho brag pa collection) that names the writer of the history.
