# The figure after «འབྱུང་ཆུས» on WB p. 30 (line «སྐར་མཚམས་ཆུ་ཚོད་འབྱུང་ཆུས(?)སྦྱངས»): the print's figure is damaged, the number words give 54, the machine read 55.

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (scan-f).** Found on the Taikhang reproduction (I5877) img. 66, line 6 (the bottom line), at about x = 4400 of 8000: «ཉི་བར་གནས་གཉིས་གཅིག་ལ་ནི། །སྐར་མཚམས་ཆུ་ཚོད་འབྱུང་ཆུས་སྦྱངས། །མ་ལྡངས་…», the words agree with the 1996 print. Above «ཆུས» two small figures stand between lines 5 and 6, a hooked glyph and, to its right, a small open box with a dot (crop x 4380–4520, y 1085–1165 at 10×). The box shape is also the one carved three times in a row in a numeral run on line 3 (x about 5300–6000, crop at 2.4×), where the gloss «དབང་༥་འབྱུང་༥་མདའ་༥་ཆུ་༤…» has three equal figures; that suggests the box is ༥ (the words under those three glyphs were not checked one by one); the two-figure numeral there reads left to right hook, box; whether the hook is ༤ (giving ༤༥, 45) or ༥ (giving ༥༥, the machine's reading) was not told apart at this size, and the number words «འབྱུང་ཆུ» (5, 4) give 54. Not settled; the Zhol print is no witness here (its wording of the passage differs, see PLAN.md, scan-f).

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (scan-h).** Two findings. (1) The order of the number words: WB lists a number's words from the units up and writes the figure from the top down, as its own pairs show on pp. 30 (img. 66 reprint text): «བུག་མདའ་༥༩» (bug 9, mda' 5) is 59, «དུས་མཚོ་༤༦» (dus 6, mtsho 4) is 46, «ཟླ་ཡོན་༣༡» (zla 1, yon 3) is 31. So «འབྱུང་ཆུ» (5, 4) is ༤༥, 45, not 54, and the corpus line «(༥༤?)» of ch. 3, p. 30 is the wrong way round. (2) On Taikhang I5877 img. 66 (the figures are in the scan's own pixels, 8700 px wide; scan-f's x are on an 8000 scale, 1.0875 smaller), the pair over «ཆུས» on line 6 stands at x about 4790–4900, y 1195–1260: a crooked left glyph and, on the right, an open U-shaped box with a dot beside it. The census of the numerals on the block's line 3 (x 5985–6665, y 790–860, seven figures over «དབང་འབྱུང་མདའ་ཆུ་ཡོན་འཁྲིག་གཟུགས», the gloss «༥༥༥༤༣༢༡»): ༥ is three equal U-shaped boxes with small ears (x 5987, 6112, 6235), ༤ a slanting leaf-shaped hook with a slit (x 6323), ༣ and ༢ blob-and-stroke glyphs (x 6405, 6535), ༡ a plain thin hook (x 6663). The right figure of the pair is the same box: ༥. The left glyph is neither the box (so not 55) nor the thin hook ༡; it is cut differently from the slit hook of line 3 but is the one place left for ༤. Words (45) and the right figure (༥) agree, the left glyph is not ༥ or ༡: 45 is the reading; the left glyph's likeness to ༤ was not shown shape for shape, so the question stays open for one confirmation (a second ༤ of this block, e.g. «མཚོ་༤», pp. 30–31 on img. 66–67, was not located).
