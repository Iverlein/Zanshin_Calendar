# Is the place shunned in the fierce rites the seat one would face?

for the fierce rites (WB vol. 2, p. 427) «ཟིན་ཕུང་བིཥྚི་མཆོག་ཡིན་ཀྱང་། །གདོང་ཐུག་གནས་ལ་འཛེམ་པར་བྱ།»: is the place shunned the lord's seat that one would face, so that the work is done with the lord behind or beside, as ch. 34 says of other works «རྒྱུ་བའི་གདོང་ཐུག་མིན་ཕྱིན་པའི» (p. 423)?
