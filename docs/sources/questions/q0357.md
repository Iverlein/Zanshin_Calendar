# The iron dog or the iron rat among the best years, p. 355

P. 355, l. 24, «ཕོ་ནི་ལྕགས་ཁྱི་མོ་ཆུ་སྤྲེལ» in the list of the best years of the three in concord: is the man's year the iron dog, as O10 read it, or the iron rat, which the list's pattern (each pair within one triad) asks for? The two letters look alike in the typesetting.

## Answered 2026-10-23

Answered 2026-10-23 (RD46): the Zhol blocks carve «ལྕགས་ཁྱི», the iron dog, as O10 read; if the list's pattern wants the rat, the blocks carry it so.
