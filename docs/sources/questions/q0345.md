# The right shoulder and «ཡམས་སྐུད» on p. 163

On p. 163, whose place is the right shoulder (the line gives only "the left, the maternal uncle"), and what is «ཡམས་སྐུད» on the left side?

RD165 (2026-11-06), narrowed: «ཡམས་སྐུད» is a harm on a kinsman beside the wife, uncle and daughter in vol. 1's birth reckonings; the right shoulder stays unnamed.
