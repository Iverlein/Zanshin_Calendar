# The right shoulder and «ཡམས་སྐུད» on p. 163

On p. 163, whose place is the right shoulder (the line gives only "the left, the maternal uncle"), and what is «ཡམས་སྐུད» on the left side?

RD165 (2026-11-06), narrowed: «ཡམས་སྐུད» is a harm on a kinsman beside the wife, uncle and daughter in vol. 1's birth reckonings; the right shoulder stays unnamed.

**Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-h).** The right shoulder is the father's place: two later published verse texts of the black reckonings carry WB's lines with the word WB leaves out, «ཕྲག་གཡས་ཕ་དང་གཡོན་ཞང་པོ། གློ་གཡས་ཕ་སྤུན་གཡོན་ཡམས་སྐུད» (Lha mo tshul khrims' *Nag rtsis rdel 'grem 'bras bshad*, MW1KG21818, etext I1KG21886, and the Dge ldan *Dpag bsam ljon shing*, MW21984_22CC1A), the man lying with the head west and the feet east. It gives «ཡམས་སྐུད» the left lung, beside the right lung's father's brothers; what the word means is still not found (no headword in Steinert's 64 dictionaries; the Blue Beryl, the Do shal and the Kun 'dus use it unglossed beside the wife's kin, the father's brother and the daughter). Etext parallel, not read on a scan: claims/kin-places-body-right-shoulder-father.md. The first half is settled, the second open.

**Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-n).** BDRC «ཡམས་སྐུད» 10 hits, read in their etexts (2026-11-21): Bai dur dkar po prints, the Dge ldan manual (I4949, printed p. 101: the same line «གློ་གཡས་ཕ་སྤུན་གཡོན་ཡམས་སྐུད»), the *Utpala Garland* (I4767, in the verses of a girl's years: «ཡམས་སྐུད་རང་ངན་བུ་མོ་དབུལ» for a girl's year, «བུ་མོ་མི་འོང་ཡམས་སྐུད་ངན»), and Bai dur dkar po's «ཚང་བླ་ཡམས་སྐུད» and «ཆུང་མ་ཡམས་སྐུད» in the birth reckonings: a kinswoman's side (wife, daughter, household partner), never glossed; «ཡམས་སྐུད་ཅེས», «ཡམས་སྐུད་ནི», «ཡམས་སྐུད་ཞེས» 0 hits. The word stays unglossed.
