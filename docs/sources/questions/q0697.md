# The twelve houses' number pairs on p. 36

p. 36, the twelve houses and their numbers («ཟླ་བ་༡༡ རི་དབང་༥༧དབྱུག» and the lines after): the figures were read from the machine text only; what each pair of numbers is (a planet's longitude, a day count?) is not told on the page. (RD187, corpus/wb/ch03.md)

## Answered 2026-11-13

Each pair is a weekday number (0 to 6) and a count of *dbyug* (sixtieths of a day, under 60); the last line of the verse multiplies the sum by 60 and takes the remainder by 7 (*thub pa*) to get the house's planet. The scan of p. 36 (RD204) corrected the machine's «11» to «(༡)» and the other figures. The step from each house to the next, modulo 7 days, gives sign-lengths of 29.35 to 31.60 days with the longest at Gemini to Cancer, so the pairs are the day of the sun's reaching each house in the Kar myang reckoning (the arithmetic's reading, not the page's wording). Claim `wb-ch5-houses-weekday-stick-pairs`.
