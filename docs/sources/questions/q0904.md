# Where the Moonbeams on the Heart of Medicine (Aṣṭāṅgahṛdaya, Body ch. 6) read «མི་ཟད་རྨི་ལམ་» in the verse Rin spungs pa quotes, do the Derge and Peking-type Tengyur etexts (MW23703_4312, MW1PD95844_3549) spell it so too, and does Byang pa's lemma of the Four Tantras' line 17 follow the Tantra or the Moonbeams?

RD302; Byang pa's lemma was not searched in this reading.

## Answered 2026-11-10

Answered by RD313 (2026-11-10): the Derge Moonbeams (read on the page) and the Peking-type Moonbeams (snippet) read «མི་བཟད», not Rin spungs pa's «མི་ཟད»; Byang pa's lemma of line 17 is «མི་བཟང་པ», following the Tantra, while his quotation of the Moonbeams reads «མི་བཟང» in the MW27496 print and «མི་བཟད» in MW15590.
