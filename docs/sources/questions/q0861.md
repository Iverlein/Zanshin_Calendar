# Is there another scan of the Serthang print, or another print whose leaf can be opened, on which the two lines of the dream by night can be read by eye?

The Serthang scan I3CN5038 lacks printed pp. 31–32.

**Narrowed 2026-11-10 (sweep from RD296).** RD296 (ROADMAP, 2026-11-10): the leaf of ST ch. 7 (printed pp. 31-32) is missing from the Serthang print's whole digitization, BDRC lists one instance (WA3CN5036) and the Serthang etext has none of the verse's words; no other Serthang scan can be opened. Two typeset etexts (Lhasa 1982, Beijing) read the verse, but they are OCR, not the Serthang print by eye.
