# Are the 七夕 strips in five colours in a published Japanese source?

The pages read give the five colours to the thread of the Chinese custom and plain 'many colours' to the strips.

## Answered 2026-11-24

RD409 follow-up. Yes: Japanese Wikipedia's 七夕 (日本) gives the five colours of the strips as those of the Five Phases, green, red, yellow, white and black (not blue), and quotes the late-Edo *Morisada mankō* on green bamboo hung with strips and coloured paper set high on every roof. Hiroshige's Tanabata print (Meisho Edo hyakkei, 1857, the Met JP62) shows the strips with cut-paper shapes.
