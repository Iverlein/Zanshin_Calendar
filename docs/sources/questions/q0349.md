# «ཁ་སྨྲང» for the king-and-minister pebbles, p. 166

On p. 166, what is «ཁ་སྨྲང» (Yigdzin-1; not clear on the reprint) in "name it" for the king-and-minister pebbles?

## Answered 2026-11-03

«ཁ་སྨྲང་བཏགས» read on p. 166 itself (Gser thang reprint img. 172, by eye): it is the word of pp. 50 and 114, an account or saying attached to the element of the pebble's place (RD140, reckonings.md).
