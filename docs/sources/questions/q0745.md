# The black-month lines of p. 183

The black-month lines on p. 183 («gang shar zla ba'i bzhi gshed nag», byi/rta … «ra bzhi'i stod nag, 'bring bzhi'i sgang, tha bzhi'i smad nag») are given only as the verse runs; what rule do they state? (RD198, corpus/wb/ch15.md)

## Answered 2026-11-14

The lines give the "black month" (zla nag) of a year. The Tshig mdzod chen mo, the Tshig gter and Ives Waldo have the headword «zla nag»: sense 2, "the month that becomes the year's four-slayer (bzhi gshed) is called black month, and it is said: the upper black of the four corner signs (ra ba bzhi), the middle black of the four middle signs ('bring bzhi), the lower black of the four last signs (tha chung bzhi); for example, when a rat year rises, the hare month and the bird month are called the black months, and especially 'the middle black'". The four-slayer is its own headword: "the fourth counted from the sign in its forward (mas) and its backward (yas) direction, as the rat's forward four-slayer is the hare and its backward the bird" (the pool's year-month-earth-lords file has the same count). So the rule of WB: in the years named, the black months are the two signs one counts as four from the year's sign inclusive, forward and backward: rat and horse years, the hare and bird months («བྱི་རྟ་གནམ་ཤར་བྱ་ཡོས་ནག»); ox and sheep, the dog and dragon («གླང་ལུག་ཤར་ཚེ་ཁྱི་འབྲུག་ནག»); tiger and monkey, the pig and snake («སྟག་སྤྲེལ་ཤར་ན་ཕག་སྦྲུལ་ནག»); and "so one should know the rest by analogy" (hare/bird, dragon/dog and snake/pig years the remaining signs). The next lines name the class: «ར་བཞིའི་སྟོད་ནག, འབྲིང་བཞིའི་སྒང་, ཐ་བཞིའི་སྨད་ནག» — a black month is "upper" when its sign is one of the corner four (tiger, monkey, pig, snake), "middle" when one of the middle four (mouse, horse, bird, hare), "lower" when one of the last four (ox, sheep, dog, dragon). This agrees with the lines: the rat/horse years' hare and bird months are middle black (the dictionary's own example), the ox/sheep years' dog and dragon months lower, the tiger/monkey years' pig and snake months upper. The Tshig mdzod chen mo's first sense gives a second, general count of a black month by the tenth year-sign backwards (rat year: hare month; ox year: dragon month), the same hare and dragon, only one of the two. «ཤར་ཚེ» of the lines is the dictionary's «shar tshe», "when it rises".

Source: tmz:box9 (read 2026-11-14), wb.1:p183; the corner, middle and last four from the pool's year-month-earth-lords file (WB vol. 1, p. 254, etext only).
