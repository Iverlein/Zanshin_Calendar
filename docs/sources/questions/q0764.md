# WB p. 247 print line 4: «ས་རྦད་ཐུ་བཅད་འདྲ» (Yigdzin-1) or «ས་ཐད་ཐུ་བཅད་འདྲ» (MITRA)? The heavy glyph is not settled and neither gives a clear sense.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-ppp)

By the dictionaries: «རྦད» (rbad) has headwords (Tshig mdzod chen mo: "boasting; all, whole"; Ives Waldo: "completely, abruptly", «rbad bcad» "absolutely determined", «rbad de» "completely"); «ས་རྦད» has none, nor has «ས་ཐད»; «ཐད» (thad) alone is "side, the direction straight forward, whole". «ཐུ་བཅད» (thu bcad) has no headword. In the line «ཐང་གི་ས་རྦད་ཐུ་བཅད་འདྲ» "the plain's ground [rbad / thad] like a thu bcad" both readings give a sense (rbad: "wholly cut off"; thad: "the ground straight on, cut off"); neither is decided by the dictionaries. Still the scan. Source: tmz:box9, wb.2:p247.
