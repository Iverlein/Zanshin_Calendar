# Do the Four Tantras' chapter counts on p. 442 join the next page's first line

On p. 442 (img. 448), where the Four Tantras' chapters are counted (6, 31, 92 and 25, 154 in all, with 56 for the conclusion), the numerals in the machine text were not all read and the last line of the page (ending «སློང་རྐྱེན་ན་», "if the cause that rouses") and the first of p. 443 (beginning «ལུགས་ཐོས་པའི་བརྟག», "the examination of what is heard") do not join into one sentence; does a line stand between them? (RD84)

## Answered 2026-10-31

**Answered 2026-10-31 (RD84, eye pass, [corpus/wb/ch35.md](../corpus/wb/ch35.md#wb-2-p442)).** Read on the Gser thang scan (img. 448–449), enlarged four times: no line stands between the pages. The last print line of p. 442 ends «སློང་རྐྱེན་ན་» with a tsheg and no shad, and p. 443 opens «ལུགས་ཐོས་པའི་བརྟག»; the two make one seven-syllable verse line across the page break. The machine had run p. 442's last three print lines into one, which made the page look two lines short. The chapter counts are spelled in words in the print, not in numerals: the Root Tantra six («དྲུག»), the Explanatory thirty-one («སོ་གཅིག»), the Instruction ninety-two («གོ་གཉིས»), the Last twenty-five («ཉི་ཤུ་ལྔ»), one hundred and fifty-four in all («བརྒྱ་དང་ལྔ་བཅུ་བཞི»), with fifty-six («ལྔ་བཅུ་དྲུག») for the conclusion.
