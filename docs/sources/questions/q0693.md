# The reductions between 601 and 578 on p. 24

p. 24, the reductions between 601 and 578 do not run as a chain on the machine readings (601, 603, 595, 578 with reductions 35, 8, 17); the figures of the lines about Gra thang, Ras chung rdo rje grags and Sa chen are not settled. What does the print read there? (RD187, corpus/wb/ch03.md)

## Answered 2026-11-07

Read on the reprint (img. 31, p. 24, lines 25–28) at 3×, RD197: the figures run as a chain: Dwags po Lha rje «༦༠༨»; reduction «༢» (mig), Gra thang «༦༠༦»; reduction «༣» (rtse mo), Ras chung rdo rje grags «༦༠༣»; reduction «༨» (klu), Sa chen «༥༩༥»; reduction «༡༧» (ri zla), the translator Blo ldan shes rab «༥༧༨»; reduction «༡» (gzugs), Phag mo gru pa «༥༧༧». The machines' 601, «༢༥» and «༣༥» were the figures run together with the neighbouring letters; Gra thang is 606, not 601.
