# Which first portion does «སྦྲང་མ་ཕུད་གཏོར་ཡོལ་བས་སྒྲིབ» scatter, and what does the cloth screen

In the line before it on p. 224, «སྦྲང་མ་ཕུད་གཏོར་ཡོལ་བས་སྒྲིབ» (the Zhol and Taikhang prints perhaps «སྦང་མ»): which first portion is scattered, the bee's (wax, honey) or another, and is the cloth screening the jar or the threshold? Narrows q404 (RD65).

## Answered 2026-11-06

RD162 (2026-11-06), on etext only (the scans are not served): SY (Nam mkha' seng ge, etext p. 373 = printed p. 345) has «སྦང་མའི་ཕུད་གཏོང་ཡོལ་བ་བཏང་བས་ཉེས་པ་ཞི།»: the first portion is the mash's, with no bee. The Drigung verse work (MW3KG218, etext pp. 57–58) has «ཆང་རྫས་ཕྱར་བ་བཀབ», a cloth over the beer things, so the cloth covers the beer and jar, not the threshold; «ཡོལ་བ་བཏང་» is also attested as spreading a screen.
