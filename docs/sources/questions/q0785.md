# What does the slash inside each cell of the 大雑書 table 夢を見て語らざる日 divide?

In the 万代大雑書 table (mdz:p11), for example 正【ひつじ さる／いぬ】: two sets of day branches, or day branches and something else (an hour, a second rule)? And is it the same table as in the 大雑書 of EB9?

## Answered 2026-11-14

The slash divides nothing in the rule. The 万代大雑書 cell is the transcriber's rendering of a two-line small note (his 凡例), and the older source of the table, the entry 不語夢日 of the 拾芥抄 (mdz:i772010, image 73 of the open NDL print, pid 772010), sets every month's cell as a two-column small note with no legend and no second rule: the day branches of a cell are one list of days of the cycle (by month) on which a dream is not told, because telling a good dream destroys it and a bad one becomes evil. Whether the two lines are two groups with a meaning is stated in neither text. That the table is the same one as in the 大雑書 of EB9 was not checked against EB9 itself; the three Edo prints read cell by cell have the same cells (claim zassho-three-prints-not-told-days-same-cells). Claims: shugaisho-fugomu-nichi-is-source-of-not-told-days, ozassho-slash-is-the-transcribers-line-break.
