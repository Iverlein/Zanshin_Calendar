# KP box 35: is «སྲོག་ཚེས» WB's «སྲེག་ཚེས», and is «གདོང་འཕུང་བྱེད» one earth lord or two words?

WB work 33 has «ས་ཡི་ཕུང་བྱེད་རྒྱུ».

## Answered 2026-11-14

«སྲོག་ཚེས» is WB's own spelling: white-beryl-ch34.md, work 33, has «བདུན་དམར་འཆར་དུས་སྲོག་ཚེས་དང་», and the claim's earlier «སྲེག་ཚེས» was a miscopy; the scan (img. 41, 7×) cannot tell the vowel sign apart, so KP is taken to agree with WB. «གདོང་འཕུང་བྱེད» is neither one nor two earth lords: the first syllable on the scan is «བར» (box 35's bad half, line 3, after Viṣṭi), so KP has the earth lord bar and the destroyer's course ('phung byed) where WB has «ས་ཡི་ཕུང་བྱེད་རྒྱུ». WB's «གདོང་ཐུག» (work 33, «གདོང་ཐུག་དུས་གནད་ཤེས་པ་དགོས») is a different phrase, in the good-and-bad dates, and is not in KP's line.
