# The limbs' verdict in Sprugtön's reckoning

Is the limbs' verdict in Sprugtön's reckoning (p. 23, l. 13, «ཁ་དམར་སྤྱི་ལྟར་གདགས») a verdict of its own over the sixteen, laid by the general rule for a run, or only the reading of the run for the rites? See verdicts.md, RD121.

RD179 (2026-11-06), narrowed: the notes' «སྤྱིའི་ཁ་དམར་རྡེལ་གཅིག» (p. 503) is one pebble counted in the 21 for a run of 20; read so, Sprugtön's «ཁ་དམར་སྤྱི་ལྟར་གདགས» lays one pebble over the limbs, but his colophon sum of 75 (50+16+5+4) leaves no seat for it (q665). Unsettled.

**Searched again 2026-11-14 (inv-jjj).** «ཁ་དམར་སྤྱི་ལྟར་གདགས» no hit outside WB; «སྤྱིའི་ཁ་དམར་རྡེལ་གཅིག» 1 hit, the notes of the 1996 etext (I1KG12907) only. Beside it, the manuals that quote the meadow's leaves (q563) give five verdicts to a group and none a verdict for sixteen limbs. Unsettled.
