# p. 185's winter solstice measure at the author's place

the figures in brackets after «ཆུ་ཚོད་གཟུགས་མེ» and «མཁའ་ཡོན» on p. 185, line 7, read on both prints as (༧) and (༡༠); the number words give 31 and 30 («གཟུགས་མེ», «མཁའ་ཡོན»), and «སྲང» follows them: is the winter solstice's measure at the author's place 18 mansion-minutes, 31 *chu tshod* and 30 *chu srang* of the mean sun, or do the printed figures stand?
