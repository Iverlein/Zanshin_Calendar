# p. 185's winter solstice measure at the author's place

the figures in brackets after «ཆུ་ཚོད་གཟུགས་མེ» and «མཁའ་ཡོན» on p. 185, line 7, read on both prints as (༧) and (༡༠); the number words give 31 and 30 («གཟུགས་མེ», «མཁའ་ཡོན»), and «སྲང» follows them: is the winter solstice's measure at the author's place 18 mansion-minutes, 31 *chu tshod* and 30 *chu srang* of the mean sun, or do the printed figures stand? (RD186, corpus/wb/ch16.md)

## Answered 2026-11-13

The number words stand, the bracketed figures (7) and (10) do not. «གཟུགས་མེ» is 31 and «མཁའ་ཡོན» is 30, so the mean sun at the winter solstice of the author's place is 18 mansions 31 *chu tshod* 30 *srang*. The check: a house is 135 *chu tshod* (p. 51), a house-day (*khyim zhag*) its thirtieth, 4;30; the place lies seven house-days from the Brahmā-line, and 7 · 4;30 = 31;30 exactly. The «རིག་བྱེད་མཁའ་མེ» of the same page (4, 0, 3) is 4;30, one house-day. The figures (7) and (10) cannot be the sun's place; they stay unexplained as a misprint or misreading of the print.
