# Whose element starts the thirty and the thirteen circles

The thirty starting points (WB vol. 1, ch. 26, p. 453) choose each point's pair of years by "the element of the one reckoned" («བརྩི་བྱའི་ཁམས»), the notes (vol. 2, p. 532) by "the element of the year-horse (ལོ་རྟ, lo rta) of whoever is reckoned"; the thirteen circles (vol. 2, ch. 27, p. 3) count "from the enemy of the reckoned person's element" («བརྩི་བྱའི་ཁམས་ཀྱི་དགྲ»). Which element of the person is meant: the year's own element (the power), the life force's, or the luck's? In ch. 26, which of the eight fighting pebbles of p. 453 (the fourth upward against the life force, the fourth downward against the body, the eleventh upward against the power, the eleventh downward as the luck's enemy, the seventh up and down, the progressed year with the power and luck) answers which of each point's eight named checks? In ch. 27, the mewa "counted up from the start of the person's mewa sequence" (the notes, p. 533, etext): from which mewa? Wanted before either reckoning can be built (ROADMAP R2).

**Narrowed 2026-10-23 (RD37).** The notes group the thirty's eight checks upper 1, 3, 5, lower 2, 4, 6 and middle 7, 8 (wb.2:p532-533), which matches WB's order of the fighting pebbles on p. 453 (upward places, then downward, then the year itself): an inference, recorded in [claims/thirty-board-groups.md](../claims/thirty-board-groups.md); question 306 asks whether the readings bear it out. The element stays open: the notes name the year-horse (ལོ་རྟ, lo rta) of whoever is reckoned.

**Answered for ch. 27, 2026-10-23 (RD38, [corpus/wb/ch27.md](../corpus/wb/ch27.md), [corpus/wb/notes-7.md](../corpus/wb/notes-7.md)).** The element is the power's: the notes gloss «བརྩི་བྱའི་ཁམས་སྟེ་དབང་ཐང་གི་དགྲ» (p. 533, wb.2:p533b), and the tenth circle's fine way counts from "the pig of the same power as the one reckoned" (p. 10, wb.2:p10-11). The mewa is counted up from the person's body mewa and the starting point's agreeing mewa; how the two combine is question 368. Ch. 26's element stays open.

**2026-10-23 (RD44, [claims/thirty-circles-whose-element.md](../claims/thirty-circles-whose-element.md)).** For the circles Zhechen Gyaltsab's worked example starts a wood-monkey king from the iron bird, iron being wood's enemy, and his tenth circle names the power's element (zg:p303-304), as the notes do. For the thirty the *Moonbeams* names the pair years by their element, that is, their power, with wood as an example; a pair's two years share their body element too, so the example does not rule the body out: still an inference for ch. 26.

**2026-10-23 (RD47, [claims/circles-servant-fine-start.md](../claims/circles-servant-fine-start.md)).** Reply 140 (ys:i497-498) gives the servants' fine start, hidden in an older text, as four nails of fixed years (earth male tiger, water male dragon, wood female pig, fire female bird); that these are WB's relations seen from a wood person, the example of the *Moonbeams* and Zhechen Gyaltsab, is an inference. Which element picks the thirty's pairs the replies do not say.
