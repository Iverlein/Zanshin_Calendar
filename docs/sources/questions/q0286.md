# «གཡང་[ཤེན]་གཅིག» and «ཚགས་བྱ» in U. Phalgunī's entry

In the U. Phalgunī entry (WB vol. 2, p. 66, l. 1), «གཡང་[ཤེན]་གཅིག་ནི་ཚགས་བྱའོ» reads ཤེན in Yigdzin-1, but the reprint's stack looks rather like རྗེན. What is the word, and what is the act «ཚགས་བྱ»?

## Answered 2026-10-23

Answered 2026-10-23 (RD46): the blocks carve «གཡང་རྗེན»; Yigdzin-1's «ཤེན» and «རྟེན» are misreadings. «ཚགས་བྱ» stays open.
