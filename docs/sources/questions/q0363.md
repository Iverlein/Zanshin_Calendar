# The la's wandering of the snake and horse, the rat and pig

The la of one born in a snake year and of one born in a horse year both wander "to the north" as printed (WB vol. 2, p. 16, l. 24, on the reprint I3CN5029 img. 22 too), and those of the rat and pig years both to the south: is one of each pair a misprint for east or west, and which?

**Narrowed 2026-10-25 (RD51, [claims/ch27-la-wandering-directions.md](../claims/ch27-la-wandering-directions.md)).** The Gser thang reprint, the Zhol print (by eye) and the Sakya etext all read rat south, snake north, horse north, pig south: not one print's slip. By the trine only the snake and the pig do not go to the opposite side (east and west). No parallel verse found (Bhutan collection, *G.ya' sel*, Dpal bsdus); the reading stays as printed.

**2026-10-25 (RD57).** The Taikhang block (I5878) carves the la's wandering as the other prints do: snake and horse north, pig south; the Zhol print reads the same by Yigdzin-1. The rat's «ལྷོར» is read «ལྟོར» by Yigdzin-1 on the Taikhang and «ལྷོར» on the Zhol print; only «ལྷོར» (south) makes sense, not settled by eye. A carving error shared by all prints stays the open reading ([ch27-taikhang-la-wandering](../claims/ch27-taikhang-la-wandering.md)).
