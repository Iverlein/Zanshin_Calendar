# Whose are the works behind rrd.21:p19 and rrd.22:p38-39

The Sgrub thabs kun btus (BDRC author Kun mkhyen 'Jigs med gling pa) may be Kong sprul's compilation; rrd.22:p38-39 is given to Nam mkha' rdo rje by the search page only.

## Answered 2026-11-11

RD402: the text holding rrd.21:p19 is not the Sgrub thabs kun btus but བླ་མེད་ལྷན་སྐྱེས་རྣམ་བཞིའི་རྫོགས་རིམ་སྙིང་པོ་དོན་གྱི་ཕྲེང་བ, whose colophon (printed pp. 513-514) names the 'Bri gung pa Chos kyi grags pa; rrd.22:p38-39 is the Nyang-tradition Sems sde pra khrid, colophon (printed pp. 361-362) by the beggar Nam mkha' rdo rje at Dpal gnam steng ri khrod, in a water-female bird year.
