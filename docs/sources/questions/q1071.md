# Is the wording «the first three of the night made four parts are not certain, so the examination is in the fourth» of the Drukpa manuals (Yon tan 'od dam bstan 'dzin don kun grub; Mkhas dbang Sangs rgyas rdo rje, who names Padma dkar po and Lha rtse ba Ngag dbang bzang po as his teachers) taken from Tsong kha pa's Sngags rim chen mo or from a Drukpa manual older than both, for instance one of Padma dkar po's?

RD377.

## Narrowed 2026-11-13

The wording was searched on 2026-11-13 in BDRC's full-text search as an exact phrase («མཚན་མོ་ཆ་བཞིར་བྱས་པའི་ཐུན་དང་པོ་གསུམ་ལ་ངེས་པ་མེད»: one hit; the shorter «ཐུན་དང་པོ་གསུམ་ལ་ངེས་པ་མེད»: four). It stands in the Drukpa manuals: the Vajrāvalī rite in the 'Brug pa'i chos mdzod chen po bsam 'phel nor bu'i bang mdzod (VEIE0OPIDDB781DE_I3CN2269), Sangs rgyas rdo rje's «dkyil chog rdo rje 'phreng ba'i dbang gzhung mtshams sbyor» (MW3CN2232_236751, the same words) and the Gene Smith copy 'Brug pa'i lugs kyi dbang chog 'dod rgu'i bang mdzod; two more etexts (VEIE0OPIB2682C2D_I2139, VEIE0OPI7F0370A0_I4161) have the shorter phrase and were not opened. The sentence follows the root tantra's four lines as the manual's own gloss on them. No Tsong kha pa text and no older Drukpa manual with the wording was found, and no etext of the Sngags rim chen mo was searched, so its source is still not named.
