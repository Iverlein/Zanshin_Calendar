# «སྤྲ་ཁྱིམ» at the door's right against the black times

On p. 236, the black times' remedy at the door's right is «སྤྲ་ཁྱིམ» (a figure? a raven's house?) with the swastika on the left. What is drawn?

## Answered 2026-10-28

«སྤྲ་ཁྱིམ» and «སྦྲ་ཁྱིམ» are two spellings of one word, the herdsman's tent (the Taikhang reads «སྤྲ», the 1996 and Gser thang etexts and the commentary *Dpyod ldan snying nor* «སྦྲ»; WB's own lists of works write «སྦྲ་ཁྱིམ་འབུབས», the tent pitched). What is drawn at the door's right is a tent. No text found says why (new question). (RD65; machine readings, the stack by eye on the Taikhang.)
