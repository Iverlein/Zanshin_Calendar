# Which water bird year dates The Precious Key, and who is Prajñāmaṇi

Which water bird year dates The Precious Key (I1KG12897, img. 384), 1873 or 1933, and who is Prajñāmaṇi, head teacher of the government's Mentsikhang? A catalogue of Mentsikhang teachers or the cycle's number in the colophon, read on the scan at 4×, would settle it.

RD168 (2026-11-06), narrowed: the colophon (I1KG12897 img. 383–385; Yigdzin-1 and 3× crops) is a pupil's, Dge tshul Ye shes chos 'phel of Rgyal byed monastery, prompted by the circle of Prajñāmaṇi; the print is kept at the Lhasa Mentsikhang. The year reads «དཔལ་གནོད» water bird (if the machine reading is right), which falls in both 1873 and 1933; the cycle number is unread (q659).

## Answered 2026-11-13

Answered by identification, not by reading the numeral: the colophon's water bird year is 1933, the sixteenth cycle's. Prajñāmaṇi, at whose presence the pupils were taught and whose circle prompted the gathering, is Khyenrab Norbu (born 1883; q672), so 1873 is out, and the print of 1987 rules out 1993. The numeral after «རབ་བྱུང་» stays unread on the scan. Claim [precious-key-water-bird-year-is-1933](../claims/precious-key-water-bird-year-is-1933.md).
