# «ཁ་ཚོན» and the speaker of p. 212's closing note

What is «ཁ་ཚོན» (*kha tshon*) in the closing note of p. 212 («ཁ་ཚོན་གྱིས་གཅོད་དཀའ»), and who speaks the note: the author of WB, or the editors of the print? The note names the *Heart-Jewel of the Discerning* (དཔྱོད་ལྡན་སྙིང་ནོར) as its source. (RD184, corpus/wb/ch18.md)

## Narrowed (2026-11-13, inv-w)

«ཁ་ཚོན» (*kha tshon*) is a deciding talk, a ruling: the *Tshig mdzod chen mo* and the *Bod yig tshig gter rgya mtsho* give «ཐག་གཅོད་པའི་གཏམ» with the example «དོན་ལེགས་པར་མ་རྟོགས་པར་ཁ་ཚོན་གཅོད་མི་ཉན» (not hearing a verdict decided before the matter is understood); Rangjung Yeshe "final decision", Valby "unanimous, final decision, resolution"; the *Sgom sde tshig mdzod chen mo* «ཐག་གཅོད་པ»; the *Dag tshig gsar bsgrigs* «ཁ་ཚོན་གཅོད» = to settle by word what is and is not. «ཁ་ཚོན་གྱིས་གཅོད་དཀའ» is then "hard to settle by a ruling". Who speaks is not answered: the note is in the voice of the chapter's own colophon (it ends «ལེའུ་སྟེ་བཅོ་བརྒྱད་པའོ», as ch. 17's note does with «བགྱིས་པ»), and no dictionary says whether that voice is the author's or the print's editors'.

## Narrowed further (2026-11-14, inv-d)

The note's own words (corpus/wb/ch18.md, p. 212) are in the compiler's voice and speak of copies: «སྟག་ཆུང་གི་ལོ་མ་ཚང་བ་རྣམས་དཔེའི་རིགས་ལ་ཡོད་པ་མ་རྙེད་ཀྱང་ཆོས་རྗེ་བདེ་ལེགས་ཉི་མ་སོགས་ཀྱི་ལེའུ་ཐོ་རྙིང་པ་རྣམས་ནས་ཁ་བསྐངས་ཏེ་བཀོད་པའི་» (the incomplete years of the Little Tiger-ear were not found among the kinds of copy, but were filled in from the old chapter lists of Chos rje Bde legs nyi ma and others and set down). The speaker did the collating and filling; the question is only whether he is WB's author or a later hand. The one name that could date the note is Chos rje Bde legs nyi ma (བདེ་ལེགས་ཉི་མ, bde legs nyi ma): BDRC titles his Phug-lugs reckoning «rtsis gzhung bde legs nyi ma blo gsal dga' byed ma» (MW1NLM6954, MW1NLM1183, MW1NLM2267), but no person record with dates was reached (the work records' purl.bdrc.io TTL gives no creator; `WA1NLM6954` 404), so it is not shown that he is earlier or later than WB's author. Open.
