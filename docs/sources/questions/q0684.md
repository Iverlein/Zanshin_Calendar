# «ཁ་ཚོན» and the speaker of p. 212's closing note

What is «ཁ་ཚོན» (*kha tshon*) in the closing note of p. 212 («ཁ་ཚོན་གྱིས་གཅོད་དཀའ»), and who speaks the note: the author of WB, or the editors of the print? The note names the *Heart-Jewel of the Discerning* (དཔྱོད་ལྡན་སྙིང་ནོར) as its source. (RD184, corpus/wb/ch18.md)

## Narrowed (2026-11-13, inv-w)

«ཁ་ཚོན» (*kha tshon*) is a deciding talk, a ruling: the *Tshig mdzod chen mo* and the *Bod yig tshig gter rgya mtsho* give «ཐག་གཅོད་པའི་གཏམ» with the example «དོན་ལེགས་པར་མ་རྟོགས་པར་ཁ་ཚོན་གཅོད་མི་ཉན» (not hearing a verdict decided before the matter is understood); Rangjung Yeshe "final decision", Valby "unanimous, final decision, resolution"; the *Sgom sde tshig mdzod chen mo* «ཐག་གཅོད་པ»; the *Dag tshig gsar bsgrigs* «ཁ་ཚོན་གཅོད» = to settle by word what is and is not. «ཁ་ཚོན་གྱིས་གཅོད་དཀའ» is then "hard to settle by a ruling". Who speaks is not answered: the note is in the voice of the chapter's own colophon (it ends «ལེའུ་སྟེ་བཅོ་བརྒྱད་པའོ», as ch. 17's note does with «བགྱིས་པ»), and no dictionary says whether that voice is the author's or the print's editors'.

## Narrowed further (2026-11-14, inv-d)

The note's own words (corpus/wb/ch18.md, p. 212) are in the compiler's voice and speak of copies: «སྟག་ཆུང་གི་ལོ་མ་ཚང་བ་རྣམས་དཔེའི་རིགས་ལ་ཡོད་པ་མ་རྙེད་ཀྱང་ཆོས་རྗེ་བདེ་ལེགས་ཉི་མ་སོགས་ཀྱི་ལེའུ་ཐོ་རྙིང་པ་རྣམས་ནས་ཁ་བསྐངས་ཏེ་བཀོད་པའི་» (the incomplete years of the Little Tiger-ear were not found among the kinds of copy, but were filled in from the old chapter lists of Chos rje Bde legs nyi ma and others and set down). The speaker did the collating and filling; the question is only whether he is WB's author or a later hand. The one name that could date the note is Chos rje Bde legs nyi ma (བདེ་ལེགས་ཉི་མ, bde legs nyi ma): BDRC titles his Phug-lugs reckoning «rtsis gzhung bde legs nyi ma blo gsal dga' byed ma» (MW1NLM6954, MW1NLM1183, MW1NLM2267), but no person record with dates was reached (the work records' purl.bdrc.io TTL gives no creator; `WA1NLM6954` 404), so it is not shown that he is earlier or later than WB's author. Open.

## Narrowed further (2026-11-16, inv-e)

The note is in only two prints in BDRC's full-text search: «ཁ་ཚོན་གྱིས་གཅོད་དཀའ» 2 hits (the 1996 print I1KG12906 and the Gser thang reprint I3CN5028), «ལེའུ་ཐོ་རྙིང་པ་རྣམས་ནས་ཁ་བསྐངས» 2 (the same), «དུས་བསྟན་མེ་ལོང་དང་སྟག་རྣ་ཆེ་ཆུང» 1; not found in the Do shal copies MW1KG1617_A9DE66 and MW2PD17386_D19206 (poor or absent OCR, so not proved absent). The «དོ་ཤལ་དཔྱོད་ལྡན་སྙིང་ནོར» the note names is the second half of the White Beryl's own full title («phug lugs rtsis kyi legs bshad mkhas pa'i mgul rgyan baiDUrya dkar po'i do shal dpyod ldan snying nor»; the Dga' ldan Phun tshogs gling history lists «…do shal dpyod ldan snying nor» as the first work of a pair, with the author's own Q&A «biDUrya dkar po'i dri lan 'khrul snang g.ya' sel» as the second): the verses before the note are marked as taken from the book's own title, a third-person self-citation that fits an editor or a later hand as well as the author. The author's own reply book, «Bstan bcos baiDUrya dkar po las dri lan 'khrul snang g.ya' sel don gi bzhin ras ston byed» (Sde srid Sangs rgyas rgya mtsho; MW2PD17386_474815, search-only, one snippet), treats the same matter in its item 9: «don dgu pa lo bshad du dgos pa'i dus bstan me long stag rna che chung phur stag sogs mi mthun pa mang dag zhig dang khyad par stag chung du lo ma tshang ba zhig byung na'ang lo yang 'dir mtshang ba med pa dang mi mthun ba rnams la bsregs bcad brdar ba'i gser ltar…» (the disagreement of the Dus bstan me long, Stag rna che chung and Phur stag, and the incomplete Little Tiger years): the author himself dealt with it, which does not show that he wrote the note.

Chos rje Bde legs nyi ma: the Dung dkar and Tshig gter dictionaries and the *Gangs can mkhas grub rim byon ming mdzod* (Steinert 61) have a «པཎ་ཆེན་བདེ་ལེགས་ཉི་མ», born in Byang in the Kre ba family «ཕྱི་ལོའི་དུས་རབས་བཅུ་དྲུག་པའི་དཀྱིལ་ཙམ» (about the middle of the 16th century), a pupil of Se ra rje btsun Chos kyi rgyal mtshan (1469–1544), teacher of Se ra Byes; his works named there are Vinaya, Mdzod, Phar phyin summaries, none of reckoning. Rangjung Yeshe: «bde legs nyi ma | pan chen, 16th cent.». Eight BDRC etexts name «chos rje bde legs nyi ma» in teaching lineages between Rje Nyi ma bkra shis and Sgom sde / Shar chen Nam mkha' rgyal mtshan. That he is the author of the Phug-lugs reckoning (MW1NLM6954) is not shown. If he is the same man he is earlier than WB's 1685, so the note's date is not fixed by him. Open: the voice.
