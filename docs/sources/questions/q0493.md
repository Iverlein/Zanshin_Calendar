# Is 日遊神's course outside the house reckoned in Japan

is 日遊神's course outside the house, on the forty-four days, reckoned in Japan at all? The 協紀辨方書 makes it 鶴神's, which is 天一's; the 暦林問答集 says only that it goes round the eight directions. Is there a Japanese reckoning, or is the day's 日遊神 only "in the house or not"? (RD94)

**Dead end 2026-11-05 (RD163).** No new reckoning: 和漢名數續編 joins 天一 to the 鶴神 but does not name 日遊; «日遊方» in the full-text search finds only the 方鑒精義大成, house only.

**Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-z1).** The Edo handbook *Mandai dai zassho kokon taisei* (Itō Takayuki's transcription, *Media communication studies* 76, 2023-03-29, vol. 13, hdl.handle.net/2115/89040, section 天一神): 日遊神 is the 善神 under 天一神, who is Taishakuten's officer; "when 天一 goes up to heaven 日遊神 comes down to the world and takes people's houses for its own; it hates impurity, so during the sixteen days of 天一's stay in heaven (from 癸巳 to 戊申) everything must be kept pure, or the 日遊神 curses; avoid pulling down or building a house; hunting deer, hawking and fishing are avoided; when 天一 returns the 日遊神 goes up again." The same section gives 天一's forty-two days on earth by direction. So this Japanese handbook has the 日遊神 only "in the house" for 天一's sixteen days in heaven, and no course outside the house on forty-four days; its course by the eight directions is not given there. One handbook of the Edo period; the other volumes read (8, 9, 10, 12, 15 and no. 77) have no 日遊 passage.
