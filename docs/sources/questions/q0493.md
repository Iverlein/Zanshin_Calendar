# Is 日遊神's course outside the house reckoned in Japan

is 日遊神's course outside the house, on the forty-four days, reckoned in Japan at all? The 協紀辨方書 makes it 鶴神's, which is 天一's; the 暦林問答集 says only that it goes round the eight directions. Is there a Japanese reckoning, or is the day's 日遊神 only "in the house or not"? (RD94)
