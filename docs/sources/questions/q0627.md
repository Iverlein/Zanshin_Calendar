# Who wrote the 假名暦略註

Who wrote the 假名暦略註: 岡部一德, whose book 保己一 says he copied, or 保己一, as his lives say?

RD172 (2026-11-06), narrowed: the Kyoto record catalogues the work as 保己一's from the colophon alone, and the colophon says «以岡部河内守一德本書寫»; no other evidence either way.


Narrowed (inv-sss, 2026-11-14): NDL Search lists some fifteen records of the work; their statements of responsibility differ: 保己一 [著] (most), 検校保己一 [校] (R100000002-I000007481882, -I000007328791), 塙保己一 校 (仮名暦略註, R100000002-I000007281037), 塙保己一 (R000000054-I040_51693). The older Japanese-book records (校) follow the colophon («以岡部河内守一德本書寫 … 檢校保己一»), the later 図書 records (著) the title page. No record, in NDL or on the Kyoto, Tsukuba and Tohoku pages, names 岡部一德 as an author or has a work of his. The catalogue evidence is divided only by cataloguers' choice of 校 or 著; it does not decide.
