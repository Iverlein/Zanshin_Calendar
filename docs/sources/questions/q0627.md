# Who wrote the 假名暦略註

Who wrote the 假名暦略註: 岡部一德, whose book 保己一 says he copied, or 保己一, as his lives say?

RD172 (2026-11-06), narrowed: the Kyoto record catalogues the work as 保己一's from the colophon alone, and the colophon says «以岡部河内守一德本書寫»; no other evidence either way.


Narrowed (inv-sss, 2026-11-14): NDL Search lists some fifteen records of the work; their statements of responsibility differ: 保己一 [著] (most), 検校保己一 [校] (R100000002-I000007481882, -I000007328791), 塙保己一 校 (仮名暦略註, R100000002-I000007281037), 塙保己一 (R000000054-I040_51693). The older Japanese-book records (校) follow the colophon («以岡部河内守一德本書寫 … 檢校保己一»), the later 図書 records (著) the title page. No record, in NDL or on the Kyoto, Tsukuba and Tohoku pages, names 岡部一德 as an author or has a work of his. The catalogue evidence is divided only by cataloguers' choice of 校 or 著; it does not decide.


Narrowed (inv-d, 2026-11-14): the NDL Lab full-text search (lab.ndl.go.jp/dl/api/book/search, pid 781882) finds the work in 渡辺知三郎 編, 塙検校伝 (黄眉山房, Meiji 25 / 1892), in the list of 保己一's own writings: «一假名曆略註 曆の諺解 壹卷», set apart from the later list of old books he collated and printed. A biography of 1892 therefore counts it as his writing, which agrees with the title-page cataloguing (著) and not with the colophon's wording (校); it is a late secondary source and may rest on the title page alone. The name 岡部一德 in the NDL full text (東京市史稿 市街篇 25; 群馬県史) belongs to a bannerman of the Edo period (a father of sons placed in other houses), with no work or office as a calendar writer; nothing ties him to 河内守 or to an author's role. A web search (Kyoto, Tsukuba and Tohoku records) repeats the colophon and gives no new evidence. Not settled: 保己一 as the writer is the better supported, 岡部一德 as the owner of the copied book.
