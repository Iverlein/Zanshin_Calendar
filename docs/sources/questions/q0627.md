# Who wrote the 假名暦略註

Who wrote the 假名暦略註: 岡部一德, whose book 保己一 says he copied, or 保己一, as his lives say?

RD172 (2026-11-06), narrowed: the Kyoto record catalogues the work as 保己一's from the colophon alone, and the colophon says «以岡部河内守一德本書寫»; no other evidence either way.
