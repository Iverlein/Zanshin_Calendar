# «རྨང» in «རྡེལ་སྨད་སྟོངས་ན་མོ་བརྒྱུད་རྨང» on p. 52

What does «རྨང» mean in «རྡེལ་སྨད་སྟོངས་ན་མོ་བརྒྱུད་རྨང» (WB vol. 2, p. 52, l. 23; read so on the 1996 scan, the Gser thang reprint and the Zhol print): the female line's foundation, or a verb that goes with «སྟོངས»?

## Narrowed 2026-11-24 (inv-k)

WB's own other uses of the word: «རྨང» with the verb «འོང» in three places, each of a line or a lineage that comes to a state: «ཁྱོད་ཀྱི་འབྲིང་བརྒྱུད་རྨང་བར་འོང» (vol. 1, I1KG12906), «གིན་ཐོག་སྐྱེས་ཚད་རྨང་བར་འོང» (same volume) and «ད་ལྟ་བརྗེ་རྨང་པར་འོང» (p. 54, both etexts of vol. 2): «your middle line will come to rmang», not «become a foundation»; with the claim rmang-po-childless-as-printed («རྨང་པོ», childless, bereft of heirs, in the same idiom «རྨང་པོར་འགྲོ») the word is the lineage's bereft state, so «མོ་བརྒྱུད་རྨང» is most likely «the female line [is] bereft (of heirs)», not a foundation and not a verb of «སྟོངས». The 1996 etext's text of this very line is damaged («མོ་བརྒྱུད་རྨོང་ཆད་པ་ན», the middle phrase «རྡེའུ་རྐེད་པར» lost) and reads «རྨོང», the only «རྨོང་ཆད» of the four etexts. Narrowed: no dictionary was looked up for «རྨང་བ» as a verb.
