# Which Sanskrit yogas are WB's good «self-born three, five, seven and two, eight, six»

Which Sanskrit yogas are WB's good «self-born three, five, seven and two, eight, six» (p. 349), the six standing in both lists?

## Answered 2026-11-06

RD174 (2026-11-06), by list order: the yogas are 2 Prīti, 3 Āyuṣmān, 5 Śobhana, 6 Atigaṇḍa, 7 Sukarman, 8 Dhṛti; the 6th is also in the bad line. «རང་སྐྱེས» stays unexplained; no Tibetan-script Sanskrit yoga names in any etext read.
