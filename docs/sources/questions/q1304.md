# WB vol. 2 p. 225: the horse lord's line, «བང་བྱིན» or «བང་ཕྱིན», and «རྟས་སྤང» or «ནས་སྤང»?

Two syllables are in doubt on the reprint (passage wb.2:p225b): «བྱིན» or «ཕྱིན», and «རྟས» or «ནས».

## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-c)

Two other prints' etexts, BDRC OCR, searched with tools/sources/locate.py for the horse lord's lines. The first doubt, «བང་ཕྱིན» (not «བང་བྱིན»): the Sakya Centre print (IE0OPI0BA3A0E0, image 327) has «མི་ཤི་ཟློས་འབྱུང་ཁང་ཕྱིན་ནི» and on image 325 «ས་མེད་འཁྱགབང་ཕྱིན» for the line of p. 224, the Zhol print (IE0OPI7850CC9C, image 943) «མི་ཤི་ཟློས་འབྱུང་བར་ཕྱིན»: both ཕྱིན, none བྱིན; with the 1996 print's and MITRA's «ཕྱིན» noted in wb.2:p224-225 and the dictionaries' «བང་ཕྱིན» (earth-lords.md), «ཕྱིན» is the reading by the weight of the prints, the glyph on the reprint staying faint. The second doubt, «རྟས་སྤང» or «ནས་སྤང»: the Sakya Centre text reads «རྟས་སང་ཤིད་སྐོརྟཐོག་འཛིམ» (རྟས་ with the ས of the instrumental), the 1996 print's etext «ནས་སྤང» and the Zhol text garbles the line («རྟ་སྦང་ཡིད་སྨོན་ཐོག་འཛག»); one print for each side, and both readings fit the line («by horse, avoid» or «barley, avoid», as in the snake day's «བཤས་སྤང»), so this syllable is not settled by the texts; it needs the Zhol or Taikhang scan. Left open.
