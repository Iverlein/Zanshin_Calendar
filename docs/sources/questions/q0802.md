# Is «པདྨ་དཀར» at the end of SKP's second text Padma dkar po of the 'Brug pa, and whose 'third of Vairocana' does it follow?

Narrowed (inv-j, 2026-11-14): the 'third of Vairocana' is the machine's reading of «གསུངས་པ» ("as taught"), by MDP p. 138 and the Klong rdol text RST (claim skp-attribution-and-padma-dkar), so it follows nobody's third. That «པདྨ་དཀར» is the 'Brug pa Padma dkar po stays unshown (no «པོ», no title); the stock-title reading remains as likely.
