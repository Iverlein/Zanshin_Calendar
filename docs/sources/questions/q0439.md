# What is the «ཡོ» of the rat day's «ཐེམ་བུར་མི་འཚག་ཡོ་ནས་འདྲེན» (p. 224), and whose is the «འདྲེན»

On p. 224 the rat day's remedy ends «ཐེམ་བུར་མི་འཚག་ཡོ་ནས་འདྲེན»; a Bhutan table of the twelve days (MW26041, printed p. 561) gives «ཁ་ཡོ་ནས། འདྲེན།». What is the «ཡོ», with or without the «ཁ», the place the corpse is led out from, and is the «འདྲེན» the corpse's or the beer's? Narrows q404 (RD65).

RD162 (2026-11-06), narrowed: SY and the Drigung verse work drop «ཡོ་ནས་འདྲེན» and give a wool-thread remedy instead, so neither glosses it; no source has «ཡོ» as a place; «ཆང་འདྲེན» (serve beer) is attested.

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-bbb).** BDRC phrase search «ཐེམ་བུར་མི་འཚག» 3 hits (WB's 1996 and Gser thang etexts, and the Bhutan anthology Dkar rtsis dang nag rtsis kyi gzhung, I1KG11905, printed p. 560); «ཁ་ཡོ་ནས་འདྲེན» 0. The dictionaries (Tshig mdzod chen mo; Brda dkrol gser gyi me long, citing the Be'u bum sngon po: «yo ni kun nam thams cad ces pa yin») give the old word «yo» as "all, everything", beside "crooked", so "lead out from everything" or "from the crooked place" are both open; neither is supported by a use in a rite. Open.
