# Does the dākinī in the dream of Rtsa gsum gling pa's life (tsl.30:i87) count months as the WB model almanac does (monkey month = Hor 5, wb.1:p163), or as the Phugpa count; do the vol. 30 dates with the author's birth year fix the year of the dream?

Raised by the 2026-11-13 investigation a; see the question's cited passages.

## Answered 2026-11-14

Answered for the count; the year is not fixed.

The count. «སྤྲེལ་ཟླ» (sprel zla) is in the dictionaries a term of the elements reckoning: the Bod yig tshig gter rgya mtsho says «འབྱུང་རྩིས་ལུགས་ཀྱི་སྟོན་ར་བ་སྤྲེལ་གྱི་ཟླ་བ་སྟེ་བོད་ཟླ་བདུན་པ», "the first autumn month, the monkey's month, the seventh Tibetan month" (and, by the old herdsmen's reckoning, the middle winter month, the eleventh), Ives and Waldo "7th month". WB's model almanac calls the same month the fifth Hor month, «ཧོར་ཟླ་ལྔ་པ་རྒྱ་རྩིས་སྟོན་ར … སྤྲེལ་ཟླ» (wb.1:p163): by the claim ch28-seasons-chinese-count the Hor numbers run two behind the Tibetan ones (Hor 11 the first month of spring), so Hor 5 and Tibetan 7 are one month under two numberings. The dākinī of tsl.30:i87 therefore does not count differently from the almanac: the month is the first autumn month by either numbering, the «སྟོན་ར» of the colophons (tsl.30:i661, tsl.28:i72). A conflicting note: the Dung dkar tshig mdzod chen mo's entry «སྤྲེལ་ཟླ་ཚེས་བཅུའི་གར་འཆམ» puts the monkey-month tenth's festival at Skyor mo lung in the fifth Tibetan month of a monkey year; it is a festival note, not a rule of the months, and it does not move the dictionary's seventh month.

The year. The life does not date the dream. Its heading in the pool said "at a year old"; the text says «དེ་ནས་ལོ་གཅིག་ལོན་པ་ཡིན་ནམ་མཚམས» (tsl.30:i86), "about a year after that", counted from the events before it: the demoness's sickness «before three» (p. 59), then a stay «of a year» (p. 76) and two stays «of a few years» (p. 76), so the dream is told after at least some years beyond three and his age is not stated. The editors' summary (vol. 30 p. 4, modern, facts only) gives the birth in the wood-dog year, 1694; no vol. 30 page found gives the dream's year or the Hor/Tibetan year of the birth. The year of the dream therefore stays open; the heading is corrected. The same kind of correction for the other tenth of early autumn: tsl.30:i661 stands in the life of Pad rgyal gling pa (printed pp. 565-750), not in Rtsa gsum gling pa's own.
