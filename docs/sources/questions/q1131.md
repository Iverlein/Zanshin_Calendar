# Are the be'u bum's Śākyamuni and White Tārā rites taken from the tradition of the Sgrub thabs kun btus, and which text names their compiler

The mantra «oṃ buddha curu mācuru ke svāhā» and the four mirrors with the letters A, NU, TA, RA also stand in the Sgrub thabs kun btus, the Sgrub thabs rin po che kun las btus pa, a Snar thang tantra and a Lam 'bras notebook (by snippet; VE23681_001 timed out).

## Narrowed 2026-11-13

The Sgrub thabs kun btus in 14 volumes (BDRC MW3KG767 Derge print, MW1KG17189 Kathmandu reprint, MW3KG633) has as creators in BDRC's record Ngor pa 'Jam dbyangs Blo gter dbang po (1847-1914) and 'Jam dbyangs Mkhyen brtse'i dbang po (1820-1892) (P560 and P258); the catalogue gives them as its authors. A compilation of the nineteenth century is later than the be'u bum's rites, so it can take them from the tradition but cannot be their source; the be'u bum's own compiler is not named in any text found. The Sgrub thabs rin po che kun las btus pa (MW3CN3381) is a separate record without a creator in BDRC. Not opened: the rites in the volumes themselves (the volume with the Śākyamuni dream rite).
