# What is the long way of the Abridged Tantra that multiplies the cut surplus by 8280 (མཁའ་ནོར་མིག་ཀླུ) in the eclipse tables (WB ch. 9 p. 60), and what does the 8280 measure?

Raised by the 2026-11-13 investigation j.

Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-ff): no text that restates the 8280 was found; what the text around it says is now clearer.

Read: the moon's rule of p. 60 and the sun's rule of p. 66 are the same lines in the Kun 'dus chen mo (MW28845, etext read in full, vol. 2 pp. 101-102 and 110): «བཞི་པ་ཆ་ཡི་ཆེ་ཆུང་ནི། །ཅུང་ཟད་དཀའ་ཞིང་མི་རྟོགས་པས། །བསྡུས་རྒྱུད་སྦྱངས་ལྷག་ཆུ་ཚོད་མན། །མཁའ་ནོར་མིག་ཀླུས་བསྒྱུར་བ་ཡིས། །གནས་ཀུན་གཅིག་བསྒྲིལ་ཉིད་ལ། །ཟབ་པའི་ཆོ་ག་དུ་ཡོད་ཀྱང་། །སྔ་འགྲོས་ཆུ་སྲང་ཡན་ཆད་ཀྱི། །ཕལ་ཆེར་ཞིབ་པར་རྟོགས་པས་ན། །སྦྱངས་ལྷག་ཆུ་ཚོད་མཁའ་རོས་བསྒྱུར། །སྲང་བཅུག་འབྱུང་ཟླས་ཐོབ་པའི་ནོར།» "the size of the portion is a little hard and not understood; for the Abridged Tantra the cut surplus in chu tshod is multiplied by «ནམ་མཁའ་ནོར་བུ་མིག་ཀླུ» 8280, rolling all places into one (གནས་ཀུན་གཅིག་བསྒྲིལ); though there is a deep procedure there, the earlier course is understood in detail as far as chu srang, so the surplus in chu tshod is multiplied by 60, chu srang put in, and the figure taken by 15", and for the sun «ཞིབ་ན་མཁའ་ནོར་མིག་ཀླུ་ཡིས། །བསྒྱུར་བ་ལ་སོགས་ཟླ་བའི་སྐབས། །བཤད་པ་ཇི་བཞིན་དང་མཚུངས་པའི། །གནད་འགག་བརྒྱད་ཀྱི་ཆ་འགྲོས་བརྩི།» "in detail, the multiplication by 8280 and the rest as told in the moon's part, the parts' motion is counted by the eight key points" (and «བསྡུས་རྒྱུད་བྱེད་རང་གཙོ། །གྲུབ་རྩིས་དང་ཡང་ཅུང་ཟད་བསྟུན།» "the Abridged Tantra's way is chiefly its own, agreeing a little with the true reckoning"). The Kun 'dus copy adds no gloss; the Do shal and the 'Bai DUrya g.ya' sel were not found to treat the line. The Derge Vimalaprabhā (VE23703_1328, etext, 592 pages, read for «བརྒྱད་སྟོང་», «ཉིས་བརྒྱ་བརྒྱད»): no 8280 and no passage that multiplies a cut surplus by a figure in word-numerals; the only «ཉིས་བརྒྱ་བརྒྱད་ཅུ་རྩ་བརྒྱད» there is 288 (36 × 8, in the passage on the feet of the moon and Rāhu), another number. BDRC phrase search «བརྒྱད་སྟོང་ཉིས་བརྒྱ་བརྒྱད་ཅུ» gives only a tax figure in Ngag dbang byams pa's works, «བརྒྱད་སྟོང་ཉིས་བརྒྱ་ཉི་ཤུ» 0.

What it is, as far as the lines say: a multiplier that turns the cut surplus, given in several places (chu tshod, chu srang, breaths and the fractions of the moon's constant), into a single figure of the smallest unit; WB declines it and uses the 60 and 15 of the long chu srang way instead. Computed, not derived: 8280 = 2³ · 3² · 5 · 23 = 138 × 60 = 23 × 360; 360 is the breaths in a chu tshod (60 chu srang × 6), but no reason for the factor 23 was found, so what 8280 measures is not settled: the Abridged Tantra's own rule (the Laghukālacakratantra's eclipse chapter in Tibetan) is the place to look and was not reached.
