# The Moonbeams' general, particular and special verdicts

Are the Moonbeams' general (སྤྱི), particular (བྱེ་བྲག) and special (ཁྱད་པར) verdicts of f. 26b–27a the same three that WB lays for the dead, and which of the four verdict pebbles (ཁ་དམར) is each? See verdicts.md, RD127.

## Answered 2026-11-16

inv-b, 2026-11-16, from the passages already read on the scan (nothing new fetched for this).

**The three are WB's three verdicts of the la pebbles.** WB (vol. 2, p. 52, wb.2:p52-54) opens its reading of the twelve la pebbles (བླ་རྡེལ, bla rdel) with «བླ་རྡེལ་བཅུ་གཉིས་ནི། །ཁ་དམར་རྣམ་པ་གསུམ་ཡིན་ཏེ། །སྤྱི་དང་ཁྱད་པར་བྱེ་བྲག་ལས།», "the twelve la pebbles have three kinds of verdict (ཁ་དམར, kha dmar): the general (སྤྱི, spyi), the special (ཁྱད་པར, khyad par) and the particular (བྱེ་བྲག, bye brag)", and goes on «དང་པོ་སྤྱི་ཡི་ཁ་དམར་ནི།»; the pool's three claims lay them out (dead-la-general-verdict: the board's shape; dead-la-specific-verdict: which places hold pebbles; dead-la-special-verdict: a black or half pebble before each place). The *Moonbeams* (dz:i055-057, f. 27a, img. 057) closes its second part, the la pebbles, with «རྡེའུ་དང་བསྟུན་ཁ་དམར་ནི། །སྤྱི་དང་བྱེ་བྲག་ཁྱད་པར་གྱི། །དུར་སའི་སྐྱོན་ཡོན་གང་ཟློས་བརྟགས། །ཐམས་ཅད་དྲིལ་ལ་སྟངས་གཅོད་བྱ།»: the verdicts follow the pebbles; of the general, particular and special, examine whichever fault or merit of the burial place (དུར་ས, dur sa) recurs, gather all and decide. The same three words, the same board (the la pebbles), and the same subject: WB's verdicts read the grave's place («ཆད་ཆེན» the place of the grave, the forefathers' skulls and crimes). The *Moonbeams*' order is general, particular, special; WB's verse names the special before the particular, but reads them in the order general, particular, special in the lines that follow. So the *Moonbeams* gives WB's three verdicts of the la board in a line, and sends the reader to the root text for their content; it defines none of them.

**"Which of the four verdict pebbles".** None. The «ཁ་དམར་རྡེལ་བཞི» (the four verdict pebbles) belong to the year of age's reckoning (f. 21b–22a); in the dead's chapter «ཁ་དམར» is the word for a verdict itself, laid by the board's pattern, not one pebble of four. The pair on f. 26b, «སྤྱི་དང་བྱེ་བྲག་ཁ་དམར་གདགས», stands in the first part (the dead taken out of the living's twenty pebbles) and says «རྒྱས་པར་ཀེག་རྩིས་བཞིན་དུ་བོར»: lay them in full as the year of age's reckoning; it names the general and the particular only and the special enters on f. 27a, so that this pair is the same two kinds of the la verdicts is likely (the same words in one chapter, no other general and particular verdict is named), not stated.

Scope: answered for the identity of the three terms and their subject; not answered, and not asked of any text found, what the *Moonbeams*' own practice counts in each (the folios give no counts, they refer to the root text).

## Audit 2026-11-16 (inv-audit): set back to narrowed

Re-read dz:i055-057 (f. 26b l. 5-6 to f. 27a l. 6) and wb.2:p52-54 (ch29.md). What stands: the *Moonbeams* closes its la pebbles with «སྤྱི་དང་བྱེ་བྲག་ཁྱད་པར་གྱི། དུར་སའི་སྐྱོན་ཡོན་གང་ཟློས་བརྟགས», the same three words that WB p. 52 gives as the three verdicts (ཁ་དམར) of the twelve la pebbles, and WB reads the grave's place; the *Moonbeams* defines none of the three. What was not supported: (1) «the three are WB's three verdicts» is an identity inferred from the same words in the same board, with no text of the *Moonbeams* saying so, and it is stated as a finding; (2) «which of the four verdict pebbles: none» is an argument from absence. The first part's line «རྒྱས་པར་ཀེག་རྩིས་བཞིན་དུ་བོར། སྤྱི་དང་བྱེ་བྲག་ཁ་དམར་གདགས» sends the laying of the twenty pebbles to the year of age's reckoning, whose own verdict pebbles are the four «ཁ་དམར་རྡེལ་བཞི» (f. 21b-22a); that the general and the particular verdict of f. 26b are not those four pebbles was not shown, and the answer itself calls the link to the la verdicts «likely, not stated». The claim that «ཁ་དམར» in the dead's chapter is only «a verdict» and never a pebble is likewise not established. Narrowed: the three terms agree with WB's three verdicts of the la pebbles (spyi, bye brag, khyad par; WB's verse names them in that order of khyad par before bye brag, and reads them general, particular, special); whether the f. 26b pair is the same two verdicts, or the verdict pebbles of the keg rtsis, stays open.
