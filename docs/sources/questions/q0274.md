# The stack «བརྫེ» on p. 54 and the other doubtful syllables of pp. 53–55

On p. 54, l. 27–28, is the stacked syllable read «བརྫེ» on the Gser thang reprint (ར over ཛ, ཇ or ད) «བརྫེ», «བརྗེ» or «བརྡེ»? A sharper print (Taikhang, WBZ) would settle it, as it would the other syllables left doubtful: «དྭའུ», «ཁྱོ» and «ན» on p. 53; «རྒན», «ཡོ», «སྦོས» and «སྡེར» on p. 55; «ལོ་ཏ» on p. 500.
