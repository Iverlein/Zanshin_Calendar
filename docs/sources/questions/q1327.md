# WB ch. 19 p. 214: is the figure after «མཁའ་མེ་ལག» 230?

By the word-numerals (0, 3, 2) yes, but its first two glyphs look alike in the print.

## Answered 2026-11-21

Yes: the figure is 230 («༢༣༠»).

Checked 2026-11-21 (rd-t). The words «མཁའ་མེ་ལག» are sky 0, fire 3, hand 2, written from the lowest place, so 230; མེ is 3 and ལག is 2 wherever WB sets a figure beside them (མེས ༣, «ཀླུ་ལག(༢༨)» and «ལག(༢)» in ch. 3). The same verse gives the other divisor of Rāhu as «མཁའ་དུས་ཆུ(༤༦༠)» (sky 0, season 6, water 4 = 460), and 230 is its half, the pair «460 or 230, whichever divides» (the pool's earlier «220» of the ch. 19 claim was a misreading of the same figure). 230 synodic months are also the node's own period, about 18.6 years (6798 days). The print's first two glyphs, which look alike, are therefore ༢ and ༣ as the words give them.
