# Does the Dpe bsdur ma's base text read «བརྒྱད་པར་ལ» and «ཕྱེད་པར་དུ» (with པ) in the second and the fourth watch lines, as entries 4 and 6 of printed p. 1301 imply, or «བརྒྱད་བར་ལ» and «ཕྱེད་བར་དུ» as the OCR gives them?

RD323; only the restricted scan of vol. 14, printed p. 1301, can show it.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-bb2)

State of the evidence, etext unless stated (vol. 14, VEIE0OPIA3E40644_I1PD95859, fetched again with `bdrc.py etext`, 1845 pages; the scans are restricted and were not read):

- The Dpe bsdur ma's base text prints the second watch's line as «བརྒྱད་བར་ཅེལ» and the fourth as «ཕྱེད་བར་ཡིདུ» (etext p. 1364, the signs «ཅེ» and «ཡི» being the lost note markers): the OCR reads བ in both. The same volume's etext carries the lines a second time, in a noisier pass at the foot of etext p. 1362 (printed p. 1299 by its footer; after it the collation table of the previous work, p. 1363): the fourth watch there reads «ཐུན་བ་ཟླ་བ་ཕྱེད་བར་དུ» (བ again) and the second watch's end «ཟླ་བ་ལ་འབད་པ་ལ», too garbled to read a letter.
- The Derge etext (MW23703_1749, the base print) reads «བརྒྱད་བར་ལ» and «ཕྱེད་བར་དུ» (svo:p271a), the Narthang etext (MW2KG5015_1416, poor) «brgyad pa la» and «phyed bar du», Padma rdo rje 2002 (MW25126) «brgyad bar la» and «phyed bar du». So three etexts of the base's own wording read བར at both places; the Peking and Narthang scans give «པ་ལ» at the second (svo:p271w). Entry 4 (Peking and Narthang «པ») is read most simply as the variant for a base «བར», the marker sitting after «བརྒྱད་བར».
- What stays open: entry 6 (Peking and Narthang «བར», marker after «ཕྱེད་བར» of the fourth watch) cannot be a variant of a base «བར»; and the Narthang etext's «phyed bar du» is «བར» too. So either the entry belongs elsewhere or the base reads «པར་དུ» there, and the OCR's བ is a confusion. Only the restricted page image of printed p. 1301 settles it.
