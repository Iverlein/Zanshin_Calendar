# Does the Derge, Chag-po-ri or Drathang print of ch. 81's verse on the ways of entering carry a clause on the dream in the second way's line, as the commentaries' glosses do, or is the Serthang's bare list of five the Tantra's text?

RD321; the verse was read on the Serthang only.

## Answered 2026-11-13

No. On the Derge (img. 436, lines 2-3) and the Chag-po-ri (img. 428, lines 1-2), read by eye on scan crops (st:p352c), the second way's line is the bare «ལོངས་སྤྱོད་རེ་འདོད་ཚུལ་གྱིས་ཞུགས་པ་དང་», between the game and the unclean, with no clause on dreams, exactly as on the Serthang (st:p352). The Drathang print's printed line has none either (st:p352d, machine reading of the printed lines; hand notes between its lines unread, so a hand-written clause cannot be excluded there). The dream «རྨི་ལམ་ཟ་ཟི་མང་བ» of the second way is therefore the commentators' own sentence (st:p352b), and the Serthang's bare list of five is the Tantra's text on three prints read on the scan and a fourth by machine. Answered from RD325 and RD356 passages already in the pool; nothing new opened.
