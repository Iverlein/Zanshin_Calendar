# «ཕ་གུ» or «ཕ་ཁུ» for the kin of wood

Searching for the ground (WB vol. 2, p. 135, l. 17, wb.2:p133-135) prints «ཤིང་གི་ཕ་གུ་མེས་གསུམ» for the kin of wood; is ཕ་གུ a misprint for ཕ་ཁུ (father and paternal uncle), as the sense suggests, or a word of its own?
