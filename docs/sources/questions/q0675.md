# p. 435's «ཉྭ་བཞིན» in WB vol. 1

What is the «ཉྭ་བཞིན» of p. 435 (every print has a ཉ-headed stack, with or without wa-zur): a hat-like thing, or a word a reader of the tradition knows? (RD175 third run, corpus/wb/ch24.md; replaces the earlier question on the Zhol print's ཉྭ/ཞྭ.)

INV-E (2026-11-14), narrowed: BDRC full-text search for «ཉྭ་བཞིན», «ཉ་བཞིན་ལ་ཁ་ནད» and «ཁ་ནད་ལྕེར་རླུང་འབྲུམ» finds nothing; «ཁ་ནད་ལྕེར» finds only WB itself (the 1996 etext I1KG12906 and the reprint I3CN5028), so no parallel text carries the line. The line's sense is of the fever of fire four and earth one («ཁ་ནད་ལྕེར», mouth illness at the tongue; «རླུང་འབྲུམ་དམར་ཙག», wind pox and red spots), and p. 436 has «ཉ་དང་ཚིགས་བཞི་ན་བ» with the same head and no wa-zur; so the word is a body part or sign of the fever rather than a hat, but no source glosses it. Steinert's dictionaries were not searched (a 4.5 GB clone; the web edition is a single-page app that a fetch cannot read).
