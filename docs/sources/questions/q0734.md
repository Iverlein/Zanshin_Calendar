# What the 24 charts of pp. 136–147 place, and the cells' lists

Do the 24 charts of pp. 136-147 place the earth lords by the year (pp. 136-141, ring charts with a standing figure) and by the date (pp. 142-147, strips with an animal), as the contents row of wb-contents.md has it, and what do the cells' lists such as «ཁ་ནུབ» (mouth west) and «མགོ་ལྟོ» (head, belly) name: the parts of the earth lord's figure that the lords are set at? A reader of the tradition is asked for the rule by which a cell's names are chosen. (RD199, corpus/wb/ch13.md)

## Read again 2026-11-21 (rd-y)

Narrowed, not answered. One text check on what the cells' words name. The etext cells (the 1996 print, pp. 136-147) are built from a small vocabulary, counted over the passages wb.1:p136 to p147: མགོ about 50 times, ཁ 35, ལྟོ 13, with the directions ཤར, ནུབ, ལྷོ, བྱང་ among them. The same words, with the same directions, are WB's rule for the sky-dog's head and mouth in ch. 23 (wb.1 «དཔྱིད་གསུམ་ཉི་ནུབ་ནམ་ཕྱེད་བར། ཁ་ནི་ལྷོ་བལྟ… ཉི་ཤར་བར་ལ་ཁ་ནུབ་བལྟ» and the sky-dog's «དཔྱིད་གསུམ་མགོ་ཤར་ཁ་ལྷོར་ཟུག་…» of the Brahmā tantra in marriage.md): the mouth faces a direction by the season and by the stretch of the day (the night's middle, ནམ་ཕྱེད; dawn, ནམ་ལངས; the sun's rising, ཉི་ཤར; noon, ཉི་ཕྱེད; sunset, ཉི་ནུབ), and p. 142's heading row names these same stretches (ནམ་ལངས, ཉི་ཤར, ཉི་ཕྱེད, see q1164). So a cell «ཁ་ནུབ» (mouth west) is, as far as the words go, a statement of a body part and a direction in the style of that rule, which fits the earth lord's figure on the woodcut as the sky-dog's does; but the rule that picks a cell's list was not found in a text, and which figure's parts (the earth lord's or the dog's) the cells name is not decided by the vocabulary. The by-year and by-date split of the two groups of charts is as the contents row has it (the rings with a standing figure, the strips with an animal); the 24 charts' cells are not read on the scan (PLAN.md Dead ends: the reset face clots). Open for a commentary with the charts' key.
