# The golden turtle's wood sheep and how a year becomes an element

In the golden turtle (WB vol. 2, p. 20, l. 14), is the wood sheep's class «ཤིང་ལུག་པ» the cow (བ, ba), as the identifying pebbles and "the cow and the turtle agree with all" suggest, and by which element does a year "become" wood, fire, iron, water or earth in this reckoning?

**Narrowed 2026-10-25 (RD51, [claims/ch27-turtle-wood-sheep-and-element.md](../claims/ch27-turtle-wood-sheep-and-element.md)).** The scan reads པ, not བ, in «ཤིང་ལུག་པ». A year becomes an element by its own element in the sixty-year cycle; every year the text names fits. The class counts add to sixty only if the wood sheep is the cow's year and the wood verse's *srin po* are nine; the printed «བཅུ་པོ» (ten) gives 61, a slip or misprint.

**2026-10-25 (RD57).** The wood verse reads «ལྷག་མ་བཅུ་པོ», ten and not nine, on the Taikhang and the Zhol print, by both Yigdzin-1 readings and agy; the syllable is a stack with ུ, not «དགུ». «ཤིང་ལུག་པ» ends in པ ([ch27-taikhang-turtle-ten](../claims/ch27-taikhang-turtle-ten.md)).

## Answered 2026-11-14

Found in UT's own verse, [ut:p170-171](../corpus/ut/ut.md#ut-p170-171) (etext only), recorded in [claims/ut-turtle-wood-sheep-cow-nine.md](../claims/ut-turtle-wood-sheep-cow-nine.md): «ཤིང་གྱུར་བཅུ་གཉིས་ཤིང་བྱི། ཤིང་ལུག་བ་ལ་ཤིང་གླང་མི། ལྷག་མ་དགུ་པོ་སྲིན་པོའི་ལོ།». The wood sheep's class is the cow («བ་ལ»), and the remaining *srin po* are nine; the other four elements' verses agree with WB's in every class and count. So the printed «ཤིང་ལུག་པ» of WB is the cow's syllable spelt with པ, and the printed «བཅུ་པོ» (ten, on five prints) is WB's own slip for nine (counted again: with nine the sixty years sum to sixty, with ten to sixty-one).

A year "becomes" an element by the element of its name in the sixty-year cycle, twelve years each (computed 2026-11-14 with a throwaway cycle; every year WB and UT name falls in the element it is put under). The text does not say so in words; no other count fits all five groups.
