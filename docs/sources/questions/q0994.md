# The Svapnohana's title stands as «སྭ…ཨུ་ཧཱ་ནཱ» on the Peking and the Narthang with a box-headed second stack («པྣ» or «བྣ»); is the Sanskrit svapnoha or svapnoddhāra, and does a Sanskritist know the work under a name that fixes the stack?

RD353.

## Answered 2026-11-14

Answered 2026-11-14: the Sanskrit is svapnohana, not svapnoddhāra. ūha (Monier-Williams: consideration, deliberation, examination) with svapna gives svapn-ohana, «examination of dreams», which is the Tibetan title rmi lam brtag pa; svapnoddhāra would need a stack ddha, and the print's stack after ཨུ is ཧ. The second stack that spells svapna is པྣ (BDRC's Peking and Narthang incipits have pa; the Derge's ba gives no word). 84000 lists Toh 1749 as Svapnohana. A Sanskritist who knows a manuscript was not found (claim svo-sanskrit-original-not-named-in-indian-sources). claims/svapnohana-sanskrit-title-incipits-bdrc-three-prints.md.
