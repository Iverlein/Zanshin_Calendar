# WB ch. 1 p. 13 line 3: the numeral after Saturn (སྤེན་པ) is a nought where the sequence gives ༧; a printer's error or another count?

The sequence of the other planets gives ༧; p. 12 prints the same nought glyph for ཐ་སྐར.

## Answered 2026-11-21

The ༠ after Saturn (སྤེན་པ) is not an error: it is Saturn's weekday number, and the print's own.

Checked 2026-11-21 (rd-t). WB counts the weekdays from the Sunday as 1 and Saturday as 0 (Sunday 1, Monday 2, Mars's Tuesday 3, Mercury's Wednesday 4, Jupiter's Thursday 5, Venus's Friday 6, Saturday 0): the model almanac's month lines print «༠ Saturday, ༡ Sunday … ༦ Friday» (read at 5–7× in month-entries.md), the table of burning dates has «༠ Saturday», and the mansions count from tha skar as 0 in the same way. The numerals on p. 13 are these numbers: the planets in the weekday order of the sun (p. 12 end, ༡), moon ༢, Mars ༣, Mercury ༤, Jupiter ༥, Venus ༦, Saturn ༠, then Rāhu ༨ and the tail ༩. So the nought after Saturn is a seventh day counted as nought, and the sequence's «༧» is not expected. The same weekday numbering explains the five planets' figures of ch. 7 (wb.1:p42), where Saturn's figure was last read as a 0 at ordinary size.
