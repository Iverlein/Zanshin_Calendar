# Which text is Negelein's 'b' (the verse he prints as b 5–6)?

Its words are the Brahmavaivartapurāṇa's Svapnādhyāya (bvp:p662-665) but the list of his letters a–e is garbled; the page image of his Introduction pp. XIX–XX settles it.

## Answered 2026-11-13

Negelein's «b» is the *Svapnādhyāya* of the *Brahmavaivartapurāṇa* (Introduction p. XIX: for its text he used the *Śabdakalpadruma* vol. 5 pp. 470–1, the Calcutta edition of Śaka 1812 vol. II p. 503 ff. and the Leipzig manuscript 1179, the last as base in case of doubt); his «c» is the same Purāṇa 2.70.1 ff., «d» 2.63.1–30, «e» its text after Leipzig 1179 against the printed 2.82.2–59; «a» is the Oxford manuscript Sansc. d 215, «f» the Leipzig manuscript 1180 (called Matsyapurāṇa's *Svapnādhyāya*), «i» the Leipzig manuscript 1178. Read again by an independent session on the page images of the Internet Archive item (leaves n24 and n25, 2026-11-13); it agrees with RD269's reading in neg.md.
