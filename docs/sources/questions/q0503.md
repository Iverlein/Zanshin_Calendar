# «ཏ་ལ་ལམ་དུ་བསྒྱུར་བ་འདྲ» on p. 453: the palm or another word

On p. 453 (img. 459), line 16, reckoners who foretell without knowing even the way are said to be «like turning a ta la into a road» («ཏ་ལ་ལམ་དུ་བསྒྱུར་བ་འདྲ», read ཏ on the scan at four times; the machine's «ཧ་ལ», the hālāhala poison, is not on the page): what is the image, the palm (ཏ་ལ, ta la) or another word?
