# «ཏ་ལ་ལམ་དུ་བསྒྱུར་བ་འདྲ» on p. 453: the palm or another word

On p. 453 (img. 459), line 16, reckoners who foretell without knowing even the way are said to be «like turning a ta la into a road» («ཏ་ལ་ལམ་དུ་བསྒྱུར་བ་འདྲ», read ཏ on the scan at four times; the machine's «ཧ་ལ», the hālāhala poison, is not on the page): what is the image, the palm (ཏ་ལ, ta la) or another word? (RD84)

## Answered 2026-11-14

Answered by q545 (2026-11-06, RD171): three BDRC etexts of the Desi's Vaiḍūrya dkar po (I1KG12907, I1KG2752, I1KG81102) read «ཧ་ལ་ལམ་དུ་བསྒྱུར་བ་འདྲ», not ཏ; ཧ་ལ is poison or black monkshood, and the lines that follow make it a dangerous thing, not a palm. The machine's «ཧ་ལ» was right and the scan's ཏ is the print's differing letter. Rechecked 2026-11-14 (inv-iii): BDRC's search for «ཏ་ལ་ལམ་དུ» finds no text with the palm in this saying.
