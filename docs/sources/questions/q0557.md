# «གནས་སྐྱུག» or «གནས་སྒྱུག» in the notes p. 526 and «ཟླ་བའི་སྣང» on p. 484

## Answered 2026-11-14

Answered 2026-11-14 (inv-rr). First part, «གནས་སྐྱུག» or «གནས་སྒྱུག» (p. 526): the same stack as question 211, answered by inv-nn the same day (open-questions.md, item 211) as «གནས་སྒྱུག» by the verse of p. 362 in three etexts and by the dictionaries («སྒྱུག་མོ», the mother-in-law; no «skyug mo»), the notes' own gloss naming the mother-in-law; the notes' lemma «སྐྱུག» is the 1996 etext's reading. Second part, the first stack of «ཟླ་བའི་སྣང» (p. 484): the same as question 179, answered by inv-nn by sense as «ཟླ་བའི་སྒང» (a gloss «X ཞེས» repeats the verse's X, here «ཟླ་བའི་སྒང་ལ་སྐྱེས»; the stock term of the calendar; the 1996 etext of the notes prints «sgang»); the glyph on Mipham's own print and the typeset edition stays scan-bound (claims/notes-p484-moon-words.md). No block print at higher resolution is needed for the word. Closed.
