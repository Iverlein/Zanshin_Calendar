# Which 'O yug pa (འོ་ཡུག་པ) is meant in the remark of the dream verses (cbl:p236-238), and does a Sakya record give this saying under his name?

RD360.

**Narrowed 2026-11-21 (inv-h).** BDRC phrase search «འོ་ཡུག་པ» (44 hits), «འོ་ཡུག་པའི» (4), «འོ་ཡུག་པས» (1), «འོ་ཡུག་པ་དགེ་བཤེས» (2), «དགེ་བཤེས་དོན་པ» (4), «འོ་ཡུག་པ་བསོད་ནམས་སེང་གེ» (3), «འོ་ཡུག་པ་ཞེས» (1), «དུས་མཚན་ནག་མོ་ཡིན» (1, the CBL itself). BDRC's person record P3089 («'u yug pa Bsod nams seng ge», also Rig pa'i seng ge; Sakya master of logic, student of Sa skya Paṇḍita, died 1253) is the man the Sakya seat-holder lists name; the Sakya company of the name stands, but the saying is in no Sakya record: its only carrier is the CBL. A different «'o yug gi dge bshes Don pa» gives a judgement of Ma gcig Ri ma's experiences in Ngag dbang kun dga' bsod nams's and Shes rab rin chen's collected works (search-only snippets), not this saying. Claim o-yug-pa-is-bdrc-p3089-and-the-other-o-yug-teacher. Still open: whether the remark means P3089.

**Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-i).** «འོ་ཡུག་པ་གསུང» and «འོ་ཡུག་པ་ཆེན་པོ» 0 hits; «གཤིན་རྗེ་དུས་མཚན» (21) and «དུས་མཚན་ནག་མོ» (45) show the second half of the remark is a known figure (Yama's night goddess, in Sakya protector rites) but never under 'O yug pa's name, so no Sakya record gives the saying to him. Still open: P3089 stays the likely man by company, not by a record.

**Narrowed 2026-11-21 (sweep from inv-h).** BDRC's person record P3089 ('u yug pa Bsod nams seng ge, Sakya master of logic, died 1253) is the man the Sakya seat-holder lists name; the saying is in no Sakya record and the Chos 'byung's carrier is the only one. Whether the remark means P3089 stays open.
