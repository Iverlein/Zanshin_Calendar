# The first syllable of «[?]ཁམ་མི་དགོས་ཞེ་ན» in reply 140

In reply 140 (I5875 img. 498, l. 2), what is the first syllable of «[?]ཁམ་མི་དགོས་ཞེ་ན», read «རྦད» by the machine?

RD151 (2026-11-06), by eye at 1.6–6× (img. 498, page x about 4,850–5,250): ra-like head bar, a loop and a long right stroke that may be a suffix da; no vowel sign above, so not «ལི». The machine's «རྦད» is not contradicted; the letter under the head (ba, ga or da) stays open.
