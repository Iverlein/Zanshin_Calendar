# The first syllable of «[?]ཁམ་མི་དགོས་ཞེ་ན» in reply 140

In reply 140 (I5875 img. 498, l. 2), what is the first syllable of «[?]ཁམ་མི་དགོས་ཞེ་ན», read «རྦད» by the machine?

RD151 (2026-11-06), by eye at 1.6–6× (img. 498, page x about 4,850–5,250): ra-like head bar, a loop and a long right stroke that may be a suffix da; no vowel sign above, so not «ལི». The machine's «རྦད» is not contradicted; the letter under the head (ba, ga or da) stays open.

## Answered 2026-11-14

The first syllable is «རྦད»: the word is «རྦད་ཁམ», boasting. BDRC's phrase search «རྦད་ཁམ་མི་དགོས» finds the line in a second print's etext of the replies (the Dpe rnying kun btus copy, MW2PD17386, I1KG81103, char. 601045), whose snippet reads «de ltar bsal na dka' ba med par rtogs pas rbad kham mi dgos zhen … 'di sogs pas ngal sogs byas pa'i danas shed can gyi rgyags pa mang du 'byung srid kyang sngar phan nang mang»: the same sentence as reply 140 with rbad kham mi dgos zhe na. That agrees with the machine's «རྦད», with RD151's eye reading (ra-like head, a loop under it, a long right stroke) and with q384 (rbad kham = boasting in Ives Waldo and the Tshig mdzod chen mo).
