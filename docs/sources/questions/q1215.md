# KD vol. 1 p. 579: what are «མིག་དམར» and «རེའུ་དྲོས» in Manjushri's birth time?

Is «mig dmar» (Mars) a year-planet of the first year of the cycle, and what hour is «re'u dros»? (KD vol. 1 p. 579, etext p. 595.)

## Narrowed 2026-11-14

Steinert's dictionaries (locate.py dict) key «mig dmar» as Mars and Tuesday (Hopkins 2015, Ives Waldo, Jim Valby, Tshig mdzod chen mo; Mahavyutpatti aṅgāraka). Rangjung Yeshe and Ives Waldo also give it as the name of the 58th year of the sixty-year cycle (rab byung), Raktaksha, the male wood mouse year (shing pho byi), not the first year (rab byung year 1 is Prabhava, rab 'byung). So in a birth-time line «mig dmar» is Mars, or at most a year name by that 58th sense; it is not the first year's planet. «re'u dros» has no headword in the 64 dictionaries; the hour stays unread.
