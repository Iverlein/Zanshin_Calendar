# The seventh of the old: «ཕན་ངོན» or «བན་བོན»

Is the seventh of the old «ཕན་ངོན» as the 1996 print and the reprint have it, or «བན་བོན» (of the monks and bon priests) as the search-only copy I1KG81102 has it?

RD180 (2026-11-06), narrowed: the 1996 etext (img. 81) has «ཕན་ངོན(༧) བཀྲ་དང་བདུན་དུ་བཤད»; «བན་བོན» occurs in pp. 60–67 only as the monks and bon priests who strike the living, never in a list of bkra.
