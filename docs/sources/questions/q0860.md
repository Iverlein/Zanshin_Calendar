# Byang pa's line has the dead drinking water (ཆུ) and dancing with fiends (སྲིན་པོ) where the Drathang has beer (ཆང) and the dead (གཤིན་པོ): his print or his own change?

His print or his own change; the Drathang has beer and the dead.


Narrowed (RD271, 2026-11-09): BDRC's exact search finds water (ཆུ་འཐུང) and fiends (སྲིན་པོ) only in Byang pa's *Bdud rtsi'i chu rgyun* and in two commentaries that may follow him; beer (ཆང་འཐུང་དེས་འདྲུད, 7 hits) and the dead (གཤིན་པོ་དག་དང་ལྷན་ཅིག་གར་བྱེད, 16 hits) in every other witness, the Drathang with them. His paraphrase repeats both, so they are his text's, whether from his print or his own hand is not told. Claim gt-water-or-beer-fiends-or-the-dead.

Narrowed again (inv-n, 2026-11-14): the line 15 «srin po dag dang lhan cig gar byed» (the fiends) is not Byang pa's alone. The three hits of BDRC's exact search are Skyem pa Tshe dbang's *Rgyud bzhi'i rnam bshad* (MW28843, I4711, char. 305621) and its second etext as the *Tshig don nyi ma* (MW00EGS1017463_51AD1D, I1PD470), one work, and Byang pa's *Bdud rtsi'i chu rgyun* in his second etext (MW15590, I1PD380). Skyem pa's paraphrase reads «gshin po dang lhan cig du chang 'thung ba … srin po dag dang lhan cig gar byed pa»: the dead drinking beer, as the Drathang has it, and the fiends dancing, as Byang pa has it. So the fiends are in two commentators, the water («chu 'thung», line 13) in Byang pa alone (MW27496, I4706, char. 244942 and 246069; each exact string 1 hit). The mixed pair in Skyem pa makes the fiends the older or the commoner reading of the print the two commentators used, or Skyem pa's borrowing from Byang pa at that line only; no text says which. His print or his own change still stays open. (The claim gt-water-or-beer-fiends-or-the-dead counts the fiends as a hit in "one more commentary"; that commentary is Skyem pa's.)
