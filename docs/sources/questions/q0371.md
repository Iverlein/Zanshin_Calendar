# The root text's count for the la board: the Moonbeams' or LT's

The *Moonbeams*' la pebbles (dz:i055-057) put half and half at the son year, black at the enemy year and two white and one black at the friend, where LT's special pebble of the birth (lt:p26-28) puts one white at the son year, half and half at the friend and three black at the enemy: which is the "root text's" count the notes refer to for the la board ("three black for the great cut-off and so on", wb.2:p499-500)?

## Answered 2026-11-03

The notes' root text for the la board is WB vol. 2, p. 45, which lists 12 places (a great and a small of six kinds of year); the Moonbeams follows it. LT's count is WB vol. 1, p. 260 (the birth's key pebble) word for word, so LT is a copy, not a second witness or a rival count; the notes' chart of 12 names fits p. 45 (RD144, verdicts.md).
