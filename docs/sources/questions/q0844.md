# In ch. 81 two later witnesses read «ལྷས་ཚོར་ངུས་ཏེ» and the Serthang scan and most others «ལྷས་ཚོར་དུས་ཏེ»: is ངུས a reading of the Bhutan print and the 2005 Lhan thabs, or a fault of the etext, and what does ལྷས་ mean?

No commentary says.

## Answered 2026-11-09

Neither is a reading of the prints. On the Bhutan print's scan (img. 267) and on the 2005 Supplement's scan (p. 325) the syllable is དུས (ད, ུ, ས); ངུས is the etext's OCR in both. The 1991 print and the Serthang scan have དུས too. ལྷས is ལྷས on every scan read, not ལུས. See the claim st-lhas-tshor-lder-bzo and the passages mnb:p267 and lth:p325.
