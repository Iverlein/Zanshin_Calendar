# Is the 黄幡 the grave of the year's triad or of the year branch's own agent

Is the 黄幡, "the grave of the 大歳" (§13), the grave of the year's triad (as the modern table has it) or of the year branch's own agent? The 暦林問答集 states neither; §10 and §46 read together give the triad's. (RD92)

## Answered 2026-11-05

The 黄幡 is «常居三合墓辰» in the 協紀辨方書, the grave of the year's triad; the 具注暦 heads agree cell by cell. ([kyureki.md](../kyureki.md), *The direction gods' last questions in the old texts (RD163)*)
