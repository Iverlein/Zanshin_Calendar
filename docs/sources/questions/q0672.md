# Is Prajñāmaṇi (པྲཛྙཱ་མ་ཎི) the Sanskrit form of Khyenrab Norbu's name?

At whose request The Precious Key was gathered; Khyenrab Norbu (མཁྱེན་རབ་ནོར་བུ) wrote the life reckoning in the same print. By prajñā and maṇi: an inference, not a stated line. (RD181, 2026-11-07)

## Answered 2026-11-13

Yes, by BDRC's catalogue. BDRC's person record P227 (rtsed thang mkhyen rab nor bu, 1883-1962) lists «pradz+nyA ma Ni», པྲཛྙཱ་མ་ཎི, as his other name beside the primary «mkhyen rab nor bu», and BDRC's full-text search finds the name used for him in his own short works (MW2DB4624) and for a venerable head of medicine and reckoning in a pupil's collected works (MW28936, snippet). Prajñā and maṇi render «mkhyen rab» and «nor bu». That the Prajñāmaṇi of *The Precious Key*'s colophon is he rests on the name, not on a line of the colophon.
