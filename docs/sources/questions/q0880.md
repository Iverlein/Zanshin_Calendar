# What is «Dpal rno bkra», and why is this part of the Gnam chos so called?

Its history («rno bkra'i lo rgyus», vol. 1 ff. 29r–29v, pp. 493–494, I1662 img. 501–502) is catalogued and not yet read (RD286).

## Answered 2026-11-10

Answered in part by RD315 (2026-11-10): see q795; the Dpal rno bkra is the name of the volume the Great Compassionate One gave the queen 'Od ldan rgyal mo, and the part of the Gnam chos is named after it; the word is not glossed.
