# The light-clearer's grave in the tiger, snake, monkey and pig months: which of ox, sheep, dragon, dog

The grave («དུར») of the light-clearer in the tiger, snake, monkey and pig months is one of ox, sheep, dragon and dog (WB's ch. 23 triads: dog, ox, dragon, sheep; Lha mo tshul khrims' elements: sheep, dog, ox, dragon): which assignment does p. 219 mean? (narrows 443, 444, 28)

RD182 (2026-11-06), narrowed by inference only: p. 219 says «ར་བ་བཞི་ལ་དུར་གྱི་སྟེང» and names no animal; Wangkhyi's p. 210 gives the grave animal by season (spring dog, summer ox, autumn dragon, winter sheep), tiger, snake, monkey and pig being the first months of those seasons, so the order is dog, ox, dragon, sheep (the ch. 23 triads), not Lha mo tshul khrims' order.

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-iii).** The Utpala Garland (MW25167, etext p338-339; state etext) gives the grave («དུར་གཤེད») of the dead by pairs of years: tiger and hare, large sheep, small ox; horse and snake, large dog, small dragon; bird and monkey, large ox, small sheep; mouse and pig, large dragon, small dog. Read by the large grave this is the element rule (wood, fire, iron, water: sheep, dog, ox, dragon) in a funeral list, and it is not p. 219's line, which names no animal; WB's own p. 210 scheme (dog, ox, dragon, sheep by season) stays the inference of RD182. Neither is a gloss of the light-clearer's «དུར». The same Utpala has «དུར་གཤེད་ར་བཞིའི་ཚེས་དྲུག། འབྲིང་བཞིའི་ཉེར་དྲུག། ཐའི་བཅུ་དྲུག» (the page after), the dates of the grave and the slayer in the first, middle and last months, another use of the same three-month division.
