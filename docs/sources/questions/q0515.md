# The light-clearer's grave in the tiger, snake, monkey and pig months: which of ox, sheep, dragon, dog

The grave («དུར») of the light-clearer in the tiger, snake, monkey and pig months is one of ox, sheep, dragon and dog (WB's ch. 23 triads: dog, ox, dragon, sheep; Lha mo tshul khrims' elements: sheep, dog, ox, dragon): which assignment does p. 219 mean? (narrows 443, 444, 28)

RD182 (2026-11-06), narrowed by inference only: p. 219 says «ར་བ་བཞི་ལ་དུར་གྱི་སྟེང» and names no animal; Wangkhyi's p. 210 gives the grave animal by season (spring dog, summer ox, autumn dragon, winter sheep), tiger, snake, monkey and pig being the first months of those seasons, so the order is dog, ox, dragon, sheep (the ch. 23 triads), not Lha mo tshul khrims' order.
