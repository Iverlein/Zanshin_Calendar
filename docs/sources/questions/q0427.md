# Is a place's verdict laid where the cutting and the rise and decline agree?

P. 166's «གཅོད་འདྲལ། དར་གུད། ཁ་དམར» three to each element: is the verdict (ཁ་དམར) of a place laid, as p. 168 says of wealth, where the cutting and the rise and decline agree (white, a good place; black, a bad), and is there a white-and-black count for each of the eight places, as p. 172 says of the fighting enemies, which the lines of pp. 166–168 do not give?

**Narrowed 2026-10-27 (RD58, second reading, [verdicts.md](../verdicts.md), section E).** On the 1996 print the wealth lines are printed lines 15–16 of img. 176 (not l. 4) and end «དེ་གཉིས་བསྟུན་ལ་ཁ་དམར་གདགས»; «དེ་གཉིས» "those two" can be the body and the power named in the line before it as well as the cutting and the rise and decline, so the line does not by itself lay the verdict where those two methods agree. The grave's lines read the forefathers' year cutting the moment («དུར་ལ་སྐྱོན་མེད་ཚུར་བཅད་སྐྱོན») apart from the luck's rise and decline. No line counts white and black for a place: still open.
