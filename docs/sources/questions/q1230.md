# KP month panels: what do the signs ++ and ×× after cells mean, and is the first wide cell (head sign) a lord of WB's list?

Head sign «ཆོད་དབང» or «ཚེ་དབང», then «བྱ་མ»? Good and bad places are a guess.

**Narrowed 2026-11-21 (sweep from inv-a).** Looked at on the scan 2026-11-21 (inv-a): KP img. 93 (BDRC I4CZ65599, tiger-month panel) at 4×. The signs are plain printed «××» and «++» after cells' words: «++» after the cell at the top right, whose word reads «ཤེལ་ཁང» (shel khang, the Crystal House, one of WB's six good gods of the sky line, ch. 31 nos. 23-28) and after the bottom-left cell, «××» after the top-left and bottom-right cells. In the machine's lists of the twelve panels every «++» or «+» follows one of the six good gods (gsas khang, rnam thos, shel khang, gser dbang, tshe dbang) and «×» follows «ཧལ་ཁྱི», a bad lord. So the signs read as good and bad marks, and the head word is plausibly «ཚེ་དབང» (the sixth of WB's six). One panel on the scan; no KP text states the meaning.
