# Who are the «རྒྱ་བལ་ཁ་ཅིག» (some of the Chinese and Nepalese) of the Mchog dga', whose lines give Saturn «bad company and climbing hills» in the dream (kd.3:p177-179)? No print of the root has the lines; BDRC's exact-phrase search finds them in the Kun 'dus chen mo only.

RD376.

## Narrowed 2026-11-13

The phrase is not peculiar to the dream lines: the Mchog dga' uses the same «རྒྱ་བལ་ཁ་ཅིག» three more times in vol. 3, at pp. 71, 77 and 87 (kd.3:p71, kd.3:p77, kd.3:p87, read 2026-11-11), each time beside the Kālacakra commentators and as the holder of a calendar reckoning (the spelling «mon gre» of the mansion, the long and short vowels set in the directions and corners, the verse of Rāhu and the planets by direction). So «some of the Chinese and Nepalese» is the Mchog dga's regular label for a body of Kālacakra-style reckoning, of outside origin, and the Saturn lines of the dream are a fourth place where it quotes their verse. No name or title is given in any of them, and the lines are found in the Kun 'dus chen mo only (BDRC search 2026-11-10), so the book is not identified.
