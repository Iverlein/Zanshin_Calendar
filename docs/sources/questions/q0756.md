# The figure after «དེ་ལ་འཁོར་ཕྱེད» on WB p. 34 is damaged in the print: a half wheel (27) is 13½, which is no whole figure; what does the line give?

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (scan-f).** The line «དེ་ལ་འཁོར་ཕྱེད(?)སྟེར» lies on the Taikhang reproduction (I5877) img. 71 or 72 (1996 pp. 34–35); the OCR of the half blocks did not find it and the line was not read by eye. Open.

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (scan-h).** Still not found. Img. 71 and 72 of I5877 were opened only as whole sides; the line «དེ་ལ་འཁོར་ཕྱེད(?)སྟེར» was not located in the time given. Open.

**Read again 2026-11-21 (rd-u).** The line is on the Gser thang reprint I3CN5028 img. 41 (p. 34), found at 1x–2x: «དེ་ལ་འཁོར་ཕྱེད( ) སྟེར་ / རམ་དམན།», the bracket is one figure wide (as wide as a one-digit bracket such as «(༠)» of the same page), with two short strokes at its top and a curl with a dot at its foot, nothing between: a one-digit figure damaged in the middle, not a two-digit «༡༣». The Gser thang etext has no figure («འཁོར་ཕྱེད༌) སྟེར»), and the typeset Kun 'dus vol. 2 (VEIE0OPI2F246A26_I4716) quotes the same sentence three times without any figure («དེ་ལ་འཁོར་ཕྱེད་དོར་རམ་དམན», «…ཕྱེད་བྱིན་ནམ་དམན», «…འཁོར་བྱེད་སྟེར་རམ་དམན»). So a figure is not needed for the sense: "to that give or subtract the half wheel", and 13½ does not arise. What the bracket holds stays unread. Open.
Note of 2026-11-24 (RD204): the bracket after «འཁོར་ཕྱེད» on pp. 32 and 34 is the same damaged mark; at 8x it has two rows, so a stacked pair, read «༡༣» over «༣༠» (the half wheel 13½; WB writes «འཁོར་ཕྱེད(༡༣)» twice elsewhere and «ཆུ་ཚོད་ཕྱེད(༣༠)» once). Not a one-digit bracket as rd-u noted. An undamaged print (Zhol, Taikhang) would confirm. Passage corrections in corpus/wb/ch03.md.
