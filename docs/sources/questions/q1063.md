# In Klu'i byang chub's line «མི་དགེ་དགེ་ནི་རྨི་ལམ་བདག །མེད་པའི་གཏམ་གྱིས་རྣམ་པར་སྦྱང་», is the sense «the dream, whether non-virtuous or virtuous, purify it by the talk of selflessness», as the Sngags rim chen mo's «བདག་མེད་པའི་དོན་བསམས་ནས་སྦྱང་བར་བཤད» takes it?

RD373.

## Answered 2026-11-13

Yes. The line is cut across the verse end, and the sense is "the dream, non-virtuous or virtuous: purify it by the talk of selflessness" (bdag med pa'i gtam); the dream is the object. Padma dkar po's prose on the lines (VE10736_016 p. 43) says «if it is bad (ངན་ན)» it is purified by the talk of selflessness, and Tsong kha pa's Sngags rim chen mo takes it the same way.
