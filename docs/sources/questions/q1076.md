# Do the interlinear glosses of the Narthang, Peking and Gser bris ma prints, which open at the dream verses with a note on whether the instant (tattāla) or the nine-part is looked at (dycn:i127-128), come from the same hand as the Mchog dga's discussion of the field lord (kd.3:p177-179)?

RD376.

## Looked at 2026-11-13 (inv-v)

Not answerable from the pool: dycn:i127-128 is the verses with the interlinear glosses left out, and the glosses of the Narthang, Peking and Gser bris ma prints were read only by machine, noisily (dyc); no passage of the pool or of the Mchog dga' (kd.3:p177-179) names an author of the glosses, and the Mchog dga' discussion of the field lord is a commentary in its own words, so a common hand cannot be shown or excluded without the glosses read on the scans. Stays open.
