# In the Yongle dadian's day entries the dream line carries the words 牛黄, 廨 or 欄 or 倉囷, and 不將 after the omen; what do they name, and what are the books 雷英發編『萬曆錦囊拾遺備用』 and 呂茂明編『陰陽備用選擇成書』 and their dates, given that the volume is of 1408 and the title names a Wanli?

RD375.

## Answered 2026-11-13

The three words name where the ox-yellow spirit (牛黃) stands for the day, a pen, office or granary; 不將 is unsettled; 雷英發's 萬曆 is an almanac word, 呂茂明's book has no date found (claim yongle-ox-yellow-bujiang-and-the-books).
