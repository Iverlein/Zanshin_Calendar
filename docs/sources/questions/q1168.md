# Is the tenth-day gathering (བཅུ་ཚོགས) of Rtsa gsum gling pa's lineage tied to Padmasambhava's tenth (Guru's day)? The pages read (tsl.30:i240, i277, i287) do not say so.

Raised by the 2026-11-13 investigation a; see the question's cited passages.

## Answered 2026-11-14

Tied in the tradition and in the lineage's own successor, but not stated on the pages of Rtsa gsum gling pa's own life that were read.

The dictionaries. «ཚེས་བཅུ» (tshes bcu) is in the Tshig mdzod chen mo and the Bod yig tshig gter rgya mtsho the tenth of the waxing and the waning half (the twenty-fifth), «ཡར་ངོ་མར་ངོའི་ཚེས་བཅུ་ལ་ཚོགས་འཁོར་གཏོང་བ», "the tenth of both halves on which the feast gathering is given" (so «བཅུ་ཚོགས» is the day's own gathering); Rangjung Yeshe: "offerings on the tenth day, in commemoration of Guru Padmasambhava", and «tshes bcu'i phan yon», "the benefits of the observance of the tenth day rites of the Nyingmapa tradition"; the Dung dkar tshig mdzod chen mo's «སྤྲེལ་ཟླ་ཚེས་བཅུའི་གར་འཆམ» is the dance of the tenth of the monkey month with the Guru's eight manifestations.

The lineage. In vol. 30 (printed pp. 565-750, the life of Pad rgyal gling pa): «ཚེས་བཅུའི་ཉིན་རང་ཉིད་ཀྱིས་བཞུགས་གནས་སུ་ཚོགས་མཆོད་མཛད་ཅིང་ཚིག་བདུན་གསོལ་འདེབས», on the tenth he himself made the feast offering at his seat and the Seven-Line Prayer (the prayer to Padmasambhava), then took out the treasure (image 624); the dākinī palaces' tenth-day offerings (pp. 265, 279); the forefathers' seat's «ཚེས་བཅུ་དུས་མཆོད» (vol. 29 p. 109); the Dgongs 'dus cycle of Pad rgyal gling pa was invited on «སྟོན་ཟླ་ར་བའི་ཚེས་བཅུ» of the earth-pig year of the sixteenth Rab byung (tsl.28:i79, i72), «མཁའ་འགྲོའི་… ཟབ་གཏེར»; its history is in the guru's own voice «ང་ཉིད་པདྨ་རྒྱལ་པོ་ནི». The pages of Rtsa gsum gling pa's own life that were read (tsl.30:i87, i240, i277, i287) do not name the guru beside the tenth; the tie is the tradition's, shown by the successor's practice and the dictionaries. The tenth of the dream is therefore the tradition's tenth, and its early autumn tenth (the monkey month, tsl.30:i87) falls on the calendar day of the Dgongs 'dus invitation (tsl.28:i79) by the almanac count of q1166, a different year. Read in the etext; no sentence found says a dream on the tenth is truer.
