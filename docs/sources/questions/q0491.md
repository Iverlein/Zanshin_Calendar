# Where does the year of the 八将神 begin

Where does the year of the 八将神 begin, 立春 or the new year? None of §6, §8–§14 says. (RD92)

## Answered 2026-11-14

The year of the year's direction gods begins at 立春, not at the new year. The 欽定協紀辨方書 (1739), 卷34, in its rules for building a house (Wikisource, 四庫全書本, 論豎造宅舍), sets the hand-over of the year's baleful spirits at 立春: «大寒五日後擇日拆屋起手、立春前擇日豎造完工、不忌開山立向年月尅山家及歲月諸凶神、謂之歲官交承。如已過立春後、年月凶神方位已定、不可修作» ("after the fifth day of 大寒 choose a day to pull the house down and begin; before 立春 choose a day to raise it and finish; the mountain's and the direction's clash with the year's and month's baleful spirits is not avoided; this is called the handing over of the year's officers. Once 立春 has passed, the places of the year's and month's baleful spirits are fixed and one may not build"). The 八将神 are the year's gods by the year's branch (簠簋内伝 巻一 §3), so their year changes at the same point. The pool's own rule for every year-keyed annotation of the Japanese almanac is the same (立春 starts the year: the year's 干支, the year star, the 恵方; kyureki.md, *立春 also starts the year*). No Japanese text read states the 八将神's boundary in words; the 暦林問答集 §6, §8-§14 say nothing, as the question had it. The Chinese handbook is the witness, and the app's 立春 stands.
