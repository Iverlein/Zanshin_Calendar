# A speckled deciding pebble in the household round

In the household round a member brings four deciding pebbles (WB vol. 1, p. 385; the notes, p. 490), and a deciding pebble of the year of age may be speckled where a place's white and black are even (the notes, p. 490, «མཉམ་ན་ཁྲ་བོ་བཞག»). Does a speckled deciding pebble enter the round as a speckled pebble, and how is it read in a place whose readings (pp. 385–387) name white, black, blue, green, yellow and red only? The *Moonbeams* lays the member's pebbles "white or black as in general" (DZ f. 22b) and so has no speckled one.

**Narrowed 2026-10-25 ([claims/household-round-colours.md](../claims/household-round-colours.md)).** No source read lays or reads a speckled pebble in the household round: the regent's question and reply 123 (ys:i096, ys:i478-479, on the scan) only defend the round as Phugpa's, from the *Clear Lamp*; LT makes each of a member's four a single plain meeting, white or black, the trigram and mewa pebbles in their own colours (lt:p20-25); Zhechen Gyaltsab lays a speckled verdict for a tied year-of-age place and then names none in the round (zg:p285-286); the Bon *Mirror* (a witness only) and KD's digest none. Blue, green, yellow and red come only from the mewa (and LT's trigram) pebbles. Only the notes' own round can bring a speckled member pebble, and how it reads in a place is not said.
