# A speckled deciding pebble in the household round

In the household round a member brings four deciding pebbles (WB vol. 1, p. 385; the notes, p. 490), and a deciding pebble of the year of age may be speckled where a place's white and black are even (the notes, p. 490, «མཉམ་ན་ཁྲ་བོ་བཞག»). Does a speckled deciding pebble enter the round as a speckled pebble, and how is it read in a place whose readings (pp. 385–387) name white, black, blue, green, yellow and red only? The *Moonbeams* lays the member's pebbles "white or black as in general" (DZ f. 22b) and so has no speckled one.
