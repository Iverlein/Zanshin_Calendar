# What does nam tshes la after nyer dgu in MRS p. 77 mean, and where does the Sngags kyi be bum fix the 15th and Mars-day for Sarasvati

From the 2026-11-21 practice-day readings.

## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-s)

BDRC phrase search «ཉེར་དགུ་ནམ་ཚེས་ལ» (nyer dgu nam tshes la) 2 hits, both the same sentence of Dilgo Khyentse's short life of Mipham: MW8LS20744 (Shechen 2013, VEIE0OPIFB44F448_I8LS20758 offset 77020) and a copy in the Rin chen gter mdzod rig gnas dar spel khang collection (MW3CN5285, VEIE0OPI7B3ADCD2_I3CN5287 offset 405565). Neither copy glosses the words; no other text uses the phrase. «སྒྲ་དབྱངས་མཆོད་པ་ཚེས་བཅོ་ལྔ» 0 hits; «གཟའ་མིག་དམར» 379 hits, none read (a general term). The Sngags kyi be bum itself was not found in the etexts, so the fixing of the 15th and Mars-day for Sarasvati stays unverified. Still open; the claim mipham-kept-the-tenth-of-both-halves-the-29th-and-the-15th keeps its note that the reading of «nam tshes la» is uncertain.

## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-b)

The Sngags kyi be bum is found: it is Mipham's «ལས་སྣ་ཚོགས་པའི་སྔགས་ཀྱི་བེ་བུམ་དགོས་འདོད་ཀུན་འབྱུང་གཏེར་གྱི་བུམ་པ་བཟང་པོ» (BDRC lists it as MW3KG160, MW3KG374 and others; the Gsung 'bum etext MW1PD76231, vol. 33, the pool's mgz). Its Sarasvatī instruction on the page the pool holds (mgz.33:p166, etext marker [p174]) says: «འཕགས་མ་དབྱངས་ཅན་མའི་བདེན་ཚིག་གི་སྔགས། … གཟའ་མིག་དམར་རེས་དང་གནམ་གང་ནམ་བྱུང་ལ་དྲི་བཟང་པོའི་དུད་པ་བཅས་ཚར་བདུན་རེ་བཟླས་པས་…» (gza' mig dmar res dang gnam gang nam byung la …): seven recitations with incense smoke on a Tuesday and gnam gang "whenever [they] occur". So the Be bum page fixes the Mars-day and gnam gang (the new moon, q1302), not the 15th; the 15th is Dilgo Khyentse's wording, and the mind-clearing (blo rig gsal ba) offering to Sarasvatī itself was not found in vol. 33 (etext searched for «བློ་རིག», «དབྱངས་ཅན་མཆོད», «ཚེས་བཅོ་ལྔ»: no hit on the Sarasvatī pages). On «ནམ་ཚེས»: the etext of MRS (MW8LS20744, p. 76) has it twice, «ཉེར་དགུ་ནམ་ཚེས་ལ» and «གཟའ་མིག་དམར་ནམ་ཚེས་ལ», both before a "without a break" clause, which matches the Be bum's «ནམ་བྱུང་ལ» ("whenever it occurs"): «ནམ་ཚེས» probably means "whenever the date comes", i.e. each time the 29th, or the 15th and Mars-day, falls. Another hit, «གནམ་ཚེས་ལ» in the Gesar episode Sum gling g.yul 'gyed («da lo gza' skar gnam tshes la»), is a different use. Searches of «ཉེར་དགུ་གནམ་ཚེས», «ཉེར་དགུ་དུས་ཚེས་ལ» 0 hits; «ཉེར་དགུ་གནམ་གང» 34 hits (the stock pair). Still open: no passage glosses «ནམ་ཚེས», and the Be bum text of the 15th was not found.
