# Rin spungs pa glosses the boy's sign as the mother's «ལུས་ཡང་ཤིང་ཆེ་བ»: is ཆེ་བ the raised right side or a second property of the body?

Rsp:p57; the body light and large.

## Answered 2026-11-10

Answered by RD313 (2026-11-10), on etexts. In Rin spungs pa (I4707 pp. 237–239) ཆེ་བ is a second word on the body («the body light and large»), not the raised right side: the raised side is given already by «མ་ལུས་གཡས་མཐོ་ཞིང་», and his girl gloss has only «heavy». The phrase is in no other text; the Moonbeams puts ཆེ་བ with the raised flank («དཀུ་གཡས་ལོགས་མཐོ་ཞིང་ཆེ་བ»), so where he takes it from stays open. Claim st-ch2-lus-yang-shing-che-ba-rin-spungs-pa.
