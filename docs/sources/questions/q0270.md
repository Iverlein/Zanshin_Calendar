# The upper, lower and middle pebbles of the la board

The general verdict speaks of the upper pebbles, the lower pebbles and pebbles cut off in the middle (WB vol. 2, p. 52). Which places of the twelve are upper, lower and middle?

**Narrowed 2026-10-23 (RD45, [claims/dz-dead-board-rows.md](../claims/dz-dead-board-rows.md), [claims/la-pebbles-colours-manuals.md](../claims/la-pebbles-colours-manuals.md)).** The *Moonbeams* lays the la places with fixed counts and colours, and has a board of its own for taking the living out of the dead: four rows from the top (the paternal kin; the mother's kin; sons-in-law, grandchildren and widows; the *do bdag*) and five columns by age and sex (dz:i055-057). This supports "upper" as the male line, by inference; no manual names WB's upper, lower and middle places.

**Narrowed 2026-11-03 (RD136, [claims/dead-la-upper-lower-waist-male-female-line.md](../claims/dead-la-upper-lower-waist-male-female-line.md)).** WB states what the three mean: the upper pebbles empty, the male line; the lower, the female line's «རྨང» (the ordinary sense, a foundation); cut at the waist, the kin splits in two or three (p. 52, read on the 1996 scan, the Gser thang reprint and the Zhol print). That is not an inference, as the earlier note had it. Which of the twelve places are upper, lower or middle stays unsaid: Mipham says no commentary names them and that the pebbles are laid "from the eldest down"; ch. 26's board of eight (wb.1:p471-472) uses the same words for forebears, oneself and demon-harm and wealth, another board.
