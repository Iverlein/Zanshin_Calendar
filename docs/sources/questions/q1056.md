# What are the Sanskrit names behind the twelve yud tsam of the day on p. 438 (དཀར, བྱམས་ལྡན, མིག་དམར, དྲག་བུ, དབང་ཆེན, དག་པ, བྱི་བཞིན, ལེགས་མཛེས, འཁོར, འཁོར་བར་བྱེད་པ, དགེ་བ་སྦྱིན, ཟླ་བ་མཆོག་སྦྱིན་པ), and do they belong to a list of muhūrta names?

RD378.

## Searched 2026-11-13, no answer

The twelve names occur in the Derge volume (VE4CZ5369_I1KG9214) only on p. 438 (དྲག་བུ nowhere else; བྱམས་ལྡན also on p. 245 in another list; ལེགས་མཛེས and དགེ་བ་སྦྱིན on other pages as plain words), and not at all in the Kun 'dus vol. 3 etext. The Sanskrit edition's text layer (Trivandrum Sanskrit Series 70, part 1) has no «muhūrta», and no list of the day's divisions near mmk.2:p158-160 or p. 175-176. No Tibetan or Sanskrit list of muhūrta names was found in the pool. The resemblance of the first two names to Śveta and Maitra remains a doubt; a published list of the muhūrta names (a Kālacakra or Jyotiṣa text) has to be found first.
