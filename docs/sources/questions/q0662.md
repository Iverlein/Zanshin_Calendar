# «སྒྲ» or «སྨྲ» in p. 380's lifespan verses

In the characters of the lifespan verses of p. 380 (the doctor, the Dharma speaker), is the print's word «སྒྲ» (sound, grammar) or «སྨྲ» (speaker) in «ཆོས་སྒྲ་ཚུལ་ཁྲིམས»? (RD158)

## Answered 2026-11-14

«སྨྲ» (smra, speaker): the Dharma speaker (ཆོས་སྨྲ, chos smra), keeping the moral rules (ཚུལ་ཁྲིམས་ལྡན, tshul khrims ldan). Read 2026-11-14 in BDRC's full-text search of the exact phrase «ཆོས་སྨྲ་ཚུལ་ཁྲིམས་ལྡན» (4 hits, WB's 1996 print MW2CZ8040 in vol. I1KG12907, the Gser thang reprint MW3CN5026 in I3CN5029, and two other volumes); the etexts of the 1996 print (OCR marked good) and of the reprint have «chos smra tshul khrims ldan pa yin» at the doctor's verse and «chos smra tshul khrims ldan pa'i mi» at the second place; «ཆོས་སྒྲ་ཚུལ་ཁྲིམས་ལྡན» gives 0 hits. With the Zhol print read by eye on img. 1138 line 2 (RD158, fourth pass: the stack carries ྨ and ྲ) the word is «སྨྲ» at the first place on a scan and at both places on two etexts; the second place was not looked at on a scan. The passage's «སྒྲ» was the machine's error and is corrected in wb.2:p378-384.
