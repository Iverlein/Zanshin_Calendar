# Is the printed figure after «གཟུགས་མེ» and «མཁའ་ཡོན» on WB vol. 1 p. 185 really (༧) and (༡༠), and do they match any misprint slip? The number words give 31 and 30 chu tshod (q676).

Raised by the 2026-11-13 investigation h.

## Answered 2026-11-21

The print has (༧) after «གཟུགས་མེ» and (༡༠) after «མཁའ་ཡོན» on p. 185 (reprint I3CN5028 img. 184, line 7, seen again at 4x on 2026-11-21 (rd-w): a single glyph of the 7 shape, then two glyphs, a ༡ and a ༠), as q676 recorded on both prints, and they are not the values. The number words give 31 and 30, and the check is arithmetic: a house is 135 chu tshod, a house-day its thirtieth, 4;30, and the place lies seven house-days from the Brahma-line (p. 185), 7 x 4;30 = 31;30, so the winter solstice at the author's place is 18 mansion-minutes, 31 chu tshod, 30 srang. The printed (7) and (10) are the print's own stray figures; no correction slip for them turned up in the searches of q680 (BDRC phrase search found no other print or slip).
