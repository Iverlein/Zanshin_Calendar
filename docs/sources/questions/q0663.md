# Is the thirty of Mipham's notes (WB vol. 2, p. 532) the starting points of ch. 27's small reckonings, so that the bare khams of p. 17 is the year-horse's element

Are the thirty «རྒྱ་བོད་སྐོར་མགོ་སུམ་ཅུ» of Mipham's notes (WB vol. 2, p. 532) the starting points of ch. 27's small reckonings, p. 17's four among them, so that the bare «ཁམས» of p. 17 is the element of the year-horse of the one reckoned and not the power's? Read so by us; qualifies the claim ch27-bare-khams-is-the-power-by-mipham. (RD179)

## Answered 2026-11-13

No. The thirty of the notes are ch. 26's thirty starting points: the notes' tenth, eleventh, bride, house and vessel are ch. 26's numbers, and their rule of the fourth upward fighting the life force is ch. 26's, while p. 17's four reckonings fight by the eighteenth and tenth. The notes say nothing of ch. 27's small reckonings, so the year-horse rule is not shown for p. 17's bare «ཁམས» and the claim that read p. 17 as one of the thirty is corrected.
