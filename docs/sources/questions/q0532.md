# The dates of the 簠簋内伝's 八将神 table and the 拾芥抄's 金神七殺方

What is the date of the 簠簋内伝's 八将神 table, and of the 拾芥抄's 金神七殺方 (both are in prints whose catalogue dates are only those of the printing)? The attributions (安倍晴明; 洞院公賢) are the books' own and the catalogue's.

**Narrowed 2026-11-05 (RD163).** The table in use by 756; the 金神 rows 戊癸 and 甲己 by 1093–1149; the 金神 section in a manuscript no later than the early Muromachi (橋本進吉). The books themselves stay undated.
