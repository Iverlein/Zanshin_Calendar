# The dates of the 簠簋内伝's 八将神 table and the 拾芥抄's 金神七殺方

What is the date of the 簠簋内伝's 八将神 table, and of the 拾芥抄's 金神七殺方 (both are in prints whose catalogue dates are only those of the printing)? The attributions (安倍晴明; 洞院公賢) are the books' own and the catalogue's.

**Narrowed 2026-11-05 (RD163).** The table in use by 756; the 金神 rows 戊癸 and 甲己 by 1093–1149; the 金神 section in a manuscript no later than the early Muromachi (橋本進吉). The books themselves stay undated.

**Searched again 2026-11-16 (inv-c).** The 方位細鑑 (frame 53) says the 三鏡暦 of 安倍晴道 has the 大將軍 rule and «此伝拾芥抄ニモ抜セリ», which dates nothing. No new date.

**Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-c).** The 簠簋内伝's date is q628's answer (the NDL note on pid 2532305: «鎌倉時代末期から室町時代までに成立した偽書と考えられる»; re-read on the NDL Search page through a JP exit, the web search's own summary of it is the same sentence); the 八将神 table's date within that span is not given anywhere seen. The 拾芥抄: NDL Search lists it as 洞院公賢 (1291–1360) 撰 with 洞院実煕 補, and a 応永18年 (1411) copy from the Daitōkyū collection (a facsimile title), but no record says in which layer the 金神七殺方 section stands or when it was added; the 故実叢書 and 古辞書叢刊 introductions were not reachable. A web search for «金神七殺方 いつ 追加» found nothing. The bound stays "no later than the early Muromachi" (橋本進吉, RD163). Not answered.
