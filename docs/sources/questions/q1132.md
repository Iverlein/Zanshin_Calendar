# Is ལྟ་བ in the Derge title of the Turning back of the bad dream a variant of ལྟས

The colophon, contents and catalogues all have ལྟ་བ; the rite says ལྟས and ལྟས་ངན; the lexica keep lta ba ngan pa (kudṛṣṭi) and ltas ngan (durnimitta) apart.

## Narrowed 2026-11-13

Not settled. BDRC's full-text search (2026-11-13) finds «ལྟ་བ» in the title and the colophon of the work in all five Tengyurs (Derge, Narthang, Peking, golden manuscript, Dpe bsdur ma) and no print with «ལྟས» in the title (three exact variants, 0 hits): claims/title-lta-ba-ngan-pa-in-all-five-tengyurs-ltas-variant-unfound.md. The lexica keep lta ba ngan pa (kudṛṣṭi) and ltas ngan (durnimitta) apart, the rite's text speaks of «ལྟས» only: claims/title-lta-ba-ngan-pa-both-prints-text-says-ltas.md (RD395). A variant spelling of ལྟས is possible and unshown.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14

BDRC's records of the work (Peking MW1KG13126_3608, Derge MW23703_2787, Narthang MW2KG5015_2406, Dpe bsdur ma MW1PD95844_1654AU) have ལྟ་བ in the title and colophon, and none carries a Sanskrit title (WA0RT1607 has only a Mongolian altLabel), so no Sanskrit (durnimitta, kudṛṣṭi) fixes the word. This work is not the Svapnohana, whose Sanskrit incipit is svapnohana (q994); note that BDRC's colophon fields of the Svapnohana's Narthang and Peking records read «rmi lam ngan pa bzlog pa rdzogs so», without ལྟ་བ or ལྟས. Not settled.
