# In the Four Tantras' ch. 2 line on the boy's dream, is ཡང light or also, and is དག clear or a plural?

«ལུས་ཡང་རྨི་ལམ་དག་ན་སྐྱེས་པ་མཐོང་།»

## Answered 2026-11-10

RD356: ཡང is the light «also/again» and དག the plural marker («men seen many times»), on the Serthang, Drathang, Derge and Zhol prints (the Chag-po-ri line is illegible); no commentary reads དག as «clear».
