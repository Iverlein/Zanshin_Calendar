# The sense of the 拾芥抄's «天一太白自大將軍王相八卦忌方重可忌避»

the 拾芥抄's sentence «天一太白自大將軍王相八卦忌方重可忌避»: are 天一 and 太白 to be avoided more strictly than the 大將軍, 王相 and 八卦 directions, or is the sense another? (RD94)

## Answered 2026-11-05

«Than»: by the 1656 print's reading marks and the old manuscript's ハ. The first kana after 方 in the manuscript is uncertain. ([kyureki.md](../kyureki.md), *The direction gods' last questions in the old texts (RD163)*)
