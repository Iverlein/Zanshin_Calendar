# From what moment are the water-hours counted in the Cakrasaṃvara commentary De kho na nyid mkhas pa, and does the first watch begin at midnight

VE23703_1333 pp. 525, 573: midnight, sunset or sunrise? Does the clause «ཉི་མའི་ཆ་ལ་མཚན་མོ་ཕྱེད་ནས་ཉི་མ་གང་ཇི་སྲིད་པར་བཞི» mean the first watch begins at midnight?

## Searched 2026-11-13, not settled

BDRC's exact-phrase search for «མཚན་མོ་ཕྱེད་ནས་ཉི་མ་གང་ཇི་སྲིད་པར», «ཉི་མའི་ཆ་ལ་མཚན་མོ་ཕྱེད་ནས» and «ཐུན་གྱི་དབྱེ་བས་བརྒྱད་མཆོད་པར་བྱའོ» finds only the Derge text (VE23703_1333) and the Dpe bsdur ma (I1PD95854); «ཟླ་བའི་ཆ་ལ་ཡང་བཞིའོ» finds besides a Narthang Tangyur volume (VEIE0OPIF6EAA761_I2KG212292, [p587]; an unreliable etext of a manuscript-like hand whose first title line reads «དཔལ་འཁོར་ལོ་སྡོམ་པའི་དཀའ་འགྲེལ་དཔའ་བོའི་དཔོན་སྐལ་ལྡན་གྲགས་པས་མཛད་པ», the Cakrasaṃvara difficult-points commentary of Skal ldan grags pa; whether [p587] belongs to that text or to the commentary «De kho na nyid mkhas pa» was not settled) in which the clause reads the same. No other commentary on the Cakrasaṃvara found that quotes the clause with a gloss. The question stays open: whether the first watch begins at midnight is not said by any witness.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14

Narrowed, not answered. The Kālacakra commentary Vimalaprabhā (VE23703_1328 pp. 537-538, svo:i538) tells its eight watches as four from midnight to noon and four from noon to midnight, the shape of this clause, which supports midnight as the first watch's beginning; on its p. 546 the watches are numbered from noon, so the ordinal and the halves differ there. No text found names the moment from which the sixty water-hours are counted (claims/kalacakra-vimalaprabha-eight-watches-four-midnight-to-noon-four-noon-to-midnight.md). BDRC phrase search «ཆུ་ཚོད་ལྔ་པའི་ཐུན» 2 hits (the Derge and the Dpe bsdur ma), «ཆུ་ཚོད་ལྔ་པ» 91 and «མཚན་མོ་ཕྱེད་ནས་ཆུ་ཚོད» 14 (Kālacakra books: "from midnight, after two chu tshod, until sunrise" in VE23703_1328 p. 319, VE23703_1329 p. 483, Padma dkar po, Tshul khrims rin chen, Dol po pa; a disciple-gift rule, not a count), «ཆུ་ཚོད་དང་པོ་ནས» 22 and «ཐུན་མཚམས་ཆུ་ཚོད་ལྔ་པ» 0; the 91 and 22 hits were not opened beyond VE23703_1328/1329.
