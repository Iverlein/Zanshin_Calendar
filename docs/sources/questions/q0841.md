# In the Sbas-yul manuscript and the Ngodrup and Sherab Drimay print the phrase begins «རི་ཏུག»/«རྟ་ཏུག» and ends «ཁྱེར»/«ཕྱེར»: the same list item under a different hand, or a different item?

First syllable unsettled across the machines' crops.


Narrowed (RD271, 2026-11-09): the Sbas-yul manuscript's «རྟ་ཏུག» and the Ngodrup and Sherab Drimay print's «རི་ཏུག» are most likely «རམ་ཏུག» (ram tug) misread in the cursive, the spelling that a Kongtrul text writes in brackets for «རམ་རུག»; «ཁྱེར»/«ཕྱེར»/«སྦར་ཁྱེར» is «སྦང་གཡེར» written by ear. So the same list item, not a different one; not seen on a crop. Claims lgd-ram-rug-is-the-ram-bu-spelling and lgd-sbang-g-yer-beer-mash-and-pepper.
