# Does the Cakrasaṃvara commentary put the first of eight watches at midnight, and from what moment are the sixty water-hours counted

«ཉི་མའི་ཆ་ལ་མཚན་མོ་ཕྱེད་ནས་ཉི་མ་གང་ཇི་སྲིད་པར་བཞི» — four in the sun's part from midnight for as long as there is sun; neither copy names the moment for the chu tshod.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14

Narrowed, not answered. The Kālacakra commentary Vimalaprabhā (VE23703_1328 pp. 537-538, svo:i538) tells its eight watches as four from midnight to noon and four from noon to midnight, the shape of this clause, which supports midnight as the first watch's beginning; on its p. 546 the watches are numbered from noon, so the ordinal and the halves differ there. No text found names the moment from which the sixty water-hours are counted (claims/kalacakra-vimalaprabha-eight-watches-four-midnight-to-noon-four-noon-to-midnight.md).
