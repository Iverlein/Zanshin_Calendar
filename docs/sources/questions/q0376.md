# Which animal a planet rises on, and the Sun, Mercury and Venus

The planets "rise" on triads of animals (lt:p9-11): is the animal the day's, the month's or the year's, and how is the state of the Sun, Mercury and Venus found, when only Saturn, the Moon, Jupiter and Mars are named as rising?

RD166 (2026-11-06), a lean from one line: the animal is the date's; WB p. 313 «ཞིབ་ན་ཚེས་གཅིག་གཟའ་ནས་བརྩི» (in detail, count the date from the weekday's planet) is how the Sun, Mercury and Venus can rise.
