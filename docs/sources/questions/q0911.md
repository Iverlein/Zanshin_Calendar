# Is the Blue Beryl's gloss of ch. 81's dream list the same text as Khro ru's, and which came first?

RD303; Khro ru's creature gloss is the Blue Beryl's with spelling changes only.

## Answered 2026-11-10

Answered by RD321 (2026-11-10): the Blue Beryl's and Khro ru's glosses of ch. 81 (and of the ch. 15 fever sentence) are one text; three stretches compared syllable by syllable are 85, 80 and 82 in 100 alike, the differences spelling, OCR and head noise plus a few real ones (Khro ru adds a pulse clause and «གཟའི་གདོན་གྱི་ཆོ་ག»; his «ཁ་ཡོ་མིག་སྤྱི་ཞེ» reads as a garbling of the Blue Beryl's «སླེ་ཞེས་ཡོ»). The direction is Blue Beryl to Khro ru: the Blue Beryl was begun in 1687 by its author's own life, Khro ru's colophon is dated 2001 (work from 1996, gathered by his students, naming no source). The Drikung (1595–1659 by the pool) has the same clauses in fewer words in the same order. Claim st-blue-beryl-khro-ru-one-gloss.
