# Whose are the numbered notes at the foot of the page in the Larung print (2006) of Klong chen pa's Phyogs bcu'i mun sel, among them note 1035, which counts the six times of the month as the 8th, 10th, 15th, 25th, 29th and the new moon?

Raised by the practice-days investigation (pd-b, 2026-11-16); see the cited passages.

**Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-b).** Not settled. The Larung print's own front matter (I1KG9074, img. 3-20: covers, plates, a dedication page, the contents) and its closing page (p. 635, img. 655: a note at the foot and a prayer) state nothing about the notes' authorship; BDRC's record MW1KG9042 has only the publisher (Gser ljongs bla ma rung lnga rig nang bstan slob grwa chen mo, 2006) and «kun mkhyen klong chen rab 'byams kyis mdzad». One find: the same note 1035 stands, inline and numbered «3035», in the 47-volume Lhasa collection «Rgyud gsang ba snying po'i rtsa 'grel phyogs bsgrigs» (MW3CN21640, Bod ljongs bod yig dpe rnying dpe skrun khang, 2019; BDRC exact search «མར་ངོའི་དུས་ཉེར་ལྔ་ཉེར་དགུ་གནམ་སྟོང་གསུམ» 1 hit, search-only etext VEIE0OPI9BEF30BC_I3CN22119): the later print carries the Larung notes with a new numbering, so the notes are older than 2019 and not that print's own; «ཡར་ངོའི་དུས་བརྒྱད་དང་བཅུ་དང་བཅོ་ལྔ་གསུམ» alone 0 hits. Whose they are (the Larung editors, or an earlier commentator) remains unstated. Open.
