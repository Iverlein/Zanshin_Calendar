# WB ch. 10 p. 74: does «མཚམས» in the figure after «དུས་མདའ་མཚམས་དབང» count 4 or 6?

The print's glyphs read ༥༦༥༦; the earlier reading was ༥༤༥༦.

## Answered 2026-11-21

The figure after «དུས་མདའ་མཚམས་དབང» is (༥༦༥༦), 5656, with «མཚམས» counting 6. Read from the units up: «དུས» season 6, «མདའ» arrow 5, «མཚམས» boundary 6, «དབང» power 5 gives 6, 5, 6, 5, written 5656. «མཚམས» is six in the *Tshig mdzod chen mo* (q0690: the six seasons, like «དུས»), «དབང» is 5 (p. 77 «སྲང་དབང(༥)», p. 6 «དབང་༥»), «མདའ» 5 and «དུས» 6 (p. 192 «དུས(༦)»), and the chapter-17 constant «དབང་མཚམས» is 65 (wb.1:p192), the same two words. A 4 would give 5456, which none of the dictionary, the neighbouring pages or the print supports. The earlier reading ༥༤༥༦ is dropped.
