# The day's animal or the birth's in the twelve, pp. 428–429

Pp. 428–429's twelve animals are headed «ཉི་མའི་ནད་དང་གདོན་ངོས་བཟུང», the day's illness: is the animal that of the day of falling ill, or the sick person's birth year (RD12's outline read it as the birth animal)?

**Narrowed 2026-10-25 (RD52, [claims/illness-day-animal-of-onset.md](../claims/illness-day-animal-of-onset.md)).** The animals of pp. 428–429 are those of the day of falling ill, by inference: WB p. 450 examines the time of onset first, the p. 431 mewa is the one under which one fell ill, reply 133 says the four times are those of the onset, and the notes' date, weekday and mansion are those of the day of falling ill; KD vol. 3 p. 701 (etext) copies WB's twelve animals under the same heading. No passage reads them by the birth year, so RD12's outline is unsupported.
