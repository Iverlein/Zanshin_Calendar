# The 雑書夢合／三世相小鑑's table (mdz:i234427) and the 万代大雑書古今大成's (mdz:p11) agree in the twelve columns legible; is the slash of the one print present in the other, and which cells of the second month and of the eighth to tenth months read the same (the cells read here in part)?

RD364.

## Answered 2026-11-13

Answered from the pool's tables. The slash of mdz:p11 is not printed in the other prints: in the 雑書夢合 / 三世相小鑑 (ORMK-02344, image 27) and in the 萬歳大雑書日用寳 (image 121) and the 国文研 print (bid 200013909, image 9) the last group of each month stands lower after a gap, and the slash of the transcription of mdz:p11 is the editor's mark for that gap (mdz:i234427b, RD371's answer 3). The second-month cell reads とり さる み, the eighth and ninth months とら たつ ね ゐ (ね written 祢) and the tenth たつ み in all three prints, differing only in spelling (ね 祢, いぬ ぬ, ゐ い); the table of mdz:i234427b sets the twelve months side by side. The cell of the first month's ひつじ is faint in the ORMK print.
