# The Moonbeams' readings 28 and 29 exchanged

In the *Moonbeams*' ch. 2 (img. 032–033), and in KD's print of it, the twenty-eighth reading, small children's body, starts from the iron bird and monkey (enemy) and the twenty-ninth, women's power, from the earth horse and sheep (friend): the reverse of WB's pair list taken in WB's reading order (vol. 1, p. 453, the Tibetan thirteenth and fourteenth), which every other point follows. Is the exchange the *Moonbeams*' teaching or a slip, and which pair does each of WB's readings 28 and 29 take?
