# On the Gser thang scan (WB vol. 1 ch. 13, img. 205–211) is the first glyph of entry 9's year name «ཨངྒིར» (Aṅgiras) a stack with nga under the head, not da (q707)?

Raised by the 2026-11-13 investigation x: the OCR etext and BDRC phrase searches favour the reading named; the scan is still to be seen.

## Read again 2026-11-21 (rd-y)

Looked at once: img. 205, line 16 (of 28), the word «ཨངྒིར་ཞེས་ཆུ་ཕོ་སྤྲེལ» at 5x. The first glyph after ཨ is a stack with a head carrying the gigu, a flat middle member with a hook at its left (the shape this print gives nga in «བཟང་»), and a lower member with a descender: ང over ག is consistent, ད over ག is not excluded, since the print's ད and ང differ only by the right-hand tail and the 1-bit stack clots it. Not decided by the shape; the reading ཨངྒིར rests on q707's evidence (the same print's etext, the dictionaries' «ang gir»). The line was not looked at again.
