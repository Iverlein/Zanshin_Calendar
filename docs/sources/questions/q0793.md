# Which work ends on TDR printed p. 343, and does the registry's TDR begin at the title two thirds down the page?

Printed p. 343 begins with the end of a Śākyamuni dream rite (head east or north, nirvāṇa posture, repeated to the third night).

## Answered 2026-11-10

Answered by RD315 (2026-11-10), from the editor's contents of the other TDR print (etext pp. 20–21): the lower part of the 'Brong rtse be'u bum has ten sections; «gsum» is section 3, the three dream examinations (Śramaṇā, Śākyamuni, Tārā), printed p. 309; «bzhi pa» is section 4, the lion-faced dakini, p. 315, a new section with its own history; sections 1 and 2 are mercury instructions, not dream sets. The earlier 'four rites' statement of tdr-work-before-and-title is withdrawn; the work ending on p. 343 is the Śramaṇā text, as RD286 had it.
