# «སྨན་གཉིས» and «མ་དགུ» on p. 276

On WB vol. 2 p. 276 (img. 282) who are «སྨན་གཉིས», the two set to right and left beyond the male and female rows, and what is the «མ་དགུ» whose ground is the last row?

RD183 (2026-11-06): «སྨན་གཉིས» answered: the dead boy (yul lha) and girl (mo sman) of WB's own list of thirteen kinds of the dead (pp. 72, 101). «མ་དགུ» left open: the third band of the grave ground after the war-god's and the spouses'; no text explains it.
