# The Moonbeams' eight pebbles without the progressed year

The *Moonbeams* (img. 033–034) fights the thirty's eight pebbles from the pair's own year, four, seven and eleven places up and down, the pair's two years for the luck, with no count through the years of the one reckoned. Is this the way "without turning the progressed year" that Mipham's notes report from elsewhere (WB vol. 2, p. 533), and does it say anything about the order of WB's eight checks, which in the *Moonbeams* come as four pairs of one aspect each?

RD166 (2026-11-06), narrowed: the Moonbeams' count is the notes' (p. 533) «ལོག་མིན་མི་སྐོར་བར». The order of WB's eight checks is a dead end: the notes split them 1,3,5 / 2,4,6 / 7,8, and the Moonbeams pairs by aspect.
