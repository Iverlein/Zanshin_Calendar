# Does the printed almanac keep the 大將軍's five-day outings

Does the printed almanac keep the 大將軍's five-day outings (§54) and the freeing of its own direction during them? And when an outing goes to the direction it already holds (甲子–戊辰 to 卯 in its eastern years), what does the old rule mean? (RD92)

## Answered 2026-11-06

RD172 (2026-11-06), for the 1937 book (萬曆三世相, 昭和12, NDL pid 1032282): it keeps the 大將軍's outings, six days each from the 子 day to the 巳 day, in the order 東 中 北 南 西 (the 暦林問答集 has five days); «此の遊行の間は本處に憚りなし», and the direction of an outing is unusable for everything. It does not discuss the overlap case. Today's paper 高島暦 was not seen.
