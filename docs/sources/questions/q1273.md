# The Lokaprajñapti names the 8th, 14th and 15th of the half-month: does any Nyingma author, the White Beryl or Mipham give the Four Great Kings' inspection as the reason for the lay fast days, and do they keep the 14th (and the 29th) beside the full and new moon?

Raised by the practice-days investigation (pd-c, 2026-11-16); see the cited passages.

## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-v)

The pool holds the Lokaprajñapti's three days (ljz:i41-47) and Lo chen Dharma shrI's bare «ཉ་སྟོང་ཚེས་བརྒྱད» (dpn:i55); no Nyingma author, White Beryl or Mipham passage giving the Four Great Kings' inspection as the reason, or keeping the fourteenth and twenty-ninth, turned up in the pool or its claims. Not searched beyond the pool in this round. Stays open.

## Searched again 2026-11-24 (inv-a)

BDRC phrase search «ཚེས་བརྒྱད་བཅུ་བཞི་བཅོ་ལྔ» 7 hits. Mipham keeps the 14th: in his *Bya spyod kyi sngags bsgrub pa'i tshul gnad mdor bsdus* (BDRC MW1PD76231, etext I1PD84582, p. 16): «དུས་ནམ་ནམ་བྱེད་པ་བྱུང་ན་མཆོག་ཏུ་ལེགས་ཤིང་། ཚེས་བརྒྱད་བཅུ་བཞི་བཅོ་ལྔ་དང་། ཆོ་འཕྲུལ་གྱི་ནི་ཟླ་ཕྱེད་ལ། ཁྱད་པར་དུ་ཡང་མཆོད་པ་བྱ། ཞེས་རྒྱུད་ལས་གསུངས་སོ།» (offering at any time is best; especially on the 8th, 14th, 15th and the half-month of miracles, 'as the tantra says'): the days of the three with no reason given, the Four Great Kings not named. A life of the Buddha by Ye shes rgya mtsho (his Collected Works vol. 1, BDRC MW29186, I00EGS1017108, p. 195, the Thirty-three heaven) says «ཚེས་བརྒྱད་བཅུ་བཞི་བཅོ་ལྔ་དག་ལ་རྒྱལ་བོ་སྡེ་བཞིས་འཇིག་རྟེན་ཉུལ། ལེགས་ཉེས་བྱེད་པ་དག་ལ་རིམ་བཞིན་བསྔགས་ཤིང་མི་མཐུན་བསྒྲོགས་པར་བྱེད།» (on the 8th, 14th, 15th the Four Kings go round the world, praising those who do good and rebuking the others); the author's school was not checked. The 'Bri gung text of q1260 gives the fast for the laity on the full moon, new moon and eighth, without the Kings. A life of Brag dkar Rta so sprul sku Chos kyi dbang phyug (BDRC MW1KG14557, etext I1KG14562, p. 303) lists nine great days of the month, «ཚེས་བརྒྱད་བཅུ་བཞི་བཅོ་ལྔ་ཉེར་གཉིས་ཉེར་ལྔ་ཉེར་བདུན་ཉེར་དགུ་གནམ་སྟོང»: the 14th, 25th and 29th with them. No WB line giving the Kings' inspection as the reason. Narrowed.
