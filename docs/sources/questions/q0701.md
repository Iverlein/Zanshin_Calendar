# «ཐིག་ཡོན» in the purified month's multiplier, p. 37

the figures set under the verses of pp. 37–40 (the editors' bracketed numbers, some stacked above and below the line) stand for the number-words beside them; the first on p. 37 whose figure does not equal its words, «dot, benefit (207)», was left unexplained: what does «ཐིག་ཡོན» stand for in the purified month's multiplier? (RD188, corpus/wb/ch06.md)

## Answered 2026-11-08

Nothing is left unexplained: the figure beside «ཐིག་ཡོན» is 30 on the scan of the Gser thang reprint (img. 44), not 207, which was the machine's misreading. The dot is 0 and *yon*, the quality, stands for 3, so the multiplier of the purified month is thirty (claim `wb-ch6-purified-month-times-thirty`).
