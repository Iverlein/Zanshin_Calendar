## Answered 2026-11-21

The 11th head has two dated entries, on the 7th and on the 27th; there is no entry «on the 10th», and the entries of the 20s are one, the 27th. The 27th's words are read: «ཉེར་བདུན་ཐོ་རེངས་ཟླ་སྐྱོར་ཨེ་འཛོམ་བལྟ།», "on the 27th, look at whether the dawn's moon-conjunction meets" (the dawn of the 27th: whether the moon's conjunction, the planets' meeting with the moon, comes together).

Checked 2026-11-21 (rd-z). (1) The Gser thang reprint's scan (I3CN5028 img. 161, p. 154), line 7 in two crops at 2x, in bold clean print: «མི་སྒྲོག །ཉེར་བདུན་ཐོ་རེངས་ཟླ་སྐྱོར་ཨེ་འཛོམ་བལྟ། ལྷ་ལྔ་ཤར། དགུ་ནུབ། ཆད་ལྷག་སོ་སོ་རྒྱག» and the line 8 weekday line; the earlier readings' «a clause of about six syllables, its last two 'dzom» is exactly the six syllables «ཐོ་རེངས་ཟླ་སྐྱོར་ཨེ་འཛོམ», and their «mi smra» is «མི་སྒྲོག» as RD2 had it. (2) The Gser thang etext of the same head (I3CN5028 [p161], an OCR of the same print) reads the same words, and ends the line before the weekday line with «གཟའ་རགས་པ།» (the "coarse weekday"), which is the closing clause of three syllables ending in «pa» that line 7 had left unread. (3) The 12th head has the same clause with the 25th: «ཉེར་ལྔའི་ཐོ་རེངས་བར་སྐྱོར་ཨེ་འཛོམ་བལྟ།» (wb.1:p155, read by eye there); the 11th's differs in the day and in «ཟླ» for «བར». (4) The line before it holds the 7th: «བདུན་ལ་དུས་ཚོད་ངན་པ་དགུ་འཛོམ། ཁ་སྨྲས་གླེང་གཞིའི་ཟས་མི་ཟ། རྒྱལ་བའི་བཀའ་གསུང་མི་སྒྲོག» (RD2). The «10th» and the «20s» of the question stood on the first look's four overbars, which are the day and night stacks of lines 3 and 5, not dates. RD2's table (month-entries.md) gives the 7th only; the 27th is added there, and wb.1:p154g's line 7 is corrected. The syllables of the 27th's clause rest on the scan and the etext; the printed overbars of line 3 remain the open point of q1161's earlier notes only as stacks, no longer as dates.
