# Does the supplement set the sky dog above the other nine checks of a bride's going?

the supplement's «ཁྱད་པར་བག་མར་གལ་ཆེ་བ། །གནམ་ཁྱིའི་སྒོས་དོན» (KD vol. 3 p. 642): is the sky dog thereby set above the other nine checks of a bride's going, or only given more detail?

## Answered 2026-11-06

RD179 (2026-11-06): the supplement's sky dog is told as seven dogs, one under each of the ten ways of sending (KD vol. 3, p. 642); it runs across the ten and is not set above them. Its weight for a bride is its mewa-seven line, under which a bride who leaves by the door dies soon: the existing claim sky-dog-weight-for-bride, unchanged. Rests on the machine-read p. 642 passage; no KD scan read.
