# Which planet is «གཟའ་ལག» in the Mchog dga's list of dream planets (the one that makes the dreamer teach and hear the Dharma)? It may be Mercury, ལྷག་པ.

RD361.

## Answered 2026-11-10

Answered from the etext (RD369). «གཟའ་ལག» is Mercury: the same book names «གཟའ་ལག་པ» as lord of Gemini and Virgo and writes «གཟའ་ལྷག་པ» in the fourth place of Mars, Saturn, Jupiter, Mercury, Venus, and the Dbyangs 'char's own verse reads «གཟའ་ལྷག་དག་ལ་རྨི་ལམ་ནི། ཆོས་བཤད་པ་དང་ཉན་པ་རྨི» in the Derge and the dpe bsdur ma etexts. The Kun 'dus etext prints both spellings.
