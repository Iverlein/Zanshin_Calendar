# WB ch. 3 p. 25 print line 14: is the last digit of the year for the Chos lung Tshogs pa monastery (432 by the chain) ༢ or ༩?

A loop with a top spur, not told at 5x.

## Answered 2026-11-21

The last digit is ༢: the year is 432.

Checked 2026-11-21 (rd-t), by the chain of p. 25 on both sides, not by the glyph. The line before gives «(༤༣༤)» (434; the Sa skya lord of all Tibet), the foundation of Chos lung Tshogs pa follows by «དེ་ལ་ཟུང(༢)ཕྲི་» (zung, the pair, 2: 434 − 2 = 432), and the next line reaches Bu ston by «དེ་ལ་མདའ་མེས(༣༥)ཕྲི་བ» (mda' 5, me 3, so 35) with the printed «(༣༩༧)»; 397 + 35 = 432. Two independent steps give 432 where a ༩ would give 439, which neither step allows. The glyph itself (a loop with a spur at the top) stays untold, and the passage keeps «༤༣༢» in the English.
