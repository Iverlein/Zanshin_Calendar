## Narrowed 2026-11-24 (inv-g), two of the three settled by checks

The prints are blotted at these places (above), so the checks decide, not the glyph.

- p. 213 «ཟླ་ཡོན»: 31. The words (moon 1, yon 3) spell 31, ch. 3 and ch. 4 print «ཟླ་ཡོན ༣༡», and the arithmetic needs it: the same five places («གཟུགས ༡, ཟླ་ཡོན, ཐིག་དབང ༥༠, ནམ་མཁའ ༠, ཐིག་ནོར་མཚོ ༤༨༠») are the pure month, 29 days 31 dbyu gu 50 srang 0 breaths 480/707, which is 29 + 31/60 + 50/3600 + 480/(707·6·3600) = 29.53059 days, the synodic month; with 30 dbyu gu it would be 29.5278, wrong by 0.0028 day a month. The print's (༣༠) is a blot or a misprint; 31 is the figure.
- p. 195 «རྒྱུ་སྐར་ཆུ་ཚོད»: 1,620. The figure is the same constant at every other place of the chapter (lines 105, 108, 115, 141, 169 of corpus/wb/ch17.md and pp. 191-194) and is 27 mansions × 60 hours; a 620 spells no word-numeral run and divides nothing the chapter divides. The glyph read 620 is a dropped ༡ of the print.
- p. 215's long figure, the 'dod: not settled. Read units first, the run «ནམ་མཁའ ཐིག སྟོང དྲག་པོ རྩ དུས དབང ཚེས ནོར ལག མིག ཀླུ བུག གདེངས དབང ཆུ འདོད» matches the printed twenty-two glyphs 2712458982281556911000 in every place up to the chu 4, then «འདོད» (13 in the key, attested in ch. 17 at pp. 191-195 as «འདོད(༡༣)») should give the digits 3, 1 (written «༡༣») where the glyphs are «༡༢». Arithmetic does not decide: with 13 the figure 2713458982281556911000 is divisible by 12 and 60 (as the first circle's figure of the same page, 82776132766945179600, is), with 12 the figure 2712458982281556911000 is divisible by 13 and 65 (65 is the divisor of the chapter, «དབང་མཚམས»); neither coincidence is rare enough to prefer one (1 in 3 and 1 in 13). The figure stays as printed (12) with the words' 13 noted; the scan is still the only judge.

## Answered 2026-11-24

Answered (inv-r). p. 215's 'dod is 13: the figure is 2713458982281556911000. The 1996 etext (I1KG12906, etext page 225) types the long figure as «ལོའི(༢༧ ༡༣ ༤༥༨༩༨༢ ༢ ༨ ༡༥༥༦༩ ༡ ༡༠༠༠)»; the rows in order are 27, 13, 458982, 2, 8, 15569, 1, 1000, which is 2713458982281556911000 (22 glyphs), with the 'dod's 13 a row of its own, as the words spell it. The scan's ༢ is the hooked ༣ that the 1996 etext is known to confuse with ༢ (PLAN tool note of inv-j). The same constant is typed «འདོད(༡༣)» at pp. 191-195 and elsewhere. Arithmetic (inv-g) did not decide, so the settling is two witnesses (etext, words) against one clogged glyph. p. 213 (31) and p. 195 (1,620) were settled by inv-g.
