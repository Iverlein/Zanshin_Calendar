## Read again 2026-11-21 (rd-ae)

p. 213 on the Zhol print (I1KG1710): the text begins on img. 254 (img. 253 is a woodcut plate; wb/zhol-map.md (local map, wb/zhol-map.md)), and the verse «བསེ་རུ་ཟླ་ཡོན་ཐིག་དབང་དང་།» is the third line of that image. The Zhol sets the figures as tiny superscripts above the words, not in brackets: over «ཐིག་དབང» a pair that reads ༥༠ (as the 1996 print's (༥༠)); over «ཟླ་ཡོན» a pair of which the first glyph looks like ༣ and the second is not resolved at 14x (about 60 px a glyph). So the Zhol does carry a figure there but this image does not settle 30 against 31; the Taikhang reproduction (I5877, Zhol image + 17, so img. 271) is the next print to look at.

## Read again 2026-11-21 (rd-aj)

p. 213 on both prints, the figures over «བསེ་རུ་ཟླ་ཡོན་ཐིག་དབང» (img. 254 of the Zhol I1KG1710, five lines of the 3,760 px image; the Taikhang I5877 at Zhol + 17 = img. 271, 8,700 x 1,800 px, same line: both begin the line «བུད་མེད་ཀྱི། …», so the offset holds here). Zhol at 10x: the group over the words is three glyphs, a hook, a Z-shaped glyph and a ༣-shaped curl, each about 13 px, too small to say which two belong to «ཟླ་ཡོན» and whether the second of them is ༠ or ༡. Taikhang at 3–4x: the same group is a ring, a large curl, a small squiggle and a ring, each 30–40 px; the reproduction is a one-bit copy, and the thin strokes of the figures clot, so it is no cleaner than the Zhol (the larger glyphs of the text are sharper, the figures are not). Neither print settles 30 against 31; the words and ch. 3 still give 31. p. 195 and p. 215 were not reached in this pass.
