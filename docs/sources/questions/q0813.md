# What does the clause «བགྲོད་མིན» in SVO's second verse limit?

The verdict 'auspicious' on a body struck with weapons, covered with small creatures, washed of dirt, following excrement and bleeding stands against every other text on four of the five images.

## Answered 2026-11-14

inv-k, 2026-11-14: the clause does not limit the other images. «བགྲོད་མིན་ལ་ཞུགས» is the Sanskrit agamyāgamana ("going to what is not to be gone to", a forbidden woman), a sixth image of the same list: the Agnipurāṇa's Svapnādhyāya, in its good dreams, has «घृतविष्ठानुलेपो वा अगम्यागमनं तथा» (v. 31, read on the scan, agp:p282), beside blood seen or bathed in (vv. 24–25) and cleansing (v. 22), and the same watches and the ten days of the dawn as the Svapnohana. So the verse reads: struck with weapons, covered with small creatures, the dirt washed away, following excrement, blood flowing, and entering the forbidden: all auspicious. This is an inference from the parallel (the Agni has no weapons or creatures on the body, and no commentary on the Svapnohana was found), set out in the claim svo-bgrod-min-la-zhugs-is-agamyagamana.
