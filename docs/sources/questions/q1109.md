# Is Marpa's saying on the midnight dream an oral saying handed down through the Karmapas, and is «gdon dbang» the reading they teach

The Karmapa gives «nam phyed kyi rmi lam gdon dbang yin» as Marpa's word, and no work of Marpa, Mi la ras pa, Ras chung pa or Sgam po pa read has it. Do the Kagyu masters hold it as an oral saying, and is gdon dbang (the power of the spirits over the sleeper, as in Marpa's Bgegs sel yid bzhin nor bu) the reading taught?
