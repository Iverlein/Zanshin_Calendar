# The thirty pairs and the thirty readings in one order

Do the commentary's thirty readings (pp. 454–471) take the thirty pairs of p. 453 in the same order, the first fifteen being the Chinese pairs and the next fifteen the Tibetan? The text numbers both lists one to thirty, or fifteen and fifteen, and never joins them, apart from the notes' «རྒྱལ་པོ་སོགས་… བྱི་གླང་སོགས་ནས་བསྐོར་བ» (p. 532).

## Answered 2026-10-23

Answered 2026-10-23 by a manual below WB (RD44, [claims/thirty-dz-bases.md](../claims/thirty-dz-bases.md)): the *Moonbeams* (DZ ch. 2, f. 13a–15b, on the scan) joins WB's reading n to WB's pair n, the fifteen Chinese pairs to readings 1–15 and the fifteen Tibetan to 16–30, readings 28 and 29 taking each other's pairs (question 377), so also KD's print; LT numbers the readings 1–30 beside the pairs without joining them. WB itself does not join them.
