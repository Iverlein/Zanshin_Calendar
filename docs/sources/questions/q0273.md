# «སྐྱུར», «ལྷོག་པ» and «ཁྲ་བོ་བློ་ཐུབ»

What do «སྐྱུར» (p. 57, l. 26 and p. 58, l. 25), «ལྷོག་པ» (p. 55) and «ཁྲ་བོ་བློ་ཐུབ» as a verdict (p. 54) mean?

**Narrowed 2026-11-03 (RD136).** «ཁྲ་བོ་བློ་ཐུབ» is the name WB gives the mewa's copper vessel that follows ("what is called ... this is the mewa's copper vessel"), as Mipham quotes it, not a verdict on the pebbles before it ([claims/dead-khra-bo-blo-thub-names-mewa-vessel.md](../claims/dead-khra-bo-blo-thub-names-mewa-vessel.md)). «ལྷོག་པ» is a disease name WB uses in ch. 22, 25, 29, 30 and 34, a swelling or sore of the nāga and plague kind; the neighbouring «གླང་ཐབས» is a second harm, not a rite ([claims/wb-lhog-pa-is-an-illness.md](../claims/wb-lhog-pa-is-an-illness.md)). «སྐྱུར» stays open between "a sour thing" (beside the mourning place's food, and the unprefixed spelling against bskyur, cast) and "cast-off things" ([claims/dead-skyur-sour-thing-of-mourning.md](../claims/dead-skyur-sour-thing-of-mourning.md)); the Zhol print's line was not clear enough to settle a prefix.
