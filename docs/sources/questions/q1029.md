# Do the 嘉永大雑書三世相 prints (Kokubunken 200024108, 300132222) and the 大雜書三世相 copies of Nagoya and Tohoku (100339325, 100445829, 100445244), and the other eleven 三世相小鑑 prints, carry the same leaf?

RD368.

## Answered 2026-11-13

Answered in the negative for every print looked at. RD371 (mdz:i426556) went through thirteen 三世相小鑑 prints, five 大雑書三世相 prints, the 萬歳大雑書日用寳 images 133-202 and the two dream books: none carries a second or third table 夢と見て語らざる日; the table stands only in the three prints of mdz:i234427b, mdz:p11 and the Ebara dream book. The 嘉永大雑書 prints of Kokubunken (200024108, 300132222) and the 大雜書三世相 copies of Nagoya and Tohoku (100339325, 100445829, 100445244) are among the unopened ones in the record; they were not opened in this session and remain the only places left.

Not advanced (inv-qq, 2026-11-14): the Kokubunken and Nagoya/Tohoku copies are not in the NDL Lab text; not opened in this session. The NDL Lab search for the table's wording gives no 三世相 print either, which is no proof, since the OCR of cursive Edo prints does not reach it.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-zzz)

The kokusho manifests of the five prints answer (`/biblio/<bid>/manifest`): 200024108 (NIIP YA5-0587, 68 images) and 300132222 (Ormk-03616, 73 images) are both the 嘉永大雑書三世相 of 嘉永4 (1851; title leaf on image 3, 嘉永四年三月 on image 70 of 300132222), and the two have the same layout leaf for leaf (title on image 3, the contents on images 5-9 or 6-10, the same picture strips). In 300132222 the general contents (総目録, 上段本文 総計百八十三条), images 6-10 at 3200 px, was read by eye for items 8 to 110: it lists days (日次), houses, the twelve 運, 七殺 and so on, and **no entry for a dream or for days a dream is not told** ; items 1-7 (on image 6: the god of the year's virtue, the lucky direction, the eight generals, 金神, the 七殺 directions and a day) are not dream items either. The strips of images 57-69 hold 相性, 運 and 星 matter (image 58: 男女相生の事), not a dream table. The edition has no 夢合 section of the kind of the 三世相小鑑. Not done: the text leaves themselves were not read through (images 11-69 only on contact sheets at 330 px), so a table inside a leaf is excluded by the contents, not by sight. The Nagoya (100339325, 113 images) and Tohoku (100445829, 26 images, microfilm, small dense print) 大雜書三世相 and the Tohoku 100445244 were not opened further than the contact sheet of 100445829: no strip with the picture of Fuji and a hawk (the place of the table in mdz:i234427b) was seen there, which is no proof.
