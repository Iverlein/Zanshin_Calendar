# «ཟ་མ» and whom the nineteen pebbles are for

P. 445 heads the nineteen pebbles «ཞག་དང་ཟ་མའི་རྩིས་ཀྱི་རྡེལ», told "for the sick and for infants (སྲིའུ, sri'u)": what is «ཟ་མ» here (food, the infant's feeding, or a misprint), and is the reckoning meant for infants only or for every sick person, as the notes (vol. 2, p. 498) treat it?

## Answered 2026-10-23

Answered 2026-10-23 (RD45, [claims/sick-nineteen-pebbles.md](../claims/sick-nineteen-pebbles.md), [claims/za-ma-the-hour.md](../claims/za-ma-the-hour.md)): the *Moonbeams* lays the nineteen pebbles for the sick or for a just-born child, over year, month, day and hour with the lunar date, the weekday and the mansion (dz:i051-052); LT names them with WB's own heading, «ཞག་དང་ཟ་མའི་རྩིས», for the sick and infants (lt:p43-44), and counts a child's nineteen day by day until it is a month old (lt:p20). So the reckoning serves every sick person, and infants besides. *Za ma* stands where the hour stands in LT's lists (lt:p12, lt:p14): "the hour" is an inference, not a gloss.
