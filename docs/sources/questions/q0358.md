# «ཟ་མ» and whom the nineteen pebbles are for

P. 445 heads the nineteen pebbles «ཞག་དང་ཟ་མའི་རྩིས་ཀྱི་རྡེལ», told "for the sick and for infants (སྲིའུ, sri'u)": what is «ཟ་མ» here (food, the infant's feeding, or a misprint), and is the reckoning meant for infants only or for every sick person, as the notes (vol. 2, p. 498) treat it?
