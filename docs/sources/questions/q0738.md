# The 10th month head's three stacked figures after dar

In the 10th month's head the three stacked figures after dar read 5, 1 and a third; do they complete the seven weekdays as 1, 3 and 5 with gud 6, skyod 0 and zhud 2 and 4? (RD202, corpus/wb/ch13.md)

## Answered 2026-11-08

Answered on the scan (1996 print img. 181 at 8x): the three figures after dar are 5, 1 and 3; with gud 6, skyod 0 and zhud 2, 4 they give the seven weekdays once each.
