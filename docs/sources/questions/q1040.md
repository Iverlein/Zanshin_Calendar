# Is the tenth day on which Rtsa gsum gling pa's goddess-sister fixes his start of Dharma, and the ninth day of the Heruka dream, tied to the lunar-day series of the tenth (Guru's day) in the lineage, or only the date of that one dream?

RD363.

## Answered 2026-11-13

The tenth is tied to the treasure's tradition, not only the date of one dream. The treasure's own verse (tsl.27:i221) says "on the tenth, meet the pure vision", the editor's prose on it (tsl.27:i225) adds in person, in experience or in dream; in the life dākinīs come to the gathering "at the tenth and other good times" (tsl.30:i287), arrive at their monastery "on the tenth" (i240) and promise to meet him "on the tenth continually" (i277), and a treasure is taken on the tenth of early autumn (i661). The ninth of the Heruka dream (i113) and the fifteenth of the tested dream (i170) are not in that series. The pages do not name the tenth as Guru's day, so a tie to a series of Guru-days is not shown. In full: claim tsl-tenth-day-is-the-series-of-the-dakini-gatherings.
