# WB p. 244 l. 2, the place-name in «ན་ཀོང་ལྗན་ལྗིན་ཀླུ་ཡི་གནས»: is the first word ལྗན (Yigdzin-1) or ལྡན (MITRA)? A heavy stack on the reprint; another print would settle it.

## Answered 2026-11-14

ལྗན (ljan) is the right first word: the pair is the headword «ljan ljin» of the dictionaries, "filth, dirt, sweepings; loathsome" (Ives Waldo, Valby, Rangjung Yeshe), "unclean; filthy ground, filthy water" (Tshig mdzod chen mo: «sa ljan ljin», «chu ljan ljin»). MITRA's ལྡན has no word «ldan ljin» and no headword in the 64 sets. The same pair stands once more in the same chapter, p. 245 (in the list of bad waters), where Yigdzin-1 and MITRA agree on ལྗན: «གཉན་གནོད་ཀོ་ཆུ་ལྗན་ལྗིན་ནི། །ནལ་གྱི་ཆུ་ཡིན་བྱིས་ཕྲུག་མང་» ("the waters of the gnyan harm, the leather-sack, the filthy: the water of the nal [a sticky bog], many children"). So the place-name line «ན་ཀོང་ལྗན་ལྗིན་ཀླུ་ཡི་གནས» is "(the) Na kong, the filthy places, the dwelling of the klu": a filthy ground as a nāga place. The glyph itself was not seen on another print; the decision is by the headword.
