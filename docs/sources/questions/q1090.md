# Is ལྟ་བ in the Derge title རྨི་ལམ་ལྟ་བ་ངན་པ་རྣམ་པར་ཟློག་པ the dream «view», or a spelling of ལྟས «sign»?

RD381.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14

The records are not of the Svapnohana: the title «རྨི་ལམ་གྱི་ལྟ་བ་ངན་པ་རྣམ་པར་བཟློག་པ» is a different work (BDRC WA0RT1607; Peking MW1KG13126_3608, Derge MW23703_2787), colophon «slob dpon a dzi ta mi tra gup+tasa mdzad … bod kyi lo tsA ba gnubs byams pa'i dpal bzang pos bsgyur ba'o» (Ajitamitragupta, translated by Gnubs Byams pa'i dpal bzang), whose BDRC records give no Sanskrit title (the work's record WA0RT1607 holds only a Mongolian title as altLabel), so a Sanskrit rendering that would fix ལྟ་བ against ལྟས is not recorded. The question stays as q1132.
