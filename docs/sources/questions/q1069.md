# Does the Vajrāvalī manual of Sa bzang 'Phags pa gzhon nu blo gros, of Mnga' ris Rab 'byams pa Tshul khrims 'od zer or of Mkhas grub Dpal ldan rdo rje pa, the sources Nam mkha' dpal bzang names for his manual, give the reason that the first watch is mixed with the day, the middle two are deep sleep and the last is clear, or the rule of the last watch at all?

RD377.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-cc)

BDRC's exact-phrase searches on the reason's words («bag chags dang 'dres cung zad mi gsal», «nam gung gi cha gnyid 'thug po», «thun gnyis pa dang gsum pa nam gung») find only copies of Nam mkha' dpal bzang's wording: the Rgyud sde kun btus (MW2PD14777, Rdzong sar and Glog klad prints) and the Kun btus' own copy of the Vajrāvalī commentary «legs par bshad pa rdo rje phreng ba mdzes rgyan nor bu'i chun 'phyang» (MW27883_E2C2E5, which also holds the sentence), and, in the shorter form, Kun dga' rin chen's empowerment manual (q1070). No manual of the three authors Nam mkha' dpal bzang names turned up: the phrase for the fourteenth step's title («rmi lam brtag cing ngan na bsrung ba», 68 hits) lists collections (Sa skya bka' 'bum, Ngag dbang kun dga' bsod nams, Ngor chen, Go rams pa, Tāranātha and others) but the first eight snippets are none of the three, and BDRC's person record of Sa bzang 'Phags pa gzhon nu blo gros (P3874, 1358-1412/24) carries no work list, nor was a person record found for «Rab 'byams pa Tshul khrims 'od zer» of Mnga' ris (nine namesakes) or a work titled for the Vajrāvalī under any of the three names (title search in the `_msearch` index: 0). So whether the three manuals give the reason, or the last watch's rule, is not known. Still open: the three manuals (not found digitised in BDRC's search) or a Sakya catalogue that lists them.
