# KP box 55: is the clause «ཀླུ་བཟློག་པ» a remedy against the naga planet or a separate rite?

Last line of box 55: «སྲང་ འཕུང་བྱེད་ ཟིན་ཕུང་ དབུལ་ ཉི་ནག་ ཀླུ་ བཟློག་པ།».

## Answered 2026-11-21

Answered from the pool's own passages and one more almanac text: it is not a remedy but an earth-lord time, the nāgas' turning (ཀླུ་བཟློག, klu bzlog), the counterpart of the nāgas' striking (ཀླུ་ཐེབས, klu thebs).

The Kālacakra reckoning of the earth lords' courses in Karma 'phrin las's text (kd.3:p740-741, etext) says of the strikes and turnings: «ཀླུ་ཐེབས་དུས་སུ་ཆར་དབབ་པ། །ཀླུ་ཆོག་ཀླུ་གཏོར་གཏོང་བ་དང་། །ཀླུ་ལ་མནན་གཏད་སྐྱེལ་སོགས་བཟང༌། །ཀླུ་བཟློག་དུས་སུ་དེ་རྣམས་འཛེམ།» (klu thebs dus su char dbab pa / klu chog klu gtor gtong ba dang / klu la mnan gtad skyel sogs bzang / klu bzlog dus su de rnams 'dzem): at the time of the nāgas' striking, bringing down rain, the nāga rite and nāga torma and the like are good; at the time of their turning these are to be avoided. WB's model almanac writes «ཀླུ་བཟློག» as a course in the day boxes (wb.1:p157, month 1 «ཀླུ་བཟློག»; the claims on the earth-lord courses list it on fixed dates of each month). KP's box 55 is the rain box, and its bad half ends in the run of earth-lord times to be shunned: srang, the destroyer, zin phung, dbul, nyi nag, «ཀླུ་བཟློག་པ» (kp:i55s). The clause is therefore the last item of that run, the turning of the nāgas, when rain-making is to be avoided; «པ» is the nominaliser of the course, as in the almanac handbook Rtsis rig shes bya kun gsal (Gnyer stag snang rgyal, BDRC MW1KG4867, search snippet only), where the same tail stands in a list of bad times: «བྱེད་ཟིན་འཕུང་དབུལ་ཉི་ནག་ཀླུ་བཟློག་པ། སྦྱིན་སྲེག་བྱེད་པ་ལ་ངན་པ།» ("zin phung, dbul, nyi nag, klu bzlog pa: bad for giving and the fire offering"). No rite named «klu bzlog» as a remedy against the nāga planet (ཀླུ་གཟའ) is found; the nāga planet's own sign in the almanacs is «klu gza' bzlog med» in a painting-explanation text (the planet without turning), which is again a time, not a rite.
