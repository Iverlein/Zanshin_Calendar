# The sealing trigram of each provision block, and khon's missing seal

In each block a seal of another trigram presses from above (li's block kham's; dwa's and khen's li's; kham's khon's; gin's zon's; zin's dwa's; zon's gin's), and khon's block has none (pp. 129–132, read on the reprint). By what rule is the sealing trigram chosen, and is khon's missing line an omission of the 1996 setting (to be checked in the Zhol or Taikhang print)?

**Narrowed 2026-11-05 (RD164, [reckonings.md](../reckonings.md)).** Answered in part: by pp. 124–125 «འབྱུང་བ་གོ་ལྡོག» the seal is the element that overcomes, which fits li, dwa, khen, kham and zin. Khon's gap is on the Zhol print too (img. 817). Khon and the gin–zon pair are their own questions now.
