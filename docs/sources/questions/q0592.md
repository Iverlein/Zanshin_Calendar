# The element of the one reckoned in each illness examination

In the illness reckoning's opening examinations (pp. 417–419) «བརྩི་བྱའི་ཁམས་ཀྱི» takes a different element in each examination (the tiger is enemy, mother, friend and son in four of them). Which element of the one reckoned does each examination take, and is it the element of the aspect examined?

RD177 (2026-11-06), narrowed (etext): each examination's element is a relation of the reckoned's own element (enemy, mother, friend, own, son tiger/hare; 1, 10, 11, 14, 15 and 16 start from the sky year), not the aspect's element; the Moonbeams' fixed signs are these relations for a person of wood. The 4th point differs: WB 'agreeing in power', LT son.

## Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-o)

A parallel list of the same examinations, in the Nag rtsis tradition: *Gtsug lag rtsis kyi rig pa snang srid me long las nag rtsis kyi rab tu byed pa* as copied in *Rgyal rong dmangs khrod kyi rtsis yig phyogs bsgrigs* (I4PD3932, etext pp. 126–128; the same lines in the Nag rtsis etext I1KG15279, BDRC search «བརྩི་བྱའི་ཁམས་ཀྱི» 15 hits). It states the rule WB leaves to the reader, with an example: «དཔེར་ན་བརྩི་བྱ་ཤིང་བྱི་ཡིན་ན་ལྕགས་སྟག་ནས་སྦུབ། དེ་ལོ་བཅོ་ལྔ་ཡིན་ན་ཤིང་འབྲུག་གི་སྲོག་ལུས་དབང་རླུང་དགོས་སོ» (if the one reckoned is a wood rat, the count falls from the iron tiger; if he is fifteen, the life, body, power and luck of the wood dragon are needed), and the iron tiger is the enemy of wood: the element of each examination is a relation (enemy, friend, mother, son, own) to the element of the one reckoned, as RD177 found, and not the element of the aspect examined. Its examinations run: 2 enemy tiger, 3 «ཁམས་མཐུན་སྟག» (agreeing tiger), 4 friend tiger, 5 mother tiger, 6 enemy hare, 7 mother hare, 8 «ཁམས་མཐུན་ཡོས» (agreeing hare), 9 son hare, 10 from the sky year up 8, 11 down 8, 12 son tiger, 13 «མེ་སྤྲེལ» (fire monkey), 14 up 5, 15 down 5. WB's 1996 etext (I1KG12906, printed pp. 417–419; machine text) puts 3 mother tiger, 4 «དབང་ཐང་མཐུན» (agreeing in power), 5 friend tiger, 7 friend hare, 8 «ཁམས་གཅིག་ཡོས» (one element hare), 12 son tiger, 13 «མ་སྤྲེལ» (mother monkey) and counts 13 up and down in 10 and 11: so the set of relations is the same, the order of the three tigers (agreeing, friend, mother) and the counts 8 / 13 differ between the Nag rtsis and WB. The Moonbeams' own reading of the 4th, and the choice between 8 and 13, are not settled by these texts. Not answered; state stays open.
