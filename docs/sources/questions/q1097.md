# Is the «history of the four sheets» that Tāranātha calls «a new piece bearing the name four sheets, now everywhere» the Lho brag pa history read here, or a different text, and is the content of the four sheets as Hevajra, Guhyasamāja, Mahāmāyā and the Four Seats an older or a younger reading than the Net-eyed wheel, Breakthrough, Cutting the bardo and Great Seal?

RD383.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-ds)

Not settled. BDRC exact phrases «ཤོག་དྲིལ་བཞིའི་མིང་ཅན» and «གསར་པ་ཟོད་མ» one hit each, Tāranātha's own passage (VE23605_018); «ཤོག་དྲིལ་བཞི་ནི་དགྱེས་རྡོར» none: no second text names the Hevajra, Guhyasamāja, Mahāmāyā and Four Seats content. What can be said of the dates: the Net-eyed wheel, Breakthrough, Cutting the bardo and Great Seal content with a history and instructions «composed by Gling» is already in Padma dkar po's record (dds.42:p165-166; he died in 1592, Tāranātha was born in 1575) and its lineage runs through Bde legs rin chen and Gling, so that content is the older by decades or more than any «new piece» of Tāranātha's time; if the Lho brag pa history is a text of that circle it cannot be what Tāranātha calls new. Tāranātha says many old writings of his own (Hevajra etc.) content stand, which would make that the older reading, but whether his «new piece» has the other content or only the name is not said by the passage. Open: a text of the Khrid brgya circle that describes the new piece.

## Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-p)

Narrowed, not answered. The Lho brag pa history read whole in its etext (Bka' brgyud kyi Gdams ngag, the 'Brug pa'i chos mdzod volume of BDRC VEIE0OPIDDB781DE_I3CN2313, pp. 511-521 and the texts that follow it; the same title in the Gdams ngag mdzod, the Bka' brgyud sngags mdzod and Marpa's collected works) names the content of the four sheets as the «'pho ba spyi rtol, rtsa rlung drwa mig 'khor lo, bar do blos chod, phyag rgya chen po tshig bsdus» (p. 517: Dwags po mdo rtse's answer to Nyi ma lung pa), the Net-eyed wheel, Breakthrough, Cutting the bardo and Great Seal; it tells that Marpa had them from Nāropa and Maitrī, bound them in sheets, gave the four to Mtshur ston dbang nge of Dol (p. 515) whose line runs to 'Dzah phu rgya gar, Stod lung 'Ga' ras, Nyi ma lung pa and Bde gshegs rin chen (p. 516). It does not mention the Hevajra, Guhyasamāja, Mahāmāyā or the Four Seats (searched in the history's text: no «མ་ཧཱ་མཱ་ཡ», «གདན་བཞི», «འབུལ་ཆེན»; «དགྱེས་རྡོར» only as a quotation in a later treatise of the same volume). Tāranātha's own passage (Gdams ngag mdzod VE23605_018, [p8]) reads: «སྙན་གྱི་ཤོག་དྲིལ་བཞི་ནི། དགྱེས་རྡོར། གསང་འདུས། མ་ཧཱ་མཱ་ཡ། གདན་བཞི་རྣམས་ཀྱི་ཟབ་དོན་ཁྱད་པར་ཅན་རེ་རེ་ཡིན་ཅིང་། མཚུར་སྟོན་དབང་གི་རྡོ་རྗེས་རྗེ་མར་པ་ལ་འབུལ་ཆེན་བཞི་ཕུལ་བའི་སྐབས་སོ་སོར་གདམས་ངག་རེ་རེ་གནང་བ་ཡིན་ལ། འདི་ལ་ཡིག་རྙིང་མང་དུ་བཞུགས། ཤོག་དྲིལ་བཞིའི་མིང་ཅན་གསར་པ་ཟོད་མ་ཞིག་དེང་སང་ཀུན་ཁྱབ་ཏུ་འདུག་གོ»: the Hevajra-Guhyasamāja-Mahāmāyā-Four Seats content is tied to Mtshur ston's four great offerings and to «many old writings»; so for Tāranātha that content is the old reading and the widespread piece «with the name» is another. The Lho brag pa history agrees with the second half (the name «four sheets», a different content, widespread in the 'Brug pa and Bka' brgyud prints), which makes it a candidate for the «new piece»; but neither text says so, and the history names no author or date (its etext's colophon belongs to the volume). Padma dkar po's collected works list «rje mar pa'i shog dril bzhi pa / dgu pa / bco lnga pa» (BDRC phrase «ཤོག་དྲིལ་བཞི་པ», 45 hits), so a «four sheets» of Marpa was a named set in his time without its content there. BDRC «ཤོག་དྲིལ་བཞིའི་ལོ་རྒྱུས» 16 hits (the prints above), «ཤོག་དྲིལ་བཞི་པའི་ལོ་རྒྱུས» 0, «ཤོག་དྲིལ་བཞིའི་ཆོས་སྐོར» 0, «ཤོག་དྲིལ་བཞིའི་ཁྲིད» 6, «ལྷོ་བྲག་པའི་ཤོག་དྲིལ» 1. Another «four sheets» also exists, of Shabaripa's lineage of the Mahākāla wheel works («shangs khrag shog dril» etc., Rin chen gter mdzod): a third thing under the name.
