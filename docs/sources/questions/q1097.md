# Is the «history of the four sheets» that Tāranātha calls «a new piece bearing the name four sheets, now everywhere» the Lho brag pa history read here, or a different text, and is the content of the four sheets as Hevajra, Guhyasamāja, Mahāmāyā and the Four Seats an older or a younger reading than the Net-eyed wheel, Breakthrough, Cutting the bardo and Great Seal?

RD383.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-ds)

Not settled. BDRC exact phrases «ཤོག་དྲིལ་བཞིའི་མིང་ཅན» and «གསར་པ་ཟོད་མ» one hit each, Tāranātha's own passage (VE23605_018); «ཤོག་དྲིལ་བཞི་ནི་དགྱེས་རྡོར» none: no second text names the Hevajra, Guhyasamāja, Mahāmāyā and Four Seats content. What can be said of the dates: the Net-eyed wheel, Breakthrough, Cutting the bardo and Great Seal content with a history and instructions «composed by Gling» is already in Padma dkar po's record (dds.42:p165-166; he died in 1592, Tāranātha was born in 1575) and its lineage runs through Bde legs rin chen and Gling, so that content is the older by decades or more than any «new piece» of Tāranātha's time; if the Lho brag pa history is a text of that circle it cannot be what Tāranātha calls new. Tāranātha says many old writings of his own (Hevajra etc.) content stand, which would make that the older reading, but whether his «new piece» has the other content or only the name is not said by the passage. Open: a text of the Khrid brgya circle that describes the new piece.
