# WB p. 36 colophon, the book title «བཻ་ཌཱུར་དཀར་པོའི་དོ་ཤལ» is underdotted in the print: are the vowel signs of «བཻ་ཌཱུར» as read, or another spelling?

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (scan-f).** The colophon of p. 36 was not reached; the Taikhang block side of p. 36 is img. 73 and its end is on img. 74 (not opened). The title is written out on img. 69 (the close of ch. 4, p. 33) as «བཻ་ཌཱུར་དཀར་པོའི་དོ་ཤལ» in the ch. 4 colophon with the vowel signs spread letter by letter (OCR of the half block), so the title's spelling there is the same as read.

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (scan-h).** The block side of p. 36 (img. 73, Taikhang folio 63) ends on its fifth line (x 5000–7600, read at 1.5×) with plain verse closing marks, no title and no colophon; img. 74 is a heading side (frontispieces left and right and a title page of the next chapter, folio 64). So the colophon with «བཻ་ཌཱུར» is not on img. 73–74; look at the foot of img. 72 (p. 35) or after the chapter's end on img. 75. Open.
