# WB p. 36 colophon, the book title «བཻ་ཌཱུར་དཀར་པོའི་དོ་ཤལ» is underdotted in the print: are the vowel signs of «བཻ་ཌཱུར» as read, or another spelling?

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (scan-f).** The colophon of p. 36 was not reached; the Taikhang block side of p. 36 is img. 73 and its end is on img. 74 (not opened). The title is written out on img. 69 (the close of ch. 4, p. 33) as «བཻ་ཌཱུར་དཀར་པོའི་དོ་ཤལ» in the ch. 4 colophon with the vowel signs spread letter by letter (OCR of the half block), so the title's spelling there is the same as read.

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (scan-h).** The block side of p. 36 (img. 73, Taikhang folio 63) ends on its fifth line (x 5000–7600, read at 1.5×) with plain verse closing marks, no title and no colophon; img. 74 is a heading side (frontispieces left and right and a title page of the next chapter, folio 64). So the colophon with «བཻ་ཌཱུར» is not on img. 73–74; look at the foot of img. 72 (p. 35) or after the chapter's end on img. 75. Open.

## Answered 2026-11-21

Yes: the title is «བཻ་ཌཱུར་དཀར་པོའི་དོ་ཤལ», with ཻ on བ and ཱུ on ཌ, the book's standard spelling (Vaiḍūrya karpo, Sangs rgyas rgya mtsho's Vaiḍūrya dkar po, whose name the Do shal borrows). The check is on text, not on the underdotted print. The typeset *Bod kyi rtsis rig kun 'dus chen mo* vol. 2 (VEIE0OPI2F246A26_I4716) spells the title word «བཻཌཱུར་དཀར་པོ» twice and «བཻ་ཌཱུར་དཀར་པོ» once, always with ཻ and ཱུ, and the Gser thang etext's colophon of ch. 4 reads «བཻ་ཌཱུར་དཀར་པོའི་དོ་ཤལ» (I3CN5028, one place in full form; its other places give the OCR variants བེ/བི/བོ for ཻ, which the 1996 etext also shows, and are OCR noise on a vowel sign above the line, not another spelling). The print marks every Sanskritised word with a dot under each syllable, so the underdots are that mark, not a correction. The colophon of p. 36 itself was not found on img. 73-74 (scan-h); the same title stands in the ch. 4 colophon of p. 32 (wb.1:p32), where the vowel signs were last read as ཱ and ཻ at 5x with doubt on the Gser thang print; the typeset witness settles the spelling.
