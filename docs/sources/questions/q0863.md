# Does «མཚན་སྟོད» mean the first part of the night up to midnight (Byang pa, 'Bum nag gsal sgron) or dusk with midnight alike (Khro ru), and is the part from midnight to dawn named anywhere in the Four Tantras' tradition?

Byang pa and the 'Bum nag gsal sgron take it as the first part up to midnight, Khro ru as dusk with midnight alike.
