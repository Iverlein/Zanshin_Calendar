# The plank-tree's fifteenth and sixteenth heads, «ལྔ་ཐུན» and «དྲུག་འབྲས»

From which places below the sky year do the plank-tree's fifteenth and sixteenth heads start, «ལྔ་ཐུན» and «དྲུག་འབྲས» as the scan prints them (KD «ལྔ་མཐུན», «དྲུག་འགྲུས»; dz:i052-055)?

**Searched 2026-11-14 (inv-ddd).** BDRC phrase search «ལྔ་མཐུན་དྲུག་འབྲས» 3 hits (WB's prints I1KG12906, I3CN5028 and the Vaidurya dkar po, MW2CZ8040), «དྲུག་འབྲས་ནས» 1 (a Tshangs chen commentary, not on the year); no dictionary of Steinert's 64 has «ལྔ་མཐུན» or «དྲུག་འབྲས/འགྲུས». In the notes' water-snake example the places are symmetric about the year (harmony bird and ox, four years either side; the seventh corner the pig, six either way), so the fifth and sixth places below the sky year are fixed as places whichever way «མར» runs; the direction of «མར» in the cycle is not stated by any passage found. The claim claims/plank-tree-places-fifth-sixth-below-sky-year.md stands.
