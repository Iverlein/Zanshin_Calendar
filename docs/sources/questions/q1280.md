# Where in the Cakrasaṃvara root tantra, the Yoginīsañcāra and Klong chen pa's works do the three quotations stand with which Dpal sprul shows that the feast on the tenth of both halves is taught in all tantras, the waxing tenth by day and the waning tenth by night?

Raised by the practice-days investigation (pd-b, 2026-11-16); see the cited passages.

**Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-b).** Narrowed: two of the three are named by older authors who quote the same lines, claim [tenth-day-three-authorities-named-by-their-quoters](../claims/tenth-day-three-authorities-named-by-their-quoters.md). The first is the Root Tantra of Cakrasaṃvara («དཔལ་འཁོར་ལོ་སྡོམ་པ་རྩ་བའི་རྒྱུད་ལས», named by Padma dkar po, Zhe chen rgyal tshab and Ngag dbang kun dga' bsod nams), in a verse on the torma «as one wishes»; the second the Yoginīsañcāra («རྣལ་འབྱོར་མ་ཀུན་སྤྱོད་ལས», Tsong kha pa, Zhe chen and the Bde mchog drug cu ma commentary). The third's lines, «ཉ་དང་གནམ་སྟོང་ཚེས་བརྒྱད་ཉི་ཤུ་དགུ། །ཚེས་བཅུ་ཡར་ངོ་མར་ངོའི་ཉིན་དང་མཚན། །བསྙེན་བསྒྲུབ་ཚོགས་མཆོད་བསྐང་བཤགས་འབད་པར་བྱ», have a different first line from Dpal sprul's and stand, as «ཀུན་མཁྱེན་ཆེན་པོས», only in Zhe chen and the Rdo rje'i dam tshig gi rnam bzhag text: BDRC's exact search of «བསྙེན་བསྒྲུབ་ཚོགས་མཆོད་བསྐང་བཤགས་འབད་པར་བྱ» (2 hits) and «ཚེས་བཅུ་ཡར་ངོ་མར་ངོའི་ཉིན་དང་མཚན། །བསྙེན་བསྒྲུབ» (2) find no work of Klong chen pa. The chapters of the first two (Kangyur etext VE23681_001 has the Cakrasaṃvara line, the window was not placed) and Longchenpa's work are not located. Open.
