# Is the earth of «ཁྱི་སྤྱང་བྲང་ཤུལ» hidden at or smeared on the lintel (p. 294)

On WB vol. 2 p. 294, l. 7 (img. 300), is «ཁྱི་སྤྱང་བྲང་ཤུལ» the old lair of dogs and wolves, or of the one wolf-like dog the Tshig mdzod chen mo calls «ཁྱི་སྤྱང», and is the earth taken from it hidden at the door's lintel («སྦས», the 1996 etext) or smeared on it as adornment («སྤྲས», Yigdzin-1 on the reprint)? The stack is a blot on both scans. (RD91)

## Answered 2026-11-05

Answered (RD156, [ch32](../corpus/wb/ch32.md)): the lintel's verb reads «སྦས» on Zhol img. 1040, so the earth is hidden there, not smeared.
