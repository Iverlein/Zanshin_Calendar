# In the Sha za brtsegs pa'i rgyud's ninth section the signs of nearness (black man, black dog, raven, tiger, thunder) stand under the title «the chapter of the dream signs»: are they dreams of the practitioner at the approach, and are the lines after «ཐོག་འཚག་ཉེ་བའི་མཚན་མའོ» a second group, signs of the work succeeding?

RD335; the machine's «བཤོར» and the colophon's first words are not settled on the scan.

## Answered 2026-11-11

Read by RD399 (2026-11-11), the page I1KG3558 img. 463 (passage szb:i463b, state machine, Yigdzin-1 with an eye on a 2x crop; claims/szb-ninth-section-two-groups-title-only-says-dream.md). The lines run in two groups: the first up to «ཐོག་འཚག་ཉེ་བའི་མཚན་མའོ» lists the signs of nearness; the second, acts and gifts ending «དེས་ནི་དགྲ་ལ་མྱུར་དུ་འགྲོ», is the success of the work. The text does not say that the signs are dreams of the practitioner: «རྨི་ལྟས་ཀྱི་ལེའུ» stands in the colophon only.
