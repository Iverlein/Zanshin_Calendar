# 天一's five-day count from 乙卯 or 庚戌 against six days in the corners

the 簾中抄, as 古事類苑 prints it, has 天一 block "five days at a time" from 乙卯 in the east, and the Meiji 大雑書 have five days each from 庚戌 in the north-east; the 暦林問答集 and the 江次第抄 have six days in each corner from 己酉. Is the five-day count a different reckoning, or a shortening of the same? (RD94)

## Answered 2026-11-05

庚戌 leaves out the day of descent, 己酉 (永暦雑書; the 1937 book says so itself). Later books make every stay five days, a schematic. ([kyureki.md](../kyureki.md), *The direction gods' last questions in the old texts (RD163)*)
