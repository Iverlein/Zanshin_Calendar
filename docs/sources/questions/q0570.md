# Which course gives each date its one direction in KP's square for Rāhu

In the nu ru course's square of KP img. 78 and the Bhutan reset, which course gives each of the thirty dates its one direction (the square's east holds the 2nd, 4th, 19th and 27th)? See verdicts.md, RD124.

**Tested again 2026-11-14 (inv-jjj).** The square's direction for each of the thirty dates (east 2, 4, 19, 27; north-east 9, 22; north 11, 17, 24, 26; north-west 6, 16, 21, 29; west 5, 18; south-west 1, 8, 23; south 10, 12, 14, 25, 28; south-east 3, 7, 13, 15, 20, 30) was set against the nu ru course's direction of setting out (rahu.md, 28 dates; the 14th and 30th have none) by its offset round the eight-point ring: 9 dates give offset 0 (1, 2, 9, 10, 11, 12, 20, 24, 25), the rest scatter over every other offset (1 to 7), so there is no constant or date-bound rotation of the nu ru course. Together with claims/kp-box78-square-is-not-the-nu-ru-course.md (starting and ending direction, and the detailed course) the square is none of the courses of pp. 236-238 by direction. «སྤྱི་བརྒྱུད་ཚེས་གྲངས» by BDRC's phrase search: 0 hits.
