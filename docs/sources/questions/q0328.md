# The black small feet's months, links and times

WB vol. 2, pp. 38–39 (img. 46–47): the black small feet give the tiger "last spring" like the ox and the hare "first spring", so that no year has the first summer month; the snake's link is printed «རེག» (contact) where craving would stand; the ox's link is printed «ཀ་ཤི» (so also the 1996 scan); and the hare's time is «རྡེལ་འགོ་དྲོས». Are these misprints, and what are the right month, link and time?
