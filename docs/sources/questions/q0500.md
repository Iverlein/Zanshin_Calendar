# Are all outer directions free on the 戊寅 days of the 金神 in the centre

The 1919 printing says of the 戊寅 days in the centre that the world's "all ten directions blocked" is wrong (absent in 1632). Does that mean every outer direction is free on those five days? (RD93)

## Answered 2026-11-05

For one almanac only: 1927 has the 戊寅 days in 土用 only, the four corners, «他の方位は差支なし». ([kyureki.md](../kyureki.md), *The direction gods' last questions in the old texts (RD163)*)
