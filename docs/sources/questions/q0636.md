# Khon's block of the provisions without a seal

Khon's block of the provisions (WB vol. 2, pp. 129–130) has no seal on the Zhol print, the Gser thang reprint or the 1996 print, and p. 124's rule would want a wood trigram there. Is the line lost from all the prints, or is khon left unsealed on purpose?

## Answered 2026-11-06

RD180 second pass (2026-11-06): in the 1996 print (Yigdzin-1 on img. 137–140 and the etext) the provisions have eight blocks; each has a hand-seal line «X་ཡི་ཕྱག་རྒྱས་སྟེང་ནས་མནན» and an earth-lord wheel line «ས་བདག་Y་ཡི་ཙཀྲ»; Khon's block (the sheep, wheel khon, closer dwa) has no seal line, every other block has one. The Zhol print and the Gser thang reprint were not checked, so whether the seal fell from all prints is not decided. The premise holds in the 1996 print.

RD180 (2026-11-07, third pass): Khon's block has no hand-seal line on the Zhol and the reprint either (machine only); the gap is common to all three prints.
