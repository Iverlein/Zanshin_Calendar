# Ten of what is «བཅུ་ཡི་བར» for the white snake seizing the right hand?

Days, as in the dawn rule's ཞག་བཅུ, or something else.


Narrowed (inv-k, 2026-11-14): the Svapnohana's own first verse gives ten days only for the dawn dream, and the Agnipurāṇa (agp:p280-281) the same; days is therefore the likelier unit, but no text found says it for the white snake: no Sanskrit parallel of the line was found in the Agnipurāṇa's dream chapter (agp:p282 has no snake, vulture, krauñca or hand), and BDRC's «ཞག་བཅུ་ཡི་བར» has two hits, neither on dreams. Still open.

**Narrowed 2026-11-17 (inv-c).** BDRC phrase search (2026-11-17) «སྦྲུལ་དཀར་པོས་ལག་པ་གཡས་པ་བཟུང» and «སྦྲུལ་དཀར་པོ་ལག་པ་གཡས»: 0 hits, so no commentary or parallel Tibetan text quotes the line with a unit. Still open.

**Narrowed 2026-11-17 (inv-e).** The line is in the Derge and the Peking-reprint Tengyur copies of the *Rmi lam brtag pa* as «གང་ཞིག་དཀར་པོའི་སྦྲུལ་གྱིས་ནི། ལག་པ་གཡས་པ་བཟུང་བ་ན། གང་ལ་ཡང་དག་བཅུ་ཡི་བར། དེ་ཡིས་ནོར་ནི་ཐོབ་པར་ངེས།» (BDRC exact search «ཡང་དག་བཅུ་ཡི་བར», 3 hits, 2026-11-17), so the words «ཡང་དག» (yang dag, "truly, rightly") sit between «གང་ལ» and «བཅུ་ཡི་བར»: the line reads "for whom, truly, within ten", with no unit in any copy. A third place quotes the verse whole, the dream commentary «Gnyid dang rmi lam bar gyi 'brel ba rnam par dpyad pa ḍā ki'i glu dbyangs» of Padma rdo rje (Kan su'u 2002; its etext is search-only, 403, so only the snippet is seen, no gloss). The 84000 translations (code search of 84000/data-tei, 2026-11-17: «white snake» gives only the Siṃhanāda dhāraṇī's trident; «dream» with «snake» and «right hand» no omen text) hold no parallel of the line. Still open; the unit is days only by the frame's ten-day dawn rule.

**Narrowed 2026-11-17 (sweep from inv-e).** The line stands in the Derge and Peking Tengyur copies as «གང་ལ་ཡང་དག་བཅུ་ཡི་བར», 'for whom, truly, within ten', with no unit in any copy; the unit is still not named (days is the likelier, from the dawn rule).
