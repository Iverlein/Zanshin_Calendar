# Ten of what is «བཅུ་ཡི་བར» for the white snake seizing the right hand?

Days, as in the dawn rule's ཞག་བཅུ, or something else.


Narrowed (inv-k, 2026-11-14): the Svapnohana's own first verse gives ten days only for the dawn dream, and the Agnipurāṇa (agp:p280-281) the same; days is therefore the likelier unit, but no text found says it for the white snake: no Sanskrit parallel of the line was found in the Agnipurāṇa's dream chapter (agp:p282 has no snake, vulture, krauñca or hand), and BDRC's «ཞག་བཅུ་ཡི་བར» has two hits, neither on dreams. Still open.
