## Narrowed 2026-11-24 (inv-g), three of the five places settled by checks

- ch. 3 p. 34, «བུ་ག་ཡོན་ཏན་མཚོ»: 439. The words (hole 9, yon tan 3, ocean 4) spell 439, and the arithmetic of the passage (corpus/wb/ch03.md, the "or, for the pure month" rule) fits it: the first way adds 209 («བུ་ག་ནམ་མཁའ་འཁྲིག་པ ༢༠༩»), the second divides by 460 («མཁའ་དུས་ཆུ་བོ ༤༦༠») after a multiplier 2, so by 230 months, and 209 + 230 = 439; 429 would be 199 + 230, which no other place gives. The 2 of the stored 429 is the clotted ༣ of the prints (rd-ak's Taikhang reading leaned the same way) and is the same sixth glyph as q1332's.
- ch. 7 p. 49, «dbang phyug, lag» and «dus, mtsho, ri»: 211 and 746. The same verse stands on p. 42, read on the scan there, with ༢༡༡ and ༧༤༦ (corpus/wb/ch07.md line 14), and the words give both (Īśvara 11 and hand 2; time 6, ocean 4, mountain 7). The stored ༣༡༡༡ and ༧༤༡ of p. 49 are the machine's 2/3 and 1/6 slips of the stacked print.
- ch. 7 p. 49, «thub pa, zla» 178 against the words' 17, and «nor, mtsho» 488 against 48: not settled. 178 is a figure that other places print (ch. 9 «རི་ཟླའི ༡༧༨», «གཟུགས ༡༧༨», the table of p. 46), but each there has its own words (klu, ri, gzugs), so it cannot be carried to «thub pa, zla»; for 488 the verse's own «ཀླུ་མཚོ་༤༨» (klu 8, mtsho 4) stands a few lines on and spells 48, which favours 48 here too, but no repeat of «ནོར་མཚོ» itself was found. They stay as noted.
