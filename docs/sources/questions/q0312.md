# Unidentified provisions of the trigrams and the old

Several provisions by trigram (p. 75) and of the old are not identified: «བྱ་ཐི་བ», «ཕུ་ཐུང», «སོག་པ་སྲང», «ཚར་ཤིང», «ཇུ་ཚེར», «བོང་གོས», «སྐུར་རྩིབ» (p. 75) against «སྐྱུར་རྩིབ» (p. 76), «སྦྱར་པ», «སྟག་ཤིང», «སོ་བ་སོར་ལ་སྟག་ལྤགས་བོང», «ཕུག་དར», «རྟ་རྨ་པུ་སྟི» (p. 76), «ཚ་རགས» (p. 77): what are they?

**Narrowed 2026-11-05 (RD161, [word-hunts.md](../word-hunts.md)).** The digest divides sog pa | srang, phug | dar, so ba | sor le, and ju rtse | ra bong; ju tshe or ju rtse is a grain from China and Jang (Blue Beryl, etext); stag shing the birch. Left: tshar shing, phug as a grain, sor le, tsha rags.

**Narrowed further 2026-11-14 (inv-mmm).** From the dictionaries (Steinert's collection): *bya thi ba* is «ཕུག་རོན» (the pigeon; *Bod yig tshig gter rgya mtsho*, *Tshig mdzod chen mo*, Ives Waldo "[arch] pigeon"), which fits the digest's bird in the khon line; *phu thung* is the short sleeve (Ives Waldo, Valby, *Tshig mdzod chen mo*); *sbyar pa* is a tree, willow or poplar (Valby; *Tshig mdzod chen mo*), the tree of the khen line. Still in no dictionary: *tshar shing*, *sor le*, *tsha rags*, *phug* as a grain (*phug* is only cave, innermost, bladder; the digest's pairing *phug* | *dar* stands).
