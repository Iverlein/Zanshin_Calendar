# Unidentified provisions of the trigrams and the old

Several provisions by trigram (p. 75) and of the old are not identified: «བྱ་ཐི་བ», «ཕུ་ཐུང», «སོག་པ་སྲང», «ཚར་ཤིང», «ཇུ་ཚེར», «བོང་གོས», «སྐུར་རྩིབ» (p. 75) against «སྐྱུར་རྩིབ» (p. 76), «སྦྱར་པ», «སྟག་ཤིང», «སོ་བ་སོར་ལ་སྟག་ལྤགས་བོང», «ཕུག་དར», «རྟ་རྨ་པུ་སྟི» (p. 76), «ཚ་རགས» (p. 77): what are they?

**Narrowed 2026-11-05 (RD161, [word-hunts.md](../word-hunts.md)).** The digest divides sog pa | srang, phug | dar, so ba | sor le, and ju rtse | ra bong; ju tshe or ju rtse is a grain from China and Jang (Blue Beryl, etext); stag shing the birch. Left: tshar shing, phug as a grain, sor le, tsha rags.

**Narrowed further 2026-11-14 (inv-mmm).** From the dictionaries (Steinert's collection): *bya thi ba* is «ཕུག་རོན» (the pigeon; *Bod yig tshig gter rgya mtsho*, *Tshig mdzod chen mo*, Ives Waldo "[arch] pigeon"), which fits the digest's bird in the khon line; *phu thung* is the short sleeve (Ives Waldo, Valby, *Tshig mdzod chen mo*); *sbyar pa* is a tree, willow or poplar (Valby; *Tshig mdzod chen mo*), the tree of the khen line. Still in no dictionary: *tshar shing*, *sor le*, *tsha rags*, *phug* as a grain (*phug* is only cave, innermost, bladder; the digest's pairing *phug* | *dar* stands).

**Narrowed further 2026-11-14 (inv-last).** A parallel list stands in a Bhutanese anthology of calendar and black-reckoning texts (*Dkar rtsis dang nag rtsis kyi gzhung*, MW26041, Thimphu 1981, VEIE0OPI1829B6AF_I1KG11905 etext pp. 551-552, mediocre OCR; found by BDRC phrase search «སོར་ལེ»), in the rite that follows the corpse-disposal by the year of death: «སྟག་ཤིང་དང་། བྱ་ནེ་ཙོ། སོ་བ། སོར་ལེ། སྟག་ལྤགས། བོང་བུའི་སྒྱུར་རྩིབ། སྐ་རགས་བཅས་དུམ་སར་རྫོངས།» (tiger-tree, parrot, hulled-barley, «sor le», tiger skin, the donkey's «sgyur rtsib», belt, ... all put in the burial place). It pairs with WB's items: «བྱ་ཐི་བ» is then a bird (the dictionary has «bya ne tso» = parrot, Rangjung Yeshe, 84000), «སོ་བ» a thick-shelled barley (Hopkins, Valby, Tshig mdzod chen mo «'bru rigs phyi lpags can»), «སྟག་ཤིང» and «སྟག་ལྤགས» as in WB, «བོང་བུའི་སྐྱུར་རྩིབ» as the donkey's «skyur rtsib» (WB p. 75 «སྐུར་རྩིབ», p. 76 «སྐྱུར་རྩིབ»: this list spells it «སྒྱུར་རྩིབ»), and «ཚ་རགས» as «སྐ་རགས» (ska rags: belt, sash; Rangjung Yeshe, Ives Waldo, Tshig mdzod chen mo «སྐེད་པ་འཆིང་བྱེད»), the sound of «tsha» and «ska» being close in a spoken or mis-copied list: a reading from the parallel, not from a gloss. «སོར་ལེ» stands between «སོ་བ» and the tiger skin there too and is in no dictionary. «ཚར་ཤིང»: BDRC finds the word (54 hits) in a chronicle's «tshar shing lug myos byed la» and Pema Karpo's «drag po tshar shing mtshon gyi rtse» (a wrathful weapon's point), and the dictionaries give «tshar» only as times, row, rosary (Tshig mdzod chen mo; *Brda dkrol gser gyi me long*: «tshar ni phreng ba»), Dan Martin "long strips of bamboo for binding (Bhutan, Sikkim)": no sense fits a provision.
