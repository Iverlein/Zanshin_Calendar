# Revatī's minor star on p. 328: «དྲོ་ལོད» or «དྲོ་ཡོད»

Is Revatī's minor star on p. 328 «ལས་སྣ་དྲོ་ལོད» or «ལས་སྣ་དྲོ་ཡོད»? The reprint looks ཡ beside a certain ལ, and the 1996 etexts have one of each.

RD176 (2026-11-06), narrowed toward «དྲོ་ཡོད»: the reprint's letter has crossing strokes and no loop, unlike the ལ of «ལས» in the same line (9×); the Zhol (img. 1084, line 1) is not told at 8×; the Zhol etext's machine text has ཡོད.
