# Revatī's minor star on p. 328: «དྲོ་ལོད» or «དྲོ་ཡོད»

Is Revatī's minor star on p. 328 «ལས་སྣ་དྲོ་ལོད» or «ལས་སྣ་དྲོ་ཡོད»? The reprint looks ཡ beside a certain ལ, and the 1996 etexts have one of each.
