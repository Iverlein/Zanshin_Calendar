# Revatī's minor star on p. 328: «དྲོ་ལོད» or «དྲོ་ཡོད»

Is Revatī's minor star on p. 328 «ལས་སྣ་དྲོ་ལོད» or «ལས་སྣ་དྲོ་ཡོད»? The reprint looks ཡ beside a certain ལ, and the 1996 etexts have one of each.

RD176 (2026-11-06), narrowed toward «དྲོ་ཡོད»: the reprint's letter has crossing strokes and no loop, unlike the ལ of «ལས» in the same line (9×); the Zhol (img. 1084, line 1) is not told at 8×; the Zhol etext's machine text has ཡོད.

RD176 second part (2026-11-06): SY has «དྲོ་ལོད» for Revatī, but SY is OCR text and cannot tell ཡ from ལ; this conflicts with the first part's eye reading ཡ; unresolved.

RD176 fourth part (2026-11-07): the Zhol img. 1084 letter is not legible at 1x-2x either; unresolved.

RD176 (2026-11-08): the 1996 scan (I1KG12907 img. 336) at 9× leans to ཡ but is not certain; the 1996 etext has «ལོད»; the Taikhang (I5878 img. 466) was searched and Revatī's line not found among rows 2–3. Unresolved.

## Answered 2026-11-24

The word is «དྲོ་ལོད» with ལ: WB has «གྲོ་ལོད» on p. 258 and p. 425 and «དྲོ་ལོད» on p. 328; «ཡོད» occurs in none; the 1996 scans of pp. 258 and 328 show a closed ལ. The first syllable (གྲོ or དྲོ) was not told on the 700 px scan. Claim wb-p328-revati-minor-star-lod (RD176).
