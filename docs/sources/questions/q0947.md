# Who wrote the Rnam dpyod blo gros spyan stong bkra ba, and which Dbyangs 'char verse does it quote?

RD322.

## Answered 2026-11-10

Answered by RD326 (2026-11-10): the Rnam dpyod blo gros spyan stong bkra ba quotes the verse as 'from the Dbyangs 'char text'; its colophon names the writer as Blo gros kyi 'jug sgo yangs pa Orgyan bstan 'dzin, a Gsho thang mantrin and corpse-carrier, whom a lord, Lho rje Drung pa Dkon mchog bstan 'dzin phrin las, called Dpal bzang po; he wrote it in his own 49th year, a fire-bird year, at Theg chen Yang rtse kun gzigs pho brang of Thub bstan bshad sgrub gling, Mdo khams, from a very corrupt copy; BDRC names no creator. The Dbyangs 'char text itself was not found.
