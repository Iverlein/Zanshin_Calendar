# On p. 375, the summer's first stretch «དབྱར་གསུམ་ཉི་གོང[?]་ནམ་ཕྱེད་བར»: is the second word «ཉི་གོང» as Yigdzin-1 has it, or «ཉི་ནུབ» as in spring and autumn?

RD333; the 1996 vol. 1 scans are about 670 px wide.

## Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-k)

Etext only; no scan seen again. BDRC exact-phrase search «དབྱར་གསུམ་ཉི་གོང་ནམ་ཕྱེད» and «ཁ་ནུབ་ཕྱེད་ནས་ནམ་ལངས་བར» 2 hits each, both WB's own etexts (the 1996 print, I1KG12906, and the Gser thang reprint, I3CN5028); «དབྱར་གསུམ་ཉི་ནུབ་ནམ་ཕྱེད་བར» 0 and «དབྱར་གསུམ་ཉི་ནུབ་ནམ་ཕྱེད» 0, while «དཔྱིད་གསུམ་ཉི་ནུབ་ནམ་ཕྱེད་བར» (spring) has 4: the two WB etexts and two more books. Read in the etexts: the 1996 etext and the Gser thang etext both have «དབྱར་གསུམ་ཉི་གོང་ནམ་ཕྱེད་བར» in the summer line and «ཉི་ནུབ» in spring and autumn; the Zhol print's etext (I1KG1710, poor OCR) reads «དབྱར་གསུམ་གྱི་གོད་ནམབྱེད་བར» (the OCR's «གྱི་གོད» for «ཉི་གོང») and a further copy (I4CZ74285, the poor OCR of a 1276-page print) «དབྱར་གསུམ་ཉི་གོངནམ་ཕྱེད་བར». So four etexts of WB's prints read a «གོང»/«གོད» syllable, none «ནུབ»; they are machine readings of the same page and can share a misreading. Not a dictionary word: no headword «nyi gong» in Steinert's public set. The question stays at «ཉི་གོང», read by Yigdzin-1 and by every etext, unchecked on a sharp scan.
