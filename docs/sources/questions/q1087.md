# Does the Tibet House manuscript catalogue (I2KG212509) carry the printed Tshangs pa'i dbyangs entries, and list the Examination of Dreams at all?

RD381.

## Answered 2026-11-13

It lists the Examination of Dreams, and in the printed catalogue's words. The Tibet House manuscript's catalogue volume (vol. 225, I2KG212509) names the Examination of Dreams at image 77, printed folio 75, directly after the Deceiving Death in 360 verses, as «བི་ཌཱུ་ཏི་ཙནྡྲའི་རང་འགྱུར» (Vibhūticandra's own translation) with no maker named (svo:i77, read by Yigdzin-1 on the whole image and looked at in two crops). The Phur lcog print of the same Tshangs pa'i dbyangs lists it after the Deceiving Death in the same words and with no author (svo:i472-473), so for this entry the manuscript carries the printed entry. The Deceiving Death's entry at image 77 is also the printed one's (maker Ngag dbang grags pa, translated by Atiśa and Rin chen bzang po); a second Deceiving Death entry at image 197 is worded otherwise (svo:i197, "two condensed meanings ... with illustrations"), which has no counterpart in the printed catalogue's entries read. So for the Examination of Dreams the manuscript carries the printed entry; that the printed text is the manuscript's own text as a whole stays unshown (answered together with q1004, 2026-11-13).
