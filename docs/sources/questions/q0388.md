# Reply 111's verdict pebble and «ངན་ཐ་ཆད»

Reply 111 bars a couple whose two life reckonings are "utterly bad", each judged by one verdict pebble from its white and black pebbles: is that verdict the birth reckoning's general verdict over its hundred pebbles (WB vol. 1, pp. 261, 329–331), and does "utterly bad" (ངན་ཐ་ཆད, ngan tha chad) mean all black or most black?

**Narrowed 2026-10-25 ([claims/life-reckoning-one-verdict.md](../claims/life-reckoning-one-verdict.md), [claims/ngan-tha-chad-bottom-class.md](../claims/ngan-tha-chad-bottom-class.md)).** The questioner calls ch. 22 "the life reckoning" (question 102, ys:i090, on the scan), and the bar of question 111 is WB p. 355's one bad result of the ma klungs, which the notes (wb.2:p524) gloss as both spouses bad by the karma reckoning: the regent's life reckoning is Mipham's karma reckoning. Its one verdict pebble is read as WB's verdict on the whole birth (wb.1:p349, results weighed by force and number), not p. 331's verdict by where the pebbles lie, which gives shapes, not a colour. WB uses «ཐ་ཆད» for the bottom class of a graded set (vol. 2, pp. 41, 333, 393), and question 115 (ys:i094, on the scan) for the mewa's body cut: a class, made binary by the one pebble, so "utterly bad" is the pebble black, the bad results greater and more, not all black. No text gives a count; a middling verdict is question 392.
