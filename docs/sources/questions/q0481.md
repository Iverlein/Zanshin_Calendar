# Who or what is «ཇི་ནོང» glossed beside Mañjuśrī on p. 3

Who or what is «ཇི་ནོང», glossed beside Mañjuśrī (འཇམ་དབྱངས, 'jam dbyangs) as the protector of the chapter's homage (WB vol. 2, p. 3, l. 4: «མགོན་པོ(འཇམ་དབྱངས་དང་ཇི་ནོང་།)»)? (RD90)

RD178 (2026-11-07): located the Zhol folio: WBZ I1KG1710 img. 629 holds the chapter's opening, the homage as an interlinear gloss under line 3 of the folio. Yigdzin-1 on that image reads the gloss «འཇམ་དབྱངས་དང་ཇི་སྐོང»; the gloss is about 25 px high there and at 8× the letters are a blur, so «སྐོང» against «ནོང» is not settled by eye. On the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5029 img. 9, line 4, 3×) the parenthesis reads «འཇམ་དབྱངས་དང་ཇི་ནོང་།» plainly. So the reprint and the 1996 print agree on «ནོང»; the Zhol reading is a machine lead only. Not answered; who the figure is remains open.
