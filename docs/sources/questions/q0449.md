# Which planet or node is «ཕུང» in «བྱ་གླང་སྦྲུལ་གསུམ་ཕུང་དར་ཚེས» on p. 313

In WB vol. 2 p. 313, line 7, «ཕུང» in «བྱ་གླང་སྦྲུལ་གསུམ་ཕུང་དར་ཚེས» (bird, ox and snake are «ཕུང»'s rising dates) names which planet or node, the third of the four triads after Saturn and Mars and before the Moon? (RD79)

## Answered 2026-11-05

Answered 2026-11-01 (RD112, [claims/wb-p313-phung-jupiter-chu-bdag-west.md](../claims/wb-p313-phung-jupiter-chu-bdag-west.md)): «ཕུང» is Jupiter, as the *Lamp* has «ཕུར་བུས» in the same line. RD159 found nothing on the reprint to change it; the line was not read on the Zhol.
