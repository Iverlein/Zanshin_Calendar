# Who is «the great siddha Gling» (གྲུབ་ཐོབ་ཆེན་པོ་གླིང) who is credited with the history, the guidance and the two oral explanations of the four sheets: Gling ras Padma rdo rje of the Drukpa lineage (the chain puts Gling after Bde legs rin chen and before Rgya ras, Dbon ras and Rgod tshang pa), and is the «I» of the history Gling?

RD383.

## Answered 2026-11-14

Gling is, on the evidence read, Gling ras Padma rdo rje (1128-1188), but that the «I» of the history is he is not stated by any line. BDRC's person record P910, «gling ras pa pad+ma rdo rje», lists as other names «grub chen gling ras» and «grub thob gling rje ras pa» (the great siddha Gling ras, the siddha Gling rje ras pa), with the note that Kong sprul's Ris med chos 'byung (p. 28) and others give «gling ras pa pad+ma rdo rje» as also called Gling rje ras pa or the siddha Gling rje ras pa; he is the teacher of P909, the first Drukpa incarnation Rgya ras Ye shes rdo rje (1161-1211), and the record calls him master of the Gling ras bla' brgyud; his teachers include P5904, Sum pa ras pa. The Drukpa chain of dds.42:p165-166 puts «Gling» (written Gling, then Gling ras) after Bde legs rin chen and before the lord Rgya ras, Dbon ras and Rgod tshang pa: the same place in the line as BDRC's teacher of Rgya ras. So «the great siddha Gling» (grub thob chen po gling) of Padma dkar po's record and of the Rgyal sras Bstan 'dzin rab rgyas biography (dds.43:p138-139) is Gling ras, and no other Gling stands in that chain.
That the «I» of the history is Gling rests on two points taken together, neither a statement: he is the chain's last holder after Bde gshegs rin chen, and the history says that the Indian roots of two of the sheets came to its «I» from the lama Sum pa ras pa (dds.19:p97-102), the very man BDRC gives as a teacher of Gling ras. The history is thus consistent with Gling as its author, and the Drukpa records credit him with the history, the guidance and the two oral explanations; the history itself names no author.
