# Does the oldest copy of the Moonbeams write «སྟངས་གཅོད» in the count of the 24 pebbles

Does the oldest copy of the Moonbeams (MW1LT2427, MW1LT0150, the NLM block MW1NLM6115) write «སྟངས་གཅོད» in the count of the 24 pebbles, f. 15b? (Question 248.)

RD168 (2026-11-06), narrowed: the NLM woodblock of the Moonbeams (I1NLM6115_001) writes «སྟངས་གཅོད་རྡེའུ་ཉི་ཤུ་བཞི» in the count verse (img. 34, by eye); «སྟངས་གཅོད» again at img. 36 (machine only). MW1LT2427 is open on BDRC; where the Moonbeams stands in it was not found (q660).

## Narrowed further (2026-11-16, inv-e)

BDRC phrase search for the Moonbeams' own count line: «སྟངས་གཅོད་རྡེའུ་ཉི་ཤུ་བཞི» 2 hits, «གཅོད་རྡེའུ་ཉི་ཤུ་བཞི» 2, «སྟངས་གཅོད་རྡེའུ་ཉི་ཤུ་རྩ་བཞི» 0, «རྡེའུ་ཉི་ཤུ་བཞི་བཀོད» 0, «ཟླ་བའི་འོད་ཟེར་ལ་སྟངས་གཅོད» 0; none is MW1LT2427, MW1LT0150 or the NLM block. The two hits are the *Mdo rgyud rin po che'i mdzod* commentary *Tshangs chen bskal pa'i mdzes rgyan* (MW28955_E5A009: «brgya 'dre'i rde'u brgya phrag cig / stangs gcod rde'u nyi shu bzhi») and Eskenazi's English book. «བརྒྱ་འདྲེའི་རྡེའུ་བརྒྱ་ཕྲག་གཅིག» 6 hits show the same «stangs gcod rde'u nyi shu bzhi» in the *Bod kyi rtsis rig kun 'dus chen mo*, the *Mdo rgyud rin po che'i mdzod kyi 'bru 'grel Gter chen bum bzang* (the snippet gives no MW id; there «stang gcod rde'u nyer bzhi»), and the *Zlos gar* («stangs gcod rde 'u nyi shu bzhi»), so the spelling «stangs gcod» is the family's, against WB's «gcod 'dral nyer bzhi» and the Nag rtsis' «ltang gcod rde'u nyi shu». The oldest copy of the Moonbeams itself was not reached.
