# How far does the count of the sixty-year pairs and of the ogre's and demon's seven run?

How many years does p. 171–172's count of the sixty-year pairs and of the ogre's and demon's seven up and down run (the asker's years lived, as in the worked example, or one place), and is a female counted up as in ch. 26 («བཀེན» "propped")?

**Searched 2026-10-27 (RD58, third reading, [verdicts.md](../verdicts.md), section A).** No line on how far the count runs in the notes' moment section on Mipham's own print (I2029 img. 739–743) or the typeset (I1PD34647 img. 682–685). Open.

**Narrowed 2026-10-27 (RD58, fifth reading, [verdicts.md](../verdicts.md), section A).** The *Utpala Garland* (MW25167, I4767) runs its counts «ལོ་གྲངས་དུ་ལོན་བར་དུ་བགྲངས» to the years lived of the reckoned one, the male counted down (སྦུབས) and the female up (བཀན; WB «བཀེན»); the anthology *Dkar rtsis dang nag rtsis kyi gzhung* (MW26041) has the same line in its birth-mansion count. Neither gives WB's sixty-year pairs or the ogre's and demon's seven; for those no length is found.

**Searched 2026-10-27 (RD58, sixth reading, [verdicts.md](../verdicts.md), section A).** MW3CN6027 pp. 57–65 (lent scan, a modern book, witness only) lay the fight between two persons by the elements of the sixty-year table and run no count of years; no length, no male down and female up. MW28959 and MW21880 have no lent scans. Open.
