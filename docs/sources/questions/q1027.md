# Are the «two condensed meanings» of the Deceiving Death by Ngag dbang grags pa in the Narthang catalogue (svo:i197) the 360-verse work that stands before the Svapnohana in the Derge? The line names no verse count.

RD365.

## Answered 2026-11-13

No. The Narthang catalogue lists the 360-verse work (ཤླཽ་ཀ་སུམ་བརྒྱ་དྲུག་ཅུ་པ, "three hundred and sixty verses") by Ngag dbang grags pa on img. 77, in the mother-tantra run with the Examination of Dreams after it, translated by the Lord and Rin chen bzang po (svo:i77); the «two condensed meanings … with illustrations» of img. 197 (svo:i197) are another entry, in the Yamāntaka run, translated by Dīpaṃkaraśrījñāna and Rin chen bzang po. Both entries name the same author; whether the two condensed meanings are an abridgement of the 360-verse work is not said by either line. Machine reading of img. 77.
