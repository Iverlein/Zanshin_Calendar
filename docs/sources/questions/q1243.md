## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-h)

Narrowed, not answered. The two phrases are not the same item in WB. WB works 59 and 60 (ch. 34) print «ལྷ་ལྔ་འཚོག་པ» (lha lnga 'tshog pa), the earth lord of ch. 33's «ལྷ་ལྔ་འཚོག / སར་གསོན་གཤིན་ལས། ཁྲོམ་གྱི་འདུ…» (wb.2:p360-361: where the five gods meet, avoid works of the living and the dead and market gatherings), a lord of the date; BDRC phrase search «ལྷ་ལྔ་འཚོག་པ» gives 2 hits, the two WB prints (I1KG12907, I3CN5029), and no other text. KP box 10's «ལྷ་ལྔ་གནས་ཕྱོགས» (the five gods' dwelling-direction; box 10's *Not calculated* list) and the «ལྷ་ལྔ» of month-entries.md («ལྷ་ལྔ་ཤར། དགུ་ནུབ») are the month-direction pair of the five and nine gods, not the 'tshog-pa lord. So «box 10 keeps ལྷ་ལྔ» is a different entry from the one boxes 17 and 20 drop. Why KP leaves out the 'tshog-pa lord (while KP boxes 17 and 20 keep the other names of WB's lists) is not said in any text found. Open.
