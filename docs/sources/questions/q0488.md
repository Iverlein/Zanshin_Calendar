# How wide is each god's direction on the printed 方位吉凶図

How wide is each god's direction on the printed 方位吉凶図? The 暦林問答集 rules for the 大將軍 that 寅, 甲, 卯, 乙 and 辰 are avoided together (§8); is that kept today, and for the other gods? (RD92)

RD172 (2026-11-06), narrowed: MS37 p. 153 gives the 寅甲卯乙辰 wide avoidance as «一說» and adds «今は之れを用ひず»; the 1931 wheel's sectors are unequal, one to three branches, no fixed width per god. Today's paper not seen.

**Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-c).** The 方位細鑑 (和佐化竜, MS, 享和頃; NDL 2541333, frames 53–55) keeps the wide rule as its own «正説»: 大將軍 avoided in two stems and three branches (quoting 安倍晴道's 三鏡暦 and the 拾芥抄), named 的殺, 対殺, 前殺, 後殺; in a 遊行 only 的殺 and 対殺 deeply. The 1901 独占易学全書 and the 1924 高島易断所's 除災招福神秘開運法 give the seat as one branch with a three-year stay («三年塞り»). Today's paper not seen.
