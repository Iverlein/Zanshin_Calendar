# The Secret Subduer's recitation instruction (Gter chos vol. 27, etext pp. 190-211) is listed in the volume's contents as printed pp. 183-204, but image minus 11 gives pp. 179-200: which numbering is right for citing it?

Raised by the 2026-11-13 investigation f.

## Answered 2026-11-14

The volume's own contents list is right: the Secret Subduer's recitation instruction (Bka' brgyad chen mo Gsang ba zil gnon las, Bzlas pa sngags kyi pra khrid) stands on printed pp. 183-204. Read on the scan of Gter chos vol. 27 (BDRC I1PD108815, 2026-11-14): image 190, the page whose title the etext gives as p190, carries the printed number 183 at its right edge, and image 211, the page with the colophon (the iron-mouse-year note at Gsang sngags chos gling, «མངྒ་ལཾ»), carries the printed number 204. So within this text the printed page is image minus 7, not minus 11 (sources.yaml's offset of 11 for vol. 27 was not true at these pages; where it was measured, and where the shift of four happens, were not looked for). For citing: use the etext marker (= image) pN as the pool does, and give the printed pages 183-204 for the work in prose; do not compute printed pages from the image with a constant for this volume.
