# The word read «ལྷང» on p. 106

On p. 106, l. 4, «སྙིང་སྣ་ར་ཡི་ལྷང[?]་ཕུག་བཅུག»: what is the word read «ལྷང»? The scan's stack does not look like lha.

## Answered 2026-11-05

Lgang phug, a bladder (the 1996 etext; the reprint agrees). The Zhol has a shorter line without it, a question of its own. ([word-hunts.md](../word-hunts.md), RD161)
