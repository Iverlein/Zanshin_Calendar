# Which six fight in the notes' 360 starting points?

In the notes' close on the 360 starting points (vol. 2, p. 538, img. 546, l. 2–4) the six that fight are written as «སྲོག་ལུས་དབང་ཀླུང་དབང་བླ་རང་དུར་གཤེད་དུར»: eight words; which six are meant, and is the second «དབང» a slip, the luck among them though the worked example does not use it?

**Narrowed 2026-10-27 (RD58, third reading, [verdicts.md](../verdicts.md), section A).** The typeset Collected Works (I1PD34647 img. 685, by eye) prints the eight words with «དབང» twice, as the 1996 print, the repeat at the end of a line in both; Mipham's own print (I2029 img. 742, row 2) is blotted at the list and cannot be read by eye. The doubling is not the 1996 print's alone; which six are meant is still open.

**Searched 2026-10-27 (RD58, fifth reading, [verdicts.md](../verdicts.md), section B).** «འཐབ་ཟླ་དྲུག» and the eight words of the list occur in no etext outside WB's prints; «རང་དུར་གཤེད་དུར» (own grave, slayer's grave) is, in the *Utpala Garland* and a verse manual of year pairings (I4717), the pair of names for how two years meet, which hints that the last two of the eight are relations and not aspects. Open.

**Searched 2026-10-27 (RD58, sixth reading, [verdicts.md](../verdicts.md), section A).** MW3CN6027 pp. 57–65 (lent scan, a modern book, witness only): the fight is always of four, life force, body, power and luck, in their own kinds; no list of six or of eight words, and «རང་དུར་གཤེད་དུར» does not occur on those pages. Open.

RD167 (2026-11-06), narrowed: the example lands six aspects in order: life force, body, power, la, own grave, slayer's grave (6×60 = 360). The luck and the printed list's second «དབང» are not used; whether that is an old doubling or the luck standing for the power's second reading is open.

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-fff, notes p. 538 read with the worked example; claim notes-worked-example-six-and-count).** The notes' own sentence is «སུམ་བརྒྱ་དྲུག་ཅུའི་སྐོར་མགོ་ཞེས་པ་སྲོག་ལུས་དབང་ཀླུང་དབང་བླ་རང་དུར་གཤེད་དུར་བཅས་དྲུག་གིས་འཐབ་ཟླ་ལོ་དྲུག་ཅུས་བསྐེས་པས་སུམ་བརྒྱ་དྲུག་ཅུ་འོང་བ་ཡིན»: the six that fight times the sixty years of opponents make 360. The example lands exactly six in order: life force, body, power, la, own grave, slayer's grave; the luck does not appear in it. That the printed list has eight words, the luck among them and «དབང» twice, is not explained; no text found says which reading of the eight the notes mean beyond the example. Open.
