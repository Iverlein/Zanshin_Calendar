## Answered 2026-11-14

inv-fff (2026-11-14), from the claims notes-p496-classes-by-surplus and illness-classes-by-difference (Mipham's p. 496 on the reprint, img. 504, and wb.1:p425): the nine classes are named by the surplus of one colour over the other among the fifteen, the door (the sixteenth pebble) left out: a third (three more, six against nine), a fourth (four more), a fifth (five more), a sixth (six more) of white or of black, and the half (level). Recounted: the notes' sums (6+9, 5+10, level with four ahead) reach fifteen with no speckled pebble, so the speckled is not counted among the fifteen there, and the notes say separately that it is counted neither outer nor inner («ཁྲ་བོ་ནི་ཕྱི་ནང་གང་དུའང་བགྲང་བ་མེད», wb.2:p495). The "four more" and "six more" are therefore classes the text names itself («བཞི་ཆས», «དྲུག་ཆས»), not inferences. A difference of one or two, and of seven or more, is named by no class on p. 425 or in the notes: they read nothing, and no text says what is done with such a count.
