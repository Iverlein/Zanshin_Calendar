# What do differences of one, two and seven and more read in the illness's nine classes?

In the nine classes (vol. 1, p. 425; the notes, p. 496), what does a difference of one, two, or seven and more of the fifteen read, and is the speckled counted in the fifteen but in neither colour (an arithmetic inference), so that "four more" and "six more" arise?

**Narrowed 2026-10-27 (RD58, second reading, [verdicts.md](../verdicts.md), sections C and D).** On the scan the notes (p. 495, img. 503, l. 25–26) say the speckled is counted neither outer nor inner but, if it falls, is «ཅུང་ཞན» "somewhat weak", and lay it aside in each pattern's rule (l. 22–23, «ཁྲ་བོ་བཏང་སྙོམས», «ཁྲ་བོ་མི་བརྩི»); p. 496's sentence on the classes stands word for word on the 1996 print and glosses the classes' own names, with no class for one, two or seven or more. Whether the nine classes count a speckled pebble among the fifteen the page does not say: still an inference.

**Narrowed 2026-10-27 (RD58, third reading, [verdicts.md](../verdicts.md), section C).** Mipham's own print (I2029 img. 662) has «ཁྲ་བོ་ལ་སྔོ་བགྲང་མེད། རགས་པར་འགྱེལ་ལ་བརྩི།», «ཁྲ་བོ་བཏང་སྙོམས» and «ཁྲ་བོ་མི་བརྩི» but not the sentence «ཁྲ་བོ་ནི་ཕྱི་ནང་གང་དུའང་བགྲང་བ་མེད་ཀྱང་བྱུང་ན་ཅུང་ཞན» ('somewhat weak') nor the living-eye and death-eye sentence: both stand in the 1996 print and the typeset (I1PD34647 img. 581–582). The class sentence of p. 496 is word for word the same on all three; no class for one, two or seven and more on any. Still an inference whether the classes count a speckled pebble.
