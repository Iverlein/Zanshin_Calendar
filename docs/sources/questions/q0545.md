# The saying «ཏ་ལ་ལམ་དུ་བསྒྱུར་བ», the palm turned into a road

Which saying is «ཏ་ལ་ལམ་དུ་བསྒྱུར་བ», the palm turned into a road, and does it name an impossible or a wasted thing?

## Answered 2026-11-06

RD171 (2026-11-06), on three BDRC etexts of the Desi's Vaiḍūrya dkar po (I1KG12907, I1KG2752, I1KG81102): all read «ཧ་ལ་ལམ་དུ་བསྒྱུར་བ་འདྲ», not ཏ. ཧ་ལ means poison or black monkshood; the lines that follow make it a dangerous thing, not an impossible or merely wasted one. The ཏ in WB's prints is a differing letter. Repeats q503.
