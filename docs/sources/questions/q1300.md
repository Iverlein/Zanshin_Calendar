# Are «དགུ་གསུམ» in the torma-repelling manual the 9th, 19th and 29th, and who wrote the Gnam lcags thog rgod?

Raised by the practice-days investigation (pd-g, 2026-11-16); see the cited passages.

**Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-b).** The second half is answered: the manual is a modern compilation, not a treasure text of Rtsa gsum gling pa. Its colophon (the etext of I1PD108816, pp. 647-649) names Gnam lcags Gsang sngags bstan 'dzin («སྒོ་ཆེན་རྒྱལ་བའི་སྐྱེས་མིང་འབོད་པ་གསང་སྔགས་བསྟན་འཛིན») as its compiler in the iron-tiger year of the 17th cycle, from an outline by Prajñā-dhyāna-kṛpa and the Gnam lcags liturgies; the same man wrote the commentary of tsl.27:i591. Claim [gnam-lcags-thog-rgod-compiled-by-gsang-sngags-bstan-dzin](../claims/gnam-lcags-thog-rgod-compiled-by-gsang-sngags-bstan-dzin.md). The first half stays open: «དགུ་གསུམ» occurs once in the volume's etext (the sentence of i569), is not explained, and BDRC's exact phrase «དགུ་གསུམ་ནི་ཚེས» has 0 hits; the 9th, 19th and 29th remain the page's likely sense, not its statement.

**Narrowed 2026-11-24 (sweep from inv-b note above).** The second half is answered (the manual is a modern compilation by Gnam lcags Gsang sngags bstan 'dzin, claim gnam-lcags-thog-rgod-compiled-by-gsang-sngags-bstan-dzin); the first half, «དགུ་གསུམ» as the 9th, 19th and 29th, stays the page's likely sense.
