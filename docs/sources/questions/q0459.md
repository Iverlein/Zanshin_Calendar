# How is the la-knower's animal chosen in the lower table of WB vol. 1 p. 133

In the lower table of WB vol. 1, p. 133, each time of the day names its la-knower («བླ་མཁྱེན») with an animal (tiger at midnight and at sunrise; pig for the ox, snake and bird; monkey for the horse, sheep, monkey and pig), but how is that animal chosen, and does the day's own animal or the time's animal give the la-knower of a day?
