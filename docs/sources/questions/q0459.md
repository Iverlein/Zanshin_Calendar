# How is the la-knower's animal chosen in the lower table of WB vol. 1 p. 133

In the lower table of WB vol. 1, p. 133, each time of the day names its la-knower («བླ་མཁྱེན») with an animal (tiger at midnight and at sunrise; pig for the ox, snake and bird; monkey for the horse, sheep, monkey and pig), but how is that animal chosen, and does the day's own animal or the time's animal give the la-knower of a day? (RD78)

RD182 (2026-11-06), a lead only: WB's almanac boxes (vol. 1, etext pp. 150–157) pair «བླ་མཁྱེན» with «སྟག་གཟིག», tiger and leopard, so the la-knower's «སྟག» may be that company and not a day animal; the boxes not read on the scan.

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-ggg).** BDRC's full-text search for «བླ་མཁྱེན་སྟག» (8 hits) finds the lower table's rows in other copies, in the order time, la-knower, animal: the Bhutan anthology *Dkar rtsis dang nag rtsis kyi gzhung* (MW26041, mediocre OCR) and a Bon students' textbook (a witness only, nothing built on it) give «བླ་མཁྱེན་སྟག» at the sunrise/«ཉི་དྲོས» row, «བླ་མཁྱེན་ཕག» at «ཉིན་ཕྱེད», «བླ་མཁྱེན་སྤྲེལ» at «ཕྱེད་ཡོལ» and «བླ་མཁྱེན་སྦྲུལ» at «ནམ་ལངས» (read from the search snippets in Wylie, not on a scan): the four animals are the la-knower's own and go by the time of the day, not by the day's animal, as the question's second half asked. Which of the twelve times takes which of the four is not read in full (the copies' OCR is broken). The «སྟག་གཟིག» lead of RD182 is the company of the la-knower in the almanac boxes, a second matter.

**Searched 2026-11-16 (inv-m).** «བླ་མཁྱེན་ཕག་ཉིན་ཕྱེད» and «བླ་མཁྱེན་སྦྲུལ་ནམ་ལངས» (time after la-knower) 0 hits, the order of inv-ggg being time first. The 1996 etext's OCR of the lower table (I1KG12906 etext p. 143 = printed p. 133) lists the times with «བླ་མཁྱེན» and an animal after each, in a hand-too-broken form (ཉི་ཤར «སྦྲུལ», ཉི་དྲོས «ལག», ཉི་ཕྱེད «སྤྲུལ», ཉི་མྱུར «བྲེལ», ནམ་ཕྱེད «སྟག»); the animals look like the tiger, snake and monkey set of the other copies, but the OCR is not read as a witness. Unchanged: which of the four goes with which time is not settled by text.

**Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-m, second pass).** BDRC «བླ་མཁྱེན་ཕག» 3 and «བླ་མཁྱེན་སྦྲུལ» 4 hits, new copies of the lower table: the Sgo bzhi mdzod lnga yang bcud nag rtsis (29), the Nag rtsis snang srid me long (the same table as MW2CZ8040's hand: «tho rangs … bla mkhyen sprel … nam phyed … bla mkhyen … phyed yol … bla mkhyen phag sa rgyal 'brug») and the Do shal Dpyod ldan snying nor. In them each time carries a la-knower animal and an earth-lord animal side by side («bla mkhyen sprel/ sa rgyal sbrul» at dawn; «phag … bla mkhyen / sprel phyed yol / bla mkhyen / sbrul nam langs»), so the la-knower's animal is named per time with the earth lord's, which supports inv-ggg (not the day's animal). The OCR of all three runs the rows together and does not show whether an animal follows or precedes its time, so which time takes which animal is still not settled. Open.

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (sweep from inv-ggg).** The note of 2026-11-14 (inv-ggg) above narrows the question without settling it; the state is set to match.
