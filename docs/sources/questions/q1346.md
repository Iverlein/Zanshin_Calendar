# Does the Mindroling 25th (mdb:p163-166) count the waning tenth by the almanac or only as the 25th of the month

From the 2026-11-21 practice-day readings (rd-f, rd-g).

## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-b)

The rule-book's etext (BDRC VEIE0OPID229B8DD_I2210, 739 pages) was searched for «ཚེས་ཆད», «ཚེས་ལྷག», «ཚེས་འགྲིབ», «ཟླ་ཚེས»: no rule on skipped or doubled days; «ཆད་ལྷག» only means a rite's omissions and surplus (p. 510, p. 695). The monthly list (pp. 164-166) names days only by number, and the 25th is given the clause «མར་ངོའི་ཚེས་བཅུ་ཚོགས་མཆོད» (mar ngo'i tshes bcu tshogs mchod) as its own name, so the house treats the 25th and the waning tenth as one day; the festival lists (pp. 170-175) likewise number days («ཉེར་ལྔའི་སྔ་དྲོ», «ཉེར་དགུའི་ཕྱི་དྲོ») and p. 133 picks a waxing tenth by its planet-star conjunction (a one-off date, not the monthly rule). Whether the waning tenth is the almanac's tenth or the 25th of the month's count is not said. Still open.
