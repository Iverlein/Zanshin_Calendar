# The skya of «སྐྱ་རྒྱལ» and «སྐྱ་འཕམ» (WB ch. 18)

What are the *skya* (སྐྱ) of «སྐྱ་རྒྱལ» and «སྐྱ་འཕམ»? The key on p. 209 defines the pair by the king and the minister contending (the minister against the king is *skya rgyal*, the king against the minister *skya* defeated) but not the word; the 1996 etext reads «སྐུ་རྒྱལ» (*sku rgyal*) throughout, Yigdzin-1 on the reprint «སྐྱ་རྒྱལ», and the one crop read at 4× (p. 198, l. 22) looks like «སྐྱ». Which word is meant, and who are the *skya*? (RD184, corpus/wb/ch18.md)
