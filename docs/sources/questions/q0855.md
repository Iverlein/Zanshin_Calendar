# In the Bṛhadyātrā 16.10 which part of the night and which humour go with which of the three seasons?

The page image (leaf 28 of 73) settles the order.

## Answered 2026-11-10

Answered by RD312 (2026-11-10). Bhaṭṭotpala's commentary on the Bṛhadyātrā (Aśvamedhīyayātrā, Kathmandu 1969, ch. 17 v. 11 = Pingree 16.10, pp. 179–180) states the pairs in words: the last third of the night goes with the rains and wind, the middle third with autumn and bile, the first third with spring (Mādhava) and phlegm; the other seasons only accumulate the humour. The pairing by position in the night is therefore the tenth-century commentator's own, not only ours.
