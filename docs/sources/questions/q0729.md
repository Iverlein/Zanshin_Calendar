# The figure sheets of pp. 127 and 129–132

What do the figure sheets of pp. 127 and 129–132 say (the grid of the eight mother-goddesses with numeral pairs, the star and plan figures with small writing), once read cell by cell on a sharper scan? (RD192, corpus/wb/ch13.md)

## Read again 2026-11-21 (rd-y)

Not settled; no new look. The cells of p. 129's table carry a name and two numerals and the numerals are not told apart on the 1-bit scan at the sizes the earlier readers tried; the check that could settle them (a numeral rule across the table's rows, as q1317-q1319 had in the number-words) needs a rule that the sheets do not state in text: pp. 127 and 129-132 are figures without a verse beside them, and the pool has no tradition's table for the mother-goddesses' pairs to hold them against. Left open for a commentary on the chapter's figures (the Mirror or the Blue Beryl's section on the turtle chart) or a sharper scan; no stack was re-looked at.

**Narrowed 2026-11-24 (sweep from I1KG12906 [p137], [p139], [p144]).** Searched 2026-11-24 (inv-t) in the 1996 etext of vol. 1 (I1KG12906 [p137], [p139]-[p142], printed pp. 127, 129-132): p. 127's sheet is not typed at all (the etext has only stray glyphs and the page number); p. 129's names are typed, its numerals only as a jumbled stream (see q753). The pool did have a tradition to hold p. 129's numerals against, in WB's own text: p. 134's verses (etext [p144], wb.1:p134-135) give every goddess her tithis (Tshangs ma 1, 9, 16, 24; Drag mo 2, 10, 17, 25; Gzhon nu ma 3, 11, 18, 26; Khyab 'jug ma 4, 12, 19, 27; Phag mo 5, 13, 20, 28; Dbang mo 6, 14, 21, 29; Tsa mu nda 7, 15, 22 and half the full-moon day; Dpal mo 8, 23 and the other half), so the cells' numerals on a sharper scan are held against these sets as a numeral rule. Not read: no cell was assigned a numeral, the figures of pp. 127 and 130-132 stay as in the passage wb.1:p127-132.
