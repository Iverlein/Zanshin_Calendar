# Why is the Pleiades month called the Dream month

The dictionaries gloss rmi lam gyi zla ba as smin drug zla ba (tenth Hor month; the Dus tshigs list has Smin drug twelfth); no source says why it is called dream.

## Narrowed 2026-11-13

Searched again on 2026-11-13 without result: the lexica (svo:p395d) and the full-text search give the name as a month name only, never with a reason. Dead end after two approaches.

## Narrowed again 2026-11-14 (inv-x)

Still no reason. BDRC's exact phrases «རྨི་ལམ་གྱི་ཟླ་བ» (17 hits) and «རྨི་ལམ་ཟླ་བ» (18): nearly all are the simile «the moon in a dream» (Sa skya bka' 'bum, the Tshad ma rigs gter commentaries, Pema Karpo), not the month. The month stands only in the Dus tshigs lists (Mkhan zur Ngag dbang nyi ma's collected works: «རྨི་ལམ་གྱི་ཟླ་བ དང་བཅུ་གཉིས», after «འཚོ་བའི་ཟླ་བ»; the Dus tshigs commentary *Lde mig*) and in the *Bod rgya skar rtsis rig pa'i tshig mdzod* (MW1PD137843, search-only), whose entry reads «ming gi rnam grangs … རྨི་ལམ་གྱི་ཟླ་བ སྨིན་དྲུག་ཟླ་བ་ལ … རྡོ་རྗེ་གདན་བཞིའི་རྒྱུད་སོགས་ནས་རྨི་ལམ་གྱི་ཟླ་བ་ཞེས་» (a synonym; it names the Catuṣpīṭha as the source of the name, snippet cut there). No reason is in the snippets. Steinert's dictionaries have only «rmi lam gyi zla ba» = the tenth Hor month (Ives Waldo; Tshig mdzod chen mo with «mngon»). The twelve names of the tantric list are the Tantra's own, so the question goes to the Catuṣpīṭha and its commentaries: not read.
