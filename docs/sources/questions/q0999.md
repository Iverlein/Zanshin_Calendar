# What are the last words of Item 687's marginal line «yāme caturthe māsaikaṃ …» (Penn Ms. Coll. first leaf), and is the month a corrector's own wording or taken from another manuscript?

RD357.

Narrowed (inv-e, 2026-11-13): the marginal line at the head of Item 687's first text page (Colenda canvas 1, read at 400 px per crop, the line at the right margin) reads «यामे चतुर्थे मासैकं …रतो दश(?)पूर्वकं ॥ २ ॥»: after मासैकं come two or three syllables (a ma/bha with a ra, then ‹to›) that do not make a word on the image, then a syllable like द, ष or ध and «पूर्वकं». Nothing settles it; the verse number 2 matches the line it repairs (the verse of the spans), and the span written, a month, is that of the other Penn copies (Item 1871 «मासैकेन»), which speaks for a text copied from a copy of the usual kind, not for a wording of the corrector's own; this is an inference, not a reading.
