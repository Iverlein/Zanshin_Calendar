# Which mewa fights the nine squares of the three mewa joined?

Which mewa fights the nine squares in the three mewa joined (WB vol. 2, p. 165)? WB names «དུས་ལོའི་སྨེ་བ», the mewa of the moment's year, as the one reckoned; Mipham's notes (p. 536) say «དུས་ཀྱི་དབང་སྨེས་སོ་སོར་འཐབ», the moment's power-mewa, and their example reads «དུས་སྨེ་ལྔ་སེར་གྱི་མར་བཞི་བརྒྱད་དཀར», with 8 white fighting the centre's 4 green. Is the 8 white the year's mewa, and what is the 5 yellow? Built (ROADMAP R2z, SPEC §5.9.5) with the year's mewa fighting and the day's mewa of the sixty-day count in the centre, which the notes' «སྨེ་ཕྲེང་ལོའི་རྗེས་སུ་འབྲང་བའི་བརྩི་ཉིན» reads as; a reader of the tradition may settle both.

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-fff, from RD145, [reckonings.md](../reckonings.md), *The fighter of the three mewa*; claim moment-power-mewa-four-down).** The notes' example makes the fighter the moment's power-mewa, four down from the 5 yellow (8 white), fighting the centre's 4 green as a friend, the east's 2 black as a son and the north-east's 7 red as an enemy; the centre is the day's mewa of reckoning (the notes' «བརྩི་ཉིན་ཉི་མའི་དབང་ཐང་གི་སྨེ་བ», as WB's «བརྩི་ཉིན་ཉི་མའི་སྨེ་བ»). The 5 yellow is the notes' «དུས་སྨེ», and the same section's «དུས་སྨེ་གང་ཤར» is the mewa of the hour, nine rising in the day; the example does not name a year, so it cannot be tied to the moment's year. Still open whether WB's «དུས་ལོའི་སྨེ་བ» is that mewa itself or its four-down.
