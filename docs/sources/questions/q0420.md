# Which mewa fights the nine squares of the three mewa joined?

Which mewa fights the nine squares in the three mewa joined (WB vol. 2, p. 165)? WB names «དུས་ལོའི་སྨེ་བ», the mewa of the moment's year, as the one reckoned; Mipham's notes (p. 536) say «དུས་ཀྱི་དབང་སྨེས་སོ་སོར་འཐབ», the moment's power-mewa, and their example reads «དུས་སྨེ་ལྔ་སེར་གྱི་མར་བཞི་བརྒྱད་དཀར», with 8 white fighting the centre's 4 green. Is the 8 white the year's mewa, and what is the 5 yellow? Built (ROADMAP R2z, SPEC §5.9.5) with the year's mewa fighting and the day's mewa of the sixty-day count in the centre, which the notes' «སྨེ་ཕྲེང་ལོའི་རྗེས་སུ་འབྲང་བའི་བརྩི་ཉིན» reads as; a reader of the tradition may settle both.
