# Which of the first works of MW1KG4326 (Kun dga' rin chen, vol. 2: the Arapacana sādhana pp. 9-22 or the Mol gtam pp. 23-38) holds the last-watch passage at etext character 14273 (q1070)?

Raised by the 2026-11-13 investigation s.

## Answered 2026-11-13

Neither. BDRC's etext of MW1KG4326 is cut into one etext volume per work, in page order, and the hit sits in the ninth, VE1KG4326_009. Exact-phrase searches (bdrc_search.py, 2026-11-13) on the work titles return their title at character -1000, the start of a segment: «'jam dpal a ra pa tsa na'i bsgrub pa byed tshul» in VE1KG4326_001, «mol gtam yid bzhin nor bu» in _002, «rab gnas don gsal gyi gsal byed» in _003, «rdo rje phur pa'i stod las byang chub sgrub pa'i dkyil 'khor gyi cho ga» in _008, «rdo rje phur pa'i dbang gi mtshams sbyor blo gsal dga' ba skyed byed» in _009. So the segments follow the catalogue's parts in page order, and _009 is the Vajrakīla (Phur pa) empowerment junction rite (dbang gi mtshams sbyor, 'the joining of the empowerments'), printed pp. 427-484 (the part's own statement «29 ff. (pp. 419-476)»; BDRC part MW1KG4326_EEFCFC). Character 14273 is some 14,000 characters into it. The segments _004 to _007 were not each tested; the correspondence rests on the five titles above. The earlier guess of the Arapacana sādhana or the Mol gtam (q1070, inv-s) is wrong. The passage is an initiation passage: the same sentence stands in the Rgyud sde kun btus (MW2PD14777, Rdzong sar print; and a Glog klad print), «dbang bskur ba'i dus dpa' bo mkha' 'gro rnams kyis rgyud byin gyis brlabs nas lung ston par mdzad pas», 'at the time of empowerment the heroes and dakinis bless the tantra and give prophecy', after «rmi lam zhes pa mi bden pa'i dper 'dzin yang».
