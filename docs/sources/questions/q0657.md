# The hand-written labels of the Lto-'phye woodcut on the Zhol print

On the Zhol print's woodcut of Lto-'phye (WB vol. 2 p. 280, I1KG1710 img. 1020) what do the hand-written labels on the four edges and the circles on the figure's body say? The east edge reads tentatively «ཤར» (boxed), «འབྲིང», «སྟོན»; the west edge and the top caption are unresolved at 3.5×. A scan at a higher resolution than 3759 px wide, or a second print's woodcut, is wanted. (RD170)

RD183 (2026-11-06): a second print of the Lto-'phye woodcut exists at 8280 px: Taikhang vol. 2 (I5878) img. 402, folio 392 (cache has img. 396–406 at 1600 px). Its labels and circles could not be read by eye; the Zhol print at 3.5× was no better. A GPU reader or agy on single crops of img. 402 is the next step.

RD183b (2026-11-06), narrowed: the Taikhang print (I5878 img. 402, 8280 px) is bilevel with strokes a few pixels wide. The right edge carries four words, read from the top of the figure down; the last, in a box, is «ཤར» (east), the one before it «འབྲིང» (middle), and the first begins with the stack RD170 read as «སྟོན» («སྟོད», upper): the east edge is divided into upper, middle and lower, the direction in a box at its end. The left edge (only the positions of four words), the top caption and the eight circles are not readable. One agy call on the right-edge strip gave no answer in 11 minutes. A grey or colour scan, or the Zhol original above 3759 px, is wanted.
