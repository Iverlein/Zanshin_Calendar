# «spo» and «spo smad»: the Phalgunī mansions

WB vol. 2 carves the same kind of stack, ས over a square-headed letter with the vowel o, for the twelfth mansion of p. 65 (where U. Phalgunī, དབོ, dbo, stands in the order; the reprint «སྤྲོ») and for the dragon's mansion of p. 122 («སྤོ»), and on the Zhol blocks the snake's mansion of p. 122 is that stack with «སྨད», "lower" («སྤོ་སྨད», where the 1996 print has «སྦ་སྨད»): is «spo» (or «spro») a name of the Phalgunī mansions, «spo smad» the lower of them, and which of the two does each line mean?

## Answered 2026-11-14

«སྤོ» is a spelling of «དབོ» (dbo, also «སྦོ» sbo), Uttaraphalgunī, as the dictionaries already recorded in claims/spo-is-uttaraphalguni.md have it (84000 no. 43 and its Sanskrit list no. 46: spo = uttaraphalgunī; the synonym list no. 45 under dbo: {me bzhi}, {spo}; Mahāvyutpatti no. 21 and the Sgra bye brag tu rtogs byed chen mo: dbo = uttaraphalgunī; Ives Waldo and the Tshig mdzod chen mo: dbo, also sbo, one of the twenty-eight, with the names khra, nyi ma'i lha ldan, phyi mo). Pūrvaphalgunī is another word, «གྲེ» (gre), in the same lists. Which line means which: the dragon's «སྤོ» on p. 122 is sbo (the digest of the notes printed p. 514, read in the pool, has «འབྲུག སྦོ་ལ»), the snake's is «སྦོ་སྨད» (sbo smad), "lower sbo", the same digest's «སྦྲུལ སྦོ་སྨད་ལ»; the horse year stands on «མཆུ་འམ་གྲེར» (mchu or gre), so the pair of Phalgunī is not the dragon and snake pair. «སྤོ་སྨད» is in none of the sixty-four dictionaries as a mansion (the only headword «spo smad» is a county in Tibet today); Pūrvaphalgunī is gre, which no dictionary calls a lower sbo. Which mansion «sbo smad» is remains with q296.
