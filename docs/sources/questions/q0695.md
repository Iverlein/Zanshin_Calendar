# «དོ་རའི་གར་མཁན» and «འདྲེན་བྱེད་གཡོས» in p. 22's verse

p. 22, the verse «བསྟན་རྩིས་ཤོག་བུའི་དོ་རའི་གར་མཁན་འདིར། །འདྲེན་བྱེད་གཡོས་ལ་དཔྱད་གསུམ་དག་པར་འཚལ།»: the sense of «དོ་རའི་གར་མཁན» (a dancer on the "corner" of the calendar leaf?) and of «འདྲེན་བྱེད་གཡོས» is not clear. What do they mean? (RD187, corpus/wb/ch03.md)

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-ppp)

The words are in the dictionaries; the sense of the phrase is still the poet's figure. «དོ་ར» (do ra) is "a dancing ground, a stage" (Tshig mdzod chen mo, Tshig gter, Ives Waldo, Rangjung Yeshe), «གར་མཁན» (gar mkhan) "dancer, actor"; so «བསྟན་རྩིས་ཤོག་བུའི་དོ་རའི་གར་མཁན» is "the dancer on the stage of the leaf (sheet) of the doctrine's chronology" (bstan rtsis: "the reckoning that tells in which years the holders of the doctrine appeared", Tshig mdzod chen mo): the author speaks of himself as a performer on the leaf that carries the chronology of the teachings, which is the matter of the chapter (the Buddha's years). «འདྲེན་བྱེད» ('dren byed) is "the leader, he who leads" (Tshig mdzod chen mo), in the mngon-brjod language "the eye", and one of the names of the horse; «གཡོས» (g.yos) from g.yo ba "to move": «འདྲེན་བྱེད་གཡོས» is then "the leader's movement" (RD187's rendering stands) or, if 'dren byed is the horse (the draught-horse that leads), the stirring of the horse; the dictionaries give no phrase «'dren byed g.yos». The bracketed gloss of the print, «གཟུགས་ཅན་འཕར་མ་དང་འབྲེལ་བའི་དངོས་པོ་ངེས་བསྟན» ("the matter bound with the addition by form is shown with certainty"), points to a chronological correction (the addition, འཕར་མ), so the "leader's movement" would be the stepping on of the reckoning that the three examinations (དཔྱད་གསུམ) are asked to purify. This is a reading of the figure, not a source; a BDRC phrase search «དོ་རའི་གར་མཁན་འདིར» and «འདྲེན་བྱེད་གཡོས་ལ་དཔྱད་གསུམ» finds only WB's own prints (four prints of the work: BDRC I1KG12906, I3CN5028, I4CZ74285, I1KG81102), no gloss.
