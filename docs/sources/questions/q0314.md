# The grave days' two accounts

The grave-day strike (p. 88) gives the 16th to the middle months and the 26th to the last, but the second account gives the middle months' 26th to the earth grave and the last months' 16th to the mountain grave: are these two traditions, and which does WB follow?

## Answered 2026-11-01

They are two traditions, both in the manuals: the last months' 26th is WB's first account and LT's; the last months' 16th and the middle months' 26th are WB's second account, Mipham's note on the four graves and UT's list of the grave executioners. WB records both; the notes go with the second for the last months (claim dead-grave-days-two-traditions).
