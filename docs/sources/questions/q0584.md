# «སྣང་པར་བཤད» or «སྣད་པར་བཤད» on p. 391

P. 391's «རྩུབ་བདུན་འདི་ནི་སྣང་པར་བཤད» (1996 print) is «སྣད་པར་བཤད» on the Zhol print by both machines (img. 513, l. 2) and the letter after སྣ is not told between ང and ད on the cursive block: which does a reader of the tradition or another impression of the blocks (the Taikhang reproduction, I5877, near img. 530) carry, and in either case is the phrase "told as they appear"?

**Narrowed 2026-11-03 (sweep from RD134).** Taikhang (I5877 img. 530) by Yigdzin-1 reads «སྣང་བར་བཤད», with the 1996 print and against the Zhol machines' «སྣད་པར»; ང or ད is not told by eye (RD134, 2026-11-03).
