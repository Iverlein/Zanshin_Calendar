# Zhe chen rgyal tshab settles the monkey month as the fifth Hor month for followers of the Phugpa: who holds the sixth Hor month, which 'Jigs med gling pa's prayer implies for the birth, and by which count?

Raised by the practice-days investigation (pd-f, 2026-11-16); see the cited passages.

## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-w)

Which Hor month holds the sixth, and the prayer's implied count: not found in BDRC phrase searches in this pass; stays open.

**Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-d).** New route: BDRC phrases «ཧོར་ཟླ་དྲུག་པའི་ཚེས་བཅུ» (192 hits) and «ཧོར་ཟླ་དྲུག་པའི་ཚེས་བཅུའི་ཉི་ཤར» (12). The twelve are all 'Jigs med gling pa's prayer itself, copied in his Collected Works (MW1KG10193), the Bsam yas liturgy (MW3CN5249) and the Klong chen snying thig 'don cha (MW00EGS1016972): no gloss on which month the sixth is. Among the 192 are copying colophons of treasure texts dated «hor zla drug pa'i tshes bcu» (Rin chen gter mdzod, MW1KG14 vol. 14: «shing stag hor zla drug pa'i tshes bcu la phab pa'o», at the Bde chen 'od 'bar lha khang), which use the phrase for the Guru's birth tenth in the Nyingma way; and the Fifth Dalai Lama's letters (MW1PD107937) use «hor zla drug pa» in the state's own count for other dates, which is not shown to be the monkey month. No text found that says who holds the sixth Hor month for the prayer. Stays open.
