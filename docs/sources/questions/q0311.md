# «འཕུང་སྲི་ཛ་མོ» or «རྫ་མོ» in the sounding

The ninth place of the sounding (p. 91) reads «འཕུང་སྲི་ཛ་མོ» on the reprint and «རྫ་མོ» in the etext: which is WB's, and what being is it?

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (scan-d).** Which spelling is WB's: «ཛ་མོ». On the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5029 img. 097 = p. 91, line 8, 4× crop) the line «དགུ་ནག་འཕུང་སྲི་ཛ་མོ་ཡིས། །རྒྱལ་པོའི་དབུ་ཕོང་ཁ་སྨྲས་འོང་།» carries ཛ as a single letter whose head lies on the line's headline like its neighbours, with no ར above it; the etext's «རྫ་མོ» is a misspelling of the machine, not a variant of the print. The Zhol print (I1KG1710, about img. 765) is cursive and too small to read at this point. What being «འཕུང་སྲི་ཛ་མོ» is stays open: the line only says that the ninth black pebble means the king's head empty and quarrels coming.
