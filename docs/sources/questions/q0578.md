# p. 381's «ངོ་ཐོག» or «ངོ་ཐེག» on the Zhol blocks

P. 381's «ངོ་ཐོག» or «ངོ་ཐེག» (Zhol img. 499, l. 2): the blocks' vowel sign over ཐ is not told at 4× and Yigdzin-1 reads ོ; does another impression of the blocks, or a reader of the tradition, carry «ཐོག», the top, or «ཐེག»? See year-of-life.md, RD126.

RD175 (2026-11-06), narrowed: Yigdzin-1 reads «ཐོག» on the Zhol (img. 499) and the Taikhang (img. 516 line 1); at 14× on the Zhol the sign over ཐ is a short stroke with a small head, nearer ེ than ོ, but the ink does not allow a verdict.
