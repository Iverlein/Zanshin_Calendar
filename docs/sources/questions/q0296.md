# The mansions «སྦ་སྨད», «ལག་སོར» and «སྤོ» on p. 122

In WB vol. 2, p. 122, which mansions are «སྦ་སྨད» (the snake year), «ལག་སོར» (the monkey and bird years) and «སྤོ» (the dragon year)?

**Narrowed 2026-11-05 (RD161, [word-hunts.md](../word-hunts.md)).** Sbo and sbo smad spelled out, lag sor = lha mtshams has a second witness; which mansion sbo smad is stays open as its own question.

## Answered 2026-11-14

«སྤོ» is «སྦོ» (sbo, also «དབོ» dbo), Uttaraphalgunī, as q380 settled from the 84000 lists, Mahāvyutpatti and the dictionaries (claims/spo-is-uttaraphalguni.md). «ལག་སོར» (lag sor) is the mansion lha mtshams (Anurādhā): the Great Tibetan Dictionary «རྒྱུ་སྐར་ལྷ་མཚམས», Ives Waldo and Valby give it, the Bhutan collection's list of mansions with second names has «བཅུ་དྲུག་ལྷ་མཚམས་ལག་སོར» (number 16), and the monkey-year line of p. 122 stands at «ལག་སོར» in WB. «སྦ་སྨད» of the 1996 print is «སྦོ་སྨད» (sbo smad), as the Zhol blocks carve it and the digest of the notes (printed p. 514) spells it: «སྦྲུལ སྦོ་སྨད་ལ», the snake year on "lower sbo". Which mansion that is stays with q634: no list of second names has it, and the one passage that names «སྦོ་སྨད» (the 1996 etext, p. 197) uses it of the lower belly.

Searched 2026-11-14 (inv-lll): Steinert's 64 dictionaries, headwords «sbo smad» and «spo smad» (only a county in Tibet); nothing new.
