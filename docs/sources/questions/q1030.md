# Is the list of figures after «the marks of a true dream» (the twelve houses, elephant, god, monkey, asura, siddha, gandharva, yakṣa; mmk.1:p403-404, mmk.2:p152-153) the content of the fourth watch's dream, or a separate statement; and what is the Tibetan «སྡིག་པ» for, where the zodiac has Scorpio?

RD370.

## Answered 2026-11-10

RD372: the list of figures after «the marks of a true dream» is the Sanskrit rāśi, the classes of beings with the twelve zodiac signs in front; it is not the fourth watch's dream (the Sanskrit goes straight on with karma-guṇa lines; the «lakṣaṇā … svapnānāṃ satyadarśanā» line closes the description of the persons by humour). The Sanskrit's rāśi lists in ch. 1 and ch. 18 give vṛścika where the Tibetan has «སྡིག་པ»; the dream chapter's eighth word is unread in the OCR (doubt kept; new question).
