# Does the 栞草's section け hold an entry 鶏旦, and does it name the 紀事 or any other source for the dawn and the night of the 初夢?

Raised by the 2026-11-13 investigation c; see the question's cited passages.

## Answered 2026-11-14

Yes. The section け of the 歳時記栞草 holds the entry 〔正月〕雞旦 (けいたん): from the 東方朔占書, "one day is the cock", whence New Year's Day is called 雞旦, as the seventh is the 人日 (mdz:i87533240, the leaf numbered 六十七 of the spring volume in the Meiji reprint of 1892, NDL pid 875332 image 40; the same in pid 875338). It names neither the 紀事 nor any other source for the dream or its night; the 初夢 entry's pointer to it (via 初鶏, which cites 御傘 for "the crowing of the cock on New Year's morning") only glosses the word 鶏旦 as New Year's Day.
