# In vol. 30 i139, what does «བཅུ་གསུམ་རང་ལོ་སྐག་ཐོག་ལ» mean (the age-thirteen term with «སྐག»)? Is it a technical year or term, or a scribal mistake?

RD363.

## Answered 2026-11-13

«སྐག» (skag) is a technical term, the obstacle of a bad year, and «རང་ལོ» (rang lo) is the own year: the term is not a scribal mistake. The same etext has «ཉེར་ལྔའི་སྐག» and «ལོ་སྐག» (i193-194) and «རྩུབ་སྐག» (i505), which are the «ཀེག» and «རྩུབ་པ» of the year-of-age reckoning of Klong chen Chos dbyings stobs ldan rdo rje's *Mdo rgyud rin po che'i mdzod* (year-of-life.md): «རང་ལོ་གནམ་ལོར་ཤར་ན་རང་ཀེག་རྩུབ་པ». Thirteen and twenty-five are the first two returns of the own birth-animal year, which is why both ages stand with it; that count is my inference. In full: claim tsl-skag-is-the-obstacle-year. Read on the etext and, for the phrase of i139, on a low-resolution scan; not on a sharp one.
