# «ཐེག» in «ངོ་ཐེག» (WB vol. 1, p. 381)

No dictionary or text gives «ཐེག» in «ངོ་ཐེག»; is it the counting of a place by its pebbles, as «ངོ» is the face or place counted?

RD174 (2026-11-06), narrowed: «ངོ་ཐོག» is a standing term for the present or 'at hand' in KD vols. 1–3 («ངོ་ཐོག་གི་ལོ» the present year, «ངོ་ཐོག་ཞག» the day at hand); «ངོ་ཐེག» occurs nowhere but WB's 1996 print, so ཐོག is most likely meant. Not glossed for pebbles.

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (scan-b).** The Gser thang reprint (I3CN5028, img. 376, WB p. 381, line 3, crop at 7×) is typeset and its vowel signs are not in doubt: «…མི་འདྲེན་པར། །ངོ་ཐེག་དཀར་ནག་བགྲངས་པ་ཡི།». The first syllable carries the two-part sign of «ོ», the second a single curved cap, «ེ»: the reprint reads «ཐེག» as the 1996 print does, so «ངོ་ཐེག» is not a slip of the 1996 etext alone. KD's «ངོ་ཐོག» (RD174) is a different phrase on its evidence; here two prints read ཐེག and none has been read ཐོག on the scan. The blocks (Zhol, Taikhang) stay undecided, see question 578.

## Answered 2026-11-16

inv-o, 2026-11-16, from BDRC full-text search and the etexts of the parallel manuals; nothing from memory.

**«ངོ་ཐེག» is the standing phrase «ངོ་ཐོག», "at hand, in the present", and the other copies of the verse read ཐོག.** The line «ངོ་ཐེག་དཀར་ནག་བགྲངས་པ་ཡི» of WB (1996 print and Gser thang reprint, both read ཐེག on the scan, see the narrowing above) is the same verse that the manuals on WB's rule carry, and they spell it with ོ:

- *Nag rtsis rdel 'grem 'bras bshad* by Dge bshes Lha mo tshul khrims (MW1KG21818; search-only, snippet): «de ltar gcod 'dral nyer bzhi yi / srog lus dbang rlung 'phreng bzhi bor / ngo* thog dkar nag bgrang ba las / gang mang de bor kha dmar bzhi» (the star is in the etext).
- *Bzo rtsis Zlos gar* vol. 2 (MW1PD95727, search-only, snippet): the same four lines, «ngo thog dkar nag bgrang ba las».
- *Do shal* (MW2PD17386_D19206, search-only, snippet), quoting WB's verse: «gang mang kha dmar rdel bor ba / gsum gnyis la sogs ma 'dren par / ngo thog dkar nag bgrangs pa yi»; the other Do shal etext (MW1KG1617_A9DE66, poor OCR) has «ngo shog», also with ོ.
- The 1996 etext and the Gser thang etext (MW2CZ8040, MW3CN5026) have «ngo theg», as the scans do.

- The Dge ldan commentary *Rgya rtsis dge ldan mkhas pa'i phyag rgyun du bstar ba'i rdel 'grem dpag bsam ljon shing* (snippet): «srog lus dbang klung 'phreng bzhi por / ngo thog dkar nag bgrang ba las / gang mang de bor kha dmar bzhi».

BDRC phrase search «ངོ་ཐོག་དཀར་ནག» 3 hits, the three commentaries above (Lha mo tshul khrims' *Nag rtsis rdel 'grem*, the *Dpag bsam ljon shing*, the *Zlos gar*); «དཀར་ནག་བགྲངས་པ་ཡི» 4 hits, all WB's own prints or the Do shal.

The sense follows from the lines: of the white and black pebbles counted «ངོ་ཐོག» (at hand, as they lie), the side with more is cast as the verdict (ཁ་དམར, kha dmar) after the 24 pebbles of life, body, power and luck. It is not a counting by pebbles of a place: the question's guess is not supported. That WB's two prints, against every parallel copy, read ཐེག is a spelling of the one phrase (the vowel sign) and is not a second word; whether the blocks (Zhol, Taikhang) read ཐོག stays with q578.
