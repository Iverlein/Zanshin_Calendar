# «ཐེག» in «ངོ་ཐེག» (WB vol. 1, p. 381)

No dictionary or text gives «ཐེག» in «ངོ་ཐེག»; is it the counting of a place by its pebbles, as «ངོ» is the face or place counted?

RD174 (2026-11-06), narrowed: «ངོ་ཐོག» is a standing term for the present or 'at hand' in KD vols. 1–3 («ངོ་ཐོག་གི་ལོ» the present year, «ངོ་ཐོག་ཞག» the day at hand); «ངོ་ཐེག» occurs nowhere but WB's 1996 print, so ཐོག is most likely meant. Not glossed for pebbles.

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (scan-b).** The Gser thang reprint (I3CN5028, img. 376, WB p. 381, line 3, crop at 7×) is typeset and its vowel signs are not in doubt: «…མི་འདྲེན་པར། །ངོ་ཐེག་དཀར་ནག་བགྲངས་པ་ཡི།». The first syllable carries the two-part sign of «ོ», the second a single curved cap, «ེ»: the reprint reads «ཐེག» as the 1996 print does, so «ངོ་ཐེག» is not a slip of the 1996 etext alone. KD's «ངོ་ཐོག» (RD174) is a different phrase on its evidence; here two prints read ཐེག and none has been read ཐོག on the scan. The blocks (Zhol, Taikhang) stay undecided, see question 578.
