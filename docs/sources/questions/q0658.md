# Which text is the Mūlatantra of the astrological manuals

Which text is 'the Mūlatantra' that the astrological manuals (WB p. 450; the Mirror of Mañjughoṣa's Mind, MW2KG5015) quote for the verse «རང་གིས་གོ་ཆ་མ་བརྩམས་ན…» and for «ལི་ཁམ་མཐའ་མནན་ཕོ་ལ་རྩུབ»: a Kālacakra, a Mañjuśrī or an astrological root text, and is it one or two? (RD168)

INV-1107 (2026-11-07), narrowed: BDRC's exact search finds the four lines «ལི་ཁམ་མཐའ་མནན་ཕོ་ལ་རྩུབ…» in WB itself (vol. 1, Gser thang etext I3CN5028, p. 392, introduced «བདུད་ཀྱི་སྒོ་མོ་རྣམ་བཞི་ཞེས», 'the four doors of the demon, so it says', no text named) and in the Mirror (MW3CN2232, etext p. 293, under «ཡང་གཅིག་བདུད་ཀྱི་སྒོ་མོ་ཆེན་པོ་བཞི» sent to «མུ་ལ་ཏནྟྲ»): for this verse the 'Mūlatantra' is the source of a verse on the four demon doors, not a Kālacakra or Mañjuśrī text shown by any line. The other verse (རང་གིས་གོ་ཆ…) is in neither, so whether it is one text or two is not shown; the other hits of «མཱུ་ལ་ཏནྟྲ་ལས» are Nyingma rites. Claim [trigram-verse-demon-doors-mulatantra](../claims/trigram-verse-demon-doors-mulatantra.md). Still open.

INV-1114 (2026-11-14), narrowed: the premise is corrected. WB p. 450 names the Mūlatantra only for «རང་གིས་གོ་ཆ་མ་བརྩམས་ན…», a warning inside its rites against earth lords (arm yourself before the rite or it strikes back at you); the demon-door trigram verse is the Mirror's quotation, and WB gives it (vol. 1) with «ཞེས» and no text. The armour verse is found in no text but WB (two etexts) and its Zhol-block do shal (BDRC exact search «གོ་ཆ་མ་བརྩམས་ན»: 3 hits), and «མུ་ལ་ཏནྟྲ་ལས» occurs once in an astrological etext (the Mirror). So the two quotations are not shown to be one text, and neither is shown to be a Kālacakra or Mañjuśrī text; the name is the Sanskrit for 'root tantra'. Claim [trigram-verse-demon-doors-mulatantra](../claims/trigram-verse-demon-doors-mulatantra.md). Still open.
