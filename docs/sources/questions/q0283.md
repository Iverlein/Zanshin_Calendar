# «ཤི་ལོའི་སྤར་སྨེ»: the year's own, or the dead's descended

Mipham's note (WB vol. 2, p. 503) names the trigram and mewa of the third examination as those of the year of death («ཤི་ལོའི་སྤར་སྨེ»). Is that the year's own trigram and mewa, as the almanac gives them, or the dead person's descended trigram and mewa in the year of death (the བབ་སྤར, bab spar, of the first examination's point 5)?

**Narrowed 2026-10-23 (RD45, [claims/dead-trigram-descended.md](../claims/dead-trigram-descended.md)).** UT matches the dead's life force with the descended trigram (ut:p69-70, etext), which leans to the dead's descended trigram and mewa; the *Moonbeams*' eleven roots leave it open (question 372).
