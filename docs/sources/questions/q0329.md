# Whether a black small foot needs year, month and hour together

WB vol. 2, pp. 38–39: does a black small foot apply only when the year, month and hour (and link) meet together, or by any one of them alone?

## Read further 2026-11-01

RD99: dead end; no text found says whether one of year, month and hour suffices (the term occurs only in WB; the notes call the twelve clear from the text).

**Searched again 2026-11-16 (inv-l), unchanged.** BDRC «རྐང་ཕྲན་ནག་པ» 2 hits and «རྐང་ཕྲན་ནག» 3, all WB's two prints and the *Do shal* copy (search-only; its snippet is the verse «ད་ནི་རྐང་ཕྲན་ནག་པ་ཡི། །བསྲུང་བར་བྱ་བ་བཅུ་གཉིས་བཤད། །ལོ་བྱི་ཟླ་བ་དཔྱིད་ཟླ་འབྲིང༌། །དུས་ཚོད་ནམ་ལངས་མ་རིག་པ། ༎», no gloss); «རྐང་ཕྲན་ནག་པོ», «རྐང་ཕྲན་བཅུ་གཉིས», «རྐང་ཆུང་ནག་པོ», «ཞབས་ཕྲན་ནག་པ» 0. The verse itself lists year, month, time and link together for each guard.

## Answered 2026-11-16

A dead end, stated as final by the readings: no text found says whether one of the year, month and hour suffices for a black small foot (RD99, 2026-11-01: «the term occurs only in WB; the notes call the twelve clear from the text»), and the search of 2026-11-16 (inv-l) found «རྐང་ཕྲན་ནག་པ» only in WB's own prints and the *Do shal* copy, with no gloss. The verse itself lists year, month, time and link together for each guard, so the pool has no rule that lets one alone decide; ROADMAP RD165 (2026-11-06) closes it as a dead end.
