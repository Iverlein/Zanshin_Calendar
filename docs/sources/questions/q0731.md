# Nagarjuna's birth year on p. 23: 2167 or the first digit

p. 23, line 12, the year of Nagarjuna's birth: the first and last glyphs of the bracketed figure «༢༡༦༧» look alike on the reprint (a looped form, as the ༧ of 2647), while the ༢ of «༢༥༢༣» in line 10 is the plain form; the chain 2523 − 356 gives 2167 and the machines read 2167. Does the print's first digit read ༢, or is the figure «༧༡༦༧» as the machine text of RD187 first had it? (RD197, corpus/wb/ch03.md)

## Answered 2026-11-13

RD197c (2026-11-08) and RD213x (2026-11-12), by eye on the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5028 img. 30, 4.5x to 8x) and on the 1996 Beijing print (I1KG12906 img. 33, 3x and 7x): the first glyph of the bracketed figure of Nagarjuna's year, p. 23 line 12, is a looped form, like the last glyph of the same figure and the ༧ of «(༡༠༦༧)» two lines below, not the plain ༢ of «(༢༥༢༣)» in line 10; so the print reads «(༧༡༦༧)», in both prints. The slip is in the typesetting the two prints share. The chain (2523 less 356) and the machine texts give 2167, and 7167 fits no year of the reckoning, so the first digit is a misprint of ༢ and the text keeps ༢༡༦༧; the English does not use the printed figure.
