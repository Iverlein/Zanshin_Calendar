# Nagarjuna's birth year on p. 23: 2167 or the first digit

p. 23, line 12, the year of Nagarjuna's birth: the first and last glyphs of the bracketed figure «༢༡༦༧» look alike on the reprint (a looped form, as the ༧ of 2647), while the ༢ of «༢༥༢༣» in line 10 is the plain form; the chain 2523 − 356 gives 2167 and the machines read 2167. Does the print's first digit read ༢, or is the figure «༧༡༦༧» as the machine text of RD187 first had it? (RD197, corpus/wb/ch03.md)
