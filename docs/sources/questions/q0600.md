# «སྤར་ཁ» in reply 157 and «ལོ་ལ» or «ཟླ་ལ»

On the Taikhang print of the Vaiḍūrya replies, is the first letter of «སྤར་ཁ» in reply 157 (I5876 img. 17, l. 3) a pa or a ba subjoined with ra, and is «ལོ་ལ» there the year or a misreading of «ཟླ་ལ»?

RD166 (2026-11-06): second part answered: «ལོ་ལ» is the year, by the sentence and the etext. The first letter of «སྤར་ཁ» in reply 157 (I5876 img. 017, l. 3), pa or ba, is not told in the running hand; a second print of the replies is needed.

## Answered 2026-11-14

The first letter is pa: «སྤར་ཁ». The word is spelled so in every dictionary that has it (the Tshig mdzod chen mo, the Tshig gter rgya mtsho, Rangjung Yeshe, Ives Waldo, Jim Valby, the Tib Term Project: spar kha, the eight trigrams), and none has «སྦར་ཁ» (sbar kha) as a headword; the machine's ba is its misreading. A second print answers as well: BDRC's phrase search «ལོ་ལ་སྤར་ཁ་ལི་ཡིན» and «སྟག་རྟ་ཁྱི་གསུམ་ལོ་ལ» find, besides the Taikhang etext of the replies (MW8226, I5876), the etext of the Dpe rnying kun btus copy of the same replies (MW2PD17386, I1KG81103, char. 694926), whose snippet has «stag rta khyi gsum lo la spar kha li yin pas zla ba yang li», the Vaiḍūrya line as the reply quotes it, with «lo la» (the year) and spar with pa. The first part was answered earlier (RD166): «ལོ་ལ» is the year. The scan's own letter at img. 017, l. 3 is still not told by eye; the answer is from the dictionaries and the second print's etext.
