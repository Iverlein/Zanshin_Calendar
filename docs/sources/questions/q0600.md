# «སྤར་ཁ» in reply 157 and «ལོ་ལ» or «ཟླ་ལ»

On the Taikhang print of the Vaiḍūrya replies, is the first letter of «སྤར་ཁ» in reply 157 (I5876 img. 17, l. 3) a pa or a ba subjoined with ra, and is «ལོ་ལ» there the year or a misreading of «ཟླ་ལ»?

RD166 (2026-11-06): second part answered: «ལོ་ལ» is the year, by the sentence and the etext. The first letter of «སྤར་ཁ» in reply 157 (I5876 img. 017, l. 3), pa or ba, is not told in the running hand; a second print of the replies is needed.
