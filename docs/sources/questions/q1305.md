# WB vol. 1 p. 77 line 8: the figure after «མཚམས་མིག», 26 or 24?

On p. 77 line 8 the figure after «མཚམས་མིག» (boundary, eye) is printed (༢༦), 26, but the code words eye 2 and boundary 4 give 24: is the boundary's number 6 here, or is the second glyph a 4?

## Answered 2026-11-21

The figure is 26, as printed. The print's (༢༦) after «མཚམས་མིག» stands, and the doubt came from counting «མཚམས» as 4. The word-numerals are read from the units up, as everywhere in ch. 10: «མཚམས» (boundary) is 6 and «མིག» (eye) 2, so the string is 6 then 2, written 26. «མཚམས» is six in the *Tshig mdzod chen mo* (Steinert's public set), for the six seasons, as is «དུས» (q0690, narrowed further 2026-11-14); the same word gives «ཐིག་མཚམས» 60 (month-entries) and, in «དུས་མདའ་མཚམས་དབང», the third digit of 5656 on p. 74 (q1318). Read again 2026-11-21 (rd-s): the ༦ of (༢༦) is the glyph the scan prints; no reading of 24 is supported by any check.
