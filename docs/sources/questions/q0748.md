# Does WB's «ས་རོད་ཡའི་རྒྱུད» (the Svarodaya) in the ch. 34 colophon name the Tanjur treatise on life-reckoning results (Derge, «སྭ་རོད་ཡ») or a tantra of that name, and which of ch. 34's kinds of works come from it?

## Answered 2026-11-14

INV-1114 (2026-11-14), answered in its first half: «ས་རོད་ཡའི་རྒྱུད» is the tantra, not the life-reckoning treatise. The Derge Tanjur (BDRC MW23703_4322, etext VE23703_1521, pp. 100-186) holds a work titled «གཡུལ་ལས་རྣམ་པར་རྒྱལ་བ་ཞེས་པའི་རྒྱུད་ཀྱི་རྒྱལ་པོ་དབྱངས་འཆར་བ་ཞེས་བྱ་བ» ('the king of tantras called Dbyangs 'char, "Victory in Battle"'), whose Sanskrit title ends «སྦ་རོ་ད་ཡ་ནཱ་མ» (BDRC's Peking record: «swa ro d+ha ya nA ma»), with the colophon «…བསྟན་བཅོས་རྫོགས་སོ». Its chapters close with the formula «གཡུལ་ལས་རྣམ་པར་རྒྱལ་བ་དབྱངས་འཆར་བའི་རྒྱུད་ཀྱི་རྒྱལ་པོ་ལས…ལེའུ་…པའོ». So the Svarodaya, the Dbyangs 'char and the Tantra of the Victor in Battle (WB ch. 12's colophon: «གཡུལ་རྒྱལ་རྒྱུད») are one text, the one WB draws on in ch. 12 and names again at the close of ch. 34. The two short Tanjur works that also carry the name (Svarodayārthasaṃgraha, «དབྱངས་འཆར་བའི་དོན་བསྡུས་པ», by Seng ge go cha, Derge p. 195; Svarodayakālayogaphalopadeśa, «དབྱངས་འཆར་བའི་དུས་སྦྱོར་གྱི་འབྲས་བུ་ཉེ་བར་མཁོ་བ», p. 199; the «ཚེ་རྩིས་ཀྱི་འབྲས་བུ་གསལ་བར་བྱེད་པ» of p. 194 has a different Sanskrit title, «ཨཱ་ཡུ་གཎི་ཏ་ཕ་ལ་བྲ་ཀ་ཤ», and is another work) are short works of the same name, not the tantra, and not read here beyond their titles and the passage on the six parts of the time-union (p. 189). The claim wb-ch34-svarodaya-title-and-colophon, which took the Tanjur treatise on life-reckoning results for WB's «ས་རོད་ཡ», is corrected.

The second half stays open: which of ch. 34's kinds of works come from the tantra. The tantra's chapters (read in the etext, outline only) are the time-union's six parts (ch. 2), the answers to the messenger's questions (3), the hundred-and-sixty-one-footed wheel (5), what is born (6), the lotus wheel (7), the fort wheel (8), the Rāhu wheel (9); its text has no entry for the digging of ground, house-building, sewing a tent or the other works of WB's list: «ས་བརྐོ», «ཁྱིམ་བརྩིགས», «ཕྱ་ཐེར» 0 hits in pp. 100-186, and «དམར་ནག» one, the colour of Mars (p. 115). So no work of ch. 34 can be tied to a passage of the tantra from its etext; the colophon's «དམར་ནག» kinds are not found there either.
