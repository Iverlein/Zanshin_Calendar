# A name for a match of mixed kinds

When the four aspects of a match fall into different kinds (some of karma, some of kin, some of enmity), does any reader of the tradition give a rule that names the couple as a whole, or is "in general" (the notes, p. 524) left to the reckoner's judgement?

## Answered 2026-11-14

Answered with item 209 of open-questions.md (inv-tt, 2026-11-14), which asks the same: no source found names a couple whose four aspects fall into different kinds; the couple is read aspect by aspect, each with its own result and remedy ([claims/wb-mixed-kinds-other-manuals.md](../claims/wb-mixed-kinds-other-manuals.md), [claims/match-kinds-in-general.md](../claims/match-kinds-in-general.md)). The one rule found for clashing aspects is in NM (the Sakya *'Bras rtsis rab gsal nor bu'i me long*, [nm:p111](../corpus/nm/nm.md#nm-p111), etext only), quoting the *Dum khrig*: when they disagree, the husband wins in the life force and luck, the wife in the body and power; an enemy-and-slayer meeting does no harm. That is its *gi gong* single reckoning, a rule for which side wins, not a name for the couple. Searched again 2026-11-14 (inv-ccc): nothing new; "in general" (the notes, p. 524) is left to the reckoner's judgement as far as the sources go.
