# KD vol. 1 p. 633: which fifteenth-circuit text is the three-headed earth lord's helping gto, and does it hold a rule for the date's earth lords?

KD vol. 1 p. 633 names the three-headed earth lord of the fifteenth circuit. Wanted: the text and whether it holds a rule for the date's earth lords.

## Answered 2026-11-21

The fifteenth circuit's «ས་བདག་མགོ་གསུམ་ཕན་གཏོ» is a line of the White Beryl's verse, not a separate text: WB vol. 1 ch. 20 closes the Chinese fifteen with «ཆད་ནག་དུར་ས་བརྩི་བཅུ་བཞི། ས་བདག་མགོ་གསུམ་ཕན་གཏོ་སྟེ། བཅོ་ལྔ་ཕན་རྒྱུའི་སྐོར་འགོའོ» (wb.1:p242-243; KD vol. 1 p. 633 renders it in prose, claim kd-p632-634-follows-wb-ch20-verse). The rite worked under that head is WB's own ch. 26, the twenty-sixth starting point «ཉེར་དྲུག་ས་བདག་མགོ་གསུམ་པའི། ཁ་ནས་གཏོ་ནི་དཀར་དང་ནག» (wb.1:p468-469, claim thirty-26-rites). It holds no rule for the date's earth lords: its eight pebbles ask whether the earth lord king is offered to, whether the earth lords are disturbed, whether Rudra is liberated, the enemy tamed, gossip sent off, the male and female lines' turning back done, and the earth lords' path mended, each with a remedy; it names no date, direction or work, and the person's own year is the one reckoned. A BDRC phrase search «ས་བདག་མགོ་གསུམ» (14 hits: WB vol. 1 and 2 in the Beijing and Gser thang prints, KD vols. 1 and 3, Nor brang O rgyan's collected writings, and the Fifth Dalai Lama's collected works, BDRC IE0OPIEFA32E69 vol. I1PD107961, etext pp. 502–503, below) and «ས་བདག་མགོ་གསུམ་ཅན» (1 hit, the Gser thang WB vol. 1 itself) shows no text with a three-headed earth lord's gto as its own title. The Fifth Dalai Lama's reply to the fourth of a set of questions on the circuits (སྐོར་མགོ) lists the circuits by their images, among them «ས་བདག་མགོ་གསུམ་གྱི་སྟེང་ནས་གཏོ་དཀར་ནག་ཁྲ་གསུམ་ཀྱི་རྩིས» (from the three-headed earth lord, the reckoning of the three gto, white, black and speckled), which is WB's twenty-sixth point's head in other words: the same one reckoning, no rule for a date.
