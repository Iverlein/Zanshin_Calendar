# «ཚད་ཟླ་མག་སྐུད», «ན་ཟླ་སྟོན་མོ», «ཞུར/འུར་ཞོགས», «ལྕོང་མོའི་ཛ་མས»

What are «ཚད་ཟླ་མག་སྐུད» (p. 9, l. 6), «ན་ཟླ་སྟོན་མོ» (p. 9, l. 16), the raid «ཞུར[?]/འུར་ཞོགས» (p. 17, l. 26) and «ལྕོང་མོའི་ཛ[?]་མས» (p. 17, l. 9)? The scan leaves the third and fourth letters in doubt; the others are clear but not understood.

**In part 2026-10-25 (RD51).** p. 17 l. 26 reads «འུར་ཞོགས» on the scan (no ཞ stem), not «ཞུར»; l. 9 «ཛ[?]་མས» stays doubtful (Yigdzin-1's «རྔ» is wrong on the scan); p. 9's «ཚད་ཟླ་མག་སྐུད» and «ན་ཟླ་སྟོན་མོ» read the same on the Zhol print (WBZ img. 638); «ན་ཟླ» recurs on p. 21 in «ན་ཟླ་བཞི»; no meaning found beyond context.

**2026-10-25 (RD57).** On the Taikhang: p. 9 l. 6 «ཚད་ཟླ་མག་རྒུད» (Yigdzin-1, eye and agy agree), where the 1996 print and the Zhol print by Yigdzin-1 have «སྐུད»; p. 9 l. 16 «ན་ཟླ་སྟོན་མོ» unchanged; p. 17 l. 9 «ལྕོང་མོའི་རྔམས» by Yigdzin-1 on both prints and agy, likely, not proved by eye; p. 17 l. 26 the first letter is a box-shaped བ or འ, never ཞ, Yigdzin-1 «བུར» (Taikhang) against «འུར» (Zhol), agy blocked by its filters. The meaning of the words stays open ([ch27-taikhang-doubtful-letters](../claims/ch27-taikhang-doubtful-letters.md)).
