# Which creatures does ST ch. 81 name among the dreams of the nāga spirits' illness, and what are the mantra and the ingredient «བ་ར» of ch. 91's thread?

Left unsettled in RD211.

## Answered 2026-11-10

RD356: the creatures of the nāga spirits' illness are tadpole and frog, fish, snake, worm and bee-grub, scorpion and spider; in ch. 91 བ་ར is milk of cattle and goats and the mantra stands as the print gives it, with its own gap.
