# How the death day's planet's state is found

The weekday entries of ch. 29's third examination (WB vol. 2, pp. 63–65) read each planet's day by its state, dar, gud, zhud and gyod. How is the state of the death day's planet found: by the month's rough rise and decline (the almanac's «དར་གུད་རགས་པ», month-entries.md), by the element relation of the day's planet to the dead's element, or otherwise? And why are Mercury's gyod and Jupiter's gud entries missing?
