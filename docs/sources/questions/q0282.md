# How the death day's planet's state is found

The weekday entries of ch. 29's third examination (WB vol. 2, pp. 63–65) read each planet's day by its state, dar, gud, zhud and gyod. How is the state of the death day's planet found: by the month's rough rise and decline (the almanac's «དར་གུད་རགས་པ», month-entries.md), by the element relation of the day's planet to the dead's element, or otherwise? And why are Mercury's gyod and Jupiter's gud entries missing?

**Narrowed 2026-10-23 (RD45, [claims/planet-state-by-rising.md](../claims/planet-state-by-rising.md)).** LT and UT give a planet's state by the triad of animals it rises on (Saturn tiger, horse, dog; the Moon pig, sheep, hare; Jupiter bird, ox, snake; Mars rat, dragon, monkey): the rising planet and its son in their rise, its mother spent, its enemy declining, its friend in strife (lt:p9-11; ut:p37); UT reads the weekday's planet in the dead's reckoning so (ut:p69-70). That the animal is the day's is an inference (question 376); no manual says why WB lacks Mercury's gyod and Jupiter's gud.
