# Where the Derge print itself has «མེ་དང་ཁྲག» or «མེད་དང་ཁྲག» and «རྨི་བ་ཡིན» or «རྨི་ལམ་ཡིན» (dyc:p121-122, dyc:i121-122), would a scan of the Derge block print at more than the 2008 px BDRC serves, or another Derge scan, say? The Narthang and Peking prints read «མེ» and «རྨི་བ».

RD376.

**Narrowed 2026-11-24 (sweep from inv-s).** BDRC's exact search (2026-11-24, inv-s), the line «རྒྱལ་པོ་དང་ནི་མེ/མེད་དང་ཁྲག» and its neighbours, by witness: «མེ་དང་ཁྲག»: the Dpe bsdur ma etext (MW1PD95844_3564), the Narthang scan by eye (dycn:i127-128), the Peking scan, Yigdzin-1 on the Derge scan, and the Kun 'dus chen mo's paraphrase («མེ དང་ ཁྲག དང་ ཉི་མ་ཤར་བ»); «མེད་དང་ཁྲག»: the Derge etext (VE23703_1521) alone, and BDRC's second hit for the string (I1PD95775) is another text. «དམར་པོའི་རིགས་རྣམས་རྨི་བ་ཡིན» (red things): Dpe bsdur ma, the Narthang etext (I2KG212495) and a further Bstan 'gyur etext (I4717), the Kun 'dus chen mo, the Gso rig collection; «རྨི་ལམ་ཡིན»: the Derge etext alone. The white things' line «དཀར་པོའི་རིགས་རྣམས་རྨི་བ་ཡིན» is in the Derge etext too, with «རྨི་བ», and the string with «རྨི་ལམ་ཡིན» finds nothing, so the Derge etext writes both words for the same line. Sense: a king, fire, blood, the rising sun, flowers, red things: «མེ» fits (the paraphrase glosses the list with «མེ དང་ཁྲག» and the red flower and cloth), «མེད» ("not having") gives no red thing. So the verse reads «མེ་དང་ཁྲག» and «རྨི་བ་ཡིན» in every witness but the Derge etext; whether the Derge block print has «མེད» and «རྨི་ལམ» (a spelling of the print or the typist's) is not told by anything seen: the Derge scan is 2008 px wide, no other Derge scan is served, and the Peking dpe bsdur ma scan answers 401. Narrowed, not answered.
