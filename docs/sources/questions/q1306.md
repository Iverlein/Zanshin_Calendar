# WB vol. 1 p. 77 line 4: «རང» or «དང» before «དཀྱིལ་ཆར»?

On p. 77 line 4 the word before «དཀྱིལ་ཆར» looks like «རང» (own) on the print; the passage keeps «དང» (and).

## Answered 2026-11-21

The word is «རང» (own): «སྲང་རི(༧)དབུགས་མེ(༣)རང་དཀྱིལ་ཆར», "its own circle part". Two checks. (1) Grammar and parallel: the same line-sets say «རང་གི་ཚེས་དཀྱིལ» (p. 76) and «རང་གི་ཚེས་ཞག་དཀྱིལ་འཁོར» (p. 77 line 9), and the verse lists the parts hour, weight, breath and then "its own circle part" before the part below, which «དང» (and) would break. (2) Scan, 2026-11-21 (rd-s), p. 77 line 4 of the Gser thang reprint I3CN5028 img. 84 at 4x: the first letter before «དཀྱིལ» has the flat head and the straight tail of ར (compare the ར of «རི» and «སྲང» on the same line), not the left-curled ད of «དཀྱིལ». The corpus already reads «རང»; the question can close.
