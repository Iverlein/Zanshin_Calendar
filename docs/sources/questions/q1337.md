# Does the Seven Chapters Prayer's 'waxing tenth' (yar ngo'i tshes bcu) exclude the waning tenth

From the 2026-11-21 practice-day readings.

Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-r): the pool's own lines settle part of it. The Seven Chapters' line names only the waxing half, with "in person" (claim tenth-day-guru-comes-on-the-waxing-tenth-in-the-seven-chapters, lbm:p29), and names no waning tenth anywhere in the seven prayers; but a text that names one half does not by that say the other half is excluded, and Zhe chen rgyal tshab (claim tenth-day-waxing-half-is-the-gurus-both-halves-kept) keeps both tenths while calling the waxing one the Guru's. No commentary on the line saying "only" or "also" was found, so the question of exclusion is not plainly settled. Still open.

Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-a): BDRC phrase search on the whole line «ཡར་ངོའི་ཚེས་བཅུའི་དུས་སུ་དངོས་སུ་བྱོན» finds seven prayer collections (Rin chen gter mdzod, Bo dong, Mdo chen, Sa skya, Rat+na'i zhal lung and others) and no commentary on it. The nearest comment is Dpal sprul's, on the tenth in general (dpk.1:p380-381, etext p423): the masters of all the new and old tantras keep the feast on the tenth of both halves (he quotes the Bde mchog root tantra, "the tenth of the waning half and the tenth of the waxing half", and Kun spyod), the night and day of the two being quick for accomplishment; yet "all the great deeds of the second Buddha Orgyan were done mostly (shas che) on the waxing tenth only (ཡར་ངོའི་ཚེས་བཅུ་ཁོ་ན་ལ)", so every tenth of every month is his occasion of work. That reads the waxing half as the Guru's chief day and the waning one as kept as well; it is the same as Zhe chen rgyal tshab's, and it is not a gloss of the Seven Chapters' line. Mgon po tshe brtan's collected writings (MW1KG6254, search only, 403) have the phrase «yar ngo'i tshes bcu kho na yin» in a sentence that names the great occasion of the blessing, quoting a Gsang 'dus them med zhal gdams; only the snippet could be read, so nothing is recorded from it. Still open: the line does not say "only", and no commentary found says that it excludes the waning tenth.
