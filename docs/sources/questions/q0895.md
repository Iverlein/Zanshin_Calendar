# On the 1996 print p. 144 l. 13 the last syllable of the riding animals is too small to tell «རྟ» from «ཧ» by eye: is a scan of the 1996 print at higher resolution to be had?

RD294.

## Answered 2026-11-14

Yes: the horse. A scan at higher resolution than the 1996 print is to be had in three prints, and read by eye they give the same syllable. The Taikhang reproduction (I5878 img. 222, 8280 px) shows the stack after the buffalo (མ་ཧེ, ma he) as a ར over ཏ with the ཏ's hook at the lower right, «རྟ» (rta); the Zhol print (I1KG1710 img. 840, 3752 px) the same stack (both read in RD294, [wb.2:p144r](../corpus/wb/ch30.md#wb-2-p144r)); the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5029 img. 150, 1905 px, line 13), looked at again on 2026-11-14 in a 5× crop, carries an ink-filled stack with a long leg to the lower left before the shad, not the shape of the ཧ of «ཧེ» beside it. The 1996 print's own small stack (about 8 px a letter) stays unreadable letter by letter, and Yigdzin-1's «ཧ» there is its misreading; the riding animals are lion, elephant, buffalo, horse.
