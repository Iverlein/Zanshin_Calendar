# «རྨི་ལྟས» or «ཕྱི་ལྟས» at the foot of p. 144

On p. 144, l. 24, does the last line end «རྨི་ལྟས» (Yigdzin-1, the etext) or «ཕྱི་ལྟས», as the Gser thang reprint looks?

## Answered 2026-11-05

Settled 2026-11-01 (RD110, [notes-8](../corpus/wb/notes-8.md), wb.2:p144e): at 6× the stack is the tall ra-over-ma letter of l. 11's «རྨི་ལྟས», and the lines before it are about dreams. «ཕྱི་ལྟས» has no headword in the dictionaries.
