# The dog day's former and next life

The dog day's former life is «ཚེ་སྔོན་ཕྱི་གུ་ཞིག་ཏུ་སྐྱེས» (p. 71, l. 16, on the scan). Is «ཕྱི་གུ» a creature (a chick, བྱི་གུ?), and is its next life really the same as the bird day's (the middle of three brothers in the west), or is that line repeated by mistake?

## Answered 2026-11-03

Former life «ཁྱི་གུ», a puppy, not «ཕྱི་གུ»: the first letter matches the «ཁྱི» of the dog day's own heading (Taikhang I5878 img. 110, l. 5, by eye). The next-life line «ཕྱི་མ་ནུབ་ཕྱོགས་བུ་སྤུན་ནི། གསུམ་གྱི་བར་པ་ཞིག་ཏུ་སྐྱེ» is carved identically on the bird day and the dog day, so the repeat is in the blocks (RD135, reckonings.md).
