# Who is the lama of the Śramaṇā rite in the be'u bum's dream set, is he in the Paṇ chen Śākyaśrī's line, and does Tāranātha's section come from the same source

The set says «the lama says, and it is established by experience» ([p339]); the Lcang lung list takes the Śramaṇā mantra from the Paṇ chen Śākyaśrī; Tāranātha's section has the same rite in the same order.

## Searched 2026-11-13 (inv-s), no answer

BDRC's phrase search for the Śramaṇā mantra «ཤྲ་མ་ཎ་པི་ཤ་ཙི་ནི་མུཎྜེ» finds four etexts only: Tāranātha's sādhana collection (two records), the Lcang lung list (MW1ALS12284E) and a modern pantheon (Bod brgyud nang bstan lha tshogs chen mo, MW28850, search only), and a search for the Lcang lung wording with «all-knowing Śākyaśrī» finds none. No text names the be'u bum's lama or the transmission beyond «from the Paṇ chen Śākyaśrī».

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-ii)

Still no name. New (etext): Tāranātha's section (ttn:p137-138) is a different text from the be'u bum's, signed by the dotted syllables of its closing verse, which read Kun dga' snying po; the be'u bum's Śramaṇā wording is that of the First Dalai Lama's collected works and has no such verse, while the sentence «the lama says, and it is established by experience» (tdr:p339) is not in Tāranātha's. So Tāranātha's section does not come from the be'u bum's text; whether both rest on an earlier source, and who the lama is, no text says. BDRC phrase search of the sentence «the Śramaṇā instruction has no like, the lama says» finds the be'u bum alone (MW1PD2_B6F70D, 'Brong rtse be'u bum dkar mo, lower part), and the BDRC records of the be'u bum's section (MW25101_4F0B43) and of the Dalai Lama's text (MW24769_B767AF) carry no author field for the lama.
