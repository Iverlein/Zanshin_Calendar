# Who is the lama of the Śramaṇā rite in the be'u bum's dream set, is he in the Paṇ chen Śākyaśrī's line, and does Tāranātha's section come from the same source

The set says «the lama says, and it is established by experience» ([p339]); the Lcang lung list takes the Śramaṇā mantra from the Paṇ chen Śākyaśrī; Tāranātha's section has the same rite in the same order.

## Searched 2026-11-13 (inv-s), no answer

BDRC's phrase search for the Śramaṇā mantra «ཤྲ་མ་ཎ་པི་ཤ་ཙི་ནི་མུཎྜེ» finds four etexts only: Tāranātha's sādhana collection (two records), the Lcang lung list (MW1ALS12284E) and a modern pantheon (Bod brgyud nang bstan lha tshogs chen mo, MW28850, search only), and a search for the Lcang lung wording with «all-knowing Śākyaśrī» finds none. No text names the be'u bum's lama or the transmission beyond «from the Paṇ chen Śākyaśrī».

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-ii)

Still no name. New (etext): Tāranātha's section (ttn:p137-138) is a different text from the be'u bum's, signed by the dotted syllables of its closing verse, which read Kun dga' snying po; the be'u bum's Śramaṇā wording is that of the First Dalai Lama's collected works and has no such verse, while the sentence «the lama says, and it is established by experience» (tdr:p339) is not in Tāranātha's. So Tāranātha's section does not come from the be'u bum's text; whether both rest on an earlier source, and who the lama is, no text says. BDRC phrase search of the sentence «the Śramaṇā instruction has no like, the lama says» finds the be'u bum alone (MW1PD2_B6F70D, 'Brong rtse be'u bum dkar mo, lower part), and the BDRC records of the be'u bum's section (MW25101_4F0B43) and of the Dalai Lama's text (MW24769_B767AF) carry no author field for the lama. See claims/ttn-sramana-section-closing-verse-spells-kun-dga-snying-po.md.

## Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-s)

A named author of the Śramaṇā dream instruction exists, not shown to be the be'u bum's lama. BDRC's phrase search «ཤྲ་མ་ཎའི་རྨི་ལམ་བརྟག་པའི་མན་ངག» and «ལྷ་མོ་ཤྲ་མ་ཎའི་རྨི་ལམ» (2 and 3 hits) finds the Sakya lineage records in the Sgrub thabs kun btus (VE23681_001, read at 9573597, in the list of transmissions of Tārā cycles by Jamyang Loter Wangpo's school), in the Sa skya Lam 'bras (I2PD19938) and in a Sa lugs biographies volume (I4CN10076): «'jam dpal dpa' bo grub pa'i rgyud nas bstan cing ba ri lo tsā ba nas brgyud pa'i lha mo shra ma Ṇa'i rmi lam brtag pa'i man ngag dwags po paṇḍi ta bkra shis rnam rgyal gyis mdzad pa» («the Śramaṇā dream instruction, taught from the Mañjuśrī hero-siddhi tantra and handed down from the translator Ba ri lo tsā ba, composed by Dwags po Paṇḍita Bkra shis rnam rgyal»), with the lineage Tārā, Jetāri, the elder and the younger Vajrāsanapa, Ba ri lo tsā ba, Sa chen Kun dga' snying po, and on through Grags pa rgyal mtshan and Sa paṇ. The collected works of 'Jigs med rol pa'i rdo rje (a record of transmissions, next to Mkhyen brtse's Buddha curuka text) has «lha mo shra ma Ṇa'i sgo nas rmi lam brtag tshul dwags po bkra shis rnam rgyal gsung» ("the way of examining dreams through Śramaṇā, spoken by Dwags po Bkra shis rnam rgyal"), and Zhu chen Tshul khrims rin chen's works have a line of a transmission «Sgrol ma'i man ngag rmi lam brtag thabs shin tu zab pa lha mo shra ma Ṇa'i sgrub thabs, kun mkhyen bkra shis rnam rgyal gyis mdzad pa'i lung dang dmigs khrid bcas thob pa'i brgyud pa» (the lineage of the one who received the reading transmission and meditation guide composed by the all-knowing Bkra shis rnam rgyal). So in the Sakya and Gelug records the Śramaṇā dream instruction is Bkra shis rnam rgyal's (the title «kun mkhyen» and «dwags po paṇḍita»), and the transmission they give is from Ba ri lo tsā ba, not from the Paṇ chen Śākyaśrī of the Lcang lung list; the Paṇ chen Śākyaśrī appears in the same Sgrub thabs kun btus list only in the lineage of the dream-prophecy Tārā sādhana of Zla ba'i bshes gnyen (Tārā, Paṇ chen Śākyaśrī, Khro phu lo tsā ba, Mkhan po Bsod nams dbang phyug…), which is not the Śramaṇā instruction. That the be'u bum's «lama» is Bkra shis rnam rgyal is not shown: the be'u bum's wording is that of the First Dalai Lama's collected works (inv-ii), and the dates of Bkra shis rnam rgyal are not in these records, so which of the two texts stands behind the other is not told: it needs both read side by side; the etext of Bkra shis rnam rgyal's instruction was not found. Dead ends: «ཤཱཀྱ་ཤྲཱི་ནས་བརྒྱུད་པའི་ལྷ་མོ་ཤྲ་མ་ཎ», «ཤྲ་མ་ཎའི་མན་ངག», «ཤཱཀྱ་ཤྲཱི་ལས་བརྒྱུད་པའི་རྨི་ལམ», «རྨི་ལམ་ངན་པ་ཤྲ་མ་ཎ», «དྭགས་པོ་པཎྜི་ཏ་བཀྲ་ཤིས་རྣམ་རྒྱལ་གྱིས་མཛད་པ་ལྷ་མོ» 0 hits; «ཤྲ་མ་ཎའི་རྒྱུན» 1 (the Bod brgyud lha tshogs chen mo: the Śramaṇā stream given by Ārya Tārā to Jetāri, then Ba ri lo tsā ba Rin chen grags; search only).
