# What does the colophon date «རབ་ཚེས་མེ་རྟ་ཟླ་ཚེས» of Mipham's Flaming Uṣṇīṣa sādhana for turning back bad dreams (Chengdu vol. 25, image 460) say?

RD362.

## Answered 2026-11-13

The colophon gives no number: «རབ་ཚེས་མེ་རྟ་ཟླ་ཚེས» names the fire-horse year of the fifteenth rab byung, 1906, with no month or day (claim mph-flaming-ushnisha-colophon-date-fire-horse-undefined; q1138).
