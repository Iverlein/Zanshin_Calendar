# What does the colophon date «རབ་ཚེས་མེ་རྟ་ཟླ་ཚེས» of Mipham's Flaming Uṣṇīṣa sādhana for turning back bad dreams (Chengdu vol. 25, image 460) say?

RD362.

## Answered 2026-11-13

The colophon gives no number: «རབ་ཚེས་མེ་རྟ་ཟླ་ཚེས» has fire-horse between two undefined words and so dates no year (claim mph-flaming-ushnisha-colophon-date-fire-horse-undefined). What rab tshes and zla tshes stand for is q1138.
