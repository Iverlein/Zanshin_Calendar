# How the circles' two mewas are combined

In the circles' mewa pebble, how are the two mewas the notes name (the reckoned person's body mewa, and the starting point's mewa that agrees with it in the mewa sequence; vol. 2, p. 533) combined into the one counted up?

**Narrowed 2026-10-23 (RD44, [claims/mewa-sequence-agreeing.md](../claims/mewa-sequence-agreeing.md)).** LT and UT make the birth mewa the body mewa; UT gives three sixty-year mewa sequences and LT takes every other mewa in the person's own sequence; Zhechen Gyaltsab fights the reached year's mewa with the body mewa (zg:p303-304). Read together, by inference: the notes' agreeing mewa is the starting year's mewa in the person's own sequence, counted upward with the years.
