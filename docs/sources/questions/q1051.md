# The 六甲の夢うらなひ takes the same lines for every dream of a day and is no list of dream objects; is it a rendering of a known older day-table for the sixty days (Chinese 六十甲子 dream tables, Japanese 夢占 of the 大雑書), and which one does the 夢合長寿鑑絵抄 follow?

RD379.

## Answered 2026-11-13

Narrowed to a negative. The sixty cells of the 六甲の夢うらなひ (mdz:i426556b) were set beside the Yongle dadian's day omens (eighteen days) and P.3908's branch chapter: agreement is what chance gives, and no cell repeats an omen of either (claim edo-sixty-days-against-the-yongle-days-and-p3908). Of the Japanese tables, the 大雑書 prints reached carry the table of the days a dream is not told (twelve months against branches), not a sixty-day table; the 夢合長寿鑑絵抄's table has no counterpart in the 夢合延寿袋大成, the 夢合早占大成 (mdz:kano-22630) or the 夢合長寿宝 on the leaves seen. The 夢合延寿袋絵抄 (Koizumi ORMK-00650, 1866, 30 images, contact sheets read 2026-11-13) has the same sections as the 延寿袋大成 (夢現幻之傳, 夢祭り供物上げやうの秘伝, 悪夢の伝) and no sixty-day table at that size. The source of the 六甲 cells is not found.
