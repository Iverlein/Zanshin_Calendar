# Does Rin chen rnam rgyal's collected works, or the catalogue of them, list a «zin bris» (སྒྲ་ཚད་པའི་ཟིན་བྲིས) on the rebirth-seeing rite of the MDP (q932)?

Raised by the 2026-11-13 investigation k.

## Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-v)

The catalogue was reached and read by machine. Its title is «sgra tshad pa nyid kyi bka' 'bum dkar chag» (BDRC parts MW1934_6FB05A, Zhol par print, vol. 28 pp. 323-326, images I0761; MW22106_C073E4, the Delhi reprint, vol. 28 pp. 327-330; MW1PD45496_3CBA25, the manuscript, printed pp. 415-420; also named in the Potala Sakya catalogue MW3CN5011, snippet). The Zhol par scan (I0761, img. 324-326 and 328, open access) was run through Yigdzin-1 (machine; not checked syllable by syllable on the image). It opens «dpal ldan bla ma dam pa mkhan chen thugs sras sgra tshad pa rin chen rnam rgyal zhabs kyis mdzad pa'i bstan bcos rnams kyi rnam grangs phyogs gcig tu bris pa 'di lta ste» ("the list of the treatises composed by Sgra tshad pa Rin chen rnam rgyal, written in one place") and names, in order: Bu ston's life (the long «Snyim pa'i me tog» and its abridgement), praises of him, «Dus 'khor gyi zin bris chen mo» (the great Kālacakra notes), the Kālacakra grammar and reckoning works, Hevajra and Cakrasaṃvara sādhanas, «'Pho ba'i man ngag mchog gi dngos grub ma», «Bar do lam 'khyer gyi rim pa gsal sgron», a Padmarig Lion's-Roar sādhana «rmi lam brtag pa bu+d+d+ha tsu ru la brten pa» (a sādhana with a dream examination, relying on a text of that name as the machine spells it), replies to questions, catalogues of the Bstan 'gyur and Bka' 'gyur, and ends «rdo rje rab brtan ma'i gtor chog rnams so». The only «zin bris» in these pages is the Kālacakra one; none on the rite of the MDP, none on a rebirth-seeing rite. This list is of his composed treatises (bstan bcos), and a note taken down from a teacher might not be counted in it, so the absence does not say that the MDP's note is not his. New lead for q809 and q877: the Padmarig sādhana with a dream examination is a dream work of his own. Not read: img. 322-323, 327, 329 and the second print's wording; the machine text of img. 324-326 was not compared with the image.
