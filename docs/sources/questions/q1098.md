# Who made the catalogue of Marpa's collected works («Rje btsun Mar pa'i rnam thar dang gsung 'bum dkar chag») in eleven sections in the 2010 Vārāṇasī edition of Dpa' bo Gtsug lag phreng ba's life of Marpa, and why does it list the root verses and commentaries of the four sheets but no history and no companion method?

RD383.

## Answered 2026-11-14

The catalogue was made by the monk Tshe ring Shar pa of Shar sba khang Don grub sdings, at the Vajra Vidya school of study near Vārāṇasī on 25 July 2010, as the closing sentence says (dds.44:p21): it names him and says he «set it down on the basis of» (གཞི་བཟུང་བཀོད་པ) the reprint of Marpa's collected works that the Drigung Kyabgon Che tshang 'Phrin las lhun grub had printed in 2009 at the Drigung Kagyu Byang chub gling in India. It is therefore not Dpa' bo Gtsug lag phreng ba's catalogue; it is the 2010 editors' own, set in the book beside Dpa' bo's life of Marpa, his autobiography and the catalogue of his works. The foreword of the same print (Vajra Vidya Library, 1,000 copies, signed Khra 'gu sprul ming pa) names the proofreader as the monk Tshe ring with Jam dbyangs bkra shis, Bstan 'dzin shes rab and Bkra shis phun tshogs; whether the monk Tshe ring there and Tshe ring Shar pa here are one man the text does not say.
Why it lists no history and no companion method: the catalogue follows the contents of the 2009 Drigung reprint, in eleven sections; it lists the root verses and the commentaries of the four sheets (dds.44:p19-20) because those are what that reprint holds under Marpa's name. The 2009 reprint's own contents were not read, so that it holds no history is an inference from the catalogue, not a check; the history and the companion methods of the four sheets are in other prints (Lho brag Mar pa lo tsa'i gsung 'bum vol. 4, dds.19:p97-102). The catalogue gives no reason for leaving them out.
