# Does Dbang chen dgyes rab rdo rje follow a text for the dream step (he cites the Mantra Stages, «སྔགས་རིམ་ཆེན་མོ»), and does the Vairocana initiation tradition name signs a teacher reads in the pupils' dawn dreams?

RD335.

## Answered 2026-11-10

RD360: Dbang chen's fifth step is the Sngags rim chen mo's fifth part of the pupils' preparation: the heading «སྤྲོ་བ་བསྐྱེད་ཅིང་བསྲུང་ཏེ་རྨི་ལམ་བརྟག་པར་གདམས་པ», the rejoicing verse «འཇིག་རྟེན་དུ་ནི་ཀུན་མཁྱེན་རྣམས», the kusha, the lion posture and «ནང་པར་ང་ལ་སྨྲོས་ཤིག» are the same, and Saraha's verse too (Vairocana in Dbang chen where Tsong kha pa has Buddhakapāla). The Sngags rim chen mo reads the dawn dream (the fourth of the night's four parts, by the Mañjuśrī root tantra's four watches); Dbang chen leaves the reading out. A Vairocana-specific text naming signs was not opened.
