# The bracketed numerals of the great number-names, p. 20

The numerals printed in brackets beside the great number-names (ther 'bum onward) on p. 20 are small glyphs that the 1996 scan and the reprint read differently from the tenth name on; what is the printed number of each name? (RD198, corpus/wb/ch02.md)

## Answered 2026-11-14

The bracketed numerals are not powers or running numbers: they count each name's place within its own run, and the runs restart. Read on the 1996 scan (BDRC I1KG12906, img. 30, p. 20, crops at 6-7×) and tested against the pattern:

- the single places: gcig 1, bcu 2, brgya 3, stong 4, khri 5, 'bum 6, sa ya 7, bye ba 8, dung phyur 9;
- run 1: ther 'bum 1, khrag khrig 2, rab bkram 3, gtams 4, dkrigs 5, mi 'khrugs 6, khyad 'byin 7, spang rten 8, ded 'dren 9;
- run 2: mtha' snang 1, rgyu rig 2, 'od mdzes 3, dbang po 4, legs 'byin 5, rtogs 'gro 6, 'bying rdul 7, rgya rtags 8, stobs 'khor 9;
- run 3: brda shes 1, rnam byung 2, stobs mig 3, khyad 'byin 4;
- run 4: byams pa 1, snying rje 2, dga' ba 3, btang snyoms 4.

So the tenth name, mtha' snang, carries a 1 (the machine readings ༡༡ and "ten" are both wrong: the glyph is the same as ther 'bum's), and the twenty-six names fall into 9 + 9 + 4 + 4. The figures read by eye on the scan, with the same glyph shapes in each run, are 2 and 3 for rgyu rig and 'od mdzes, 4-5-6 for dbang po, legs 'byin, rtogs 'gro (the machine's 5 for rtogs 'gro is wrong), 7-8-9 to stobs 'khor, and 2-3-4 for rnam byung, stobs mig, khyad 'byin. The glyphs for 1 and 7 are alike in this face and, where the shape alone did not decide, the place in the run did; the Yigdzin-1 figures after the tenth (༩ for 2 and 3, ༡༡ for 1) are exactly the errors the earlier note records. Two doubts remain, both of shape, not of place: rgya rtags/stobs 'khor (8 and 9 are close) and the 1 of byams pa. Why the runs restart at these points the text does not say here; they are not exponents.
