# Does UT's chase over the fifteen places read the notes' «ཡར་གསུམ་སོགས»

Does UT's chase upward and downward over the fifteen places (UT pp. 224–226) read the same count as the notes' «ཡར་གསུམ་སོགས» (p. 537)?

RD179 (2026-11-06), narrowed: UT's yar ded and mar ded number fifteen places and do not count three or eight years from a landing as p. 172 and notes p. 537 do; UT's third chase gives each place a pair of year signs with a harm (ox and sheep: heap and lawsuit; dog and dragon: the sky door; bird and hare: separation; pig and snake: the slayer, where WB p. 172 has the earth door); Sprugtön has the same 5/8 up and down counts as 'rope and stake' (q664).
