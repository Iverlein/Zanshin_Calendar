# In «འཁོར་བར་ལྟུང་ན་ངེས་ཤེས་ཀྱིས༔ གློ་སྙིང་བྲང་གསུམ་ང་ལ་གཏོད༔», is the sense 'should he fall into saṃsāra, [blame me]: entrust yourself wholly to me with certainty'?

Raised by the practice-days investigation (pd-a, 2026-11-16); see the cited passages.

## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-e)

New route: the line is read differently in the Rin chen gter mdzod (BDRC MW1KG14, Gsang thig snying po introduction, p. 9, etext) and in a modern Lhasa article (Bod ljongs nang bstan 1988, snippet): «འཁོར་བར་ལྷུང་ན་ངས་ཤེས་ཀྱི༔ གློ་སྙིང་བྲང་གསུམ་ང་ལ་གཏོད༔» (ngas shes kyi), 'if [he] falls into saṃsāra, I shall know [it]; [so] entrust lungs, heart and chest to me', while the Sgrub thabs kun btus and its Si khron print (MW23681, MW3CN3381) and a Bka' thang phyogs bsgrigs print have «ངེས་ཤེས་ཀྱིས» as Mipham does («nges shes kyis»), and so does the other print of Mipham's White Lotus (BDRC I1PD84591, p. 304, etext). So two readings are in print: with «ngas shes» the Guru himself says he will know/care for one who falls; with «nges shes» the sense is the proposed 'with certainty'. No commentary found that explains either. The sense proposed in the question fits only the second reading. Left open.

## Searched again 2026-11-24 (inv-a)

The variant is placed more exactly. Jamyang Khyentse Wangpo's tenth-day ritual (Collected Works vol. 12, BDRC etext VEIE0OPIC2BFA6FE_I1KG81124, p. 481–482) quotes the lines «ཡང་སྙིང་འདུས་པའི་སྐུལ་བྱང་ལས། … འདི་ལྟར་ང་ཡི་ཚེས་བཅུ་ལ༔ བརྩོན་ཅིང་གསོལ་བ་འདེབས་པའི་བུ༔ འཁོར་བར་ལྟུང་ན་ངས་ཤེས་ཀྱིས༔ བློ་སྙིང་བྲང་གསུམ་ང་ལ་གཏོད༔» as from the *Yang snying 'dus pa'i skul byang* (ཡང་སྙིང་འདུས་པ, yang snying 'dus pa; a practice of the same cycle stands in the same volume, p. 461, under «གུ་རུ་ཆོས་ཀྱི་དབང་ཕྱུག་གི་ཟབ་གཏེར་དམ་ཆོས་འདུས་པ་སྐོར་དགུ», the nine of the 'Dus pa cycle of Guru Chos dbang), with «ངས་ཤེས་ཀྱིས» (ngas shes kyis), as the Rin chen gter mdzod and the Lhasa article read. The «ངེས་ཤེས་ཀྱིས» (nges shes kyis) of Mipham and the *Sgrub thabs kun btus* is then a variant of the same line; the Mipham reading has no gloss in any text found, and no commentary on either was found. Sense with «ngas shes»: 'if (one) falls into saṃsāra, I shall know it; entrust lungs, heart and chest to me'; the question's 'with certainty' fits only Mipham's spelling. Narrowed.
