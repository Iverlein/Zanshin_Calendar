# Is KD's Chinese title the one Kun dga' rig 'dzin's register entry means

Is the Chinese title of KD the one Kun dga' rig 'dzin's national-register entry (《西藏天文历算总汇》) means, or another compilation under that name?

RD168 (2026-11-06), narrowed: the heritage register page (ihchina.cn ccr_detail/3653) gives only the title 《西藏天文历算总汇》, with no publisher or year; nothing identifies it with KD.

Narrowed (inv-d, 2026-11-14): one more web search (Chinese, 贡嘎仁增 + 藏传历算学大全 + 主笔) gives the register entry of 贡嘎仁增 (born 1942, 藏族天文历算, X-121) and a report of the Qinghai 藏历算大全 (30 volumes, 民族出版社), but no page with 《西藏天文历算总汇》 as a book or with KD's editorial list. Nothing new; the answer in claims/kd-chinese-title.md stands (KD's Chinese title is 《藏传历算学大全》; the register's title is not found as a book).
