# Which earth lords of the date follow the Black Moon on pp. 361–363, and in what order

Pp. 361–363 list the date's earth lords after the Black Moon (the Sky Dog, Sky-joining, Planet-ogre, Planet-envoy, Wood-ape, Garuda-leg, Great Net) with works spoiled and remedies; the machine reading gives the names from the line heads only; read the nine verses on the scan, and say whether these are the earth lords of the date by number or of another reckoning, and in what order they run.
