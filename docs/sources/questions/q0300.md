# The five clans of the strikes, p. 114

In the clans' strikes (p. 114), are *keg*, *ji*, *kungs*, *shang* and *'u* the five clans of wood, fire, earth, iron and water, as their slips suggest, and from which input of the dead is the clan taken?

## Answered 2026-11-05

WB p. 92 gives kungs earth, ji fire, shang iron, 'u water; the Bhutan collection's verse primer (img. 15) adds keg wood. The clan is one of the five great clans, told by voice or old clan name, not computed from the dead's dates. ([reckonings.md](../reckonings.md), RD164)
