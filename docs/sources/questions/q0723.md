# The seven-star enjoyment's rule on p. 102

p. 102's rule of the seven-star enjoyment (*smin bdun longs spyod*): the arithmetic as the machine gives it (sixty, seven, six, seven and the divisions) is not settled, and the sentence that follows the twelve results («ཞེས་དཔལ་དབྱངས་འཆར་རྒྱུད…», "thus directly spoken in the Glorious Rising-of-the-vowels") may close the rule or open the next paragraph.
