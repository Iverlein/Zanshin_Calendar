# The source of the NDL exhibition's «家畜を求めるを吉»

Where does the NDL exhibition's «家畜を求めるを吉» under the 歳破 come from? The page names no source.

RD172 (2026-11-06): dead end; repeats q489/q533 of RD163.


Narrowed (inv-sss, 2026-11-14): the page is https://www.ndl.go.jp/koyomi/chapter3/s2.html (干支② 方位神, 八将神 table). Its 出典 note (/koyomi/about/index.html): the electronic exhibition (2001) rests on the 1979 catalogue «国立国会図書館所蔵個人文庫展 その3 日本の暦», the catalogue's wording and titles kept, with explanations of the 大小暦 etc. added; the reference list (/koyomi/references/) names 内田正男, 岡田芳朗 (two books), the 1984 catalogue, 暦の会, 長谷部言人, 矢野憲一 and two others, none tied to the sentence. The table has two columns: the kana line quoted from an almanac (歳破 «むかひてわたましせず / ふねのりはじめず», no livestock) and an editor's gloss; the gloss «家畜を求めるを吉とする» is in the second column only. The 豹尾's kana line on the same page is misprinted «くるいもとめす» (the ち of ちくるい dropped) and its gloss says «家畜類を求めることを忌む». So the sentence is the editors' gloss, not a quotation; a slip between the 歳破 and 豹尾 glosses is likely but cannot be shown. The sources of the gloss column (the books above) were not read.
