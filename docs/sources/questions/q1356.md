# Is the sword on Tsongkhapa's lotus painted flaming in the Gelug tradition?

HAR names a sword and a book on flower blossoms and no flame; the app's plan has a flaming sword.

**Answered 2026-11-24 (inv-h).** A flaming sword on the left lotus and a red-covered book on the other: Smithsonian NMAA, Lotus Lama, an appliqué thangka of 1834 from historical Tibet. The school of its maker is not stated, so "Gelug tradition" is not confirmed by the page; HAR names no flame. See [festivals.md](../festivals.md).
