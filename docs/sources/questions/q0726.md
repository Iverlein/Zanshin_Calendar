# Columns 12–14 of the sixty-years table (pp. 105, 107, 109)

What are columns 12 and 13 of the right half of WB's table of the sixty years (pp. 105, 107, 109; headed with words that read «se shig …» and «… gi ming» on the scan), what are the words in their cells, and what is the three-part column 14 whose cells read «[n] phud», «gsum ga bzhi phud» and so on, with merged cells in years 2, 16 and two later rows? (RD192, corpus/wb/ch13.md)

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-ppp)

Column 12 is identified. Its words are the five year-kinds of the elemental reckoning ('byung rtsis) that the Tshig gter rgya mtsho calls «kha yan lo lnga» (the five kha yan years): «kha yan, khong nang, se zhig, kha ral, mdun khur». Each says how the year-sign's power-element (dbang thang, the element of the year) stands to the sign's life-element (srog): kha yan, both are one element; khong nang (also spelled khong nongs), the power-element is the life's mother; se zhig, its son; kha ral and mdun khur, "the life's friend" in the dictionary's text (two different relations cannot both be "friend"; one of them is presumably the enemy, and the dictionary's repetition is its own slip). The words of the scan, «སེ་ཞིག» and «ཁ་རལ», are the dictionary's; the scan's «ཁ་ཡག» is kha yan (ཡན) with the last letter misread or misprinted, «འདུག་ཁུར» is mdun khur (མདུན་ཁུར), and «འཆང་ཚོང» of the pages not yet matched may be «khong nang» / «khong nongs» (the two machine readings of the same hand). Column 13 is the wind-horse (klung) of the year by planet, already read. Column 14's «n ldan» cells are not explained by any dictionary (no headword «gsum ldan», «bzhi ldan» in the 64 sets; «lnga ldan» means "the five qualities" in unrelated senses), and «phud» ("first part, offering; to expel") did not help: still open. The scan's spellings were not looked at again. Source: tmz:box9, wb.1:p105b.
