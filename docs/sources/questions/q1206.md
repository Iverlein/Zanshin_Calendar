# KP label 107: is the first lord of the month named «ཐུག་ལྷ་གཱ་པ»?

The first lord of the month's remedies on label 107 reads «ཐུག་ལྷ་གཱ་པ» in the machine reading (an image of a white lioness); the name is not settled. Wanted: the name on the scan.

## Answered 2026-11-14

The name is «འབྲུག་ལྷ་གཱ་པ» ('brug lha ga pa, the dragon lord Ga-pa), not «ཐུག»/«འཐུག». On BDRC label 107, line 1 (pool img. 105), at 6× and 12×, the syllable after the shad is an a-chung followed by a ba whose subjoined ra runs as a diagonal below the line and ends in the curl of a «ུ» vowel; there is no round bowl of «ཐ». It is the same lord as WB's «དབུས་ཀྱི་འབྲུག་ལྷ་ག་པ» (year-month-earth-lords.md, the central dragon lord Ga-pa, whose remedy there includes a white lioness), so the machine's «ཐུག་ལྷ» is a misreading (the third syllable's long-ā mark of the machine's «གཱ», against WB's «ག», was not looked at). Recorded in kp:i105-106.
