# «ས་དགའ་མ» on p. 288

On WB vol. 2 p. 288 (img. 294, l. 24) what is «ས་དགའ་མ», the house-site where people die out and leprosy is not cut off?

RD183 (2026-11-06), narrowed: the scan confirms the reading «ས་དགའ་མ» (p. 288); a kind of house-ground, or its lord, followed by a rite of vases from a potter's place. No gloss found (14 hits, only WB copies).

**Searched 2026-11-14 (inv-ttt).** «sa dga'» in the 64 dictionaries is the county of Sa dga' and, as a poetic word, the kumuda flower (Ives Waldo, Tshig mdzod chen mo, Lokesh Chandra); no «sa dga' ma», and the county and the flower do not fit a house-ground with those results. BDRC «ས་དགའ་མ» 14 hits as before (WB prints, Do shal copies). Still narrowed.
