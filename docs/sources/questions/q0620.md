# «ས་དགའ་མ» on p. 288

On WB vol. 2 p. 288 (img. 294, l. 24) what is «ས་དགའ་མ», the house-site where people die out and leprosy is not cut off?

RD183 (2026-11-06), narrowed: the scan confirms the reading «ས་དགའ་མ» (p. 288); a kind of house-ground, or its lord, followed by a rite of vases from a potter's place. No gloss found (14 hits, only WB copies).

**Searched 2026-11-14 (inv-ttt).** «sa dga'» in the 64 dictionaries is the county of Sa dga' and, as a poetic word, the kumuda flower (Ives Waldo, Tshig mdzod chen mo, Lokesh Chandra); no «sa dga' ma», and the county and the flower do not fit a house-ground with those results. BDRC «ས་དགའ་མ» 14 hits as before (WB prints, Do shal copies). Still narrowed.

**Searched 2026-11-16 (inv-x).** «ས་དགའ་མའི» 1 hit (a Tibetan elegy, a Sanskrit-flavoured line with «sa dga' ma'i bdag po'i mtshon cha», no gloss), «ས་དགའ་མོ» 11 hits, all the colloquial «sa dga' mo», a pleasant or well-liked place: «'brog sa dga' mo rang zhig», "a pleasant pasture of our own" (Mi la ras pa's Rnam mgur; the Gces btus account of the same), «bca' sa dga' mo zhig med par», "without a good place to settle" (Rta nag sgrog 'gros), «sa dga' mo gcig la dga' mo gnyis» (a folk song). That is a possible plain sense of the name (a "liked ground", set after the list of blessed grounds), but no earth-divination text uses it as a ground's name, so it is a guess, not a gloss. Still narrowed.

**Narrowed 2026-11-06 (sweep from RD183).** The scan confirms the reading «ས་དགའ་མ» on p. 288: a kind of house-ground or its lord, followed by a rite of vases from a potter's place. No gloss found (14 BDRC hits, only WB's copies); the dictionaries give only the county and the kumuda flower.
