# WB ch. 16 p. 185 ma nu (18 or 14) and vol. 2 p. 334's three digits

From the number-word sweeps of 2026-11-21 (rd-ac, rd-ad). Ch. 16 p. 185 «ཚེས་གྲངས་མ་ནུ(༡༨)»: ma nu is 14 in the pool's other attestations (ch. 10 line 106, ch. 17) but the stored figure is 18, like the neighbouring «སྐར་མ་བཅོ་བརྒྱད(༡༨)». The line was not found on the reprint (img. 184: scans.py counts half-lines). If the print has 14, the solstice wheel claim "date 18" (wb-solstice-sixty-wheels) changes too.
