# The Sanskrit term of ch. 8 for the instant union of times

the Sanskrit term of chapter 8 for the instantaneous union of times (*ཏཏྐཱལ*, as p. 54 reads, *ཏནླལ* and *ཏརྐྵ* on p. 52, *ཏ་ཏཱལ* and *ཏནྐཱལ* on p. 55): is it *tātkāla* ("the present moment") and how is it spelled in the blocks? (RD188, corpus/wb/ch06.md)

## Answered 2026-11-13

The term is Sanskrit *tatkāla* (*tat-kāla*, 'that time', adjective *tātkālika*, 'instantaneous, happening at that very time'; Monier-Williams, Sūryasiddhānta vii, 12). The print spells it «ཏཏྐལ» in every place; the forms the question lists were the machine's misreadings. Claim `wb-ch8-tatkala`.
