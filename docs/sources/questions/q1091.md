# What is དུས་དྲུག in the Showing of dreams rite (Dpe bsdur ma I1PD95882 pp. 660-661): six periods of the day and night, or six times of reciting?

RD381.

## Answered 2026-11-13

Six times of day and night, three of the day and three of the night, in the lexica's second sense; and the line «དུས་དྲུག་གཟིགས» says the god looks on beings at those times, so it is no rule for the dream and not six recitations; the rite's own showing is at dawn. Read by RD395: claims/dus-drug-avalokitesvara-looks-six-times.md; passages svo:i329-330, svo:p395c. The Dung dkar and a quotation in Dan Martin's collection name the six as forenoon, midday, afternoon, evening, midnight and dawn.
