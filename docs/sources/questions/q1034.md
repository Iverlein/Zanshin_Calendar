# What is «མར་ངོའི་རྨི་ལམ་མང་དུས་སུ། ཞག་དགུའི་བར་དུ་གཏང་བར་བྱ» in the rite for conceiving a boy? Is «རྨི་ལམ» the dream or a misreading, and what is given for nine days? One print has «མ་དུས་», the other «མང་དུས་».

RD367.

Narrowed 2026-11-13: the print was read again (BDRC I1PD440, etext; the other print, I1KG81094, answers 403 to the etext API again). In the same print «རྨི་ལམ» is used for the dream of a nocturnal loss: p. 148 closes a medicine for the leaking of the seed with «ས་བོན་འཛག་པ་དང༌། རྨི་ལམ་བུད་མེད་ཤོར་བ་དང་» (dds.12:p148, the word between «dream» and «loss» unsettled). So «རྨི་ལམ» is the word and not a misreading in this text, and the line may mean "when the dreams (of loss) are many in the waning fortnight, give (the powder) nine days"; that is a reading from the print's usage, and the line stays without a gloss. «མར་ངོ» occurs elsewhere in the print (etext pp. 159 and 509) as a simile of the waning moon (p. 159) and as the time good for the suppression of the *sri* (p. 509, «མར་ངོ་ལ་སྲི་མནན་བཟང་»), a time rule that does not mention dreams. The variant «མ་དུས» was not found in this print.
