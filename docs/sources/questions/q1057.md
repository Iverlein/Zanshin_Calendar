# The Derge has the measure of time and the earthquakes by watch; the Sanskrit edition (as far as its OCR shows) has neither: does the Sanskrit of another print or of part 2 carry them, or is this a different recension?

RD378.

## Answered 2026-11-13

In part: a second scan of the same edition (Trivandrum Sanskrit Series 70, part 1) has the opening of the measure, the moment, the eye's opening and closing and (probably) the finger-snap, after the four chief planets, at the end of the chapter that holds the dream and births (mmk.2:p158-160), so the first OCR had lost it. It has not the chain of units from se gol to the year, nor the earthquakes by watch (its earthquake pages go by asterism, with midnight once). No other print was found, and parts 2 and 3 (Trivandrum 1922, 1925; Internet Archive) were not searched: ch. 18's matter is in part 1. Whether the Derge expands a short text or the Sanskrit's manuscript is shorter is open. Claim mmk-sanskrit-has-the-opening-of-the-measure-not-the-chain-nor-the-watch-quakes.
