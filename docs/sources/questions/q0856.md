# In the Aṅgavijjā the bright 'beginning and root' is the forenoon in ch. 42 and the afternoon in ch. 41: which is the original reading?

By the manuscripts of Puṇyavijaya's apparatus.

Narrowed (inv-j, 2026-11-14): the apparatus of Puṇyavijaya's print has no note on the word at ch. 41 p. 185 line 28, so by the editor's practice his seven manuscripts all read «avaraṇhesu» there, or he did not record a difference (claim angavijja-ch41-afternoon-no-variant-in-apparatus); chs. 42 and 53 print the forenoon. The original reading cannot be set from the print; no second edition, collation or translation of ch. 41 with its own apparatus was found open, and the manuscripts (Haṃ and Ta of saṃvat 1505 and the rest) are not served. Open until a collation is seen; see q892.
