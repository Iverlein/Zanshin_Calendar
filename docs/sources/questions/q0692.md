# Srong btsan's coming to the kingship, p. 23: 1048 or 1058

p. 23, the year of Srong btsan's coming to the human kingship: both machine readings give «༡༠༤༨», the chain 1063 − 5 gives 1058 and the next year, 1046 = 1058 − 12, is read the same on both. Does the print read ༡༠༥༨ (the machines confusing ༥ and ༤), or is 1048 the print's own slip? (RD187, corpus/wb/ch03.md)

## Answered 2026-11-07

Read on the reprint (img. 30) at 4×, RD197: the figure prints «༡༠༤༨», 1048: the third place is the flat black ༤ also seen in 2648 and 2564, not a ༥, and the fourth the ༨. It is the print's own slip against its chain (1063 − 5 = 1058; the next year, 1046, is 1058 − 12). No figure was changed in the passage's chain.
