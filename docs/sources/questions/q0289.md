# The five parts of each direction on the grid of the sun and moon

«ཆ་རེར་ཤར་ཤིང་ཡངས་ཆ་གཉིས» (p. 96): how are the five parts of each direction laid out on the line-grid of the sun and moon (the east's wood, the two "open" parts), and are the "four others, the open ones" («གཞན་བཞི་ཡངས་སྟེང») of the year's earth lords the same parts?

## Read again 2026-11-21 (rd-z)

Not advanced; the dead end of RD96 stands. Only a reading of the words: «ཤར་སྟོད་སྟག་ལ་རྣམ་ལྔ་ཡོད» says the upper east, the tiger, has five kinds, and the next verse's "the harmful lord first on the iron tiger, the water tiger and the rest; the other four open" is about the year's earth lord, a different reckoning from the line-grid; whether the two share the five parts is not told by any text read, and no figure of the grid is in the pool.
