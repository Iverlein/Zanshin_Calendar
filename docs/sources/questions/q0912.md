# On the Narthang manuscript (I2KG212330 img. 290, end of line 2) is the bird «བྱ་ཤང་ཏེའུ» as the Dpe bsdur ma note gives it, or «བྱ་ཤང་ཤང་ཏེའུ» with the full name?

RD309; a reader of the manuscript's hand or a better scan is needed.


Narrowed (inv-ooo, 2026-11-14): the Dpe bsdur ma's note gives the Narthang as «ཤང་ཏེའུ», and since «བྱ» is common to the prints the entry replaces «ཤར་ཏེ་རུ» (q953), so the editors read the manuscript as «བྱ་ཤང་ཏེའུ», not with the full «ཤང་ཤང་»; the dictionaries have both «bya shang shang te'u» and no «bya shang te'u», so the editors' form is probably a one-syllable haplography, but the manuscript's own line (a smear at img. 290) is not read by eye and no better scan exists.
