# The fifty water-hours and the fourth system, p. 37

WB vol. 2, p. 37 (img. 45): in «ཉི་སྐར་གནས་སུ་སྨིན་དྲུག་དང་། །ཆུ་ཚོད་ལྔ་བཅུར་ལངས་པའི་ཉིན།», are the fifty water-hours the sun's progress through the Pleiades' mansion, or an hour of the day; and which is "the fourth" system («ལུགས་གཞན་བཞི་པ») that uses the moon in *skag*?

## Read further 2026-11-01

Narrowed in RD99: the sun's progress in the Pleiades' mansion, fifty of sixty water-hours; the 'fourth system' not found (claims/ch28-fifty-water-hours.md).

## Read further 2026-11-14

Unchanged. The verse (p. 37, scan) itself says the first way is "Phug's own reckoning, in accord with the siddhantas" and the other, "the fourth", uses the moon's mansion skag rising; Mipham's notes (pp. 537-538, scan) skip the rite, and no text found explains which are the other three systems.

The same lines, the same «ལུགས་གཞན་བཞི་པ» and the same "Phug's special reckoning, in accord with the siddhanta reckoning" were copied with the verse.
