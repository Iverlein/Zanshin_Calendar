# Is the verse list at tsl.27:i203-205 Rtsa gsum gling pa's own treasure text (the Gsang ba zil gnon) quoted by the compiler, or the compiler's verse?

RD363.

## Answered 2026-11-13

Rtsa gsum gling pa's own treasure text, not the compiler's verse (etext, 2026-11-13). The list of tsl.27:i203-205 lies inside the Secret Subduer's recitation instruction («Bka' brgyad chen mo Gsang ba zil gnon las, Bzlas pa sngags kyi pra khrid»), which the volume prints whole, title on p190 to colophon on p211 (tsl.27:i190-211); there the treasure-revealer says «I, Gter bdag gling pa… copied it from the yellow scroll» after the treasure's seal formulas, so the text is given as his treasure's, with the Guru as speaker. The compiler adds only the volume's title and preface. Etext only; the colophon was not read on the scan.
