# The mewa that fights the nine squares: the year's or four down

In WB vol. 2, p. 165, is the mewa that fights the nine squares the moment's year's mewa itself, as «དུས་ལོའི་སྨེ་བས» says, or its four down, as Mipham's «དུས་ཀྱི་དབང་སྨེ ... མར་བཞི» has it, and what year is the example's 5 yellow?

## Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-o)

The Dge ldan commentary on the Nag rtsis, *Rgya rtsis dge ldan mkhas pa'i phyag rgyun du bstar ba'i rdel 'grem dpag bsam ljon shing* (I1KG21886 etext pp. 77–79; the same lines in I4949 pp. 102–103), has WB's verse word for word, and with its plain sense: «ཡང་གཅིག་སྨེ་བ་གསུམ་བསྡེབས་ཞེས། །བརྩི་ཉིན་ཉི་མའི་སྨེ་བ་དེ། །དབུས་སུ་བཅུག་པའི་དགུ་གླིང་གི ། དང་པོ་དབུས་སྣེ་གཉིས་པ་ཤར། … དགུ་པ་བྱང་ཤར་མིང་ངེས་བཟུང༌། །དུས་ལོའི་སྨེ་བས་བརྩི་བྱ་བྱས། །དབུས་ཀྱི་དགུ་གླིང་དང་འཐབ་པས། །མ་གྲོགས་ཤར་ན་བཟང་བ་སྟེ། །གཞན་པ་རྣམས་ལ་བཏང་སྙོམས་གསུངས།» (the day's mewa put in the centre of the nine lands, named by their order; the time-year's mewa taken as the one reckoned fights the nine lands of the centre; mother or friend in the east is good, the others indifferent). So the Gelug text reads «དུས་ལོའི་སྨེ་བས» as WB's prints do, with no «four down»: BDRC phrase search «དུས་ལོའི་སྨེ་བས» 5 hits: WB's 1996 and Gser thang etexts and three commentaries (the Nag rtsis commentary *Nag rtsis rdel 'grem 'bras bshad*, the *Dpag bsam ljon shing*, the *Zlos gar*), all «dus lo'i sme bas brtsi bya byas». Mipham's «མར་བཞི» is not in this family's wording; a place for a «four down» is shown by a snippet of Rme'u tsha bstan 'dzin rnam rgyal's *Bod kyi 'byung rtsis rnam gzhag* (MW28959, search-only; «སྨེ་བ་མར་བཞི» 2 hits): «…yar bzhi bgrangs ba brtsi bya'i srog gi sme ba, mar bzhi bgrangs ba brtsi bya'i dbang thang gi sme ba…», four up gives the reckoned's life mewa and four down his power mewa, so Mipham's «mar bzhi» may be the power mewa (an inference; Mipham's passage was not read again here). By the Dge ldan text the mewa that fights the nine squares is the time-year's own, as WB's «དུས་ལོའི་སྨེ་བས» says; the example's 5 yellow was not reached. Not answered.
