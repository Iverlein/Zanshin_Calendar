# KP §13–§15: the remaining panels of the year ring and the month charts, cell by cell

The cells of KP's §13–§15 charts after the Rat panel were seen at 3x but not entered: the Ox and Tiger panels (img. 87), the Hare, Dragon and Snake (img. 88, in part), the Horse, Sheep, Monkey, Bird, Dog and Pig (img. 89–90), the twelve framed panels of img. 91–96 and the twelve lotus panels of img. 97–100 (kp:i88b). Wanted from a reader who can read the print's cursive at the scan: each cell's names in the ring's order, to see whether the lords keep their offsets from the year in every panel, and what the framed panels (an animal-headed figure with the nine-number names round it) and the lotus panels (a large letter or word in the middle) set out for the month; the Yigdzin-1 reading of the strips (done for img. 87–90, rough) names the same lords as the year ring in other cells.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (rd-kp3)

The year ring is shown for the Rat, Ox, Tiger, Hare, Dragon and Snake panels: the ring's places are the twelve animals, fixed, and the lords keep WB's offsets from the year animal for the king and his group, the queen, Te-so, Hal-khyi's girl, the time-holder, the mount and horse-leader, the watchman, the general, the splitter, Se-byi, Se-shar, the outer life-spirit knower, Wang-ging, Pi-ling and the eleven kings; three lords differ in every panel (Se-lo upward not downward, Phe'u in the other direction, Kungs-myul with no fixed offset) (claim [kp-year-ring-offsets-against-wb](../claims/kp-year-ring-offsets-against-wb.md); passages [kp:i87c](../corpus/kp/kp.md#kp-i87c), [kp:i88c](../corpus/kp/kp.md#kp-i88c)). Still wanted: the Horse to Pig panels (img. 89–90), the framed panels of img. 91–96 and the lotus panels of img. 97–100.
