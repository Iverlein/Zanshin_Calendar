# The dotted syllables of pp. 70, 72 and 75

What are the dotted (underpointed) syllables in the print of pp. 70, 72 and 75 (for example under «བཅུ་གསུམ་ལྔ་ཆའི» on p. 72)? An acrostic, a name, or a mark of the verse heads? (RD189, corpus/wb/ch10.md)

## Read again 2026-11-21 (rd-w)

Looked at p. 72's first line on the reprint (I3CN5028 img. 79, whole line at 1x): four dots stand under its first four syllables «བཅུ་གསུམ་ལྔ་ཆའི», the name of the table kind the page then teaches («thirteen, five-part tables»); the corpus records the other dotted places as the first syllables of lines 1, 2, 7 and of the lines «ཐོག་མར་དྲག་གསུམ…» and «ཞི་གཉིས…», each where a new table or planet group is named. So the dots look like a mark of the topic words of a section (a head), not an acrostic or a name; this rests on their position alone and no other print or a statement of the author was found, so it stays narrowed, not answered.
