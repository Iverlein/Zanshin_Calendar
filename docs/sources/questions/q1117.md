# In the life of Rig 'dzin Chos kyi grags pa is the earlier dream of dusk the vidyā-king in the dusk session, and does nam gung mean the midnight session

Is the srod kyi rmi lam that brings the nāga story to his mind the sight of the vidyā-king in the srod thun, or another dream, and does nam gung there mean the midnight session?

## Answered 2026-11-13

Yes to both, as far as the texts go. The «earlier dream of dusk» (སྔར་སྲོད་ཀྱི་རྨི་ལམ) is the sight of the vidyā-king in the first dusk session (srod thun), in the short account of the same night (dds.50:p6-8) Nāgarakṣa himself, which brings the nāga history to his mind; «nam gung» is a time of the night after that session, the midnight session being the reading both tellings allow, and the life uses nam nyin gung, nam srod and nam gung as the names of sessions. The word is never defined.
