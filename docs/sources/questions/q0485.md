# Which old text gives the 歳殺's branch by the year

Which old text gives the 歳殺's branch by the year (the modern 申子辰 → 未, 巳酉丑 → 辰, 寅午戌 → 丑, 亥卯未 → 戌)? The 暦林問答集 §12 gives only that it moves among 丑未辰戌. (RD92)

## Answered 2026-11-05

The 歳殺's branch stands at the head of every 具注暦 from 756 on, all twelve years seen, in agreement with the 簠簋内伝's table; the 協紀辨方書 gives it as a rule by the triad. ([kyureki.md](../kyureki.md), *The direction gods' last questions in the old texts (RD163)*)
