# Revatī among p. 348's very bad and accomplishing mansions

P. 348 and the notes (p. 489) name Revatī among the very bad mansions for a birth and, with Puṣya, Viśākhā and Uttarāṣāḍhā, among the four accomplishing, good for anything; does a reader of the tradition take the birth's own list of the very bad as the rule over the general good? See year-of-life.md, RD122.

RD174 (2026-11-06), narrowed (repeats q237): WB's ch. 31 class verse (vol. 2 p. 328) puts Revatī, with Puṣya, in the class whose child has a 'very bad nature'; Puṣya is also one of the four accomplishing mansions; Uttarāṣāḍhā and Viśākhā read otherwise, so for a birth the accomplishing four are not all good. Agrees with p. 348's 'very bad' for Revatī. LT restates p. 348 with «ནམ་གྲུ་བཞི»; no reader says which line rules.
