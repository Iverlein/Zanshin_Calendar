Vol. 28 (I1PD108816) is now a row of the tsl siglum (scan I1PD108816, etext VEIE0OPI46409446_I1PD108816, 662 pages, etext page N = image N, printed page = image − 10: image 72 carries the folio «61-62»), and the three places the claims cited by volume and image only are passages: tsl.28:i72 (the colophon of the Dgongs 'dus cycle), tsl.28:i79 (its history: invited on the tenth of the first autumn month of the earth-pig year of the sixteenth Rab byung) and tsl.28:i612 (the turning-back prayer with the skag kinds of year, month, day and time; the same list at pp. 615 and 637-638).
