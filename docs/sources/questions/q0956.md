# The unsaddled donkey, «རྟ་བོང་རྒ་མེད་ཞོན» in the manuscript (machine) against «སྒ་མེད» in RST, KBS and WB: is the manuscript's first letter «རྒ» (old age) or «སྒ» (a saddle)?

RD324; the crop of img. 90 did not land on the right line.

Narrowed (inv-kkk, 2026-11-14): by the dictionaries (Steinert's 64 sets, sparse clone) «sga» is the saddle (Rangjung Yeshe, Ives Waldo, Valby, Dan Martin; the Tshig mdzod chen mo and the Dag tshig gsar bsgrigs: the gear laid on the back of horses, donkeys (rta bong) and oxen for riding and loads) and «rga» is growing old (Hopkins, Ives Waldo, Valby); «rga med» has one headword, Negi's Sanskrit glossary (= rga ba med pa, ajara, ageless), none has «sga med». «རྟ་བོང་རྒ་མེད་ཞོན» "riding an ageless donkey" gives no sense beside nakedness and a planned work come to nothing, and BDRC phrase search «རྟ་བོང་རྒ་མེད་ཞོན» finds no text with it; «sga med» is what RST, KBS and WB have, so «རྒ» is most likely the scribe's or the machine's form of «སྒ». The letter itself stays unread by eye: img. 90 (2400 px) lines 4-6 were enlarged and the cursive heads of the letters are not resolved.
