# In Karma chags med's verses on the hours, are Māra's obstacles from evening to midnight the reason the midnight dream is not Māra's, or only hours of recitation

From dusk to midnight the heroes and ḍākinīs gather and Māra's obstacles begin; from midnight the clear light of sleep is held.
