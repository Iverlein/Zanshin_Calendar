# A Kālacakra or Sarvadurgati commentary naming the Sanskrit of «འཇུག་ཕོད»

Does a Kālacakra or Sarvadurgati commentary name the Sanskrit of «འཇུག་ཕོད» (BDRC's search gives nine hits in the etexts of the Sde dge and Dpe bsdur ma Tengyurs; the scans of VE23703_1382 and 1383 are open and were not read)?

## Answered 2026-11-06

RD168 (2026-11-06): the Sarvadurgati commentaries (VE23703_1382/1383) give glosses only; Shakya mchog ldan gives the Sanskrit as viṣṭi; Tshe mchog gling and Stag tshang list 'jug phod as the seventh karaṇa, Tshe mchog gling spelling piṣṭi; the Indian verse «གཟའ་སྐར་འཇུག་ཕོད་དང་» (planet, mansion, karaṇa) agrees. The claim sdp-jug-phod-is-vishti stands.
