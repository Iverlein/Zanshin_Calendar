# Is the last letter of «ལྷས་ཚོར» in ch. 81 on the Chag-po-ri print (I1KG13487 img. 426, line 6) a ར or a ང (Yigdzin-1 reads ལྷས་ཚོང)?

RD290; moderate readings, soft rendering.

Looked at again 2026-11-10 (after RD290): img. 426 of I1KG13487 is 2305 × 387 px for the whole page, eight lines of about 15 px letters. Lines 6 and 7 cropped in three overlapping pieces at 2.2×; the clause «ལྷས་ཚོར་» could not be told from its neighbours there, and the last letter's lower stroke (ར against ང) is not resolved at this size. Still open: needs the Derge img. 434 or the Zhol img. 483 at a higher resolution, or the print's other copy.

## Answered 2026-11-10

Tried 2026-11-10 (RD352). Chag-po-ri img. 426 is 2305 px at BDRC's best (--width 4000 returns an empty file); a 14x crop of line 6 shows the last letter of ཚོར་ with a long tail running down and left below the line, a ར's; weak at 15 px letters. Derge img. 434 at --width 4000 is an upscale of the same bitonal image, broken into dashes at 8-20x: ར against ང not told. Not settled by sight, but the other prints on their scans (Serthang, Bhutan, the 2005 Supplement) and five commentaries all have ཚོར and none ཚོང, so the answer on the evidence is ར and Yigdzin-1's ཚོང is its slip. Still needed for a sure reading: a colour or higher-resolution image of the Chag-po-ri or Derge page (the physical print or another scan), the Zhol img. 483 stays too crowded.
