# WB vol. 2 p. 236 line 5: the earth kings' times, the second digit of 14

Is the second digit of the second-to-last number «༡༤» in the earth kings' times of line 5 (p. 236) a blot over «༤»? It was read from the order only; the eleven numbers 5–15 were counted at 6x.

## Answered 2026-11-24

Fourteen, by the run. Line 5 of p. 236 sets the earth kings' times in one bracket of eleven numbers, «ས་རྒྱལ་(༥ ༦ ༧ ༨ ༩ ༡༠ ༡༡ ༡༢ ༡༣ ༡༤ ༡༥)»: eleven numbers counted at 6x, the first printed 5 and the last 15. Eleven consecutive numbers from 5 end at 15, so the second-to-last is 14; no other figure fits the count, and the run has no gap or repeat that a blot over the ༤ could hide (wb.2:p236d).
