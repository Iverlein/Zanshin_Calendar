# WB vol. 2 p. 236 line 5: the earth kings' times, the second digit of 14

Is the second digit of the second-to-last number «༡༤» in the earth kings' times of line 5 (p. 236) a blot over «༤»? It was read from the order only; the eleven numbers 5–15 were counted at 6x.

## Answered 2026-11-21

Yes, «༡༤»; the second-to-last number is 14.

The page's numbers run consecutively wherever it numbers a list (the earth lords 3 and 4 on line 1, the earth kings' times 5 to 15 on line 5, the black times 16 on line 13, then Rāhu's counsels 1 to 13), and the eleven groups of line 5 were counted on the scan from 5 to 15 (wb.2:p236d). A run of eleven that begins at 5 and ends at 15 passes 14 before 15, so the blotted second digit of the second-to-last group is the ༤ of «༡༤»; a figure other than 4 there would leave a gap in the 5-to-15 run that the next number, the black times' 16, does not have. The digit stays a blot at 6x; the answer is from the order, which is firm.
