# The five topics of the Clear Lamp's root

Which three of the five topics of the Clear Lamp's root were divided into four (p. 249; the reprint I3CN5028 img. 244 reads «སྐབས་གསུམ་བཞིར་བགོས» without «པ»), and does the root Skabs don lnga pa gtsug lag khog don gsal ba'i sgron me (BDRC MW2PD17476_B43210, also MW2PD17386_6117E1; open scans, no etext) list the five? (RD169; the book is the lead for next time)

RD173 (2026-11-06), narrowed: the root is I4PD636 img. 413–419 (vol. 74 pp. 413–418; a handwritten dbu med of three folios, Yigdzin-1 only, not read by eye). It says the general meaning is two (the reckoned and the reckoner), the reckoned elements have five things (nature, etymology, divisions, definition, function) and the means of reckoning are nine; it lists no «སྐབས་དོན་ལྔ», and none of its pages says which three were divided into four.
