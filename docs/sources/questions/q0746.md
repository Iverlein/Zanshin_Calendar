# The Sanskritised month, mansion and yoga spellings of pp. 16 and 19

which of the Sanskritised month, mansion and yoga spellings on pp. 16 and 19 does the print itself carry, at 3× on the scan? (RD203, corpus/wb/ch02.md)

## Looked at 2026-11-14 (inv-p)

Partly looked at, not settled. The 1996 scan (BDRC I1KG12906, img. 26 for p. 16) is 702 px wide, so a 4-10× crop of a Sanskritised syllable is soft. What the scan does settle: the print marks every Sanskritised word with a dot under each of its syllables (visible on p. 14, p. 16 and p. 212 alike), so the set the question asks about is exactly the dotted words, and the machine reading can be checked word by word against the dots' count. The third month's name on p. 16 line 1 reads «ཙི་ཏྲ་མོ་ཧ་ཏི» on the scan as in the machine text (two dots under «ཙི་ཏྲ»; the fourth syllable's vowel and the ཏི not certain). The other month, mansion and yoga spellings (Sāga, Kirti(?), Śrīga(?), Rgaśi(?), Vyākuli, Svanda; p. 19's mansion and yoga names) were not read syllable by syllable. Stays open for a pass over the dotted words with the Gser thang reprint beside the scan.

## Narrowed 2026-11-17 (inv-b)

A text comparison of three witnesses of the month verse of p. 16, to see which spellings are stable. Witnesses: the 1996 etext (I1KG12906 [p26]), the Gser thang etext (I3CN5028 [p23]) and the typeset *Bod kyi rtsis rig kun 'dus chen mo* vol. 2 (MW28845, VEIE0OPI2F246A26_I4716, [p267], which copies WB's verse). Stable in all three: «ཙི་ཏྲ་མོ་ཧ་ཏི» (the Kun 'dus has «རྩི་ཏྲ»), «ས་ག» ([p23] and [p26] ས་ག), «བེ་ཤ་ཁ» (the Kun 'dus «བེ་ཤཱ་ཁ»), «ཨཱ་ཥ…» for the sixth, «ཀརྟི་ཀ», «ཨ་ཤི་ནི» (the eighth). They differ at: the 5th month, «ཛེ་ཏྟཱ» (1996 etext) / «ཛེ་ཀླུ» (Gser thang) / «ཛེཥྛ» (Kun 'dus); the 4th month's last name, «རེག་པ་རཾ་ཥ» / «རེག་པ་རྀ་ཥ་བྷ» / «རེག་པ་རི་ཥབྷ»; the sixth, «ཨཱ་ཥ''ང» / «ཨཱ་ཥཱང» / «ཨཱ་ཥདྷར»; «ཀུརྨ» (Gser thang, Kun 'dus) for the 1996 etext's broken «མ་ཀ་ར»-like words; the 7th, «ཐ་བ་ཎ» / «ཤ་བ་ཎ» / «བྲ་བ་ཎ»; the 9th's last name, «མི་ཤི་རི» / «མི་ཀི་རི» / «མི་ཤི་རི»; the 10th, «བི་ཊྚི» / «བི་ཎྜི» / «བི་ཊི»; the 10th's pair, «མགོ་ཟླ་མི་ག་ཤིཅུ» (Gser thang) / «མགོ་ཟླ་མྲྀ་ག་ཤི་ཥ» (Kun 'dus); the 12th, «བོ་ཥ» (both WB etexts) / «སི་ཥ» (Kun 'dus). The two WB etexts are OCRs of the two prints, the Kun 'dus a typeset copy that may have normalised the spellings, so the table does not say what the print itself carries; where the three readings differ, no letter is settled by a vote, and the scan at 3× is still the only judge. What it does give: the list of words where the readings part, which is the short list for a pass over the scan; the Kun 'dus is not a witness to the print. Stays open for those words.

## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-k)

A third text copy of the month verse found by BDRC exact «ཙི་ཏྲ་མོ་ཧ་ཏི»: the collected works of Bstan 'dzin chos kyi rgyal mtshan (Bri gung Chung tshang 4, Songtsen Library 2008, MW3JT13322, etext VE3JT13322_001, "best quality, unaligned", search-only, 403 to the etext API), whose snippet reads in Wylie «tsi tra mo ha ti gsum pa dang* / sa ga zla ba med tog ldan / sgrub byed me tog sbrang rtsi bskyed / be sha kha dang b+ha dra dang*» (the Kun 'dus' «རྩི་ཏྲ» is then a normalisation, the Citra and Saga stable in four copies). It is a different work's copy of the verse, not the White Beryl's print, and only this window is readable, so the doubtful words of the later months (Jyeṣṭha, Āṣāḍha, Śrāvaṇa and the rest) cannot be compared; «རྀ་ཥ་བྷ» against «རི་ཥབྷ»: exact «རེག་པ་རྀ་ཥ་བྷ» finds the Gser thang etext only and «རེག་པ་རི་ཥབྷ» the Kun 'dus only. Scan-bound, open.

## Read again 2026-11-21 (rd-u), narrowed

No month, mansion or yoga spelling of the dotted words was newly settled letter by letter. The q1329 line of p. 19 (first syllable «རྒུའི») is settled by sense and the two texts (see q1329).
