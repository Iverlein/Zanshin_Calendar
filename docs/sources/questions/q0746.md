# The Sanskritised month, mansion and yoga spellings of pp. 16 and 19

which of the Sanskritised month, mansion and yoga spellings on pp. 16 and 19 does the print itself carry, at 3× on the scan? (RD203, corpus/wb/ch02.md)

## Looked at 2026-11-14 (inv-p)

Partly looked at, not settled. The 1996 scan (BDRC I1KG12906, img. 26 for p. 16) is 702 px wide, so a 4-10× crop of a Sanskritised syllable is soft. What the scan does settle: the print marks every Sanskritised word with a dot under each of its syllables (visible on p. 14, p. 16 and p. 212 alike), so the set the question asks about is exactly the dotted words, and the machine reading can be checked word by word against the dots' count. The third month's name on p. 16 line 1 reads «ཙི་ཏྲ་མོ་ཧ་ཏི» on the scan as in the machine text (two dots under «ཙི་ཏྲ»; the fourth syllable's vowel and the ཏི not certain). The other month, mansion and yoga spellings (Sāga, Kirti(?), Śrīga(?), Rgaśi(?), Vyākuli, Svanda; p. 19's mansion and yoga names) were not read syllable by syllable. Stays open for a pass over the dotted words with the Gser thang reprint beside the scan.
