# What is «ཙབ་ཙུབ» (tsab tsub) in «རྨི་ལམ་ཙབ་ཙུབ་ཤིན་ཏུ་མང» (the five yellow of birth, kd.3:p597, WB vol. 1 pp. 290-291)? The pool's «རྨི་ལམ་རྩུབ» (rough) and «ཟ་ཟི» (confused) do not gloss it.

RD385.

## Answered 2026-11-14

A hurried, restless pair, not a named kind of dream. «ཙབ་ཙུབ» (tsab tsub) is the short form of «ཙབ་བེ་ཙུབ་བེ»: the Tshig mdzod chen mo and the Tshig gter rgya mtsho give only «ཙབ་བེ་ཙུབ་བེའི་བསྡུས་ཚིག», Rangjung Yeshe and Ives Waldo "crude, restless, nervous, hurried, rushed", Valby and Dan Martin (from Das) "in a hurry", the Dag tshig gsar bsgrigs «ལྷུན་ཆགས་པ་མ་ཡིན་པའི་བྱ་སྤྱོད» (conduct that is not settled; «རྟ་ཐོ་རུ་འདུལ་སྐབས་ཙབ་ཙུབ་བྱེད»); all read again in Steinert's dictionaries on 2026-11-14. «རྨི་ལམ་ཙབ་ཙུབ་ཤིན་ཏུ་མང» is then "dreams, restless and jumbled, very many". No dream text uses the pair (claim tsab-tsub-is-a-hurried-restless-pair-no-dream-gloss).
