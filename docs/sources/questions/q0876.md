# Is «མཉམ་པོར་འོང་བའི་བདུད» the demon of self-clinging that Chöd teaches as the co-born demon (ལྷན་ཅིག་སྐྱེས་པའི་བདུད), or an attendant adversary of the person set beside «ལྷན་ཅིག་སྐྱེས་པའི་ལྷ»?

The pair god and demon is stock in the Rin chen gter mdzod; no snippet glosses the phrase (RD284).

## Answered 2026-11-16

It is the first: «མཉམ་པོར་འོང་བའི་བདུད» (mnyam por 'ong ba'i bdud, the demon who comes as an equal) is, in the Chöd texts that gloss it, a name of one's own self-clinging, not an outside adversary (inv-y, 2026-11-16; BDRC exact-phrase search, 68 hits for the phrase, snippets and one etext window read).

The Chöd life and instruction texts of Ma cig Lab sgron (nine copies by «མཉམ་པོར་འོང་བའི་བདུད་ཅེས», among them the biography by Khams smyon Dharma seng+ge, MW3CN4777, and the Chöd instruction «ma cig lab sgron gyi gcod kyi 'khrid yig»; the window read in the volume I1KG24707, «dus gsum rgyal ba kun gyi yum gcig 'phags ma lab kyi sgrol ma'i rnam par thar pa») say of the karmic spirit (ལས་གདོན): «ལས་གདོན་ཞེས་པ་ནི། ལྷན་ཅིག་སྐྱེས་པའི་མ་རིག་པའམ། ངའོ་སྙམ་པའི་བདག་འཛིན་ནམ། མཉམ་པོར་འོང་བའི་བདུད་ཅེས་སམ། མིང་གང་ལྟར་བྱས་ཀྱང་ཐར་པ་ཐོབ་ཏུ་མི་སྟེར་ཞིང་ཉེས་པ་ཐམས་ཅད་སྐྱེད་པར་བྱེད་པའི་རྩ་བ…»: whether it is called the co-born ignorance, the clinging to «I», or the demon who comes as an equal, it is the root that gives no liberation and begets every fault. So the Chöd name equals the self-clinging, and «co-born ignorance» is a second name beside it. Kong sprul's text in the Bi ma la'i zab tig volume (MW1KG12985, snippet) uses it as a known phrase: «མཉམ་པོར་འོང་བའི་བདུད་ཟེར་བ་ལྟར་མཉམ་འགྲོགས་ཀྱི་ཚུལ་དུ་བྱུང་བ» (as the so-called demon who comes as an equal, it arises in the manner of a companion). In the rites' lists it stands as the pair of the god: «ལྷན་ཅིག་སྐྱེས་པའི་ལྷ་དང་ཕྱེས། མཉམ་པོར་འོང་བའི་བདུད་དང་སྤྲོད» (Rin chen gter mdzod, MW1KG14, Ngag dbang dpal bzang's works, Se ra mkha' 'gro's treasures, Kar ma chags med's «ལྷན་ཅིག་སྐྱེས་པའི་ལྷ» … «མཉམ་པོར་འོང་བའི་བདུད་གཉིས»): the co-born god and the demon who comes as an equal are the two that dwell with a person from birth, and are the first dispelled; here the second is the one that the Chöd texts call self-clinging, so the lists and the glosses agree. No text read says «ལྷན་ཅིག་སྐྱེས་པའི་བདུད» for it. What the author of the Lama Gongdü (q908) meant on p. 970 stays his own: the pair stands there among the causes of a confused dream and is not glossed.

Answered (inv-z2, 2026-11-16; claims lgd-mnyam-por-ong-ba-bdud-is-the-companion-demon-of-self-clinging and lgd-co-born-god-and-companion-demon-keep-white-and-black-pebbles, written from RD307, and a fresh BDRC search): it is both, one figure taught in two registers, and it is not the Chöd's «ལྷན་ཅིག་སྐྱེས་པའི་བདུད». Machik's Chöd texts gloss «མཉམ་པོར་འོང་བའི་བདུད» as the demon of self-clinging, one's own co-born ignorance, "the demon who comes alongside". The exact phrase has 68 hits (Rin chen gter mdzod, the Kongtrul and Karma chags med volumes, Chöd lives), «ལྷན་ཅིག་སྐྱེས་པའི་བདུད་ལ» only 3, so the Chöd's own name for it is the «mnyam por 'ong ba» one. As an attendant it stands beside the co-born god: Karma chags med's black rite (A rA ga'i bka' 'bum, pp. 255–256, etext) sets on the enemy, "above him his co-born god, below the demon who comes alongside, the essence of the two being the red and black sins", and the rites of the Rin chen gter mdzod and of Ngag dbang dpal bzang say «ལྷན་ཅིག་སྐྱེས་པའི་ལྷ་དང་ཕྱེས། མཉམ་པོར་འོང་བའི་བདུད་དང་སྤྲོད།» ("part from the co-born god, hand over to the demon who comes alongside", Ngag dbang dpal bzang's spelling, snippet). The demon is therefore the person's own companion-adversary in the god-and-demon pair; the Chöd explains what it is (self-clinging), the rites use it as the keeper of one's sins.

**Two answers on one day, noted 2026-11-16 (launcher).** inv-y: the demon who comes as an equal is the Chöd name of self-clinging; inv-z2: both, one figure in two registers (Chöd self-clinging and the attendant beside the co-born god). They agree on the Chöd gloss and differ on whether the treasury rites' sense is separate; narrowed until the audit.
