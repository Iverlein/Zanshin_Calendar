# The father wheel and mother wheel of a wheel with rims

What are the father wheel and mother wheel of a «wheel with rims» (འཁོར་ལོ་མུ་ཁྱུད་མ): two discs facing each other, as «letters face down» and «letters face up» suggest, or something else?

RD171 (2026-11-06), narrowed: མུ་ཁྱུད is a rim of the wheel, so a 'wheel with rims' has concentric rims. The father and mother wheels are a pair of separate diagrams, accomplished separately and 'joined face to face' (ཞལ་སྦྱར), male right, female left, in a 'Bri lugs text; a rain-wheel text adds a middle wheel (བར་འཁོར). No text says the prayer wheel's contents are such a pair in so many words.
