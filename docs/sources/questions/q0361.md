# The sky year of p. 426's table

P. 426 reads the illness by element and pair of year animals, and the notes (vol. 2, p. 496) take the element as the person's power and the animals as the sky year's: is the sky year the year of falling ill or the year of reckoning, and does the same table serve both readers of ll. 6–9 (the progressed year reaching one's own, the enemy, the mother or the son)?

**By analogy 2026-10-23 (RD48, ys:i491).** Reply 133 makes p. 435's inputs the time of falling ill; it does not speak of p. 426's table.

## Answered 2026-11-14

The sky year of p. 426's table is the year of falling ill, by the notes' division of the times, an inference and not a sentence about the table. WB p. 426 opens the table with «ནད་དང་གདོན་ངོས་བཟུང་བ་ནི», "identifying the illness and spirit"; the notes (wb.2:p497) say of identifying the illness («ནད་ངོས་བཟུང་ལ») that it takes the year, month, day and hour of falling ill («ནམ་ན་བའི་ལོ་ཟླ་ཞག་དུས»), and keep the time of the reckoning («བརྩི་དུས», the year when the question is asked) for judging whether the years are good or bad. The notes' p. 496 then call the table's year the sky year (གནམ་ལོ): «wood reaching rat and ox» is a person whose power is wood and the sky year falling on rat or ox. Where the illness is recent the two years are one; where it is old, the table follows the year of falling ill, the reckoning's own year being the one read for good and bad. BDRC phrase «ནམ་ན་བའི་ལོ་ཟླ་ཞག་དུས» has 2 hits, both WB's notes (reprint and 1996 print); «གནམ་ལོ» with the illness found nothing else.

The second half of the question: the two readers are not one table. Lines 6-9 (the progressed year reaching one's own, the enemy, the mother or the son) read the relation of the progressed year's life force to the sick person's, and the notes gloss them as the life force fighting the progressed year's life force; the table reads the sick person's power element against the sky year's animal pair. Each has its own input (the progressed year for the first, the power element and the sky year for the second); the notes do not apply one to the other. Read, not computed.
