# p. 187's fifth-place count: «བུ་གས» and the quarters' clearing

p. 187, the fourth place of the five-place count, «མཚོ(༤)བརྒྱན་བུ་གས་བགོ། ལྷག་མ་ཕྱོགས་སྦྱངས་ལྷག་མེད་ན། སླར་ཡང་བུ་གས་སྦྱང་བར་བྱ།»: what is *bu gas* (nine?) and how is the remainder cleared by the quarters («ཕྱོགས») to leave the *sme ba*? (RD186, corpus/wb/ch16.md)

## Narrowed 2026-11-13

«བུ་ག» is nine: p. 30 writes «བུ་ག་༩» and «བུ་ག་མཁའ་མེ་༣༠༩», so «བུ་གས» is "by nine" and the fourth place is the day's number with 4 added, divided by nine. The clearing of the remainder «ཕྱོགས་སྦྱངས» (by «ཕྱོགས» 10, as the word is 10 in the verses of pp. 29-32) and the repeated clearing of nine when nothing is left (10 − 0 = 10, then 9 away: 1) was not tested against a dated *sme ba*; the engines' *sme ba* is not tied to WB's elapsed-day count of this place. Left open.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-qqq)

«བུ་ག» as nine is confirmed by the dictionaries: «ལུས་ཀྱི་བུ་ག་ལ་དགུ་ཡོད་པས་གྲངས་དགུ་མཚོན» (Tshig mdzod), "9 [because the body has 9 holes]" (Rangjung Yeshe, Ives Waldo, Valby). BDRC phrase search «མཚོ་བརྒྱན་བུ་གས་བགོ» and «ལྷག་མེད་ན་སླར་ཡང་བུ་གས» 0 hits, so no parallel print gives the clearing; the arithmetic against a dated sme ba is still untested.
