# p. 187's fifth-place count: «བུ་གས» and the quarters' clearing

p. 187, the fourth place of the five-place count, «མཚོ(༤)བརྒྱན་བུ་གས་བགོ། ལྷག་མ་ཕྱོགས་སྦྱངས་ལྷག་མེད་ན། སླར་ཡང་བུ་གས་སྦྱང་བར་བྱ།»: what is *bu gas* (nine?) and how is the remainder cleared by the quarters («ཕྱོགས») to leave the *sme ba*? (RD186, corpus/wb/ch16.md)

## Narrowed 2026-11-13

«བུ་ག» is nine: p. 30 writes «བུ་ག་༩» and «བུ་ག་མཁའ་མེ་༣༠༩», so «བུ་གས» is "by nine" and the fourth place is the day's number with 4 added, divided by nine. The clearing of the remainder «ཕྱོགས་སྦྱངས» (by «ཕྱོགས» 10, as the word is 10 in the verses of pp. 29-32) and the repeated clearing of nine when nothing is left (10 − 0 = 10, then 9 away: 1) was not tested against a dated *sme ba*; the engines' *sme ba* is not tied to WB's elapsed-day count of this place. Left open.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-qqq)

«བུ་ག» as nine is confirmed by the dictionaries: «ལུས་ཀྱི་བུ་ག་ལ་དགུ་ཡོད་པས་གྲངས་དགུ་མཚོན» (Tshig mdzod), "9 [because the body has 9 holes]" (Rangjung Yeshe, Ives Waldo, Valby). BDRC phrase search «མཚོ་བརྒྱན་བུ་གས་བགོ» and «ལྷག་མེད་ན་སླར་ཡང་བུ་གས» 0 hits, so no parallel print gives the clearing; the arithmetic against a dated sme ba is still untested.

## Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-q)

A parallel manual's verse for the same count was found and read: Kun 'dus vol. 2, section 11 (etext VEIE0OPI2F246A26_I4716, printed p. ~233; the verse form of the almanac's day-reckoning, with its own constants): «གསུམ་པར་ཀླུས་བརྒྱན་བུག་གས་བགོས། །ལྷག་མས་ཕྱོགས་སྦྱངས་ལྷག་མེད་ན། །སླར་ཡང་བུ་གས་ཕྱོགས་ལ་སྦྱངས། །ལྷག་མ་ཉིན་དེའི་སྨེ་བ་ཡིན།» ("third: adorn with the nagas (8), divide by *bu gas* (nine); by the remainder clear the quarters; if nothing remains, clear again by nine against the quarters; the remainder is that day's sme ba"), followed by «བཞི་པའི་གནས་ལ་བསེ་རུ་བྱིན། །ཀླུས་བགོས་ལྷག་ནི་སྤར་ཁ་སྟེ།» (fourth place: add the rhinoceros; the remainder of the division by the nagas is the trigram). So «བུ་གས» is read twice as the divisor nine, the remainder of that division is "the day's sme ba" («ཉིན་དེའི་སྨེ་བ»; WB: «ལྷག་མ་ཉི་མའི་སྨེ་བ་ཡིན»), and the clearing «ཕྱོགས་སྦྱངས» is a step on the remainder in both manuals; the constants differ (WB adds 4, this manual 8), so the one cannot check the other. The reading of the step that gives a falling cycle: sme ba = 10 − r for the remainder r of 1 to 8, and r = 0 giving 10, which is cleared by nine again to 1; then successive days run 1, 9, 8, ..., 2, 1, 9, one less a day as the daily sme ba does. The other reading (r = 0 giving 9) repeats a number and is no cycle. Not tested against a dated day (WB's count of elapsed days from the rab byung's start is not tied to the engine's day number).
