# Is gnam stong in the Kun bzang bla ma'i zhal lung the new moon, as lcb:p568's note says, and is the six times (dus drug) of kzl:i315 the list of lcb:p568

From the 2026-11-21 practice-day readings.

Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-r): the two readings are as filed. Gnam stong as the new moon rests only on the Larung print's footnote (lcb:p568, the note numbered 1035, which glosses the six times as the 8th, 10th, 15th, 25th, 29th and gnam stong); no other text in the pool glosses gnam stong in kzl:i177-178, and the same note is on a different work (Longchenpa's Guhyagarbha commentary), so that kzl:i315's "nya stong dus drug" is the same list stays an inference, as in claim kzl-offerings-to-the-three-jewels-on-nya-stong-and-the-six-times. Not answered. Still open.

Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-a): the first half is settled by the dictionaries (tmz:box10): the Great Dictionary and the Bod yig tshig gter rgya mtsho give gnam stong as "the thirtieth lunar day of a month" (zla re'i tshes sum cu pa), with the Kalacakra/Chinese-style doubt whether the month has one, and Ives Waldo "new moon day, 30th"; nya stong is "the abbreviation of nya gang (the fifteenth) and gnam stong (the thirtieth)". So gnam stong is the new-moon day, and lcb:p568's note is right on the word. The second half is not: Ives Waldo has «nya stong dus drug» only as "the six days such as full moon, new moon etc", with no list, and dus drug in the Great Dictionary means the six watches of day and night or the six seasons; no published text found that lists the six times of kzl:i315, so whether it is the list of lcb:p568 (8th, 10th, 15th, 25th, 29th, gnam stong) stays an inference. Still open on that half.
