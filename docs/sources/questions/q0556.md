# The hand reckoning's word after the year's count: «རྒྱན་ཀྭང» or «ཐུན་ཀུང»

Is the hand reckoning's word after the year's count «རྒྱན་ཀྭང» (Mipham's print, machine) or «ཐུན་ཀུང» (1996)? The line end on I2029 img. 744 at 11× is not settled.

## Answered 2026-11-06

RD165 (2026-11-06): the hand reckoning's word after the year is «ཐུན་ཀུང» in the typeset notes, the 1996 print and the Gser thang etext; «རྒྱན་ཀྭང» is Yigdzin-1's reading of Mipham's own block print, unsettled by eye.
