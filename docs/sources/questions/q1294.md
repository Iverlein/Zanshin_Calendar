# Who wrote the Skar rtsis kyi lag len byed grub sgang dril ltar bkod pa, and which text of Atiśa gives a hundredfold for the full moon, new moon and eighth, a thousand for a lunar and ten thousand for a solar eclipse?

Raised by the practice-days investigation (pd-h, 2026-11-16); see the cited passages.

## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-x)

BDRC exact search for «སྐར་རྩིས་ཀྱི་ལག་ལེན་བྱེད་གྲུབ» gave 5 hits (links in I2PD18782 at offset 17419 and I5743 at offset 16451); the title as the Skar mdzod catalogue spells it («སྒང་དྲིལ་ལྟར་བཀོད») gave none, and the windows were not read in this pass, so the author is not identified and Atiśa's text is not found. Not answered.

**Narrowed 2026-11-24 (sweep from BDRC etext I3CN8749 colophon; BDRC catalogue; four etexts of the verse).** The author is found: Dkon mchog bstan 'dzin (his own colophon in the etext; BDRC's catalogue: Gzu ru Dkon mchog bstan 'dzin), claim [skl-manual-by-dkon-mchog-bstan-dzin](../claims/skl-manual-by-dkon-mchog-bstan-dzin.md). The Atiśa text is still not named: four etexts quote the verse as «from what Dīpaṃkara composed» or «what is known as composed by the Lord», none gives a title. BDRC phrase search «ཉ་སྟོང་ཚེས་བརྒྱད་བརྒྱ་འགྱུར» 4 hits (the manual, Dpal sprul's collected works, a Khams collection, the Ri thu sbyar 'grel manual); a «Dus bzang dge ba'i bsgyur khyad bstan pa» that Ngag dbang chos grags quotes carries a different verse (the sun and moon seized), not this one.
