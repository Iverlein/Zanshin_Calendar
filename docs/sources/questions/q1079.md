# In the Yongle dadian's lines the word 不將 stands after 牛黃's place on every day, yet 選擇要略 says 壬子 and 癸丑 have no 不將; is the Yongle line's 不將 the marriage term or a word of the ox-yellow note?

RD380.

Narrowed (inv-vv, 2026-11-14): the 欽定協紀辨方書 juan 35 (zh.wikisource, Siku print) uses 不將 as the marriage term («嫁娶吉日宜不將», the pairing of yin and yang stem and branch) and has no 牛黃 line; no other text found that joins 牛黃 and 不將. The earlier reading stands (the claim yongle-ox-yellow-bujiang-and-the-books): the word is the marriage term printed on every day by the book's own layout or another system; not settled. Juan 1-10 and 22-31 have no 牛黃 (22-31 are stubs of about 2.5k characters); juan 11-21 and 32-34, 36 were not read (Wikisource answered 429).
