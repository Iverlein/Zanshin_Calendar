# «འཚོལ་བ» or «འཆོལ་བ»: the six funeral shares

What are the six "disordered" funeral shares, «འཚོལ་བ» in the count line (p. 68) and «འཆོལ་བ» in their own heading (p. 69): "sought" or "disordered"? Both spellings are so printed.

## Answered 2026-11-05

«འཆོལ་བ» at both Zhol places, by eye at 8–9× (fair confidence), and by Gyurme Dorje's "irregular". ([word-hunts.md](../word-hunts.md), RD161)
