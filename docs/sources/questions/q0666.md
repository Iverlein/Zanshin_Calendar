# WB vol. 1 p. 65: the true sun and the nearer node differ by one mansion at most?

In WB vol. 1, p. 65, does «སྐར་མ་གཅིག་ལ་བབས་པ་འམ། གཅིག་ཁྱད་འདུག་ཀྱང་ཉུང་གིས་མང» say that the true sun and the nearer node differ by one mansion at most, and does the Mercury and Venus clause of p. 64 take the sun of that test? (RD179; the line is machine-read, the scan blurred.)

INV-1107 (2026-11-07), narrowed: on the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5028 img. 72, sharp) lines 1–4 of p. 65 read by eye as the machine text had them, «སྐར་མ་གཅིག་ལ་བབས་པ་འམ། །གཅིག་ཁྱད་འདུག་ཀྱང་ཉུང་གིས་མང་། །སྦྱངས་པའི་ལྷག་མ་བརྟག་གཞི་སྟེ། །གདོང་གིས་ཉི་སྦྱངས་སྐར་གནས་སྟོངས།» (the last two read by Yigdzin-1 as well): «ཉུང་གིས་མང» is an instruction to subtract the fewer from the more and the remainder is the test, so 'one mansion, or with a difference of one' is supported but the clause is a subtraction, not a bare 'at most one'; that p. 64's Mercury and Venus clause takes this sun remains by context only.
