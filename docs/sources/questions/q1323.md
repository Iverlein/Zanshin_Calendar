# WB ch. 13 p. 166: is the second upper figure of the 7th head's night-length stack a thin 3 (37 + 23 = 60) or an 8?

First stack of the night length, line 3.

## Answered 2026-11-21

An 8: the night length is 28 over 50 over 0, and the day 31 over 10 over 0.

The 7th Hor month's head says that the sun arrives at the lion (Leo): «སེང་གེས་སླེབས» in line 4 and «སེང་གེའི་ཁྱིམ» in line 1 (wb.1:p166d, p166c). WB's table of the length of the day by house (wb.1:p51-52, the claim wb-ch8-day-length-by-house, figures read on the scan) gives Aries and Leo the day of 31;10 hours over srang, and so the night of 28;50, the two making 60. The middle tiers read on the page, 10 for the day and 50 for the night (wb.1:p166e), are exactly those srang. The night's upper tier is therefore 28, and its second figure an 8, not the 3 of 23 (37 + 23 = 60 would not agree with the table, and it would put the day's first tier at 37 against Leo's 31). The day's upper tier is 31: its second figure, read 7 at wb.1:p166e, is the thin hooked 1 (the machine text's first reading «31» was right).
