# Which view of the 13 that IDP gives for PT 55 holds the dream text (the 19 lines below the diagram), and can it be read at a higher resolution (Gallica, or the BnF's own viewer) than IDP's 1024 px?

## Answered 2026-11-14

Answered 2026-11-14: the IDP record (idp.bl.uk/collection/8F997A5E41D90F41AD24A4C68ACDE288, BnF, 13 views, IIIF manifest data.idp.bl.uk/iiif/3/manifest/8F997A5E41D90F41AD24A4C68ACDE288, opened in the headed browser) holds the diagram and the dream text in view 3 of 13 (the manifest's canvas labelled «2», 1024 × 1471 px): the circular diagram in twelve spokes fills the upper part and the text under it begins with «རྨི་ལམ» (rmi lam). The other views are text pages (1, 4, 5, 7, 11-13), strips (2, 6, 10) and fragments (8, 9). Resolution: IDP serves nothing above 1024 px (the full image is 1024 px on its short side; four canvases are plain JPEGs with no tile service); Gallica answers a Cloudflare 403 to the headed browser as well, so no higher resolution was reached. Looked at, not read: state scan-seen only; the cursive was not transcribed.
