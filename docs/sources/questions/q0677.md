# The unit of p. 185's solstice measure and the 18 «སྐར་མ»

«ཚད་གཟུགས་མེ…» (img. 195 of the 1996 print) has the *chu tshod* as its unit; but the same verse on the Gser thang reprint begins the line with «ཆུ་» at the end of the line above: is the unit *chu tshod* of the day (a sixtieth), and of what is the 18 «སྐར་མ» (minutes of a mansion?) a measure? (RD186, corpus/wb/ch16.md)

## Answered 2026-11-13

The 18 is whole mansions (*skar ma*) of the mean sun; the unit after it is the *chu tshod*, a sixtieth of a mansion (not of the day), then the *srang*, a sixtieth of that: the sun's mean place is written mansion, *dbyu gu* (*chu tshod*), *srang*, as on p. 30 (the sun's five places, the star-place by 27 and then 60, 60, 6). A house of the sun is 135 *chu tshod* (p. 51's divisor 135; 12 · 135 = 27 · 60 in sixtieths of a mansion). Claim `wb-ch4-mean-month-and-sun-divisors`.
