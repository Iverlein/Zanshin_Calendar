# The old text of the servants' fine start

The old text of reply 140, which hides the servants' fine start at its end as four nails with fixed years (the earth male tiger, the water male dragon, the wood female pig, the fire female bird): which text is it, Khyung's root pebbles or *The Magic Sieve* (འཕྲུལ་གྱི་མིག་ཚགས, 'phrul gyi mig tshags) of WB's colophon, and are its years an example for a wood person, as WB's relations make them?

**Narrowed 2026-11-01 (RD104).** Re-read against Yigdzin-1 on the new reading of img. 497–498: the reply names the old text only as «རྩ་བ», the root: "it was hidden in the root; in the body (དཀྱུས་སུ) «ཆུ་བྱ» (water bird) is set down; at the end, after all the circles are done, the four unchanging nails", each with its «…ནས་སོ» (earth male tiger, water male dragon, wood female pig, fire female bird), and the root's own line «བཅུ་པ་དེ་ཡང་འབྱུང་བ་བར་གྱི་གཟེར་བཞི་ལས། བྲན་གཡོག་ལས་བྱེད་རྣམས་ལ་རྩིས་འགོ་ལྔ» is cited as the plain proof. Khyung's root pebbles and the *Magic Sieve* are not named; the colophon's pairing (WB) is neither confirmed nor refuted. The years are stated as the nails' own, with no word of a wood person, so that they are an example for wood stays WB's relations' inference ([claims/circles-servant-fine-start.md](../claims/circles-servant-fine-start.md)), not the reply's.
