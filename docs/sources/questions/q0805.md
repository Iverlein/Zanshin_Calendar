# Which demon is «མཉམ་པོར་འོང་བའི་བདུད» among the causes of dreams in LGD?

The demon born with the person, or the attendant demon; the text names it next to «ལྷན་ཅིག་སྐྱེས་པའི་ལྷ».


Narrowed (RD284, 2026-11-10): the phrase is confirmed on the scan; "god born together / demon who comes alongside" is a stock pair in the Rin chen gter mdzod; whether it is the Chöd self-clinging demon stays open (q876).

## Answered 2026-11-14

Answered (inv-f, 2026-11-14): «མཉམ་པོར་འོང་བའི་བདུད» (mnyam por 'ong ba'i bdud, the demon who comes alongside) is the demon of self-clinging that arrives as a companion, one's own co-born ignorance, not a demon from outside. The Chöd texts gloss it in so many words (Gser thang life of Pha Dampa Sangye and Machik Labdron, I3CN4779; the Chöd instruction I1KG24707; nine Chöd and Machik texts carry the sentence): the karmic spirit (ལས་གདོན, las gdon) "is the co-born ignorance, or the self-clinging that thinks «I am», or the one called the demon who comes alongside". Kongtrul's Bima la'i zab tig (I1KG13062) says it has arisen "in the manner of a companion (མཉམ་འགྲོགས, mnyam 'grogs)". So in LGD p. 970 it is the second of the pair with «ལྷན་ཅིག་སྐྱེས་པའི་ལྷ» (the god born together), the god and demon each person is born with, set beside the outer spirits' tricks as a cause of a confused dream. LGD glosses neither; that the author meant this sense and not the death narrative's keeper of the black pebbles is moderately sure. BDRC's phrase search finds «མཉམ་པོར་འོང་བའི་བདུད» in 68 etext volumes, «མཉམ་པོར་འོང་བ» in 8 more (inv-f, 2026-11-14), the stock phrase confirmed. Claims lgd-mnyam-por-ong-ba-bdud-is-the-companion-demon-of-self-clinging, lgd-co-born-god-and-companion-demon-cause-disturbance-and-dream-confusion. q876 is the same question and can be closed with it.
