# KP box 34: which half does the last line «བདུད་རྩི་ཐུན་མཚམས་གཟའ་སྐར་ཆུ་ཕྲད་སོགས» belong to?

WB work 32 lists it as good («བཟང་བ་ཡིན»); KP seems to place it at the foot of the bad half, or the layout is ambiguous.

## Answered 2026-11-14

On the scan (img. 41 at 3× to 6×, kp:i41s) the line «བདུད་རྩི་ཐུན་མཚམས་ … གཟའ་སྐར་ཆུ་ཕྲད་བཟང་བ་ཡིན།» is the short fourth line of box 34's left panel, the good half; the bad half has four lines of its own, and its last line is «… མི་འཕྲོད་ཉི་མ་ … ངན།», the end of WB's work 32. The machine reading of the folio had run the good half's last line into the bad half. KP and WB agree: the nectar periods are good. Two syllables before མི་འཕྲོད་ and the stretch after ཉི་མ་ are not settled.
