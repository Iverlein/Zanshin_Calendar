# The White one (དཀར་པོ) among the lords of the year, p. 199

Which planet is "the White one" (དཀར་པོ) in the list of the planets that are lord of the year (p. 199, the sixth after the Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter): Venus, as the order suggests, which the second list (p. 200) calls *pa sangs*? (RD184, corpus/wb/ch18.md)

## Answered 2026-11-13

Yes, Venus. The short list of p. 199 follows the order of the weekdays (Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, the White one, Saturn), so the sixth is Venus's place, and the second list of p. 200 names the same place «པ་སངས» (*pa sangs*, Venus). The dictionaries confirm «དཀར་པོ» (*dkar po*) as a name of the planet: Rangjung Yeshe "14) the planet Venus", Ives-Waldo "4) Venus", Valby "friday, venus" (and Rangjung Yeshe, Valby and Hopkins give «པ་སངས», *pa sangs*, as Venus or Friday, Ives-Waldo with *khu ba dkar po* among its synonyms). The two lists then agree (fruit trees and crops flourish, the royal clan and people comfortable).
