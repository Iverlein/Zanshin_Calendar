# What is the 「假名暦略註」 the 古事類苑 quotes

What is the 「假名暦略註」 KJ quotes, and is it 塙保己一's 『假名暦本名頒暦畧註』 (1806)? (RD93)

## Answered 2026-11-05

The 假名暦本名頒暦畧註, NDL pid 14487066, read by eye; the 古事類苑's quotations are on its pages. The colophon reads 岡部河内守一德本書寫 文化三年 檢校保己一. Its author is the new question filed with RD163. ([kyureki.md](../kyureki.md), *The direction gods' last questions in the old texts (RD163)*)
