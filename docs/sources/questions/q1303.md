# WB vol. 2 p. 176: the number printed after the east lord (Thesé), (༣), (༡) or (༢)?

The glyph is a hooked upright stroke, unlike the earth-holder's ༢ on p. 175; the run 1, 2, east, 4, 5, 6 suggests ༣, but it cannot be told from ༡ or ༣ on the reprint.

## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-y), no answer

A glyph on the reprint scan, not a matter for phrase search; the pool's passage wb.2:p176b already records the run 1 (p. 175), 2 (earth-holder), east, 4, 5, 6 and both machine readings giving ༢, none settled. No second print or published text with the number was reached. Still open.

## Answered 2026-11-24

Three, by the run of the numbers. The brackets after the lords' names on wb.2:p175-176 count on without a gap: the earth-holder 2, the east lord (Thesé, ཐེ་སེ, the se) the one place left, then the south 4, the west 5, the north 6, the four between 7 to 10, the four of the second set 11 to 14 and the field's 15 (wb.2:p176b). Nothing but 3 fits the place between 2 and 4, as the same run already told the north lord's glyph for 6 against the machine's ༡. The glyph itself (a hooked upright stroke with a tail) was not read: the answer is by the sequence, as the print's own numbering demands, not by the shape.
