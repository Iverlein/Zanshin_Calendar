# What is the first syllable of «?་རང་གི་དུས་ཚོད» on p. 682 of the magic mirror, and does the phrase fix a watch of the night?

The machine readers give བཻ and ཕམ; the scan's cursive hand is a ligature. Does the phrase mean the signs come at their own time, at whatever hour the sleeper wakes, or does it fix a watch of the night? DTD's manuscript (p. 158) fixes the last of four watches.

## Answered 2026-11-14

See q801: the first syllable is ཐོ (tho), the phrase «ཐོ་རངས་ཀྱི་དུས» / «ཐོ་རེང་གི་དུས», "the time of dawn" (kcm:p474 and the Rin chen gter mdzod etext). So it does fix a part of the night, the last one, the dawn: not "its own time", not any hour of waking. This agrees with DTD's manuscript (p. 158), which fixes the last of four watches. Reserve as in q801: the cursive syllable on the Tashijong scan is a ligature, read by inference from the two other prints.
