# The six fang-holders under seven heads, pp. 305–306

Pp. 305–306 promise six fang-holders (མཆེ་བ་དྲུག་འཛིན) and give remedies under seven heads (birth place, work, Rnam dgyes, Rgya chen, Ananta, Pinaśaka, Yid can): which six are the holders, and what do these names count?

## Answered 2026-11-06

RD177 (2026-11-06), etext: a KD commentary on the dbyangs 'char counts from the birth mansion: birth place, work place (10th mansion), Rnam 'gyes (16th foot), Rgya can (18th foot), Pinaśaka (23rd), Yid can (25th); Ananta (19th foot) is added and 'not among the six'. WB's seven heads are these in this order.
