# WB vol. 1 p. 16 lines 15–18 and 22–28: the syllables and the letter rows

On WB vol. 1 p. 16 (reprint img. 23) the machine text stops in line 14; lines 15–18 have no reading, and lines 22–28 hold rows of five single stacks that match the Sanskrit consonant series, with the formula «…བཞིན་འཆར་བ་དང་» closing half-lines 25–28 after house names. What are the syllables of lines 15–18, and which letters stand in the rows of lines 22–28? The stacks were not told apart at 2×; 4–5× crops, half a line at a time, are needed.
