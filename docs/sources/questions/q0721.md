# The six mansions of the strongest harms on p. 98

p. 98 names the mansions counted from the birth-place that hold the strongest harms: the tenth (the place of works), the sixteenth «རྣམ་དགྱེས», the eighteenth «རྒྱ་ཅན», the nineteenth «ཨ་ནནྡ», the twenty-third «བི་ན་ཤ་ག», the twenty-fifth «ཡིད་ཅན». Do these six names stand for a known Indian series of the mansions counted from the birth mansion, and what are their other spellings? (RD191, corpus/wb/ch12.md)

## Answered 2026-11-14

Yes: the six places are the "special" mansions counted from the birth mansion, the Indian series called Ādhānādi (Janma, Karma, Sāṃghātika, Sāmudāyika, Ādhāna, Vainaśika, Mānasa). Published lists of it (web, 2026-11-14): Karma, the 10th from the Janma star; Sāṃghātika, the 16th; Sāmudāyika, the 18th; Ādhāna, the 19th or the 21st; Vainaśika (Vināśa), the 23rd or the 22nd; Mānasa, the 25th (also given as the 11th). So WB's tenth «ལས་ཀྱི་ས» is Karma, the 16th «རྣམ་དགྱེས» Sāṃghātika by place, the 18th «རྒྱ་ཅན» Sāmudāyika by place, the 19th «ཨ་ནནྡ» Ādhāna by place (the counts 19 and 21 are both in print: shrifreedom.org/vedic-astrology/navatara-chakra/ has 19, Wikipedia's Sarvatobhadra Chakra 21), the 23rd «བི་ན་ཤ་ག» Vainaśika by name (Vināśaka; Wikipedia writes Vinasa) and the 25th «ཡིད་ཅན» Mānasa by name ("having mind"). WB's counts, 10, 16, 18, 19, 23 and 25, are those of the lists that put Ādhāna at 19 and Vainaśika at 23, and agree with every list on the other four. What stays unfound: a source that gives «རྣམ་དགྱེས», «རྒྱ་ཅན» and «ཨ་ནནྡ» as the Tibetan or Sanskrit names of Sāṃghātika, Sāmudāyika and Ādhāna (the matching is by position only; Ānanda is not a name in the lists read), and the Sanskrit verse itself (the Nārada Purāṇa's muhūrta chapter, as presented on vedicpeople.com, does not hold it). The effects WB gives (the fang's pain, the mansion's harms) were not compared.

## Audit 2026-11-14 (inv-audit): downgraded to narrowed

The identification rests on position only, from popular astrology pages (shrifreedom.org, Wikipedia's Sarvatobhadra Chakra, blogs); no verse was found, and three of the six Tibetan names («རྣམ་དགྱེས», «རྒྱ་ཅན», «ཨ་ནནྡ») are not matched to any Sanskrit name. A better source, checked: Monier-Williams's dictionary (Cologne digital edition, getword.php, MW) gives «sāmudāyika: the eighteenth Nakṣatra after that in which the moon was situated at the birth of a child» and «vainaśika: the 23rd Nakṣatra from that under which any one is born, VarYogay.» (Varāhamihira's Yogayātrā). That fixes two of the six counts (18 and 23, so the 22nd of some lists is not WB's) in a published lexicon with a primary-text pointer. The counts 10, 16, 19 and 25 still stand on web pages only (the Brihat Parashara Hora Sastra of the archive.org copies read, R. Santhanam's and two others, has no such series: «Adhana» there is the conception lagna; the Hindi Muhurta Chintamani scan on archive.org (OCR) has no «आधान», «सांघातिक», «सामुदायिक» or «वैनाशिक» either). Narrowed: the six places are, by position and by two names, the Ādhānādi series; the other four are by position only, and the Tibetan names of 16, 18 and 19 are unexplained. The next source to look for: Varāhamihira's Yogayātrā (the verse MW cites).
