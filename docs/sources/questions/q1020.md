# Is the Padminī's verse of the bed rite (Derge vol. 13 p. 320) the Vimalaprabhā's own ritual text, or another work's, and whose?

RD361.

## Answered 2026-11-13

It is not the Vimalaprabhā's own text: the Vimalaprabhā's rite is prose (kcd.12:p228-229), the Padminī's lines are verse interleaved with its own prose, and on p. 323 the Padminī sends the extended rite to the Vimalaprabhā. The verse's author is not named on the pages (claim padmini-verse-rite-not-vimalaprabha-own-text).
