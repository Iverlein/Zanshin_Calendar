# The meaning of «རྟ་ཞག་སྔོན་པོ» and «ཐེ་ཁྱིམ་བཙུན་མོ»

What is the meaning of «རྟ་ཞག་སྔོན་པོ» (vol. 1, p. 460) and of «ཐེ་ཁྱིམ་བཙུན་མོ» (p. 462)?

RD166 (2026-11-06): «ཐེ་ཁྱིམ» is answered by the pool's claim. KD's Garland (p. 556) writes «རྟ་ཞོག» and «སྔོན་པོའི་ཕྱིར» where WB has «རྟ་ཞག» and «ཕྱི་ལ»; the sense is unsolved.

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-eee).** The dictionaries (Steinert's 64 sets, sparse clone) have no «rta zhag» or «rta zhog»; they give «zhag» as fat, grease, body oil, and as a day (Ives Waldo, Rangjung Yeshe, Valby: also "film on a liquid", "clot of blood", and the divisions of time «khyim zhag, nyin zhag, tshes zhag»), and «zhog» as the imperative of «'jog/bzhag», "put, let be, leave" (Rangjung Yeshe, Ives Waldo). So WB's «རྟ་ཞག» can be a "horse-fat" or "horse-day", and KD's «རྟ་ཞོག» only "horse, leave it"; neither gives a blue thing (BDRC finds no phrase «རྟ་ཞག་སྔོན་པོ»). The sense stays unsolved; no scan of KD p. 556 was read.
