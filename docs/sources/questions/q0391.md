# The meaning of «རྟ་ཞག་སྔོན་པོ» and «ཐེ་ཁྱིམ་བཙུན་མོ»

What is the meaning of «རྟ་ཞག་སྔོན་པོ» (vol. 1, p. 460) and of «ཐེ་ཁྱིམ་བཙུན་མོ» (p. 462)?

RD166 (2026-11-06): «ཐེ་ཁྱིམ» is answered by the pool's claim. KD's Garland (p. 556) writes «རྟ་ཞོག» and «སྔོན་པོའི་ཕྱིར» where WB has «རྟ་ཞག» and «ཕྱི་ལ»; the sense is unsolved.

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-eee).** The dictionaries (Steinert's 64 sets, sparse clone) have no «rta zhag» or «rta zhog»; they give «zhag» as fat, grease, body oil, and as a day (Ives Waldo, Rangjung Yeshe, Valby: also "film on a liquid", "clot of blood", and the divisions of time «khyim zhag, nyin zhag, tshes zhag»), and «zhog» as the imperative of «'jog/bzhag», "put, let be, leave" (Rangjung Yeshe, Ives Waldo). So WB's «རྟ་ཞག» can be a "horse-fat" or "horse-day", and KD's «རྟ་ཞོག» only "horse, leave it"; neither gives a blue thing (BDRC finds no phrase «རྟ་ཞག་སྔོན་པོ»). The sense stays unsolved; no scan of KD p. 556 was read.

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-last).** WB's own etext (Beijing, MW2CZ8040, VEIE0OPI51524892_I1KG12906 p. 470, found by BDRC phrase search «རྟ་ཞག», 55 hits) has the passage: in the eight horse omens by the pebbles, the fourth white «ལྟག་ཚར་སྤུ་མ་བུད» and the black «ལྟག་ཚར་སྤུ་བུད་དེ། རྟ་ཞག་སྔོན་པོའི་ཕྱི་ལ་ནི། གཡང་ནི་ཉམས་པར་བཤད་པས་ན། རྟ་ཡི་གཡང་ལོན་གཡང་སྒྲུབ་དོན།» ("... since the luck is said to be lost after the «rta zhag sngon po», the horse's luck is to be called back and made firm"). So the clause is about the horse's luck (g.yang) being damaged, and «ཕྱི་ལ» is the word of WB's etext. The phrase «རྟ་ཞག་སྔོན་པོ» itself has no hit elsewhere; the other BDRC hits for «རྟ་ཞག» are a Lam 'bras death-sign table (a count «རྟ་ཞག་གཅིག་རྒྱུ་ན་བཅུ་ནས། གཉིས་རྒྱུ་ན་ལོ་བརྒྱད་ནས»), where it is a unit between «ཉི་མ་གཅིག» and two, three and four, not a sense for the horse omen. «ཐེ་ཁྱིམ་བཙུན་མོ» (19 hits, e.g. Rin chen gter mdzod, Karma chags med's collected works) stands there as the earth lord «the khyim btsun mo» beside «the se rgyal po», as the RD166 claim has it. Still no gloss of «ཞག» + «སྔོན་པོ»; the scan was not read.
