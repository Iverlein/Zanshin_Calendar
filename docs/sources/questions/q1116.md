# What does ས་ནི་སྲོས་ནས་ནམ་ཕྱེད་བར་ mean in Marpa's dream-practice verses, and what text is it

Vol. 6 names the stretch «ས་ནི་སྲོས་ནས་ནམ་ཕྱེད་བར» as the field of Padma mgon po: is ས་ནི་སྲོས་ནས 'from the evening'?

## Answered 2026-11-13

Yes: «ས་ནི་སྲོས་ནས» is "from dusk". The dictionaries give sa sros (ས་སྲོས) as the name of the time not long after the sun has gone down (Dag tshig gsar bsgrigs, Bod yig tshig gter rgya mtsho; Ives Waldo and Rangjung Yeshe: at dusk, towards evening; Valby: «sa sros nas», when it grew dark) and sa srod as the time between sunset and midnight (tmz:box2). WB's list of the night's hours also has dusk (ས་སྲོས) between sunset and the evening round, before midnight (wb.1:p17b). So the line reads "from dusk until midnight is the field of the protector Padma"; the ni is the particle between the two words. The Rin chen gter mdzod's «ཉི་ནུབ་ས་སྲོས་བར་དུ» (RD397) is the same two words after sunset. What the line means for the practice, that the first part of the night belongs to Padma mgon po, is in rrd.25:p60-61 and is not explained further in any text read.
