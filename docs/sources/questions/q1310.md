# WB vol. 2 p. 236 line 19: the fourth item of Rāhu's reckoning, «བྱི» or «ཕྱི»?

The stack ending line 19 of p. 236, after «སྤྱི་འདོམས(༣)», is smudged, its first letter «བ» or «ཕ». Is the fourth item of Rāhu's reckoning «བྱི» or «ཕྱི» (outer, the pair of «ནང», inner (5))? The machine text and the older English skip number 4.

## Answered 2026-11-21

«ཕྱི», outer. The fourth item of Rāhu's reckoning is the outer, the first of the formula «ཕྱི་ནང་གསང་» (outer, inner, secret).

The list of p. 236 numbers its items in brackets, and the fifth and sixth are «ནང(༥)» and «གསང(༦)». That the three stand together as outer, inner, secret is the fixed formula of the published texts: «ཕྱི་ནང་གསང་བ་གསུམ» (the three, outer, inner and secret) opens the death-sign teaching of dds.9:p173; «ཕྱི་ནང་གསང་བའི་བར་ཆད» stands in sk.13:p258 and «ཕྱི་ནང་གསང་བའི་མཆོད་བཤམས» in tsl.28:i212-213, each time in this order. «བྱི» (a mouse) cannot stand before «ནང» and «གསང» in such a series, so the smudged first letter of the stack is the ཕ of «ཕྱི», and the machine text's «བྱི» and the older English's missing number 4 are wrong: the list has the outer (4) between the general counsel (3) and the inner (5), and all thirteen numbers of the print, 1 to 13, are used (wb.2:p236d, line 19). The page's glyph stays a smudge at the size read; the reading is the formula's, not the letter's.

Audit 2026-11-21 (aud-1): confirmed. The three cited places only show the formula «ཕྱི་ནང་གསང་» in other texts, so alone they carry the idiom, not the glyph; the answer is carried by the page's own order (3 general counsel, the stack, 5 inner, 6 secret) together with the Zhol print's etext (BDRC IE0OPI7850CC9C, image 958), which reads at this place «སྤྱིའི་དོམས་ཕྱི་ནང་གསེང» (OCR garbled, but «ཕྱི་ནང་» plain, no «བྱི»).
