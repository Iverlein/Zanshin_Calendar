# Which text is the confession with «ཉ་སྟོང་ཚེས་བརྒྱད་ལ་སོགས་དུས་ཀྱི་མཆོད་པ་ཆག» in the Sgo chen liturgy collection, and does "and the rest" include the tenth and the twenty-fifth?

Raised by the practice-days investigation (pd-e, 2026-11-16); see the cited passages.

## Answered 2026-11-16

The confession is the closing confession of the Stainless Confession Tantra, «ཞི་ཁྲོ་ན་རག་དོང་སྤྲུགས་རྣལ་འབྱོར་གྱི་སྤྱི་ཁྲུས་འགྱོད་ཚངས་ཀྱི་ཆོ་ག་དྲི་མེད་བཤགས་རྒྱུད», which begins on etext p. 580 of the collection (sgc:i580) and whose colophon on etext pp. 590–591 calls it the King of Remorse and Purification that Churns the Depths of Hell, taught by Padmasambhava to Ye shes mtsho rgyal as a continual practice, and a treasure of Nyang ral Nyi ma 'od zer (sgc:i590-591). It is a text common to the Nyingma school, not a treasure of Rtsa gsum gling pa's lineage. The like line on etext p. 568, "the offerings of the full moon, the new moon and the six times", stands in the fulfilment and confession of the Peaceful and Wrathful Ones joined from Guru Chos dbang's and the Northern Treasure's Eight Pronouncements (sgc:i568).

What "and the rest" covers the text does not say: neither confession names the tenth or the twenty-fifth, and the head of the tantra sets no day for its own recitation. Read in the etext only.
