# Is the Vāgbhaṭa Śārīrasthāna 6 passage in the Śabdakalpadruma the Aṣṭāṅgahṛdaya's, and what does its line on the early night and the cows' path say in an edition?

The middle syllable of vipāt is blurred on the print.

## Answered 2026-11-10

Answered by RD295: the passage is the Aṣṭāṅgahṛdaya's Śārīrasthāna 6.61–64 (Kunte, Bombay 1880, pp. 655–656). The word read as «[vi]pāt» is «cirāt» (fruit comes late for a dream of the early night); the cows' line is «gosarge tadaharmahat», at the time the cows are released.
