# Where does the Gaṇapati Śāstrī edition's chapter 15 of the Mañjuśrīmūlakalpa end, and does its printed pp. 158-160 carry «kṣaṇamātraṃ tathonmeṣa-nimeṣaṃ cāpi acchaṭam» (the Tibetan se gol) as the TSS 70 text layer suggests? A page image would settle the page and the reading.

Raised by the 2026-11-13 investigation b; see the question's cited passages.

## Answered 2026-11-14

Chapter 15 of Gaṇapati Śāstrī's edition ends at the foot of printed p. 165 and chapter 16 begins at the head of p. 166; the line «kṣaṇamātraṃ tathonmeṣanimeṣaṃ cāpi acchaṭam» is on the page image at p. 159, inside the range the text layer suggested. Read on the Internet Archive's page images of the Trivandrum Sanskrit Series 70 (leaf n = printed page + 5): p. 158 is leaf n163 (number «१५८» printed, running head of p. 157 «pañcadaśaḥ paṭalavisaraḥ»), the line closes the page of leaf n164 (p. 159 by count), and leaf n170 (p. 165) ends with the colophon, followed on leaf n171 by «atha ṣoḍaśaḥ paṭalavisaraḥ». The print reads «kṣaṇamātraṃ tathonmeṣanimeṣaṃ cāpi acchaṭam» with the unmeṣa spelling the text layer lacked (its OCR gave «ommeṣa»); «acchaṭam» is clear on the page, so the Tibetan se gol has a Sanskrit word in the edition. The colophon names the chapter «trayodaśamaḥ sarvakarmakriyārthaḥ», the thirteenth, against the running heads' fifteenth: the colophon's ordinal is not the chapter number. Passages mmk.2:p159 and mmk.2:p165-166.
