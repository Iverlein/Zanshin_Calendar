# The thirteen who may not be offered food

«ཟན་གཏད་མི་རུང་བཅུ་གསུམ་ནི། །ཟན་བརྣངས་ལར་ན་བརྣངས་ནས་ཤི། །ཟན་གཏང་མི་རུང་བཅུ་གསུམ་མོ།» (p. 69) names only one person who may not be offered food (one who choked on food). Who are the other twelve? The notes (p. 503) only reorder the phrase.

**Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-d).** BDRC phrase search: «ཟན་གཏད་མི་རུང་» 2 hits, «ཟན་གཏང་མི་རུང་» 1, «ཟན་བརྣངས་ལར་ན» 1, «ཟན་བརྣངས» 3, «བརྣངས་ནས་ཤི» 9, «འདུར་མི་རུང་བཅུ་གསུམ» 0; the hits are the verse itself in the two WB etexts (MW2CZ8040, MW3CN5026) and the notes' line (p. 503, which only turns the phrase round). «བརྣངས་ནས་ཤི» elsewhere is a kind of death (choked, smothered: Po to pa's *Be'u bum* commentary, a Bu ston text, a rebirth manual), not a list of thirteen. One poor-OCR print of the *Vaidurya dkar po* (MW4CZ74207) has in a verse on corpses that cannot be buried the words «skyes bsubs zan brnangs nu mas bsubs … dud pas bsubs pa dad // kun gyi zla da ra 'bring la // shi ba rnams ni sdu mi rung», so «zan brnangs» (choked on food) stands among kinds of death smothered or choked there too; the OCR is too broken to take the list from it. The twelve others are not named by any text found.
