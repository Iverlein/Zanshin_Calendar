# The Lord Lama's replies and the iron bird and monkey

Reply 137 cites "the precious Lord Lama's replies on the reckoning of stars and the Chinese reckoning" («རྗེ་བླ་མའི་རྩིས་སྐར་ནག་གི་དྲི་ལན»), which say the thirty are "turned from upon the iron bird and monkey": whose replies are these, where are they found, and is the iron bird and monkey a fixed starting pair or an example?

**Narrowed 2026-11-01 (RD104, [claims/lord-lama-replies-on-star-and-chinese-reckonings.md](../claims/lord-lama-replies-on-star-and-chinese-reckonings.md)).** The work is cited a second time, by reply 156 (ys.2:i016, machine), under the same title and the same mark of honour but another epithet, «༧རྣམ་འདེན་མཆོག» ("the Supreme Guide"): no author's name stands in either place, so whose replies they are is not read from the YS. On the second half: reply 137 itself gives the pair as the stated point of turning for the thirty of China and Tibet («ལྕགས་བྱ་སྤྲེལ་སྟེང་ནས་བསྐོར»), takes it as the pointer to the source of the treatise's way of reckoning, and adds the male or female of the reckoned person's year as the treatise's own division; it gives no other pair and does not call the iron bird and monkey an example.
