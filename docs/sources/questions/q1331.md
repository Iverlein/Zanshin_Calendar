# WB ch. 1 p. 10 lines 26-28 (after the bracketed figure): what do they say?

The lines begin «ཞག་སྟོང་མཐའ་ཡས…» and follow the bracketed figure; reprint img. 17.

## Answered 2026-11-21

Lines 26–28 give the number-words of the second bracketed figure and what follows from it, and the first of them begins «ཐིག་སྟོང་མཁའ་ཁྱིམ…», not «ཞག་སྟོང་མཐའ་ཡས».

Read by eye on 2026-11-18 (y10, quarter-lines at 4×, the doubtful syllables at 6–9×) and checked again 2026-11-21 (rd-t) against the figure itself. The lines are «ཐིག་སྟོང་མཁའ་ཁྱིམ་གཟུགས་ཐིག་བུག། གཏེར་ཀླུ་ཐུབ་རྩ་བུག་མཁའ་མིག། འབྱུང་ཀླུ་རྩ་དང་མི་བདག་བུག། ཆུ་ཐུབ་ཡོན་མཚོ་ཀླུ་ཐིག་མེ། ཟུང་སྟེ་གནས་ནི་ཉི་ཤུ་དགུར། ངེས་པའི་སྟོང་པར་འཇུག་པར་བཞེད། དེ་དག་དུས་བཞི་ཆེན་པོ་སྟེ། ཆུང་ངུའི་དུས་བཞིར་མི་ཡི་ལོ། ཉིས་ཁྲི་ཆིག་སྟོང་དྲུག་བརྒྱར་བཤད། ནུས་པའི་ཉིན་» (to p. 11's «ཞག་གཅིག་ཡིན་ལ»): dot, void, sky, house, form, dot, hole; treasure, naga, sage, root, hole, sky, eye; element, naga, root, lord of men, hole; water, sage, [yon], ocean, naga, dot, fire; "paired, the places are twenty-nine; they are held to enter the certain void. These are the four great times; the human years of the four small times are said to be twenty-one thousand six hundred; the day of the capable one…". Worked as word-numerals from the lowest place (thig 0, stong 0, mkha' 0, khyim 12, gzugs 1, thig 0, bug 9 | gter 9, klu 8, thub 7, rtsa 9, bug 9, mkha' 0, mig 2 | 'byung 5, klu 8, rtsa 9, mi bdag 16, bug 9 | chu 4, thub 7, yon, mtsho 4, klu 8, thig 0, me 3 | zung 2; mi bdag is 16 by the Kar-rtsis line of ch. 5 and ch. 19, «བུག་མཁའ་ … མི་བདག(༡༦༠༩)») they make exactly 29 places, as the verse says, and agree with the 29 printed numerals in every place but the 1/9 pairs of the print and one ༢ against ༣ (see q1332), so the words read on the scan are the right words.
