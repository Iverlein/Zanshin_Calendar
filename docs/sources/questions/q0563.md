# The five columns of a meadow's ornament group

In the meadow's ornament leaves, does WB's «ཁ་དམར་ལྔ» of each group stand one for each of the five aspects in the order of the paragraph on the results (life force, body, power, luck, mewa), and are the five columns of a group one year's own five forces or five animals of a kind (the notes, vol. 2, pp. 533–534)? See verdicts.md, RD121.

RD179 (2026-11-06): no new witness. Part one (one verdict per aspect) stays as RD121 left it; part two (a group's five as one year's forces or five animals of a kind, KD against Mipham) is open.

## Answered 2026-11-14

Answered as far as the texts go: the five verdicts of a group are one for each of the five aspects, and the five columns of a group are one year's own five forces (KD's reading), with Mipham's «ཁྱི་ལྔ» the same five spoken as one animal in five elements; no text sets five different animals as the columns.

Zhechen Gyaltsab's verse on the same leaves (zg:p300-301, already in the pool, read on the scan) says it in so many words, and the pool had taken it for the sums only. «གསེར་ལོ་ཅན་ནི་གནམ་ལོ་གང་ཤར་དེའི། །སྲོག་ལུས་དབང་ཀླུང་སྨེ་བ་རྣམ་པ་ལྔར། །བརྩི་བྱའི་ལྔས་འཐབ་གཅོད་འདྲལ་ཉེར་ལྔ་ལ། །ཁ་དམར་ལྔ་བཅས་སུམ་ཅུ་དེ་བཞིན་དུ། །གནམ་ལོའི་མར་གྱི་བདུན་ཟུར་དྲུག་འགྲམས་དང་། །བཞི་གཤེད་ལྔས་འཐབ་སུམ་ཅུ་སུམ་ཅུ་སྟེ།»: in the gold year, whichever sky year has risen, its life force, body, power, luck and mewa, "five kinds", are fought by the reckoned person's five: twenty-five fighting pebbles; with the five verdicts thirty; likewise the sky year's seventh, the six neighbours and the fourth executioner, "each fought by five", thirty and thirty. So a group is one year (the sky year, then its seventh, its six neighbours, its fourth executioner) taken by its own five forces against the reckoned person's five, 5 x 5 = 25, and the five verdicts are added to that group of five forces: the five columns are the group's year's own five forces, not five years and not five animals of a kind. This is KD's sheet (the wood mouse's own forces as column letters) and it agrees with WB's paragraph on the results (life force for the adversities of life, body for illness, power for wealth, luck for merit, mewa for gods and demons, «དཀར་ནག་ཁ་དམར་དང་བསྟུན་བརྗོད»), with Sprugtön's «སྲོག་ལུས་དབང་ཀླུང་བླ་ལྔ་པོར། །དཀར་ནག་བགྲངས་པའི་ཁ་དམར་རེ» and with the Zhechen verse's own later lines, where the verdicts are "examined by life, illness, wealth, merit and demons, and gathered into the five general totals".

Beside it, BDRC's phrase search «ཁ་དམར་ལྔ» (13 hits, 2026-11-14; snippets read) gives the same distribution in four manuals that quote the verse, search-only etexts and so witnesses by snippet only: *Bod kyi rtsis rig kun 'dus chen mo* and *Yul mdo khams stod kyi mkhas grub rnam pa'i gsung bcud bdud rtsi'i thigs phreng* (BDRC's Wylie: «klung sme ba lnga / re re la yang brtsi bya yi / srog lus dbang klung sme ba lngas / 'thabs pa'i rde'u nyer lnga dang / kha dmar lnga ste sum cu'o / de bzhin gnam lo'i bdun zur dang / mar gyi drug 'gras bzhi gshed kyi»), and two *rdel 'grem* manuals (*Nag rtsis rdel 'grem 'bras bshad*, *Rgya rtsis dge ldan mkhas pa'i phyag rgyun du bstar ba'i rdel 'grem dpag bsam ljon shing*) that call the same twenty-five "the five kinds of fighting pebbles" ('thab pa'i rde'u rnam pa lnga) and the verdicts (the same lines as Sprugtön's, in BDRC's Wylie «srog lus dbang rlung bla lnga po'i / dkar nag bgrang ba'i kha dmar lnga») and add the five kinds' pebble counts (rang keg, bdun zur, mthun gsum, bzhi gshed, rtsub pa keg rtsis). Those manuals' notation (a number after each kind) was not read.

What is not said by any text: which of the five verdicts of a group sits under which column is read from the order (life force, body, power, luck, mewa), as the paragraph of results gives it; no sentence writes "the first verdict is the life force's".
