# «མཛེས་ཅན་མིན་པའི་མི» on p. 278

On WB vol. 2 p. 278 (img. 284) is the thumb-sized bone in the earth-deposit vase that of «a person not of beauty» or of «a person not a leper» («མཛེས་ཅན་མིན་པའི་མི»)?

RD183 (2026-11-06), narrowed: the print (p. 278) reads «མཛེས་ཅན» with the ས, literally 'not beautiful'; a leper reading would need «མཛེ་ཅན»; «mdze can» appears only in a search snippet of the Bzo rtsis zlos gar, perhaps OCR.
