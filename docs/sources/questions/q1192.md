# Does a Kagyu catalogue (a dkar chag of the Bka' brgyud sngags mdzod or the Lho brag pa collection) name the writer of the history of the four sheets (dds.40:p207-209, q1064)?

Raised by the 2026-11-13 investigation n.

## Narrowed 2026-11-13 (inv-s)

BDRC's phrase search for «ཤོག་དྲིལ་བཞིའི་ལོ་རྒྱུས» lists the history in the Gdams ngag mdzod (MW23605), the Bka' brgyud sngags mdzod (MW20876, MW20877), the 'Brug lugs chos mdzod chen mo (MW23779), the 'Bri lugs bang mdzod skor lnga (MW3KG218), a Chos kyi blo gros gsung 'bum, a Bde mchog snyan rgyud collection of twenty texts and a 'Brug pa'i gdams zab collection. In each the part is titled «rje btsun lho brag pa'i khyad par gyi gdams pa snyan gyi shog dril bzhi'i lo rgyus gzhung lhan thabs dang bcas pa», and the work records (WA0XLF2F780077D61, WA0XL33216E5F7BC4) carry no author; the compilers named are of the collections (Kong sprul for MW20876). The dkar chag of none of these was read (not in the etext search).
Still open: the dkar chag of the Bka' brgyud sngags mdzod or the 'Brug lugs chos mdzod, and the Chos kyi blo gros volume.

## Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-v)

No dkar chag reached, but the text itself was read for a colophon: the Gdams ngag mdzod etext volume VE23605_008 (fetched with bdrc.py etext), title line at [203] «rje btsun lho brag pa'i khyad par gyi gdams pa snyan gyi shog dril bzhi'i lo rgyus gzhung lhan thabs dang bcas pa bzhugs so», the history from [204] «na mo gu ru / rje btsun mar pa lho brag pa'i khyad par gyi gdams pa snyan gyi shog dril bzhi'i lo rgyus mi bla ma 'di la …». It runs through Mar pa and the tale of the Dol mtshur ston dbang nge, the Dbus ras shog dril and the Sum ras, and ends «… 'bum gcig phul ba las dga' gsungs nas bzhad skad do» with no colophon and no writer: the next words are «rgya gar skad du / tri+na pra+Na dza la tsakra / bod skad du / rtsa rlung dra mig 'khor lo» (the root text's Indian-language title), so the history is an unsigned introduction to the Lhan thabs in this witness. The question's catalogue half stays open; the writer is not named in the text of this volume.

## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-o)

Two dkar chags and two more witnesses read, by etext (bdrc.py etext), and none names a writer. (1) The dkar chag of the 'Bri lugs gdams ngag bang mdzod vol. 2 (BDRC etext VE1ER71, [p4]-[p8]) lists the history as item 24, «རྗེ་བཙུན་ལྷོ་བྲག་པའི་ཁྱད་པར་གྱི་གདམས་པ་སྙན་གྱི་ཤོག་དྲིལ་བཞིའི་ལོ་རྒྱུས་གཞུང་ལྷན་ཐབས་དང་བཅས་པ» at p. 607, with no author after the title, where the item before it (23, the lineage prayer «སྙན་གྱི་ཤོག་དྲིལ་བཞིའི་བརྒྱུད་རིམ་གསོལ་འདེབས») is marked «རིག་འཛིན་ཆོས་གྲགས་གསུང་»; that prayer's colophon (etext [p610]-[p611]) names Chos kyi grags pa (Rig 'dzin Chos grags) as its writer, a different text from the history. (2) The 'Brug lugs chos mdzod chen mo (BDRC etext VEIE0OPIDDB781DE_I3CN2313): its dkar chag ([p6]-[p7]) lists the history at pp. 479-502 (25 folios) with no author, and the text (etext [p511]-[p521]) is bare of colophon; the colophon just before it (etext [p510]) is of the preceding text, by Chos kyi grags pa through the scribe Bkra shis dges pa. (3) In both witnesses the history, like the Gdams ngag mdzod's (inv-v), runs from «ན་མོ་གུ་རུ། རྗེ་བཙུན་མར་པ་ལྷོ་བྲག་པའི་ཁྱད་པར་གྱི་གདམས་པ…ལོ་རྒྱུས་ནི» to «…འབུམ་གཅིག་ཕུལ་བ་ལས་དགའ་གསུངས་ནས་བཞད་སྐད་དོ» and passes straight to the root text's Indian-language title, with no «བྱས་པ» line. The narrator speaks once in the first person («དེ་ལ་བདག་གིས་ཞུས་ཏེ», «I asked», etext VE1ER71 [p623]-[p624]) without saying who he is, so the text does not name its writer in the three Tibetan witnesses. Not reached: the dkar chags of the Bka' brgyud sngags mdzod (MW20876, MW20877: scans only, eight volumes, the volume with the history unknown) and the Chos kyi blo gros volume. Still narrowed.
