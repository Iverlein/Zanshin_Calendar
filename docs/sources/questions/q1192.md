The dkar chag of none of these was read (not in the etext search).

## Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-v)

No dkar chag reached, but the text itself was read for a colophon: the Gdams ngag mdzod etext volume VE23605_008 (fetched with bdrc.py etext), title line at [203] «rje btsun lho brag pa'i khyad par gyi gdams pa snyan gyi shog dril bzhi'i lo rgyus gzhung lhan thabs dang bcas pa bzhugs so», the history from [204] «na mo gu ru / rje btsun mar pa lho brag pa'i khyad par gyi gdams pa snyan gyi shog dril bzhi'i lo rgyus mi bla ma 'di la …». It runs through Mar pa and the tale of the Dol mtshur ston dbang nge, the Dbus ras shog dril and the Sum ras, and ends «… 'bum gcig phul ba las dga' gsungs nas bzhad skad do» with no colophon and no writer: the next words are «rgya gar skad du / tri+na pra+Na dza la tsakra / bod skad du / rtsa rlung dra mig 'khor lo» (the root text's Indian-language title), so the history is an unsigned introduction to the Lhan thabs in this witness. The question's catalogue half stays open; the writer is not named in the text of this volume.
