# Is the «སྲང» before «ཀཊའི» in the p. 51 verse on the length of the day really «གླང» (Taurus)?

Raised by the 2026-11-13 investigation h.

## Answered 2026-11-14

Yes. Read and computed, 2026-11-14 (inv-ff).

Read on the page: on the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5028 img. 58, scan 2072 px, the line cropped at 3×) the stack before «ཀཊའི» in the line «ཆུ་སྲང་མཁའ་མིག(༣༢/༢༠)སྲང་ཀཊའི» has the letter ས with a subjoined ར, the same shape as the «སྲང» of «ཆུ་སྲང» in the same line; the print has «སྲང» (srang, the Scales), and the pool's reading of the page and of the 1996 print has it so (wb.1:p51, RD206). It is not «གླང» on the page, but it is a misprint, for three reasons.

Computed: the page lists seven day-lengths in chu tshod over chu srang for eleven names: Fish and Maiden 30; Ram and Lion 31;10; «སྲང་» and Crab 32;20; Pair 33;30; Jar and Scales 28;50; Sea-creature and Scorpion 27;40; Archer 26;30. The Scales (སྲང, srang) are already in the Jar's line («བུམ་སྲང་གཉིས»), so a second «སྲང» there would name the Scales twice; and the list then has eleven names, with the Bull (གླང, glang, Taurus) missing. The engine's day length (DayTimes.dayLength, from ch. 15 p. 182: 30 at the equinoxes, 1;10 more or less each sign-month), evaluated at the twelve sgang terms of the months 2 to 1 (SUN_TERMS), gives 30;00, 31;10, 32;20, 33;30, 32;20, 31;10, 30;00, 28;50, 27;40, 26;30, 27;40, 28;50 (computed, 2026-11-14): the run of the verse, in the order Fish, Ram, Bull, Pair, Crab, Lion, Maiden, Scales, Scorpion, Archer, Sea-creature, Jar, symmetrical about the Pair and the Archer; the third and fifth places are both 32;20, and the verse's «སོ» (32) and «མཁའ་མིག» (20) are that figure. The twelve signs are all there but the Bull, which the line must name.

Read in other texts (BDRC phrase search «མཁའ་མིག་གླང», 4 hits, snippets): the line stands with «གླང» in the Kun 'dus chen mo (MW28845, «chu srang mkha' mig glang dang karka Ta'»), in the collected works of Bstan 'dzin chos kyi rgyal mtshan (MW3JT13322, «chu srang mkha' mig glang kar+Ta'i / nyin mo'i tshad yin chu tshod du / yon tan rtse mo thig le'i srang», the verse of WB with the Bull), in the Do shal commentary (MW2PD17386, «ma sring mkha' mig glang karba'i», with the OCR garbled around it) and, in another verse list of the signs' lengths, in 'Jigs med phun tshogs' skar rtsis commentary (MW00KG03976, «glang kar mkho' ro ri thub pa»). The searches «ཆུ་སྲང་མཁའ་མིག་སྲང་ཀཊའི» and «སྲང་ཀཊའི» find no etext at all (WB's print has the figures in brackets in between). The Taikhang or Zhol image of the line was not cropped.

So the line is «ཆུ་སྲང་མཁའ་མིག་གླང་ཀཊའི» in meaning: the Bull and the Crab, 32;20; the print's «སྲང» is a slip for «གླང», which these copies read correctly.
