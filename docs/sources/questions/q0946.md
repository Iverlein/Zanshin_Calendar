# Are the Dbyangs 'char's eight half-watches of the day-and-night equal?

RD322.

## Answered 2026-11-10

Answered by RD326 (2026-11-10), on etexts: the Dbyangs 'char verse (in the 'Bri lugs bang mdzod vol. 39, the Rnam dpyod blo gros spyan stong bkra ba, etext p. 26 = printed p. 17) says only 'cut into eight'; the later books count 4 great watches by day and 4 by night, each halved, so 8 half-watches by day and 8 by night (16 in all), each an eighth of the day's or the night's measure, equal within the day and within the night; the Kun 'dus chen mo, quoting Mchog dga', gives four chu tshod each; the Kālacakra commentary Dri med 'od has 8 great watches halved into 16. This corrects RD322's reading of eight in all.
