# The weekday lines of the 2nd, 3rd and 6th month heads

In the 2nd month's weekday line the figures read dar 1, 3; gud 6; skyod 0; zhud 2, 4, which leaves the weekday 5 out, while the 5th month's line (dar 3, 1, 0; gud 2, 4; skyod 6; zhud 5) gives the seven once each. Is the 5 of the 2nd month's line lost or misprinted, and what are the 3rd head's dar figures and the 6th's gud, skyod and zhud figures? (RD201, corpus/wb/ch13.md)

## Answered 2026-11-12

Read on the scan (RD201, third pass): the 2nd head lacks 5 again on a second 3x reading, so the gap is in the print. The 3rd head: dar 0, 6; gud 5; skyod 2, 4; zhud 1, 3. The 6th head: dar 5, 3, 1; zhud 2, 4; gud 6; skyod 0, with the braces in the order dar, zhud, gud, skyod; the labels are told apart only by the loop under the first. The 1st head wraps gud 2, 4.
