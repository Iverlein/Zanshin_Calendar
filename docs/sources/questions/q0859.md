# What is meant by the «rme can» (རྨེ་ཅན; Byang pa and the Drikung རྗེ་ཅན) that rings the dreamer with crows and hungry ghosts?

Left untranslated.

## Answered 2026-11-09

རྨེ་ཅན (rme can) is a person with a black birthmark on the face (Bod yig tshig gter rgya mtsho, citing the Rgyud bzhi'i bka' 'grel of Dpal spungs Dbon Karma bstan 'dzin 'phrin las rab rgyas and the glossary of De'u dmar Dge bshes; the medical dictionary Bod lugs gso rig tshig mdzod chen mo likewise, with རྨེ་ཤ་ཅན, rme sha can, a base-born butcher caste as the other sense in the general dictionaries). Byang pa's own paraphrase writes «རྨེ་ཅན་རྣམས» with the plural while his verse and the Drikung have རྗེ་ཅན, a variant spelling. The dreamer is ringed with crows, hungry ghosts and such people. Which of the two senses the verse means stays open (the first is the medical one). See gt-rme-can-a-man-with-a-black-birthmark.
