# «དགྲ་དར» or «དགྲ་ལོ» in the special verdict

In the special verdict the enemy year is printed «དགྲ་དར» (p. 54, l. 3, dots under it), where the half-and-half list has «དགྲ་ལོ». Is «དར» a misprint, or does it name a different place?

**Narrowed 2026-11-01 (RD104, [claims/regent-dgra-dar-black-and-white-in-reply-148.md](../claims/regent-dgra-dar-black-and-white-in-reply-148.md)).** The regent takes «དགྲ་དར» over unchanged in his restating of the second examination (reply 148, ys:i506-508, on the scan: «སྲོག་ལ་དགྲ་དར…», black), so the word is a term of the dead's reckoning; the print's «དར» at p. 54 is probably no misprint. Whether it names there the same thing as «དགྲ་ལོ» of the half-and-half list is not said.

**Narrowed 2026-11-05 (RD164, [reckonings.md](../reckonings.md)).** «དགྲ་དར» is one of a regular series (WB vol. 1, p. 363, machine reading only), so a misprint is unlikely.
