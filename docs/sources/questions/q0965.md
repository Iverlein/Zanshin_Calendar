# Which colours of light go with humans and demi-gods in Bu ston's rite (the machine reads green for humans and yellow for demi-gods)?

RD329; to be settled on the scan by a reader of the hand.

## Narrowed 2026-11-17 (inv-g)

A witness for the pairing from outside Bu ston's rite: in the Ras chung snyan brgyud collection (MW1AC375, Ras chung Rdo rje grags pa, good-OCR etext; BDRC exact phrase «ལྷ་མིན་ལྗང་གུ», 1 hit) the six paths are given by the passion that leads to each: hatred to hell, desire to the hungry ghosts' red path, dullness to the animals' white path, «phrag dog lha min ljang gu» (envy: the demi-gods' green path), and pride, which makes the gods and humans one «lam ser po» (yellow path). So in that Bka' brgyud text the demi-gods are green and the humans share yellow with the gods, which is the common pairing and the reverse of the machine reading («green human, yellow demi-god») that stands in all three prints of the rite (claim dream-texts-second-prints-rd332). This does not say what Bu ston's print has; it makes a scribal or machine swap of the two words the likelier cause only if the scan shows it, and the question stays a scan question. Other exact phrases for the six colours in a row («ལྷ་མ་ཡིན་ལྗང་ཁུ», «ལྷ་མིན་ལྗང་ཁུ», «ལྷ་མ་ཡིན་ལྗང་གུ», «ལྷ་མ་ཡིན་ལྗང་»): 0 hits.
