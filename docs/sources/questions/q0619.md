# «བྱན་ཚང» on pp. 287 and 294

What is «བྱན་ཚང» (byan tshang) on WB vol. 2 p. 287 (img. 293) and p. 294 (img. 300)? It has a door turned east and a yard to the south, it stands at the west by the power-work, and a lotus with the heart of dependent origination on its door keeps thieves away. An earlier summary said stable, without ground in the lines.

RD183 (2026-11-06), narrowed: the scan (reprint img. 282/293) confirms the spelling «བྱན་ཚང»; a building or room of the house with its own door, in the west with the power-works, kept against thieves; the Rmad byung restates it: the goat-head and ram-head seals hang on its door.
