# Is the entry for the Examination of Dreams in the Narthang catalogue's images 3–140 or 207–288

Not seen in images 39, 154 and 141–206 (I2KG212509); the scan cache holds about 175 images of the group, cut off.

## Answered 2026-11-13

In the odd images, within 3–140: img. 77 of I2KG212509 (vol. 225, printed folio 75) lists the Examination of Dreams (རྨི་ལམ་བརྟག་པ) directly after the Deceiving Death in 360 verses, Vibhūticandra's own translation, no maker named (svo:i77; RD406, machine reading by Yigdzin-1 on the whole image, not read syllable by syllable). RD406 and RD407 (img. 3–140) found no second entry, so there is one entry, not two; img. 39 and 154, 141–206 and 208–240 hold none (RD401, RD365, RD404). The even images 208–238 and 241–288 need not be read for this entry. Duplicate of q1146 and answered with it; the claim is narthang-catalogue-img-77-examination-of-dreams-after-the-deceiving-death.
