# Why does the Gdams ngag mdzod's teaching name the midnight dream (ནམ་ཕྱེད) as the place of the death-sign images, when the Vajrāvalī rite says the middle watches give no dream, the Karmapa's saying (after Marpa) makes the midnight dream the power of spirits, and the Four Tantras name only the early night and dawn: is nam phyed there «the midnight dream» or a way of saying «the night's dream»?

RD374.

## Answered 2026-11-13

Narrowed 2026-11-13: the word cannot be read as "the night's dream" by the dictionaries. nam phyed is, in every keyed dictionary, midnight, one of the twelve double-hours of the day-and-night (the rat's in the Tshig mdzod chen mo, the ox's in the Bod yig tshig gter rgya mtsho), or in Negi «half of the night», and none gives it the sense of the night as a whole (tmz:box5). So «ནམ་ཕྱེད་ཀྱི་རྨི་ལམ» in the Gdams ngag mdzod (dds.9:p173-174) is the dream of midnight in the word's own sense, unless the teaching uses it loosely, which no entry shows; the question why it names the midnight dream when the Vajrāvalī rite says the middle watches give no dream, and the Karmapa's saying (after Marpa) gives the midnight dream to the spirits, is not answered: that the text takes it from the transference teaching of Marpa's snyan gyi shog dril bzhi, with the death signs of the sick yogin, is all the passage says, and no commentary on it has been found.
