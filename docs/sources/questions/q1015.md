# In the 清明秘鈔夢判事's ten cells (mdz:i65134) what are the second lines of each cell (the place and manner of sending the dream away), and why do 子 and 丑 have no cell?

RD364.

## Answered 2026-11-13

Narrowed. Read again at 6000 px (Koizumi ORMK-00651, image 34) and on a second copy (Tohoku THKK-22631, image 35, 6240 px), the same cells: every cell opens "on a day of [branch] the person who has a bad dream (悪夢, with the gloss あくむ) ..." with the branch's gloss (とら, たつ, うま, さる, いぬ; う, み, ひつじ, とり, い), a second line that in ten cells begins with 墨 (gloss すみ) or 朱 (in the 午 cell) and ends 去べし or 流 (the glosses さる, ごとく), and in the 未 cell 房 (ねや) の門 (かど). The cells therefore differ in the thing (ink, vermilion) and the place (door, bedchamber, water) and share one formula of sending the dream away. The cursive middle of each second line is not read letter by letter (墨ゆく... in most of them) and stays open. Why 子 and 丑 have no cell: nothing on the leaf or in the neighbouring text says; the book gives no reason, and the leaf's box covers any dream whatever the day (read in part).
