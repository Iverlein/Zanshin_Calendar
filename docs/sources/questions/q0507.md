# The stack «བརྗེ» or «བནྡེ» on p. 23 and p. 17: a monk's set or the spiritual friend's

Is the stack of p. 23, l. 22 and l. 25 and of p. 17, l. 6 the block's «བརྗེ» or «བནྡེ» (a monk), and does the set of the four reckonings in four then belong to the monk, as against Zhechen Gyaltsab's spiritual friend? BDRC's etext reads «བནྡེ» (a machine reading); RD89 read «བརྗེ» on the 1996 scan at 9–10 times; the reprint at 4 times does not settle it.

RD178 (2026-11-07): Narrowed: on the Zhol print (img. 659, line 1, 8×) the stack of the black power verse reads as «བརྗེ» (flat head, e, the tail of ཇ), not an «ནྡ» stack; Yigdzin-1's «བནྡེ» on the Zhol print is a machine reading. The other places (p. 23 l. 22, p. 17 l. 6) not looked at by eye.
