# The Taikhang's «བེ་ལུགས་རུ་བལྟ» against «བེ་ལུག་ས་རུ་བལྟ»

The Taikhang's «བེ་ལུགས་རུ་བལྟ» (Yigdzin-1, img. 450) against the print's «བེ་ལུག་ས་རུ་བལྟ»: is the syllable after «ལུག» one with «ས» (the genitive-of-place, as read) or «ལུགས» (way, manner), when the line is read on the block by eye?

**Narrowed 2026-11-03 (sweep from RD134).** RD134 (ROADMAP, 2026-11-03): by eye the Taikhang has one syllable «བེ་ལུགས་རུ», with no tsheg between ག and ས; which word it is stays open.
