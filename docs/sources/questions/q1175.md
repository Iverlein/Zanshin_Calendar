# Do the 夢合延寿袋大成 copies of Tohoku (22629, 22653) and the Edo-Tokyo Museum (11383, 天保6) and the 夢合延寿袋絵抄's running text hold any wording on a day or a night for telling a dream, in the 悪夢の伝 or 夢祭り sections?

Raised by the 2026-11-13 investigation c; see the question's cited passages.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-zzz)

The contents (目録) of the Edo-Tokyo Museum copy (11383, 天保6) was read by eye on images 5-6 at 3000 px (mdz:i1138301): the sections are the dream objects, 雑の部, 夢現幻の伝, 悪しき夢をけすでん, よい夢をまつるでん, 思ふ事を夢にみるでん, the ways to fall asleep and wake, 五夢の伝, 見徳夢占 and 一切のうらない, with no entry for a day or a night for telling a dream. The Tohoku copy 22629 (bid 100445302, 72 images) has the same contents and preface on the same images (contact sheet, images 3-6). The Tohoku copy 22653 (bid 100445324, 73 images, publisher 東都書肆 金幸堂) is another issue: its contents (image 5) end at 一切のうらない, leaf 77, and a leaf headed 安部清明八卦占 with hexagram marks (image 6 on) adds a fortune by the month, day, hour and year numbers added together and divided by eight, a divination for one's own fortune and not a rule on telling a dream; read in part at 2800 px. The running text of leaves 60-70 (悪夢の伝, 夢祭り) was not read through in these three copies; the NDL OCR of the 1814 print (pid 2533938, all 74 images, searched for 語, かたら, 咄, 二日, 初夢 and 甲子) gives 初夢 only in the first article (Fuji) and nothing for a day of silence. The 夢合延寿袋絵抄 was not opened again.
