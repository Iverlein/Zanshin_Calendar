# Are the second and fourth band labels of WB vol. 1 p. 44 (img. 51) Mercury and Venus, and is the Venus word «པ་སངས»?

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-scan)

Looked at on the reprint (I3CN5028 img. 051, label cells at 3x). The five band labels of p. 44 read, from the top: «དམར» (Mars, with the lower sub-band beside it), «ལྷག» (the stack with ག: Mercury, ལྷག་པ short), «ཕུར» (Jupiter), «སངས» (Venus) and «སྤེན» (Saturn), the order Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn. The Venus label shows no «པ་» before it and its first letter is ས or ལ (the print's ས and ལ are close); so the planets are as the question supposes, the word is «སངས», not «པ་སངས», on this print. Confidence medium (single eye).
