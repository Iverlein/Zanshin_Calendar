# In the passage about conception dreams (tsl.30:i38) what do «སུན་ཀ» and, in the diviner's words, «ལན་འདུག» mean? Is the reading «a harm» and «the place has a debt» right?

RD363.

Narrowed 2026-11-13: «སུན་ཀ» is read as a harm: in i37-38 the people's «བྱད་ཁ» (a sorcery) is met by «བྱད་བཟློག» and the household's «སུན་ཀ» by «སུན་ཟློག» done by the mantra-holders, the same pattern. «ལན་འདུག» is read with «ལན» as the creditor's claim of «ལན་ཆགས», which the volumes name among the debts and obstacles (and «བོན་བྱད་ལན», i505). Both come from the words' company in the text, not from a dictionary (locate.py dict needs a 4.5 GB clone; BDRC's search could not be run, its Chromium missing). On the scan (vol. 30 img. 38) «སུན» is the same stack in both words; the line of «ལན་འདུག» was not read on the scan. Claim tsl-sun-ka-and-lan-in-the-conception-passage.
