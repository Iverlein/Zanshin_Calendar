# The first year of p. 23: 2587 or 2567 (the Kālacakra taught when the Buddha passed?)

p. 23, line 2: the first year of the page reads «༢༥༨༧» on both machine readings, but the chain 2592 − 25 gives 2567, and 2567 is the year that the text's own reckoning gives for the Buddha's passing (2647 less 80 years = 2567, if the verse is read as the passing: «གཟུགས་སྐུའི་རྣམ་པ་བསྡུས་ནས», "having gathered in the form body"). Is the print's figure ༢༥༦༧ (the machines confusing ༦ and ༨) and does the line say that the Kalacakra was taught in the year the Buddha passed? (RD187, corpus/wb/ch03.md)

## Answered 2026-11-07

Read on the sharp reprint (I3CN5028 img. 30) at 4×, RD197: the figure prints «༢༥༨༧», 2587, not «༢༥༦༧». Its third digit has the shape of the last digit of 2648 (༨), not the bowl shape of ༦ in 2566, 2564 and 2563 on the same page, and its fourth is the looped ༧. So the machines read it rightly, and it is a slip of the print against the chain (2592 − 25 = 2567, and 2566, 2564, 2563 follow from 2567). The clause names the Kalacakra's teaching at Dhanyakataka in the month Saga, at midnight of the fifteenth, and the gathering in of the form body; it does not give the Buddha's passing, which 2567 would not be in this text's own chain (the birth is 2647).
