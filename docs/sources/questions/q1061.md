# On the scan of the Derge Kanjur's Common Tantra (I1KG9222, image 295), does the line read «ཤར་ཕྱོགས་སྔས་ལྟས» (the etext) or «སྔས་བལྟས» (the Sngags rim chen mo)?

RD373.

**Not answered 2026-11-13.** The Derge Kanjur image (I1KG9222, img. 295, 4357 x 787 px, seven lines on a landscape leaf) is served at full width but is faint; two crops at 1x and 2x of line 1 did not show the line with «ཤར་ཕྱོགས་སྔས་ལྟས», which is not in the first line, and the line was not located in the time given. Needs the remaining six lines cropped at 2x with the etext's page order.

**Narrowed 2026-11-13.** The line is located: etext [p295] is the passage's start in the middle of a leaf, and on I1KG9222 img. 295 it is the seventh (last) text line, which opens «སློབ་མ་རྣམས་ལ་བཤད་ནས་ནི། ཀུ་ཤ་བཏིང་བའི་ས་གཞི་ལ། ཤར་ཕྱོགས་…» (read by eye at 1.2x from x 380; the line ends short, at about 80% of the width, with «རྨི་ལམ་སངས་རྒྱས་»). The third phrase's syllable after སྔས is at about x 1550-1700, y 555-640 of the image; crops at 3.5x and 7x of it are too blurred by the faint print and the JPEG to tell ལྟས from བལྟས (whether a ba prefix stands before ལ). Needs a sharper scan (the BDRC image is 4357 px wide) or another Derge Kanjur print; not settled.

**Narrowed 2026-11-24 (sweep from inv-s).** BDRC's exact search (2026-11-24, inv-s), the witnesses of the line «ཤར་ཕྱོགས་སྔས་ལྟས/བལྟས་ཉལ་དུ་གཞུག»: the Common Tantra itself is typed in two etexts only, the Derge Kanjur (MW4CZ5369_0806, VE4CZ5369_I1KG9222) and the Nyingma Kama copy of Gene Smith's collection (MW1PD100944_01F4F1, VEIE0OPI48DFFA46_I1PD106520): both read «སྔས་ལྟས», two independent typings of two prints. No Peking, Narthang, Co ne, Lhasa or Dpe bsdur ma etext of Toh 806 is found by «ཤར་ཕྱོགས་སྔས་», «ཀུ་ཤ་བཏིང་བའི་ས་གཞི» or «སློབ་མ་རྣམས་ལ་བཤད་ནས་ནི» (the hits of the last are the same two plus commentaries and the Dpe bsdur ma of another work, MW1PD95844_1531). The spelling «སྔས་བལྟས» is found only where the line is quoted: Tsong kha pa (Sngags rim chen mo, MW1ALS05281E and MW3PD188), the Rdo rje phreng ba gtor chog in the 'Brug pa'i chos mdzod (MW3CN2232_75FA70), the Sngags rim blo sbyong (MW1PD95727); a quotation in Lobzang Tshulkhrims (MW23872_30FDFF) has «khu bltas», one in the Rgyud sde kun btus (MW27883_ADD197) «sngas bltas», other quoters «sngas bstan» (Shes rab rgyal mtshan, MW1AC374_80B41A), «mgo bstan» (Rgyud sde kun btus MW2PD14777). Count: «ལྟས» 2 typings of the tantra; «བལྟས» in 4-6 quotations, none of the tantra. So the tantra's own wording in both its typed prints is «སྔས་ལྟས», and the quoters write a different, regularised word; the sense is the same, the pillow side to the east. That the Derge scan has «ལྟས» is thus likely (the typist of the Derge etext had it before him), but the line on the scan itself was not read again: still narrowed.
