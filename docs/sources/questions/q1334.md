# In which chapter of the Cakrasamvara root tantra are the lines mar gyi ngo yi tshes bcu dang..., and in which work of Klong chen pa are the lines nya dang gnam stong tshes brgyad nyi shu dgu...

From inv-b (2026-11-21) on q1280: the older authors who quote the lines name the Cakrasamvara and the Yoginisancara; no work of Klong chen pa with the lines was found.

## Answered 2026-11-21

Settled by the Derge texts and Longchenpa's own commentary. The lines «mar gyi ngo yi tshes bcu dang…» stand in the Smaller Śaṃvara (Toh 368, the Cakrasaṃvara root tantra), ch. 27, «the rite of conduct, vow, offering and torma», Derge p. 467 ([lsv:p467](../corpus/lsv/lsv.md#lsv-p467)), claim [cakrasamvara-root-tantra-ch27-torma-on-the-waning-tenth](../claims/cakrasamvara-root-tantra-ch27-torma-on-the-waning-tenth.md); the Derge's second line reads «ཟླ་བ་ཡར་ངོའི་གང་གྱུར་ལ», not «…ཚེས་བཅུ་ལ». The Yoginīsañcāra line stands in Toh 375, ch. 10, pp. 79-80 ([ysn:p79-80](../corpus/ysn/ysn.md#ysn-p79-80)), claim [yoginisancara-ch10-offering-on-the-tenth](../claims/yoginisancara-ch10-offering-on-the-tenth.md), in the wording «དགུན་ཟླ་འབྲིང་པོ་ལ་སོགས་པའི། །ཚེས་བཅུ་ལ་ནི་དད་པས་མཆོད». Longchenpa's lines «nya dang gnam stong tshes brgyad nyi shu dgu…» are the root verses of ch. 9 of his Sems nyid ngal gso ([nsg:p57-58](../corpus/nsg/nsg.md#nsg-p57-58)) and are explained in his own Shing rta chen po ([nsc:p271-272](../corpus/nsc/nsc.md#nsc-p271-272)): six days a month, the tenth above all (claim [longchenpa-ngal-gso-ch9-days-of-feast-and-confession](../claims/longchenpa-ngal-gso-ch9-days-of-feast-and-confession.md)). 84000 holds Toh 368 and Toh 375 as placeholders only (no translated text), so the Derge etext of BDRC is the witness. Etext readings, no scan opened.
