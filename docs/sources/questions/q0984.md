# Is the 夢合長者鑑, a dream book listed at forty sen in the publisher's list of the 万代大雑書三世相 (Suzuki Kiemon, 1886), set out in the NDL or any library, and does it carry the table of the days on which a dream is not told with a legend for the slash, or a 初夢 night?

RD339.

## Answered 2026-11-13

Answered by RD340 (mdz:i899567), recorded here as the answer of the question: the 夢合長者鑑 is set out in the NDL (pid 899567, 特38-696, 1886, Suzuki Shooku, pictures by 前北齋爲一翁, 34 images, open). It has no table of the days on which a dream is not told, no slash legend and no 初夢 night on the leaves looked at (the running text not read letter by letter). Its colophon names the price as fifteen sen (十五錢), the publisher's list of mdz:i190 names forty (四十銭): either another issue or another price. The book is the dream part of the 万代大雑書 plates issued separately.
