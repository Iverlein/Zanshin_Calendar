# The 8 white's direction in the mewa rite

In WB vol. 2, p. 112, the mewa rite sends both the 5 yellow and the 8 white to the north-east corner (Yigdzin-1 «བྱང་ཤར»); is the eighth's direction a misreading, and which direction does the print give?

## Answered 2026-11-14

Not a misreading. The print gives the north-east corner for the 8 white as for the 5 yellow («བརྒྱད་དཀར་རྟ་ཡི་ཐོད་པར་བཅུག །བྱང་ཤར་མཚམས་སུ་སྦ་བར་བྱ»), read on the scan of the 1996 print (img. 120) and on the Gser thang reprint (img. 118, ll. 13 and 15) at the earlier reading; BDRC's exact phrase search finds the 8 white's line only in the 1996 etext (I1KG12907), none of the other prints' etexts carry it as one string. The Taikhang and Zhol images of the rite's page were not read for this line (the images fetched, Taikhang img. 172 and Zhol img. 790, hold the ur mnan wheels of the next folio). So the two prints read agree; no print is known to give another direction, and the duplication of the north-east corner stands as WB's own.
