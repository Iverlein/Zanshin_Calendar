# «འདུག་པར» or «གདུགས་ལྟར» in the Gangtok print

Is «འདུག་པར» in the Gangtok print (printed p. 22) a corruption of «གདུགས་ལྟར», "like a parasol", which 'Jam dbyangs don yod has at that place?

## Answered 2026-11-06

RD171 (2026-11-06), as far as the prints go: a second print of the benefit text, Bod ljongs nang bstan 1991 (MW20819, snippet), has «gdugs ltar bskor na gzugs bzang…»; no hit for «འདུག་པར་བསྐོར་ན». Two prints read གདུགས་ལྟར and the Gangtok print stands alone, so a corruption is likely. The Gangtok scan was not looked at again.
