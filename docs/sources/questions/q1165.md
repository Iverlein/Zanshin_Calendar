# Does a published astrology text define «སྐག», spelled so, as the keg obstacle year and give the own-year returns (ages 13 and 25) as its dates? Only the keg spelling is verified in a published work (tsl.30:i139).

Raised by the 2026-11-13 investigation a; see the question's cited passages.

## Answered 2026-11-14

Yes for the meaning and for the ages; not for the exact spelling, which the astrology manuals write «ཀེག» or «སྐེག».

The word. Steinert's dictionaries give «སྐག» (skag) in the Tshig mdzod chen mo (and, word for word, the Bod yig tshig gter rgya mtsho and the Dag tshig gsar bsgrigs) as "an obstacle of a fixed time: lo skag, zla skag, zhag skag, dus skag" (year, month, day, time), and «ཀེག» (keg) in the same two as "the same as skag in the sense of an obstacle of a fixed time"; Ives and Waldo give keg as "obstacle having a certain time; obstacle year", and Rangjung Yeshe «ལོ་སྐག» as "probably the same as lo skeg, obstacle year". So the dictionaries treat the two spellings as one word, and the four-fold list of the dictionaries is the list of the prayers of vol. 28 (tsl.28:i612, pp. 615 and 637, «ལོ་ཟླ་ཞག་དུས་སྐག་རིགས»).

The ages. The astrology verse manuals define the first of the seven rough obstacles as «རང་ཀེག་རྩུབ་པ» (rang keg rtsub pa), "the rough own obstacle": «རང་ལ་གནམ་ལོར་ཤར་དུས་ཏེ། བཅུ་གསུམ་ཉེར་ལྔ་སོགས་ལ་འོང་། ཁྱད་པར་ཉི་ཤུ་རྩ་ལྔ་དང་། ཞེ་དགུ་དྲུག་ཅུ་རྩ་གཅིག་གཉན།», "it is the time when one's own (year) falls on the sky year; it comes at thirteen, twenty-five and the like; especially at twenty-five, and the forty-ninth and sixty-first are the risky ones". It stands in the Rgyal rong dmangs khrod kyi rtsis yig phyogs bsgrigs (etext I4PD3932 p. 81, quoting the nag rtsis chapter of the *Gtsug lag rtsis kyi rig pa snang srid me long*), in the Sgo bzhi mdzod lnga'i yang bcud, Nag rtsis (29) (I4PD3282 pp. 98-99, by Hor btsun Bstan 'dzin blo gros, with a gloss that the thirty-seventh is for the wind-horse and the forty-ninth and sixty-first for a life-practice), and in the Bhutan collection (I1KG11905 pp. 35-36, the line written «རང་སྐེག»). The phrase of the life, «བཅུ་གསུམ་རང་ལོ་སྐག་ཐོག་ལ» (tsl.30:i139) and «ཁྱད་པར་ཉེར་ལྔའི་སྐག» (tsl.30:i193), is this verse's «རང་ལོ … བཅུ་གསུམ» and «ཁྱད་པར་ཉི་ཤུ་རྩ་ལྔ» in other words; the reading of 13 and 25 as the returns of the own year, which the claim had as an inference, is stated by the manuals.

The spelling «སྐག» for an age is found outside the life too: «བདག་གི་ལོ་ཉེར་ལྔའི་སྐག་ཐོག་ཡིན་པས» ("it was the skag of my twenty-fifth year") in a collected works of Chos kyi grags pa, and «ཡུམ་དགུང་ལོ་ཉེར་ལྔའི་སྐག་སྟེང་» in a Gcod yul lineage life; with «ཀེག» «ཉེར་ལྔའི་ཀེག» (six etexts, among them Mkhyen brtse 'od zer's life), «རང་ལོ་བཅུ་གསུམ་གྱི་ཀེག་ཐོག» (Padma 'phrin las's autobiography, two more lives) and the prayer «བཅུ་གསུམ་ཀེག་སྲིའི་གནོད་པ» of the Rin chen gter mdzod. No astrology text was found that writes «སྐག» in the definition itself.

Limits: every text here was read in its BDRC etext (search snippets and whole etexts, not on the scans), and the manuals are modern prints of verse manuals; WB's own wording of the first rough obstacle was not looked up here (the Mdo rgyud rin po che'i mdzod's prose version in year-of-life.md has no ages).
