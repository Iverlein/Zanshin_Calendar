# Are the male and female spirit dreams of lying postures in 'Jig rten mgon po the same as the spirit dreams that lose the drop in Marpa's inner-heat manual

Left side down brings a male spirit's dream, the back a female spirit's and the loss of the drop (dds.24:p190-191); are these the dreams of the spirit of a horse, dog or woman that lose the drop in dds.19:p187-189?

## Answered 2026-11-14

No text equates them (etext, 2026-11-14). In 'Jig rten mgon po (dds.24:p190-191) lying on the left side brings hatred and the dream of a male spirit (ཕོ་གདོན) with obstacles, lying on the back desire, the magic of a female spirit (མོ་གདོན) and the loss of the drop (ཐིག་ལེ་ཉམས་པ); no image of the spirit is given. Marpa's inner-heat manual (dds.19:p187-189) knows the loss of the drop through spirits (གདོན) by a dream of a horse, a dog, an ugly old woman or a woman, and names no posture and no male or female spirit. The only overlap is that both tie a woman's dream and the loss of the drop to a spirit; that the female spirit of the back is Marpa's woman, or the male spirit his horse or dog, would be a reader's join, which neither text makes. Phrase searches of both teachings' wording on BDRC (2026-11-14) found the 'Jig rten mgon po lines in three copies and no text that sets the two side by side.
