# The *Nges 'byed bla ma* Longchenpa quotes

Which text is the *Nges 'byed bla ma* (ངེས་འབྱེད་བླ་མ) that Longchenpa quotes for the mother tantras' counter-clockwise?

RD171 (2026-11-06), narrowed to a family: Longchenpa's Grub mtha' mdzod (7 etext editions) cites the Nges 'byed bla ma beside the Hevajra, the Ra li, the Bde mchog and the Mkha' 'gro ma gsang ba'i rgyud; the Sgrub thabs kun btus writes «ར་ལི་ངེས་འབྱེད་བླ་མ» as one citation; other Sakya texts cite «rdo rje a ra li» (Vajra Arali), suggesting the Ra li is its short name. No title for the Nges 'byed bla ma found; Kangyur and Tengyur catalogues not searched.

## Answered 2026-11-14

The citation is to the Cakrasaṃvara explanatory tantra known as «Mngon brjod bla ma» (མངོན་བརྗོད་བླ་མ). The couplet Longchenpa gives after «ངེས་འབྱེད་བླ་མ» (Grub mtha' mdzod, MW1KG4884; also the Mdzod bdun and the Snga 'gyur rnying ma'i glegs bam), «གཡོན་སྐོར་དུ་ནི་བསྐོར་བ་ཡིས། མཆོད་པ་དགོད་དང་གཏོར་མ་བྱ», is cited by Tsongkhapa's Bde mchog mngon rtogs commentary 'Dod 'jo ba (MW1ALS05320E) as «མངོན་བརྗོད་ལས», for the offerings laid out to the left, and by Lam mkhyen rgyal po's Lhan skyes lha lnga commentary in the 'Bri lugs collection (MW3KG218) as «ངེས་བརྗོད་ལས» (found with `bdrc_search.py` on «གཡོན་སྐོར་དུ་ནི་བསྐོར་བ་ཡིས», 8 hits; the Gsang sngags volume of the Gangs ljongs rig bcu collection has it too under «མངོན་བརྗོད»). BDRC's catalogue titles the Cakrasaṃvara's explanatory tantra «bshad rgyud mngon brjod bla ma». So «nges 'byed bla ma» in the Nyingma and Sakya citations (Sgrub thabs kun btus: «ར་ལི་ངེས་འབྱེད་བླ་མ») is a variant citation of this work, and the Ra li (Vajra Ārali) beside it is another text cited next to it, as RD171 found. Not checked: the couplet in the Kangyur text of the tantra itself (no Kangyur etext among the hits).
