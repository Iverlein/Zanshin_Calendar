# Is the string of six ornamental glyphs after the page-opening Om in the first line of LGD p. 962 (and p. 526 of the Palri print) a string of seed syllables, a mantra in ornamental letters or an invocation, and in plain letters what does it read?

Seen at tenfold in two prints (RD284); no glyph is settled. The Zhe chen, Kazi and Sbas-yul prints' first lines would give plain letters.

**Narrowed 2026-11-10 (sweep from RD355).** The string stands in four copies (the Rin chen gter mdzod, the Palri Parkhang print, the Ngodrup and Sherab Drimay print, the Sbas-yul manuscript), each time between the opening Om and the homage «namo guru deva ḍākinī hūṃ», its six shapes in the same order, and no copy writes it in plain letters; the Shechen etext marks it with six placeholders. So it is part of the text, but what it is and what it reads stay open (claims/lgd-p962-seed-string-is-ornamental-and-unread.md).
