# What is the «ru rta'i lde gur» of the be bum's dream rites? Read as rue-root only as a guess.

RD362.

## Answered 2026-11-13

A paste of ru rta, a decoction herb: «lde gu» is a paste or dough and the terminative makes it «(made) into a paste of ru rta»; the botanical plant is not named by the dictionaries (claim tdr-ru-rta-lde-gur, RD393).
