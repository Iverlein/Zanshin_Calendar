# WB p. 52: is the Crab's own srang measure 360 (as the print's glyphs read on p. 52) or 340 (as p. 53's table reads)? Which glyph reading is right?

## Answered 2026-11-17

Answered (inv-e, 2026-11-17): the Crab's own srang measure is 360, and the curled glyph of p. 53's table is a 6, not a 4.

Four checks agree. (1) The number-words «sky, time, fire» (ནམ་མཁའ་དུས་མེ, nam mkha' dus me) are 0, 6, 3 read from the low place, and on this very page the editors' own bracket glosses «time» as 6 (ཐིག་རོ(༦༠)དུས(༦)ཀྱི་བར); the word is 4 only where it means «four times», which this line does not. (2) BDRC's full-text search for «ནམ་མཁའ་དུས་མེ» (2026-11-17) finds this verse in two etexts of the White Beryl itself (the Beijing 1996/1997 edition, MW2CZ8040, and a second print of «Phug lugs rtsis kyi legs bshad bai ḍūrya dkar po»), both with the bracket «(360)» after the words; they are machine texts and read the same glyphs, so they back the number-words but are not a second look at the print. (3) The six measures of the signs Fish to Lion, 305, 290, 200, 270, X, 375, sum to 1800 with X = 360 («sky, dot, naga, form», 1800, the divisor of the same page), to 1780 with 340; and the steps between neighbours are −15, −90, +70, +90, +15 with 360, mirror-pairs in the outer places, against −15, −90, +70, +70, +35 with 340. (4) The verse's six water-srang figures 725, 1380, 1965, 2480, 2925, 3300 step down by exactly 70 each time (655, 585, 515, 445, 375); the rough divisions of p. 53 (9000, 8415, 7740, 7035) have the same total fall 1965, and the step pattern gives 585, 655, 725, that is 9000, 8415, 7760, 7035; so the curled glyph read as 4 in «7740» is probably the same 6, giving 7760 (an inference from the pattern, not seen on the scan). The Crab's cell in piece 1 of p. 53 therefore reads 360, and the pool's passage there (340) is a misreading of one glyph that p. 52 reads rightly. Claim wb-ch08-crab-srang-measure-is-360.
