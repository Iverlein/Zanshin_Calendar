# The Bhutan digest's writer and date

Who is the digest's writer, read here with doubt as the novice Ye shes chos 'dzin of Skye mo rgyal byed, and what year does the colophon's date on img. 600 give?

RD173 (2026-11-06), narrowed: on img. 600 by eye the writer is Ye shes chos 'phel (not 'dzin) of the monastery Snye mo rgyal byed (not Skye mo); the colophon is word for word that of *The Precious Key* in the 1987 print (q526), so this repeats q526 and q257. The date is a water bird «དཔལ་གདོང» year (the 7th of a cycle) with no cycle number in the words read: 1873 or 1933 (1993 is after the 1981 print).
