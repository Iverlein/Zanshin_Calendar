# The Bhutan digest's writer and date

Who is the digest's writer, read here with doubt as the novice Ye shes chos 'dzin of Skye mo rgyal byed, and what year does the colophon's date on img. 600 give?

RD173 (2026-11-06), narrowed: on img. 600 by eye the writer is Ye shes chos 'phel (not 'dzin) of the monastery Snye mo rgyal byed (not Skye mo); the colophon is word for word that of *The Precious Key* in the 1987 print (q526), so this repeats q526 and q257. The date is a water bird «དཔལ་གདོང» year (the 7th of a cycle) with no cycle number in the words read: 1873 or 1933 (1993 is after the 1981 print). ([word-hunts.md](../word-hunts.md), *The catalogue's questions, the second round*)

RD173 (2026-11-06), narrowed; repeats q526 and q257: on img. 600 by eye the writer is Ye shes chos 'phel, not 'dzin, of the monastery Snye mo rgyal byed; the date is a water bird «དཔལ་གདོང» year, the 7th of a cycle, so 1873 or 1933 (1993 is excluded by the 1981 print).

## Answered 2026-11-14

Answered, as to the writer and by a bound on the year. The digest's closing lines (the Bhutan collection I1KG11905, img. 600 by eye, etext p. 600–601) name the writer «དགེ་ཚུལ་ཡེ་ཤེས་ཆོས་འཕེལ» (dge tshul ye shes chos 'phel), the reckoning pupil of the monastery Snye mo rgyal byed (སྙེ་མོ་རྒྱལ་བྱེད, snye mo rgyal byed), "the lowest of the pupils of the Mentsikhang" (སྨན་རྩིས་ཁང་གི་སློབ་མའི་ཐ་ཤལ, sman rtsis khang gi slob ma'i tha shal), who gathered it "in the water bird year Dpal gdong (དཔལ་གདོང, dpal gdong) of the rab byung named «རབ་བྱུང་རྒྱལ་པོའི་ནང་མཚན»". These lines are word for word the close of *The Precious Key* (pk:i383-385), whose water bird year is 1933 (claim precious-key-water-bird-year-is-1933); the digest's own opening of the colophon differs (it thanks an "incomparable teacher" and does not name Prajñāmaṇi: BDRC etext of the Bhutan collection, p. 600), so the identification with Khyenrab Norbu does not carry over to it by name.

The year is bounded without it. The writer calls himself a pupil of the Mentsikhang (the Medicine and Astrology Institute of Lhasa), which the Men-Tsee-Khang's own history dates from 1916, founded by the 13th Dalai Lama (Wikipedia, "Men-Tsee-Khang", 2026-11-14; a modern encyclopaedia, used for the date only). The water bird years in reach are 1873, 1933 and 1993: 1873 is before the school, and 1993 is after the Bhutan collection's printing (Thimphu 1981). So the digest's colophon is dated 1933, the sixteenth cycle's water bird year. The cycle's number is still not written in the words read. The same writer, name, monastery and rank stand in both colophons, so it is one man and one date; the digest is a second text of his, or his colophon copied unchanged onto it.
