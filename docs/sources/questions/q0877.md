# Who is the monk Sgra tshad pa (དགེ་སློང་སྒྲ་ཚད་པ) whose notes close MDP's rebirth-seeing rite on p. 137, and is the Rga lo of its lineage the translator Rga lo tsā ba?

The lineage reads Vikhyātadeva, Śākyaśrī, Dānaśrī, 'Jam gsar, Rga lo, Zed dmar ba, Bu ston (RD284).

## Answered 2026-11-14

Answered 2026-11-14 for the monk, very probably Rin chen rnam rgyal of Zhwa lu (1318–1388), Bu ston's pupil, whose title in BDRC's person record P154 is «Sgra tshad pa» (see q932, which holds the evidence). The lineage's Rga lo (རྒ་ལོ) is a Rga/Rgwa lo tsā ba, not a misreading of G.yag sde lo, and probably Rgwa lo tsā ba Rnam rgyal rdo rje (1203–1282); see q931.
