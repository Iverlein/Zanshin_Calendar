# Does the note «ཡར་ཡར་གྱི་ཚེས་དགུ་དང་བཅུ་གཅིག་མར་གྱི་ཉེར་དྲུག་དང་ཉེར་བཞིའོ» gloss Palden Lhamo's days as the 9th, 11th, 24th and 26th?

Raised by the practice-days investigation (pd-d, 2026-11-16); see the cited passages.

## Answered 2026-11-21

Read on the Palampur scan (I2496 img. 415, passage rczp:i415, 2026-11-21, rd-c): the note inside the verse line «ཡར་མར་དགུ་དང་བཅུ་གཅིག་དུས། དཔལ་ལྡན་ལྷ་མོ་དཔོན་གཡོག་རྒྱུ» reads «ཡར་གྱི་ཚེས་དགུ་དང་བཅུ་གཅིག་མར་གྱི་ཉེར་དྲུག་དང་ཉེར་བཞིའོ», so Palden Lhamo's days are the waxing 9th and 11th and the waning 26th and 24th. The Gser thang print sets the note after the verse's first word and made the line look like the waning half only. The verse says only that she roams: no offering, fast or vow is named. Claim palden-lhamo-roams-on-the-9th-11th-24th-26th.

## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-t)

Whether any text besides Karma chags med names the 9th, 11th, 24th or 26th for Palden Lhamo (དཔལ་ལྡན་ལྷ་མོ, dpal ldan lha mo), Ma gcig Lha mo or Rdo rje legs pa, or the 28th/29th for the Sakya Mahākāla: BDRC full-text search, exact phrases, 2026-11-21, no hit: «ཉེར་དྲུག་དཔལ་ལྡན་ལྷ་མོ», «ཉེར་བཞི་དཔལ་ལྡན་ལྷ་མོ», «ཚེས་དགུ་དཔལ་ལྡན་ལྷ་མོ», «ཉེར་དྲུག་མ་གཅིག་ལྷ་མོ», «ཉེར་བརྒྱད་མགོན་པོ་ནག་པོ», «ཉེར་དགུ་མགོན་པོ་ནག་པོ», «ཉེར་དགུ་ཆོས་སྲུང», «ཉེར་བརྒྱད་མགོན་པོ», «ཚེས་བཅུ་གཅིག་ལྷ་མོ», «ཉེར་དྲུག་ལྷ་མོ», «ཉེར་དགུ་མགོན་པོ». «དཔལ་ལྡན་ལྷ་མོ་དཔོན་གཡོག» gives 35 hits, but the eight snippets read are the ritual title "Palden Lhamo with lord and servants" (a Gnam chos epidemic-averting rite, Rin chen gter mdzod's rebirth passage, Zhu chen Tshul khrims rin chen's invocation) and name no day of the month; the one date in them is a Gnam chos outline note that sets the rite for the second day of the first month (dbo zla ba'i tshes gnyis), not a monthly day. The Kagyu Mahākāla/Palden Lhamo ritual calendars and the Sakya 28th/29th were not found by phrase; the search needs the day words as the text spells them, which are not known without opening the volumes. No claim is added: the 9th, 11th, 24th, 26th remain Palden Lhamo's in Karma chags med alone, as protectors-fulfilment-on-the-14th-and-29th-in-a-gelug-round says.
