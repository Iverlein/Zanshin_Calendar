# The 7th month head's weekday line

In the 7th month's head the weekday line (dar 0 and a second figure, gud 5, skyod 2 and a figure that looks like 3, zhud 1 and 3) repeats 3 and lacks 4 if the second figure of dar is 6; which figures does the print give, and is the skyod pair 2 and 4? (RD202, corpus/wb/ch13.md)

## Narrowed 2026-11-17 (inv-b)

The 1996 etext of the 7th head (I1KG12906 [p177]) gives the weekday line in digits: «དར། ༠༦ གུད། ༥ གྱོད། ༢༣ ཞུད། ༡༣» (dar 0 6, gud 5, skyod 2 3, zhud 1 3): an OCR of the same print independent of the eye readings, and it agrees with them, so «2 3» and «1 3» are what the 1996 print prints (3 twice, no 4). The Gser thang etext (I3CN5028) loses the line (its [p173] ends at «ཉེར་བདུན་ལ་ཁྱི་གསར་མི་» and [p174] starts at an unrelated page). A search of BDRC for the head's clauses («ཡོ་ལང་མི་བྱེད» 12 hits, «འདི་དང་ཕྱི་མར་རི་ཏི་ནམ་ཤར་འགོད» 1, «དག་རྩིས་ལ་སེང་གེའི་ཁྱིམ» 1) finds the model almanac only in WB's 1996 etext; the other hits for «ཡོ་ལང་མི་བྱེད» are other works (the weekday line is WB's own table), so no third copy tells which 3 is the misprint. Which of the two 3s is the misprint (the 4 missing from skyod, as the 3rd and 11th heads' skyod 2 4 would suggest, or from elsewhere) is not told by any text found; the rule that each of 0 to 6 stands once fixes only that a 4 is missing. Open.


## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-k)

No new copy. The same line in the 2nd head (p. 159) reads, in the pool's passage, «དར། ༡༣ གུད། ༦ སྐྱོད། ༠ ཞུད། ༢༤» (dar 1 3, gud 6, skyod 0, zhud 2 4: six figures, the 5 absent, so that line is a different shape and shows no skyod pair for comparison). The 7th head's clause «ཟླ་བ་འདི་དང་ཕྱི་མ་གང་འདུག» is in two other search-only handbooks (a Blo bzang sbyin pa collected-works handbook, the Do shal), whose snippets stop before the weekday line. Unchanged.

**Narrowed 2026-11-17 (sweep from inv-b).** The 1996 etext reads the weekday line «དར། ༠༦ གུད། ༥ གྱོད། ༢༣ ཞུད། ༡༣», agreeing with the eye readings: the print has 3 twice and no 4. Which 3 is the misprint is not told.
