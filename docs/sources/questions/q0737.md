# The 7th month head's weekday line

In the 7th month's head the weekday line (dar 0 and a second figure, gud 5, skyod 2 and a figure that looks like 3, zhud 1 and 3) repeats 3 and lacks 4 if the second figure of dar is 6; which figures does the print give, and is the skyod pair 2 and 4? (RD202, corpus/wb/ch13.md)

## Narrowed 2026-11-17 (inv-b)

The 1996 etext of the 7th head (I1KG12906 [p177]) gives the weekday line in digits: «དར། ༠༦ གུད། ༥ གྱོད། ༢༣ ཞུད། ༡༣» (dar 0 6, gud 5, skyod 2 3, zhud 1 3): an OCR of the same print independent of the eye readings, and it agrees with them, so «2 3» and «1 3» are what the 1996 print prints (3 twice, no 4). The Gser thang etext (I3CN5028) loses the line (its [p173] ends at «ཉེར་བདུན་ལ་ཁྱི་གསར་མི་» and [p174] starts at an unrelated page). A search of BDRC for the head's clauses («ཡོ་ལང་མི་བྱེད» 12 hits, «འདི་དང་ཕྱི་མར་རི་ཏི་ནམ་ཤར་འགོད» 1, «དག་རྩིས་ལ་སེང་གེའི་ཁྱིམ» 1) finds the model almanac only in WB's 1996 etext; the other hits for «ཡོ་ལང་མི་བྱེད» are other works (the weekday line is WB's own table), so no third copy tells which 3 is the misprint. Which of the two 3s is the misprint (the 4 missing from skyod, as the 3rd and 11th heads' skyod 2 4 would suggest, or from elsewhere) is not told by any text found; the rule that each of 0 to 6 stands once fixes only that a 4 is missing.
