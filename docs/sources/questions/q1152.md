# Does the tradition take the Karmapa's bile of the midnight dream (rrd.7:p102) and his power of the spirits (rrd.5:p57-59) as one teaching said two ways or as two sayings

His commentary gives the midnight dream as bile's by the Mañjuśrī root tantra; his verses give Marpa's gdon dbang.
