# In the lama's remark in the dream verses (cbl:p236-238) the etext reads «འོ་ཡུག་པ་གཤིན་རྗེ་དུས་མཚན་ནག་མོ་ཡིན་གསུང་སྐད» after the saying that the black girl's binding is the sign of the time of death's arrival: who or what is the «འོ་ཡུག་པ», and is the remark the old annotator's or Mkhyen brtse's?

RD331.

Looked at again (inv-y, 2026-11-16): the only BDRC hit of «འོ་ཡུག་པ་གཤིན་རྗེ» and of «གཤིན་རྗེ་དུས་མཚན་ནག་མོ» is the CBL itself (MW3PD1002, Mkhyen brtse's works); nothing new. The remark stays in the CBL alone (see claim o-yug-pa-in-the-death-dream-remark and q1010).

**Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-h).** See q1010: «འོ་ཡུག་པ» is the name of a Sakya teacher, most likely BDRC's P3089 (died 1253); the saying has no Sakya carrier. «དུས་མཚན་ནག་མོ་ཡིན» finds the CBL alone again; whether the remark is the old annotator's or Mkhyen brtse's is not told in any text reached.
