# Does the 9th hair date's result «ན་ཆུང་མོད་འགྱུར» count as a gain or a warning

RD162 (2026-11-06), narrowed (etext only): the Drigung verse work has WB's list of hair dates with different results at the 3rd, 4th, 5th, 6th, 7th and 12th; its 9th reads «དགུ་ལ་ན་ཆུང་འགྲོགས», 'company of the young', with no mark of harm.

**Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-q).** BDRC exact phrase «ན་ཆུང་མོད་འགྱུར» 1 hit, WB's 1996 etext only (I1KG12907, offset 904543): «དགུ་ནི་ན་ཆུང་མོད་འགྱུར་ཞིང། །བཅུ་ལ་དབང་ཐང་ཆེ་བ་དང། །བཅུ་གཅིག་དབང་རྣོ་བཅུ་གཉིས་ནད། །བཅུ་གསུམ་བཟང་…» (snippet). In the line the 9th's clause is joined by «ཞིང» to the 10th's «great power», with no word of gain or harm; the neighbours (10th great power, 11th sharp power, 12th illness, 13th good) do not settle the sense. No other text repeats the phrase. Stays narrowed.
