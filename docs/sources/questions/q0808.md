# Where does Klong rdol bla ma's own text on examining the omens of dreams, the source of KBS's verses, stand in his collected works?

KDL (MW29669_5E6E42) is another text and does not hold the verses. Needs a locating search in BDRC or the table of contents of his gsung 'bum.

## Answered 2026-11-09

In BDRC's ACIP etext MW1ALS06558E, *Rmi ltas sna tshogs brtag thabs* (RST), pp. 3-8, a text of twenty pages that BDRC gives to Klong rdol bla ma and that ends with his colophon (rst:p17-20; the claim rst-is-klong-rdols-dream-text). It is not in his collected works as a print found on BDRC: the etext has no print identified, and KDL's print (MW29669_5E6E42) holds only the Garga part that follows the dream part. The wording of KBS's quotations differs from the etext in a few lines (rst-and-kbs-wording).
