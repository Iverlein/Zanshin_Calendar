# Is Mayadevi's dream shown with a six-tusked elephant in a painted set of the twelve deeds?

The pages read give a white elephant with a lotus and no tusk count. Which published set shows six tusks?

**Answered 2026-11-24 (inv-h).** Yes in one published set: Sunlal Ratna Tamang's 2011 painting of the Twelve Great Deeds (Robert Beer Archive, tibetanart.com/artworks/273) has a white bull elephant with six tusks circling Mayadevi three times and entering her right side; no lotus named there.
