# Do the third triad's «ཕུང» and the editors' note on Bharaṇī (p. 329) rest on a nameable source

Do the third triad's «ཕུང» (p. 313) and the editors' note on p. 329 about Bharaṇī (བྲ་ཉེ) as a fifth stable and accomplishing mansion rest on a source that can be named? (RD79)

## Answered 2026-11-05

Answered 2026-11-05: the first half as q449 (RD112). The second (RD159, [claims/wb-p329-editors-note-bharani.md](../claims/wb-p329-editors-note-bharani.md)): the note reads whole on the reprint: most texts bring Bharaṇī in as a fifth, but the four are thought right. It names no source, and the source behind "most" was not searched.
