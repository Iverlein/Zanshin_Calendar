# Does Karma chags med take «the midnight dream is the display of gods and spirits» from Marpa's line on Māra's emanation, and does ལྷ་འདྲེ there mean the gdon of the Karmapa's gdon dbang

«ནམ་ཕྱེད་ལྷ་འདྲེའི་ཆོ་འཕྲུལ་ངེས་པ་མེད»: from Marpa or from another teaching?

Narrowed (inv-hh, 2026-11-14): the verses are a rewording of the same three-part rule, not a quotation of Marpa, and the text names no source. The structure is Marpa's (rrd.23:p102-104): evening, midnight, dawn; habit with «nothing to put weight on» (Marpa «de la gza' gtad ci yang med», Karma chags med «bag chags nges pa med»); a midnight line with «not to be trusted» (Marpa «yid brtan byar mi rung», Karma chags med «nges pa med»); a dawn with a result. The midnight wording differs from all: «gods and spirits» (ལྷ་འདྲེ, lha 'dre) for Marpa's Māra's emanation and the Karmapa's «gdon dbang», and «cho 'phrul» is shared with Gtsug lag phreng ba's Life («bdud kyi cho 'phrul») and the Black Smith tantra (lgd.38:p7). Whether «'dre» is the Karmapa's «gdon»: in the following lines of the same passage Karma chags med himself writes «gdon» for the spirits to be subdued («gdon sogs drag pos btul ba»), so he uses the two words of one class, and «lha» is his addition; no text states the identity.
