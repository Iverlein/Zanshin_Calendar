# Does Karma chags med take «the midnight dream is the display of gods and spirits» from Marpa's line on Māra's emanation, and does ལྷ་འདྲེ there mean the gdon of the Karmapa's gdon dbang

«ནམ་ཕྱེད་ལྷ་འདྲེའི་ཆོ་འཕྲུལ་ངེས་པ་མེད»: from Marpa or from another teaching?

Narrowed (inv-hh, 2026-11-14): the verses are a rewording of the same three-part rule, not a quotation of Marpa, and the text names no source. The structure is Marpa's (rrd.23:p102-104): evening, midnight, dawn; habit with «nothing to put weight on» (Marpa «de la gza' gtad ci yang med», Karma chags med «bag chags nges pa med»); a midnight line with «not to be trusted» (Marpa «yid brtan byar mi rung», Karma chags med «nges pa med»); a dawn with a result. The midnight wording differs from all: «gods and spirits» (ལྷ་འདྲེ, lha 'dre) for Marpa's Māra's emanation and the Karmapa's «gdon dbang», and «cho 'phrul» is shared with Gtsug lag phreng ba's Life («bdud kyi cho 'phrul») and the Black Smith tantra (lgd.38:p7). Whether «'dre» is the Karmapa's «gdon»: in the following lines of the same passage Karma chags med himself writes «gdon» for the spirits to be subdued («gdon sogs drag pos btul ba»), so he uses the two words of one class, and «lha» is his addition; no text states the identity. BDRC's exact search «ནམ་ཕྱེད་ལྷ་འདྲེའི་ཆོ་འཕྲུལ» (2026-11-14) 4 hits, the four prints of his own text only (MW23259, I2PD18241, I2496, I3CN4737); no other author has the line, so no second teaching that he could have taken it from was found. ## Narrowed again 2026-11-16 (inv-p)

«ལྷ་འདྲེའི་ཆོ་འཕྲུལ» is a stock phrase of the meditation literature, not Karma chags med's coinage: BDRC's exact phrase has 467 hits, among them the Gdams ngag mdzod, the Rin chen gter mdzod («ལྷ་འདྲེའི་ཆོ་འཕྲུལ་ལས་ཤར་བ», the experiences that arise from gods' and spirits' display), Padma dkar po («གནས་དེ་དག་ཏུ་ལྷ་འདྲེའི་ཆོ་འཕྲུལ་བར་ཆད་ཀྱི་ལྷོངས་བྱུང་ནས་འཇིགས་ན», in the obstacle section of a retreat teaching) and Dri med 'od zer («ལྷ་འདྲེའི་ཆོ་འཕྲུལ་ཅི་བྱུང་རུང་། ཏེ་ཀའི་ངང་ལ་འཛིན་མེད་དུ་གྲོལ་ཐོངས», 'whatever display of gods and spirits comes, release it unholding'); in all of them it names obstacle-making apparitions, which is the class of the Karmapa's «gdon» (snippets read, texts not opened). The whole line «ནམ་ཕྱེད་ལྷ་འདྲེའི་ཆོ་འཕྲུལ་ངེས་པ་མེད» still has 4 hits, his own prints (VE23259_001 and others), and the variants «ནམ་ཕྱེད་ལྷ་འདྲེ», «ནམ་ཕྱེད་བདུད་ཀྱི་ཆོ་འཕྲུལ», «ནམ་ཕྱེད་བདུད་ཀྱི་སྤྲུལ་པ», «ནམ་ཕྱེད་ཀྱི་རྨི་ལམ་ཡིད་བརྟན…», «ནམ་ཕྱེད་ཀྱི་རྨི་ལམ་བདུད་ཀྱི» 0. So the midnight line is his wording of Marpa's rule with a common phrase, not a quotation from a second teaching found; the identity of «'dre» with «gdon» stays unstated.
