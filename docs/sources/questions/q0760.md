# WB p. 243 l. 3, «འཁྲུག་སྲིད་པའི་ས»: is it སྲིད (Yigdzin-1) or སྒྲིད (MITRA)? A heavy stack on the reprint.

## Answered 2026-11-14

སྲིད (srid) is the word. «འཁྲུག་སྲིད་པའི་ས» is "a ground where quarrels may come about" (srid pa, to be possible, to come to be; in the sentence «ཕ་ཚན་ནང་འཁྲུག་སྲིད་པའི་ས» "a ground where there may be quarrels within the clan"). «སྒྲིད» has no headword in the 64 dictionary sets, as a verb or otherwise. The stack on the reprint was not looked at again; the choice is by the word.

Source: tmz:box9 (read 2026-11-14: no headword «sgrid», «sgrid pa»), wb.2:p243. The answer rests on absence in the dictionaries and on the sense; a scan of another print would show the glyph.
