# Does the Leipzig manuscript 1178 read «tripakṣake», «trimāsake» or another word for the third watch, and the Ramtek M 856 «trimāsake»?

RD312.

Narrowed (inv-rrr, 2026-11-14): Aufrecht's 1901 catalogue of the Leipzig Sanskrit manuscripts (Internet Archive in.ernet.dli.2015.313499, printed pp. 368-370, read on the page image n369, 770 px wide; the text layer is noise) describes no. 1178: «Svapnādhyāya in 88 Ślokas», a bad manuscript of about 1830 ("Schlechte Handschrift von etwa 1830"), and quotes only its end (v. 87-88, «etatpuṇyaṃ ca pāpaghnaṃ … guruktaṃ» with Bṛhaspati as the teacher). It quotes nothing of the watch verse. So the manuscript is about 1830 and the catalogue cannot decide the word. Ramtek M 856 was not reached again.
