# Does a reader of the cursive confirm every cell of the Kokubunken table's hentaigana, in particular the first and second glyph of the first, third, eleventh and twelfth months

The hentaigana (single glyphs, 祢 for the rat) are matched to mdz:p11 by the number of glyphs and the place of the gap only.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-zzz)

Image 9 of the Kokubunken Ebara dream book (bid 200013909) was cropped at 6000 px and the twelve columns read by eye. The hentaigana are clear and the same shape recurs for the same word in different months, which fixes them without a reader of cursive: 邑 stands first in 正月 and 三月 and second in 十二月 (ひつじ in all three places of mdz:p11), 挙 first in 四月, 五月 and 十二月 (むま in all three) and second in 三月 (むま), 祢 first in 六月 and 七月 (ね), the sa-glyph first in 十一月 and second in 正月 and 二月 (さる), a ミ glyph last in 二月 (ミ), い last in 三月 and 十二月 (ゐ) and the same last-group glyph in 正月 and 十一月 (いぬ). So the first and second glyph of months 1, 3, 11 and 12 and the cells of month 2 agree with mdz:p11 as to shape and word. Month 4 and month 5 have the same four shapes, months 6 and 7 differ in the last group, months 8 and 9 have the same shapes. Not settled: the last-group glyph of 正月 and 十一月 (read ぬ from p11) is a rare shape, and the glyphs of 八月 to 十月 (と, た…) were matched by their place only; the matching of 邑, 挙 and 言 to ひ, む and さ is by agreement with p11, not by an independent reading of the cursive.
