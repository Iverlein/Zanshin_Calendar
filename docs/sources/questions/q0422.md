# How are the thirteen gates counted?

How are the thirteen gates counted (WB vol. 2, pp. 170–171)? Built (ROADMAP R2z) with these readings, each open: «ལྔ་ནས» after the start of gates 6, 10, 11 and 13 («གྲོགས་ཡོས་ལྔ་ནས», «རང་ཁམས་སྤྲེལ་ལྔ་ནས», «ཕག་ལྔ་ནས», «བྱི་ལྔ་ནས») read as no count, since gate 5 alone gives its five as one («ཕོ་ནི་ཐུར་ལ་མོ་ཡར་ལྔ»); gate 9's «ལུག་ཉིད་ཀྱི། །བདུན་ཟུར་རང་ཁམས་ས» as the own element's ox; "eight up", "three up" and "thirteen down" counted inclusively, the year reached the first, as the thirty count (D22); a man and a woman by the birth year's sex, as D22; the year reached fought against the moment's sign by element, cutting inward where its element overcomes the moment's. Two words of the harms are left out of the readings: «རྒྱ་མའི་ཐག་པ» (the steelyard's rope, or the line of births?) and «ལྷས་ངན་བབས» (by the gods, or «ལྟས་ངན», a bad omen? check on the scan, I3CN5029 img. 176).
