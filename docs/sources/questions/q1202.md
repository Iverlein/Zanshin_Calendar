# KP label 117: «མཁར་སྒོ» (fortress) or «མཁའ་སྒོ» for the six-three sky door?

KP's six-three sky door head reads «མཁའ་སྒོ» in the machine reading where WB has «མཁར་སྒོ» (fortress); the remedy given (the enemy's name pressed into a wall foundation) is a fortress remedy. Wanted: the letter on BDRC label 117, line 4 or 5 (pool img. 115), at 2.5×.

## Answered 2026-11-14

It is «མཁར་སྒོ» (fortress door), the reading WB has. On BDRC label 117, line 5 (pool img. 115), at 6× and 10×, the heading «༧དྲུག་གསུམ་མཁར་སྒོ་སྒྲིབ་པ་ན» has after the ཁ a final that is an angular hooked stroke with a pointed diagonal foot, not the round bowl of the «འ» of this block's carving; the remedy (the enemy's name pressed into the herd's pen or a wall's foundation) is a fortress's. The machine's «མཁའ» was a misreading. Read by eye on the scan; the confidence is good but not complete, the stroke being the one the carver uses for ར elsewhere in the line only by resemblance, so the KP2 manuscript (I3CN12074, a faint hand copy) was tried as a second witness and could not be read at this place. Recorded in kp:i114-116 and the claim kp-sky-doors-same-pairs-as-wb.
