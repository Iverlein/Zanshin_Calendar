# The black nine-eye's sky years on p. 16: «མི་སྟག» and «ས་ཡོས»

The flesh's secret reckoning (WB vol. 2, p. 16) lists the sky years that put a person's year into the black nine-eye (དགུ་མིག་ནག་པོ, dgu mig nag po): the wood male rat, the wood ox, «མི་སྟག», «ས་ཡོས», the earth snake, the iron horse, the iron sheep, the water monkey, the water bird, the wood dog and the wood pig. Read with their elements, the others are the run of the sixty from 1984 to 1995, where the third and fourth would be the fire tiger (མེ་སྟག) and the fire hare (མེ་ཡོས), and the earth dragon of 1988 is missing. Is «མི་སྟག» the fire tiger, or a man's tiger year as against the woman of the first line, and is «ས་ཡོས» a slip for the fire hare, the earth hare of 1999 standing outside the run? To be read on the other prints (the Gser thang reprint, the Zhol print, the Taikhang). Until it is settled the table is not built (ROADMAP R6).

RD178 (2026-11-07): Narrowed: Zhol img. 649 (Yigdzin-1, by eye at 4×–6× for «ས་ཡོས»): the table is the pairs «X ཤར་ན Y ཚུད»; «ས་ཡོས» is the rising earth hare of the water bird (earth over water); Zhol reads «མེ་སྟག»; the vowel not told from i at 4×.
