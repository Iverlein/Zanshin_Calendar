# Is the «Rga lo» of the lineage on MDP p. 135 the translator Rga lo tsā ba, or a misreading of «G.yag sde lo tsā ba» (Bsod nams bzang po), whom three Sa skya lineages name as the teacher of Ze dmar ba Sangs rgyas ye shes?

RD315.

## Answered 2026-11-14

Answered 2026-11-14, as probable. The lineage's «'Jam gsar» and «Rga lo» stand next to each other in BDRC's records as a teacher and his pupil: «'Jam gsar ba Shes rab 'od zer» (P2585, 12th century, "teacher in the 'Bro transmission of the Kālacakra") is recorded as the teacher of «Rgwa lo tsā ba Rnam rgyal rdo rje» (P1244, 1203–1282 by Khams sprul's dates, also called Rong po Rga lo; a master of the Vajrabhairava lineage), and P1244 is recorded as the teacher of Mang mkhar lo tsā ba, who also studied with G.yag sde lo tsā ba. The G.yag sde lo tsā ba of the question (P2138, here Bsod nams seng ge, 12th–13th century) is recorded as the teacher of Bre dmar ba (Ze dmar ba) Sangs rgyas ye shes (P3215), and Ze dmar ba as a teacher of Bu ston (P155); nothing in BDRC makes P1244 Ze dmar ba's teacher. So the reading «Rga lo» is a lo tsā ba of the Rga/Rgwa family; it is not a misreading of «G.yag sde lo» (the three prints of Bu ston's text spell the same «རྒ་ལོ», so a machine slip is out too), though which Rga lo is meant is shown only by the pair 'Jam gsar - Rga lo; a different, earlier Rgwa lo tsā ba, Gzhon nu dpal (P3674, 1105–1198), cannot be excluded by BDRC's records, and the dates of P1244 against P2585 (who is placed before 1199) do not agree well. The lineage's order therefore does not coincide with the lines BDRC records for Ze dmar ba (G.yag sde lo); the rite's line is a second one into him.
