# The asterism's motion on p. 14: 1620 and 301

The line on the asterism's motion «stong dang ni drug brgya nyi shu» and the line «zhag po sum brgya don gcig» on p. 14: what do these numbers measure? (RD198, corpus/wb/ch02.md)

## Answered 2026-11-13

RD (inv-j, 2026-11-13). The first line, «རྒྱུ་སྐར་ཆུ་ཚོད་སྟོང་དང་ནི། །དྲུག་བརྒྱ་ཉི་ཤུ»: 1620 is the sun's round counted in the stars' hours, 27 asterisms × 60; WB ch. 17 (wb.1:p191) uses the same 1,620 as the constant «རྒྱུ་སྐར་ཆུ་ཚོད» and divides the house-day circle (360) by it. The second line, «ཚེས་ཞག་གིས་གཞལ་ན། །ཞག་པོ་སུམ་བརྒྱ་དོན་གཅིག་དང་། །དབྱུག་མཚོ་དུས་མེའི་ཆུ་ཡི་སྲང་། །དབུགས་མདའ་འདོད་ཆ་ཐུབ་པས་འཁོར»: the same round measured in tithi-days. Not 301 but 371 days, then 4 chu tshod (dbyug mtsho), 36 chu srang (dus me, units first) and 5 and 7 parts of 13 breaths (mda' 5, 'dod cha 13, thub pa 7): 804 × 30 / 65 = 371 1/13 tithi-days by the repo's constants (SPEC 5.1; the mean sun's 65 revolutions in 804 months), and 1/13 of a day is exactly 4 chu tshod 36 chu srang 5 7/13 breaths. The match to the 7/13 is the evidence; that «དོན» stands for the tens figure 7 is inferred from it. The verse reads the same in four other BDRC etexts (search snippets). Claim wb-ch2-sun-round-1620-stars-hours-371-tithi-days.
