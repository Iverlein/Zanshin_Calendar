# Bharaṇī's minor star on p. 314: «ཕུང་རྒྱབ་ཅན» or «ཕུང་རྒྱང་ཅན»

The minor star of Bharaṇī on p. 314 looks «ཕུང་རྒྱབ་ཅན» on the Zhol print (WBZ img. 1066) and «ཕུང་རྒྱང་ཅན» on the Gser thang reprint (img. 320): which is the star's name?

RD176 (2026-11-06), narrowed toward «ཕུང་རྒྱབ་ཅན»: on the Zhol (img. 1066, line 5) the boxy final matches the «པ» of «ཞེས་པ» in the same crop at 6×; the reprint's final is of the ང/ན/ད family, which that print does not keep apart; the 1996 etext and the Sakya etext (p. 452) both give «རྒྱབ». New question: is the reprint's final a ང printed for བ, or is there a Gser thang block reading «རྒྱང»?
