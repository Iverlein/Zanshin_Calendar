# The marks' line of p. 170 and the earth hour's hair

On p. 170, the marks' line «ཡང་ནི་ཤ་མཚན་ཤའི་ལྔ་ཅན། །དྲུག་གུ་མི་ལ་རྨེན་པའམ།» seems to have lost the wood and fire hours; what does it say? And is the earth hour's hair «བནྡེ» (Yigdzin-1) or «བཛེ», as the reprint seems to read?

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (scan-d).** Read again on the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5029 img. 176 = p. 170, ll. 5–7, 1.0× line crops). The marks' line stands on the scan exactly as quoted, «ཡང་ནི་ཤ་མཚན་ཤའི་ལྔ་ཅན། །དྲུག་གུ་མི་ལ་རྨེན་པའམ།», and is followed by «སྨེ་བ་ས་ལ་སྐུར་པོ་ཡོད། །ལྕགས་ལ་མཆན་ཁུང་སྨེ་བ་ཡོད། །ཆུ་ལ་ཤོ་རེ་ཡོད་པར་ཤོད།»: no word is dropped by the print or by Yigdzin-1. The verse is built like the hair verse before it (ll. 5–6), where the wood and the fire share the first line («ཤིང་ལ་སྐྲ་ཕྲུག་མེ་རལ་ཅན») and the earth and the iron the next («ས་ལ་བནྡེ་ལྕགས་མགོ་རེག»): so the marks' two opening half-lines are likely the wood's and the fire's, unnamed, and the three that follow name the earth, the iron and the water, five elements in the order wood, fire, earth, iron, water. That is an inference from the layout; what «ཤ་མཚན་ཤའི་ལྔ་ཅན» and «དྲུག་གུ» say is not read. The earth hour's hair «བནྡེ» (not «བཛེ») was settled by RD61 ([wb.2:p170](../corpus/wb/ch30.md)) and is unchanged.
