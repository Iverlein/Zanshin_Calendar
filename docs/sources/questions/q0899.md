# The Narthang manuscript and the Peking print share «ཛ་ཏ་པ» and «སྭ་པ་ནེ་ཀྵ» against the Derge etext: is the Derge etext's «ཛཱ་ཏ་ཀ» (jātaka) a correction by the Derge editors, or does the Sanskrit of the Peking line stand for another word (jātapa)?

RD298.

**Narrowed 2026-11-17 (inv-d).** The Siddhasāra volume (MW1KG14614, a modern typeset print) carries the whole Svapnekṣā and reads «ཛཱ་ཏ་ཀ་ཡི་ཕྲེང་བ་བཅིང» as the Derge etext does (sds:p471-472, etext only; claim svi-garland-jataka-in-the-siddhasara-print): BDRC's exact search finds «ཛཱ་ཏ་ཀ་ཡི་ཕྲེང་བ» in these two texts only and «ཛ་ཏ་པ་ཡི་ཕྲེང་བ» in no etext, so «ཛ་ཏ་པ» stays confined to the Peking and Narthang prints and «ཛཱ་ཏ་ཀ» is not the Derge editors' lone correction unless that volume was set from the Derge. The dictionaries have «dza ti» (jasmine, nutmeg) and no «dza ta ka» or «dza ta pa»; whether either is a spelling of jāti is not stated anywhere. Open.
