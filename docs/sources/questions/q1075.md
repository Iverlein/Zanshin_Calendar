# Does the Mchog dga' take its lines on Saturn in the tenth and eleventh place (kd.3:p187) and on Saturn with Ketu (kd.3:p315) from a book of its own reckoning? They are in no text of the root.

RD376.

## Searched 2026-11-13 (inv-s), no answer

BDRC's exact-phrase search for the two lines («བཅུ་གཅིག་པའི་སར་ཉི་མ་ཡོད་ན», «སྤེན་པ་ཡོད་ན་ནོར་སྣ་ཚོགས་ཐོབ», «སྤེན་པ་ཡི་ནི་ཀེ་ཏུ་ལ», «མཁྲིས་ནད་ཚད་ནད་གྲང་ནད་འོང») gives two hits each, both in the same two books: Bod kyi rtsis rig kun 'dus chen mo (VEIE0OPI2F246A26_I4717) and the Bzo rtsis zlos gar, vol. 2 (VEIE0OPI51EA6051_I2PD16001), which prints the same Mchog dga' text. No third work has the lines, so no separate book of the reckoning (the root text lacks them) was reached; whether the Mchog dga' takes them from an Indian planet-in-place (bhāva) table or from its own author is not shown.

## Searched 2026-11-14 (inv-kk), no answer

Nothing beyond inv-s: no further phrase of these lines was tried to a third work; the two prints of the Mchog dga' remain the only hits, so a separate book of the reckoning is not reached.

## Searched 2026-11-14 (inv-yyy), the root read for the places

The whole Derge etext of the Dbyangs 'char (VE23703_1521) was searched for the lines' words: «ནོར་སྣ་ཚོགས», «ཚད་ནད», «གྲང་ནད» have no hit, «ཀེ་ཏུ» six hits (pp. 117, 144, 148, 185 of the etext: a Ketu line of the seventh place, the years of life, the cycle of ornament and the offerings), none «Saturn's Ketu»; «རྣོ་བ» occurs but not with a dream. The root does give the planets in the places, but as signs for the question: Saturn and Mars and the others in the third, fourth, fifth, seventh, eighth, ninth, tenth places («གསུམ་དང་བཅུ་ལ་སྤེན་པ་གནས། ཡོངས་སུ་རྫོགས་པ་སྟེར་བ་ཡིན», [p116]; the tenth and eleventh place for the asker's victory and loss, [p118]-[p119]); the Mchog dga's tenth- and eleventh-place table with one result for each of the seven planets (kd.3:p187) is not in them, nor is a dream. The Ketu line of the seventh place ([p117], «ཀེ་ཏུས་དོན་ཉམས») and the years of Ketu ([p144], «ཀེ་ཏུའི་བདུན་ལ་བུ་བརླག་ཅིང་། ནོར་ཟད་སྤུན་ཟླ་སྲིང་མོ་འཆི») resemble the Mchog dga's themes (loss, strife with kin) but not its words. So the root confirms again that the Saturn dream lines are not in it; the Mchog dga' takes them from outside the tantra or makes them, and no book of the reckoning is named. (Rāhu by half-watch, which the task named: the root has it, [p171]-[p172] «ཐུན་ཕྱེད་རཱ་ཧུའི་རིམ་པ་བཞིན། ཤར་ཕྱོགས་བདག་པོ་ཉི་མ་སྟེ…» and [p181]-[p182] the eight half-watches of the day with the planets in order; that is the matter of q967, not of the Saturn lines here.)
