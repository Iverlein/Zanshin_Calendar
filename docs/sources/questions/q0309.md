# The ages with no old kind

WB names no old kind for a man of 61–69 or 83, nor for a woman of 66, 70–71 or 73–77 except the general *bsrung med bkra* "from the seventies to ninety" (pp. 77–79): do these ages take the nearest kind, the general "old" rule, or none?

## Answered 2026-11-05

WB's own second round (p. 79): a man from 61, a woman from 60, the kind by companion and son, not by age. ([reckonings.md](../reckonings.md), RD164)
