# Which tantra do Rang byung rdo rje and Kong sprul mean by «rgyud rnams las», and the Blue Beryl and Zur mkhar ba by «རྒྱུད་ནས་མང་དུ་གསུངས་པ», for the verse «སྲོད་ལ་བད་ཀན་ཤས་ཆེ་སྟེ། ནམ་ཕྱེད་དུས་སུ་མཁྲིས་པར་བརྗོད། ཐོ་རངས་རླུང་གི་དུས་ཉིད་དེ»?

RD313; it is not in the commentaries on the Four Tantras read here.

Narrowed (inv-s, 2026-11-14): Rang byung rdo rje's own commentary on his Zab mo nang don (BDRC VE30541_013, etext p. 288, again p. 292 as «གོང་དུ་བཤད་པའི་སྲོད་ལ་བད་ཀན་ཤས་ཆེ་བ») and Kong sprul's Zab don snang byed (VEIE0OPI3809385D_I5759, etext p. 235, where the verse is «རྒྱུད་ལས») introduce the lines with «རྒྱུད་རྣམས་ལས» / «རྒྱུད་ལས» and no title; neither names a tantra anywhere near them (Kong sprul names the Kālacakra just before, p. 235, but for the south-going sign of death, a separate point). Zhwa dmar IV alone names one («དཔལ་རྩ་རྒྱུད་ལས», the Mañjuśrī root tantra, rrd.16:p220), and the Derge text of that tantra does not hold the verse (claim rrd-the-humour-verse-is-not-in-the-derge-manjushri-root-tantra).
