# The Moonbeams' order of the Tibetan thirteenth and fourteenth pairs: another print or its author's change

The Moonbeams gives the enemy's bird and monkey before the friend's horse and sheep in the Tibetan fifteen, where the Zhol blocks, the Taikhang and the 1996 print all give the friend's first; does the Tibetan commentary or any other print of the White Beryl, such as the Derge or a manuscript, keep the Moonbeams' order, or did its author exchange the two pairs himself?

INV-b (2026-11-14), narrowed: no other print or commentary found keeps the *Moonbeams*' order; two Tibetan manuals of the element reckoning (MW1KG21818, MW21984_22CC1A) and BDRC's etexts of WB quote the verse in WB's order, and the search for the other order finds nothing; in the *Moonbeams* itself the thirteenth and fourteenth are the only Tibetan pairs out of the animals' rotation (claim [thirty-wb-13-14-order-in-commentaries](../claims/thirty-wb-13-14-order-in-commentaries.md)). Whether it is a slip or its author's change no text read says. Open.
