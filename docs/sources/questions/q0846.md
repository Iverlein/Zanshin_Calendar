# The 'Bri gung glosses the dreams of water, trees and the green meadow as «གཏད་དུ་ཚུད་པ» (Khro ru «བཏད་དུ་ཚུད་པ»): a pledge, a trust, or a different word?

Printed p. 580 of the Dka' mchan.

## Answered 2026-11-09

Not "a pledge": གཏད (gtad) is to hand over, aim, and, in the rites' vocabulary, a spell or harm sent at another (མཐུ་གཏད, mthu gtad; མནན་གཏད, mnan gtad); བཏད (btad) is the same verb's past stem, so Khro ru and the Drikung write one word. The Drikung's next gloss for the pit and the narrow door is «མནན་པར་ཚུད་པ» (to fall under pressing-down), the same construction. The sign is "having fallen within the aim of (the nāga's) sent harm"; moderate. See st-gtad-du-tshud-pa-under-a-sent-harm.
