# The strip table of pp. 122–125 and དུས་འདེབས after gshed po

Where in WB is the strip table of pp. 122–125 (nine named spirits placed round each year's animal) used or explained, and what are the words དུས་འདེབས (dus 'debs) after gshed po in strips 5 and 12? (RD192, corpus/wb/ch13.md)

## Answered 2026-11-17

Both halves answered from the etexts; the scan was not read.

The extra words are «དུར་འདེབས» (dur 'debs), not «དུས་འདེབས». The spirit of the fifth place is named in full «གཤེད་པོ་དུར་འདེབས» (gshed po dur 'debs, the slayer who buries), and strips 5 and 12 are the two in which the strip prints the whole name instead of «གཤེད་པོ» alone. Both etexts of WB's strip pages read ར, not ས, the 1996 etext (I1KG12906) at [p133] and [p135] («གཤེད་པོ་དུར་ … འདེབས», the two words split by the OCR's line breaks) and the Gser thang etext (I3CN5028) at [p130] and [p132]; they are the OCR of two different prints, so «དུས» is the machine reading of the scan's cursive. The name itself is WB's own: the notes' verses on the nine (Gser thang etext of the notes, I3CN5029 [p197] = printed p. 191) head the fifth «གཤེད་པོ་དུར་འདེབས(༥) ཀླུང་རྟའི་དུར། གཡས་སྐོར་ཞག་བརྒྱད་གོང་ནས་བསྐྱེད།» and the summary on [p198] has «༧གཤེད་པོ་དུར་འདེབས་མར་ཐང་ཡག»; a note on [p221] glosses the same lord as «འདི་གཤེད་པོ་དུར་ཐེབ།» (dur theb). BDRC's phrase search «གཤེད་པོ་དུས་འདེབས» finds nothing; «གཤེད་པོ་དུར་འདེབས» is the one that WB's own notes print.

Where the strip table is used or explained: in the notes on the nine that the same passage gives (wb.2:p190-192, in the pool as the nine black ones, wb.2:p191, and in year-month-earth-lords.md items 1-9): each spirit's seat on the year-horse (klung rta) or beside it, the direction of its course (right or left), the day-count after which it begins, what it harms (for the fifth the notes say the harm falls on the house-head, «ཁྱིམ་དཔོན», and those near him; the sense of «ཀོ་ལོང» is the note's own and not looked up here) and the mending. The strips of pp. 122-125 draw those nine round each year's animal; WB's page does not say so in a sentence of its own, so what the strips are for still rests on the pool's comparison, not on a caption.
