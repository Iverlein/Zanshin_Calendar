# Do the British Museum's three carp kites in Hiroshige's Surugadai print stand against Wikipedia's single black carp?

To be judged on the print itself, not on the two descriptions.

## Answered 2026-11-24

RD409 follow-up. Both stand. On the Tokyo National Museum's copy (A-10569-7353) one great black carp fills the left half and two smaller dark carp fly farther off: three carp, none red. See [claims/surugadai-carp-three-all-black.md](../claims/surugadai-carp-three-all-black.md).
