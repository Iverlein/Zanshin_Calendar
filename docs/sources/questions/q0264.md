# What the road omens of the joined hands say

The road omens that close cutting the joined hands (p. 140, ll. 27–28): meeting a person of another clan talking, or a man and woman whose line was cut, "takes hold" («ཐེབས»); a horse, donkey, widow or driven sheep is "the omen of turning back" («ལྡོག་པའི་ལ་ཉེ»); none, "wandering" («འབྱམས»). What takes hold, turns back or wanders (the cutting, the dead, the run of deaths), and is anything done after each?
