# Do the older manuscripts of the Aṣṭāṅgahṛdaya all read «gosarge» at 6.63, and is the Saṅgraha's «sadyaḥ» against the Hṛdaya's «tadahaḥ» a difference of the two works or of their copies?

RD312.


Narrowed (inv-i, 2026-11-13): the Tibetan translation in the Derge Tengyur (ahd:p257) gives the line as «nang par mthong dang gnyid ma log … de yi nyin par 'bras chen 'gyur» («de yi nyin par», on that very day), so the Hṛdaya's «tadaharmahat» stands in the Tibetan too and the time-word «nang par» fits «gosarge»; the Saṅgraha's «sadyaḥ» against the Hṛdaya's «tadahaḥ» is not found in the Tibetan of the Hṛdaya. Whether the two works differ or their copies, no manuscript decides.
