# «སྐར་འབྲས» on p. 351: the planets' and mansions' results or the match's

Is «སྐར་འབྲས» (p. 351) the results of the planets and mansions (points 14 and 15) or of the match as a whole?

RD174 (2026-11-06), narrowed: «སྐར་འབྲས» is a standing heading for the mansion's results (NM's election table: «གཟའ», «སྐར་འབྲས», «ཚེས་འབྲས»; WB «གཟའ་སྐར་འབྲས་བུ», pp. 448–449). Whether the planet (point 14) is included is not said.
