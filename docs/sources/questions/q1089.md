# What does the verb གདོན mean in the remedy durations of the Kanjur Svapnanirdeśa-parivarta (Toh 48, etext p. 417: «four years» for the fourth stage)?

RD381.

## Answered 2026-11-13

It is the infinitive of «འདོན་པ» ('don pa), "to recite", not a length of time: «གདོན་པར་བྱ» is "is to be recited". The remedy of the stages is the Dharma-teaching of the Three Heaps recited three times by day and three by night, and the years (seven at the first stage down to one at the seventh) are the nouns before the verb. The lexica (Rangjung Yeshe, Tshig mdzod chen mo, Negi, with the same formula in the Bodhicaryāvatāra) give the verb.
