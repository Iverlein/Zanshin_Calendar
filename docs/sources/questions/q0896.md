# In ch. 91's remedies for a spirit that leads the sleeper away in his dream, what does ཕྱྭ (phywa) mean beside མོ (mo, divination)?

RD294.

## Answered 2026-11-10

RD356: ཕྱྭ is the omen: the prints read ཕྱྭ (Serthang, Bhutan, Drathang), the Blue Beryl glosses «མོ་ཕྱྭ་བྱས་ནས་གདོན་ངོས་བཟུང་» (divination done with its omen, the spirit identified), and the dictionaries list མོ་ཕྱྭ as divination's omen.
