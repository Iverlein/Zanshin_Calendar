# Is the Garland of the Sngags rim chen mo the Vajrāvalī of Abhayākaragupta, as the matching wording of the whirling-mantra rite and of «ཐེག་པ་ཆེན་པོ་ཉིད་རྒྱུད་ནས་མཆོག་ཏུ་ཆེར་འབྱུང་བ» suggests, and what does the Derge print of the Vajrāvalī read at the line the OCR gives as «ཨ་རི་ལ་ལྷུང་བ» (the Sngags rim chen mo: «རི་ལས་ལྷུང་བ», falling from a mountain)?

RD373.

## Answered 2026-11-13

Yes, and the Derge reads «རི་ལ་ལྷུང་བ». The Derge Tengyur's Vajrāvalī (Toh 3140, etext VE23703_1391) holds the Sngags rim chen mo's sentence in the pupil's-staying rite with the same words as the Peking print: «ཐེག་པ་ཆེན་པོ་ཉིད་རྒྱུད་ནས་མཆོག་ཏུ་ཆེར་འབྱུང་བ་མངའ་བ་གང་ཡིན་པའོ», the whirling mantra, «tell me the dream at dawn», the lion's posture, the torma at dawn and the question about dreams (vjv:p230-231b, etext p. 140). At the line the Peking OCR gives as «ཨ་རི་ལ་ལྷུང་བ» the Derge etext reads «རི་ལ་ལྷུང་བ» (falling at/onto a mountain), without the ཨ, so the ཨ is the Peking OCR's stray; the Sngags rim chen mo's «རི་ལས་ལྷུང་བ» (falling from a mountain) differs from both in the particle (la against las). The claim vjv-garland-whirling-mantra that the Garland is the Vajrāvalī stands, now on two prints; both are etext (OCR), not read on a scan.
