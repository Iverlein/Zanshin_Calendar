# Padma dkar po's «ཆ་དགུའི་ས» and WB's «དགུ་ཆའི་སར» on p. 282

Does Padma dkar po's «ཆ་དགུའི་ས» (the nine cells of the serpent's breadth) hold for WB's «ལྟོ་འཕྱེ་གནས་པའི་དགུ་ཆའི་སར» on p. 282 as well, or is WB's «དགུ་ཆ» the nine parts of p. 281's ten great parts? The commentator is a witness on the ground rite, not a gloss on WB.

RD169 (2026-11-06), narrowed: WB's own p. 281 gives a great part of nine small parts, and one *mtho* of the body equals one great part, so «དགུ་ཆའི་སར» can be that great part, not only Padma dkar po's nine cells. «རང་ཞལ་དོ» (img. 287) unsettled.
