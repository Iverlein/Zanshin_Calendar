# Which white and which red in the household's birth mewa

The household's birth-mewa readings (WB vol. 2, p. 52) and the mewa's copper vessel (pp. 54–55) say "white" and "red" without a number. Which of 1, 6 and 8 white, and of 7 and 9 red on p. 52, is meant?

## Answered 2026-11-14

The red of p. 52 is the seven red, and the white is the one white of the three: p. 52's red readings («མཚོན་གྲིར་ཤི་བ… བྲག་དང་ཆུ་འཐབ་བར་ལུས») are the vessel's «བདུན་དམར» (died by the blade, hidden between rock and water, pp. 54–55); the nine red is the fortune's and has no row on p. 52. The white is numbered nowhere, in the vessel or the notes («དཀར་པོ» only), so one reading serves 1, 6 and 8 ([claims/wb-household-mewa-white-red-by-the-vessel.md](../claims/wb-household-mewa-white-red-by-the-vessel.md)). An inference from the matching consequences; nothing numbers the whites apart.
