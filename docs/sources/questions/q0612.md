# «ཆུབ» in the Chub day of pp. 335 and 341

What does «ཆུབ» mean in the Chub day (ཆུབ་ཉི) of p. 335 and p. 341, the Paired with Jupiter on Pūrvabhādrapadā?

RD177 (2026-11-06): dead end; no commentary glosses Chub; the Bhutan digest lists «ཆུབ་ཉི་བདུན» (etext p. 421) without a gloss.

**Narrowed further 2026-11-14 (inv-ttt).** BDRC phrase search «ཆུབ་ཉི་ཞེས» 5 hits: WB's two prints and the Zhol, and the Vaiḍūrya dkar po's own line with the Do shal (MW1KG17539 and others): «zung sbyor dang mtshungs chub nyi zhes / bzang las phal cher thams cad bzang» (the same as the Paired, called Chub nyi: of good works nearly all good), no gloss of «chub»; «ཆུབ་ཉི་བཟང» one hit (the Bod ljongs sman rtsis khang's 2013 almanac, no text). Steinert's 64 dictionaries: no headword «chub nyi» beyond Laine's list of abbreviations (Dzongkha «chubi», a day-name abbreviation, no sense given); «chub pa» is "accomplished, perfected, complete" (Rangjung Yeshe, Ives Waldo, Barron: paripūrṇa, Mahāvyutpatti 1612-13), which fits a day on which works come to completion but is not a gloss of the day-name.
