# Is «ཚད་ཟླ» on p. 9 a word of its own or a slip for «ཚང་ཟླ»

The 1996 print and its Gser thang reprint print «ཚད་ཟླ» clearly, with a final da, in the black luck pebble's verse of the seventh circle (WB vol. 2, p. 9, l. 6), where Zhechen Gyaltsab's verse has «ཚང་ཟླ»: is «ཚད་ཟླ» a word of its own for a kind of kin, or a slip of the block for «ཚང་ཟླ»? (RD90)

RD178 (2026-11-06): Yigdzin-1 reads «ཚད་ཟླ་མག་སྐུད་བུ་ཚར་ངན» on Zhol img. 638 (p. 9, l. 6), with the da: a third print with the da, machine reading only; by eye at 3× ཚད against ཚང is not certain. Stays open.

RD178 (2026-11-07): RD178 second run: on the Zhol print (img. 638, line 2, 4×) the second letter of «ཚད» has the curl of da; the Taikhang is fused. Stays open.

RD178 (2026-11-13, seventh run): Zhol img. 638, line 2, 4×: the hand is cursive, da against nga not told apart; unchanged.
