# Is བཤོར in the Sha za brtsegs pa'i rgyud's «རི་དགས་བཤོར་དང་ཤ་ཁྲག་སྟེར» a word of the text, and does རྨི་ལྟས in the colophon mean the practitioner's dreams

The ninth section's second group describes acts and gifts and the work succeeding; the title appears only in the colophon.

**Narrowed 2026-11-13.** The first half: «བཤོར» is a word of the text, «བཤོར་བ» (bshor ba) "to hunt, to snare, to catch game": Rangjung Yeshe "hunting; to hunt", Valby "chase", the Tshig mdzod chen mo and Tshig gter rgya mtsho «རྔོན་རྒྱག་པའི་དོན་དུ་གོ་བ» ("hunting"), Dan Martin from Btsan lha «རྔོན་པའི་བྱ་དང་རི་དྭགས་སོགས་འཛིན་པའམ་གསོད་པ» ("to seize or kill birds and wild animals as a hunter"), the Brda dkrol gser gyi me long quoting the Mdzangs blun: «བྱ་རྣམས་བཤོར་བ»; Negi's Sanskrit dictionary: bandhanam, ākarṣaṇam (snaring birds, fish). So «རི་དགས་བཤོར» is "hunting deer", and the BDRC search (2026-11-13) finds it in 38 hits, among them the Ngor chos 'byung's «རི་དགས་བཤོར་དུ་སོང་» ("went deer-hunting"). The line reads "hunting deer, giving flesh and blood". The second half (whether «རྨི་ལྟས» in the colophon means the practitioner's dreams) is not settled: the lines name signs of nearness, none says "in a dream".

**Narrowed again 2026-11-14.** The second half: «རྨི་ལྟས» (rmi ltas) is, in the Tshig mdzod chen mo and the Tshig gter rgya mtsho, «རྨི་ལམ་བཟང་ངན་གྱི་མཚན་མ» (the sign of a good or bad dream), and in Ives Waldo "signs in dreams, omens in dreams", Hackett "dream signs" (tmz:box8): so the chapter's title names signs seen in dreams, and the title does not say whose. The Great Black One is asked for «བསྙེན་སྒྲུབ་མཚན་མ» (the signs of the approach and accomplishment), the rite being the practitioner's; that the dreamer is the practitioner is then the reading the question suggests, not a line of the text. BDRC's exact-phrase search of 2026-11-14 finds the nine lines «རི་དགས་བཤོར་དང་ཤ་ཁྲག་སྟེར» in a second carrier of the tantra (MW2CZ7955, its chapters etexts of poor quality, snippets in Wylie only): the 18th chapter «drag sngags kyi le'u», the 19th «rmi ltas kyi le'u», the 20th «sbyin bsreg gi le'u», the 21st on the black raven-faced one; the same verses stand there as the 19th chapter, spelled «ri dgas bshor dang sha khrag ster», which confirms «bshor» as a word of the text and the title as the chapter's own. No commentary found; the phrases «བསྙེན་སྒྲུབ་མཚན་མ་བསྟན་དུ་གསོལ», «ཤ་ཟ་བརྩེགས་པའི་རྒྱུད་ལས་རྨི་ལྟས» and «རྨི་ལྟས་ཀྱི་ལེའུ་སྟེ་དུམ་བུ་དགུ་པ» 0 hits.
