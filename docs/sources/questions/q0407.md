# Are the Taikhang reproduction and the Zhol print two impressions of different blocks?

The Taikhang reproduction and the Zhol print read the same on most lines of ch. 27 but not on p. 9 l. 6 («མག་རྒུད» against «མག་སྐུད»), p. 17 l. 26 («བུར» against «འུར») and the same line's water source («ཆུ་མིག» against «ཆུ་མག»), by Yigdzin-1: are they two impressions of different blocks, or one block read differently by the machine, and which of each pair does a reader of the print see?

**Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-z1).** BDRC's records: the Taikhang reproduction MW30116 (T. Tsepal Taikhang, New Delhi, 2 vols., relief woodblock) carries the note "reproduced from a print from the Lhasa blocks of Burmiok Athing" (bdo:biblioNote), and the Zhol print MW1KG1617 is "633 ff., relief woodblock", Zhol par khang; the records do not say that the blocks are the same (sources.yaml's "the same blocks" rests on layout alone). A Lhasa print of another house's blocks, not an impression of the Zhol blocks, is what the catalogue allows, which would fit the three lines that part. Not settled: the two prints' colophons were not compared.
