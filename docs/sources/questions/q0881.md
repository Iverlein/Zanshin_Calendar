# Who or what is the «dmar mo» (the Red One, a red woman) whose cremation ashes are used in the rite on GNC p. 558?

RD286; Steinert's dictionaries not consulted.


Narrowed (inv-i, 2026-11-13): Steinert's 64 dictionaries (a fresh clone, locate.py dict, 2026-11-13) have «dmar mo» as a headword in four: Valby «red, red cow, red», Ives Waldo «red [cow]», the Tshig mdzod chen mo and the Bod yig tshig gter rgya mtsho «like dmar po» (both only the feminine of the colour word); «dmar mo 'das pa» has no headword. So the lexica know «dmar mo» as the colour and, in two glossaries, as a red cow; a dead red cow whose ashes are used would suit the rite, but no text says so and nothing names a red woman or a being.
