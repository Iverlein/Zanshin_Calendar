# WB ch. 19 p. 213: do the wheel (འཁོར་ལོ) and the star (སྐར་མ) each count 27 in the first circle's long figure?

The figure reads 82,776,132,766,945,179,600 on the reprint and fits the word-numerals ཀླུ, འཁོར་ལོ, ཐུབ་པ, རོ, འདོད, སྐར་མ, མཚམས, དུས, བུག, མཚོ, དབང, གཟུགས, རི, བུག, རོ only if both count 27.

## Answered 2026-11-21

Yes. Both the wheel (འཁོར་ལོ) and the star (སྐར་མ) count 27 in the long figure of the first circle, which is 82776132766945179600.

Checked 2026-11-21 (rd-t), by the word-numerals and the print's own usage (the full working is in q690's answer of 2026-11-16, which this confirms). Written from the lowest place up the words are: nam mkha' 0, mkha' 0 and ro 6 (together «མཁའ་རོ» 60), bug 9, ri 7, gzugs 1; dbang 5, mtsho 4, bug 9, dus 6, mtshams 6, skar ma 27; 'dod 13 (the print's «འདོད(༡༣)» on p. 192 and «འདོད(༡༣༨༠)» in ch. 8), ro 6, thub pa 7, 'khor lo 27, klu 8. That is 0,0,6,9,7,1 | 5,4,9,6,6,7,2 | 3,1,6,7,7,2,8, and written from the top 82776132766945179600, twenty digits, the string both etexts print. The wheel is 27 elsewhere in WB («འཁོར་ལོ ༢༧ བསྣན» on pp. 34 and 48) and the sun's mansions are 27 (ch. 4: «the remainder multiplied by 27 (the wheel)»); with ༢ alone for the wheel the figure would be one digit shorter and could not carry the «77» after «82». No glyph reading was needed.
