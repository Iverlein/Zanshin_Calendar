# What are the three words «རམ་རུག་སྦང་གཡེར» among the gossip goods in LGD's bad list?

Not in any dictionary consulted: goods, a place or persons?


Narrowed (RD271, 2026-11-09): the «རམ་རུག་སྦང་གཡེར» is a short list of goods of a rite (མི་ཁའི་རྫས, mi kha'i rdzas, the substances of a rite against gossip), not a three-word name or a place or persons: «རམ་རུག» holds the slot of the plant ram bu or ram pa, «སྦང» is སྦང་མ (the beer's mash) and «གཡེར» is གཡེར་མ (the Sichuan pepper), each written short; Kongtrul's treasury's lists name «རམ་བུ་སྦང་མ་གཡེར་མ» together as goods of gossip. What ram rug is exactly (the plant, by a spelling by ear) stays open; the spelling «རམ་ཏུག»/«རམ་བྲུག» occurs. Claims lgd-mi-kha-goods-of-gossip-are-rite-substances, lgd-ram-rug-is-the-ram-bu-spelling, lgd-sbang-g-yer-beer-mash-and-pepper.

Narrowed further (inv-f, 2026-11-14, BDRC phrase search «རམ་རུག་ལ», «ཞབས་རམ་རུག», «རམ་རྒྱུག»): a third place of the Lama Gongdü cycle itself has the word, in Sangs rgyas gling pa's practice texts (Bla rung 2006, MW1GS60392, the volume 'Bla ma dgongs pa 'dus pa las: sngon 'gro chos spyod sogs kyi skor'): «…བྲ་སྲན་རམ་རུག་ལ་སོགས་མི་ཁའི་རྫས་བསགས་པ་དང༌།» (snippet only), "gathering the goods of gossip, buckwheat-and-pea, ram rug and the like", set against the good store of grain, fruit and the rest laid up in the nest (tshang); so the cycle's own wording again names ram rug a good of gossip, in the company of the cheap field grains (the Karma chags med ransom has it beside 'bru nag, black grain, and «བྲ་སྲན་རམ་རུག»). That points to ram rug as a coarse food or crop of the same family as ram bu/ram pa, not a pungent spice; it does not settle which plant. The search «རམ་རྒྱུག» (15 hits) finds only «དཀྱིལ་འཁོར་རམ་རྒྱུག་རིས» (a mandala's drawn line, a different word), so that spelling is not this item. Still open: no source glosses the word; the three spellings of the earlier notes remain.

## Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-j)

BDRC phrase search «རམ་རུག་སོགས» (3 hits, 2026-11-16) gives two more contexts. Karma chags med's ransom (MW2PD20252) has «'bru nag tshwa sgog ram rug sogs rdzongs su 'debs», black grain, salt, garlic, ram rug and the like thrown into a ravine; the Mgo log sprul sku's Bde mchog empowerment (MW3KG448, MW3KG454) lays «btsod mtshur ram rug sogs mi kha'i rdzas» in the corners of the platform beside black thread and dog hair. So ram rug is again named among the «mi kha'i rdzas», here beside salt and garlic (pungent or coarse household goods) and not only the field grains; the plant stays unidentified, no text glosses it.

**Narrowed 2026-11-17 (inv-c).** BDRC phrase search «རམ་རུག་ཞེས་པ» and «རམ་རུག་ནི» (2026-11-17): 0 hits, so no text defines the word in a gloss of the form "ram rug means". Still open.

**Narrowed 2026-11-17 (inv-e).** BDRC phrase search «རམ་རུག་སྦང» and «རམ་རུག་དང་» (2026-11-17): the word comes in one more fixed company. The *Rin chen gter mdzod chen mo* (Kongtrul's treasury) has «ram rug sbang g.yer la sogs mi kha'i rdzas la spyod pa» (the very three of LGD, used as goods of gossip), and, with the Lama Gongdü cycle's «Mkha' 'gro lnga'i mo skor» (the divination of the five ḍākinīs), the passage «snum skye khra bo cig … nang du ram rug dang sbang ma la sogs mi kha'i rdzas kyis bkang» (a speckled bag … filled within with ram rug, beer-mash and the like, the goods of gossip); the Karma chags med ransom has «'bru nag ram rug sbang ma sogs», black grain, ram rug, beer-mash. So «རམ་རུག་དང་སྦང་མ» go as a pair, the word named as a thing that fills a bag, beside grain and beer-mash: a coarse substance of the same class as these, not a person, a place or a spice. Tibetan–Sanskrit side: Monier-Williams (2026-11-17) has no «ramyaruc», «rāmaruk» or «ramaruk»; only «ramaṭha / rāmaṭha», asafoetida (a western people's name, also the spice), a word the Tibetan sources write hing, not ram rug, so the guess of a Sanskrit loan has nothing under it. Steinert's dictionary site's web API answers an empty list to every term for the dictionary names tried (the 64 dictionaries are not read here); the Mahāvyutpatti was not reached. Still open: the plant or substance is not glossed.
