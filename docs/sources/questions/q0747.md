# Does the Zhol print or the 1996 edition write «ཀྱང» and «ཁུངས» where the Gser thang reprint has «ཀུང» (pp. 329–330: «གྲོགས་ཀུང་དར», «ལུགས་ཀུང་ཡོད་མོད་ཏུང») and «ཁུང» («ལུང་ཁུང་དབེན»), or is the reprint's spelling the older one?

## Looked at 2026-11-16 (inv-u)

Narrowed by the etexts, the scans not read. BDRC exact search: «གྲོགས་ཀུང་དར» 0 hits, «གྲོགས་ཀྱང་དར» 8 hits, among them the Beijing 1996 etext (MW2CZ8040, I1KG12907, offset 762025), the Gser thang etext (I3CN5029) and two Do shal copies; so the 1996 etext has «ཀྱང» (the Gser thang etext too, though the reprint's scan reads «ཀུང», RD159: the etext of the reprint corrects its spelling, so it is no witness for the scan). «ལུང་ཁུང་དབེན» 2 hits: the 1996 etext and the Gser thang etext; «ལུང་ཁུངས་དབེན» 3 hits: the Do shal (two copies) and the *Bai dur g.ya' sel* (I5875); so the 1996 etext has «ཁུང» as the reprint does, and «ཁུངས» is the spelling of the later books. «ལུགས་ཀུང་/ཀྱང་ཡོད་མོད་ཏུང» 0 hits in both spellings (the etext's wording of that line differs). So: the 1996 print probably reads «ཀྱང» in «གྲོགས་ཀྱང་དར» but «ཁུང» in «ལུང་ཁུང་དབེན», i.e. the reprint's «ཀུང» is a spelling the 1996 edition mends and its «ཁུང» is not; the Zhol print not seen.
