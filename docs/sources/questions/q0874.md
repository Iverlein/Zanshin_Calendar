# Is the first segment of the Svapnohana's second-watch line «བརྒྱད་པ་ལ» (Narthang, Peking) or «བརྒྱད་བར་ལ» (Derge etext) the older reading, and which does the Dpe bsdur ma's collation give?

The collation markers of the Dpe bsdur ma notes are lost in the OCR (RD280).

**Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-z2).** BDRC's exact search «ཐུན་གཉིས་ཟླ་བ་བརྒྱད་བར་ལ» has 4 hits, all with «བར»: the Derge etext of the Svapnohana in two copies (the work and the text inside the Chi ba bslu ba'i man ngag), Bya mdo Klu byams rgyal's *Bod lugs gso rig gi sems khams rig pa* (quoting «rgyal ba'i bstan 'gyur rin po che las»), and Padma rdo rje's 2002 book; «ཐུན་གཉིས་ཟླ་བ་བརྒྱད་པ་ལ» and «…བརྒྱད་པར་ལ» give 0. The verse's own series, «ཐུན་གཉིས་ཟླ་བ་བརྒྱད་བར་ལ། ཐུན་གསུམ་ཟླ་བ་གསུམ་གྱི་བར། ཐུན་བཞི་ཟླ་བ་ཕྱེད་བར་དུ», uses «བར» in all three lines ('up to'), which makes «བརྒྱད་བར་ལ» the reading that fits its neighbours, and «པ» Narthang's and Peking's variant, as claim svo-dpe-watch-line-entries has it. Which is older is not shown by any text; the Dpe bsdur ma etext is not among the hits, so its base reading is still read from the note only.
