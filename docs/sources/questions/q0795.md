# What are «Dpal rno bkra» and «ma mar mo» in the GNC heading and the mantra-water rite?

Read by machine, unclear.

## Answered 2026-11-10

Answered in part by RD315 (2026-11-10): GNC's history of the Dpal rno bkra (vol. 1 pp. 493–494, I1662 img. 501–502, Yigdzin-1 through the slot) names Rno bkra as a volume (glegs bam) that the Great Compassionate One gave to the queen 'Od ldan rgyal mo; earthquake and hail followed, the gods fled, and Hayagrīva punishes wrong view; it is said to be spoken by the Great Orgyan on the 23rd of a month of a fire-monkey year (month word doubtful). The word «rno bkra» itself is not glossed.
