# Work 14: the 14th, and the 21st to 25th as a run or two dates

Is the 14th in work 14 bad (in «དྲུག་ནས་བཅུ་བཞིའི་བར») or good (the good line's «བཅུ་བཞི་དང་»), and does the 21st to 25th run or are the 21st and 25th two dates, when the 1996 editors number the bare pair «བཅུ་དྲུག་བཅུ་དགུའི་བར» as a run and «ཉེར་གཅིག་ཉེར་ལྔའི་བར» as two dates? See verdicts.md, RD124.

## Answered 2026-11-14

Answered: the 21st to 25th is a run, and the 14th stands in both lines, bad and good, in the Vaidurya dkar po's own wording.

The run. The 21–25 part was answered on 2026-11-14 by q156 (inv-yy): three manuals that quote the *Vaidurya dkar po* carry «ཉེར་གཅིག་ཉེར་ལྔའི་བར» as the one good run.

The 14th. The same manuals carry both lines whole, and the 14th is in both. BDRC's exact-phrase search «བཅུ་བཞི་བཅོ་ལྔ་ཉེར་གཅིག་ཉེར་ལྔའི་བར» (2 hits, 2026-11-14) and «བཅུ་དྲུག་བཅུ་དགུའི་བར» (2 hits) give the Rebkong anthology of reckonings (MW1KG6014) and Kon mchog skyabs's *Bod kyi dmangs srol dang 'brel ba'i skar rtsis* (MW29040), and the snippets read, in the Wylie of BDRC's etext:

> tshes gcig dang drug nas bcu bzhi'i bar dang / bcu drug bcu dgu'i bar dang / nyer drug rnams ngan pas spang / gsum bzhi lnga bcu bzhi bco lnga nyer gcig nyer lnga'i bar bzang / gzhan ma rnams btang snyoms yin zhes bai dkar las gsungs

("the 1st, the 6th to the 14th, the 16th to the 19th and the 26th are shunned as bad; the 3rd, 4th, 5th, 14th, 15th, 21st to 25th are good; the others neutral, so the *Bai dur dkar po* says"). So the bad line's «དྲུག་ནས་བཅུ་བཞིའི་བར» ends at the 14th and the good line's «བཅུ་བཞི» is the 14th: the quoting manuals have the same overlap as WB's print, in the same words, and each says the verse is the *Bai dur dkar po*'s. The overlap is therefore not an editors' slip in the 1996 numbering and not a variant of the Zhol print; it stands in the text as the tradition quotes it. None of the manuals (snippets only, search-only etexts) says which line rules for the 14th, and none lays the 14th in one line only. The bare pair «བཅུ་དྲུག་བཅུ་དགུའི་བར» is a run (16–19) here as in the editors' numbering.

State of the evidence: BDRC search snippets of etexts (machine), not read on a scan; the run for the 21st to 25th is the more firmly supported, the 14th's two verdicts are a fact of the text, not a reading.
