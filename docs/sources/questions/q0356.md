# «གནས་སྐྱུག» or «གནས་སྒྱུག» on higher-resolution blocks

The in-laws of WB p. 362 and the notes p. 526, «གནས་སྐྱུག» or «གནས་སྒྱུག», and «སྐྱུག་མོ» or «སྒྱུག་མོ»: the typeset stacks are filled with ink and Mipham's print is low in resolution; which spelling do the blocks of WB (Zhol, Taikhang) and of Mipham's notes carve, read at a higher resolution?

## Answered 2026-11-14

Answered 2026-11-14 (inv-rr) on question 211's answer by inv-nn (open-questions.md, item 211): the same stack, the in-laws of WB p. 362 and the notes' p. 526. The word is «གནས་སྒྱུག»: the verse of p. 362 reads «gnas sgyug spar kha 'thab na ngan» in the 1996 etext, the *Do shal* and the *Kun 'dus chen mo*; «སྒྱུག་མོ» is the mother-in-law in every dictionary read, there is no headword «skyug mo», and «སྐྱུག» is to vomit; the notes' own gloss «གནས་མ་སྟེ་མནའ་མ་དང་སྒྱུག་མོ» names the mother-in-law. The notes' lemma «གནས་སྐྱུག» is the 1996 etext's reading. Which letter the Taikhang and Zhol blocks carve under the ས (the Taikhang stack leaned «སྐྱུག», RD133) is scan-bound and no longer decides the word: the spelling by sense and by every witness that can be read is «སྒྱུག». Closed.
