# The 六甲の夢うらなひ of the 夢合長寿鑑絵抄 (image 27) gives a meaning to a dream for each of the sixty days; do its articles for the days of the table (the days named by branch in mdz:p11, e.g. 未 申 戌 in the first month) speak of keeping the dream silent, and what are its sixty articles in full?

RD371.

## Answered 2026-11-10

All sixty cells of the 六甲の夢うらなひ and the twelve of the 十二直夢占 are written out in mdz:i426556b, from canvases 26-30 of the Kyoto copy read by eye at full size. None of the seventy-two cells speaks of keeping a dream silent or of telling it; the days of the table of mdz:p11 (未 申 戌 in the first month and so on) have no cell of their own, either in kind or in tone: the five cells of each branch carry mixed readings.
