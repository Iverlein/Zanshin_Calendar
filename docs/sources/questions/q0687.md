# The small stacked figures beside the line ends of pp. 190–196

What are the small stacked figures that the print of WB vol. 1 sets beside many line ends of pp. 190-196 (three or four rows of tiny digits with dots between, as after «ཚེས་དཀྱིལ་འགྲུབ།» on p. 190) and in the cells of the Jupiter and Saturn table on p. 195 (the house-circle, tithi-circle, solar day, house-day, tithi-day and the place's enjoyment)? Are they the whole numbers and fractional parts (ཆ་ཤས) of the quantities each line names, and where can they be read in a larger print (the Zhol print WBZ, I1KG1710, or the Taikhang I5878)? (RD185, corpus/wb/ch17.md)

## Read again 2026-11-21 (rd-w)

Not advanced by a check: the stacked figures are tiny glyphs that earlier readers blotted (PLAN.md Dead ends) and that I did not look at again; no word-numeral run or arithmetic fixes a value for them. Where they stand in pp. 70-80 of ch. 10 (the brackets after «ཉིན་ཞག་གཅིག་གི་ལོངས་སྤྱད་དེ», «དལ་རྐང་», «སོ་སོས»), the bracket is the print's place for a figure, so the stacks are probably small whole-and-part figures; still open, the larger print (Zhol I1KG1710, Taikhang I5877) of the pages to be located first.
