# The small stacked figures beside the line ends of pp. 190–196

What are the small stacked figures that the print of WB vol. 1 sets beside many line ends of pp. 190-196 (three or four rows of tiny digits with dots between, as after «ཚེས་དཀྱིལ་འགྲུབ།» on p. 190) and in the cells of the Jupiter and Saturn table on p. 195 (the house-circle, tithi-circle, solar day, house-day, tithi-day and the place's enjoyment)? Are they the whole numbers and fractional parts (ཆ་ཤས) of the quantities each line names, and where can they be read in a larger print (the Zhol print WBZ, I1KG1710, or the Taikhang I5878)? (RD185, corpus/wb/ch17.md)

## Read again 2026-11-21 (rd-w)

Not advanced by a check: the stacked figures are tiny glyphs that earlier readers blotted (PLAN.md Dead ends) and that I did not look at again; no word-numeral run or arithmetic fixes a value for them. Where they stand in pp. 70-80 of ch. 10 (the brackets after «ཉིན་ཞག་གཅིག་གི་ལོངས་སྤྱད་དེ», «དལ་རྐང་», «སོ་སོས»), the bracket is the print's place for a figure, so the stacks are probably small whole-and-part figures; still open, the larger print (Zhol I1KG1710, Taikhang I5877) of the pages to be located first.

**Narrowed 2026-11-24 (sweep from inv-j).** Narrowed on 2026-11-24 with the 1996 etext (I1KG12906, etext pages 200–206 = printed pp. 190–196), which types these brackets and marks: the stacked figures are not a separate kind of figure.
- The stacked brackets of pp. 190–196 are typed as groups, and the groups read together are the word-numeral run before them: «ཀླུ་མིག་ཀླུ་ལག(༢ ༨༢ ༨)» = 2828, «བླ་མ་སོ་མེ་རྒྱ་མཚོ(༤༣ ༣༢)» = 4332 (Jupiter's circle), «ཉི་བུ་དུས་མཚམས་རི་ཕྱོགས(༡༠ ༧༦༦)» = 10766 (Saturn's), «དབུགས་ཉིས་ཁྲི་ཆིག་སྟོང་དྲུག་བརྒྱའི(༢ ༡༦༠༠)» = 2·1600. So the rows are one figure broken for the line's width, not whole and part (the case of p. 41, where whole days over sixtieths are the sense, is q779).
- The tiny marks at line ends (the «3 or 4 rows with dots» after «ཚེས་དཀྱིལ་འགྲུབ།» of p. 190) the etext types as plain small numerals after the stop: p. 190 «ཚེས་དཀྱིལ་འགྲུབ། ༥ ༦»; p. 192 «ཡུལ་ལོངས་སྤྱོད། ༤», «འགྲུབ། ༢༡» twice; p. 193 «འཁོར་ཡིན། ༧», «ཡིན། ༩»; p. 194 «ཚེས་ཞག ༡༤ ༢», «༢༡», «༡༤», «༣༡», «༥», «༡», «༢༠»; p. 195 «ཡིན། ༢༡», «ཡིན། ༡»; p. 196 «༨», «ཡིན། ༢༠». Whether they number the rows of the print's table, the steps of the method or give a count of places is not told by the words; no word-numeral run stands beside them. Cells of the Jupiter and Saturn table of p. 195 (etext «(༡༠ ༡༠ ༢༧༤༧༢)», «(༧༧༧ ༡༣༤༤)») are typed too: the bracket's rows read together do equal the runs before them where those are word-numerals, and the table was not checked cell by cell.
