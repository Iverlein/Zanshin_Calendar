# The 夢合延寿袋大成 (1814, 夢占道人), the 夢合判断大成 (1830) and the 清明秘鈔夢判事 (1862), dream books listed in the catalogue without a digitised copy at the NDL, may be held open at another library (Waseda, the University of Tokyo, a university's collection): does any of them set out the table of the days on which a dream is not told, with a legend for the slash, or a 初夢 night and its calendar?

RD340.

## Answered 2026-11-13

Narrowed. Of the three titles: the 夢合延寿袋大成 is open at the NDL (pid 2533938, mdz:i2533938), Kyoto, Kyushu (two copies), Osaka, Tohoku (22629, 22653) and the Edo-Tokyo Museum (11383); the Kyoto, Kyushu and Osaka copies were read for a table in RD344 and RD364 and have none. The 清明秘鈔夢判事 (1862) is open in two copies, Koizumi ORMK-00651 and Tohoku THKK-22631 (37 images, contact sheets and image 35 at 6240 px, 2026-11-13): the same book (藤村秀賀輯, 錦耕堂), running articles of dreams by group with pictures, and on the leaf before the colophon the same day-branch charm table as mdz:i65134 (ten cells, no 子 and no 丑, the box of the vermilion charm); no table of the days not to tell, no 初夢 calendar. The 夢合判断大成 (1830) is not in Kokubunken; the nearest, the 夢判斷大成 (Tohoku THKK-22655, 17 images), was seen in RD364 without a table. Waseda's holdings (HTTP 503 in three sessions) were not reached.
