# The 夢合延寿袋大成 (1814, 夢占道人), the 夢合判断大成 (1830) and the 清明秘鈔夢判事 (1862), dream books listed in the catalogue without a digitised copy at the NDL, may be held open at another library (Waseda, the University of Tokyo, a university's collection): does any of them set out the table of the days on which a dream is not told, with a legend for the slash, or a 初夢 night and its calendar?

RD340.

## Answered 2026-11-13

Narrowed. Of the three titles: the 夢合延寿袋大成 is open at the NDL (pid 2533938, mdz:i2533938), Kyoto, Kyushu (two copies), Osaka, Tohoku (22629, 22653) and the Edo-Tokyo Museum (11383); the Kyoto, Kyushu and Osaka copies were read for a table in RD344 and RD364 and have none. The 清明秘鈔夢判事 (1862) is open in two copies, Koizumi ORMK-00651 and Tohoku THKK-22631 (37 images, contact sheets and image 35 at 6240 px, 2026-11-13): the same book (藤村秀賀輯, 錦耕堂), running articles of dreams by group with pictures, and on the leaf before the colophon the same day-branch charm table as mdz:i65134 (ten cells, no 子 and no 丑, the box of the vermilion charm); no table of the days not to tell, no 初夢 calendar. The 夢合判断大成 (1830) is not in Kokubunken; the nearest, the 夢判斷大成 (Tohoku THKK-22655, 17 images), was seen in RD364 without a table. Waseda's holdings (HTTP 503 in three sessions) were not reached.

Not advanced (inv-qq, 2026-11-14): the NDL Lab full-text search for the table (夢を見て語らざる日 and variants) finds no dream book of the three titles; their other-library copies were not reached (Waseda not tried again in this session).

## Answered 2026-11-14

Narrowed, one new find. The 夢合判断大成 (1830) is the Waseda copy bunko30_e0278 (錦森堂, 馬喰町, 題簽 ゆめはんじ, 版心 夢合, formerly Nakano Koichi's; NDL Search record R000000050-I000028251, link http://archive.wul.waseda.ac.jp/kosho/bunko30/bunko30_e0278/); the Waseda site answered HTTP 503 "Site Maintenance" on the page, the image path and the viewer on 2026-11-14 for the fourth session and the Wayback Machine holds no snapshot, so it was not read. Other records of the title: a paper copy at the NDL (大島屋, 1868 or later, W19-R1, not digitised), a bound pair 大雑書三世相 ; 夢合判断大成 (版心 雑書, 夢合; CiNii BD10251669, same title as the 雑書夢合 of mdz:i234427) and 夢合長寿鏡絵抄 : 夢合判断大成共 (Nagano prefectural library, 富山堂, Meiji): the 判断大成 was printed with the books already read, which makes a table of the days not told in it likely only if the 雑書夢合 is the same text. The 夢合延寿袋大成 and the 清明秘鈔夢判事 stand as in the answer of 2026-11-13; the 夢相金鏡録 of 1790 (not asked about) carries the charm table of the 清明秘鈔 with twelve cells (mdz:i15617). No table of the days not told and no 初夢 night was found in any copy open to read.
