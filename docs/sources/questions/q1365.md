# The two boxes of the model almanac that break the mewa rule (p. 154 box 11, p. 161 box 19)

Two boxes of the model almanac break the rising-by-one rule of the mewa numerals: p. 154 box 11 (11th month) has no trigram letter and no numeral where the rule gives 2, and p. 161 box 19 (3rd month) carries the round 6 where the rule gives 4 (the neighbours run on). A misprint or a mis-cut block? (RD196 follow-up, passage wb.1:p154-166m)
