# What is the sun's klung (ཉི་མའི་ཀླུང) in Langging's list

On p. 186 Langging the red enters «ཉི་མའི་ཀླུང་དང་ལྷན་ཅིག» (the print reads klung, not glang): what is the sun's klung, and is it the Sun's counterpart of the planets' klung in ch. 13's «ཟླ་ཀླུང» column? (RD63; supersedes the ox reading of question 35)

RD162 (2026-11-06): not advanced; «ཟླ་བའི་ཀླུང» appears in almanac year headings (consistent with RD65) without a gloss; the Bon hits are excluded.

**Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-z1).** BDRC phrase «ཉི་མའི་ཀླུང» 5 hits: WB's Beijing print (the Langging line, p. 186), a Bon canon and two Bon works (excluded), and the *Bod kyi rtsis rig kun 'dus chen mo* (MW28845, vol. 2, p. 545, etext): in the verses on the Chinese spring-ox reckoning, which colour the ox and its herdsman by the year's element and the day of the breath-gaining (དབུགས་ཐོབ), the herdsman's parts are matched to the sun's power (ཉི་མའི་དབང་ཐང་), life (སྲོག་ཁམས་), body (ལུས་ཁམས་) and the line closes «དབུགས་ཐོབ་ཉི་མའི་ཀླུང་རྟས་ནོར་བུ་འཆང་» (the sun's klung rta of the breath-gaining holds a jewel). So «ཉི་མའི་ཀླུང» is the sun's *klung rta*, the fourth of power, life, body and klung rta that the almanac gives each year and planet. The *Tshig gter rgya mtsho* glosses «klung rta»: «klung» is a gathering, «rta» help; the outer and inner conditions (place, time, food) that make power, life-breath and the elements firm and growing, «klung rta dar» when they agree, «gud» when they do not; Valby: protective energy tied to bla and phywa, from the five elements; Tshig mdzod chen mo: = «rlung rta». So the column «ཟླ་ཀླུང» of ch. 13 and the sun's klung of Langging's line are the same word, the klung rta of the moon and of the sun. What WB means by «the sun's klung together with» Langging, that is, which element of the sun's klung rta it names, no text read says. Open.
