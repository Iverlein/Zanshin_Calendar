# The eight near planets of p. 99

p. 99 lists eight "near" planets (*nye ba'i gza' brgyad*): lightning, *shu la*, dragon, tail, *btso*, thunder, earthquake, pestle (with their numbers 1 to 8), each placed by counting from the sun's mansion (5th, 8th, 14th, 18th, 20th, 22nd, 23rd, 24th as the text lists them). What are *shu la*, *btso* and *'brug* here, and is the series a known one? (RD191, corpus/wb/ch12.md)
