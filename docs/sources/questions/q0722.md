# The eight near planets of p. 99

p. 99 lists eight "near" planets (*nye ba'i gza' brgyad*): lightning, *shu la*, dragon, tail, *btso*, thunder, earthquake, pestle (with their numbers 1 to 8), each placed by counting from the sun's mansion (5th, 8th, 14th, 18th, 20th, 22nd, 23rd, 24th as the text lists them). What are *shu la*, *btso* and *'brug* here, and is the series a known one? (RD191, corpus/wb/ch12.md)

## Narrowed 2026-11-13

The numbers are counts of feet (*rkang pa*, four to a mansion) from the sun's foot, with the sun in the Pleiades' first foot: the 5th is the first foot of Snar ma, the 8th its last, the 14th a foot of Lag, the 18th and 20th of Nabs so, the 22nd to 24th of Rgyal (feet 1-4 Smin drug, 5-8 Snar ma, 9-12 Mgo, 13-16 Lag, 17-20 Nabs so, 21-24 Rgyal); the text itself says the 5th is the beginning of Snar ma. The names: «ཤུ་ལ» is the Sanskrit *śūla* ('spear, pike', as the same page's spear unions), the rest are lightning, dragon, tail, thunder, earthquake, pestle; «བཙོ» (read «བཅོ» in RD191's translation) and «འབྲུག» were not identified. Searched without a result: the pool (the two pages of WB only) and a web search for the series in Tibetan and for the Sanskrit upagraha lists (Dhūma, Vyatīpāta, Parivesha, Indracāpa, Upaketu; Gulika, Māndi), which are a different series.
