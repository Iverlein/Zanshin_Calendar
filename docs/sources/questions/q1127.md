# Is ཙབ་ཙུབ in the dream line said of the dreams' number and haste only, or is it a kind of dream a reader names

«རྨི་ལམ་ཙབ་ཙུབ་ཤིན་ཏུ་མང» (kd.3:p597): many, hurried, jumbled, or a named kind of dream?

## Answered 2026-11-13

It is an adverbial of manner, not a named kind of dream. «ཙབ་ཙུབ» (tsab tsub) is the contraction of «ཙབ་བེ་ཙུབ་བེ» (Tshig mdzod chen mo, Tshig gter rgya mtsho); the dictionaries give "hurried, rushed, restless, crude" (Rangjung Yeshe, Valby, Ives Waldo, Dan Martin from Das and from Samdo and Zhi byed texts), and the Dag tshig gsar bsgrigs' own example is «བྱ་བ་གལ་ཆེན་བསྒྲུབ་པ་ལ་ཙབ་ཙུབ་མ་བྱེད།» ("do not be hasty in doing the weighty work"). No lexicon or parallel lists it as a kind of dream; the line «རྨི་ལམ་ཙབ་ཙུབ་ཤིན་ཏུ་མང་» says the dreams are very many, and «ཙབ་ཙུབ» says they are restless, hurried, unsettled (jumbled), the same disturbance as «རྨི་ལམ་འཚུབ» ("the dream is troubled") in the iron entry.
