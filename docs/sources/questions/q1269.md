# In 'Jigs med gling pa's tenth-day prayer the birth is on the tenth of the sixth Hor month and the other eleven tenths are named by mansion month (Rgyal, Mchu, … Mgo): which Hor number does each mansion month take there, and is the sixth Hor month the monkey month of the treasure verses (q1166: monkey month = Tibetan 7 = Hor 5)?

Raised by the practice-days investigation (pd-a, 2026-11-16); see the cited passages. First filed as q1252, a number another session used at the same time for the name in the White Beryl p. 70; that question keeps q1252.

## Answered 2026-11-16

Answered for both parts.

The mansion months. The White Beryl's model almanac heads each Hor month with its mansion and its animal (wb.1:p154-171): Mgo is Hor 11 (tiger), Rgyal 12 (hare), the first month 1 (dragon; the almanac prints «རྟ» where the prayer has Mchu), Dbo 2 (snake), Nag 3 (horse), Sa ga 4 (sheep), Snron 5 (monkey), Chu stod 6 (bird), Gro bzhin 7 (dog), Khrums 8 (pig), Tha skar 9 (mouse), Smin drug 10 (ox). The prayer's eleven named months are therefore Hor 12, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, with the numbered sixth month, Chu stod, in the gap between Snron and Gro bzhin.

The monkey month. It is not the sixth Hor month by the White Beryl or by the Phugpa tradition: Zhe chen rgyal tshab writes that the monkey month, as all the learned of Dbus and Gtsang who follow the Phugpa tradition hold, is the fifth Hor month, Snron, and that Gter bdag gling pa and his sons kept the tenth-day offering in it (zgp.13:p541, read on the scan). Two lists that name the months by animal put the birth on the monkey month's tenth and the other eleven deeds, in the prayer's order, in the tiger, hare, dragon, snake, horse, sheep, bird, dog, pig, mouse and ox months (sgc:i208-214, scan; zgp.13:p537-540, machine reading). Numbered by the White Beryl, every tenth of 'Jigs med gling pa falls one month after the same deed's tenth in those lists, and his sixth Hor month stands in the monkey month's place. His list is recorded as differing (claim tenth-day-months-jigme-lingpa-one-month-later-than-the-animal-lists); the monkey month of the treasure verses is Hor 5 (claim monkey-month-is-the-fifth-hor-month-snron), as q1166 found.
