# WB p. 52: what is the exact spelling of the Sanskrit word printed «ཏཏྐལ» (is its second letter ཏྐ?), and of the Crab's name («ཀཎྜར», «ཀརྚ་ཊ», «ཀརྐ», «ཀརྞ» in one stacked-letter form)?

## Answered 2026-11-17

Answered (inv-e, 2026-11-17).

The Sanskrit word is tatkāla (Monier-Williams: «tat-kāla, happening at that same time, immediately; the time referred to», cited from Kātyāyana's Śrautasūtra, Varāhamihira and the Laghujātaka), Tibetanised without the long ā as ཏཏྐལ (ta tka la, Wylie «tat+kal»): the second letter is the stack ཏྐ (t over ka), as the print shows. BDRC's catalogue titles spell it the same way: «དུས་སྦྱོར་ཏཏྐལ་དང་དུམ་དྲུག་གིས་རྩིས་གཞི་ཤིན་ཏུ་གསལ་བ» (dus sbyor tat+kal dang dum drug gis rtsis gzhi shin tu gsal ba, in two collections of medical-astrology prints), «dus sbyor gyi skabs 'dir tat+kal 'char ba'i yun tshad» (Blo bzang bstan pa'i rgyal mtshan, MW30533), «shing khyi zla tshes kyi tat+kal dus sbyor la dpyad pa'i zin tho»; the search «ཏཏྐལ» has 18 hits, all of the time-junction (dus sbyor) literature.

The Crab's name is the Sanskrit karkaṭa (Monier-Williams: «karkaṭa, a crab; the sign Cancer»; karka also «a crab; the sign Cancer»), Tibetan ཀརྐ་ཊ (karka Ta, ka + r over ka, then ṭa). The ཀཎྜར, ཀརྚ་ཊ and ཀརྞ of the machine texts are one stacked letter (ར over ཀ) read four ways; ཀཎྜར is not a Sanskrit word for Cancer (kaṇḍara is not in Monier-Williams, 2026-11-17). Other texts quoting the same verse spell it «karka Ta» (Bstan 'dzin chos kyi rgyal mtshan's collected works: «nam mkha' dus me karka Ta», «sky, time, fire: the Crab»; Stag tshang lo tsā ba's «mkha' so karka Ta»; Padma dkar po's «mkha' mkha' me ni karka Ta»); the White Beryl's own two etexts give «kaN+Da Ta» and «kaN Ta», OCR of the same stack. Not looked at again on the scan: the answer rests on the Sanskrit and on the quoting texts. Claim wb-ch08-tatkala-and-karkata-spellings.
