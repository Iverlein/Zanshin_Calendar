# «ཡི་བམ» and «འབྱུང་ཆུང» in reply 152

In reply 152 (ys:i513-514, img. 514, l. 3), is the hand's «ཡི་བམ» «ལི་ཁམ», and what is the «འབྱུང་ཆུང», "the small element", that "does not go beyond rat, dragon and monkey" when a woman's trigram has fallen on li or kham?

**Narrowed 2026-11-01 (RD104, [claims/small-element-and-do-gcig-in-reply-152.md](../claims/small-element-and-do-gcig-in-reply-152.md)).** The hand's «ཡི་བམ» is «ལི་ཁམ»: the sentence repeats the question's «li or kham» and Yigdzin-1 reads «ལི་ཁམ» in both places. «འབྱུང་ཆུང» is WB's own term for the small element of the letters (wb.2:p39-41, the five classes' elements against the great elements of the vowels); in the reply it is the element carried by the woman's year animal, which "does not go beyond rat, dragon and monkey", one triad, so that her animal stands within the triad whatever trigram she has fallen to. A reading from the context, not a gloss.
