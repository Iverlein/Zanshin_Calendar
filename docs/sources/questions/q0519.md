# «ཀླུང་གི་དུར་ཕུགས» or «ཀླུང་གི་དུར་ལུགས» on p. 335

Which letter does the Zhol block carry (img. 435, line 5), and what is the luck's tomb (དུར) that the line asks one to know?

RD175 (2026-11-06), narrowed: the first letter of the syllable after དུར is a closed-loop ཕ on the Taikhang (img. 452, 3×); Yigdzin-1 reads «ཕུགས» on both prints; on the Zhol (img. 435) at 10× ཕ and ལ cannot be told apart. What the luck's tomb (དུར) means is untouched.

## Answered 2026-11-14

The letter is ཕ: «ཀླུང་གི་དུར་ཕུགས». The Zhol blocks read «དུར་ཕུགས» by eye at 3x with Yigdzin-1 and MITRA agreeing (RD129, year-of-life.md; «དུར་ལུགས» is the Zhol etext's OCR), the Taikhang's letter is a closed-loop ཕ (RD175), and BDRC's phrase search «ཀླུང་གི་དུར» finds the Beijing 1996 etext and the Sakya Centre print with «dur phugs» (inv-tt, q205). What the compound means is not glossed anywhere: «ཕུགས» is the far end, the lasting result (Tshig mdzod chen mo, Dag tshig gsar bsgrigs, Hopkins, Ives Waldo), so «ཀླུང་གི་དུར་ཕུགས» is the far end of the luck's tomb, as in claims/wb-luck-dur-phugs-variant.md; q205 carries the sense.
