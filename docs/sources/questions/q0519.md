# «ཀླུང་གི་དུར་ཕུགས» or «ཀླུང་གི་དུར་ལུགས» on p. 335

Which letter does the Zhol block carry (img. 435, line 5), and what is the luck's tomb (དུར) that the line asks one to know?

RD175 (2026-11-06), narrowed: the first letter of the syllable after དུར is a closed-loop ཕ on the Taikhang (img. 452, 3×); Yigdzin-1 reads «ཕུགས» on both prints; on the Zhol (img. 435) at 10× ཕ and ལ cannot be told apart. What the luck's tomb (དུར) means is untouched.
