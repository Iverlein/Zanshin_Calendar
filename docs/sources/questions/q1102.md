# In the Mahākāla commentary of Dpal rta dbyangs (Derge Tengyur vol. 28 p. 378) what does «ནམ་མཁའི་མཐར་ནི་རྨི་ལམ་བརྟགས» («at the end of the sky the dream is examined») name: the dawn, a place in the visualisation, or the end of the meditation on space?

RD386.

## Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-p)

Narrowed, not answered. The line's place in the rite (VE23703_1344, p. 378, read whole): after the disciple has been given the ten-syllable mantra to recite («ཡི་གེ་བཅུ་པ་བཟླས་པར་སྦྱིན།»), «དེ་ནས་དགའ་བའི་སེམས་ཀྱིས་ནི། །ནམ་མཁའི་མཐར་ནི་རྨི་ལམ་བརྟགས། །དེ་ལྟར་སྟ་གོན་བྱས་ནས་ནི།» («then with a glad mind, at the end of the sky the dream is examined; thus the preparation done …»), and only then the offering of the torma and the thread-line of human hair. In the parallel rites the same slot holds the bedtime mantra, the sleep, and the master's asking at dawn (q1103), so by position the line is the examining of the dream, after the night's sleep; why «ནམ་མཁའི་མཐར» («at the end of the sky») stands for it no text says.
