# WB p. 34, the first two syllables of the first verse gloss («གོ་ཌཱ་བ་ལྡོག་པའི་མཛེས་པ་བསྔགས་པ»): «གོ་ཌཱ» or «ཀོ་དྲ»? The print is heavy there.

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (scan-f).** The first gloss of ch. 5 is on the Taikhang reproduction (I5877) img. 70 (the opening of ch. 5: a frontispiece figure at the left, the title and two verse lines, then a line of small script at the foot of the verse block that starts at x about 1500, y 1040; crop x 1450–2050 at 7×). The small letters are joined and the first two syllables do not read clearly as either «གོ་ཌཱ» or «ཀོ་དྲ»; the third syllable «བ», then «ལྡོག་པའི་མཛེས་པ་བསྔགས་པ་ལ་སྦྱར་བ» agree with the 1996 print. Yigdzin-1 on the half block gave «རྫོགས་པ» there, a misreading. Not settled.
