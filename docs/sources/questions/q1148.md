# Is the animal chain of the Yu thog instruction the spirits' own forms in the dream or a training of any dream

Two other teachings (dds.52:p19-20, p245-246) give the chain as training against what is tamed in the world with antidote deities (garuḍa for nāgas, Hayagrīva for kings) as a second part.
