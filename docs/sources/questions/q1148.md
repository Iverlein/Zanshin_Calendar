# Is the animal chain of the Yu thog instruction the spirits' own forms in the dream or a training of any dream

Two other teachings (dds.52:p19-20, p245-246) give the chain as training against what is tamed in the world with antidote deities (garuḍa for nāgas, Hayagrīva for kings) as a second part.

## Narrowed 2026-11-13

Neither is shown. The Yu thog text (dds.47:p293-295) gives the animal chain (dog by wolf, wolf by tiger, tiger by lion) inside «the dream of spirits» (གདོན་གྱི་རྨི་ལམ) and says «whichever animal is seen», without saying that the spirit shows as the animal; the two other teachings (dds.52:p19-20, p245-246) put the same chain as training against what is to be tamed in the world, and the spirits as a second part with antidote deities (garuḍa for nāgas, Hayagrīva for kings). They agree on Hayagrīva for kings and differ on the nāgas (garuḍa against Nāgarakṣa). So only the Yu thog text calls the animal dream the spirits' own; no commentary on it was found. See claims/dds-yu-thog-gdon-classes-have-antidotes-and-the-animal-chain-is-in-the-dream-of-spirits.md (RD403). Narrowed, not settled: the text does not say what the animal is.
