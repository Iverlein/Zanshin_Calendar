# The 歳刑 in a whole quarter or in one branch

The 暦林問答集 places the 歳刑 in a whole quarter by the triad (§10); the modern table in one branch of it. Does an old text give the single branch for the 子, 寅, 卯, 巳, 午, 申, 酉, 亥 years (RM's earth line gives it for 丑, 辰, 未, 戌 only, and as an explanation), and does the printed 方位吉凶図 shade the quarter or the branch? (RD92)

## Answered 2026-11-05

The 歳刑's quarter and its single branch are one rule, the triad laid on the quarter in order (曾門經, 考原, in the 協紀辨方書). What today's paper shades is q530. ([kyureki.md](../kyureki.md), *The direction gods' last questions in the old texts (RD163)*)
