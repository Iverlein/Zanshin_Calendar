# 日遊 in the house: seventeen days and the 戊・己 days, or sixteen

the 暦林問答集 keeps 日遊 in the house seventeen days, 癸巳 to 己酉, and on every 戊 and 己 day besides; the 曆例 (in the 協紀辨方書) and the 頭書長暦 keep it sixteen days, 癸巳 to 戊申, with a place in the house for each day and nothing of 戊 and 己. Which does an onmyōdō practitioner follow, and does the printed almanac mark the 戊・己 days as 日遊 days? (RD94)
