# Khro ru glosses the creatures of ch. 81 with «རྟ་བླ» and «གྲོག་མ», and Sde srid's verse has «རྟ་བླ» where the Four Tantras have «འབུ་སྦྲང»: what is the rta bla?

A variant of the creatures' line or a correction?

## Answered 2026-11-09

རྟ་བླ (rta bla) is a many-legged worm, the centipede (Tshig mdzod chen mo, Bod yig tshig gter rgya mtsho, Ives Waldo, Dag tshig gsar bsgrigs; a medical dictionary's entry: reddish-black, ridged back, "hundred-footed"), named in the creatures' lists of Kongtrul's treasury beside the scorpion and spider. In Khro ru and Sde srid it is a creeping creature of the same list as the bee-grub, so the variant is another creature's name, not a correction of sense. See st-rta-bla-is-the-centipede.
