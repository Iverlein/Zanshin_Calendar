# KP year and month panels: which side of its cell does each name stand on?

The machine reading of KP §13–§15 (labels 89–102, pool img. 87–100) gives the names but drops the cell boundaries, and the cursive names do not resolve at 2.4×. Wanted: 4–5× strips of each year panel and month panel, read by eye, with the side of each name.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (scan-g)

The machine's failure is the machine's, not the scan's: BDRC label 89 (pool img. 87), at 3× (a strip of 1060 × 280 px of the original, enlarged to 3180 px wide), shows every cell's text legibly in the print's Uchen. The page is a grid of ruled cells with double vertical rules between the columns: a top row of cells with a name at the head of each (some cells carry a second line under it), middle rows that hold either more cells of two or three short lines or an animal's picture (here a boar-like animal at the left, a deer or ox in the middle, a tiger at the right), a bottom row of small-print cells, and one empty cell in the top row beside the deer. A name therefore stands at the head of its cell, and the side of a cell is its column's, marked by the double rules. Reading all the cells (about twelve a page, four to five lines each, 14 pages) needs about three strips of 3–4× a page and was not done in this time box, the first strip only; no cell was entered in the pool from it. Method for the next reader: a plain Pillow crop of the original, 3× (x 340–1400, y 50–330 shows the top row of the left half of label 89), a few columns of cells per view.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (rd-kp2)

The side of a cell is settled for the year chart (§13, img. 87–90): Pillow strips of 3.1× (the page cut in four, 4–8× for single cells) show each panel to be a ring of twelve cells round the animal's drawing (four across the top, two down each side column, four along the bottom), parted from the next panel by a double rule; a cell holds one to five names, one to a line, and a name's place is its cell's place in the ring, which is one of the twelve animals' places. The Rat panel is entered cell by cell ([kp:i87b](../corpus/kp/kp.md#kp-i87b), scan) and the Hare and Dragon panels in part ([kp:i88b](../corpus/kp/kp.md#kp-i88b)). Read against WB 2, ch. 31 the Rat panel puts thirteen lords or groups where WB's rules for the mouse year put them and four elsewhere (claim [kp-year-ring-rat-panel-against-wb](../claims/kp-year-ring-rat-panel-against-wb.md); q1210 the four, q1208 the two life-spirit knowers WB does not list). Still unread cell by cell: the other eleven year panels, and the §14 and §15 pages (img. 91–100): img. 91–96 are twelve framed panels, two to the page, each a small animal-headed figure with the nine-number names beside it, and img. 97–100 twelve lotus-shaped cartouches, three to the page, with one large word in each middle; q1209 asks for them. The machine's drop of the cell boundaries is the reason the earlier reading failed; Yigdzin-1 over the strips (3× bands of a page's quarter, img. 87–90) reads the names well enough to be a guide but runs the cells together, so the place of each name still needs the eye.
