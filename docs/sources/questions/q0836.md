# In the examination of dreams, Peking shows «ཐ་མར་རྨི་ལམ་བརྟགས་པ་ལ་ཉལ» with an extra པ; the Derge etext has «བརྟགས་ལ་ཉལ»: which is the text?

Yigdzin-1 reads «བཏབ་ལ».

## Answered 2026-11-16

Answered (inv-z2, 2026-11-16, BDRC phrase search; etext snippets): the text is «ཐ་མར་རྨི་ལམ་བརྟགས་ལ་ཉལ», without པ. The exact phrase has 3 hits: the Derge etext of the Svapnekṣā «rmi lam brtag pa» (two copies of the verse, Derge), the Dpe bsdur ma etext of the same work (an editor's quote mark stands before «la nyal»), and the same verse in the Sman dpyad gces pa grub pa of Slob dpon Klu sgrub and Candranandana (Derge): «dang po'i cha ni bag chags 'khrug / gnyis par yi dwags rgyu bar 'gyur / tha mar rmi lam brtags la nyal». The same line with པ, «ཐ་མར་རྨི་ལམ་བརྟགས་པ་ལ་ཉལ» and «རྨི་ལམ་བརྟགས་པ་ལ་ཉལ», gives 0 hits; with eight syllables it also breaks the seven-syllable line of its neighbours. Peking's པ is then its own addition, and Yigdzin-1's «བཏབ་ལ» is a machine reading of the same strokes or of the Dpe bsdur ma's recorded variant: the Dpe bsdur ma's note at «བརྟགས་ལ་ཉལ» gives Peking and Narthang «བཏབ» (claim svi-dpe-bird-note-and-brtags-note). No etext has «བཏབ»; that variant is attested only by the notes and the Narthang manuscript.

## Audit 2026-11-16 (inv-audit2): set back to narrowed

Re-ran BDRC phrase search: «ཐ་མར་རྨི་ལམ་བརྟགས་ལ་ཉལ» 3 hits (Sman dpyad gces pa grub pa MW1KG14614, Derge Svapnekṣā MW23703_2100, Dpe bsdur ma MW1PD95844_1009, where a quote mark stands before «la»); «བརྟགས་པ་ལ» and «བཏབ་ལ» 0 each. What was not supported: (1) the three hits are one tradition: the Derge text, the Dpe bsdur ma whose base it is, and a Derge-based collection quoting the verse; they do not witness the reading three times. (2) Zero hits for the form with པ say nothing about the Peking: the Peking etext is not searchable text (RD401), and the pool's eye reading of the Peking print (claim svi-peking-btab-or-brtags-reread, I1KG13215 img. 310 line 1) has «བརྟགས་པ་ལ»; «Peking's པ is its own addition» is an inference. (3) «Yigdzin-1's བཏབ is a machine reading of the same strokes» is not shown: the Narthang manuscript reads «བཏབ» by eye (claim svi-narthang-ms-btab-la-nyal, I2KG212330 img. 289), a different word with no པ, and the Dpe bsdur ma's own note gives «བཏབ» for Peking and Narthang. The metre (seven syllables without པ) argues against the Peking's form with པ, not against «བཏབ་ལ» (also seven). What stands: the Derge and Dpe bsdur ma text is «བརྟགས་ལ་ཉལ»; the form with པ is not in any searchable etext; «བཏབ» is a recorded variant of two prints. Which of «བརྟགས» and «བཏབ» is the text is open.
