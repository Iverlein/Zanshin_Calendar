# Which authority is behind Karma chags med's yar mar dgu dang bcu gcig for Palden Lhamo (a Kagyu protector text?)

From the 2026-11-21 practice-day readings.

## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-s)

The pool holds the verse and its note only as the retreat manual's own line (rczp:i415, q1263, claim palden-lhamo-roams-on-the-9th-11th-24th-26th), and the line names no source of its own. Pool search («Palden Lhamo», «Karma chags med») finds no text that gives the verse's authority. Still open; it needs a witness that cites the manual's source, or the manual's own colophon.

## Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-a)

BDRC phrase search on the line «དཔལ་ལྡན་ལྷ་མོ་དཔོན་གཡོག་རྒྱུ» finds four etexts, all prints of this same retreat manual (Ri chos mtshams kyi zhal gdams: Gser thang, Brag dkar, Palampur, Lhasa 2012); no other work has the verse. Read in the Lhasa etext (MW1AC387, p368-369): the run of lines is introduced as drawn «མདོ་སྡེ་འཇིག་རྟེན་བསྟན་བཞག་ལས» ("from the Sūtra's teaching on the world") for the eighth and twenty-third, then the 14th and 15th, the 8th (Medicine Buddha, White Tārā, Tshe ring ma), the 10th, the 15th, the three nines, the nineteenth, and Palden Lhamo's line, with no source named for this line; the only authorities the manual names after it are for the lama's memorial offering («mgon po'i rgyud sogs mang por bshad», Jo bo rje and Srong btsan). The manual's own author, Karma Chags med, is a Kamtsang Kagyu master (BDRC note), but that says who wrote the line down, not whose it was. Still open: a witness citing a protector tantra or rite for these days is needed.
