# The thirty 'bri wang and their results

The thirty *'bri wang* (or *brda than*, p. 538): what are the thirty, in order, from the starting emblems the note gives (father tortoise, treasury of wealth, blue water dragon, white monkey carrying fire, Chinese dog-headed horn-skull, reed-bamboo comb, peacock with crossed necks, nāga face), and which text gives their results ("as the text says")?

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-b).** A second witness names the thirty and sends the reader elsewhere: the *Do shal* (UT, MW25167, etext volume I4767, p. 286, printed p. 264), at the end of its list of remedies for the planets' harms, closes with «ཡང་ཅིག་འབྲི་ཝང་སུམ་ཅུའི་རྩིས། །བལྟ་བར་འདོད་ན་ཟུར་དུ་ཤེས།» (the OCR has «ཟུཪ་དུ»), "and another, the thirty 'bri wang's reckoning: if you wish to see it, know it apart", and passes at once to a section on particular harms (the 'than' of the trigrams). So the thirty are a separate reckoning outside the manual, as Mipham says of the ordinary copies («དཔེ་ཕལ་ན་བརྩི་ཚུལ་མི་འདུག»); neither list nor results are given there. BDRC's phrase search «འབྲི་ཝང» gives 7 hits (WB's two etexts, UT, a Drukpa monastery history where «'bri wang» is a layman's name, a Sera Yangtrul line «'bri wang mo'i mad 'pho 'byed», a Panchen biography line, an unreadable OCR), «འབྲི་དབང་སུམ་ཅུ» and «འབྲི་ཝང་སུམ་ཅུ» 0; «བརྡ་ཐན» 28 hits, besides WB the Nyingma «brda than sum bcu'i than bsgyur» (a bird-garuda rite in the Bka' brgyad bde gshegs 'dus pa outline, MW3PD988) and other treasure texts' «brda than ngan pa zlog», a different thing (the thunderbolt-signs of harm, not a count of years). The list and its results stay unfound; open.
