# The five of the thirteen gates' fifth and sixth, and the own-element signs

In the thirteen gates (pp. 170–171), what is the "five" of the fifth gate (the son monkey, "the male five down, the female five up") and of the sixth (the friend hare «ལྔ»): five years counted, or the hare and monkey of a given element? And how are the "own element" signs of gates 9–13 fixed: by the element of the asker's year?

**Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-h), no new sense.** BDRC phrase search for the gates' names finds only WB's own copies and unglossed quotations: «གཡུང་དྲུང་ལྔ་སྐོར་རྩིས» 3 hits (WB's three etexts), «ཉི་མ་དམག་བསྡུས» 3 (WB's copies, the Do shal), «འཕུང་རྟ་རྔ་ཞོན» 3 (WB's copies and the Bai DUrya g.ya' sel, I5875, whose OCR is too broken to read: it lists a doubt about the thirteen gates ending at the «འཕུང་རྟ་རྔ་ཞོན» and the "gates of sky and earth" that follow, «རྩིས་སྒོ་བཅུ་གསུམ་འཕུང་རྟ་རྔ་ཞོན་ཡན་གྱི་རྫོགས་པའི་དེ་འཕྲོས་སུ་གནམ་སའི་སྒོ་བྱུང་»), «རྒྱ་མའི་ཐག་པ་ཆད» 2 (a Do shal and the Tshangs pa lha rgyud, the latter search-only: it is the Tshangs pa's rope in a rite, not the gate), «ཚངས་པ་སྤྱི་རྒྱུད» 20 hits (WB, the Do shal, Srid pa 'phrul gyi me long, the 'Jam mgon dgongs pa'i me long; none quotes the thirteen gates). The five of the fifth gate and the hare's «ལྔ» of the sixth are therefore not told by any text found; the reading «male five down, female five up» of p. 171's own wording («ཕོ་ནི་ཐུར་ལ་མོ་ཡར་ལྔ»: the male down, the female five up) stays the page's.
