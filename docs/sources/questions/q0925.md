# Does Bya mdo Klu byams rgyal join the Klong rdol line and the Four Tantras' first line into one rule (dusk and midnight without fruit, dawn with), or only set them side by side as two authorities?

RD313; his etext is closed, the snippet shows «ji skad du» for the first and «rgyud rang las» for the second.

## Answered 2026-11-13

Only side by side, as two authorities. In the sentence on the dawn dream, Bya mdo says that a dream seen at dawn is shown to bring its fruit surely, «ji skad du», "as is said", and gives Klong rdol bla ma's line «tho rengs gsum cha phyis 'byung gsal byed yin» in quotation marks closed by a footnote mark, then «zhes dang», "and", then «rgyud rang las», "from the Tantra itself", the Four Tantras' line «mtshan stod rmi lam brjed nges 'bras bu med» in quotation marks of its own; the sentence ends there and the sixth chapter begins. The first line is not called the Tantra's, and nothing joins the two into one rule of dusk and midnight without fruit and dawn with it. The footnote's content is not in the snippet.
