# Do the two editions of Byang pa's commentary (MW27496, the Si khron print of 2001, and MW15590, the Mi rigs dpe skrun khang first printing) differ on the page in the Moonbeams' line (མི་བཟང་ against མི་བཟད), and which is the author's own?

RD313.

## Narrowed 2026-11-13

The two editions do differ on the page, in all three places: the first printing (MW15590, etext VEIE0OPIB55EB1A5_I1PD380, p. 184, printed p. 164) reads «མི་བཟད» in the lemma of the Four Tantras' line, in Byang pa's own sentence and in the Moonbeams' quotation, where the 2001 print (MW27496) reads «མི་བཟང» in the lemma and the quotation: st:p30-31b. Which is the author's own is open: the first printing agrees with the Moonbeams' word and not with the Tantra's line as the pool reads it, which can mean a hand that harmonised it; the scan of MW15590 is not served (no image of I1PD380 img. 183-186), and the etext is OCR with editors' brackets.
