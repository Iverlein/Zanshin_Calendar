# The constant «མེ་ཚེས་ལག་པ»: 2,152 or 2,153

The constant «མེ་ཚེས་ལག་པ» (fire-tithi-hand) appears at pp. 194-196 and is named in the colophon (p. 197) as one of the figures taken from the whole in the earlier reckoners' mistakes; is the right value 2,152 or 2,153, and what is the correct form of the Jupiter house-circle the colophon points to? (RD185, corpus/wb/ch17.md)

## Answered 2026-11-08

RD185 second look (2026-11-08): on the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5028 img. 195) the constant «མེ་ཚེས་ལག་པ» is printed ༢༡༥༣ (2,153) at all three places on p. 196; the fourth glyph has the split top of the ༣ seen in «(༡༡༣༡༢)» and «(༡༡༡༣༥)», and the value agrees with its word-numerals (fire 3, tithi 15, hand 2, units first). The two machine readings' 2,152 is a misreading. The form of the Jupiter house-circle the colophon points to is not settled here; the pages 193–195 places were not rechecked.
