# The author's name at the close of ch. 12: རྙིང་ནོར or སྙིང་ནོར

the author's name at the close of ch. 12 (p. 103) is read «དཔྱོད་ལྡན་རྙིང་ནོར» by the machine, and «དཔྱོད་ལྡན་སྙིང་ནོར» at the close of ch. 11 (p. 90) and of ch. 10. Which is the printed spelling on the scan? (RD191, corpus/wb/ch12.md)

## Answered 2026-11-14

INV-1114 (2026-11-14): «སྙིང་ནོར». On the Zhol print (BDRC I1KG1710, img. 141, the last line, read by eye at 3×) the author's name at the close of ch. 12 is «དཔྱོད་ལྡན་སྙིང་ནོར», the stack after «ལྡན་» with its i-vowel and the ng suffix as at the other chapter closings. BDRC's exact phrase search finds «དཔྱོད་ལྡན་སྙིང་ནོར» in 89 etexts and «དཔྱོད་ལྡན་རྙིང་ནོར» in none, and the Zhol etext spells «སྙིང་ནོར» at every colophon it holds (pp. 23, 46, 56, 62, 70, 77, 123, 141, 206) and on the title page («…དཔྱོད་ལྡན་སྙིང་ནོར་ཞེས་བྱ་བཞུགས་སོ»). The «རྙིང་» of Yigdzin-1 on the 1996 and Gser thang pages is a misreading of the same stack; the name is «སྙིང་ནོར», 'heart-jewel' (the pen name read in the colophons of ch. 10 and 11), «རྙིང་ནོར» being 'old wealth'. The 1996 print's own page (img. 113, 670 px) and the Gser thang reprint were too small to settle the stack by eye.
