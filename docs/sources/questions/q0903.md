# Does the Moonbeams' gloss of the Eight Limbs' «རྨི་ལམ་སྐྱེས་པ་མཐོང» (a man and an elephant) belong to ch. 2's line, or only to the Eight Limbs' text that the Blue Beryl quotes beside it?

RD301.

## Answered 2026-11-10

Answered by RD313 (2026-11-10): the Moonbeams' «man and an elephant» glosses the Eight Limbs' verse only; it occurs in three copies of the Moonbeams and in no commentary on the Four Tantras (seven etexts searched whole, and BDRC's exact-phrase search). Claim st-ch2-moonbeams-elephant-gloss-is-the-eight-limbs-only.
