# What is «ཕྱ་ཐེར» in KP's «སྦྲ་དང་ཕྱ་ཐེར་འཚེམ་པ»

What is «ཕྱ་ཐེར» in KP's «སྦྲ་དང་ཕྱ་ཐེར་འཚེམ་པ», if not a form of the awning «ཕྱར»?

RD171 (2026-11-06), narrowed: «ཕྱ་ཐེར» is a pitched cloth shelter or tent-roof, always named beside the tent (སྦྲ): Longchenpa's life ('some seventy sbra and phya ther made'), the Tai Situ Byang chub rgyal mtshan testament, a levy list, the Fifth Dalai Lama's Vinaya commentary. Not a form of ཕྱར. Origin not found; KP's own etext not among the readable hits.
