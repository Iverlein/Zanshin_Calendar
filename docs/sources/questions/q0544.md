# What is «ཕྱ་ཐེར» in KP's «སྦྲ་དང་ཕྱ་ཐེར་འཚེམ་པ»

What is «ཕྱ་ཐེར» in KP's «སྦྲ་དང་ཕྱ་ཐེར་འཚེམ་པ», if not a form of the awning «ཕྱར»?

RD171 (2026-11-06), narrowed: «ཕྱ་ཐེར» is a pitched cloth shelter or tent-roof, always named beside the tent (སྦྲ): Longchenpa's life ('some seventy sbra and phya ther made'), the Tai Situ Byang chub rgyal mtshan testament, a levy list, the Fifth Dalai Lama's Vinaya commentary. Not a form of ཕྱར. Origin not found; KP's own etext not among the readable hits.

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-bbb).** BDRC phrase search «ཕྱ་ཐེར» 19 hits, «སྦྲ་དང་ཕྱ་ཐེར» 3 (the Longchenpa biography; Rtsis rig shes bya kun gsal; Bod kyi skar rtsis rig pa'i rnam bzhag), «ཕྱ་ཐེར་སྦྲ» 3 (WB's etexts and the Do shal), «སྦྲ་ཕྱར» 21. The Vinaya manual 'Dul ba las chog rnam rgyal gser mdog (and its 'khrul spong) glosses the fly-cover: «sbrang skyabs bcang ba ni sbrang sogs te / phya ther chung ngu'i dbyibs can no», "shaped like a small phya ther" (snippet): the one gloss, saying a phya ther is a cloth cover of a tent's shape. Still no headword; the pair varies with «sbra phyar» in manuals of one family, so the two are probably one thing under two spellings, not proved.
