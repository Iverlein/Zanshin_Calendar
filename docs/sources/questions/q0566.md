# p. 349's «དྭ་ཟླ་རུས་ཁམས་ཟླ་བ་ན»: the clan element's months and the executioner year

P. 349's «དྭ་ཟླ་རུས་ཁམས་ཟླ་བ་ན། རུས་ཆེན་ཁམས་ཀྱི་གཤེད་ལོར་སོང་»: which months are the clan element's, and is the executioner year here the four-executioner or the element's grave? See year-of-life.md, RD122.

## Answered 2026-11-06

RD174 (2026-11-06), new find on the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5029 img. 495, lines 1–8, 1.1–4×): Mipham glosses p. 349's «དྭ་ཟླ་རུས་ཁམས་ཟླ་བ་ན» on vol. 2 p. 489 (RD122's 'none' was an etext gap). His example: earth clan, tiger month falls; 'executioner year of the great clan element' is the executioner year of the clan's element. Tiger is wood, which overcomes earth, so the clan element's month is a month of the element that overcomes the clan's; WB vol. 1 p. 300 lists it the same way (wood clan: iron monkey and bird; water clan: earth's 'four executioner years'). Doubts: the letter ས in «རུས་ས་པ» (read at 4×) and one lost word in the sentence about «སྤྱིར་མེར».
