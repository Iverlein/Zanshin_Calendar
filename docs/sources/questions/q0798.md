# How are the four watches (ཐུན) of the Svapnohana bounded: four equal parts of the night, or the monastic watches?

The text gives the spans but not the bounds of the watches.

Narrowed (inv-g, 2026-11-14): the Svapnohana states no bound, and none of the prints, colophons, commentaries or modern books read gives one (claims svo-watch-bounds-not-in-colophons, svo-four-watches-three-reckonings-beside-it). The only reckonings of four watches to a night are equal quarters: the Mañjuśrī root tantra's ch. 18 («four watches are the daytime, and the nights likewise; being eight, day and night is reckoned», mmk.1:p438-439), the Hevajra books of Ratnākaraśānti and the fourth Zhwa dmar (four by day, four by night, a water-hour a quarter of a watch, svo:p271o) and a meditation hand-book (a quarter of the night by season); the monastic watches are three (thun gsum, svo:p271p) and the Mahāvyutpatti's list of the night has five, so neither is the Svapnohana's four. Equal fourths of the night is therefore the only reading the tradition's own wording supports, but no text says the Svapnohana means it. BDRC searches of 2026-11-14 («མཚན་མོ་ཐུན་བཞིར་བགོས», «ཐུན་བཞི་བགོས་པའི» one hit in I4PD4091, not a dream text) found no statement.
