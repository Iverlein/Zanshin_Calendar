# What is «རམ་རུག» (ram rug) in the rites' lists: the plant ram bu or ram pa by a spelling by ear, or a separate item, and what does «ཞབས» in «ཞབས་རམ་རུག» of the Rin chen gter mdzod's second text add?

RD307.

## Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-j)

BDRC phrase search (2026-11-16): «རམ་རུག་དང་» 2 hits, both the same sentence, in the Rin chen gter mdzod (MW1KG14, VE1KG14_037) «snam sgye khra bo'i nang du zhabs ram rug dang sbang ma la sogs mi kha'i rdzas kyis bkang» and in the Bla ma dgongs 'dus Mkha' 'gro lnga'i mo skor (MW00EGS1017462_F68564) with «nang du ram rug dang sbang ma la sogs mi kha'i rdzas kyis bkang», without «zhabs»: so the word «zhabs» is not part of the name in the parallel copy and may be a stray or a separate syllable (zhabs, 'foot') of the Rin chen gter mdzod's wording; the Rin chen gter mdzod's other line (VE1KG14_037) has «ram rug sbang g.yer» without it. Ram rug is a good of gossip (mi kha'i rdzas) in every place; also «'bru nag tshwa sgog ram rug sogs» (Karma chags med, MW2PD20252). Which plant stays open; see q804.
