# «ཀླུམ་མནན» or «གྲུམ་མནན» on p. 169

On p. 169, «ཀླུམ་མནན» (Yigdzin-1) or «གྲུམ་མནན» (the etext): what is pressed down in "breath-taking, womb, body and bath, the four"?

RD165 (2026-11-06): the 1996 scan is too blurred for «ཀླུམ»/«གྲུམ»; stays a doubt.

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-bbb).** BDRC's phrase search «གྲུམ་མནན» finds the 1996 etext and the Gser thang etext (both read «grum mnan»), «ཀླུམ་མནན» only the Zhol etext (I1KG1710): the split is between prints, not a misreading of one. Neither word is a headword (Steinert's 64 dictionaries have «grum» as lame, broken, crippled and «mnan» as pressed down, burial sorcery). The line sits in the beer reckoning (the strained beer finished or not; rise and decline of the body-power; «gud med», breath, womb, body and bath the four), which fits «mnan» as a pressing-down charm named with the four, but what «grum» or «klum» adds is not found. Open.
