# WB p. 249 print line 1: «བྱ་ཟེ་གློ་སྙིང» and «ཁྲག་ཟོ་ནམ་མཁའ་ནས» (Yigdzin-1) or «བྱ་རྩེ» and «ཁྲག་ཐོ» (MITRA)? The top strokes of ཟ, ཙ and ཐ cannot be told apart.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-ppp)

«བྱ་ཟེ» (bya ze) is a headword (Ives Waldo: "crest, tuft"; Valby: "21st constellation or lunar mansion"), «བྱ་རྩེ» (bya rtse) is not; in the line «བྱ་ཟེ་གློ་སྙིང་གྱེན་བཀེན་འདྲ» "like a crest (or a bird's crop) leaning up on lung and heart" the first reading has the headword. «ཁྲག་ཟོ» (khrag zo) and «ཁྲག་ཐོ» (khrag tho) have no headword in the 64 sets; «ཁྲག་འཐུང» is the word that would be expected, so the pair stays for the scan.
