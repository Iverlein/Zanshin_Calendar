# The hal khyi's five mothers and five sons as the five elements' pairs

The hal khyi's five mothers and five sons (p. 185), if the five elements' mother-son pairs: in which order do the pairs follow, and where does each sit, with nine numbers carved for ten beings? (narrows 36, 433, 470)

RD182 (2026-11-06), narrowed: the five pairs each hold 22 days of 110 (5×22), turning to the right; the commentary says «བརྒྱ་དང་རྩ་བཞི» (124) in the Rin chen kun 'dus, Lung kun 'dus and Lha rgyud gsal sgron; the order of the pairs is not on the page.
