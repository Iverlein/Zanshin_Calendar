# Ka-Nying Shedrub Ling's dates for 2027 (monlam.org) fall one month ahead of the Phugpa reckoning as computed (a doubled second month in 2027) from the third month on: does the Men-Tsee-Khang almanac of 2027 double the second month too, or does the monastery follow another reckoning (Tsurphu)?

Ka-Nying Shedrub Ling's dates for 2027 (monlam.org) fall one month ahead of the Phugpa reckoning as computed (a doubled second month in 2027) from the third month on: does the Men-Tsee-Khang almanac of 2027 double the second month too, or does the monastery follow another reckoning (Tsurphu)?

## Answered 2026-11-24

The monastery follows the Tsurphu reckoning. Edward Henning's computed calendars for 2027 (kalacakra.org, tdata/pl_2027.txt and tdata/ts_2027.txt, fetched 2026-11-24) give the two reckonings side by side:

- Phugpa: the second month doubled, the intercalary second from 9 March and the delayed second from 7 April 2027; the fourth month from 5 June, Saga Dawa Düchen on 18 June and Lhabab Düchen on 20 November; the ninth month from 30 October. The app's engine gives the same months, and its day-by-day test against Henning's Phugpa table already covers 2027 (henning-phugpa.tsv, 2027-02-07 onwards) and passes; 2028 also matches day by day (738 rows of 2027-2028, run on 2026-11-24).
- Tsurphu: no leap month in spring; the ninth month doubled instead (intercalary from 1 October, delayed from 30 October 2027). Its Saga Dawa Düchen falls on 20 May and its Lhabab Düchen on 22 October 2027, which are Ka-Nying Shedrub Ling's dates exactly (ksl:p1, ksl:p2).

So the month's difference from the third month to the ninth is the two reckonings' different leap month, not an error of the engine. The Men-Tsee-Khang almanac of 2027 itself was not read; Henning checked his Phugpa computations against printed almanacs. The festivals of a Karma Kagyu or Kagyu-Nyingma house that keeps the Tsurphu calendar fall a month earlier than the app shows them in 2027, from the third month to the ninth.
