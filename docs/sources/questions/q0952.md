# Does the Dpe bsdur ma name the Co ne in every place where the Co ne differs from the Derge base (its siglum is in about 120 entries of vol. 14), so that its silence at the watch lines, «བགྲོད་མིན», «འགྲོན་བུ», «རྟ་བླ་མོ» and the Gāyatrī means the Co ne agrees with the base?

RD323; the explanation of the sigla is in the Tengyur series' first volume.

## Narrowed 2026-11-13

The etext of the Tengyur series' first volume (VEIE0OPIA3E40644_I1PD95846, pp. 3-1528) has no editors' preface and no explanation of the sigla: only a title page, the contents and the first work's notes; its practice is in svo:i38-50 (a collation table is left empty where a print lacks the work; the marks are the prints' names in guillemets). That the Co ne is named wherever it differs from the base is therefore not stated in any text read; the machine readings of the Co ne print (svo:p271m) give two places where the Co ne differs from the Derge base and the notes do not name it, but they are machine votes. A page image of the series' introduction (Chinese or Tibetan) is what would settle it.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-bb2)

The vol. 14 etext (VEIE0OPIA3E40644_I1PD95859) was fetched and searched for any statement of the notes' practice. It has none: no editors' note on the sigla; the term «ཅོ་ནེ།» occurs 91 times, all as the column head of the collation tables (བསྡུར་འབྲས་རེའུ་མིག); the contents list «བསྡུར་མཆན» and «བསྡུར་འབྲས་རེའུ་མིག» for each work and nothing more. Counts in the OCR: «ཅོ་» in guillemets 84 (156 with the damaged closing mark), a «པེ་»«སྣར་»«ཅོ་» triple 6 times. The Co ne is thus named in about 1% of the entries that name the Peking (about 10,500); that is consistent with a Co ne close to the base and with the Co ne being named only where it departs, but the etext does not say which. At the Svapnohana the Co ne is named twice (printed pp. 1302 and 1303, «རྒྱུ» and «ཕྱུག»), and the machine reading of the Co ne scan has the second watch «བརྒྱད་པར» (svo:p271m), a place where no Co ne entry stands; whether that machine vote is right is not settled. The preface that explains the sigla is on a page of the series' first volume the etext does not carry. Stays open.
