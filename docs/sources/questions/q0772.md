# p. 411: «འཆོལ» or «འཚོལ», «ཐུར་མ» or «ཟུར་མ»

Is the print's «འཆོལ» (p. 411, the faults that «disorder») rather «འཚོལ» (seek), and «ཐུར་མ» rather «ཟུར་མ» (p. 411, the arrows' list)? RD158 (2026-11-08), Yigdzin-1 against the machine passage; not yet looked at on the scan.

## Answered 2026-11-08

RD158 (2026-11-08), Zhol I1KG1710 (pp. 407–411 are on img. 1172–1179, not page + 8): the right-ear line reads «སྐྱོན་མི་འཚོལ་བར» (does not look for one's faults), not «འཆོལ»; the ཚ/ཆ stack is not beyond doubt, but the Zhol machine reading and the sense agree (img. 1172 l. 6 area). «ཐུར་མ» stands against «ཟུར་མ» (img. 1178 l. 1, 6×; ཐ is a closed round letter), moderate confidence.
