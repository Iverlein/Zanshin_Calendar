# The order of the twelve strip charts, Zhol against the 1996 print

In the Zhol print's strip charts (img. 151-153) the rat chart comes first, in the 1996 print's pp. 142-147 it comes last; is the order of the twelve charts the same set in both, and where does the cycle begin for these charts? (RD199, corpus/wb/ch13.md)

## Narrowed 2026-11-24 (inv-l)

The 1996 print's order, tiger first and ox last, is the order of the months of the year as the verse lists them (spring 1 to winter 3): each chart's nine-corpses cell has its month's date and time of day, 12 of 12 (wb1-ch13-strip-charts-ro-dgu-by-month), and the nāga-planet captions agree month by month too; so the cycle begins, for these charts, at the first spring month (tiger), as in the verses of pp. 216-222. The Zhol print's rat-first order is the animal cycle's; each woodcut is an animal, and animal and month go together (tiger first spring), so the two prints hold the same twelve charts in two orders, if the Zhol woodcuts are the months' animals. That the Zhol cells and captions say the same was not read (the cursive hand, q1163; the caption not made out at 0.45x). Open for the Zhol cells.
