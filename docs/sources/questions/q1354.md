# What is the Tibetan name of the deer beside the Dharma wheel on the Jokhang's roof?

Ri dwags, g.ya' or another word in the Tibetan description of the roof ornament.

**Narrowed 2026-11-24 (inv-h).** The word for the deer beside the Dharma wheel on roof ornaments in Tibetan texts is ri dwags (རི་དྭགས), found in 24 BDRC etext hits that describe a mandala palace's gate-roofs (wheel, parasol, male and female deer); no Tibetan text read describes the Jokhang's roof itself.
