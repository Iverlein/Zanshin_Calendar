# The departure's singled-out lords: of the date, the year or the hour, barred or remedied

In the departure (WB vol. 1, p. 375) the the se, the khyim, bya rab, si byi, hal khyi, bstan ma and the two treasuries are "singled out in particular" after the general earth lords: do the commentaries (Mipham's notes, p. 531, say only "examine as the text has them") say which of them are the earth lords of the date, of the year or of the hour, and whether a day on which one of them stands is barred or only to be remedied?
