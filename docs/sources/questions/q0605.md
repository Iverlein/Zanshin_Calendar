# The departure's singled-out lords: of the date, the year or the hour, barred or remedied

In the departure (WB vol. 1, p. 375) the the se, the khyim, bya rab, si byi, hal khyi, bstan ma and the two treasuries are "singled out in particular" after the general earth lords: do the commentaries (Mipham's notes, p. 531, say only "examine as the text has them") say which of them are the earth lords of the date, of the year or of the hour, and whether a day on which one of them stands is barred or only to be remedied?

## Answered 2026-11-06

RD177 (2026-11-06), as far as the sources go (etext): Thé-se, Thé-khyim, Se-byi and the watchman (bya ra) are the year's lords (WB p. 96, nos 1, 2, 7, 10); Tenma is the month's (no. 10); the Hal-dog is in the year's and the month's lists and the date's own run (pp. 362–363); the two treasuries are the day's and the hour's gods (pp. 96, 370). The bar and remedy are given by the verse itself (seat and course shunned, screening remedy when met); Mipham p. 531, UT (which calls the Hal-dog the earth lord's sky dog) and KD grade none and never speak of a day on which one stands.
