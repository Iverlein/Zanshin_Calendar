# «ཀླུ་བརྒྱད» or «གླུ་བརྒྱད» in the digging rite of p. 387

In the digging rite of p. 387 the body is complete with «ཀླུ་བརྒྱད» (the eight nāgas): is this the reading of the Zhol print, where the first stroke of the word may be ཀ or ག? (RD158, corpus/wb/ch34.md, fifth pass)

## Answered 2026-11-14

INV-1114 (2026-11-14): «ཀླུ་བརྒྱད», the eight nāgas. BDRC's exact search finds «ལུས་ལ་ཀླུ་བརྒྱད་ཚང» in two etexts of WB, the 1996 print (vol. 2, etext I1KG12907) and the Zhol print (I1KG1710, etext p. 1147, line 5, read on the scan at 4×: «དེ་ཡི་ལུས་ལ་ཀླུ་བརྒྱད་ཚང་», the first stroke of the word with the head letter of ka), and «ལུས་ལ་གླུ་བརྒྱད་ཚང» in none (0 hits). The Bhutan digest's «ཀླུ་བརྒྱུད» agrees (RD64), and the sense settles it: «གླུ་བརྒྱད» would be 'eight songs', while the figure's body carries the eight parts named in the verse. The Zhol scan is cursive and the ka/ga stroke is small, so the scan alone is not conclusive; the two typed etexts and the sense are.
