# A half in the Moonbeams' count of white and black

In the Moonbeams' year of age, when the stage and place pebbles of one aspect include a half, is the count «དཀར་ནག་མང་ཉུང་བགྲང» made on whole white and black pebbles only, with the half not counted, or does a half count as one of each? See verdicts.md, RD127.

**Searched again 2026-11-21 (inv-m).** BDRC «དཀར་ནག་མང་ཉུང་བགྲང» 5 hits: the Moonbeams' verse in other copies (MW1PD152297, search only) and two Bon-and-Gyalrong manuals of the same pebble scheme (MW1KG13780 *Nag rtsis snang srid me long*, good OCR, vol. I1KG15279 etext pp. 47-48; MW1KG16672, the Gyalrong collection) with the rule spelt out: of the five life-pebbles' white and black, «དཀར་མང་དཀར་གཅིག་ནག་མང་ན། ནག་གཅིག་སྙོམས་ན་དཀར་ནག་ཟུང་» (the notes: «དཀར་པོ་མང་ན་དཀར་རེ་དང་། ནག་པོ་མང་ན་ནག་རེ་བོར»): more white, one white; more black, one black; level, a white and a black pair. These count whole pebbles only and have no half in the twenty (a witness for the count, Bon-side and no source for the Moonbeams); none says how a half is counted. Open.

**Narrowed 2026-11-24 (sweep from MW1KG13780 (witness, Bon side)).** Narrowed: a Bon-side manual spells the rule for whole pebbles only (more white, one white; more black, one black; level, a pair); none has a half in the twenty, so how a half counts is not told.
