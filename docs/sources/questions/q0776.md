# The figures beside the houses on p. 13

## Answered 2026-11-21

The figures beside the houses on p. 13 are single bracketed numerals, one to a planet or node, the weekday numbers of WB's count; Rāhu's «88» and «99» are the machine's doubled reads of one ༨ and one ༩.

Checked 2026-11-21 (rd-y) with q1330 (rd-t): the planets stand in the weekday order sun ༡ (end of p. 12), moon ༢, Mars ༣, Mercury ༤, Jupiter ༥, Venus ༦, Saturn ༠ (the nought is Saturday's number in WB's count, Saturday 0, as the model almanac's month lines print it), Rāhu (སྒྲ་གཅན) ༨ and the tail (མཇུག་མ) ༩. The page's eight numerals were read by eye on 2026-11-08 (rd203b: ༨ and ༩ single, the machine's ༨༨ and ༩༩ doubled) and again on 2026-11-18 (y1, 24 ink lines at 2x, all agree with the print), and the weekday sequence ༡ to ༦, ༠ then ༨ and ༩ explains each one, so no digit rests on the blot alone. The "small figures after the first points" are the planets' numbers, not figures of the houses.
