# In the Svapnekṣā did the editors replace «ཤར་ཏེ་རུ» or «བྱ་ཤར་ཏེ་རུ» by «ཤང་ཏེའུ» for the Narthang (and by «ཤིང་ཏེའུ» for the Peking)? (the lemma of entry 4 of printed p. 355)

RD323.

## Answered 2026-11-14

«ཤར་ཏེ་རུ», without «བྱ». The note gives for the Peking «ཤིང་ཏེའུ» and for the Narthang «ཤང་ཏེའུ», but the Peking print read on the scan has «བྱ» before «ཤིང་ཏེའུ» (svi:p310b) and the Narthang line ends «བྱ» before its doubtful syllables (svi:p289-291b), so «བྱ» agrees in the three prints and is not what the entry replaces; an entry that began at «བྱ» would have carried it. The editors thus replace the base's «ཤར་ཏེ་རུ» (the base's line breaks after «བྱ་ཤར་», which is why the lemma can start on the new line) by «ཤིང་ཏེའུ» and «ཤང་ཏེའུ». This is an inference from the Peking scan and the note's wording: the lemma itself is not quoted in the etext.
