# Where the year's three sme ba (columns 9–11) are used

Where else in WB, or in the Bhutan collection, are the three sme ba of a year (columns 9–11) used, and does the year's first sme ba of this table agree with the earth-lord chapters? (RD192, corpus/wb/ch13.md)

Narrowed (inv-j, 2026-11-13): the first part stays open (no second use of the three columns was found). On the second part: the first column agrees with the earth-lord and sme ba chapters. WB p. 255 (img. 265) gives the present rab byung's fire hare (1687) the natal sme ba 7 (red), and the table's year 1 (the fire hare) has 7 in the first column; the engine's `YearOfLife.yearSmeBa(year) = 7 - (year - 1687) mod 9` is the table's first column, ((8 - n) mod 9), for the years 1687-1746 and continues it year by year. The next two cycles of the engine's count are the table's other columns: 1747 (year 1 of the next rab byung) gives 1 (second column, year 1) and 1807 gives 4 (third column, year 1), 1867 gives 7 again. So the three columns are the three successive sixty-year cycles of the 180-year sequence, an inference by our arithmetic; WB's text of the table does not say so (its headings are first, second, third).

## Answered 2026-11-16

The three columns are the three *sme phreng* (the rings, the "chains" of sme ba) of the 180-year sequence, in this order: the first ring begins with 1 (white) and ends with 5 (yellow), the second begins with 4 (green) and ends with 8 (white), the third begins with 7 (red). Sources read on 2026-11-16 in BDRC's search snippets (etexts search-only or unaligned, so snippets only): *Bod kyi 'byung rtsis rnam gzhag*: "there are three rings of the sme ba: the first ring begins from 1 white and ends at 5 yellow, the second from 4 green and ends at 8 white, the third begins from 7 red" («སྨེ་ཕྲེང་དང་པོ་གཅིག་དཀར་ནས་དབུ་ཚུགས་ཏེ་སྨེ་བ་ལྔ་སེར་ལ་རྫོགས་པ། སྨེ་ཕྲེང་གཉིས་པ་བཞི་ལྗང་ནས་དབུ་ཚུགས་ཏེ་སྨེ་བ་བརྒྱད་དཀར་ལ་རྫོགས་པ། སྨེ་ཕྲེང་གསུམ་པ་བདུན་དམར་ནས་དབུ་ཚུགས»); the same words in *Bod kyi thog gi lo rgyus skor* and *Krung go'i bod rig pa'i dpyad rtsom*; *Sme ba'i skor* and the Bon *Gshen rab kyi 'das lo*: "each 60-year turn is to be turned three times; they are called the first, second and third sme phreng". The arithmetic agrees: a ring of 60 years falls by one a year (mod 9), so a ring beginning at 1 ends at 5 (1 − 59 ≡ 5), one beginning at 4 ends at 8, one at 7 at 2; the engine's count gives the fire hare of 1687 the 7 of the table's first column, 1747 the 1 of the second, 1807 the 4 of the third, so the first column is the ring that opens at 1 on the wood rat year before (1684), the second the ring of 1744 (4), the third that of 1804 (7). The almanac prints that copy WB's table carry the same column heads: the Bai DUrya dkar po / *Skar rtsis kyi lag len byed grub sgang dril ltar bkod pa* etext (MW3CN3480) and the *Sgo bzhi mdzod lnga'i yang bcud* (MW3PD398) read "lo rgan, sme phreng dang po, sme phreng gnyis pa, sme phreng gsum pa, se zhig sogs kyi ming, klung rta'i khams …" in one row of the table, so the three sme ba columns (9–11) are headed "first, second, third ring" there too (the Wylie of these tables is OCR, the order of the cells is theirs). Where else the rings are used: the *Chos rnam kun btus*' seventh section (etext MW1KG2733_E3027F, search-only) has "a table by which to see easily in which general year the three sme rings fall" («སྨེ་ཕྲེང་གསུམ་པོ་སྤྱི་ལོ་གང་དུ་འཁེལ་བ་བལྟ་བདེའི་རེའུ་མིག»); its cells give the rings with years such as shing byi and chu phag (the snippet's OCR is noisy). Inside WB no second use of the three columns was found (the earth-lord and ch. 16 sme ba rules give a day's or a year's single sme ba).

## Audit 2026-11-16 (inv-audit): set back to narrowed

Re-read WB's table (wb.1:p105, corpus/wb/ch13.md: the columns are headed «སྨེ་ཕྲེང་དང་པོ། གཉིས་པ། གསུམ་པ», year 1 reads 7, 1, 4) and the snippet of the *Bod kyi 'byung rtsis rnam gzhag* (MW28959, search «སྨེ་ཕྲེང་དང་པོ་གཅིག་དཀར་ནས་དབུ་ཚུགས», 1 hit): «སྨེ་ཕྲེང་དང་པོ་གཅིག་དཀར་ནས་དབུ་ཚུགས་ཏེ་སྨེ་བ་ལྔ་སེར་ལ་རྫོགས་པ། སྨེ་ཕྲེང་གཉིས་པ་བཞི་ལྗང་ནས…». What stands: the books name three sme phreng of 60 years each, the first from 1 white to 5 yellow, the second from 4 green to 8 white, the third from 7 red; the arithmetic of the falling run (1 - 59 = 5 and so on) is right. What was not supported: the answer says the three columns of WB are the three rings «in this order», the first column the ring that opens at 1 «on the wood rat year before» 1687. By the books' own definition the first ring begins with 1 and the third with 7, while WB's first column begins with 7 at the fire hare (1687), its second with 1 and its third with 4: by their opening digits the columns are the third, first and second rings, not the first, second and third. The three-year shift to 1684 is an adjustment of the answer's making, with no source for it, and the table's heads «first, second, third» do not say which cycle they stand for. The other prints' column heads are the same words as WB's and prove nothing about the alignment; the *Sgo bzhi mdzod lnga'i yang bcud* and the *Gshen rab kyi 'das lo* named among the witnesses are Bon (witnesses only, no source), and the rest are 20th-century manuals read in snippets. Narrowed: the sme phreng are the three 60-year runs of the 180-year sequence (as the narrowing of 2026-11-13 said); which ring each of WB's three columns is, and whether the table is by cycle position or by named ring, is not settled. The second part (the first column agrees with the earth-lord chapters) stands as narrowed on 2026-11-13.
