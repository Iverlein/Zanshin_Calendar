# Where the year's three sme ba (columns 9–11) are used

Where else in WB, or in the Bhutan collection, are the three sme ba of a year (columns 9–11) used, and does the year's first sme ba of this table agree with the earth-lord chapters? (RD192, corpus/wb/ch13.md)

Narrowed (inv-j, 2026-11-13): the first part stays open (no second use of the three columns was found). On the second part: the first column agrees with the earth-lord and sme ba chapters. WB p. 255 (img. 265) gives the present rab byung's fire hare (1687) the natal sme ba 7 (red), and the table's year 1 (the fire hare) has 7 in the first column; the engine's `YearOfLife.yearSmeBa(year) = 7 - (year - 1687) mod 9` is the table's first column, ((8 - n) mod 9), for the years 1687-1746 and continues it year by year. The next two cycles of the engine's count are the table's other columns: 1747 (year 1 of the next rab byung) gives 1 (second column, year 1) and 1807 gives 4 (third column, year 1), 1867 gives 7 again. So the three columns are the three successive sixty-year cycles of the 180-year sequence, an inference by our arithmetic; WB's text of the table does not say so (its headings are first, second, third).
