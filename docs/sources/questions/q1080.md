# What does «བྱེས་སུ» (byes su) mean in «རྨི་ལམ་བྱེས་སུ་ཉལ་བ་རྨི» (he dreams of lying down «byes su»; kd.3:p647-648, WB vol. 1 p. 381)? WB's verses use it also in «བྱེས་སུ་འཁྱམས» and «བྱེས་སུ་ཤི་བ» (wandering, dying «byes su»), and no gloss has been found.

RD385.

## Answered 2026-11-14

Away from home, in a far place. «བྱེས» (byes) is the place far from one's home ground, the pair of «གཞིས» (gzhis, the home ground): the Tshig mdzod chen mo and the Tshig gter rgya mtsho «གཞིས་ལས་རིང་དུ་ཆོད་པའི་གནས» with «ཁྱིམ་ནས་བྱེས་སུ་བཏེག་པ» (carried from the house to a far place); the Dag tshig gsar bsgrigs «རང་ཡུལ་དང་བར་ཐག་རིང་བའི་གནས»; Rangjung Yeshe "foreign country, abroad"; Ives Waldo "abroad, away from home base"; Valby "place of temporary residence, foreign country, abroad". All read again in Steinert's dictionaries on 2026-11-14 (the headword «byes»; no headword «byes su»). So «རྨི་ལམ་བྱེས་སུ་ཉལ་བ་རྨི» is the dream of sleeping away from home, in a strange place; «བྱེས་སུ་འཁྱམས» and «བྱེས་སུ་ཤི་བ» are wandering and dying away from home. No text glosses the phrase inside a dream; the sense is the word's own (claim byes-su-is-away-from-home-in-a-far-place).
