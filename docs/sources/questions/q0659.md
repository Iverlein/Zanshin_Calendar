# The cycle number of the Precious Key's colophon

The cycle's number in The Precious Key's colophon (I1KG12897, img. 384, last line, the words after «རབ་བྱུང་»): fifteenth (1873) or sixteenth (1933)? The year reads «དཔལ་གནོད» water bird, which fits both. (RD168)

INV-1107 (2026-11-07), dead end for now: img. 384's last line enlarged to 3–7× by eye (the print is low resolution, 1251 px) and read by Yigdzin-1 on a 3× crop gives the same words as RD168, «…རབ་བྱུང་རྒྱལ་པོའི་ནང་ཚན་དཔལ་གནོད་ཆུ་བྱ་དཀའ་ཐུབ་ཟླ་བའི་ཚེས་ལ…», where the machine has «རྒྱལ་པོའི» after «རབ་བྱུང་» and no numeral; by eye the shapes after «བྱུང་» could as well be «བཅུ་དྲུག་པའི» (sixteenth), but no stroke settles it (a final ག is not seen). No number was read, so neither 1873 nor 1933 is chosen; a sharper scan (the 1987 print's other copies) or the Mentsikhang's teacher list is needed. The pool holds no source for the Mentsikhang's founding (q642 notes 'founded 1916, not in a source read here'), so no date can be argued from it. Open.

## Answered 2026-11-13

Answered by identification, not by reading the numeral: the colophon's water bird year is 1933, the sixteenth cycle's. Prajñāmaṇi, at whose presence the pupils were taught and whose circle prompted the gathering, is Khyenrab Norbu (born 1883; q672), so 1873 is out, and the print of 1987 rules out 1993. The numeral after «རབ་བྱུང་» stays unread on the scan. Claim [precious-key-water-bird-year-is-1933](../claims/precious-key-water-bird-year-is-1933.md).
