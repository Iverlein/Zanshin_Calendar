# The chu srang figures of p. 62: holding, release, pressing

What is the rule of the chu srang figures of p. 62 (30, 20 and 40 for the fast, crooked and slow courses): which is the holding, the release and the pressing time? RD203 (2026-11-08).

Narrowed (inv-j, 2026-11-13): the 30, 20 and 40 are chu srang (the text says so); 30 lengthens the holding of the fast course and the release is short, 20 delays holding and release of the crooked course, 40 shortens its pressing; the numbers do not split the 30 between holding and release, and no commentary was found that does. Claim wb-ch9-eclipse-table-figures-in-quarter-chu-tshod-and-corrections-in-chu-srang.

Narrowed (inv-q, 2026-11-14): the courses are the moon's four courses by foot (wb.1:p58-59; see q773), so the fast course is the one with an even quotient and the earlier foot. The sun's rules of p. 66 and p. 67 use the same pairs for the sun's eclipse: seized in the morning the time is 30 chu srang ahead, after noon behind; with the face's seizing not reversed the covering is two chu tshod quicker and the release long; reversed, the holding is slow and the release fast by one chu tshod each. So the pairing holding long, release short (fast course) is WB's own idiom, but no number-wise split of the 30 between holding, pressing and release is given in WB, and no published manual found states one (Henning's book could not be opened).
