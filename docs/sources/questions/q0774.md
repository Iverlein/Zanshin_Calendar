# The chu srang figures of p. 62: holding, release, pressing

What is the rule of the chu srang figures of p. 62 (30, 20 and 40 for the fast, crooked and slow courses): which is the holding, the release and the pressing time? RD203 (2026-11-08).

Narrowed (inv-j, 2026-11-13): the 30, 20 and 40 are chu srang (the text says so); 30 lengthens the holding of the fast course and the release is short, 20 delays holding and release of the crooked course, 40 shortens its pressing; the numbers do not split the 30 between holding and release, and no commentary was found that does.
