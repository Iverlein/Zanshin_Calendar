# The pairs of houses of the six summarised meanings (pp. 70–71)

The pairs of houses to which the six summarised meanings give wealth and debt (pp. 70–71) are not settled (Aries and Virgo, Pisces and Libra, Taurus, Leo, Aquarius, Scorpio, Sagittarius and Capricorn are named in an order not fixed). What are the pairs? (RD189, corpus/wb/ch10.md)

## Narrowed 2026-11-13

The verses of pp. 70-71 (machine reading, the signs by their Tibetan names) give six pairs, each the two houses of one round of the sun's six: Aries and Virgo (debt, «བུ་ལོན»), Pisces and Libra (wealth, «ནོར»), Taurus and Leo (debt; the line reads «གླང་སེང་ཆུའི་བུ་ལོན»), Aquarius and Scorpio (wealth), Gemini and Cancer (debt), Sagittarius and Capricorn (wealth). Each of the twelve signs stands once, the debt pairs are the six signs from Aries to Virgo and the wealth pairs those from Libra to Pisces, and the pairs sum to 7 or 19 by sign number (1+6, 12+7, 2+5, 11+8, 3+4, 9+10): a structure that would not come out of a misreading of the order. The «ཆུའི» after «སེང» in the Taurus-Leo line was not read; the passages are machine state (RD189) and the lines were not read on the scan. Left open for the scan.

## Answered 2026-11-14

The six pairs of p. 70-71 stand as the narrowed note gave them, and the doubtful «ཆུའི» is real. A BDRC phrase search «གླང་སེང་ཆུའི་བུ་ལོན» finds the line in a second, independently keyed etext, the collected works of the fourth Bri gung Chung tshang Bstan 'dzin chos kyi rgyal mtshan (MW3JT13322, Songtsen Library 2008, best-quality etext, search snippet): «lug dang bu mor gzugs 'bras kyi / phrogs pa'i bu lon 'grub par 'gyur / nya srang gnyis la byin pa'i nor / de bzhin glang seng chu'i bu lon / bum sdig gnyis po'i nor yin la / 'khrig pa kar+Ta dus bu lon / gzhu dang chu srin nor yin pas / de yis bsdus don drug pa 'grub». So the order is Aries and Virgo debt; Pisces and Libra wealth; Taurus and Leo debt ("chu'i" is in the line); Aquarius and Scorpio wealth; Gemini and Karkaṭa (Cancer, spelled with the Sanskritised «ཀར་ཊ») debt; Sagittarius and Capricorn wealth, six pairs of the twelve houses, each house once. «ཆུ» in «ཆུའི་བུ་ལོན» is therefore not a house of its own (Cancer already stands in the Gemini line); it is part of the Taurus-Leo line as printed and in the quoting etext (read as "the water's debt", a filler or an epithet of the pair; no source glosses it).

Source: wb.1:p70, wb.1:p71, and the search snippet named above (a copy of WB's lines in Wylie, not scanned). The scan of p. 71 was not re-read; the agreement of two etexts on «ཆུའི» is the evidence.
