# What is the animal «བླ་མོ» in «རྟ་བླ་མོ་དང་ལྟོ་འཕྱེ་དང་», and what is the clause «བགྲོད་མིན་ལ་ཞུགས་ཤིས་པའོ»?

Both left as doubts in the passages.


Narrowed (RD271, 2026-11-09): «རྟ་བླ་མོ» is the centipede (རྟ་བླ་མོ, rta bla mo, a stock word in tantras in Tibetan: «a body of many limbs, like the rta bla mo»; the dictionaries' རྟ་བླ), so the line names creatures, a centipede and a snake (ལྟོ་འཕྱེ, lto 'phye), and the Peking and Narthang «རྟ་ལ་མོ» is a shortening of the same word; the passage is a machine reading and was not checked on the scan here. Claim svo-rta-bla-mo-is-the-centipede.


Narrowed (inv-k, 2026-11-14): the second half is answered in q813 (the clause is the Agnipurāṇa's agamyāgamana, an image of its own). For the animal: BDRC's exact search of «རྟ་ལ་མོ» (14 hits) finds only an astrological term in Sde srid Sangs rgyas rgya mtsho's White Beryl and later almanacs («pho rta la mo byi ba», a year-sign phrase) and the Gesar «dmag bu rta la mo» (a rider), no creature and no gloss of the word; so Peking and Narthang's spelling is no evidence for another animal, and the centipede stands on svo-rta-bla-mo-is-the-centipede.
