# What is the animal «བླ་མོ» in «རྟ་བླ་མོ་དང་ལྟོ་འཕྱེ་དང་», and what is the clause «བགྲོད་མིན་ལ་ཞུགས་ཤིས་པའོ»?

Both left as doubts in the passages.


Narrowed (RD271, 2026-11-09): «རྟ་བླ་མོ» is the centipede (རྟ་བླ་མོ, rta bla mo, a stock word in tantras in Tibetan: «a body of many limbs, like the rta bla mo»; the dictionaries' རྟ་བླ), so the line names creatures, a centipede and a snake (ལྟོ་འཕྱེ, lto 'phye), and the Peking and Narthang «རྟ་ལ་མོ» is a shortening of the same word; the passage is a machine reading and was not checked on the scan here. Claim svo-rta-bla-mo-is-the-centipede.


Narrowed (inv-k, 2026-11-14): the second half is answered in q813 (the clause is the Agnipurāṇa's agamyāgamana, an image of its own). For the animal: BDRC's exact search of «རྟ་ལ་མོ» (14 hits) finds only an astrological term in Sde srid Sangs rgyas rgya mtsho's White Beryl and later almanacs («pho rta la mo byi ba», a year-sign phrase) and the Gesar «dmag bu rta la mo» (a rider), no creature and no gloss of the word; so Peking and Narthang's spelling is no evidence for another animal, and the centipede stands on svo-rta-bla-mo-is-the-centipede.

**Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-z2).** «རྟ་བླ་མོ་དང་ལྟོ་འཕྱེ» (3 hits: Derge, Dpe bsdur ma, Padma rdo rje) always has «བླ»; and «བགྲོད་མིན་ལ་ཞུགས» (8 hits, Derge, Dpe bsdur ma and Tārā sādhana copies quoting the Svapnohana's closing verse) reads «བགྲོད་མིན་ལ་ཞུགས་ཤིས་པའོ» in all, so no etext offers «བཟློག་མིན».

## Answered 2026-11-21

The animal is the centipede, and Bhavabhaṭṭa's commentary on the Vajraḍāka says so in a gloss: «རྟ་བླ་མོ་ཞེས་བྱ་བ་ནི་སྲོག་ཆགས་ཀྱི་ཁྱད་པར་གང་དཀྱུས་རིང་ཞིང་མདོག་དམར་ལ་རྐང་པ་མང་བ་ཞིག་གོ» (rta bla mo zhes bya ba ni srog chags kyi khyad par gang dkyus ring zhing mdog dmar la rkang pa mang ba zhig go): "the rta bla mo is a kind of creature, long in body, red, with many legs" (Derge Tengyur etext MW23703_1415, volume VE23703_1335, p. 104; same words in the Dpe bsdur ma and Narthang etexts). The Drang srong zhal lung on the Four Tantras gives the same: «རྟ་བླ་ཞེས་པ་འབུ་དམར་པོ་ཡན་ལག་མང་པོ…» (the red worm of many limbs). The line therefore names creatures, a centipede and a snake (lto 'phye). The second half of the question, the clause «བགྲོད་མིན་ལ་ཞུགས་ཤིས་པའོ», was answered in q813. Claim rta-bla-mo-glossed-long-red-many-legged.
