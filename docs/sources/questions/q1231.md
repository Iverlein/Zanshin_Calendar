# KP box 13's last line: «གདོང་ཐུག» or «གདོང་མ་ཐུག» as in WB?

At 1.35× KP prints no ma, but a faint one cannot be ruled out, and the sense reverses (meet head on / do not meet head on). WB work 13 has «ཧལ་ཁྱི་མེད» and «གདོང་མ་ཐུག»; KP has a dotted gap and «གདོང་ཐུག».
