# KP box 13's last line: «གདོང་ཐུག» or «གདོང་མ་ཐུག» as in WB?

At 1.35× KP prints no ma, but a faint one cannot be ruled out, and the sense reverses (meet head on / do not meet head on). WB work 13 has «ཧལ་ཁྱི་མེད» and «གདོང་མ་ཐུག»; KP has a dotted gap and «གདོང་ཐུག».

## Answered 2026-11-21

KP prints «གདོང་ཐུག» (no ma): the box-13 line looked at again at 4× (KP img. 31) shows the syllable «གདོང་» followed directly by «ཐུག», and a Bhutanese table manuscript (BDRC MW26041) prints the same line to its close: «… གཅིག་དཀར་རྭ་བརྒྱད་གདོང་ཐུག །ཁྱད་པར་ཆུང་སྲི་བུ་ཟན་བཅས་ལ་འཛེམ།», with «གདོང་ཐུག་འཛེམ» (the head-on meeting is avoided) twice more in the same book. So in KP the bad half says the head-on meeting (of the first and the eighth) is to be avoided; WB's «ཧལ་ཁྱི་མེད། … གདོང་མ་ཐུག» says the opposite of the same circumstance in its own wording and is another reading, not KP's (claim bhutan-table-book-parallels-kp-boxes).
