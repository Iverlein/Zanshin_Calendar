# The unsettled clauses of Little Tiger-Ear entries 44–60

the clauses of entries 44–60 that are left unsettled: མནའ་ཡང་ཟ and རྙིང་རིང་འོང (44), སྒོ་སྒེར (45), ཡོར་འོང and ཁོང་མཁྲིས (46), ཤྭས་མི་གནོད and ཁྲེལ་འཆོར (49), གཡང་འབབ and the line on pregnant women (59), ཐོག་སེར (60): what do they mean? (RD194, corpus/wb/ch18.md)

## Looked at 2026-11-16 (inv-f)

Narrowed, not closed. A second recension of the same year-omens in verse, the *Sgo bzhi mdzod lnga'i yang bcud* (BDRC MW3PD398, etext VEIE0OPI10F74A1D_I4PD3283, pp. 305-319, read in part; it differs in wording from WB's list, so each line below is a parallel, not a gloss), and the Gser thang etext of WB (MW3CN5026) give:
- «རྙིང་རིང» (44) is «སྙིང་རིང»: the Gser thang etext reads «བུད་མེད་ཆེན་རྣམས་སྙིང་རིང་འོང» and the *Sgo bzhi* «བུད་མེད་ཆེན་རྣམས་སྙིང་ཐག་རིང»; the key's gloss (q714) is that buckwheat and *nyung ma* are bad.
- «མནའ་ཡང་ཟ» (44): the Gser thang etext and the Beijing etext have it as WB does; «mna' za ba» is to disregard an oath, to renege on a pledge (Ives-Waldo; *Tshig mdzod chen mo* «mna' dam la mi rtsi ba», «snga tshig dam bca' ras su bor nas mna' za ba»); the *Sgo bzhi* has «བྱིས་པ་མནའ་ཟ་ཉུང་མ་ལེགས» (p. 311) and «བྱིས་པ་མནའ་ཟ་སྔ་བཏབ་ཉེས» (p. 317, next to «བུད་མེད་སྙིང་རིང་ནམ་ཞུག་ཐུང»), so the clause says children renege on oaths and the early-sown is bad or *nyung ma* good, by year. No source says why children.
- «སྒོ་སྒེར» (45): the *Sgo bzhi* has the same line «སྒོ་སྒེར་བསོད་ནམས་བསྐྱེད་ན་ཕན། ལོ་ནི་ཕྱི་བཏབ་བཟང་བ་ཡིན» (p. 311) in the earth-bird year, so it is no misprint; no headword «sgo sger» in the 64 dictionaries; «sger» alone is "private" and «sgo» door, not a gloss.
- «ཡོར་འོང» (46): the *Sgo bzhi* has «ཡོར་ཡོང་ཀུན་འབྲིང་ལོ་འགོ་བཟང་» (p. 311) in the iron-dog year; Ives-Waldo has «yor» "rinderpest" only; «ཁོང་མཁྲིས» has no parallel and no headword. Open.
- «ཤྭས་མི་གནོད» and «ཁྲེལ་འཆོར» (49): the water-mouse year of the *Sgo bzhi* (p. 312) has «ཤྭ་འོང་རྒྱ་གར་རྒྱལ་བློན་འཛེར། ཉིན་མོ་ཚད་པས་ཚ་ཞིང་གདུང་» and «གནམ་བཟང་ལྟོག་ནས་ཁྲེལ་འཚོར་ཞིང་། ནད་མང་གྲོ་དང་དཀར་མོ་ལེགས» (the sky good, but from hunger «khrel 'tshor»; many diseases). «khrel» is also a kind of millet (Valby, Ives-Waldo), «'chor» to lose or escape; none glosses the phrase. Open.
- «གཡང་འབབ» (59): the *Sgo bzhi* has «གཡང་དང་རྦབ་འདྲིལ་ཤི་བ་མང» (p. 310, "falls from precipices and avalanches, many die"), which fits «g.yang» as a precipice with «'bab» to fall, in another year; the line on pregnant women has «བུད་མེད་སྦྲུམ་མ་ཤི་བ་མང» (p. 305) and «སྦྲུམ་མ་རི་དྭགས་འབྲུ་སྙི་ཞིང་» (p. 317) in the *Sgo bzhi*, so «སྦྲུམ་མ» is plain "pregnant" and «འབྲུ་སྙི» recurs.
- «ཐོག་སེར» (60): hail and thunderbolts (Ives-Waldo «thog ser»; Negi's *karakā, varṣopala*), and the *Sgo bzhi* has «ཐོག་སེར་རྒོད» in many years: settled as "hail".
Dead ends: no headword «khong mkhris», «sgo sger», «yor 'ong», «shwas», «khrel 'chor» in Steinert's sets; BDRC phrases «ཁྲེལ་འཚོར་ཞིང» 1 hit, «ཡོར་ཡོང་ཀུན་འབྲིང» 1 hit, «གཡང་འབབ» 121 hits (not read), «མནའ་ཡང་ཟ» 6 hits (WB's prints, the Do shal, the *Dgongs pa'i me long* MW3CN2232_B4D38B on the dead, no gloss).

## Looked at 2026-11-16 (inv-u)

Narrowed further. New phrases and sources:
- «ཤྭས» (49) is «shwa», flood: Ives-Waldo «shwa» "flood"; Valby "overflowing of rivers and lakes"; *Tshig mdzod chen mo* «shwa» 2) «ser ba drag tu babs te shwa dang rbab rgod kyis ri lung khengs» (hail fell hard and the flood and the avalanche filled the hills and valleys). So «ཆར་ཆེ་ཤྭས་མི་གནོད» reads "heavy rain, floods do no harm", and the *Sgo bzhi*'s «ཤྭ་འོང» (p. 312) is "floods come" in the same year. The Do shal (BDRC MW2PD17386_D19206, etext I1KG1710 p. 251) has the line as «ཆ་ཕྱིར་འཚོ་ཤཱ་མ་མི་གནོང», garbled OCR but the same words in the same place; its next line has «ཁྲེལ་འཆོར» as WB does. Settled as "flood" by the dictionaries.
- «ཁྲེལ་འཆོར» (49): the Do shal has it as WB. «khrel» is shame (Ives-Waldo, Rangjung Yeshe), «'chor ba» is to lose, to slip away (Rangjung Yeshe, Ives-Waldo; the *Tshig mdzod chen mo*'s examples include «g.yang 'chor ba»), so "shame is lost" is the literal reading and fits "from hunger"; no text glosses the phrase itself.
- «གཡང» (59): «g.yang» is fortune, luck or precipice (Rangjung Yeshe, Ives-Waldo); the *Tshig mdzod chen mo* has «g.yang 'chor ba» (fortune lost) under «'chor ba» but no «g.yang 'bab» headword; «'bab» "come down" with «g.yang» as fortune or as precipice stays open.
- «ཁོང་མཁྲིས» (46): the Do shal (etext I1KG1710, p. 251) has the line «ཡིན་ཡང་བོད་མཁྲིས་འགྱུར་བར་བཤད», the same place after «སྒོ་སྒེར་བསོད་ནམས་བསྐྱེད་ན་ཕན། ནས་བྱ་བྲ་ཉེས་ཕྱེ་བཏབ་ཟད་» (OCR, garbled): so «mkhris» (bile) is in both copies; «khong» is within, inside (Rangjung Yeshe «khong» 6 interior; «khong nad» internal illness). "Inner bile" is a reading from the words; no source glosses it. The Do shal's «བོད» for «ཁོང» and its «ཡང» for «ཡོར་འོང» are probably the OCR's; not settled.
- «སྒོ་སྒེར» (45): same words in the Do shal (p. 251), the Beijing etext and the Gser thang etext (5 hits for «སྒོ་སྒེར་བསོད་ནམས», all copies of the same verse or the *Sgo bzhi*); «sgo» means, door; «sger» individual, private (Rangjung Yeshe, Ives-Waldo, *Tshig mdzod chen mo* «sger dgrar gyur»): "merit made on one's own" is the plain reading, not a gloss.
Dead ends 2026-11-16: «ཁོང་མཁྲིས», «ཤྭས་མི་གནོད» (1 hit: the Peking etext I01JW179, closed 403), «སྦྲུམ་མ་གཡང་འབབ» 0 hits; «ཁྲེལ་འཆོར» 30 hits and «ཡོར་འོང» 5 hits (the copies of WB, the Do shal, the *Sgo bzhi*; I3CN4791, I3CN2301, I6465 not opened); «སྒོ་སྒེར་བསོད་ནམས» 5 hits, all this verse. No headword «shwas», «khrel 'chor», «g.yang 'bab», «sgo sger» in Steinert's sets. «ཡོར་འོང» (46) and «ཐོག་སེར» stay as before.
