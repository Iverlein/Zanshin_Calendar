# Ch. 6 p. 41: the stacked pairs of figures

Ch. 6 p. 41 prints stacked pairs (59 over 15 after «tithi», 89 over 10 after «direction», 10 over 15 after «twenty-five»). What is the sense of these pairs (rod and tithi, rod and direction)? RD205 (2026-11-08); the upper rows are less sure.

## Read again 2026-11-21 (rd-u)

Not advanced: no new check beyond q0778's note on the print's ༡ and ༢ shapes; the pairs of p. 41 were not re-read. Open.

## Answered 2026-11-24

The pairs are a whole number of days over a part of a day in sixtieths, the planet's days in a house (a sign): the stack beside the days' words gives them as a figure, and the second row is the sixtieths. The check is arithmetic on the page's own circles (p. 40), and it fits all three stacks and explains why Jupiter has none.

- Mars: «མིག་དམར་རི་མདའ་དབྱུག་ཚེས(stack)སྦྱངས»: the word-numerals «རི་མདའ» (mountain 7, arrow 5, read last first) spell 57 days; the stack's lower row is ༡༥ = 15 of 60, so 57 15/60 = 57.25 days, which is Mars's circle 687 (p. 40, «མིག་དམར་རི་ཀླུ་རོ ༦༨༧») divided by 12 houses, 57.25 exactly. The upper row read ༥༩ by RD205 is ༥༧: the print's hooked ༧ looks like ༩ (RD205w, ch07 p. 43).
- Saturn: «སྤེན་པ་རི་བུག་ཀླུ་ཞག་དང་། དབྱུ་གུ་ཕྱོགས(stack)»: «རི་བུག་ཀླུ» spells 897 days (mountain 7, hole 9, nāga 8 read last first), and «ཕྱོགས» is 10, the lower row ༡༠: 897 10/60 = 897.1667 days = Saturn's circle 10766 (p. 40, «ཕྱོགས(༡༠༧༦༦)») divided by 12, 897.1667 exactly. The upper row was read ༨༩ at two glyphs; the third glyph (༧) is the one the words need.
- Mercury and Venus: «ཞི་གཉིས་མཁའ་མེའི་ཞག་དང་ནི། དབྱུ་གུ་ཉེར་ལྔར(stack)»: «མཁའ་མེ» is 30 days, and «ཉེར་ལྔ» 25: the stack is 30 over 25 (30 25/60 = 30.4167, the sun's days in a sign, 365.25 / 12 = 30.44, to the precision of the sixtieths). RD205c's upper row ༡༠ is a ༣༠ printed with the hooked ༣ that looks like ༡ (RD205w, p. 43: 30 printed like ༡༠), and its lower ༡༥ is ༢༥ with the same confusion of ༢ and ༡ at this size; only this third stack is not told glyph by glyph, the values rest on the words and the sun's year.
- Jupiter has no stack: «ཕུར་བུ་གཟུགས་དུས་མེ ༣༦༡»: its circle 4332 divided by 12 is 361 exactly, so no part of a day is left to write.

So the rod (དབྱུ་གུ, dbyu gu) is the sixtieth part of a day-night used for the remainder: "Mars's 57 days and 15 sixtieths are cleared", and the figures are the stack beside the word-numerals of the same line, upper whole, lower part. The 1996 etext leaves these brackets empty (( ) three times), so the checks are the arithmetic and the word-numerals, not a second print.
