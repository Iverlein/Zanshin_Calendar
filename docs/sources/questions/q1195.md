# Is BDRC's person record P7962 (Kaḥ thog Nam mkha' rdo rje) a duplicate of P6025 (Nyin dgon bya bral ba Nam mkha' rdo rje)?

Raised by the 2026-11-13 investigation s.

## Narrowed 2026-11-13 (inv-t)

BDRC (purl.bdrc.io records, 2026-11-13) keeps two records. P7962 «kaH thog mkhas grub nam mkha' rdo rje»: seat Kaḥ thog (G17), flourished «14», century field 15, student of P4312 Yag sde Paṇ chen (1299-1378, whose teacher-of list contains P7962), alternative titles «bya bral ba nam mkha' rdo rje» and «rtogs ldan nam mkha' rdo rje», one note (MW20396 p. 164, 'enumerates his sems sde pra khrid'), no works. P6025 «nyin dgon bya bral ba nam mkha' rdo rje»: seat Nyin dgon (G516), flourished «17», century field 18, student of P2943 and P6013, teacher of P5994 and P6010, with two authored works (the sems sde pra khrid man ngag MW8LS19380 and a reply on the Zhi khro dgongs pa rang grol). Same name, same school, same text type (the mind-series instruction), the same epithet «bya bral ba»; the sole fact apart is the tie to Yag sde Paṇ chen, which no other record confirms, and P7962's note cites a modern history of Kaḥ thog that BDRC dates to 1996, whose p. 164 was not read. The records of the pupils and of the Kaḥ thog history (q1145) name only one Bya bral ba Nam mkha' rdo rje, of the 18th-19th century. This favours one man entered twice, with P7962's 14th century a misdating; BDRC itself does not say so, and the Yag sde Paṇ chen link could also be a real older namesake. Still open: MW20396 p. 164 and a source that names the Yag sde Paṇ chen pupil.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-vvv)

One more witness for the single later man: the life of Padma 'gro 'dul gsang sngags gling pa (BDRC etext VE23945_001) knows only one «bla ma Bya bral ba Nam mkha' rdo rje», in a 19th-century setting; no text found names the Yag sde Paṇ chen pupil. BDRC still does not say the two records are one. p. 164 of MW20396 unread.

## Narrowed 2026-11-16 (inv-v)

Counter-evidence to the single later man. The Kaḥ thog's own short history of the thirteen great instructions («Rgyal ba kaH thog pa'i grub mchog rnams kyi nyams bzhes khrid chen bcu gsum gyi lo rgyus mdor bsdus zung 'jug grub pa'i lam bzang», BDRC etext VEIE0OPI48005610_I5994, OCR with errors; etext pages 105-107 and 118; read 2026-11-16 by etext only) names two Nam mkha' rdo rje, not one. (1) Under the second great instruction (the sems sde pra khrid of «mkhas grub Nam mkha' rdo rje», written down from Sprul pa'i sku Dga' rab rdo rje) the lineage lists «rtogs ldan Nam mkha' rdo rje» as the one on whom the Yang tig / Mkha' 'gro snying thig transmission «fell» from Vajraśrī (Badzra shrī), after Rtsa sde Nam mkha' rdo rje, Drung Thugs rje dbang phyug and Ston pa dbang 'byor dpal; and the chief of the line, «dpal 'bar ba Nam mkha' rdo rje», is said to be the son of Grags pa dbang 'byor's relative («dbon po»), to have come young to Kaḥ thog, met Bla ma Gyan pa rin po che and Ston pa dbang 'byor, studied under Byang chub dpal and Gnam stong pa Rdo rje dpal ba, taught at Rgyal mo tsha ba rong, and newly written the sems sde instruction texts from Dga' rab rdo rje's line; his pupils are listed down through «dge paN» and «Nyin dgon Gzhan phan rdo rje». (2) The line of the Kun mkhyen Klong chen pa's Yang tig (p. 118) runs «Kun mkhyen chos kyi rgyal po (Klong chen pa) → Mkhas grub Bde legs rgya mtsho → Bya bral chos rje → Gzhan phan rdo rje → Kaḥ thog pa Mkhas grub Nam mkha' rdo rje → Sman shod Sangs rgyas rdo rje»: a Kaḥ thog Mkhas grub Nam mkha' rdo rje some generations after Klong chen pa and after a Gzhan phan rdo rje. So the Kaḥ thog tradition itself has an earlier Nam mkha' rdo rje (the teachers named are of the Kaḥ thog lineage of the thirteenth and fourteenth centuries, the shape of P7962's record) and a later one placed after Gzhan phan rdo rje (the 17th-18th century setting of P6025); no text reads «bya bral ba» with the first. The text gives no year for either; whether the history that BDRC cites for P7962 (MW20396 p. 164) means the earlier or the later was not read. This keeps P7962 and P6025 as possibly two real people (an earlier Rtogs ldan / Dpal 'bar ba and a later Mkhas grub), against the 'one man entered twice' reading of the earlier notes; BDRC's two records are not shown to be one, nor shown to be two.
