# Is BDRC's person record P7962 (Kaḥ thog Nam mkha' rdo rje) a duplicate of P6025 (Nyin dgon bya bral ba Nam mkha' rdo rje)?

Raised by the 2026-11-13 investigation s.

## Narrowed 2026-11-13 (inv-t)

BDRC (purl.bdrc.io records, 2026-11-13) keeps two records. P7962 «kaH thog mkhas grub nam mkha' rdo rje»: seat Kaḥ thog (G17), flourished «14», century field 15, student of P4312 Yag sde Paṇ chen (1299-1378, whose teacher-of list contains P7962), alternative titles «bya bral ba nam mkha' rdo rje» and «rtogs ldan nam mkha' rdo rje», one note (MW20396 p. 164, 'enumerates his sems sde pra khrid'), no works. P6025 «nyin dgon bya bral ba nam mkha' rdo rje»: seat Nyin dgon (G516), flourished «17», century field 18, student of P2943 and P6013, teacher of P5994 and P6010, with two authored works (the sems sde pra khrid man ngag MW8LS19380 and a reply on the Zhi khro dgongs pa rang grol). Same name, same school, same text type (the mind-series instruction), the same epithet «bya bral ba»; the sole fact apart is the tie to Yag sde Paṇ chen, which no other record confirms, and P7962's note cites a modern history of Kaḥ thog that BDRC dates to 1996, whose p. 164 was not read. The records of the pupils and of the Kaḥ thog history (q1145) name only one Bya bral ba Nam mkha' rdo rje, of the 18th-19th century. This favours one man entered twice, with P7962's 14th century a misdating; BDRC itself does not say so, and the Yag sde Paṇ chen link could also be a real older namesake. Still open: MW20396 p. 164 and a source that names the Yag sde Paṇ chen pupil.

## Narrowed 2026-11-14 (inv-vvv)

One more witness for the single later man: the life of Padma 'gro 'dul gsang sngags gling pa (BDRC etext VE23945_001) knows only one «bla ma Bya bral ba Nam mkha' rdo rje», in a 19th-century setting; no text found names the Yag sde Paṇ chen pupil. BDRC still does not say the two records are one. p. 164 of MW20396 unread.
