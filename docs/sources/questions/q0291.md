# Who the «བཙད་པོ» is

«བཙད་པོ» (pp. 97, 127): who is the *btsad po* whose corpse is not sent on the one-white or the nine-red, and whom the nine-red's peg guards with the patron?

## Answered 2026-11-05

Btsad po is a lord, a rank (the digest p. 556, and the verse manual I4949/LT). ([word-hunts.md](../word-hunts.md), RD161)
