# Which ch. 27 starting points does WB vol. 2 p. 17 belong to, if not the thirty of the notes (p. 532)? p. 17 calls the weapons' one a «སྐོར་འགོ» (starting point) but fights by a rule of its own (the eighteenth and tenth).

Raised by the 2026-11-13 investigation g.

## Answered 2026-11-14

Read from WB's own wording, the chapter's table of contents on BDRC, and a questioner's list of doubts, 2026-11-14 (inv-ff). p. 17's four reckonings (the great spiritual friend or chief, the ministers, the soldiers, the weapons) are not among ch. 27's starting points of the thirteen circles (five to a circle, pp. 3-14) and not among ch. 26's thirty of the notes: they are the third group of the chapter's «scattered ones from the sixty cavities», the offshoots of the secret reckoning.

(1) The verse says so itself. p. 17 opens «ཡན་ལག་གབ་བརྩིས་ལས་བྱུང་བའི། །དགེ་བཤེས་ཆེན་པོར་བརྩི་བ་ནི།» "arisen from the limbs of the secret reckoning (gab brtsis), the reckoning for a great spiritual friend" (wb.2:p17). The secret reckonings (གབ་རྩིས, gab rtsis) are the three before it: the heart's, «ཐུགས་ཀྱི་གབ་ཚེའི་རྩིས་མིག» (pp. 14-15), the bones', «རུས་པའི་གབ་རྩིས» (pp. 15-16) and the flesh's, «ཤ་ཡི་གབ་རྩིས» (p. 16), and the heart's one is introduced by «གཞན་ཡང་ཁོག་པ་དྲུག་ཅུ་ལས། །འབྱུང་བའི་ཁ་འཐོར་འགའ་ཞིག་བསྟན།» "further, some scattered ones that arise from the sixty cavities are shown" (wb.2:p14-15). So p. 14's line gives the group's name (the sixty cavities' scattered ones), and p. 17's first line says the next four are its limbs (ཡན་ལག, yan lag).

(2) The chapter's contents as BDRC catalogues it (the chapter record under the Taikhang reproduction MW30116, the field «CHAPTER TIBT», a search snippet) lists, in this order: «བྱེས་སོང་ལ་གཅེས་པའི་རྩིས། ཁོག་པ་དྲུག་ཅུ་པ་ལས་ཁ་འཐོར། རུས་པའི་གབ་རྩིས། ཤ་ཡི་གབ་རྩིས། ཕོ་མོའི་བླ་འཁྱམས་མ་འཁྱམས། ཡན་ལག་གབ་རྩིས་ལས་བྱུང་བའི་དགེ་བཤེས། བློན་པོ། དམག་མི་དར་འཛིན། མཚོན་ཆ། འཁོར་ལོ་གཏིང་རྫོགས། མོ་སྐོར། ལྷ་འདྲེ་ཉོ་ཚོང ...» (Wylie as given: byes song la gces pa'i rtsis / khog pa drug cu pa las kha 'thor / rus pa'i gab rtsis / sha'i gab rtsis / pho mo'i bla 'khyams ma 'khyams / yan lag gab rtsis las byung ba'i dge bshes / blon po / dmag mi dar 'dzin / mtshon cha / 'khor lo gting rdzogs / mo skor / lha 'dre nyo tshong ...). The spiritual friend, ministers, soldiers and weapons stand as four items under «ཡན་ལག་གབ་རྩིས་ལས་བྱུང་བ», and the wheel (p. 17-19) follows. The same list is in the Gser thang White Beryl etext (MW2CZ8040, snippet).

(3) The colophon agrees (wb.2:p25): «ཁོག་པ་དྲུག་ཅུ་ལས་འབྱུང་བའི་གཞན་མ་རྣམས་རང་རང་གི་ལེ་ཁོངས་སུ་བསྡུས། ཁ་འཐོར་ལུས་པ་རྣམས་དང་ ... རྩིས་ཕྲན་རྣམས་བཀོད་པའི་ལེའུ» "the others that come from the sixty cavities are gathered each into its own chapter; the scattered ones that were left, and the small reckonings, are set out: chapter twenty-seven"; and the chapter's title is «... དང་རྩིས་ཕྲན་གྱི་སྐོར» (wb.2:p3), the circles «and the group of small reckonings». So p. 17 belongs to the small reckonings' left-overs from the sixty cavities.

(4) Sangs rgyas rgya mtsho's own reader's list of doubts, the «Bai DUrya g.ya' sel» (Taikhang 1971, MW8226, etext read: its item 141, «བརྒྱ་ཞེ་གཅིག་པ») names the same group: «ཁོག་པ་དྲུག་ཅུ་པ་སོགས་པའི་ས་ཕྲན། དཔེར་ན་ཐུགས་ཀྱི་གབ་ཚེའི་རྩིས་ཡིག་ལས་གསེར་གྱི་རྐང་བཞི་ཁ་བཀྱམ་གྱི་རྩིས་སོགས་ཀེག་རྩིས་ལྟ་བུ་དང་། ཁོན་ཐོག་བུ་མོ་བཙས་པ་ན། །མ་སྒོ་བུ་མོས་བཀག་པ་སྟེ། ཞེས་སོགས་བཙས་ཀྲུངས་ཀྱི་རྩིས་ལྟ་བུ ...» "the small ones such as the sixty cavities: for example from the heart's secret-life reckoning book the golden four legs, and the rest like a keg reckoning; and the birth-reckonings like «khon above, a girl born ...»" (these are the lines of p. 15), asking whether such lines, which belong to no reckoning of their own, should stand. The doubts do not name p. 17; they confirm that the sixty-cavities' group is the heart's secret reckoning and its kind.

What is computed, what is read: nothing computed. The rule of p. 17 (the eighteenth upward fights the life force, the tenth the power, the eighteenth and tenth downward the luck and the body) is its own and is not the rule of the circles' starting points or of ch. 26's thirty (claim notes-thirty-are-ch26-not-ch27-p17); the word «སྐོར་འགོ» on p. 17 is used for the weapons' one only («མཚོན་ཆའི་སྐོར་འགོ»), as the notes' «སྐོར་འགོ» of the thirty and the circles' «སྐོར་འགོ་ལྔ» are others. Not found: a commentary that says why the four are called limbs of the secret reckoning (the doubt-list does not answer).
