# The figure after «འཁོར་ཕྱེད» on p. 32, line 3 of the verse

The figure after «འཁོར་ཕྱེད» on p. 32 (line 3 of the verse) is damaged in the print: the etext gives 12, but a half wheel of 27 is 13½; what does the line give? RD204 (2026-11-08).

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (scan-f).** Located on the Taikhang reproduction (I5877) img. 68 (folio side of 1996 p. 32), line 3: «…འཁོར་ལོ་༢༧་བསྣན་ལ་འཁོར་ཕྱེད(?)དོར།», the figure above the line after «འཁོར་ཕྱེད» at about x = 5250–5350 of 8000 (crop x 5210–5360, y 690–790 at 10×). It is a two-figure group of touching glyphs, a curled one and a «ཅ»-like one with a dot, which looks more like ༡༣ (13) than 12; the print leaves no room for «½». Not settled at this size; the Zhol wording of the line differs («འཁོར་ཕྱེད་དོར་བསྣན་མ་དོར་ཕྲི») and carries no figure there.

**Narrowed 2026-11-14 (scan-h).** Re-seen on img. 68 (crop x 5500–6100, y 640–900 at 3×): over the line after «འཁོར་ཕྱེད» there stands a round-dotted blob with a hat above a «3»-like lower stroke, a shape like the ༣ of img. 66 line 3 (blob, dash, stroke; x 6405), but it is read as one glyph or two (༡ and ༣) neither way with the references; ༡༣ (13) stays likely, 12 is not supported (no ༢ shape). Not settled.
