# The kun phan me long

Sigla (WB, NM, SY, KP, Rabten), the numbering of mansions and weekdays, and
the rules for quoting are in [README.md](README.md).

Mtho las dgon print, BDRC W4CZ65561, 120 images, open; one to three boxes
per folio, each headed by an activity, with "good" (*bzang ba*) and "bad"
(*ngan pa*) halves listing weekdays (*gza'*), dates (*tshes*, in numerals),
mansions (*skar ma*, abbreviated; see [mansions.md](mansions.md)), day animals, trigrams,
signs and conjunctions (*dus sbyor*). Each box carries its number in an
arch above the heading; Henning's numbers ("6. Offerings to deities") are
these box numbers.

Two other copies were tried. W21970 (Kan su'u mi rigs dpe skrun khang,
2000) is restricted ("not accessible under fair use"). The volume BDRC
lists as a dbu med manuscript (MW3CN12069_4F9795, vol. 4, scans I3CN12074,
414 images, open) holds, at img. 218–345, **a second dbu can print of the
*kun phan me long***, called **KP2** below: the same tables in the same
layout, found 2026-10-03 by running the Yigdzin OCR over every tenth
image. Its image numbers run 219–225 ahead of KP's (KP img. 22 = KP2
img. 241, KP img. 55 = KP2 img. 278). It has boxes 50 and 51, which KP
lacks.

## The book's own list of contents

Img. 8–11 list the boxes by number, each name under its numbered arch, as a
table of contents (img. 8 ends the introduction and gives items 1–4, img.
9–10 items 5–61, img. 11 items 61–65 before item 1 itself begins). It
names every box in a word or two and so settles headings that are hard to
read in the boxes themselves. Read on the scan (2026-10-03, img. 9–10;
img. 8 and 11 from the Yigdzin OCR); […] marks what the print does not show
clearly. Items 1–4:

> ༼༡༽ ཚེས་རྩིས་པ་རྣམས་ཀྱི་འཁྲུལ་སྤོང་རྣམ་བཤད་རིགས་ལྡན་བརྒྱུད་པའི་ཞལ་ལུང་། ༼༢༽ མིང་འདོགས་པ། ༼༣༽ གོས་གསར་དྲ་བ། ༼༤༽ གོས་དྲ་གྱོན་པ་ཐུན་མོང་གི་ཙཀྲ།

Item 1 is not a table but the prose that opens the book (img. 11–20):
"clearing away the errors of the calendar-makers".

## The rest of the book

Read 2026-10-03 from the Yigdzin OCR of every folio (the first lines of
each, and the book's own lists of contents), not by eye; it says what each
folio is, which is all this inventory needs. It replaces a description
by agy from reduced scans, most of which was wrong (it had img. 11–16 on
yearly elements and img. 87–101 as woodcuts of animals).

- **1, 3:** title, འབྲས་རྩིས་བཻ་དཀར་དགོངས་དོན་དྲི་མེད་ཀུན་ཕན་མེ་ལོང; **2** blank.

- **7–8:** prose introduction: outer and inner Kālacakra; the book
  condenses the White Beryl's electional results into tables; then the
  list of contents, items 1–4 (above).
- **9–11:** the list of contents, items 5–65 (above).
- **11–20:** item 1, prose (see the inventory).
- **21–63:** the boxes, items 2–65 (above).
- **63–64:** the second part (སྨད་ཆ, "the results of the individual factors,
  with remedies for the bad"), with its own list of 18 sections:
