# The kun phan me long

Sigla (WB, NM, SY, KP, Rabten), the numbering of mansions and weekdays, and
the rules for quoting are in [README.md](README.md).

Mtho las dgon print, BDRC W4CZ65561, 120 images, open; one to three boxes
per folio, each headed by an activity, with "good" (*bzang ba*) and "bad"
(*ngan pa*) halves listing weekdays (*gza'*), dates (*tshes*, in numerals),
mansions (*skar ma*, abbreviated; see [mansions.md](mansions.md)), day animals, trigrams,
signs and conjunctions (*dus sbyor*). Each box carries its number in an
arch above the heading; Henning's numbers ("6. Offerings to deities") are
these box numbers.

Two other copies were tried. W21970 (Kan su'u mi rigs dpe skrun khang,
2000) is restricted ("not accessible under fair use"). The dbu med
manuscript (BDRC MW3CN12069_4F9795, vol. 4, scans I3CN12074, 414 images,
open) is written as prose but worn; BDRC's Ume OCR returns noise on it, and
which of its images hold the *kun phan me long* is not yet known.

## The book's own list of contents

Img. 9–10 list the boxes by number, each name under its numbered arch, as a
table of contents. It names every box in a word or two and so settles
headings that are hard to read in the boxes themselves. Read on the scan
(2026-10-03); […] marks what the print does not show clearly. Img. 9 begins
with an item before 5 whose arch is not on the image (*sngo chog*?); items
1–4 are not on img. 9.

## The rest of the book

Img. 21–65 above were read by eye. The other folios were described by agy
(Gemini 3.1 Pro) from reduced scans and only spot-checked, so the lines
below say what kind of page each is, not what it says; on the table folios
the same reading proved unreliable (wrong box numbers, invented headings).

- **1–3:** title folios (*'bras rtsis le tho …*).
- **4–8:** prose: homage, introduction, the waxing and waning moon.
- **9–10:** the list of contents above (read by eye).
- **11–16:** prose on the yearly element calculation for men and women
  (*pho lo / mo lo khams*), life force and power (*lus srog dbang thang*),
  and their use.
- **17–20:** prose on good and bad days, naming children, places.
- **64–65:** prose: the great compatibility (*'phrod chen*) and numbered
  short paragraphs.
- **66:** a table of the 28 mansions by element (fire, earth, wind, water),
  names spelled out and numbered — *chu smad* under earth and *chu stod*
  under water, as in WB; then prose.
- **67–75:** prose and tables: the changing year, *kun dga'* and other
  combinations, the eight classes of spirits, the eleven karaṇas
  (*byed pa bcu gcig*) and their times.
- **76–83:** tables: black dates, a magic square, the turtle diagram, the sme
  ba, day and night, weekdays, the karaṇa times, month stars.
- **84–86:** prose: offerings and repairs to the earth lords (*sa bdag*), a
  ransom rite.
- **87–101:** woodcuts: the twelve animals, then figures (ransom effigies?),
  the animals again.
- **102–118:** a diagram of the periods of the day, then prose extracts on
  electional astrology (temples, brides, digging, armies …).
- **119:** title of an appended text (*skar rtsis kun 'dus snang srid gsal
  ba*, from the Kālacakra) and colophon; **120** blank.
