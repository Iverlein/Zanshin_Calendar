# Lineage days

Sigla, numbering and quoting rules are in [README.md](README.md).


## The dates of Rtsa gsum gling pa, Machig Labdrön (མ་གཅིག་ལབ་སྒྲོན, ma gcig lab sgron) and Phadampa Sangye (ཕ་དམ་པ་སངས་རྒྱས, pha dam pa sangs rgyas) ([T6.4](#t6-4)): birth, passing and the other days their lives and their lineages (Chöd, གཅོད, gcod; Shijé, ཞི་བྱེད, zhi byed) mark, each with its Tibetan date and how the day is kept (RD411)

Read 2026-11-24 (RD411), on the scan wherever the state below says so; the passages, Tibetan with the English beside, are in the corpus files named by their ids, the facts in the claims. No year is converted here: each date stands as the text gives it (element and animal, or the cycle; a month by number, by its animal or by its season).

### Rtsa gsum gling pa (རྩ་གསུམ་གླིང་པ, rtsa gsum gling pa)

- **Birth**, no month or day anywhere: the wood-dog year (ཤིང་ཁྱི་ལོ, shing khyi lo) of the twelfth sixty-year cycle (རབ་བྱུང, rab byung), «༡༦༩༤», by the modern editor of TSL vol. 30 ([tsl.30:i4-5](corpus/tsl/tsl.md#tsl-30-i4-5), scan); «རབ་འབྱུང་བཅུ་གཅིག་པའི་ནང་འཁྲུངས། ལོ་རྟགས་གསལ་བར་མི་སྣང་།», "born within the eleventh cycle; the sign of the year does not appear clearly", by Jamyang Khyentse Wangpo ([tsl.30:i565](corpus/tsl/tsl.md#tsl-30-i565), scan), followed by KTD's translation ([pbc:p2](corpus/pbc/pbc.md#pbc-p2)); 1685 in the introduction to KTD's translation of the autobiography ([pbc:p1](corpus/pbc/pbc.md#pbc-p1)). Claim: [tsl-birth-year-three-versions](claims/tsl-birth-year-three-versions.md).
- **Passing**, no month or day: «དགུང་གྲངས་ཞེ་ལྔར་བཞེས་པ་རབ་བྱུང་བཅུ་གཉིས་པ་ས་རྟ་ལོ», "at forty-five, in the earth-horse year of the twelfth cycle" (1738 by the editor, [tsl.30:i8](corpus/tsl/tsl.md#tsl-30-i8), scan); «དགུང་ལོ་བཞི་བཅུ་རྩ་བཞི་པ་ཙམ་ལ་སྐུ་གཤེགས», "passed at about forty-four", by Jamgön Kongtrul ([tsl.30:i575](corpus/tsl/tsl.md#tsl-30-i575), scan). Claim: [tsl-passing-earth-horse-or-about-forty-four](claims/tsl-passing-earth-horse-or-about-forty-four.md).
- **Other dated events**: the first treasure at Ti sgro in the fire-monkey year (མེ་སྤྲེལ་ལོ, me sprel lo, 1716 by the editor); the ḍākinī's bidding for «the tenth day of the Monkey month» (སྤྲེལ་ཟླའི་ཚེས་བཅུ, sprel zla'i tshes bcu, [tsl.30:i86-88](corpus/tsl/tsl.md#tsl-30-i86-88), etext, RD363); the count of his treasures «up to the fire-horse year» (མེ་རྟ་ཡན་ཆད, me rta yan chad, [tsl.30:i569](corpus/tsl/tsl.md#tsl-30-i569), scan); the autobiography «arranged in the second month of the fire-sheep year» (མེ་ལུག་ཟླ་བ་གཉིས་པ, me lug zla ba gnyis pa, [tsl.30:i564](corpus/tsl/tsl.md#tsl-30-i564), scan). Claim: [tsl-life-dated-events](claims/tsl-life-dated-events.md).
- **Kept as a day of practice**: none found. No anniversary of his is named in TSL vols. 27-30, in the Sgo chen liturgy (the etext of MW00EGS1016761), or on padmasambhava.org's pages on him.

### Machig Labdrön (མ་གཅིག་ལབ་སྒྲོན, ma gcig lab sgron)

- **Conception and birth**, in the *Phung po gzan skyur gyi rnam bshad* (Lhasa 2016), the year by its animal only: the consciousness enters the womb ⟨«རྟ་ལོ་ཟླ་བ་ལྔ་པའི་ཚེས་བཅོ་ལྔ་ལ།»⟩, "on the fifteenth of the fifth month of the horse year", an interlinear note ([pgz:p5](corpus/pgz/pgz.md#pgz-p5), scan); she is born «ཚེས་བཅོ་ལྔའི་ ⟨ལུག་ལོ་ཟླ་བ་གསུམ་པའི།⟩ སྐྱ་རེངས་ཤར་ཙམ», "as dawn broke on the fifteenth ⟨of the third month of the sheep year⟩", the main line having named the month before as «the dragon month» (འབྲུག་གི་ཟླ་བ, 'brug gi zla ba) ([pgz:p9](corpus/pgz/pgz.md#pgz-p9), scan). Claim: [machig-born-fifteenth-of-the-dragon-month](claims/machig-born-fifteenth-of-the-dragon-month.md).
- **Passing**, no year, month or day: «དགུང་ལོ་དགུ་བཅུ་རྩ་ལྔ་ལ་གཤེགས», "at ninety-five", in the Blue Annals ([dsn.2:i477-478](corpus/dsn/dsn.md#dsn-2-i477-478), scan); «དགུ་བཅུ་གོ་དགུ་ལ … སྐུ་བདེ་བར་གཤེགས», "at ninety-nine", in the Gzan skyur ([pgz:p65](corpus/pgz/pgz.md#pgz-p65), scan). Claim: [machig-passing-age-ninety-five-or-ninety-nine](claims/machig-passing-age-ninety-five-or-ninety-nine.md).
- **Other dated event**: at forty-one, Tārā's empowerment «དཔྱིད་ཟླ་ཐ་ཆུང་གི་ཚེས་བརྒྱད་ཀྱི་ནུབ་མོ», "on the evening of the eighth of the last spring month" ([pgz:p43](corpus/pgz/pgz.md#pgz-p43), scan). Claim: [machig-tara-empowerment-eighth-of-last-spring-month](claims/machig-tara-empowerment-eighth-of-last-spring-month.md).
- **Kept as a day of practice**: the Mindroling (སྨིན་གྲོལ་གླིང, smin grol gling) rule-book keeps every month, on the twenty-first, «མ་གཅིག་གི་དུས་མཆོད», "the seasonal offering (དུས་མཆོད, dus mchod) of Ma gcig", with a painted scroll and the Bde gshegs kun 'dus accumulation-offering ([mdb:p163-166](corpus/mdb/mdb.md#mdb-p163-166), read again on the scan at image 165, folio 80); which Ma gcig is meant the page does not say. Claim: [machig-mindroling-twenty-first](claims/machig-mindroling-twenty-first.md). No yearly day found.

### Phadampa Sangye (ཕ་དམ་པ་སངས་རྒྱས, pha dam pa sangs rgyas)

- **Birth**: no date in the sources read.
- **In Tibet**: five comings (ལན་ལྔ, lan lnga); the last after twelve years in China, to Ding ri (དིང་རི, ding ri) «མེ་མོ་གླང་གི་ལོ་ལ», "in the fire-female-ox year" ([dsn.2:i354](corpus/dsn/dsn.md#dsn-2-i354), [dsn.2:i399](corpus/dsn/dsn.md#dsn-2-i399), scan). Claim: [phadampa-five-comings-ding-ri-fire-ox](claims/phadampa-five-comings-ding-ri-fire-ox.md).
- **Passing**: «མེ་མོ་གླང་གི་ལོ་ནས་མེ་མོ་བྱའི་བར་དུ་ལོ་ཉི་ཤུ་རྩ་གཅིག་བཞུགས་ནས … གཤེགས», "he stayed twenty-one years, from the fire-female-ox year to the fire-female-bird year, and passed away" (the Blue Annals, [dsn.2:i402](corpus/dsn/dsn.md#dsn-2-i402), scan); in the Ding ri tradition's own collection, Khams smyon Dharma seng ge's life: ill from «མེ་མོ་བྱའི་ལོ་དགུན་ཟླ་འབྲིང་པོའི་ཚེས་བཞི», "the fourth of the middle winter month of the fire-female-bird year" ([zbc.5:i123](corpus/zbc/zbc.md#zbc-5-i123), scan), wrapped in white silk «ཚེས་བཅུའི་ཐོ་རེངས», "at dawn on the tenth" ([zbc.5:i152](corpus/zbc/zbc.md#zbc-5-i152), scan), and «ཚེས་བཅུ་ལ་གྲོངས་ནས་ཚེས་བཅུ་བདུན་གྱི་སྔ་དྲོ་སྤུར་སྦྱངས», "he died on the tenth and was cremated on the morning of the seventeenth" ([zbc.5:i157](corpus/zbc/zbc.md#zbc-5-i157), scan). Claim: [phadampa-passing-tenth-of-middle-winter-month](claims/phadampa-passing-tenth-of-middle-winter-month.md).
- **Kept as a day of practice**: none found.

### Open

- Question: Rtsa gsum gling pa's birth is given as the twelfth cycle's wood-dog year (1694) by TSL vol. 30's modern editor, as within the eleventh cycle with the year unclear by Jamyang Khyentse Wangpo, and as 1685 in the introduction to KTD's translation on padmasambhava.org; on what does each rest, and which does the lineage at Sgo chen and Padma Samye Ling hold? (tsl.30:i4-5, tsl.30:i565, pbc:p1)
- Question: Does the lineage of Rtsa gsum gling pa keep the day of his passing (forty-five in the earth-horse year by the editor, about forty-four by Kongtrul), and if so on which month and day? No source read names one. (tsl.30:i8, tsl.30:i575)
- Question: Is «Ma gcig» of the Mindroling rule-book's monthly offering on the twenty-first Machig Labdrön, or another lady of the house (the same list has the Mother, Yum, on the fifteenth)? (mdb:p163-166)
- Question: In the 2016 Gzan skyur the child calls «Mother» on «the third of the dragon month» (འབྲུག་གི་ཟླ་བའི་ཚེས་གསུམ), in the 1992 edition on «the thirteenth of the third month» (ཚེས་བཅུ་གསུམ); which do the older prints of the Gzan skyur read? (pgz:p9)
- Question: Machig's age at her passing is ninety-five in the Blue Annals and ninety-nine in the Gzan skyur; which does the Chöd lineage hold, and does any Chöd text give the day? (dsn.2:i477-478, pgz:p65)
- Question: Is the tenth on which Phadampa Sangye died the tenth of the middle winter month named with the fourth, as the narrative implies, and does the Ding ri tradition keep that day? (zbc.5:i123, zbc.5:i152, zbc.5:i157)
- Question: TSL vol. 30 prints the fire-horse year (མེ་རྟ) for Rtsa gsum gling pa's count of his treasures, KTD's translation the Fire Dog year of 1726; is the translation's year name a slip, or does another print read «ཁྱི»? (tsl.30:i569, pbc:p2)
