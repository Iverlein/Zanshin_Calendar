# The hair dates: washing the hair, and FPMT's haircut list

Sigla and quoting rules are in [README.md](README.md). WB vol. 2, 1996
Beijing edition, p. 404 (img. 412 of I1KG12907, 703 px, read enlarged
2.6–5×), chapter 34's work 34, «ཁྲུས་དང་སྐྲ་འཁྲུ་བྱེད་པ», bathing and washing
the hair. Read on the scan on 2026-10-09, with BDRC's etext
(IE0OPI51524892, whose page markers are the image numbers here) for
orientation. Every quotation below was read on the scan. ROADMAP T2.15.

## The question

The app's haircut sheet shows a result for cutting the hair on each lunar
date, after FPMT's translation of the *Sutra Chapter of Bodhisattva's
Hair* (Lama Zopa Rinpoche, 2008). WB has no list of haircut results by
date: the haircut is named in the weekday and mansion verses and in
chapter 34's work 50 (cutting hair and nails, p. 420), which the app
weighs. A Russian WB-based calendar shows a hair-*washing* list that
matched FPMT's haircut list on several dates ([tibetastromed.md](tibetastromed.md)).
Is FPMT's list WB's washing list?

## What p. 404 says

After the weekdays, mansions and rising signs for bathing and washing the
hair, and the twelve links, WB turns to the dates:

> «ཚེས་འབྲས་གོང་དང་ཅུང་ཟད་ཙམ། །མི་མཐུན་འདིར་ནི་སྐྲ་ཚེས་བསྟན།»
> — the dates' results differ a little from those above; here the hair
> dates (*skra tshes*) are shown.

(The etext has སྒྲ for the print's སྐྲ.) "Above" is the date readings of
chapter 33 (pp. 297–304, [lunar-dates.md](lunar-dates.md)), which name
washing the hair good or bad on every date. Then thirty results, one per
date, and after them:

> «གོང་གི་ཚེས་འབྲས་དང་བསྟུན་ན། །གསུམ་བཞི་ལྔ་དྲུག་བརྒྱད་དང་བཅུ། །བཅུ་གཅིག་བཅུ་གསུམ་བཅོ་ལྔ་དང་།
> །བཅུ་དྲུག་བཅོ་བརྒྱད་བཅུ་དགུ་དང་། །ཉེར་གཉིས་ཉེར་གསུམ་ཉེར་དྲུག་བཟང་། །གཅིག་གཉིས་བདུན་དགུ་བཅུ་གཉིས་དང་།
> །བཅུ་བཞི་བཅུ་བདུན་ཚེས་ཉི་ཤུ། །ཉེར་གཅིག་ཉེར་བཞི་ཉེར་ལྔ་བརྒྱད། །ཉེར་དགུ་གནམ་གང་ངན་པར་བརྗོད།»
> — following the date results above: 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 11, 13, 15, 16,
> 18, 19, 22, 23, 26 good; 1, 2, 7, 9, 12, 14, 17, 20, 21, 24, 25, 28,
> 29, 30 bad.

The 27th is on neither side. (The etext garbles both lines: «བཅུ་ རྡོ་ལྔ»
for བཅུ་གསུམ་བཅོ་ལྔ, «གཅིག་གཉི་ཉིས་དང» for the whole of གཅིག་གཉིས་བདུན་དགུ་བཅུ་གཉིས་དང.)

## The thirty results, against FPMT

The verse, read on the scan: «ཚེས་གཅིག་ཚེ་ཐུང་གཉིས་ཁ་མཆུ། །གསུམ་ལྔ་ཕྱུག་ལ་བཞི་མལ་འཆོར། །དྲུག་ལ་མདངས་ཡལ་བདུན་ཁ་མཆུ།
།འཕམ་ཞིང་བརྒྱད་ལ་ཚེ་རིང་དང་། །དགུ་ནི་ན་ཆུང་མོད་འགྱུར་ཞིང་། །བཅུ་ལ་དབང་ཐང་ཆེ་བ་དང་། །བཅུ་གཅིག་དབང་རྣོ་བཅུ་གཉིས་ནད།
།བཅུ་གསུམ་བཟང་བསྐྱེད་བཅུ་བཞི་དང་། །བཅོ་ལྔ་ཕྱུག་ཅིང་བཅུ་དྲུག་ནད། །བཅུ་བདུན་ལ་ནི་ཤ་ཙི་སྔོ། །གདོན་གནོད་བཅོ་བརྒྱད་ནོར་སྟོར་ལ།
།བཅུ་དགུ་བཟང་གྲོགས་ཉི་ཤུ་ལྟོགས། །ཉེར་གཅིག་ནད་འོང་ཉེར་གཉིས་གསུམ། །ཟས་སྐོམ་རྙེད་ཅིང་ཉེར་བཞི་ལ། །དལ་ཡམས་ཉེར་ལྔ་མིག་ཚག་ཅིང་།
།ཉེར་དྲུག་ཉེར་བདུན་རྟག་ཏུ་སྐྱིད། །ཉེར་བརྒྱད་འཐབ་མོ་ཉེར་དགུ་ལ། །བླ་འཁྱམས་གནམ་གང་གཤིན་དང་ཕྲད།»

The 4th: *mal* is a bed or resting place, a dwelling (Rangjung Yeshe
dictionary, rywiki.tsadra.org, *mal*, *mal sa*), *'chor* to be lost; SN
writes «མལ་ས་འཆོར» (below). The 9th's «མོད་འགྱུར» is not settled; the
rendering keeps only «ན་ཆུང», youth.

**Result.** The two lists are one family: the same result on 17 dates
(1, 3, 5, 8–11, 14, 17, 19–22, 26, 28–30), the 24th and 25th swapped, the
6th and 7th close to swapped, the 18th reversed. Their good and bad
dates agree on 25 dates and part on five (6 and 16 good in WB, bad in
FPMT; 9 and 14 the other way; 27 good in FPMT, neither in WB). **FPMT's
list is not WB's**, and WB gives its own for washing the hair, not for
cutting it.

The good and bad dates follow chapter 33 (as WB says) on every date but
two: chapter 33 names washing the hair good on the 14th and the 27th.

## The witness: SN

SN, chapter 20 «ཁྲུས་དང་སྐྲ་འཁྲུ་བའི་རྩིས» (etext pp. 264–265, not read
on the scan), has nearly the same weekdays, mansions and rising signs as
WB's work 34, then a table «ཚེས་འབྲས་ཀྱི་རེའུ་མིག» of the same thirty results
in shorter words: 4 «མལ་ས་འཆོར», 9 «ན་ཆུང་དང་འཕྲད», 17 «གདོན་འབེབ», 18 «ནོར་གཏོར»,
24 «དལ་ཡམས་འོང», 25 «མིག་ཚ», 27 «རྟག་ཏུ་དགེ», 30 «གཤིན་རྗེས་བཟུང». It confirms that
the list belongs to washing the hair, and the reading of the 4th. Its own
haircut chapter (21, «བཟོ་དང་སྐྲ་སེན་བྲེག་པའི་རྩིས») has no list by date, only
«ཚེས་གཉན་འཛེམ», avoid the *gnyan* dates.
Nothing is built from SN (SPEC §8.1).

## Built (2026-10-09)

- **The lunar date's sheet** carries a detail row, "Washing the hair",
  with WB's result for the date (`Texts.HAIR_DATE`, `reading.HairDate.<n>`
  in English and Russian), cited as WB p. 404 beside the date's readings.
- **The haircut sheet** keeps FPMT's day for the date, weighed as before,
  and says under "In the White Beryl" that WB has no haircut results by
  date and that its hair dates are for washing, with the lunar date.
- **KP box 37** (bathing and washing the hair): its good dates are WB's
  good dates exactly; its bad half's second line is worn
  ([kp-activities.md](kp-activities.md)), and the complement taken for it
  had put the 27th bad. Its bad dates are now WB's, the 27th on neither
  side (`ElectionalTest`, the vector the two verse lines above).

## Not built

- The rest of work 34, qualified by a kind of the work or by a moment
  (SPEC §5.10): the Sun good for bathing, bad for washing the hair;
  Wednesday good for a king's bath; Bharaṇī good for washing the hair at
  the end of widowhood («ཡུགས་སྐྲ་བཀྲུ་བ»), and the green four («བཞི་ལྗང»)
  bad for it; Śravaṇa (*gro bzhin*) good for shaving the head («སྐྲ་བཞར»):
  a kind of cutting, named here and not in work 50, so not added to the
  haircut; Kṛttikā on the 8th of the *mgo* month and Puṣya (*rgyal*) on
  the 25th of the Horse month for particular merits; the "eight white"
  («བརྒྱད་དཀར») good for bathing; the rising signs, the twelve links and the
  *li khen* dates.
