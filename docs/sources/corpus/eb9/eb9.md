## <a id="eb9-p1-378"></a>eb9:p1-378 The volume's contents, from the NDL record (the book not opened)

*Read: etext, 2026-11-09.*

> 毎年暦考見／付、〔天明八年伊勢暦〕／大雑書／夢占吉凶秘伝書（いろは引夢うらなひ）／東見記

The volume's contents as the National Diet Library's record gives them (R100000002-I029406484, from the publisher's data; the book itself not opened, so this is the order of the texts, not a page reading): "Each year's calendar examined" (毎年暦考見, by the retired Hōseiji priest Junkō), with an Ise almanac of Tenmei 8 (1788) appended; the 大雑書; the "Secret book of good and bad dream omens" (夢占吉凶秘伝書, with the second title "Dream divination by the iroha", いろは引夢うらなひ); and 東見記. Edited by the Kyoto University Department of Japanese Language and Literature, 臨川書店, December 2018, 378 pp., ISBN 978-4-653-04329-4; the series transcribes selected titles from the collection of Ebara Taizō, a scholar of early modern Japanese. The page numbers of each text are not given by the record.

## <a id="eb9-p2"></a>eb9:p2 RD256: the Ebara dream book is the 1801 墨屋 print of 新版ゆめあはせ; two online copies of its plates, unread

*Read: etext, 2026-11-09.*

> 巻末に「安永三午春新板/享和元□□新板/京松原通冨小路西入/墨屋吉兵衛板」とあり
> 題簽書名:新版ゆめあはせ 版心書名:ゆめ
> 安永3年刊の新板

Read 2026-11-09 (RD256) in a headed browser from CiNii Books (ncid BB15658308) and the 国書データベース (kokusho.nijl.ac.jp/biblio/200013909). CiNii: the title いろは引夢うらなひ (イロハ引 on the title slip as a heading line), other titles 夢占吉凶秘傳書, ゆめあわせ, 夢うらなひ; 墨屋吉兵衛, Kyoto, 享和1 (1801); the colophon says "newly cut Anei 3 (1774) spring / newly cut Kyōwa 1 / Matsubara-dōri, Tomi-no-kōji, Kyoto, 墨屋吉兵衛"; 4-12 leaves, 21 lines in three tiers (the middle tier pictures), the 4th leaf front picture carries the title 夢占吉凶秘傳書; held by Kyoto University, 潁原文庫 (call no. Pp 261029029): this is the book of EB9, so EB9’s dream book is the Anei 3 text and its second title is its running title. The 国書データベース lists the same work (著作ID 4188072, 新版ゆめあはせ, 占卜, 安永三刊) and a copy of its Kaei 5 (1852) printing by the same 墨屋吉兵衛, 8 leaves, 国文学研究資料館 (松野陽一文庫 54-254), whose 10 IIIF images are open to use without restriction (manifest kokusho.nijl.ac.jp/biblio/200013909/manifest); Waseda’s 古典籍総合データベース has another copy (bunko30_e0277, 嘉永5) but answered 503 (site maintenance) all through the reading. The 10 images were opened (frames 3 and 4 seen): an iroha-ordered book, each page headed by an iroha letter, short entries of the form "if this is seen, very good / bad" in three tiers with pictures; the hand is small cursive and the text was not read through, so what the book says of a date, a day of the cycle or a season is still unknown (q786 stands). The 大雑書 of EB9 is a separate text (the volume's second), not located online.

## <a id="eb9-i4"></a>eb9:i4 A season in an entry: a sight good, and in the autumn better

*Read: machine, 2026-11-09.*

> （神仏を…するを）見れば それは吉　又秋のころ見れば かつて（？）よろし

Image 4, right page, the top tier, in the entry run (under the head ま) whose subject is a Buddha or a rite of the gods (the subject word is cursive and not certain): "if this is seen, it is good; and if it is seen in the autumn, it is [altogether] good". The words 又秋のころ見れば are clear on the image (秋 plainly); 神仏… and かつてよろし are read by eye at doubt. One season mention in the whole book outside the table, attached to one entry.

## <a id="eb9-i9"></a>eb9:i9 夢人に咄さぬ日: the table of the days a dream is not told, one column to a month

*Read: machine, 2026-11-09.*

> 夢（を見て）人に咄さぬ日
> 正月　ひつじ さる／いぬ
> 二月　とり さる／み
> 三月　ひつじ むま／ゐ
> 四月　むま うし／う ゐ
> 五月　むま うし／う ゐ
> 六月　ね うし／ゐ
> 七月　ね うし／たつ
> 八月　とら たつ／ね ゐ
> 九月　とら たつ／ね ゐ
> 十月　たつ／み
> 十一月　さる ひつじ／いぬ
> 十二月　むま ひつじ／ゐ

Image 9, left page, the top tier: a table headed "days on which a dream is not told to people" (the title's second and third glyphs, 夢 and 人, are clear; the particle between them and the 見て I supply is cursive and doubtful), twelve columns, one for each month 正月 to 十二月, each holding day branches of the sexagenary cycle written in kana, three glyphs or glyph groups to a column and a slash between the groups, as in the 万代大雑書's item ゆ六 (mdz:p11). Read by eye on the 5616-px image against mdz:p11's cells: the month labels are clear; the branch glyphs are hentaigana in a heavy hand and each is matched to mdz's reading by shape (clear: 二月 酉 申 / 巳, 四月 = 五月, 六月 子 丑 / 亥, 七月 子 丑 / 辰, 八月 = 九月, 十月 辰 / 巳, 十二月 午 未 / 亥; the others agree in count and outline but are not independently decoded). The cells above are mdz's, which this table repeats; they are not decoded here glyph by glyph.

## <a id="eb9-i9b"></a>eb9:i9b The closing paragraph on a bad dream, and the colophon of 1852

*Read: machine, 2026-11-09.*

> 右の悪夢あらば（毎朝）早く起き 元水を以て悪夢を（祓ひ）…（夢占の）理をくるしむことなく…よく慎むべし

Image 9, left page, the lower tier, the book's last paragraph before the colophon (the cursive is read in part): "If there is the bad dream above [one of the ones listed as ill], rise early [?each morning], and with 元水 (the ruby is unclear; perhaps water) [wash away] the bad dream; ... the reasoning of the dream divination ... one should heed it well." It gives no day, month or season and no 初夢. Beside it, the colophon on the same leaf's picture panel: 安永三午春新板 / 嘉永五子春新板 / 京松原通冨小路西入 墨屋吉兵衛板 (matching RD256's reading of the 1801 colophon with the year changed).

## <a id="eb9-i9c"></a>eb9:i9c A dream shows within two or three days

*Read: machine, 2026-11-09.*

> （凶夢の）…人は二日…三日目にて…さとるべし

Image 9, right page, the lower tier, under the head す, the last entry of its column (the head words are cursive and doubtful): of a dream, "people [feel its sign] within two days, on the third day ... [one] knows". The figures 二日 and 三日目 are clear. A time to fulfilment, as in ymk:p63 ("within three days"), not a calendar rule.
