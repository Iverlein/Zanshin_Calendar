## <a id="ak-p268"></a>ak:p268 The Medicine Buddha rite: the short form of the mantra

*Read: scan, 2026-11-04.*

> བདག་མདུན་ཐུགས་ཀར་ཧཱུྃ་ལ་སྔགས་ཕྲེང་གིས་བསྐོར་བར་དམིགས་ལ༔ ཏདྱ་ཐཱ༔ ཨོཾ་བྷཻ་ཥ་ཛྱེ་བྷཻ་ཥ་ཛྱེ་མ་ཧཱ་བྷཻ་ཥ་ཛྱེ་རཱ་ཛ་ས་མུངྒ་ཏེ་སྭཱ་ཧཱ༔ ཞེས་ཅི་ནུས་བཟླས་ནས་མཇུག་ཏུ༔ སྡིག་ལྟུང་ཀུན་བཤགས་

Img. 272, l. 5, in the rite signed རཱ་ག་ཨ་སྱ (rA ga a sya, colophon on img. 273). At the heart of oneself and of the one in front, a ཧཱུྃ ringed by the mantra's garland; say the mantra as much as one can, and at the end confess all wrongs. The short form as the rite is recited: with ཏདྱ་ཐཱ, a single བྷཻ་ཥ་ཛྱེ after མ་ཧཱ, རཱ་ཛ without the long vowel on ཛ, no ཡ, and ས་མུངྒ་ཏེ as in the Four Tantras. The two ring marks after ཐཱ and ཧཱ are the text's own punctuation (༔), used all through the rite.
