Karma chags med's «རྨི་ལམ་ལུང་བསྟན་གྱི་བསྟབ་པ་ཚིགས་བཅད་འདོན་བདེ་བར་བཀོད་པ» (title on img. 281, printed p. 273, checked on the scan: "verses easy to recite, to make firm the dream prophecy"). The text begins with the refuge field (the root lama as Khrag 'thung / Mkha' spyod pa, the Wheel-Bound (Cakrasaṃvara) with dakinis, the four-armed Mahākāla with his retinue), then the offering of one's body as a feast («ལུས་ཤ་ཁྲག་ཚོགས་ཀྱི་མཆོད་པ»): the consciousness leaves by the central channel as a white-red drop, becomes Vajravārāhī, cuts off the skull with the curved knife, which fills with nectar, and the guests are fed in turn, each verse ending «ཕཊ་ཕཊ་ཕཊ»; it is a chod-like feast, not a dream method, and no dream sign is named in the verses. The colophon (img. 287) says: when many people came asking for an examination, a visualisation of this kind was said to extend the life of one fated to die by thirteen years, and a dream prophecy of that sort appeared, though dreams are not certain; the unwritten oral teaching of Mkha' spyod pa's four-armed (Mahākāla) feast matched it, and it was written as a memorandum, then as verses for recitation at the request of Mkha' spyod pa's line-holder Anu 'tsho in a long illness; set down on the second day of the waxing moon of the star month of the Dragon year. Signed «རཱ་ག་ཨ་སྱ», in retreat (img. 286). Read only by Yigdzin-1; no second reader.
