## <a id="skf-p1"></a>skf:p1 The poem on the first dream, headed 'composed on the morning of the beginning of spring'

*Read: etext, 2026-11-09.*

> 立つ春の朝よみける
> 年くれぬ春来べしとは思ひ寝にまさしく見えてかなふ初夢

Headnote: "composed on the morning of the beginning of spring (立春, risshun)". The poem: "The year has ended; that spring was to come I had thought as I slept, and it was seen clearly, the first dream fulfilled." Both Japanese Wikipedia (初夢) and the reading blog give the poem so, Wikipedia has 思ひ寝む where the blog has 思ひ寝に; the blog glosses 年くれぬ as the eve of 立春, the day of 節分. Wikipedia calls it the earliest written occurrence of 初夢 and says the dream here is that of the night from 節分 into 立春, whatever the calendar's new year. Two more etexts of the 山家集, read 2026-11-09 (RD224): an Ameba blog (ameblo.jp/namuko06, entry 11486920073) quotes the poem as 年くれぬ 春来（く）べしとは 思ひ寝に まさしく見えて かなふ初夢 under the headnote たつ春の朝（あした）よみける, from the 岩波書店 日本古典文学大系 *山家集 金槐和歌集* (13th printing, 16 October 1971), and counts it as the first poem of the spring book of its own series; the Utakura etext (itaka84.upper.jp/bookn/koka/yamagasyuu.htm, no edition named, historical kana) has 立春の朝よみける, 春くべしとは, 思ひ寐に, and the poem stands seventh in the spring book, after the poems on spring coming within the old year, the mountain water, 難波 and the 逢坂 haze. So the headnote 立つ春の朝 / 立春の朝 and the reading 思ひ寝に (never 思ひ寝む) are the witnesses' own; 思ひ寝む appears only in Wikipedia. The Shinpen Kokka Taikan number and the base text of the 大系 are not stated in any page reached; no etext reached gives a number for the poem. Text and numbering from the critical editions not seen: still etext only, not to be built on.

## <a id="skf-p3"></a>skf:p3 The poem in the 異本山家集 (1906), p. 96: headnote 'on the feeling of spring beginning', third line おもはねど, 'おもひねに' in a note

*Read: machine, 2026-11-10.*

> 新古
> たち入らで雲まを分し月影は、またぬけしきや空に見ねけん。
> 春たつこゝろを。
> 年くれぬ、春くべしとはおもはねど、まさしくみえてかなふはつ夢。
> （傍注：おもひねに）
> 六花
> とけそむるはつ若水の氷にて、春たつことのまづくまれぬる。
> （傍注：くまれぬるかな）

Shin-kokin [marker: the poem before is also in the Shinkokinshū]
"Without stepping in, the moon that parted the clouds: did it see, in the sky, a look of one not waited for?" (the reply to Horikawa)
Headnote: "On the feeling of spring beginning."
"The year has ended; that spring was to come I did not think, yet it was seen clearly, and the first dream is fulfilled."
(Small note beside the third line: おもひねに, the reading of the common text.)
Rokka [marker: also in the Rokkashū]
"Melting at the start, the first young water, with its ice: the drawing of spring's beginning is the first thing drawn."
(Small note: くまれぬるかな.)

Read by eye on the NDL image (pid 872857, image 61 of 189, the right-hand page, printed p. 96; open) of 藤岡作太郎's 異本山家集 (本郷書院, 1906), a print of an old manuscript, not the common text of the 山家集: its preface (田中義成, May 1906; read in the NDL's OCR text, not on the image) says the manuscript is some three hundred years old, holds fewer poems than the printed 山家集 but 166 that the printed book lacks, and bears the colophons of Ton'a and Shūshi; the book's editorial notes (例言, OCR pages 10-11, not read on the image) say a circle before a poem marks one absent from the common text (this poem has none, so the common text has it too), a shoulder word such as 新古 or 六花 says the poem is also in that anthology (here 新古 stands above the reply poem before the headnote and 六花 above the poem after it; the poem itself has no marker), and a bracketed small note gives the common text's variant. So this old text reads おもはねど ("though I did not think") in the third line where the common text reads 思ひねに ("as I slept"); the headnote differs too: 春たつこゝろを ("on the feeling of spring beginning") for the common text's 立春の朝よみける. Its place is not the first of the spring book: it follows the exchange with 待賢門院堀河 (the poem 'about the moon that did not stop at the gate'), as the 1930 reprint (skf:p4) has it. The readings of the two prints were checked on both page images; the OCR of the NDL agrees up to its own misreadings.

## <a id="skf-p5"></a>skf:p5 The poem first of the spring book in the 日本歌学全書 vol. 8 (1891): headnote 立春の朝よみける, おもひねに

*Read: machine, 2026-11-10.*

> 山家集上
> 春
> 立春の朝よみける
> 年くれぬ春來べしとハ思ひねにまさしく見えてかなふ初夢

Read by eye on the NDL image (pid 993408, image 120 of 275, the first leaf front of the 山家集上, printed leaf "一"; open, the NDL's mark PDM), in 佐々木弘綱 and 信綱's annotated edition, 日本歌学全書 vol. 8 (博文館, 1891; the volume also holds the 林下集, the 源三位頼政集 and the 金槐和歌集). "Sankashū, upper book. Spring. Headnote: 'Composed on the morning of the beginning of spring.' The poem: 'The year has ended; that spring was to come I had thought as I slept, and it was seen clearly, the first dream fulfilled.'" The ハ of とハ and the ぬ of くれぬ were checked on the enlarged crop (the ハ is the same katakana-shaped は as in the margin notes). It is the first poem of the book, the headnote is the 流布本's 立春の朝, the phrase 思ひねに stands as the poem's third line, and no variant is noted. The upper margin carries short notes, read in part: one on まさしく (a sense of "for sure"), one on 音羽山 (a mountain of 山城), one on くまれぬるかな (a word of the young-water poem two poems on); the notes were not read through. The NDL's OCR gives the same text (年くれぬ春來べしとい思ひねにまさしく見えてかなふ初夢).
