## <a id="skf-p1"></a>skf:p1 The poem on the first dream, headed 'composed on the morning of the beginning of spring'

*Read: etext, 2026-11-09.*

> 立つ春の朝よみける
> 年くれぬ春来べしとは思ひ寝にまさしく見えてかなふ初夢

Headnote: "composed on the morning of the beginning of spring (立春, risshun)". The poem: "The year has ended; that spring was to come I had thought as I slept, and it was seen clearly, the first dream fulfilled." Both Japanese Wikipedia (初夢) and the reading blog give the poem so, Wikipedia has 思ひ寝む where the blog has 思ひ寝に; the blog glosses 年くれぬ as the eve of 立春, the day of 節分. Wikipedia calls it the earliest written occurrence of 初夢 and says the dream here is that of the night from 節分 into 立春, whatever the calendar's new year. Text and numbering from the critical editions not seen: the variant 思ひ寝に / 思ひ寝む is open.
