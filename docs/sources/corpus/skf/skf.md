## <a id="skf-p1"></a>skf:p1 The poem on the first dream, headed 'composed on the morning of the beginning of spring'

*Read: etext, 2026-11-09.*

> 立つ春の朝よみける
> 年くれぬ春来べしとは思ひ寝にまさしく見えてかなふ初夢

Headnote: "composed on the morning of the beginning of spring (立春, risshun)". The poem: "The year has ended; that spring was to come I had thought as I slept, and it was seen clearly, the first dream fulfilled." Both Japanese Wikipedia (初夢) and the reading blog give the poem so, Wikipedia has 思ひ寝む where the blog has 思ひ寝に; the blog glosses 年くれぬ as the eve of 立春, the day of 節分. Wikipedia calls it the earliest written occurrence of 初夢 and says the dream here is that of the night from 節分 into 立春, whatever the calendar's new year. Two more etexts of the 山家集, read 2026-11-09 (RD224): an Ameba blog (ameblo.jp/namuko06, entry 11486920073) quotes the poem as 年くれぬ 春来（く）べしとは 思ひ寝に まさしく見えて かなふ初夢 under the headnote たつ春の朝（あした）よみける, from the 岩波書店 日本古典文学大系 *山家集 金槐和歌集* (13th printing, 16 October 1971), and counts it as the first poem of the spring book of its own series; the Utakura etext (itaka84.upper.jp/bookn/koka/yamagasyuu.htm, no edition named, historical kana) has 立春の朝よみける, 春くべしとは, 思ひ寐に, and the poem stands seventh in the spring book, after the poems on spring coming within the old year, the mountain water, 難波 and the 逢坂 haze. So the headnote 立つ春の朝 / 立春の朝 and the reading 思ひ寝に (never 思ひ寝む) are the witnesses' own; 思ひ寝む appears only in Wikipedia. The Shinpen Kokka Taikan number and the base text of the 大系 are not stated in any page reached; no etext reached gives a number for the poem. Text and numbering from the critical editions not seen: still etext only, not to be built on.

## <a id="skf-p2"></a>skf:p2 The poem's place in the 和歌データベース: no. 00001 of the spring book

*Read: etext, 2026-11-09.*

> たつはるのあしたよみける
> としくれぬ春くへしとはおもひねにまさしく見えてかなふ初夢

Read 2026-11-09 (RD256) in a headed browser: the 日文研 和歌データベース (lapis.nichibun.ac.jp/waka/waka_i133.html), 山家集＿西行, 巻上：春, entry 00001, in the 六家集 print (開版 about 慶安, 1648-1652): headnote "composed on the morning the spring began" (たつはるのあしたよみける), fourth line おもひねに (never 思ひ寝む), fifth line かなふ初夢; entry 00002 and 00003 carry the same headnote. The database numbers its entries in running order, so this is the first poem of the book by that print (as the Ameba blog counts it in the 大系), where the Utakura etext has it seventh. A second page (575.jpn.org, 年くれぬ) says 巻上（1首目）. The 新編国歌大観 number is still not seen: the database keeps its own running number, not that of an edition of 山家集, and no page reached gives the 国歌大観 number.
