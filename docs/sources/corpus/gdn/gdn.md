> (img. 253, the dawn third) ཐོ་རངས་༤་ཡི་ནཾ་གྱི་༣་ཆ་ལ༴ བཟངན་འབྲསུ་འབྱུང་ཕྱིར་བརྟག་པ་གཅེས༴
> (img. 254, the bad signs) གསྱེཾད་ཐྱིཾ་མང་དུ་ལོངསྤྱོདང༴ རྟ་བེང་རྒ་མེད་ཞེན་དང་གཅེར་བུ་སའོ༴ སྲང་བསཾ་ལས་ལ་བཤེལའི་ཕྱི་ཐལ་ཡིན༴

The text of the Mongolian National Library volume W1NLM9044, part 30 (BDRC MW1NLM9044_O1NLM9044_030, 6 folios, images 252-263; BDRC's part 9, MW1NLM9044_O1NLM9044_009, images 153-164, is a rite for the king spirit and not this text). A running hand, in places with a numeral for a syllable and with small notes between the lines. The title leaf reads "the method of examining the various dream signs, here it stands". The text begins as RST and the other manuscripts do: with the head I rely on the lama; as taught in the Mother's profound Chöd visualisation collection, the sorting of the fruits of night dreams; not to be examined when ill, in grief or with the mind disturbed. Then the night is divided into thirds: the dusk third is the earlier imprint, the middle one the display of demons and flesh-eaters, the dawn one what makes clear what comes later. The machine reads the number three as a numeral (༣) at «ཐུན་གྱི་༣་མ་བགོས», at the middle and the clarifying thirds, and at the dawn line «ཐོ་རངས་༤་ཡི་ནམ་གྱི་༣་ཆ་ལ»; only the dusk third is spelled «སུམ་ཆ» («སུཾ་ཆ»). So the manuscript writes the word by a numeral and the choice between «སུམ་ཆར»/«གསུམ་ཆ» and between «དུས་ཀྱི» and «ཁ་ཡིན» is not shown by this copy: the syllable before «ཡི/ཀྱི» in the dawn line is one the machine takes as a numeral. By eye (img. 253 line 2, enlarged 3x and 5x): the line is as the machine has it, with a short syllable before «བགོས» which stands as a numeral or a contraction and is not the full «སུམ་ཆར»; not settled. The unsaddled donkey: «རྟ་བོང་རྒ་མེད་ཞོན» in the machine reading, twice (img. 254 line 2; the same spelling in the machine's second pass); on the scan (img. 254 line 2, enlarged 5x) the first letter of the word after «བོང» is a single ga-like form in a running hand, neither a ra nor a sa head plain: not settled.

The colophons of the copy in W1NLM9044 (part 30, img. 262), machine reading, the shapes by eye (img. 262 line 1 at 0.59 of its length, enlarged 3x and 5x; line 7 at 0.82, enlarged 3x and 5x). After the verses on the signs of women, on the first line: "this too was written by Klong rdol bla ma Ngag dbang ..." (the machine's «ཀློང་དེས» for the closing of the name is unsettled: on the scan the line ends «… བླ་མ་ཀློང་/ངག་དབང་ … བྲིས་པའོ», the same kind of colophon as RST's «ཀློང་རྡོལ་བླ་མ་ངག་དབང་བློ་བཟང་གིས་བྲིས་པའོ»). So this copy of the text carries Klong rdol bla ma's name where RST does, and the Dga' ldan Byang rtse manuscript, whose last leaf is lost, cannot show it. The second sentence, on the seventh line, follows the verses on the marks of men (a block on people by the year of birth comes after it) and is a second writer's: «ཅེས་པ་འདི་སྔ་སུམ་པ་མཁན་པོས་བྲིས་པའོ», "this was written by the abbot of ... (the machine's «སྔ་སུཾ་པ», not settled: «སུམ་པ མཁན་པོ», Sum pa mkhan po, would be Sum pa mkhan po Ye shes dpal 'byor, 1704-1788, but the name is not read on the scan; the three last syllables «མཁན་པོས་བྲིས་པའོ» are plain)". No date. The page after (img. 263) is empty. The copy and RST both give Klong rdol bla ma as writer; the copy is undated and BDRC gives no date for the volume.
