## <a id="kp-i14"></a>kp:i14 The weighing's last sentence: the earth lords met by screening and remedy

*Read: scan, 2026-10-26.*

> …ཡིན་ལ། གཟའ་སྐར་བྱེད་པ་སྦྱོར་བ་བཞིའི་ལས་ཀྱང་དུས་སྦྱོར་དེའི་ཁྱིམ་ལ་ཚང་བའི་ལྷག་པར་དུ་དུས་སྦྱོར་གཙོ་ཆེར་བཟུང་དགོས་པའི་ཁྱད་པར་ཤིན་ཏུ་ཆེ་ཞིང་། ས་བདག་སོགས་དང་ངན་པའི་རིགས་ལ་སྤྱི་བཅོས་ཀྱིས་མཚོན་རྒྱས་བསྡུས་སྒྲིབ་བཅོས་ཡོད་པ་རྣམས་ཀྱང་སྐབས་ཐོབ་སྦྱར་ཤེས་པ་དགོས། གཟའ་སྐར་ཕྲད་འབྲས་ངན་ན་གཟའ་སྐར་དེ་གཉིས་ཀྱི་སྒྲིབ་བཅོས་ཀྱང་འགྲོ་བདེ་གཉིས་ཀྱི་འབྲས་བུ་ལས་ལོག་འགྱུར་མེད་ཅིང་། …

KP's prose on the weight of the factors, the sentence that closes it: img. 14 (this pool's numbering, the file's; BDRC's label 16), l. 1 to the middle of l. 3, read by eye on the scan at 2× in thirds of the folio, against Yigdzin-1 (run over BDRC labels 9–20 for this reading), 2026-10-26. The weighing itself, which ends at «…ཤིན་ཏུ་ཆེ་ཞིང་།», is quoted in weighing.md, *The text*; the sentence does not stop there. Corrections of the machine reading: «ཀྱིས» (Yigdzin-1 གྱིས), «ངན་ན» (དན་ན). The Bhutan reset reprint of KP (*Collected Astrological and Prognosticatory Texts from Bhutan*, etext VEIE0OPI1829B6AF_I1KG11905, p. 362) has the same words.

"… and since the works of the four, planet, mansion, karaṇa and yoga, are complete in the house of the combination period, the combination period above all must be held as the chief, and the difference this makes is very great. For the earth lords and the other bad kinds, too, one must know how to apply, as the occasion comes, the screenings and remedies (སྒྲིབ་བཅོས, sgrib bcos) that exist in full and in short, the general remedy standing for them. Where the meeting of planet and mansion bears a bad result, the screening and remedy of those two also …"

What it shows: KP, having stated the weighing of the seven and the combination period, does not put the earth lords into it; it turns to them in the next clause as something met with screening and remedy, applied as the occasion comes. The rest of the clause, «…ཀྱང་འགྲོ་བདེ་གཉིས་ཀྱི་འབྲས་བུ་ལས་ལོག་འགྱུར་མེད་ཅིང་།», is not clear here and is left untranslated. KP gives the remedies themselves in its §18 (img. 105–118: those for the month's earth lords, the planets, the mansions, the date, the trigram and mewa, the sky doors and the earth lords in general), after its charts of the year's, month's, day's and hour's earth lords (§13–17).

The Rat panel of KP's year chart, §13, read cell by cell on the scan (BDRC I4CZ65599 label 89, pool img. 87) at 3.1x in four strips of the page, 4x for the right column and the bottom row. The panel is a ring of twelve cells round the drawing of the animal: four across the top (T1 to T4, left to right), two down each side column (L1, L2 on the left from the top; R1, R2 on the right), four along the bottom (B1 to B4 from the left). Double vertical rules, as wide as two strokes, stand between the panels of a page and at no other place; the cells of a panel are parted by single rules. A cell holds one to five names, a name to a line, set at the head of the cell, and the cell itself is the unit: no name stands "at a side" of its cell other than by the cell's place in the ring. The panel holds 11 cells with names and one empty (B1).

T1: the heading «§13» (the cell's second word is not resolved). T2: Hal-khyi's girl (ཧལ་ཁྱིའི་བུ་མོ, hal khyi'i bu mo), the son-in-law (མག་པ, mag pa), Tsang-kun the dog-headed (ཁྱི་མགོ, khyi mgo). The machine's reading of the cells is rough (stray «X» and «+» marks, repeated words), so only these pairs are quoted in English; the rest is not translated.

§18's end and the book's last lines (BDRC labels 119 and the first three lines of 120; machine reading, gist only). The general door's last couplets: for a person lacking a limb, the poison water of the nine bad things is given, and the *snang brgyad* and the Mañjuśrī rite are read. **The general remedy of the earth lords** (ས་བདག་སྤྱི་ཡི་སྒྲིབ་བཅོས): the three supports of the blessed (རྟེན་གསུམ་བྱིན་ཅན) with the *snang brgyad*; a tortoise shell and a peacock's wing-feather; a mirror, a tiger-skin and a figure; a weapon's knot, the nine eyes; the five-coloured banner, a yak's head set at the side of the *'gron bu* and the like, or held by the one to be guarded, or an image of the earth lord like itself adorned with a jewel: «there is no doubt that it screens». The last lines: «for the earth lords mostly harmed, the *tog gzungs*, the *snang brgyad*, the fasting and the *mdo thig* (the thread-scripture) recited, the *dong tso*, and the *ya 'khrug* sent away, the water thrown and the *sātsa* pressed — it is said that every aim is thereby accomplished.» Then the colophon begins on the same image (see [kun-phan-me-long.md](../../kun-phan-me-long.md), *The colophon*).
