## <a id="agp-p280-281"></a>agp:p280-281 Agnipurāṇa, the Svapnādhyāya: the watches and the spans (vv. 16–19)

*Read: scan, 2026-11-09 (RD269), verses 9–19 syllable by syllable (p. 282); the summary of the other verses below: machine, 2026-11-09 (RD265), seen on the images for its sense.*

> (*भूयश्च स्वपनं तद्वत्कार्यं स्नानं द्विजार्चनम् । तिलैर्होमो हरिब्रह्मशिवार्कगणपूजनम् ॥ १५
> तथा स्तुतिप्रपठनं पुंसूक्तादिजपस्तथा । स्वप्नास्तु प्रथमे यामे संवत्सरविपाकिनः ॥ १६
> षड्भिर्मासैर्द्वितीये तु त्रिभिर्मासैस्त्रियामिकाः । चतुर्थे त्वर्धमासेन दशाहादरुणोदये ॥ १७
> एकस्यामथ चेद्रात्रौ शुभं वा यदि वाऽशुभम् ।) पश्चाद्दृष्टस्तु यस्तत्र तस्य पाकं विनिर्दिशेत् ॥ १८
> तस्मात्तु शोभने स्वप्ने पश्चात्स्वापो न शस्यते ॥ १९

*Agnipurāṇa*, the *Svapnādhyāya*, ch. 229 of the Ānandāśrama print (Ānandāśrama Sanskrit Series 41, Poona 1900; Internet Archive item SP4182AgniPuranaAnandAshram411900AnandAshramSeries), headed "svapna-śubhāśubha-duḥsvapna-haraṇa-kathanam", 31 verses, **pp. 281–282, not pp. 280–281**: p. 280 (image n307) is ch. 227's end (*daṇḍapraṇayana*); ch. 228 (*yuddhayātrā*, 8 verses) and the beginning of ch. 229 are on p. 281 (n308: the chapter's first line "svapnaṃ śubhāśubhaṃ vakṣye duḥsvapnaharaṇaṃ tathā", spoken by Puṣkara), vv. 9–31 and the colophon "ityādimahāpurāṇa āgneye svapnaśubhāśubhaduḥsvapnaharaṇakathanaṃ nāmaikonatriṃśadadhikadviśatatamo 'dhyāyaḥ ‖ 229 ‖ āditaḥ ślokānāṃ samaṣṭyaṅkāḥ — 6471" on p. 282 (n309), after which ch. 230 (*Śakunāni*) begins. Read on the images by RD269. **The lines above stand in a bracket**: the print opens a round bracket marked with an asterisk before v. 15 and closes it after the first half-verse of v. 18 ("…śubhaṃ vā yadi vā 'śubham ‖ ) paścād dṛṣṭas tu…"), and the footnote of p. 282 says "granthoʼyaṃ dhanuścihnāntargataḥ ka. ṅa. pustakayor nāsti": the passage between the brackets is absent from the editor's manuscripts ka and ṅa. So the spans of v. 16–17 are in a stretch that two of the manuscripts lack; the verses 18b–19 (the later dream decides) follow without a gap. The numerals are the print's: 15 to 19 as above.

In our words: the first 13 verses are the bad dreams (the same list as mtp:p453-455: a plant growing from the body, shaving, nakedness, falling from a height, riding a boar, ass or camel, the mother's womb, the sun falling, anger of gods and brahmans), then "not to tell them is good", sleeping again, a bath, burnt offerings of sesame, worship of Viṣṇu, Brahmā, Śiva, the sun and Gaṇapati, praise and the recitation of the *Puruṣasūkta* (14–16). Then the times: the first watch of the night, a year; the second, six months; "those of the three-watch" (*triyāmikāḥ*, the third) three months; the fourth, half a month; at the dawn (*aruṇodaya*), ten days. If a good and a bad dream come in one night, the later decides, so after a good dream there is no more sleep (18–19). The rest (20–32) is the good dreams.

Against the *Matsya* the only difference is the fourth watch: half a month here, a month there. A copy of the Mitra text reads "prathame bhāge" for "prathame yāme" (one manuscript, his *kha*).

No tie to a lunar day, a weekday, a nakṣatra, a month or a season.

Doubt: the chapter was looked through on the images for the sense and verses 9–19 read syllable by syllable; the number of verses is 31 by the print, not 32; the bracket's meaning (a stretch missing in two manuscripts) is the print's, not studied further.

## <a id="agp-p282"></a>agp:p282 Agnipurāṇa, the Svapnādhyāya: the good dreams, vv. 22–31 (blood, excrement, agamyāgamana)

*Read: scan, 2026-11-14 (inv-k), the ten verses syllable by syllable on image n309 of the item named above (2500 px wide, the verses 22–31 in the lower half).*

> चन्द्रार्कताराग्रहणं परिमार्जनमेव च । शक्रध्वजालिङ्गनं च ध्वजोच्छ्रायक्रिया तथा ॥ २२
> भूम्यम्बुधाराग्रहणं शत्रूणां चैव विक्रिया । जयो विवादे द्यूते च सङ्ग्रामे च तथा द्विज ॥ २३
> भक्षणं चार्द्रमांसानां पायसस्य च भक्षणम् । दर्शनं रुधिरस्यापि स्नानं वा रुधिरेण च ॥ २४
> सुरारुधिरमद्यानां पानं क्षीरस्य वाप्यथ । अस्त्रैर्विचेष्टनं भूमौ निर्मलं गगनं तथा ॥ २५
> मुखेन दोहनं शस्तं महिषीणां तथा गवाम् । सिंहीनां हस्तिनीनां च बडवानां तथैव च ॥ २६
> प्रसादो देवविप्रेभ्यो गुरुभ्यश्च तथा द्विज । अम्भसा चाभिषेकस्तु गवां शृङ्गच्युतेन च ॥ २७
> चन्द्राद्भ्रष्टेन वा राम ज्ञेयं राज्यप्रदं हि तत् । राज्याभिषेकश्च तथा छेदनं शिरसोऽप्यथ ॥ २८
> मरणं वह्निलाभश्च वह्निदाहो गृहादिषु । लब्धिश्च राजलिङ्गानां तन्त्रीवाद्याभिवादनम् ॥ २९
> यस्तु पश्यति स्वप्नान्ते राजानं कुञ्जरं हयम् । हिरण्यं वृषभं गां च कुटुम्बस्तस्य वर्धते ॥ ३०
> वृषेभगृहशैलाग्रवृक्षारोहणरोदनम् । घृतविष्ठानुलेपो वा अगम्यागमनं तथा ॥ ३१

Verses 22–31 of the Agnipurāṇa's Svapnādhyāya (ch. 229 of the Ānandāśrama print), the good dreams, read on the page image n309 (p. 282, lines 22–31 of the chapter's verses, the print's numerals): the sun, moon, stars or planets seized, wiping; embracing Indra's banner, raising a banner; seizing the earth, water or a stream; the enemies' discomfiture; victory in dispute, dice and battle (22–23). Eating raw meat, eating milk-rice; seeing blood or bathing in blood; drinking spirits, blood, wine or milk; struggling on the ground with weapons; a clear sky (24–25). Milking buffaloes, cows, lionesses, elephant-cows and mares by the mouth; the favour of gods, brahmans and teachers; consecration with water or with water fallen from a cow's horn or from the moon (26–28): "that is to be known as giving kingship". Royal consecration; one's own head cut off; death; getting fire; one's house burning; getting the royal insignia; playing the lute (28–29). "Whoever sees at the end of a dream a king, an elephant, a horse, gold, a bull and a cow: his family grows" (30). Verse 31: "climbing a bull, an elephant, a house, the peak of a mountain, a tree, and weeping; smearing with ghee or excrement; and agamyāgamana (going to what is not to be gone to, intercourse with a woman forbidden)". With v. 31 the good dreams end and ch. 230 (Śakunāni) begins. Verses 20–21 are above the crop and were not read here.
