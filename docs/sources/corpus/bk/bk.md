## <a id="bk-1-p613"></a>bk.1:p613 The mantra of Vajrapāṇi with ཕཊ, in an empowerment

*Read: scan, 2026-11-04.*

> སྙིང་པོ་ཕྱག་ན་རྡོ་རྗེའི་གང་བསྡུ་བ༔ སྐལ་ལྡན་ལུས་དབང་སྨིན་པའི་ནུས་རྫོགས་ཤོག༔ ཨོཾ་བཛྲ་པཱ་ཎི་ཧཱུྃ་ཕཊ༔ ཀཱ་ཡ་ཨ་བྷི་ཥིཉྩ་ཨོཾ༔

Vol. 1, img. 616, ll. 2–3. Gathered by the heart, Vajrapāṇi: may the fortunate be given the power that ripens the body. Then the mantra, with the empowerment's formula after it. The form with ཕཊ, against Mipham's without it (lg:i42). ཕཊ is plain: ཕ and ཊ with no halanta, the long stroke below ཊ being the letter's own tail, and the mark after it is the text's punctuation (༔).
