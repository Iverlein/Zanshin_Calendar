## <a id="mdz-p5"></a>mdz:p5 The dream judgments (夢はんじ), 132 articles: the preface and the first articles

*Read: scan, 2026-11-09 (the page image of the transcription; the pool tool accepts the state scan only for Tibetan script).*

> ゆ二　夢はんじ画抄同辯
> 夢の説いろ〳〵ありといへども古へ周礼に占夢の官を置れたり。既に漢の丁公ハ松をミて三公の位にすゝミ、唐の李白ハ筆頭に花咲と見て古今独歩の文人となりしも皆夢のしるしにたがはず。○唐の段成式が酉陽雑俎に悪夢をさけんとならバ主夜神の呪を書て寝所の上に張置てあしき夢を除くべしといへり。則主夜神の呪ハ「婆珊婆演帝」如此書てはり置べし。
> 古人のとける夢の占ひを左にしるす。これ借身の一術なりと知べし。
> ゆめはんじ百三十二ケ条
> ○天にのぼるとミれば大にたつとき身となるべし
> ○そらはるゝと見れハ大によし
> ○そらあかくなると見れバよし
> ○夜あくるとミれバやまひほんぶくすべし
> ○月日おつると見れハちゝはゝをうしなふ
> ○月日をのむとミれバたつとき子をまうく
> ○かミなりにうたるゝとミれハ大によし
> ○くも四方におこるとミれハあきなひごとよし
> ○ゆきふると見れハよろこびあり
> ○かミなりをきくとミれバりつしんする也
> ○しものふるを見れバあし
> ○あめにあふとミれバ酒しよくにあふべし

Heading: "Item yu-2 (ゆ二), the dream judgments, with pictures, abridged and with distinctions." The preface says that opinions on dreams differ, that the old Rites of Zhou set an office of dream-diviners, that Ding Gong of Han, who saw a pine and rose to the highest of the three offices, and Li Bai of Tang, who saw a flower bloom at the tip of his brush and became a peerless writer, both bore out their dreams, and that whoever wishes to avoid bad dreams should write the charm of the Night-Ruling God (主夜神, shuyashin) and fix it over the sleeping place; the charm is given in five characters, 婆珊婆演帝. Then: "the dream readings of the ancients are set down below"; the heading "dream judgments, 132 articles", and the articles themselves, each a plain "if one sees X, Y": climbing to the sky, one will become very noble; the sky clearing, very good; the sky turning red, good; the night dawning, the sick will recover; sun and moon falling, one loses father and mother; swallowing sun and moon, a noble son; struck by lightning, very good; clouds rising on all sides, good for trade; falling snow, joy; hearing thunder, success in the world; frost falling, bad; meeting rain, one will meet wine and food. Only the first twelve articles are quoted here; they name no date, no day of the cycle and no season.

## <a id="mdz-p11"></a>mdz:p11 The days on which a dream seen is not told (夢を見て語らざる日), by month

*Read: scan, 2026-11-09 (the page image of the transcription; the pool tool accepts the state scan only for Tibetan script).*

> ゆ六　夢を見て語らざる日
> 正【ひつじ　さる／いぬ】　二【とり　さる／ミ】
> 三【ひつじ　むま／ゐ】　四【むま　うし／う　ゐ】
> 五【むま　うし／う　ゐ】　六【ね　うし／ゐ】
> 七【ね　うし／たつ】　八【とら　たつ／ね　ゐ】
> 九【とら　たつ／ね　ゐ】　十【たつ／ミ】
> 十一【さる　ひつじ／いぬ】　十二【むま　ひつじ／ゐ】
> 右の日の夢ハ善悪ともに語らず

Heading: "Item yu-6 (ゆ六), the days on which a dream seen is not told." A table of twelve cells, one for each month (正 the first month to 十二), each holding day branches of the sexagenary cycle written in kana, with a slash inside the brackets: month 1 ひつじ さる／いぬ (goat, monkey / dog); 2 とり さる／ミ (rooster, monkey / snake); 3 ひつじ むま／ゐ (goat, horse / boar); 4 むま うし／う ゐ (horse, ox / hare, boar); 5 the same as 4; 6 ね うし／ゐ (rat, ox / boar); 7 ね うし／たつ (rat, ox / dragon); 8 とら たつ／ね ゐ (tiger, dragon / rat, boar); 9 the same as 8; 10 たつ／ミ (dragon / snake); 11 さる ひつじ／いぬ (monkey, goat / dog); 12 むま ひつじ／ゐ (horse, goat / boar). Closing line: "of the dreams on the days above, neither good nor bad is told." The page does not say what the slash divides (two groups of day branches, or days and something else); the heading and the closing line settle only that a dream seen on a listed day is not spoken of. The reading of each cell was checked on the page image (p. <11>, the transcription's own numbering).
