(After the colophon of the eighth section, on the fierce mantras:) Again Great Black One was asked: "Please show the signs of the approach and accomplishment: a black man, a black dog, a raven, the vajra master (ācārya), a mantra-holder with a black man and a black horse, tiger, leopard, bear, dhole (or lynx), the sound of thunder, black cloud and light, lightning near: these are the signs of nearness. Deer, a hide(?), flesh and blood given; heart and lungs, entrails released; the heart of a hare(?) given; keeping company with those who do the killing work; when one makes war and a great army, by this one goes swiftly to the enemy." (Colophon, the first words garbled in the print or by the machine, "from the Sha za brtsegs pa'i rgyud:") the chapter of the dream signs, the ninth section. The lines are verse in seven-syllable lines; no line says "in a dream"; the images are listed as signs of nearness (nye ba'i mtshan ma) of the accomplishment of a wrathful, killing work.

Sha za brtsegs pa'i rgyud (Sha za, "Flesh-eater heap" tantra), Peking-type volume I1KG3558 img. 463 (printed p. 449), lines 1-5 (machine text of Yigdzin-1, checked against the whole-page image at 1x and the colophon at 3x by eye; lines 1-3 as in the earlier passage szb:i463, which this one completes). After the eighth section's colophon ("the chapter of the fierce mantras, the eighth section") Great Black One (Nag po chen po) is asked: "Show the signs of the approach and accomplishment: a black man, a black dog, a raven, the vajra master (ācārya), a mantra-holder with a black man and a black horse, tiger, leopard, bear, dhole, the sound of thunder, black cloud and light: lightning nearby, these are the signs of nearness. [Second group:] deer, a «bshor», flesh and blood given; heart, lungs and entrails released; the heart given; keeping company with those who do the killing work; when one makes war and a great army, by this one goes swiftly to the enemy." Colophon (the tantra's name in small letters, then): "from the Sha za brtsegs pa'i rgyud, the chapter of the dream signs (rmi ltas kyi le'u), the ninth section." The tenth section follows at once: again the Great Black One is asked about the fire offering: a fierce hearth one cubit square, three hands high, thorny firewood, poison-tree wood, black homa imagined as the Great Black One, the twenty-one burnt; colophon "the chapter of the fire offering, the tenth section".
