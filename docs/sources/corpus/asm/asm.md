## <a id="asm-p179-180"></a>asm:p179-180 Ch. 17 v. 11 of the Aśvamedhīyayātrā and Bhaṭṭotpala's gloss: the humours by the part of the night and the season

*Read: scan, 2026-11-10 (RD312), leaves n106 and n107 of the Internet Archive item at full size and in crops of 2×; the commentary line by line.*

> Aśvamedhīyayātrā, ch. 17 (Svapnādhyāya), v. 11 (printed p. 179, right-hand page of the spread, leaf n106):
>
> अधुना कालेन धातुप्रकोपमाह—
> जघन्यमध्यप्रथमे निशांशे
> प्रावृट्शरे माधवसंज्ञिते च ।
> काले मरुत्पित्तकफप्रकोपः
> साधारणः स्यात्खलु सन्निपातः ॥ ११ ॥
>
> Bhaṭṭotpala's Vivaraṇa on it (printed p. 180, left-hand page of the next spread, leaf n107, lines 1–11):
>
> निशा रात्रिः तस्याः, जघन्यांशेऽन्त्ये त्रिभागे मरुत्प्रकोपो वायोः प्रकोपो भवति । मध्ये रात्रित्रिभागे पित्तस्य प्रकोपः, प्रथमे त्रिभागे कफस्य श्लेष्मणः प्रकोपः । अथ प्रावृट्शरमाधवसंज्ञिते च काले यथाक्रमं मरुत्पित्तकफप्रकोपः, प्रावृषि वर्षा रात्रे मरुत्प्रकोपः, शरदि पित्तप्रकोपः, माधवसंज्ञिते वसन्ते श्लेष्मप्रकोपः, अन्यर्तुषु सञ्चयं गच्छति । स्वस्व काले च ते प्रकोपं यान्ति कुपिताश्चेल्लक्षणं प्रकटीकुर्वन्ति अतिशयेन, यथा स्वभावितोऽनिलात्मा जघन्ये रात्रित्रिभागे प्रावृषि अतिशयेन वातचिह्नानि पश्यति । एवं पित्तान्मध्ये रात्रित्रिभागेऽतिशयेन पित्तचिह्नानि पश्यति । तथा साधारणः स्यात्खलु सन्निपातः, सन्निपातः सर्वेषां वातपित्तश्लेष्माणां प्रकोपः । तत्र साधारणः सर्वचिह्नमयः स्वप्नः स्यात् । यथाऽनिलात्मकस्य विषये न वातप्रकोपः कालवशाद्रात्रिमध्यभागे पित्तप्रकोपः, तथा पित्तात्मकस्यातिशयेन पित्तप्रकोपः । कालवशादन्ते रात्रित्रिभागे वातप्रकोपः । कालवशात्तु वसन्ते श्लेष्मप्रकोपः । तथा श्लेष्मात्मकस्यातिशयेन श्लेष्मप्रकोपः । कालवशाच्छरदि पित्तप्रकोपः । एवं स्वभावप्रकोपातिशयात्सर्वेषां चिह्नानि पश्यति । मिश्रो मिश्रं स्वप्नं पश्यतीत्यर्थः ॥ ११ ॥

Verse 11, "Now he says the aggravation of the humours by time": "In the last, the middle and the first part of the night, and in the time called the rains, autumn and Mādhava (spring): the aggravation of wind, bile and phlegm; the common (sādhāraṇa) mixture indeed it is." The verse is the one of Pingree's ch. 16 v. 10 (bya:p41-46) with "prāvṛṭśare" where the apparatus of that edition has the variants of its manuscripts for the season names.

Bhaṭṭotpala: "Night is the night; of it, in the last part, the third at the end, is the aggravation of wind (marut); in the middle third of the night the aggravation of bile; in the first third that of phlegm. Then, in the time called the rains, autumn and Mādhava, in order the aggravation of wind, bile, phlegm: in the rains, the rainy season, in the night, wind; in autumn, bile; in the spring called Mādhava, phlegm; in the other seasons it goes to accumulation. And in each one's own time they reach aggravation and, aggravated, show the sign strongly: as one of a windy nature in the last third of the night, in the rains, sees the signs of wind strongly; so one of bile in the middle third sees the signs of bile strongly. Likewise 'the common mixture indeed it is': the mixture (sannipāta) is the aggravation of all of wind, bile and phlegm; there the common dream is made of all the signs. ... one of a mixed nature sees a mixed dream: this is the meaning."

The gloss states the pairs the verse leaves to its reader: the last third of the night, the rains, wind; the middle third, autumn, bile; the first third, spring (Mādhava), phlegm. (The printed lines are legible; the page is a modern edition, the Sanskrit quoted for the facts it holds.)
