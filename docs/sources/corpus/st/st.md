Serthang p. 21, img. 34, ll. 8–12 of the scan (the passage is the end of the chapter's account of the child in the womb, the second chapter by its colophon on p. 22 of the print, on the formation of the body, ལུས་ཀྱི་ཆགས་ཚུལ, lus kyi chags tshul, the Explanatory Tantra, བཤད་རྒྱུད, bshad rgyud, by the run of colophons that begins again at the second after the Root Tantra's sixth on etext p. 28). When the nine months are over it is the time of birth. The mother lies on her right side with the right hip high. The reverse of these is the sign of a girl. A mixture of them gives a child who is neither (མ་ནིང, ma ning). If the middle is low and both sides are high, twins are born.
