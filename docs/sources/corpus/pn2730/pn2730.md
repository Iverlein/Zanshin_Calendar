## <a id="pn2730-i0"></a>pn2730:i0 The first text page of the Svapnādhyāya of Item 2730: verses 1–5, the spans of the watches in v. 2–4

*Read: scan, 2026-11-10 (RD357), on the full-size page image.*

> ॥ श्रीगणेशाय नमः ॥ स्वप्नाध्यायं प्रवक्ष्यामि नानास्वप्नसमुच्चयं ।
> येन विज्ञानमात्रेण मनुष्याणां शुभाशुभं ॥ १ ॥
> स्वप्नस्तु प्रथमे यामे संवत्सरफलं भवेत् ।
> द्वितीये चाष्टभिर्मासैस्त्रिभिर्मासैस्त्रियामिकं (?) ॥ २ ॥
> चतुर्थे प्रहरे दृष्टं मासेन फलं भवेत् ।
> अरुणोदयवेलायां दशाहेन फलं भवेत् ॥ ३ ॥
> गोविसर्जनवेलायां सद्यः स्वप्नो विधीयते ।
> वातिकं पैत्तिकं चैव चिंतितं चानुवर्जयेत् ॥ ४ ॥
> अत ऊर्ध्वं प्रवक्ष्यामि पुण्यापुण्यं निबोध (?) …
> आरोहणं गोवृषकुंजराणां प्रासादशैलाग्रवनस्पतीनां ।
> विष्ठानुलेपोपरुदितं (?) … सुगम्यागमनं च धन्यं ॥ ५ ॥

The first text page of Item 2730 (f. 1r, canvas 0, one long page of ten lines), after the salutation to Gaṇeśa. Verse 1: "I shall tell the chapter on dreams, the collection of the various dreams, by which, by mere knowing it, men's good and bad is known." Verse 2: "A dream of the first watch bears its fruit in a year; of the second, in eight months; of the third watch, in three months." Verse 3: "A dream seen in the fourth watch bears its fruit in a month (māsena); at the dawn's red, in ten days." Verse 4: "At the cows' release, at once; the dream of wind and of bile, and what was thought of, one is to set aside." Then "Henceforth I shall tell of what is meritorious and not meritorious ..." (the end of this line is not read), and verse 5 begins the list of dreams with "climbing a cow, a bull, an elephant, a palace, a mountain peak, a tree".
The verses are numbered by the scribe from 1 (v. 1 to v. 5 read in the first nine lines; the numbers run on to 42 and beyond on the next leaf, f. 1v–2r). **The fourth watch is a month**, with the same dawn and cows' release lines as pn1871 and with an own wording of the opening verse ("nānā-svapna-samuccayam"); no half-month, no three fortnights.
Doubts: the last word of verse 2 ("triyāmikam" or "triyāmake") is not sure; the words after "nibodha(?)" in the line after verse 4 and the middle of verse 5 are not read.
