## <a id="ksr-p367-368"></a>ksr:p367-368 The foundation's monthly cycle: the 7th, 8th, 10th, 15th, 25th and 29th, the year's festivals

*Read: etext, 2026-11-21.*

Facts only, in the pool's own words (modern copyrighted volume; the Tibetan is not quoted beyond the terms). The life of Kong sprul Yon tan rgya mtsho in this volume describes the shrines, books and stupas of his foundation (the page names the catalogue of Tsā 'dra Rin chen brag; the place of this list is not named in the pages read, p. 364 starting mid-list) and then the order of its rites, first by the months of the year and then for every month.

**By the months of the year**: in the first month the offering to the images of Mar [pa] and Mi [la] (མར་མི, mar mi) with the cotton-garment rite and the Ocean of Songs (ras 'bud, mgur mtsho); the second the offering to the image of the Mindroling treasure-master (སྨིན་གླིང་གཏེར་ཆེན, smin gling gter chen) with the feast fulfilment of the Rig 'dzin thugs thig, and the offering to the image of the Rnge btsun Tārānātha with the accomplishment offering of the five tantra classes in the Shangs tradition, three days; the fourth month (ནག་པ་ཟླ་བ, nag pa zla ba) the Kālacakra accomplishment offering with the rite of offering to the hundred yoginī families, seven days; the fifth the filling rite of the Lama Gongdü (བླ་མ་དགོངས་འདུས, bla ma dgongs 'dus); the ninth, on the festival of Khyung po, the Shangs offering to the lama; the twelfth, on the festival of Kun mkhyen Klong chen pa, the Nyingthig offering to the lama; the eleventh the changing of the standing torma with a long petition (རྟག་གཏོར་བརྗེ་བའི་གསོལ་ཁ་འཕར་མ); at the year's end the torma accomplishment of Ye shes mgon po Myur mdzad; on the festivals of the four months (ཟླ་བཞིའི་དུས་ཆེན, zla bzhi'i dus chen) the gathering of merit and the full restoring-and-vow rite (གསོ་སྦྱོང་རྒྱས་པ, gso sbyong rgyas pa).

**In every month**: on the **seventh** the offering to the image of Rje Padma Nyin byed and the Vajrasattva accomplishment offering (རྡོར་སེམས་སྒྲུབ་མཆོད, rdor sems sgrub mchod); on the **eighth** the long-life accomplishment of Guhyasamāja (ཚེ་སྒྲུབ་གསང་འདུས) and the White Tārā accomplishment offering (སྒྲོལ་དཀར་སྒྲུབ་མཆོད); on the **tenth** the Lama Gongdü, the guru accomplishment with its phrase «སྐབས་བབ» (skabs bab, not glossed) and the feast offering of Vimalamitra (བི་མའི་ཚོགས་མཆོད, bi ma'i tshogs mchod); on the **fifteenth** the Kālacakra offering rite; on the **twenty-fifth** the Cakrasaṃvara accomplishment offering (བདེ་མཆོག་སྒྲུབ་མཆོད, bde mchog sgrub mchod) and the Khecarī, white and red, in two turns (མཁའ་སྤྱོད་དཀར་དམར་གཉིས་རེས་མོ, mkha' spyod dkar dmar gnyis res mo); on the **twenty-ninth** the fulfilling-and-petition with a long petition (སྐོང་གསོལ་འཕར་མ, skong gsol 'phar ma). Through the six months of the long days the Guhyagarbha tantra (གསང་སྙིང་རྒྱུད, gsang snying rgyud) is read, through the six of the short days the Names of Mañjuśrī (འཇམ་དཔལ་མཚན་བརྗོད, 'jam dpal mtshan brjod), each tantra with its own reading rite. On p. 368: every month the monks keep the restoring and vow of the full and the new moon (ཉ་སྟོང་གི་གསོ་སྦྱོང, nya stong gi gso sbyong) each by his own vows, and those who live in the elders' hall (གནས་བརྟན་ཁང, gnas brtan khang) do a Sangs gling gnas chog rite (the term not identified) on every eighth, for the lasting of the teaching.

The tenth and the twenty-fifth are thus both a lama-and-feast day and a Cakrasaṃvara day in this house; the 29th is the day of the protectors' fulfilment (skong gsol). The source is a Rime (Kagyu and Nyingma) house's rule, not a Nyingma customary alone.
