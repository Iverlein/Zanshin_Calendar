## <a id="wb-1-p257"></a>wb.1:p257 Ch. 21: the wrong ways of turning the circuits end in a list of rejected variants, among them the reckoned's cutting, parting and spreading

*Read: scan, 2026-11-07.*

> བརྩི་བྱ་གཅོད་འདྲལ་འགྱེད་པ་སོགས། །མི་འདྲའི་འཁྲུལ་པ་འགའ་ཡོད་ཀྱང༌། ། … གསལ་བར་གསུངས་ཕྱིར་དགག་བྱའི་གནས། །

Gser thang reprint I3CN5028 img. 252 (printed p. 257), lines 3–4, the first clause read letter by letter at 3×, the closing words by eye, the words between them from the 1996 etext (I1KG12906, page 257 of the etext) and not re-read: "…the reckoned [one] cut, parted, spread out, and the rest; though some mistaken [ways] differing from one another exist, … because it is plainly said [in the tantra], they are places to be refuted."

The sentence closes the verse of ch. 21 that lists the wrong ways of turning the circuits of the sme ba (the outer circuit, the inner circuit, the secret circuit, the descent of the spar kha). «བརྩི་བྱ་གཅོད་འདྲལ་འགྱེད་པ» stands in that list of rejected variants beside «སོགས», and the verse ends by putting all of them among «དགག་བྱའི་གནས», the places that are refuted. So the phrase names a method the author rejects, not a count of his own.
