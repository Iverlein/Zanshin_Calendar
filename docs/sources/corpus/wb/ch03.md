Chapter three: on the chronology of the doctrine (bstan rtsis).

Homage in verse. "The Victor's eighty-four thousand teachings, all of them without remainder, known as one: what wonder is greater? I bow to the wonderful lord Jam mgon Bla ma, who thrives on the seat of the Three Secrets at 'Gog ri." (A bracketed gloss: the praise by the display of power.) "Whoever wishes to speak as a Vedic scholar, to find the certainty of the true years: on this dancer on the corner of the leaf of the doctrine's reckoning (the sense of «དོ་རའི་གར་མཁན» is not clear), I ask that the leader's movement be purified by the three examinations." (A bracketed gloss: the matter of the addition with form.)

Then the matter. The refuge of all beings, the Teacher's year of birth, the standing-measure of the doctrine, the throne of Shambhala, the Pandit's and the Dharma-king's years of coming to noble India and to Tibet: each has many views. The intended sense of the "Lotus-white Oral Tradition" (Pad dkar zhal lung), the view of the unmistaken Nor bzang rgya mtsho and of Phug pa Lhun grub rgya mtsho, is set down in writing, with what makes up for what lacks. For the totalled years, 2648 (the number is given in words: Klu, mtsho, mtshams, 'khrig), from the first of the rab byung, the fire-hare year and the rest, the passed years are given. (The Upper Commentary, Stod 'grel, has the earth-sheep year Don grub, the cloud season, the upper water month, the full-moon day.)

The all-knowing, unequalled crest of the Shakyas leaves the Dga' ldan place for Jambudvipa, to lead on the true path: the city Ser skya looked on with five (signs); the emanation of Manjushri, Zas gtsang, and of Avalokiteshvara, Sgyu 'phrul ma: he entered the womb; the passed year is 2648. Reduced by 1 (the rhinoceros): in the fierce iron-monkey year, in the month Saga, the seventh day, the mansion Rgyal; after ten months in the womb, in the Lumbini grove the body was born, in the year 2647. Reduced by 28 (gdengs, mig): [he] blessed the queen Grags 'dzin, chief of eighty-four thousand, a self-form of the heart of Vajrasattva, to be as a son. From the sound of music he went forth; roused, he came to the four city gates; seeing old age, death and a monk, he was moved; he gave up worldly existence and went forth, the passed year 2619. Then, after six years of austerities, in a male horse year, on the fifteenth of the month Saga he went to the foot of the Bodhi tree; in one sitting in the vajra posture he defeated Mara's host at dusk; in the first watch the four meditations, at midnight the five clairvoyances were produced; from the taking up of the vajra yoga, at dawn when the Victor's drum was beaten, he was perfectly awakened as a Buddha. Sgra gcan 'dzin (Rahula) was born, an emanation of Phyag rdor (Vajrapani); Kun dga' bo (Ananda) was born as the dancer of the emanation. The Kar myang reckoning (kar myang rtsis) that the Nepalese scholars hold: with 9 (bug), 0 (mkha') and Lord of Men 16 [i.e. 1609; the print has «(༡༦༠༩)», four figures; the machine reading «༡༦༩» dropped the 0] for the years, from the first of the rab byung's sun [cycle], mix in the passed years up to the one counted: this is known as the Shaka year. Multiply this by 2 (mes, as printed «༢»), divide by the seven rishis (drang srong, 7); put the obtained "nor" aside; the remainder, for the rain's sake, multiply by 2 (mig); give 5 (the elements): this is the rain-fruit. Multiply the earlier obtained "nor" by 3 (me); subtract 7 (ris); and so on, eight times (len brgyad): grain, grass, coolness, heat, wind: the growth and the finish; the disturbances are known in order. The least is 5 (the elements); at the most the "ri zla" 17; the middle is the sun, 12: to each, much or little fruit is given.

Again, that Shaka passed year: subtract 11 (dbang phyug); the remainder: throw away by 4 (mtsho); if 1 (gzugs) rises in the east: "Entering the pond" (rdzing bu 'jug): great drought. If 2: "Entering the limb" (yan lag 'jug): in some regions rain falls, in some it does not fall and the grain-heaps fail. If 3 (me) rises in the east: it is said "All-entering" (kun 'jug): in all regions a fine rain falls. If 0 (thig, the printed figure «(༠)»; the machine had 4): "Flood" (zhod): the rivers swell.
