… [the weekday] Friday (པ་སངས), of varied colours: four-footed livestock and women prosper; people are gentle and have neither strife nor hunger. [Saturday], the Rākṣasa yoga is told: sinners and low clans are happy; people are rough, with strife and hunger. In short, the red sun and Saturday are rough. Where the month is in excess (ཟླ་ལྷག) with Thursday (ཕུར་བུ) and Friday, the moon's lord is the lord of the month and the month's results are good. Then: the drawing of the lord (དབང་ཕྱུག), and the two planets of the house-change: judge by more or fewer; if they are equal, [the two,] Īśvara and Umā (དབང་ཕྱུག་ཨུ་མ), sleep and meet, and the result is: earthquakes, many thieves; a great wind; if it is summer, hail and a king's battle; father and son fight; fever seizes. If many planets of the lunar day meet rising (བཞེངས): the crops ripen, the harvest is great; rain falls in season, no famine; all kings are virtuous, strife is quiet; the strength of virtue grows and beings are happy. If many planets of the house meet seated (བཞུགས): beings suffer, minds are not at ease; many diseases and great fear come; strife comes and many die; strength is lost, there are lies and friends fight; whatever is eaten does not satisfy. If an evil planet (སྡིག་གཟའ) rises over all twelve [houses], that whole year is very rough; for all of them the "merit" (བསོད་ནམས) is very good; judge the month of the "mixed" thang-ka. Further, the black month: whichever rises, the month's "four-killer" is black: when rat and horse rise in the sky [rises], hen and hare are black; ox and sheep rising, dog and dragon black; tiger and monkey rising, pig and snake black: know the rest by analogy. Know the black of the upper part of the four ra, the middle of the four 'bring (འབྲིང), the lower of the four tha. Though there are many ways of drawing in the separate systems of postponing and bringing forward the month, in this system the measure of the middle, told earlier: on whichever skipped day (གནམ) it rises, that month is piled up; and when it rises on the first lunar day, that month is postponed to the next. This is pure and established; the maker comes out when the months increase.
Thus, from the "Neck Ornament of the Learned, the White Beryl Necklace, the Heart-jewel of the Analytic", the good exposition of the Phugpa reckoning, the fifteenth chapter, explaining the necessary intent of the house-change, the obtaining of breath, the postponing of the month and the pairing of asterism and black [month].
