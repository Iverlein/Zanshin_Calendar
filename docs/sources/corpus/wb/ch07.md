Chapter seven: on the pure tithi-conduct of the five planets (*tshes spyod gzhar lnga rnam dag*).

Four stanzas of praise, each closed by the name of its figure: homage to Ngag dbang lha, Brahmā's daughter (Sarasvatī), whose face enchants and whose long eyes the young moon, the lotus stalk and the *utpala* have robbed; the author's wish that the "sage" Agastya of his mind (*drang srong a gsta*) draw out the rivers of the planets' conduct, muddied by murk, so that the reckoning of the planets, by the tithi and the day in agreement and pure, appears from the peak of the earth-holder of Yama; an account of the practice done as it is, without hidden parts, in the manner of the earlier good ones; and a stanza on the praise of the beauty of the *Gaṃ ḍā pa* (as the page reads; the sense of the name is not settled) whose voice, sweet beyond the three realms, is carried far.

The procedure. From the sun-year of the Rabjung (the 12-fold set, "sun" 12), take the elapsed years from the fire-hare year; multiply by "houses" (12) and add the black (*nag pa*) and the rest; mix in the elapsed months and set in two places. The lower is multiplied by "pair" (2) and "power, moon" (15) added; the quotient of the division by "element, taste" (65) is thrown up: this is the purified month. For the sake of the five planets (the figures given for them on the page: Mars 3, Mercury 4, Jupiter 5, Venus 6, Saturn with the figure "0", the weekday numbers counted from the sun as 1), set the six places in five groups. In the first, "sky, moon" (1) hours, "root" (9); "naga, fire" (38) *srang*, with breath and "dot" (0); "ocean, mountain, rhinoceros" (174) the lower fraction; multiply by "wealth and sage" (78); "mountain, sky, mountain" (707) and "hole, eye, hand" (229), "time" (6), "dot, taste" (60), "sky, taste" (60), "wheel" (27) obtained and the remainder: the movement (*rgyu ba*) of Mars (the first of the five, here called *sa skyes*, "earth-born"), freed from continuation.

So in the second: "hole" (9) the mansion, hours "peak" (2) the *srang*; "naga" and "Veda" (48), breath, "power" (5), own fraction "eye, element, mountain, naga" (8752); the lower scattered by "boundary" (69) multiplied; from below "mountain, sky, mountain" (707), "mountain, hole, mountain, naga" (8797), "time" (6), "sky, taste" (60), "dot, time" (60) and the mansions (27) cast out as the remainder: the moon's son (Mercury) accomplished, freed of continuation. Third: "dot" (0) and hours "fierce" (11); *srang* "eye" (2), breath "fire" (3), "power, hand" (211); "time, ocean, mountain" (746); "man, mind" (1414), "form, time, fire" (361), "taste" (6), "sky, taste" (60), "dot, boundary" (60), mansions (27) cast out: the lama's (Jupiter's) movement accomplished. Fourth: "fire, tooth" (323), "ocean, power" (54), *srang*; "breath, planet, time, power, benefit" (3567); then, on the last lines of the page, "fire, day" (73), "mountain, sky, mountain" (707), a figure of three digits after "breath, ocean, mountain" (not settled), "time" (6) and "sky, taste" (60) (the page ends here; the white one's *dhruva* closes on the next page).
