## <a id="wb-1-p64-65"></a>wb.1:p64-65 The Mercury and Venus clause, and the solar rule of the true and the abridged reckoning

"Further, when either of the two, Mercury or Venus, stays together with the sun in one mansion within 135 sixtieths, it cannot be seized. For the first, whether it is seized or not, the chapter on the true reckoning (གྲུབ་རྩིས, grub rtsis) and some agreement are needed. (p. 65) … the true sun (ཉི་མ་དག་པ) of the three, and whichever of Rahu's head and tail is nearer, of the two: they fall on one mansion, or … (the rest of the line is not settled). … Where the abridged reckoning (བྱེད་རྩིས, byed rtsis) seizes but the true reckoning's mansion is void so that it does not seize, the intent of the unerring Abridged Tantra is principal. In particular, when the sun stands over the seven eastern mansions, though the true reckoning's place of the mansion be missed, the abridged reckoning is principal; apart from the abridged reckoning's sixtieths, if it is void there is no solar eclipse."
