## <a id="wb-1-p355"></a>wb.1:p355 The bad years' end and the third point: the kinds of spouse

*Read: scan, 2026-10-23.*

> ཡོད་མྱ་ངན་དག །བཟློག་གཏོ་མི་ཁ་དགྲ་གྱོད་ལ། །མི་ཁ་དགྲ་བཟློག་མཆུ་ལྔ་སྒྲོག །རབས་ཆད་ངོ་ལ་སྲིད་མཁར་འཛུགས། །ཚེར་གནོད་སྲོག་བསྐྱེད་ལོངས་སྤྱོད་ངན། །དབང་བསྐྱེད་ནད་གཅོང་ལུས་གསོ་བའི། །ཆོས་རྩིས་སྔགས་བོན་སོ་སོ་ཡི། The second point's end. Where the bad years meet, rites against separation, sorrow, enmity and lawsuits and the rest are done; "who could tell them all? Shoot the great arrow of knowledge." A bride of a harsh year is given to a groom (བག་ཕོ, bag pho) sought for karma's sake; a man of a harsh year takes a wife sought for karma's sake.

The results told by the mother's luck (མ་ཀླུངས, ma klungs): one bad result shared; birth planets in threes («གསུམ་འདོམ») are not joined within. Mars's three joined: people and wealth bad, the companion dies, separation, bad for children. Saturn's three: begging for food, the earth lords' harm, life unhappy, faults of grave and funeral, children lost. Mercury's three: losses before king and in petitions, of men and horses, children lost. Jupiter's three, two together bad: above and below joined, the father's line dulled, death. Of the man's outer planet besides: born on Saturn and joined within, children hard to raise; on Mars, deaths soon, people and wealth bad, life short; on Jupiter, children hard to keep alive; on the moon, harm to livestock, dry goods and farming bad.

The Chinese white years (ལོ་དཀར་ཧ་ཤ, lo dkar ha sha, the print's dots under «ཧ་ཤ»), six pairs not to be joined: the rat and the hare, the ox and the tiger, the dragon and the snake, the pig and the dog, the horse and the bird, the monkey and the sheep; some also count the small one, the seventh. Their results pair by pair (the dragon and snake, the jaw filled with a son's corpse; the dog and pig, seven years in which the line does not go on; the bird and horse, the husband dies within a year; the ox and tiger, one year and three years bad; the rat and hare, brother-in-law and son die), and if joined, the white year's own ransom (མདོས, mdos) and the five fierce sūtras.

The three in concord joined are three ruins, by the power's element of the triad: tiger, horse and dog earth; rat, dragon and monkey fire; pig, sheep and hare water; bird, ox and snake wood. Then the best of the three in concord: man wood rat with woman fire monkey, wood tiger with fire dog, earth tiger with iron dog, and so on to water tiger with wood dog: "these years of joining are good for the luck."

The third point opens (ll. 26–28), the single power, «གསུམ་པ་རྐྱང་པ»: of the four, life force, body, power and luck (སྲོག་ལུས་དབང་ཀླུང, srog lus dbang klung), the man's life force and luck strike at the woman, the woman's body and power at the man. All four meeting as mother and son is called the spouse of karma (ལས་ཀྱི་ཆུང་མ, las kyi chung ma), and each one meeting as mother and son is the same; meeting as friends is held a spouse of kin (གཉེན, gnyen), as enemies a spouse of enmity (དགྲ, dgra); of the inner meetings, an element with itself, fire, iron and wood are middling, earth and water good. The page ends on «རྡེལ་བོར་», the pebbles laid as in the obstacle reckoning.

**The ninth, the mothers' clans** (མ་ཡི་རུས, ma yi rus).
