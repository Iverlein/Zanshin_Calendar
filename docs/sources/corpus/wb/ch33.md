## <a id="wb-2-p364-365"></a>wb.2:p364-365 The date's and hour's fierce earth lords in general: virtuous and living works avoided, fierce works by their course

*Read: scan, 2026-10-26.*

> …ཉི་ཚེས་དུས་ཚོད་ལ་སྤང་བའི། །སྤྱིར་ནི་དྲག་པོའི་ས་བདག་རྣམས། །དགེ་ལས་གསོན་པོའི་བྱ་བ་སྤང་། །དྲག་ལས་རྒྱུ་ཕྱོགས་[p. 365] བསྟུན་པས་ཐེབས། …

WB vol. 2, ch. 33, pp. 364–365 (1996 edition, img. 372–373), after the strikes and turnings of the nāgas and the eight classes and before the trigrams and mewa that rise on the date: read on the scan of the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5029 img. 370, the last line at 1.7×, «ཉི་ཚེས» at the end of the line before at 1×); «བསྟུན་པས་ཐེབས», the first words of p. 365, on img. 371 at 1.5×.

"Of the fierce earth lords to be avoided on the day, the date and the hour, in general: virtuous works and the works of the living are to be avoided; fierce works, done in accord with the direction of their course, strike home."

What it shows: inside the results chapter, a general rule for all the date's and hour's fierce earth lords. It sorts works by kind (virtuous and living against fierce) and joins the fierce works to the lord's direction of travel. It weighs the lords against no factor of the day.

## <a id="wb-2-p369"></a>wb.2:p369 The general remedy against the harm of year, month, day, hour, planet, mansion and earth lord

*Read: etext.*

> …ད་ནི་སྤྱི་ཡི་བཅོས་ཐབས་བསྟན། །གང་དག་གསོན་གཤིན་བྱ་བའི་ལས། །གཡོ་འགུལ་རྔམ་ཆེན་གང་བྱ་རུང་། །མི་ནོར་ཟས་གཡང་མི་ཉམས་ཤིང་། །ལོ་ཟླ་ཞག་དུས་གཟའ་སྐར་མ། །ས་བདག་ངན་གནོད་མི་འབྱུང་ཞིང་། །བཀྲ་ཤིས་བདེ་ལེགས་འབྱུང་བའི་གནད། །སྒམ་ཆིངས་ཟབ་མོ་འདི་ལྟར་བྱེད། །…

WB vol. 2, ch. 33, p. 369 (1996 edition, img. 377), after the sky doors: BDRC's etext only (etext p. 377), not read on the scan.

"Now the general remedy is taught. Whatever works of the living and the dead, whatever moving and grave works one does, so that people, wealth, food and fortune do not decline, and no harm of the bad year, month, day, hour, planet, mansion or earth lord arises, and good fortune comes: the profound binding of the box is done thus …" (the golden turtle in the box, pp. 369–371).

What it shows: one remedy is set against the harm of all of them together, the day's factors and the earth lords in one list, without an order.

## <a id="wb-2-p377"></a>wb.2:p377 Chapter 33's closing note: the date's earth lords' forms and courses set down by custom, their source slight

*Read: scan, 2026-10-26.*

> …གཟའི་ཁམས་རྣམས་གདན་དུ་བཏིང་། ཚེས་ཀྱི་ས་བདག་གི་གཟུགས་འགྲོས་རྣམས་ས་བདག་ལེའུར་མེད་པ་གང་ཡིན་རྟོག་བཟོ་ལྟ་བུ་ཁུངས་ཧ་ཅང་མི་ཆེ་ཡང་རིན་ཆེན་ཕྲེང་བ་ལྷན་ཐབས་སོགས་ལ་འདྲི་སྲོལ་འདུག་པ་ལྟར་བཀོད། སྦྱོར་བར་བུ་སྟོན་འབྲི་ཁུང་ཆོས་མགོན་པ་སོགས་ལྷ་ཡོད་པར་བཞེད་ཀྱང་། …

WB vol. 2, ch. 33's closing note, p. 377 (1996 edition, img. 385): read on the scan of the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5029 img. 383, the page number 377 at its foot), four lines at 1.8×. The note's earlier part (from etext p. 385): the results of the sūtras, tantras and Indian translations differ, the Phugpa works (the *Rin chen phreng ba* inner and outer, the *Lhan thabs*, the *Kun 'dus nor bu'i phreng ba*) differ a little, "the authoritative ones were taken as chief where most agree" («ཁུངས་ཐུབ་རྣམས་ཤས་ཆེ་མཐུན་གང་ཆེ་གཙོ་བོར་བཟུང», etext only), and Kālacakra and the black reckoning were both set down where they are closely joined.

"… The planets' elements were laid as the seats. The forms and courses of the date's earth lords (ཚེས་ཀྱི་ས་བདག, tshes kyi sa bdag): those that are not in the earth lords' chapter are like contrivances, their source not very great (ཁུངས་ཧ་ཅང་མི་ཆེ, khungs ha cang mi che); but since it is the custom to write them in the *Precious Garland* (རིན་ཆེན་ཕྲེང་བ, rin chen phreng ba), the *Supplement* (ལྷན་ཐབས, lhan thabs) and the like, they were set down. In the yogas, Bu ston, 'Bri khung Chos mgon pa and others hold that there are gods, but …"

What it shows: WB grades the date's earth lords of ch. 33 by their source, not by strength: those not found in ch. 31 are kept only as custom. Ch. 31's own close (wb.2:p240) throws out the date-moving earth lords' visualisations that it does not set down as unsourced. Neither line ranks the date's earth lords against the factors of the day.
