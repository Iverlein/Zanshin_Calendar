How these pages were read. Every page is state *machine*: Yigdzin-1 over the page, MITRA beside it, the words read against the page image where they differ. Chapter six: on the days of the five planets: the day-count (*nyin zhag*) and the rest.

Praise. Five stanzas, each closed by the name of its poetic figure. First: in the wrathful form of the youthful Mañjughoṣa (Jam mgon gzhon nu gtum drag rngams pa), "rising, I crush the host of the maras without remainder"; I need fear nothing, I take refuge in his loving compassion and am guarded by that god who frees from fear. Second: of the full measure of the days of the five planets, the "sages" (*drang srong*), a good exposition of their conduct, new and never made before, is spread; with the toil of the "special day" (*sgos zhag*) put aside, it is grasped rightly, and a hundred gates of understanding open to the end. For those who go only by what they wish, it is a boat over the sea of the reckoning-treatises, a mother-line of the moon's reckoning, a banner set up in the heart. Third and fourth: in praise of the Phug-pa school's good exposition, the drink of the gods for the hard points of the sky-fire (*mkha' mes*) counting of the passing and the giving, and the sweet stream of the Ganges of its words, brought down into the field of memory.

The procedure. From the first year of the Rabjung (the sixty-year cycle) take the years elapsed, from the fire-hare year onward. Multiply by "eye, form" (12, the signs), add the black (*nag pa*) and the rest, mix in the elapsed months, and set the result in two places. Multiply the lower by "pair" (2) and add "arrow, form" (15). Divide by "element, taste" (65) and throw the quotient up above: this is the purified month (*zla ba rnam par dag pa*). Multiply the purified month by "dot, quality" (*thig yon*, the figure is 30; the word *yon* stands for three), mix in the tithi being reckoned, and set the result in three places. In the middle put "eye, arrow" (52); to the lower add "sky, eye and hand" (220); then "mountain, sky, mountain" (707) and "water, taste" (64) clear it, and the quantity found is thrown up above. This is the day-count, also known as the common day (*spyi zhag*).

For the sake of the day-count of the month: take the common day without the tithi mixed in and set it in five places (sky 0, ocean 4, "time, eye" 26, dot 0 and the fractions); multiply it by the series "time, tithi, fire, hole" (93156); from the top "dot" (0), "boundary, hand" (26), "arrow, fire" (35) and the element-fractions (5), clear with "power, taste, sky, time, naga" (86065); then, from below, "hole, sky, eye, hole, mind" (69209: the first two digits of the figure are smudged on the scan and are given as the number-words require), "boundary" (6), "sky, taste" (60), "dot, taste" (60) and the wheel (27) are cast out; what remains is for the sun and the moon. Then, for the sake of the fierce three (*drag gsum*), the slow pace (*dal ba*), the peaceful two (*zhi gnyis*) and the foot-holders (*rkang 'dzin*): take the common day with the tithi mixed in, set it in five places, five times in order. First, for the sake of the slow pace of the first planet, the "auspicious" one (*bkra shis*): "dot" (0), "eye" (2), "moon-pair" (21), "union" (2); it is multiplied by "naga, sky, twins" (208) and the division made; from the top "mountain, moon" (17), "ocean, fire" (34), "peak" (2), "water" ...
