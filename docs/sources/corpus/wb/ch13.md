*Page 134 (img. 141).* From the glorious Kālacakra the eight yoginīs (རྣལ་འབྱོར་ལྷ་མོ་བརྒྱད, rnal 'byor lha mo brgyad) were taught; the way they appear, in order, is this. Of all twelve months, on the 1st, 9th, 16th and 24th the Brahmā-goddess (ཚངས་མ, tshangs ma) dwells in the east. Page 154 (Gser thang reprint, image 161), the first fifteen day boxes of the model almanac's first month (the 11th Hor month, Sagittarius), read by eye on crops at 2-6x. Only the cells whose letters are clear are written; the rest of each box is described, not copied.

Each box, from the top: (1) the date's numeral, its five-fold class word (the cycle dga' ba, bzang po, rgyal ba, stong pa, rdzogs pa, repeating: boxes 1, 6, 11 are "joy", 2, 7, 12 "good", 3, 8, 13 "victory", 4, 9, 14 "empty", 5, 10, 15 "full"), then the Kalacakra vowel and consonant, the element and the object: box 1 a, ksa, space, sound; box 2 i, si, wind, touch; box 3 ri, sri, fire, taste (the glyph is ro, the "taste"); box 4 u, hu, water, form; box 5 li, sli, earth, smell; box 11 ha, ksha, space, sound. (2) In a scalloped oval the vowel-arising (dbyangs 'char) set: box 1 a, dha, earth, smell; box 2 i, na, water, taste; box 3 u, pa, fire, form; box 4 e, pha, wind, touch; box 5 o, ba, space, sound; box 11 a, va, earth, smell. (3) Under the oval a line of stage word (child, youth, prime, old, ripe: box 1 to box 5 in that order), a second word that is an animal's name or similar, a trigram letter with a numeral below it (box 1: the trigram li over the numeral 7), and one or two further words of the earth lords' courses, which are not settled. (4) Sky-door words, boxed or not: box 1 war and fort (the box holds fort), box 4 war boxed, box 5 alliance boxed. Box 15 has the course word ki kang in its lower line.
