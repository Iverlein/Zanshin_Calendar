*Page 134 (img. 141).* From the glorious Kālacakra the eight yoginīs (རྣལ་འབྱོར་ལྷ་མོ་བརྒྱད, rnal 'byor lha mo brgyad) were taught; the way they appear, in order, is this. Of all twelve months, on the 1st, 9th, 16th and 24th the Brahmā-goddess (ཚངས་མ, tshangs ma) dwells in the east. On the 2nd, 10th, 17th and 25th the fierce one (དྲག་མོ, drag mo) is in the north; on the 3rd, 11th, 18th and 26th the youth (གཞོན་ནུ་མ, gzhon nu ma) is in the south-east; on the 4th, 12th, 19th and 27th the Vishnu-goddess (ཁྱབ་འཇུག་མ, khyab 'jug ma) is in the south-west; on the 5th, 13th, 20th and 28th the sow (ཕག་མོ, phag mo) dwells in the south; on the 6th, 14th, 21st and 29th the powerful one (དབང་མོ, dbang mo) is in the west; the 7th, 15th and 22nd and the half of «fish-upper/fish-thousand» (doubt: ཉ་སྟོད / ཉ་སྟོང) are Cāmuṇḍā (ཙཱ་མུཎྚ) in the north-west; the 8th, 23rd and the later half of «fish-thousand» are the glorious one (དཔལ་མོ, dpal mo) in the north-east. Of these the tithi's long part, before and after the half, is divided by eight; the hours and the like become sixteenths of the tithi. The watch (ཐུན་ཚད, thun tshad) of each yoginī: the 1st and 9th the first, the 2nd and 10th the second, and so on to the 8th and 16th the eighth. Each follows its own tithi's lord, beginning from the Brahmā-goddess; the order is the Brahmā-goddess, the fierce one, the youth, the Vishnu-goddess, the sow, the powerful one, Cāmuṇḍā, the glorious one; the strike (ཐེབས, thebs) falls from oneself and the right-hand side to the front and the left.

Viṣṭi's (བིབྷི / བིཥྚི, doubt) manner of dwelling: the 4th, 18th, 8th, 22nd, 11th, 25th, 15th and 29th, in order, east, south-east, south, south, south-west, west, north-west, north, north, north-east (the directions as the verse lists them; the pairing of dates and directions is not settled here), counted from each one's own side as above, increases the strength of a deed.

The wheel of the yoginīs as the vowel-arising text (དབྱངས་འཆར་གཞུང, dbyangs 'char gzhung) tells it: from the east the letters a, ka, ca, ṭa, ta, pa, ya, śa stand in the eight directions, with the Brahmā-goddess, the youth, the lady of power, the Vishnu-goddess, the sow, the powerful one, the elder-maker (རྒན་བྱེད་མ) and the coquette (སྒེག་མོ) in order; the tithis are given again, 1, 9, 16, 24 to the Brahmā-goddess and so on, the 8th and 23rd to the coquette; the manner of striking agrees with the general one.

The arising of Viṣṭi (བིཥྚི) from the east in order, with tithi and name in the eight directions: 18, 15, 11, 8, 4, 29, 25, 22, named Karāla, the Blazing one, Time's tongue, Mahāmārī, Vitavāla, Mahājika, the Auspicious one and Mahāghora.
