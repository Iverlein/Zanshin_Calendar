The notes of Mipham (མི་ཕམ, mi pham) on the match's second to fourth points.

**The bad years** (from p. 523): if man and woman both meet them it is bad, the deciding pebble (བཏང་གཅོད, btang gcod) one black; if one, speckled; if neither, a white pebble; a bad year of the woman's above all is bad. These are not joined pair by pair but reckoned in general (སྤྱིར, spyir). "Meeting each other on their own grave or the grave-executioner": a water rat man with a water dragon woman, or a water dragon man with a water rat woman, the grave-executioner (དུར་གཤེད, dur gshed), and one's own executioner, the small grave and the like, explained by that. "One bad result told by the mother's luck" means that, the karma reckoning (ལས་རྩིས, las rtsis) done, both are bad; and both born on a triad of planets joined within, as "Mars's three joined bad" and the rest, as the root text says; the man by the mother's progressed sign alone, Saturn-born joined within and so on, as the root text; "moon-born" is a name for Mercury (as printed: «ཟླ་སྐྱེས་ཞེས་ལྷག་པའི་མིང»). The great white years: rat and hare; the small: rat and horse.

**The third point**: how the man's and woman's life force and the rest strike is as the root text says. And: join each separately, the life force a spouse of karma (ལས་ཀྱི་ཆུང་མ, las kyi chung ma) and so on, and look at good and bad in general (སྤྱིར་བཟང་ངན་བལྟ). The inner meetings of earth and water count as a spouse of karma, the other three as a spouse of kin, as the *Removing the Tarnish* (གཡའ་སེལ, g.ya' sel) says. Reckoned so, apply the results, "if the life force's element agrees" and the rest.

**The fourth point, the hidden of the *gi gong*** (གི་གོང, gi gong): the nine good and bad ways in which the mewa (སྨེ་བ, sme ba) of the life force and the rest meet. As they must be joined with the trigrams (སྤར་ཁ, spar kha), the five yellow is set in the middle, li on the nine red, and so on. For the body, the man's five yellow is made the two black and the woman's five yellow the seven red, and whichever of the sky goddess and the rest falls is examined as the root text says. To make them easy to recognise, they are examined by these nine speckled *ling tse* (ལིང་ཚེ, ling tshe):

[The drawing: nine frames, each with its wheel's name in the middle and four pairs of trigrams with their mewa: the sky goddess, life, raising glory, luck gained, luck equal, harm, the five demons, the demon's cut and the body cut; the body cut's pairs li–khon, zon–dwa, gin–zin, kham–khen.]

These are three good, three middling and three bad; by these nine examine the mewa of man's and woman's life force, body, power and luck; examine also the trigram of the mother's luck. Further, "the hidden's start" is the fight of the progressed signs (ལོག་མིན, log min) of man and woman at birth: of it, the outer is the progressed signs counted at the birth of the son and the bride from the mother's progressed sign, wherever they fall, the two fought; the middle is counted up and down from wood's rat and ox, or properly from the rat and ox of each one's own element: for the son, from the rat … (p. 525 goes on).

The notes on the match's last points, from the eleventh (the trigram) to the twelve throne doors.

… is looked at. The luck gained (ཕྱ་ལོན, phya lon) and luck equal (ཕྱ་མཉམ, phya mnyam) reckoned as one, the trigrams can be called "four good"; taken apart, nine come, good, bad and middling.

**The in-laws.** "If the in-laws' trigrams fight, it is bad": the «གནས་མ», that is the daughter-in-law (མནའ་མ, mna' ma), and the «སྐྱུག་མོ» (as read above), that is the son's mother, the mother-in-law: if the birth trigrams of the two meet as the demon's cut (བདུད་གཅོད, bdud gcod) and the like and fight, it is bad.

**The broken line** (རབས་ཆད, rabs chad): the husband's birth trigram zin and the bride a sheep-year woman, a wood sheep or another, then, the «ཤིད་དུར» (Mipham's print «ཤིང་དུར», wood's grave) being the sheep, it is bad; and so on for the rest. "The unchanging crown" (གཙུག་མི་འགྱུར་བ, gtsug mi 'gyur ba): when any one of these four particular years-with-trigrams that come together occurs for both man and woman; so, one a wood tiger born on zin and the other a fire horse born on li, that fault strikes; both wood tigers born on zin, the same. The eight "beckoning hands" (ལག་གཡབ, lag g.yab) are alike: a husband born on gin in a bird or monkey year, the earth lords harm the wife; earth's four-executioner born on zin is a bride too; "born on khon, little merit" is either the husband or the widow. The mewa are clear in the root text. Of the body cut and the like too, know that good and bad come from the elements fighting within as enemies.

**Rise and decline** (དར་གུད, dar gud, the thirteenth point): for the son's and bride's life force and the other three, divide own, mother, son, friend and enemy, take breath in the general way and carry it to the birth year for the life force, the birth month for the body and so on, examined as in the section of the karma reckoning (ལས་རྩིས་སྐབས་ལྟར); to each of the four apply the taking of breath, the inner joining and the rest. The root rise and decline takes breath in the general way and is carried to the son's and bride's years: the man's rise and decline carried onto the bride's year, the horse, the bride's onto the groom's year.

**The karma reckoning over the match.** "Further, an exception: though the marriage reckoning agrees and the match is good, when the karma reckoning (ལས་རྩིས, las rtsis) is made, 'life exhausted' is the life force bad; 'vitality exhausted' (ཟུང་ཟད, as printed), though it is identified as being old (རྒས་པར), is the body's reckoning bad; 'chronically ill', having flaws of the year and the like; 'poor', the power small in the karma reckoning; 'in danger of ill speech', the luck bad; 'of bad karmic result', the reckoning bad in general. Or else the plain sense is the poor, the old and so on, and the hidden sense (སྦས་དོན, sbas don), like it, the karma reckoning bad."

**The twelve throne doors** (ཁྲི་སྒོ་བཅུ་གཉིས, khri sgo bcu gnyis), needed for both the joining and the setting out. "Destroying the enemy's house": the sky year's life force become the enemy of the bride's life force: a rat- or pig-year woman going in an ox or sheep year, a tiger- or hare-year woman in a bird or monkey year, is good, and so on; "opening the door" is explained as sending her in that year. If it is objected that below, among the twenty brides of misfortune (བྱུར་ཐག, byur thag), this is called the ladle and so contradicts: here the existence of a bride's road (བག་ལམ, bag lam) is shown in general, below an exception, a little weak for the father's name; such differences between the earlier and the later appear in a few other places too. The month's enemy house: the bride goes on the executioner, the enemy, of the month she was born in, a tiger-month bride sent in a bird or monkey month; the day, that month's life-force enemy, a tiger month going on a bird or monkey day; that the tiger with the monkey and the hare with the bird are good is plain in the root text. The bride's trigrams dwa and zin are Saturn: when the inner falls on dwa or zin the inner planet is Saturn, and the outer progressed sign, though not reversed, does not pass beyond the tiger, horse and dog, so the outer and inner planets being Saturn, she is sent to zin, the enemy trigram of the earth element, and so on. "No meaning of this is explained, but sending onto the enemy seems one point of certainly obstructing, as when a pale arrow comes one escapes by stepping aside: so I think, my own idea." "Taking the house of wealth" (ནོར་ཁྱིམ་བླང་བ, nor khyim blang ba): the couple meeting as seventh-corners, which may be joined if their power meets as mother and son, as when one of the man and woman (p. 527 goes on: is a water horse and the other a wood rat).
