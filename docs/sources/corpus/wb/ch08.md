Chapter eight: the *dus sbyor* (the "union of times").

Two stanzas of praise. The *Dbyangs 'char* tantra, the peak of all treatises, has no splendour like the light of the *makura* (a jewel), yet it can show the visible and the becoming (*snang srid*) clearly: for the sake of the favour of the translator Glo bo lo tsā (Glo bo Lo tsā ba, the translator of Glo), I bow down. Then, under the figure "ornament of the cause of the reasoning": the "special ornament", the quality-lacking pure good exposition drawn by Keta-ka; the clear-water store of the *dus sbyor*, never before understood in its hard points of the stainless tradition of the *Dus 'khor dbyangs 'char*, set to appear like the moon to the eyes of memory.

The measure of the day, by the houses (the twelve signs, *khyim*). The verses give, sign by sign, the measure of the day as hours (*chu tshod*) and weights (*srang*), each in the number-word code with the editors' bracketed figures: "Fish and Maiden (Pisces, Virgo): day and night alike, 'sky, fire' hours (30)"; then "hours 'moon, benefit', *srang* 'direction' (10)" for the Ram and the Lion, "hours 'tooth' (*so*), *srang* 'sky, eye' (32)" for the Crab (*kartaka*), "hours 'benefit, peak', *srang* 'dot, fire' (33)" for the Pair (Gemini), "hours 'wealth, hand', *srang* 'sky, arrow' (35)" for the Jar (Aquarius) and a second sign, "hours 'Thub-pa, eye', *srang* 'sky, ocean' (46)" for the Sea-creature (Capricorn) and the Scorpion (*sdig pa*), "hours 'boundary, eye', *srang* 'sky, fire' (36)" for the Archer. These measures of the day are cleared from "dot, boundary" (60): each gives the night's measure. (How the number-word strings fall on the signs, and what the editors' bracketed figures stand for, was not settled on the page: see Open.)

Then the true-day way for the tithi being reckoned: the mansion-place of the pure *grub rtsis* sun is multiplied by "sky, taste" (60); the lower hours are put above; "arrow, benefit, moon" (125) obtained is the elapsed house; the remainder is multiplied by "sky, fire" (30); "arrow, benefit, moon" (135) obtained, the elapsed days of the house, the "seen-first" (*ngo thog*) house; the remainder of all the places is multiplied by "sky, taste" (60), wrapped by the wheel, divided by "arrow, benefit, moon" (135): what is obtained is the "stick" (*dbyug*, the *srang*); the remainders by "sky, taste" (60) gives *srang*; again by "time" (6) the breath; the remainder by "mountain, mountain" (77), all places multiplied and wrapped by the wheel, divided by 135: this is the fractions; no remainder appears. This pure way, though it was lost in the middle, was spread by the diligence of the learned. This is the movement of the house in meaning: add to the elapsed day (the above) "mountain, mountain" (77) subtract; for the "not-risen" (*ma ldangs*) house of the same, the wheel "12" is added; the remainder is the elapsed-house moon's count, taken as the "seen-first" own house; this is for ease of working. The remainder-day by which "mountain" (7) is reduced is put in two places, both multiplied by "eye" (2), the lower divided by "time, mountain" (67); the upper by "dot, taste" (60); of the *srang* obtained: of the "seen-first" house, the Scales etc. are cast away for the day of going south (*lto bgrod*) and the Archer etc. are added for the day of going north (*byang bgrod*); the day's measure of that day is without error; with it "dot, taste" (60) is cleared: the night's measure is understood.
