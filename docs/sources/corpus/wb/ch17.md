Chapter 17. The analysis of the three kinds of day.

Homage. To the cloud of knowledge of the compassion of Jam-mgon, crest of good exposition; to the Phugpa translator who turns difficult points into verse in tens of thousands, a victor on the crown ("the one who has the intention", in brackets). Whatever was said of all the difficult points of the analysis of the three days gladdens the learned and the author too; adorned here with the oral instructions of the tradition, it is told so that the company of the young of keen memory may rejoice ("on the words", in brackets).

To give understanding of the analysis of the tithi-day (ཚེས་ཞག, tshes zhag), the solar day (ཉིན་ཞག, nyin zhag) and the house-day (ཁྱིམ་ཞག, khyim zhag) as the tenets agree, a little is said to clear the ear. First, each is identified. When each of the fifteen parts of the moon's waxing and waning is complete, it is a tithi-day. From seeing the lines of the hand at rising to seeing the lines of the hand the next time is one span of the solar day. The time in which the sun moves through one house (sign), divided into thirty, is called a house-day. Each of the planets has its own division of years, months and days. The sun's 360 days are one house-year, or what is fixed as the number of the circle of house-days; from this all the rest is understood.

The sun's house-circle: "sky, season, fire" (0, 6, 3: 360). Of the two places set down, the lower is multiplied by "eye" (2), and the wealth (nor, the quotient) gained by "arrow-taste" (65) joined to the upper is the day (zhag po). The remainder is multiplied by "sky-juncture" (60); what "arrow-taste" (65) gains is the hour (chu tshod). From "sky-taste" (60), "season" (6) up to "desire" (13), multiplied in turn, the gain makes the tithi-circle.

The tithi-circle's place of days is multiplied by "zero-taste" (60), the hours are put in, and again in turn by "sky-taste" (60), "season" (6) and "bodiless" (13), each with its own part put in; the sum is multiplied by "mountain-sky-mountain" (707) and is 73,668,268,800. Of three places set down, the lower is what dividing by "thub-sky-mountain" (707) gives; the middle takes what "water-taste" (64) gains; the upper is the remainder of the subtraction, 72,515,574,000. What is left from dividing by "mountain-sky" (707) is the divisor's part; with the wealth made "desire" (13), the remainder carried over, the desired part, is found; the remainder from dividing that wealth by "season" (6) is the breaths; the remainder from dividing by "sky-taste" (60) is the chu srang; what "sky-juncture" (60) gains is the hour. That wealth is the place of the day, and so the circle of the solar day is made.

Then the way of seeking the time's enjoyment (dus kyi longs spyod) of the days of each circle: of the two ways, the way that comes out in order (lugs 'byung) and the way that turns back (lugs ldog), the way that turns back, not in order, is this: 21,600 breaths, "season" (6) and "zero-taste" (60) are gathered into hours; (the sentence goes on over the page).

The year-lord (lo bdag) and minister (blon po): the number of years in between up to what is reckoned is multiplied by its own multiplier, gathered by each circle, and the turned-back year's making-year (lo 'phor) is cleared; thus the reckoned year is shown clearly.
