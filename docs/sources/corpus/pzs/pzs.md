## <a id="pzs-i102-103"></a>pzs:i102-103 The Svapnodhyāya of the Peerzada volume: "Bṛhaspati said", verses 1–4 with the spans of the watches

*Read: scan, 2026-11-09 (RD277), PDF pages 102–103 of the item at 260 dpi and the span lines at 400 dpi; the text beyond verse 4 and the other leaves (PDF pages 104–106 and 3–10) not read.*

> ॐ श्रीगणेशाय नमः ॥ बृहस्पतिरुवाच ॥
> ॐ स्वप्नाध्यायं प्रवक्ष्यामि यथोक्तं मुनिभिः पुरा ।
> येन विज्ञानमात्रेण ज्ञायते च शुभाशुभं ॥ १ ॥
> स्वप्नस्तु प्रथमे यामे संवत्सरफलं लभेत् ।
> द्वितीये चाष्ट[?]भिर्मासैस्त्रिभिर्मासैस्त्रियामके ॥ २ ॥
> चतुर्थे प्रह(?)रे मासेन फलं चात्र न संशयः ।
> अरुणोदयवेलायां दशाहेन फलं लभेत् ॥ ३ ॥
> वातिकं पैत्तिकं … श्लैष्मिकं चिंतितं … ।
> अतः ऊर्ध्वं प्रवक्ष्यामि पुण्यापुण्यफलं शृणु ॥ ४ ॥

PDF page 102 is the first page of the text, written in black ink with the headings in red, in a large Devanagari (a Kashmir hand), within a ruled frame; the line before it closes a hymn ("iti śrī-kāśīkhaṇḍa-vibhūti-stuti-sampūrṇā", the end of a Kāśīkhaṇḍa hymn) and is followed by "oṃ śrī-gaṇeśāya namaḥ | bṛhaspatir uvāca". Page 103 continues the verse at its second line (the page break falls inside "dvitīye cāṣṭ-|…bhir māsais"). The digitiser's list of the volume's contents puts the *Svapnodhyāya* at folios 67–72, "verses 1–13 on PDF pages 102–106, verse 14 to the end on pages 3–10" (the leaves were bound out of order).

What the passage says, in our words:

- **Bṛhaspati is the speaker** ("bṛhaspatir uvāca"), which is the attribution of the Penn colophons (pn1871: "bṛhaspatyukta-svapnādhyāyaḥ") and of Negelein's i ("bṛhaspatiproktaṃ svapnādhyāyam", neg:p32); the verse that opens the chapter says "I shall tell the chapter of dreams as it was said by the sages of old; by merely knowing which the good and the bad are known".
- **The series**: a dream of the first watch bears fruit in a year; of the second, in eight months (the first syllable of "aṣṭabhir" lies at the page break and is not clear); of the third watch, in three months; of the fourth watch, in **one month** ("māsena phalaṃ cātra na saṃśayaḥ": "here is no doubt that its fruit comes in a month"); at the dawn's red in ten days. It is the pn1871 and pn673 series, in a third hand; the early-morning "at once" line is not among the four verses.
- Verse 4 sets aside the dreams of wind, bile and phlegm and the dream of what was thought of, then turns to "the fruit of merit and demerit" (*puṇyāpuṇyaphala*), the list of good and bad dreams.

Doubts: the "prahare" of the fourth line is not clear (the glyph reads "pra-ha-re" or "pra-ḍa-re", and is not needed for the span); the first words of verse 4 are read only in part; the second verse line's "aṣṭa" is split over two pages.

## <a id="pzs-i102-103b"></a>pzs:i102-103b The Srinagar Svapnodhyāya again at full size: the third watch triyāmake, the fourth prahare

*Read: scan, 2026-11-10 (RD312), leaves n101 and n102 at full size.*

> Peerzada volume, Srinagar, PDF pages 102–103 (leaves n101, n102 of the Internet Archive item), the last lines of p. 102 and the lines of p. 103 on the spans, read at full size:
>
> n101, lines 6–7: स्वप्नस्तु प्रथमे यामे संवत्सरफलं लभेत् ॥ द्वितीये चाष्[ट…]
>
> n102, lines 1–6: …ष्टभिर्मासैस्त्रिभिर्मासैस्त्रियामके ॥ चतुर्थे प्रहरे मासेन फलं चात्र न संशयः ॥ अरुणोदयवेलायां दशाहेन फलं लभेत् ॥ ३ ॥ वातिकं पैत्तिकं चैव श्लैष्मिकं चापि चिन्तितम् ॥ अतः ऊर्ध्वं प्रवक्ष्यामि पुण्यापुण्यफलं शृणु ॥ ४ ॥ कथ्यते …

"A dream of the first watch bears fruit in a year; in the second with eight months; in the third watch, three months; in the fourth watch, in a month, there is no doubt here; at dawn's red the fruit comes in ten days (3). [The dreams] of wind, of bile, of phlegm and the thought one: hereafter I shall tell the fruit of merit and demerit, hear (4)."

Read again on the full-size images: the page break falls inside "aṣṭabhir"; the words "triyāmake" and "caturthe prahare" are clear in the Kashmir hand (the third glyph of "triyāmake" a plain ya); there is no "pakṣa" and no "trimāsake" in this copy. The first letter of each half-verse closes with the red double stroke; the numerals ३ and ४ are red.
