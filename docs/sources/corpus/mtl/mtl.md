## <a id="mtl-p2"></a>mtl:p2 The 2015 almanac of the Tsurphu reckoning: the first Karmapa Dus gsum mkhyen pa's day on the 3rd of the earth-mouse month, the reckoners' middle winter month

*Read: etext, 2026-11-24 (RD410; BDRC search snippet, BDRC's transliteration).*

> «དུས་གསུམ་མཁྱེན་པའི་དུས་མཆོད» (the phrase searched): rtsis pa'i dgun 'bring po sa pho byi ba'i zla ba chung ba/_zhing 'brog sde dmangs phal gyis sprel zla zer/_tshes_3_la karma pa dang po dus gsum mkhyen pa'i dus mchod dang*/_tshes_12_nyin nyi ma chu srin khyim du 'byor/

The reckoners' middle winter month (dgun 'bring po), the male earth mouse month, a short month, which most farmers and nomads call the monkey month; on the 3rd the offering of the time (dus mchod) of the first Karmapa, Dus gsum mkhyen pa; on the 12th the sun enters the house of the makara. Which Hor month this is the snippet does not say; the mouse month is the 11th in the White Beryl's model almanac (wb.1:p154-171).
