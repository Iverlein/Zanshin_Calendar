## <a id="lt-p8"></a>lt:p8 The mewa of the four aspects, and every mewa in the reckoned person's sequence

*Read: scan, 2026-10-23.*

> …ཡར་བཞི་སྲོག་ལ་མར་བཞི་དབང་། །གང་སྐྱེས་ལུས་སྨེ་མཐུན་གསུམ་ཀླུང་། …སྐྱེས་སྨེ་ཉིད་ལ་ངོས་འཛིན་བྱ། །དེ་ཡང་བརྩི་བྱ་སྨེ་འཕྲེང་གར། །གནས་པ་དེ་བསྟུན་གཞན་ལ་འགྲེ། །…

LT, the first branch, the mewa of the four aspects (p. 8, l. 2–7; scan I1KG21886 img. 016, read by eye; the etext, p. 16, has «རླུང» for the print's «ཀླུང»). Only the lines that state the rule; between them stand four examples by the luck's triads with their mewa numbers in brackets, not quoted.

The fourth [mewa] upward is the life force's, the fourth downward the power's; the mewa of one's birth is the body's; the agreeing three [the triad of years that share a luck] give the luck's … identify them from the birth mewa itself; and as the one reckoned stands in the mewa sequence (སྨེ་འཕྲེང, sme 'phreng), so the others are applied in agreement with it.

So LT makes the birth mewa (སྐྱེས་སྨེ, skyes sme) the body mewa (ལུས་སྨེ, lus sme), derives the life force's and the power's from it by four places, and takes every other mewa in the same mewa sequence as the one reckoned. That is the rule the notes' circles need (WB vol. 2, p. 533; question 368): the body mewa is the birth year's mewa, and a starting point's mewa "that agrees with it in the mewa sequence" is that year's mewa in the reckoned person's own sequence (claim mewa-sequence-agreeing).
