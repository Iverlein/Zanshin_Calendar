LT, the marriage reckoning, its second section, the road (ལམ་རྩིས, lam rtsis) of the bride's going: printed pp. 38–41 (etext pp. 46–49; BDRC scan I1KG21886, img. 046–049), read by eye on the scan, 2026-10-26, on the lines quoted only; a modern work, so quoted no further than its facts need. LT's verses follow Kun bzang blo gros's supplement to the *Moonbeams* closely (kd.3:p634, kd.3:p641-642), and its numbered heads are the supplement's ten ways with the twenty, the sky dog and the persons needed set out as heads of their own.

A verse names what the road is reckoned by (p. 38): the *kham yu*, the twenty *byur thag*, the trigram, the mewa, the *'dom sbrags*, the year, the month, the date and the day, the hour, the planet and mansion, the earth lords and the directions, and in particular the sky dog's own reckoning, and the persons needed. Taken one by one and numbered to the sixteenth (pp. 38–41), the eleventh is the bad earth lords: "the black day, the poor day, *gi gang*, *pi ling*, *zin phung* and the like: flee the fierce kind of earth lord" (p. 40); the thirteenth the directions, the good ones (*gnam sman*, *srog 'tsho*, *dpal bskyed*, *phywa yon*) to be taken and the others shunned; the fourteenth the sky dog, given at length (p. 41). The list ends (p. 41) with the three that cannot be mended, «བཅོས་མི་ནུས»: the mansion *lha mtshams*, the weekday Mars and the sky year's fire rising; and with what is done when the going must happen though it is not allowed: the Buddha's relics and other supports of great blessing, the *Mañjuśrī-nāmasaṃgīti*, and so on.

What it gives: the earth lords are the eleventh of the numbered heads, avoided ("flee"), with no rank among the heads. The only grade LT gives among them is that three, a mansion, a weekday and the sky year, cannot be mended; no earth lord is among them.
