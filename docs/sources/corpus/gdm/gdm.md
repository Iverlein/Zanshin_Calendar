(img. 88) Homage and aim. With the head I rely on the lama, the gathering of all the refuges. As taught in the Mother's (མ་གཅིག, ma gcig) profound Chöd visualisation collection (གཅོད་ཀྱི་དམིགས་འབུམ, gcod kyi dmigs 'bum), the sorting of the good and bad fruits of night dreams is shown here. Not to be examined when the dreamer is tormented by illness, in grief, or when awareness is disturbed.

The thirds of the night. The night is divided into thirds of the watch (ཐུན, thun). The dusk third (སྲོད, srod) is the earlier imprint (བག་ཆགས, bag chags); the middle third (གུང, gung) is the magic display of demons (བདུད, bdud) and flesh-eaters (སྲིན, srin); the dawn third (ཐོ་རངས, tho rangs) is what makes clear what comes later. What comes at dusk is mostly earlier deeds, attachment, places and companions, food, talk, near kin, the last three days' imprint, above all what is fixed in the mind; the imprint arising, it is dreamed again and again; some is the deeds of former lives; nothing is fixed in it and all kinds come. In the middle third spirits, flesh-eaters, asuras and demons move about, so whatever appears, good or bad, is the magic display of the mind's one-pointedness being upset; the wise say nothing is fixed in it. (img. 89) In the dawn third of the night good and bad fruit come, so examining it is prized.

Good (img. 89-90). Climbing a high mountain top: a great position. A cloudless sun rising: happiness. Crops, grain and plant sap flourishing, food and clothes found, treasure enjoyed: grain and wealth certain. Fine clothes: praise and honour from others. Armour: illness and spirits cannot stay. A fine hat: good for lama and lord. Sitting in a fine house: auspicious signs. A weapon in the hand: enemies do no harm. Ornaments: fame. A river crossed, arrival on the far side in comfort: the best, every wish fulfilled. Music, great drum and conch blown and struck: renown on earth. Riding a lion, tiger, kyilin or dragon and running upward: a high position. Wealth found, honour; sitting on a throne: glory and power grow. Drinking sweet water: long life. In short, fine forms, savoury food, pleasant words, friends met, good deeds, as dreamed, and if on waking the mind is at ease and glad, a glad fruit for certain; blessing, long life, no illness, wealth grows (the last words are not settled in the machine reading).

For practitioners (img. 90). Repeatedly falling into abysses, going up and down over steep ground, entering a very narrow path, going toward sorrow, gloom and fear: the karmic obscurations of the way to liberation, as the Blessed One said in a sūtra.

Bad (img. 90-91). Blizzard and mire crossed and sunk in, running on unclean ground, stinking clothes, filth, finding pollution and defilement. Much golden sand enjoyed, riding a donkey without a saddle (the word «རྒ» as the machine reads it, not settled), nakedness: the result of a work planned earlier. Clothes without a collar, no hat, looking in a mirror, going on a holiday: a dream of sorrow. Rags, filth eaten and drunk, creatures on the body, wolves seizing: illness. Barley, peas and empty vessels, pieces of bow and arrow: gossip soon. Arrows in the body, chased by an army, meat-light and fire-light (the word before the first «འོད» is not settled), hail falling, going into a pit: others' sorcery. Bound, going underground, imprisoned, the head cut off: harm from spirits sent and suppression. Naked on a donkey going south, red flowers: an obstacle to life ahead.

Whose display (img. 91-92). A lone white man, a white bird soaring, snow mountains and the like: the display of the gods. An old ruined temple, a fox, a small child: the 'gong po. Snakes, frogs, a blue-green girl: the nāgas. Rocks, plants, horsemen, soldiers: the btsan. An ash-coloured child, cliffs and ravines: the the'u rang. Monks, a donkey, a monkey, a cat, a horse, a dog: the rgyal po. If these give food, speak agreeably, laugh and look kindly, a sign of joy; if they show fearful forms, scowl and rage, a sign of non-virtue, the wise say. A dream without imprint and very wondrous, glad on waking: good fruit for certain; not glad on waking: bad; terror: a demon's trick; a marvel repeated: the power of mantra or of the eight classes, to be told apart by the wise.

For the one who meditates (img. 92-93). Finding a book, going in the sky, climbing a tree, reciting verses, debate and questions and eloquence, pleasant gardens, flowers, fruit and leaves, music, a jewelled sword in the hand, fine clothes and ornaments: the sign of one's own practice reaching its mark. Lands and houses never seen, lovely boys and girls smiling and giving flowers, fruit, food and drink: the qualities of realisation, great and small, arising by the gods. A boat made, a bridge built, a vessel found or a lost one found, sun and moon rising, darkness clearing: good for helpers and disciples. Rain from cloud, staying in water, a great flood suddenly falling: things found.

Omens of death in dreams (img. 93). A black woman with the entrails (the machine reads «ཐལ་བ», ashes, where KBS has «བྲལ་བ», parted from), ashes, climbing a mountain to a stupa; a camel facing south, a donkey east, a monkey facing north ridden away; red clothes worn, red flowers, going over a plain, the head bound: dreaming this one dies; drinking beer and dancing with the dead: these are the death omens of the examination of dreams. Then follows the examination of the signs of death by body, speech and mind (not given here).
