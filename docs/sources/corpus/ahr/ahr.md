## <a id="ahr-p436"></a>ahr:p436 Śārīrasthāna 6.62–64 in the Nirṇayasāgara edition: the cows' release (gosarge), with the variants of the page

*Read: scan, 2026-11-10 (RD312), leaf n622 of the item at full size, the verse lines and the foot-notes in crops of 2×.*

> Aṣṭāṅgahṛdaya, Śārīrasthāna 6, printed p. 436 (leaf n622 of the Internet Archive item Ashtanga.Hridaya.of.Vagbhata), left column, verses 62–63 and the beginning of 64, as the print sets the lines:
>
> तेष्वाद्या निष्फलाः पञ्च यथास्वप्रकृतिर्दिवा ।
> विस्मृतो दीर्घह्रस्वोऽति
> पूर्वरात्रे चिरात्फलम् ॥ ६२ ॥
> दृष्टः करोति तुच्छं च
> गोसर्गे तदहर्महत् ।
> निद्रया वाऽनुपहतः प्रतीपैर्वचनैस्तथा ॥ ६३ ॥
> याति पापोऽल्पफलतां दानहोमजपादिभिः ।
>
> The commentary under the verse, on the cows' line (the same column): गोसर्गे—गवां मोक्षकाले, यो दृष्टः स्वप्नः स तदहः—तस्मिन्नेवाहनि, फलं महत्करोतीति योज्यम् ।
>
> The variant readings printed under the text of this page (the notes number 1 to 17, with the letters of the manuscripts अ इ ट ड ण त स म): 11 'वा' अ, 12 'न स्वकीय फ' ड, 13 'थाभाषित ददाति' ट, 14 'चानु' अ इ ड त, 15 'स्वप्नो यतोऽनिष्टनिद्रया चानु' ड, 16 'स सम्पूर्णफल' ड, 17 'पोऽप्यफल' इ.

Śārīrasthāna 6, vv. 62–63 and the opening of 64, in the Nirṇayasāgara edition of 1939 (Paraḍkar's, with Aruṇadatta and Hemādri). "Of these the first five are fruitless; as one's own nature; by day; forgotten; too long or too short; in the early night, the fruit late (62). Seen [at the hour of] the cows' release it makes it great that very day, if the sleep is not broken and no contrary words are spoken; the evil dream turns to a small fruit by gifts, burnt offerings and recitations (63–64)" (the print divides the half-lines so that the verse numbers stand after "pratīpair vacanais tathā"; the translation follows the sense).

The cows' line reads "gosarge" in the print, and no variant mark stands on that word. The marks of the page lie elsewhere: 11 on the "ca" of "tucchaṃ ca" in the line before it (manuscript अ reads "vā"), 12 on "kālena" and 13 on "yathā-paribhāṣitaṃ" in the commentary of v. 62, 14 on the "vā" of "nidrayā vā 'nupahataḥ" (four manuscripts, अ इ ड त, read "cānu", "and not broken"), 15 and 16 in the commentary on that half-verse, 17 on "pāpo 'lpaphalatāṃ" (इ "pāpo 'pyaphalatāṃ"). (Eye reading of the marks over the words on the page image at 2×; the notes at the foot are legible.) So the manuscripts of this edition give no other word for "gosarge": a "gomārge" or "gomarge" is not in its apparatus.

The commentary printed under the verse (the gloss of Aruṇadatta in Kunte's print is the same, gavāṃ mokṣakāle): "gosarge: at the time of the release of the cows; the dream that is seen then, that day (tadahaḥ), in that very day, makes a great fruit: so it is to be joined."
