> རྒྱ་གར་སྐད་དུ། སརྦ་མཎྜལ་སཱ་མཱ་ནྱ་བི་དྷི་ན་གུ་ཧྱ་ཏནྟྲ། བོད་སྐད་དུ། དཀྱིལ་འཁོར་ཐམས་ཅད་ཀྱི་སྤྱིའི་ཆོ་ག་གསང་བའི་རྒྱུད།

The text stands at etext [p284]-[p338] and is followed at [p338] by Toh 807; the etext's heads {D806} and {D807} give the Derge numbers. Etext, 2026-11-10.

Etext pages [p295]-[p297] of the Derge Kanjur's Common Tantra (Toh 806, the Sarvamaṇḍalasāmānyavidhiguhyatantra, "the secret tantra of the general rite of all mandalas", Spyi rgyud), in the rite of the pupils' entering: after the Dharma has been taught to the pupils, the lying down, the dawn question, the long list of good dream signs, and the remedy of the bad.

[p295] Having taught them to the pupils, have them lie down on the ground spread with kusha (ཀུ་ཤ, ku sha) grass, the pillow side toward the east (the etext's «སྔས་ལྟས», where the Sngags rim chen mo quotes «སྔས་བལྟས»; see the claim on the wording). Then, when risen at dawn (ནང་པར, nang par), ask what dream was seen; having heard it, good or not good, let it be told without doubt. [p296] The good signs: seeing a Buddha is like seeing the ocean of qualities before one; a stupa (མཆོད་རྟེན, mchod rten) or an image; beginning offerings to them; hearing the Dharma from monks or from another, practising the Dharma, reading a book; seeing the Sangha or a single monk, talking with him, or becoming a monk; seeing monasteries and nuns; the Buddha's sons; parents, the lama, friends of every kind: all auspicious; gaining a knowledge-mantra (རིག་སྔགས, rig sngags) and accomplishment; taking up the trainings; joys of many kinds; waterfalls, the sea, mountains, elephants: declared good. Seeing kings is auspicious; ascetics and brahmins whose way accords with the Sugata's; the rich, noble men; a cow, a horse, a swan, a lion, deer; auspicious birds; gold and treasures, jewels and silver, wealth of many kinds, in short all kinds of sources of wealth; weapons, grains, fruits, flowers, many kinds of ornament; food, clothes; children and youths, beautiful women; or receiving and touching and speaking with them; being consecrated; the riches of having become a king; becoming a general; winning in battle and killing enemies; being with relatives; seeing gods; riding a mount or a chariot; climbing a mountain or a roof; wonders of many kinds; making a fire offering of fuel sticks in the fire; [p297] entering good acts; swimming in water and crossing a gorge; punishing thieves; defeating the strong; wrestling and standing; seeing games of many kinds, practising feats: all auspicious things, seen, are declared very virtuous. Hearing the rite of secret mantra; seeing a festival of virtue; beginning a good sādhana; seeing the practitioners; and of such gods many kinds of virtue: let them be examined with the mind and the opposite avoided. The virtuous will be accomplished, the non-virtuous will not; knowing non-virtues, the wise avoid them. The signs of the virtuous are best, middle and lowest, and the three accomplishments of secret mantra, best, middle, lowest, are accomplished. For the non-virtuous (the signs), the pacifying fire offering (ཞི་བའི་སྦྱིན་སྲེག, zhi ba'i sbyin sreg) with the pacifying secret mantra, with butter from a cow, one hundred and eight times, is to be done to pacify according to the rite. Then, for the pupils free of sin and wholly purified, in order to be kind to them, let them take what they wish.

No watch of the night, no day of the month and no season is named in these lines: the dream is asked at dawn, whenever it was seen, and is sorted by what it shows.
