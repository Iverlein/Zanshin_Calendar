## <a id="pn687-i1"></a>pn687:i1 The first text page of the Svapnādhyāyastotra of Item 687: six months for the second watch, the fourth watch added in the margin

*Read: scan, 2026-11-10 (RD357), on the full-size page image.*

> (margin above the first line, in a second hand:) यामे चतुर्थे मासैकं … पूर्वकं ॥ २ ॥ (last words not read)
> ॥ श्रीगणेशाय नमः ॥ स्वप्नाध्यायं प्रवक्ष्यामि यथोक्तं गुरुभाषितं ।
> स्वप्नास्तु प्रथमे यामे संवत्सरविपाकिनः ॥ १ ॥
> षड्भिर्मासैर्द्वितीये तु त्रिभिर्मासैस्तृतीयके । (the omission mark ˇ after तृतीयके)
> अरुणोदयवेलायां दशाहेन फलं भवेत् ॥ २ ॥
> अत ऊर्ध्वं प्रवक्ष्यामि पुण्यपापफलं शृणु ।
> तच्छ्रुत्वा सर्वपापेभ्यो मुच्यते नात्र संशयः ॥ ३ ॥
> आरोहणं गोवृषकुञ्जरा[णां] …

The first text page of Item 687 (ff. 1v–2r, canvas 1, upper page; two leaves in all, the title in the catalogue "Svapnādhyāyastotra"). After the salutation: "I shall tell the chapter on dreams as it was spoken, as the teacher said it." Verse 1: "Dreams of the first watch ripen in a year." Verse 2: "in the second watch, in **six** months; in the third, in three months" — and here a caret in the line shows that something was left out and a line written above the first line of the page, in a second hand and with the verse number 2, says "in the fourth watch, one month ..." (its last words, "... pūrvakam" or similar, are not read). Then "at the dawn's red, in ten days"; then "Henceforth I shall tell the fruit of merit and of sin, hear; having heard it one is freed from all sins, there is no doubt here" (v. 3); then the list of dreams begins ("climbing a cow, a bull, an elephant ...").
So this copy has the opening of the Atharvaveda-pariśiṣṭa's verse ("svapnās tu prathame yāme saṃvatsara-vipākinaḥ", apv:p445) and **six months** for the second watch, as the Matsya and the Agni have; its copyist had left out the fourth watch and the corrector put it back as one month. It has no cows' release line before the dawn line in the first three verses and the dawn's span is ten days.
Doubts: the end of the marginal line; "ṣaḍbhir" (the conjunct ḍbh and the ra are plain at 3×, the whole word clear); the verse numbers 1 to 3 are the scribe's and are plain; the rest of the page was not read through.
