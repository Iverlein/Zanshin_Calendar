## <a id="bgs-i658"></a>bgs:i658 Householders take the one-day fast on the full-moon and new-moon days (nya stong) and the four great occasions

*Read: etext, 2026-11-21.*

> ཁྱིམ་པ་རྣམས་ཀྱིས་ཉ་སྟོང་བརྒྱད་གསུམ་དང་། ས་ཆེན་བཞི་གཙོ་བོར་གྱུར་པའི་དུས་ལ་བླང་ཞིང་

"Householders take [the one-day vow of the eight-limbed fast, the fast, བསྙེན་གནས, bsnyen gnas] on the full-moon and new-moon days (ཉ་སྟོང, nya stong) 'eight, three' (བརྒྱད་གསུམ, brgyad gsum, the words as the etext has them) and on the times in which «ས་ཆེན་བཞི» (as the etext has it; the sense is probably the four great occasions, དུས་ཆེན་བཞི) are chief, and keep it..."

Bden gnyis shing rta, Gangtok print, the etext's [p658] = image 658 (printed p. 650), within the exposition of the lay vow of the one-day fast (བསྙེན་གནས, bsnyen gnas) after the Abhidharmakosa-bhasya and the *Suhrllekha*, the page before (p657) naming the eight limbs of the fast (གསོ་སྦྱོང་ཡན་ལག་བརྒྱད་པ, gso sbyong yan lag brgyad pa). The words of the clause are those of the etext (the OCR of this print is poor); on the scan (a 1595 px strip, read at 4x) the sentence begins «ཁྱིམ་པ་རྣམས་ཀྱིས་ཉ་སྟོང་» in the second line from the end of the first third of the page, but «བརྒྱད་གསུམ» and «ས་ཆེན་བཞི» were not made out syllable by syllable;  «ས་ཆེན་བཞི» is the etext's reading and is not corrected here. The page names no day of the month by number: the days are «ཉ་སྟོང» and the unexplained «བརྒྱད་གསུམ». Read in the etext, the scan only partly.
