## <a id="kgo-p1"></a>kgo:p1 The Karmapa's 2023 conference on the anniversaries of Marpa, Milarepa and Gampopa: the dates not yet settled

*Read: etext, 2026-11-24 (RD410; read in the headed browser).*

The Kagyu Gunchoe of 24-26 January 2023 held a three-day conference to establish correctly the anniversaries of Marpa, Milarepa and Gampopa, with Tsurphu Labrang as sponsor and the historian Tashi Tsering among the speakers. The Karmapa said that the accounts' differing dates may come from different calendars; that in many old sources the months are named by the animals of the Chinese zodiac, and the numbered "Hor months" probably came in with Chögyal Phagpa in the 13th century; that months were also reckoned by the moon's place among the mansions; and that which calendar was used in the forefathers' time has to be settled before the dates can be fixed. He called the conference a starting point, not the finish: no date was fixed there. The page gives no date for any of the three.
