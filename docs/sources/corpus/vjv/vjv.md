It follows the verse that rouses joy (the same verse as snr:p367-368, at [p229]-[p230]).

[p230] "...(the verse) is heard by having them listen. 'To be born' is by having entered the mandala. 'The Great Vehicle itself, greatly arising supremely through (the) lineage': that which has it. Then, having guarded them with the whirling mantra (འཁྱིལ་པའི་སྔགས, 'khyil pa'i sngags; the Sngags rim chen mo spells འཁྱིལ་བ), the whirling mantra is given for recitation; saying "tell me the dream at dawn" they are made to lie down in the lion's posture inside the mandala house, or at the edge of the mandala ground, or cut off by a curtain, or elsewhere. The teacher himself, having entered the mandala house, is to recite the whirling mantra for guarding."
