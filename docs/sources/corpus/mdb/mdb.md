(Etext of the rule-book of the Mindroling assembly, the section on what is done within each month; the etext's OCR garbles some letters, kept as read, and a bracket marks the etext's page break. The English follows the sense of each line; where the OCR leaves a word unclear the line says so.)

Within each month, the order of what is done:
First, from the dawn of the 3rd, the monks, eight from each college (grwa), go ahead with the horn and the rgya gling pipe and do the smoke offering (bsang mchod) with its accompaniments (the exact sense of «snyan gsan 'bebs pa'i dung rgya gling» and «brgyags brngan» is not settled).
On the 4th, for the commemorative offering of the Omniscient Holder of Sutra and Mantra: the two root-tantra recitations (rgyud 'don), the Guhyagarbha (Sgyu 'phrul rtsa rgyud gsang ba snying po) and the Kabgyé Deshek Düpa (Bka' brgyad bde 'dus rtsa rgyud).
On the 8th, the smoke offering as before, and the long-life rite (tshe chog) of the Deathless Heart-essence Kundü (Mchi med yang snying kun 'dus).
On the 10th, by way of the peaceful and wrathful Guru (Gu ru zhi drag), the offering of the tenth day (tshes bcu'i mchod pa) with the extended supplication, and following it the fulfilment-and-offering (bskang ba gsol mchod) chiefly of the treasure protector and the mantra guardian, of Vajrakāra Śānta (as the etext reads); the monthly tea-day (zla ja) of Smon grub pa; the Bsam pa lhun grub ma prayer.
On the 12th, for the commemoration of Khri lcam, the two recitations.
On the 14th, the rite of the Phur pa spu gri (the Phurba knife) and following it the fulfilment-and-offering chiefly of the planets (gza') and Vaiśravaṇa (Rnam sras).
On the 15th, the smoke offering as before; for the seasonal offering of the Mother (Yum), by way of a painted scroll (ras bris), the Vajrasattva accumulation-offering with the self-entry (bdag 'jug), following it the common torma and the fulfilment-and-offering of the mantra guardians; the monthly tea-day for the memorial of Rgyal tshab Drung pa [p165] [a gap in the etext before «gcig la»], the Prayer of Good Conduct (Bzang spyod) is recited, and the restoring and vow (gso sbyong) of the waxing half is done.
On the 18th, for the commemoration of the Omniscient Great One and of his younger brother, the Jina's son: with the rite of the Eight Pronouncements Deshek Düpa (Bka' brgyad bde 'dus), the fulfilment-and-offering chiefly of Phyag bzhi pa and the mothers of the charnel ground (dur khrod ma mo).
On the 21st, for the seasonal offering of Ma gcig, by way of a painted scroll, the accumulation-offering of the Deshek Kundü (Bde gshegs kun 'dus) with the self-entry, following it the fulfilment-and-offering chiefly of the great gods.
On the 22nd, for the commemoration of the Lord Lama, by way of a painted scroll, the accumulation-offering of the peaceful and wrathful (zhi khro) with the self-entry.
On the 24th, the two recitations as before.
On the 25th, by way of Lama Sangdü (Bla ma gsang 'dus), the feast offering of the tenth of the waning half (mar ngo'i tshes bcu tshogs mchod) with the extended supplication, and following it the fulfilment-and-offering chiefly of the Ma ning.
On the 29th, the rite of Yama the Slayer (Gshin rje 'joms byed) with the casting of the ritual weapon (gdab kha gtor zor), following it the torma of the Lord of Death (gshin gtor) and the common torma; and the averting of the nine (dgu bzlog) aimed at the black and the beer ordinances (nag khrims, chang khrims) and the like.
On the 8th, the 15th and the 29th (the etext's «yig» before «nyi shu dgu» is a garble), in the protectors' chapel (mgon khang) five monks each make an extra seasonal torma and fulfilment of the Dharma protectors in common (chos skyong spyi'i dus gtor bskang gso 'phar ma).
On the 8th of each half, waxing and waning, in the middle of the protectors' chapel an extra seasonal torma of the keeper (dkon gnyer) is given.
On the full-moon day (gnam gang), the rite of the Lord of Life (tshe bdag), with the two (spyi ring and spyang mo), the recitation, and the fulfilment and confession of the torma of Legs ldan, Lord of the Feast Host (tshogs kyi bdag po).
The restoring and vow of the waning half (mar ngo'i gso sbyong) is done without a break, each in its turn.

## <a id="mdb-p172"></a>mdb:p172 The second Hor month in the Mindroling rule-book: the yearly offering in remembrance of the two root lamas, from the 19th to the 29th

*Read: etext, 2026-11-24 (RD410; image 172, folio 83, fetched: the opening clause, ཧོར་ཟླ་གཉིས་པ and the two root lamas, read on the scan by eye; the rest of the passage etext).*

> ༈ དེ་ནས་ཧོར་ཟླ་གཉིས་པའི་ནང་དུ་རྩ་བའི་བླ་མ་རྣམ་པ་གཉིས་ཀྱི་དུས་དྲན་ལོ་མཆོད་ལ་དམིགས་ཚེས་བཅུ་དགུའི་དགོང་ཇའི་ཐོག་ས་བསླང་སྦྱང་བ་སྔོན་འགྲོས། རྒྱལ་ཐོ་དམ་སྲི་མནན་པ། སྒོ་བྱང་ཕུར་གདབ་སོགས་ས་ཆོག་ཚང་བ་བྱ། ཉི་ཤུའི་ཞོགས་པར་ལྷ་བུམ་སྟ་གོན། ཉེར་གཅིག་ནུབ་རྡུལ་ཚོན་གྱི་དཀྱིལ་འཁོར་བཞེངས་ཞིང་རྒྱན་འགྲེམ་པ་རྣམས་འགྲུབ་པར་མཛད་ནས། ཉེར་གཉིས་ནས་བཟུང་ཞོགས་པ་རིག་འཛིན་མཆོད་པའི་དབང་ལེན་པའི་གསོལ་འདེབས་ཀྱི་གོང་དུ་སྙིང་ཐིག་བརྒྱུད་པའི་ཕྱག་འཚལ་སྦྱར་བ་སྔོན་དུ་འགྲོ་བས་ཐུགས་རྗེ་ཆེན་པོ་བདེ་གཤེགས་ཀུན་འདུས་ཀྱི་སྒྲུབ་མཆོད་བདག་འཇུག་ཚོགས་གཏོར་བཀའ་སྲུང་ལྷ་ཆེན་གཙོར་གྱུར་གྱི་བསྐང་བཤགས་གསོལ་མཆོད་རྣམས་སྒྲུབ་ཞག་བརྒྱད་ཀྱི་བར་དང་། ཁྱད་པར་ཉེར་ལྔའི་སྔ་དྲོ་མཆོད་པ་དང་འབྲེལ་བར་མཆོད་གར་དང་། ཉེར་དགུའི་ཕྱི་དྲོ་གྲྭ་པ་གྲངས་བཅད་ཀྱིས་མཆོག་གི་སྲེག་བླུགས་འབུལ་བ་དང་། …

Then, in the second Hor month (ཧོར་ཟླ་གཉིས་པ, hor zla gnyis pa), for the yearly offering in remembrance (དུས་དྲན་ལོ་མཆོད, dus dran lo mchod) of the two root lamas (རྩ་བའི་བླ་མ་རྣམ་པ་གཉིས, rtsa ba'i bla ma rnam pa gnyis): at evening tea on the 19th the full rite of the ground, the ground claimed and cleansed, the earth spirits pressed down, the kīla driven at the doors; on the morning of the 20th the deity vases prepared; on the night of the 21st the sand maṇḍala made and adorned; from the 22nd, for eight days, at dawn the homage to the lineage of the Heart Essence (སྙིང་ཐིག, snying thig) before the prayer of taking the empowerment of the Offering to the Awareness-holders (རིག་འཛིན་མཆོད་པ, rig 'dzin mchod pa), the accomplishment offering of the Great Compassionate One, the Sugatas Gathered (ཐུགས་རྗེ་ཆེན་པོ་བདེ་གཤེགས་ཀུན་འདུས, thugs rje chen po bde gshegs kun 'dus), with self-entry, feast and torma, and the fulfilment, confession and offering of the Word-protectors led by Lha chen; above all, on the morning of the 25th the offering dance (མཆོད་གར, mchod gar) with the offering, and in the afternoon of the 29th the supreme fire offering by a fixed number of monks; then the siddhi taken and the maṇḍala dissolved (p. 173).

The rule-book does not name the two root lamas. In its monthly order (mdb:p163-166) the same house keeps monthly remembrances (དུས་མཆོད, dus mchod) of the Khri lcam on the 12th, of the Yum on the 15th and of the Ma gcig on the 21st. The OCR's marginal «བཛཱ་ཀཱ་ར་ཤཱནྟཾ» (a page-margin line) is left out.

## <a id="mdb-p177"></a>mdb:p177 The fifth month in the Mindroling rule-book: the tenth day with the Bla ma gsang 'dus rite and the dance, rehearsed from the 2nd

*Read: scan, 2026-11-24 (RD410; image 177, folio 85 or 86, the lines quoted read on the scan by eye; the etext's «མནིང» is «མ་ནིང» on the scan).*

> ༈ ཟླ་བ་ལྔ་པའི་ཚེས་གཉིས་གསུམ་ནས་འཆམ་དབྱངས་ཀྱི་སྦྱོང་བརྡར་དང་། ཚེས་བརྒྱད་ལ་དངོས་གཞི་ཇི་ལྟ་བའི་འཆམས་སྒྲིག །དགུ་ལ་ཡར་ངོའི་ཚེས་བཅུ་མཆོད་པ་དང་འདྲ་བའི་གསུང་ཆོག །ཚེས་བཅུའི་ཉིན་བླ་མ་གསང་འདུས་ཀྱི་ཆོ་ག་དབྱངས་རོལ་རྒྱས་པ། གསོལ་འདེབས། མ་ནིང་གཙོར་གྱུར་གྱི་གཏོར་བསྐང་། ཕྱི་དྲོ་གར་འཆམ་གྱི་ཕྱག་བཞེས་རྒྱས་པ་དང༌། དངོས་གྲུབ་ལེན་པ་དང་བསྟན་འདུས་པར་གནད་ཀྱི་ཤོག་གཅིག་མའི་གཏོར་དབང་རྣམས་མཛད་ཅིང་།

In the fifth month (ཟླ་བ་ལྔ་པ, zla ba lnga pa): from the 2nd and 3rd the rehearsal of dance and chant; on the 8th the dance rehearsed as it will be done; on the 9th the recited rite like that of the offering of the waxing tenth (ཡར་ངོའི་ཚེས་བཅུ, yar ngo'i tshes bcu); on the day of the tenth the rite of the Guru's Secret Gathering (བླ་མ་གསང་འདུས, bla ma gsang 'dus) with full chant and music, the prayer, the torma fulfilment led by Ma ning; in the afternoon the full performance of the dance (གར་འཆམ, gar 'cham); the siddhi taken and the torma empowerment of the one-page essentials of the Bstan 'dus.

The rule-book names its months by number and, for the first, sixth and eighth, by the lunar mansion of the full moon (Mchu, Chu stod, Khrums), so its "fifth month" is the fifth Hor month, Snron, which Zhe chen rgyal tshab and the White Beryl make the monkey month (claim monkey-month-is-the-fifth-hor-month-snron). The rule-book itself does not say "monkey month" or "birth" here.

## <a id="mdb-p179"></a>mdb:p179 The ninth month in the Mindroling rule-book: the passing offering of the Lord Bdud 'dul rab brtan, moved from the Saga month to the descent from the gods

*Read: scan, 2026-11-24 (RD410; image 179, folio 87, the line read on the scan by eye: the etext's «ཧོར» before «བདུད་འདུལ» is «རྗེ» on the scan; the rest of the month's order etext).*

> ༈ ཟླ་བ་དགུ་པ་ལྷ་བབས་དུས་ཆེན་ཐོག་རྗེ་བདུད་འདུལ་རབ་བརྟན་གྱི་དགོངས་རྫོགས་སྒྲུབ་མཆོད་སྔར་ས་ག་ཟླ་བར་གནང་བ་འདིར་སྤོས་ཏེ། ཀུན་ར་ནས་ཐོན་པའི་དགེ་འདུན་ཆོ་ག་གསུམ་པོ་ལོ་རེ་བཞིན་ཉིས་རྒྱུགས་འཕྲོད་པ་རྣམས་གྲལ་གཡས་གཡོན་ལོ་རེས་མོས་ཀྱིས་ཕྱེད་རྡོར་སེམས་ཐུགས་ཀྱི་སྒྲུབ་པ་དང་། ཕྱེད་ཀྱིས་ཐུགས་རྗེ་ཆེན་པོ་འཁོར་བ་དབྱིངས་སྒྲོལ། … བཅོ་ལྔ་ལ་སོ་སོའི་ས་ཆོག་དང་ཐུགས་རྗེ་ཆེན་པོའི་སྟ་གོན་ཡང་དེ་ཉིན་སྦྲེལ། བཅུ་དྲུག་ལ་དབུས་སུ་རྡོར་སེམས། གཡས་སུ་དབྱིངས་སྒྲོལ། གཡོན་དུ་རྣལ་འབྱོར་མའི་དཀྱིལ་འཁོར་རྡུལ་ཚོན་གྱིས་བཞེངས། བཅུ་བདུན་ནས་ཉེར་གསུམ་གྱི་བར་ … ཉེར་བཞིར་རྡུལ་ཚོན་གཤེགས་པའི་བར་མཛད་པ་སོགས།

In the ninth month, at the great festival of the descent from the gods (ལྷ་བབས་དུས་ཆེན, lha babs dus chen), the accomplishment offering for the passing (དགོངས་རྫོགས, dgongs rdzogs) of the Lord Bdud 'dul rab brtan (རྗེ་བདུད་འདུལ་རབ་བརྟན, rje bdud 'dul rab brtan), which was formerly held in the Saga month (ས་ག་ཟླ་བ, sa ga zla ba), is moved here: the monks who have passed the assembly's three rites in two examinations each year take turns, half the Vajrasattva heart accomplishment and half the Great Compassionate One Who Frees Saṃsāra into the Expanse, with fifteen to twenty monks the Nyang treasure's Wrathful Black One; on the 15th the rites of the ground and the preparation, on the 16th the sand maṇḍalas of Vajrasattva in the centre, Dbyings sgrol on the right and the Yoginī on the left, from the 17th to the 23rd the rites, on the 24th the sand dismissed.

Who Bdud 'dul rab brtan is the page does not say; no day of his passing is given, only the months, the old (Saga, the fourth) and the new (the ninth, from the 15th to the 24th).
