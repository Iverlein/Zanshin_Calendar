## <a id="pn1871-i2"></a>pn1871:i2 The first text leaf of the Svapnādhyāya: the spans of the watches (lines 1–4)

*Read: scan, 2026-11-09 (RD277), lines 1–9 of the leaf at 2× on the partly delivered image (1632 × 1800 px, the lower part of the sheet not yet delivered when read); the catalogue's colophon and date from the TEI file.*

> ॥ श्रीगणेशाय नमः ॥ स्वप्नाध्यायं प्रवक्ष्यामि यथोक्तं गुरुभाषितं ।
> येन विज्ञायते सर्व(?)नराणां वस्तु शुभाशुभं ॥
> स्वप्नस्तु प्रथमे यामे संवत्सरफलं भवेत् ।
> द्वितीये चाष्टभिर्मासैः त्रिभिर्मासैस्त्रियामके ॥
> चतुर्थे प्रहरे स्वप्नो मासैकेन विपच्यते ।
> अरुणोदयवेलायां दशाहेन फलं भवेत् ॥
> गोविसर्जनवेलायां सद्यः स्वप्नो विधीयते ।
> वातिकं पैत्तिकं चैव चिंतितं च विवर्जितं ॥ आरो[हणं …]

Image 2 (3014_0002) of the OPenn item, labelled in the TEI "1v [=2v]-2r [=3r]": the first leaf of text, the sheet 13 × 24 cm with a double red margin line at the sides, ten lines a leaf, the sacred syllables and the verse ends touched with red and yellow. The page opens, after the salutation to Gaṇeśa, with the promise of "the chapter of dreams as it was spoken, as the teacher said it". Our Roman reading of the verses: *svapnas tu prathame yāme saṃvatsara-phalaṃ bhavet | dvitīye cāṣṭabhir māsais tribhir māsais triyāmake || caturthe prahare svapno māsaikena vipacyate | aruṇodaya-velāyāṃ daśāhena phalaṃ bhavet || go-visarjana-velāyāṃ sadyaḥ svapno vidhīyate | vātikaṃ paittikaṃ caiva cintitaṃ ca vivarjitaṃ ||*

What the passage says, in our words:

- **The series**: a dream of the first watch (*yāma*) has its fruit in a year; of the second, in eight months (*aṣṭabhir māsaiḥ*); of the third, in three months; a dream of the fourth watch (*caturthe prahare*) ripens in **one month** (*māsaikena*); at the dawn's red (*aruṇodaya*) in ten days; at the hour of the cows' release, at once.
- The dream of wind and of bile and the dream of what was thought of are set aside (line 4), as in the other texts of this family (the Svapnohana's "what was in the mind by day", svo:p268).
- The lines 5 to 9 begin the list of good dreams ("ārohaṇaṃ go-vṛṣa-kuñjarāṇāṃ prāsāda-śailāgra-vanaspatīnāṃ …", climbing a cow, bull, elephant, palace, mountain peak, tree, as in the printed Svapnādhyāya of osv:i3) and were read for the match only.
- **What the TEI file says**: the title *Svapnādhyāya* (alternative title Svapnādhyāyastotra, given by the cataloguer), the colophon of the first work "iti bṛhaspatyuktasvapnādhyāyaḥ samāptah" (f. 4r: "the dream chapter spoken by Bṛhaspati is finished"), a table on f. 1r, a hymn to the nine planets (Navagrahastotra, f. 4v) attributed in the catalogue to Kālidāsa, the date "circa 1700–1850, 18th–19th century", no scribe's name, and the Pariśiṣṭa label of the catalogue's summary. The colophon was not seen on an image (see Doubts).

Doubts: line 1's "sarva(?)" before "narāṇāṃ" is not clear to the eye. "aṣṭabhir" in line 2 is clear (the conjunct ṣṭa and the bhi are plain at 2×); "māsaikena" in line 3 is clear; the same wording as manuscript a of Negelein's list for the second half-line (neg:p14-16). Not read: lines 5–9 beyond their start, the rest of the sheet and f. 2–5 (the download had delivered only their upper lines).
