## <a id="mdp-p134-137"></a>mdp:p134-137 The dream part is a dream-incubation rite, read from the archive.org scan

*Read: scan, machine (Yigdzin-1) with the first page checked by eye, 2026-11-09, RD259.*

> རྨི་ལམ་བརྟག་པ་བཞུགས་སོ། །ན་མོ་བུདྡྷ་ཡ། སྐྱེ་བ་དང་འཆི་བ་བརྟག་པར་འདོད་པས། ཤིན་ཏུ་དབེན་པའི་གནས་དག་ཏུ། །ཁྱད་པར་ཡར་ངོའི་ཚེས་གཅིག་ལ། …ཨོ་བུདྡྷ་ཙུ་རུ་མ་ཙུ་རུ་ཀེ་སྭཱཧཱ། …རིགས་དྲུག་གི་སྒྲ་དང་རྟགས་དང་མཚན་མ་རྣམས་ལན་གཅིག་ལ་མ་བྱུང་ན། བསྐྱར་གྱིན་བྱའོ། …དཀར་པོ་ལྷ། ལྗང་ཁུ་མི། སེར་པོ་ལྷ་མིན། སྔོན་པོ་དུད་འགྲོ། དམར་པོ་ཡི་དྭགས། ནག་པོ་དམྱལ་བ་སོགས་…

The MDP (BDRC MW21880, *Mo dpe dang sa dpyad rmi lam brtag thabs*, 1997) is on archive.org as `bdrc-W21880` (open access, CC BY; `bdrc-W21880_bdrc.zip`, 79 MB, 216 TIFFs of 1504 × 2175 px, a clean typeset print), which BDRC's own manifest restricts to the first images; image n of the zip is `W21880-4992/4992` + n, and printed p. 134 is img. 142 (img. 140 = p. 132). The dream part, pp. 134–137 (img. 142–145), is not a catalogue of dream images with verdicts. It is a rite of seeing the place of one's next birth (or one's death) in a dream: on the first day of the waxing moon, in a quiet place, with a mandala of nine heaps, an image or relic of the Buddha Śākyamuni, five offerings, the seven-limb offering, a torma for the spirits; the mantra «ཨོ་བུདྡྷ་ཙུ་རུ་མ་ཙུ་རུ་ཀེ་སྭཱཧཱ» recited 110,000 times as the approach, 108 at bedtime, with the intention not to let the wish in the dream go; lying in the lion's posture; if no sign of the six kinds comes the first time, it is repeated. The signs are told by colour only: white a god, green a human, yellow an asura, blue an animal, red a hungry ghost, black hell (seen, heard or touched; bad signs: dedicate body and life and give up evil). The lineage line names Vikhyātadeva, Paṇchen Śākyaśrī, Dānaśrī, the teacher 'Jam gsar, Bla ma Rga lo, Zed dmar pa Sangs rgyas ye shes, Mkhan chen Bu ston Rin po che; the colophon: a monk's note on the sound-heat ("dge slong sgra tshad pa'i zin bris"). The text begins below the heading on p. 134 and ends on p. 137 before «མི་ལྟས་སྣ་ཚོགས་བརྟག་ཐབས» (p. 138). It has no list of dream images and so gives nothing to the dream reading's images; it bears only on the sign of the colours of the six realms. Doubts: the Yigdzin-1 transcription is not checked syllable by syllable beyond the first page; the line «ཁྱད་པར་ཡར་ངོའི་ཚེས་གཅིག་ལ» and the mantra's spelling are as the machine gave them; «ཆང་སྒོག་པ» (meat, beer, garlic) and the number of the approach are not weighed.
