## <a id="pbc-p1"></a>pbc:p1 The Tsasum Lingpa Autobiography page (KTD's translation): born 1685

*Read: etext, 2026-11-24 (RD411): the web page read as text in a headed Chromium; an English translation, facts only, no Tibetan on the page.*

(padmasambhava.org, «Tsasum Lingpa Autobiography», read 2026-11-24 in a headed Chromium: an unsigned one-line introduction, then the opening chapters of the Zhal gsung ma, «Clear Garland Crystals of Fire», "translated by Ven. Khenpo Tsewang Dongyal Rinpoche". Modern and copyrighted: facts only.) The introduction says Rtsa gsum gling pa was born in Eastern Tibet in 1685, to the mother Gelekma (Dge legs ma) and the father Tashi (Bkra shis). No Tibetan year name, month or day is given on the page, and the translated chapters shown (the homage, the previous lives, the bardo of the womb, the birthplace under Jo bo Gze rgyal) give none either.

## <a id="pbc-p2"></a>pbc:p2 The Who is Tsasum Lingpa? page (KTD's translation of Khyentse's life): born in the eleventh rabjung, the year unclear

*Read: etext, 2026-11-24 (RD411): the web page read as text in a headed Chromium; an English translation, facts only, no Tibetan on the page.*

(padmasambhava.org, «Who is Tsasum Lingpa?», read 2026-11-24 in a headed Chromium: Jamyang Khyentse Wangpo's brief life, "translated by Khenpo Tsewang Dongyal and Pema Dragpa … at Padma Samye Ling, in the seventh month of the Fire Dog year, August 2006". Modern and copyrighted: facts only.) The translation follows tsl.30:i565-572 and gives the same facts: born in the eleventh sixty-year cycle, the year of birth unclear; the arrival at 'Bri gung after his root lama Dkon mchog phrin las bzang po had passed, on the day of the cremation, "around 1718" by the translators' dating, in the time of the Dzungar invasion. Its note 3 renders the year up to which the treasures were counted as "the Fire Dog year of 1726", where the Tibetan print reads «མེ་རྟ», fire-horse (tsl.30:i569). Which reading the translators followed is not said. The page gives no passing date and no day of practice.
