## <a id="ksl-p1"></a>ksl:p1 Ka-Nying Shedrub Ling's annual activities: the passing days of Mipham, Chokgyur Lingpa, Tulku Urgyen and Tsikey Chokling, the Guru's birth, by Western date

*Read: etext, 2026-11-24 (RD410; read in the headed browser; converted with the app's Phugpa engine, cli, the same day).*

Western dates for 2026-2027 at Ka-Nying Shedrub Ling Monastery and Nagi Nunnery (Nepal): the anniversary of the passing (parinirvāṇa) of Tsikey Chokling Rinpoche, 12 December 2026; of Tulku Urgyen Rinpoche, 29 January 2027; of Mipham Rinpoche, with the Mawey Senge pūjā, 3 June 2027; of Chokgyur Dechen Shikpo Lingpa, with the Kusum Rigdu pūjā, 5 June 2027; the anniversary of the birth of Guru Padmasaṃbhava, 14 June 2027 (p2); the Songs of the Kagyu masters (Kagyu Gurtso), 17 August 2027; Losar 7 February 2027, Chötrul Düchen 20 February, Saga Dawa Düchen 20 May, Chökhor Düchen 7 July, Lhabab Düchen 22 October 2027.

The app's Phugpa engine gives for these days (2026-11-24): 11/3 (Tsikey Chokling), 12/23 (Tulku Urgyen), 3/29 (Mipham), 4/1 (Chokgyur Lingpa), 4/11 (the Guru's birth), 6/15 (Gurtso), 1/1 and 1/15 (Losar, Chötrul), and 3/15, 5/4, 8/22 for the three later düchen. From the third month on the monastery's 2027 is one month ahead of the engine's: the engine doubles the second month of 2027 (leap 2nd month, 2027-03-25 in it), the monastery's dates do not. The tbc list's dates (Mipham 4/29, Chokgyur Lingpa 5/1, Tulku Urgyen 12/24, Tsikey Chokling 11/4) fit the monastery's days if its months are counted without that leap month and with a day's difference at three of them.

## <a id="ksl-p2"></a>ksl:p2 Ka-Nying Shedrub Ling: the Guru's birth on the 10th of the 5th Tibetan month, with the Thukdrup Trinley Gyepa pūjā

*Read: etext, 2026-11-24 (RD410; read in the headed browser).*

Each year on the 10th day of the 5th month of the Tibetan lunar calendar the monks of Ka-Nying Shedrub Ling and the nuns of Nagi Nunnery perform the elaborate Thukdrup Trinley Gyepa pūjā (the Tukdrup Barché Künsel of the Chokling Tersar, all twelve manifestations of the Guru) to mark the birthday of Guru Padmasaṃbhava; for 2027 the page gives 14 June.
