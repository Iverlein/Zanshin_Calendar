## <a id="rmk-i2"></a>rmk:i2 The Ramtek Svapnādhyāya: "Bṛhaspati said", the spans of the watches (lines 1–4)

*Read: scan, 2026-11-09 (RD277), lines 1–4 of the first text page at 70 dpi in two halves; the words in brackets are less clear.*

> श्रीगणेशाय नमः । अथ स्वप्नाध्याय निरूप्यते । बृहस्पतिरुवाच ।
> स्वप्नाध्यायं प्रवक्ष्यामि यथोक्तं मुनिभिः पुरा ।
> येन विज्ञायते (?) नित्यमेव शुभाशुभं ।
> स्वप्नस्तु प्रथमे यामे संवत्सर(विपाकदः ?) ।
> द्वितीये चाष्ट(भि)र्मासैस्तृतीये त्रिमासकः ।
> चतुर्थे यामे तु पुनः स्वप्नो मासेन फल[दः ?] ।
> अरुणोदयवेलायां दशाहेन फलं … ।
> अतः परं प्रवक्ष्यामि …

A single page of eight lines in a black-ink hand, a facsimile of a paper leaf with the library's stamp and the accession "M 856", the title "svapnādhyāya" written by the cataloguer, and the note "Checked 2012" in the margin; no date and no scribe appears on the page.

What the passage says, in our words:

- It opens like the Srinagar volume (pzs:i102-103) with "bṛhaspatir uvāca" and "I shall tell the chapter of dreams as it was said by the sages of old".
- **The series**: first watch a year; second, eight months; third, three months; fourth watch, **a month**; dawn, ten days: the series of pn1871, pn673 and pzs, again in a hand of its own (three of the four spans are as the Svapnohana's; the fourth is a month, not half a month).
- Then "ataḥ paraṃ pravakṣyāmi …", the list of good and bad dreams.

Doubts: the verse is faint in places; the words in brackets and the verbs of the last two lines are not certain; the rest of the 7 pages was not read.

## <a id="rmk-i2b"></a>rmk:i2b The Ramtek Svapnādhyāya again at full size: the third watch in a locative ending in -māsake

*Read: scan, 2026-11-10 (RD312), leaf n1 at 2520 px width, crops of 2×.*

> Ramtek M 856, the text page (PDF page 2 = leaf n1 of the Internet Archive item; a ruled frame, eight lines, black ink), lines 1–5, read in crops of 2× (the words in brackets are less clear):
>
> श्रीगणेशाय नमः । अथ स्वप्नाध्याय निरूप्यते । बृहस्पतिरुवाच । स्वप्नाध्यायं प्रवक्ष्यामि यथोक्तं मुनिभिः पुरा । येन विज्ञायते नित्यमेव शुभाशुभम् । स्वप्नस्तु प्रथमे यामे संवत्स[र]विपाक[दः?] । द्वितीये चाष्टभिर्मासैस्तृतीये [त्रि]मासके । चतुर्थे यामे तु यः स्वप्नो मासेन फल[दः स्मृतः?] । अरुणोदयवेलायां दशाहेन फलं [स्मृतम्?] । अतः परं प्रवक्ष्यामि शृणु पुण्य[...]फलम् ।

"Salutation to Gaṇeśa. Now the chapter of dreams is set out. Bṛhaspati said: I shall tell the chapter of dreams as it was said by the sages of old, by which the good and the bad is always known. A dream of the first watch (yāma), its ripening is in a year; in the second, with eight months; in the third, in a three-month [period] (trimāsake); in the fourth watch, the dream that [comes] has its fruit in a month; at the time of dawn's red the fruit in ten days. Hereafter I shall tell, hear, the fruit of merit …"

What the larger crop shows over RD277's reading (rmk:i2): the third watch is given in a locative with the ending "-māsake" ("tṛtīye … māsake"; the conjunct before "māsa" looks like "tri", moderate), not as "triyāmake" (Srinagar, pzs) and not as a fortnight; the fourth watch's verse is in the form "caturthe yāme tu yaḥ svapno māsena phala[daḥ]", the form of Negelein's i ("tūrye yāme tu yaḥ svapno māsena phalado bhavet"), where the Srinagar and Penn copies have "caturthe prahare". The ending of the dawn line is faint.
