Yongle dadian (永樂大典), the two surviving rhyme-volumes 20181 and 20182 (the character 日, "day"), in the Zhonghua facsimile as transcribed on Chinese Wikisource (zh.wikisource.org, 永樂大典/卷20181, /卷20182, from the Commons PDFs 永樂大典20181.pdf and 永樂大典20182.pdf); the sheet numbers 九三三七 f. appear on the page images. The entry 日 gathers, for each day of the sexagenary cycle, the sayings of several selection-of-days books one after the other: here the days from 甲辰 (day 41) to 辛酉 (day 58) survive. One of them, 雷英發編『萬曆錦囊拾遺備用』 (the title as printed), gives under each day a line "(the months) 占夢 (a short omen) 牛黄 ... 不將 ... (the months) 占恠": for each day, a list of months in which a dream seen ("占夢", divining by a dream) on that day has the omen named (孝服 mourning dress; 行人凶 a traveller in danger; 竈不安 the stove not at peace; 天火 fire from heaven; 忌行人 a traveller to be avoided; 十人吉 good for ten people; 主喜 gladness; 大吉 great good; 酒食 wine and food; 口舌 quarrels; 信至 a letter comes; 半吉 half good), then the months in which "占恠" (divining a strange sign, a portent) is done. Another, 呂茂明編『陰陽備用選擇成書』, puts a short "夢" omen after each day's birth-star data (second paragraph). In both the dream is an omen of the day, not a rule of silence: nothing in the two volumes says that a dream is not told. The month lists are 8 or 9 months long (5 or 6 on three days), so they do not read as Edo's three branches a month. The strings were read from the Wikisource transcription; I checked the layout and two clauses (甲辰, 辛亥) on the page images (20181 sheet 5, 20182 sheet 5), the other sixteen not checked on the images; the lists' numerals 十十一, 十一十二, 十一一 (likely 10 and 11, 11 and 12, 11 and 1?) are as printed in the transcription and are not certain.

The only published record found of the author 雷英發: the catalogue of the National Central Library, Taipei, as copied on the file page of Wikimedia Commons (the scan of the library's copy, 79 pages of a pocket-size woodblock book, 842 x 545 px a spread). It names 雷英發 as author of the 新編萬曆錦囊星家總括 ("the newly edited ten-thousand-calendars brocade bag, the general summary of the star families"), a Yuan printing from a commercial workshop (1280-1368), in three parts for the year, the month and the day, with the printer's marks 雷氏錦囊 ("Lei family brocade bag") at the end of the first part. The title's 萬曆 therefore stands in a Yuan book and cannot be the Ming reign Wanli (1573-1620): it is the "ten thousand calendars" of an almanac title (the Yongle dadian's volume 20121 quotes, besides, 萬曆撮要 and 萬曆會同 among the books of its entry 日). The Yongle dadian's 雷英發編萬曆錦囊拾遺備用 is, by the name, a gleaning from or a supplement to this book of the same family; that it is the same text is not shown. I tried to read the scan: it is a low-resolution derivative (842 px a spread, the characters 8 to 12 px high), the star tables of the pages tried (image 12 of 79) are not legible, and no dream or day-omen table could be found in it; the day part was not read.

## <a id="cdb-box3"></a>cdb:box3 李純之 選擇要略: the row 牛黃殺 and the 不將 days (RD380)

*Read: etext (Wikisource's transcription of 選擇要略/上 and /下), 2026-11-10; the table's columns counted from the wikitext, the page images not seen.*

> 〔李純之『選擇要略』（朝鮮世宗年間）、zh.wikisource.org 選擇要略/上、月ごとの凶神の表（列は月建 寅卯辰巳午未申酉戌亥子丑）から、牛黃殺の一行〕
> 牛黃殺〔忌出糞。〕　‖　月2（卯）倉　月6（未）廨　月10（亥）欄　（他の月は空）
> 〔同書 下、不將吉日の条〕
> …厭前屬陽，取干日，厭後屬陰，取支日。如正月月厭戌，以戌加月厭前，辛，庚，丁，丙爲陽，亥，子，丑，寅，卯爲陰。用支干日，配陰陽比和，故爲不將。《緫聖》云：「陰將女死，陽將男亡。陰陽俱將，男女相傷，陰陽不將，乃得吉昌。」《撮要》云：「若非不將日，雖得天德，月德，吉星，亦不可用。」…
> 通用吉日，六甲中，只有乙丑，丁卯，丙子，丁丑，辛卯，癸卯，乙巳七日有不將，壬子，癸丑，己丑三日無不將。今乃以爲全吉，是不可拘也。…

李純之 (Yi Sun-ji), 選擇要略, a Joseon work "of the years of King Sejong" as the Wikisource page's header gives it (no years are given there), a handbook of days and places in the Chinese manner; text from zh.wikisource.org (選擇要略/上 and /下), read as transcribed; the table's cells (the row as the wikitext spells it, the twelve columns being the months in the order of the row 月建 寅卯辰巳午未申酉戌亥子丑 above it) were counted, the page images were not seen. The row 牛黃殺 (the "ox-yellow killer", with the note "avoid carrying out dung") puts the spirit in a granary (倉) in the month of the hare, in an office or hall (廨) in the month of the goat and in a pen (欄) in the month of the boar, and leaves the other nine months empty. These are the three words that follow 牛黄 in the Yongle dadian's day lines (cdb:box1: 廨, 欄, 倉囷): the Yongle lists put the ox-yellow in the office on 辰 巳 午 未 days, in the pen on 申 酉 戌 亥 days and in the granary on 子 丑 寅 卯 days, which is the same three places in the same order, in steps of four branches (the month table: hare, goat, boar months, four apart); on two days, 戊午 and 己未, the Yongle line names no place at all (牛黄。不將). Whether 牛黄 is one spirit that the month's table and the day's line place in two ways, I did not settle. The second text is the book's explanation of 不將, "not carrying": a marriage day on which the yin and yang of stem and branch agree is called 不將, and the verse quoted from the 緫聖 says that where yin carries, the woman dies, where yang carries, the man dies, and where neither carries there is good fortune; the same book lists seven days of the sixty with a 不將 (乙丑 丁卯 丙子 丁丑 辛卯 癸卯 乙巳) and three without (壬子 癸丑 己丑). The Yongle lists print 不將 on all eighteen days including 壬子 and 癸丑, so the Yongle line's 不將 is either a different thing or a different system; it is not settled which.

玉匣記, the Yushi ji, a popular handbook of days attributed to Xu Xun, text from zh.wikisource.org as transcribed (the images not seen). In its section on the lucky days for buying an ox and for building an ox-pen it adds "the Ox-yellow Scripture (牛黃經) has also four lucky days: 戊辰, 戊午, 己未, 辛酉": so 牛黃 belongs to the lore of oxen and their pens, and the four days are given for them; of these four days the Yongle dadian prints 戊午 and 己未 with the omen 大吉 and 辛酉 with 吉 (cdb:box1), 戊辰 not surviving there. The same book's 嫁娶不將圖 gives the same explanation of 不將 and the same verse as 選擇要略 (cdb:box3). This is the only text found that names a 牛黃經; nothing in it says that a dream is read from the days.

Chen Zhensun, 直齋書錄解題 juan 12 (a Song catalogue of books), as transcribed on zh.wikisource.org (also in 文獻通考 juan 220, which copies it with variant glyphs). The entry 《陰陽備用》 in twelve rolls is by 胡舜申 (the district-judge of Shuzhou, a man of Xin'an), a book "originally on the forms of the land, in which the rules of the selection of days of the various houses are all found". This Song book is not the Yongle dadian's 吕茂明編陰陽備用選擇成書 (cdb:box1: the title there is longer and the author another), but it shows that the words 陰陽備用 were already a book-title in the Song, so that 呂茂明's 陰陽備用選擇成書 may be a later book named after or built on it (not shown). No date of 吕茂明 was found anywhere on Wikisource (the search for 吕茂明 and 呂茂明 gives only this volume): the Yongle dadian, completed in 1408, is the latest date before which his book was written.
