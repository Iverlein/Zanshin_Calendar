Nam mkha' seng ge's (ནམ་མཁའ་སེང་གེ, nam mkha' seng ge) *Mirror of What to Take and Leave* (བླང་དོར་གསལ་བའི་མེ་ལོང, blang dor gsal ba'i me long), the chapter of the results of the star chart (སྐར་ཁྲ་འབྲས་བཤད, skar khra 'bras bshad), its close: pp. 389–392 of the volume (BDRC I1PD30933, open, img. 417–420; image = etext page; printed page = image − 28), read by eye on the scan at 2×, 2026-10-26: p. 389 (img. 417) its last paragraph, p. 390 (img. 418) its last nine lines, p. 391 (img. 419) the paragraph above the chart, p. 392 (img. 420) the paragraph below the box. Corrections of BDRC's etext: «སྐར་མ་གཙོ» (etext «སྐུར་མ»); the syllable after «ཏ» of «ཏ་ཀླ་ལ་ལམ», the printer's rendering of WB's *tatkāla*, is not sure.

"The sky dog of the hour: at the hour when the seventh mewa rises, for a funeral, a bride and the like, facing north is very bad; it is mended by the sky dog's ransom. The date's earth lords up to here are written as they come in the *Bo dkar* and others, the view of the *nag rtsis khang btsun* (ནག་རྩིས་ཁང་བཙུན, nag rtsis khang btsun; who or what is meant is not clear); the strikes and turns of the Protector, the Goddess, the Great God, the oath-bound and others are not written, since no pure source for them was found; … [the results of some famous days, the *dpal nyi* and others] … The way to practise them, the essentials gathered: where planet and mansion differ in good and bad, the planet is the chief; where the mansion and the date differ, the mansion is chief; where yoga and karaṇa disagree for a work, the karaṇa is chief; where the *nyi ma* and the hour differ, the hour, being a sharp weapon, is chief; where all differ in good and bad, the concord ('phrod) must be held the chief. Rāhu, weekday, mansion, date, karaṇa, yoga, *nyi ma*: of the seven in order, the earlier is the stronger, the later the weaker; the powers of all are complete in the *tatkāla* combination period, because the works of the four, planet, mansion, karaṇa and yoga, are complete in its house; therefore the combination period must be held the chief, it is said. The general screening and remedy of the earth lords: [the chart] … Where the earth lords are for the most part impaired, reciting the *Tog gzungs*, the *Eight Appearances*, the *Spang skong*, the *Mdo thig* and the like, the nine-round *dong tse*, the rite of the disturbed earth lords (ས་བདག་འཁྲུགས་གཏོ, sa bdag 'khrugs gto), water offerings and planting *tsha tsha* overcome all ill, it is said."

What it shows: SY follows KP's digest of the weighing nearly word for word (as KP, without WB's line «དམིགས་བསལ་བྱུང་ན་དེ་ཉིད་གཙོ»), and closes it as KP does, by passing to the earth lords' general screening and remedy. The date's earth lords stand before the weighing as results of their own; the weighing names none of them; the earth lords that follow it are met by screening (the chart places the objects and images in the direction where the earth lord goes) and by rites. «ཉམས», "impaired", may mean offended earth lords; the clause is not clear.
