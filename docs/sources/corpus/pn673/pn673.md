## <a id="pn673-i1"></a>pn673:i1 The first text leaf of the Svapnādhyāya dated saṃvat 1881: the spans of the watches (lines 1–6)

*Read: scan, 2026-11-09 (RD277), lines 1–6 at 2× on the partly delivered image; the colophon and date from the TEI file (f. 6v, not seen on an image).*

> ॥ श्रीगणेशाय नमः ॥ स्वप्नाध्यायं प्रवक्ष्यामि यथोक्तं गुरुभाषितं ॥ फलं विज्ञायते येन नि[?]मे वस्तु शुभाशुभं ॥१॥
> स्वप्नस्तु प्रथमे यामे संवत्सरविपाकदः ॥ द्वितीये चाष्टमे मासै स्तृतीये चैव त्रिमासकः ॥२॥
> चतुर्थे तु पुनः स्वप्नो मासेन फलदो भवेत् ॥ अरुणोदयवेलायां दशाहेन फलं लभेत् ॥३॥
> वातिकं पैत्तिकं चैव श्लेष्मजं चिंतयायुतं ॥ गुरुप(?)… जपेत्स्वप्ने … ॥४॥ आरोहणं गो-वृषभ-कुंजराणां प्रासादशैलाग्र-वनस्पतीनां …

Image 1 (5905_0001) of the OPenn item: the first text leaf of six, 11 × 25 cm, written in 7 or 8 long lines, the margin title "sva. pra." (an abbreviation of the title, read by eye) and the leaf number 1 on the left. The verses are numbered 1 to 4 by the scribe. Our Roman reading of verses 2–3: *svapnas tu prathame yāme saṃvatsara-vipākadaḥ | dvitīye cāṣṭame māsais tṛtīye caiva trimāsakaḥ || caturthe tu punaḥ svapno māsena phalado bhavet | aruṇodaya-velāyāṃ daśāhena phalaṃ labhet ||*

What the passage says, in our words:

- **The series is the same as the Penn manuscript pn1871's**: a year, eight months (*aṣṭame māsai[ḥ]*, the scribe's spelling), three months, **a month** for the fourth watch, ten days at the dawn's red (the cows' release line is not in this copy's verse 3; verse 4 follows with the bad dreams of wind, bile, phlegm and thought).
- **The date**: the TEI file's colophon, f. 6v, reads "iti svapnādhyāyaḥ samāptaḥ // śrī // śrī saṃvat 18 / 81 jeṣṭe likhitaṃ" (written in the month Jyeṣṭha of Vikrama year 1881, which is 1824 CE; the catalogue gives 1824). This is the only dated Svapnādhyāya with a span verse read so far.
- It names no author in the catalogue and no author on the leaf's first lines; the Bṛhaspati of the other copies is the colophon's (pn1871, pn2730, pn687 by the catalogue).

Doubts: "nimame" (?) in line 2 of the verse 1 is unclear and is not used; the end of verse 4 ("gurupa … japet svapne") is not read; the lower lines of the leaf were not delivered; f. 2–6 not read.

## <a id="pn673-i6"></a>pn673:i6 The last leaf of the copy dated saṃvat 1881: verses 68–69 and the colophon

*Read: scan, 2026-11-10 (RD357), on the full-size Colenda image (canvas 6) at 1.3×.*

> … तांग्रसेवनंनैवही (?) ॥ ६८ ॥ कामः कार्तिकश्चैव (दृष्टः?) स्वप्नो न विद्यते ।
> दुःस्वप्नो दृश्यते येन तेषां निद्रां (?) …रयेत् ॥ ६९ ॥ इति स्वप्नाध्यायः समाप्तः ॥ श्री ॥
> श्रीसंवत् १८।८१ जेष्टे लिखीतं ॥ श्री ॥

The last leaf (f. 6v, canvas 6, one page of three lines): the end of verse 68, verse 69 and the colophon: "iti svapnādhyāyaḥ samāptaḥ | śrī | śrī saṃvat 18/81 jeṣṭe likhītaṃ | śrī" — "the dream chapter is finished; written in Jyeṣṭha of saṃvat 1881" (Vikrama 1881 = 1824 CE). The numerals are written 18 and 81 with a stroke between them; read on the image at 1.3×. The scribe's own spelling "jeṣṭe", "likhītaṃ". The verse numbers 68 and 69 show that this copy has a chapter of 69 verses, against the 52 numbered verses of pn1871 and the 42 and more of pn2730's first two pages: the longer copy.
The two verses before the colophon name desire and the month Kārttika and say, in the first, that a dream is "not found" (*na vidyate*) for them, and, in the second, that a man who sees a bad dream should (do something with) their sleep (*nidrāṃ ... *): the words are partly not read, and the sense of them is not used. The colophon names no author and no place; the margin has the abbreviated title of the work ("sva. prā.") on the first leaf only.
