*Read: etext, 2026-11-24 (RD410; read in the headed browser).*

A list by Tibetan month and day ("Jeff Watt 7-1996 [updated 9-2019]"), no source named. The Guru's days: 2/10 Guru Shakya Sengge and Guru Loden Chogse; 6/10 Guru Padmasambhava, Guru Tsokye Dorje and Guru Sengge Dradog; 8/10 Guru Nyima Özer; 10/10 Pema Jungne; 11/10 Guru Dorje Drolo; 12/10 Guru Pema Gyalpo.
