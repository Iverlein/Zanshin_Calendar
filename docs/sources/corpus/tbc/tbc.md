## <a id="tbc-p1"></a>tbc:p1 The Rigpa students' calendar: anniversaries by Tibetan month and day, the masters of the Nyingma and Kagyu among them

*Read: etext, 2026-11-24 (RD410; read in the headed browser).*

The page *Yearly events overview* gives the special days (Losar 1/1, Chötrul Düchen 1/15, the Birth 4/7, Saga Dawa Düchen 4/15, the local deities' day 5/15, Chökhor Düchen 6/4, Lha Bab Düchen 9/22, Gutor 12/29) and "anniversaries and birthdays according to the Tibetan calendar", month and day in Arabic and Tibetan numerals, names in English only, without saying which of them are passing days. Those of the masters RD410 asks for and of the Nyingma treasure revealers and Kagyu founders (month/day): Tertön Sogyal Lerab Lingpa 1/10; Milarepa (1040-1123) 1/14; Garab Dorje and Marpa Lotsawa (1012-1097) 1/15; Jamyang Khyentse Wangpo 1/21; Orgyen Terdak Lingpa (1646-1714) 2/2; Do Khyentse 2/20; Tertön Mingyur Dorje 3/16; Tertön Sangye Lingpa 3/30; Patrul Rinpoche 4/18; Jamgön Mipham 4/29; Nyala Pema Dündul 4/30; Terchen Chokgyur Lingpa 5/1; Jamyang Khyentse Chökyi Lodrö 5/6; the 3rd Karmapa Rangjung Dorje (1284-1339) 6/14; Gampopa (1079-1153) 6/15; Jomo Menmo 7/10; Phagmodrupa 7/25; Dilgo Khyentse 8/19; Rigdzin Kumaradza and Katok Tsewang Norbu 8/25; Rigdzin Jigme Lingpa (1729-1798) 9/3; the 16th Karmapa (1924-1981) and Khenpo Karthar Rinpoche 9/9; Adzom Drukpa 9/25; Dudjom Rinpoche (1904-1987) 11/18; the 1st Jamgön Kongtrul 11/26; Longchen Rabjam (1308-1364) 12/18. No Tilopa, Nāropa, Düsum Khyenpa or other Karmapa but the 3rd and the 16th; no tenth day of the Guru stands in the yearly list.
