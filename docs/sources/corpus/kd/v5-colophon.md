# KD vol. 5: the Tibetan and the Chinese colophon

The last leaves of the fifth volume of Bod kyi rtsis rig kun 'dus chen mo (Chengdu 1998), read on the lent scans (archive.org, `bdrc-W28845-4`, 814 leaves) for the Chinese title.

## <a id="kd-5-i812-813"></a>kd.5:i812-813 The Chinese colophon: 藏传历算学大全

*Read: scan, 2026-11-01.*

> བོད་ཀྱི་རྩིས་རིག་ཀུན་འདུས་ཆེན་མོ། དེབ་ལྔ་པ། སྐར་རྩིས་ལ་ཉེར་མཁོའི་རེའུ་མིག་གི་སྐོར།

KD vol. 5, the last two leaves (archive.org file bdrc-W28845-4, leaves 812 and 813 of 814; the reader's screenshots at 1008 px, the Chinese enlarged 1.6 times and read by eye, RD108, 2026-11-01).

Leaf 812 is the Tibetan colophon: the publisher's number «(བྲིག)གསར་འགོད་ཡིག་002» as the print gives it, the editors' names in two lines (not read at this size), then the title as a block (the work's title, and below it in bold the volume's, which the Library of Congress record gives as Skar rtsis la ñer mkho'i re'u mig gi skor, the fifth volume's own title, quoted above from the catalogue and not read letter by letter at this size) and a line of the compilers' group (not read), Sichuan Nationalities Publishing House, Chengdu, August 1998, ISBN 7-5409-1922-1/Z·54 for the set, 350 yuan.

Leaf 813 is the Chinese colophon, read in full: the publisher's number (川)新登字002号; 责任编辑 戴作民 (editor in charge); 封面设计 梅定开 (cover); 内文设计 戴作民 (inner design); 封面画电脑制作 梅曦 (cover picture on computer); 责任校对 黄延静, 仁青措, 索朗次仁, 木兰扎桑, 多杰工布 (proof-readers). Then the title page of the colophon: 藏传历算学大全 (Zang chuan li suan xue da quan, "The Great Compendium of Tibetan Reckoning"), 藏传历算学大全编写组 (the compilers' group of the compendium), 四川民族出版社出版 (成都盐道街3号), 发行, 四川新华印刷厂印刷, format 787×1092 mm 1/16, 1998 年 8 月第一版, 1998 年 8 月第一次印刷, 印张 243, 印数 1–1200 套, 书号 ISBN7-5409-1922-1/Z·54(民文), 全套定价 (平) 350.00 元.

No person is named as a writer on the Chinese page: the work is credited to a compilers' group. Kun dga' rig 'dzin's name is not on it. The title is not 西藏天文历算总汇.
