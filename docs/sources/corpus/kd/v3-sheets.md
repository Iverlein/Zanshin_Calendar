# KD vol. 3, Kun dga' rig 'dzin's *Sheets of Some Needed Examples*: the birth's pebbles

The sheets (ཉེར་མཁོའི་དཔེར་བརྗོད་ཁག་ཅིག་གི་ཤོག་ཁྲ, nyer mkho'i dper brjod khag
cig gi shog khra) of Kun dga' rig 'dzin (ཀུན་དགའ་རིག་འཛིན, kun dga' rig 'dzin),
KD vol. 3, pp. 713–729; their title leaf and the list of all the sheets are
in [verdicts.md](../../verdicts.md), *RD28*, section 2. Here, the three
sheets of one worked birth, read 2026-10-22 on the lent scans (archive.org
`bdrc-W28845-2`, leaf = printed page + 19: p. 716 is leaf 735, p. 718 leaf
737), the reader's screenshots at 1002 px, the cells enlarged 2× and read
by eye; BDRC's etext (VEIE0OPI2F246A26_I4717, etext page = printed page +
20) beside, which keeps the headings but loses the cells. The sheets are
charts: their words are headings and cell labels, quoted here as the scan
shows them. They answer open question 184.

## <a id="kd-3-p716"></a>kd.3:p716 The four precious pebbles of the worked birth

*Read: scan, 2026-10-22.*

Under the moon's house, the birth's conjunction and the vajra cross, the
box «གཅེས་པའི་རྡེལ་བཞི།», the four precious pebbles (WB vol. 1, p. 260):

> གནམ་ལོ་རྒྱལ་པོ་ཤིང་བྱི། ཟླ་བ་བློན་པོ་ཆུ་སྤྲེལ། ཞག་པོ་དམག་མི་ས་ལུག དུས་ཚོད་མཚོན་ཆ་ཆུ་འབྲུག

The year as king a wood mouse, the month as minister a water monkey, the
day as soldier an earth sheep, the hour as weapon a water dragon, each with
its pebbles drawn. The person of the three sheets is born in a wood mouse
year, in a water monkey month.

## <a id="kd-3-p717"></a>kd.3:p717 The five unchanging luck pebbles

*Read: scan, 2026-10-22.*

The box «མི་འགྱུར་ཀླུང་ལྔ།», the five unchanging luck pebbles (WB vol. 1,
p. 261), a row for each and a column for each aspect, «སྲོག», «ལུས», «དབང»,
«ཀླུང»; the cells hold the element, by its first letter (ཤ wood, མ fire, ས
earth, ཅ iron, ཆ water):

| Row on the scan | Life force | Body | Power | Luck |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| «ཕ་ཀླུང་ཤིང་ལུག», the father's luck, a wood sheep | ས | ཅ | ཤ | མ |
| «མ་ཀླུང་ལྕགས་རྟ», the mother's luck, an iron horse | མ | ས | ཅ | ཅ |
| «བབས་སྤར་དྭ», the trigram fallen (at the birth), dwa | ཅ | ཅ | ཅ | ཅ |
| «བབས་སྨེ་༧», the mewa fallen, seven | མ | མ | མ | མ |
| «རང་ཀླུང་ཆུ་སྤྲེལ», one's own luck, a water monkey | ཅ | ཅ | ཆ | ཤ |

These are WB's five in WB's order: the father's year, the mother's
progressed sign at the birth (WB's «མ་ཡི་ལོག་མིན་གར་བབས»), the trigram and
the mewa fallen at the birth, and the birth month (the water monkey of p.
716). A year sign has the four aspects' elements of its own (Forces.of: the
wood sheep's life force earth, body iron, power wood, luck fire); a trigram
and a mewa have one element, set in all four columns.

**What the cells are.** The column's element is the aspect's own element
and its mother, son, friend and enemy (the wood mouse's life force water:
iron its mother, wood its son, fire its friend, earth its enemy). Each of
the five rows is one of the five unchanging lucks of p. 717, the same
aspect's element of it: row 1 the father's wood sheep (life force earth,
body iron, power wood, luck fire), row 2 the mother's iron horse, row 3
the trigram dwa (iron), row 4 the mewa seven (fire), row 5 the birth month
water monkey. Every one of the hundred cells is the pebble of Mipham's
rule (WB vol. 2, p. 475): white where the row's element is the mother or
the friend of the column's element, black where it is its son or its
enemy, and where the two are the same element black for fire, iron and
wood, white for earth and water. Taking each row as one element, the
cells of each aspect admit exactly one element per row, and it is the
element p. 717 gives (checked mechanically for all twenty rows).

**The sixth row** is the twenty deciding pebbles of WB p. 261 («བཏང་གཅོད་རྡེའུ་ཉི་ཤུ»),
one laid under each column on the greater of its five: in the sixteen other columns
it is the colour of the majority. In the four enemy columns it is drawn
the other way (life force: two black, three white, drawn black; body: two
black, drawn black; power: four black, drawn white; luck: four black,
drawn white): a white enemy pebble is the bad one (WB p. 329–331, the
enemy's readings), so the sheet draws the enemy's deciding pebble by what
it means, black for bad.

**The board.** So the hundred lie in four blocks of twenty-five, the life
force, body, power and luck left to right, each in five columns, own,
mother, son, friend, enemy, and five rows, the father's (or for a daughter
the mother's) year, the mother's progressed sign, the trigram, the mewa
and the birth month, top to bottom: five rows of twenty, with the twenty
deciding pebbles in a row below. WB's general verdict (vol. 1, p. 331)
reads on this board: "where the son pebbles lie" are the four son columns,
"above", "in the middle" and "below" its rows, its "four corners" the
corners of the board.

The lower chart of the page («གཙུག་ལག་སྤོར་ཐང་ལས་གཙོ་སྐྱོབ་སྐྱིད་ཀྱི་ཁྲ་མ།»,
the chart of the chief, protector and happiness, with «སྐུ་ཚེའི་སྒང» and «ཚེ་སྨད»)
belongs to another reckoning and was not read.
