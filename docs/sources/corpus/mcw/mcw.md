## <a id="mcw-32-p289"></a>mcw.32:p289 The feast on the Seven-Line Prayer: its Vajra Guru cut short

*Read: scan, 2026-11-04.*

> ཨོཾ་ཨཱཿཧཱུྃ་བཛྲ༴

Vol. 32, img. 301, l. 5: the end of Rdo rje'i tshig bdun dang 'brel ba'i tshogs mchod dpal gyi bum bzang. This print gives only the first words and the sign of abbreviation (༴); the reset copy in the Bka' ma shin tu rgyas pa (VEIE0OPI48DFFA46_I1PD106559) writes the mantra out, scans on archive.org only, so Mipham's own full spelling is not read. What the page does fix is the opening: ཨཱཿ with the visarga, ཧཱུྃ with the crescent, no tsheg between them.
