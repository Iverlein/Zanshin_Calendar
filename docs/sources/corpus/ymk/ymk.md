## <a id="ymk-p6"></a>ymk:p6 The first dream of Fuji, and the heading of the five dreams (frame 6)

*Read: machine, 2026-11-09.*

> ふじ山をゆめに見れば貴人高位の人にとり上られねがひのぞみ事心のまゝに叶ふわけて初夢にふじをみれば高位の御方は申におよばす四民ともに至て仕合大きによし是第一のゆめ也

NDL OCR, the first column of the line checked on the image (the earlier words and 初夢 at its end), the rest machine only. "If Mount Fuji is seen in a dream, one is taken up by a noble of high rank and wishes are fulfilled as the heart wills; above all, if Fuji is seen in the first dream (初夢), then, not to speak of the high-born, all four estates alike do very well; this is the first of dreams." No date, night or season is named for the first dream. The same frame heads the list: five dreams (五夢の伝: 霊夢, 心夢, 実夢, 虚夢, 雑夢), read on the image.

## <a id="ymk-p30"></a>ymk:p30 Seasons of a dream: spring to autumn good, winter not (frame 30, the scarecrow dream)

*Read: machine, 2026-11-09.*

> しかしながら春夏秋九ヶ月のゆめは何れもよし分て秋の最中に見るゆめは四民ともに大きに仕合よく
> さりながら冬三ヶ月の夢はよろしからず

First line checked on the page image (春夏秋 / 九ヶ月 / 何れもよし / 分て秋の最中), the second from the OCR only. In the entry for dreaming of a scarecrow or bird-clapper: "but a dream of the nine months of spring, summer and autumn is good in every case, and one seen in the very middle of autumn is best for all four estates; but a dream of the three winter months is not good." A season rule, attached to one dream, not to the first dream.

## <a id="ymk-p52"></a>ymk:p52 A horse dreamed in spring, summer, autumn and winter (frame 52)

*Read: machine, 2026-11-09.*

> 春三月の中に駒をゆめにみれば其年より大きに仕合よし
> 又夏三月の変[夢]よろしからずそのとしのうちは火なんをつゝしむべし又秋冬のゆめは半吉なり

First line checked on the image, the rest OCR (it reads 変 where 夢 is meant). "If a colt (駒) is seen in a dream within the three spring months, one prospers greatly from that year; but a dream of the three summer months is not good, guard against fire that year; a dream in autumn or winter is half-good." The one dream with a rule for each season.

## <a id="ymk-p55"></a>ymk:p55 Setsubun bean-scattering seen in a dream (frame 55)

*Read: machine, 2026-11-09.*

> 節分まめまきのていをゆめにみれば何事もそろりと立身出世のくちびらきとしるべし

OCR only. "If the bean-scattering of the eve of spring (節分, setsubun) is seen in a dream, know it as the opening of every kind of rise in the world, slowly." The festival is the subject of the dream, not the night of dreaming; no date of dreaming is given.

## <a id="ymk-p59"></a>ymk:p59 Telling and honouring a dream: by the morning, three days, the months (frames 58-60)

*Read: machine, 2026-11-09.*

> よいゆめをみたる時は明る朝はやくおきてよし一切ゆめを人にはなすべからず
> 夢祭くもつ上やうの秘伝御神酒あづき餅は月のかす十二あげるなり但しうるふのある年は十三あげるとしるべし
> もちろんゆめをまつりし日ゟ三日の間はゆめばなし一切すべからず

OCR only. "When a good dream is seen, rise early the next morning; never tell the dream to anyone." "Offerings for the dream festival (夢祭): rice wine and red-bean cake, as many as the months, twelve; in a year with a leap month know that thirteen are offered." "From the day the dream is honoured, for three days no dream talk at all." Frame 58 also gives a verse to wash a bad dream away, written three times on paper and sent down running water by the fourth hour (四ツ) of that morning. These name the morning, three days and the count of months, never the first dream.

## <a id="ymk-p63"></a>ymk:p63 The real dream shows within three days (frame 63)

*Read: machine, 2026-11-09.*

> このゆめはきわめて三日のうちにしるしあり

OCR only. Of the 実夢, the real dream, dreamt in deep sleep and remembered from beginning to end: "this dream surely shows its sign within three days"; of the 心夢 (a dream from what one has thought or heard) "the sign is slight". A time to fulfilment, no calendar rule.

## <a id="ymk-p66"></a>ymk:p66 The count by eights: dream, month, day, hour and year (frames 64-67)

*Read: machine, 2026-11-09.*

> 月のかず日のかす時のかず年のかず右の四ツのかずを合ゆめうらなひの通り八ツづゝいくたびも引おとしのこりたるかずにてうらなふべし

OCR only. Frames 64-65 list the things seen in dreams with a number each (Fuji 3591, 宝船 924, 羽子板, 春駒, 万歳, 七種, 福引, 鏡餅 among them); frame 66 gives the method: the number of the thing dreamed, taken away by eights until one to eight remain, is the figure to read; without a dream, the number of the month, day, hour and year joined are taken away by eights the same way. "Join the four numbers, the month, day, hour and year, and subtract eight at a time as often as possible; divine by what remains." Frame 67: seen twelve times in a year, once a month, it comes true every month. The New Year things (宝船, 羽子板, 七種) are objects with numbers; the 初夢 has no entry of its own.
