# School days

Sigla, numbering and quoting rules are in [README.md](README.md).


## The days of the year the Nyingma and Kagyu schools keep, beyond the düchen the app has ([T6.4](#t6-4)): for each day its Tibetan date (month and lunar day), what it commemorates and how it is kept, with the school that keeps it: Guru Rinpoche's days of the year (the tenth of the Monkey month and the others), the passing days (*dus mchod*) of the Nyingma masters (Longchenpa, Jigme Lingpa, Mipham, Dudjom Rinpoche and the treasure revealers the almanacs name) and of the Kagyu (Tilopa, Nāropa, Marpa, Milarepa, Gampopa, the Karmapas) (RD410)

Read on 2026-11-24 (RD410) in the headed browser and on BDRC: the etexts and,
for the Mindroling rule-book and Khenpo Tsewang Dongyal's life of Dudjom
Rinpoche, the scans by eye; the search-only etexts in BDRC's search snippets
alone. The passages, in Tibetan with the English beside them, are in the
corpus files named below; the facts are claims. No model was run.

Where every source read agrees and a Tibetan text stands behind the date:
Longchenpa 12/18, Jigme Lingpa 9/3, Mipham 4/29, Dudjom Rinpoche 11/18 (KTD),
and the Guru's tenth of the fifth (monkey) month. Where they differ: Milarepa
and Marpa (three versions), Gampopa (two), the 3rd Karmapa (two), Terdak
Lingpa (the lists' 2/2, the Tibetan read gives the month only); the Karma
Kagyu days of a Tsurphu almanac need not fall on the Phugpa dates.

- Question 1373 ([q1373](questions/q1373.md)): Who are "the two root lamas" whose yearly remembrance the Mindroling rule-book holds from the 19th to the 29th of the second Hor month (MDB, img. 172), and is it Terdak Lingpa's own day, which the calendars put on 2/2?
- Question 1374 ([q1374](questions/q1374.md)): Who is Rje Bdud 'dul rab brtan, whose passing offering the Mindroling rule-book moved from the Saga month to the ninth month's descent from the gods (MDB, img. 179)?

- Question 1377 ([q1377](questions/q1377.md)): Is any day of the year kept for Tilopa and Nāropa in the Karma Kagyu, and with what source?
