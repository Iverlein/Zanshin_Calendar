# The verse 'the Heart recited once is twenty 'Bum rounds' is Atiśa's, and 'bum tshar is a full reading of the Hundred Thousand

The verse «ཤེས་སྙིང་ཚར་གཅིག་བཏོན་པ་ན། འབུམ་ཚར་ཉི་ཤུ་བཏོན་པར་འགྱུར» (ckg.13:p19) is said by Atiśa, and its unit is the 'Bum, not a count of Heart Sūtra recitations.

Speaker. Of the BDRC etexts of the verse, the Rin chen gter mdzod copy (ckg's other print) and the *Mdo khams gnas chen nyer lnga'i gnas yig phyogs bsgrigs* give it to «ཇོ་བོ་རྗེ» (jo bo rje, the Lord, Atiśa), and two further etexts (one of them listed under Chag lo tsā ba Rin chen chos rgyal) introduce the same lines with «དཔལ་ཨ་ཏི་ཤས» (dpal a ti shas, "the glorious Atiśa"); the Collected Works of 'Bri gung Chung tshang Chos kyi grags pa read «རྡོ་རྗེ་དཔོན་སློབ» (rdo rje dpon slob), a variant that has no second witness. The Lord is the reading.

Unit. The Abhisamayālaṅkāra commentary of Sa skya yongs 'dzin Ngag dbang chos kyi grags pa (three BDRC etexts, I2142 and its copies) quotes the verse among sayings that "the Indian and Tibetan scholars and siddhas said in agreement", and the verse pairs one «ཚར» (tshar, a full round) of the Heart with «འབུམ་ཚར» ('bum tshar, a full round of the 'Bum). «འབུམ་ཚར» is used elsewhere as a complete 'Bum: «འབུམ་ཚར་གཅིག་བཀླགས» ('bum tshar gcig bklags, "read the 'Bum once through", in a chos 'byung, BDRC snippet), and in a catalogue «གསུང་འབུམ་ཚར་གཉིས» ("two sets of collected works"). So one recital of the Heart gives the merit of twenty complete readings of the Hundred Thousand (འབུམ, 'bum, the Perfection of Wisdom in a hundred thousand lines), not twenty hundred-thousands of Heart recitations. One Khams text reads «ཉི་ཤུ་རྩ་དྲུག» (twenty-six); every other witness reads twenty.

The first verse of the group in the commentary also reads «ས་ཡ་ཕྲག་བརྒྱ» (sa ya phrag brgya) for the monk's meal: the same reading as ckg.
