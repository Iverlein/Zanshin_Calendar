# Yuasa 1995 lists the 23 chapters of the 新集周公解夢書: the officers are 21 and there is no ten-stem chapter

Yuasa Kunihiro, «夢の書の行方: 敦煌本『新集周公解夢書』の研究» (Machikaneyama Ronsō, Tetsugaku-hen 29, 1995, pp. 1-15; open on Osaka University's repository, ir.library.osaka-u.ac.jp, mrp_029-001.pdf), lists the book's 23 chapters: 1 天文, 2 地理, 3 山林草木, 4 水火盗賊, 5 官禄兄第, 6 人身形貌, 7 飲食, 8 仏道音楽, 9 荘園田宅, 10 衣服, 11 六畜禽獣, 12 龍蛇, 13 刀剣弓箭, 14 夫妻花粉, 15 楼閣家具銭財, 16 舟車橋市穀物, 17 生死疾病, 18 家墓棺財凶具, 19 十二支日得夢, 20 十二時得夢, 21 建除満日得夢, 22 悪夢為無禁忌等, 23 献撰悪夢 (read from an OCR text layer, some characters garbled). So the officers' chapter is 21 and no chapter of the ten stems stands in the book; the days by the twelve branches (十二支日, chapter 19) are the only day-count chapter before it. A reference list with «十干日得夢章» as 21 and the officers as 22 is therefore not this book's list; it most likely confuses 十二支 with 十干 and shifts the numbering by one. Yuasa names no other copy with a ten-stem chapter and says the book is complete only in P.3908; the other Dunhuang dream-book fragments he mentions are divided into 天部, 日月部 or 天事, 地理, 雑事.
