# Ch. 32: earth from a dog's and wolf's old lair at the door's lintel against loss

WB vol. 2, ch. 32, p. 294, l. 7–8 (img. 300 of the Gser thang reprint, and the 1996 print's img. 302; read on both scans by RD91, 2026-10-28): among the works of a new house, earth is taken from the old lair (བྲང་ཤུལ, brang shul) of dogs and wolves, or of the wolf-like dog called khyi spyang (ཁྱི་སྤྱང, khyi spyang), and put to the door's lintel (ཡ་ཐེམ, ya them); by it loss (གོད་ཁ, god kha) and hindrance cannot arise. Whether the earth adorns the lintel («སྤྲས», Yigdzin-1 on the reprint) or is hidden in it («སྦས», the 1996 etext) is not told on either scan.

RD114 (2026-11-01, etext): the BDRC full-text search finds «ཡ་ཐེམ་སྦས» (hidden at the lintel) in three etexts (the 1996 print, the Gser thang reprint, a copy in the *Bzo rtsis zlos gar*) and «ཡ་ཐེམ་སྤྲས» in none; so the earth is hidden at the lintel, on etext evidence, the scan's stack being a blot. The etexts are not independent witnesses of the block.

RD156 (2026-11-05): on the Zhol print (I1KG1710 img. 1040, l. 1), another print of the text, the stack reads «སྦས»: sa over ba, as the 1996 etext has it. The earth is hidden at the lintel. The same line of the Zhol print has «ཁྱི་སྤྱང་བྲང་ཤུལ».
