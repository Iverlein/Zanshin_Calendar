# The lexica give snying sdug as a loved one, not as heartache (RD322, q940)

No lexicon among Steinert's 64 gives སྙིང་སྡུག (snying sdug) the sense of heartache. Rangjung Yeshe, Valby, Ives–Waldo and the Tshig gter rgya mtsho give it as a person or thing held dear: "a loved one", "sweetheart", "darling", "a friend or a man or woman who strongly appeals to the mind"; Negi sends it to སྙིང་དུ་སྡུག་པ (snying du sdug pa), the adjective priya, dayita, manohara, hṛdayaṃgama, hṛdya (hṛdyam, ma.vyu. 2725); the Mahāvyutpatti gives snying du sdug pa = hṛdyam (p. 183 of no. 63); the Sgra bye brag tu rtogs byed chen mo: "pleasing to the heart, dear, beloved". So «སྙིང་སྡུག་འཐོབ» of the Svapnohana reads in the lexica's sense as "gets what is dear to the heart (a beloved, a dear one)". The only grounds for the other reading are the separate verb སྡུག་པ (sdug pa, "sorrow, to weep from sadness", Tshig mdzod chen mo), which no lexicon applies to snying sdug as a compound. The medical dictionaries of the collection (no. 35, no. 48) have no entry. A commentary on the Svapnohana that decides it was not found (q799, q940 stay open on that).
