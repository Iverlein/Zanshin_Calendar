# A camel facing south, a donkey east, a monkey north; a turban bound; ash; lac

Image group: body and mind. Read by 4 texts: Four Tantras (for a sick dreamer), Svapnohana (SVO), ACIP method (RST), Klong rdol's verses as the article quotes them (KBS).
Offered to any dreamer; the Four Tantras' or WB ch. 25's rows join when the dreamer is ill.

RD274 (2026-11-09) after RD268 and RD271: the Four Tantras' ch. 7 verse (Drathang print, machine) names the same dream for a sick dreamer, the naked ride to the south, and the animals are a buffalo, a horse, a pig, a donkey and the camel. The word རྔ་མོ is the camel (gt-rnga-mo-is-the-camel, RD271: the dictionaries and the verb of riding; Byang pa's and Rin spungs pa's རྔ་མོང is the same word, Khro ru's ཇ་མོ "female dzo" is not a dictionary form, and the Chag-po-ri print I3CN1711, read by eye at 5–12× by RD270, has རྔ་མོ with a head that is not Khro ru's ཇ: gt-dream-verse-chag-po-ri-prints), so RST's and KBS's camel facing south and the Four Tantras' ride agree on the camel and the south. The Four Tantras' row comes first, above RST and KBS (medicine); WB ch. 30's riding without a saddle is another dream (dream-riding-donkey-bare). The SVO row's she-camel of the cart is the same animal. The nakedness of the ride: dream-naked.
