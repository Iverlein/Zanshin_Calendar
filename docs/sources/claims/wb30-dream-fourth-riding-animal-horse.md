# Ch. 30's good dreams name four riding animals: lion, elephant, buffalo, horse

The good dream of riding upward on a well-saddled animal names the lion, the elephant, the buffalo and the horse (རྟ, rta). The glyph that is ink-filled on the Gser thang reprint reads as the ར-over-ཏ stack on the Taikhang and Zhol prints, both by eye and by Yigdzin-1; the 1996 print's machine reading ཧ is a misreading of a small stack. The bad dreams' unsaddled horse or donkey is a different line.
