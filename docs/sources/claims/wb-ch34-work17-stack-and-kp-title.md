# Work 17 on p. 378: the stack before «ཕྱར» has the height of a two-tier stack; KP's title lists «སྦྲ» and «ཕྱ་ཐེར» as two things

# Work 17 on p. 378: the stack before «ཕྱར» is a closed blot, as high as the two-tier stacks beside it; KP's title lists «སྦྲ» and «ཕྱ་ཐེར» as two things

The line of work 17 on the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5029 img. 384; the print's 1996 original is img. 386) reads, at 4× and at 10×: «…བྱ་སྦ[ྲ?]་ཕྱར(༡༧)འཚེམ» (the word before «བྱ» was not read again; RD64 has «བསྡུམས»). The stack of the syllable before «ཕྱར» is a closed black blot of two tiers, the head letter and the subjoined letter, of the same height as «བྱ» and «ཕྱ» beside it, with no separate third tier and no ra's foot under it. The reprint's bitmap is too coarse to show a ra under a ba, so this does not rule «སྦྲ» out; the Taikhang print (RD68) showed a ra, and the verses spell «སྦྲ». What the reprint gives is only that the printer's letter has the height of a two-tier stack: if the typesetting of this print wrote a ra it was drawn without a tier.

New witness: the Kun phan me long (KP) lists the work by name. Its list of contents (BDRC I4CZ65599 img. 9, the fifth line; Yigdzin-1 and MITRA both) reads «༼༢༤༽སྦྲ་དང་ཕྱ་ཐེར་འཚེམ་པ», "(24) sewing «སྦྲ» and «ཕྱ་ཐེར»", and the box's own heading on img. 37 (and KP2 img. 258) begins «…དང་ཕྱ་ཐེར་འཚེམ». The same two things in the same work as WB's p. 317 («ཕྱ་ཐེར་སྦྲ་འཚེམ») and p. 378. KP's «དང་» ("and") makes them two named things, not one compound, so in KP «ཕྱ་ཐེར» stands beside the tent where WB's list of p. 378 has «ཕྱར». Machine readings only; the contents line on the woodblock could not be found by eye at 2×, see the section RD88. Neither «ཕྱར» nor «ཕྱ་ཐེར» has a gloss found, so the question of what they are stays as it was.
