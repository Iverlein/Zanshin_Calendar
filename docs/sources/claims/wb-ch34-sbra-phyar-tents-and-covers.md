# Work 17 is the sewing of tents and covering sheets: sbra is a yak-hair tent, phyar a covering; phya ther stays unglossed

The list of works (p. 378) was read on the Taikhang reproduction of a Lhasa print (BDRC MW30116, scans I5878, 8280 px, img. 517, the print's own page number 507; Yigdzin-1 over img. 494–528, the line of work 17 by eye at 3×). Yigdzin-1's line has «འགྲིས་པ་བསྡུམས་བྱ་སྤྲ་ཕྱིར་འཚེམ» for work 17: the stack of the syllable before «ཕྱིར» shows a subjoined ra (a curved foot under the head letter), which the two smaller prints could not show; its head letter (sa or pa) and the vowel on «ཕྱིར» are not told by eye. The verses of the same print (img. 508, 510, 511) have «སྦྲ་ཕྱར་འཚེམ» three times with the ra, as the 1996 etext's verses do.

What the words are, from the dictionaries (Steinert's collection, 2026-10-28; entries are the dictionaries', not WB's): «སྦྲ» (*sbra*), a black tent of yak hair or felt (Rangjung Yeshe, Jim Valby, *Tshig mdzod chen mo*; «སྦྲ་གུར» the same tent); «ཕྱར་བ» (*phyar ba*, noun), a covering sheet or tarpaulin woven of wool, hair, hide or cloth (*Tshig mdzod chen mo* sense 2: «ལྡེ་ཕྱར», «རས་ཕྱར», «རྩིད་ཕྱར»; *Dag tshig gsar bsgrigs*: a square woven fabric of wool and hair), and the verb «འཕྱར» to hoist, raise or let float. «འཚེམ» is to sew. So «སྦྲ་ཕྱར་འཚེམ» is the sewing of tents and covering sheets, as the earlier reading guessed, now with «ཕྱར» a covering and not only a flutter. «ཕྱ་ཐེར» has no headword in the 64 dictionaries; it is the same word's pair in «ཕྱ་ཐེར་སྦྲ་འཚེམ» (p. 317 of the 1996 etext) and stays unglossed.
