# WB's model almanac: the great occasions and festivals it writes at the heads of months 1, 3, 4, 6 and 9

The White Beryl (rank 1) rules in ch. 14 that the Sage's entry into the womb, birth, turning of the wheel, miracles and passing, "and the other great occasions" (དུས་ཆེན, dus chen), are written at the head of the month as one's own tradition explains them (wb.1:p174-178). Its own model almanac in ch. 13 does so in five month heads, each read on the scan:

| Hor month | Day | What the head writes | Word used |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1st to 15th | the Victor showed miracles | great occasion |
| 3 | 15th | the nondual Kālacakra was taught at 'Bras spungs | great occasion |
| 4 | 7th | the Lord of Sages was born | festival (དུས་སྟོན, dus ston) |
| 4 | 15th | his awakening and his passing beyond sorrow | festival |
| 6 | 4th | he turned the wheel of Dharma | festival |
| 6 | 15th | he entered his mother's womb | festival |
| 9 | 22nd | the Victor came down from the gods | great occasion |

The heads of months 2, 5, 7, 8, 10, 11 and 12, as the pool holds them, name no festival. Each entry states the day and the event only: no practice, vow or offering is prescribed for it, and no multiplying of merit is attached to it in these lines. No festival of Padmasambhava, no tenth day and no twenty-fifth is written in any head.
