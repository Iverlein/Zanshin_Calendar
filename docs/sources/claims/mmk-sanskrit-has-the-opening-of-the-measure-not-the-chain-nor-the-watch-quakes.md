# The Sanskrit has the opening of ch. 18's measure (moment, eye's opening and closing, finger-snap) in a second scan of the edition, but neither the chain of units nor the earthquakes by watch (RD378, q1057)

The Sanskrit edition does have the opening of ch. 18's measure of time, and still no chain of units and no earthquakes by watch; the earlier claim mmk-sanskrit-edition-lacks-the-measure rests on one text layer that lost the lines.

A second scan of the same edition (Trivandrum Sanskrit Series 70, Gaṇapati Śāstrī, part 1, 1920; its own Devanagari text layer, much garbled) has, at the end of the chapter that holds its dream and births, the four chief planets (Venus, the Moon, Jupiter, Mercury) and then «kṣaṇamātraṃ tathonmeṣa-nimeṣaṃ cāpi acchaṭam» and «etan-mātra-pramāṇaṃ tu grahāṇām»: a moment, the opening and closing of the eye and, probably, a snap of the fingers (the sense of «acchaṭa» is my guess from the Tibetan's se gol, a doubt), as the units of the planets' swift course (mmk.2:p158-160). That is the Derge's lines «མིག་ནི་འབྱེད་དང་འཛུམས་པ་དང་། སེ་གོལ་མྱུར་བའི་འགྲོས་འདི་ནི། འདི་དག་དུས་ཀྱི་ཚད་དག་ནི། རྒྱ་ཆེར་ཡང་ནི་བསྟན་པར་བྱ» after its four best planets (mmk.1:p438a), so the Sanskrit's se gol is probably acchaṭa. What follows in the Derge (se gol, rtsa rgyun, chu tshod, watch, skad cig, yud tsam, the thirty yud tsam, the half-month to the great year) is not in the Sanskrit's page; there the text goes on to the setting of the planets and to birth. Search of the second text layer: no «muhūrta», «ghaṭikā», «nāḍikā» or «truṭi» in all of part 1 (323,957 characters), «nimeṣa» (निमेष) once, here, the other hits being «animiṣa» (not winking); the earthquake passage of part 1 (about printed pp. 208-210 in the OCR's marks) is by asterism, with «ardharātra» (midnight) once and «divā» (by day), no watches of the night.

So the Sanskrit and the Derge agree for the opening of the measure, and the Derge has more; whether it has expanded the text or the Sanskrit's manuscript had a shorter one is not shown. The Sanskrit's order of the material also differs: it puts the dream, the births and this measure in one chapter, where the Derge puts the dream in its ch. 15 and the births and the measure in its ch. 18.
