# «སྐར་འབྲས་སྤྱི་སྡོམ» heads the match's counts of pebbles; «སྐར་འབྲས» itself stays open

Narrowed 2026-11-01. On the scan (wb.1:p351b, Gser thang) the three prints agree on «སྐར་འབྲས་སྤྱི་སྡོམ», and the lines after it give the sum of the pebbles, thirteen in the root (from the *Clear Lamp*), twenty-nine thousand and twenty-five if expanded, and then the pebbles counted (1996 etext p. 361): the word «སྤྱི་སྡོམ» heads a sum of pebbles, not a second conclusion of the match. Mipham's notes use it so (vol. 2, p. 521: «སྤྱི་སྡོམ་ལྟར་ཉིས་བརྒྱ་…», "by the general sum, two hundred and …", of the household's identifying pebbles, and again at p. 513 as the heading of a tally). «སྐར་འབྲས» is not glossed; it follows the list of fifteen points that ends with the planets (14) and the mansions (15), so "the star results" are most likely the results of those two; not settled.
