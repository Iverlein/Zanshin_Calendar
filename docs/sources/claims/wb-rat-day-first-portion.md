# The rat day's remedy lines on p. 224 are read, their sense is not settled

The lines the old question 404 cites stand on p. 224 (img. 230 of the Gser thang reprint), not on p. 225, in the rat day's paragraph: «སྦྲང་མ་ཕུད་གཏོར་ཡོལ་བས་སྒྲིབ། ཐེམ་བུར་མི་འཚག་ཡོ་ནས་འདྲེན།». The words are certain on the scan (the second line's first syllable has a subjoined ra, as the 1996 print's etext has it; RD56 read «སྤང»); 'screened with a cloth' (ཡོལ་བ) and 'the threshold' (ཐེམ་བུ) are plain, «སྦྲང་མ་ཕུད» (a first portion of bees' wax or honey, or of the bee?) and «ཡོ་ནས་འདྲེན» («ཡོ» a window? the hare, the east?) are not. The Zhol print's etext is the same work and no independent witness.
