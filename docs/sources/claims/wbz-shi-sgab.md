# The run of deaths is «sgab» on the Zhol blocks

The term for the run of deaths in WB vol. 2, pp. 104–108 is carved with ས over ག, «སྒབ» («ཤི་སྒབ», «སྤར་ཁའི་སྒབ»), at the two places read on the Zhol blocks (img. 778, l. 4; img. 784, l. 4), as the grave chapter's «ན་སྒབ་ཤི་སྒབ»; the WBZ etext reads «སྒབ» at both. The other places of the question were not read on the blocks.
