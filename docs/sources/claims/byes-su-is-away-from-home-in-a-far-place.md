# «བྱེས་སུ» in the black pebbles' dream is "away from home, in a far place": the lexica and WB's other lines (RD396, q1080)

«བྱེས» (byes) is a place far from the home ground, and «བྱེས་སུ» (byes su) is "away, to or in a far place, abroad". The Bod rgya tshig mdzod chen mo and the Bod yig tshig gter rgya mtsho define it «གཞིས་ལས་རིང་དུ་ཆོད་པའི་གནས» (a place cut off far from the home ground) with the examples «འགྲུལ་པ་བྱེས་ནས་འོངས་པ» (the traveller came from far) and «ཁྱིམ་ནས་བྱེས་སུ་བཏེག་པ» (carried from the house to a far place); the Dag tshig gsar bsgrigs: «རང་ཡུལ་དང་བར་ཐག་རིང་བའི་གནས་ཀྱི་མིང» (the name of a place far from one's own country), «བྱེས་པ» the one who goes there, set against «གཞིས་པ» the one at home (the home-ground and the far place, «གཞིས་བྱེས», "at home and abroad" in the Tibetan Language School's wording); Rangjung Yeshe "foreign country, abroad"; Valby "place of temporary residence, foreign country, abroad", «བྱེས་པ» "foreigner, stranger, traveller"; the Sgom sde tshig mdzod: a place divided off from the root place (RD396, 2026-11-11, Steinert's collection, Wylie headwords, the Tibetan converted from the Wylie).

The same phrase is used elsewhere of people away from home, as BDRC's exact phrases «བྱེས་སུ་ཤི» (24 hits) and «བྱེས་སུ་འཁྱམས» (113 hits) show (RD396, 2026-11-11, snippets only, the Wylie of the search results): the White Beryl's lines «skyes pa byes su shi ba yod» (a man dies «byes su») and «byes su 'khyams dang mi go 'grims» (wandering «byes su»), the same «skyes pa yin na byes su shi» in the earth-snake year's lines of the *Nag rtsis snang srid me long*, «mi rigs byes su 'khyams nas 'chi» (a person dies after wandering «byes su») in the lines of the black pebbles of the *klung* in the White Beryl and the Gser thang print, «gnyen med byes su 'khyams pa 'dra» (like a kinless one wandering «byes su») in a Four Tantras text (BDRC's "gso rig rgyud bzhi"), «bu byes su 'khyams pa pha khyim du 'gro ba ltar» (like a son who wandered «byes su» going to his father's house) in a lama's life, and in the Vinaya commentary «byes su song ba'i bud med» (a woman who went away «byes su») and «byes su shi» (died away). So the dream «རྨི་ལམ་བྱེས་སུ་ཉལ་བ་རྨི» of the black pebbles is the dream of lying down (sleeping) away from home, in a strange or far place, among strangers, with no kin about; it goes with the lines of the same pebble («the black crow of the mara above, the dog of the dam sri below») as the sign of a life cut off from its own ground, and is not a place with its own name. No text found glosses the phrase in the dream; the sense is that of the word in the lexica, and its use in the pebble's dream is read from it.
