# Which dreams of the night are of habit, the afflictions and omen (LGD)

LGD ties the character of a dream to the part of the night. At dusk (སྲོད, srod) the dreams are for the most part dreams of habit (བག་ཆགས, bag chags); at midnight (གུང, gung) dreams of the afflictions (ཉོན་མོངས་པ, nyon mongs pa); at the early dawn (ཐོ་རངས, tho rangs) the dreams of good and bad omen (རྟེན་འབྲེལ, rten 'brel), the ones that are examined as signs; and for some people they come with no fixed time (ལ་ལར་ངེས་མེད་དུ་འབྱུང་བའང་ཡོད). The three parts of the night are named, the spans of each are not given in this text. A mixture of good and bad at dusk and dawn is uncertain (lgd:p969), a mixture the dreamer cannot recognise is a trick (ཆོ་འཕྲུལ, cho 'phrul).
