# The remedy for a bad dream, per text, once

Each text's remedy for a bad dream is given once here and not in the image rows. None of them is graded by the image, except where the row says so; the app shows one Remedy row per text that is in the reading.

| Text | Remedy | Passage | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WB ch. 30 | purify with incense smoke (བསང, bsang) and washing, and strive again for good dream omens; said of the bad dreams before the asking or the reckoning | `wb.2:p144` | scan |
| Four Tantras (ch. 7, closing lines), for a sick dreamer | a dream is a distant and uncertain sign of death: turned back by gathering merit, recitation and the practice of the yidam; the near signs call for the death ransom (the commentaries give each kind of person his means, dream-sick-any-dream-death-sign) | `st:p34` | scan |
| Four Tantras (ch. 7 verse), for a sick dreamer | the verse itself: the sick who dream the bad dreams constantly die; for one without illness it is not certain, and the rite of service (རིམ་གྲོ, rim gro) frees him, as gathering merit, reading and meditation turn the harm back (RD268, RD255) | `gt:i42-43` | machine |
| Four Tantras (ch. 15, 23, 27), for a sick dreamer | the confused dreams of the unripe fever, the unripe pestilence and the cham of the pestilence are signs of the illness; no remedy for the dream, the illness is ripened with the four manu decoction or the three fruits with sle tres (RD229) | `st:p185-186`, `st:p222-223`, `st:p241-242b` | scan |
| Four Tantras (ch. 91), for a sick dreamer | for the dream of women leading one away, a sign of a spirit: divination with its omen (མོ་ཕྱྭ, mo phywa), the rite of service, the dependent link, and a thread twisted by a clean boy, with seven vajra knots and a mantra spoken a thousand times, bound on (the mantra is read letter by letter, RD227; its meaning is not asked) | `st:p493` | scan |
| WB ch. 25 (the sick), dragon year | the shapes of whatever bad came in dreams are poured into a human skull and sent north-west with the ransom | `wb.1:p430` | scan |
| LGD | "if a bad one comes, do the rites that reverse the omen"; the passage does not name the rites; the rite of the text (approach, application, examination) is for obtaining and catching a dream, not for turning a bad one | `lgd:p970`, `lgd:p961-966` | scan; the rite machine |
| SVO | a fire offering (སྦྱིན་སྲེག, sbyin sreg) of red sandalwood sticks smeared with butter, the Gāyatrī (ག་ཡ་ཏྲི) recited a thousand times; whoever reads the text early in the morning turns back his bad dreams (the mantra's spelling is settled by Peking and the two etexts, RD228; the remedy is the same on the Peking print, RD233, and on the Co ne print, whose machine reading has «གས་ཏྲི» for the mantra's name, not checked by eye, RD273) | `svo:p271`, `svo:p271b`, `svo:p271d`, `svo:p271g` | machine; the Peking print by eye |
| SVI | for a bad sign, a sesame fire offering with a thousand recitations in that family; for a fortunate dream, begin the practice by the rite of the Jewel Seal | `svi:p262` | etext |
| RRT, for a practitioner's obstacle | the heart-pledge of the protectors fulfilled, clay votive images, water and grain offerings, much virtue, the obstructors driven off; not a remedy for an ordinary bad dream | `rrt:p186-195` | etext |

No remedy is given by SKP, RST (its verses end with the death signs), KBS (none in the verses the article quotes), KCM, DTD, RBG or KDL. WB ch. 30's and SVO's are the two remedies the roadmap's reading shows for an ordinary dreamer; the Four Tantras' and WB ch. 25's join when the dreamer is ill.

RD274 (2026-11-09) adds the Four Tantras' ch. 7 verse's own rule (the rite of service frees the one not yet ill; gt-dream-death-rule) and the treatment of the chapters where the confused dream is a sign of an illness (the illness is treated, not the dream), and settles two words of the table: the Gāyatrī's spelling (svo-gayatri-spelling) and the mantra of ch. 91 (RD227).
