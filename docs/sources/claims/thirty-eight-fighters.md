# The thirty: the eight fighting pebbles and their colours

The eight fighting pebbles of each starting point (WB vol. 1, pp. 453–454), counted from the year reached (claim thirty-count), each a contest between an aspect of that place and the same aspect of the one reckoned (question 97, answered 2026-10-19):

| Pebble, in WB's order | Place | Aspect fought |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | the fourth upward | life force (སྲོག, srog) |
| 2 | the fourth downward | body (ལུས, lus) |
| 3 | the eleventh upward | power (དབང་ཐང, dbang thang) |
| 4 | the eleventh downward, "the place of the luck's enemy" | luck (ཀླུང་རྟ, klung rta) |
| 5 | the seventh upward | life force |
| 6 | the seventh downward | body |
| 7 | the year reached itself | power |
| 8 | the year reached itself | luck |

The colour of each pebble is the relation of the place's element to the reckoned person's element of the same aspect: mother or friend, and earth or water meeting its own element, white; son or enemy, and wood, iron or fire meeting its own element, black (p. 454). One pebble a contest; the son is black here, unlike ch. 25's pair (question 98).

30 points × 8 = 240 root pebbles; WB says 241 (p. 454) and gives no account of the one more; the *Moonbeams* says 240 (claim thirty-241-pebbles).
