# p. 63: the day's life-cutting months agree with the hour entries where both are given

The day's-animal entries (p. 63) and the hour entries (pp. 66–68) both name a month "in which the life was cut". Where an animal has both, they agree: the monkey, the dog month (the day's «སྤྲེལ་ཉིར་ཤི་ན་ཁྱི་ཟླར་བཅད»; the monkey hour's «ཁྱི་ཡི་ཟླ་བར་བླ་ཡང་བཅད»); the sheep, the sheep month on the day, the ox or the sheep month on the hour («གླང་ངམ་ལུག་ཟླར་བཅད»). The other three day animals (tiger, snake, horse) have no month in the hour entries. The months are not the clash months of the second examination (p. 58: tiger monkey, hare bird, dragon dog, snake pig, horse rat, sheep ox, monkey tiger, bird hare, dog dragon, pig snake, rat horse, ox sheep), which differ from the day's for tiger, snake, horse, sheep and monkey. So the day's months are the hour entries' kind, one entry per animal and no rule found; the six days are a short list, and the text says «གཞན་མར་བཟང་ངན་བཤད་པ་མེད» for the rest.
