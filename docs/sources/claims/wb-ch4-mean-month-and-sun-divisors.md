# The divisors 39592 and 804 and the units dbyu gu, srang, dbugs, cha of the grub rtsis

The grub rtsis (Phug pa) mean month and the sun's mean monthly motion are each one fraction with one divisor, written once and understood afterwards. «བགོད་བྱེད» (*bgod byed*, "the divisor") is 39592 for the planet's (weekday) reckoning and 804 for the sun's. The pure month wanted is adorned with 7313, multiplied by 8657, divided by 39592, the quotient thrown away, and the remainder multiplied by 7: the whole is the weekday number (days, to be thrown away by sevens). Each later step multiplies the remainder and divides by the same divisor: by 60 gives the first sixtieth (*dbyu gu*), by 60 again the *srang* (*chu srang*), by 6 the breath (*dbugs*), by 707 the part (*cha*). So the units descend from the whole by 60, 60, 6 and 707; a day is 60 · 60 · 6 = 21600 breaths (the figure p. 55 gives).

For the sun the pure month is adorned with 309, multiplied by 65, divided by 804, the remainder multiplied by 27 (the wheel): the whole is the star-place (the mansion), then by 60 / 804 the *dbyu gu*, by 60 / 804 the *srang*, by 6 / 804 the breath, by 67 (not 707) / 804 the part. So for the sun the *dbyu gu* is a sixtieth of a mansion, for the weekday a sixtieth of a day.

Worked with exact fractions: 7 · 8657 / 39592 = 1 day 31 *dbyu gu* 50 *srang* 0 breaths 480/707, remainder zero, which is the five places of the pure month's constant on p. 30 (1, 31, 50, 0, 480; the lunar month 29;31;50;0;480 less four weeks). 65 · 27 / 804 = 2 mansions 10 *dbyu gu* 58 *srang* 1 breath 17/67, remainder zero, which is p. 30's sun constant «2, 10, 58, 1» and «multiply by 17». Both divisions end with no remainder, as the verses say («ལྷག་མེད»).
