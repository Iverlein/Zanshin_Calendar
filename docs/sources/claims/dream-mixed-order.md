# A mixed dream turns toward the second image (LGD)

LGD (scan) rules on a dream that holds both good and bad: good first and bad after, it turns to the side of the bad; bad first and good after, it turns good; a mixture the dreamer cannot recognise is a trick; a mixture of good and bad at dusk and dawn has no certainty. No other text read gives this rule. It is a rule over two images in one dream, not a verdict for either image alone, and the catalogue does not use it to sum a dream: the screen never calls a dream good or bad as a whole.
