# «ལྒང་ཕུག» is the bladder as a bag; WB spells it with ག

WB's etext spells the goat's container of p. 106 «ལྒང་ཕུག» (lgang phug) in four places of vol. 2 (pp. 106, 277, 298, 301), never «ལྷང་ཕུག»; WB's own word for the bladder is «ལྒང་པ» (lgang pa, vol. 1, pp. 262, 443, 447–448). So the heart, nose and bladder of the goat are put into the corpse's mouth in the dwa passage; the Zhol print has both lines (img. 780) and the short line on img. 783 is the zin passage's own, so neither print lost or added the line. The spelling rests on the etext's repeats and the Zhol machine reading; the letter under the Zhol's pink wash was not seen.
