# The model almanac: twelve months of thirty boxes, five to a row

The model almanac of WB vol. 1 is twelve months of day boxes, five to a row, six rows to a month (30 boxes), one month after another from the 11th Hor month (Sagittarius) to the 10th, with the month's head of seven lines of prose before each month's boxes. The rows run across the page breaks: p. 154 has the 11th month's head and boxes 1-15, p. 155 its boxes 16-30 and the 12th month's head, p. 156 twenty-five boxes, p. 157 the end of the 12th, the head of the 1st month and its boxes 1-10, and so on. The heads begin on pp. 154 (11th), 155 (12th), 157 (1st), 158 (2nd), 160 (3rd), 162 (4th), 163 (5th), 165 (6th), 166-167 (7th), 168 (8th), 170 (9th) and 171 (10th), after RD2's list. The tenth month's boxes, and the verse that closes the chapter, fall on pp. 172-173, beyond the range RD196's row names.
