# Three words of the illness identified looked at on the scan: ཉ, རྙོངས, བླངས་ལན་པའི

Three words of the passages on the illness identified, looked at on the scan by RD53 (2026-10-25):

- «ཉ་བཞི་ན་ལ» (WB p. 435, in the line «[ཉ]་བཞི་ན་ལ་ཁ་ནད་ལྕེར»): the first letter of the stack reads «ཉ» on the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5028 img. 430, row 21, at 3×), not «ཞྭ» as the etext has it. It agrees with p. 436, which prints «ཉ་དང་ཚིགས་བཞི་ན་བ» ("the «ཉ» and the four joints ache", wb.1:p436). What «ཉ» is there is not told: a body part, with «ཚིགས་བཞི» the four joints; no gloss is found.
- «[རྙོངས]་ཚད» (WB p. 435, the fever of fire four and wind one): on the reprint the stack is a clot of ink (img. 430, row 26, at 8×) with a tail below: «རྙ» cannot be told from «ཀྱ» or «རྟ». The 1996 print's Yigdzin-1 reads «རྙོངས», the reprint's «སྟོངས», agy (one crop) offered «སྟོངས» or «རྟོངས»; the sixth fever above is already «སྟོངས་ཚད» (fire three and earth two), and a different split would be unlikely to repeat the name, which speaks for a different word, but nothing on the page settles it.
- «བླངས་ལན་པའི» (Mipham's notes, wb.2:p497, line 2, img. 503): read as printed, syllable by syllable, in «རང་ལོ་ཡི་སྲོག་དང་སྐྱེས་པའི་སྤར་སྨེ་གསུམ་གྱི་ཁམས་དབུགས་སྤྱི་ལྟར་བླངས་ལན་པའི་དུས་ཀྱི་ལོ་ཟླ་ཞག་དུས་སྟེང་ཁྱེར་བས་ཤེས་སོ»: it qualifies the elements of the life force, birth trigram and mewa, "whose breath is taken (བླངས) in the general way", as carried onto the year, month, day and hour of the time «ལན་པའི». «ལན» is not a word the pool's sources gloss in this place; no reading of its sense is made.
