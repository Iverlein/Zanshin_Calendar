# CBL's explanation is an old annotation supplemented by Mkhyen brtse, not his own work

The text that Mkhyen brtse's collected works print (CBL) is the Indian root work with a running explanation (the title's "with the explanation of the outer and inner death signs", cbl:p217), and the explanation is not Mkhyen brtse's own composition. The editor's note at the end (cbl:p278-279) says that this note on 'Deceiving Death' is an old note of earlier time, supplemented in the handwriting of the Lord Mkhyen brtse, which the editor thought important enough to print; that the scribe of the print's base made mistakes and the model copy and its notes are unclean, in old script and much in need of examination; and that the text was copied from the hand-marks the Lord Lama put on the margin of a copy whose old annotator is not known. So the glosses between the verses (for example the lama's remark in the dream verses: "the lama says: it is the sign of the arrival of the time of death", cbl:p236-238) are the old annotator's, with Mkhyen brtse's additions, and not the root work. The remark names a lama and gives no source for the verses. The explanation says nothing of the dream verses' own origin; where it speaks of sources at all, it is at the end of the work (claim chi-bslu-names-its-sources). State: etext only.
