# The rebirth by the dead's aspects against the time of death

The fifth examination's reading by the dead's aspects against the year, month, day and hour of death (wb.2:p70-72; the notes, wb.2:p503-504, count it as twenty-five fights and five verdicts, thirty).

| The dead's aspect | Rebirth |
| --- | --- |
| life force rising | a god |
| body defeated | an asura |
| power defeated | a human |
| luck defeated | an animal |
| la | a hungry ghost |
| deeper luck defeated | hell |

Some read the relation of the dead's year to the time of death: life force or inner meeting, a human; mother, a god; friend, an animal; son, a hungry ghost or yakṣa; enemy, an asura or hell. By body, illness, power, wealth and luck, the birth is graded: mother and itself best, friend and son middling, enemy last; purifying sin turns it. WB calls these examinations a great source of error. The notes take the aspects' rise and decline on the year of death and name the la "the body-mother".
