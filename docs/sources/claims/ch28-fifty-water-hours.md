# The fifty water-hours of the leaking-roof day

WB vol. 2, p. 37: «ཉི་སྐར་གནས་སུ་སྨིན་དྲུག་དང་། ཆུ་ཚོད་ལྔ་བཅུར་ལངས་པའི་ཉིན།». «ཉི་སྐར» is the sun's mansion in WB's other chapters (vol. 1, p. 80, with the moon's, as the mansion the sun is in; «བྱེད་རྩིས་ལ་ཉི་སྐར», the sun's mansion in the working reckoning), and a water-hour (ཆུ་ཚོད, chu tshod) is a sixtieth part of a day, and of a mansion's course; the verse names the mansion first (the Pleiades) and then the water-hour in it to which the sun has "risen": the day the sun, in the Pleiades' mansion, has reached the fiftieth sixtieth of it. This is a reading from the words' usage; the hour-of-the-day reading would have no use for «ཉི་སྐར་གནས་སུ». No other text searched (BDRC's full-text search, the Bhutan and Chengdu collections) repeats the verse beyond copies of WB. The "fourth" system («ལུགས་གཞན་བཞི་པ») is not explained by the notes (p. 537, which skips the rite) or any text found: it is the fourth of the other systems; what the other three are is not in the pages.
