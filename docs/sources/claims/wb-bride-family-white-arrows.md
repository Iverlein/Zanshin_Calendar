# The family wheel, the white wheels and the arrows wheel

The family wheel, the white wheels and the arrows wheel of marriage (WB vol. 2, pp. 409–411). Family: Gyal, Tha-skar, Nabso, Mgo, Namgru, Lha-mtshams, Sari, Gro-bzhin and the four fires are of the gods' family; the others of the jewels'; the husband's and wife's name-stars agree by their families: gods and men middling, gods and rākṣasas likewise, man and rākṣasa die together, a god's family wins over all, man and rākṣasa are as fire and water. The white wheel for a young girl (three stars at a time on heart, head, ears, back, hands and feet): body, brothers, sons, friends, wealth, death, emptiness, gain and the dharma are joined to those places, with the gain measured one for the sun and seven for Saturn. The white wheel proper (a woman's figure, the first star at the mouth): where the husband's name-star goes decides the wife's regard (crown: honoured like a king; brow: sweet words; right eye: heat; left eye: taking and leaving; nose: no bad woman found; ears: an earlier wife; heart: honoured, long-lived; hands: illness; back: an earlier wife dies; secret place: nine sons; feet: prison, flight, theft). A second white wheel (ash, from the star where the white companion stands): crown for the brahmin and women teachers; palm the gaining of power; feet wealth spent. The arrows wheel (ཤ་ལ་ཀ, śalākā): seven lines each way, twenty-eight stars from the east; the star opposite a sinful planet in front embraces, behind burns, to the rear is smoke: burning kills, embracing brings illness, taming takes desire. The quarters: the sun in a quarter-star is unlucky; planets of merit in a quarter make it good; planets opposite the moon lose the bride's fruit, opposite the sun the husband dies, the Red-Eye parts from a son.
