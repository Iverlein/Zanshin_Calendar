# RRT: what is done when signs cannot be told apart

RRT p. 193 (etext). When blessing and capacity do not agree, or the signs of accomplishment come while the obstruction is a demon's (virtue does not grow, the heart is unhappy, the sweet smell does not come), and the kind cannot be told: fulfil the heart-pledge of the outer and inner protectors, make clay votive images, water and grain offerings, gather much virtue, and drive off and subdue the obstructors much. If it is an obstruction, the signs that came are cut off; if not, they grow clearer. The text carries the remedy for a practitioner's obstacle, not for an ordinary bad dream. The key to telling the good and bad of signs is ascribed to Gnubs Sangs rgyas ye shes.
