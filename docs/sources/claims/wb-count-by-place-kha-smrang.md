# «ཁ་སྨྲང» and «ངོ་ཐེག» at p. 381: the count by account and by place

P. 381's «དེ་ལ་ཁ་སྨྲང་རྡེལ་འགྲེམ་ལུགས» is the manner of laying the pebbles by account: «ཁ་སྨྲང» (kha smrang) is speech, talk, an account (Rangjung Yeshe, Ives Waldo, Tshig mdzod chen mo; WB vol. 2, p. 50 and the notes, p. 518 use it so, question 174). The stack after ཁ་ is a solid blot on the Gser thang scan, so «སྨྲང» rests on the etext and the dictionaries; «ཁ་སྦྲང» (kha sbrang), mouth-honey, a newborn's first taste, is another word, in Sakya and Zhe chen texts, and does not suit. «ངོ་ཐེག» (ngo theg, read so on the Gser thang crop) is in none of the 64 dictionaries and in no text outside WB's prints and copies (BDRC's 55 hits are other words run together); in «ངོ་ཐེག་དཀར་ནག་བགྲངས་པ་ཡི། གང་མང་ཁ་དམར་རྡེལ་བོར་བ» it counts the white and black by ངོ, the face, the person or place counted (the notes use ངོ so in «མི་ངོ་དུ་ཡོད», how many people there are), and the more of the two gives the verdict pebble. ཐེག is not glossed anywhere.
