# The doubled ma of «སྲོག་མ་མ་གུད་པ» stands on all three prints

Narrowed by three prints, read by eye (RD143). The line «སྲོག་དགྲ་དར་ཀྱང་སྲོག་མ་མ་གུད་པ་དང་སྤར་སྨེ་ངན་པ་མ་འཛོམ་ན་དེ་ཙམ་མི་སྐྱོན། འཛོམ་ན་ངན་ནོ།» (the last lines of p. 483) stands with the doubled «མ་མ» on the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5029 img. 489, l. 28, 4×: «སྲོག་མ་མ་གུད་པ», two separate ma), on the 1996 print (I1KG12907 img. 491, l. 28, Yigdzin-1, the same string) and on Mipham's own print (I2029 img. 641, l. 1 at 2.4×, the two ma side by side before «གུད་པ»; the sentence opens that folio). So the doubling is Mipham's own spelling and not a typesetter's dittography of the reprint; the earlier guess that an editor doubled it is dropped. Mipham's print also continues at once «ས་བཞི་ནི་མཚམས་བཞིའོ», as the typeset editions. The grammar stays open: "the life force's mother, not in decline" (no. A of the earlier claim) or a doubled syllable. What Mipham's own spelling does settle is that no print has a single ma to be read as «སྲོག་མ་གུད་པ», "in decline".

A new point from the page before it (p. 483, ll. 26-27, by Yigdzin-1 on the reprint, the 1996 print and Mipham's print; not separately read by eye): the list that precedes the sentence names what is called "in decline" (གུད, gud) when its verdict on the hundred is black: the life force by its own pebble, the power by its own, mother, son and the four friends, the body by its own and its two friends; and «དགྲ་དར» is then set apart: "each one's enemy pebble white".
