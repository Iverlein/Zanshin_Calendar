# Ch. 30's places have no single verdict rule

The text's «ཁ་དམར» (verdict) of ch. 30's places is not given by one rule: p. 163's earth examinations (Yigdzin-1 of img. 171, l. 12–13) say «ས་དཔྱད་གོང་བཞིན་བརྩིས་པ་ལ། ཁ་དམར་མ་ནོར་སོ་སོར་གདགས», the verdict "set correctly, each its own"; the house, field and grave of pp. 166–168 are read by the cutting and by rise and decline each with its own aspect (the house by the life force cut by the moment, the field by the power's rise and decline with «དར་ཡོད་གུད་ན་མེད», the grave by the luck's rise and decline); only the wealth's lines (img. 176, l. 15–17) end «བྱ་རས་ལུས་དབང་གཅོད་ཀྱང་མེད། དེ་གཉིས་བསྟུན་ལ་ཁ་དམར་གདགས», where «དེ་གཉིས» follows two statements (rise and decline gives having or lacking; the cutting gives lacking) and names the body and power as well, so both readings stand; no place has a white-and-black count of its own. Still open.
