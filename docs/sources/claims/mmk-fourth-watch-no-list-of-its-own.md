# No list of signs under the fourth watch; the true dream's marks and the houses (RD370)

The root tantra gives no list of signs under the label of the fourth watch: after the lists of the first three (each in its own run of verses) the next lines are on the three poisons, the temperaments and the mantra suited to each (mmk.1:p401-403), then «རྨི་ལམ་བདེན་པར་མཐོང་བ་ནི། མཚན་ཉིད་འདི་དག་གསུངས་པ་ནི།» ("the marks of seeing a true dream, these were said"), which is followed by a list of figures (the twelve zodiac names in the Sanskrit, then an elephant, a god, a monkey, an asura, siddhas, gandharvas, yakshas) called the "houses" of beings (mmk.1:p403-404, mmk.2:p152-153). Whether that list is the content of the fourth watch's true dream or a separate statement is not said in these lines and is a doubt; the Sngags rim chen mo does not use it.
