# The koma-e, the inset picture of a nishiki-e, as a museum catalogue names it

The koma-e (コマ絵), the inset picture of a nishiki-e, as the Tokyo National Museum's catalogue names it (ColBase, read through a Japanese exit on 2026-11-24): Utagawa Kuniyoshi's series *Sankai medetai zue* (山海めて度図会, 1852) pairs a woman's gesture, titled "wanting to …", with a koma-e of a province's famous product; in *Tsuzuki ga mitai* (A-10569-5396) the koma-e quotes Shitomi Kangetsu's illustration in the *Sankai meisan zue* of whitebait fishing at Nishinomiya in Settsu. Toyokuni's *Fūryū hakkei, Aki no tsuki* (A-10569-2362) has the autumn view of Ishiyama-dera in a koma-e at upper right. Every koma-e seen is rectangular; no catalogue or print read has a round one, which stays an open question. The print chosen as the brief's style reference and its description are in [festivals.md](../festivals.md), RD409 follow-up.
