# WB ch. 33: the religious works among the works good and bad on each of the thirty dates

From the White Beryl's readings of the thirty lunar dates (rank 1; vol. 2, pp. 297-302, as lunar-dates.md's table holds them from the scan; the lines were not read again for this claim). They are works to be elected by date, for whoever undertakes them, and not observances that return each month:

- Teaching the Dharma: good on the 29th; to be avoided on the 1st and the 14th. Hearing the Dharma: to be avoided on the 6th. "Dharma" among the good works of the 15th.
- Worship of the gods: bad on the 1st.
- Ordination: good on the 3rd, 5th, 10th, 11th, 14th and 23rd; to be avoided on the 1st, 4th, 6th, 7th, 8th, 12th and 15th.
- Consecration: good on the 2nd, 3rd and 5th to 15th; to be avoided on the 4th, 16th and 19th to 30th.
- Empowerment: good on the 15th.
- Building temples and stūpas, making images: good on the 10th, 12th, 13th and 15th (images also on the 8th and 18th); to be avoided on the 19th to 22nd and 27th.
- Fire offerings: good on the 3rd, 13th, 18th, 23rd and 28th; to be avoided on the 2nd, 12th, 17th, 22nd and 27th.
- Prostrations: good on the 4th. Virtuous work: good on the 18th.
- The four kinds of rite: pacifying on the 1st, 5th, 9th, 12th, 16th, 19th and 23rd; fierce rites on the 4th, 8th, 11th, 18th, 25th, 26th and 29th; increasing and power rites on other dates (the table in lunar-dates.md).

The 15th gathers the most religious works and is the only date with empowerment; the 10th is good for ordination, consecration and building temples; the 8th forbids ordination; the 25th and 29th are dates of fierce rites and the 30th of power rites, with consecration to be avoided on all three. No date is given the one-day vow, a fast, recitation or a feast.
