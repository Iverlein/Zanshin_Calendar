# White Tārā rite before sleep: the tāṃ, the four mirrors and the lion posture

The White Tārā (sgrol ma dkar mo) rite for examining dreams, as the 'Brong rtse collection has it: the practitioner first generates one's own deity and does the self-generation once; from it, White Tārā with a white tāṃ in a heart lotus, her retinue drawn from Akaniṣṭha and asked that dependent origination (rten 'brel) arise as clairvoyance; four silver mirrors are imagined at the four sides with the letters A, NU, TA, RA; the wish is imagined as two white tāṃ and one with a light like a star-arrow striking the point between the brows, then dissolved into the heart tāṃ; one lies down in the lion posture. Read by machine on printed p. 343-344 (img. 347-348); the hand is a manuscript and the lines are not checked on the scan. No lunar day, hour of the night or number of nights is stated in these two pages.
