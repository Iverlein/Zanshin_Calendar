# The 8th to 10th heads read by eye agree with the machine; two corrections

Second pass by eye at 3x (RD202, 2026-11-12). The 8th head (p. 168, 1996 print img. 178) and the 9th (p. 170, img. 180) agree with the machine text word for word, the only correction being "skyod" for the machine's "gyod". The 9th head's weekday line reads dar 3 1 0, gud 2 4, skyod 6, zhud 5; the 8th's dar 2 4 5, gud 0, skyod 1 3, zhud 6. The 10th head (p. 171, img. 181) opens "the coarse winter beginning, the month 'smin drug', the house of the scorpion", the clause on the monastic-rule holders ('mchims' and others) taking the year's head from the 16th is the same as on p. 168, and the machine's doubled "chad lhag so so" is not in the print. The 9th head carries "lha lnga nub" (five: west) where the 7th and 8th have "lnga" alone. No stacked figure of the three heads could be read as digits at 3x in the 1996 print.
