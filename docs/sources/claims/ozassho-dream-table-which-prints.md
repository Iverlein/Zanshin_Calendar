# The days-a-dream-is-not-told table is in the 万代大雑書 and the Ebara dream book, not in the two 永代 prints read

The table of the days on which a dream seen is not told (夢を見て語らざる日, ゆ六 of the 万代大雑書, mdz:p11) was found, with the same twelve cells, in the dream book of the Ebara volume (eb9:i9, a Kyōwa 1 (1801) print of the 新版ゆめあはせ, read in a 嘉永5 (1852) printing of the 国文研 copy), cell for cell as far as read. It was not found in two other open 大雑書 prints whose dream sections were read leaf by leaf on the NDL's images: the 永代大雑書 of 1856 (pid 2539476, images 31-37, the whole dream section, ending 夢はんじ畢; mdz:i31) and the 永代万暦大雑書 of 1883 (pid 759984, images 72-79; mdz:i72; images 72, 73, 78 and 79 by eye, 74-77 in the OCR only). Both hold the 夢はんじ articles, the lucky dreams of Fuji, hawk and aubergine, and a charm against bad dreams, and neither has a month-by-month table of day branches. So the table is not part of every 大雑書: it is the mark of the 万代大雑書 family and of the Kyoto dream book printed beside a 大雑書 in the Ebara volume, and the Edo 永代 prints leave it out. The slash inside each cell is explained in none of the places that have it, and no print that has it was found whose layout shows what the slash divides (q785 stays open). The earliest print, the 星槎ラボ 延宝2 大ざつしよ, was not reached, so whether the table is a late addition is not settled.
