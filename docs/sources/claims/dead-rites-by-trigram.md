# The rites for a death on each trigram

The dead's trigram of death (སྤར་ཁ, spar kha) selects one of eight rites (WB vol. 2, pp. 105–108, Yigdzin-1 read through on the Gser thang reprint). Each has the same parts: an ordinary remedy on the corpse with another trigram's seal drawn on a stone, the grave's mouth broken with two animals, a slip (ཐེམ་བུ, them bu) of a named craftsman pressed at a place, then a white rite (an offering-recipient marked by a defect reads named texts and is fed and paid), a speckled rite (thread-crosses, མདོས, mdos) and a black rite (the trigram's earth-lord wheel in a skull pressed on a road, a stupa built on it). The last line names how many years of the run of deaths it cuts.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *li* | "blind" | *kham*, round black stone of the north | a blind potter of the south; threshold of the four sites, south | blind or squint-eyed; south; the *Uṣṇīṣa* goddess | horse or snake; three-cornered road, south; red, four steps | 9 |
| *khon* | "the mountain behind collapses" | *zin*, long green stone of the east | a blind old woman of the south-west, in a sheep's horn; a temple's threshold, south-west | hunchbacked; south-west; *Questions of the Old Woman*, *Avataṃsaka*, *Laṅkāvatāra* | an old woman's or widow's; road's mouth, south-west; yellow, two steps | 10 |
| *dwa* | "the heart's strike" | *li*, red three-cornered stone of the south | an unclean smith of the west, in a white dog's skull; the smith's charcoal bed or anvil-stone, west | left-handed or with a facial mole; west; names and *Tog gzungs* | an unclean smith's; road, west; white, five steps | 11 |
| *khen* | "the sky's plague falls" | *zin* (east, green) and *li* (south, red) | a lame old man of the north-west, with a dog's skull; a white rock's foundation, north-west | lame; north-west; *Sky-Iron Beak*, *Golden Light*, *Avataṃsaka* | a blind or lame dog's; road's mouth, north-west; yellow, two steps | 12 |
| *kham* | "the demon's belly undone", "the slip's mouth lost" | *khon*, yellow square stone of the south-west | a ferryman or butcher, closed with boat wood; a river bank, north | hunchbacked or crawling; north; *Vajravidāraṇā*, *Great Cloud*, *Mahābala* | a night-born's, rat's or pig's; road, north; black, three steps | 13 |
| *gin* | *bhai 'u de shan* (not understood) | *zin* (east, green) and *zon* (south-east, yellow) | on an ox's right leg-bone, closed with silk and yellow wool; foot of a rock, north-east | mute; north-east; *sdong rgyan*, *Prayer of Good Conduct*, *Ri rab zom*, *Prophecy of Ox Horn* | an exchanged ox's; road, north-east; yellow, two steps | 14 |
| *zin* | "the corpse of the heart's strike" | *dwa*, white crescent stone of the west | a carpenter, on a lone man's spindle, in hare's fur; under a bridge, east | a twin; east; *sdong rgyan*, *Twenty Thousand*, *Questions of the Tigress* | a twin's, donkey's, tiger's or hare's; road, east; green, built with five irons | 15 |
| *zon* | "the robe's hem torn" | *zin*, long green stone of the east | a mad sieve-maker woman and a squint-eyed girl; under a pass cairn, south-east | mad; south-east; *Benefits of a Stupa* | a madwoman's or hybrid's; road, south-east; yellow, two steps | 16 |

The directions follow the trigrams' places (*li* south, *khon* south-west, *dwa* west, *khen* north-west, *kham* north, *gin* north-east, *zin* east, *zon* south-east), and the years cut run from nine to sixteen in the trigrams' order. The speckled rites differ by trigram (the *zor* and four-continent thread-crosses for *li*; the disturbed earth lords' rite for *khon*; the king's thread-cross for *dwa*; the planets' *gung phur* for *khen*; the lake goddesses' tent for *kham*; *sha ri bhyo* for *gin*; the *bstan ma* thread-cross for *zin*; the *ma tram* blood thread-cross for *zon*), and several add a rite "in the Bon way" (བོན་དུ, bon du).
