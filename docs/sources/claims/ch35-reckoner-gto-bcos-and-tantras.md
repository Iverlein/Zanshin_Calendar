# WB ch. 35 pp. 449–451: the reckoner's rites of the earth lords and the reckoning tantras named

Pages 449–451: the person who practises reckoning is to know the corrective rites (གཏོ་བཅོས, gto bcos) that go with the year, month, day and time (ལོ་ཟླ་ཞག་དུས, lo zla zhag dus) and with the earth lords, white, black and mottled, to tell the nāgas from the earth lords and the greater strength from the lesser; the reckoning tantras and the rites each one serves are listed (burial and corpse rites, the black burial ground, the earth tests, the mirror for casting out a corpse, the Khra mo, the rites of the house-spirit and of the road obstacle). The merit of reckoning is stated from the Rin chen spungs rgyud: a good house site, a good marriage, a burial, the planets and mansions, and the rite that turns death aside; of the twelve kinds of death one is the end of life and eleven come by conditions. Only the names of the texts and rites are given; none of their rules is quoted. Read on the scan (RD84, 2026-10-31); the instruction quoted on p. 450 is the Instruction of Clear Appearance (མན་ངག་སྣང་གསལ, man ngag snang gsal), not the machine's «སྡང་གསལ».
