# The hearth god's side in p. 133's cells is p. 224's side for the day

The hearth god's side in each cell of the upper table of p. 133 is the side p. 224 gives for that day: mouse «སྲང» (as printed, a lane), ox under the hearth («ཐབ་འོག»), tiger south, hare east, dragon west, snake north, horse east, sheep west, monkey north, bird west, dog north, pig south (earth-lords.md, *The hearth god's place*). The machine readers' east, south, west and «ཐབ་འོག» are these sides; the Bhutan table's fragment for the ox («བཞིང་ནས་གཏོར། ཐབ་ལྷ་ཐབ་འོག་གཉན…», search snippet of MW26041) has the ox's hearth god under the hearth, as p. 224. Not collated cell by cell on the scan; the identity is by the same page's verse. Narrows question 461 to the unread crops.
