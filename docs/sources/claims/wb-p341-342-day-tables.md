# WB pp. 341–342: the mansion of every named day-combination by planet, read cell by cell on two prints

The tables of pp. 341–342 give, for each of the seven weekdays' planets in the order Sun to Saturn, the mansion of each named combination, in mansion numbers (Aśvinī 0, Bharaṇī 1 to Revatī 26): Accomplishing, Paired, Mara-and-Victor, Accomplished (two ways), Auspicious (two), Increasing (two), Chub, Agreeing, Joining (two), Mara day, Dying, Unfit (two), Disagreeable (two cells, one double), Destroying (two, one double), Burning or Avoided, and by the Vajra Peak (རྡོ་རྗེ་གཙུག་ལག, rdo rje gtsug lag) Accomplished, Mara day, Disagreeable and Destroying in four ways each and Unfit in three. The cells are written out at wb.2:p341-342. Each cell was read on the reprint and on the Zhol print, the two agree, and each agrees with the mansion named in the verses of pp. 334–336 and p. 337 (etext). The one difference: the seven Increasing days' Saturn is ༡༤ in the verse on p. 335, ༢༤ in the table and under the name Pūrvabhādrapadā elsewhere, so the table stands. The verse of the Mara days and Dying combinations names by images; the table supplies the numbers.
