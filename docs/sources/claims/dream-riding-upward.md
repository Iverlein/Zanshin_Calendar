# Riding upward on a lion, elephant, buffalo or horse, well saddled

Image group: journey and mounts. Read by 7 texts: WB ch. 30, Lama Gongdü (LGD), Svapnohana (SVO), Svapna-īkṣā (SVI), Method under Sakya Paṇḍita's name (SKP), ACIP method (RST), Klong rdol's verses as the article quotes them (KBS).
Offered to any dreamer.

WB's fourth animal is «རྟ» (horse): on the Gser thang reprint the glyph is ink-filled (RD230), but on the Taikhang print (vol. 2 img. 222, printed folio 212) it is a crisp ra-over-ta after «མ་ཧེ», and on the Zhol print (WBZ img. 840) the same word is legible; the three prints agree on the line (RD260).
