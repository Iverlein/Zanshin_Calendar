# Whose year serves for the son and bride: the bride's going

# Whose year serves for the son and bride: the bride's going, and the *Garland* names the bride alone

Question 303, in part.

The notes name «བུ་བག» (the son's and the bride's year-horses; wb.2:p532-533) between the horse owner's and the house owner's years, so among points 12–15. The *Garland of Stars* (KD vol. 3, p. 538, etext) gives the fourteenth point, the bride's going (བག་མ་འགྲོ་ལམ, bag ma 'gro lam), eight checks that all name the bride and her father's line and none a groom; the thirteenth, the husband and wife (བཟའ་མི, bza' mi), has none of «བུ» or «བག». So «བུ་བག» belongs to the fourteenth, the son being the household's son for whom the bride goes. The *Garland* does not say whose year is counted; no text found says it for points 12–13, 16, 18–20, 22 or 26–30, which stay as the notes leave them.
