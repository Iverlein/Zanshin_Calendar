# The Brahma wheel's nine sets of three mansions and their results (WB p. 95)

The Brahma wheel (p. 95) tests the state of the one who asks, good or not: three rows of nine syllables, and nine sets of three mansions counted from the mansion of the given name: Born-place (*skyes sa*), Prosperity (*phun tshogs*), Exhaustion (*zad pa*), Separate (*so so*), To-be-accomplished (*grub bya*), Certain-giving (*nges sbyin*), Love (*byams*), Others'-love (*pha rol byams*), All-entering (*kun 'jug*). A work begun on the three of the born-place or of To-be-accomplished succeeds; on Prosperity wealth grows; on Exhaustion it is spent; on Separate the matter is lost and friends part; Certain-giving brings bodily illness; Love, the love of kin; Others'-love, a post of burden for others; All-entering agrees with all. If four fierce planets set their faces on the three mansions of the given name: death by the upper and lower pair, loss and suffering right and left; a merit planet makes it good, the mixed gives a mixed result.
