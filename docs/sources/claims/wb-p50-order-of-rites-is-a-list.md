# p. 50: «གཏོ་རིམ་ཆེ་ཆུང་འབྲིང» names the three sizes; it does not pair them with white, black and even

At the close of the fourteenth examination's list (p. 50, ll. 26–28) the verse reads: many white, white; many black, black; if even, the white-black pair; «གཏོ་རིམ་ཆེ་ཆུང་འབྲིང་ཇི་ལྟར། དགོས་པའི་ཁ་སྨྲང་འདི་ཡིས་ཤོད»: how the order of the rites, large, small, middling, is to be, this verdict-reckoning tells as needed. The three words are a list of the three sizes, not a pairing with the three colours before them: the verse does not say that white is small and black great. The pairing comes from elsewhere: the LTWA manuscript of RD29 closes on «white small, black great, mixed the intermediate state», and the notes (p. 517) size the rites of the thirteenth point by the colour. So the order «ཆེ་ཆུང་འབྲིང» is the verse's way of naming the sizes, and which colour takes which size is the notes' and the manuals'.
