# 'Jigs med gling pa's twelve tenths fall one month after those of the animal-month lists when numbered by the White Beryl

'Jigs med gling pa's prayer puts the birth on the tenth of "the sixth Hor month" and the eleven other deeds in Rgyal, Mchu, Dbo, Nag, Sa ga, Snron, Gro bzhin, Khrums, Tha skar, Smin drug and Mgo (jgl.8:p739-742). The same deeds in the same order stand in the monkey, tiger, hare, dragon, snake, horse, sheep, bird, dog, pig, mouse and ox months in the prayer of Sgo chen's liturgy collection (sgc:i208-214) and in Zhe chen rgyal tshab's summary of the treasure texts (zgp.13:p537-540, machine reading). By the White Beryl's model almanac (wb.1:p154-171) Rgyal is the hare month, Hor 12, and the tiger month is Mgo, Hor 11; the monkey month is Snron, Hor 5, and the sixth Hor month is Chu stod, the bird month. So, numbered by the White Beryl, each of the twelve tenths of 'Jigs med gling pa falls one month after the tenth of the same deed in the two animal lists: birth Hor 6 against 5, enthronement 12 against 11, and so on to the treasures, 11 against 10. The shift is the same in all twelve rows, and 'Jigs med gling pa leaves out Chu stod among his eleven mansion months exactly where the animal lists leave out the monkey: his sixth Hor month stands in the monkey month's place. The prayer itself does not use the word monkey month.

Zhe chen rgyal tshab states the identification in so many words: for the followers of the Phugpa tradition in Dbus and Gtsang the monkey month is the fifth Hor month, Snron, and Gter bdag gling pa and his sons kept the tenth-day offering in that month (zgp.13:p541, scan). The words "as for identifying the monkey month" show that the identification was a matter to be settled; the pages read name no one who holds the sixth.

Which stands: the White Beryl (rank 1) and Zhe chen rgyal tshab agree on Hor 5 for the monkey month, and the list of the lineage's own liturgy collection names the months by animal, so its tenths are those of Hor 5, 11, 12, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10. 'Jigs med gling pa's months (rank 3) are recorded as his and not followed. That his list is the same list read with the monkey month as the sixth Hor month is an inference from the twelve rows; no page read says it.
