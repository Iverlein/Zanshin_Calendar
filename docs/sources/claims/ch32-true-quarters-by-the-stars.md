# Ch. 32: the true quarters found by the stars' rising and setting

WB vol. 2, ch. 32, p. 283 (img. 289 of the Gser thang reprint, read by eye, RD156): the true quarters of a building are found by the stars. The midpoint of where the Pleiades (སྨིན་དྲུག, smin drug) and Rohiṇī (སྣར་མ, snar ma) rise is east, the midpoint of where they set is true west; the midpoint of the rising and setting of the Ṛṣis (རི་ཥི, ri Shi) is true south, of the Seven (རྨེ་བདུན, rme bdun) true north. At these four points, an arm-span outside the foundation, four stones the size of a yak's carcass are set. The foundation is rmang (རྨང, rmang) throughout, as the scan and the 1996 etext have it; Yigdzin-1 read it «གླང», «མང» and «རླང».
