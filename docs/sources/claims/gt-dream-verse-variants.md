# The Drathang's dream verse against the four commentaries' quotations: the syllable variants

Where the Drathang print's verse of the bad dreams (gt:i42-43, the Explanatory Tantra ch. 7, read by Yigdzin-1 and by eye) differs from the verse as the four commentaries quote it (bjp:p177-181, rsp:p208-211, ktr:p303-307, bdk:p53-54; all etexts). No difference changes the list of images or what each marks. The commentaries are not the print, so a reading of theirs is a witness to what their authors had before them, not to the Drathang's.

For the sense of a dream, three of these matter: the beer and the water in line 13 (a drink with the dead), the dead and the fiends in line 15 (the dancing partners), and the head broken or cut in line 8; the rest is spelling. The Drathang agrees with Khro ru and the Drikung more often than with Byang pa and Rin spungs pa; what print each commentator had before him is not known, so the count proves nothing about descent.
