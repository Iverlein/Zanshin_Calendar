# Za ma, beside the day, is the hour

In LT the word *za ma* (ཟ་མ) stands where a unit of time after the day is wanted. The twelve stages of rise and decline reach the year, the month, "the day and the *za ma*" (lt:p12); the time pebbles drive "the day and the *za ma*" singly and then make the day the mother and the hour (དུས་ཚོད, dus tshod) the son (lt:p14); the nineteen "of the day and the *za ma*" are fought on the year, month, day and hour (lt:p43-44), as the *Moonbeams*' nineteen are (dz:i051-052).

Inferred, not stated: in WB's heading «ཞག་དང་ཟ་མའི་རྩིས» (wb.1:p445) *za ma* is the hour, the unit after the day, not food or a misprint; no manual read glosses the word.
