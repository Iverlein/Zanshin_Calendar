# Klong rdol, signs of a good dream, for all and for a practitioner

In the verses the article quotes (pp. 72–73), Klong rdol bla ma's signs of good fortune in the dawn dream are: climbing a high mountain (a great land is gained); a cloudless sun rising (happiness); grain and the essence of plants growing, finding food and clothing, enjoying a treasure (grain and wealth come); being praised in fine clothes; wearing armour (sickness and harm cannot take hold); a fine hat (good for the lama and the lord); sitting in a fine house (auspicious signs); a weapon in the hand (enemies cannot harm); ornaments (renown); crossing a river to the far bank in peace (the best: a wish is fulfilled); drums, conches and music sounding (renown on earth); riding a lion, tiger, garuda (གྱི་ལིང, gyi ling) or dragon upward (a high place is gained); sitting on a throne (wealth and rule grow); drinking sweet water (long life). Signs for a practitioner: a tree growing leaves, music, jewelled swords, fine clothes, an unseen beautiful land and house, a smiling boy or girl giving flowers, fruit, food and drink (qualities of realisation); building a boat or bridge, finding a vase, sun and moon rising and darkness clearing (a good one to be taught); rain from clouds, staying in water, sudden floods (wealth found).
