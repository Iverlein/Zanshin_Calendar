# Nīlakaṇṭha's Ācāramayūkha quotes a Svapnādhyāya with a month for the fourth watch (RD317)

# Nīlakaṇṭha's Ācāramayūkha quotes a *Svapnādhyāya* with the Matsya-type series, a month for the fourth watch (RD317)

Negelein's letter q is the *Svapnādhyāya* of the *Ācāramayūkha* with Rāmanātha's Hindi commentary (Benares 1876; neg:p14-16 and the list of letters, Introduction p. XX). The open copy of Nīlakaṇṭha Bhaṭṭa's *Bhagavantabhāskara*, *Ācāramayūkha* part on dreams (Internet Archive item bhagavanta-bhaskara-acaramayukha-nilakantha-bhatta, a Romanian translation with the Sanskrit beside it), introduces the time of the fruit with "so, in the chapter on dreams, the fruit differs by the time of seeing" and quotes the verses as from the *svapnādhyāya*: a year for the first watch, eight months for the second, three months for the third, a **month** for the fourth, ten days at the dawn, and at once at the cows' release. This is the series of Negelein's f, m, n and q (neg:p14-16), the Odia and Penn copies (penn-odia-svapnadhyaya-series), not the Brahmavaivarta's: no half-month.

State: text layer only, **machine**; the Sanskrit was not copied and the page image was not opened, because the item is licensed CC BY-NC-ND (the translator's file; the verses are Nīlakaṇṭha's, c. 17th century, but the licence forbids our use of this file). It serves as a lead: the public-domain Benares 1876 print or an old Bombay or Nirnayasagar print of the *Ācāramayūkha* would give the verses on a page image. The two other Internet Archive scans of the *Bhagavantabhāskara* (ids bhagavantabhaskara_acharamayukha_-CGV-SDPB-0005 and ‑CGV-PSS-0417-0626) are photographs of manuscripts whose text layer is noise.

What it settles: of the 20 texts Negelein collated plus this one, **only the Brahmavaivarta (b) has the half-month for the fourth watch**; every other text with an eight-month second span has a month. So the Svapnohana's «ཟླ་བ་ཕྱེད» matches the Purāṇa alone among the Indian witnesses read.
