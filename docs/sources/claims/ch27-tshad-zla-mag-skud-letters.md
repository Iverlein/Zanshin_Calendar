# Ch. 27, p. 9, l. 6: «ཚད» and «སྐུད» as the 1996 setting prints them

On p. 9, l. 6, the seventh circle's black luck pebble reads «ཚད་ཟླ་མག་སྐུད་བུ་ཚར་ངན», among its ills (the water source dried, plague, the household moving, hostile talk).

Read on the scan, 2026-10-28 (RD90), the letters of the 1996 setting:

- «ཚད»: the final letter has the long falling tail of the «ད» of «ནད» earlier on the same line, not the short «ང» of «འབྱུང» beside it, on the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5029 img. 15, at 3× and 4×) and on the 1996 scan (I1KG12907 img. 17, at 9×). Zhechen Gyaltsab's (ཞེ་ཆེན་རྒྱལ་ཚབ, zhe chen rgyal tshab) verse for the same pebble has «ཚང་ཟླ» on its own scan (zg:p352, RD75); the 1996 setting does not: «ཚད» is what it prints, and whether it is a slip for «ཚང» stays a reading of sense.
- «སྐུད»: the stack after «མག» has, on the 1996 scan at 9× and 16×, the head as wide as the letter beneath and the crossing stroke of a ཀ, the same shape as the «སྐ» of «བསྐམས» at the head of the same line, and not that of «བརྒྱུད» on l. 8 (a flat ར head over a ག with no crossing stroke); on the reprint at 5× the two stacks are one blot each with the same white diagonal inside. So the 1996 setting prints «སྐུད», as Zhechen Gyaltsab's verse does; the Taikhang's «རྒུད» (wb.2:p9b) rests on Yigdzin-1 and agy only, its ink not settling it (RD77).
- «ཚར» in «བུ་ཚར» is read so on both scans (MITRA «ཚང»).

What the words mean is in [ch27-words-in-steinert-dictionaries](ch27-words-in-steinert-dictionaries.md): «མག» the son-in-law's side either way.
