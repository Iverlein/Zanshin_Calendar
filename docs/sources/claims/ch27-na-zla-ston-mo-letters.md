# Ch. 27, p. 9, l. 16: «ན་ཟླ་སྟོན་མོ་གཏང», the letters clear on both scans

On p. 9, l. 16, the eighth circle's remedy for a black life pebble ends «བྱིས་སྟོན་ན་ཟླ་སྟོན་མོ་གཏང», "at the children's feast give the feast of the age-mates (ན་ཟླ་སྟོན་མོ, na zla ston mo)".

Read on the scan, 2026-10-28 (RD90): every syllable is clear on the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5029 img. 15, at 2.5×) and on the 1996 print's own scan (I1KG12907 img. 17, at 6×): «ན» stands alone before «ཟླ», and the verb is «གཏང» with the prefix ག in this setting, where the Taikhang has «བཏང» (wb.2:p9b). The words are glossed by the dictionaries as the feast of those of the same age ([ch27-words-in-steinert-dictionaries](ch27-words-in-steinert-dictionaries.md)); the letters leave no other reading. What the feast holds is not given on the page.
