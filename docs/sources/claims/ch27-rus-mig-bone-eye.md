# Ch. 27: the limb is «རུས་མིག», the bone-eye, on the reprint and the Zhol etext

In Sprugtön's reckoning (WB vol. 2, pp. 22-23) the limb that closes the nine-eye and the grave-eye is «རུས་མིག», the bone-eye, with a ra, not «དུས་མིག», the time-eye. The Gser thang reprint reads ra at both places (RD38). Two witnesses beside it, both OCR only: the etext of the Zhol print (WBZ, VEIE0OPI7850CC9C_I1KG1710) reads «དགུ་དུར་རུས་མིག་གསུམ་སྟེ་བརྒྱད» at the first place (its p. 657) and is garbled at the second («དུམམག»); and the same etext's p. 525 names the same limb in a verse of its own, «སྤར་ཁ་རང་གི་ལོ་ཐོག་ཕྱིན། དེ་ལ་རུས་མིག་ཞེས་བྱ་སྟེ། རང་སེམས་རང་གིས་ཚིག་པ་ཟ», "the trigram reaching its own year: this is called the bone-eye; one's own mind burns oneself", the same event as the limb's «རྟ་སྦྲུལ་ལི་སོགས་རང་ཐོག་ཕྱིན» in the second list. The Zhol scan itself (WBZ img. 657, a hand-written print, the last line of the folio) was looked at and the ra and da are not told apart at the size of the lent crops; the Sakya print's etext answers 403 and the Taikhang reproduction was not opened. So the ra stands on the reprint and on the Zhol etext, with the sense of the verse on p. 525 agreeing.
