# The 清明秘鈔 夢判事 table of day branches is the charm for a bad dream by day, not the days a dream is not told

In the 清明秘鈔 夢判事 of 1862 (mdz:i65134) the table of day branches is not a table of days on which a dream is not told: it has ten cells (寅 辰 午 申 戌 above, 卯 巳 未 酉 亥 below; no 子 and no 丑) and a box, and each cell says how a person who has a bad dream on a day of that branch is to send it away (with ink, with vermilion, at the door, at the door of the bedchamber), the box giving a general charm to be written in vermilion. It has no month and no slash. Read at 6000 px by eye; the second lines of the cells are only partly read, so the exact words of each cell stay open.
