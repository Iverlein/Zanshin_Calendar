# A host reads a guest's dream as an omen for his own household, the morning after (TSL life)

Another person's reading of Rtsa gsum gling pa's dream appears once more: the host of the house where he stayed, bringing morning tea, asks whether he slept well and, told that the dream "was like a magical display", says it is a rough experience of the kind so called and "for our household it will be an omen of misfortune" (རྐྱེན་ལྟས, rkyen ltas; i297), after the btsan's night warning about demon-emanations. The host thus reads the guest's dream as an omen for his own household, the time being the morning after (the host brings the tea at dawn: «ཞོགས་ཇ»). Nothing follows on these pages to say whether the omen came true. The passage is a record of how a dream was heard, not of a rule.
