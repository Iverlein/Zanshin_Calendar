# Ch. 91's two remedies are notes in small type on the Derge and the Chag-po-ri, both ending before «དགོས་པ»

On the Derge print (img. 605, last line, and img. 606, line 1) the two notes of ch. 91's remedy for the spirit that leads the sleeper away are in small type, the verse's own words «སྔགས་ཀྱིས་གསོ» ("healed by mantra") stand between them in large type, and the verse returns in large type at «དགོས་པ་ནད་མེད་»; the small type does not run past «བཅིང་།» (RD325, 2026-11-10, by eye on crops at 2–4×). On the Chag-po-ri print (img. 602 last line, img. 603 line 1) the whole of line 1 after «འབྲེལ» is in small type, including «སྔགས་ཀྱིས་གསོ», and the large type returns at «དགོས་པ». So on both prints the two bracketed remedies of the Serthang are interlinear notes in smaller type that the prints set into the verse's line, and both stop at the same word; the Serthang's brackets mark the same extent. What the letters of the small type say is less settled: the second note (the thread of wool with seven vajra knots and its mantra) is the same words on both prints by the machine reading, the first note reads on the Chag-po-ri «འབྲེལ་བར་འོ་མ་སུ་སྦྱར་བ་རྐང་མཐིལ་ལ་བྱུག» (milk ..., rubbed on the soles), clear by eye in «འོ་མ་སུ», and on the Derge the machine text «རང་རེ་འོས་ས་སྦྱར་བ་རྐང་མཐིལ་འཇུག» without the milk, which the blurred small letters neither confirm nor refute. The corrupt Derge words of q914 remain a machine reading.
