# A black year's rite is done on a day good by planet, mansion and concord

In a black year (ལོ་ནག, lo nag), when a grave work cannot be put off, WB does not let a good day cancel the earth lord: the rite of the Great Black One (ནག་པོ་ཆེན་པོ, nag po chen po) is drawn and performed "on a day good by planet, mansion and concord" (wb.2:p189b). The day's factors serve the remedy; the earth lord is answered by its rite, not outweighed.
