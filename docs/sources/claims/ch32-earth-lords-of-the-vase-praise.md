# Ch. 32: the earth lords praised at the vase, by place and by quarter

WB vol. 2, ch. 32, pp. 291–292 (img. 297–298 of the Gser thang reprint, read by eye, RD156), in the praise at the vase for bad ground. Earth lords, each by its place: Li-me (ལི་མེ, li me) in the midst of fire; the boy Dwa (བུ་ཕོ་དྭ, bu pho dwa) in the blazing iron box; Kham-chu (ཁམ་ཆུ, kham chu) in the great ocean; the girl Zin-shing (ཟིན་ཤིང, zin shing) in the shade of the wish tree; Khon-ma the old woman (ཁོན་མ་རྒན་མོ, khon ma rgan mo) on the wide earth; Khen-pha the old man (ཁེན་ཕ་རྒད་པོ, khen pha rgad po) in the high sky; Gin-ri (གིན་རི, gin ri) on the top of Meru; mad Zon-rlung (སྨྱོན་མ་ཟོན་རླུང, smyon ma zon rlung) in the black whirlwind. Earth lords of the quarters, each a man's body: east, tiger's head, white, a wish tree; south, dragon's head, blue, a heap of fire; west, bird's head, red, a water monster (ཆུ་སྲིན, chu srin); north, frog's head, black, a fish's head.
