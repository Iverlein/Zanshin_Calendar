# WB pp. 306–307: the remedies run through twelve vowels; the three peaceful planets are Moon, Mercury and Venus

Pp. 306–307 give remedies by the twelve vowels in order, A, Ā, I, Ī, U, Ū, E, Ai, O, Au, Aṃ, Aḥ, each a recitation, a rite or a gift (the list at wb.2:p305-307; the machine's "Ya" was Ā). The three peaceful planets (ཞི་གཟའ, zhi gza') that follow need no guarding in the four strengths and the house's wheel, and the lines then take the Moon, Mercury and Venus in turn, so those are the three. Saturn's remedies stand on p. 306 under «སྤེན་པར», which the machine read «གླེན་པར».
