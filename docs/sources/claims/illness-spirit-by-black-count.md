# The spirit by the number of black pebbles in the body's plank-tree

The spirit behind the illness is named by the number of black pebbles in the body's plank-tree, one count to one spirit: 1 the land *btsan* (ཡུལ་བཙན, yul btsan); 2 the lord demon and lord *btsan*; 3 the possessions-owner and the *dam sri*; 4 the *ma mo*; 5 the *ma mo* above; 6 the male and female demons; 7 the Bon priest's dagger in the mouth; 8 the demon with the fist; 9 the hungry *'gong* entered for good; 10 the sri risen; 11 the ground lord; 12 the grave sri risen (move the grave); 13 the *ka kang*, yellow lightning; 14 the *dmu* spirit; 15 Indra (བརྒྱ་བྱིན, brgya byin). Each count is printed "if N come, …", which settles the split RD12's outline left open.
