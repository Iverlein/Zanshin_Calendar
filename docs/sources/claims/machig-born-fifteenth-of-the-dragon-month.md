# Machig Labdrön born at dawn on the fifteenth of the dragon (third) month of a sheep year, conceived on the fifteenth of the fifth month of a horse year

The life of Machig Labdrön (མ་གཅིག་ལབ་སྒྲོན, ma gcig lab sgron) in the *Phung po gzan skyur gyi rnam bshad* gives her conception and birth by month and day, with the year named by its animal only:

| Event | Main line | Interlinear note | Passage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| conception: the consciousness enters the mother's womb | — | the fifteenth of the fifth month of the horse year (རྟ་ལོ་ཟླ་བ་ལྔ་པའི་ཚེས་བཅོ་ལྔ, rta lo zla ba lnga pa'i tshes bco lnga) | pgz:p5 |
| the mother hears the child counting «A» | from the twenty-fifth of the hare month (ཡོས་བུ་ཟླ་བ, yos bu zla ba) | the second month of the sheep year (ལུག་ལོ, lug lo) | pgz:p9 |
| the child calls «Mother» | the morning of the third of the dragon month (འབྲུག་གི་ཟླ་བ, 'brug gi zla ba) | of the sheep year | pgz:p9 |
| birth, as dawn broke | the fifteenth | the third month of the sheep year | pgz:p9 |

So the birth is the fifteenth day (the full moon) of the third month, the dragon month, of a sheep year; the conception the fifteenth of the fifth month of the preceding horse year. The notes number the months that the main line names by animal (hare = second, dragon = third); the element of the years is given nowhere, and nothing is converted. The 1992 Zi ling edition prints the notes as running text and has the thirteenth, not the third, of the third month for the child's call (etext p. 264). The Blue Annals names her birthplace and parents but no date (dsn.2:i477-478).
