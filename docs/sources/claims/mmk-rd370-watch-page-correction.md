# Correction: the watches of the omens are at [p436]-[p439], not at [p699] and [p656] (RD372)

Correction to RD370's claim «mmk-no-day-or-month-for-dreams» (made by RD372, 2026-11-10): the words «མཚན་མོའི་ཐུན» do not stand at [p699] ff. nor «ཐུན་ནི་ཐ་མར་ཉལ། ཐོ་རངས་ལྡང་བ» at [p656] of the etext. A search of the whole etext of Toh 543 for «ཐུན» (not preceded by «མ») finds the watch only at [p360], [p397]-[p400] (the dream verse), [p408] (the last watch and dawn), [p430]-[p432], [p436]-[p439] (the omens by watch and the measure), [p451], [p454], [p456], [p474], [p503], [p574], [p633]; [p656] and [p699]-[p704] have no «ཐུན» (they are the end of the 35th-36th chapters in verse). The row's numbers were probably the search's character offsets, not the pages.
