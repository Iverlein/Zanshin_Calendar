# The thirty's 241 pebbles against 240

WB closes the thirty's count with the words «ཉིས་བརྒྱ་དང་བཞི་བཅུ་རྩ་གཅིག་གཏན་འབེབས་ཡིན» (vol. 1, p. 454, l. 2): the pebbles are settled at 241. The same words stand on the 1996 print, the Gser thang reprint (read by eye) and the Zhol print (machine, wbz:i602). The form is the tens-and-one («བཅུ་རྩ་གཅིག», as in «ལྔ་བཅུ་རྩ་གསུམ», 53), so it is two hundred and forty-one, not a slip for «བཞི་བཅུ» alone.

Against it: WB itself two verses earlier gives 240 root pebbles and 390 of Khyung's oral instruction, 630 in all; all thirty readings have eight checks each (the corpus, 30 × 8 = 240); and the *Moonbeams* closes the same thirty at «ཉིས་བརྒྱ་བཞི་བཅུས་གཏན་ལ་དབབ», settled at 240 (dz:i029-034, img. 030, the first lines). No text read names a 241st pebble. The notes (wb.2:p532-533) and the short commentary (wb.1:p471-472) add none: the "general board of eight" is the eight again, read whole, not a ninth. Which is meant (a slip, the whole read as one more, a pebble in a lost line of Khyung's tradition) is not stated; the thirty's pebbles that can be laid are 240.
