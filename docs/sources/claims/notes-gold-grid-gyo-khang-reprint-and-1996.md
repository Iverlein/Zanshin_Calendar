# The gold reckoning's grid is gyo khang on the reprint and the 1996 print too

Read by eye (RD143, the Gser thang reprint I3CN5029 img. 544, l. 23 at 6× and the 1996 print I1KG12907 img. 546, l. 23 at 6×): the word after «ཞག་གི་ལོ་འབྱུང་རྣམས» is «གྱོ» on both prints, a ག with ya-btags under it and the ོ sign on top; no ར-mgo over the ག (which «རྒྱ» would have), so «གྱོ་ཁང་རེའུ་མིག་བཞིན་ལ». Yigdzin-1 reads «གྱོ» on the reprint and «རྒྱ» on the 1996 print; the eye follows the first on both prints, so «རྒྱ» is the model's slip. With Mipham's own print (I2029 img. 744, see [notes-gold-grid-gyo-khang](notes-gold-grid-gyo-khang.md)) the spelling is the same on three prints, the 1996 etext's «གྱོ་ཁང» confirmed (question 352 answered as to the reading). The word's meaning, and the gold reckoning's grid, are not explained on the page.
