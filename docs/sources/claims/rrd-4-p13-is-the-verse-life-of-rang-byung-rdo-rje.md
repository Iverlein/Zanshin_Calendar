# The verses of rrd.4:p13 are in the verse life of Rang byung rdo rje (vol. 4, third text), written in his own person, with no title of their own (RD402)

The verses quoted at rrd.4:p13 are part of the verse life of the third Karmapa, not a separate song. In the etext of his collected works vol. 4 (nga) the volume's contents list gives as item 10 the life of the omniscient Precious One Rang byung rdo rje in verse (tshigs su bcad pa), and the text itself (its page numbers begin again at p1; the verses lie on its p13) opens with homage and then says that the writer, who calls himself Rab byung rdo rje, writes a little of the life at others' urging, not to proclaim it. The verses of rrd.4:p13 therefore are his own, in the first person, one stanza-group of that life among many that tell his visions and experiences place by place; they have no title of their own in the etext, the title is that of the whole verse life. The colophon (p22) says the Chos rje Rin po che composed it at the palace of Tai tu on the eighth of the second month of a hare year, and names the scribe, Dkon mchog 'byung gnas; the verses in the last pages tell of his passing, so those pages cannot be his own writing and the colophon does not say who added them. The question of the id: rrd.4:p13 is the thirteenth page of this third text of the volume, not of the volume.
