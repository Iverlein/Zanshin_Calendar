# Any dream as a distant, uncertain sign of death

Image group: the dreamer ill. Read by 2 texts: Four Tantras (for a sick dreamer), WB ch. 25 (the sick).
Offered only when the dreamer is ill: every row is a reading for a sick dreamer (the Four Tantras above WB ch. 25).

| Text | Verdict (tone) | Passage | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Four Tantras (for a sick dreamer) (for a sick dreamer) | dreams and changes of bearing are the distant and uncertain signs of death: turned back by gathering merit, by recitation and by the practice of the yidam; the near signs call for the death ransom (ཆི་བསླུ, 'chi bslu), the very near cannot be turned back (mixed or turning) | `st:p34` | scan |
| WB ch. 25 (the sick) | in the sick person's dream the ritualist is bitten by a dog or kicked by a horse: the omens did not gather, the time is missed: bad; turned back, good (pig year) (bad) | `wb.1:p430b` | scan |

ST is the text on medicine and stands above WB ch. 25; WB ch. 25's pig-year line is a dream of the ritualist, not of death as such.
