# Any dream as a distant, uncertain sign of death

Image group: the dreamer ill. Read by 2 texts: Four Tantras (for a sick dreamer; the closing lines on the Serthang print, the list of images on the Drathang print), WB ch. 25 (the sick).
Offered only when the dreamer is ill: every row is a reading for a sick dreamer (the Four Tantras above WB ch. 25).

Corrected 2026-11-09 (RD268): until then this claim said the Four Tantras read a dream only as such, by the closing lines of ch. 7. The Drathang print of ch. 7 (the Serthang scan lacks the page) carries the verse with the images, so the Four Tantras do name them; the list, image by image, is in gt-dream-death-images, and the rule on who dies in gt-dream-death-rule. The dreams of that verse mean death for the sick only; the Four Tantras' own good dreams (a god, a fire, a lake, a tree with fruit, climbing a mountain and the rest) are for any dreamer after them (gt-dream-death-rule).

ST is the text on medicine and stands above WB ch. 25; WB ch. 25's pig-year line is a dream of the ritualist, not of death as such.

RD274 (2026-11-09): each image of that verse now has its own claim or its Four Tantras' row in the claim of the image: dream-sick-cat-monkey-tiger-fox, dream-sick-riding-a-corpse, dream-death-animals-and-directions (the naked ride south), dream-sick-shoot-and-nest-on-the-crown, dream-sick-palm-or-thorn-tree-in-the-heart, dream-sick-lotus-drawn-out, dream-pit-fall-chasm, dream-sick-lying-in-a-charnel-ground, dream-sick-head-broken, dream-sick-ringed-by-crows-hungry-ghosts-rme-can, dream-body-failing (skin of the legs, hair and beard shaved), dream-sick-entering-the-womb-again, dream-water-rain-swimming, dream-filth, dream-sick-devoured-by-fish, dream-treasure-wealth-found, dream-sick-trade, dream-contest-dice-debate, dream-sick-levy-demanded, dream-sick-taking-a-bride, dream-naked, dream-the-dead, dream-beer-wine-blood-drunk, dream-red-clothes-flowers. The words RD271 read: the camel (gt-rnga-mo-is-the-camel), a man with a black birthmark (gt-rme-can-a-man-with-a-black-birthmark), the levy (gt-bro-ba-khral-ded-a-levy), taking a bride (gt-bag-ma-lan-pa-is-bag-ma-len-pa); Byang pa's water and fiends are his alone (gt-water-or-beer-fiends-or-the-dead).

What the commentaries add to the closing lines (RD229, st-dream-death-signs): Byang pa repeats the grades of remedy (merit, recitation and the yidam made threefold turn the distant signs back; for the near ones the death ransom, tsha tsha and the ransom of the la, བླ་བསླུ), and Rin spungs pa gives each kind of person his means, the practitioner the yidam, recitation, the great seal and the aggregates given as a meal to the six classes, the layman offerings to the Three Jewels, alms, the Buddha's word, the long-life rite and the yidam's approach; for the very near signs nothing turns them back, and one purifies the veil by good accumulation. Khro ru's close of the chapter (ktr:p323-324) says the same.
