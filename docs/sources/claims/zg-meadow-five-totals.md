# ZG gathers the meadow's verdicts into five totals by domain

Zhechen Gyaltsab's *Garland of White Utpalas* (zg:p300-301, BDRC's open scan I4PD2444 img. 344-345, image = printed page + 44), in its verse on the meadow's ornament leaves, says that the five verdicts of each group of twenty-five are gathered into five general totals by domain: life with lifespan, body with illness, power with food and wealth (ཟ་ཐང, za thang), luck with merit, and the mewa with the making of demons. The first line of the gathering reads «སྲོག་ཚེ་ལུས་ནད་དབང་ཟ་ཐང་། །ཀླུང་རྟ་བསོད་ནམས་སྨེ་བ་གདོན་བྱེད་ལ། །བརྟགས་ནས་ཁ་དམར་སྤྱི་བསྡོམས་ལྔ་རུ་དྲིལ།».

For question 216 this is a third witness to what the five verdicts are, beside KD's column cells and Mipham's five of an animal. ZG does not say the five verdicts are of the five years; he says the verdicts are gathered by the five aspects of the reckoned and their domains, a verdict (a general total) for each aspect, after the twenty-five are fought. That reads as "one for each aspect" (the first branch of question 216), not as one for each year of a group. It is Mipham's pupil, so it ranks beside the notes; it does not settle the notes' "five of one animal", which tells what the five *against* the reckoned five are.

For question 225 the same lines give WB ch. 27's totals the very pairs of the *Moonbeams*: life and lifespan, body and illness, power with **food** («ཟ་ཐང»), luck with merit, mewa with demons. So the *Moonbeams*' «ཟ་ཐང» for the power is not a slip of that text: ZG reads ch. 27's five totals so too, while WB's own wording on the pebble (p. 22) names wealth («དབང་ནོར»). Whether the totals may stand for ch. 24 is not touched: ZG writes of ch. 27 only.

The sums: gold year four groups of thirty (twenty-five pebbles and five verdicts each) = 120; turquoise 120; conch 120; together 360, as WB's colophon. The scan prints the gold year's and the conch year's sum as «བརྒྱ་དང་ཉེར་ཐམ» ("a hundred and nyer tham") and the turquoise year's as «བརྒྱ་དང་ཉི་ཤུ» ("a hundred and twenty"); «ཉེར་ཐམ» is not understood, read here as twenty by the sums.
