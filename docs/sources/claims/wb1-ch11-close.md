# WB ch. 11's last pages: the quick tables of Rāhu, the Kar myang reckoning, the year's actor, Īśvara and the king and minister, and Nor bzang rgya mtsho's abridgement

The last pages of the eleventh chapter (pp. 86–90) carry the end of the chapter's last numeric table (p. 86: a "foundation" row by the remainder of a division by 14, with rows for the ranges 101–125, 32–41, 44–50, 63, 76–83, 85–94, 96–100), a rough rule for the dates, and then short working rules for the tables of Rāhu (*sgra gcan*), the *Kar myang* reckoning of the Śākya years (rain and crops, grain, grass, coolness, heat, wind, growth, decline and disturbance each from a remainder after division by 7), the year of the *byed pa*, Īśvara's meetings (*dbang phyug*) and the king and minister (*rgyal blon*) tables, and the day-by-day working of the seven planets by general days (*spyi zhag*) and special days (*sgos zhag*). The author names Nor bzang rgya mtsho as the master whose abridged table for the three fierce and two gentle planets' foot-taking (*rkang 'dzin*) he gives (pp. 88–89). The chapter closes on p. 90 with the colophon: the eleventh chapter, on the quick reckoning that holds the new and old tables, from the *Garland of White Beryl* by Dpyod ldan snying nor. The rules are written in code-numbers whose bracketed figures the machine reads unreliably, so none is quoted as a constant here.
