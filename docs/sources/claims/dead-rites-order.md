# The order of the rites against a run of deaths, and the grave rule by date

WB vol. 2, pp. 104–126 (Yigdzin-1 read through on the Gser thang reprint) sets the remedies in an order of force:

1. For the trigram of death, its ordinary remedy with the white, speckled and black rites (pp. 105–108), and the trigram's wheel from the smith's thirteen paper rolls (pp. 108–109); for the dead's year with the trigram, the eyes and horns (pp. 109–111); for a bad mewa, date, planet, mansion, sky year, month or hour, its own wheel pressed in a skull (pp. 112–124).
2. Where the deaths go on, the nine *spud pa* rite for the four executioners and *zin* (p. 111), and the crucial pressing-down wheels (pp. 112–114).
3. Where the run is still not cut: sending it into the black dog's mouth (p. 115), and as a last resort the reversal called Brahmā's horn, done on the corpse (p. 124). WB warns that the black dog's rite begun first, before the others have failed, ruins the one who does it (p. 115).
4. The size of the rites by the household's means: full where they suffice, each one's root rite where they do not (p. 114); for those who cannot do the remedies at all, the white protection, a nine-ringed wheel worn at the neck (pp. 125–126).

*The grave rule of kung kung* (p. 112): a death of the four *ra ba* on the 6th is a sky grave, the corpse not shown to the sky; of the four *'bring* on the 16th a middle grave, the corpse not burned; of the four *tha chung* on the 26th an earth grave, the corpse not buried. A grave-strike of a death by illness takes the rite sealing the grave's mouth.

*The five graves not allowed* (pp. 124–125): when none of burning, sending, hiding, sinking or casting into water is allowed, the trigram seals and animal-headed figures are set in the five directions by the elements reversed; where only one way is barred, WB gives a substitute for each (burning: cards and cairns by direction, or the fire put out by a rat- or pig-year man of the water element; hiding where hiding is barred: beat with thorns and send; and so on).
