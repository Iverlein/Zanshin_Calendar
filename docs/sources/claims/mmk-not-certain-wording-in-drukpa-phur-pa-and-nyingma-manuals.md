# The Drukpa, Phur pa and Shes rab 'od zer manuals give the reason in Tsong kha pa's words, not the Sakya reason (RD377)

A second group of manuals gives the reason in Tsong kha pa's words, not the Sakya reason: «the first three of the night made four parts are not certain, so the examination is done in the fourth»: the Drukpa Vajrāvalī manual arranged by Yon tan 'od dam bstan 'dzin don kun grub (dkb:p163-164, also in the same collection's other volume at VEIE0OPIDDB781DE_I3CN2263 [p321] and in the PL480 print VEIE0OPIB2682C2D_I2139 [p137]), the Drukpa manuals of Hevajra (BDRC MW3CN2232_236751), of Cakrasaṃvara's snyan rgyud (BDRC MW19223_54039E) and Mkhas dbang Sangs rgyas rdo rje's Vajrāvalī manual (BDRC MW23556_AC28CF, written at the request of the 5th 'Brug chen Dpag bsam dbang po and, the record says, on the teachings of Padma dkar po and Lha rtse ba Ngag dbang bzang po), and in the teacher's address of the Phur pa manual (bpp:p236-237). Shes rab 'od zer (1518-1584) has the same «cha bzhi» wording and gives only that the night is cut into four equal parts and the last is examined (snz:p428-429). All these are of the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries or later by what could be established, and none cites a source for the reason; whether the Drukpa ones take it from the Sngags rim chen mo (snr:p370) or from a common older manual is not shown (a doubt). Padma dkar po's own wording was not found: no exact-phrase search returned a text of his. Chos kyi grags pa's (1595-1659) Yang zlog empowerment text has a third form of it: from the verse he concludes that «what comes in the last watch at dawn alone is taken as the measure (tshad ma)», with no reason (ckg:p16-17).
