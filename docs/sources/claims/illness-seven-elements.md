# The notes' seven: three life forces, the hour's power, the descending trigram and mewa, and the kind of person

Mipham's seven (wb.2:p497, scan: «ལོ་སོགས་དང་མི་རིགས་བཅས་བདུན་པོ») are counted in the notes' own gloss of WB p. 438's «ལོ་ཟླ་ཞག་དུས་སྤར་སྨེ་བསྟུན», given just before: the life force (སྲོག, srog) of the year, month and day of falling ill, three; the power (དབང་ཐང, dbang thang) of the hour, four; the elements of the descending trigram (བབ་སྤར, bab spar) and descending mewa (བབ་སྨེ, bab sme), six; and the sick person's kind (མི་རིགས, mi rigs), which the notes gloss as his birth luck (བཙས་ཀླུང, btsas klung), seven. The illness is the element most and greatest in share among them.

The other count, WB's five of p. 435 with the kind and "the time of reckoning", does not fit the notes: they explain WB's "reckon the fire time" as "the time of reckoning, that is, the year and the rest told above", so the time of reckoning is the six already counted, not a seventh; their remark that "the hour at which the reckoning is made is not without need" says only that the hour belongs among them. The seven of p. 438 are therefore another set than the five of p. 435 (four life forces and one trigram pebble): p. 438 takes the hour by its power and adds the descending trigram and mewa and the kind.

What the kind of person is, besides the notes' birth luck: the regent's questioner (ys:i099) took WB's kind by element as the element of the birth's life-wind (སྲོག་རླུང, srog rlung) and asked whether the rough clan element (རུས་ཁམས, rus khams) could stand for it; the reply (ys:i491-492) keeps the clan element as rough, for want of scriptural proof, and adds the examination by shape and colour. Reply 135 (ys:i492-493) makes the life force's element the chief for the five people needed.
