# No text of the pool names the source of WB ch. 30's dream list, and Mipham's note gives it no gloss (RD319)

Neither WB nor Mipham's notes, nor any other text of the pool, names the source of WB ch. 30's list of dream omens (p. 144, ll. 11–24, [wb.2:p144l](../corpus/wb/ch30.md#wb-2-p144l)).

What is on the page. The list stands as the preliminary of the moment's reckoning, introduced by one line, «ཐོག་མར་རྨི་ལྟས་བསྟན་པ་ལ།» ("to show the dream omens first"), after the chapter's heading, the verses that praise the *White Beryl* and give the chapter's size, and before the asker's arrival. No line of it names a text, a teacher or a tradition, and its closing lines (the remedy, the verdict of a defect) are WB's own.

What Mipham's notes say. The notes' section eight (`wb.2:p535-537`, read on the scan, 2026-10-23) lists the preliminaries of the moment's reckoning, «སྔོན་འགྲོ་རྨི་ལམ་བརྟག་པ། འདྲི་བྱེད་ལ་བརྟག་པ། ལམ་ལྟས་བརྟག་པ།» (examining the dream, the asker, the omens of the road), and says of the whole run, up to the oath-takers, «གཞུང་ན་གསལ» ("it is clear in the text"). He gives no gloss of the list and no source for it, and calls it «རྨི་ལམ་བརྟག་པ», "examining the dream", where WB's line has «རྨི་ལྟས», "dream omens".

What the parallels say of themselves. RST, the dream text of Klong rdol bla ma, says its verses are given as taught in the Chöd visualisation collection of Ma gcig (rst-is-klong-rdols-dream-text); SKP gives the same verses under Vairocana's name (skp-second-text-is-the-rst-verses) and is itself attributed to Sakya Paṇḍita by its title; LGD is the treasure of Sangs rgyas gling pa (1340–1396); SVO is Vibhūticandra's translation and SVI Advayavajra's in Prajñāśrījñānakīrti's; RRT ascribes the key of telling good signs from bad to Gnubs Sangs rgyas ye shes (rrt-what-is-done); the Four Tantras' verse is the Four Tantras' own. None of them cites WB, and WB cites none, so the concordance (dream-wb30-concordance) and the shared wording (dream-wb30-wording-shared-with-klong-rdol-and-rrt) show likeness and no descent.

Searched and not found, in the pool (2026-11-10, kb.py search and a search of every corpus file but WB's for the list's phrases): a text with WB's list in WB's lines or order; a statement in WB ch. 30 or in the notes (`wb.2:p535-537`) of where the dream examination comes from. The pool's earlier readings of the sources' own accounts of themselves are the only other witness.
