# Sixteen half-watches: Śaṅku's Garuḍa treatise and the Kālacakra's great commentary (RD326)

In the Tengyur's Garuḍa treatise of the brahmin Śaṅku the lines say that the nāgas enjoy, in the half-watches of the day-and-night, what comes under the planets' power, and that the sixteen junctions where day and night shift are the Rig ldan time (བཅུ་དྲུག་པོ་དེ་རིགས་ལྡན་དུས་ཡིན་ནོ). A verse manual (the Skar rtsis kyi lag len byed grub sgang dril ltar bkod pa, p77) says the same count of sixteen from the Kālacakra's great commentary Dri med 'od: eight great watches, halved. So the sixteen is the count of the Tengyur text and of the Kālacakra's commentary; that the Dbyangs 'char's eight-by-day-and-eight-by-night rests on the same sixteen is a reading from the numbers.
