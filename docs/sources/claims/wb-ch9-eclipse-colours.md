# WB ch. 9 gives the colour of an eclipse by the part of Rāhu that seizes

Sixth point of the closing verses (machine reading of p. 67): when the moon or an asterism falls on the head or hand, whatever the portion, the colour is the blue-green of the sky. For a whole seizure the beginning and end are smoke-coloured; in the middle seizure by the face is red-black, seizure by the tail red-yellow like lichen (li khri); a seizure of a part is black, seizure by the 'maker' blue; two parts of three seized is brown (kham pa); more than that is the colour of burnt iron. Read from machine text only.
