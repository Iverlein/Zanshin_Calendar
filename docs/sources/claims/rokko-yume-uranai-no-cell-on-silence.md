# No cell of the 六甲の夢うらなひ or the 十二直夢占 speaks of keeping a dream silent

The Kyoto copy (RB00012284, canvases 26-29) gives each of the sixty days of the cycle one sentence, and canvases 29-30 one for each of the twelve day-officers; each opens with a quarter or a place, a person or a thing that comes, and a result good or bad. Read over all seventy-two cells (RD379): none speaks of keeping a dream silent or of not telling it, and the days of the table of days a dream is not told have no cell marked in kind or in tone. The table is a day-keyed reading of a dream, nothing about silence.
