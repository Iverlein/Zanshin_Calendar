# The midnight dream is the first of the outer death-signs in a teaching on the transference of consciousness

# The midnight dream (ནམ་ཕྱེད་ཀྱི་རྨི་ལམ) is the first of the outer death-signs in a teaching on the transference of consciousness (RD367)

In a teaching preserved in several collections (the Gdams ngag mdzod, Shechen print; two further prints under Mar pa Chos kyi blo gros's name and a Lho brag pa Snyan brgyud collection) the yogin who takes up the heat of the transference of consciousness (འཕོ་བ, 'pho ba) must know the death-signs (འཆི་ལྟས, 'chi ltas) of three kinds, outer, inner and secret; the outer has two, the examining of the midnight dream and of body and mind by day (dds.9:p173). The midnight dream's signs given: going alone to a place one has never gone to, riding a donkey naked, facing outward, on the road to the south, getting a red flower on the Gya skag mountain. It is a time-of-night tie of the kind the pool holds in the watch texts, here with the midnight alone named (the 3rd Karmapa's «the dream of midnight is the power of spirits», lgd, names the same hour for another verdict), not a lunar-day tie, and it is a death sign, as the pool's Tibetan texts on dreams tend to be.
