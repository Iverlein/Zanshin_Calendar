# The earthquake omens of ch. 18 name three watches, midnight, dawn and sunrise, and never a fourth watch (RD378)

The omens run by the day first (p. 435: dawn, a little after, a little later, midday and afternoon, after the sun has passed, sunset, the end of the afternoon) and then by the night (pp. 436-437): the first watch (with its decline and its end), the beginning of the second watch, the middle of the second watch, midnight (མཚན་ཕྱེད་དུས), the "middle watch" (བར་མའི་ཐུན) declining and then a shaking near its end, the end of midnight (མཚན་ཕྱེད་མཐར), going into the third watch (ཐུན་ནི་གསུམ་པར་ཕྱིན་ནས), dawn (ཐོ་རངས) and sunrise (ཉི་མ་འཆར་བའི་དུས). Three watches are named by number; the fourth is never named. Midnight is set after the middle of the second watch and before the third, so it falls at the close of the second watch, as it does in a night of four equal watches; dawn follows the third. So the omens leave the stretch from the third watch to dawn, where the fourth watch of the dream verse would lie, without a word of its own. Whether the omens' "middle watch" (བར་མའི་ཐུན) is the second watch or a middle part of the night is not said; ch. 18's births (mmk.1:p430-432) use the same two words, a "last watch of the night" (མཚན་མོའི་ཐུན་གྱི་ཐ་མ) and a "middle watch" (བར་མ), beside the sun's rising.
