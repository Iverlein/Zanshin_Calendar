# The stacked figures of the 2nd and 3rd heads, read in part

RD201, 2026-11-17, Gser thang reprint img. 165 and 167, crops at 5x to 6x. The print's figures are in a small hand: a hooked figure with a tail (taken as 1, as RD200 settled for the 12th head), a flat-topped wedge (2), a curved swoop (7), a circle (0), and a figure like a 4 that is close to 5. 2nd head (p. 158): three stacks of line 3, two tiers each: before the second's sgang 15 over 17; before the full moon 10 over 17; before the sun's arrival at Pisces 11 over 24 (the lower first figure is wider than the 1s, so 24 is doubtful). The stack of line 2 (after the snake's great and small) shows 12 over a swoop and a hooked figure, order and values not settled. 3rd head (p. 160): the stack after the horse's great and small, upper 16 (an earlier pass of 2026-11-08 read 26: the first figure is the hooked one read here as 1), lower 22; the stack after the willow, 16 with a dot above it; the day-length stack three tiers, 17 over 10 over a bare circle; the night-length stack 22 over 40 over a bare circle. What the tiers count is not settled by the print, and the day and night lengths do not sum to a whole day on any reading of the tiers, so these are readings with doubts, not data. The stacks of the 4th to 6th heads were not read again.
