# WB ch. 13: columns 12 to 14 of the table of the sixty years, years 1-30

The right half of WB's table of the sixty years has three more columns after the three sme ba rings (pp. 105, 107, 109, years 1 to 30). They are numbered 12, 13 and 14 in the table's own head row.

Column 12, headed "the names of se zhig and the rest" (སེ་ཞིག་སོགས་ཀྱི་མིང), has one short word per year. The words read on the scan are སེ་ཞིག, ཁ་རལ, ཁ་ཡག, འཆང་ཚོང and འདུག་ཁུར, each in single-letter doubt; no rule in their order was found, and the heading's meaning is not settled.

Column 13 is the wind-horse of a planet's name (ཀླུང, klung) and goes round a cycle of four: the moon's (ཟླ་བའི་ཀླུང), Mars' (མིག་དམར, mig dmar), Jupiter's (ཕུར་བུ, phur bu) and Saturn's (སྤེན་པ, spen pa), with the moon in years 1, 5, 9, 13, 17, 21, 25 and 29. Page 105 writes the names out, pages 107 and 109 abbreviate them. The cycle fits all thirty rows.

Column 14, headed with the number of occurrences of each sme ba ring by year and letter, is three parts numbered 1, 2 and 3. Each part is a number-word, two (གཉིས), three (སུམ), four (བཞི) or five (ལྔ), with ལྡན (ldan, "endowed"). In years 2, 16, 25 and 28 the three parts are merged into one cell that says the three sme ba rings are all (གར) so-many-endowed: three in years 2, 25 and 28, four in year 16. No rule for the numbers of the other years was found, and what they count is not told on these pages.
