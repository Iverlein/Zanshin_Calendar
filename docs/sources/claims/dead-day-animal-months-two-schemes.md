# The day's animal entries: the month is the month the life was cut, and no rule of the opposite month

# The day's animal entries: the month named is the month the life was cut, and it is no rule of the opposite month

**The month is the one of the cut.** WB vol. 2, p. 63 (wb.2:p63; 1996 scan img. 71 ll. 3–5, Gser thang reprint img. 069, Zhol print img. 715, which no reader could read; the Taikhang I5878 img. 097, line 5, read by Yigdzin-1 and by eye at 1–2.6×) has six entries; three of them say what the month is in the words themselves: «སྲོག་བཅད་སྦྲུལ་ཉིར་ཤི་བ་ན། །སྤྲེལ་ཟླར།», «ལུག་ཉི་ལུག་ཟླར་ཚེ་སྲོག་བཅད།», «སྤྲེལ་ཉིར་ཤི་ན་ཁྱི་ཟླར་བཅད།»: died on a snake day, "the life cut" in the monkey month; on a sheep day "the life and breath (ཚེ་སྲོག, tshe srog) cut" in the sheep month; on a monkey day "cut" in the dog month. So the month is the month in which the life was cut before the death, as in the hour entries (wb.2:p66-68), which say the la (བླ, bla) was cut. The page says «སྲོག» (srog, life) where the hour entries say «བླ», not "the la".

**No rule is found behind the months.**

| Day animal | month of the cut | the animal's opposite (seventh) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| tiger | bird | monkey |
| snake | monkey | pig |
| horse | bird | rat |
| sheep | sheep | ox |
| monkey | dog | tiger |
| ox | none; bad for the near ones | sheep |

- The second examination's reason by year, month, day and hour (wb.2:p57-58) gives for all twelve animals the opposite animal's month (tiger: monkey; hare: bird; dragon: dog; snake: pig; horse: rat; sheep: ox; monkey: tiger; bird: hare; dog: dragon; pig: snake; rat: horse; ox: sheep). The day's entries here are not those: not one of the five months is the opposite, though the second examination covers the day too. So p. 63 is a second scheme in the same chapter, not that one restated.
- The hour entries' months (dead-demon-by-hour: hare, monkey month; dragon, dog; sheep, ox or sheep; monkey, dog; bird, snake; dog, dragon; pig, ox) agree with the day's where both exist: the monkey's dog month, the sheep's sheep month (one of the hour's two). The other three day entries (tiger, snake, horse) have no hour entry to compare, and the hour entries are not the opposite either (hare–monkey, bird–snake). So the day's and the hour's months go together, and neither is the seventh.
- The months show no pattern of count either (counting the animals from the rat as 1: tiger to bird +7, snake to monkey +3, horse to bird +3, sheep to sheep 0, monkey to dog +2).
Lha mo Tshul khrims's verse restatement (MW1KG21818, her pp. 54–55) has no day-animal entry; it has, after the first examination, a count of the life left by the hour of death (hare three years, horse and dragon five, snake ten, monkey and sheep two, ox and dog one year, pig six months, tiger one month, bird and rat none), a different table from these months.

The monkey day's month is «ཁྱི་ཟླར» (the dog month) on the 1996 print and the Gser thang reprint (read by eye, RD31); on the Taikhang Yigdzin-1 reads «བྱི་ཟླར» (the rat month), and at 2.6× the letter looks like a ཁ with its subjoined ya but is not settled. The dog month agrees with the hour entries' monkey (afternoon, the dog month).
