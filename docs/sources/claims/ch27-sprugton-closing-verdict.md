# Ch. 27: Sprugtön's closing line lays the verdict as in general, after the limbs

In Sprugtön's reckoning (WB vol. 2, pp. 22-23) the sixteen limbs are listed after the verdict sentence, and the section's last line is «ཁ་དམར་སྤྱི་ལྟར་གདགས་པར་བྱ», "lay the verdict as in general". The list of what is counted names «ཁ་དམར་རྡེལ་ལྔ» (five verdict pebbles) and four cherished pebbles after the limbs, so the colophon's 75 pebbles are 50 root, 16 limb, 5 verdict and 4 cherished (ch27-sprugton-limbs-beside-the-five). The closing line follows the limbs: it sends the verdict of the whole to the general rule, which is the five verdict pebbles tied to the aspects, and no line gives the limbs a verdict of their own or counts them into the five. So the limbs are sixteen pebbles of their own, black or white by whether each falls, and the five verdicts are laid over the whole; this rests on the order and the sum, no sentence states it. No other witness names the limbs.
