# The Vimalaprabhā's short and long vowels are the same two words on p. 409 and p. 538, but no sentence joins the dream's long vowels to the hours of midnight to midday (q1017, RD401)

On p. 409 the Vimalaprabhā gives the short vowels from the middle of the day to the middle of the night and thirty long ones from the middle of the night to the middle of the day, and the five Rāhus as waking, dream and deep sleep. On pp. 538-539 it says of the three states that the coarse (waking) is the 360 letters with the twelve vowels, the other (deep sleep) is the five empty vowels and the sun, moon, Rāhu and time, and «likewise the long, the dream's nature» (དེ་བཞིན་དུ་རིང་པོ་སྟེ་རྨི་ལམ་གྱི་རང་བཞིན). Both pages use the same two words, short (ཐུང་ངུ) and long (རིང་པོ), of the vowels, and p. 538 gives the long ones to the dream, so a reader who takes them as one list gets the dream in the half from midnight to midday. The text does not join them: p. 409's sentence is about hours and the Rāhus, p. 538's about natures and does not name an hour, and the list of «other» vowels on p. 538 mixes vowels with ཧ ཡ ར ཝ ལ. Etext only (no scan opened this time); the answer to the question is "not said".
