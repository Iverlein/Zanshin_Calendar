# In the rite of the ground the dream is the spirits' answer, "that night" after the request, one of dream, water, fire and corpse (RD386)

In two Tengyur rites the dream answers the request for the ground, not the disciple's night. The Vajrayoginī mandala rite of Umāpatidatta (VE23703_1340 p. 100): after the spirits of the place are asked three times for the ground, "if in a dream and the like the spirits have given no permission and no prohibition", the request is made again in the first session of the dawn and at midday. The Guhyasamāja commentary of Candrakīrti (VE23703_1351 p. 431): the steps of the ground are examining, requesting, "examining whether it is given or not", purifying, blessing, taking, guarding; "that night" (དེའི་ནུབ་མོ) the examining by dream, water, fire and a corpse shows whether it was given. Both fix the dream to the night after the request; neither names a part of the night, a list of images or a remedy. The same «dream shows whether the earth was given» stands in VE23703_1337 p. 103 (RD373 read that work) and in the Padma-net and other rites for the ground not read here.
