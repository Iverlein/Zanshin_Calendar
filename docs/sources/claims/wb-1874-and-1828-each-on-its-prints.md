# 1,874 is WB's on three prints, 1,828 Mipham's on two; neither closes the twelfth's 3,186

WB's figure is on the 1996 scan, the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5029 img. 056, line 4, read at 1.7×) and the Taikhang print (I5878 img. 077, Yigdzin-1): «དོན་བརྒྱད་ངོས་འཛིན་སྟོང་བརྒྱད་བརྒྱ། བདུན་བཞི་བོར་བར་བྱ་བའོ།», seventy-eight result pebbles and one thousand eight hundred and seventy-four identifying (བདུན་བཞི, bdun bzhi, "seven-four", written for seventy-four). Mipham's figure is «ངོས་འཛིན་སྟོང་བརྒྱད་བརྒྱ་ཉེར་བརྒྱད» (ཉེར་བརྒྱད, nyer brgyad, twenty-eight), one thousand eight hundred and twenty-eight, on the 1996 scan (img. 521) and on the Gser thang reprint (img. 519, line 1, read at 1.6×), the same words on both. So the 46 is not a misreading of one print: «བདུན་བཞི» and «ཉེར་བརྒྱད» are the two numerals, the one WB's and the other Mipham's, each as printed on each of the prints that carry the line. The blocks of the twelfth do not decide between them: with 1,874 the ten blocks add to 3,176 against the stated 3,186 (ten short), with 1,828 to 3,130 (fifty-six short), so the stated total is nearer 1,874 but not closed by it. Mipham says «བགྲང་འདུག» (it is seen counted) of the 1,828 and calls the round an elaboration; his other figures of that passage are WB's. The difference stays unexplained; WB's 1,874 is the figure of the text.
