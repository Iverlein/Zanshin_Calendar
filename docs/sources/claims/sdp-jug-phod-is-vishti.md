# «འཇུག་ཕོད» in Sādhuputra's ground rite is Viṣṭi

In Sādhuputra's ground rite the time is to be free of the fierce planets and of the «'jug phod» (འཇུག་ཕོད, 'jug phod) and the like. Lokesh Chandra's Tibetan-Sanskrit dictionary (Steinert's set, no. 49) gives «'jug phod» = *viṣṭi*, the karaṇa Viṣṭi (WB's «བིདྡྷི», the harmful, much-feared karaṇa of karanas.md): so the line reads "free of the fierce planets, Viṣṭi and the like", the times of the day to be left out of the rite. Nothing else in the 64 dictionaries has the headword. BDRC's full-text search has 61 hits for «འཇུག་ཕོད», which were not read one by one.
