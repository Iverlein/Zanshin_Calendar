# The saying of dusk habit, midnight the power of spirits and dawn true is found only in the third Karmapa's verses: no work or song of Marpa, Mi la ras pa, Ras chung pa or Sgam po pa read has it (RD387)

BDRC's exact-phrase search (2026-11-11, RD387) finds the Karmapa's «ནམ་ཕྱེད་ཀྱི་རྨི་ལམ་གདོན་དབང་ཡིན» and «སྲོད་ཀྱི་རྨི་ལམ་བག་ཆགས» in the third Karmapa's vol. 5 only. «ནམ་ཕྱེད་ཀྱི་རྨི་ལམ» stands in the death-sign teaching of the Lho brag pa (six prints of one text, `dds.9:p173-174`), in two biographies and a defence of the treasure-finder Padma gling pa; nothing in the songs, lives or counsels of Marpa, Mi la ras pa, Ras chung pa or Sgam po pa read by words (Marpa's vol. 4 and Ras chung pa's whispered lineage on the etext, the rest through the search). The saying, with «gdon dbang», is therefore known from the Karmapa alone (`rrd.5:p57-59`); where he took it from is not found.
