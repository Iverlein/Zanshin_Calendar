# MDP's second dream item (p. 138) is the RST text of Klong rdol bla ma

The second dream item of MDP, "The method of examining the various dream signs" at printed p. 138 (the contents' second dream entry, pp. 138–159 to the next entry), is the text known from the ACIP etext RST, the dream text of Klong rdol bla ma: its heading is that of RST, and the first eleven verse lines of p. 138 repeat RST's opening word for word (the lama and the three refuges, the Mother's Chöd visualisation collection, the three parts of the night, with the dusk third habit, the midnight third the tricks of demon and srin, the dawn third the clear one). The hits that BDRC's full-text search gave for RST's verses in MDP (MW21880) are therefore from this text from p. 138 on, not from the dream part of pp. 134–137, which holds the other, rebirth-seeing rite. The first letter of the heading cannot be settled on the scan (rmi ltas as in RST, or mi ltas); the contents line of the volume has the same doubt.
