# The household's years counted together

WB, vol. 2, p. 54: over one paternal kin (ཕ་ཚན, pha tshan) taken together, by which of the twelve la places its members' years fall on most (input: the board of [dead-la-pebbles-layout](dead-la-pebbles-layout.md), counted over all the living).

| Many years on | Reading |
| --- | --- |
| the great cut-off | many leaders who do not agree; bad for the brides |
| (the head pebbles empty) | no one will lead |
| the power year | each builds his own fort, "a good fort of men"; they gather when an enemy crosses |
| the mother year | much support, mothers and kin increase |
| the friend year | friends build one's fort, descendants increase beyond holding, quarrels among many paternal relatives |
| one's own year struck by the clan element | offer respect to the enemy (the notes, p. 502: one's year has become the clan's enemy) |

The section closes "speckled, the mind can bear it" (ཁྲ་བོ་བློ་ཐུབ, khra bo blo thub), which the notes quote with the mewa's copper vessel; whether it names a verdict for a mixed board is not said.
