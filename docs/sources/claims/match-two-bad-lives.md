# The match barred by two utterly bad lives, each judged by one verdict pebble

The regent's reply 111 (ys:i467-468): in the match, the bar on joining two bad lives of the life reckoning (ཚེ་རྩིས, tshe rtsis), the *ma klungs* and the rest, means *not joining two utterly bad ones* (ངན་ཐ་ཆད, ngan tha chad). Each spouse's life reckoning is gathered into one verdict pebble (ཁ་དམར, kha dmar), and whether it is utterly bad or not is decided by gathering its white and black pebbles; the single bad findings of the life reckoning are not each a bar. The one pebble that records the rejection falls within the match's pebble count.

That this verdict is the birth reckoning's verdict over its hundred pebbles, and what count of black makes "utterly bad", the reply does not say (inference; question in other-reckonings.md); what bears on both since 2026-10-25: [life-reckoning-one-verdict](life-reckoning-one-verdict.md) and [ngan-tha-chad-bottom-class](ngan-tha-chad-bottom-class.md). It bears on the notes' karma reckoning over a match (question 355): there too the bad would be the whole reckoning's verdict, not a single aspect's finding, by analogy only.
