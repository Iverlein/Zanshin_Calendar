# A bride is sent by ten checks, the earth lords one of them, unranked

In the manuals of the element reckoning a bride is sent by a list of checks in which the earth lords (ས་བདག, sa bdag) are one entry, last or near the end, never ranked. Dharmaśrī's *Moonbeams* names ten in one line, the year, mewa, trigram, rise and decline, month, day and hour, planet and mansion, and earth lord (dz:i045); Kun bzang blo gros's supplement to it takes the same ten one by one (kd.3:p634), and for each says what to avoid and, where it cannot be avoided, the rite that mends it, the earth lords last with the rite of the disturbed earth lords (kd.3:p641-642); Lha mo tshul khrims sets them out as numbered heads, the earth lords the eleventh (lt:p38-41). None of the three says which check counts more when they disagree, and none weighs a bad earth lord against a good date, weekday or mansion: each bad check is avoided or mended in its own right.
