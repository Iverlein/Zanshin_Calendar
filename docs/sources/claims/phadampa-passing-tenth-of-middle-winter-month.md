# Phadampa Sangye passed on the tenth day, in the middle winter month of the fire-female-bird year; cremated on the seventeenth

The passing of Phadampa Sangye (ཕ་དམ་པ་སངས་རྒྱས, pha dam pa sangs rgyas), as the Ding ri tradition's own collection prints Khams smyon Dharma seng ge's life of him, and as the Blue Annals dates it:

| Step | Date as printed | Passage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| he says «I am ill» | the evening of the fourth day of the middle winter month (དགུན་ཟླ་འབྲིང་པོ, dgun zla 'bring po) of the fire-female-bird year (མེ་མོ་བྱའི་ལོ, me mo bya'i lo) | zbc.5:i123 |
| four days of signs; his body wrapped in white silk | at dawn on the tenth (ཚེས་བཅུའི་ཐོ་རེངས, tshes bcu'i tho rengs) | zbc.5:i152 |
| his mind dissolves into the expanse | after sunrise of that day | zbc.5:i153 (etext) |
| the summary: died, then cremated | died on the tenth; the remains cremated on the morning of the seventeenth | zbc.5:i157 |
| the year | he stayed at Ding ri (དིང་རི, ding ri) twenty-one years, from the fire-female-ox year to the fire-female-bird year, and passed away | dsn.2:i402 |

The two sources agree on the year. The day is the tenth; its month is the middle winter month by the narrative, which names the month with the fourth and does not repeat it with the tenth. The month is named by its season and not numbered here, and nothing is converted. No source read says that the day is kept as a day of practice.
