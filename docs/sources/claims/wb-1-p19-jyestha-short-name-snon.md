# WB vol. 1 p. 19 line 8: the short name of Jyeṣṭhā is «སྣོན» (Snon) in WB, never «སྣྲོན»

Counting each spelling over the whole 1996 etexts of WB (vol. 1 I1KG12906, vol. 2 I1KG12907): «སྣོན» stands as the name of the seventeenth mansion and of the fifth Hor month in some twenty places (vol. 1 etext p. 26 «སྣོན་ཟླ་ངེས་སྲེག», the same month name that the pool's p. 16 passage reads «སྣྲོན་ཟླ»; p. 33 «སྣོན་ཟླ»; p. 267 «སྣོན(༡༧)»; vol. 2 pp. 338–344 and 401–430 in the mansion lists «སྣོན(༡༧)»); «སྣྲོན» does not occur once, and «སྣྲོ» alone not at all. The mansion lines of p. 19 close each with a short name and a closing word («…ལྷ་མཚམས་མིང», «…སྣུབས་ཀྱི་མིང»); line 8's stack before «དུ་འདོད» is therefore WB's «སྣོན», 'Jyeṣṭhā and the rest are taken as Snon', and not «མོ», «སྒོ» or «སྒྲོ» (the machine and the etext are OCR guesses at the same clotted stack). By eye at 4× (I3CN5028 img. 26 line 8) the stack has ོ above a tall ས-over-ན head; whether the print's final ན is lost in the clot is not told, so the syllable stays «སྣོན» by usage, not by letter. The same count reads the p. 16 month name as «སྣོན་ཟླ», against the stored «སྣྲོན་ཟླ» of the machine.
