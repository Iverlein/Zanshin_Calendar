# The run of placeholder marks before the homage stands in seven Lama Gongdü texts of Shechen vol. 42, five to eight marks each, and in none of four in vol. 7: not a fixed opening of the cycle (RD403, q1005)

RD403 (q1005). The run of placeholder marks stands before the homage in seven texts of the Lama Gongdü in the Shechen etext of vol. 42, not in four (dds.54:p1-2): five marks before the text on drawing the chakra of liberation by wearing, eight before «For others' benefit, liberation by seeing», eight before the small writing on drawing the chakra of liberation by seeing, five before the Garland of Vajras of the protection hundred thousand, eight before the order of «the fulfilment of all wishes», five before the lamp-examination Blazing light, and six before the dream text. The mark is U+0F88 (the sign lce tsa can), the etext's stand-in for a form it did not read, and its count varies from five to eight, so it is not one fixed string of one length. The same cycle's texts in the Shechen vol. 7 (dds.53:p1-2), four of them opening with the same title formula «From the Lama Gongdü», have no mark and no string between title and homage; and the other Lama Gongdü texts of vol. 42 that open with the homage (for instance those that begin with a different title formula, or with «namo guru deva ḍākinī yai») have none either. So the string is not the fixed opening of the cycle's texts: it belongs to the seven texts of one group in vol. 42 (all of the form «༁ྃ༔ From the Lama Gongdü» and a title, the typist's mark), and the print of the dream text shows the string on the page (lgd.42:p962b). What it is, a seed string, a mantra in an ornamental letter or an invocation, no source says: no etext was found that writes it in plain letters.

This corrects the earlier sentence of the claim on the string (lgd-p962-seed-string-is-ornamental-and-unread) that the string is the opening of the cycle's texts. The earlier count of seven marks before «For others' benefit» is eight in the etext as served.
