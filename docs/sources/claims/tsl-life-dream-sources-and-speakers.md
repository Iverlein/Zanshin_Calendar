# The dreams in Rtsa gsum gling pa's life carry a named speaker, and each gives something to be checked; a demoness's grudge is a source of a bad dream

The dreams in Rtsa gsum gling pa's life come with a named source, the speaker of the dream: a red woman adorned with bone ornaments who says she comes from Rnga yab (i138), a red man dressed as a btsan (i140, who names himself Chu mig byang chub btsan rgod), the god Gze rgyal Lha btsan 'bar ba with nine attendants (i169), a leper who yields (i321-322), an Indian ācārya for the mother and a Heruka for the father at conception (i36-38). Each speaker gives something to be checked: a past commitment (the entrusted instruction and the prophecy, i139), the lama's name and a former pledge (i140), the place and day (i170). The treasure-revealer himself sets such a dream beside two other things: a "prophecy" (ལུང་བསྟན, lung bstan) tested later (i49), and a later occurrence (i119, three months). The same pages show the other source of a dream: a grudge of a demoness of the class «བདུད་རིགས» (bdud rigs) after an insult (i56-57), which is why the filthy-woman dream is read as an obstacle (བར་ཆད, bar chad) and not as a sign.
