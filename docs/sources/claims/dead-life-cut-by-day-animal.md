# The day's animal of death: the month the life was cut

The third examination of the dead by the day's animal of death (WB vol. 2, p. 63) gives six entries and says the other days have no good or bad stated: an ox day is bad for the near ones; on a tiger day the life was cut in the bird month, a snake day in the monkey month, a horse day in the bird month, a sheep day in the sheep month, a monkey day in the dog month (the reprint's «ཁྱི་ཟླར», where Yigdzin-1 read «བྱི་བླར»). No rite is given for these entries.
