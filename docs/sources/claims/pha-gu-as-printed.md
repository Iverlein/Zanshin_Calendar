# P. 135: «ཕ་གུ» is what the reprint prints for the kin of wood

The Gser thang reprint (img. 141, p. 135, l. 17, 2026-11-01) prints «ཤིང་གི་ཕ་གུ་མེས་གསུམ་གྱི», ཕ་གུ with the vowel u under gu, clearly not ཁུ; the sentence goes on «ན་སྒབ་ཤི་སྒབ་འགྲམས་སྒབ་ཆོད» (the runs of illness, death and the one beside it cut). So the print has «ཕ་གུ» and the line names, for wood, the father(s) and uncle(s) of the kin-group (ཕ་ཁུ) only by inference from the sense; no other print was read, and the form stays as printed.
