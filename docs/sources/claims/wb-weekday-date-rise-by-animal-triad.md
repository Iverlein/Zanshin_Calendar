# The rough way of the planets' dates: four triads of day animals rise with a planet, and the date is rising, withdrawing, contested or declining

The next lines of p. 313 give "the rough way of the planets' dates": the twelve animals fall into four triads, and the dates of the weekday's planet that fall on a triad's animals are its rising dates (དར་ཚེས): tiger, horse and dog for Saturn; mouse, dragon and monkey for Mars; bird, ox and snake for a planet written «ཕུང» (not identified); pig, sheep and hare for the Moon. "In detail the date is counted from the weekday's planet." Whichever date is the rising one, that is rising, and the son also; the mother is «ཞུད» (withdrawing), the enemy «གུད» (declining), the friend «གྱོད» (contested). The results: for the rising there is no ground of illness; the withdrawing is lived through by a small rite; the contested needs a great ransom rite; the declining brings death or cutting off and a great ground of illness, and every work of the living and the dead is avoided, its remedy and the rite of care being done; for peaceful and fierce works alike the rising is good. The same four words (dar, gyod, zhud, gud) are used on p. 329 for the mansions (wb.2:p329-330), where WB sets that reckoning aside for want of a scriptural source; here no such remark follows.
