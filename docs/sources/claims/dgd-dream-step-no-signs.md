# Dbang chen dgyes rab rdo rje's dream step: kusha, lion posture, dawn question, the Vairocana mandala; no sign named

In Dbang chen dgyes rab rdo rje's preparation for a Vairocana mandala initiation (vol. 4, printed pp. 22-24, images 80-82 of I4CZ30840) the examination of dreams is the fifth step and a rule for the teacher, not a list of signs. Kusha grass is given to the pupils "for the examination of dreams" (img. 80). The teacher drives off the obstructors of the dream with vajra yakshas and vase water (img. 81). The pupils sleep free of unvirtuous thought, devoted to the lama and deity, on large kusha as seat and small as pillow, head toward the side where the sal tree falls or toward the lama, on the right side in the lion's posture, and tell the dawn dream. The teacher's verse says that tomorrow they will see the great mandala of Vairocana (img. 82). The text names no good or bad image, no verdict and no remedy; the only dream expected is the mandala. The same dawn question and posture are in Tsong kha pa's rite (tkg:i385-388), which does name two images. Machine reading; img. 82 eye-checked on the typeset page.
