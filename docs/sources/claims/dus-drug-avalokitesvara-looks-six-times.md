# «དུས་དྲུག» in the Showing of dreams is the six times of day and night at which the god looks, not a rule for the dream (RD395, q1091)

In the Avalokiteśvara rite «The Showing of dreams» (Derge Tengyur VE23703_1389 p. 330; Dpe bsdur ma svo:i660-661), «དུས་དྲུག་གཟིགས་དེས་མཁྱེན་པ་འཇུག» is said of the god: he "looks on the six times and enters with that knowledge". The «དུས་དྲུག» are not the six seasons: in a line about a being who looks at beings, the lexica's second sense stands, three times of the day and three of the night (nyin lan gsum, mtshan lan gsum); the Sgom sde tshig mdzod chen mo has the phrase «nyin mtshan dus drug la gzigs», "to look [upon beings] at the six times of day and night". The six are named in the Dung dkar and in a quotation in Dan Martin's collection as the forenoon, midday and afternoon, the evening, midnight and dawn.

So the line is a praise of the god's constant watchfulness and gives no rule for a dream: the dream-showing in the rite is at dawn (ཐོ་རངས) by a white monk and a woman with a lotus sign, and the god's "six times" are not the times of the rite. It is not six recitations.
