# The Svapnohana's remedy for bad dreams

The remedy of the *Svapnohana* for a bad dream is a fire offering (སྦྱིན་སྲེག, sbyin sreg) of red sandalwood sticks smeared with butter, with the Gāyatrī recited a thousand times, "by this peace is obtained"; and whoever reads the text "in the early morning" (ནང་པར, nang par) turns his bad dreams back; the text names this as the intention of Ngag dbang (ངག་དབང, ngag dbang, here probably the Vāgīśvarakīrti whose *Deceiving Death* stands before it in the volume; not settled) (p. 271). The remedy is one for all bad dreams; it is not graded by the image, by the watch or by the dreamer. The mantra's spelling, «ག་ཡ་ཏྲི» in the etext and «ག་ཡ་ཏཱི» in the machine reading, is not settled.
