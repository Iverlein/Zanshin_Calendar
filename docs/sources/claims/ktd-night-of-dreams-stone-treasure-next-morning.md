# A night of dreams with signs, a stone treasure taken out next morning, a helper by name (KTD)

In the biography of Dudjom Rinpoche (image 31, the life of the elder khenpo-tulku at the foundation of his monastery), after a Guru Rinpoche feast offering and an offering to the lord of the place, "that night" many dreams with signs came; at early morning the lama told the pupils that a stone treasure would be taken out that day and needed a helper with a "stone" in his name; Rdo rje grags pa, a craftsman, was found; stones of all wanted kinds came out where he dug. The night, the morning after and the helper's required name tie the dream to the finding, as in the cave book of the ninth day in Rtsa gsum gling pa's life (tsl.30:i112-114). Who dreamed is not settled in the OCR.
