# KP boxes 37 to 55: which boxes name earth lords, and box 42 against WB work 39

KP's box 38 (the divination box, titled «མོ») and box 37 (bathing) have, in their bad halves, no earth lord at all: the bad half names the mansions, the days and the links (birth, becoming, ignorance, old age and death), and box 38 ends with the other kinds being fitted to the peaceful and the fierce («གཞན་རྣམ་ཞི་དྲག་བསྟུན»). Of the boxes of KP's Henning list between 34 and 55 the earth lords are named in 34, 35, 39, 42, 44 to 49, 52 to 55. Box 42 (the bride) has by far the longest run, and it is WB's work 39's run (vol. 2, p. 421, etext) in another order: WB's «ས་སྨན་བར་གནམ་ཧལ་ཁྱི་རྒྱུ» (the earth medicine, the mid-sky, the hal khyi's course) stands in KP as «བར་གནམ» at the head, with no hal khyi course there; the hal khyi follows the khyim and the destroyer is the last name in KP, in WB the middle; KP has no «འཆི་སྦྱོར་འཇིག་ཉི» and no «རྒྱལ་མོ་བཞི» (WB's four queens); and KP ends with «བག་མའི་ལོ་ལག་ཙ་ཀྲ་སོགས་ཀྱི་ལྷ་དྲུག་གནས་ཕྱོགས», where WB has «གདགས་ཀྱི་ལྷ་དྲུག་གནས་པའི་ཕྱོགས». This is the machine and by-eye reading at 1.4× to 2×, not a settled one.
