# Marpa's vol. 2 copy of the inner-heat manual: the spirits' dream is known by its content, no hour named

Marpa's collected works have, besides the copy in vol. 4 (dds.19:p187-189), the same manual of the faults of the inner heat in vol. 2: a dream of lying with a dog, donkey, old woman, girl or leper woman that loses the drop is the sign of a spirit (གདོན, gdon); the cure is love, the gift of the body to the spirits (cut up by an imagined yogī and eaten), the unlovely, emptiness, done three times by day and three by night, and at bedtime the white thread round the waist tied to the vajra. As in vol. 4 no part of the night is named for the dream; the spirits' dream is known by what is dreamed.
