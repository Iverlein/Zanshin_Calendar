# The Svapnohana's unfortunate images and their verdicts

Unfortunate images of the *Svapnohana*, with the text's verdicts:
- The ground shaking greatly: goods lost, or illness in the body (p. 269).
- Horned or fanged beasts, a monkey or a pig appearing: fear from the lord (p. 269).
- Being smeared with sesame oil, milk or butter and the like: illness (p. 269).
- Climbing a cart drawn by a donkey and a she-camel, alone, and waking seated on it: quickly death (p. 269).
- A woman in red clothes with a red garland embraced: no next night (p. 270).
- Other than white, "black things, very bad": brahmins, cattle, elephants and gods excepted (p. 270).
- A blazing fire with smoke: fear (pp. 270–271).
The text has no more bad images than these; the long catalogue of bad signs is Advayavajra's (claim svi-bad-signs).
