# The middling yogin's line: «རང་བཞིན་གྱིས་ལ་ཉེ་བརྟག» in Palri and Kazi, «རང་གཞན་ལ་ཉེ་བརྟགས» in the etexts

In the middling yogin's line two wordings stand. «རང་བཞིན་གྱིས(ི)་ལ་ཉེ་བརྟག» (by their own nature examine the omens): Palri (img. 544, by eye at 3×, «བཞིན» and «བརྟག» without suffix clear), Kazi (img. 561, machine «རང་བཞིན་གྱི་ལ་ཉེ་བརྟག») and the Zhe chen hand (machine, garbled but «..ིན་གྱི་..ཉེ་བརྟགས»). «རང་གཞན་ལ་ཉེ་བརྟགས» (the omens of self and other): the Rin chen gter mdzod and Shechen etexts, and by machine the Sbas-yul hand («རང་གཞན»). Neither is a misreading of the other by shape: two real variants, the first (rang bzhin, "by nature, of themselves") in the Palri family with Kazi, the second in the etexts. The sense of the sentence stands either way: the middling take the dream's signs, examine them and match the good and bad to what fits. Which wording Sangs rgyas gling pa's text had is not decided; WB-style rank cannot decide it, as no other source quotes the line.
