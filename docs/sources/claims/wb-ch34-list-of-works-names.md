# The names skyas, sba phyar, shwa rags and sen in ch. 34's list of works

The four names of ch. 34's list of works (p. 378, read on the Gser thang reprint, img. 384, with the texts of the works themselves):

- «སྐྱས» (24, «སྐྱས་བྱེད་པ»): the doing of *skyas*, gifts; the work's own verse (p. 400) opens «སྐྱས་ཀྱི་བྱ་བར»; KP's box 23 has «སྐྱས་བྱེད་པ», glossed in kun-phan-me-long.md as giving gifts. WB's day-animal verses set it among the bride-taking and seeking-business works (dog day «བག་མ་དོན་གཉེར་སྐྱས», tiger day beside robbery), and the Bhutan digest lists it between sowing and the tent (p. 357) and as «བྲ་ཉེ་སྐྱས་འདེབས» in its table (p. 408). "Dowries" is not in any text read and stays unsettled; "gifts" is.
- «སྦ་ཕྱར» (17): sewing (འཚེམ་པ) tents or covers: the work's own head is «སྦ་དང་ཕྱ་ཐེར་འཚེམ་པ» and its verse «ཕུར་བུ སྦ་ཕྱར་འཚེམ་པ་བཟང་», with «འཚེམ» good where «འབུབས་པ» is bad on the fire-four and the reverse on the Pleiades. The first syllable is «སྦ» on the list (p. 378, scan) and on the work's head; the mansion verses write «སྦྲ་ཕྱར». What «ཕྱར» and «ཕྱ་ཐེར» are is not settled: a hoisted flag or awning is likely, not proved.
- «ཤྭ་རགས» (49): a dike: the work is «ཤྭ་རགས་བརྩིག་དང་ཆུ་བོ་བསྲུང་», building a dike and guarding against the river, and the mansion and day-animal verses name the same work «ཆུ་རགས» beside ponds, pits and tree-planting. Settled as dike.
- «སེན» (50): nails (སེན་མོ): the work's own head is «བཟོ་དང་སྐྲ་སེན་འབྲེག་པ», crafts and cutting hair and nails, and the verse has «སྐྲ་དང་སེན་མོ་འབྲེག་པ». On p. 378 the list reads «བཟོ་སྒྲ་སེན» in the machine reading; the stack under བཟོ་ is sa with ra and an indistinct head letter, and the sense with p. 420 gives སྐྲ་, hair: crafts, hair and nails.
