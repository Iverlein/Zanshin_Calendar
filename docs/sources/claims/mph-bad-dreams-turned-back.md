# Mipham turns back bad dreams by a mantra and names no time

Mipham's sādhana of Flaming Uṣṇīṣa (mph.25:i459-460) turns back bad dreams and bad omens (རྨི་ལམ་ངན་པ་དང་ལྟས་ངན་སོགས་བཟློག་པ): the mantra "dispels bad dreams and bad signs (མཚན་མ་ངན) by being recited once". The same kind of line, the dream turned back by a mantra, is found in other pages of his collected works that the search found and that were read only in short windows: the be bum (vol. 28, images 25 and 147–151: a bad dream seen, recite this and sleep and a good dream is seen; recite a thousand times, clap three times and meditate dream and appearance as without good or bad). Nothing in these gives a time or a day for the bad dream; the cure is recited at once or at bedtime. Etext of the be bum read in short windows only; the Chengdu volume by machine.
