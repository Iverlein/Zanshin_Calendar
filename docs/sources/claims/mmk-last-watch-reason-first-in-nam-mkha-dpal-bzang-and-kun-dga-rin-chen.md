# The three-part reason for the last watch is in Nam mkha' dpal bzang's manual and, by snippet, Kun dga' rin chen's, with no source named (RD377)

The reason for taking the last watch, that the first watch is mixed with the day's habits, the middle two are deep sleep and the last is clear, stands in the Vajrāvalī initiation manual of Brang ti paN chen Nam mkha' dpal bzang (1535-1602 by BDRC), the manual that the Rgyud sde kun btus prints; its colophon says it was written in an Iron-Female-Snake year (1581 is the only one in his life, by my reckoning) and that it gathers the good parts of the Mnga' ris Tshul 'od pa's (Rab 'byams pa Tshul khrims 'od zer) Vajrāvalī manual, of Sa bzang 'Phags pa gzhon nu blo gros's and of Mkhas grub Dpal ldan rdo rje pa's, with the words of lamas and scholars (rsk:p875-878 of the etext; the colophon was read in the etext and is not a passage here). The manual names no source for the three-part reason. Kun dga' rin chen's (1517-1584) wording, known only by BDRC's search snippet (his volume answers 403), is the same in sense and nearly in words («srod kyi cha nyin bar gyi snang ba'i bag chags dang 'dres pas cung mi gsal zhing / nam gung gnyid mthug po zhes rmi lam mi 'byung / de nas tho rangs kyi cha'i rmi lam brtag dgos pas / de la rmi lam bzang po'i ...»), so the two are of one generation and one of them copies the other or both a common source: which is earlier is not shown by what was read (a doubt). The text of Kun dga' rin chen's volume 2 that holds it was not identified.
