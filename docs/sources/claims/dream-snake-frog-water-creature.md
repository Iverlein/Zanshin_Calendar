# Snakes, frogs and water creatures

Image group: body and mind. Read by 6 texts: Four Tantras (for a sick dreamer), Svapnohana (SVO), Svapna-īkṣā (SVI), Method under Sakya Paṇḍita's name (SKP), ACIP method (RST), Klong rdol's verses as the article quotes them (KBS).
Offered to any dreamer; the Four Tantras' or WB ch. 25's rows join when the dreamer is ill.

The texts differ on this image (some read it good, some bad); no verdict is chosen between them, each row speaks for its text, WB ch. 30 first where it reads the image.

RD274 (2026-11-09) after RD271 and RD227: the SVO line names creatures, not "a horse and a bla mo": the centipede, the snake and a water creature (svo-rta-bla-mo-is-the-centipede; the dictionaries' རྟ་བླ is the centipede, st-rta-bla-is-the-centipede), so the horse of the earlier row is dropped. The word is settled; the row still rests on the unit of the white snake's "within ten", which no text names (dream-unsettled-words). The creatures of the Four Tantras' ch. 81 list are settled on the scan and in the dictionaries (RD227, dream-sick-naga-illness-dreams); the Four Tantras' fish for the sick dreamer in ch. 7 (devoured by fish) is dream-sick-devoured-by-fish.
