# In the Derge's Mañjuśrī root tantra «སྡིག་པ» is the sign Scorpio (vṛścika) and «སྦལ་པ» the sign Cancer (karkaṭaka), the same words in ch. 1, ch. 15 and ch. 18 (RD372, q1047)

The Derge's list of the twelve houses at mmk.1:p403 is the translation's own list of signs, the same words as in its first chapter and in its births chapter, so the two doubts about it have an answer in the volume itself.

**«སྡིག་པ» is the scorpion.** The first chapter's list of thirty-six houses (mmk.1:p246) runs ལུག, གླང, སྦྱོར་བ, སྦལ་པ, སེང་གེ, བུ་མོ, སྲང, སྡིག་པ, གཞུ, ཆུ་སྲིན, བུམ་པ, ཉ, མིའུ་ཐུང, ཉེ་བའི་བུམ་པ, བྷྲིང་ག་ར, རལ་གྲི, གླང་པོ་ཆེ, མ་ཧེ ..., and the Sanskrit list that stands for it (mmk.2:p19) is meṣa, vṛṣabha, mithuna, karkaṭaka, siṃha, kanyā, tulā, vṛścika, dhanu, makara, kumbha, mīna, vānara, upa-kumbha, khaḍga, mahiṣa. The two run together word for word, and the later words that are not signs (མིའུ་ཐུང for vānara, ཉེ་བའི་བུམ་པ for upa-kumbha, རལ་གྲི for khaḍga, མ་ཧེ for mahiṣa) hold the alignment, so «སྡིག་པ» at the eighth place renders vṛścika; the dream chapter's Sanskrit, whose eighth word the OCR does not read (mmk.2:p152-153), therefore most likely has vṛścika there too, and mmk.1:p403 has the same twelve Tibetan words in the same order as p246. Elsewhere the pool already has the word at the eighth place of the sign list of the Mchog dga' (kd.3:p84-85) and of the White Beryl's rising signs (the rising-signs chapter lists «སྲང་དང་སྡིག་པ») and the Mchog dga' gives «ས་སྐྱེས» (Mars) as lord of the ram and «སྡིག་པ» (kd.3:p84-85): it is a Tibetan name for Scorpio, not a different Sanskrit word.

**«སྦལ་པ» is the crab, a name, not a slip.** At the fourth place the Derge has «སྦལ་པ» in all three places where the twelve are listed (mmk.1:p246, mmk.1:p403 and, as «the house of the frog», mmk.1:p429, where it is a house with two named mansions, Rgyal and Skag, born in it yellow, virtuous and great in miracles), and the Sanskrit has karkaṭaka for it in the list. The translation uses one word every time; the Mchog dga' by contrast writes «ཀརྐ་ཊ» (kd.3:p84-85) and WB's period for the same sign is «སྤལ་བ» (etext «སྦལ་བ»; combination-period.md). So «སྦལ་པ» is not an error of one page, it is the Derge translators' (or the Tibetan tradition's) name for Cancer; why a frog stands for a crab is not said in any page read.
