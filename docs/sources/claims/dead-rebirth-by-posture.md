# The rebirth by the manner of dying and the posture

The fifth examination's readings by the manner of dying and the posture of the dead (wb.2:p70-72).

- Face down, head to the south-west, warmth gathered at the soles, a hard death with wailing: a lower realm whatever is done.
- The head's direction gives the caste of the next birth: south, the lord caste; east, royal; west, commoners; north, brahmins; an intermediate direction, outcastes.
- Cross-legged with hands in meditation: a protector who guides beings; half cross-legged pressing the earth: one who has reached the paths and stages; squatting in the giving gesture: no fear of the lower realms; mouth closed and eyes shut: good.
- Lying: the lion's posture (on the right side, right hand at the cheek, legs stretched, left hand on the hip) best, lying across middling, on the back last; legs stretched best, drawn up middling, crossed last.
- All of it is outweighed by the roots of virtue: dear and good virtue brings a good result despite unlike signs, weak virtue a bad one.
