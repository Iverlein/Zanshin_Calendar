# Yongle dadian 20181-20182: per-day lists of months for the omen of a dream seen on that day; not the Edo table

The two surviving volumes of the Yongle dadian that hold the character 日 (20181-20182) set out, for each day of the sexagenary cycle from 甲辰 to 辛酉 (day 41 to day 58 survive), the sayings of several selection-of-days books; one of them gives a list of months in which a dream seen on that day (占夢) has a named omen (mourning, a traveller in danger, the stove not at peace, fire from heaven, great good, wine and food, quarrels, a letter coming, half good) and a list of months for 占恠, divining a portent; another, a short omen headed 夢 for each day. This is a Chinese table that joins dreams with the day and the month, but it is arranged by day (the months under each day), gives omens and not a rule of silence, and its lists (eight or nine months for most days) do not match the Edo table: put beside the Edo months of each day's branch (the table of mdz:p11 turned from months to branches), no day's list is equal to them, and the Edo months lie wholly inside the Chinese list for only two of the eighteen days (庚戌, 甲寅). The Edo table is therefore not a copy or a thinning of this table. Doubts: the transcription was checked on the images for two of the eighteen days only, and the numerals 十十一, 十一一 are not certain.
