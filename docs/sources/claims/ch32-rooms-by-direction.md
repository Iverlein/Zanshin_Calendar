# Ch. 32: the rooms and works of a house by direction

WB vol. 2, ch. 32, p. 287 (img. 293, machine): where the rooms and works of a house go, by direction. East: the empowerment and teaching room, the work of pacifying, the loom; south: the cooking and hand-store, the work of increase, the cattle and horse pen, the water vessels; south-west: the donkeys, the hearth shelf; west: the grain store above and the pen below (or the pen south), the stable by the power-work; north: the store, the wrathful work, the tea-churn; north-east: the small bedroom and the protector's room; the prayer room on the right or in the north; the hearth south-east or west. The stairs are never turned to the east; the pillars not three-cornered or eight; the inner form a square. Doubts: the line «ནུབ་ཏུ་བྱན་ཚང་དབང་ལས་ཤིས» and the tea-churn are Yigdzin-1's reading only.
