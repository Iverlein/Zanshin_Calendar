# Ch. 32: the rooms and works of a house by direction

WB vol. 2, ch. 32, p. 287 (img. 293, read by eye, RD156, 2026-11-05): where the rooms and works of a house go, by direction. East: the empowerment and teaching room, the work of pacifying, the water mill (ཆུ་བསྐོར, chu bskor); south: the cooking and hand-store, the work of increase, the cattle and horse pen, the water vessels; south-west: the donkeys, the hearth (ཐབ་ཀ, thab ka); west: the grain store above and the pen below (or the pen south), the «བྱན་ཚང» by the power-work; north: the store, the wrathful work, the tea-churn; north-east: the small bedroom and the protector's room; the prayer room on the right or in the north; the hearth south-east or west. The stairs are never turned to the east; the pillars not three-cornered or eight; the inner form a square. The bellows are blown from the south-east. Doubt: what «བྱན་ཚང» (byan tshang) is in «ནུབ་ཏུ་བྱན་ཚང་དབང་ལས་ཤིས»; the reading «stable» has no ground in these lines. The tea mortar (ཇ་གཏུན, ja gtun) is the scan's.
