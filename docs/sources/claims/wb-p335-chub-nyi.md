# WB p. 335: the Chub day is the Paired with Jupiter on Pūrvabhādrapadā

The Chub day (ཆུབ་ཉི, chub nyi) of p. 335 is the Paired combination with Jupiter moved from Uttaraphalgunī (11) to Pūrvabhādrapadā (24); nearly all good works go well on it. The table of p. 341 has its own column with the same seven cells. The word is «ཆུབ» on the reprint, in the Zhol print and in both etexts; the "Rough" (རྩུབ) of RD80 was the machine's. WB does not gloss it.
