# WB p. 328: Aśvinī's numeral is printed ༤

In the class list of pp. 328–329 the editors number the mansions from Aśvinī as 0. On the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5029 img. 334, at 4×) the numeral after Aśvinī (ཐ་སྐར, tha skar) is the solid shape of Mṛgaśiras's (༤) and Svātī's (༡༤), not the open-eyed zero of (༢༠). The count wants 0, since Mṛgaśiras is 4 in the same list. The print is quoted as it stands and the sense kept as 0: a slip in the numeral, on this print at least. The 1996 print was not compared.
