# Dpal sprul, Words of My Perfect Teacher: lay people who cannot give up killing pledge it for two months of the year, or the fifteenth and the new moon

Dpal sprul's *Words of My Perfect Teacher* (Kun bzang bla ma'i zhal lung), in the part on the ten virtues (kzl:i177-178), speaks to those who cannot give up killing for good: out of a year they should pledge not to kill in two months, the first Hor month (the month of miracles, ཆོ་འཕྲུལ་གྱི་ཟླ་བ, cho 'phrul gyi zla ba) and the fourth (the like of Saga, ས་ག་ཟླ་བ, sa ga zla ba), or on the fifteenth of every month (ཟླ་བ་བྱུང་ངོ་ཅོག་གི་བཅོ་ལྔ, zla ba byung ngo cog gi bcho lnga) and the new moon (གནམ་སྟོང, gnam stong); a pledge for a year, a month or a day brings great benefit as well.

- Days: the 15th and the new-moon day (the 30th, by the Larung print's note lcb:p568 on gnam stong); two whole months of the year as the other choice.
- Practice: not killing living beings, taken as a pledge, best before a lama, a virtuous friend or a support of the Three Jewels.
- For whom: householders and others who cannot give up killing altogether.
- The 8th, 10th, 25th and 29th are not named; nor is any day of the waning half but the new moon.
- Tradition: Nyingma (the Longchen Nyingthig preliminaries), a lama's counsel to lay people; rank 3.
