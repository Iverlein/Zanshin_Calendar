# Ch. 13 strip charts: p. 142 legible by place on the reprint, p. 147 mostly empty cells

On the Gser thang reprint the strip charts of pp. 142-147 can be read by eye as to which word stands in which cell: the heading row of p. 142 names the day's course (nam langs, nyi shar, probably nyi phyed) with numerals, and the cells hold the repeated names nang bdud, snang sel, gza' thog, bla mkhyen, dur len, bshan pa and gshed po, with klu gza' bco lnga as a strip caption. Many cells are drawn and left empty (p. 147: the rat chart mostly, the ox chart's second and fourth bottom cells). The reprint's face is a large bitmap face, clear enough at 1.5x to 2.2x on the strip charts, unlike the narrow cells of the ring charts of pp. 136-141; the first pass's verdict that no cell is legible holds for those narrow cells. Syllables inside the multi-line cells, the numerals and the strip captions were not settled; what each cell's names mean for the chart is not stated on the page.
