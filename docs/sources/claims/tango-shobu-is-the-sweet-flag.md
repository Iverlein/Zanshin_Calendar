# The 端午 plant 菖蒲 is the sweet flag, not the garden iris

Japanese Wikipedia's ショウブ (read 2026-11-24, facts only): the sweet flag (Acorus calamus) grows in clumps by water, 50–100 cm, fragrant throughout, its leaves flat and sword-pointed like the garden iris's, its flower a small yellow-green cylindrical spike that looks like no flower; it is a quite different plant from the garden iris (ハナショウブ), with which the shared name confuses it. It is the plant of the 端午 bath (菖蒲湯) and the eaves. So the 5/5 frame's plant is the sweet-flag leaf, not a flowering iris. Answers q1357.
