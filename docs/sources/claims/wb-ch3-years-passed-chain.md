# WB ch. 3 counts passed years back to the writing year, each date the previous one less a reduction

The chronology of WB's chapter 3 counts the passed years (ཕྱིན་གྱི་ལོ, phyin-gyi-lo; འདས་ལོ, 'das-lo) back from the writing year. Every event is given as a year with its animal and element, then the next event by a reduction (ཕྲི་བ, phri-ba, "subtract"; དམན་པ, dman-pa; སྦྱངས་པ, sbyang-pa) written as number words with the figure beside it, so that each year is the previous year less that reduction. The number words give the digits from the units upward: «མཁའ་མཚོ» (0, 4) is 40, «མདའ་མིག» (5, 2) is 25, «རོ་མདའ་མེ» (6, 5, 3) is 356, «མཁའ་མེ་མདས» (0, 3, 5) is 530. Chain checked on pp. 22–23: 2648 (conception) − 1 = 2647 (birth) − 28 = 2619 (the going forth) − 6 = 2613 (the first turning of the wheel) − 21 = 2592 − 25 = 2567 − 1 = 2566 − 2 = 2564 − 1 = 2563 − 40 = 2523 − 356 = 2167 − 204 = 1963 − 530 = 1433 − 79 = 1354 − 40 = 1314 − 247 = 1067 − 4 = 1063 − 5 = 1058 − 12 = 1046 − 12 = 1034 − 77 = 957 − 19 = 938. The count ends on p. 28: at 34, reduced by 34 («མཚོ་མེས» 4, 3), the year is the Prabhava fire-hare year (པྲ་བྷ་ཝ་ཞེས་མེ་ཡོས), the first of the twelfth rab byung, the year the book was written: the count reaches 0 there. Several figures on these pages stand differently on the machine readings (2587, 2649, 2643, 1048, 790, 690, 607); the chain gives 2567, 2619, 2613, 1058, 720, 670, 647 (the sign of the confusion is in the passage text and its Open questions). Read again by eye on the Gser thang reprint in RD197: of these, 2587 (p. 23) and 1048 (p. 23) are what the print itself prints, slips of the print against its chain, and so is 278 for 178 on p. 27 (the chain 207 − 22 − 7); the figures between 608 and 578 on p. 24 run as 608 − 2 = 606 − 3 = 603 − 8 = 595 − 17 = 578. Where the print departs from the chain, the chain is the reading of the text's own count.
