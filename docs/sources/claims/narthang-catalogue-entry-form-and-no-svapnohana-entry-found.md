# The Narthang catalogue's entries are title, made-by, translation-of; no Examination of Dreams entry found in the images read (q1087, RD401)

The Narthang catalogue's entries (vol. 225, I2KG212509) are in the form title, «made by» (མཛད་པ), then the paṇḍita and lotsāba's «translation» (འགྱུར), in runs by deity: the Kālacakra run on img. 39 (machine reading) has the same form as the Yamāntaka entry on img. 197. This is the form of the Tengyur catalogues; whether the manuscript's text is the printed Tshangs pa'i dbyangs of Phur lcog is not shown by one sample, since the one Deceiving Death entry is worded otherwise than Phur lcog's (svo:i197). No entry for the Examination of Dreams was found: not on img. 141-206 (RD365), not on img. 39 or 154 (RD401, machine) and not in the garbled etext by «རྨི་ལམ», «འཆི་བ་བསླུ» or «བརྟག་པ» (the only «རྨི་ལམ» hits are on etext pp. 141, 259 and 283, titles of other works or verse). Images 3-140 and 207-288 were not read page by page; the catalogue is not shown to omit the work.
