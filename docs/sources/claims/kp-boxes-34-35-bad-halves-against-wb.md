# KP boxes 34 and 35: the bad halves keep WB works 32 and 33 earth lords, shortened

KP's box 35 (bloodletting, moxibustion, treatment), bad half, names for the bad days the same things as WB's work 33 (vol. 2, p. 412, etext «སྤར་ཁ་ལི་དང་སྨེ་གཉིས་ནག །བདུན་དམར་འཆར་དུས་སྲོག་ཚེས་དང་། །འཆི་སྦྱོར་ས་ཡི་ཕུང་བྱེད་རྒྱུ། །བི་ཏྠི་རྒྱུ་བའི་དུས་ཚོད་ལ། །འཛེམ་པའི་དུས་ཡིན་ཁྱད་པར་དུ། །རིགས་ལྡན་ཐུག་ན་ཤིན་ཏུ་འཛེམ།»): the sme ba 2 and 7 (WB: the 2nd black, the 7th red rising), the burning dates, the joining of death, the earth's destroyer, Viṣṭi, and the meeting with the Rigs ldan as very bad. KP's «༢ ༧» stands before the combination period where WB's trigram li and the sme ba come first; KP has «བར་འཕུང་བྱེད» (on the scan of 2026-11-14, kp:i41s; the machine's «གདོང་» is «བར»: the bar and the destroyer), WB «ས་ཡི་ཕུང་བྱེད་རྒྱུ» (the earth's destroyer, its course); KP has a name, bar, that WB's work 33 lacks, and KP's «སྲོག་ཚེས» is WB's own spelling (white-beryl-ch34.md, work 33: «སྲོག་ཚེས», the earlier copy here with «སྲེག» was a slip). No earth lord is in KP that WB lacks. KP's box 34 (medicine) names only dbul in its run, where WB's work 32 (p. 411) names the poor-one's lord and the Sun «དབུལ་ལག་མི་འཕྲོད་ཉི་མ» and «ས་ཡི་ཕུང་བྱེད་རྒྱུ་དུས»: KP drops the destroyer's course and the Sun that does not agree. Box 34 on the scan (kp:i41s): the bad half's last line is the Sun that does not agree (མི་འཕྲོད་ཉི་མ), as WB's work 32 ends; the nectar-period line is the foot of the good half's panel, as in WB, where it is listed good.
