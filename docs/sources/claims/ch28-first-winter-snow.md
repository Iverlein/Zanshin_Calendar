# First winter's snow-rain verse: inner strife on the 5th

WB vol. 2, p. 31: the snow-rain verse of the first winter month, «དགུན་ར་ཤར་མ་ཐག་ལ་བབས། །ནང་འཁྲུག་ལྔ་ལ་མ་བབས་ན།»: every other entry of the page has the shape "[month, day] not falling: [harm]", with a bad result after «མ་བབས་ན» (and the first winter's 10th, in the next line, again «བཅུར་མ་བབས»). Read the same way, the first line states that it falls as the month rises, and the inner strife comes when it does not fall on the 5th: it is the lack of snow on the 5th, not the early fall, that brings inner strife. This reading follows the page's own pattern; a note of Mipham's on the verse was not found (the notes, p. 537, skip it). The middle winter's «བུད་མེད་མི་འབྲུ་སྙི» (the 4th and 11th, not falling) has no word «སྙི» in the dictionaries; read with the others as "women, people, grain: [harm]", it is a harm to women, people and grain; the word stays unexplained.
