# Rāhu's course by the lunar date, the general counsel

Rāhu's course by the lunar date, the general counsel (ནུ་རུ, nu ru) (WB vol. 2, pp. 236–237). The planet goes for each date (1st to 29th) from a quarter to its opposite, at a named hour, with a named retinue (the mamo army-leader on the 4th, the rākṣasa Bhitar on the 15th, the nāga Rājā on the 18th, Yama on the 22nd, the nāga-tsen and Viṣṇu on the 25th, the eight pieces rolled into one on the 29th). Examples: 1st, half day, south-west to north-east; 2nd, noon, east to west; 5th, noon, east to west; 8th, early morning, south-east to north-west; 10th, dawn, south to north; 15th, mid-day, south-west to north then east; 17th, midnight, west to east; 21st, dawn, east to west; 25th, dawn, south to north; 29th, north-east to south-west. The year's and the month's seeking of food are given first: in the bird, ox and snake years he seeks in the year, in the three autumn months and the hare, sheep and pig months in the sky's void. The twelve counsels of Rāhu are named: year, month, general counsel, the inner, the secret, the head-severing treasure, father, mother, emanation, messenger, hand-seal, and the day-time.
