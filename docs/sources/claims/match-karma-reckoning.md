# The karma reckoning over the match is each spouse's birth reckoning

WB closes the match (vol. 1, p. 364): those whose life is exhausted, whose
vitality is exhausted, the chronically ill, the poor, those in danger of
ill speech and those of bad karmic result are bad "even if they agree".
Mipham's notes (vol. 2, p. 526) gloss it as a reckoning made apart from the
match: "though the marriage reckoning agrees and the match is good, when
the karma reckoning (ལས་རྩིས, las rtsis) is made", and they give each
word an aspect:

| WB's word | The notes |
| --- | --- |
| life exhausted (ཚེ་ཟད) | the life force bad |
| vitality exhausted (ཟུངས་ཟད; the notes «ཟུང་ཟད») | the body's reckoning bad, "though identified as being old" (རྒས་པར) |
| chronically ill (གཅོང་ཅན) | flaws of the year and the like |
| poor (དབུལ་པོ) | the power small in the karma reckoning |
| in danger of ill speech (ཁས་ཉེན) | the luck bad |
| of bad karmic result (ལས་འབྲས) | the reckoning bad in general |

"Or the plain sense, the poor, the old and so on, and the hidden sense
like it, the karma reckoning bad."

**Which reckoning.** In the notes «ལས་རྩིས» is the name of their first
section, the comment on WB ch. 22, the reckoning of lives and karma
(ཚེ་རབས་ལས་རྩིས, tshe rabs las rtsis): it opens «སྐབས་དང་པོ་ཚེ་རབས་ལས་རྩིས་ལ»
(p. 474) and closes «ལས་རྩིས་ཀྱི་སྐབས་སོ» (p. 489); p. 478 speaks of "the
power in the hundred pebbles of the karma reckoning" (ལས་རྩིས་བརྒྱ་རྡེལ་གྱི་དབང་ཐང),
the birth's hundred (vol. 1, p. 261); and p. 526 itself, two lines before,
has the match's rise and decline examined "as in the section of the karma
reckoning" (ལས་རྩིས་སྐབས་ལྟར), «སྐབས» being the notes' word for their
sections («སྐབས་དྲུག་པ་བག་རྩིས», p. 523). The karma reckoning is therefore
ch. 22's birth reckoning, made for each spouse from his or her own birth;
WB's list names persons, so either spouse found so makes the couple bad.

**Not stated.** Which of ch. 22's findings make an aspect "bad" is not
said on these pages. The notes point the power to the hundred's power
block; by the same board the life force, body and luck would be read on
their blocks' deciding pebbles (pp. 261, 329–331), a reading, not a
statement. "Flaws of the year" (ལོ་སྐྱོན) names the harsh years of the
year of age, not the birth.

Since 2026-10-25 the passages that bear on it (Mipham's "decline" a black verdict,
wb.2:p483; WB's own black readings of the four aspects; a manual's karma
reckoning with one verdict for each aspect) are gathered in
[karma-reckoning-aspect-bad](karma-reckoning-aspect-bad.md).
