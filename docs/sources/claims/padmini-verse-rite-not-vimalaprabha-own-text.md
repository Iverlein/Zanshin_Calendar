# The Padminī's verse of the bed rite is not the Vimalaprabhā's text; its author is not named

# The Padminī's verse of the bed rite is interleaved with the Padminī's own prose, and the page sends the extended rite to the Vimalaprabhā; the verse's author is not named (q1020)

In Derge vol. 13 (the Padminī pañjikā, etext pp. 312-323) the rite of the ground is given as short prose lines, each mantra closed by «ཞེས་བྱ་བ», alternating with verse lines in seven-syllable metre (the placing of the ten guardians, the lines of the offering, the bed rite at p. 320: «དེ་ནས་བཟང་ངན་ཤེས་པའི་ཕྱིར། ལྷོ་ཡི་ཕྱོགས་སུ་ཉལ་ས་ནི།», kcd.13:p320). The verse is not the Vimalaprabhā's own sentence: the Vimalaprabhā's rite (kcd.12:p228-229) is prose and the verse repeats it point by point in other words, adding the flute and the ḍāru-drum, «who mock the wise» and the mantra's few spelling differences. On p. 323 (kcd.13:p323) the Padminī's prose says after a blessing of the vases «དེ་ལ་རྒྱས་པའི་ཆོ་ག་ནི། འགྲེལ་པ་དྲི་མ་མེད་པ་ལས་ཤེས་པར་བྱའོ།» ("the extended rite should be known from the commentary Vimalaprabhā"), and the verse goes on at once with «དེ་ལྟར་སྲུང་བ་ལ་སོགས་ནི། བྱང་ནས་ཐིག་སྐུད་གདབ་པར་བྱ།». So the Padminī treats the Vimalaprabhā as another text from which the long form is learnt, and gives here a short rite of its own, partly in verse. No line names a source for the verse, and none of the pages 316-323 introduces it as a quotation; whether it is the Padminī author's own verse (BDRC: Dus zhabs pa chen po 'Jam pa'i rdo rje) or a verse rite of the Kālacakra tradition taken in is not told. Etext only, no scan of pp. 312-323.
