# Ch. 27: the golden turtle's classes of years

The golden turtle (WB vol. 2, pp. 20–22) sorts the sixty years into classes by the element each "becomes", and sets the classes of a household's members against one another.

| Element | Years | Class |
| --- | --- | --- |
| wood | wood male rat; wood ox; wood sheep; the other ten | god; man; cow (as the pebbles show; the print's letter in doubt); *srin po* |
| fire | fire male monkey; fire female snake; fire female bird; fire male dragon; the other eight | animal; hungry ghost; hell being; asura; planet |
| iron | iron male tiger; iron male monkey; iron female bird; the other nine | god; asura; hell being; *re 'jam* |
| water | water rat, horse, ox, sheep; the other eight | garuḍa; *klu* |
| earth | the earth fourth-executioner years; the other eight | turtle; earth lord |

Identifying pebbles: god four white, man three, cow two, *klu* one white; asura one white and two black; animal one white and one black; *re 'jam* five white; garuḍa one black, hungry ghost two, *srin po* three, hell being four, earth lord five, turtle six, planet seven black. Enemies: planet and *klu*, *srin po* and *re 'jam*, garuḍa and earth lord, garuḍa and *klu*; cow and turtle agree with all. Parents and child both of a *klu*, asura, hell, hungry-ghost or animal year bring *klu* illness, quarrels, illness, poverty or dullness. Each class has its reading and remedies (the passage).

Which "becoming" of the year is meant (its own element, or another reckoning's) is not stated on these pages.
