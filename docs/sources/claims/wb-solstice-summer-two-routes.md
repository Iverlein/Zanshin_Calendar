# The summer solstice of the author's place: both of WB's ways give 5;1;30, and «skar drug zhe lnga» is 6;45

The two ways of taking the summer solstice of the author's place on wb.1:p185 give the same figure, 5 mansions (*skar ma*), 1 *chu tshod*, 30 *srang* of the mean sun, and the figure «སྐར་དྲུག་ཞེ་ལྔ» is 6 mansions 45 *chu tshod*, the *Lamp that Makes the Solstice Clear*'s summer-solstice measure at the middle Brahmā-line (*tshangs thig*) of the southern continent.

The units are those of the claim `wb-ch4-mean-month-and-sun-divisors`: the mansion, then the *chu tshod* (a sixtieth of a mansion), then the *srang* (a sixtieth of that). One house-day (*khyim zhag*) is 4 *chu tshod* 30 *srang* («རིག་བྱེད་མཁའ་མེ», 4 and 30; q678 of 2026-11-13), thirty of them a house of 135 *chu tshod* = 2 mansions 15 *chu tshod*.

The figures come from a commentary of the Gser thang etexts, the *Yang dag lam gyi 'jug sgo blo gsar yid kyi dga' ston* (BDRC etext I3CN4644, vol. 4, pp. 45-47; machine reading of a poor scan, the digits as the etext prints them): it quotes the *Lamp* as giving «སྐར་མཚོ་དབྱུག་གུ་མཁའ་མེ» (4 mansions, 30 *chu tshod*, written «༤. ༣༠») for the summer solstice at the Brahmā-line of the eastern part of the southern continent, and «སྐར་དྲུག་ཆུ་ཚོད་ཞེ་ལྔ» (6 mansions, 45 *chu tshod*) for the middle Brahmā-line; it says the Phug-pa school reads the place as seven portions (*sa tshigs*) past the eastern line, and that after twenty-three house-days from the summer solstice of the place the first portion of the middle continent has the solstice measure «སྐར་དྲུག་ཞེ་ལྔ». The two lines are thirty house-days apart, and the check holds: 6;45 − 4;30 = 2;15 = 135 *chu tshod* = 30 · 4;30.

WB's two ways (the *Lamp*'s lines, quoted on wb.1:p185, seven house-days from the eastern line and twenty-three from the middle one):

- from the eastern line: 4;30 + 7 · 0;4;30 (= 0;31;30) = 5;1;30;
- from the middle line: 6;45 − 23 · 0;4;30 (= 1;43;30) = 5;1;30.

They agree, as WB says («དོན་ལ་ཁྱད་མེད»), and the number words WB gives for the result, «སྐར་དབང་དབྱུག་གཟུགས་མཁའ་མེ», read as mansions 5 («དབང་»), *chu tshod* («དབྱུག་»; «དབྱུག་གུ» is the *chu tshod*, as in the *Lamp*'s «སྐར་མཚོ་དབྱུག་གུ་མཁའ་མེ») 1 («གཟུགས»), *srang* 30 («མཁའ་མེ»), are 5;1;30. The same constant «སྐར་དྲུག་ཞེ་ལྔ» (6 mansions 45 *chu tshod*) stands in the handbooks as the purification (*sbyang*) of the sun in the Siddhānta reckoning, «ཉི་མ་སྐར་དྲུག་ཞེ་ལྔས་སྦྱངས» («byang bgrod skar drug zhe lnga dor», Kun 'dus chen mo, vol. 2, BDRC I4716), so that the 6;45 is a standing quantity of the system and not a figure of this page only.

Not settled: WB's «ཕྲི་བ་གཉིས» ('subtract, two'): the working above subtracts the twenty-three days' difference once from 6;45; whether «གཉིས» is 'the second (way)', 'twice' or the two places (mansions and *chu tshod*) is not decided by the figures.
