# Rtsa gsum gling pa's passing: forty-five in the earth-horse year, or about forty-four; no month or day

Two accounts of the passing of Rtsa gsum gling pa (རྩ་གསུམ་གླིང་པ, rtsa gsum gling pa), neither with a month or a day:

- The modern editor's preface to TSL vol. 30: at forty-five, in the earth-horse year (ས་རྟ་ལོ, sa rta lo) of the twelfth sixty-year cycle (རབ་བྱུང, rab byung), given as 1738, "his mind dissolved into the expanse of phenomena" (tsl.30:i8).
- Jamgön Kongtrul's life, printed in the same volume: he passed away at about forty-four, so that his deeds were not brought to completion; no year (tsl.30:i575).

The ages differ by one and Kongtrul hedges his with «about» (ཙམ, tsam). Khyentse's brief life and KTD's translation of it (pbc:p2) give no passing date; the autobiography ends before it (the preface: it runs to his twenty-eighth year, tsl.30:i8). No source read names a day of the year on which his passing is kept.
