# WB pp. 343–344: the eleven doers by date and half

WB's table of pp. 343–344 sets the doers (བྱེད་པ, byed pa, karaṇas) by date and half: Unlovely (མི་སྡུག, mi sdug) at the 1st's earlier half; the seven movable ones in unbroken order Spreading, Wiping, Virtue, Sesame-pounding, House-born, Merchant, Viṣṭi from the 1st's later half to the 29th's earlier half, eight rounds; Auspicious (བཀྲ་ཤིས, bkra shis) at the 29th's later half, Crossroads (བཞི་མདོ, bzhi mdo) and Good Nāga (ཀླུ་བཟང, klu bzang) on the 30th. Viṣṭi falls on the 4th, 8th, 11th, 15th, 18th, 22nd, 25th and 29th. Every cell is at wb.2:p343-346.
