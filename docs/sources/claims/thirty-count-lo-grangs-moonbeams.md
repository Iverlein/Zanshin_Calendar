# The thirty's count of years: the *Moonbeams*' reversed turning counts «ལོ་གྲངས» without a number

Question 301, in part.

The *Moonbeams* (kd.3:p489-490) words the count as WB's notes do: a man counts down (སྦུབ, sbub) and a woman up (བཀེན, bken) «ལོ་གྲངས་བགྲང», "counts the number of the years", from a starting year chosen by the element, and takes where it falls as the commentary says; its example gives the starting years and no number. Its illness reckoning counts «དུ་ལོན» (du lon, years lived) down from named years (printed pp. 510–511). Neither text says whether the one reckoned's birth year is counted as one. The *Moonbeams*' own thirty (kd.3:p498-500, claim thirty-dz-eight) counts four, seven and eleven from the base year itself, without the person's age: that is the way the notes call "elsewhere" («ལོག་མིན་མི་སྐོར་བར»). So the age count is WB's and the notes', and the question of inclusive or lived years is not answered by any text found.
