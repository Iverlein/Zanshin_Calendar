# The Guru's deed on the tenth of each of the twelve months, after 'Jigs med gling pa

'Jigs med gling pa's Prayer on the Benefits of the Tenth Day (ཚེས་བཅུའི་ཕན་ཡོན་གསོལ་འདེབས, tshes bcu'i phan yon gsol 'debs; jgl.8:p739-742) gives one deed of Padmasambhava for the tenth day (ཚེས་བཅུ, tshes bcu) of each of the twelve months. It numbers only the first, the sixth Hor month (ཧོར་ཟླ་དྲུག་པ, hor zla drug pa); the other eleven are named by their lunar mansion, in the order below, and the prayer does not use animal or season names.

| Month as the prayer names it | Deed remembered on its tenth |
| --- | --- |
| the sixth Hor month, at sunrise | birth in a lotus bud at Dhanakośa |
| Rgyal (རྒྱལ་ཟླ, rgyal zla) | invited by Indrabodhi, enthroned as prince |
| Mchu (མཆུ་ཟླ, mchu zla) | gives up the kingdom; yogic conduct in the Cool Grove, the ḍākinīs brought under his power |
| Dbo (དབོ་ཟླ, dbo zla) | goes forth under the master Prabhāhasti |
| Nag (ནག་ཟླ, nag zla) | burned alive by the king of Zahor, turns the fire into water |
| Sa (ས་ཟླ, sa zla) | burned with his consort by the king of Uḍḍiyāna, brings the hostile to faith |
| Snron (སྣྲོན་ཟླ, snron zla) | destroys hostile non-Buddhists in a southern border kingdom with thunderbolts |
| Gro bzhin (གྲོ་བཞིན་ཟླ་བ, gro bzhin zla ba) | thrown into the river in a copper vessel by the king of Copper Island; rises into the sky, the river turns back, the king takes refuge |
| Khrums (ཁྲུམས་ཟླ, khrums zla) | given poison by non-Buddhists, unharmed, his radiance greater |
| Tha skar (ཐ་སྐར་ཟླ, tha skar zla) | at Yang le shod, as Vajrakumāra binds the gods and demons of Nepal and Tibet |
| Smin drug (སྨིན་དྲུག་ཟླ་བ, smin drug zla ba) | comes to central Tibet, binds the gods and rākṣasas on Has po ri |
| Mgo (མགོ་ཟླ, mgo zla) | at Mchims phu prepares his leaving, fills Tibet with treasures and gives their charge to the lords of the ground |

The Hor number of each mansion month is not given by the prayer, and no second list was read, so where sources differ on a month's deed is not established. The monkey month (སྤྲེལ་ཟླ, sprel zla) is not named in the prayer. Tradition: Nyingma (Klong chen snying thig); written for a monk of Bsam yas, recited by anyone.
