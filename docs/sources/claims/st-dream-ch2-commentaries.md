# How the commentaries take ཡང, དག and the dream in ST ch. 2's line on the boy

Ch. 2's line «ལུས་ཡང་རྨི་ལམ་དག་ན་སྐྱེས་པ་མཐོང་» (st:p21), looked up in the BDRC etexts by an exact-phrase search (2026-11-10: «ལུས་ཡང་རྨི་ལམ་དག་ན་སྐྱེས་པ་མཐོང» 19 hits, «རྨི་ལམ་དག་ན» alone 106, mostly other works) and each hit's volume fetched and read around the line. Of the 19 volumes, 13 were fetched with text; six (I4723, I2PD18140, I2PD16002, I3CN6530, I4PD4564, I1KG2751) came back empty and are not counted.

Editions of the root text (five volumes; the line is the same word for word, no variant of ཡང or དག): Gansu (I4PD4022; དག), Serthang etext (I3CN5038), Yu thog's Explanatory Tantra (I1KG25029), a Chinese-annotated edition (I4839), the Drathang (I1PD464).

Commentaries that gloss the line (four works in six volumes):

1. Byang pa Rnam rgyal grags pa bzang po's *Bdud rtsi'i chu rgyun* on the Explanatory Tantra (I4706 and I1PD380, two editions): «ལུས་ཡང་ཞིང་རྨི་ལམ་དག་ན་སྐྱེས་པ་ཡང་དང་ཡང་དུ་མཐོང་བ» — the body is light, and in her dreams she sees a man again and again. ཡང is "light" (it takes the body as light, ལུས་ཡང་ཞིང), དག is not glossed as a word, and the dream is of a man, many times. (I1PD380's text has the variant «རྨི་ལམ་དགུ་ན», a slip.)
2. Bsod nams ye shes rgyal mtshan's *Dri med kun gsal* (I4707): «མའི་ལུས་གཡས་མཐོ་ཞིང་མའི་རྨི་ལམ་དུའང་སྐྱེས་པ་ཡང་ཡང་མཐོང་བ་དང་མ་དེ་ཉིད་ལུས་ཡང་ཞིང་…» — the mother sees men again and again in dreams, and her body is light. The dream is repeated; དག is "again and again".
3. The great commentary on the Four Tantras (I5618, an unsigned etext; its Instruction-Tantra volume I5622 quotes the line only): «ལུས་ཡང་རང་བཞིན་གྱིས་ཡང་བ་དང་། རྨི་ལམ་དག་ན་ཡང་སྐྱེས་པ་མང་དུ་མཐོང་ཞིང་འཁྲིག་པ་འདོད་པའི་ཆགས་པ་མི་སྐྱེ་བ» — the body is light of itself, in her dreams she also sees many men, and no desire for union rises. It takes དག as "many" (མང་དུ) and adds that she has no desire for union, the reverse of the girl's signs, where she longs to meet men and delights in song, dance and ornament.
4. An 'Bum chung / Thum nag *gsal sgron* (I1PD409): «ལུས་ཡང་རྨི་ལམ་དུ་སྐྱེས་པ་མང་པོ་མཐོང་བ» — in her dreams she sees many men. Again "many".

The Drikung dka' mchan (I1KG24731, MW1KG24729) quotes the line with the mother (མའི) added before «དཀུ་གཡས་མཐོ» and no gloss of ཡང or དག.

The count: four commentaries, in six volumes, take the dream as of men seen many times (many, or again and again); none reads དག as "clear" or restricts the verdict to a clear dream; two (1 and 2) take ཡང as "light" in the body's clause, one (3) the same plus "also" in the dream's clause (རྨི་ལམ་དག་ན་ཡང), and one (4) leaves it unglossed. The Blue Beryl and the Moonbeams are not among the 19 hits; their etexts did not carry the line.

So q823 is answered in sense: དག is the plural of the dream or its repetition, not "clear"; q789's ཡང as "light" is the reading of two commentaries and not contradicted by the others; q818: the woman sees a man (many men, again and again), the dream's clarity is never the point. Facts only; the commentaries' wording is not ours.
