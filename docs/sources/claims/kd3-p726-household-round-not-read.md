# KD vol. 3, p. 726: the household round of seven members, what the lent scan shows and what it does not

KD's sheet of the household round for seven members (vol. 3, p. 726, leaf 745) was looked at on the lent scan at 1002 px, and it shows the fold of the thirty-five pebbles but not which are left over, and no member brings a speckled pebble. What the page does show: seven member-columns of five numbered cells each (four aspects and the fallen mewa, thirty-five cells), the pebbles drawn white (empty circle, singly, two or a cluster of three) and black (filled, one or two), one white with one black as a pair; no pebble is drawn speckled, and the colour words of the etext's last row («དཀར», «དམར», «མཐིང», «སེར») belong to the fallen mewas, not to pebbles. A second table of four rows under the first has three boxes marked first, second, third and five numbered pebble cells in each, eight to a row; read at 3 times, the rows hold 1 to 8, 35 down to 28 (the people's), 20 up to 27 (the house's) and 16 down to 9 (the enemy's), which fits the numbers the etext keeps; 17, 18 and 19 are not in the table. So the page folds the thirty-five in four runs of eight with three over, not by halves; which of the three numbers is the leftover and what the etext's «ཕྲེང་འདི་དག་དོར་བར་བྱ», "these strings are to be cast away", over the top row's right-hand cells casts away is not read, and the digits of the upper table's cells are not told apart one by one at this size.
