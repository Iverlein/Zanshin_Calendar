# The living household's birth mewa many on one colour

WB, vol. 2, p. 52: when many of the living were born on one birth mewa (སྐྱེས་སྨེ, skyes sme), a cause in the forefathers and a remedy (input: the count of living members per birth mewa).

| Many born on | Reading | Remedy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| white (དཀར་པོ) | the gods' punishment threatens; beware of feud (ཁོན, khon) and impurity | the vase ablution, "fleeing and chasing" (འབྲོས་སྙེག, 'bros snyeg) |
| black (ནག་པོ, 2) | a forefather's corpse left abroad; many die by the blade and colic | turn the eating demon's face away |
| four green | earth lords (ས་བདག, sa bdag) harmed the forefathers or dwell in field and house | the nāga rite (ཀླུ་ཆོག, klu chog), the northern release (བྱང་འགྲོལ, byang 'grol) |
| five yellow | the forefathers quarrelled with Buddhist and Bon priests, or the protectors left | offerings and turning back (བཟློག་པ, bzlog pa) |
| red (དམར་པོ) | forefathers died by weapons or lie between rock and water | changing body and mind (ལུས་བསྒྱུར་བློ་བསྒྱུར, lus bsgyur blo bsgyur) |

"White" and "red" are not numbered here: which of 1, 6, 8 white and 7, 9 red is meant is not said; the household's mewa on pp. 54–55 ([dead-mewa-copper-vessel](dead-mewa-copper-vessel.md)) numbers them. "Many" is not given as a count.
