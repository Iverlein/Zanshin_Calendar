# The mansions of the sounding under other names: eight of ten identified

The sounding's examination of the dead's mansion (WB vol. 2, p. 92) names the mansions under aliases that WB's own numbered lists of the 28 mansions (vol. 2, pp. 337 and 417–418, etext only) show beside the usual names: *rta chen* (རྟ་ཆེན, "great horse") is Maghā, *mchu* (མཆུ), number 9; *rta chung* (རྟ་ཆུང, "small horse") is Pūrvaphalgunī, *gre* (གྲེ), number 10; *lde'u* (ལྡེའུ) is Jyeṣṭhā, *snron* (སྒྲོན), number 17 (the dictionaries give *lde'u* "the star *snron*"); *bya spo* (བྱ་སྦོ) is number 21, *byi bzhin* (བྱི་བཞིན, Abhijit, also *gro bzhin*); *lha mtshams* (ལྷ་མཚམས) is 16 (Anurādhā), *chu smad* (ཆུ་སྨད) 20, *khrums stod* (ཁྲུམས་སྟོད) 24, *khrums smad* (ཁྲུམས་སྨད) 25, *bra nye* (བྲ་ཉེ) 1 and *mgo* (མགོ) 4. Not found with a number: *bya 'u* (བྱ་འུ), which stands between 10 and 16 in the verse's order (so one of 11–15), and *nor phyugs* (ནོར་ཕྱུགས, "cattle"), which stands between 20 and 24 (21–23). Mipham's note counts fourteen mansions "Kṛttikā and the rest" for the sounding (and with the clan's enemy mansion and the four of earth and water on Aśleṣā, *skag*, nineteen). Answered for eight of the ten; *bya 'u* and *nor phyugs* narrowed to a range.

Narrowed 2026-11-14 (question 316): the dictionaries give *bya 'u* as the star nag pa (Citrā, 13), which fits the slot 11–15; they give *bya sbo* as gro bzhin where this claim has byi bzhin, which would move *nor phyugs* to 22 or 23.
