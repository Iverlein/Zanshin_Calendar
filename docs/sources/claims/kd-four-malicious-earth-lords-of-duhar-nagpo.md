# Duhar Nagpo's four malicious earth lords: sky dog, hal khyi, Seshar and the bride's sister

Nor brang O rgyan's history of the Black reckoning (the work of kd.1:p643-646) tells of four malicious earth lords (ས་བདག་གདུག་པ་ཅན་བཞི) that the teacher Duhar Nagpo (དུ་ཧར་ནག་པོ) tamed on his journey to China, Mon and the north: a harmful spirit, Drang rkun nag po, and a nāginī, Kha sprin mi sdug, were laid in one bed, and the four demon (བདུད) children of the pair are: the great, the god-demon Black sky dog (གནམ་ཁྱི་ནག་པོ), the earth lord who sends misfortune upon life (སྲིད); the middle, Mara of death, the Black hal khyi (ཧལ་ཁྱི་ནག་པོ), who sends it upon corpses (རོ); the smallest, Mara of the afflictions, Red Seshar (སེ་ཤར་དམར་པོ), who sends it upon corpses and war (རོ་དང་དམག); and a sister, Mara of the aggregates and desire, who sends it upon the bride and the house (ཟེ་ཁྱིམ་བག་མ). He then sought the four helping gods of the directions (tiger east, dragon south, bird west, tortoise north) and made the *Snying po bsdus pa* and the *Rin chen gsal ba'i sgron me*, whose subject falls under six heads including the keg, the bride, the earth examination and the enemy-killing reckonings.

For the earth lords of WB's lists this gives an origin the pool did not hold: the sky dog, the hal khyi and Seshar are kin of one lineage of demons, and the bride and the house stand with them. It is a late (modern) retelling of the Clear Lamp's tradition and states no date, direction or weight for any of them; it is not a rule for D4. WB's own list names the same three (year-month-earth-lords.md).
