# The root tantra's remedy of bad dreams is the wrathful king's mantra; it does not name the Ender of Obstructors (RD370)

For a bad dream the root tantra's dream chapter does not give the Sngags rim chen mo's remedies: its p404-405 gives, in the setting of success in mantra, the mantra of the great wrathful king with six arms and six faces, who "removes bad dreams" when recited always, and says that for mantra that do not succeed sleep and drowsiness are to be examined. It does not name the Ender of Obstructors (བགེགས་མཐར་བྱེད, bgegs mthar byed), whom the Sngags rim chen mo gives from Mar me mdzad bzang po (snr:p370), nor the amṛta whirling mantra of the Garland; so the remedies there come from the other sources the Sngags rim chen mo names, and the root tantra is quoted there for the four watches only.
