# RST: whose apparition each figure of a dream is

RST names the spirit behind the figure: a lone white man, a white bird soaring, a snow mountain: the gods (ལྷ, lha); an old ruined temple, a fox, a small child: the 'gong po spirits; snakes, frogs, a blue-green girl, meadows: the nāgas (ཀླུ, klu); rocks, plants, horsemen and soldiers: the btsan (བཙན, btsan); an ash-coloured wondrous child, cliffs and ravines: the the'u rang; monks, a donkey, a monkey, a cat, a horse, a dog: the rgyal po (རྒྱལ་པོ, rgyal po) spirits.
