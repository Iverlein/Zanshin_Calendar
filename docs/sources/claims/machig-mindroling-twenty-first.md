# Mindroling keeps a monthly offering for «Ma gcig» on the twenty-first

The rule-book of the Mindroling (སྨིན་གྲོལ་གླིང, smin grol gling) assembly sets, among the rites of every month, on the twenty-first day a seasonal offering (དུས་མཆོད, dus mchod) for «Ma gcig» (མ་གཅིག, ma gcig): by way of a painted scroll (རས་བྲིས, ras bris), the accumulation-offering of the Deshek Kundü (Bde gshegs kun 'dus) with the self-entry, and after it the fulfilment-offering chiefly of the great gods (mdb:p163-166, read on the scan at image 165, folio 80, on 2026-11-24). It is a monthly day, not a yearly one, and the page does not say which Ma gcig is meant: the same list keeps the fifteenth for the seasonal offering of «Yum», the Mother, and other days for masters of the house. Whether «Ma gcig» here is Machig Labdrön (མ་གཅིག་ལབ་སྒྲོན, ma gcig lab sgron) is open. It is the only day of practice found for any of the three persons of RD411.
