# The third examination's inputs: the dead's year, the death year's trigram and mewa, day, weekday, mansion, hour

The third examination of the dead reads seven inputs (WB vol. 2, pp. 58–68): the dead's own year animal (whom the executioner follows, where it hides), the trigram and the mewa (which spirit carried the dead off), and the day's animal, the weekday, the mansion and the double hour of death. Mipham's note (p. 503) says the trigram and mewa are those of the year of death («ཤི་ལོའི་སྤར་སྨེ»), the executioner's hiding follows the year the dead was born in, and the day, hour, weekday and mansion results are clear in the root text. Whether "the trigram of the year of death" is the year's own trigram or the dead's descended trigram in that year is not said here.
