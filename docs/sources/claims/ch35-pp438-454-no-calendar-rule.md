# WB ch. 35 pp. 438–454: no calendar rule stands in these pages

Read on pp. 438–454 (reprint img. 444–460), the rest of chapter 35 holds no calendar content: no day, month or year rule, no list of lunar days, planets or mansions, no calculation, no figure of the Phugpa reckoning or of the 旧暦. What stands there is the end of the crafts (scripts, stūpas, music, dance, trades), the field of healing with the Four Tantras' counts of chapters and diseases, the inner science with the history of the canon in Tibet and the Madhyamaka translations, and, on pp. 447–453, the account of star and black reckoning as a field (its rank, its sources, the order of study, the rites of the earth lords, the vessel and the pledges); the chapter closes on p. 454. The only dates are those the chapter itself marks for grammar (p. 432, already read) and the passing mention of the days to beware of for the sciences in ch. 34. Machine reading with a witness; a search for words of the calendar (ཚེས, ཟླ་ངོ, ཉི་མ, སྐར་མ, ཞག) over the Yigdzin-1 text of pp. 438–454 found only the places above.
