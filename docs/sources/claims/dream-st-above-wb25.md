# Where the Four Tantras and WB ch. 25 both read a sick dreamer's dream, the Four Tantras' row is shown

The only dream the Four Tantras and WB ch. 25 both read for a sick person is the confused, rough dream as a sign of the illness (ST ch. 15, 23 and 27; WB ch. 25 on an ox-day illness and on the mewa five yellow). On medicine the Four Tantras stand above WB (SPEC §8.1), so ST's rows come first and WB ch. 25's follow, marked as under it. The two other WB ch. 25 dream lines (the ritualist bitten by a dog, the shapes of bad dreams poured into a skull) have no Four Tantras counterpart and stand alone. See dream-sick-confused-dreams.
