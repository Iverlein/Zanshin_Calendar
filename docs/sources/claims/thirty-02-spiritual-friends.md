# The thirty: the second point, the white snow lioness, for spiritual friends

The second of the thirty starting points (སྐོར་འགོ, skor 'go), from the mouth of the white snow lioness (གངས་སེང་དཀར་མོ, gangs seng dkar mo), is read for spiritual friends (དགེ་བཤེས, dge bshes). Its eight pebbles are laid as for every point (claims thirty-count and thirty-eight-fighters), each white or black, and read so:

| Pebble | Asks | White | Black | Remedy for black |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Whether the lion throne has fallen | The lion throne does not move; in the long run people and the Dharma increase | The lion seat falls from its height; a great rise and decline (དར་གུད, dar gud) in the father's and uncles' place; only somewhat good in one's own place; in the father's and uncles' place one has left one's country and wanders abroad | — |
| 2 | Whether the silk curtain (དར་ཡོལ, dar yol) leaks | The curtain does not leak; reputation (ཁ་རྗེ, kha rje) grows | The demons' deceit and obstacles come | The hundred-and-four offering (བརྒྱ་བཞི, brgya bzhi), the *sdong rgyan* rite (སྡོང་རྒྱན, sdong rgyan; not identified), striving at the white side |
| 3 | Whether the golden and turquoise maṇḍala is broken | Reputation and merit grow; the aims in mind are achieved | The turquoise maṇḍala is broken; reputation falls, blame comes, disciples rise as enemies, aims are not achieved | Strive at the virtue of the white side |
| 4 | Whether the string of the pearl rosary is cut | No obstacle to life | The string of the precious rosary is cut; obstacles to life, swept away by circumstances | As remedy rite (གཏོ, gto), the nine turnings back of Ma traṃ (མ་ཏྲཾ་དགུ་བཟློག, ma traM dgu bzlog) |
| 5 | Whether the protecting mountain has fallen | No turning from the Dharma, no broken vows | The protecting mountain falls; even white acts go wrong | Gather the accumulations, give, strive at the white side |
| 6 | Whether the vase of life is lost from the hand | No obstacle to life | The vase of life is lost from the hand; obstacles to life | The long-life rite (ཚེ་ཆོག, tshe chog), ransoming from death (འཆི་བསླུ, 'chi bslu) and long-life practice (ཚེ་སྒྲུབ, tshe sgrub) |
| 7 | Whether the messengers' path between gods and men is cut | The war god (དགྲ་ལྷ, dgra lha) and the local god lead | An obstacle between gods and men: the local god is displeased, the god-ogres rise | Fulfilment and confession (བསྐང་བཤགས, bskang bshags) and offering to the gods (ལྷ་གསོལ, lha gsol) |
| 8 | Whether Dharma practice is left behind to the residue of karma | Good former karma; Dharma practice and virtue grow strong | Dharma practice is left to the residue of karma; no mind for virtue, sin is practised; obstacles to life and Dharma | Strive at white acts |

Whose year is reckoned: the person's own (the notes, vol. 2, p. 533: «གཞན་རྣམས་སོ་སོའི་ལོ་རྟ»).

Which of the eight fighting pebbles is which check is not stated; the order of p. 453 is the likely one (claim thirty-board-groups; question 259).
