# A middling life reckoning does not bar the match by reply 111's letter

Narrowed 2026-11-01. Reply 111 (ys:i467-468) says the bar is on joining two lives that are «ངན་ཐ་ཆད», utterly bad, each gathered into one verdict pebble by its white and black, and no more ([life-reckoning-one-verdict](life-reckoning-one-verdict.md), [ngan-tha-chad-bottom-class](ngan-tha-chad-bottom-class.md)). WB's own grades of a verdict are three, good, middling (even), bad (p. 349), and the notes' nine of the match are good, bad and middling each in three (p. 526); the bar's words name the lowest class only, so a life judged middling is not "utterly bad" and by the letter of the reply is not barred. Whether a plain "bad" (not utterly bad) bars is the reply's silence; no text found says so, and none says a middling life reckoning bars. A reading from the reply's wording, not a stated rule.
