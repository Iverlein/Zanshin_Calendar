# WB p. 314: Bharaṇī's minor star is «ཕུང་རྒྱབ་ཅན», with a final བ; vol. 1's list has «ཕུང་སྐྱབས་ཅན»

Bharaṇī's minor star is «ཕུང་རྒྱབ་ཅན» on p. 314, never «ཕུང་རྒྱང་ཅན»: counted over the two 1996 etext volumes of WB (the print's typing), «ཕུང་རྒྱབ» stands once and «ཕུང་རྒྱང» nowhere, and the ང of the Gser thang reprint has no second place in the book. WB's own list of the mansions' minor stars in vol. 1 (p. 258, img. 268 of the 1996 scan, read at 3-4x) names the same star «ཕུང་སྐྱབས་ཅན» (phung skyabs can), with the final བ (and ས) plain; the name of vol. 2's verse, with རྒྱ for སྐྱ, is thus a variant of that spelling and not a different star, and every witness that has a final at all has བ (the 1996 print, the Zhol print, the Sakya Centre etext, SY). What no text tells is which spelling the author meant: «སྐྱབས» (shelter) in vol. 1 against «རྒྱབ» (back) in vol. 2.
