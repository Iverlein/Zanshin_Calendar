# WB ch. 35 sends the history of star reckoning back to chapter 1

On p. 433 the verse says that the origins of star reckoning were told at length in the first chapter of the book; for the rest it names Sa pan, Thar lo Nyi ma rgyal mtshan, and Shong ston's translation, and gives only the short account here. A reader seeking the history of the reckoning therefore goes to chapter 1, not to this chapter. Read on the scan (RD84, 2026-10-31).
