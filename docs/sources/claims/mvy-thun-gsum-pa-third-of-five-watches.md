# The Mahāvyutpatti's thun gsum pa is the third of five watches of the night (RD322)

The Mahāvyutpatti (the Sanskrit–Tibetan list in Steinert's no. 21, paged in no. 63 at p. 426) does not give ཐུན་གསུམ་པ (thun gsum pa) as a name of the night: its list of the night's times runs in five watches, and the thun gsum pa, joined by "or" to a second Tibetan word, ལྔ་ཚིགས (lnga tshigs), is the third watch, tṛtīyapraharaḥ, between ཐུན་གཉིས་པ (the second) and ཐུན་བཞི་པ (the fourth). Negi's dictionary cites the same entry as ma.vyu. 8242 (the list's own number is not in Steinert's file).
