# The Svapnohana's colophon on three prints: translator only; the work before it by Dīpaṃkaraśrījñāna and Rin chen bzang po

The colophon of the Svapnohana reads alike on the Derge etext, the Peking print (img. 318 line 6) and the Narthang manuscript (img. 287 line 3): "end of the examination of dreams; made by the eastern Indian paṇḍita Vibhūticandra as his own translation" (rang 'gyur du mdzad pa'o). It names no author, no co-translator and no reviser, and no print adds a name; the BDRC colophon transcription is the same. Peking's catalogue entry (Otani 2621) and the later surveys that call Vibhūticandra the composer therefore do not rest on any wording of the colophon in the three prints read; the claim svo-authorship-witnesses stands, with this check added. The colophon of the work before it (Deceiving Death, 'chi ba bslu ba) is read on the Peking print at the end of img. 314 line 8: translated and revised by the Indian abbot Dīpaṃkaraśrījñāna and the lotsāba Rin chen bzang po, as BDRC's transcription of the Derge colophon gives it; the Narthang manuscript's end of the same colophon (img. 284 line 1, right) agrees in its shapes, not read syllable by syllable. State: scan, moderate for the Peking line.
