# RRT: no watch of the night and no day count

The 'Bri gung extended method (RRT pp. 186–195, etext) gives no watch of the night, no hour and no lunar day for a dream's fruit; it asks only which deity is practised and whether the sign agrees with the practice. Its only dates are in the added accounts: a vision at the dawn of the twenty-seventh, fever the evening of the twenty-eighth, death on the second of the next month (pp. 194–195), told as a case, not stated as a rule.
