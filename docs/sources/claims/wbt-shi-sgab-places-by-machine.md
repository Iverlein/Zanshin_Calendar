# The run of deaths' places on the Taikhang located, the stack not yet read by eye

On the Taikhang (I5878) Yigdzin-1 reads «ཤི་སྒབ» (with ག) at every place, which locates them (2026-11-03): p. 105, l. 15 on img. 161, p. 106, l. 2 and l. 15 on img. 162, p. 108, l. 11 on img. 165 and p. 108, ll. 24 and 26 on img. 166 (the second as «བཤད་པའི་ཤི་སྒབ་བཅད»). The machine is no evidence for ག against ཀ and no stack was read by eye in this reading, so question 594 stays open on a sharp print.
