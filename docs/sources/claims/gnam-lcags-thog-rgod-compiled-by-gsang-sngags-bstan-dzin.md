# The torma-repelling manual Gnam lcags thog rgod was compiled by Gnam lcags Gsang sngags bstan 'dzin in 2010

The torma-repelling manual of tsl.28:i569, «དཔལ་ཆེན་དྲེགས་པ་ཟིལ་གནོན་ལས། གཏོར་བཟློག་གི་ལས་རིམ་འགྲིགས་ཆགས་སུ་བཀོད་པ་གནམ་ལྕགས་ཐོག་རྒོད», is no treasure text of Rtsa gsum gling pa but a modern compilation, and it names its compiler. Its colophon (etext of I1PD108816, pp. 647-649, read in the etext only, 2026-11-21) says that it is the one called Gsang sngags bstan 'dzin, «སྒོ་ཆེན་རྒྱལ་བའི་སྐྱེས་མིང་འབོད་པ་གསང་སྔགས་བསྟན་འཛིན» (named for a Sgo chen rgyal ba; the same man as the writer of tsl.27:i591, aged 51 in the water-sheep year of the 17th cycle), who set it out on the lines of a rite outline (ཆོག་ཁྲིགས, chog khrigs) by Prajñā-dhyāna-kṛpa (པྲཛྙཱ་ཛྷཱ་ན་ཀྲི་པ; the etext writes his name twice, the second time པྲཛྙཱ་ཛྙཱན་ཀྲི་པ) and from the Gnam lcags liturgy of Vajrapāṇi Dregs zil (ཕྱག་རྡོར་དྲེགས་ཟིལ་གྱི་ལས་བྱང་གནམ་ལྕགས་གོ་ཆ) and the lower part of the work of the earlier Gnam lcags master, "with the scribe called Maṇi and a pair of supreme vidyādharas as helpers". The compiler's concluding line is dated «རབ་བྱུང་བཅུ་བདུན་པ་ལྕགས་སྟག་སྣོན་ཟླའི་དམར་ཕྱོགས་དགའ་བ་གསུམ་པར», the iron-tiger year of the 17th cycle (2010 by the cycle's count), «dmar phyogs» and «dga' ba gsum» as the etext prints them.

The «དགུ་གསུམ» of the page-569 sentence is not explained anywhere in the volume (the only «དགུ་གསུམ» of the volume's etext, 379623); BDRC's catalogue lists the manual among the Rtsa gsum gling pa volume's pieces as pp. 575-658 of a different pagination.
