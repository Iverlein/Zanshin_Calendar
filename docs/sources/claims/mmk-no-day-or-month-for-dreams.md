# The root tantra's dream chapter gives no lunar day or month for a dream, only the half-month, a good day and the watches (RD370)

The root tantra's dream chapter gives no day of the month, lunar day or month of the year as the time at which a dream bears fruit or is to be read: in the 15th chapter of the Derge (pages [p396]-[p405] of the etext) the times named are the half-month and "a good day" for the rite, and the four watches of the night for the dream itself. A search of the whole etext of Toh 543 for the word for dream (རྨི་ལམ) found it on pages [p252], [p318], [p371], [p374], [p396]-[p403], [p405], [p416], [p422], [p423], [p461], [p473], [p482], [p485], [p487], [p574], [p575], [p599] and [p629]; those outside the 15th chapter were read in the line or two round the word and are about other rites (a dream that shows whether a rite will succeed, the dream bestowed by a mantra, the bad dream removed), none counts the watches of the night. The words «མཚན་མོའི་ཐུན» also stand at [p699] and following, where they give the watches of the night for the omens of an earthquake (not read further), and «ཐུན་ནི་ཐ་མར་ཉལ། ཐོ་རངས་ལྡང་བ» at [p656] (not read). This is a reading of the Tibetan etext only; the Sanskrit's rest was not searched.
