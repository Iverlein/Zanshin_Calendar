# The root tantra's dream chapter gives no lunar day or month for a dream, only the half-month, a good day and the watches (RD370)

The root tantra's dream chapter gives no day of the month, lunar day or month of the year as the time at which a dream bears fruit or is to be read: in the 15th chapter of the Derge (pages [p396]-[p405] of the etext) the times named are the half-month and "a good day" for the rite, and the four watches of the night for the dream itself. A search of the whole etext of Toh 543 for the word for dream (རྨི་ལམ) found it on pages [p252], [p318], [p371], [p374], [p396]-[p403], [p405], [p416], [p422], [p423], [p461], [p473], [p482], [p485], [p487], [p574], [p575], [p599] and [p629]; those outside the 15th chapter were read in the line or two round the word and are about other rites (a dream that shows whether a rite will succeed, the dream bestowed by a mantra, the bad dream removed), none counts the watches of the night. The watches of the night (the word «ཐུན») stand, outside the dream chapter, only in the omens and rites listed in mmk-watches-elsewhere-omens-rites-births (corrected by RD372: the earlier text here put them at [p699] and [p656], which have no such word). The pages outside the 15th chapter were read whole by RD372 (mmk-other-dream-pages-none-counts-the-watches), and the Sanskrit's dream chapter by the OCR text layer.
