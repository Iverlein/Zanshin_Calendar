# Fulfilment and confession of tantric commitments on the full moon, the new moon and the eighth

Lo chen Dharma shrI, on mending the tantric commitments (dpn:i334, etext only): the three fulfilment-and-confessions (བསྐང་བཤགས, bskang bshags) of the Na rak dong sprugs (ན་རཀ་དོང་སྤྲུགས, na rak dong sprugs) instruction, outer, inner and secret, done on the full moon, the new moon and the eighth day, restore every breach of commitment up to then; where that cannot be done, the short manual of prostrations kept as a daily practice cleanses them too.

- Days: the full moon, the new moon, the eighth (the second copy of the sentence, etext p. 604, adds "and the like").
- Practice: fulfilment and confession.
- For whom: yogins who have entered the three yogas, that is, holders of tantric commitments, lay or ordained; not householders as such.
- Tradition: Nyingma.
