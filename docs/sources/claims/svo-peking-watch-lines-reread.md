# The Peking's second watch ends «བརྒྱད་པ་ལ» and its third «གསུམ་གྱི་བར་དུ»; the fourth watch's line is absent (RD327)

On the Peking print (I1KG13197 img. 315 line 2) the second watch's first segment reads «བརྒྱད་པ་ལ» with པ (eye at 1× and 2×, and three Yigdzin-1 runs), not «བརྒྱད་བར་ལ» as RD233 to RD235 had it; this agrees with the Dpe bsdur ma's entry (Peking and Narthang «པ»). The third watch's segment reads «ཐུན་གསུམ་ཟླ་བ་གསུམ་གྱི་བར་དུ» in full (བ, ར, དུ read, three machine runs the same), so the short line «ཐུན་གཉིས་གསུམ་གྱི་བར» is the Co ne's alone; the Dpe bsdur ma has no entry for this «དུ», so its silence does not show that the prints agree with the base. The fourth watch's line is not in the Peking. The Co ne's «པར» (machine) is not the Peking's.
