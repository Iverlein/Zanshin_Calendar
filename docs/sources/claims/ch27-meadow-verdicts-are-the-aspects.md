# Ch. 27: the meadow's five verdicts are the five aspects', by WB's results and Sprugtön's wording

For question 216 the text of ch. 27 itself leans to one verdict for each of the five aspects. After the twenty-five pebbles of a group WB lays «ཁ་དམར་རྣམ་པ་ལྔ» / «ཁ་དམར་ལྔ», five verdicts (p. 22), and at the close tells the results by the aspects: for the life force the adversities of life, for the body illness, for the power wealth, for the luck merit, for the mewa gods and demons, "as the white and black verdicts fall"; a verdict has its domain, and the domains are the five aspects'. Sprugtön's reckoning says the same of its five: «སྲོག་ལུས་དབང་ཀླུང་བླ་ལྔ་པོར། དཀར་ནག་བགྲངས་པའི་ཁ་དམར་རེ» (p. 23), "to each of the five, life force, body, power, luck and la, one verdict of the counted white and black".

Mipham's notes (pp. 533-534) call a group "the life force, body, power, luck and mewa, five, of the five dogs, the fire dog, the wood dog and the rest": the five dogs are named for the five aspects' elements, so the group of five is the sky year's five aspects spoken as one animal in five elements, and the "five of one animal" of the notes and KD's column of one year's five forces (ch27-meadow-verdict-per-column) may be one thing. This is our reading of the notes' wording, not a sentence of them. Then the five verdicts of a group are the five aspects', not five years'; which axis (the sky year's five or the reckoned person's five) a verdict sums is not said. The branch "one for each year over the five aspects" has no support in any witness.
