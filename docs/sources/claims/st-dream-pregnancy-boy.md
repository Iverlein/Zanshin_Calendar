# The mother's dream of a man among the signs of a boy

How a woman's dream in the last month of pregnancy bears on the child's sex, in the Four Tantras.

The Explanatory Tantra (བཤད་རྒྱུད, bshad rgyud), ch. 2, on the formation of the body, closes its account of the months in the womb with the signs of the child's sex at the ninth month. A boy is shown by several signs together: she lies on her right side with the right hip high, her body is light (ལུས་ཡང, lus yang), the right breast gives milk first, she wishes to meet men and delights in song, dance and ornaments, and in her dreams (རྨི་ལམ, rmi lam) she sees a man. The reverse of these is the sign of a girl; a mixture of them, a child who is neither (མ་ནིང, ma ning); a low middle with both sides high, twins. The dream is one sign among the rest, not a rule of its own, and no remedy is given: it is a sign of the child's sex, not of an illness. The first of the two doubtful words, ཡང (yang), is settled by RD227 as "light" (the dictionary has ལུས་ཡང, "the body is light"; the Drathang print reads the line the same). One word stays open: དག (dag) between "dreams" and "if" is the plural marker or "clear"; the dream is a sign of a boy on either, a clear dream only on the second (st:p21). The chapter is the second by its colophon, p. 21 of the print (img. 34).
