# Ch. 27: the black nine-eye's sky years follow an element rule, so «ས་ཡོས» stands as printed

In the flesh's secret reckoning (WB vol. 2, p. 16) the sky years that put a person's year into the black nine-eye (དགུ་མིག་ནག་པོ, dgu mig nag po) are printed: the wood rat, the wood ox, «མི་སྟག», «ས་ཡོས», the earth snake, the iron horse, the iron sheep, the water monkey, the water bird, the wood dog, the wood pig. On the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5029 img. 22, at 3 times) the third is «མི་སྟག» with the vowel i, in «མི་སྟག་ཤར་ན་ལྕགས་སྟག་ཚུད»; the fourth was seen only in the running line, «ས་ཡོས་ཤར་དུས་ཆུ་བྱ་ཚུད», its first letter not enlarged.

A check of our own, not a word of the text: in ten of the eleven lines the sky year's element overcomes the element of the person's year the line names (wood the earth: rat, ox and dog; fire the iron: the tiger; earth the water: the hare and the snake; iron the wood: horse and sheep; water the fire: monkey and bird); the one exception is the wood pig, whose person is a wood bird. Read so, «མི་སྟག» is the fire tiger (the vowel mi for me, fire) against the iron tiger, and «ས་ཡོས», the earth hare, against the water bird fits the same rule as printed; had it been a slip for the fire hare, the person would be an iron year, not the water bird. So the printed «ས་ཡོས» is not shown to be a slip, and the table is not the unbroken run of 1984-1995 but a list by elements, where the earth dragon of 1988 need not be missing. Which prints read «མི་སྟག» or «ས་ཡོས» otherwise was not looked at (the Zhol print and the Taikhang were not opened for these lines).
