# KD's meadow sheet: the first group reproduced, the other three not

KD's worked sheet of the meadow's ornament (vol. 3, p. 723; [kd.3:p723](../corpus/kd/v3-sheets.md#kd-3-p723), [kd.3:p723b](../corpus/kd/v3-sheets.md#kd-3-p723b)) was recomputed on 2026-10-31 to see whether its verdict row decides how a group's twenty-five pebbles are laid. Taken as the sheet's headings give them, the one reckoned and the sky year a wood mouse (life force water, body iron, power wood, luck wood, mewa 7, fire), the seventh the iron horse, the sixth the earth snake and the fourth executioner the fire hare (each the next forward from the wood mouse by six, five and three, as Mipham's notes count the fire dog's: the water dragon, the iron hare, the earth ox, [wb.2:p533-534](../corpus/wb/notes-7.md#wb-2-p533-534)), every cell was laid four ways: the column's element as the one met or as the one meeting, with the year of age's pebbles (mother three white, friend two, son one of each, enemy two black, the same one) or with ch. 27's colours (p. 3).

The first group fits one of them: the column's element meeting the person's five with the year of age's pebbles gives margins of 6, 1, −1, −1 and 5, in the order of the sheet's three white, one white, one black, one black and two white. The other three groups fit none: under that way the seventh's (the iron horse's) columns 2–4 come out white by one where the sheet draws two black, and the sixth's (the earth snake's) second column black where the sheet draws white; the other three ways fail on the first group already. So either the three later columns are not the iron horse, earth snake and fire hare (the sheet's numbers 55, 56 and 58 are not settled at the screenshot's size), or KD lays the pebbles by a rule not among the four. The sheet does not, at the resolution read, decide question 216 or the meadow's mechanics; a reading of the leaf at full size would.
