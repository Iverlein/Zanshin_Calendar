# The household round: the readings of each place by colour

The readings of the household round's places, by the colour of the pebble that falls there (WB vol. 1, pp. 385–387). The colours are those of the pebbles laid: white or black for a member's deciding pebbles, and the mewa pebbles' own colours (white, black, blue, green, yellow, red).

| Place | Colour | Reading | Remedy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| god's place (ལྷ་ས, lha sa) | black | grudges and impurity | purifying smoke and washing |
| god's place | red | impurity of the hearth | keep the hearth clean |
| god's place | blue and red | — | a rite to bring the nāgas and *btsan* together |
| god's place | green | fights and grudges | the rites of the nāgas, *btsan* and lake goddesses |
| god's place | yellow | an epidemic | the Uṣṇīṣa's repelling and the king's thread-cross |
| god's place | white | good, auspicious | thanksgiving, offering to the gods, the gods' rite |
| people's place (མི་ས, mi sa) | white | wishes fulfilled, happiness, no death among the people | — |
| people's place | black | sudden misfortune | longevity-dhāraṇī rites |
| people's place | blue | swellings and dropsy; do not dig fierce earth or stone | the earth lords' and nāgas' rites |
| people's place | green | — | the thread-cross of the eight nāgas and nine *klu bsen* |
| people's place | red | blades and weapons | the black three-headed one against enemies |
| people's place | yellow | contagious illness | the māra, *ma ṭaṃ* and blood thread-crosses |
| tail of the first two | white / black / yellow / blue / red | good news / sickness and epidemic / contagion / nāga and *gnyan* illness / blades | — |
| house's place (ཁྱིམ་ས, khyim sa) | white | auspicious, good | — |
| house's place | black | much illness in the house | the māras' *bla khrom*, three magic rites, white earth and swastikas in the four quarters, white liquid offered |
| house's place | blue or green | — | the eight nāgas' and earth lords' rite, the *Klu 'bum* |
| house's place | yellow | contagion | the *ma mo*'s repelling rite |
| house's place | red | red at the hearth | the red *gtor ma*, the *zor* thread-cross |
| house's leftover (རྡེལ་རོ, rdel ro), two pebbles | two white / white and black / one black / two black | good fortune / mixed / illness / death and loss, "a corpse in the end" | the household's trigram rites; each member's four restored and raised |
| enemy's place (དགྲ་ས, dgra sa) | white | the enemy's head high | turning back, pressing down and destroying enemies |
| enemy's place | black, red, green, yellow or blue | the enemy's head brought low: good | — |
| god's place first white, fold's and refold's remainders white | — | a gods' illness comes | — |
| god's place first black, fold's white, refold's black | — | a māra has come down on the guardian gods | prayer flags on the hill-top, offering and praise to the local god |
| people's place white | — | the people at home well | — |
| house's place white | — | no loss, grain and wealth increase | — |
| top of the enemy's place white | — | lawsuits | — |

The leftover's other readings: white, black and a third, "a bone in the butter, the fang broken"; blue, red and yellow, "water from under the hearth-stone"; green, gossip; a leftover is called bad. The wealth's place is named (one each in the god's and people's places, two in the house's) but given no readings of its own.
