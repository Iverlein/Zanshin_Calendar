# The twelve results of the seven-star enjoyment (WB p. 102)

A rule of the seven-star enjoyment (*smin bdun longs spyod*, p. 102) gives, by a remainder after division by seven, twelve numbered results said to be spoken in the *Glorious Rising-of-the-vowels, King of Tantras* (*Dpal dbyangs 'char rgyud kyi rgyal po*): (1) Pra ba si, bad; (2) Bde legs, prosperity; (3) 'Chi bdag, one dies; (4) Tri ṣṭi, virtuous; (5) Skye ba, wealth is gained; (6) 'Bar ba, killed by others; (7) Dga' ba, virtuous; (8) Spyod pa ngan, bad conduct; (9) Nges pa, enemies arise; (10) Rgyal ba, victory; (11) Su pa ta, one dies; (12) Legs so, virtuous. The machine reading had the first number as 11; on the scan (a crop of the lines) the numbers read 1 to 12 in order. If the remainder equals the day's planet, the day is excellent for burning, for power-accomplishing and for expelling, and pacifying works are to be avoided.
