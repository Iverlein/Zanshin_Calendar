# The model almanac's month head is one fixed run of phrases (12th and 1st heads read line by line)

The head of each month of the model almanac is a run of fixed phrases in the same order: the season and the bright half first, the sign of the house, the Hor and Chinese month names, "look at the elements", the month's animal "great and small", the day of the breath-taking, the "sgang", day and night lengths, the second-link word (the twelve-link chain: 'du byed in the 12th month, rnam shes in the 1st), the sun's arrival in the sign, the work that succeeds, the month's lord, the empty vase's direction, the dated avoidances, the five and nine gods' directions, excess and deficit, and the weekday line with "look at the fine" and "set up the Powerful One". Read in full on p. 155 (12th month) and p. 157 (1st month). The 12th's 14th-day entry and its "five and nine: south" agree with RD2's table (month-entries.md); the 12th's weekday line reads: dar 2, 4, 5; gud 0; skyod 1, 3; zhud 6, which is the same set RD2 read for the 4th month, so two months share one line. RD2's table of the 1st head (lord, vase west, "five west, nine east", 21st and 25th) agrees with the print.
