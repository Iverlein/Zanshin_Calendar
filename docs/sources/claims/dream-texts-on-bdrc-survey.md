# Dream-examination texts BDRC holds that the pool did not cite (RD320)

RD320 (2026-11-10) searched BDRC's full text for the title words of a dream examination («རྨི་ལམ་བརྟག་པ», «རྨི་ལམ་བརྟགས་པ», «རྨི་ལམ་བརྟག་ཐབས», «རྨི་ལྟས», «རྨི་ལམ་གྱི་ལྟས», «རྨི་ལམ་ལུང་བསྟན», «རྨི་ལམ་བཟང་ངན», «རྨི་ལམ་གྱི་རྟགས», «རྨི་ལམ་དཔྱད») and read the catalogue records of the works that carry them against the registry. What the pool already holds is left out (svo, svi, lgd, kcm, rst, rrt, dtd, rbg, skp, kdl, kbs, mdp, tdr, srm, gnc and their other prints). The title phrase gives 357 hits for «རྨི་ལམ་བརྟག་པ»; from the fourth page of hits on they are only running text, mostly the step of a rite ("then the examining of dreams") and are not listed. Sizes and places are BDRC's; "etext" says what the etext API answered.
