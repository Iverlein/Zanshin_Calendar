# The Vimalaprabhā reckons the death omen by the wind in the channels on a twelve-spoke wheel, with no dream sign

The Vimalaprabhā (Dri med 'od, དྲི་མེད་འོད) on the individual chapter reckons the death omen (འཆི་ལྟས, 'chi ltas) by the flow of the life-wind in the channels, laid on a wheel of the twelve spokes (the houses) with thirty-six months of life in the first petal: an unbroken flow on the left for a day and night, a side (or a fortnight), a month gives a year, a year and a half, three months. The unit is the month and the year of the wind's course; the year-and-day count is in the body, not in the sky. The word dream (རྨི, rmi) does not occur in the etext from p. 494 to p. 506, where the signs are listed, so the Kālacakra's death signs have no dream signs.
