# The 4th, 5th and 6th heads by eye: words agree with the machine text, line counts differ, the 6th's Cancer word is damaged in the print

Read by eye on the Gser thang reprint (RD200, 2026-11-17), line by line in halves at 1.0x: the 4th head (p. 162, img. 169) has eight printed lines, the 5th (p. 163, img. 170) six and the 6th (p. 165, img. 172) seven, where the machine passages counted seven for each of the first two. Every word agrees with the machine text and with RD2's quotations, and the weekday lines are confirmed (4th dar 2 4 5, gud 0, skyod 1 3, zhud 6; 5th dar 3 1 0, gud 2 4, skyod 6, zhud 5; 6th dar 5 3 1, zhud 2 4, gud 6, skyod 0, each of 0 to 6 once). One difference from the machine: in the 6th head's fourth line the print gives the sign Cancer as a single letter, a gap and the letters ཊའི, so the full Karkata of line 1 is the machine's restoring, not the letters of that line. The 7th head's first four lines (p. 166 foot) were read over the whole width and agree with the machine text. The 11th head (p. 154, reprint img. 161) has eight lines; lines 1 to 3 read: the month mgo with one cursive syllable after nya unsettled, the house of the bow, the clause that the tantra and the Phug school hold the year-head without error, Hor month eleven with the Chinese reckoning's spring beginning, the tiger, the breath-taking called the great snow; RD2's solstice clause on the nines stands in line 4 and the nine bad times of the 7th in line 6, as RD2 quotes them. Stacked figures: pairs of small figures over a figure with a ring, none separated into digits; the machine's 31, 58 and 9 on the 7th head's foot are not what the pairs show.
