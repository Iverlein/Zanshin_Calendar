# The fulfilment offering falls on the twenty-ninth in a Nyingma master's round and in a Drikung rule

In the sources read, the twenty-ninth of the lunar month is the day of the fulfilment offering (བསྐང་གསོ, bskang gso). Zhe chen rgyal tshab's life (zgt.13:p441) lists "the fulfilment of the twenty-ninth" among what he did every month. The Drikung Kagyu rule of Yang ri (ckg.2:p7) has, on the twenty-ninth, those who know the fulfilment and petition perform the torma of the ninth (དགུ་གཏོར, dgu gtor) and the extended enthronement, "without fail", and on the new moon the extended Cakrasaṃvara. The note in the Larung print of Longchenpa's commentary counts the twenty-ninth and the new moon among the six times of the month (lcb:p568).

- Days: the 29th (fulfilment; in the Drikung rule with the torma of the ninth); the 30th (Drikung: Cakrasaṃvara; Zhe chen rgyal tshab: offerings, and the protectors' tormas in the evening, as on the full moon and the eighth).
- For whom: a master's own round (Nyingma); a monastic assembly (Drikung Kagyu). No passage read lays the twenty-ninth's fulfilment on lay practitioners.
- Tradition: common to the two schools read, in practice; no tantra or earlier authority is quoted for the day in either passage.

Neither phrase names the Dharma protectors beside the twenty-ninth: to whom the fulfilment of the twenty-ninth is addressed is not stated in these lines. The Drikung passage rests on the etext only.
