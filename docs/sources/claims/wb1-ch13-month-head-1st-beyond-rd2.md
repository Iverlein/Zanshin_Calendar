# The 1st month's head: the festival of wonders and an unsettled weekday line beyond RD2's table

Where RD2's table of the 1st month's head stops, the print goes on: from the 1st to the 15th the head names the great festival of the Victor's showing of wonders (rgyal bas cho 'phrul bstan pa'i dus chen), which RD2's dated-entries table omits; and after "five west, nine east" and "strike each excess and deficit" comes the weekday line, whose three numerals after "dar" read 3, 0, 1 on a crop at 3x (doubtful; the first may be 2), followed by "gud" at the end of the line and, on the next line, the words with 6 and 5; that does not make the seven weekdays once each, so the line is not entered. The weekday lines and the heads of the 2nd to 10th months were not read in this reading.
