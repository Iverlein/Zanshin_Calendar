# The 1st month's head: the festival of wonders and an unsettled weekday line beyond RD2's table

Where RD2's table of the 1st month's head stops, the print goes on: from the 1st to the 15th the head names the great festival of the Victor's showing of wonders (rgyal bas cho 'phrul bstan pa'i dus chen), which RD2's dated-entries table omits; and after "five west, nine east" and "strike each excess and deficit" comes the weekday line, whose three numerals after "dar" read 3, 0, 1 on a crop at 3x (doubtful; the first may be 2), followed, on a second pass at 4x (2026-11-08), by "gud" with two figures, 2 and 4, then "skyod" 6 and "zhud" 5 on the next line; the three braces give 3, 0, 1, 2, 4, 6, 5, the seven weekdays once each, so the line is complete and enters like the others. The heads of the 2nd to 10th months are in the claims of RD201 and RD202.
