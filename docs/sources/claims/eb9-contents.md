# The volume's five texts and the dream book beside the 大雑書

Volume 9 of the Ebara collection puts five texts in this order: an almanac commentary (毎年暦考見) with an Ise almanac of 1788 appended, a 大雑書 (ōzassho, the compendium of calendar lore), the dream book 夢占吉凶秘伝書, which has the second title いろは引夢うらなひ ("dream divination by the iroha", so ordered by the first syllable of the dream's image), and 東見記. The dream book is therefore a text of its own beside the 大雑書, not a section of it. Known from the library record only: which dreams the book lists, and whether it or the 大雑書 names a date, were not seen, and the book is in no online form found (2026-11-09).
