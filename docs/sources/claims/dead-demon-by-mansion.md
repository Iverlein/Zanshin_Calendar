# The mansion of death: the cause and what is laid on the corpse

Terms as named below: the fierce spirits (བཙན, *btsan*), the water spirits, the lu (ཀླུ, *klu*), the lu-*btsan* (ཀླུ་བཙན, *klu btsan*), the lu-demons (ཀླུ་བདུད, *klu bdud*), the *gnyan* spirits (གཉན, *gnyan*), the harmful spirit of the dead (སྲི, *sri*) and the sri of small children (ཆུང་སྲི, *chung sri*), the *'gong* demon (འགོང, *'gong*), a pouch (ཁུག་མ, *khug ma*), chanted rituals (གྱེར, *gyer*), the *Twenty Thousand* and *Eight Thousand* (ཁྲི, བརྒྱད་སྟོང; *Khri*, *Brgyad stong*), Matraṃ's *ru bsdums* (རུ་བསྡུམས, *ru bsdums*), Aśvinī as the staff (དབྱུ་གུ, *dbyu gu*); «ཡས་གདོན» (*yas gdon*) and «དམེ་བོ» (*dme bo*) are not explained in the text.

The third examination of the dead (WB vol. 2, pp. 65–66) by the mansion of death, twenty-eight entries with Abhijit, each with its cause or carrier, the person at risk and what is laid on or under the corpse.
