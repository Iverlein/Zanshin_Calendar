# A dream's date set beside the day of a death, after the event

# A dream's date set beside the day of a death, after the event (RD367)

One passage sets the date of a dream beside a date, and it does so afterwards as a proof: a lama's dream of a woman in blue clothes bearing the dripping head of Pho lha ba, and, a little later, news from Tibet that Pho lha ba had died; «the date of the earlier dream agreed with the day of Pho lha ba's departure» (སྔར་གྱི་རྨི་ལམ་རྨིས་པའི་ཚེས་གྲངས་ཕོ་ལྷ་བ་གཤེགས་ཉིན་དང་འགྲིག་བྱུང་བ, dds.8:p259; the etext volume, I00KG09714, is damaged with gaps in the line and its title is a section of a collection, MW00KG09693_D010B7). It is a coincidence of dates told as a sign, the dream's fruit seen in the death of the one it showed, with no rule about what a date does to a dream: the opposite of a rule, which would be read before the event.
