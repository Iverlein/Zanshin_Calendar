# Help by virtue: the twelve ways and the texts by the year of death

The sixth examination, help by virtue (ར་མདའ, ra mda'): twelve ways of funeral, and the texts and works by the animal of the year of death (wb.2:p72). WB itself says the year table has no pure, certain source, and that whatever white work can be done is best.

Twelve ways: Bon (the royal rite, the day-long rite, the secret rite and *zu*, the *bang sa* with the *du ru*); Dharma (the purifying rite leading out of the six realms, the meditation rite of the assembly funeral, planting white aspirations); treasure (a temple or stūpa built, offerings to the saṅgha, offerings to lay priests and mantra practitioners); aspiration (giving to the people without one's name, roads, paths, boats and rafts made or mended, feeding the hungry and giving water).

| Year of death | Texts and works |
| --- | --- |
| mouse | the *Vijaya* and *Amoghapāśa* dhāraṇīs |
| ox | a *maṇi* built, twenty thousand recitations |
| tiger | the *Tog gzungs*, the *Diamond Cutter*, *tsha tsha* of Akṣobhya of five kinds |
| hare | the *Diamond Cutter*, a thousand *tsha tsha* |
| dragon | the *Sūtra of the Wise and the Foolish* recited |
| snake | the *Hundred Thousand Names*, a stūpa restored |
| horse | the *Hundred Thousand Names*, the *Diamond Cutter*, the *Collected Dhāraṇīs* |
| sheep | the *Eight Thousand*, the *'Bum tog*, the *Golden Light*, the *Good Conduct* |
| monkey | the *spang skong*, the *Good Conduct*, a stūpa restored, a banner raised |
| bird | water offerings, the *Confession of Downfalls* |
| dog | a burnt offering, the *Fortunate Aeon* |
| pig | the *Destroyer of All Sin and Obscuration*, the *Confession of Downfalls* |
