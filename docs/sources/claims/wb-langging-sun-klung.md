# Langging the red enters with the sun's klung: the print reads «ཀླུང», not «གླང»

The line on p. 186 for Langging the red, of war (no. 19) reads «ཉི་མའི་ཀླུང་དང་ལྷན་ཅིག་བསྐྱེད» on the scan: «ཀླུང» (klung), not «གླང» (ox). The old question 35 (the solstice? the sun in the ox month? the sun in Taurus?) rests on the misreading and falls with it. What is left is the meaning of the sun's klung: ch. 13's year tables head a column «ཟླ་ཀླུང» filled with a planet's klung each year, and the hal khyi sits on the previous year's wind-horse (ཀླུང་རྟ, p. 185); the sun's klung may be the Sun's counterpart, but no text read says so. No day of entry is named.
