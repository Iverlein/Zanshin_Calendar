# WB p. 305: the four meetings are given by names, no numerals on the page

The four meetings (འཛོམ་པ, 'dzom pa) of p. 305 name a date-class, a planet and a mansion for each vowel, by name only: the page, read on the 1996 scan (img. 313, 2×), has no numerals between the lines, so no digit is in doubt. The verses give: on a joyful date (དགའ་བ, nanda: 1, 6, 11) Mars and Kṛttikā (སྨིན་དྲུག) meet for A; on a good date (བཟང་པོ) Mercury and Maghā (མཆུ) for I; on a victorious date (རྒྱལ་བ) Jupiter and Āśleṣā (སྐག) for U; on an empty date (སྟོང་པ) Venus and Dhaniṣṭhā (མོན་དྲེ) for E; on a full date (རྫོགས་པ) Saturn with Bharaṇī (བྲ་ཉེ) and Uttaraphalgunī (དབོ) for O. Then, again, the joyful date with the Sun and the good date with the Moon, as bad for A and I, "as before". The lines were checked against the machine reading by eye at this size only; a second look at the vowel signs on a larger crop is still owed.
