# The Peking print reads «རྟ་ལ་མོ» with a plain la at 5×

RD305 read img. 317 line 5 of the Peking print (I1KG13197) at 5×: after «མངོན་པར་དགའ། །» come རྟ, a ལ with its wedge and no subjoined ླ, and མོ (high confidence), so the Peking agrees with the Narthang prints against the Derge etext's «རྟ་བླ་མོ»; it does not decide which is the older spelling. «བགྲོད་མིན» (img. 315 line 5) and «བཅུ་ཡི་བར» (img. 317 line 3) show no variant. The Derge group I00JW501163 named in the row is the Four Tantras, not the Svapnohana.
