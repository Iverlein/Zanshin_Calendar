# WB ch. 2 gives the year's measures: 360 breaths to a chu tshod, 21,600 breaths a day, 360 sign-days and 365 civil days

The coarse arrangement of ch. 2 (machine reading of p. 14): three hundred and sixty in-and-out breaths fill one chu tshod (ཆུ་ཚོད); a day and night has twenty-one thousand six hundred breaths (ཉིས་ཁྲི་སྟོང་དྲུག་བརྒྱ); thirty days make the month and twelve months the year. The days of the year are counted in two ways: civil days (ཉིན་ཞག) three hundred and sixty-five, and the days of the house (ཁྱིམ་ཞག, the zodiac's going round) three hundred and sixty, which the text calls the same as a human year. A further line on the sun's round in the stars' hours (1620) and one on the same round in tithi-days (371 days 4 chu tshod 36 chu srang 5 7/13 breaths) are settled in [wb-ch2-sun-round-1620-stars-hours-371-tithi-days](wb-ch2-sun-round-1620-stars-hours-371-tithi-days.md) (q744).
