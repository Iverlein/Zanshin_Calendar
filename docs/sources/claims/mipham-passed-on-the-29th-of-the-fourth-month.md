# Mipham passed on the 29th of the Saga (fourth) month, a Friday under Snar ma

Dilgo Khyentse's short life of Mipham (mrs:p117, etext of two prints) gives the day of Mipham's passing as the twenty-ninth of the Saga month (the fourth month): he stayed until the twenty-ninth, and on that day, a Friday under the mansion Snar ma, his body came to rest at sunset. No thirtieth is named. So the Rigpa students' 4/29 agrees with the life; Namdroling's 4/30 does not, and the life gives no ground for it.

- Day: the 29th of the fourth month, water-mouse year (1912 by the usual count; the text gives the cycle, not the Western year).
- Rank 3, a modern author of Mipham's lineage; read on the etext only (no scan is served).
- A caution: a date kept in a monastery's almanac may fall on the 30th when the 29th is skipped or doubled; the life states the date of the day itself.
