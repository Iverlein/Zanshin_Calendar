# The Śabdakalpadruma prints the Brahmavaivarta's series of spans on p. 470, with two lines the other print lacks (RD272)

The *Śabdakalpadruma*'s article *svapna* quotes the Brahmavaivarta's chapter of the good dream, *Kṛṣṇajanmakhaṇḍa* (numbered 77 in this printing), with the series of the *Svapnohana*: a year for the first watch, eight months for the second, three months for the third, half a month for the fourth, ten days for a dream at the sight of dawn, at once for a morning dream if one is woken. RD272 (2026-11-09) read the lines on the page image of vol. 5 p. 470 (printed ४७०; leaf n474), so the series now stands on a second print of the Purāṇa: Jivananda's of 1888 (bvp:p662-665) and the Basu printing of the *Śabdakalpadruma* (skd:p470-471), which Negelein used for his "b".

Two lines follow the series on the page, not in the lines the pool had from the other print: a dream's matter that was in the dreamer's mind during the day is had surely, and the dream of one burdened with worry or illness is fruitless. Before the chapter the Lord says that "in the Kāṇva branch, in the book of merit" of the Sāmaveda the good dream is set out; the Purāṇa thus points its teaching to a Vedic source, which is not named elsewhere in the pool and is not the Atharva appendix.

The print's chapter numbers (77, 82, 83) differ from Jivananda's (73 for the series), which is why one cannot cite a chapter number across prints.

Doubt: both copies read are reprints of one setting (the Nag Publishers facsimile of 2002 and the Chowkhamba Sanskrit Series reprint, same plates on pp. 470–471); the Basu printing of 1886–1893 itself was not found at the page (the Internet Archive item in.ernet.dli.2015.345206, labelled "Vol. 4-5, 1892", holds the entries from ka to ha, so not the volume with svapna; the Bengali-script first edition, Parts V and VI on the Internet Archive, end at śrīhi, so the svapna article would be in its Part VII, not found).
