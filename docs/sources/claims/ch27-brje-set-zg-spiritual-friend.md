# Ch. 27: ZG puts the «བརྗེ» set at the spiritual friend's

The set headed «བརྗེའི་རྩིས་སྐོར་བཞི» in WB vol. 2, p. 23, l. 22 (golden vajra, crystal of discipline, silver maṇḍala, vase of discipline, begging in the villages for its black end; [ch27-brje-set-is-the-monk](ch27-brje-set-is-the-monk.md)) is the second of the thirteen circles' cherished pebbles after the king's and before the mantra-holder's, and Zhechen Gyaltsab, who lists the same sets in the same order, names the second "the life-force pebble of the spiritual friend (དགེ་བའི་བཤེས, dge ba'i bshes)". His set holds the same things: the golden vajra that does not vanish in the sky, the empowerment vase, the crystal rosary with its string unbroken, a maṇḍala (his is of turquoise, WB's of silver) and, as the mewa pebble, the lion throne not empty; its black ends are obstacles to the body's life, few followers, obstructed work for others, rumour and a change of seat. The third, the mantra-holder's, begins with a golden vajra rising in the sky, as WB's mantrin set does (zg:p348-349, p. 348 last line and p. 349 line 1 read on the scan, the rest on the typeset etext).

So the set's person is by this witness the spiritual friend, which WB's own second circle (the white snow lioness, [wb.2:p4-5](../corpus/wb/ch27.md#wb-2-p4-5)) is also for; a monk is not named. The earlier reading "the monk's set" (RD51) is thus narrowed to "the spiritual friend's", a teacher who is usually a monk but is not called so. What «བརྗེ» itself spells stays unshown: the dictionaries give it only as "exchange" (brje, brje ba, brje bo "barter"; Hopkins, Rangjung Yeshe, Valby, Ives-Waldo), none as "monk" or "spiritual friend". WB's own funeral lists (the etext of the 1996 print, vol. 2 printed p. 195, «ཉ་སྦལ་སེར་པོ(༣) བརྗེའི་རོ», the words «བརྗེའི་རོ» read on the scan at 1.6 times) set a «བརྗེའི་རོ», the corpse of the brje, between «དགེ་བཤེས་རོ» (the spiritual friend's) and «བན་རོ» (the monk's), so there the word is neither: unsettled whether it names a class of the dead and the living apart from both.
