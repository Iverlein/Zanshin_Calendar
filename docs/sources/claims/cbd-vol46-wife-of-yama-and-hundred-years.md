# The Dpe bsdur ma volume 46 prints 'Deceiving Death' with the girl of the noose as "the wife of the Lord of Death" and the outer signs «ལོ་བརྒྱར» (RD360)

The Dpe bsdur ma's volume 46 (the volume of the Peking and Narthang group of "remaining tantras", whose catalogue says the work stands in it and was collated with the Derge and Co ne text; nos. 2678-2680) holds, besides the Bsdus don, the work itself with small-letter glosses that the etext's OCR runs into the verses (cbd:p1065-1066). At the death-dream verses it reads the noose girl's sign as «དེ་ནི་གཤིན་རྗེའི་ཆུང་མ་དུས་མཚན་ཡིན་ཞེས་བྱ» ("that is called the wife of the Lord of Death, the time-sign"), where the Derge (cbs:p247-249) and Dpe bsdur ma vol. 14 read «དེ་ནི་དུས་མཚན་ཡིན་ཞེས་བྱ»; the anointing «དྲི་དམར» with the Derge (CBL has «དྲི་མར»); and at verse 21 «ལོ་བརྒྱར» ("a hundred years"), with CBL (cbl:p238b), where the Derge has «ལོ་བརྒྱད» ("eight years"). Whether «གཤིན་རྗེའི་ཆུང་མ» is a gloss or the verse cannot be told from the OCR; the scan was not reached. So the "hundred years" is not CBL's alone (it bears on the question of the two readings, which this reading did not take up). State: etext only.
