# Whether the funeral feast is held

Whether a funeral feast (ཤིད་སྟོན, shid ston) is held is the fourteenth examination's first step (WB vol. 2, p. 126). Not held, but the merit sent out unseen as a "hidden funeral" (ལྐོག་ཤིད, lkog shid), when the living have great obstacles and repetitions and the dead does not want food. Bad if not held: the dead's mewa on a white one (the husband's side struck by repetition) or on the nine-red (bad for the women). Good if held: the dead stays well, the living are unharmed, the living's power arises, and no repetition follows. Bad if held, even when the dead wants food: the dead's sky-year or month meeting the living's power as enemy or seventh; then a great funeral is bad now and later.

Mipham's notes (p. 519, l. 28, to p. 520, l. 4) count the same as pebbles: nine identifying for "hold or not" (the living's obstacles great, middling or small; the dead wanting food, not, or middling; and the three named above), two "bad if not held" (the white three and the nine-red), three "held, no repetition" (rebirth good and staying well, the living unharmed, the main living person's power raised by the dead's), four "bad now and later" (the dead's year and month meeting the living's power as enemy or seventh).
