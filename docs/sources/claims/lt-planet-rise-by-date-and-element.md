# A planet rises on the day's animal; the Sun, Mercury and Venus are reached through their elements

LT's formula for the planets' rising (lt:p10-11) uses the same verb as the trigram rule just before it, stated for each day (ཞག་རེར): the animal is the day's. Four planets are named as rising (Saturn, Moon, Jupiter, Mars); the line before lists each planet's element: Sun and Mars fire, Moon and Mercury water, Jupiter wood, Venus iron, Saturn earth, Rahu all five. The states (rise, the son's rise, spent mother, declining enemy, friend in strife) are relations of elements, and LT's table of the five elements' mother, son, friend and enemy stands on p. 14; so the Sun, Mercury and Venus take their state by their element's relation to the rising planet's element. Inferred, not stated: no line applies it to them.
