# The 山家集 first-dream poem: the common text and the old 異本 text differ in headnote and in the third line

The 山家集 poem on the first dream, 年くれぬ ... かなふ初夢, is read on the page images of two different texts of the book, and they differ in the third line and in the headnote.

The common text (流布本), in 佐々木弘綱 and 信綱's 日本歌学全書 vol. 8 (1891), is the first poem of the spring book of the upper volume, headed 立春の朝よみける ("composed on the morning of the beginning of spring"), and reads 年くれぬ / 春來べしとハ思ひねに / まさしく見えてかなふ初夢, with no variant noted. This agrees with the 思ひねに of the 六家集 print in the 和歌データベース (skf:p2, there the first poem too) and with the Utakura etext (skf:p1, where it stands seventh, a different order). The reading 思ひ寝む of Japanese Wikipedia is in none of the printed texts seen.

The old manuscript text printed as the 異本山家集 (1906; reprinted in the 西行全集 of 1930) has the poem in another place, after the exchange with 待賢門院堀河 and not at the head of the spring book, with the headnote 春たつこゝろを ("on the feeling of spring beginning") and おもはねど ("though I did not think") for 思ひねに, which it gives in a small note as the common text's reading. So the headnote "on the morning of the beginning of spring" and the picture of the first dream as that of the night into 立春 belong to the common text only; the old text's headnote names no morning. The poem's number in an edition (新編国歌大観, 日本古典文学大系, 和歌文学大系) was not found in any open source: the 1891 and 1906 prints do not number their poems.
