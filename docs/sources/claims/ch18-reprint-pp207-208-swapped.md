# The Gser thang reprint binds the images of WB vol. 1 pp. 207 and 208 in the other order

In the Gser thang reprint of WB vol. 1 (I3CN5028) the images of two pages are bound in the other order: img. 206 carries the printed page number 208 and img. 207 the number 207. The text confirms it: p. 206 (img. 205) ends in the entry Subduer, p. 207 (img. 207) continues it and runs from Subduer to Possessing All, p. 208 (img. 206) begins with the end of Possessing All and runs on to Aim-accomplished, and p. 209 (img. 208) begins with the end of that entry. The 1996 print (I1KG12906, img. 216–219) carries the numbers 206 to 209 in the order of the text. The offset image = page − 1 therefore holds for pp. 206 and 209–211 only; for p. 207 the image is page, for p. 208 it is page − 2.

The 1996 etext's passages of the first reading of this chapter (RD184) had followed the reprint's order of images, so that what is now p. 207 stood under p. 208 and the other way round; the passages wb.1:p207 and wb.1:p208 were rewritten from the scan.
