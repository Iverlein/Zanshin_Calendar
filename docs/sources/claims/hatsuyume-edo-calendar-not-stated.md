# No open Edo source reached that names the calendar of the 初夢 night of 2 January

The 初夢 of the 日本大百科全書 (nip:p1) says only 正月2日, and no Edo source naming the calendar was found here: the 永代大雑書 of 1856 has no 初夢 at all (mdz:i31), the 永代万暦大雑書 of 1883 names 初夢 once as the dream of the year's first night with Mount Fuji as the good sight and gives no night and no calendar (mdz:i72). Whether the 2 January of the Edo 初夢 was counted by the lunar first month stays open (q788): the Edo almanacs and gazetteers that state the night (the 東都歳時記, the 江戸歳事記, the 守貞謾稿) were searched for in open images and etexts and not reached.
