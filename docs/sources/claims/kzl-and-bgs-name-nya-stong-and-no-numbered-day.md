# Dpal sprul's and Jigs med gling pa's own commentary name the nya stong for offerings and the lay fast, and no numbered day

In the Words of My Perfect Teacher (Gser thang print, printed p. 270, read on the scan) Dpal sprul says only that, on the full-moon and new-moon days and the six times (ཉ་སྟོང་དུས་དྲུག, nya stong dus drug) and the like, offerings are made to the Three Jewels as far as one can; no day is numbered and the six times are not listed on the page. Jigs med gling pa's own commentary on his Yon tan mdzod (Bden gnyis shing rta, vol. 1 of his Gsung 'bum) in the exposition of the lay fast says, in the etext, that householders take the one-day fast on «ཉ་སྟོང་བརྒྱད་གསུམ» and on the four great occasions (the words uncertain); like the Dpal sprul passage, it numbers no day of the month on the pages read.

- The root verses of the Yon tan mdzod were not read: the commentary of vol. 1 was searched in the etext for «བསྙེན་གནས», «ཉ་སྟོང», «ཚེས་བཅུ», «ཚེས་བརྒྱད», «ཉེར་ལྔ», «གནམ་སྟོང», «བཅོ་ལྔ», «དུས་དྲུག», «སྨྱུང་གནས»; «ཉ་སྟོང» occurs once (p. 658), «ཚེས་བརྒྱད», «དུས་དྲུག» and «སྨྱུང་གནས» not at all; «གནམ་སྟོང» (p. 590) is the new moon of a moon simile for the aspiring mind, no practice day.
- The etext of this print is poor OCR; the pages were not read whole on the scan.
