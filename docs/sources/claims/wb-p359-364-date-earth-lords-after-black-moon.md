# WB pp. 359-364: after the Black Moon the date earth lords are a named run of fierce lords of day, date and hour, not the lords of the date by number

Read 2026-11-01 on the 1996 etext (IE0OPI51524892, markers [p367]-[p372] = img. 367-372 = printed pp. 359-364), the headings by eye on img. 367 and 368 only; the verses of pp. 361-363 (img. 369-371) were not read on the scan, the Yigdzin-1 run for them waiting for a free card slot. The date's earth lords (ཚེས་ཀྱི་ས་བདག, tshes kyi sa bdag) open at the end of p. 359 ("now the good and bad particulars of the date's earth lords") with the Black Moon (ཟླ་ནག, zla nag): its works and its remedy, a white-headed yak or sheep dressed in white silk. The line that follows on pp. 360-364 is a run of named lords, each with the same frame (works by the lord's day, washing, clothes, the four directions, birth with years and months and the day of death, the thief and the days to recover, illness, the remedy): the Black Sun's reckoning in brief and Little Black (ཉི་ནག, ནག་ཆུང) and a lord with the "eleven kings and ministers" (p. 360), the Black Sky (གནམ་ང་ནག་པོ), the Heap with the spike (ཕུང་པོ་ཟོར་ཐོགས, p. 361), the Hungry Dog (ཧལ་ཁྱི, hal khyi) and the Sky Dog (གནམ་ཁྱི, p. 362), the Sky-joining (གནམ་སྦྱོར), the Planet-ogre (གཟའ་རྒོད), one beginning "the poor hand" (དབུལ་ལག, p. 362), the Seven planets' envoy (གཟའ་བདུན་ཕོ་ཉ, p. 363), one beginning ངམས་ཤིང, the Dog of the interval (བར་གནམ་ཧལ་ཁྱི), the Garuda-leg (ཁྱུང་ཀི་ཀང) and the Great Net (དྲ་ཆེན, p. 363), then the Black Rahu and the Rahu (རཱ་ཧུ་ནག་པོ, སྒྲ་གཅན, p. 364) and then the eight trigrams and the nine mewa (p. 365). Read this way these are not the earth lords of the date by its number: they are a named series of "fierce" lords of day, date and hour, which p. 364's end says are to be avoided for peaceful and living works, the fierce works going with the lord's direction of travel. The order of the run is the one above, as the etext has it; where the p. 360 headings stand in the etext's confused lines is not settled (see the doubts). Doubts: p. 360's etext lines are scrambled (the pictures between the paragraphs), so the number and place of its headings (the Black Sun, Little Black, "Nga-phar-ma" and the eleven kings and ministers) are not certain; whether ངམས་ཤིང is the Wood-ape of the question's wording was not read on the scan.
