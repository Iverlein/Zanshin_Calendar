# «དུས་ཚོད་ཚང་གི་རྩིས»: the hour of the reckoning and the household, ch. 22's line carried over from ch. 24

Narrowed 2026-11-01. The line «དུས་ཚོད་ཚང་གི་རྩིས་ཀྱང་སྦྱོར» closes ch. 22 (wb.1:p349) and ch. 24 (vol. 1, p. 416) in the same words, and BDRC's full-text search for «དུས་ཚོད་ཚང་གི་རྩིས» finds it in WB's prints (the 1996, Gser thang, Zhol, Sakya Centre prints, the Sde srid's *Dpyod ldan snying nor* copy and the *Bod kyi rtsis rig kun 'dus* reprint) and nowhere else: it is WB's own closing formula. Two things that WB's year-of-age reckoning (ch. 24) makes of the words: the hour of the time of reckoning is a root pebble («རྩིས་བྱེད་དུས་ཀྱི་དུས་ཚོད», the notes, vol. 2, p. 490: the sixth of the six unchanging root pebbles) and the household («ཚང», the household reckoned by its members' lots, p. 380, the notes, pp. 490–491) is ch. 24's own subject. So, as a reading, the birth reckoning ends by sending the reader on to apply "also the reckoning of the hour and the household", that is, the year of age's moment and the household's round, which are ch. 24's. No gloss in the notes to p. 349 itself, no other reader quotes the line.
