# Ch. 27: the notes name the circles' bare «ཁམས» the power's, the answer for the circles

Mipham's notes on the circles (p. 533) say outright «བརྩི་བྱའི་ཁམས་སྟེ་དབང་ཐང་གི་དགྲ», "the element of the one reckoned, that is the enemy of the power", the highest-ranked witness for the circles' bare «ཁམས» (wb.2:p533b). WB p. 17 names the power in two of its four parallel reckonings and the bare word in the other two (ch27-start-element-wording); that the bare word is the power's there too is the reading that agrees with the notes and Zhechen Gyaltsab; no witness gives another element for it. So the circles' «ཁམས» is the power's element by Mipham; p. 17's two bare words are read by the same family, not by a sentence.
