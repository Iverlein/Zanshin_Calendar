# The birth wheel: stars on a boy's body

WB's birth wheel (vol. 2, p. 379), the first of ch. 34's works: a handsome boy is drawn and the stars of the birth day set on his body: the sun-embraced star on the crown, three at the mouth, two at the shoulders, two at the wrists, two at the hands, five at the heart, one at the navel, one at the secret place, two at the knees, eight at the feet. Born in the feet: tall or short; in the knees: wanders; in the secret place: ruined by sex; at the navel: poor; in the heart: has enjoyment; in the hands: a thief; at the wrists: suffering; at the shoulders: a burden-carrier; in the mouth: has authority of the date; at the crown: becomes a king without doubt.
