# A planet's rise, decline, spent and strife by the planet that rises on the animal

LT states how a planet's state is found (lt:p9-11): by triads of animals one planet "rises" (ཚེས, tshes): Saturn on tiger, horse and dog, the Moon on pig, sheep and hare, Jupiter on bird, ox and snake, Mars on rat, dragon and monkey. The rising planet is in its rise (dar), its son too; its mother is spent (zhud), its enemy declining (gud), its friend in strife (gyod). UT states the same rule for the planet and the mansion on the illness board (ut:p37), and in the dead's reckoning reads the weekday's planet by it: dying in its rise good, spent middling, strife or decline bad (ut:p69-70).

Inferred, not stated: the animal is the day's, since LT's day trigram rises by the same triads just before; the state of the death day's planet is then its element's relation to the planet that rises on the day's animal, not the month's rough rise and decline and not the dead's element. No manual read says why WB's weekday table lacks Mercury's gyod and Jupiter's gud.
