# The dream month (rmi lam gyi zla ba) is the twelfth of twelve month names of the Catuspitha list

# «རྨི་ལམ་གྱི་ཟླ་བ», the dream month, is the twelfth month of a list of twelve month names (RD367)

The 17 hits of «རྨི་ལམ་གྱི་ཟླ་བ» (rmi lam gyi zla ba) are, in the 14 that BDRC lists in its snippets, mostly the simile of the moon in a dream in the Tshad ma rigs gter and its commentaries (like a dream's moon, or the hair-fall of the cataract-sufferer: the line «rmi lam gyi zla ba lta bu'am rab rib can rnams kyis skra shad snang ba ltar», in Wylie), some the prayer «by night let the moon of the dream appear» (མཚན་ལ་རྨི་ལམ་གྱི་ཟླ་བ་སྣང་བར་གྱིས་ཤིག, read in etexts I3CN7656 p. 231 and I4248 p. 153, a prayer that stands in the Gesar-epic literature) and two poems; and three are a month name: the list of twelve months taken from the Catuṣpīṭha (གདན་བཞི, gdan bzhi) and the Vajradākatantra (རྡོ་རྗེ་མཁའ་འགྲོའི་རྒྱུད), «Lord of the Earth, Increase, Delusion, the Good, the Bull, the Tortoise, the Crocodile, the Yugma, Rme ba, 'Bug pa, the Life, the Dream month» (dds.10:p38, Khal kha Ngag dbang dpal ldan's Dus tshigs commentary; the same list in Mkhan zur Ngag dbang nyi ma, etext I4810 p. 586). BDRC's snippet of the Tibetan–Chinese dictionary «bod rgya skar rtsis rig pa'i tshig mdzod» (a modern work, search only) glosses «རྨི་ལམ་གྱི་ཟླ་བ» as «སྨིན་དྲུག་ཟླ་བ», the Pleiades month (Kṛttikā), «from the Vajra Foundation's four tantras» (རྡོ་རྗེ་གདན་བཞིའི་རྒྱུད་སོགས་ནས). The word dream is thus in the calendar only as the name of a month; the month name does not say that dreams in it are read differently, and nothing in the three texts relates dreams to it. A text that read the month of a dream by the Pleiades month would be a different thing from this, and none was found.

Doubt: only the 12th place in the list and the gloss are read; the list's first names and the order of months in the year (whether this «dream» month is the 8th or the 12th of the year) are not settled here.
