# The five days: Gyā, Jung-wa, Yangdag, Nectar, Destroyer, Demon

The five-days teaching in the earth lords' chapter (WB vol. 2, p. 225): for any work the day is classed by the element of the day against one's own. One's own element, male day: Gyā (རྒྱ, rgya); female: Jung-wa (འབྱུང་བ, 'byung ba). Mother's element, male: Yangdag (ཡང་དག, yang dag); female: Nectar (བདུད་རྩི, bdud rtsi). The enemy's element, male: Destroyer (འཇིག་ཉི, 'jig nyi); female: Demon (བདུད་ཉི, bdud nyi). The order of worth: Gyā only good, Jung-wa better, Yangdag better still, Nectar's summit the best; the Destroyer is bad and the Demon day worse; a wake or a war begun on them fails. The text adds that Mipham and Gelek and others join this to the reckoning for the dead alone and mix it with unsourced fabrications, and sends the fine points to the reckoning for war.
