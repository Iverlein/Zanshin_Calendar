# A shoot with a bird's nest growing from the crown

Image group: the dreamer ill. Read by 1 text: Four Tantras (for a sick dreamer).
Offered only when the dreamer is ill: every row is a reading for a sick dreamer (the Four Tantras above WB ch. 25).

| Text | Verdict (tone) | Passage | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Four Tantras (for a sick dreamer) | from the crown (སྤྱི་བོ, spyi bo) a shoot (ལྕུག་མ, lcug ma) grows with a bird's nest on it: a sign that the lord of death (འཆི་བདག, 'chi bdag) has seized the sick; dreamed constantly by the sick it means death, for one without illness it is not certain, and the rite of service (རིམ་གྲོ, rim gro) frees him (bad) | `gt:i42-43` | machine |

What a commentary adds (Byang pa's gloss from *Moonbeams*, ཟླ་ཟེར, zla zer; etext, a commentary's reading and not the verse's): a thorny shoot, a reed or a palm grown in the heart marks a tumour (སྐྲན, skran); the gloss names the heart, not the crown. Khro ru's spelling is ལྕག་མ, the same word (gt-dream-verse-variants). The palm and the thorny tree of the next line: dream-sick-palm-or-thorn-tree-in-the-heart.

The verse is the Explanatory Tantra's (བཤད་རྒྱུད, bshad rgyud) ch. 7 on the Drathang print (RD268); the Serthang scan lacks the page. The rule on who dies, the cause and the six kinds of dream are in gt-dream-death-rule, the whole list in gt-dream-death-images, the one-syllable differences from the four commentaries in gt-dream-verse-variants. This image claim was added by RD274 (2026-11-09) so that the picker holds every image of the verse; the Drathang passage is a machine reading, the lines read by eye in part (gt:i42-43).
