# Ka-Nying Shedrub Ling's 2027 days fall a month ahead of the app's Phugpa reckoning from the third month on

Ka-Nying Shedrub Ling's annual activities for 2027 (ksl:p1) put Saga Dawa Düchen on 20 May, Chökhor Düchen on 7 July and Lhabab Düchen on 22 October 2027. The app's Phugpa engine (cli, 2026-11-24) gives those days as 3/15, 5/4 and 8/22: it doubles the second month of 2027 (a leap second month), and the monastery's calendar does not double it there. Losar (7 February) and Chötrul Düchen (20 February) agree. The masters' days on that page agree with the Rigpa students' list (tbc:p1) once the month is shifted, with one day's difference at three of them (Tulku Urgyen 12/24 against the engine's 12/23, Tsikey Chokling 11/4 against 11/3, the Guru's birth 5/10 against 4/11).

Which reckoning the monastery follows (the Men-Tsee-Khang's Phugpa, or the Tsurphu reckoning, which differs from the Phugpa in its leap months and days: claim tsurphu-almanac-festival-days-after-tsurphu-and-mi-bskyod-rdo-rje) the page does not say. Until it is known, the 2027 Western dates of a Kagyu or Rime house are no check on the app's dates.
