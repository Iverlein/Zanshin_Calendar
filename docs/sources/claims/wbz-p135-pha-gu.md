# The kin of wood are «pha gu mes» on the blocks

In searching for the ground (WB vol. 2, p. 135, l. 17) the Zhol blocks carve «ཤིང་གི་ཕ་གུ་མེས་གསུམ», the letter after ཕ flat-headed (ག), not ཁ: «ཕ་གུ» is WB's own spelling, whatever word it stands for.
