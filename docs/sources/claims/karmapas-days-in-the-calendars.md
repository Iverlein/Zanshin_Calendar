# The Karmapas' days: the first on the 3rd of the mouse month in the Tsurphu almanac, the third on 6/14 or 6/10, the sixteenth on 9/9

- The first Karmapa, Dus gsum mkhyen pa (Düsum Khyenpa): his offering of the time on the 3rd of the reckoners' middle winter month, the earth-mouse month, in the 2015 almanac of the Tsurphu reckoning (mtl:p2, search snippet), which notes that most farmers and nomads call that month the monkey month. The mouse month is the eleventh in the White Beryl's model almanac; the almanac's own number for it was not read.
- The third Karmapa, Rang byung rdo rje (Rangjung Dorje): 6/14 in the Rigpa students' calendar (tbc:p1), 6/10 in Jeff Watt's list (har:p1).
- The sixteenth Karmapa, Rang byung rig pa'i rdo rje: 9/9 in both (tbc:p1, har:p1).

No other Karmapa, and neither Tilopa nor Nāropa, stands in any list read; BDRC's full-text search found no day for them (school-days.md, *Searched and not found*).
