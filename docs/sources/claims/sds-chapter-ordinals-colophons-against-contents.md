# The Siddhasāra's colophon numbers of chapters 4 to 6 run one too high and are corrected in brackets; the opening list and the contents differ in the order of chapters 4 and 5

The print of the Siddhasāra (SDS) numbers its chapters in three places that do not agree on the order and, for three chapters, not on the number.

| Chapter by the contents (sds:p19) and the headings | Folios | Ordinal the colophon prints | Editors' bracket |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 the tantra | 4 | first | none |
| 2 the groups of medicines | 11 | second | none |
| 3 foods | 18 | third | none |
| 4 healing the fevers (རིམས་ནད་གསོ་བ, rims nad gso ba) | 28 | fifth | «[bzhi pa]», fourth |
| 5 the signs of death (འཆི་ལྟས, 'chi ltas) | 49 | sixth | «[lnga]», fifth (the brackets lost in the etext) |
| 6 healing the diarrhoea (འཁྲུ་བ, 'khru ba) | 54 | seventh | «[drug]», sixth |
| 7 the discharge of blood | 66 | seventh | none |
| 8 to 10 | 72, 78, 84 | eighth, ninth, tenth | none |
| 11 to 30 | | the right number each (sds:p34-113 and the colophons on etext pp. 117-226) | none |

So the old text's own colophons passed from «third» to «fifth» and then counted two sevenths, and the editors of the Mtsho sngon print corrected the three wrong numbers in square brackets. The «sixth» at the end of the chapter on the signs of death is therefore a wrong numeral that the editors corrected to «fifth»; the etext prints the two numerals together, «དྲུག་ལྔ །པ», because the bracket marks did not survive. It is not a second, older count of a chapter that was later split: no chapter is missing from the contents, which hold thirty-one entries to the thirty-one the colophon claims (sds:p230-231). The scan was not served, so the bracket on p. 77 is inferred from pp. 72 and 89.

The order is a separate matter. The opening list of the text (sds:p27) names the signs of death fourth and the fevers fifth; the contents, the chapter headings and the body have the fevers fourth and the signs of death fifth. The colophons' wrong numbers (fevers fifth, signs of death sixth) fit neither order: the fevers' «fifth» is the place the opening list gives it, the signs of death's «sixth» is not any list's. Whether the chapters were once in the opening list's order and were moved is not settled by the etext.
