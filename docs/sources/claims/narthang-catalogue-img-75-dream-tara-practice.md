# The Narthang catalogue has a Dream Tārā practice on img. 75, by Candragomin's pupil, translated by Sugataśrī and Byams pa'i dpal

Img. 75 of I2KG212509 (vol. 225), machine reading 2026-11-11: «རྨི་ལམ་སྒྲོལ་མའི་སྒྲུབ་ཐབས» made by «སློབ་དཔོན་ཟླ་བའི་བཤེས་གཉེན» (the teacher Candragomin's pupil), translation of the paṇḍita Sugataśrī and the lotsāba Byams pa'i dpal. A Tārā practice, not the Examination of Dreams; the line does not say whether it is the Svapnohana's Dream Tārā practice.
