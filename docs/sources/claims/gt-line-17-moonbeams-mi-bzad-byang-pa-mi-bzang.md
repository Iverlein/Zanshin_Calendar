# Line 17: the Moonbeams reads མི་བཟད, Byang pa's lemma follows the Tantra's མི་བཟང་པ, the Drikung works the verse's words into prose

Ch. 7's line 17 of the bad-dream verse, in Byang pa, Rin spungs pa, the Blue Beryl and the Drikung dka' mchan, against the Moonbeams' verse that they quote beside it (etexts, 2026-11-10; st:p30-31).

The Moonbeams (Sde dge etext VE23703_1517 p. 159; also the Peking-type MW1PD95844_3549, a Tengyur chapter copy MW00PD1011353, the Eight Limbs itself in the Derge Tengyur MW23703_4310, and another edition of Byang pa's work, MW15590) reads «མི་བཟད་རྨི་ལམ་མཐོང་འགྱུར་ཏེ». So the answer to the first half of q904 is no: the Derge and the Peking-type Tengyur both read མི་བཟད, not Rin spungs pa's མི་ཟད (the Peking-type one by the search snippet; the Derge on the page). Rin spungs pa quotes it as «མི་ཟད་རྨི་ལམ་མཐོང་གྱུར་» (a different word, not a copy of the Tengyur's), Byang pa's print MW27496 as «མི་བཟང་རྨི་ལམ་མཐོང་འགྱུར་ཏེ». The Blue Beryl p. 208 glosses the Moonbeams' word as "unbearable and harmful" («མི་བཟད་ཅིང་གནོད་པ་བསྐྱེད་ལ»), so it too reads མི་བཟད in the Moonbeams.

Byang pa's lemma of the Four Tantras' line (p. 216, printed p. 180) is «དེ་ལྟ་བུ་ལ་སོགས་པ་རྨི་ལམ་མི་བཟང་པ། ཡིད་འཇུག་བུ་ག་ནད་ཀྱིས་བཀག་ཕྱིར་མཐོང་།» (བཟང་པ for བཟང་བ after ང), and his gloss repeats «རྨི་ལམ་མི་བཟང་བ». So he follows the Tantra, not the Moonbeams: the second half of q904. His quotation of the Moonbeams a page later has «མི་བཟང་» in the Si khron print and «མི་བཟད» in the other edition, which leaves open whether he or an editor wrote the Tantra's word into the Moonbeams' verse.

The Drikung (q905). The words «དེ་ལ་སོགས་ཏེ་རྨི་ལམ་མི་བཟང་བ» are the verse's own, in its own order, but the sentence goes straight on in prose, «རྨི་བའི་རྒྱུ་མཚན་ནི། སྙིང་གི་ཡིད་འཇུག་པའི་བུ་ག་རྣམས་ནད་ཀྱིས་བཀག་ཕྱིར་རྨི་ལམ་དེ་འདྲ་མཐོང་བ་ཡིན་ནོ», and ends at the first single shad after ནི (the verse lines before it end in double shads). The etext sets no lemma off. So མི་བཟང་བ is the verse's word worked into the commentator's sentence, not his gloss; it cannot be said whether his print had a lemma line there; it agrees with Skyem pa's lemma («མི་བཟང་བ») and with the Drathang print's reading, and with neither the four prints that read མི་བཟད་པ nor the Moonbeams (claims gt-line-17-in-five-commentaries, gt-dream-verse-chag-po-ri-prints).
