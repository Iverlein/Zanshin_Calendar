# WB p. 370: the invocation names Thé-se, Li and the nine mewa for the year, Grwa-pa for the month, the Golden Treasure Yellow for the day and the Turquoise Treasure Blue for the hour

Read 2026-11-01 on the 1996 scan (I1KG12907 img. 378 = printed p. 370, 2.5x crops by eye of the opening and of the last third) beside the 1996 etext ([p378]). After four invocations of the four directions' goddesses (the sun at the east, south, west, north; each "here the sponsor be fortunate"), the page invokes the earth lords of time in this order, as the scan reads: Thé-se (ཐེ་སེ) the king and the others, the year's lords; Li (ལི) and the other seven trigrams and the nine mewa from "one white", those of the trigrams and numbers; the dragon-god Grwa-pa (འབྲུག་ལྷ་གྲྭ་པ, written Grwa-pa on the scan, not Gwa-pa) and the others, the lords of the month's wheel (ཟླ་འཁོར); the Golden Treasure Yellow (གསེར་མཛོད་སེར་པོ) and the others, the lords of the day (ཞག); the Turquoise Treasure Blue (གཡུ་མཛོད་སྔོན་མོ) and the others, the lords of the hour (དུས་ཚོད); the fourteen kings and ministers (རྗེ་བློན་བཅུ་བཞི); then three of three: the month's, the fierce day's and the sharp hour's lords (the lines read, as far as the crop shows, "the friendly one of the month, the fierce one of the day, the sharp one of the hour"), and last the troop Wang-king (ཝང་ཀིང) and Hal-dog with the corpse (ཧལ་ཁྱི་རོ), each "bound by the Kolong-sdom". The year's, month's, day's and hour's lords are thus those four: Thé-se with Li and the nine mewa for the year, Grwa-pa for the month, the Golden Treasure Yellow for the day, the Turquoise Treasure Blue for the hour. Doubts: the word read "friendly" before "the month" (གཉེན་པོ) is the etext's, the crop shows it blurred; the other names of each group (the "and the others") are not on this page and were not looked for in the earlier pages (ch. 31 of the etext names them).
