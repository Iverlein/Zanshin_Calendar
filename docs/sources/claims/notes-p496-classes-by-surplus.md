# The illness's classes are named by the surplus of one colour: a third, four, five

Mipham's sentence on the illness's classes (WB vol. 2, p. 496, Yigdzin-1 of img. 504, l. 1–8; the printed letters are the 1996 print's, the classes' names are the sentence's own) names the classes by the surplus of one colour over the other among the fifteen pebbles «with the door not counted» (རྡེལ་སྒོ་མ་བརྩིས་པའི་བཅོ་ལྔ): six white and nine black is "ahead by a third black" (སུམ་ཆས་ལྷག་ནག), the reverse "ahead by a third white"; five white and ten black "ahead by five" (ལྔ་ཆས་ལྷག); with the colours level and the whites four ahead "ahead by four" (བཞི་ཆས་ལྷག). The examples add to fifteen with no speckled pebble, so the speckled is not counted among the fifteen there; the "door" left out is the sixteenth pebble, not the speckled. Mipham adds that a pair like five black and ten white would then have to be "ahead by a third white" and is not counted so; in the meaning three more of one colour read as the third and the four- and five-parts. So the surplus is three, four or five for these named classes; for a difference of one, two, or seven and more, this sentence names nothing. The sentence is the notes' gloss on «སུམ་ཆས་ལྷག་དཀར» in vol. 1's chapter 25 (health, ch25.md).
