# The ur mnan wheel's spoke seeds on the Taikhang: hrī-like letters

The eight seed syllables of the first spoke row of the pressing-down wheel (WB vol. 2, p. 112, the 1996 print; Taikhang I5878 img. 172, folio 162, line 3 about three-fifths along; the Zhol print's img. 790) are set in large decorative letters and are legible on the Taikhang, which the Gser thang reprint is not (2026-11-03, cropped at 2-3x). Seven of the eight have ཧ with a subjoined ར and a long vowel sign, the shape of «ཧྲཱི», some with the dot of the anusvara over them; the fifth has no subjoined ར and is a plainer letter (ཨཱ or བཱ, not settled). The line at the foot of the page (line 6, the second wheel's row) is of the same kind: a run of ཧྲཱི-like letters with a plainer one. So the spokes' seeds are, at the most, the one seed «hrī» repeated, not eight different seed syllables of the eight trigrams; which dots they carry and the fifth letter stay open, and the second wheel's row and the later rows (p. 113, ll. 4, 7-8, 11-12, 20) were not opened.
