# The tenth examination: the 304 results close; the 1,952 identifying are 126 short on the etext's figures

The tenth examination (pp. 48–49, 2,256 pebbles) is stated as 304 result pebbles and 1,952 identifying. The results close from the verse's own pieces: eleven root pebbles, 136 small strikes, 153 sound-producing, four sitting-postures, 11 + 136 + 153 + 4 = 304 (new). The identifying 1,952 do not close from the 1996 etext's figures: its eleven roots, 19 (year) + 4 + 3 + 55 + 7 + 3 + 2 + 7 + 10 + 11, are 121; the strike lists (19, 43, 9, 3, the burial strikes' 1,200, 9, 3, 19, 16, 20, 9) are 1,350; the sound-producing list (4, 8, 11, 5, 4, 9, 4, 6, 4, 5, 9, 5, 12, 5, 8, 30, 19, 144, 18, 9, 18, 3, 3, 12), 355; together 1,826, 126 short of 1,952, with the OCR's noise in several figures. By eye on the Gser thang reprint (img. 054, ll. 25–28, 0.75×) the figures 19, 43, 9, 3 and «སྟོང་དང་ཉིས་བརྒྱ» (1,200) are as the etext has them; the other figures were not read on the scan, so the 126 stay unexplained and the grouping of the strike lists among the three parts is a reading, not the verse's. The fourteenth's sums (2,920 = 136 + 2,784) were not redone line by line.
