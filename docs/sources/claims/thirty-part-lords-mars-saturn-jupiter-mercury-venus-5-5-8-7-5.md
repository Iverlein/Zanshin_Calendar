# The thirty parts of a junction are shared among five planets, Mars 5, Saturn 5, Jupiter 8, Mercury 7, Venus 5, in the unequal houses

In the Mchog dga' (མཆོག་དགའ, Mchog dga') chart of the thirty-part junction the thirty parts of a house are given to five planets in order: in the unequal houses (the book says «Aries and the rest»; that these are every other house, Aries, Gemini, Leo and so on, is my reading) Mars parts 1-5, Saturn 6-10, Jupiter 11-18, Mercury (གཟའ་ལྷག་པ) 19-25, Venus 26-30; in the equal houses (the book says «Taurus and the rest») the same five stretches are counted the other way from Venus: Venus 1-5, Mercury 6-12, Jupiter 13-20, Saturn 21-25, Mars 26-30, which the author's example of twenty-seven, Mars, fits. This is a different use of the parts from the dream's hour: nothing in the dream passages applies it. Whether a part of the night has a planet by this chart is not said. Etext only; the chart's picture is not in the etext, and the left row's numbers are printed unreversed.
