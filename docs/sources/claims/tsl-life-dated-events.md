# The dated events of Rtsa gsum gling pa's life in TSL vol. 30

The other dated events of Rtsa gsum gling pa's (རྩ་གསུམ་གླིང་པ, rtsa gsum gling pa) life found in TSL vol. 30, as the pages name them:

| Event | Date as printed | Passage | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| the key to the treasures comes into his hands at A mye Mu khri | at twenty-one (no year) | tsl.30:i4-5 | scan |
| the first treasure, from the ḍākinīs' assembly hall at Ti sgro of 'Bri gung | the fire-monkey year (མེ་སྤྲེལ་ལོ, me sprel lo), given as 1716 | tsl.30:i4-5 | scan |
| a ḍākinī in a dream bids him begin the Dharma | from then on, the tenth day of the Monkey month (སྤྲེལ་ཟླའི་ཚེས་བཅུ, sprel zla'i tshes bcu) | tsl.30:i86-88 | etext |
| his own count of the treasures written down | up to the fire-horse year (མེ་རྟ, me rta) | tsl.30:i569 | scan |
| the autobiography arranged | a day of the second month of the fire-sheep year (མེ་ལུག, me lug) | tsl.30:i564 | scan |

The years are named by element and animal and not converted (the editor alone adds a common-era year). The life names other days of the month for its visions and treasure findings (the fifteenth at tsl.30:i169-170 and i417, the tenth at i240, i277, i287), none of them tied to a month of the year. KTD's translation of Khyentse's life calls the year of the count "the Fire Dog year of 1726" (pbc:p2), where the print reads fire-horse.
