# RST: the good, the bad and the death dreams, as the verses give them

Good (RST, etext only): climbing a high mountain, a cloudless sun rising, crops and treasure found, fine clothes, armour, a fine hat or house, a weapon in hand, ornaments, a river crossed (the best: every wish fulfilled), drums and conches, riding a lion, tiger or dragon upward, sitting on a throne, sweet water drunk. Bad: crossing a blizzard or mire, running on unclean ground (defilement found), riding a donkey or horse bare, nakedness (a work put off), clothes without a collar, weeping, looking in a mirror (sorrow), filth eaten, creatures on the body, wolves seizing (illness), empty vessels, broken arrows (gossip), arrows in the body, fire, hail, a pit (others' sorcery), being bound, going underground, head cut off (harm from spirits sent), a naked rider going south, red flowers only (an obstacle to life). The dreams of death are a short list: a black woman tearing out the entrails, lac, ash, climbing a stupa or mountain, a camel ridden facing south, a donkey east, a monkey north, red clothes, red flowers, walking on a plain, a turban bound, drinking and dancing with the dead.
