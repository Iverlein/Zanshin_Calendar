# The moment's trigram starts as the Phugpa practice has it, not the General Tantra's halves of the year

The regent's reply 157 (ys.2:i016-017, read on the scan) gives the moment's trigram the same start as Mipham's notes (wb.2:p535-537) and names the authority: where the *General Tantra* of Brahmā (སྤྱི་རྒྱུད, spyi rgyud) lets the three spring and three summer [months] begin their dates with li and autumn and winter with kham, counting the day's eight in the male circuit and the night's in the female one, WB follows the practice of the Phugpa school, which goes no further than Duhar Nagpo's *Clear Lamp* (གསལ་སྒྲོན, gsal sgron) and its essence, the *Speckled Sheet* (ཤོག་ཁྲ, shog khra): "the tiger, horse and dog begin their dates with li, and the first time of the date's day is li too" (the dawn hour, the hare). The notes' words (p. 535) are the same clause with «ཟླ་བ», the month, set in it: «སྟག་རྟ་ཁྱི་གསུམ་ཟླ་བ་ལི་ཡིས་ཚེས», then the pig, sheep and hare with zin, the bird, ox and snake with dwa, the rat, dragon and monkey with kham: the triads of the month's animal, not a season's halves. The regent calls the *General Tantra* a pure tantra and does not say it is wrong; he says only that WB sets the moment after the Phugpa practice, which rests on Duhar Nagpo.

So reply 157 confirms the notes' rule for the date's first trigram (the build's month triad, question 164 answered by O16) and gives WB's source for it; it gives no count of its own beyond the first trigram and the first hour.
