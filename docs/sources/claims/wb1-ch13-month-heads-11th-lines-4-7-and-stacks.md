# The 11th head's lines 4-7 and stacked figures, and the 7th head's end, read again by eye

RD202, 2026-11-17. Third pass over the 7th to 11th heads, by eye. Gser thang reprint img. 161 (p. 154), the 11th head: lines 5 and 6 are whole and agree with RD2 and with the earlier machine text (the day and night stacks, the skar ma and full moon, ignorance, the sun's arrival at Sagittarius, expelling works, the month's lord, the vase east, the 7th's nine bad times, the feast that gives rise to talk, the Victor's word not proclaimed); line 7 opens "mi smra", has a dated clause for the 27th that is not read, then the five gods east and nine west and "strike each excess and deficit", and ends in a closing clause of three syllables not read and not found in the 7th or 8th heads; line 4 shows the earth-lord clause and "in Tibet the nines are drawn too" with its first and last words unread. The 1996 print's p. 167 (img. 177), lines 1 to 3 of the 7th head, read in halves at 4.5x: every word agrees with the earlier readings; the weekday line is dar 0 6, gud 5, skyod 2 3, zhud 1 3.

The stacked figures of the 11th head were set beside the numerals of the same page (weekday line, boxes 6 to 10). Tiger's great and small: upper 1 and 7, lower a ring-headed figure (9?) and 7 or 8. Day: upper a ring-headed figure (9?) over 6; middle 1 and 0; lower 0. Night: upper 1 and 1, middle 1 and 0, lower 0. Ignorance: 1 and 7 or 8 over 0. The heavy hooks are read as 1 as RD201 read them; the weekday line's 1 is the same shape in a lighter stroke. Day and night do not sum to a day on these readings, so what the tiers count is not decided; the figures are readings with doubts, not data. The stacks of the 7th foot, the 8th, 9th and 10th heads were not read again: the 1996 print (700 px wide) did not separate them at 8x in the earlier passes and was not tried again here.
