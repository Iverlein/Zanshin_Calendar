# The year charts of ch. 13 carry the lords' names only

The year charts of ch. 13 (vol. 1, pp. 116–121, Gser thang reprint I3CN5028 img. 123–128; Yigdzin-1, the pages viewed) write only the names of the year's lords in the cells round the year's animal, the hal khyi and the girl, Langging the red (spelt «ལྦང་གིང», «ལང་གིང», «ཝང་གིང» in different cells), the earth kings and the rest; not the hal khyi's five mothers and five sons, not «ཉི་མའི་ཀླུང», no day counts. So the charts do not seat the mothers and sons or gloss the sun's klung; the question of where they sit (q36) and which klung it is (q35) stays as RD65 left it.
