# 'Jigs med gling pa's passing offering is kept on the 3rd of the ninth month

The day of Kun mkhyen 'Jigs med gling pa (Jigme Lingpa) is the 3rd of the ninth month: the Rigpa students' calendar and Jeff Watt's list (tbc:p1, har:p1), and a Tibetan life of a Rwa skor abbot, which dates a teaching «in the ninth month, the 2nd being skipped, on the 3rd, the day of Kun mkhyen 'Jigs med gling pa's passing offering» (tbn:p1, a search snippet).

The Tibetan witness shows the day kept as the 3rd by its number, in a month where the 2nd was skipped; it does not say what happens when the 3rd itself is skipped. Rank 3; his own autobiography and the lives by his disciples were not read for the day.
