# The hal khyi's five mothers and sons are probably the five elements' mother-son pairs

The hal khyi's five mothers and five sons (p. 185: 22 days to each pair, 110 days in all) are most likely the five elements' mother-and-son pairs, one pair at a time, wood-fire, fire-earth, earth-iron, iron-water, water-wood in the order of generation: 5 pairs of 22 days is the 110 of the text. The dictionary «མ་བུ» of black reckoning, «འབྱུང་བ་མ་བུ» (the elements' mother and son), and the manuals' lines on the five elements' «མ་ལྔ་བུ་ལྔ» (a Bon students' textbook, I4PD3282 etext p. 29, a witness only: «ཤིང་མེ་ས་ལྕགས་ཆུ་ལྔ་ཡིས། འབྱུང་བ་མ་ལྔ་བུ་ལྔ་རྩི»; with its table «ཆུ་ལ་ཆུ་མ་ལྕགས། ལྕགས་མ་ས་ལ་ས་མ་མེ…»: each element has one mother and one son) give the pairs. No text gives the pairs a seat or a direction, and the order is the generation cycle's by this reading only. The nine numbers 17–25 of the print after «མ་ལྔ་བུ་ལྔ» and the ten beings are not reconciled: nine places for ten (question 470) stays open. Narrows questions 36 and 433; leaves 470.
