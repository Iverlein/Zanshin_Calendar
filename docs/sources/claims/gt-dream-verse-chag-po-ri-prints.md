# The dream verse of ch. 7 in three Chag-po-ri prints: the unsettled places against the Drathang

Three other open prints of the Four Tantras carry the verse of the bad dreams (the Explanatory Tantra, ch. 7), all Lokesh Chandra Collection scans of the Chag-po-ri (lcags po ri) type on BDRC, each folio side an image of 3500 px (about 55 px a syllable, against about 35 on the Drathang): MW3CN1709 (scans I3CN1711, the Zungchu monastery coloured print; the verse on img. 40), MW3CN1694 (I3CN1696; img. 44) and MW3CN1700 (I3CN1702; img. 36, faint and blurred). None of them is the Serthang print; they are witnesses of the Chag-po-ri tradition and of the machine reader, not proof of the Drathang's own letters.

On the Chag-po-ri copy read best (I3CN1711) the five places stand as ལེན་པ, འདེད, བྲོ་བའི, རྟ་ཕག and རྔ་མོ. That agrees with the commentators who have ལེན་པ (Byang pa, Khro ru, the Drikung) and with Khro ru's བྲོ་བའི, and goes against Byang pa's འདོད and བྲོ་པའི. Against the Drathang's machine reading ལན་པ it differs in the vowel. Line 17 may split the prints: བཟད་པ in the eye reading of I3CN1696 and in the reader's text of I3CN1711, བཟང་བ in the Drathang and in the reader's text of two others; unsettled. No change of sense in any of these.

## RD275 (2026-11-09): two more prints, the Zhol and a second Chag-po-ri

The verse stands, in these two whole-work volumes, in ch. 7 of the Explanatory Tantra, not near img. 36–45 (that figure holds for the Lokesh Chandra scans above and the Drathang only): on the Zhol print MW30137 (BDRC I5910, 2280 px, six lines a page) the verse runs from line 3 of img. 56 into img. 57, and ch. 7's colophon is on img. 59; on the Chag-po-ri print MW1KG13115 (I1KG13487, 2275 px, six lines a page) it is on img. 48 and the first line of img. 49, ch. 10's colophon on img. 55. Both pages were found with Yigdzin-1 (img. 36–45 of I1KG13487, I5907 (verse on img. 66) and I5910 and the cached later images; the verse is not there) and then looked at by eye, 8–14× contrast stretched. Both prints are bilevel or faint scans (the Zhol's glyphs are broken at 8×, the Chag-po-ri's are grey and blurred), so the eye settled less than on I3CN1711.

A third print was found late: the Chag-po-ri reprint MW30134 (I5907, 2000 px, six lines a page, about 45 px a syllable) has the verse on img. 66 (ch. 6's colophon is on its img. 44). Its reader's text has རྟ་ཕག, རྔ་མོ, **བྲོ་པའི** (the only one of the three new prints with པ), འདེད, ལེན་པ and **མི་བཟད་པ**; by eye at 9× the line-17 words stand as མི, then a boxed བ over a ཟ with a ད-type hook and no ring of ང, then པ (weak to moderate); the other places were not looked at.

A fourth print was read last: the Derge MW00EGS1016257 (I00JW501163, 7504 x 1200 px, six lines a page, a crisp bilevel print), where ch. 7's colophon is on img. 44 and the verse on img. 41 (from the third verse line on; the page before holds its first two lines). The reader's text has རྟ་ཕག, **མ་ཧེ་རྟ་ཕག་རོང་བུ་རྔ་མོས** (a different line 3: རོང་བུ for བོང་བུ, and མོས for མོ་ལ), བྲོ་བའི, འདེད, བག་མ་ལེན་པ (the last word split over two lines of the print) and མི་བཟང་བ. By eye at 5×, line 17 on line 4 of the page: མི, then a boxed བ, a ཟ, a ང and a closed boxed བ, then the shad: **མི་བཟང་བ**, good. Line 11 (end of line 2 of the page) is bilevel and broken at 5×: not settled.

The count of readings by print for line 17 (reader unless marked): བཟད་པ on I3CN1711 (reader), I3CN1696 (eye, moderate), the Zhol I5910 (reader, and eye, moderate) and I5907 (reader, and eye, weak to moderate); བཟང་བ on the Drathang (eye, RD264), the Derge I00JW501163 (reader, and eye, good), I3CN1702 (reader) and I1KG13487 (reader; the eye leans the same, weak). Four prints for བཟད་པ, four for བཟང་བ, five of the eight settled to some degree by eye; the Chag-po-ri prints stand on both sides, so the reading does not follow the print's family as far as it was read; the one print where the eye is good, the Derge, has བཟང་བ, as the Drathang has. The same places read the same on both new prints by the reader: རྟ་ཕག, རྔ་མོ, བྲོ་བའི, འདེད, ལེན་པ and འཕན; none of these, on the Zhol and I1KG13487, is a witness against the commentators who have ལེན་པ and Khro ru's བྲོ་བའི, none for ལན་པ, འདོད or བྲོ་པའི. No change of sense in any of these. The Derge prints (I00JW501163, I1KG12862), the Drathang I1KG116089, I3CN1705, I4CZ327147 and the Chag-po-ri copy I1KG10010 and the second Derge volume (MW1KG12776, I1KG12862) were not read to the verse in the time; I1KG10010 and I1KG12862 img. 30–52 were fetched into the shared cache.
