# The Sngags rim chen mo quotes the verse word for word and the reason for the fourth watch is Tsong kha pa's own (RD370)

The six lines that Tsong kha pa quotes in the Sngags rim chen mo as the root tantra's («དང་པོའི་ཐུན་གྱི་རྨི་ལམ་གང། བད་ཀན་ལས་བྱུང་ཤེས་པར་བྱ། ... བཞི་པ་བདེན་པར་འབྱུང་བ་ཡིན།») stand word for word in the Derge Kanjur's etext at [p397]; the same six lines are quoted by the other etexts that the exact-phrase search found (the Rgyud sde kun btus' etext, Shes rab 'od zer's, the Drikung Chos kyi grags pa's, and others). The reason Tsong kha pa gives, that the first three watches are "not certain" (ངེས་པ་མེད་པ) so the dream is looked at in the fourth, is not in these lines: the root tantra names the first three as phlegm, bile and wind (a cause of the body), the fourth as true, and says nothing about which part of the night the pupil should be asked about. The "dawn" (ཐོ་རངས, tho rangs) of the Sngags rim chen mo's question is not in the verse either: the verse says "the fourth", and the other etexts the exact-phrase search found that quote it say, in their own words and beside the verse, that the last part of the night's four is the dawn part (BDRC's snippet of Shes rab 'od zer's etext reads, in Wylie, «mtshan mo'i cha mthun bzhir bcad pa'i tha ma tho rengs kyi cha la brtag par bya zhing», and the Sta gon texts' snippets «nam la cha bzhir byas pa'i tha ma tho rangs kyi dus su rmi lam la rtog dgos»; the Tibetan letters of these were not opened); the root tantra's dream chapter has no word for dawn ("tho rangs") on its pages [p396]-[p405].
