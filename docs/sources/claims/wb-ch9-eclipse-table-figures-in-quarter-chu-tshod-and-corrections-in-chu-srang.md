# WB ch. 9 pp. 60-62: the eclipse table's figures are counted in 15 chu srang, and the courses' corrections of 30, 20 and 40 are chu srang

WB ch. 9 p. 60 (wb.1:p60) and p. 62 (wb.1:p62) turn the remainder of the cut (སྦྱངས་ལྷག, sbyangs lhag) into the figures of the table of the moon's seizing, and the table's figure into the covering's length, in steps the text states in words. p. 60: the cut remainder in chu tshod is multiplied by "sky-taste" (མཁའ་རོ, 60) to make chu srang (སྲང, srang), and the chu srang divided by "arising-moon" (འབྱུང་ཟླ, 5 and 1 read units first: 15) gives the figure («སྲང་བཅུག་འབྱུང་ཟླས ༡༥ ཐོབ་པའི་ནོར») that is looked up in the table (83, 81, 79, 77 for the slow, crooked, arising and fast courses as the limits of total covering, 84 to 92 for fourteen parts, and so on to 183 for one part). By the text's own steps, then, the table's figures are counted in units of 15 chu srang, a quarter of a chu tshod (60 / 15 = 4 to the chu tshod); the figures are neither sixtieths of a chu tshod nor of a day. The 8280 (མཁའ་ནོར་མིག་ཀླུ, units first 0, 8, 2, 8 read as 8280) belongs to the Abridged Tantra's long way, which p. 60 declines to give ("slightly difficult and not understood"); the abbreviated way of the text does not use it, and what it measures is not stated. The four courses (འགྲོས་བཞི, slow དལ་འགྲོས, crooked འཁྱོག་འགྲོས, arising འབྱུང་འགྲོས, fast མྱུར་འགྲོས) are named in the table's four limiting figures and in the corrections of p. 62; the passages in the pool do not define them as stretches of the true reckoning, and no commentary was found that does.

p. 62, the length of the covering (ལྔ་པ་སྒྲིབ་ཡུན, "fifth, the length of the covering"): total covering is fifteen chu tshod, a half seizing seven chu tshod and 30 chu srang; the seized part divided by 3 (མེ), the remainder in chu tshod multiplied by 60, and what 3 gets "is chu srang: the covering, the pressing and the release lengths" («སྒྲིབ་དང་མནན་དང་གྲོལ་ཡུན»). The three words name three lengths: covering (སྒྲིབ, sgrib), pressing (མནན, mnan) and release (གྲོལ, grol). The corrections are then in chu srang (the text says so: «ཆུ་སྲང་མཁའ་མེ ༣༠»): in the fast course «འཛིན་ཡུན་རིང་»  the holding is long by 30 chu srang (sky-fire, 0 and 3: 30) and the release short; in the crooked course «འཛིན་གཏོང་གཉིས» holding and release are both delayed (འགོར) by 20 chu srang (sky-eye, 20), and «མནན་ཡུན» the pressing length is short by 40 chu srang (ཐིག་མཚོ, thig 0 and mtsho 4, units first: 40); the slow and the arising courses take nothing added or taken. So: 30, 20 and 40 are chu srang, thirty sixtieths of a chu tshod being half a chu tshod; the 30 belongs to holding (longer) and, by the word «ཐུང» (short), the release of the fast course; the 20 to holding and release of the crooked course; the 40 to the pressing of the crooked course. Whether the 30 of the fast course is added to the holding and taken from the release in the same amount, and whether the pressing time is the middle of the covering or the whole of the covering, the text does not say in numbers. The lines stand in the same words in four other etexts (the Gser thang and 1996 White Beryl, a collected manual of rtsis, and Bstan 'dzin chos kyi rgyal mtshan's collected works; BDRC search snippets); none has a gloss, and the phrase searches for a commentary that restates them found none.
