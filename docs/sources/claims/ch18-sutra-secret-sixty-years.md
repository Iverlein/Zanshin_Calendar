# The *Sūtra of the Secret*'s list of the sixty years' results, entry by entry (WB ch. 18, pp. 206–209)

The list is the third of the four of the chapter (see claim `ch18-four-lists-of-the-years`). It names each year of the cycle by its Tibetan name together with its element, sex and animal, in the order of the cycle from wood mouse (1) to water pig (60); the name that stands first is the one the table of ch. 18 gives as no. 58, Red-eyed. The results are in the notation of the key (claim `ch18-tiger-ear-key`): the crops with their marks, the sky-army and the drought, rain and hail, the unrest of the king and the minister, the epidemics and the famine. Words that are not settled stay in Wylie. Some entries run over a page break: 17 (pp. 206–207), 37 (pp. 207–208) and 56 (pp. 208–209); their halves are on both pages.

1. Red-eyed (མིག་དམར), wood male mouse: the black [crop] middling; rice and the border lands good; in the middle summer month grain and food are scarce; *sad* comes upon the crops; robbers and thieves are rowdy.
2. Wrathful, masculine (ཁྲོ་བོ), wood ox: *so* and *zar* good; unrest rises and thieves are rowdy; at the end of spring discomfort of many kinds comes; goods and livestock are cheap; epidemics come *khre'ur*.
3. Exhauster (ཟད་པ), fire tiger: disease of the white; the sky-army is raised; rice and the rest good; in the north many people lose face; early drought and hail in spring come.
4. Excellent Arising (རབ་བྱུང), fire female hare: buckwheat and peas good, the white locked up; epidemics come *khre'ur*; little rain; unrest of king and minister and the like; war and unrest; the outer sea dries.
5. Distinct Arising (རྣམ་བྱུང), earth dragon: *so* and *zar* and the black middling (the one mark stands over the three, as the lines run); lord and minister stir; people leave their lands, move and flee (འཕོ་སྐྱས, not settled); many kinds of epidemic.
6. White (དཀར་པོ), earth snake: unrest turns back; "many, and the rule good" (མང་ལ་སྲིད་བཟང, not settled); the minister contends against the king; *nam stod* dry, *nam smad zhod*; buckwheat, peas and the like do not ripen; the white good.
7. Greatly Intoxicated (རབ་མྱོས), iron male horse: *khre* harmed; the sky-army is raised from the sky; heavy rain, *skya rgyal*; the fruit of the trees *g.yer* (not settled); *sad*, and thieves are rowdy; *so ba* and *zar ma* locked up.
8. Lord of Beings (སྐྱེས་བདག), iron sheep: wheat and barley locked up; *gshongs* and disease of the eyes come; many epidemics; sesame and rice good; heavy rain, the year good of itself.
9. Aṅgiras (ཨདྒིར, so printed for the name a ngi ra), water male monkey: *ju tse* wins; rice and *so* locked up; *khre* and the black good; unrest comes in the west; *nam stod* dry; famine in the border lands and *nam smad zhod*; "through merit all that is done is good".
10. Glorious Face (དཔལ་གདོང), water female bird: wheat and barley die; the minister contends against the king; in the middle of summer *skya rgyal* and much bad conduct; famine and the like come; illness and epidemics come suddenly (*khol bur*).
11. Thing (དངོས་པོ), wood dog: rice and *so* good; unrest of king and minister, strong hail in spring; crops sown early good; *nam smad zhod* and swellings increase.
12. Possessing Age (ན་ཚོད་ལྡན), wood female pig: famine comes and unrest in the west and north; the black good, the white middling, *khre* harmed; epidemics come suddenly; the minister contends against the king.
13. Holder (འཛིན་བྱེད), fire mouse: rice and *so* locked up; wheat and barley are both made middling; enemies, thieves and epidemics, much to fear; in early autumn drought comes; in gardens mice do harm.
14. Mighty One (དབང་ཕྱུག), fire female ox: buckwheat and peas damp, the white *wolf*; "as to weapons, water and earth-wind are strong" (མཚོན་དུ་ཆུ་དང་ས་རླུང་ཆེ, the sense not settled); unrest comes suddenly; *ju tse* flourishes, *khre* harmed.
15. Much Grain (འབྲུ་མང་པོ), earth male tiger: famine; hail comes early and suddenly; the black middling; people wander and many hold grudges; the damp wins; epidemics (the next word, དམ, is not settled); "the north sinks, the sky's dew falls on the earth" (བྱང་བྱིང་གནམ་ཞག་ས་ལ་ལྷུང, not settled); a sign for "the long heart-thread" (see the key).
16. Intoxicated (མྱོས་ལྡན), earth hare: *so* and *zar* locked up; the black middling, white mustard good; the damp wins and epidemics are many; "the dew falls on the earth"; property and barley good (དཀོར་ནས་བཟང, not settled).
17. Subduer (རྣམ་གནོན), iron male dragon: wheat and the white good; *so* and *zar* [the entry goes on at the head of p. 207].
18. Foremost of the Herd (ཁྱུ་མཆོག), iron female snake: rice, *so ba*, buckwheat and peas good; in the border lands *ju tse* flourishes; *khre* harmed and epidemics many.
19. Manifold (སྣ་ཆོགས་མིང་ལྡན, "bearing the name Manifold"; the print has «ཆུ་ཕོ་སྟ» for water male tiger), water male tiger: *so ba*, *zar ma* and *ju tse* flourish; the white and the black die; epidemics come suddenly; in the south famine comes.
20. Sun (ཉི་མ), water female sheep: the army stirs; unrest of lord and minister; disease of people and livestock comes; buckwheat and peas damp; *sad* comes; rice, *so* and *khre* good.
21. Sun's Liberation (ཉི་སྒྲོལ་བྱེད), wood male monkey: *so ba*, *zar ma* and *ju tse* good; heavy rain in *nam gzhug* (not settled); thieves are rowdy; the black good, *khre* harmed; *nam stod* dry; in the north-east famine, and epidemics come suddenly.
22. Earth-protector, the strong one (ས་སྐྱོང་སྟོབས་ལྡན), wood female bird: wheat, barley and the black middling; epidemics of people and livestock come *khre'ur*; *sad* comes; *khre* becomes good.
23. Inexhaustible, the treasure-store (མི་ཟད་གཏེར་མཛོད), fire male dog: early drought and epidemics come; famine and thieves; rice, *so*, buckwheat and peas damp.
24. Tamer of All (ཐམས་ཅད་འདུལ), fire female pig: on the earth rain and drought even; *khre* harmed, the year good of itself; unrest within; *so* and *zar* middling.
25. Holder of All (ཀུན་འཛིན), earth male mouse: robbers, thieves and the like, and all the epidemics are many; rice and *so ba* locked up; the black middling, the year good, in mid-autumn famine.
26. Contrary (འགལ་བ), earth ox: *so* and *zar* die; the sky-army of the east, *nam stod* dry; epidemics come; *ju tse* wins; for children "the heart-thread is long" (see the key).
27. Changing (རྣམ་འགྱུར), iron tiger: wheat and barley middling; buckwheat and peas harmed; *so ba* and *zar ma* die; in the direction of the «ལྟོ» (so printed; the south, ལྷོ, would fit) unrest of the season; in the south-west famine; women at ease.
28. Donkey (བོང་བུ), iron female hare: rice and *so ba* only fairly good; the year good of itself, "the track long, spread out" (ཤུལ་རིང་བསྲིང, not settled); the white dies and early drought comes; buckwheat and peas harmed and many lose their lands.
29. Joyful (དགའ་བ), water dragon: disease and unrest come; in the early-middle autumn month the sky-army is rowdy; the minister contends against the king; *khre* good, the white and wheat die; rain falls and buckwheat and peas are locked up.
30. Victorious (རྣམ་རྒྱལ), water female snake: epidemics come *khre'ur*, the *khre* grain good; *nam stod* dry, *nam smad zhod*; the black good, wheat and the white middling; the king comes to contend against the minister.
31. Conqueror (རྒྱལ་བ), wood horse: wins from the head of the year; hunger and thirst "of the door" (སྒོ་ཡི, not settled); *sad* comes; *gshongs* and barley is not brought forth; in early-middle spring epidemics come; much unrest is said to come.
32. Intoxicator (མྱོས་བྱེད), wood sheep: rice and *so* locked up; little rain, the border good, *sad* comes; grain scarce; unrest of lord and minister; famine in the east; the black middling.
33. Ugly Face (གདོང་ངན), fire male monkey: sesame flourishes; wheat and barley both middling; the black good; many enemies; children sing (དྭ་ཕྲུག་གླུ་ལེན, not settled); *nam smad zhod*.
34. Golden Hanging (གསེར་འཕྱང), fire bird: "when the sky is in the east" (གནམ་ཤར་ཚེ); the few win; *khre* dies, *so* and *zar* locked up; disease of people and of livestock comes *khre'ur*; the crops are good in the end; buckwheat and peas damp; the wheel of rule turns back.
35. Distinct Hanging (རྣམ་འཕྱང), earth male dog: the black middling, the white dies; earth-wind strong and *sad* comes; unrest and epidemics come; the white dies and the royal law decays.
36. Transformer (སྒྱུར་བྱེད), earth pig: early hail; *nam stod* dry, *nam smad zhod*; many die by weapon and knife; the black good, *so ba* and *zar ma* middling; livestock good in the later year.
37. Possessing All (ཀུན་ལྡན), iron male mouse: *so ba*, *zar ma* and the white locked up; buckwheat and peas damp and epidemics come; in the middle autumn month "thang bud" is critical (ཐང་བུད་གནད, not settled); *nam smad zhod*, rowdy people appear [the entry ends at the head of p. 208].
38. Leaping (འཕར་བ), iron ox: rice and *so* locked up; wheat and barley middling and epidemics come; snow-white, the sun in the east (གངས་དཀར་ཉི་ཤར, not settled); buckwheat and peas damp; *khre* and sesame middling and unrest within.
39. Joy-maker, the glorious (དགའ་བྱེད་དཔལ་ལྡན; ch. 13's list, and the table above, have དགེ་བྱེད, Virtue-maker), water male tiger: the whites *wolf*; famine from the west; *ju tse* middling; the black middling; the king contends against the minister; *sad* of the trees, *khre* harmed, the fresh green wins (སྔོ་ངད་རྒྱལ, not settled).
40. Beautifier, the beautiful woman (མཛེས་བྱེད་མཛེས་མ), water female hare: famine comes suddenly; epidemics, moving and flight (འཕོ་སྐྱས); wheat and barley locked up; the later year better than the earlier; rice and *so* die and *ju tse* middling; what is called the sky-army, drought, comes.
41. Wrathful, feminine (ཁྲོ་མོ), wood dragon: the white good; rice and *so* locked up, the black middling; rain falls, *skya rgyal*, father and son in conflict; the crops are good in the end.
42. Manifold Wealth (སྣ་ཚོགས་དབྱིག), wood female snake: *ju tse* flourishes for the most part; rice, *so*, wheat and the white middling; *nam stod* dry, *nam smad zhod*; unrest and discomfort of many kinds come; "on the lcog frost-water (ཝ་ཆུ) sets in" (ལྕོག་ལ་ཝ་ཆུ་འཛུགས, not settled).
43. Overpowering (ཐིལ་གནོན), fire male horse: heavy rain and buckwheat and peas damp; barley and sesame die and there is unrest; "the army of the *shwa rags* harms the trees" (not settled); the white locked up, rice and the grains good; epidemics come *khre'ur*.
44. Monkey (སྤྲེའུ), fire sheep: *ju tse* flourishes; *khre* middling, epidemics, and *sad* comes too; the mountain field good, *gshongs* and bad; rain and water even.
45. Peg (ཕུར་བུ), earth monkey: rice and *so* middling; *khre* harmed and the black good; famine comes from the north-east; unrest comes and the later year is good.
46. Peaceful (ཞི་བ), earth bird: the black middling; barley good, rice dies, *so* and *zar* locked up; "the newly born beaten by those in armour" (བཙས་མ་གོ་ཆ་ཅན་གྱིས་བརྔ, not settled); bad for the *rmig pa kha dbrag* (not settled); fighting comes and the head of the year is good.
47. Common (ཐུན་མོང), iron male dog: wheat and the white all good; *nam stod* little rain; buckwheat, peas and *khre* middling; "*so nam* is done by the brave" (not settled); rice and *so ba* good; epidemics come *khre'ur*.
48. Contradictor (འགལ་བྱེད), iron pig: *so* and *zar* locked up; buckwheat and peas damp and bad for the subjects; heavy rain in the south and famine comes; *ju tse* wins and the capital is taken.
49. Fully Holding (ཡོངས་འཛིན), water male mouse: wheat and the white locked up; *khre* and *zar* middling, *ju tse* flourishes; unrest rises and epidemics come *khre'ur*; the seed is got in a cold place.
50. Heedless (བག་མེད), water ox: the white bad; *so ba* and *zar ma* good; the black middling; "wa sa wa rtse brdol" (not settled); on the *lcog* the frost-water sets in; *sad* rises and epidemics come suddenly.
51. All-joyful (ཀུན་དགའ), wood tiger: rice and *so* flourish; the black good; *nam gzhug* short; in the east *mug* comes (མུག, so printed; མུ་གེ, famine, would fit) and thieves are rowdy; fools; hail early; the year is good (ཀླུངས་ཤོད་ལོ).
52. Ogre (སྲིན་པོ), wood hare: *khre* flourishes; the plain fields good and *skya rgyal* comes; in the middle autumn month the year has many enemies; buckwheat and peas do not come in, rice and *so* scarce; *skya rgyal*; the iron ... "weapons become" (ལྕགས་ཕྱ་གོ་མཚོན་འགྱུར, not settled).
53. Fire (མེ), fire dragon: *so* and *zar* good; sesame dies, those with *gang bu* bad (not settled); hail is rowdy and the black middling; *so* and *zar* good and the white middling; a little famine comes.
54. Red-yellow (དམར་སེར), fire snake: the later sowing good; the black good; bad omens, the planets rise; *sad* and hail come and earth-wind is strong; wheat, the white and *ju tse* flourish; the sky-army in the east, much unrest and quarrel.
55. Messenger of Time (དུས་ཀྱི་ཕོ་ཉ), earth male horse: *so ba* and *zar ma* locked up; early drought strong and *sad* comes too; wheat and the white flourish; the black middling; epidemics come *khre'ur*; *khre* harmed; "harm cuts the yoke" (གནོད་པས་གཉའ་བ་གཅོག, not settled).
56. Aim-accomplished (དོན་གྲུབ), earth sheep: father and brothers in conflict; famine comes suddenly; the black die; wheat and the white good [the entry goes on at the head of p. 209].
57. Fierce (དྲག་པོ), iron monkey: *so* and *zar* flourish; the white middling and the black good; grain and food scarce and swellings; disease comes, *gshongs* and flourishes; *nam stod* heavy rain, *nam smad* little.
58. The name is printed གདང་ངན (the table has བློ་ངན, Bad-minded), iron bird: the year is also good; *so* and *zar* harmed and *ju tse* flourishes; "*god* and epidemics come in the south" (གོད, not settled); grain, food and thieves all come.
59. The name is printed ཇ་ཆེན (the table has རྔ་ཆེན, Great Drum), water dog: the white and wheat good; rice and peas win; drought in spring, the commons rise against the king; famine in the west, *ju tse* middling; disease of livestock comes *khre'ur*.
60. The name is printed ཁྲག་སྨྱུག་པ (the table has ཁྲག་སྐྱུག, Blood-vomiting), water female pig: from the west the sky-army rises; the black good, *khre'ur* locked up; many epidemics and the white ripens with difficulty; the plain fields flourish and the end is good.
