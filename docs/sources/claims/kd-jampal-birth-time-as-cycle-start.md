# Mañjuśrī's birth time in the black reckoning: first year, wood male rat, tiger month, the 15th, Saturn, mansion Head

Nor brang O rgyan gives the time of Mañjuśrī's birth, in the view of the "pure" tradition of the Gtsug lag reckoning, as the first year of the cycle, called «mig dmar» (Mars) in the etext, the wood male rat year; the head month (མགོ་ཟླ་བ), the spring month of the tiger, its 15th day; the day-planet Saturn (སྤེན་པ); the mansion «mgo» (the Head); and the hour «re'u dros» (not understood); and that time he places in the first part of the Chinese Zhou dynasty.

Of use as a statement of the convention that the Black reckoning begins its sixty-year cycle with the wood male rat, the tiger month as the spring head and the full moon (15th) as the day; the weekday and mansion of that day and the pairing of Mars with the year are given here without a source and are not a rule for any reading. Modern author, etext only.
