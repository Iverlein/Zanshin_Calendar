# The ages of the old with no kind named: the general rule and the Clear Lamp's sorting

WB's nine kinds of old men and women (བཀྲ, bkra, vol. 2, pp. 77–79) are named by the age at death in pairs of years ("seventy-one, seventy-two") and WB's own text leaves some ages without a name: 61–69 and 83 for men, and 66, 70–71 and 73–77 for women apart from the general *bsrung med bkra* (བསྲུང་མེད་བཀྲ) of the seventies to ninety. Searched in the root text, in Mipham's notes, in LT and UT and in the etexts of the Gser thang and Sakya prints: no passage gives a kind to these ages, and no commentary says what to do. What WB itself gives for them is the general rule that a man from sixty-one and a woman from sixty is old (p. 79), and the *Clear Lamp*'s other sorting of the old, by white, speckled and black according to descendants and companion (pp. 80–81), which does not depend on age. The gap at 83 is one year between *sems med* (81–82) and *smyos bkra rkun bu* (84–85) and looks like a slip of the pairs (each name is given for two years, "rtsa X rtsa Y"). Answered 2026-11-05 (q309, [reckonings.md](../reckonings.md)): WB's own second round on p. 79 takes these ages, by companion and son, not by age. Built 2026-11-06 (block 66): the women's general *bsrung med bkra* keeps the seventies to ninety but the 72nd, and the other round takes a man of 61–69, 83 or from 88 and a woman of 60, 66 or from 91.
