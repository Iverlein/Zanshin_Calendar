# Ch. 7's lines 13 and 15: water and fiends are Byang pa's alone among the etexts found

Which print has water for beer and fiends for the dead in ch. 7's verse (RD271, 2026-11-09; BDRC's exact full-text search, etexts, no scan): the readings of lines 13 and 15 counted.

BDRC's exact search, 2026-11-09: «ཆང་འཐུང་དེས་འདྲུད» (drinking beer with the dead, being dragged) has 7 hits, in the 'Bri gung *Dka' mchan*, the Rgyud bzhi etext, the *'Bum nag gsal sgron*, two further commentaries and others; «ཆུ་འཐུང་དེས་འདྲུད» (water) has 1 hit, in Byang pa's *Bdud rtsi'i chu rgyun* (`bjp`); «ཆང་འཐུང་ངེས་འདྲུད», the Drathang's spelling with ངེས, none (as Yigdzin-1 reads it; the particle is དེས in the others, see the claim gt-dream-verse-variants). «གཤིན་པོ་དག་དང་ལྷན་ཅིག་གར་བྱེད» (dancing with the dead) has 16 hits in 13 etexts (the Drikung, two copies of the Bshad pa'i rgyud, the Rgyud bzhi etext, the *'Bum nag gsal sgron*, the Dri med kun gsal, the *Gso bya lus kyi rnam bshad*, the *Lus kyi 'jig ltas bstan pa* (ch. 7's own etext) and others); «སྲིན་པོ་དག་དང་ལྷན་ཅིག་གར་བྱེད» (with the fiends) has 3 hits, in Byang pa's two etexts and one more commentary (the Rgyud bzhi'i rnam bshad or the Tshig don commentary, which may follow him).

**So:** the water and the fiends are Byang pa's alone among the Four Tantras' etexts found; every other witness has beer and the dead, as the Drathang does. His verse in the etext carries both changes together and his paraphrase repeats the fiends («སྲིན་པོ་དག་དང་ལྷན་ཅིག་ཏུ་གར་བྱེད་པ») and the water («ཆུ་འཐུང་ཞིང་དེས་འདྲུད་པ»). Whether the print before him had them or he changed them is not told by any text found; the weight of witnesses makes them the less likely reading of the Four Tantras (q860), and the sense of the line would not change the rule of ch. 7 (a sign of being in the lord of death's power). Rank: the count is a count of etexts (some are copies of one another), not of independent prints.
