# What the mansion that is lord of the year brings, by pairs of mansions

The chapter's list of what the mansion that is lord of the year brings goes by pairs of the twenty-seven: Aśvinī and Bharaṇī, heavy rain and a strong king; Kṛttikā and Rohiṇī, disease among woodworkers, smiths and cattle, and a quarrelling king; Mṛgaśiras and Ārdrā, no rain and quarrelling kings; Punarvasu and Puṣya, kings reconciled and grain increasing; a pair of mansions named by their synonyms (*wa*, the Great Horse and the Small Horse), rain and a flourishing year for all beings; Uttaraphalgunī and Hasta, a good year in some regions and ripening failed in others; Citrā and Svātī, thieves and fear of the king; Viśākhā and a mansion called "the hand-finger", a good year; Jyeṣṭhā and Mūla, the holy friends prosper; Pūrvāṣāḍhā and Uttarāṣāḍhā, uneven rain, virtue and sin mixed; Śravaṇa and Dhaniṣṭhā, a good year; Śatabhiṣaj and Pūrvabhādrapadā, no disease and a good year; Uttarabhādrapadā and Revatī, a good year, the king keeps the law. The list is read here from the machine's text with one correction on the scan (the synonyms of the third pair); the mansions' own names are checked against the verses of mansion-verses.md, not against this chapter.
