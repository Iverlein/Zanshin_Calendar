# The tables of p. 133 do not gloss «ཁ་ཡོ» or «སྦྲང་མ»

No cell of either table of p. 133 glosses «ཁ་ཡོ» or «སྦྲང་མ». The rat's remedy «ཁ་ཡོ་ནས་འདྲེན» stands beside eleven other remedies that are acts done to the thing at the seat; the thing it is bad for, «ཆང་མ», stands where the other cells name a person (the corpse-bearer, the leader, the earth-diggers, the hunter), so it may be the beer-maker, which narrows nothing on what «ཡོ» is. «སྦྲང་མ» is not in the tables at all.
