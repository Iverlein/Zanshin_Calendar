# The 七夕 strips' five colours and the Edo bamboo over the roofs

Japanese Wikipedia's 七夕 (日本) (read 2026-11-24, facts only): the "five colours" of the strips in the song are the five colours of the Five Phases, green, red, yellow, white and black; strips on bamboo are a Japanese custom of the Edo period. The page quotes the late-Edo *Morisada mankō* and *Tōto saijiki*: every house, rich or poor, set green bamboo hung with strips and coloured paper high on its roof on the 6th; the paper was cut in shapes such as ground cherries, watermelons and account books. Hiroshige's *Tanabata Festival in the Flourishing City* (Meisho Edo hyakkei, 1857, the Met JP62) shows such bamboo over the roofs with strips and cut-paper shapes. Answers q1358.
