# The thirty 'bri wang: only the starting emblems are given

The thirty *'bri wang* (འབྲི་ཝང) or *brda than* (བརྡ་ཐན) of the notes' postscript (wb.2:p538-539b), as Mipham's own print gives their starting emblems (the machine's reading, the 1996 print agreeing but for the first):

| Group of three years | male begins from | female begins from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| rat, dragon, monkey | the tortoise (the 1996: «རུས་སྦལ»; Mipham's print by the machine «བཅུས་སྦལ») | the treasury of wealth |
| pig, sheep, hare | the blue water dragon (ཕོ་ཆུ་འབྲུག་སྔོན་པོ) | the white monkey carrying fire |
| bird, ox, snake | the Chinese dog-headed skull-horn | the reed-bamboo comb |
| tiger, horse, dog | the peacock with crossed necks | the face of the nāga |

The male is taken as "the supporting one", the female as "the one leaned on"; one counts to one's own age in years and tells the result "as the text says". Neither the thirty nor their results are given: «འདི་ལ་དཔེ་ཕལ་ན་བརྩི་ཚུལ་མི་འདུག», "no ordinary copy shows how they are reckoned", so Mipham gives only where each begins. BDRC's full-text search for «འབྲི་ཝང་སུམ་ཅུ» finds nothing and for «བརྡ་ཐན་སུམ་ཅུ» only the 1996 and Gser thang prints of WB and a Nyingma treasure text on the thirty signs of women (not the same list; 2026-11-01): the text that gives the results is not found (question 351).
