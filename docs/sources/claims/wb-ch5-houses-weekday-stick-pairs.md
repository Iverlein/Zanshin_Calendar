# The twelve houses' pairs of the Kar myang reckoning: a weekday and a dbyug

Each of the twelve houses has a pair of numbers: the first is a weekday number (0 to 6; the divisor of the last line is 7), the second a count of *dbyug* (the sixtieth, *dbyu gu*, of a day, always under 60). The verse names them «གཟའ» (planet = weekday) and «དབྱུག» (stick): Aries (1, 57), Taurus (4, 53), Gemini (1, 17), Cancer (4, 53), Leo (1, 22), Virgo (4, 24), Libra (6, 51), Scorpio (1, 47), Sagittarius (3, 16), Capricorn (4, 37), Aquarius (6, 4), Pisces (0, 52). To each the "not-errors" are added, the sum multiplied by 60 and the remainder after throwing away sevens taken: the house that rises is the planet of the twelve houses.

Check by the arithmetic: the step from each house to the next, taken modulo 7 days, is 2.93, 3.40, 3.60, 3.48, 3.03, 2.45, 1.93, 1.48, 1.35, 1.45 and 1.80 days; add 28 and these are 30.93, 31.40, 31.60, 31.48, 31.03, 30.45, 29.93, 29.48, 29.35, 29.45, 29.80 days, the shape of the sun's passage through the signs (longest in Gemini to Cancer, shortest in Sagittarius to Capricorn). So the pairs are the day (weekday and sixtieths) at which the sun reaches each house in the Kar myang reckoning. The twelfth step (Pisces to Aries) is 29.08 days, as it must be if the Aries pair is the next year's (a year of 365.24 days is 1.24 days beyond 52 weeks). This reading is the arithmetic's, not a statement of the page.
