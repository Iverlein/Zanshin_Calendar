# The sky dog of particular weight for a bride; no earth lord among the unmendable

Among the earth lords the manuals single out the sky dog (གནམ་ཁྱི, gnam khyi) for a bride's going: the supplement to the *Moonbeams* calls its points «of particular weight for a bride» (kd.3:p641-642), Lha mo tshul khrims gives it a head of its own (lt:p38-41), and the *Garland of Blue Utpalas* calls avoiding the faces of the year's, month's, day's and hour's bad earth lords in the direction of the going «the crux» and the day's and month's sky dog «the chief» (ut:p240-243; read from BDRC's etext only, no scan of the edition being on BDRC). This ranks within the earth lords and within the bride's going only. The one grade Lha mo tshul khrims sets across the checks is that three cannot be mended, the mansion *lha mtshams*, the weekday Mars and the sky year's fire rising, and no earth lord is among them (lt:p38-41).
