# The Dpe bsdur ma's notes at the Svapnohana's watch lines

The Dpe bsdur ma's notes for the Svapnohana's watch verse (printed p. 1301): entry 4, Peking and Narthang «པ», belongs to the second watch's first segment; entry 5 is the Peking's omission of the fourth watch's line; entry 6, Peking and Narthang «བར», belongs to its end. No entry records a Co ne variant at the watch lines, and none records a variant of the third watch's line. The Narthang and Peking «བརྒྱད་པ་ལ» is therefore the editors' variant against the base text, which answers the first half of q874; whether the base itself reads «པར» (as the entries 4 and 6 and the Co ne machine tally would together suggest) or «བར» (as the OCR gives it) is not settled by the OCR. The editors' silence is weak evidence of agreement: the Peking's «བར་དུ» at the third watch's end, seen by eye on the print, is recorded by no entry.
