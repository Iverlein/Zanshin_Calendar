# The Kanjur sūtra's remedy for the silent Tathāgata dream: the Three Heaps recited for seven years at the first stage down to one at the seventh (RD395, q1089)

Toh 48 gives a remedy that falls with the stage of the bodhisattva who had the dream of the Tathāgata who does not speak: seven years of the daily recitation for the first stage, six for the second, five for the third, four for the fourth, three for the fifth, two for the sixth, one for the seventh. The person is first "to be taken as on any one of the first seven stages". The remedy is a count of years, not of nights, and no part of the night, day of the month or hour of the dream is named.

| Stage (sa) | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Years of reciting the Three Heaps | 7 | 6 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 |

Read in the etext only; the same page goes on to other dreams of the Tathāgata.
