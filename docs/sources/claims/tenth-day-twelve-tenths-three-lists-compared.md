# The twelve tenths in three lists: the same deeds in the same order, 'Jigs med gling pa's months one later than the animal months

Three lists of Padmasambhava's deed on the tenth day (ཚེས་བཅུ, tshes bcu) of each of the twelve months, set side by side. JGL: 'Jigs med gling pa's prayer (jgl.8:p739-742, scan), which numbers the birth month and names the others by lunar mansion. SGC: the prayer Drop of Blessing in the liturgy collection of Sgo chen dgon, by 'Jam mgon Kong sprul under his treasure-revealer's name (sgc:i208-214, scan), which names every month by its animal and the tenth as that of the waxing half (ཡར་ངོ, yar ngo). ZGP: Zhe chen rgyal tshab's prose summary of "the texts of the Benefits of the Tenth from the treasures of Mgon po rin chen and others" (zgp.13:p537-540, machine reading), by animal month. The Hor number in brackets is the one the White Beryl's model almanac gives the name (wb.1:p154-171: Mgo and the tiger Hor 11, Rgyal and the hare 12, the dragon 1, Dbo and the snake 2, Nag and the horse 3, Sa ga and the sheep 4, Snron and the monkey 5, Chu stod and the bird 6, Gro bzhin and the dog 7, Khrums and the pig 8, Tha skar and the mouse 9, Smin drug and the ox 10; the almanac prints «རྟ» for the first month's mansion, where the prayer has Mchu).

The three lists hold the same twelve deeds in the same order. SGC and ZGP agree month for month; JGL stands one month later than both in every row when its names are numbered by the White Beryl (claim tenth-day-months-jigme-lingpa-one-month-later-than-the-animal-lists). The deeds differ in three details: the master of the going forth (claim tenth-day-going-forth-under-ananda-or-prabhahasti), who burned him in Uḍḍiyāna, and the names of the Guru joined to the months, which SGC and ZGP give differently for the monkey, hare, snake, horse, dog, pig and mouse months (ZGP's names read by machine only). ZGP's order of telling starts at the monkey month and runs on through bird, dog, pig, mouse and ox to tiger and sheep; SGC puts the monkey month first and then runs from the tiger month.

Padma gling pa's Benefits of the Guru's Tenth Day (pgl.1:p835-837, scan) is not a fourth list of this kind: it names twelve events as all having fallen "at the time of the tenth" without a month (the light sent out by Amitābha, the birth, the prince, the kingdom given up, the Cool Grove, the gurus of India, the coming to Tibet, the gods and demons bound, the empowerments, the places blessed, the counsel to those to come, the going to Rnga yab), and then gives a benefit for each animal month (claim tenth-day-benefit-of-each-animal-month-padma-lingpa).

Not read: Bdud 'dul rdo rje's and Bdud 'joms Rin po che's texts; the treasure text of Mgon po rin chen that SGC's colophon and ZGP both name as their base.
