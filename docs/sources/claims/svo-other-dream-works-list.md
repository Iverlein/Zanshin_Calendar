# The dream works of the Tengyur and Kanjur: eleven, of which only two name a part of the night (RD381)

Eleven dream works are named in the catalogues and etexts read: in the Tengyur the Svapnohana (no author named by any catalogue, Vibhūticandra's own translation), Advayavajra's Svapnekṣā (Prajñāśrījñānakīrti's own translation) and his Svapnanirdeśa (Vajrapāṇi and Nag tsho Tshul khrims rgyal ba), Candramitra's Dream Tārā sādhana (Sugataśrī and Byams pa'i dpal), three pieces in Ajitamitragupta's Avalokiteśvara run (the Turning back of the bad dream, the Showing of dreams, the Meditation on the stainless dream; translated by Gnubs Byams pa'i dpal bzang po), the Padma-net offering rite's dream night and a dream test at the end of a Vajrapāṇi rite; in the Kanjur the Showing of Dreams sūtra (Ratnakūṭa ch. 4, Toh 48) and the dhāraṇī Seeing a dream (Toh 460A, also listed as Toh 952). Only the Svapnohana and the Svapnekṣā name a part of the night; the dream rites name the dawn (the Turning back of the bad dream, the Showing of dreams, the Padma-net rite), or no hour at all.
