# The mewa of falling ill: illness, spirits, ransom and the spirit's kind

The mewa (སྨེ་བ, sme ba) under which one fell ill names the illness, the spirits and a ransom sent in its colour: one white eyes and dizziness, the god-spirits, *rgyal po* and ground lords; two black a severe illness of eyes and stomach, demons; three blue dropsy and tumours, the earth *srin* and ground lords; four green pox, wasting and leprosy, ground lord and nāga *sman*; five yellow dumbness and stomach, the *gnam the* and *rgyal po*; six white mouth, bones and heart, the *rgyal po* of an empty house; seven red the *btsan*'s stabbing, hot and blood illness; eight white madness and stabbing, the angered male god and war god; nine red maroon illness and plague of the pregnant, demons and the *bstan ma*. The ransom is sent at the mewa's time: the whites at sunrise, black and blue at sunset, green at «མྱུར་སྨད», yellow and the reds when the sun is warm, or at noon. One's own birth mewa fought against the descending mewa then names the spirit's kind: the mother or itself the going god, the enemy or son the debt demon, the friend the *rter btsog* demon, each with its rites (wb.1:p433-435).
