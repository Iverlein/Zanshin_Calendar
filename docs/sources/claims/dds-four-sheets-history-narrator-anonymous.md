# The «I» who received the two short texts alone is not named in the history; two Drukpa records credit the history, guidance and oral instructions to the great siddha Gling, who follows Bde legs rin chen in their chain (RD383)

RD383. The «I» (བདག) of the history is not named in any print. He says he asked for the sheets after the monk practitioner and his fellow students had offered three times and the sheets had been given to the last of them, and that he received «the two short texts» (the Net-eyed wheel of channels and winds and Cutting the bardo by mind, whose Indian roots he says later came from the lama Sum pa ras pa) with the oral instruction «to me alone» (dds.19:p97-102). He speaks of Bde gshegs rin chen in the third person, as a man who made request of Nyi ma lung pa, so the «I» is not Bde gshegs rin chen by the words; the history's chain ends with Bde gshegs rin chen. Two Drukpa records name the maker of «the history, guidance and oral instructions» of the four sheets as the great siddha Gling and put Gling right after Bde legs rin chen in the chain (dds.42:p165-166, dds.43:p138-139); that is consistent with the «I» being Gling, a disciple of Bde gshegs rin chen, but no line read says that the «I» is Gling, and the two records are late and not dated in the lines read. A doubt, not a finding.
