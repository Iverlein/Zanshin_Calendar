# The Mchog dga' print (img. 626) writes ཐུན་ཕྱེད and བརྒྱད, and no number follows «ཆུ་ཚོད་རེ་བཞི་བར» (RD341, q987)

On the National Library of Mongolia dbu can print of the Mchog dga' (img. 626, the page that holds the end of chapter 9's half-watch Rahu text) the sentence on the unit of the half-watch's four dbyu gu reads as in the Kun 'dus chen mo etext: the 56 and a quarter chu srang (ཆུ་སྲང་ང་དྲུག་རྐང་བཅས) is the chu tshod, and the day-and-night is then made «ཆུ་ཚོད་རེ་བཞི་བར». The sentence closes there with «བྱེད་དགོས་སོ»; no number follows it on the scan, so the etext did not cut a number off: sixty-four is the Karma pa gsum pa's figure (svo:p271z), not this print's. The print writes «ཐུན་ཕྱེད» and the eight as «བརྒྱད»; the etext's «ཐུན་ཕྱིད» and «བརྒྱུད» are the etext's. Doubt: whether the print's last word is «བཞི་བར» or «བཞིར», at the crop's resolution.
