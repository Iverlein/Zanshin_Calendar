# The trigram's grave joined: a broken line

WB's trigram point (vol. 1, p. 362) lists the joinings of a trigram with
the grave (དུར, dur) of its element as "broken line" (རབས་ཆད, rabs chad):

| One spouse born on | The other born in | The grave |
| --- | --- | --- |
| zin (wood), the husband | the five sheep years, the bride | wood's grave, the sheep |
| dwa (iron), the bride | the five ox years, the husband | iron's grave, the ox |
| li (fire), the husband | the five dog years, the bride | fire's grave, the dog |
| kham (water), the bride | the five dragon years, the husband | water's grave, the dragon |

The graves are ch. 21's (p. 258: wood's the sheep, fire's the dog, iron's
the ox, earth's and water's the dragon). Mipham's notes (vol. 2, p. 526)
explain the first and extend it to the rest: "a broken line: the husband's
birth trigram zin and the bride a wood sheep or another sheep year, then,
the «ཤིད་དུར» being the sheep, bad; and so on." The typeset notes print
«ཤིད་དུར» (the Gser thang reprint, l. 4, at 5×); Mipham's own print of the
notes carves «ཤིང་དུར» (I2029 img. 720, l. 2, by eye at 4×), wood's grave,
the trigram zin being wood and wood's grave the sheep.
