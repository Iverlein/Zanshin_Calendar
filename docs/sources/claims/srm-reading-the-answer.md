# Śramaṇā rite: how the answer is read, changed and repeated

How the answer is read and treated in the Śramaṇā text. A dream is only provisionally settled as good or bad by the instruction's strength; it can change by the strength of virtue and sin (dge sdig). A good one becomes still better; for a bad one a means of turning it back is asked for in the request and, when shown, performed. The unclear answers (given as question and answer, or as signs and hints) are judged by discernment or all dropped and the request repeated, after which the answer becomes clear. Success in 3, 5 or 7 nights; if it does not come it shows heavy shadow (grib) and sin: shadows are avoided, the boundary kept, confession made; three repetitions of the request cannot fail, it is said. What comes is not told to others, for then it is unclear.
