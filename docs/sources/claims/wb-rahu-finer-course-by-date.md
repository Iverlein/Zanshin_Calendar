# Rāhu's finer course by date, with what to do and avoid

The finer way of Rāhu's course, dated, and what to do and avoid (WB vol. 2, p. 237). 4th: dusk, enters the west, dwells two days in the east; good for seeking work, taking a bride, trade, felling trees, white acts, brewing; the funeral feast and ending a quarrel. 6th: goes round the west; good for turning-back rites, burnt offering, rain. 8th: enters the south-east, early morning in the north-west; severe talk, attack, theft, the oath-sending are good; washing clothes, taking a bride and washing bad; three days in the north-west. 12th: enters the north door at dusk; washing, feast, paying debts, washing the hair, sowing are good; bondsmen's day and taming bad; two days in the south. 15th: enters the south-west at dawn, the north-east at half day; offerings to the gods, taking back fortune, practice, empowerment good; paying back, the oath, taking a bride and giving a child bad. 17th: hail work. 18th: monastery, ordination, temple, stūpa, taking the road, drawing the army good; the oath, prostration, fort work, the door, the thatch bad. 21st: fierce work. 22nd: craft, writing and arithmetic, learning, water channels, deposits good; seeking a house, grave or ground, taming bad. 24th: hail and cloud. 25th: buying a field, fort work good. Where the dates are not named the text does not say.
