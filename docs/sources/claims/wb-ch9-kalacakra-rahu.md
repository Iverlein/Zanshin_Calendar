# WB ch. 9: the Kālacakra's account makes Rāhu half the sphere, head and tail of beryl, and body empty space

The definitive meaning as p. 57 gives it (machine reading): outwardly Rāhu's body covers half of the sphere (གོ་ལ); head and tail are of the nature of beryl; the parts of the body are empty space; those with merit travel clockwise, while the sun, moon and other planets go anticlockwise; whatever the body meets, there is no obscuring. The beryl's light dims the sun's and moon's crystal lustre when head or tail meets them. Inwardly: the white element 'haṃ' at the top of the central channel, the red at its lower tip, the eight petal-channels of the heart with the eight planets, the sun channel on the right (red-yellow) and the moon channel Iḍā on the left; a solar eclipse arises when the white and red are joined, the moon alone enters with Iḍā's element; consciousness is Rāhu, and in the womb it goes with the white and red elements to make the body.
