# The Derge print reads «བགྲོད་མིན», «བཅུ་ཡི་བར» and «བླ་མོ»

On the Derge Tengyur frames (I1344 img. 268 line 7, img. 270 lines 3 and 5, crops of 10–20×, Yigdzin-1 on line crops as second witness) the Svapnohana reads «བགྲོད་མིན» (not «བཟློག»), «བཅུ་ཡི་བར» (no variant) and «རྟ་བླ་མོ» with a subjoined ླ (two stacked loops, moderate-low), so the Derge agrees with its etext, while the Peking and Narthang prints agree with each other on a plain ལ against it. The word after it reads «ལྟོ» (the belly-walker, ལྟོ་འཕྱེ, lto 'phye), the distinction from «ལྷོ» not made at 8 px. The Derge frames give no sense for the clause (q813) and no unit for the ten (q814); the Co ne by eye was not reached.
