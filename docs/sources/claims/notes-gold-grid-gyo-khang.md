# The gold reckoning's grid: gyo khang on Mipham's own print

The gold reckoning's grid: Mipham's own print (I2029 img. 744, l. 2, the stack by eye at 9×; Yigdzin-1 the same) reads «ཞག་གི་ལོ་འབྱུང་རྣམས་གྱོ་ཁང་རེའུ་མིག་བཞིན», with «གྱོ» (a ག with ya-btags and the ོ sign above it, no ར over it), not «རྒྱ་ཁང» of Yigdzin-1 on the 1996 print: the grid with little eyes (རེའུ་མིག) is the «གྱོ་ཁང», as the 1996 etext reads it (question 352); the word itself, a grid or board of the gold reckoning, is not explained in the notes.
