# The doors of sky and earth: trigrams by the mewa, the se zhig years, the black days

WB vol. 2, p. 42: the doors of sky and earth by the mewa. The sky door's mewa are 3, 5 and 6 and the earth door's 2 (black) and 4 (green); the standard trigram of each mewa then fixes the printed trigram names: 6 is *khen* (sky), 3 is thunder (ཐོག, thog = *zin*), 5 is the centre; 2 is *khon* (earth), 4 is the wind trigram (*zon*), so the earth door's «བུ» [?] and the sky door's «མ» [?] are reading slips for these (the sky door: *khen* and thunder; the earth door: *khon* and the wind trigram). That is a deduction from the mewa, not read; in vol. 1 WB writes a trigram on which a year falls as «ཁེན་ཐོག», «ཟིན་ཐོག» (the trigram *khen* "on top": ch. 22, vol. 1 etext pp. 316–336), so «ཁེན་ཐོག» of p. 42 is "on *khen*" (the progressed year falling on it), not two trigrams. The year words: the dictionary of the *Bod yig tshig gter* has «སེ་ཞིག» as a term of the element reckoning («སེ་ཞིག་ལོ་པ»: a year whose power element is the son of its life element) and none for «ས་ཞིག»; vol. 1's tables carry «སེ་ཞིག» too (vol. 1 etext pp. 115, 117), so «སེ་ཞིག» is the reading; the dictionary does not say which animal years bear the term, so that the pig and snake are those is WB's own sentence, not checked. The black days of the sentence «ཉི་ནག་དྲུག་གསུམ» are two lists: the great black day (ཉི་ནག, nyi nag) of the month (WB p. 226, great-black-day.md) and "the three sixes" (དྲུག་གསུམ), the 6th, 16th and 26th, by the chapter 31 usage that «X་གསུམ» is the Xth, X+10th and X+20th; the line then goes on with the earth lords' *gnyan* days.
