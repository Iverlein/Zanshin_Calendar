# «བསྒྲིལ་བས» sums the list before it where it can be tested; the fourth's 87 is not such a sum

«བསྒྲིལ་བས» (bsgril bas, "rolled together") occurs twice in WB vol. 2, pp. 44–143 and the notes pp. 490–522 (Yigdzin-1's reading of img. 52–151 and 498–530 searched): at the fourth examination's close (p. 47, "with the verdict, eleven pebbles; rolled together, 87") and at the twelfth's (p. 50, «བསྒྲིལ་བས་རྡེའུ་བཞི་བཅུ་འབྱུང་», "rolled together, forty come", of the la-stone's 5, the not-allowed 26, the repair's 6 and the three's result pebbles, which is 5 + 26 + 6 + 3 = 40 exactly). So where the verb can be tested it is a sum of the items listed just before it, results included. Applied to the fourth, that would be the 11 pebbles or the 66 with the roots, and neither is 87; the fourth's own «བསྒྲིལ་བས» gives a figure that does not belong to its list. The first examination's 87 is stated at the foot of p. 45 in different words (གྱ་བདུན, gya bdun) against the fourth's «བརྒྱུད་ཅུ་བདུན» (brgyad cu bdun, spelt with u); the Taikhang print has the fourth's line the same. Mipham's notes (pp. 499–522, Yigdzin-1's reading searched for «བརྒྱད་ཅུ་བདུན» and «གྱ་བདུན») say nothing on the fourth's total. The question stays open on the evidence: 87 is not a sum of the fourth's pebbles and no commentator in the notes explains it.
