# The Guru's tenth days in Jeff Watt's list follow 'Jigs med gling pa's months; the houses keep the birth on 5/10

Jeff Watt's list gives the Guru's day under six names (har:p1): 2/10 Shakya Sengge and Loden Chogse, 6/10 Padmasambhava, Tsokye Dorje and Sengge Dradog, 8/10 Nyima Özer, 10/10 Pema Jungne, 11/10 Dorje Drolo, 12/10 Pema Gyalpo. Set against the twelve tenths of claim tenth-day-twelve-tenths-three-lists-compared, the months are 'Jigs med gling pa's (JGL), one month after the animal-month lists of Sgo chen's liturgy (SGC) and Zhe chen rgyal tshab (ZGP):

| HAR | Deed (the three lists) | JGL | SGC/ZGP |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 6/10 Tsokye Dorje | the birth | 6 | 5 (monkey) |
| 12/10 Pema Gyalpo | enthroned in Uḍḍiyāna | 12 (Rgyal) | 11 (tiger) |
| 2/10 Shakya Sengge, Loden Chogse | the going forth | 2 (Dbo) | 1 (dragon) |
| 8/10 Nyima Özer | poisoned, his radiance greater | 8 (Khrums) | 7 (dog) |
| 10/10 Pema Jungne | the gods and demons of Tibet bound | 10 (Smin drug) | 9 (mouse) |
| 11/10 Dorje Drolo | the treasures hidden | 11 (Mgo) | 10 (ox) |

The one exception is Sengge Dradog, whom SGC and ZGP give the sheep month (4) and JGL's numbering would give 5: Watt puts him on 6/10 with the birth.

The houses read keep the birth in the fifth month: Ka-Nying Shedrub Ling marks the Guru's birthday on the 10th of the 5th month (ksl:p2), the Mindroling rule-book's great tenth with dances is in the fifth month (mdb:p177; mlc:p1, 5/8 to 5/10 in 2026). That is the monkey month of the animal lists and of the White Beryl (claim monkey-month-is-the-fifth-hor-month-snron), not 'Jigs med gling pa's sixth. The Rigpa students' yearly list has no tenth of the Guru at all (tbc:p1).
