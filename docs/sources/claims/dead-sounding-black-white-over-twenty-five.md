# The sounding's black and white are counted over the twenty-five deciding pebbles together

In the copper vessel's sounding (ཟངས་ཀྱམ་སྒྲ་འབྱིན, zangs kyam sgra 'byin, WB vol. 2, p. 91) the closing summary, "two black: the twenty-five demons; two white: the twenty-five great gods; one black: the twenty-five *'dre* and *srin*; one white: the twenty-five well-beings", counts over the **twenty-five deciding pebbles** (ཁ་དམར, kha dmar) together, wherever the whites and blacks fall among them, and not over one place's pair or one column of four. Mipham's note on the sounding says so: the hundred cutting and splitting pebbles lie in a grid twenty-five across and four down (life force, body, power, luck), the verdict pebbles are the twenty-five that come of five groups of five (the note names those of the clan, the year, the day and the hour, and the month is read in with them), the root text's "in general know thus" and "the two whites and the rest, wherever they fall" are the general statement, and "whichever demon falls" and "the great gods, sons for people" give the results (the notes, p. 506, read on the scan 2026-10-23).
