# The Yoginisancara line on the tenth stands in ch. 10 of Toh 375, on the torma and the offering on the tenth

The line «the tenth of the mid-winter month and the others, honour with faith» that Tsong kha pa, Zhe chen and the Bde mchog drug cu ma's commentary give as from the Yoginīsañcāra stands in the Derge Kanjur's Yoginīsañcāra (Toh 375) at its tenth chapter, «the torma and the offering on the tenth, the teaching of guarding» (gtor ma dang mchod pa tshes bcur bya ba srung ba bstan pa), Derge pp. 79-80 (ysn:p79-80). The Derge wording is «དགུན་ཟླ་འབྲིང་པོ་ལ་སོགས་པའི། །ཚེས་བཅུ་ལ་ནི་དད་པས་མཆོད།», not «དགུན་འབྲིང་ལ་སོགས་ཟླ་བ་ཡི། །བཅུ་པ་ལ་ནི་…» as quoted. The chapter has two settings of the tenth: «the tenth of the waning moon and the same of the waxing moon» with a torma, beer and the offering to the heroes and the vajra goddesses with the mantras OM ĀRALLI HOḤ and the rest, facing south at a high place; and «the tenth of the mid-winter month and the others» honoured with faith, with a meal whose remainder is kept in a vessel, so that the glory that comes into the hand cannot be destroyed; and again at night, with loosened hair and naked, the torma that pleases the Mothers. So this tantra gives the tenth of both halves and then of the months by name, with the torma and the heroes' feast. Etext reading of the Derge Kanjur, no scan opened.
