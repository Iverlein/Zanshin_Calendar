# The kung of the hand reckoning is told by no text; one lead: gung, noon

The kung (ཀུང) of «ཐུན་ཀུང» in the notes' hand reckoning (wb.2:p538-539) is told by no text found: «ཐུན་ཀུང» occurs nowhere else in the etexts of WB, KD vol. 3, LT, UT, the Bhutan collection or the *Lotus Pond* (the 1996 etext of the notes reads «ཐུན་ཀཱང»). One lead from the same notes: Mipham writes the noon among the day's hours as «གུང» («གུང་གཉིས་ཟེར་གཉིས», the two noons and two rays, wb.2:p531), so «ཀུང» after the watch (ཐུན, thun) may be a spelling of «གུང», noon: the count would then run year, month, date, watch and noon-division, all divisions of the day. No text says so; it is a lead, not a reading.
