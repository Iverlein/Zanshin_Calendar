# The dead's burial reckoning: eleven root pebbles of time, the mewa and trigram among them

The *Moonbeams*' third part, the dead's burial and funeral reckoning, sets eleven root pebbles of time (dz:i057-061): the sky year, the progressed year (ལོག་མེན, log men), the mewa, the trigram, the rise and decline, the month, the day, the hour, the planet, the mansion and the living's year. The dead's life force, body, power, luck and clan element fight each, five a root, and "examine the rise and decline" on it. The same eleven, less the living's year, return in the repetition, the next birth and the closing rite; the dead's own trigram has a group of its own (the trigram's illness lines, demons and the rest).

Stated: the mewa and trigram among the root pebbles are listed with the time, after the sky year and the progressed year. Not stated: whether they are the year's own (as the almanac gives them) or the dead's descended ones in the year of death; the order of the list sets them with the year's items. The rise and decline is examined by the dead's aspects on each root pebble, the planet among them, not taken from the season alone.
