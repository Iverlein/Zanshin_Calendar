# WB ch. 9 gives the side of an eclipse's seizing and the side of its recovery

Seventh and eighth points (machine reading of p. 67): the seizing begins from where it first seizes and goes leftward, obscuring in order; the shadow remains on the west side of the seized quarter. Recovery begins from the quarter that was seized; for a whole seizure it recovers from the body not seized, going leftward in order.
