# The thirty: the twenty-seventh point, the golden crossed vajra, for men, at the place of the life force

The twenty-seventh of the thirty starting points (སྐོར་འགོ, skor 'go), from the mouth of the golden crossed vajra (གསེར་གྱི་རྡོ་རྗེ་རྒྱ་གྲམ, gser gyi rdo rje rgya gram), is read for men, at the place of the life force (སྲོག, srog). Its eight pebbles are laid as for every point (claims thirty-count and thirty-eight-fighters), each white or black, and read so:

| Pebble | Asks | White | Black | Remedy for black |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Whether the tree of life is broken | The tree of life grows | Broken | Planting the dagger that supports the life of people and wealth helps |
| 2 | Whether life is deceived by demons | Not deceived | Deceived | Offering to the gods (ལྷ་གསོལ, lha gsol), ransoming the la, the life-spirit (བླ, bla) and the like |
| 3 | Whether the la and life are carried off by spirits | Not carried off | Carried off: recite the *nye'u* (ཉེའུ, nye'u; not identified); the life ransom and life-castle have collapsed | Set up the life-castle (སྲོག་མཁར, srog mkhar) and the like |
| 4 | Whether the forefathers' death spirits lurk | They do no harm | They harm | Offer to the grave's *mtshun* (དུར་མཚུན་གསོལ, dur mtshun gsol) |
| 5 | Whether one has fallen into the mouth of the maternal uncle's luck-spirit (མ་ཀླུངས་འདྲེ, ma klungs 'dre) | It does no harm | It harms | Binding with the life dagger and the life peg |
| 6 | Whether the outer net of the trigram (སྤར་ཁ, spar kha) is torn | Not torn | Torn | The trigram's rite |
| 7 | Whether the inner life has entered the year's tomb | The year-house has not entered the tomb | It has entered the tomb | Recite the tomb multiple (དུར་མིག, dur mig) |
| 8 | Whether the secret nine-multiple (དགུ་མིག, dgu mig) has fallen | Not entered; no illness in the body | The lasting nine-multiple has entered | The rite of the year-star nine-multiple |

Whose year is reckoned: the person's own (the notes, vol. 2, p. 533: «གཞན་རྣམས་སོ་སོའི་ལོ་རྟ»).

Which of the eight fighting pebbles is which check is not stated; the order of p. 453 is the likely one (claim thirty-board-groups; question 259).
