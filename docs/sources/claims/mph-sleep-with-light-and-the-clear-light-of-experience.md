# Mipham: falling asleep in light-perception; the clear light of experience as a warning; no phrase for the state (RD389)

In Mipham the words for falling asleep with awareness are practice instructions, not a naming of a state. (1) In the Padma dkar po (reset copy, vol. 1 image 13) one is told to fall asleep "in the state of the perception of light" (སྣང་བའི་འདུ་ཤེས, snang ba'i 'du shes), the Teacher's body-light making all directions like a bright day; the Sde dge print (I4PD554 p. 992-993, poor OCR) gives the sutra behind it («Don rnam par nges pa», the light perception as a way to see wisdom), where a monk holds the perception of day by night and of night by day. (2) In his Dzogchen volume «Gnyug sems skor gsum dang gzhung spyi'i dka' gnad» (MW1KG13017 and the excellent copy MW3CN4311, p. 165-166) "the clear light of experience" (ཉམས་ཀྱི་འོད་གསལ, nyams kyi 'od gsal), clear and cloudless like the sky, is said to arise from holding the mind by the thin-line practice, and he judges it a meditation that does not liberate (the person knows to meditate but not to be freed, like a god of meditation): the same word as in the Gdams ngag mdzod text of rrd.21:p19, used by Mipham as a warning, not as a sign. (3) No Mipham text found says "sleep and not-sleep" (BDRC author facet P252: 0 hits; the unfiltered 22 hits belong to other authors) or "between waking and dream".
