# The rebirth by the mewa of death, with the image that turns it

The fifth examination's detailed reading by the mewa (སྨེ་བ, sme ba) of death, with the image to make or the practice to do to turn a bad birth (wb.2:p70-72; the notes, wb.2:p503-504, for two black).

| Mewa of death | Rebirth | Remedy, and the birth if it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 white | a man keen on the Dharma | an image of Avalokiteśvara, the six syllables recited |
| 2 black, with year, month, day and hour all black | hell, hard to turn | — |
| 2 black otherwise (the mewa of death alone) | a bear's or monkey's body | a stūpa and an image of Vajrapāṇi; the result changed |
| 3 blue | an ox or a snowcock | the hundred-syllable Vajrasattva and alms: a girl of another tongue in the west |
| 4 green | a cuckoo | a Vajrapāṇi image and *tsha tsha*: a rich man's daughter in the west |
| 5 yellow | a monkey or a cock | an image of the Sage and the *Diamond Cutter*: a man in the south |
| 6 white | a bad person or a girl | an image of the Sugata Uṣṇīṣa as la-image: one of good qualities in the south |
| 7 red | a demon or a wolf | an image of Tārā: a monk |
| 8 white | a woman | an image of the Teacher: a sculptor of gods |
| 9 red | a dog or a donkey | an image of Mañjuśrī and dhāraṇīs: a rich man in the southern Mon land |

The notes count "all black" on the mewa of the year and month by the year's mewa row, of the day by the date, of the hour by the nine of day and nine of night.
