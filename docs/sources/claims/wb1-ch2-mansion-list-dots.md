# WB ch. 2 p. 19: the dots under the mansion-name syllables are printed marks

On p. 19 (reprint I3CN5028 img. 26), in the verses that give the twenty-eight mansions their names, a small black dot is printed under the syllable of each line that is the mansion's own short name (for example under the name given to the Nabs sor mansion, under Skag, Mchu and Gre, Mon dre and Mon gru, Snubs, Chu stod and Chu smad). Looked at on the scan at 1x and 2x, lines 2 to 13 of the page, the dots stand where the machine reading has the combining sign U+0F37 after a syllable in the passage wb.1:p19. That sign is therefore the print's own marking of which syllable of the line names the mansion, and it stays in the Tibetan of the passage. Two spellings read on the scan: the print has མོན་དྲེ (Mon dre) for the mansion of Dhanishtha, and སྣུབས (Snubs) for Mula. The line for Jyeshtha, where the machine has མོ་དུ, was cropped but not settled.
