# The illness-death-grave strike of the five aspects

The illness-death-grave strike of the life force, body, power, luck and la (WB vol. 2, p. 88): each of the dead's five, taken as own, mother, son, friend and enemy, is carried into the year, month, day and hour of death and read by rise and decline; falling on illness, death or grave is a strike, and the harmed are named by aspect and relation. Life force: own, fathers, uncles, household lord and elders in order; mother, grandsons; son, children; enemy, horses and wealth; friend, horses and donkeys. Body: own, sons and servants; mother, widows; son, sisters; friend, young men; enemy, widows. Power: own and mother, loss; son, those who lift the dough; friend, cattle and servants; enemy, good. Luck: own and mother, Bon priests; son, sons-in-law and nephews; friend, wives and fire and water fetchers; enemy, friends. La: own, neighbours; mother, wives; son, children; friend, wealth; enemy, enemies. The notes (p. 506, read on the scan 2026-10-23) make it the dead's life force, body, power, luck and birth mewa × five relations × four times = 100, each split by the twelve stages, 1,200, and allow the la for the birth mewa.
