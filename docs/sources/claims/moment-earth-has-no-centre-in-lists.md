# Where WB's lists give earth a direction it is the south-west, not the centre

WB vol. 2, pp. 168–169 (Gser thang reprint img. 174–175, read by eye): in the lists by the moment's element, earth's kin and demons are not set in the centre. The sick: «ས་དུས... ཤར་རམ་ལྷོ་ནུབ་གཉེན་རྒན་མོ», for an earth moment the kinsman is an old woman from the east or south-west; the demon: «ས་བདག་དགེ་བསྙེན་ནམ། དམ་འགོང་ལྷོ་ནུབ་ཤར་ཕྱོགས་ནས», the earth lord or the dge bsnyen, the oath-bound gongpo, from the south-west or east. The other elements stand wood east (or west), fire south or north, iron south and west, water north. So where WB gives a direction for earth it is the south-west (with the east), the quarter of the earth trigram khon, not the centre. The own-place passage itself (p. 168, l. 5) reads «རང་ས་དུས་ཚོད་དབང་འབྱུང་དངོས། ཕ་ཚན་སྲོག་ལ་ཞང་པོ་ལུས། བྱན་མོ་གྲོགས་ལ་རྗེ་དཔོན་ཀླུང་། གང་ཤར་གང་ལ་དགྲ་དེ་དགྲ། འཁྲུགས་པ་བསྡེབས་ལ་ཤིང་མེ་ས། ལྕགས་ཆུའི་འབྲས་བུ་གོང་ལྟར་བཤད།»: kin by the life force, uncle by the body, maid by the friend, lord by the luck, the enemy where the enemy element stands; the results of wood, fire, earth, iron and water as above. Earth is named among the results but no line puts a person of the own place on it. Narrowed: earth has no seat in the notes' four directions; WB's other lists give it the south-west, the built centre is not in the text.
