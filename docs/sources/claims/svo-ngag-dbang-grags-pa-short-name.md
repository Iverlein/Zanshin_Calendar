# «ངག་དབང་གྲགས་པ» is the Jo nang catalogue's spelling of Vāgīśvarakīrti's name: the remedy's Ngag dbang is probably he

"Ngag dbang grags pa" is how the Jo nang Tengyur catalogue (Phyogs las rnam rgyal, 2010; etext p. 64) writes the name of the author of the Deceiving Death, whom the Derge colophon and the Derge catalogue write Ngag gi dbang phyug grags pa (Vāgīśvarakīrti). So the shortening «ངག་དབང» of the Svapnohana's closing line «འདི་ནི་ངག་དབང་དགོངས་པ་སྟེ» is an attested form of his name, and his work stands directly before the Svapnohana in the Derge, Peking and Narthang volumes and in both catalogues. No text read says that the remedy's Ngag dbang is he, and none names another man by that short name: the identification is probable by form and by neighbourhood, not stated (q796 stays open, narrowed). The Jo nang catalogue gives the Deceiving Death's translators as Jo bo rje and Rin chen bzang po, the Derge colophon as Dīpaṃkaraśrījñāna and Rin chen bzang po. State: etext.
