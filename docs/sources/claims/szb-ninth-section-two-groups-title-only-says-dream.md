# The Sha za brtsegs pa'i rgyud's ninth section has two groups of lines and says dream only in its colophon

The Sha za brtsegs pa'i rgyud's ninth section (printed p. 449) is one answer of the Great Black One to a request for "the signs of the approach and accomplishment" of a fierce work. It has two groups of lines. The first, up to «ཐོག་འཚག་ཉེ་བའི་མཚན་མའོ», "lightning nearby, these are the signs of nearness", lists a black man, a black dog, a raven, the vajra master, a mantra-holder with a black man and black horse, tiger, leopard, bear, dhole, thunder, black cloud and light. The second, after it, lists acts and gifts (deer, flesh and blood given, heart, lungs and entrails released, keeping company with killers, making war) and ends "by this one goes swiftly to the enemy": these are acts and signs of the work's success, not further signs of nearness. The words "dream" and "in a dream" occur only in the colophon, «རྨི་ལྟས་ཀྱི་ལེའུ་སྟེ་དུམ་བུ་དགུ་པའོ», "the chapter of the dream signs, the ninth section", the tantra's name before it written in small letters; the tenth section (the fire offering) follows on the same page. So the text gives no answer whether these are dreams of the practitioner: the title says dream signs and the verses never say so. One word of the second group, «བཤོར», is not settled.
