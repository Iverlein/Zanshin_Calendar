# The Eight Appearances and the quarter and corner lords of p. 175–176

The Eight Appearances and the lords of the quarters (WB vol. 2, pp. 175–176). Each is described by his colour or seat, what he holds, the quarter he rules and the work he is to be shunned in, and the kolong (ཀོ་ལོང, ko long) is assigned to a person. The Great Gyu (གཱིའུ་ཐེན་པོ, gi'u then po; the earth god, numbered 18 as printed): yellow, holds the wishing tree; he parts the months at becoming; the kolong is the householder's. The Earth-holder (ས་འཛིན, sa 'dzin, 2): the world's body, the head parting becoming; the kolong goes to the dead and kin. East: the Thesé (ཐེ་སེ, 2), golden banner, a snow leopard on a golden throne; shun severing kin and taking a bride. South: Tsangkun (ཙང་ཀུན, 4), silver plough-pole, iron wheel; army commander, tax-taker. West: Hangphan (ཧང་ཕན, 5), snake-headed, sun-ray noose; shun every bad direction, the kolong to the traveller. North: Beu-byin (བེའུ་འབྱིན), water noose, lives in water; shun entering boats. The four corners and four further lords are numbered (7)–(15): Piling Pharma (7), Grubnyan Lagpa-can (8), the straight great man (9), Golden Sun-man (10), the Black Wheel (11) in the east, the Yellow Banner (12) in the south, the Tiger's Tail (13) in the west, Zeba-can (14) in the north, and the field's (15). The numbers are the printed ones, partly read by eye (the bracketed 7 to 15 on the Gser thang reprint); the lords numbered 1 to 6 carry the printed numbers of the page above.
