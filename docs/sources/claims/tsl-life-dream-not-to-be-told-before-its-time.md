# A dream of the life is not to be told before its time: the pledge, the obstacles, the slander

A dream of this kind is not to be told. The goddess-sister in the year-old dream says "the pledge is the key; tell no one" and the mother, hearing it, says "do not tell others, do not make much talk" (i88). The btsan who names the three masters in the evening dream says: "if these signs, before their time has come, are told to people even by a fragment of speech, obstacles will cut you off at a thread" (i108). The conception dreams are the instance of what telling brings: told to the people, they were read as sorcery and counter-rites followed, "telling others brought much slander" (i37). So a dream's timing is also the time at which it may be told, which the dream itself sets: "before its time" (དུས་ལ་མ་བབ་པར).
