# Ch. 27: the doubtful letters of pp. 9 and 17 on the Taikhang

The doubtful letters of WB vol. 2, pp. 9 and 17, read on the Taikhang reproduction (I5878 img. 20, 32, 33; block folios 10, 22, 23) with Yigdzin-1 on it and on the Zhol print (img. 638, 650, 651), the eye at 3 to 6 times and agy on single crops.

- p. 9, l. 6: the Taikhang reads «ཚད་ཟླ་མག་རྒུད་བུ་ཚར་ངན», «རྒུད» (decline), where the 1996 print and Yigdzin-1 on the Zhol print have «སྐུད»; the eye and agy agree on the Taikhang's ra over ga with ུ. In the black water-spirit pebble's verse the line then lists the sons-in-law's line, mag (མག), in decline with the children's side bad, among the black pebble's ills. The Zhol print at 5 times was not told from «སྐུ». The first two syllables «ཚད་ཟླ» and the line's context stay as RD51 left them.
- p. 9, l. 16: «བྱིས་སྟོན་ན་ཟླ་སྟོན་མོ་བཏང» on the Taikhang by Yigdzin-1 only, as the Zhol print has it; no new letter.
- p. 17, l. 9: the syllable after «ལྕོང་མོའི» is «རྔམས» in Yigdzin-1 on both prints and in agy; the eye could not tell it from the «ཛ[?]» of the 1996 print, so the doubt of «ལྕོང་མོའི་ཛ[?]་མས» is that the letter is a stack of ra over nga printed to look like ཛ on the 1996 setting, a reading that gives «རྔམས», "majesty", in «the lcong mo's majesty stirs the ocean»: stated as likely, not proved.
- p. 17, l. 26: «ཞག་བཅུ་ཚུན་ལ་[?]ུར་ཞོགས་དང་»: the first letter of the doubtful syllable is a box-shaped བ or འ (Yigdzin-1 «བུར» on the Taikhang, «འུར» on the Zhol print), never ཞ; agy refused the crops (its filter blocked the line three times), and the eye does not settle between བ and འ.
