# The thirty: the twenty-fourth point, the pale wooden box, for the deciding pebbles for burning or keeping the remains

The twenty-fourth of the thirty starting points (སྐོར་འགོ, skor 'go), from the mouth of the pale wooden box (ཤིང་སྒྲོམ་སྐྱ་མོ, shing sgrom skya mo), is read for the deciding pebbles (བཏང་གཅོད, btang gcod) for burning or keeping the remains. Its eight pebbles are laid as for every point (claims thirty-count and thirty-eight-fighters), each white or black, and read so:

| Pebble | Asks | White | Black | Remedy for black |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Whether a red corpse or a smallpox corpse is burnt in fire | A fire corpse and a fever corpse are good to burn | It may not be burnt | — |
| 2 | Whether an earth corpse or a water corpse is allowed in a box | They may be cast into water | They may not be cast; avoid it | — |
| 3 | Whether the turning direction of the box's mouth is reversed | An iron corpse is burnt at the foot of a rock, the head not shown to the east | An iron corpse is not burnt at the foot of a rock; avoid it and the like | — |
| 4 | Whether the killer's corpse clings to livestock and wealth | It does not cling, good | It clings to the substances and utensils of the planets' and stars' strikes | Turn the killer's face |
| 5 | Whether the road of the corpse is blocked by the *hal khyi* (ཧལ་ཁྱི, hal khyi; a spirit, not identified) | Not blocked | The road is faulty, blocked by the hal khyi | Turning the hal khyi's face and the like |
| 6 | Whether the earth lord (ས་བདག, sa bdag) are tamed | Tamed, good | Not tamed, bad | — |
| 7 | Whether the corpse load does harm | It does no harm | The corpse load's strength is spent: it is said to be sent at the edge of exhaustion («ཆད་ཁར་བཏང་བ») | — |
| 8 | Whether one bone is sent to the ground | Sending one bone is allowed | One bone is not sent to the ground | — |

Whose year is reckoned: The dead's year and age (notes, p. 532: «དུར་གང་ཤིས་ཀྱང་དེ་འདྲ»).

Which of the eight fighting pebbles is which check is not stated; the order of p. 453 is the likely one (claim thirty-board-groups; question 259).
