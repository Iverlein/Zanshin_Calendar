# The skl manual (Skar rtsis kyi lag len byed grub ...) is by Dkon mchog bstan 'dzin; the multiplier verse is a stock verse in the reckoners' manuals

The manual *Skar rtsis kyi lag len byed grub sgang dril ltar bkod pa dri med 'od kyi snang ba* (skl:i210's text) is by Dkon mchog bstan 'dzin, so a modern or at least named author, not an anonymous old text.

Evidence: the colophon of the BDRC etext (VEIE0OPI17D73134_I3CN8749, etext p. 217-218) names the arranger as «རྩིས་རིག་འཆང་བ་དཀོན་མཆོག་བསྟན་འཛིན» (rtsis rig 'chang ba dkon mchog bstan 'dzin, "holder of the science of reckoning"), written at the urging of Karma bstan dpal; a later colophon of the same volume names «མཁས་དབང་དཀོན་མཆོག་བསྟན་འཛིན» as the maker of a Skar 'byung rtsis digest; and BDRC's other two records of the title (MW1KG5793_8D0D11, MW30023_FBE81D, the latter in the collection *Rtsis rig*) give the author as Gzu ru Dkon mchog bstan 'dzin. The etext's diagram note says the figure was copied from a draft of Karma mkhan chen Sangs rgyas chos 'phel.

The multiplier verse (skl:i210) is the same in three other etexts (a Dpal sprul passage that says "what is known as composed by the Lord", a Khams collection, and the *Skar rtsis kyi rjes kyi rim pa ri thu sbyar 'grel* manual): all give «དཱི་པཾ་ཀ་རས་མཛད་པ་ལས», none names the Atiśa work. The Atiśa text itself is not found.
