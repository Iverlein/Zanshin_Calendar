# No print or digest gives a seat to the hal khyi's mothers and sons

Every print and the commentary's etext carve the hal khyi's five mothers and five sons as p. 185 has them in the 1996 print (22 days to each pair, 110 in all, 104 in the note's other texts), and none gives a seat for any mother or son; BDRC's full-text search for the lines' own words finds only these copies of WB, and the Bhutan collection's tables of the year's lords list the hal khyi with the girl and the rest, not with mothers or sons. The nine numbers «༡༧ … ༢༥» after the hal khyi's own «༡༦» count nine places for five mothers and five sons; what they refer to is not said. The next lord, Dkar-sham the white (no. 26), sits on the hal khyi's own seat and is produced three days later turning left, so the lords after the hal khyi follow to the left; whether the mothers and sons do is not said. Question 433 stays open.
