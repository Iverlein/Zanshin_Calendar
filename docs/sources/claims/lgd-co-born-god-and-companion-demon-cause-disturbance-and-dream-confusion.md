# The co-born god and the demon who comes alongside as causes of body's pains and of dreams in tumult (Bdud 'joms gling pa, Dpa' bo)

The pair is named in two further places as the cause of bodily and mental disturbance and, in one, of confused dreams (RD307, 2026-11-10; etexts, no scan opened). Bdud 'joms gling pa's *Myur lam 'pho ba chen po'i gdams pa* (I4636, etext p. 600; the same words in Kun bzang bde skyong dbang mo's collection and its Palri print, BDRC search): «མདོར་ན་ལྷན་སྐྱེས་ཀྱི་ལྷ་དང་། མཉམ་འོང་གི་བདུད་འདྲེའི་ཆོ་འཕྲུལ་དུ། ལུས་ལ་ན་ཚ་ཟུག་རྡུ་མ་ངེས་པ་སྣ་ཚོགས་གཏོང་བ་དང་། མཐོང་བའི་ཡུལ་དུ་ཐན་དང་ལྟས་ངན་སྣ་ཚོགས་འབྱུང་བ་རྣམས་ལ་ཉམས་ནད་དུ་བཟུང་ནས་མོ་རྩིས་བྱེད་པ་དང་…ད་དུང་མོ་རྩིས་དང་རྨི་ལམ་…ཐམས་ཅད་རབ་ཏུ་འཁྲུགས་ནས་སེམས་ཀྱི་རྣམ་པར་རྟོག་པ་ཡང་སློང་བར་བྱེད་པ་འབྱུང་ངོ༌།» — "in short, by the tricks of the co-born god and the demon-spirit who comes alongside, the body is sent various uncertain fevers and pains, and various misfortunes and bad omens appear to sight; those who take these for experience-sickness and cast a divination, or take the support as a spirit and do rites and medicine, find that none of it helps; and further the divinations and the dreams are all thrown into tumult and the mind's thoughts too are stirred". The spelling is «མཉམ་འོང་» (mnyam 'ong), the short form, with «བདུད་འདྲེ» (demon-spirit) in place of the plain demon. The same short spelling is in Dpa' bo Chos dbyings rdo rje's commentary (I0392, etext p. 184): the inner god born together with the elements of one's body «མཉམ་འོང་གི་བདུད་དུ་གྱུར་ནས» "turns into the demon who comes alongside" and takes the life-syllable and the three poisons' weapons, when one holds the Great Perfection wrongly.

LGD's line is of one kind with these: «རང་དང་ལྷན་ཅིག་སྐྱེས་པའི་ལྷ་དང༔ མཉམ་པོར་འོང་བའི་བདུད་དང༔ ཕྱི་རོལ་གྱི་ལྷ་འདྲེ་འཁྲུགས་པའི་ཆོ་འཕྲུལ» names, as one of the causes of a confused dream, the tricks of the god born with oneself and of the demon who comes alongside, then of outer gods and spirits in tumult. The Nyingma instruction states it for the disturbances of sleep as for the body. Rank: moderate; it reads the same two names, in a text of the same current (a treasure of the Nyingma), and the dreams named there are those thrown into tumult.
