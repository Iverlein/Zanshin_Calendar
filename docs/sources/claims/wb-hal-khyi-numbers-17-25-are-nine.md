# The reprint carves nine numbers 17–25 after the hal khyi's mothers and sons

On the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5029 img. 191, p. 185, by eye 2026-11-01) the hal khyi is numbered (༡༦) and after «མ་ལྔ་བུ་ལྔ་བཅས་པ་སྟེ» the bracket reads (༡༧ ༡༨ ༡༩ ༢༠ ༢༡ ༢༢ ༢༣ ༢༤ ༢༥), nine numbers, each under its own small arc, the next lord Dkar-sham being 26: the reprint agrees with the etexts, so no misprint of the etext is the cause. Ten beings, nine numbers: one of the ten counts in the hal khyi's own 16 or shares a number. Where each sits is not settled; question 470 stands.
