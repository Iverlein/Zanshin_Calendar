# Klu'i byang chub's line takes the dream as object; Padma dkar po's prose says «if bad» (RD395, q1063)

In Klu'i byang chub's Guhyasamāja mandala rite in twenty parts (Derge VE23703_1351 pp. 273–274) the line «མི་དགེ་དགེ་ནི་རྨི་ལམ་བདག །མེད་པའི་གཏམ་གྱིས་རྣམ་པར་སྦྱང་» is cut across the verse end between «བདག» and «མེད་པའི་», and the sense is "the dream, non-virtuous or virtuous: purify it by the talk of selflessness" (bdag med pa'i gtam). Padma dkar po's prose of the same lines (VE10736_016 p. 43) settles that the dream is the object: "at dawn he asks; if it is bad (ངན་ན), it is to be purified by the talk of selflessness". Tsong kha pa's Sngags rim chen mo takes it the same way («བདག་མེད་པའི་དོན་བསམས་ནས་སྦྱང་བར་བཤད»). The virtuous dream is increased (the previous line) and the bad one purified; Padma dkar po names the bad dream only, and for the middle word the grammar of the verse is a little looser than his prose.
