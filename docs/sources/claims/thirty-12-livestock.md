# The thirty: the twelfth point, the young wild yak of the north on the blue goat, for wealth and livestock, goats, sheep and the rest

The twelfth of the thirty starting points (སྐོར་འགོ, skor 'go), from the mouth of the young wild yak of the north on the blue goat (བྱང་ཁའི་འབྲོང་ཆུང་ར་སྔོན, byang kha'i 'brong chung ra sngon), is read for wealth and livestock, goats, sheep and the rest. Its eight pebbles are laid as for every point (claims thirty-count and thirty-eight-fighters), each white or black, and read so:

| Pebble | Asks | White | Black | Remedy for black |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Whether the white god-yak leaks at its edge | It does not leak, good | It leaks at its edge: bad in the place of wealth, loss of wealth | The livestock's pledge-binding (གཏའ་སྡོམ, gta' sdom) and calling back prosperity (གཡང་ལོན, g.yang lon) help |
| 2 | Whether the prosperity of the god-dri (ལྷ་འབྲི, lha 'bri) has declined | Not declined; good for mounts | The god-dri's prosperity has declined: livestock there now, gone now | In the enclosure recite the protecting prosperity (གཡང་སྐྱབས, g.yang skyabs) and plant the prosperity dagger (གཡང་ཕུར, g.yang phur) |
| 3 | Whether the tether of the white prosperity sheep is cut | Not cut; they grow, good | Cut: the sheep's prosperity declines | Recite the sheep's dhāraṇī and send the sheep ransom |
| 4 | Whether the ogre goats are chased by the wolf | Not chased; the goats grow, good | Chased by the wolf: bad for goats | Send the goat ransom to every direction, above all to the west |
| 5 | Whether the wish-granting cow has dried | Not dried; good for food, wealth, treasury and enjoyments | Dried like a cut stream: there now, gone now; it burns away and dies of sickness («ཡལ་གྱིས་འབར་ནས་ཐུམས་ཀྱིས་ཤི», not understood) | Summoning the cow's wind and the livestock's prosperity is auspicious |
| 6 | Whether the golden nose rope of the turquoise mdzo has gone | Not gone; good for the mdzo | Gone, bad | — |
| 7 | Whether the bottom of the curd pail is broken | Not broken; not carried off by spirits, good | Broken: the white liquids are carried off by the white spirits | Separate off the white spirits |
| 8 | Whether the lump of prosperity butter is lost down the precipice | Not lost, good | Lost: prosperity declines, reputation is small | Calling back prosperity and the like |

Whose year is reckoned: the person's own (the notes, vol. 2, p. 533: «གཞན་རྣམས་སོ་སོའི་ལོ་རྟ»).

Which of the eight fighting pebbles is which check is not stated; the order of p. 453 is the likely one (claim thirty-board-groups; question 259).
