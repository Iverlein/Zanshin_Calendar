# LT and Mipham's notes give no pair for WB's readings 28 and 29

Question 377: in the *Moonbeams* (dz:i029-034, img. 033, l. 1, read by eye again) the twenty-eighth reading, the small children's body, starts from the iron bird and monkey with the small gloss «དགྲ» (enemy), and the twenty-ninth, the women's power, from the earth horse and sheep with «གྲོགས» (friend): «ལྕགས་(དགྲ)ཀྱི་བྱ་སྤྲེལ་སྟེང་ནས་བརྩི། བཅུ་བཞི་བུད་མེད་དབང་ཐང་རྩིས། … ས་(གྲོགས)ཡི་རྟ་ལུག་སྟེང་ནས་བརྩི།». WB's Tibetan fifteen (wb.1:p453, img. 463, read by eye): «བཅུ་གསུམ་གྲོགས་ཀྱི་རྟ་ལུག་གཉིས། བཅུ་བཞི་དགྲ་ཡི་བྱ་སྤྲེལ་གཉིས། བཅོ་ལྔ་མ་ཡི་ཁྱི་ཕག་གཉིས།»: the thirteenth the friend's horse and sheep, the fourteenth the enemy's bird and monkey; WB's readings 28 and 29 are the Tibetan thirteenth and fourteenth by the count of fifteen and fifteen.

Read again for the question, none decides it. LT (lt:p65-67, etext pp. 73–75; the numerals (28) children's body, (29) women's power, (30) great men's luck read on img. 074) prints the same pair verses as WB word for word and numbers the readings in WB's order: it copies WB's list and makes no join of reading to pair of its own. Mipham's notes on the thirty (wb.2:p532-533, etext pp. 540–541) say whose year each starting point is counted from, for the tenth to the fourteenth, and that «the others are each one's own year-sign», and give no pair for any reading, none for the twenty-eighth or twenty-ninth. WB's own results for the twenty-eighth and twenty-ninth (wb.1:p469–470, img. 479–480) name no year. The Zhol blocks' page with the same verses (I1KG1710 img. 602) was looked at and the hand is too small to read syllable by syllable at this scan; its machine etext is too broken to cite. The exchange stands in the *Moonbeams*' print and in the other prints found (claim thirty-dz-28-29-exchange); WB's order stands in WB and LT. Still not decided whether it is the *Moonbeams*' teaching or a slip, and WB's own pair for reading 28 is the friend's by WB's list and the shared count alone.
