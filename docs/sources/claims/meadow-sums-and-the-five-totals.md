# The meadow's sums, 120, 360 and 600, and what the five totals gather

The sums settle the count behind q216 and q225 and bear on their readings only by one inference of ours.

What is now sure (claim [zg-nyer-tham-is-twenty](zg-nyer-tham-is-twenty.md)): the meadow's ornament leaves are three years of 120 pebbles, four groups of thirty each (twenty-five fought and five verdicts), 360 in all, in WB (vol. 2, p. 22), in the *Moonbeams* (dz:i050, f. 23b) and in Zhechen Gyaltsab (zg:p300-301), who adds that the leaves may be fitted to days and hours too; the *Moonbeams* gives the days' and the hours' sums as 120 each and the whole as six hundred (360 + 120 + 120), a view it ascribes to Khyung lha Gzhon nu bsod nams. So there are twelve groups and sixty verdicts in the 360, a hundred and twenty each for the days and the hours.

For q216 (are a group's five verdicts one for each aspect of the reckoned, or one for each year?) the sums do not choose, but the closing gathering does constrain it. Both the *Moonbeams* (dz:i050-051) and Zhechen Gyaltsab say the verdicts are "examined for life, illness, food, merit and demons by the life force, body, power, luck and mewa" and then gathered into five general totals (ཁ་དམར་སྤྱི་བསྡོམས་ལྔ). Five totals of sixty verdicts come out even, twelve each, if each of a group's five verdicts belongs to one of the five aspects, one under each column, so that the twelve groups give twelve verdicts to each aspect; a verdict "of the year" would have no aspect to be gathered by. That fits the reading of [ch27-meadow-verdict-per-column](ch27-meadow-verdict-per-column.md) (KD's sheet, one verdict under each of a group's five columns) and the totals by aspect of [dz-five-totals-and-ch27-domains](dz-five-totals-and-ch27-domains.md); it is our deduction from the arithmetic and the order of the pairs, and neither text says "twelve to each total". Whether the five columns are the year's own five forces (KD) or the five of an animal (Mipham) stays open; so does whether the five totals gather the 60 verdicts of the three years, of one year (twenty verdicts, four to each total), or of one group.

For q225 (may the five totals stand for ch. 24?) nothing new: the *Moonbeams* places the five totals in the year-of-age chapter at the close of the meadow's leaves, and Zhechen Gyaltsab in his verse on the same leaves, so both gather the meadow's verdicts, not the year of age's household round; WB gives no totals for either.
