# The three mewa joined and the nine eyes

WB vol. 2, p. 165: "the three mewa joined". Set the mewa of the day of reckoning in the centre of the nine-square and name the squares 1 centre, 2 east, 3 south-east, 4 south, 5 south-west, 6 west, 7 north-west, 8 north, 9 north-east. The mewa of the moment's year, taken as the one reckoned, fights the mewa in each square: a mother, good guardian gods; a friend, harmful demons; otherwise neither. The squares are kin and matters; the harm comes when the square cuts inward (the moment's mewa), not when cut outward.

| Square | Concerns | Inward cut |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 east | father, grandfather, great-grandfather | a line ended or a death by the knife |
| 3 south-east | mother, grandmother, great-grandmother | defilement (the scan «མཚོལ») and widowhood |
| 4 south | sons, grandsons, great-grandsons | the line ends |
| 5 south-west | house site, field, grave | a feud with nāgas and earth lords |
| 6 west | brothers, father's and mother's brothers | defilement and inner quarrels |
| 7 north-west | sisters and aunts | the siblings' seat of luck unstable (generating inward, stable) |
| 8 north | the uncles of three generations | the road of counsel cut |
| 9 north-east | daughters and granddaughters | separation |

The nine eyes: the mewa at the door asks whether a person is near: 1 white a king, one better than you; 2 black a wealthy one; 3 blue rise and fall, one with a theurang; 4 green mourning, one ill-omened; 5 yellow a storm, one bringing loss; 6 white «ཐང་ཤིང», one with many brothers; 7 red a «བརྗེ», one rough; 8 white a fierce enemy, one with a king spirit; 9 red a butcher, a companion with a drawn knife. The notes (p. 536) work an example and give the colours: mother a white pebble; enemy, friend and a wood or iron same-element meeting black; son and an earth or water same-element meeting speckled (Yigdzin-1, the notes' range of RD38).
