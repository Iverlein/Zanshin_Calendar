# The Tsurphu almanac is Palpung's almanac of the Tsurphu reckoning; its festival days follow Tsurphu 'Jam dbyangs chen po and Mi bskyod rdo rje

Khro ru Tshe rnam: in the water-mouse year of the twelfth cycle the Palpung Rje dbon Karma Nges legs bstan 'dzin issued an almanac of the Tsurphu reckoning (མཚུར་ལུགས, mtshur lugs), the Palpung almanac, with its festival days (དུས་ཀྱི་གཟེར, dus kyi gzer) set out after Tsurphu 'Jam dbyangs chen po and the eighth Karmapa Mi bskyod rdo rje; differing a little from the Phugpa reckoning, it became known as the Tsurphu almanac (mtshur lugs lo tho) and many follow it (tsr:p408-409). The Tsurphu almanac of 2015 names the Palpung religious calendar beside Marpa's day (mtl:p1). So the Kagyu masters' days in a Tsurphu almanac are Palpung's and Tsurphu's, and its months and days need not fall on the Phugpa calendar's (the app's) dates.
