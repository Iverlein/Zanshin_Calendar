# The moment's mewa in the centre: kin's places and the forefathers' readings

WB vol. 2, pp. 163–164: the moment's mewa set in the centre of the figure: the centre is one's own household, the crown the forefathers, the left shoulder the maternal uncle, the right side the father's brothers, the right foot brothers and dependants, the left servants and fields, the lap the mother and spouse (the right shoulder and the left side's «ཡམས་སྐུད» not explained). Then the mewa arising in the forefathers' place, read so in every place:

| Mewa | Reading |
| --- | --- |
| 1 white | forefathers flourished, lapsed between; a single line; a death in a fight; fair, great rise and fall |
| 2 black | within three generations a line ended, widowhood; feud with earth lords; drowning or knife, chronic cold illness; gossip |
| 3 blue | five generations; drowning or knife, chronic cold illness; a stake struck; press down the sri of ruin |
| 4 green | after four generations a quarrel with the nāgas; a crooked beam and a demon in the house; travel, a drawn knife |
| 5 yellow | five or two generations; feud with earth lords; Bonpo, learned or monk; earth lords from field and house |
| 6 white | a line with tumours, a death by tumour; white of mouth, black of heart; a demon on the wealth |
| 7 red | the fifth generation or the maternal line died by the knife; nāgas and tsen; hearth spoilt; ruddy, loud, moles |
| 8 white | a young line; a death by the knife without wound; a move of country |
| 9 red | rich at first, a beggar at last; fortune lost outside |

As birthmarks: 2 black a woman's mole, 7 red a man's. The mewa as shapes and as food follow before it (pp. 162–163): the three whites crescent and dairy, black and blue round and fish, meat, tea, beer, 4 green long and vegetables, tsampa and beer, 5 yellow square and fried pastry, the reds triangular and flesh and blood.
