# The Mchog dga's five places are the instant, its thirty parts, the water-hour, the water-srang and the breath; the parts are 30 times the instant over its house's water-srang (q1018)

In the Mchog dga' (མཆོག་དགའ, Mchog dga') the five places (གནས་ལྔ, gnas lnga) of a junction of time are what the instant junction (ཏཏྟཱལ, tattāla) is broken into: the instant itself in the water-srang (ཆུ་སྲང, chu srang) of its house, then its part of thirty (ཆ་ཤས, cha shas), then the staff (དབྱུ་གུ, dbyu gu, named «chu tshod», water-hour, in the pages that work it: the same place, not settled), then a sixtieth of that as water-srang, then a sixth as breaths (དབུགས, dbugs). The parts are got by multiplying the instant's water-srang by thirty and dividing by the house's own water-srang: the book's example is 200 water-srang in Virgo, whose own count is 400, so 200 x 30 / 400 = 15 parts, no remainder; what remains is multiplied by sixty and divided again to give the next place, and so on to the breath. The parts place can hold at most 29 before a remainder is rounded up, so the thirty-part junction's chart (ch. 3 p. 180: the thirty parts of the five places and the chart come out as one line at the boundaries of the parts) counts 1 to 30, one more than the places' own count wherever the lower places are not empty (p. 129: adding one to the parts place is the easy practice). This answers q1018's «five places»: the parts are thirty-fold divisions of the instant junction (the junction of time that holds the moment of the question, one of the twelve of a day), not of the night. Etext only; the third place's two names and the Mchog dga's wording «ཏཆ་ལ», «ཏངྐ་ལ» for tattāla are the etext's.
