# The Nyingma masters' and treasure revealers' days in the Rigpa students' calendar and Jeff Watt's list, with the houses that keep them

The days (month/day) of the Nyingma masters and treasure revealers (གཏེར་སྟོན, gter ston) in the two published lists, the Rigpa students' calendar (tbc:p1) and Jeff Watt's (har:p1), with the houses read that keep them. Neither list says which are passing days; both call them anniversaries.

The two lists agree on every master they share but Nyag bla Padma bdud 'dul (4/30 against 4/18). The Mindroling rule-book's second-month remembrance names no master, and its day (19th to 29th) is not Gter bdag gling pa's 2nd; whether the two root lamas are Gter bdag gling pa and his brother the page does not say.
