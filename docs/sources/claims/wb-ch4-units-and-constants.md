# WB ch. 4: the chain of units and the constants as printed

The units of the accomplishing reckoning of WB's chapter 4, as the verses chain them (pp. 29–30): a remainder multiplied by 60 («མཁའ་རོ») and divided by the dividing number gives the dbyu gu (དབྱུ་གུ); that remainder multiplied by 60 again and divided gives the srang (སྲང); then multiplied by 6 («དུས») gives the breaths (དབུགས); then by 707 («རི་མཁའ་རིས») gives the part (ཆ་ཤས). The month's constants appear as «ཟླ་ཡོན ༣༡, མཁའ་དབང ༥༠, སྟོང་པ ༠, མཁའ་ནོར་ཆུ་བོ ༤༨༠» (31, 50, 0, 480) on p. 30, the planet's dhruva as «བུག་མདའ ༥༩, སྲང་ཡོན ༣, ཆུ ༤, མི་བདག ༡༦» (59, 3, 4, 16) and the sun's as «དབྱུག་མཚོ ༤, ཟླ་ཟུང ༢༡, འབྱུང ༥, མེ་ཆུ ༤༣» (4, 21, 5, 43); the moon's feet are counted «དབང ༥, འབྱུང ༥, མདའ ༥, ཆུ ༤, ཡོན ༣, འཁྲིག ༢, གཟུགས ༡» with a reversal at the place of fourteen (ཡིད). The figures of p. 29's first rule are 7313, 8657, 39592, then 1041, 253, 3528, 126; the sun's dhruva uses 309, 65, 804, 27 and 67. These are read from the machine text with the number words beside them as the check; what each computes is not reconstructed here.
