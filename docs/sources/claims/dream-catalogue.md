# The dream images: every image with each text's verdict, WB first (RD216)

The catalogue of the dream images, built from the passages RD59, RD207–RD212 read, so that the picker of block 73 (SPEC section S2) can be made without reading the corpus again. One claim per image (`claims/dream-<name>.md`) names the texts that read it and each text's verdict with its passage and the passage's state; this file indexes them. It keeps no net verdict: no text sums images, and nothing here calls a dream good or bad as a whole.

**Order inside a claim.** WB ch. 30 first (rank 1, the app's `MomentSign.DREAM` rows are its thirteen groups, never copied); then the Four Tantras' rows (ST), marked as readings for a sick dreamer; then WB ch. 25's lines on the sick, under ST where both read one dream (claim dream-st-above-wb25); then the rank-3 texts, which no source outranks: LGD (scan), SVO, SVI, SKP, RST, KBS, RRT, the scan before the machine before the etext.

**State of each passage** is carried into every row: scan (every quoted syllable read on the page image), machine (Yigdzin-1 only, the layout checked by eye), etext (the printed etext, not read on a scan). SVO's and SVI's passages are etext or machine; RD207's pp. 268–269 are etext only, p. 270–271 machine, and SVI is etext only. SKP's two texts, KCM and DTD are machine; RST and RRT are etext; KBS is an article quoting Klong rdol's verses (machine), his own text not located (RD221). WB ch. 30 p. 144 is scan; the Four Tantras' dream passages are scan; WB ch. 25 pp. 428–429 scan, pp. 429–433 machine; LGD is scan except its rite pp. 961–966 (machine).

**Cells of the table at the end:** the tone (G good, B bad, X mixed or turning, N a sign only) and the state (S scan, M machine, E etext), `?` where the verdict rests on an unsettled word. Texts that give an image more than one row show each.

**Texts with no image rows.** KCM and DTD are rites for asking a dream (KCM's prophecy sign is the one row); RBG is King Kṛki's eighteen dreams, no method (claim rbg-eighteen-dreams-of-the-latter-times); KDL has no dream in it (kdl-no-dream-rule); MDP's dream part is not reachable (RD221); SKP's second text repeats RST's verses (skp-second-text-is-the-rst-verses) and adds no rows. Time rules, which are RD215's and RD209's, are not repeated here: dream-manuals-time-rules, svo-watches-and-spans, lgd-dream-times-of-night, kbs-three-thirds-of-the-night.

**Rules that are not images:** dream-mixed-order, dream-spirit-classes, dream-practice-signs, dream-st-above-wb25; the remedy per text once: dream-remedies; the images on an unsettled word: dream-unsettled-words.

- [Small creatures, worms or wild beasts on the body](dream-creatures-on-body.md)

- [Filth: a mire, excrement, eating or washing in filth](dream-filth.md)

Other images show good and bad rows too, but as opposite forms of the image (fine clothes against bad clothes, a boat set against a boat broken), not as one dream read two ways. The disagreement is kept: each row speaks for its text, WB first where it reads the image. SVO is the odd one out on the body struck with weapons, covered with small creatures, following excrement and blood flowing, where its verdict rests on the unsettled clause, and on a house burning at the waking, which it reads good; SVO also reads being seized and bound or lassoed good (a son, a great station), and reads a white snake seizing the hand as good where SKP reads snakes as the harm of spirits; RST and KBS read much gold dust enjoyed as bad against the wealth found of WB, LGD, SKP and the rest.
