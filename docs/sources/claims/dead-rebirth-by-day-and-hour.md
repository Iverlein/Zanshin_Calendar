# The rebirth by the day's animal, the hour and the stage of death

The fifth examination's readings by the day's animal, the hour and the stage of rise and decline on which the dead died (wb.2:p70-72; the dog day is on the scan, p. 71, l. 16, and missing from Yigdzin-1's reading).

| Day of death | Former life | Next life |
| --- | --- | --- |
| mouse | a snake, then a horse | a monk with a share of the Dharma |
| ox | a human | a good teacher |
| tiger | a horse within ten days | a single man |
| hare | a bird in the east | an elephant, or a single man |
| dragon | a snake for some days | an ox, then a man of the Bon priests |
| snake | an ox | the son of a newly married pair in the south |
| horse | a demon | the son of an old couple |
| sheep | a snake | a monk |
| monkey | a mouse in the north | a bachelor |
| bird | a goose | the middle of three brothers in the west |
| dog | a *phyi gu* (not identified) | the middle of three brothers in the west |
| pig | a mantra practitioner | a teacher |

| Hour of death | Stage of rise and decline | Rebirth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| hare, tiger | whole body, clothes worn | a god |
| dragon, ox | birth, work | an asura |
| snake, mouse | bathing, rising | a human |
| horse, pig | breath-taking, illness | an animal |
| sheep, dog | in the womb, death | a hungry ghost |
| monkey, bird | decline, entering the grave | hell |
