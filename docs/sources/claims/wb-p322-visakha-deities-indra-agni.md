# WB p. 322: «དབང་པོ་མེ་ལྷ» in Viśākhā's verse is Indra and Agni by the verses' own usage

Within the White Beryl's own mansion verses the two words are used elsewhere as single names of a deity: Jyeṣṭhā's verse (p. 323) has «ལྷ་དབང་པོ་དང་རུས་ལེགས་བྱེད» (deity Indra, clan Legs byed), and Kṛttikā's (p. 314) and Hasta's (p. 320) verses name the deity «མེ་ལྷ» (Agni): Hasta's reads «རུས་ནི་འོད་སྲུངས་ལྷ་མེ་ལྷ» (clan Kāśyapa, deity Agni). So «དབང་པོ་མེ་ལྷ» in Viśākhā's line is the two deities Indra and Agni, and the clan's place is taken by «རུས་མཚན» alone, with no clan's name after it on either print. This settles the sense of the deities from the pool's own lists; why the clan's name is missing is not told, and no outside list was consulted.
