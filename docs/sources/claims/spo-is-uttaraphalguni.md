# «སྤོ» is a name of U. Phalgunī; «ལག་སོར» is lha mtshams

Dictionaries, 2026-11-01. The 84000 dictionary (no. 43) glosses «spo» as Uttaraphalgunī, and its synonyms (no. 45) list under «dbo»: {me bzhi}, {spo}; the Great Tibetan Dictionary and Ives-Waldo give «dbo» as also written «sbo», one of the 28 mansions; Mahāvyutpatti (no. 21) «dbo» = uttaraphalgunī. So the twelfth entry of p. 65, printed «སྤྲོ» or «སྦྲོ» (q284), is a spelling of «dbo» (spo, sbo), not another mansion; the dragon's «སྤོ» on p. 122 (q296, q380) is U. Phalgunī. «lag sor» (q296) is a name of the mansion lha mtshams (Great Tibetan Dictionary «རྒྱུ་སྐར་ལྷ་མཚམས», Ives-Waldo, Valby), and in the KP verse the pool holds (combinations.md) «ལག་སོར(༡༦)» stands at number 16, where lha mtshams is. «སྤོ་སྨད» is in no dictionary as a mansion (only as lower Po, a place, Rangjung Yeshe no. 2); by the pairs chu stod and chu smad, khrums stod and khrums smad it would be 'the lower spo', the mansion next to the dragon's, but that is an inference, not proved, and p. 122 prints «སྦ་སྨད».
