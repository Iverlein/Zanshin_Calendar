# RST: no remedy for a bad dream

The ACIP method gives no rite, mantra or offering against a bad dream: its verses end with the death signs and the text turns to omens of the rainbow. Its only handling of a bad dream is the sorting of the watch, the dreamer's state and the mark of the spirit; what is done afterwards is not said. (Compare the 'Bri gung extended method, which has a remedy only for the obstacles of a practitioner: claim rrt-what-is-done.)
