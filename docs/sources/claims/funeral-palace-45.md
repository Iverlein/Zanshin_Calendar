# The forty-five pebbles of the palace: a scale of fathoms

The forty-five pebbles of the palace (ཕོ་བྲང་ཞེ་ལྔའི་རྡེལ, pho brang zhe lnga'i rdel) of the funeral assembly are a scale of fathoms, not a board (Mipham's notes, WB vol. 2, p. 521). The ground is measured from the outer wall at the patron's threshold in fathoms (འདོམ, 'dom) of the main owner. With the sky's mewa laid with the eight-white in the centre and the assembly gathered to the east, the line runs straight east, touching no other direction's mewa, and the forty-five palace gods of the east sit one to a fathom, the mewas' gods in runs of their own number:

| Fathoms | Mewa | Its gods, first and last named |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–6 | six-white | the father-god who holds (ཡབ་ལྷ་ཐོགས་པ, yab lha thogs pa) … the father-god Phyug brdal |
| 7–13 | seven-red | the red bird … the yellow khram god |
| 14–21 | eight-white | the auspicious virtue … the auspicious Rongbo |
| 22–30 | nine-red | the luck planet Rmu dgu … the *rim 'dre* |
| 31 | one-white | the king who craves corpses (རྒྱལ་པོ་རོ་འདོད, rgyal po ro 'dod) |
| 32–33 | two-black | the black bear (དོམ་ནག, dom nag), the black brown bear (དྲེད་ནག, dred nag) |
| 34–36 | three-blue | the black small bird (ཉེའུ་ནག་པོ, nye'u nag po) … the grey tiger |
| 37–40 | four-green | the blue mouse road … the shadowed one (གྲིབ་ཅན, grib can) |
| 41–45 | five-yellow | the golden skin … the mouse road |

The feast's works are then placed by this scale (the nine pebbles of the workers, p. 521): within the twenty-one fathoms of the six-white, seven-red and eight-white the nine-fathom place, or by the sky's mewa laid in the centre, each mewa's own ground. WB's own list of the forty-five palace gods is vol. 2, pp. 195–196 (year-month-earth-lords.md).
