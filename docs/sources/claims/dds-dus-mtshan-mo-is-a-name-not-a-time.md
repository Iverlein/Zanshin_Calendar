# Time-Night (dus mtshan mo) in the dream verses is the black woman's name, not a part of the night (RD374)

RD374. «དུས་མཚན་མོ» (dus mtshan mo, Time-Night) in the dream verses is the name of the black woman who comes for the dying, not a time of the night: «a woman in black clothes … know her as Time-Night; it is shown that she goes to Yama's» (dds.16:p11-13), «a black girl wildly agitated, bound with a binding: that is called Time-Night; death after seven days» (dds.19:p53-55), the medical commentary «the death-night of Yama's retinue» (གཤིན་རྗེའི་འཁོར་དུས་མཚན, dds.20:p213-220, the teachers' view), and the 'Chi bslu's «dus mtshan nag mo» in the pool (cbl). It is not to be read as 'night-time' beside the midnight dream; the phrase «མཚན་མོ་ཐ་མ» (mtshan mo tha ma, 'his last night') of the Hevajra rite is the monkey rider who wakes and is a span of life (that night), not a watch.
