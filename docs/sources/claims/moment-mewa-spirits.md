# The moment's mewa as spirits claiming their share

WB vol. 2, pp. 164–165: the moment's mewa (as it rises in the moment, the mewa of p. 162's spans) speaks as a spirit claiming its share (དྲོ, dro); each with the harm it sends unpaid and the rite.

| Mewa | Spirit | Harm | Rite |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 white | one moving in sky, sea and space, dwelling on the snow peak | dizziness, upper stabbing pains | (none named) |
| 2 black | the demon on a black horse with black dog and bird | obstacles, a corpse into the hands, sudden misfortune | reversing the trap, turning the face |
| 3 blue | the chief of the sen-mo, the oath-breaking sri of lonely places | paralysis of the hands, lameness, sores, dysentery | the sen-mo's nine thread-crosses, the black one's ransom |
| 4 green | the four great nāgas, chief of the eight | nāga illnesses: swellings, dropsy, mottled skin, sores, *dreg*, *grum bu* | nāga offerings and cures |
| 5 yellow | the lord of the land, its channels, fields and house ground | frost, hail, famine, cattle plague, heavy illness | offer to the local god, the earth lords' treasure |
| 6 white | the sman-mo of snow, slate, meadow and lake, on a deer | illnesses on people and cattle | the lake sman's nine thread-crosses |
| 7 red | the tsen of the red rock | knife fights and stabbing pains | the tsen's ransom and liberation |
| 8 white | the lord of the Dharma's offerings | madness, the king spirit's illness, heart-wind | the king spirit's rites, many ransoms |
| 9 red | a woman on a black mule from the charnel grounds, mistress of the *smug mo* | epidemic, quarrels among the father's brothers | the black one's thousand ransoms, Lhamo Matram's thread-cross |
