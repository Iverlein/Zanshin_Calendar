# The black times' door figure «སྤྲ་ཁྱིམ» is a tent

On p. 236 the black times' remedy is a figure drawn at the door's right: «སྤྲ་ཁྱིམ» or «སྦྲ་ཁྱིམ», two spellings of one compound that BDRC's full-text search shows in many texts for the herdsman's tent (poles of a «སྦྲ་ཁྱིམ», «སྦྲ་ཁྱིམ་པ» against «ས་ཁྱིམ་པ», "before he had entered the «སྤྲ་ཁྱིམ»"), and that WB itself writes in its lists of good works as «སྦྲ་ཁྱིམ་འབུབས», the tent pitched. The Taikhang print reads «སྤྲ» on a 3x crop (Yigdzin-1 the same); the 1996 print, the Gser thang reprint and the outline etext of the commentary *Dpyod ldan snying nor* have «སྦྲ»; the spelling changes nothing. What is drawn is therefore a tent, with a swastika at the left and the sun and the moon. Why a tent is the remedy, and how it is drawn, no text found says.
