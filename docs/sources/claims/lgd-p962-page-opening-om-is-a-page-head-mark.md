# The page-opening Om of the Rin chen gter mdzod is a page-head mark, found on the page before LGD

The Om of «ཨོཾ༔» at the left end of the first line of printed p. 962 (and of pp. 961, 963 and 965) in the Rin chen gter mdzod is a page-head mark of the print: the page before the title page (I1KG57 img. 978, printed p. 959, in the previous text of the volume) opens with the same Om at the left of its first line, a gap, then the text; and the line of p. 963 begins mid-clause (RD355, 2026-11-10, by eye on img. 978 and 981). It is not a syllable of LGD's sentence and is not counted in the line. The Palri print's page heads carry it too (lgd.42:p533 and p535).
