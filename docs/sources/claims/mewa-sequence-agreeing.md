# The circles' mewa: the body mewa and the person's own mewa sequence

The mewa in the circles (question 368), from the manuals:

- The reckoned person's body mewa (ལུས་སྨེ, lus sme) is the mewa of his year of birth (LT, lt:p8; UT, ut:p5); the life force's mewa is the fourth upward from it and the power's the fourth downward.
- The mewa run in three sequences (སྨེ་ཕྲེང, sme phreng), upper, middle and lower, of sixty years each, 180 in all; a year of the sixty has one mewa in each (ut:p5). LT: the other mewa are taken in the same sequence as the one reckoned stands in (lt:p8).
- Zhechen Gyaltsab fights the reached year's mewa with the reckoned person's body mewa (zg:p303-304).

Read together, as an inference: the notes' "starting point's mewa that agrees with it in the mewa sequence" (wb.2:p533b) is the starting year's mewa in the person's own sequence of sixty, and counting it upward with the years lived reaches the reached year's mewa in that sequence; that mewa is fought by the body mewa, the birth's. No text read states the two steps as one rule.

UT's page stands on BDRC's etext only (ut:p5): BDRC lists no scans of that edition; LT says the same on its scan (lt:p8).
