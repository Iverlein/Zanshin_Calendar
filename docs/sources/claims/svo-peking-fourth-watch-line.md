# The Peking print of the Svapnohana lacks the fourth watch's line

On the Peking print (img. 315 line 2) the verse of the watches reads «ཐུན་གཉིས་ཟླ་བ་བརྒྱད་བར་ལ། །ཐུན་གསུམ་ཟླ་བ་གསུམ་གྱི་བར་དུ། །མཐོང་བར་འགྱུར་བ་ཐེ་ཚོམ་མེད།» and has no line for the fourth watch. The line «ཐུན་བཞི་ཟླ་བ་ཕྱེད་བར་དུ» ("the fourth watch, within half a month") stands between the third watch's line and «མཐོང་བར…» in the Derge etext (svo:p268-271), on the Narthang print W22704 (I3279 img. 286) and on the same page of the Narthang digital replica of RD228 (I2KG212312 img. 284), in the gser bris ma W23702 (I1PD31365 img. 386–387, across the page turn) and in the Dpe bsdur ma text, which in its notes lists the line, with a minus sign, under Peking's siglum alone (svo:p271e). So four prints (Derge, two Narthang scans of one print family, the gser bris ma) and the collated edition have four watches; only Peking has three. The omission is Peking's, not a Derge addition, and "half a month" for the fourth watch is the reading of the Tibetan tradition as printed everywhere but Peking; the metre does not decide (a skipped line leaves the verse's nine syllables intact). Checked on the Peking scan a second time (contrast stretched, 2× crops, three overlapping): nothing stands between the third watch's line and «མཐོང་བར». The Co ne print is not seen: it has no scan served, and the Dpe bsdur ma notes name it nowhere at this place. The note's markers were lost in the OCR, so its entry is placed by order only (moderate confidence).
