# The Peking print of the Svapnohana lacks the fourth watch's line

On the Peking print (img. 315 line 2) the verse of the watches reads «ཐུན་གཉིས་ཟླ་བ་བརྒྱད་བར་ལ། །ཐུན་གསུམ་ཟླ་བ་གསུམ་གྱི་བར་དུ། །མཐོང་བར་འགྱུར་བ་ཐེ་ཚོམ་མེད།» and has no line for the fourth watch (the Derge etext has «ཐུན་བཞི་ཟླ་བ་ཕྱེད་བར་དུ» between the third and «མཐོང་བར…»). If the print is right, it names three watches and then dawn, not four; the span «half a month» of the fourth watch then stands only in the Derge text. Read on a 2× crop, moderate confidence: the omission could be a skipped line of the block, since the verse's metre (nine syllables) is kept either way. Not yet checked against the Narthang prints (RD233's witness list did not reach that line) or the Dpe bsdur ma notes (whose note markers did not survive); it is a doubt for the next reading of the opening on a second print.
