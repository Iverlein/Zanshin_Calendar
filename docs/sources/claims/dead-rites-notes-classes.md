# Mipham's thirteen classes of remedy and the chapter's sections

Mipham's notes (vol. 2, p. 517, read on the scan for RD23) count the thirteenth examination's 202 identifying pebbles as remedies in thirteen classes, each with its verdict. Set against the chapter as read for RD35 (pp. 104–126), the classes appear to follow its sections in order:

| Notes' class (p. 517) | Count | Chapter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the trigram's remedies, eight trigrams, each with its general remedy, slip, white, black and speckled rite and the smith's paper roll | 14 or 15 | pp. 105–109 |
| the year's trigram in pairs, "bird and monkey dying on *li*" | 10 | pp. 109–111 |
| sweeping the corpse's traces, "the horse and the rest" | 24 | p. 111 (each one's livestock tramples the trace) |
| the nine *spud pa* and six *rgyug*: the four executioners and *zin*, entering the grave, *kung kung* | — | pp. 111–112 |
| the mewa, the One White and the rest | 9 | p. 112 |
| the small remedies: the twelve bad lucks, a bad sky year, a bad month | 20 | pp. 112–113 |
| month, year, day and hour; the four *ra* | 13 | pp. 113–115 |
| the thirty dates | 30 | pp. 116–118 |
| the planets | 8 | pp. 118–119 |
| the mansions | 27 | pp. 119–122 |
| the first mansions and the like | 17 | p. 121 |
| the five mansion graves with the year's mansions | 24 | p. 122 |

The matching is by the order and the examples the notes name ("bird and monkey dying on *li*" is the first line of p. 109; "the four executioners and *zin*" is the nine *spud pa* of p. 111); the counts were not checked against the chapter's own items, and the snake marks the notes say select the counted items were not looked for on the scan.

The table has twelve rows where the notes say thirteen classes; which group the notes count as two is not clear from the line.
