# Ch. 27: the turtle's other classes, paired («བསྡེབས་པ»), have no enemy-executioner

In the golden turtle's strength (WB vol. 2, p. 20, l. 24), the line «གཞན་རྣམས་བསྡེབས་པ་དགྲ་གཤེད་མེད» reads «བསྡེབས་པ», "paired" or "put together", where RD38 left «བརྗེབས[?]». On the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5029 img. 26, the line cut and enlarged 5 to 6 times) the stack has the same shape, head letter, lower letter and vowel e, as the «བསྡེབས» of «བྲན་གཡོག་འགྲོགས་བསྡེབས་ཐམས་ཅད་ལ» on the page's last line; on the 1996 scan (I1KG12907 img. 28, 7 times) both are a sa over a da with e, «བསྡེབས». Yigdzin-1 drops the line on the 1996 scan.

The verse thus says: planet and klu (ཀླུ, klu), srin po (སྲིན་པོ, srin po) and re 'jam (རེ་འཇམ, re 'jam), garuḍa (ཁྱུང, khyung) and earth lord (ས་བདག, sa bdag), garuḍa and klu are enemy-executioners of each other; the other classes, put together in pairs, have none; the cow (བ, ba) and the turtle (སྦལ་པ, sbal pa) agree with all. The same page reads, on both scans, «ཤིང་གླང་མི་ལོ་ཤིང་ལུག་པ» with the person-suffix པ (l. 14) and «ལྷག་མ་བཅུ་པོ», ten (the same line), as RD51 and RD57 found on the reprint, the Taikhang and the Zhol print. The wood sheep's class is not named in the verse; the cow stays an inference from the pebble counts (question 364).
