# The body cut's turtle: the blocks carve the left side 7 and the left leg 6

The body cut (ལུས་ཆད, lus chad), the last of the hidden's nine wheels,
names the trigram numbers by the parts of the turtle (WB vol. 1, p. 358).
The 1996 typesetting prints «མགོ(༩)དང་ལག་གཡོན(༢)རྐང(༨)གློ་གཡས(༣)། །གཞོགས་གཡོན(༦)ལག་གཡས(༤)རྐང་གཡོན(༥)མཇུག(༡)»,
head 9 and left hand 2, leg 8 and right lung 3, left side 6 and right hand
4, left leg 5 and tail 1. The Lhasa blocks, in the Zhol print (WBZ,
I1KG1710 img. 468, l. 4) and the Taikhang print of a Lhasa impression
(I5877 img. 485, l. 4), carve the left side ༧ and the left leg ༦, the
other numbers as the typesetting.

The blocks stand: with 7 and 6 the four pairs are 9 with 2, 8 with 3, 7
with 4 and 6 with 1, each trigram once (5 has no trigram), and they are
the pairs of the notes' drawing of the body cut (vol. 2, p. 524): li with
khon (fire and earth), gin with zin (mountain and wood), dwa with zon
(iron and wind), khen with kham (sky and water), the four pairs of the
eight trigrams that no other wheel holds. The 1996 numbers ༦ and ༥ stand
one place off, a slip of the typesetting like its «ཤིང(༢)ས(༣)» in the harm
wheel on the same page, which the blocks also carve the other way.
