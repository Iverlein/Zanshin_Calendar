# The Blue Beryl's and Khro ru's glosses at ch. 15 and ch. 81 are one text, and the Blue Beryl (1687-88) is the earlier

Question q911 asked whether the Blue Beryl's gloss of ch. 81's dream list is the same text as Khro ru's, and which came first (q910 asked whether the Blue Beryl's printed page reads ལྷས or ལྟས in the closing clause).

The text is the same. Three stretches were set side by side syllable by syllable (RD321, 2026-11-10; the Blue Beryl's etext VEIE0OPICF5AF372_I1PD411 and _I1PD412, Khro ru's VEIE0OPI4CEE4109_I5621 and _I5622; the running heads and page numbers taken out, nothing else): the fever sentence of ch. 15 (st:p183b, 101 and 113 syllables; 85 in 100 alike), the gloss of the five ways of entering in ch. 81 (st:p352b, 160 and 156; 80 in 100), and the gloss of ch. 81's dream list (st:p350-351 and RD303, 141 and 138; 82 in 100). The unlike places are of three kinds. Spelling and the etexts' OCR: ཕྲུམ་སེར and ཕུམ་སེར, སྦལ་པ and སྦུལ་པ, གཏད་ and བཏད་, ས་སྲོས and ས་སྤོས. Marginal numbers and running heads of Khro ru's etext that stay inside his sentences (བརྒྱ་བདུན་ཅུ, a stray རྒྱུ). And a few words that differ in sense: at the fever Khro ru has «ཤེས་པ་སྨྱོས་ཏེ་མི་ཤེས་པ» where the Blue Beryl has «ཤེས་པ་མྱོས་ཏེ་གང་ཡང་མི་རྟོགས་པ», and he adds a clause on the pulse (hard to count, turbulent) that the Blue Beryl lacks; at the entering ways he has «གཟའི་གདོན་གྱི་ཆོ་ག» (the rite of the planets' spirits) where the Blue Beryl's etext has «གཟབ། གདོན་ཆོག་བྱ» (care; do the spirit rite); and the Blue Beryl's «ཁ་ཡོ་མིག་སླེ་ཞེས་ཡོ་ཞིང» is in Khro ru «ཁ་ཡོ་མིག་སྤྱི་ཞེ: ཞིང», which reads as a misreading of the Blue Beryl's letters.

The Blue Beryl is the earlier. By its own words Sde srid Sangs rgyas rgya mtsho, born in 1653 by the etext's own life, began the commentary on the fire-hare year, the 26th of the month Saga, at 35 (so 1687), and in the colophon is hindered at 36 by his reply to the critics of the White Beryl (so 1688). Khro ru's colophon (I5624, pp. 818-821) says the commentary was written at the Lhasa medical college by Khro ru Tshe rnam (1928-2005 by BDRC) in the iron-dragon year (2000), after five winters of work from 1996, with his students gathering and arranging the text (ཕྱོགས་སྒྲིག); it is signed 2001 and names no source. So a modern text, assembled by many hands, repeats a three-century-older commentary almost whole, with spelling modernised, a few clauses added and the older's oddities carried in. The direction is the Blue Beryl to Khro ru: the older print's readable «སླེ་ཞེས་ཡོ» is garbled in the newer one and not the other way round.

The 'Bri gung dka' mchan (1595-1659) is older than the Blue Beryl, and its wording at the entering ways is shorter but has the same clauses in the same order (sudden onset, many confused dreams, merit to gather; unclean: washing and incense; the sunk awareness): the Blue Beryl may be built on it or on a source both used. That is not settled by the etexts. Skyem pa's closing verses (I4711, p. 1266) name Yu thog pa, Karma pa Rang byung rdo rje, Zur mkhar ba and others among the sources of the commentary, not Sde srid; the print is Qinghai 2000 (its imprint), and the one dated colophon in the etext (p. 1267, on the Latter Tantra's practice handbook) gives a sheep-year, the 9th month, the 1st, with no year, so Skyem pa's own date is not found.

Left open (q910): the Blue Beryl's page images answer 401 for this volume, so the printed page's ལྷས or ལྟས (etext: ལྟས once, ལྷས in the outline) was not seen; Khro ru's printed page has ལྷས.
