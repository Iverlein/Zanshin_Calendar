# Ch. 27: the month reckoning for one gone abroad

Brahmā's reckoning for one gone abroad (WB vol. 2, pp. 13–14): the month of his going, or the month someone asks after him, is set against the absent person's life force, body, power and luck. The month cutting the life force: death abroad or an enemy; the body: illness from prison; the power: harm to food and wealth; the luck: deceit by someone trusted. The person cutting the month: a quick return without death. Friend and mother meetings are good, empty aspects bad, and inner meetings by element (fire, earth, water, wood, iron) are read for going, staying and coming back. The month's element alone: earth much loss, water purpose not achieved, fire a quick return with success, iron a swift return without success, wood slow and fearful.
