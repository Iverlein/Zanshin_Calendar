# The stacked figures of the 4th and 5th heads, read in part

RD201, 2026-11-18, Gser thang reprint img. 169 and 170, crops at 6x to 8x (4th) and about 5x (5th). The 4th head's day- and night-length stacks have three tiers like the 3rd's: day, upper pair 1 and a figure like 2 or 9, then 1 and 0, then a bare circle; night, 1 and a swoop (7), then a 5-or-4 figure with a circle, then a bare circle. Line 2's stacks have an upper 1 over one or two lower figures (a 5-or-4 and a 7) and the link's stack an upper 1 over a pair like 24 or 74; no value is settled. The 5th head's day stack reads 11 over 1 over 0 and its night stack 26 over 10 over 0 at 2x to 3x only. The 6th head's stacks were not read. These are shapes with doubts, not data.
