# ལྷས་ཚོར་དུས་ཏེ in ch. 81: lhas as the god's agent case, against rang tshor

The word ལྷས (lhas) in «ལྷས་ཚོར་དུས་ཏེ» of ch. 81 (RD271, 2026-11-09; dictionaries by Steinert's collection and BDRC etexts, no scan opened): the dictionaries give a livestock pen for ལྷས, which does not fit, and the agent form of the god (ལྷ, lha), which does.

**The dictionaries.** ལྷས (lhas) as a headword: a pen or fold for cattle (Rangjung Yeshe, Jim Valby, Ives Waldo, Dan Martin, Tshig mdzod chen mo, Dag tshig gsar bsgrigs, Brda dkrol gser gyi me long, Sgom sde tshig mdzod chen mo; the Brda dkrol cites a Vinaya line); the Verbinator's lhas is the past of "to braid"; the Sgom sde and Negi's Sanskrit dictionary also give "lha yis", the agent of lha ("by the god"), as a second sense. A pen does not make a clause with ཚོར (tshor, to feel).

**BDRC's full-text search (etexts).** «ལྷས་ཚོར» has 41 hits, among them the Four Tantras tradition's own copies of the line (the Gso rig rgyud bzhi etexts, the 'Bri gung and the Mchan brgyab lhan thabs) and, outside it, the idiom itself: "even if men do not feel it (མིས་མ་ཚོར, mis ma tshor), the god feels it (ལྷས་ཚོར, lhas tshor)" in two Bon histories by Shar rdza and Bkra shis rgyal mtshan, and "though the gods felt (ལྷས་ཚོར) the dead raven, men did not yet" in the Bon Kangyur (Bon texts, a witness for the grammar only; nothing is built on their content). In them ལྷས is the god in the agent case, the opposite of the human who does not notice. In the Four Tantras tradition the Mes po'i zhal lung's commentary sets the dream line (རྨི་ལམ) on «the time felt by the gods» (ལྷས་ཚོར་གྱི་དུས) and the marks of body, speech and mind on «the time felt by oneself» (རང་ཚོར་གྱི་དུས), the two times the verse names with the pair ལྷས་ཚོར and རང་ཚོར.

**The sense.** ལྷས་ཚོར་དུས་ཏེ is then "the time felt by the god (the spirit)", opposed to "the time felt by oneself" (རང་ཚོར་དུས་ཡིན, rang tshor dus yin) a few lines on; it agrees with the commentaries' «the time of feeling» (Khro ru and the annotator of the Supplement) and with the verse's own conclusion, "the sign that a spirit strikes" (གདོན་གྱིས་འདེབས་པའི་རྟགས). The agent reading needs no emendation of the letters (no text has ལུས). Rank: moderate; it rests on the grammar of the idiom and on one commentary's pairing, not on a gloss of the word, and no dictionary glosses the clause.
