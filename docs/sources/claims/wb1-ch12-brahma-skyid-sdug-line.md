# Ch. 12 p. 95: the Brahma wheel's line reads skyid sdug, not skyid dug

On the Gser thang reprint of vol. 1 p. 95 the line under the Brahma wheel (the wheel for testing the good and the not good of the questioner's state) reads «སྐྱིད་སྡུག་ནས་བརྩམས་ཞེས་བཤད་པོ»: the happiness-and-suffering pair (སྐྱིད་སྡུག, skyid sdug) is written with the stacked «སྡུག», not «དུག» (poison), and the line's last word «བཤད་པོ» is split over the line break, «བཤད་» ending the first line and «པོ» opening the second. The sense, "it is told as begun from happiness and suffering", is the one the text gives. Which of the wheel's nine sets of mansions the phrase refers to is not said; it is not a count or a reckoning rule.
