# The 夢合長者鑑 of 1886 (NDL pid 899567) is the dream part of the 万代大雑書 plates and has no table of days and no 初夢

The 夢合長者鑑 (Suzuki Kiemon, 1886; NDL pid 899567, open, 34 images) was looked at image by image: its leaves of running text are plain dream articles in groups, with the pictures and arrangement of the dream section of the 万代大雑書三世相 (mdz:i108), and a folio mark beginning with 万代. It has no table of the days on which a dream is not told, no legend for a slash and no 初夢, so it settles neither q785 nor q788. Its colophon names the price as fifteen sen where the publisher's list of mdz:i190 names forty, which is not explained. The text itself was not read letter by letter; a sentence on the days could hide in the cursive.
