# Sgo chen's liturgies: the offerings of the times, the full moon, the new moon and the eighth, are a tantric commitment whose neglect is confessed

A confession in the liturgy collection of Sgo chen dgon (rank 3 as a compilation; the text's own title and author were not read; etext only) lists among the breaches of the branch commitments (དམ་ཚིག, dam tshig) "the offerings of the times, the full moon, the new moon (ཉ་སྟོང, nya stong), the eighth (ཚེས་བརྒྱད, tshes brgyad) and the rest, left undone", beside unclear approach and accomplishment, lack of cleanliness, laziness and miserliness; a like confession on etext p. 568 has "the offerings of the full and new moon and of the six times".

So for a holder of tantric commitments in this lineage's daily liturgy the 15th, the 30th and the 8th are days on which an offering is owed, "and the rest" leaving the list open (the tenth is not named in the line). No benefit is stated; the line states an obligation by naming its breach. Lay people are not addressed apart from those who hold the commitments.
