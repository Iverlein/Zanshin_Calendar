# The fast days: the 8th, 14th and 15th of each half, or the full moon, the new moon and the eighth

The sources read do not give one list of the fast days.

- The *Lokaprajñapti* (ljz:i41-47, etext): the 8th, 14th and 15th of each half-month, and the half-month of miracles.
- The commentary on the *Treasury* (akb:i364-365, etext): only the pledge to keep the vow "always on the eighth"; no other day.
- Lo chen Dharma shrI (dpn:i55, dpn:i58, scan): householders take it on the full moon, the new moon and the eighth, «ཉ་སྟོང་ཚེས་བརྒྱད»; no 14th, and no word on the eighth of the waning half.
- The app (SPEC §5.8, after Edition Rabten, festivals.md): the 8th, the 15th and the 30th as days for the eight precepts.

Lo chen's three and the app's three agree if "the eighth" is the eighth of the waxing half. The *Lokaprajñapti*'s 14th (and the 29th it implies for the dark half) and the 23rd are in neither. All three sources are rank 3; none of them is weighed against another here, and the White Beryl and Mipham were not read for this. Until they are, the app's three days stand on Lo chen's sentence as well as on Edition Rabten, and the six days of the *Lokaprajñapti* are recorded, not built.
