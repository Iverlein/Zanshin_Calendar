# The Sha za brtsegs pa'i rgyud's dream-sign chapter lists signs of approach to a killing rite, not dream images

The Sha za brtsegs pa'i rgyud's "chapter of the dream signs" (BDRC's 19th section, the tantra's own colophon: the ninth) is on one page, printed p. 449 (img. 463 of I1KG3558; the volume's image is the printed page plus 14). On the scan its colophon reads "the chapter of the dream signs, the ninth section", but the verses before it are not a list of dream images. They are the answer to Great Black One's request to show "the signs of the approach and accomplishment" of a wrathful, killing work: a black man, a black dog, a raven, the vajra master, a mantra-holder with a black man and a black horse, tiger, leopard, bear, thunder, black cloud, lightning near, and then flesh and blood given, keeping company with killers, making war, so that one goes swiftly to the enemy. No line says "in a dream", no image is called good or bad, and no remedy or rule is given; the tone is that of omens of success for a destructive rite. Whether the colophon's title means these signs are seen in dreams is not settled by the page. None of the images is in WB ch. 30's list ([dream-wb30-concordance](dream-wb30-concordance.md)): the nearest are the dark and the thunder of B2, which WB takes as bad where this text gives thunder as a sign of nearness, and animals ridden in G6, where here the animals only appear. Read by Yigdzin-1 on the scan; lines 1-2 and the colophon eye-checked on a 2x crop.
