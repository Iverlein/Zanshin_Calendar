# The servant's fine start, and the old text's wood example

Reply 140 (ys:i497-498): the tenth circle's fine starting point, "by the four nails of the elements between" (wb.2:p10-11), is the servants' own; an older text hid it at its end, after all the starting points, as four unchanging nails, each with a fixed year: tiger, horse and dog from the earth male tiger (the east); rat, dragon and monkey from the water male dragon (the south); pig, sheep and hare from the wood female pig (the north); bird, ox and snake from the fire female bird (the west); and in its body the rough start "water bird".

Stated: the four nails and their years, and that they are the servants' fine start. Inferred: the four years are WB's relations seen from a wood person: earth is the friend of wood (the element wood overcomes), water its mother, wood the same, fire its son, and "water bird", the rough start from the mother's bird, fits wood too. So the old text, like the *Moonbeams* and Zhechen Gyaltsab, works its example for wood (claims/thirty-circles-whose-element.md); the regent does not name the element. Which year the servant is reckoned by, his own or the master's (wb.2:p533b), the reply does not say.
