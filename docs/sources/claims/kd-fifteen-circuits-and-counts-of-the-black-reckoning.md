# The fifteen circuits of the Chinese side, the cherished things of man, child and woman, and the counts of gates, circuits and hidden points

Nor brang O rgyan lists fifteen "circuits" (སྐོར་འགོ, skor 'go) of the Chinese side, each an image tied to a reckoning: mother-and-child concord, the bride's road, site and house, field and land, wealth, the child's birth-womb, recognising the god, recognising the 'dre, the deeds of lives, the keg reckoning, dar gud, burning the corpse, the grave's slayer, the grave site, and the three-headed earth lord's helping gto; and fifteen for the Tibetan side by class of person. What is cherished for man, child, woman and great person is the crossed vajra as life, the eight-spoke wheel as body, the turquoise swastika as power (དབང་ཐང) and the eight-petalled lotus as the klung. The gates of the reckoning are 84,000, the circuits of the elements 1,624 in six groups (trigrams, mewa, dar gud, year and month, planets and mansions, zangs kyam), and the hidden points 120, 180 and 180 of sky, middle and earth, or 360 each, gathered into eight kinds.

An outline of how the Black reckoning counts its own parts, from a modern author; the counts (84,000; 1,624; 120+180+180) are not reconciled with anything in WB and give no rule. The "three-headed earth lord" (item 15) is the only earth lord named, as the object of a helping gto.
