# The thirty's element: WB's own «ལོ་ཁམས», the year's element, in a count of the same kind

In the household round's close (WB vol. 1, p. 387, img. 397, l. 2–6, on the scan; read again on the Gser thang reprint I3CN5028 img. 382 for RD138) WB gives a count of the same kind as the thirty's: «བརྩི་བྱའི་ལོ་ཁམས་གང་ཡིན་གྱི། སྐྱེས་པ་བུ་[འདོད]་སྟག་ཐོག་ནས། … ཐུར་དུ་འདྲེན། བུད་མེད་མ་ཡི་སྤྲེལ་ཐོག་ནས། གྱེན་དུ་ལོ་གྲངས་བརྩིས་པ་ཡིས།», "of whatever the year-element (ལོ་ཁམས, lo khams) of the one reckoned is: a man from the tiger of the son (the syllable after བུ is read «འདོད» on the etext, a doubt left on the scan), down by the years lived; a woman from the monkey of the mother, up". The start animal is picked by a relation (son, mother) to an element, the direction by the sex, the places reached then fight the one reckoned's life force, body, power and luck: the thirty's frame (p. 453) in the year of age.

Here the element the relation is taken from is named by WB as the year-element of the one reckoned, «ལོ་ཁམས». The *Moonbeams*' worked count for a wood male rat starts the man from the fire tiger, the woman from the water monkey (claim thirty-element-moonbeams-example): fire is wood's son and water its mother, so «ལོ་ཁམས» is wood, the name-element of the year, the power's. This is WB's own wording for the element of a start in a count of this kind, and it fits the notes' «ལོ་རྟ་དང་ཁམས་མཐུན» (the year's element) for the thirty (wb.2:p532-533) and «བརྩི་བྱའི་ཁམས» of p. 453.

Not settled: p. 387 is the year of age's outer round, not ch. 26, and no sentence of ch. 26 says «ལོ་ཁམས». The reading that the thirty's «བརྩི་བྱའི་ཁམས» is the year's name-element (the power) is now backed by WB's own word in a parallel count, the notes' «ལོ་རྟ» and the *Moonbeams*' example, and contradicted by none; it stays an inference, and the body element is not excluded by any of them.
