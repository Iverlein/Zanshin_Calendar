# Death dreams of 'Deceiving Death', the Svapnohana and Klong rdol, figure by figure

Which of the death dreams of 'Deceiving Death' (CBL verses 19-20, the Derge's cbs:p247-249) return in the Svapnohana (SVO, the whole dream list read in the pool as svo:p269-271) and in Klong rdol bla ma's verses (KBS, the claim kbs-death-omen-dreams). A figure counts as shared when the same person, beast, colour or act stands in both; the sign's meaning is compared in the last column.

| Figure | 'Deceiving Death' | Svapnohana | Klong rdol |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Riding a donkey | south, not returning: dies; riding it fast south, body anointed, in red: six months; carried off on a donkey: a year | a cart drawn by a donkey and a she-camel, climbed alone and waking seated on it: "quickly death" (svo:p269): the same sign | a donkey's mouth turned east, a camel's south |
| Riding a monkey | south and waking there: dies; east and waking: last night | a monkey (or pig) appearing among horned and fanged beasts: fear from the lord, not death (svo:p269) | a monkey turned north and ridden away |
| Woman in black | a woman in black with a man of desire and a red garland of karavira: goes to the Lord of Death's place | a woman in black clothes with a black garland, embraced: glory grows (svo:p270): the reverse | a black woman walking without a belly |
| Red garland, red clothes | scent, red garland or red clothes found in a dream: eight months | a woman holding red clothes with a red garland and unguent, embraced: "for him there is no next night" (svo:p270): death, the same sign | wearing red and gathering red flowers |

Result. Three signs of 'Deceiving Death' are death signs in the Svapnohana too (the donkey cart, the woman in red with a red garland, sesame oil as a lesser sign); three figures stand in both but with the opposite meaning (the woman in black, the bound person, the fruit tree); the Svapnohana has no climb of ash, sand, a stupa or an ant-hill, no darkness, pit or prison, no iron staff and no being eaten, and none of 'Deceiving Death''s months appears in it (it sorts by the watch of the night, svo:p268). Klong rdol shares the donkey, the monkey, the black woman, red clothes and flowers, and the ash and stupa with 'Deceiving Death', and shares with the Svapnohana only the drinking of beer (as a death sign for him, a good one in the Svapnohana). No line is common to any two of the three texts. Of the Svapnohana's list only "the donkey cart" and "the red woman" are death signs at all (the rest is fortune and illness), so the Svapnohana is no source of the death verses and the death verses are no source of the Svapnohana; Klong rdol's list agrees with 'Deceiving Death' in more figures than with the Svapnohana (the comparison of contents of claim death-omen-dreams-indian-layer, here worked figure by figure).
