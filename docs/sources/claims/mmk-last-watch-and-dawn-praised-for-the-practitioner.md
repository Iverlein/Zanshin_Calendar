# The last watch and dawn are praised for the practitioner's sleep and rising (RD372)

For the practitioner the root tantra praises the last watch of the night and dawn: "whoever sleeps in the last watch, those who rise at dawn: his accomplishment is always the best; he is seen doing all acts" (Derge etext [p408], among the marks of one who accomplishes), and a birth at dawn, when the sun's disc rises, "success appears". This is the only place where a watch is called good, and it is about the sleep and the rising, not about the dream; it is not said that the last watch is the "true" watch of the dream verse, but the two lines (the fourth «བདེན་པར་འབྱུང་བ» and the last watch's accomplishment) fit the later reading that the dream of the last watch is the one to ask for.
