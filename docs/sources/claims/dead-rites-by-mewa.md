# Death on a bad mewa: the skull and the place by the mewa

# Death on a bad mewa: the skull and the place by the mewa

WB vol. 2, p. 112 (Yigdzin-1, read through on the Gser thang reprint): for a death on a bad mewa (སྨེ་བ, sme ba) a wheel of three rims is drawn with the dead's own nine-island square in the centre, a mantra for each mewa on six spokes from the One White on, and "the bad strikes of year, month, day and hour of the One White (and so on) that lead the dead". The wheel goes into a skull by the mewa and is hidden at a place; each one's own thread-cross then reverses it.

| Mewa | Skull | Place |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 white | yak, with horse blood | a crossroads in the north |
| 2 black | sheep | inside a mountain in the south-west |
| 3 blue | ox | a rock in the east |
| 4 green | bird | a mountain in the south-east |
| 5 yellow | ox | the north-east corner |
| 6 white | dog | the north-west |
| 7 red | man | the west |
| 8 white | horse | the north-east corner |
| 9 red | man | a mountain in the south |

Five and eight both go to the north-east as Yigdzin-1 reads them; the eighth's direction was not checked on the scan. Mipham's notes count nine mewa remedies (p. 517).
