# P. 112: the 5 yellow and the 8 white both go to the north-east corner in the print

On p. 112 of WB vol. 2 (Gser thang reprint I3CN5029 img. 110 +2, i.e. img. 118, ll. 13 and 15, 1.3× line crops, 2026-11-01) the mewa rite lays the 5 yellow in an ox's skull «བྱང་ཤར་མཚམས་སུ་སྦ་བ…» and the 8 white in a horse's skull «བྱང་ཤར་མཚམས་སུ་སྦ་བར་བྱ», both at the north-east corner as the 1996 setting prints, with Yigdzin-1. The print reads «བྱང་ཤར» at both lines, so the 8 white's direction is not a machine slip. Whether the print's second «བྱང་ཤར» is a copying slip for another corner is not decided by the page; no other witness read.
