# The measure of life from the moon-star's hour at birth

The measure of life from the hour of the moon-star at birth (WB vol. 2, p. 379): from the moon-star's hours one to sixty at the time of birth, the first fifteen give a lifespan of a hundred years, hours 16 to 30 eighty, 31 to 45 sixty, 46 to 60 forty. For an exact figure the hours elapsed are multiplied by 120 and divided by 90: the quotient is years, and the remainders are carried to months and days by the multipliers sun (12) and sky-fire (30), then to hours and breaths. Deceptions of death (འཆི་བསླུ, 'chi bslu) are then subtracted and added by the planets, none for the first part; the text says this is the rough lifespan and that the fine reckoning by the nine planets is kept hidden, apart.
