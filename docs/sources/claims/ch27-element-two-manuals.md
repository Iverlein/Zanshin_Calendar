# Whose element: two manuals' example of the thirty, wood rat from the iron tiger

Question 259 for ch. 26's thirty and for the tenth circle's master, from two further manuals found by BDRC's search (2026-10-28; snippets of etexts, read in romanisation, not on a scan):

- *Nag rtsis snang srid me long dang 'bras rtsis bden don snying po* and a *Sgo bzhi mdzod lnga'i yang bcud* (Nag rtsis, 29) have the same lines for the thirty's second starting point: "the enemy of the element of the one reckoned from the tiger (brtsi bya'i khams kyi dgra stag nas); for example, if the one reckoned is a wood rat, count from the iron tiger; if he is fifteen, from the wood dragon, the mother's tiger", counted "downward" for the years lived. The example's element is the year's own (the wood of «wood rat»), not the body element, which for a rat would be water: for the thirty too the element is the year's own, as the notes say for the circles (p. 532–533). Snippets only; the line is cut before the rest of the example.
- Zhechen Gyaltsab's servant circle («དེ་ཡང་བདག་པོའི་ལོ་དང་སྦྱར་བར་བཤད», zg:p303-304; his etext) is already in claim circles-zg-worked.

Nothing more on whose element the thirty uses beyond this example; no text read says "year" outright.
