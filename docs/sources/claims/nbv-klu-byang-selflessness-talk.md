# Klu byang's remedy of the dream is the talk of selflessness (RD373)

Klu byang is Klu'i byang chub (Nāgabodhi), and the "two remedies" the Sngags rim chen mo credits to Mar me mdzad bzang po and Klu byang are two different things: Klu byang's is a teaching, not a rite. In his rite of the Guhyasamāja mandala in twenty parts (Derge Bstan 'gyur etext VE23703_1351, pp. 263-292) the dawn passage says: after the kusha and the dream, "at dawn, having heard the virtuous and the non-virtuous, let the virtuous be increased again; the dream, whether non-virtuous or virtuous, purify by the talk of selflessness" (nbv:p273-274). The remedy is the talk (གཏམ, gtam) of selflessness (བདག་མེད་པ, bdag med pa), which the Sngags rim chen mo takes as "thinking on the meaning of selflessness". The text names no fire offering, no mantra, no watch and no day. The line break falls between «བདག» and «མེད་པའི» and the clause's grammar (what is said of the virtuous dream) is a doubt.

The pupil's dream in this text is the dream of the night asked at dawn, as in the others.
