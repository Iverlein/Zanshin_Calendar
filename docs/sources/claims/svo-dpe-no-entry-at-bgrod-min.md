# The Dpe bsdur ma's notes record no print differing at «བགྲོད་མིན», «འགྲོན་བུ», the Gāyatrī; «ལ» at «རྟ་བླ་མོ»

In the Dpe bsdur ma's notes for the Svapnohana (printed pp. 1302-1304), no entry records a variant at «བགྲོད་མིན», at «འགྲོན་བུ» or at the Gāyatrī «ག་ཡ་ཏྲི» in any print, the Co ne included; the single «ཟློག» entry of p. 1302 is Peking's and the last of its page, at «ལྡོག་པར་ངེས»; the third entry of p. 1304, Peking and Narthang «ལ», is at «རྟ་བླ་མོ» (the base «བླ»), and no Co ne is named there. The entries have no quoted lemma, so each is placed by its page and order; the silence about the Co ne does not agree with the Co ne's machine readings «བཟློག» (2 of 4) and «གས་ཏྲི» (9 of 9) and is of the weak kind described in svo:p271u.
