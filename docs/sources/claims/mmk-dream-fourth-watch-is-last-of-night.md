# The dream verse's fourth watch is the night's last, before dawn; the pages do not say 'the one between midnight and dawn' (RD378, q1050)

The dream verse's fourth watch (mmk.1:p397: the true dream) is, as far as the tantra goes, the last watch of the night before dawn; nothing says it is the one "between midnight and dawn" and no other. What the pages give: (1) a watch is an eighth of the day-and-night, four by night (mmk.1:p438-439), so the night's four lie one after another from dusk to dawn; (2) the earthquake omens set midnight at the second watch's close and dawn after the third (claim mmk-ch18-night-omens-name-three-watches); (3) ch. 18's births name "the last watch of the night" (mmk.1:p430) and ch. 15's marks of the one who accomplishes name sleeping in the last watch and rising at dawn (mmk.1:p408). From these the fourth watch is the last of the night, ending at dawn; the stretch after midnight holds the third and the fourth watch, so "between midnight and dawn" would name both. This is our reading of the pages; the Sanskrit edition has no passage that settles it (it says «tṛtīye yāme» on p. 175 and p. 199 and «yāmānte» p. 176 and uses a "quarter of the night" on p. 199, mmk.2:p179-181).
