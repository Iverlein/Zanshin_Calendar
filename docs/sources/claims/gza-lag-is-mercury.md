# «gza' lag» in the Mchog dga's dream list is Mercury: the book gives it as lord of Gemini and Virgo and writes it «gza' lhag» in the root (q1019)

The planet called «གཟའ་ལག» (gza' lag) in the Mchog dga' (མཆོག་དགའ, Mchog dga') is Mercury. The book's table of the twelve houses gives seven planets in order and names «གཟའ་ལག་པ» as the lord of both Gemini and Virgo, which only Mercury is; in the chart of the thirty parts it writes «གཟའ་ལྷག་པ» (gza' lhag pa) and «ལྷག་པ» (lhag pa) in the fourth of the five planets Mars, Saturn, Jupiter, Mercury, Venus; and the root text of the Dbyangs 'char (དབྱངས་འཆར) spells the dream line «གཟའ་ལྷག་དག་ལ་རྨི་ལམ་ནི། ཆོས་བཤད་པ་དང་ཉན་པ་རྨི» in the Derge etext and in the dpe bsdur ma etext alike. So the planet that gives dreams of teaching and hearing the Dharma is Mercury, ལྷག་པ, and «ལག» in the Kun 'dus etext is the etext's spelling for «ལྷག» (it prints both).
