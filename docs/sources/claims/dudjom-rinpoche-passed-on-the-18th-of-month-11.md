# Dudjom Rinpoche passed on the 18th of the eleventh month of the fire-hare year (17 January 1987)

Khenpo Tsewang Dongyal's life of Bdud 'joms 'Jigs bral ye shes rdo rje (Dudjom Rinpoche), the lineage's own text (KTD), opens its tenth chapter with the day: 17 January 1987 by the Western calendar, the Tibetan fire-hare royal year 2114, the 11th month, the 18th day (ktd:i215, read on the scan). The Rigpa students' calendar keeps his anniversary on 11/18 (tbc:p1); a monastery in exile holds his passing offering (དགོངས་རྫོགས་དུས་མཆོད, dgongs rdzogs dus mchod) in Tibetan month 11 without a day (gtb:p2). Jeff Watt's list has no day for him.

- Day: the 18th of the eleventh Tibetan month.
- Source: KTD, of Rtsa gsum gling pa's lineage at Padma Samye Ling (rank beside the manuals, owner 2026-11-07); facts only, the book being copyrighted.
- The life gives the Tibetan month by number (Hor month) and the royal year, not the Phugpa month's name; which almanac's eleventh month it is the page does not say.
