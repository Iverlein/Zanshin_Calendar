# WB pp. 359–363: the date's earth lords after the Black Moon, in the print's order

The date's earth lords open at the end of p. 359 with the Black Moon (ཟླ་ནག, zla nag). The run that follows, in the print's order:

1. p. 360: the Black Sun (ཉི་ནག, nyi nag), its reckoning in brief.
2. p. 360: Little Black (ནག་ཆུང, nag chung).
3. p. 360: Pi ling 'phar ma (པི་ལིང་འཕར་མ, pi ling 'phar ma), of eleven lords and ministers.
4. p. 360: Zin-phung the Black (ཟིན་ཕུང་ནག་པོ, zin phung nag po).
5. p. 361: Phung-po Zor-thogs (ཕུང་པོ་ཟོར་ཐོགས, phung po zor thogs).
6. p. 361: the nine gods' meeting (ལྷ་དགུ་འཚོག, lha dgu 'tshog) and the five gods' meeting (ལྷ་ལྔ་འཚོག, lha lnga 'tshog).
7. p. 361: Ki kang (ཀི་ཀང, ki kang).
8. p. 362: the Hal-dog (ཧལ་ཁྱི, hal khyi), the Sky Dog (གནམ་ཁྱི, gnam khyi), Sky-joining (གནམ་སྦྱོར, gnam sbyor), the Planet-ogre (གཟའ་རྒོད, gza' rgod), the Poor-hand (དབུལ་ལག, dbul lag).
9. p. 363: the seven planets' envoy (གཟའ་བདུན་ཕོ་ཉ, gza' bdun pho nya), Ngams-shing (ངམས་ཤིང, ngams shing), the Bar-gnam Hal-dog (བར་གནམ་ཧལ་ཁྱི, bar gnam hal khyi), Khyung Ki-kang (ཁྱུང་ཀི་ཀང, khyung ki kang), the Great Net (དྲ་ཆེན, dra chen).

Items 1–7 were read line by line on the reprint (img. 366–367) on 2026-11-05. Items 8–9 are the 1996 etext and the machine reading collated, not confirmed by eye. The passage held under wb.2:p360-361 until that date was the text of pp. 358–359. None of these lines gives the rule that brings a lord to a day, and none numbers the lords as lords of the date by number.
