# The time of a dream's fruit by the watch of the night in the Indian texts: the Svapnohana's series is the Brahmavaivartapurāṇa's (RD265)

RD265 (2026-11-09) read nine more Indian texts on the time of a dream's fruit by the watch of the night, in the text layers of published editions on the Internet Archive, and RD269 (2026-11-09) then read the verses on time of seven of them on the page images (the Brahmavaivarta, Negelein's print, the Kālaprakāśikā, the Bṛhadyātrā, the Matsya, the Agni and the Aṅgavijjā's time lists); it corrected the fourth span of the Kālaprakāśikā and the pages of three passages (below). Together with RD263's two (apv, kps) and the Svapnohana (svo) they give this table. "Dawn" is the hour before sunrise (*aruṇodaya*); "cows" the hour of the cows' release (*go-visarga*).

| Text | 1st watch | 2nd | 3rd | 4th or last part | dawn | cows / sunrise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Atharva-Veda *Pariśiṣṭa* 68.2.58–59 (apv:p445) | a year | 8 months | half of that | — | at once | a month |
| *Brahmavaivartapurāṇa*, *Kṛṣṇajanmakhaṇḍa* 73.5–6 (bvp:p662-665, p. 661 of the print) | a year | 8 months | 3 months | half a month | 10 days | a morning dream: at once if one is woken |
| **Svapnohana** (svo:p268) | a year | 8 months | 3 months | half a month | 10 days | — |

Negelein prints variants beside the *Svapnacintāmaṇi* (neg:p14-16, read on the scan by RD269, with his own list of what each letter is): **b 5–6**, "dvitīye cā'ṣṭabhir māsais tribhir māsais tṛtīyake | caturthe cā'rdhamāsena … daśāhe phaladaḥ svapno 'py aruṇo-'daya-darśane" (the Brahmavaivarta's series, which is the Svapnohana's); **f 2** (a Leipzig manuscript called a *Svapnādhyāya* of the *Matsya*; with m, n, q): a year, "eight months … six months" (so printed, marked (!)), "triyāmakaiḥ", a month for the fourth, ten days at dawn, at once at the cows' release; **h 16f** (Agni 229, as the *Adbhutasāgara* p. 514 quotes it): a year, six months, three months, a month; **i 2f** (a Leipzig manuscript): "dvitīye cā'ṣṭame māse triyāme ca tripakṣake | tūrye yāme tu yaḥ svapno māsena phalado bhavet | aruṇodaya-(pra)velāyāṃ daśāhena" (eight months, three fortnights, a month, ten days); **a 3–5b** (the Oxford manuscript, "cf. AP 68.2.58f."): a year, eight months, three months, a month for the fourth, the dawn line garbled. The layer RD265 read had lettered the "i" verse as "s". The *Adbhutasāgara* (adb:p513-515) quotes for the dreams (p. 514, read on the image by RD269) a verse of Parāśara (a year, half a year, three months and a month, "growing quarter on quarter"; the *Kālaprakāśikā*'s series), the *Bṛhadyātrā* verse as "Varāha's" ("varṣārdhārdha-bhāge" for the third watch), and the *Matsya* and *Viṣṇudharmottara* verse, so it adds no series of its own. A late Arab report in Negelein (from Zedler's lexicon) has 20 years, 10 years, a month and 10 days for early night, midnight, morning and noon: a stepped series of the same kind with other numbers.

What follows:

- **The Svapnohana's series is the Brahmavaivartapurāṇa's, word for number**: a year, eight months, three months, half a month, ten days at dawn. RD263 found that the Svapnohana agrees with the Atharva appendix in its first two spans only and with the *Kālaprakāśikā* in kind; the series it follows whole is this Purāṇic verse (and Negelein's parallel "b 5–6", the same words: Negelein's own list names b as "the *Svapnādhyāya* of the *Brahmavaivarttapurāṇa*", Introduction p. XIX). The eight months of the second watch are the Atharva appendix's, kept by the Brahmavaivarta; the six months of the second watch belong to the *Agni*, the *Matsya*, the *Bṛhadyātrā*, the *Svapnacintāmaṇi* and the *Kālaprakāśikā*.
- One verbal echo: the Svapnohana's fourth-watch line says "half a month, seen without doubt" (svo:p268, claim [svo-watches-and-spans](svo-watches-and-spans.md)), and the *Matsyapurāṇa*'s fourth-watch line ends "paśyato nātra saṃśayaḥ" ("of him who sees it, no doubt", mtp:p453-455; the *Adbhutasāgara*'s quotation has "pacyante nātra saṃśayaḥ"), while the *Brahmavaivarta*'s fourth-watch line has "half a month" (bvp:p662-665): the Svapnohana's numbers are the Brahmavaivarta's and its phrase the *Matsya*'s, so its source was a text of that family, not the Atharva appendix.
- The two Purāṇas the *Adbhutasāgara* names together, the *Agni* and the *Matsya*, differ only in the fourth watch (half a month; a month); the *Agni*'s verses with the spans stand in a bracket of the print that two of its manuscripts lack; the *Adbhutasāgara*'s quotation under the name of both mixes their words.
- The spans at dawn are ten days in the Purāṇas and the *Svapnacintāmaṇi*, twelve in the *Kālaprakāśikā*, "at once" in the Atharva appendix; the cows' release is a month in the appendix, "at once" in the *Bṛhadyātrā* and the same day in the *Kālaprakāśikā*.
- **None of the texts ties a span to a lunar day, a weekday, a nakṣatra or a month.** The only clock is the watch of the night and the hour of dawn or of the cows' release. What the dream texts do tie to the season and the lunar day is the *kind* of dream and the time at which it was *seen*: [bya-dream-humours-parts-of-night-seasons](bya-dream-humours-parts-of-night-seasons.md) and [angavijja-dream-time-fortnight-season](angavijja-dream-time-fortnight-season.md); the omens other than dreams are tied to the lunar day, the nakṣatra and the season: [indian-omens-tied-to-lunar-day-nakshatra-season](indian-omens-tied-to-lunar-day-nakshatra-season.md).
- Of two dreams in a night the later counts, and after a good dream one does not sleep again, in the Atharva appendix, the *Agni*, the *Matsya*, the *Bṛhadyātrā*, the *Svapnacintāmaṇi* and the *Kālaprakāśikā*; the Brahmavaivarta says that sleep after a good dream loses its fruit.

**Corrected by RD269 (2026-11-09):** the *Kālaprakāśikā*'s four spans are a year, half a year, three months, a month (the print: "varṣād varṣārdhāt trimāsān māsataḥ tathā"); RD265's "(half a month?)" for the fourth, "a month" for the third and its restoration were wrong. The *Bṛhadyātrā*'s "varṣa-pāda" is on the page. Where the text said that the *Matsya* ch. 242 stands on pp. 453–455, the *Agni* ch. 229 on pp. 280–281 and the *Brahmavaivarta* verses on p. 662, the page images put them on pp. 493–494, 281–282 and 661 of their prints.

Doubt: the *Matsya*, *Agni* and *Brahmavaivarta* rest each on one print of the text (the Ānandāśrama and Jivananda prints; Negelein's b 5–6 is a second witness to the Brahmavaivarta); the Atharva appendix (apv) and the Svapnohana's own line were not read again on the images.
