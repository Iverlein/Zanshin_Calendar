# The time of a dream's fruit by the watch of the night in the Indian texts: the Svapnohana's series is the Brahmavaivartapurāṇa's (RD265)

RD265 (2026-11-09) read nine more Indian texts on the time of a dream's fruit by the watch of the night, in the text layers of published editions on the Internet Archive (machine readings of OCR, the page images not read; the layers differ in slips but the verses on time agree where two copies exist). Together with RD263's two (apv, kps) and the Svapnohana (svo) they give this table. "Dawn" is the hour before sunrise (*aruṇodaya*); "cows" the hour of the cows' release (*go-visarga*).

| Text | 1st watch | 2nd | 3rd | 4th or last part | dawn | cows / sunrise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Atharva-Veda *Pariśiṣṭa* 68.2.58–59 (apv:p445) | a year | 8 months | half of that | — | at once | a month |
| *Brahmavaivartapurāṇa*, *Kṛṣṇajanmakhaṇḍa* 73.5–6 (bvp:p662-665) | a year | 8 months | 3 months | half a month | 10 days | a morning dream: at once if one is woken |
| **Svapnohana** (svo:p268) | a year | 8 months | 3 months | half a month | 10 days | — |
| *Agnipurāṇa* 229 (228 in Mitra) 16–17 (agp:p280-281) | a year | 6 months | 3 months | half a month | 10 days | — |
| *Matsyapurāṇa* 242.17–20 (mtp:p453-455) | a year | 6 months | 3 months | a month | 10 days | — |
| *Bṛhadyātrā* 16.30 (bya:p41-46) | a year | half a year | a quarter-year | a month | — | at once |
| *Svapnacintāmaṇi* 1.15–17 (neg:p14-16) | a year | half a year | 3 months | a month | last 48 minutes of the night: within 10 days | sunrise: at once |
| *Kālaprakāśikā* 42.66–67 (kpd:p184-189; Iyer's English kps:p232-236) | a year | half a year | a month | (half a month?) | 12 days | that same day |

Negelein prints variants beside the *Svapnacintāmaṇi* (neg:p14-16): "aṣṭabhir māsais tribhir māsais … ardhamāsena … daśāhe" (the Brahmavaivarta's series, which is the Svapnohana's) and "dvitīye cāṣṭame māse triyāme ca tripakṣake … māsena … daśahena" (eight months, three fortnights, a month, ten days). The *Adbhutasāgara* (adb:p513-515) quotes for the dreams a verse of Parāśara (garbled), the *Bṛhadyātrā* verse as "Varāha's", and the *Matsya* and *Viṣṇudharmottara* verse, so it adds no series of its own. A late Arab report in Negelein (from Zedler's lexicon) has 20 years, 10 years, a month and 10 days for early night, midnight, morning and noon: a stepped series of the same kind with other numbers.

What follows:

- **The Svapnohana's series is the Brahmavaivartapurāṇa's, word for number**: a year, eight months, three months, half a month, ten days at dawn. RD263 found that the Svapnohana agrees with the Atharva appendix in its first two spans only and with the *Kālaprakāśikā* in kind; the series it follows whole is this Purāṇic verse (and Negelein's parallel "b 5–6", the same words). The eight months of the second watch are the Atharva appendix's, kept by the Brahmavaivarta; the six months of the second watch belong to the *Agni*, the *Matsya*, the *Bṛhadyātrā*, the *Svapnacintāmaṇi* and the *Kālaprakāśikā*.
- One verbal echo: the Svapnohana's fourth-watch line says "half a month, seen without doubt" (svo:p268, claim [svo-watches-and-spans](svo-watches-and-spans.md)), and the *Matsyapurāṇa*'s fourth-watch line ends "paśyato nātra saṃśayaḥ" ("of him who sees it, no doubt", mtp:p453-455; the *Adbhutasāgara*'s quotation has "pacyante nātra saṃśayaḥ"), while the *Brahmavaivarta*'s fourth-watch line has "half a month" (bvp:p662-665): the Svapnohana's numbers are the Brahmavaivarta's and its phrase the *Matsya*'s, so its source was a text of that family, not the Atharva appendix.
- The two Purāṇas the *Adbhutasāgara* names together, the *Agni* and the *Matsya*, differ only in the fourth watch (half a month; a month); the *Adbhutasāgara*'s quotation under the name of both mixes their words.
- The spans at dawn are ten days in the Purāṇas and the *Svapnacintāmaṇi*, twelve in the *Kālaprakāśikā*, "at once" in the Atharva appendix; the cows' release is a month in the appendix, "at once" in the *Bṛhadyātrā* and the same day in the *Kālaprakāśikā*.
- **None of the texts ties a span to a lunar day, a weekday, a nakṣatra or a month.** The only clock is the watch of the night and the hour of dawn or of the cows' release. What the dream texts do tie to the season and the lunar day is the *kind* of dream and the time at which it was *seen*: [bya-dream-humours-parts-of-night-seasons](bya-dream-humours-parts-of-night-seasons.md) and [angavijja-dream-time-fortnight-season](angavijja-dream-time-fortnight-season.md); the omens other than dreams are tied to the lunar day, the nakṣatra and the season: [indian-omens-tied-to-lunar-day-nakshatra-season](indian-omens-tied-to-lunar-day-nakshatra-season.md).
- Of two dreams in a night the later counts, and after a good dream one does not sleep again, in the Atharva appendix, the *Agni*, the *Matsya*, the *Bṛhadyātrā*, the *Svapnacintāmaṇi* and the *Kālaprakāśikā*; the Brahmavaivarta says that sleep after a good dream loses its fruit.

Doubt: the fourth span of the *Kālaprakāśikā* line is garbled in the layer (the restoration "half a month" is ours); the numbers of the *Bṛhadyātrā* (a quarter-year) are the layer's "varṣa-pāda"; the table rests on OCR of one print of each text.
