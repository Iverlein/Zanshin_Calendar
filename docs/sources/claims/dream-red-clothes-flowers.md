# Red clothes, red flowers, a red woman

Image group: body and mind. Read by 5 texts: Four Tantras (for a sick dreamer), Lama Gongdü (LGD), Svapnohana (SVO), ACIP method (RST), Klong rdol's verses as the article quotes them (KBS).
Offered to any dreamer; the Four Tantras' or WB ch. 25's rows join when the dreamer is ill.
