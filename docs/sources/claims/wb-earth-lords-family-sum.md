# WB's sum of the earth-lord families before the fixed list

Before the list of the fixed earth lords, WB (vol. 2, p. 175) sums the whole family: the fifteen time gods (དུས་ལྷ, dus lha) and the black times, the male and female sky dogs (གནམ་ཁྱི, gnam khyi), the Silma (སིལ་མ, sil ma) and the Dud (བདུད, bdud), the Gogku (འགོག་ཀུ, 'gog ku) and the twelve Gabpa (གབ་པ, gab pa); with the Khol's gods the time gods come to thirty-seven, and Rāhu (རཱ་ཧུ, rā hu), the chief of the planets, though one of the Khol's gods, is set apart and counted as one. A number «༨༩» is given with the root holes and the nāgas (89). The line «བཞི་བརྒྱ་དོན་བཞི» for the comings of the year, month, day and time gods is not settled (404?).
