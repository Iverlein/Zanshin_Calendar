# Rtsa gsum gling pa holds to his dream-prophecies in place of an empowerment and challenged for them

Rtsa gsum gling pa reasons from his dreams to his practice: with "no empowerment and transmission" asked of any lama, "nothing better can be sought elsewhere than those former dream-prophecies" (i114), and the monks said he would go mad with a prophecy without authority (i112). Later in the life (outside the passages above, vol. 30 image 339) people "all slander" him over such dream prophecies; the life does not give a rule on how much trust a dream carries, only that he held to them and was challenged.
