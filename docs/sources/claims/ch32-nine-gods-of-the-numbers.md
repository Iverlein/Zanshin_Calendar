# Ch. 32: the nine gods by number and colour

WB vol. 2, ch. 32, p. 291 (img. 297 of the Gser thang reprint, read by eye, RD156): nine gods of the *gtsug lag* (གཙུག་ལག, gtsug lag), said to come from the mark (ཐིག་ལེ, thig le) on the tortoise's body, numbered one to nine, each with a colour: one, white, The-se rgyal po (ཐེ་སེ་རྒྱལ་པོ, the se rgyal po); two, black, the black hal khyi (ཧལ་ཁྱི, hal khyi); three, dark blue, Byi lam phag mgo (བྱི་ལམ་ཕག་མགོ, byi lam phag mgo); four, green, the blue Byi lam (བྱི་ལམ་སྔོན, byi lam sngon); five, yellow, Pra ha (པྲ་ཧ, pra ha); six, white, the six father gods (ཡབ་ལྷ, yab lha); seven, red, the seven Ya ba rkya (ཡ་བ་རྐྱ, ya ba rkya); eight, white, the eight great lucky gods; nine, red, the mistress of fortune (གཡང་གི་བདག་མོ, g.yang gi bdag mo).
