# UT's year-store places beside WB's four counts

# UT's year-store places and WB's four counts, place by place

For question 664, narrowing the claim ut-chase-not-wb-up-three-count. Set side by side, UT's third chase (ut:p224-226) and the four counts that close WB's year reckoning (wb.2:p171-172) line up four against four, and each count has one place:

- UT second place (the child), store ox: «གླང་ལུག་ཕུང་གྱོད» against WB's three down, «མར་གསུམ་གླང་ལུག་བབས། དེ་ཚེ་འཕུང་གྱོད» (landing on ox or sheep): same pair, same harm.
- UT fifth place (the uncle's brothers'), store dragon: «ཁྱི་འབྲུག་གནམ་སྒོ» against WB's eight up, «ཁྱི་འབྲུག་བབས་ན་གནམ་སྒོ་བྱེ»: same pair, same harm.
- UT fourth place (the father's brothers'), store hare: «བྱ་ཡོས་བྱེ་བྲལ» against WB's three up, «བྱ་སྤྲེལ་བྱུང་ན་བྱེ་བྲལ»: the same harm and the bird, the second sign of the pair differs (hare in UT, monkey in WB).
- UT sixth place (the uncle's brothers' again), store snake: «ཕག་སྦྲུལ་དགྲ་གཤེད» against WB's eight down, «ཕག་སྦྲུལ་ས་སྒོ་བྱེ… གནམ་སྒོ་ལྟར་དུ་བརྩི»: the same pair, the harm differs (the slayer in UT, the earth door in WB).
- UT first (rat and horse, «ཆོད་མ་བབས») and third (tiger and monkey, the seven corners) have no count in WB's four.

So the four counts of WB match the year-store pairs of UT's second, fourth, fifth and sixth places, in the order three down, three up, eight up, eight down; UT keys the harms by place, WB by distance from a landing. That UT's list restates WB's four counts is likely by the four pairs (two the same, one with a different second sign, one with a different harm) but is not said by UT, which does not name WB in these lines; the pairs of the two texts are given, the identification is ours. Read from the etext of UT (the only witness) and the scan of WB; the passages' own states apply.
