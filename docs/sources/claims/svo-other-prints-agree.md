# Peking, Narthang and the Dpe bsdur ma text settle the spelling of the Svapnohana's bcu yi bar, bgrod min and 'gron bu

In the Peking and the Narthang print the Svapnohana reads «བཅུ་ཡི་བར» ("within ten", no unit named) where the Derge etext reads it, so the spelling of the white snake's clause is settled by two prints (Peking clearly on a 40-pixel scan, Narthang by eye) and by the Dpe bsdur ma text, which notes no variant there. The unit of the ten is still not named in any of them. Likewise the clause «བགྲོད་མིན» ("not a road taken") and the traveller «འགྲོན་བུ» are the same in Peking by eye, in Narthang by machine and in the Dpe bsdur ma text: the readings «འགྲོ་ན་བུ» of the Derge machines were noise; the word is «འགྲོན་བུ», traveller or guest. The sense of «བགྲོད་མིན» stays open.
