# A dream sign of the enemy crushed is followed in three months; a bad night dream's loss shows at sunrise (TSL life)

Two short ties of a dream to later events in Rtsa gsum gling pa's life. After a fierce invocation against enemies, a "dream sign of the enemy crushed" (རྨི་ལམ་དགྲ་བོ་སོད་རྟགས) came with hail, and "about three months later" a plague struck the livestock of two thieves and a pair of men died (i119); the interval is three months and the sign's ground is the rite, not the dream's hour. The reverse: after a night dream of a filthy woman smearing dirt on him, at sunrise (i57) his bodily glow, signs and marks, his earlier trained habits, pure visions and clairvoyance were gone "like the traces left when a dream fades"; his mother held it an obstacle; the rite to remove faults followed, and the cause is told as an old insult to a demoness (i56-57). A dream is thus both a forecast (i119) and a sign of a lapse whose effect is on waking, after the night.
