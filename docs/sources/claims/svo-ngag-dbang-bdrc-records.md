# BDRC's records of Vāgīśvarakīrti and Vibhūticandra: dates overlap, no link to the remedy's Ngag dbang

Who the «ངག་དབང» of the remedy's line «འདི་ནི་ངག་དབང་དགོངས་པ་སྟེ» is, the prints do not say: the line is the same in the Derge etext, Peking (img. 318) and Narthang, and no colophon, title or note names him. The only man called Ngag dbang in the neighbourhood is the author of the work printed before it, Ngag gi dbang phyug grags pa, whom the Derge colophon calls teacher (slob dpon) and BDRC (P8946) gives as Vāgīśvarakīrti, flourishing 1000–1199 (ATII record, no dates of birth or death), the teacher of Shunyatasamadhi (P4CZ10577, 11th century). BDRC's record of Vibhūticandra (P00EGS1017688, ATII) gives flourished 1100–1299, centuries 12–13, other names Bhūticandra and «རབ་འབྱོར་ཟླ་བ», and one pupil, the Gnyal translator Mi mnyam bzang po (P6532, 12th century): so the two men's dates overlap in the 12th century. That is all the records say; they do not state that the verse is Vāgīśvarakīrti's, and a shorter name for him in this form is not shown anywhere read. The identification stays probable by neighbourhood and by date, not established (q796 stays open). State: BDRC catalogue records (2026-11-10), not scan.
