# RRT: the six examinations of a practitioner's signs

The 'Bri gung extended method (RRT, vol. 41, pp. 186–195, etext) is for whoever practises a deity or a worldly proud spirit. The signs and marks that arise are divided into six: a good sign shown as good; a bad sign as bad; a bad sign as good; a good sign as bad; the signs of obstacles; the signs of the accomplishment together with the obstruction it brings. Its images are the dreams and visions of the retreat, read against the practice in hand; the text asks what deity is being practised and gives a verdict per deity.
