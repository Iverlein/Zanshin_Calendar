# Śramaṇā rite: the preparation, the visualisation, the request and the lying down

The rite before sleep in the Śramaṇā text: a fast with the abstinences (no afternoon food, meat, beer, garlic and onion, travel, shadowed food), a clean place with a square earth mandala, a vase of water and nectar dressed with white wool, leafy twigs and a blue canopy; refuge, awakening mind and the four immeasurables; Śramaṇā (lha mo shra ma Na), blue, holding a mirror and a branch of the wish-granting tree, on Mt Meru; seven pure grains held in joined palms and the request to show, without deceit, the place of one's next birth or whatever matter is asked, three to twenty-one times; the mantra «oṃ śramaṇa piśācani vimunda svāhā» (twelve times ten thousand for the strongest result); lying down with head north, feet south, face west, back east, head imagined in the goddess's lap; one matter a night, the weightiest first.
