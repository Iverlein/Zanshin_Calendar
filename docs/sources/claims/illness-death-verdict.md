# The illness reckoning's last verdict: death or not; the five people

At the end of the illness reckoning WB lays the verdict on death: if the four aspects (life force, body, power, luck) are in decline, ill, dead or entered the tomb, the element's great enemy has risen and obstacle and tomb meetings are many in the household, death; if the year, month, day and hour carry no obstacle and no tomb meeting, the element meets its mother and the demon-cutting is calm, there is no death however it looks, and rites prevail. Five people serve the sick, chosen by the relation of their element to the sick person's life force: the nurse the mother, the physician the son, the messenger the friend, and the pillow-guard (wb.1:p445; the notes, p. 498).
