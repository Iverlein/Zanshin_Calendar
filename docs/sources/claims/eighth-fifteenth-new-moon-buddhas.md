# Which buddha comes on the 15th and which on the new moon: two orders

Two texts name the buddhas of the month's three chief days and differ on two of them.

- RCZ (rcz:p286-288, scan): the Medicine Buddha (སྨན་བླ, sman bla) on the 8th, Amitābha (འོད་དཔག་མེད, 'od dpag med) on the 15th, Śākyamuni with the thousand buddhas on the new moon (གནམ་གང, gnam gang).
- DTN (dtn:i316, etext only), as the view of "some scholars": the Medicine Buddha on the 8th, the Lord of Sages (ཐུབ་དབང, thub dbang), that is Śākyamuni, on the 15th, Amitābha on the new moon.

Both are rank 3, so neither stands over the other; the White Beryl and Mipham were not read on this point here. DTN itself gives the buddhas as one of three explanations of why the three days are good (the others: the moon's three clear phases; the Four Great Kings, their sons and their servants) and says the three do not contradict each other. Edition Rabten's front matter, after which the app's monthly observances are set, names no buddha for these days as festivals.md records it. DTN shows that the order opposite to RCZ's was held by scholars known to an eighteenth-century Gelug author. DTN's passage rests on the etext and waits for the scan.
