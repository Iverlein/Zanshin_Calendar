# Birth by the twelve links of dependent arising

Birth by the twelve links of dependent arising (WB vol. 2, p. 384): for each link, the days, months and years that the child must live through, the lifespan and the cause and the next link on whose day he dies. Ignorance: nine days, nine months, nine years; eighty-one; dies on the compositional factors' day. Compositional factors: eight, eight, eight; eighty-one; consciousness. Consciousness: five days, five years; sixty-two; by weapon on the name-and-form day. Name-and-form: six days, six months, twelve years; eighty-one. Sense-fields: five days, three months; eighty. Contact: five days, three months, five or nine years; sixty-three. Feeling: twelve days, two months, eight years; sixty. Craving: three or ten days, five months, nine years; eighty-four. Grasping: nine days, two months, eight or nine years; sixty-four. Becoming: nine days, two or three months, nine years; sixty. Birth: nine days, nine months, ten years; sixty. Old age and death: two days, one month, nine years; sixty-four. The figures are as the machine reading gave them, checked against the pattern but not syllable by syllable on the scan.
