# Signs of the second watch's dream, of bile (RD370)

Second watch, of bile: the signs listed are what is yellow, golden or hot: blazing fire, fire-burning, falling stars and burning mountains, jewels such as the ruby, warming at a fire, a scorching sun, a golden sky and earth, gold in vessels, carriages and ornaments, and oneself clothed, garlanded and anointed in yellow; "seen in the second watch, know it as of bile" (mmk.1:p399-400).
