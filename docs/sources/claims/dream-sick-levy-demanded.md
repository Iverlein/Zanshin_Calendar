# A levy of the bro demanded

Image group: the dreamer ill. Read by 1 text: Four Tantras (for a sick dreamer).
Offered only when the dreamer is ill: every row is a reading for a sick dreamer (the Four Tantras above WB ch. 25).

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Four Tantras (for a sick dreamer) | asking the levy of the bro (བྲོ་པའི་ཁྲལ་འདེད, bro pa'i khral 'ded; the commentaries and the other prints བྲོ་བའི་ཁྲལ་འདེད, bro ba'i khral 'ded): a sign that the lord of death (འཆི་བདག, 'chi bdag) has seized the sick; dreamed constantly by the sick it means death, for one without illness it is not certain, and the rite of service (རིམ་གྲོ, rim gro) frees him (bad) | `gt:i42-43` | machine |

RD271 (2026-11-09; dictionaries and BDRC etexts, no scan): a levy of provisions or the like being demanded, an exaction as of a government (gt-bro-ba-khral-ded-a-levy); the dancer's tax (བྲོ་པ) is no text's. Khro ru's gloss is the share of food and goods. Which bro it is, provisions or an oath, is not settled; the verdict does not rest on it. The Drathang's pa and Byang pa's 'dod are minority spellings against the common bro ba'i khral 'ded; RD270 (2026-11-09) read the line by eye on the Chag-po-ri print I3CN1711 and finds བྲོ་བའི (the closed box of ba, unlike the open pa of len pa in the same line; moderate) and འདེད (moderate), so the common spelling stands on a second print (gt-dream-verse-chag-po-ri-prints).

The verse is the Explanatory Tantra's (བཤད་རྒྱུད, bshad rgyud) ch. 7 on the Drathang print (RD268); the Serthang scan lacks the page. The rule on who dies, the cause and the six kinds of dream are in gt-dream-death-rule, the whole list in gt-dream-death-images, the one-syllable differences from the four commentaries in gt-dream-verse-variants. This image claim was added by RD274 (2026-11-09) so that the picker holds every image of the verse; the Drathang passage is a machine reading, the lines read by eye in part (gt:i42-43).
