# The forefathers' tomb obstacle: the trigram, mewa and progressed sign of the forefathers' death

Mipham's notes (vol. 2, p. 492), on the typeset Chengdu Collected Works page, read «ཕ་མེས་ཤི་དུས་ཀྱི་སྤར་སྨེ་ལོག་མིན་ལ་སླེབས་ན་སྲི་ཀེག་གོ», if one reaches the trigram, mewa and progressed sign of the forefathers' time of death (ཕ་མེས་ཤི་དུས, pha mes shi dus) it is the *sri* obstacle (སྲི་ཀེག, sri keg). The 1996 print's «ལོག་མིན་ག་གོ» is a slip for «ལ་སླེབས་ན་སྲི་ཀེག་གོ». So the trigram and the rest are the forefathers' own at their death, not those the death year gives the person reckoned, and 'reaching' is the person's year-of-age trigram, mewa and progressed sign coming onto them; Klong chen's restatement (year-of-life.md) says the same. It needs the forefather's death year, a datum of the family, not of the calendar. BDRC's full text finds WB's line «ཕ་མེས་ཤི་བའི་སྲི་དུས» only in WB's prints.
