# KD's digest: the nine bad yogas, their hours to avoid, and the weight of the meetings

The digest of WB's fifth section (KD vol. 3, p. 733) lists the nine bad yogas as Clearing (སེལ་བ), Very swollen (ཤིན་ཏུ་སྐྲངས), Pain-pin (ཟུག་རྔུ), Swollen (སྐྲངས), All-destroying (ཀུན་འཇོམས), Vajra (རྡོ་རྗེ), Very falling (ཤིན་ཏུ་ལྟུང), All-crushing (ཡོངས་འཇོམས) and Grudge-holder (ཁོན་འཛིན), "the other eighteen good". For each bad yoga it gives the number of water-hours to avoid, counted from the yoga's arrival: Clearing 3, Pain-pin 5, Swollen and Very swollen 6 each, All-destroying and Vajra 9 each, Very falling, All-crushing and Grudge-holder 3 each. Then each yoga's own result in a line, and the Grudge-holder's closes «ཁོན་འཛིན་བུད་མེད་མཛའ་དང་འབྲལ» (a woman is parted from her friend), the reading [yogas.md](../yogas.md) already took from this digest. The hours are the almanac's yoga hours; the digest is a second witness for them, etext only, and WB's own yoga hours are the rank-one source.

The same page weighs the meetings above the weekday and the mansion: even when planet and mansion are each good, a bad meeting turns into its result, and if both are bad a good meeting brings it; the seven days that conquer Mara (བདུད་ལས་རྒྱལ་བ) are good for all works and above all for fierce ones, while the destroying days ('jig nyi), the non-meeting days and the disagreeing days avoid a bride and a funeral feast.
