# BDRC has one Peking Tengyur only, W1KG13126; no second scan of the Peking exists to check the Dpe bsdur ma's Peking entries (RD327)

Only one Peking Tengyur is on BDRC: W1KG13126 (224 volumes, Raghu Vira's copy from Kumbum, a gift of Zhou Enlai), image groups I1KG13197 (vol. 28, the Svapnohana) and I1KG13215 (vol. 46, the Svapnekṣā). The abstract works of the Tengyur (WA0BC002) and of the Svapnekṣā (WA0RT4282) list no second Peking record; archive.org holds `bdrc-W1KG13126` only; W1KG13703 is the catalogue of Otani University's Peking print, without page images. The Dpe bsdur ma's Peking therefore cannot be set beside another scan of the Peking, and the places were read again on this one (svo:p271w).
