# The four Bon reckonings of the Gtsug lag: hidden point, garden of trigrams and mewa, wheel of the elements, life reckoning

In Nam mkha'i nor bu's account (quoted by Tshul khrims rgyal mtshan) the earliest Tibetan reckoning held, besides the three named inner kinds (the reckoning of what is seen, the monthly reckoning of the old woman Bal ma, the spoken reckoning of the old man of Zhang zhung), four reckonings given as the chief of the Bon gates of the Gtsug lag reckoning taught by Gshen rab mi bo che: the hidden point (གབ་རྩེ, gab rtse), the garden of trigrams and mewa (སྤར་ཁ་སྨེ་བའི་གླིང་སྐོར), the wheel of time of the elements (འབྱུང་བ་དུས་ཀྱི་འཁོར་ལོ) and the reckoning of deeds by dependent origination from the birth (ཇུ་ཞག་རྟེན་འབྲེལ་ལས་རྩིས). The hidden point is the elements of the year, month, day and hour with the mewa, trigrams and the gods, nāgas, gnyan or earth lords that rule them, hidden from common sight and sharp in result; the garden sets the nine mewa in the nine cells and the trigrams around them and compares the elements of year, month, day and hour of what is reckoned; the wheel is the twelve-year circuit with the four seasons and the planets and mansions and the meetings of elements in it.

The passage places the trigram and mewa square, the twelve-year circuit and the elements of the year, month, day and hour in a Bon tradition older than the Chinese reckoning of the previous claim. A Bon source (rank: witness only, and KD is a modern author reporting it); it builds nothing.
