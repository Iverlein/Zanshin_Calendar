# The thirty of Mipham's notes (p. 532) are ch. 26's, not ch. 27's p. 17

For question 663 (and q479). The thirty «རྒྱ་བོད་སྐོར་མགོ་སུམ་ཅུ» of Mipham's notes (wb.2:p532-533) are ch. 26's thirty starting points (wb.1:p453-472), not ch. 27's small reckonings of p. 17. Three things in the notes' own words show it. (1) The notes' details are ch. 26's numbers: the tenth is for subjects and servants, the eleventh for horses, "son and bride" (བུ་བག), the house owner, and the copper vessel, and ch. 26's tenth is the palace of the nine continents for subjects, its eleventh the supreme horse, its fourteenth the bride, its fifteenth the house, its seventeenth armour and the copper vessel. (2) The notes' rule of the fight, «ཡར་བཞིའི་སྲོག་ལ་བརྩི་བྱའི་སྲོག་གིས་འཐབ», "the life force of the fourth upward is fought by the life force of the one reckoned", is ch. 26's own (the fourth upward fights the life force, the eleventh upward the power: wb.1:p453-454). (3) p. 17's four reckonings use another rule: the eighteenth upward fights the life force, the tenth upward the power, the eighteenth and tenth downward the luck and the body (wb.2:p17); and they are not on ch. 26's list of subjects: great spiritual friend or chief, ministers, soldiers and weapons (the weapons' one is called «མཚོན་ཆའི་སྐོར་འགོ», the starting point of weapons, only by its own line). The notes name no page of ch. 27 for the small reckonings and say nothing of them in section seven, whose four parts are the thirty, the Balmo circles, the earth lords and the reckoning of results.

So «ལོ་རྟ་དང་ཁམས་མཐུན་པ» (the element agreeing with the year-horse) is the notes' rule for ch. 26's pairs of years, and does not by itself decide the bare «ཁམས» of p. 17: the claim ch27-p17-bare-khams-is-the-year-horse-by-mipham, which read p. 17 as one of the thirty, is not supported; p. 17's bare «ཁམས» has no witness of its own and stays as in ch27-bare-khams-is-the-power-by-mipham only by analogy with the circles. The starting animals of p. 17 (rat of one's own element for the first; tiger of the friend for the second; dragon of the mother of the power for the third; horse of one's own for the fourth) do agree in animals with entries of ch. 26's table (thirty-starting-pairs) but not in fighting rule, so they are of the same family at most.
