# SKP: the Sa skya Paṇḍita attribution rests on the title; the colophon's name is his, but unread by eye; «པདྨ་དཀར» is not tied to a person (RD286)

BDRC records the first text of the bundle (MW1NLM933_A02C49, 8 ff. from p. 40) with the title «rmi lam brtag thabs dpal sa skya paN+Di tas mdzad pa» and no author field; the abstract work (WA0XLA02C496D030E) has this one instance only. The attribution to Sa skya Paṇḍita therefore rests on the title alone, the same title the machine read on p. 40. The colophon on p. 50 (machine, only its opening «ཤཱཀྱཱའི», "of the Śākya", seen on the scan at the end of line 3) names Kun dga' rgyal mtshan dpal bzang po. The two do not disagree: in BDRC's own titles the name of Sa skya Paṇḍita is written in full «sa skya paN+Di ta kun dga' rgyal mtshan dpal bzang po» (autocomplete of BDRC's title index, 2026-11-10), and sources.yaml gives his name as Kun dga' rgyal mtshan; so the title page and the colophon both name him, once by his title and once by his name. That the Sa skya Paṇḍita of the Sa skya bka' 'bum wrote this method is not shown by either: the colophon has «ཤཱཀྱའི་དགེ་སློང་» and the place of writing, the Sa skya monastery, and the registry notes that the attribution is the manuscript's, not the Sa skya bka' 'bum's. The colophon's name itself was not read syllable by syllable: the cursive hand of p. 50 lines 3–4 stayed machine.

The last lines of the second text (skp:p55h, scan) end «ལེགས་པར་བཤད་པ་པདྨ་དཀར། རྨི་ལམ་བརྟགས་པའི་ལེའུ་སྟེ་དག་པའོ།», "well explained by Padma dkar; the chapter on the examination of dreams is complete". BDRC's full-text search for the chapter's closing words (བརྟག་པའི་ལེའུ, spelling with brtag) finds only the sixth chapter of the Nyingma "Man ngag rin chen nyi ma'i sgron ma" in the Rin chen gter mdzod and an unrelated dbu can manuscript of the NLM; the spelling «བརྟགས་པའི་ལེའུ» and «རྨི་ལམ་བརྟག་པ་པདྨ་དཀར» find nothing. So no catalogued text is found that carries the verse, and Padma dkar po of the 'Brug pa (1527–1592) is not shown to be meant: the word has neither «པོ» nor a title.

**RD315.** (1) The name in the colophon of the first text. The formula «ཤཱཀྱའི་དགེ་སློང་ཀུན་དགའ་རྒྱལ་མཚན་དཔལ་བཟང་པོ» has 47 hits in BDRC's full-text search, among them the Sa skya bka' 'bum etext (MW00EGS1017151, 4 hits; one snippet is a petition to an abbot closing «dpal ldan sa skya'i dgon par gnas pa shAkya'i dge slong kun dga' rgyal mtshan dpal bzang po zhes bya bas gsol ba lags»): Kun dga' rgyal mtshan dpal bzang po is the full name of Sa skya Paṇḍita and «the Śākya monk dwelling in the glorious Sa skya monastery» is how he signs there. The title page's «sa skya paN+Di ta» and a colophon in this form name the same man, so q803's two names do not conflict; that he wrote this text is the manuscript's claim only: the Sa skya bka' 'bum etext search for the title «ས་སྐྱ་པཎྜི་ཏ་རྨི་ལམ་བརྟག་ཐབས» has no hit. The colophon on p. 50 itself was looked at again on the scan (2x and 3x): the hand is a running cursive and its syllables were not resolved by eye; it stays machine. (2) «གསུམ་པ་ཇི་བཞིན» in the second text's opening is, by the parallel text on MDP p. 138 (mdp:p138), almost certainly «གསུངས་པ་ཇི་བཞིན», "as it was taught": MDP reads «མ་གཅིག་ཟབ་མོ་གཅོད་ཀྱི་དམིགས་འབུམ་ལས། གསུངས་པ་ཇི་བཞིན་འདིར་ནི་མཚན་ལམ་གྱི། འབྲས་བུ་བཟང་ངན་ཀུན་གྱི་རྣམ་དབྱེ་བསྟན།»; so the 'third of Vairocana' is the machine's reading of «གསུངས་པ», not a third chapter or a third of anything; on the scan (img. 50 line 4) the word cannot be told from «གསུམ་པ» in this hand. The line before it names «མཁས་པ་གྲུབ་པཎ་ཆེན་བཻ་རོ་ཙ་ན» as the speaker in SKP where MDP names the Gcod text of Ma gcig; whether Vairocana stands for the source of the Gcod text is not shown. (3) «པདྨ་དཀར»: the string «ལེགས་པར་བཤད་པ་པདྨ་དཀར» has 34 hits in BDRC and every one is a title (Phug pa Lhun grub rgya mtsho's «legs par bshad pa pad+ma dkar po'i zhal gyi lung» and works commenting on it, Mkhas grub Bstan pa dar rgyas's «legs par bshad pa pad+ma dkar po'i 'phreng ba» on the Abhisamayālaṅkāra, a «legs par bshad pa pad+ma dkar po'i chun po»), none a dream text; the phrase is a stock title, "the white lotus of good explanation", so «པདྨ་དཀར» at the end of the verses may be the verse's own epithet or the title of the work it belongs to, as «well explained: the white lotus», rather than the 'Brug pa Padma dkar po's name; not decided.
