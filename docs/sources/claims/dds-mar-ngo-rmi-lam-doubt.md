# mar ngo'i rmi lam in a rite for conceiving a boy: doubtful

# «མར་ངོའི་རྨི་ལམ» in a rite for conceiving a boy: the one hit where a fortnight stands beside «dream», doubtful (RD367)

Two prints of a medical or rite text on making the child a boy (BDRC «byis pa'i chung dpyad (51)» and «gdams ngag bka' rgya ma / dar mo sman rams pa») have a line «མར་ངོའི་རྨི་ལམ་མང་དུས་སུ། ཞག་དགུའི་བར་དུ་གཏང་བར་བྱ» (dds.12:p439): at the time (of the) many «dreams» of the waning fortnight, it is to be given or let go for nine days. The other print has «ma dus su» in the snippet. The sense of «རྨི་ལམ» here is not settled (the rite asks of the woman nothing about dreams); if it is the dream, the line ties the number of dreams, not their fruit, to the waning fortnight, and the instruction is a rite's, not a dream's reading; if it is a misreading, the hit is false. It is recorded only so that the search is complete.
