# The hour of death: the month the la was cut, the eating demon, the next victim

Terms as named below: the *Eight Appearances* (སྣང་བརྒྱད, *Snang brgyad*), the *Sūtra of Long Life* (ཚེ་མདོ, *Tshe mdo*), a sack (ཕད་བུ, *phad bu*), an ogre (སྲིན་པོ, *srin po*); «ཕྱ་ར» (*phya ra*) and «སྐྱར་མ» (*skyar ma*) are not explained in the text.

The third examination of the dead (WB vol. 2, pp. 66–68) by the double hour of death: the month (by its animal) in which the la (བླ, bla) or the life was cut before the death, where the eating demon came from and hides, the count by which the next victim is named, and the rite.
