# The date's earth lords outside ch. 31 are kept by custom, their source slight

WB grades the date's earth lords (ཚེས་ཀྱི་ས་བདག, tshes kyi sa bdag) by their source, not by strength. Chapter 33's closing note says the forms and courses of the date's earth lords that are not in the earth lords' chapter (ch. 31) are "like contrivances, their source not very great", and were set down only because the *Precious Garland* (རིན་ཆེན་ཕྲེང་བ, rin chen phreng ba) and the *Supplement* (ལྷན་ཐབས, lhan thabs) customarily write them (wb.2:p377). Chapter 31's own close throws out, as unsourced, the visualisations of the date-moving earth lords it does not set down (wb.2:p240). The grade bears on which date lords stand on a sound source; it says nothing of how they weigh against the day's factors.
