# The feast and the torma under the commitment of not interrupting mantra and seal: the lowest practitioner does not let a month or a year pass

For a holder of the tantric commitments (དམ་ཚིག, dam tshig) of the Guhyagarbha tantra, the feast and the torma (ཚོགས་དང་གཏོར་མ, tshogs dang gtor ma) fall under the third of the five root commitments, not to interrupt mantra and seal, and have a least measure in time. Longchenpa (lcb:p568): the best practises without a break, like a river; the middling keeps the sessions of day and night; even the lowest practises without fail at the times of the month and of the year, the full moon, the new moon, the eighth and the others. The verse he quotes from the tantra Awesome Lightning (རྔམ་གློག, rngam glog) sets the lowest yogin's recitation at each month or the times of the first months, calls the neglect of it the loss of the seed of awakening, and adds that the feast and the torma are likewise: the lowest does not let a year or a month go by. Zhe chen rgyal tshab repeats the verse in his list of the commitments for explaining the tantra (zgt.11:p292), reading the last line as "at the very least not beyond the year and the month".

- Days: none by number in the verse; Longchenpa's prose names the full moon, the new moon and the eighth "and the others"; the note of the Larung print gives six (claim six-times-of-the-month-in-the-larung-note).
- Practice: recitation of mantra with the seals, the feast offering, the torma.
- For whom: holders of the commitments of the inner tantras (those who have received empowerment), lay or ordained; the texts make no difference between the two.
- Fault named: the seed of awakening is lost (of the recitation not done even monthly); for the feast and the torma the limit is stated, not a separate fault.
- Tradition: Nyingma, the Guhyagarbha's own commitments (Mahāyoga); stated as an obligation of the commitment, not as advice.

Both passages were read on the page images. The sense of «ར་བའི་དུས» (taken here as the first months of the seasons) is a doubt.
