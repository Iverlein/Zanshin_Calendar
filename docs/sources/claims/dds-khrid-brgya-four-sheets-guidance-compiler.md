# The Khrid brgya's guidance text of the four sheets (no. 89) hides the name Kun dga' rnam grol mchog in its closing verse and is extracted from the old writing of Mtshur dbang's vajra (RD383)

RD383. The Khrid brgya's guidance text of the four sheets (no. 89, in the Gdams ngag mdzod vol. 18) closes with a verse whose four syllables, marked with a dot in the etext, read «Kun dga' rnam grol mchog» (the name of the Jo nang compiler of the hundred and eight guidances), and with the line «extracted from the old writing of Mtshur dbang's vajra» (dds.41:p304-306). So the guidance text is a compilation by the Khrid brgya's compiler from a writing that stood with Mtshur ston Dbang gi rdo rje; it is not the history and not the companion method. The reading of the dotted syllables as the name is the reader's, from the same verse form that closes other texts of the Khrid brgya; it is not stated in words.
