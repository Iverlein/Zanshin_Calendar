# Searching for the ground: the elements' gods and the kin each one shields

In searching for the ground (WB vol. 2, p. 135, read on the scan), the
offerings are made to the gods of the five elements, each seated in its
direction on the throne of its animals, and each is asked to cut off the
obstacles of illness, death and harm (ན་སྒབ་ཤི་སྒབ, na sgab shi sgab) from
one group of the patrons' kin:

| Element | Direction, throne | Kin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| wood | east | the father, paternal uncle and grandfather («ཕ་ཁུ་མེས་གསུམ») |
| fire | (south), horse and snake | one's own children and grandchildren |
| iron | west, bird and monkey | maternal uncles and close friends |
| water | north, rat and pig | the wife and the in-laws |
| earth | the four corners, ox, sheep, dog and dragon | land, house and the forebears («མ་ཕྱི», read as printed) |

The list is fixed: it does not depend on any pebble.
