# The verse of the three parts by constituent is not in the Derge Mañjuśrīmūlakalpa, whose verse has four watches; Zhwa dmar IV names that tantra for it, Zur mkhar ba and the Gser mchan quote the four-watch verse apart (RD384)

The verse of the three parts of the night by constituent (dusk phlegm, midnight bile, dawn wind) is not in the Derge Mañjuśrīmūlakalpa (Toh 543), whose verse has four watches (phlegm, bile, wind, the fourth true); Zhwa dmar IV names the Mañjuśrī root tantra for the first, Zur mkhar ba quotes the second under that name and the first under «the tantra» (RD384, 2026-11-10, etext only).

The whole etext volume of Toh 543 (VE4CZ5369_I1KG9214, 697 pages, as RD370 read it) has no place for «བད་ཀན་ཤས་ཆེ», «མཁྲིས་པར་བརྗོད» or «རླུང་གི་དུས་ཉིད» (searched with locate.py, which ignores tsheg, shad and line breaks). RD370 found there, in the 15th chapter, the verse «the dream of the first watch is of phlegm; the second, bile, blamed in the world; the third, wind; the fourth comes true» (mmk-dream-verse-four-watches-phlegm-bile-wind-true). Zur mkhar ba (rrd.17:p352-355) quotes it right after the three-part verse, introduces it with «and in the Mañjuśrī root tantra too» (འཇམ་དཔལ་རྩ་རྒྱུད་ལས་ཀྱང་), and says the rest, «said very fully», is not quoted for fear of many words; so for him the two are two sayings, and the Blue Beryl (VEIE0OPICF5AF372_I1PD411, pp. 211-212) copies his page; Dar mo sman rams pa's Gser mchan (rrd.18:p285-286) does the same in verse, the three-part verse first (with «mostly strong» at midnight and at dawn) and then «and that too, from the Mañjuśrī root tantra» with the four-watch lines. Zhwa dmar IV (rrd.16:p220) writes «the Mañjuśrī root tantra says» before the three-part verse; the etext of Toh 543 does not bear that out, and the page is OCR that was not read on a scan.

The two verses give the same constituents in the same order (phlegm, bile, wind), the first to the "dusk", "midnight", "dawn" of the three-part verse and to the first, second and third watches of the root tantra; the root tantra's fourth watch, the true one, is the part the three-part verse does not have and the Karmapa's saying calls «dawn». Which tantra the three-part verse is quoted from remains open (q923): the Zab mo nang don's commentators do not name it, one names a book that does not have it.
