# KD's digest orders the date's earth lords by what they spoil and names the run the earth lords of the date

The digest of WB's fifth section (KD vol. 3, pp. 738-740) heads its twelfth part «ཚེས་ཀྱི་ས་བདག» (the earth lords of the date) and runs through them in an order that is not WB's print order (wb-p359-363-date-lords-in-order.md): Ngams shing, Poor-hand (དབུལ་ལག), the Planet-ogre arising (གཟའ་རྒོད་འབྱུང་བ), Black Moon, Black Sun, the Bar-gnam Hal-dog moving, the Hal-dog seeking food, the Sky Dog, Little Grave (ཆུང་དུར), Ki kang, Pi ling 'phar ma, the seven planets' envoy, Phung-po Zor-thogs, Little Black (ནག་ཆུང), Rāhu the Black, the Great Net, Sky-joining, Zin-phung the Black, the nine gods' and the five gods' gathering places, the black dates (ཚེས་ནག), the Gnyan dates and the bad gathering days (ཞག་པ་ངན་འདུས). Pi ling 'phar ma is called here «ས་བདག་རྗེ་བློན་བཅུ་གཅིག་པ», "the eleventh of the lords and ministers", as in WB p. 360's line (wb-p359-363-date-lords-in-order.md, item 3), and the Poor-hand (དབུལ་ལག) stands directly after Ngams shing, which agrees with q465's note that the second «gza' rgod» of the first machine reading is the Poor-hand. The digest gives no rule that brings a lord to a day and numbers none of them by date, as WB does not; so for q465 it only confirms the name, «the earth lords of the date», and that the run is one list of named lords with what each spoils, in a grouping of the digest's own. Etext only, one witness.
