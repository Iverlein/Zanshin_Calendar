# The notes' p. 475 table prints a cross and a dot with no legend

The notes' sentence under the worked table (WB vol. 2, p. 475, read in the etext of the 1996 print, [p483], and on the Gser thang reprint img. 481) says «འདིར་འཐབ་པའི་སྐབས་སུ་མ་གྲོགས་ལ་དཀར་པོ་རེ་རེ། བུ་དགྲ་ལ་ནག་པོ་རེ་རེ། ནང་ཕྲད་ལ་མེ་ལྕགས་ཤིང་གསུམ་ནང་ཕྲད་ནག་པོ་རེ་རེ། ས་ཆུ་ནང་ཕྲད་དཀར་པོ…»: in fighting, mother and friend one white each, son and enemy one black each, the fire, iron and wood inner meetings one black each, the earth and water inner meetings white. The table itself prints only a cross and a dot with no legend and no stated elements for its example, so the sentence cannot be set against the cells: which mark is white is not told by the page. The *Moonbeams* (DZ, I2533 img. 035–037, f. 16b–17a) has no table at all on those leaves, only the sentences. The *Little Red Tiger-Head* is not in the pool's registry and was not looked up.
