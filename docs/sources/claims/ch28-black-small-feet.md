# The twelve black small feet by year, month, hour and link

WB vol. 2, pp. 38–39: "the twelve guards of the black small feet" (རྐང་ཕྲན་ནག་པ, rkang phran nag pa). Each joins a year animal, a month, a time of day and a link; the work named, done then, ends badly.

| Year | Month (as printed) | Time | Link | Work | End |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| rat | middle spring | dawn | ignorance | business to the south and west | avoid |
| ox | last spring | sun on the peaks | aging and death («ཀ་ཤི») | a woman's business | ruin |
| tiger | last spring | sun reaches the valleys | birth | seeking grain | store empty, death by hunger and knife |
| hare | first spring | «རྡེལ་འགོ་དྲོས» [?] | becoming | seeking livestock | in the end seeking thread |
| dragon | middle summer | noon | grasping | seeking clothes | death by cold and knife |
| snake | last summer | noon | contact («རེག») | marrying | the line cut off |
| horse | first autumn | past noon | feeling | giving out nourishment [?] | begging a head-thread |
| sheep | middle autumn | shadows' heads appear | contact | seeking armour and weapons | death under the knife |
| monkey | last autumn | small shadows | six sense fields | quarrelling | left alone |
| bird | first winter | small shadows | name and form | holding a feast | ends a beggar |
| dog | middle winter | the sun's red line | consciousness | taking a bride | separation |
| pig | last winter | the ocean takes its lustre | formation | (sons) | the line cut off |

As printed no year has the first summer month and the tiger repeats the ox's; the links run backward from ignorance to formation but name contact twice and craving never. The reading of a given year is calculable from the year animal, the month and the hour; whether a "small foot" applies only when all three meet, or each alone, the text does not say.
