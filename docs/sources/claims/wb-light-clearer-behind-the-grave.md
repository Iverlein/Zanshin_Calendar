# The light-clearer sits behind the grave in the first months, then on the lower neighbour and the upper slayer

On p. 219 the light-clearer (no. 15) stays, in the four first months of the seasons, «དུར་གྱི་རྟིང» at the back of the grave, in the middle months on the lower neighbour (མར་གྱི་ཁྱིམ་མཚེས) and in the last on the upper slayer (ཡར་གྱི་གཤེད). The third syllable is blotted in the print and reads «རྟིང» (behind); the old reading «དུར» alone gave no direction, and the new one gives none either: he sits behind the month's grave, and which animal the grave is stays as the old question 28 narrowed it (the grave animal of the month's animal, the count of ch. 31 not said).
