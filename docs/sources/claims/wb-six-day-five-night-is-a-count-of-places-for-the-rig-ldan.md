# WB's six rounds of the day and five of the night are the Rig ldan's count of places (RD326)

WB vol. 2 p. 403's «six rounds of the day, five of the night are to be treasured» (ཉིན་མོ་དྲུག་སྐོར་ཏེ། མཚན་མོ་ལྔ་སྐོར་བྱ་བ་གཅེས) has its words in the Rig ldan piece of the Gso rig gces btus rin chen phreng ba (p114): «count from the day's planet, six for the day, five for the night go round; at their junction, in the middle of Saturn's enjoyment, the Rig ldan arises for the measure of one watch» (ཉིན་དྲུག་མཚན་ལྔ་བསྐོར). So the six and five are a count of places from the day's planet on which the Rig ldan's half-watch falls, not a count of watches in the day-and-night; the page's table of chu srang after it is too damaged to read.
