# The three grades of yogin before a dream in LGD

The three grades of yogin in LGD (lgd:p971): the best knows dreams to be unreal and a dream as a dream, so has nothing to examine; the least goes into dullness in the dream and cannot hold its sign; the middling examine for themselves and others, joining good and bad to what each deserves. The text ends with its own colophon: set down for later generations, a treasure (གཏེར་མ, gter ma) of the emanation Sangs rgyas gling pa (སངས་རྒྱས་གླིང་པ).
