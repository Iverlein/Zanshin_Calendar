# The title's lta ba stands in all five Tengyur outlines; the variant ltas has no hit (RD-o, q1132)

BDRC's full-text search of 2026-11-13 for «རྨི་ལམ་གྱི་ལྟ་བ་ངན་པ་རྣམ་པར་བཟློག་པ» gives 14 hits, among them the outline titles of the work in the Derge, Narthang, Peking, golden-manuscript and Dpe bsdur ma Tengyurs (all with «lta ba», in BDRC's Wylie: MW23703_2787, MW2KG5015_2406, MW1KG13126_3608, MW23702_1616, MW1PD95844_1654AU), each followed by the colophon «rmi lam gyi lta ba ngan pa rnam par bzlog pa rdzogs so». The same title with «ལྟས» («རྨི་ལམ་གྱི་ལྟས་ངན་རྣམ་པར་བཟློག་པ», «…ལྟས་ངན་པ་…», «རྨི་ལམ་ལྟས་ངན་…») has no hit. So the spelling «ལྟ་བ» is that of every Tengyur edition's title and colophon, and no print was found that writes «ལྟས» in the title. Whether the scribes meant «bad view» or used «lta ba» for «sign» stays what the lexica leave it (the earlier claim title-lta-ba-ngan-pa-both-prints-text-says-ltas): not settled. Search and catalogue records only; the Narthang, Peking and golden-manuscript texts were not read.
