# The black reckoning is the old Chinese reckoning of trigrams, mewa and earth lords; the Chinese reckoning is the later astronomical one

KD vol. 1 names the two Chinese reckonings that reached Tibet. What came from the time of Srong btsan sgam po, the trigrams (སྤར་ཁ, spar kha), the mewa (སྨེ་བ, sme ba), the earth lords (ས་བདག, sa bdag) and the like, brought nothing of the star reckoning (སྐར་རྩིས, skar rtsis); this is the old Chinese reckoning (རྒྱ་རྩིས་རྙིང་མ, rgya rtsis rnying ma), commonly the black reckoning (ནག་རྩིས, nag rtsis). What was translated under the Manchu dynasty is the new Chinese reckoning (རྒྱ་རྩིས་གསར་མ, rgya rtsis gsar ma), commonly just the Chinese reckoning (རྒྱ་རྩིས, rgya rtsis), whose subject is the eclipse examination and the working of the almanac in the Chinese manner. No Chinese star-reckoning text was translated before the great Chinese reckoning (རྒྱ་རྩིས་ཆེན་མོ).

So in KD's usage "nag rtsis" is the trigram, mewa and earth-lord side of the reckoning, with no astronomy in it, and "rgya rtsis" is the later astronomical one. A modern work; it fixes the two names' reference and is no rule for a date. It does not say whether the Chinese-reckoning pages of the Phugpa almanac (the day's trigram, mewa and earth lords) are drawn from the old or the new one.
