# The second examination: the outer reason by the four deciding pebbles and the elements

The second examination asks what cause (བསྙད, bsnyad) and reason (ལེ་ལན, le lan) the death met (WB vol. 2, pp. 57–58), in three: the outer reason by the course of the years, the inner by the trigram (སྤར་ཁ, spar kha) ([dead-cause-trigram](dead-cause-trigram.md)), the speckled middle one by the mewa ([dead-cause-mewa](dead-cause-mewa.md)). The outer has eight parts: life force, body, power, luck, and year, month, day, hour ([dead-cause-by-time](dead-cause-by-time.md)). Mipham's notes: the first four are read from the deciding pebbles (བཏང་གཅོད, btang gcod) of the ten roots, the same pebbles as the first examination's, and the element cuts from the fights within the pebbles (p. 503).

| Input | Pebble | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| life force | enemy rising | went to a place of feasting [?] and broke an oath, not doing the white |
| life force | met the mother or the friend | the kin's and close relatives' rites (རིམ་གྲོ, rim gro) |
| body | black | an ill omen; wealth taken from one possessed |
| body | white | a feast of the paternal kin eaten |
| power | black | fortune and wealth lost |
| power | white | food from the son-in-law's and sisters' wealth eaten |
| luck | black | feud with Buddhist and Bon priests or defilement; a red [offering] or people's talk; gods and protectors angered |
| luck | white | red meat and red lungs; gods and protectors angered |

Where pebbles turned black, the cause is the elements' enmity and meetings within (the notes: iron meeting its enemy fire many times within the pebbles gives "iron cut by fire"):

| Cut | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| iron cut by fire | grief, south or north-east |
| fire cut by water | a widow's place's «སྐྱུར» (skyur, not understood) [?], north |
| earth cut by wood | relying on a red hill, east |
| water cut by earth | feud, south-west |
| wood cut by iron | a red [offering] and the hearth, west |
| two fires within | a red [offering], hearth and hearth smoke |
| two woods within | a night journey to the north-west |
| two waters within | a black house stripped [?] |
| two irons within | a burnt offering (སྤྱན་སྲེག, spyan sreg) or a burnt-offering (ཧོམ, hom) fire's oath |
| two earths within | digging the soil (ཕྲུ་རློག, phru rlog) |

WB gives these as reasons only, with no rite; the rites are the thirteenth examination's.
