# WB pp. 335-336: the print gives each planet and mansion its number in brackets, Saturn as 0; the 1996 etext carries them, the reprint machine reading does not reliably

Read 2026-11-01 on the 1996 scan (I1KG12907 img. 343, printed p. 335, the top 3/4 of the page at 2.6x by eye) beside the 1996 etext (IE0OPI51524892, [p343]-[p344] = pp. 335-336) and the earlier machine reading of the reprint. The print itself puts a number in round brackets after every planet and mansion of the combinations, so the digits are the print's own and not an editor's: planets by the weekday count with the Sun 1, the Moon 2, Mars 3, Mercury 4, Jupiter 5, Venus 6 and Saturn 0 (the print writes a circle, not 7), mansions numbered from Bharani 1 (Krittika 2, Rohini 3 ... Maghā 9, Pushya 7, Aśleṣā 8 ... Dhaniṣṭhā 22). The scan agrees with the etext on the lines looked at (the Accomplishing combination's closing lines and the Paired combination, from "the Sun (1) with Maghā (9)" to "Saturn (0), Purvāṣāḍhā (ཆུ་སྟོད, 19)" as the etext has them), the circle after Saturn being seen on the scan wherever it was looked for; the earlier machine reading of the reprint is the one that took Saturn's circle for a 4 or a 7 in places, so a number from it is not to be used where the etext and the print differ. So the digits of pp. 335-336 can be taken from the 1996 etext, where it reads cleanly, and a number that matters is to be seen on the scan; this reading checked only the first 2 of the 8 pages' combinations on the scan (the Accomplishing and the first half of the Paired), and the tables of pp. 341-342 by weekday were not opened. The rest of the etext's digits (the Mara-and-Victor, Accomplished, Increasing, Rough, Agreeing and the seven Mara days, and the Disagreeable) are the etext's, with a few of its brackets damaged (a lost digit or bracket here and there), not checked against the scan.
