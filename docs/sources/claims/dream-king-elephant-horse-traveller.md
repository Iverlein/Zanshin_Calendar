# A king, an elephant, an excellent horse, a traveller, oxen and cows at the end of sleep

Image group: the holy. Read by 1 text: Svapnohana (SVO).
Offered to any dreamer.
The word that was unsettled, «འགྲོན་བུ» (traveller, guest), is settled by Peking (by eye), Narthang (machine) and the Dpe bsdur ma text (svo-other-prints-agree, RD228; the Derge machines' «འགྲོ་ན་བུ» was noise); this image is no longer listed apart in dream-unsettled-words.
