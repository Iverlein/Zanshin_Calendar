# Repayment and recovery of an illness by mansion, link and date

Recovery and repayment of an illness (WB vol. 2, pp. 415–416). By the mansion in which one falls ill: the illness is repaid by a named deed (wandering and water, a guest's entry, digging a ledge, cutting wood, a night walk, a fight, red meat, drowning, ploughing, an eclipse of food, cold beer) and the sick is freed in a stated number of days (three, five, eight, ten, twelve, twenty, a month, or after a long time). By the link of dependent arising on which he falls ill: ignorance, fifteen days if he lives; compositional factors, seven then three; consciousness three to eight; name-and-form three, five or seven, and at eight no recovery; sense-fields ten days; contact three then eight; feeling five then nine, freed at ten; craving eight then ten; birth five then fifteen; old age and death three or seven. By the lunar date, in four groups (1, 9, 17, 25; 2, 10, 18, 26; 3, 11, 19, 27; 4, 12, 20, 28; 5, 13, 21, 29; 6, 14, 22 and the void; 7, 15, 23; 8, 16, 24 continuing on p. 417): each group names the pain, the repayment (the hearth pit, digging, oath, food of the yugs place, long wandering) and the harmer (nāga, tsen, earth lord, god-demon, king, gre-mo, ghost, mo-demon).
