# P.3908 chapters 19-21: a dream's omen by day branch, double-hour and day officer, not by month; no cell for the Edo table

Chapters 19 to 21 of P.3908 give a result for a dream by the day branch (twelve lines, rat to boar), by the double-hour (twelve) and by the day officer of the 建除 series (twelve), in a few words each (wine and meat, a guest, quarrels, official business, loss or gain of wealth, sickness). They are omens read from the time of the dream, not rules about telling it, and none of the three sets is arranged by month: the day-branch lines carry no month, so the Edo table's twelve months, each with two groups of day branches, has no cell in them to compare. The day-branch results do not follow the Edo table either: for the three branches the Edo table lists in the first month (goat, monkey, dog) P.3908 gives joy with wine and meat (goat), official business and quarrels (monkey) and a guest from afar with outside wealth (dog), which share no sign.
