# The Mchog dga's left row for the equal houses: the etext's 5, 5, 8, 7, 5 is not shown to be a slip, and the example 27 = Mars does not decide between the two readings (q1043)

The Kun 'dus etext's «ལྔ་དང་། ལྔ་དང་བརྒྱུད་བདུན་ལྔ» (five, five, eight, seven, five; the «བརྒྱུད» is the etext's for «བརྒྱད», eight) is not necessarily a slip for 5, 7, 8, 5, 5.

The grid has the five planets in the middle column (Mars, Saturn, Jupiter, Mercury, Venus, top to bottom) and a column of number-letters on each side. The right column, 5, 5, 8, 7, 5 top to bottom, gives the unequal houses' parts (Mars 1-5, Saturn 6-10, Jupiter 11-18, Mercury 19-25, Venus 26-30). The «earlier followers» say that the left column is laid «in the reverse order» (རིམ་པ་བཟློག་སྟེ) and print «5, 5, 8, 7, 5». The equal houses are counted «from the left cell of Venus, not in order», that is from the foot of the left column upward. Two readings of the string fit the words:

- (A) The string is the cells' content top to bottom, the same as the right column (the question's «unreversed»). Counted from the foot upward it gives Venus 5, Mercury 7, Jupiter 8, Saturn 5, Mars 5: Venus 1-5, Mercury 6-12, Jupiter 13-20, Saturn 21-25, Mars 26-30. «Reverse» is then the direction of counting.
- (B) The string is the order in which the numbers are laid, from the foot upward, so the cells hold 5, 7, 8, 5, 5 top to bottom; counted from the foot it gives Venus 5, Mercury 5, Jupiter 8, Saturn 7, Mars 5: Venus 1-5, Mercury 6-10, Jupiter 11-18, Saturn 19-25, Mars 26-30. «Reverse» is then the laying.

The author's example, 27 in Taurus giving Mars, does not choose between them: 27 falls in 26-30 in both, which is Mars. So the claim thirty-part-lords-mars-saturn-jupiter-mercury-venus-5-5-8-7-5 («Venus 1-5, Mercury 6-12, Jupiter 13-20, Saturn 21-25, Mars 26-30») is reading (A) and the etext read to the letter can be (B). The two differ for Mercury and Saturn only. The chart's picture is not in the etext, and the Mchog dga' itself says that the order is not explained in the treatise and that «the former» (the unequal houses' order for both) is perhaps better. The grid is to be read on the print's page: the loaned scans (kd.3, printed p. 129) were not opened here.
