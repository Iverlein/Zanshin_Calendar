# On the earth lords' best day for fierce works, the place facing them is still shunned

For the destructive rites (work 63, WB vol. 2, p. 427), the black year, black month and black day are good, and the times when black Rāhu goes, the ruin of zin (ཟིན་ཕུང, zin phung) and viṣṭi are the best; "yet one should shun the place one faces them" (གདོང་ཐུག་གནས་ལ་འཛེམ་པར་བྱ). The direction rule holds apart from how good the day is: on the earth lord's best day the work is still not done facing its seat. The same chapter's general line for the date's and hour's fierce earth lords joins the fierce works to the direction of the lord's course (wb.2:p364-365). Neither passage says how a work avoided in one direction counts against the day's general good or bad.
