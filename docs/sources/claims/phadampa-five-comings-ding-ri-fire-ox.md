# Phadampa Sangye came to Tibet five times, the last to Ding ri in a fire-female-ox year after twelve years in China

The Blue Annals counts five comings of Phadampa Sangye (ཕ་དམ་པ་སངས་རྒྱས, pha dam pa sangs rgyas) to Tibet (ལན་ལྔ, lan lnga), the first by 'Brin thang to Tsa ri (dsn.2:i354); before the last he stayed twelve years in China, where his teaching spread, and "some say" he also showed his passing there; then he came to Ding ri (དིང་རི, ding ri) in the fire-female-ox year (མེ་མོ་གླང, me mo glang) and settled at Glang 'khor (dsn.2:i399). Only the coming to Ding ri is dated; no month or day. No date of his birth was found in the sources read: Khams smyon Dharma seng ge's life (here read in the etext of its 1992 edition, pp. 25-27) tells of his birth in India after ten months and twenty-one days in the womb, without a year.
