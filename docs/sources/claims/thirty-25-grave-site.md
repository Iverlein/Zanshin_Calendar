# The thirty: the twenty-fifth point, the nine stacked skulls, for the grave site good or bad

The twenty-fifth of the thirty starting points (སྐོར་འགོ, skor 'go), from the mouth of the nine stacked skulls (ཐོད་པ་དགུ་བརྩེགས, thod pa dgu brtsegs), is read for the grave site good or bad. Its eight pebbles are laid as for every point (claims thirty-count and thirty-eight-fighters), each white or black, and read so:

| Pebble | Asks | White | Black | Remedy for black |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Whether the dead goes into the forefathers' grave | It does not go | It goes into the grave, bad | — |
| 2 | Whether, by the good grave site, the living are auspicious | The living are auspicious | Somewhat bad for the living | — |
| 3 | Whether the *nal ma*'s (ནལ་མ; not identified) ornament is tied | Not tied | Tied, bad | — |
| 4 | Whether the grave's misfortune (བྱུར, byur) wins over the ornament | One's own ornament wins | The misfortune spirit wins, bad | — |
| 5 | Whether a child's la is stolen | Not stolen | Bad | Send the small sri (ཆུང་སྲི, chung sri) the small ransom |
| 6 | Whether the grave sri (དུར་སྲི, dur sri) has entered the castle | Not entered and has not stolen existence, good | It has stolen | The grave's laying-down rite (དུར་སྐྱས, dur skyas) |
| 7 | Whether the spirits and ogres obscure the ornament | (read with the seventh pebble pair below) | The living's pebble white and the dead's black: the ruin sri (འཕུང་སྲི, 'phung sri) harms | The ruin sri's skyas 'debs and the like |
| 8 | Whether the ruin sri opens the grave | The grave site is not opened | It is opened | Lay the ruin's skyas |

The board as a whole: the pebbles speckled, do the rite for disturbed earth lords (ས་བདག་འཁྲུགས་གཏོ, sa bdag 'khrugs gto); all the pebbles black, Rudra's (རུ་ཏྲ, ru tra) black rite, the Protector's turning back of war, taming enemies and the weapon rite. Whether «རྡེལ་ཁྲ» means the whole board or the seventh pebble pair is question 215.

Whose year is reckoned: The dead's year, or applied to the asker as well (notes, pp. 532–533).

Which of the eight fighting pebbles is which check is not stated; the order of p. 453 is the likely one (claim thirty-board-groups; question 259).
