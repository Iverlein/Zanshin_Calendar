# The first-person life in VE22082_001 is by the 'Bri gung pa Rig 'dzin Chos kyi grags pa Phrin las rnam rgyal sde (1595-1659 by BDRC) (RD391)

RD391. The first-person life quoted in dds.49:p110-112 (BDRC VE22082_001, the volume's text begun at its second [p1], Rang gi tshul gyi rtogs pa brjod pa'i gtam gsong po'i dga' ston, pp. 1-150) is the work of the 'Bri gung pa Rig 'dzin Chos kyi grags pa Phrin las rnam par rgyal ba'i sde. The life itself gives the name: the lord Zhwa dmar, the sixth who holds the crown, names the young author «Rig 'dzin Chos kyi grags pa Phrin las rnam par rgyal ba'i sde» and calls him the horn-tuft of the 'Bri gung pas (dds.49:p14-15); the text before it in the same volume closes, in its colophon, with the name 'Bri gung pa Shākya'i dge slong Rig 'dzin Chos kyi grags pa Phrin las rnam par rgyal ba'i sde, aged 52, in a fire-dog year, written at the palace of Rtse ba. BDRC's record of the volume's work (MW22082 of the work WA22082, 15 volumes, Drikung Kagyu Institute) names it the collected works of the all-knowing Awareness-holder Chos kyi grags pa, catalogued as «Collected Works by Drikung Chungtsang 01 Rigzin Chokyi Drakpa (1595-1659)», the same person as the author of the Four Tantras' Dka' mchan used in bdk (the registry's 'Bri gung Chung tshang Chos kyi grags pa, 1595-1659). Age 52 in a fire-dog year fits 1646 for a man born in 1595. The words «Chung tshang» do not stand with his name in the life; the etext has Chung tshang only as the name of a place's temple (Khams skyo, p72). The figure rests on BDRC's cataloguing, not on the etext.
