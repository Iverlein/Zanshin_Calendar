# The Dream Tārā sādhana is a rite to be shown a dream: recitation, pills against obstacles, sleep; no part of the night and no remedy for a bad dream (RD381)

The Dream Tārā sādhana of Candramitra is a rite for being shown a good or a bad thing in a dream: rise early, offer, recite the secret mantra twenty-four hundred thousand times, make an offering to girls at an auspicious time (no hour named), keep the celibate vow, make a mandala, bless pills of pea size with seven Kuṇḍali mantras and throw twenty-one in the ten directions against the dream's obstacles, then ask and lie down in the lion's posture until sleep; what one sees, by the thing, by sign or by words, is virtuous or not. It gives no part of the night, no day of the month, no list of images and no remedy for a bad dream. Maker Candramitra, translators Sugataśrī and Byams pa'i dpal (Derge and Phur lcog catalogues).
