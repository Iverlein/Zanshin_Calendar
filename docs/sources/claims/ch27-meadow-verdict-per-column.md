# Ch. 27: the meadow's ornament lays one verdict under each of a group's five

In ch. 27's meadow's ornament leaves (WB vol. 2, p. 22) each group of twenty-five pebbles, the five of the fought-against year against the reckoned person's five, ends in «ཁ་དམར་ལྔ», five verdicts. Both witnesses that lay the group out give the five as the five columns of the group and put one verdict under each column, the reckoned person's five being the rows laid against every column: KD's worked sheet (vol. 3, p. 723) has a verdict row of twenty cells, one under each of its twenty columns (the sky year, its seventh, the sixth and the fourth executioner downward, five columns each), and Mipham's notes (vol. 2, pp. 533-534) call the group of five «ཁྱི་ལྔ», «སྡེ་ལྔ», the five of one animal, "fought: five verdicts". So the five verdicts are not one for each of the reckoned person's aspects over the five; they are one for each of the group's five.

They differ on what the five of a group are. KD takes them as one year's own life force, body, power, luck and mewa (its column letters on the first group are the wood mouse's own forces, the same as the person's rows because the person is born in that year). Mipham takes them as the five of an animal, "the five dogs, the fire dog, the wood dog and the rest", "the five dragons headed by the water dragon", «གཙོ» naming the head. By the sources rule Mipham ranks above KD. WB's own «རྣམ་ལྔ», "the five forms" (p. 22, l. 7, 15, 18), is open to both. Whether the five animals of Mipham's group stand for the five aspects (each aspect's element naming the animal of that element) or are five different years is not said.

The verdict row of KD's sheet, for the record: group 1 three white, one white, one black, one black, two white; groups 2 and 4 three white, then two black three times, then one white; group 3 white, white, white, white, white.
