# The solstice wheel: the year's cell by the years elapsed plus 26 over 65

The solstice wheel (WB vol. 1, ch. 16) is Nor bzang rgya mtsho's single wheel with 65 cells that stands for sixty wheels of twelve petals. Each cell gives, for one year of the 65-year round, the date of the summer solstice and of the winter solstice, each with its desired part (*'dod cha*) and its fifth part (*lnga cha*); the inner ring is the summer solstice, the outer ring the winter. To find the year's cell: to the years of the rab byung elapsed add 26 and take away 65 as often as it goes (the printed «དུས་མིག(༢༦)» and «མདའ་རོ(༦༥)»); the remainder is the cell. The wheel itself is a picture whose figures are not transcribed in the pool.
