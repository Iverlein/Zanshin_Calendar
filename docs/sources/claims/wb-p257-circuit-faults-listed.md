# Ch. 21's verse puts the reckoned's cutting, parting and spreading among rejected circuits

The verse on WB vol. 1, p. 257 lists «བརྩི་བྱ་གཅོད་འདྲལ་འགྱེད་པ» (brtsi bya gcod 'dral 'gyed pa, the reckoned cut, parted, spread) with «སོགས» (sogs, and the rest) among the differing mistaken ways of turning the circuits, and closes by calling them places to be refuted (དགག་བྱའི་གནས, dgag bya'i gnas). The phrase is therefore a method the author rejects, so it cannot be his own leaping count; whether it means a count that skips years or a cutting and scattering of the reckoned's elements the page does not say.
