# WB ch. 2: in the Kālacakra's arrangement Aries is the first house, the sphere turns to the right, and Aśvinī rises first

After listing the several views on the head of the year (sūtra, tantra, abhidharma; three in the Vinaya; the four seasons and five elements of medicine; the Hor way of pairing the moon with the full-moon asterism), the author says each has its own sources and none is to be disputed. For the Kālacakra, the non-dual all-pervader, taught at the great stūpa of Dhānyakaṭaka, he states: the first of the houses is Aries (ལུག); the sphere (གོ་ལ) turns to the right over the southern continent; the first of the eastern risings is Aśvinī (ཐ་སྐར); and since Aries is the house, on the first day Aśvinī rises.
