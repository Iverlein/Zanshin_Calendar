What the two further prints of Bu ston's text settle against the 2008 print (bsr:p825-828):

- The six lights: all three prints list white god, then green and yellow, then blue, red, black; the doubted order "green human, yellow demi-god" stands in both further prints, so it is not reversed. The green word is ལྗང་ཁུ in the Zhol par khang print and read ལྡང་ཁུ by the machine in the Delhi print (same word, the scan too small to tell by eye).
- The text is a verse rite of seven-syllable lines that the 2008 print runs together; the two further prints agree with each other against it in the lines the first print garbles: the wish for the dream "is not let go" (འདུན་པ་མི་གཏང་), asked "strongly three or seven times" (not "three in front"), the water is drunk (བཏུང་), the request is "the prophecy of my birth" (སྐྱེ་ལུང་) against «སྐྱེ་ལུགས», the lineage names Vikhyātadeva as «བི་ཁྱཱ་ཏ་དེ་བ» (the 2008 print «བི་ཁྱད་དད་དེ་པ»).
- The title's first syllable is བརྟག (in both further prints, read by the machine; the title lines are small), not the first print's «རྟག».
- The text names no source apart from "the tantra", and still no dream image.

Karma chags med's text is not a list of dream signs: it is a body feast offering with Mahākāla and the dakinis, the dream prophecy of its title being the one reported in the colophon (a life extended by thirteen years); the colophon says dreams are uncertain.

Not settled: the second print's mantra of the first verse (the machine reads «ཨོཾ་གུ་རུ་སུ་རུ་མ་པུ་རུ་ཀེ»), the mantra syllables altogether (three machine spellings), whether «བཏུང»/«བཏུགས» is the Delhi hand; no second reader (MITRA) was run and no disagree.py sheet read.
