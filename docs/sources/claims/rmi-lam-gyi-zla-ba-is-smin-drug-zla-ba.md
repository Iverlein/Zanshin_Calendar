# The Dream month of the Catuṣpīṭha list is the Pleiades month; the name is not explained (RD395, q1035)

«རྨི་ལམ་གྱི་ཟླ་བ», the Dream month of the Catuṣpīṭha and Vajradākatantra list, is the Pleiades month (smin drug zla ba, Kṛttikā): the Tshig mdzod chen mo and the Bod yig tshig gter rgya mtsho gloss it, with the mark «mngon» (a poetic synonym), as «smin drug zla ba ste hor zla bcu pa» ("the Pleiades month, the tenth Hor month"), and Rangjung Yeshe, Ives Waldo and the Tibetan-language school's glossary agree. The month runs from the sixteenth day of the ninth month to the fifteenth of the tenth. In Khal kha Ngag dbang dpal ldan's Dus tshigs commentary, twelfth in the tantric list, it matches Smin drug, the twelfth name of the ordinary list on the same page. No source read says why the Pleiades month is called "the dream month". The name does not tie dreams to the month.
