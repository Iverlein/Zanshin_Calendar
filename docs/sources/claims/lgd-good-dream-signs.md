# The good dream signs of LGD

The good dream signs of LGD (lgd:p967), thirty-odd: dawn breaking, the sun rising, plants and forests flourishing, grain ripening, boat and bridge set, a great river crossed, escape from a fearful path, going upward, the body adorned, tools fine, new clothes, full vessels, honour from others, winning a quarrel, goods coming as attainments, bathing, a pleasing friend met, music and a good voice, trees planted, a great banner raised, a good fort built, a pond made, a channel dug, being ringed by an iron-mountain wall, others beaten and subdued, a missile hitting the mark, grain enjoyed, eating and drinking to satiety, fine offerings laid out, a treasure found. The text sums them: obscurations purified, wealth increasing, life lengthened, wishes met. No ranking among them and no time rule is given.
