# Using goods of gossip (མི་ཁ, mi kha)

Image group: people and contests. Read by 1 text: Lama Gongdü (LGD).
Offered to any dreamer.
At least one verdict below rests on an unsettled word: this image is listed apart in dream-unsettled-words and is not offered by the picker until the word is settled.

| Text | Verdict (tone) | Passage | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lama Gongdü (LGD) (rests on an unsettled word) | using things such as «རམ་རུག་སྦང་གཡེར», goods of gossip, among the bad signs (the first words are not understood) (bad) | `lgd:p968-969` | scan |

RD271 (2026-11-09): the goods of gossip (མི་ཁའི་རྫས) are the substances of a rite against gossip, and the three words are a short list of them: ram bu (written ram rug), the beer's mash and the Sichuan pepper (lgd-mi-kha-goods-of-gossip-are-rite-substances, lgd-ram-rug-is-the-ram-bu-spelling, lgd-sbang-g-yer-beer-mash-and-pepper). The exact plant of the first item and why handling them is a bad sign are not told, so the image stays unsettled for the picker.
