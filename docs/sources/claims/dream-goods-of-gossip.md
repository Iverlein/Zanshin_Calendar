RD271 (2026-11-09), narrowed again by RD274 (2026-11-09): the three words are no longer unclear as a kind, only the first item's plant is; the image is kept in dream-unsettled-words for that alone. The goods of gossip (མི་ཁའི་རྫས) are the substances of a rite against gossip, and the three words are a short list of them: ram bu (written ram rug), the beer's mash and the Sichuan pepper (lgd-mi-kha-goods-of-gossip-are-rite-substances, lgd-ram-rug-is-the-ram-bu-spelling, lgd-sbang-g-yer-beer-mash-and-pepper). The exact plant of the first item and why handling them is a bad sign are not told, so the image stays unsettled for the picker.
