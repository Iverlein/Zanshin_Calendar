# «རྨི་ལམ་ཟློས་ཤིག» is «tell the dream back»: ཟློས is the imperative of ཟློ་བ, to repeat, recite, make known (RD395, q1103)

«ཟློས་ཤིག» in «ནང་པར་སྔ་བར་རྨི་ལམ་ཟློས་ཤིག» (Derge Tengyur, VE23703_1344 p. 446 and VE23703_1340 p. 110) is the imperative of «ཟློ་བ» "to repeat, to say again", so the order is "tell the dream back (to me) at early dawn". The lexica give the sense "repeat, recite" (Rangjung Yeshe, Valby, Ives Waldo, the Tshig mdzod chen mo), the Mahāvyutpatti vīpsā "repetition", the Yogācārabhūmi glossary ārocayati, "makes known, tells"; none gives "to dream again". In the same volume the word is used with a mantra, «ཟློས་ཤིག་ལྷ་མོ་རྣམས་ཀྱི་སྔགས» (p. 133), "recite the goddesses' mantras". The rites that say «སྨྲོས» (speak) or «བོས» (call) ask the same: the disciple tells the dream of the night at dawn.
