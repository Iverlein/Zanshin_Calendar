# Gampopa's day: 6/15 in the calendars, the full moon of the first autumn month at Tsurphu

The Rigpa students' calendar and Jeff Watt's list give Sgam po pa (Gampopa) the 15th of the sixth month (tbc:p1, har:p1). The Tsurphu catalogue, in words it shares with the life of the first Karmapa, has his passing offering held «at the full moon of the first autumn month» (སྟོན་ཟླ་ར་བ, ston zla ra ba; mtk:p1, search snippet). Both are full moons; whether the first autumn month is the sixth month depends on how the seasons are counted, and the snippet does not number the month. Rank 3, snippets and web pages; the date of his death in his own lives was not read.
