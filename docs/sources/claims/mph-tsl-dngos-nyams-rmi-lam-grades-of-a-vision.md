# The actual sight, experience-appearance and dream as grades of a deity's vision, a sign of accomplishment (RD389)

Mipham and Rtsa gsum gling pa's lineage rank the visions of a deity-practice in three grades by their clearness: the actual sight (དངོས, dngos), the experience-appearance (ཉམས་སྣང, nyams snang), and the dream (རྨི་ལམ, rmi lam). In the treasure text of vol. 29 (images 154 and 171-172) the best yogin sees the deity actually after the recitation, the middling has the occasion of experience-appearance, the lowest meets the face and the prophecy in dreams, and these are given as the "secret signs" of accomplishment (གསང་བའི་རྟགས, gsang ba'i rtags) of a guru-yoga recitation, graded by the height of the practitioner's view. In Mipham's rites of the Ge sar and the wrathful protectors the line is "the actual, the experience and the dream" (དངོས་དང་ཉམས་དང་རྨི་ལམ) or "in experience and dream see the signs of accomplishment" (ཉམས་དང་རྨི་ལམ་ལ་བསྒྲུབ་པའི་རྟགས་མཐོང་), read in the BDRC etexts of the Sde dge copy (poor OCR, vol. I1KG8544 pp. 636, 688) and in the Ge sar g.yang sgrub (MW20097). So in these two lineages an experience-appearance or a dream vision of the deity is read as a sign of the rite's success, by degrees, inside a practice; no text read applies this to a state at the edge of sleep.
