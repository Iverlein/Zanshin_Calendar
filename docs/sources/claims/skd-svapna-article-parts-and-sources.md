# What the Śabdakalpadruma's article svapna holds, and where it names its sources (RD272)

The article *svapna* of the *Śabdakalpadruma* (vol. 5 pp. 470–473) is a dictionary entry of five parts, each ending with the name of its source; RD272 read it on the page images (2026-11-09). (1) The definition: sleep (*nidrā*, the Amarakośa), and the dream as the knowledge of the sleeper, from the Suśruta, *Śārīrasthāna* ch. 4, with the lexicon Medinī's "sight". (2) The Brahmavaivarta's chapters of the *Kṛṣṇajanmakhaṇḍa*: the good dream with the series of spans (77), Akrūra's good dreams, the bad dream with its remedies (82), Kaṃsa's bad dream (83). (3) A list of other places, with chapter numbers: Paraśurāma's bad dreams and Kārtavīryārjuna's in the Brahmavaivarta's *Gaṇeśakhaṇḍa* (33, 34), the demon Ghora's in the *Devīpurāṇa* (22), the *Kālikāpurāṇa*'s *puṣyābhiṣeka* (87) and the *Matsyapurāṇa*'s dream chapter on the march (242). (4) Verses "said in medicine", from Vāgbhaṭa's *Śārīrasthāna* ch. 6: the bad dreams of the dying, then the seven kinds of dream, of which five bear no fruit, a line on the time of the fruit and the remedy by gift, offering and recitation. (5) Further dictionary entries (*svapnakṛt*, *svapnadoṣa* with a medicine, *svapnavicārin*).

Only one passage gives a span of years, months or days: the Brahmavaivarta's series. The article gives no other series, no tithi, weekday, nakṣatra or month, and does not name the *Svapnohana*, a Śaunaka or Bṛhaspati *Svapnādhyāya*, nor any Atharvan text. The "Vāgbhaṭa *Svapnādhyāya*" that Negelein's letter v names is, on the page, this passage of Vāgbhaṭa's *Śārīrasthāna*; the print does not call it a *Svapnādhyāya*.
