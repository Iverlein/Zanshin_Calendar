# Dpal sprul, Words of My Perfect Teacher: offerings to the Three Jewels on the full and new moon and the six times, no days listed

At the end of the counsel on refuge Dpal sprul tells the practitioner to offer whatever root of virtue is accomplished to the Three Jewels and, on "the full-moon and new-moon days and the six times" (ཉ་སྟོང་དུས་དྲུག, nya stong dus drug), to make offerings to the Three Jewels as far as one can, and never to break the regular periodic offerings (kzl:i315). The page lists no days: the six times are named, not counted. The Larung print's note on the six times (claim six-times-of-the-month-in-the-larung-note, lcb:p568) gives the 8th, 10th, 15th, 25th, 29th and the new moon; that this is the list meant here is an inference from the shared phrase "nya stong" and "dus drug", and no page read says so.

- Days: the full moon and the new moon, with "the six times" beside them.
- Practice: offerings to the Three Jewels as far as one can; the periodic offering kept unbroken.
- For whom: the practitioner of the preliminaries, lay or ordained.
- No fast, no vow, no deity's day is named on the page.
