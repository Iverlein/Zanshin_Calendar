# The thirty: the eighteenth point, the tree in full leaf, for the la tree good or bad, or a child's existence

The eighteenth of the thirty starting points (སྐོར་འགོ, skor 'go), from the mouth of the tree in full leaf (ལྗོན་ཤིང་འདབ་རྒྱས, ljon shing 'dab rgyas), is read for the la tree (བླ་ཤིང, bla shing) good or bad, or a child's existence (སྲིད, srid). Its eight pebbles are laid as for every point (claims thirty-count and thirty-eight-fighters), each white or black, and read so:

| Pebble | Asks | White | Black | Remedy for black |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Whether the water of existence has dried | The spring of existence is full, good | It has dried: bad for the whole circle of women | Each carries the *'brang ma* protection (འབྲང་མ; not identified) |
| 2 | Whether the dagger of existence has gone up into the sky | Not gone up; it stands, good | It has gone up: children are hard to raise | The rite of existence and planting the dagger of existence help |
| 3 | Whether the fire spirit and the water spirit harm | No harm, good | They harm: bad for people and wealth | — |
| 4 | Whether the *the'u bsen* (ཐེ་བསེན, the bsen; spirits, not identified) acts as son and husband | The the'u brang spirit (ཐེའུ་བྲང, the'u brang) and the *bsen mo* spirit (བསེན་མོ, bsen mo) do not take the place of son and husband | They enter the womb: bad for children and descendants | Service rites (རིམ་གྲོ, rim gro) and the like |
| 5 | Whether the ocean of existence swirls | It swirls: good for sons and wealth | It has dried, bad | Strive at the rites of expelling misfortune and raising glory (བྱུར་ཕུད་དཔལ་བསྐྱེད, byur phud dpal bskyed) |
| 6 | Whether the *rgyal bsen* (རྒྱལ་བསེན, rgyal bsen; a spirit, not identified) harms continually | No harm: the life force firm, sons and grandsons good | It harms continually | The king spirits' *gzhi mgyogs* rite (རྒྱལ་པོ་གཞི་མགྱོགས, rgyal po gzhi mgyogs; not identified) and the *dkor mdos* (དཀོར་མདོས, dkor mdos; a thread-cross, not identified) |
| 7 | Whether the maternal uncle's spirit harms from the west | No harm from the place of the mother's father's line, good | The local spirits of the uncle's side and the *glen zhugs* spirits (གླེན་ཞུགས, glen zhugs; not identified) harm always | As remedy rite (གཏོ, gto), turning the spirits' crowd helps |
| 8 | Whether the the'u brang and bsen mo have entered the womb door | Not entered, good | Entered, bad | The *g.yo 'khrug* rite (གཡོ་འཁྲུག, g.yo 'khrug; not identified) of existence, the king thread-cross (མདོས, mdos) and the the'u brang's laying-down rite (སྐྱས་འདེབས, skyas 'debs) thread-cross |

Whose year is reckoned: the person's own (the notes, vol. 2, p. 533: «གཞན་རྣམས་སོ་སོའི་ལོ་རྟ»).

Which of the eight fighting pebbles is which check is not stated; the order of p. 453 is the likely one (claim thirty-board-groups; question 259).
