# The Narthang manuscript reads «རྟ་ལ་མོ» with a plain la

The Narthang manuscript reads «རྟ་ལ་མོ» with a plain ལ (img. 286 line 4, 8×, medium-high), as the Peking print and the two other Narthang scans (RD231): four prints by eye against the Derge etext's and the Dpe bsdur ma etext's «རྟ་བླ་མོ»; the Co ne is a machine vote for «བླ་མོ» (RD276). The next word looks «ལྷོ་འཕྱེ» (ལྷ), not the etext's «ལྟོ་འཕྱེ», not told apart at 8× (a doubt). The word's sense is as in q825; the manuscript adds a fourth witness to the plain spelling and nothing to the sense.
