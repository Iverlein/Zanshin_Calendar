# Phur lcog's catalogue Tshangs pa'i dbyangs lists the Examination of Dreams after the Deceiving Death with no author (q1004, in part)

Phur lcog Ngag dbang byams pa's Tengyur catalogue «Bstan bcos 'gyur ro cog gi dkar chag Tshangs pa'i dbyangs», printed in his collected works vol. 3 (the title of the Narthang manuscript's catalogue volume too), lists the Examination of Dreams after the Deceiving Death, as Bi bhA ti tsandra's own translation and with no author, in the words and order of the Derge and Jo nang catalogues; it spells the translator «བི་བྷཱ་ཏི་ཙནྡྲ» where the Derge has «བི་བྷཱུ་ཏི་ཙནྡྲ». Whether the Tibet House manuscript's own volume carries the same entry is not shown (its Deceiving Death entry is worded otherwise, svo:i197). It answers q1004 only for the printed text.
