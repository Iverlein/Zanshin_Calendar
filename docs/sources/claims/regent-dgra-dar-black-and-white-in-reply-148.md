# The enemy's rise, the empty word and the poles of black and white in reply 148

The regent restates WB's second examination of the dead (vol. 2, p. 57) and the weight of its black and white verdicts (reply 148, ys:i506-508, scan):

- WB's lines «སྲོག་ལ་དགྲ་དར་ཤི་བ་ན» and «མ་འམ་གྲོགས་དང་ཕྲད་པ་ན» are the verdict as the regent gives them: «སྲོག་ལ་དགྲ་དར་སྟོངས[?]་སམ་ནང་ཕྲད་ན» is black, and «མ་འམ་གྲོགས་དང་ཕྲད་པ་ན» white. The enemy's rise (དགྲ་དར, dgra dar) is thus a term the regent takes over unchanged from WB's line, and Mipham's note on the birth reckoning has it (wb.2:p483): so that «དགྲ་དར» is a term of the dead's reckoning and its «དར» at p. 54 (question 271) is probably no misprint, though what it names there is not settled.
- Within the black the reply names three: the enemy meeting (དགྲ་ཕྲད), the son meeting (བུ་ཕྲད) and the inner meeting (ནང་ཕྲད); the white does not go beyond mother and friend. The doubtful word «སྟོངས», in the slot between the enemy's rise and the inner meeting, is then not a fourth state; in this restating it stands where the son meeting stands, but the letters are read «སྟོངས» by eye and by Yigdzin-1 alike, and the sense is not settled.
- "Enemy" and "inner" apply alike to life force, body, power and luck: the black verdict is the same for each of the four aspects. For the karma reckoning's «དགྲ་དར» of wb.2:p483 (question 393, 396) the reply is no witness: it speaks of the reckoning for the dead. It does show the pole of the white as the mother and the friend, and the pole of the black as the enemy, so that "the life force's mother not in decline" (the reading of «སྲོག་མ་མ་གུད་པ» as a condition that keeps the aspect from being bad) stands on the white side; as a reading only.
