# The colophon's «ས་རོད་ཡའི་རྒྱུད» is the Svarodaya tantra, the Dbyangs 'char («གཡུལ་ལས་རྣམ་པར་རྒྱལ་བ…»), which stands in the Tanjur; «ཨ་འཐས་བདེན་ཞེན» is attested elsewhere

**Corrected 2026-11-14 (q748):** the first paragraph below took the Tanjur's short treatises of the same name for WB's source. The Derge Tanjur (MW23703_4322, etext VE23703_1521 pp. 100-186) holds the tantra itself, «གཡུལ་ལས་རྣམ་པར་རྒྱལ་བ་ཞེས་པའི་རྒྱུད་ཀྱི་རྒྱལ་པོ་དབྱངས་འཆར་བ་ཞེས་བྱ་བ», Sanskrit title ending «སྦ་རོ་ད་ཡ་ནཱ་མ»: WB's «ས་རོད་ཡའི་རྒྱུད», the Dbyangs 'char and ch. 12's «གཡུལ་རྒྱལ་རྒྱུད» are that one tantra; the treatises of p. 195 and p. 199 are short works of the same name. Which of ch. 34's works come from it is not found (q748).

BDRC's search (exact phrase, 2026-11-08; snippets of etexts, not a scan) shows what the colophon's «ས་རོད་ཡ» (sA rod ya) is.

The string «ས་རོད་ཡ» has no hit, and «ས་རོད་ཡའི་རྒྱུད» none either; the search for «སྭ་རོད་ཡ» (swa rod ya) has four. They are the Derge and Narthang Tanjur work whose Sanskrit title BDRC gives as Svarodayārthasaṃgraha (swa rod ya a r+tha saM gra ha), in Tibetan «དབྱངས་འཆར་བའི་དོན་བསྡུས་པ» (dbyangs 'char ba'i don bsdus pa), by the teacher Seng ge go cha, and the Derge etext titled «ཚེ་རྩིས་ཀྱི་འབྲས་བུ་གསལ་བར་བྱེད་པ» (tshe rtsis kyi 'bras bu gsal bar byed pa), "making clear the results of life-reckoning", with the same Sanskrit title, credited to 'Jam dpal gzhon nu. So WB's «ས་རོད་ཡ» is the transliteration of Svarodaya, "the rising of the vowels or sounds" (dbyangs 'char); the life-reckoning title, which the first draft thought suited a chapter on results, belongs to a different Tanjur work (see the correction above). The verse's «དབྱངས་འཆར་རྒྱུད» (q473) is the same Svarodaya in translation; the identification is by title, and no line of the Tanjur work was read.

The colophon itself is found in two Gser thang and 1996 etexts with «ས་རོད་ཡའི་རྒྱུད»; a third etext of it (the collection «བོད་ལུགས་གསོ་རིག་རྩ་ཆེའི་དཔེ་རྙིང་ཀུན་བཏུས», bod lugs gso rig rtsa che'i dpe rnying kun btus) spells the name «སྔ་རོ་ད་ཡའི་རྒྱུད» (snga ro da ya'i rgyud) and its last clause otherwise, a witness that the name was copied by ear. «དམར་ནག་སོགས་ཆེས་ཟབ» finds only the three colophons and nothing else, as RD113 saw in the 1996 etext.

Also found: «ཨ་འཐས་བདེན་ཞེན» (a 'thas bden zhen) has 36 hits, among them Longchenpa's collected works and Chos kyi grags pa's, in lists of what is to be given up ("clinging to the real as solid"), so the verse's phrase is an ordinary Dzogchen-register compound, not a printer's cut (q472).
