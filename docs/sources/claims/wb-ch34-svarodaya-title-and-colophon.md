# The colophon's «ས་རོད་ཡ» (sA rod ya) is the transliteration of the Tanjur's Svarodaya, «དབྱངས་འཆར་བའི་དོན་བསྡུས་པ»; «ཨ་འཐས་བདེན་ཞེན» is attested elsewhere

BDRC's search (exact phrase, 2026-11-08; snippets of etexts, not a scan) shows what the colophon's «ས་རོད་ཡ» (sA rod ya) is.

The string «ས་རོད་ཡ» has no hit, and «ས་རོད་ཡའི་རྒྱུད» none either; the search for «སྭ་རོད་ཡ» (swa rod ya) has four. They are the Derge and Narthang Tanjur work whose Sanskrit title BDRC gives as Svarodayārthasaṃgraha (swa rod ya a r+tha saM gra ha), in Tibetan «དབྱངས་འཆར་བའི་དོན་བསྡུས་པ» (dbyangs 'char ba'i don bsdus pa), by the teacher Seng ge go cha, and the Derge etext titled «ཚེ་རྩིས་ཀྱི་འབྲས་བུ་གསལ་བར་བྱེད་པ» (tshe rtsis kyi 'bras bu gsal bar byed pa), "making clear the results of life-reckoning", with the same Sanskrit title, credited to 'Jam dpal gzhon nu. So WB's «ས་རོད་ཡ» is the transliteration of Svarodaya, "the rising of the vowels or sounds" (dbyangs 'char), and the title's second form, a work on life-reckoning results, suits a chapter on the results of works. The verse's «དབྱངས་འཆར་རྒྱུད» (q473) is the same Svarodaya in translation; the identification is by title, and no line of the Tanjur work was read.

The colophon itself is found in two Gser thang and 1996 etexts with «ས་རོད་ཡའི་རྒྱུད»; a third etext of it (the collection «བོད་ལུགས་གསོ་རིག་རྩ་ཆེའི་དཔེ་རྙིང་ཀུན་བཏུས», bod lugs gso rig rtsa che'i dpe rnying kun btus) spells the name «སྔ་རོ་ད་ཡའི་རྒྱུད» (snga ro da ya'i rgyud) and its last clause otherwise, a witness that the name was copied by ear. «དམར་ནག་སོགས་ཆེས་ཟབ» finds only the three colophons and nothing else, as RD113 saw in the 1996 etext.

Also found: «ཨ་འཐས་བདེན་ཞེན» (a 'thas bden zhen) has 36 hits, among them Longchenpa's collected works and Chos kyi grags pa's, in lists of what is to be given up ("clinging to the real as solid"), so the verse's phrase is an ordinary Dzogchen-register compound, not a printer's cut (q472).
