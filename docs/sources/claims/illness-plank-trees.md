# The illness reckoning: the four plank-trees read

The first of ch. 25's seven great reckonings reads each aspect's plank-tree (ཐང་ཤིང, thang shing) off the board of the fifteen starting points: life force (སྲོག, srog), body (ལུས, lus), power (དབང་ཐང, dbang thang), luck (ཀླུང་རྟ, klung rta). Its verdict is white (stands), black (broken), speckled, mostly black or mostly white, each with its reading and rites:

| Aspect | Stands | Broken: rites | Speckled | Mostly black | Mostly white |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| life force | the pillar of life firm, no sudden mishap | life declines, a great fault on the life: ransom of life force and la, the long-life ritual and empowerment, paying the dead's tax | illness, spirits, disputes: give, recite, clay images | a fierce spirit: care with food and conduct, bind the eight classes | no fault, but an acute hot illness: the trigram's dharma |
| body | freed of bodily illness | lustre and life decline, long illness: a substitute, a new name, clay images, stūpa, the *Four Hundred*; half and half: purging and vomiting, fulfilment and offerings to the ground lords | (as half and half) | more rites | small fault, easy to live |
| power | wealth and men gather | glory robbed, wealth lost: giving, lamps, rites against gossip and raids | bile and phlegm: care with food, the *Four Hundred* | a bad year, shade and vengeance: the *'Bum* of tormas | spirits and conditions: rites of the white side |
| luck | the pillar of existence | demons, moving country, disputes: burnt offering, pressing down demons and the enemy sri | uncertain, danger from sky demons: the planets' thread-cross | bad food, violence, losses: dhāraṇīs, ransom from death | shade and conditions, illness on alternate days: the *Twenty Thousand* |

By the number broken: two, life and fortune decline; three, look beyond the boundary; four, the boundary crossed. The luck's plank-tree broken at the top, middle or bottom of the board points to the grave, the house or the field, each with its remedy.
