# The twenty of p. 503 add up with the last group's three

The notes' p. 503 (Yigdzin-1 of img. 511, l. 1–6) counts the twenty so: trigram, mewa and year, three; the four rises and declines, four; the single and the person's nine-eyes, two; "life force, entering the tomb and the rest, up to the la" (བླ་བར), five; the trigram's and the mewa's nine-eyes, two; the bone-and-element, one; «ལོ་ཟླ་ཞག་གསུམ་གྱི་གསུམ», three: twenty in all (3+4+2+5+2+1+3). So the last group is three pebbles making up the twenty and the group's «བླ་བར» is the five that end at the la. Whose year the group takes is still not told; the same passage names, among the other systems it rejects as home-made, «ཤི་ལོའི་བབ་སྤར», the trigram the year of death falls on, which shows the year of death is a quantity such systems used, not that this group does. The county gazetteer's «ཤི་བའི་ལོ» (q429, RD58 sixth reading) as the year of dying is a witness for it.
