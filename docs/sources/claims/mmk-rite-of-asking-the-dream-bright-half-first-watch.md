# The root tantra's own rite for asking a dream: bright half, first watch, kusha, head east (RD370)

The root tantra's rite for asking a dream (the Derge chapter 15, Sanskrit p. 146): on the bright half of the month or the half of the Great Miracle (the Sanskrit adds "or another auspicious day"; the Derge says "certainly on a good day"), in the first watch of the night, the practiser makes a fire offering of 1,008 and a further 1,008 lotus flowers before the painted cloth, offers with the mantra that asks "show me my dream truly", spreads kusha with its tips outward, makes a pillow of kusha stems, lies with the head to the east before the cloth, keeps awake and at effort in the first watch, bows to the Buddhas and bodhisattvas, confesses sin, gives himself to all Buddhas and then sleeps as is easy. This is the practiser's own rite for his own dream, not a pupil's night before an initiation; the common detail with the Sngags rim chen mo's preparation is the kusha and the head to the east (snr:p369 quotes the Common Tantra for that).
