# The la pebbles' specific verdict: empty and filled places

WB's second verdict on the twelve la pebbles (བྱེ་བྲག་ཁ་དམར, bye brag kha dmar; vol. 2, pp. 52–53), by which places hold pebbles (input: the board of [dead-la-pebbles-layout](dead-la-pebbles-layout.md)).

| Condition | Reading |
| --- | --- |
| the mother year's pebbles "coarse" (notes, p. 502: pebbles before the mother year but none before the son, or none before the mother year), with a black before the power year and the mother year empty | the people's prospects (མི་འགྲོ, mi 'gro) good, the "navel" prospects (ལྟེ་འགྲོ, lte 'gro) bad; thriving now, declining at the end |
| many white before the power year | the people's prospects bad, the navel prospects good; hard work, whatever the hand reaches is gained |
| before the great and small mother years not empty | though now a single man [?], in the end he holds the centre of the land, his own master |
| the four executioners' pebble door empty | the forefathers' crimes and broken oaths; skulls incomplete, an empty cut-off with no corpse; a water hole under the roof [?] |
| before the small cut-off empty | brides holding the house wander off; the female forebears' skulls incomplete; leprosy and epidemics now and then, fires and pollution |
| four pebbles risen in the enemy's place | strife in the land |
| the friend's place empty | servants and sisters lost; fools, the maimed and the faulty born; sisters given in marriage die in the father's place |

No remedy is given in this verdict. "The four executioners' pebble door" names no place of the notes' twelve; which pebbles it means is not stated.
