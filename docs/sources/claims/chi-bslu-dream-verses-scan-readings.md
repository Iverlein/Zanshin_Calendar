# The dream verses' doubted places on the scans: CBL's three readings are the print's own

On the typeset CBL print the places RD331 doubted read as the etext has them: the annotation «འོ་ཡུག་པ» (img. 237 line 2), «དྲི་མར» without da (img. 238 line 1) and «ལོ་ནི་བརྒྱར» (img. 238 line 8). So the difference of CBL from the Derge's «དྲི་དམར» and «ལོ་བརྒྱད» is the print's, not the etext's. The Derge scan (8 px letters) does not settle its own da by eye; Yigdzin-1 reads «དྲི་དམར» and «ལོ་བརྒྱད» as the Derge etext, and on both prints the other differing readings of claim chi-bslu-dream-verses-cbl-against-derge hold by the machine. Yigdzin-1 reads «ཀོ་བི་དར» on the Derge where the etext has «ཀོ་པི་ད་ར»: a doubt.
