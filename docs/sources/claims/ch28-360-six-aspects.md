# The 360's six aspects by the notes; the word phyi rtsis

WB vol. 2, pp. 42–43 and the notes, p. 538 (BDRC etext only; p. 538 not read on the scan): Mipham's notes say the 360 starting points are the six, life force (སྲོག, srog), body (ལུས, lus), power (དབང་ཐང, dbang thang), la (བླ, bla), one's own grave (རང་དུར, rang dur) and the executioner's grave (གཤེད་དུར, gshed dur), each binding sixty years, so six times sixty is 360; and their worked example of a person of the water rat year goes through exactly these six, sought in turn from the enemy's, one's own, the son's, the mother's, one's own and the enemy's year. WB's verse names the same six when it says what is sought, and then lists the six years fought as life force, body, power, la, life force, life force; the notes thus support reading the fifth as the grave and the sixth as the executioner's grave, and the two repeats as slips (or a verse that wrote «སྲོག» for «དུར» and «གཤེད་དུར»). The scribe's word «ཕྱི་རྩིས» on p. 43: the 1996 etext reads «ཕྱི་རྩིས», and vol. 1 (p. 309, etext p. 319) has «ཕྱི་རྩིས་པ», a reckoner who condenses the twelve planets, so the word exists in WB; but the Gser thang reprint's etext reads «བྱི་རྩིས» at the end of the notes' section (reprint etext p. 541) where the 1996 print has «སྤྱི་རྩིས་ཀྱི་སྐབས་སོ», "the section on the general reckoning", so a stacked «སྤྱི» is read «བྱི» by that OCR. The third candidate, «སྤྱི་རྩིས» (the general reckoning, which the boasters would hold secret), is therefore as likely as «ཕྱི་རྩིས» on p. 43; the print was not settled by eye.
