# Who is not made an old one, and the Clear Lamp's kinds

Some dead are not given the old one's rites (WB vol. 2, pp. 79, 82): an old one burnt by fire, carried off by water, or the woman's *bkra sri kha dmar* (བཀྲ་སྲི་ཁ་དམར) get no funeral share; and no one dead by the blade, of tumour, dropsy or another infectious illness, of leprosy, gall or ulcers, is made an old one (the blade would take the descendants, nāga illness would come). Born and dead on the mewa two-black as a person of the tiger, monkey, pig or snake year, or born and dead on the trigrams *kham* and *gin*: not made an old one, lest the old one's door of the descendants be emptied. The *Clear Lamp* sorts the old by circumstance into five kinds and nine heads (white, black, speckled of each) with thirteen down to no fortune offerings (pp. 80–81). These are judged from the life, not reckoned from dates, except the mewa and trigram tests.
