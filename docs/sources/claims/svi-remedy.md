# The Svapna-īkṣā's remedy and method

Advayavajra's remedy and method (p. 262): for a fortunate dream, begin the practice by the rite told for the Jewel Seal (རིན་ཆེན་ཕྱག་རྒྱ, rin chen phyag rgya, the Ratnasambhava family's); for a bad sign, a sesame fire offering (ཏིལ་གྱི་སྦྱིན་སྲེག, til gyi sbyin sreg) and a purification by a thousand recitations of that family's mantra. The dream is no more than the rest of saṃsāra: all its dharmas are like a dream and the yogin should see them so, for the delusion is not real; wisdom is born from realising the good, and what is made by the mind has no birth, so in secret mantra the means that depend on dependent arising are to be taken as chief. The closing verse says the dreams of generating the mind of awakening and of empowerment are gathered from Nāgārjuna's text. The remedy is by the family of the sign and not by the watch or the day.
