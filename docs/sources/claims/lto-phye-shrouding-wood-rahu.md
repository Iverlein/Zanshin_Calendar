# Lto-'phye's shrouding wood draws a Rahu on the elephant's head: the earth's Rahu, not Vishnu's

Reply 170 (ys.2:i026, machine) explains that the Rahu drawn on the elephant's head in Lto-'phye's shrouding wood is the earth's own Rahu, known by the earth lord's name, not Vishnu's Rahu. It bears on q463 only in this: the elephant (གླང་ཆེན, glang chen) of WB p. 282 is Lto-'phye's figure, and the reply names no tail, no horse-mother and no part of the ground, so the harm by part and «དགུ་ཆའི་སར» (q482) are untouched. Replies 171-208 were not read (RD104b).
