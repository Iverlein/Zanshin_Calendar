# The four courses of a planet by foot: swift east, slow south, crooked west, elemental north

Each of the five planets moves, in WB's account of the foot (*rkang*) of the swift and slow paces, in one of four courses by whether its reckoning is ordered (*rim pa can*) or unordered (*rim min*) and by whether it is the earlier or the later foot: the earlier foot of the ordered is the swift course (*myur 'gros*) and the planet appears in the east; the later foot of the ordered is the slow course (*dal 'gros*), south; the earlier foot of the unordered is the crooked course (*'khyog 'gros*), west; the later foot of the unordered is the "elemental" course (*'byung ba'i 'gros*), north. The same four lines stand on p. 40 (where the machine reads the south as "belly", *lto*, and p. 49 reads it *lho*) and p. 49.
