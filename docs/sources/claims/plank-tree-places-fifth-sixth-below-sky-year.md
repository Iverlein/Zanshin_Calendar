# The plank-tree's fifteenth and sixteenth heads start at the sky year's fifth and sixth places below

Question 374 asks where the plank-tree's fifteenth and sixteenth heads start: «ལྔ་ཐུན» (the five in harmony) and «དྲུག་འབྲས» (six fruit) as the *Moonbeams*' scan prints them (dz:i052-055, img. 054, l. 1–2, read by eye: «གནམ་ལོའི་མར་གྱི་ལྔ་ཐུན་ནས། དུ་ལོན་གྱེན་དུ་བགྲངས་ལ་བརྩི།» and «གནམ་ལོའི་མར་གྱི་དྲུག་འབྲས་ནས། …»; the scan has «ཐུན» without མ and «འབྲས», KD «མཐུན» and «འགྲུས»).

What the White Beryl and Mipham's notes give. (a) WB lists four places of the sky year in a row, «བཞི་གཤེད་ལྔ་མཐུན་དྲུག་འབྲས་ཏེ་བདུན་ཟུར» (wb.1:p413, img. 423): the four slayers, the five in harmony, six fruit, the seventh corner. (b) The notes' water-snake example (wb.2:p534, etext p. 542) lists the places of a water-snake year in the notes' own numbered order: its own year, the seventh corner (བདུན་ཟུར, bdun zur) the pig, the three in harmony (མཐུན་གསུམ) bird and ox, the four slayers (བཞི་གཤེད) tiger and monkey. Counting the year itself as the first, the seventh corner is six years off (snake to pig), the harmony is four years off on either side (snake, bird, ox are the trine) and the slayers three years off: the places of (a) are the fourth, fifth, sixth and seventh places, in that order, counted with the year as one. By that order «དྲུག་འབྲས» is the sixth place and «ལྔ་མཐུན» the fifth. (c) WB's own ordinary use agrees for the fifth: the notes speak of «གནམ་ལོའི་མར་ལྔ་མཐུན་གསུམ», the sky year's down-fifth with its three in harmony (wb.2:p494, etext p. 502), and WB's keg reckoning calls the support-peg's place the down-five, «མར་ལྔ་བགྲངས་པ་རྟེན་ཕུར་ཡིན» (wb.1:p387, img. 397, read by eye; the up-five is the sky rope), the same peg that is the fifteenth head. (d) For the sixth, WB places the earth lord Tsangkun the black «གནམ་ལོའི་མར་གྱི་དྲུག་པའི་སྟེང་» (wb.2:p188, etext p. 196), on the sky year's down-sixth.

So the fifteenth head starts at the fifth place below the sky year, the year's harmony (four years from it as the years are counted), and the sixteenth at the sixth place below it (five years); "below" is the text's «མར». Not settled: which way «མར» runs in the years of the cycle (earlier or later), which these passages do not state; and that «དྲུག་འབྲས» is a place of its own is read from its position in the list (a) and from (d), not from a gloss. Narrows question 374.
