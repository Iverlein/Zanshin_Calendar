# The mantras of the prayer wheel, as printed

The seven mantras the prayer wheel carries, each spelled as printed on the page named, read on the scan. The White Beryl (བཻ་ཌཱུར་དཀར་པོ, bai DUr dkar po, WB) prints only the first; of the others it has at most a name or a fragment (PLAN.md, *Dead ends*).

| Mantra | As printed | Passage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mañjuśrī (འཇམ་དཔལ, 'jam dpal) | ཨོཾ་ཨ་ར་པ་ཙ་ན་དྷཱིཿ | wb.2:p128-129 |
| Avalokiteśvara (སྤྱན་རས་གཟིགས, spyan ras gzigs) | ཨོཾ་མ་ཎི་པདྨེ་ཧཱུྃ | lg:i63, Mipham |
| Padmasambhava (པདྨ་འབྱུང་གནས, pad+ma 'byung gnas) | ཨོཾ་ཨཱཿཧཱུྃ་བཛྲ་གུ་རུ་པདྨ་སིདྡྷི་ཧཱུྃ | sk.13:p258; opening also mcw.32:p289, Mipham |

| Medicine Buddha (སྨན་བླ, sman bla) | ཏདྱ་ཐཱ། ཨོཾ་བྷཻ་ཥ་ཛྱེ་བྷཻ་ཥ་ཛྱེ། མ་ཧཱ་བྷཻ་ཥ་ཛྱེ། བྷཻ་ཥ་ཛྱེ་རཱ་ཛཱ་ཡ། ས་མུངྒ་ཏེ་སྭཱ་ཧཱ | st:p92, the Four Tantras |

Where the witnesses part:

- **Vajrapāṇi**: Mipham prints no ཕཊ, and the verse he quotes gives the mantra six letters, the count of that form's Sanskrit syllables. ཨོཾ་བཛྲ་པཱ་ཎི་ཧཱུྃ་ཕཊ stands in bk.1:p613, a lower witness, ཕཊ without a halanta. Mipham stands by rank.
- **Medicine Buddha**: the short form of the recited rite is ཏདྱ་ཐཱ༔ ཨོཾ་བྷཻ་ཥ་ཛྱེ་བྷཻ་ཥ་ཛྱེ་མ་ཧཱ་བྷཻ་ཥ་ཛྱེ་རཱ་ཛ་ས་མུངྒ་ཏེ་སྭཱ་ཧཱ༔ (ak:p268). The Four Tantras stand above it. Both print མུངྒ, the stack checked on the scans; མུདྒ and མུཏྒ are BDRC etext spellings of other works (a *Gsung 'bum* of Chos kyi grags pa, of Grags pa bshad sgrub, the *Sgrub thabs kun btus*, the Sde dge *gzungs bsdus*), not read on a scan.
- **Padmasambhava**: Mipham's print in mcw.32:p289 stops at བཛྲ with the sign of abbreviation, so the full form is from 'Jam dbyangs mkhyen brtse'i dbang po's prayer, sk.13:p258.
- **Avalokiteśvara**: Mipham's blocks have ཧཱུྃ, the reset copy of the same work ཧཱུཾ. The blocks stand.
- **Opening**: Mipham's rite for Śākyamuni allows the mantra from ཨོཾ without ཏདྱ་ཐཱ (mgz.6:p4).

In every print read, ཨོཾ has the plain ring (U+0F7E), ཧཱུྃ the ring in a crescent (U+0F83), and ཨཱཿ and དྷཱིཿ the visarga (U+0F7F).
