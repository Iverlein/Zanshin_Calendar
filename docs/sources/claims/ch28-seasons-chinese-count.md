# Chapter 28's seasons are the Chinese reckoning's (Hor 11 the first spring month)

WB vol. 2, p. 26 with the notes, p. 537: chapter 28 counts its seasons by the Chinese reckoning, not the Kālacakra's. The notes name the three spring dates (27th, 25th, 23rd) as those of the "eleventh Hor month", the *rgyal* month (རྒྱལ་ཟླ, rgyal zla; the twelfth, by the dictionaries) and the Miracle month (ཆོ་འཕྲུལ, cho 'phrul; the first): the first spring month is Hor month 11. WB's own model almanac (vol. 1, pp. 154–171, read on the scan 2026-10-08 and quoted in tibetastromed.md, *WB's months and seasons*) heads Hor month 11 "by the Chinese reckoning the first month of spring, the tiger month", month 12 the middle of spring, month 1 the last of spring, and so on round: summer is Hor months 2–4, autumn 5–7, winter 8–10, two months before the Kālacakra's seasons. Every season word of chapter 28 (the first thunder of "first summer", the dates of snow-rain by "first/middle/last" of each season, the black small feet) is read by this count. The tenth month's 15th, the day of the six full moons (p. 27), is then the last winter month; the first of the eleventh month (p. 30) the start of spring, the Hor year's own turning.
