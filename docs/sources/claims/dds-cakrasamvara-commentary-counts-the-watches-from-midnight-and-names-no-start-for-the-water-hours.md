# The Cakrasaṃvara commentary counts its eight watches from midnight in both copies and names no moment from which the water-hours are counted (RD403, q1124)

RD403 (q1124). The commentary has two measures: the water-hour (ཆུ་ཚོད) in a scheme of sixty to the day and night, five of them to a junction (svo:i521-523), and the eight watches (ཐུན) in svo:i525, where the clause reads «in the sun's part, from midnight for as long as the sun lasts, four; likewise in the moon's part four; thus it is from the session where night and day are equal». The Bstan 'gyur Dpe bsdur ma copy of the commentary (dds.51:p707-708) has the clause word for word and no gloss, and states that this is the provisional meaning; the definitive meaning that follows it («by the distinction of times: where one has power over the life and blesses, there the deities of that mandala are honoured with the life-flowing») is not about hours at all. So the one place that names a starting moment names midnight (མཚན་མོ་ཕྱེད་ནས), and it does so for the watches: the eight are counted from it. Whether the first watch begins exactly there or the clause only places the night's middle is still the clause's own ambiguity («from midnight for as long as the sun lasts» has the sun's four watches start at midnight), and the reading «the first watch begins at midnight» is the natural one but not an explicit statement.

For the water-hours the answer is that the commentary names no moment from which they are counted, in either copy; the order of the sādhana (the practitioner sleeps, wakes at midnight, then «the fifth water-hour» session, then the early morning one, svo:i572-573, and in this copy at p764-765) is the only evidence, and it fits a count from midnight (see the earlier claim on the fifth chu tshod). The BDRC search for the exact phrases of this clause gave no other text.
