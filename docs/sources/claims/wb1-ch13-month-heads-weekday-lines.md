# The weekday lines of the 2nd to 6th heads and the wrapped line

The weekday lines of the 2nd to 6th heads, as far as read: the 4th (p. 162): dar 2, 4, 5; gud 0; skyod 1, 3; zhud 6, the same set as the 12th (p. 155, RD200), figures read on a 1x crop; the 5th (p. 163): dar 3, 1, 0 (read at 3x), gud 2, 4; skyod 6; zhud 5, the seven weekdays each once; the 2nd (p. 159, read at 3x): dar 1, 3; gud 6; skyod 0; zhud 2, 4, which gives six weekdays, five missing; the 3rd (p. 160, 3x): gud 5; skyod 2, 4; zhud 1, 3; its dar figures lie at the end of the line before and were not read; the 6th (p. 165, 3x on the first figures): dar 5, 3, 1, the rest not read. In the 5th head the line wraps: "dar" and its figures stand at the end of one line and gud, skyod and zhud with theirs on the next, under arcs; that is how the 1st head's three figures after "dar" (read 3, 0, 1 by RD200) and the "6, 5" beyond them fit: gud's figures (2, 4 by this pattern) probably stand on the next line, so the 1st would be dar 3, 0, 1; gud 2, 4; skyod 6; zhud 5, the same set as the 5th. This was not looked at again on the 1st head's scan in this reading.
