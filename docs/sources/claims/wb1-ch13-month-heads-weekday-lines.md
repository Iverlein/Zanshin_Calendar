# The weekday lines of the 2nd to 6th heads and the wrapped line

Read figure by figure at 3x on the scan (passage wb.1:p159z, 2026-11-12), the weekday lines of the 2nd to 6th heads are: 2nd (p. 159): dar 1, 3; gud 6; skyod 0; zhud 2, 4, so six weekdays and 5 absent; 3rd (p. 160): dar 0, 6; gud 5; skyod 2, 4; zhud 1, 3; 4th (p. 162): dar 2, 4, 5; gud 0; skyod 1, 3; zhud 6; 5th (p. 163): dar 3, 1, 0; gud 2, 4; skyod 6; zhud 5; 6th (p. 165): dar 5, 3, 1; zhud 2, 4; gud 6; skyod 0. The 3rd, 4th, 5th and 6th each hold the seven weekdays 0 to 6 once; the 2nd alone lacks 5, and its figures read the same at 3x on two passes, so the gap is in the print or in what the sentence meant, not in the reading. The 6th head's braces stand in the order dar, zhud, gud, skyod, not dar, gud, skyod, zhud; the labels are told apart by the loop under the first (zhud), and the order is the one doubt left on this line. The wrapped line: in the 5th and the 6th head "dar" and its figures stand at the end of one line, the other three braces on the next; the 1st head's (p. 157, img. 164) the same, and read on the scan it is dar 3, 0, 1; gud 2, 4; skyod 6; zhud 5, the same set as the 5th head's.
