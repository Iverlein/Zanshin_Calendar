# Winning or losing at contests, dice and debate; throwing dice

Image group: people and contests. Read by 3 texts: WB ch. 30, Four Tantras (for a sick dreamer), Lama Gongdü (LGD).
Offered to any dreamer; the Four Tantras' or WB ch. 25's rows join when the dreamer is ill.

| Text | Verdict (tone) | Passage | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WB ch. 30 | winning at contests, dice and debate is a good omen; losing at them is a flaw in what is to be reckoned (mixed or turning) | `wb.2:p144` | scan |
| Four Tantras (for a sick dreamer) | losing in a fight (འཐབ་མོ་ཕམ, 'thab mo pham): a sign that the lord of death (འཆི་བདག, 'chi bdag) has seized the sick, who dies of it if he dreams it constantly (gt-dream-death-rule) (bad) | `gt:i42-43` | machine |
| Lama Gongdü (LGD) | winning when quarrelling with others (good) | `lgd:p967` | scan |
| Lama Gongdü (LGD) | throwing dice: a bad sign that looks good, immediate good, in truth bad (mixed or turning) | `lgd:p969-970` | scan |

WB reads winning at dice as good; LGD lists throwing dice among the bad signs that look good: the two do not say the same dream, and the picker keeps both.

RD274 (2026-11-09): the Four Tantras' line is a fight (འཐབ་མོ), not a contest of dice or debate; it is shown beside WB ch. 30's losing at contests because it is the nearest image of the pool, and the picker keeps the two apart by the word of each row.
