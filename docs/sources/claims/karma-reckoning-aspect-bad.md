# An aspect is bad in the karma reckoning when its block's verdict is black

The notes gloss WB's list of those who are bad "even if they agree" (vol. 1, p. 364) as findings of the karma reckoning (ལས་རྩིས, las rtsis), each spouse's own birth reckoning of ch. 22 ([match-karma-reckoning](match-karma-reckoning.md)): "life exhausted" the life force bad, "vitality exhausted" the body's reckoning bad, "poor" the power small, "in danger of ill speech" the luck bad, "of bad karmic result" the reckoning bad in general (wb.2:p526). What makes an aspect bad is not said there. Three passages bear on it.

- **Mipham, on the birth itself** (wb.2:p483, on the scan): an aspect "in decline" (གུད, gud) is one whose verdict (ཁ་དམར, kha dmar) on its pebbles of the hundred is black, that is, the deciding pebbles under its columns ([birth-hundred-board](birth-hundred-board.md)); "the enemy rising" (དགྲ་དར, dgra dar) is its enemy pebble white. One such finding alone is "no great fault"; with the mother in decline and a bad trigram and mewa it is bad. The same notes measure a person's power by "the power in the karma reckoning's hundred pebbles" (wb.2:p478).
- **WB's own readings of the hundred** (wb.1:p329-331; these four lines read on the Gser thang reprint, I3CN5028 img. 325–326, 2026-10-25): the life force's pebble bad, «ཆུང་དུས་གསོ་དཀའ་ཚེ་ཡང་ཐུང», hard to raise when small and the life short; the body's own pebble black, «ནད་མོད་དབང་པོ་སྐྱོན་ཅན་འོང», frequent illness and faulty senses; the power's pebble black, «རྒས་ཁར་ནོར་ཕྱུགས་གང་སྟོངས་ཆེ», wealth and livestock emptied in old age; the luck's own pebble black, «རང་གིས་བྱས་ཀྱང་ངོ་རེ་ཆུང་། །ཕ་ཚན་ནང་ནས་ངན་གཤོམ་ཡོད», little regard for what one does and ill plots within the father's clan. In the general verdict by where the pebbles lie (p. 331, on the scan), a white rim and black centre is «ཟས་ནོར་དབང་ཐང་ཆུང་བ», food, wealth and power small: the notes' own words for "poor", the power small (དབང་ཐང་ཆུང་བ, dbang thang chung ba).
- **A manual's karma reckoning** (UT, ut:p153-155 and ut:p175, etext only): the chapter it calls the karma reckoning closes with one verdict for each aspect, the life force's pebbles white long life, black practices for long life, and so for the body, the power and the luck, beside verdicts by kind of pebble across the aspects, where the friend pebbles black give "poor".

So, as a reading: in the karma reckoning over a match an aspect is bad when its block's verdict is black (Mipham's "decline"), and WB's words on p. 364 answer the black readings of the life force's, body's, power's and luck's own pebbles; "the reckoning bad in general" is the whole birth judged by WB's rule for the whole (wb.1:p349: the bad results greater and more, bad), with the general verdict by where the pebbles lie (p. 331) as the other candidate. No text says in so many words that the karma reckoning over a match is read this way. The rise and decline at birth (pp. 331–333) is a separate measure: the notes send only the match's own rise and decline to "the karma reckoning's section" (wb.2:p526).
