# The regent: where the mansions' grouping and particular results disagree, the particular leads

The regent answers, for the mansions, that where their grouping (ཕྱོགས་བསྡེབས, phyogs bsdebs) and their particular results (སྒེར་འབྲས, sger 'bras) disagree, the particular result, told in detail, is the chief (ys.2:i049, reply 187). It is the rule of WB's weighing, "where a particular case occurs, it leads" (WB vol. 2, p. 376), applied by the author to one factor's two kinds of result. He does not extend it to the earth lords.
