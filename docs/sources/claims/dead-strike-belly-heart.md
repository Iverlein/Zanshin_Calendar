# The demon's belly strike and the heart strike

The first two of the tenth examination's eight great strikes are read from the trigram of death and the animals of the dead and of the death's year, month, day and hour (WB vol. 2, pp. 86–87; the notes, pp. 505–506, read on the scan 2026-10-23). The demon's belly strike: dying on the trigram of one's own direction (horse or snake on *li*, sheep on *khon*), or, with the trigram on *li*, a death in a horse or snake year, month, day or hour, or of a rat or pig; the fire horse and snake above all. Remedy if the death-opening is not cut: on the enemy trigram's day and hour, a person of the enemy year carries the corpse toward the enemy direction and makes the enemy's grave. The heart strike: dying on *dwa* or *zin*. On *dwa* a bird or monkey with bird or monkey year, month, day and hour is the dear heart strike, bad for whoever is dearest to the dead; other years on *dwa* the milder limb strike; on *zin* tiger or hare, milder again. Another view adds bird or monkey on *li* and the four executioners on *zin* (the heart's rope cut), and horse or snake on *li* with horse or snake times (the heart strike of becoming). The notes count 19 and 43 identifying pebbles for the two.
