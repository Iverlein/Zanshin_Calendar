# The Gdams ngag mdzod texts of rrd.21:p19 and rrd.22:p38-39 are by Chos kyi grags pa and by Nam mkha' rdo rje, by their colophons (RD402)

The text of Gdams ngag mdzod vol. 9 that holds rrd.21:p19 (the clear light of experience, rrd.21 etext p19, printed pp. 488-489) is headed «Bla med lhan skyes rnam bzhi'i rdzogs rim snying po don gyi phreng ba», a short completion-stage text of the four innate ones of the unsurpassed (not a sgrub thabs compilation), and its colophon names the Bri gung pa Chos kyi grags pa as the one who set it together, at 'Bras spungs, in a fire-female ox year, with the scribe Rgyal grags pa dbang po. So the BDRC author field «Kun mkhyen 'Jigs med gling pa», which the search page gave for the volume's sgrub thabs text, does not belong to this text, and Kong sprul is not named in it. The text of vol. 1 that holds rrd.22:p38-39 (the border of sleep and not-sleep, the habit-appearances, the dream testing the equipoise) is headed «Slob dpon Dga' rab rdo rje nas brgyud pa'i rdzogs pa chen po sems sde'i pra khrid kyi man ngag», of the Khams tradition of the mind series, and its colophon, at printed pp. 361-362, names the beggar Nam mkha' rdo rje as the one who set it together at Dpal gnam steng ri khrod in a water-female bird year, from the root verses, the Nyams sgron and other sources; the page does not say which water-bird year, nor give his lineage, so the identification with the Nyang-tradition master rests on this colophon only.
