# The illness's five pebbles: the trigrams of the time of falling ill and the commonest element

The illness identified by five pebbles (WB vol. 1, p. 435): by the regent's reply 133 (ys:i491), the first four are the trigram elements (སྤར་ཁམས, spar khams) of the year, month, day and hour *in which one fell ill* («གང་གི་ཐོག་ཏུ་ན་བ»), the trigram counted with li on the tiger, horse and dog [years]; the fifth is an element pebble of whichever element is most among the four. "The element of the five constitutions for the illness of the four times" in another inner reckoning is not medicine's seasons of wind and phlegm but this same fifth pebble, as the *thang shing* of the first translation of the *Khams khra* has it.

The notes (wb.2:p497) also take the year, month, day and hour of falling ill, but make the four pebbles their life-force elements (སྲོག་ཁམས, srog khams) and the fifth the element most common among their trigrams; the reply's «དེ་བཞིའི་སྤར་ཁམས» names the four's trigram elements: the two differ on what the first four pebbles are, which the regent's short reply may only abbreviate. The reply is about p. 435's identification, not p. 426's table; that the "sky year" of p. 426 is likewise the year of falling ill is an inference (question 361).

Read again 2026-10-25 with the reply's question and WB's prints: [illness-first-four-pebbles-life-force](illness-first-four-pebbles-life-force.md) takes the first four as the life-force elements, by WB's words and the notes, and the reply's summary as being about the fifth.
