# The trigram's road of the remains

The trigram's road of the remains (སྤར་ཁའི་རོ་ཡི་ལམ, spar kha'i ro yi lam, WB vol. 2, p. 98; read on the scan, the Gser thang reprint, 2026-10-23):

- **In general** the directions of the four good ones, one's own and the mother are auspicious; the four bad are avoided. In particular the corpse is not sent toward the dead's or the corpse-bearer's demon-cut (བདུད་གཅོད, bdud gcod) direction; one who died on li is not sent onto kham; onto the trigram's enemy-grave (དགྲ་དུར, dgra dur) is bad. Some say: sent onto the enemy kham, bad for horse and snake years; sent onto kham, the six-fire is bad for the six-earth; the wind-horse sent to li, bad for horse and snake; again, bad for the life force's friend, the body's son, the power's wealth, the luck's enemy: endless such, but the life force is chief, and it is bad where the survivors are rough. If the death-mouth (ཤི་ཁ, shi kha) is not closed, the corpse is sent onto the enemy and the grave.
- **Corpses not sent onto a trigram**, lest that illness strike:

| Trigram | Corpse not sent onto it |
| --- | --- |
| li | the blind, the one-eyed, the *tshag po* |
| khon | the stammerer(?), the *sha ya*, death by suffocation |
| dwa | the one with hair(?) and the *gri bo* |
| khen | death by *dmu chu* or by poison |
| kham | the *skyur po* and those with the *rdel* illness |
| gin | the line-ended, death by a dagger's stroke |
| zin | the lame, death by choking |
| zon | the mad and the mute |

- Some, by the same reasoning, say the father's elders are not drawn onto one's own direction, khen; but this is little to be feared. One who died sick is not sent to dwa: illness and epidemic fall. One who died on death (?) avoids khen: if sent, a corpse follows the corpse. One who died a grave-death, sent to kham, is bad for children.
- **The four planets' corpse road** (གཟའ་བཞིའི་རོ་ལམ, gza' bzhi'i ro lam), the living and the dead joined: Saturn living with many li, not sent onto the element's enemy kham, nor onto the fighting enemy khen; the male and female planets likewise. A man who died in a mouse, dragon or monkey [year] not sent onto dwa or zin; a woman guarded on li and kham; Mars on Mars, Saturn on Saturn, Jupiter on Jupiter, Mercury on Mercury avoided. The trigram's *rkya mda' thug*: when the household has many on khen, avoid khen. "Do not send onto the piled enemy": khen piled, not sent onto li. The dead's five demons harm, the body cut; guard and so on by reasoning. Allowed even so: "drawn with a twisted neck", a li corpse drawn to khon or zon without danger.
