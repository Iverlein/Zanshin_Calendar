# The bad that resembles good and the good that resembles bad in LGD

Two kinds that reverse their appearance (lgd:p969-970). The bad that resembles good: another's banner on one's house, being lifted and carried by many, new red clothes, a black hat or black and blue turban, climbing a great tree's top, crossing a great pass into an unknown valley, being given black food such as melted butter or molasses, a garden of red flowers, dice, paying homage, being wrapped in silk, butter: immediate good, in truth bad. The good that resembles bad: carrying, burning, eating or cutting one's own body, carrying a great corpse, the body butchered, blood and pus flowing, eaten by others with no remainder, carried off by water and swimming, flying, all appearance burnt, washed or scattered, a great rain: terrifying but signs of release from illness and spirits, of purified obscurations and attainment near.
