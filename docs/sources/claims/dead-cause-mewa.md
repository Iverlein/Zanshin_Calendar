# The second examination: the speckled middle reason by the mewa

WB vol. 2, p. 58: the reason by the mewa (སྨེ་བ, sme ba) the death fell on, the living reckoning's protections neglected; in particular:

| Mewa | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| 1 white | a widow's place and impure food |
| 2 black | a night journey |
| 3 blue | a ritual weapon (ཟོར, zor) struck |
| 4 green | digging the soil (ཕྲུ་རློག, phru rlog) |
| 5 yellow | «སྐྱུར» (skyur, not understood) [?] or offerings misused |
| 6 white | impurity, a seasonal offering broken; a grave Bon and a bride |
| 7 red | the hearth, hearth smoke and a red [offering] |
| 8 white | the offerings of Buddhist and Bon priests and the saṅgha misused |
| 9 red | the fortune (ཕྱྭ་གཡང, phywa g.yang) declined |

Which mewa is meant (the dead's descended mewa at death, or the year's) is not stated on p. 58; the notes read the third examination from "the year of death's trigram and mewa" (p. 503).
