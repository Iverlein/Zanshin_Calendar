Every one falls in one of these classes: (a) a biography, song, colophon or treasure text that dates a dream as a log does (claim dds-dated-dreams-are-log-entries); (b) a name of a calendar month that contains the word «dream» (dds-dream-month-is-a-month-name); (c) the word «རྨི་ལམ» beside «ལོ», «ཉིན», «དུས» in a different sense: «རྨི་ལམ་ལོ» is mostly «dream, a hundred years» in the Bodhicaryāvatāra verse of the dream of a hundred years of joy (རྨི་ལམ་ལོ་བརྒྱར་བདེ་མྱོང་ནས), «རྨི་ལམ་ཉིན» and «ཉིན་མོའི་རྨི་ལམ» dream against day, the day-dream (ཉིན་མོའི་རྨི་ལམ, nyin mo'i rmi lam, "a daydream") of modern prose, the six yogas' «dream and day» pair, and a Lam 'bras passage that sorts dreams by the day's deed (Wylie of BDRC's snippet, not read in the etext: «mtshams sbyor gyi rmi lam la nyin par dge sbyor gang byas pa de rmi ba'o», the dream of the joining is of the virtue one did by day); «དུས་ཀྱི་རྨི་ལམ» a dream of a time (of sleep, of childhood, of the thesis' day), never a date; «སྟོན་གྱི་རྨི་ལམ» is «the dream of showing» (ལུང་སྟོན་, དགེ་སྟོན་, ཚུལ་སྟོན་: prophecy, the name Dge ston, an appearance), not autumn; «དཔྱིད་ཀྱི་རྨི་ལམ» («the dream of spring»), «དབྱར་གྱི་རྨི་ལམ», «ཟླ་བའི་རྨི་ལམ» and «ཉི་མའི་རྨི་ལམ» are titles and figures of modern prose and verse; «རྨི་ལམ་གྱི་འབྲས་བུ» «the fruit of a dream» in the debates on whether dream and its fruit differ, with no date; (d) the list «གཟའ་དང་རྒྱུ་སྐར་རྨི་ལམ་ངན་, ལྟས་ངན་སེལ་བར་བྱེད་པ» (the verses of Jambhala's hundred and eight names, «gza' dang rgyu skar rmi lam ngan», in Wylie in BDRC's snippet; the list of rites «gza' skar rmi lam ngan pa bzlog pa» in the Fifth Dalai Lama's Thob yig, MW1PD107937) that names bad planets, stars and dreams as three evils to be turned back, tying none to the others; (e) the time-of-night ties already in the pool (the midnight and dawn dreams), with one new witness (dds-midnight-dream-in-the-death-signs).

So the pool's finding stands, now on a wider net: the Tibetan texts that rule on dreams (the Four Tantras, Svapnohana, the Garga-based Klong rdol texts, Lgd, KCM, Rst) fix a dream's fruit by the image, by the part of the night and by the dreamer's state, and no text that BDRC's full text search reaches fixes it by the lunar day, the weekday, the month, the season or the day animal. The one date in a rule is KCM's tenth of the waxing or waning moon as the day for the rite that asks a dream (claim dream-manuals-time-rules), which is the day for the rite, not for the dream's verdict.

Limits: BDRC's search is exact-string over etexts only (no scans); a sentence that words the tie otherwise (a table of days, a verse by the day animal in a divination book without the word «རྨི་ལམ» in one clause) is missed; texts that are «search only» answer 403 to the etext API and could be read only by snippet; the Japanese 大雑書 tables that do tie dreams to days (claims ozassho-dream-not-told-days, ozassho-dream-table-which-prints) have no Tibetan counterpart found.
