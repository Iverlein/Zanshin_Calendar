# གཏད་དུ་ཚུད་པ in ch. 81's commentaries: within the aim of a harm sent, not a pledge

The gloss «གཏད་དུ་ཚུད་པ» (gtad du tshud pa) that the Drikung puts on the dreams of water, trees and the green meadow in ch. 81 (RD271, 2026-11-09; dictionaries by Steinert's collection, BDRC etexts, no scan opened): not "a pledge" but falling within the aim of a harm sent at one.

**The dictionaries.** གཏད (gtad): to hand over, entrust, set in a place; to aim, direct at (Rangjung Yeshe, Jim Valby, Ives Waldo); also a spell or curse (Rangjung Yeshe: "a curse that harms another by substances and mantras"; Dan Martin: evil spells; Tshig mdzod chen mo: mthu gtad, the power sent at another, and mnan gtad, the pressing-down spell). བཏད (btad), Khro ru's spelling, is the past stem of the same verb (Jim Valby, Hopkins's table of tenses), so Khro ru's «བཏད་དུ་ཚུད་པ» and the Drikung's «གཏད་དུ་ཚུད་པ» are one word. ཚུད་པ (tshud pa): to go into, to be put inside, to belong to a class (Rangjung Yeshe, Jim Valby, Ives Waldo; Tshig mdzod chen mo: to come into the ranks of the learned). No dictionary has the phrase.

**In use (BDRC).** The Drikung's own next gloss for the narrow doors and the pit is «མནན་པར་ཚུད་པ» (mnan par tshud pa, to fall under pressing-down), the spell's other half; the phrase «གཏད་དུ་ཚུད་པ» stands in a list of misfortunes in a collection of rites ascribed to the teacher Padma (Slob dpon Pad+ma; among a king lodging in the house, hated enemies and thieves) where the sense is a curse that has been laid on. Ten etexts have «ཆུ་དང་ཤིང་དང་ནེའུ་གསིང་རྣམས་ནི་གཏད་དུ་ཚུད་པའི་རྟགས» (the Gso rig nyer mkho gces bsdus, the Drikung, Karma Ratna's medical handbook, the Gso ba rig pa, the Drang srong zhal lung and others), all copies of the Four Tantras' commentary tradition, none with a gloss; one has «ནེའུ་གསིང་གི་གསེབ་ཏུ་ཚུད་ནས་དཀའ་ངལ་མཐོང་བ» (entering the midst of trees and meadow and seeing hardship) for it.

**The sense.** "A sign of having fallen into the aim of (the nāga's) sent harm", the water, the tree and the meadow being the nāga's own places, with the creatures next listed as the sign of the flesh-eaters and the narrow places as being pressed down. "Pledge" or "trust" of the question q846 is the dictionaries' first sense of གཏད but does not fit the sentence; the curse sense does and is common. Rank: moderate, from the dictionaries' two senses and the parallel གཏད and མནན; no commentary glosses it.
