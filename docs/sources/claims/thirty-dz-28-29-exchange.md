# The Moonbeams' readings 28 and 29 exchanged in every print found

The *Moonbeams* gives the twenty-eighth reading (small children's body, the eight-spoked wheel) the iron bird and monkey (enemy) and the twenty-ninth (women's power, the coiled swastika) the earth horse and sheep (friend), the reverse of WB's list in WB's order (claim thirty-dz-bases). Read again on the scan (I2533 img. 033, the first lines; the glosses «དགྲ» and «གྲོགས» small above «ལྕགས» and «ས») and the print is clear.

The same two lines stand in the other prints BDRC's full-text search finds (2026-10-25), in BDRC's own transliteration: «bcu gsum byis pa chung ngu'i lus / rtsis ni 'khor lo rtsibs brgyad dang / lcags (dgra) kyi bya sprel steng nas brtsi» and «bcu bzhi bud med dbang thang rtsis / byed na g.yung drung 'khyil ba dang / sa (grogs) yi rta lug steng nas brtsi». The thirteenth reading's lines: in KD's collection *Bod kyi rtsis rig kun 'dus chen mo* (MW28845, good OCR; KD vol. 3), in the *Yul mdo khams stod* collection (MW1PD152297), in Tshul khrims rgyal mtshan's collected works (MW1KG10375, there under the *Moonbeams*' own title) and in *Bzo rtsis zlos gar* (MW1PD95727). The fourteenth reading's lines: in the first three. The last three are search-only etexts, read by their snippets. Five printings with the scan, all with the exchange: it is in the text's whole transmission, not a slip of one print. They are copies of one text, so this is one witness repeated, not five.

So the exchange is the *Moonbeams*' own, or an early slip in its line. Nothing read decides which pair belongs to WB's twenty-eighth and twenty-ninth: WB's own order gives the twenty-eighth the Tibetan thirteenth (friend, horse and sheep) and the twenty-ninth the fourteenth (enemy, bird and monkey), as for every other point. Followed here only where WB is silent, the *Moonbeams* ranks below it.
