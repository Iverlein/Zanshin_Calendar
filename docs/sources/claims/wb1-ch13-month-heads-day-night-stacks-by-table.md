# The month heads' day and night stacks are the table of the day's length by house

RD200, 2026-11-24. The two stacks on the third line of each month head (day, then night) are the length of the day and of the night as hours over srang over a bare circle, and they are the figures of the table by house (wb-ch8-day-length-by-house), taken for the sun's sign of the month: 11th Sagittarius 26;30 and 33;30; 12th Capricorn 27;40 and 32;20; 1st Aquarius 28;50 and 31;10; 3rd Aries 31;10 and 28;50; 4th Taurus 32;20 and 27;40; 5th Gemini 33;30 and 26;30; 6th Cancer 32;20 and 27;40; 7th Leo 31;10 and 28;50. The stacks of the 11th, 12th, 1st, 3rd, 4th, 5th, 6th and 7th heads were each looked at on the reprint at 2x to 6x against this expectation and agree with it in tier layout and in the distinctive figures (the curled 7 of the night of the 4th and 6th and of the day of the 12th; the flagged 6 of the night of the 5th; the wedge 8 of the night of the 3rd and 7th and the day of the 1st; the 5 of the srang tier of the 1st, 3rd and 7th). The 9th and 10th heads (1996 print) give in the machine text 58 and 21, and 27 and 32: the upper tiers of 28;50 with 31;10 and of 27;40 with 32;20, so they agree too, but their stacks are not legible on the print. The 2nd and 8th heads, at the equinoxes, have no day and night stacks: day and night are equal (30 hours), and the 8th prints one stack after the autumn equinox, read 30 by the machine. What this corrects: RD201's day and night of the 3rd head (17/10/0 and 22/40/0) are 31/10/0 and 28/50/0; RD202's day of the 7th (37 over 10) is 31 over 10, the second upper figure being the thin hooked 1; RD202's day of the 11th (36 over 30) is 26 over 30, so the upper tiers of the 11th head make 26 and 33, and with the middle tiers (30 and 30 srang, one hour) they make 60: the question RD202 raised on this is answered. The stacks of line 2 (the animal's great and small), the dates after the breath-taking and the sgang, and the link's figure are not lengths and were not settled by this check.
