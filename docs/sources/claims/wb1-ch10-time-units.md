# The units of the tables' base and the six summarised meanings given to house pairs

The chapter's base (p. 70) is one set of time-units on which all the tables rest: the hour (*chu tshod*), the "hole" (*bug*), the arrow (*mda'*), the water-weight (*chu srang*), fire, breath, sea, the part (*cha shas*) and the "lord of men" (*mi bdag*), "one base for knowing the movement of lunar days, days, ages and planets". The sun's lunar-day enjoyment (*tshes longs*) is counted in these units on a lunar-day circle of 301; multiplied by each one's number it is called the sun's "halves"; and the planet's mean place, with the house of birth purified and the true-moving hour, is worked "as in the five-summaries" (*lnga bsdus*) as each table's root working (*rtsa sgrub*). The "six summarised meanings" (*bsdus don drug*) are completed by giving money and debt to the pairs of houses in pairs of the twelve houses (the passage names Aries and Virgo first, Pisces, Taurus, Leo, Aquarius, Scorpio, Sagittarius and Capricorn after; the pairing is not settled here, pp. 70–71); thirty days are given to Aries (or Leo, p. 71) and thirty-one to the other houses.
