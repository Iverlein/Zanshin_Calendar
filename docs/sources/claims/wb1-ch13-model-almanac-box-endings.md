# The model almanac's day boxes: the oval's element and object follow the date's place; the framed words follow nothing found

In the model almanac the two element-and-object endings of a day box depend only on the date's place in the five-fold cycle (joy, good, victory, empty, full), not on the month. The oval's last two words run earth smell, water taste, fire form, wind touch, space sound for the five places; the first line's run space sound, wind touch, fire taste, water form, earth smell for boxes 1-5 of p. 154 and boxes 11-15 of p. 166. This was read by eye on the reprint in the ovals of every box of p. 154 (1-15), p. 155 (16-30), p. 156 (1-25), p. 157 (1-10 and 26-30) and p. 166 (11-30), each box checked at 1.05x or more, with no exception found; the first line of boxes 16, 21 and 26 seems to end in earth smell, not space sound, and was not settled. What changes from box to box is the pair of letters in each line (stacked vowel signs, not copied), the stage line and the framed sky-door words. Of the framed words, each row of five boxes holds three or four, with none in the other one or two, in no pattern that the place in the row or the month predicts (75 boxes counted on pp. 155, 156, 157 and 166 in wb.1:p155d, p156d, p157d, p166d).

The same holds for the 135 boxes of pp. 158-164 (the 1st month's boxes 11-30, the 2nd month's 1-30, the 3rd's 1-30, the 4th's 1-30 and the 5th's 1-25, read by eye on whole-row crops at 1.2x, 2026-11-17): every oval ends in the five-place cycle with no exception, every numeral at a box's head reads, and the framed sky-door words number 0 in 39 boxes, 1 in 90 and 2 in 6 (wb.1:p158d to p164d); boxes with three or more were not met, and again no pattern by the box's place in its row or by the month shows. The numerals under the trigram letters stay unread: at 4x on boxes 1-5 of p. 164 they are hooked and curved strokes not told apart as digits.
