# The month heads of the 2nd to 6th months: link word, work and months' names by the fixed run

The second link of the twelve-link chain named in each month's head moves one link per month: the 12th month's head names 'du byed (formations, the second link), the 1st rnam shes (consciousness, the third), the 2nd ming gzugs (name and form, the fourth), the 3rd skye mched (the six sense-fields, the fifth), the 4th reg pa (touch, the sixth), the 5th tshor ba (feeling, the seventh) and the 6th sred pa (craving, the eighth). The word stands after "look at the full moon" in the head's fixed run, with a stacked figure after it (read only as a figure, the digits not settled), and just before "the sun arrives at <sign>". The work that succeeds is the next phrase: expelling (2nd, the word of the work not clear), summoning (3rd), empowering (4th), enriching (5th), pacifying "is good" (6th); the 12th and 1st named killing works. RD2's table of the heads (month-entries.md) reads the month's lord, the empty vase, and "five and nine" and all the 2nd to 6th heads agree with it: 2nd lord "bltas" vase north, five and nine north; 3rd vase east, five east and nine west, with "tell the results, mild and harsh" after the lord; 4th vase south, five and nine south; 5th vase west, five west, nine east; 6th vase north, five and nine north. The month names of the Kalacakra's first line run Phalguna (dbo, 2nd), Chaitra (nag, 3rd), Vaisakha (sa ga, 4th), Asadha (snron, 5th) and Sravana (chu stod, 6th), the Chinese season beginning (ra), middle ('bring) or end (tha) in turn from the 12th's "spring middle": 2nd summer beginning, 3rd summer middle, 4th summer end, 5th autumn beginning, 6th autumn middle.
