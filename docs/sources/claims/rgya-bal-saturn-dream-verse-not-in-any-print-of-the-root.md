# «Some of the Chinese and Nepalese say: by Saturn, bad company, and climbing hills are dreamt»: the lines are in no print of the root

The Mchog dga' (etext p. 198, printed p. 178) says after the Venus line: «འདིར་སྤེན་པ་མ་བྱུང་བ་ནི་རྩ་བའི་གཞུང་ལ་ཆད་པའམ་རྒྱུ་མཚན་གང་ཡོད་བརྟག་པར་བྱ་ཞིང་། རྒྱ་བལ་ཁ་ཅིག་གིས་སྤེན་པས་མི་ངན་རྣམས་དང་འགྲོགས། རི་དང་བྲག་ལ་འཛེག་པ་རྨི། ཞེས་འཆད་དོ།» («that Saturn does not appear here: look whether the root text is broken or what the reason is; and some of the Chinese and Nepalese say «by Saturn, bad people as company, and climbing hills and cliffs are dreamt»»). Checked: the dream verses of the Derge etext, the dpe bsdur ma etext and the Narthang, Peking and Gser bris ma scans (with their interlinear glosses, by the machine reading) have no Saturn, so the root is not broken in any print read; BDRC's exact-phrase search finds the two lines «སྤེན་པས་མི་ངན་རྣམས་དང་འགྲོགས» and «རི་དང་བྲག་ལ་འཛེག་པ་རྨི» only in the Kun 'dus chen mo etexts. «རྒྱ་བལ» (rgya bal, the Chinese and Nepalese) therefore names no print of this translation: it points to teachers or books of a Chinese and Nepalese reckoning that the commentary does not name. Who they are is not answered by these sources.
