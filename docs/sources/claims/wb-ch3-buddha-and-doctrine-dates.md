# The dated acts of the Buddha and of the doctrine in Tibet, WB pp. 22–28 (by the chain)

Of the dated acts of the doctrine's history on pp. 22–28 the first part gives the Buddha's life by Phug pa's count, reading each year as years passed up to the writing year: the entry into the womb in the iron-monkey year at 2648; the birth at 2647 (iron-monkey year, month Saga, seventh day, the mansion Rgyal; ten months in the womb, Lumbini); the going forth at 2619, after seeing old age, sickness, death and a monk; six years of austerities and the awakening in a male-horse year on the fifteenth of Saga (2613 for the first turning of the wheel at Vārāṇasī, on the fourth day of the upper water month); the defeat of the six teachers of the other schools in the wood-hare year Srin po at 2592; the Kālacakra taught in the iron-dragon year at Shri Dhanyakataka on the fifteenth of Saga in the midnight session (the year 2567 by the chain). The next: Zla bzang gathers the root tantra at 2566 and takes the enjoyment body at 2564; Padma Ka ra is born miraculously in the wood-monkey year at 2563; Jam dpal dbyangs is born at the five-peaked mountain at 2523; Klu sgrub at 2167; the Abridged Tantra taught at 1963; the first Tibetan king Tho tho ri gnyan btsan born at 1433; the first spread of the doctrine in Tibet 1354; the doctrine's practice-times begin at 1067; Srong btsan comes to the kingship at 1058 and Kong jo is invited at 1046; the Lha ldan 'phrul snang temple 1034; Khri srong born 957; Padma 'byung comes to Tibet 938; Khri srong passes 899; Glang dar seizes the kingdom 786 and is killed 785; the Wheel of Time is brought by Chi lu pa Ṇa chen and Dus zhabs pa at 720; Atisha born 705; the Kalacakra's arrival in Tibet and the first count of the rab byung 661–660.
