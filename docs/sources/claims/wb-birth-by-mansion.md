# Birth by the lunar mansion

Birth by the lunar mansion (WB vol. 2, pp. 380–382): for each of the twenty-eight mansions, by the editors' bracketed number, a verse gives lifespan, character and sometimes the cause of death or the next life. Examples: Dbyugpa, sixty to eighty years, handsome, devout, a doctor; Smindrug, long-lived, famous, brave, a girl is bad; Snarma, long-lived, victorious; the Hand (5), thirty-five or sixty-five, a robber's livelihood; the Great Horse (9), if he lives to nine he lives to fifty-five, bad for a boy; the Little Horse (10), a bad nature; Khra (11), a hundred years, death by poison; Phul (20), a hundred years or eighty-five; Byasbor (21), sixty, the best of friends, a king; Khrums-stod (24), thirty-five, a thief and killer; Khrums-smad (25), fifty or sixty, giving the dharma; Shesang (27), twenty-eight or ninety. Three lines at the foot of p. 381 (the Bre's last lines) were dropped by the machine reading and are supplied from the scan: «ཆོས་སྤྱོད་སྦྱིན་པ་དག་ལ་དགའ། །འཁོར་ལ་དུངས་ཤིང་ཀུན་ལ་དད། །ང་རྒྱལ་ཅུང་ཟད་ཆེ་ན་ཡང་། །ཚུལ་ཁྲིམས་ལྡན།» (the Bre-born is devout, loves giving, is faithful to all and, if a little proud, wins the higher realms by discipline).
