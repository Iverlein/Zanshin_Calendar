# Dpal sprul's calendar treatise carries the multiplier verse in the Sgang dril manual's words, credited to the Lord

The verse on the multiplied merit of the times that stands in the Sgang dril manual under Dīpaṃkara's name (skl:i210; for the claim of its Śākyaśrī verse see shakyashri-verse-on-the-festivals-and-eclipses) stands in Dpal sprul's calendar treatise (dpk.2:p51-52) in the same words, credited to "the Lord" (ཇོ་བོ, jo bo, Atiśa): a hundredfold on the full moon, new moon and eighth; a thousandfold at the moon's eclipse, ten thousandfold at the sun's; a hundred thousandfold on the four great festivals; the Nirvāṇa and Sil bu a hundred million-fold on them; the Root Tantra of Mañjuśrī ten million on the full moon, new moon and eighth, seven ten-millions at the moon's eclipse, a hundred thousand ten-millions at the sun's. BDRC's phrase search finds the two lines «ཉ་སྟོང་ཚེས་བརྒྱད་བརྒྱ་འགྱུར» and «ཟླ་འཛིན་སྟོང་དང་ཉི་འཛིན་ཁྲི» in four etexts (the Sgang dril manual, Dpal sprul's text, and two more: I2PD18782 and I5743, not opened).

- Days: the full moon, the new moon and the eighth (ཉ་སྟོང་ཚེས་བརྒྱད, nya stong tshes brgyad), the same three as WB's and the Sgang dril manual's list; no 10th, 25th, 29th.
- Dpal sprul's version differs from the Sgang dril manual's only in the name credited (the Lord for Dīpaṃkara) and in lacking «རིམ»; he adds no practice of his own beside the verse.
