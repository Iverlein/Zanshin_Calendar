# Carried off by water and swimming; a great rain; staying in water

Image group: water. Read by 4 texts: Four Tantras (for a sick dreamer), Lama Gongdü (LGD), Svapna-īkṣā (SVI), Klong rdol's verses as the article quotes them (KBS).
Offered to any dreamer; the Four Tantras' or WB ch. 25's rows join when the dreamer is ill.

RD274 (2026-11-09): the texts read this image two ways. LGD reads being carried off by water and swimming as a sign that looks bad and is good (release from illness and spirits); the Four Tantras read being carried off by water, for the sick, as a sign of the lord of death's power. The Four Tantras' row is for a sick dreamer and stands above LGD's on medicine; no text sums the two, and each row speaks for its text. Sinking in mud, the next image of the verse: dream-filth. Devoured by fish: dream-sick-devoured-by-fish.
