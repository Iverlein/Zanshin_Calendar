# The Śākyamuni rite printed before White Tārā: the posture, the repetition and the grades of answer

The frame the White Tārā text shares with the Śākyamuni rite printed just before it on p. 343: recitation first (500,000 or 700,000), offerings and shrine as before, the seven-limb prayer seven times, washing with mantra water before lying down, holding the matter in mind with one pointed intent; the head turned east or north and the sleeper lying like the Buddha in nirvāṇa; the Buddha prophesies and is seen; if it does not come on the first night the rite is repeated to the third and beyond; the answer is best as an appearance (nyams snang), then another's showing it, lowest a sign and hint. The Śākyamuni text prescribes no lunar day in the part read.
