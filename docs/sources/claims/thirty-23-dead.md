# The thirty: the twenty-third point, the red corpse, for the dead

The twenty-third of the thirty starting points (སྐོར་འགོ, skor 'go), from the mouth of the red corpse (མི་རོ་དམར་པོ, mi ro dmar po), is read for the dead. Its eight pebbles are laid as for every point (claims thirty-count and thirty-eight-fighters), each white or black, and read so:

| Pebble | Asks | White | Black | Remedy for black |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Whether the death's eye (ཤི་མིག, shi mig) looks at the living | It does not look, good | It looks at the living, bad | The rite for the dead (གཤིན་གཏོ, gshin gto), reconciling the earth lord (ས་བདག, sa bdag); where the trigram (སྤར་ཁ, spar kha) and mewa (སྨེ་བ, sme ba) meet on one seat, the rite of cutting the joined hands of the living and the dead (ལག་སྦྲེལ་བཅད, lag sbrel bcad) |
| 2 | Whether the living and the dead have joined hands | Not joined: good for those after | Where the living and the dead meet on the seat of trigram and mewa | Apply the means of cutting their joined hands |
| 3 | Whether the earth lord of the corpse has fallen to the demons | Not fallen | Fallen, bad | Strive at the rite of the earth lord's turning remedy rite (གཏོ, gto) |
| 4 | Whether the dead desires the food of the corpse | It does not: good for those after | Black or speckled: the dead desires food | Rely on white food; give food and the like in full |
| 5 | Whether the direction of the corpse's head is reversed | Not reversed; turned to the enemy's direction, good | The face shows the friend's direction; the head's direction is reversed | Kong tse's (ཀོང་ཙེ, kong tse) turning remedy rite and the like |
| 6 | Whether the dead is born in a good place | Born among gods, men and the like, good | Born in the three bad realms | Strive at the white side of virtue |
| 7 | Whether the great strike (འགྲམས, 'grams) comes on those after | It does not return on the household, good | The great strike falls on the household | The planets' rite, mending the strike and the like |
| 8 | Whether the star strike has fallen on the country | No star strike, good | The star strike strikes the whole country, above all the townspeople: many die at that time | Mending the star strike and other cures |

Whose year is reckoned: After a death, the dead's year and age (notes, p. 532).

Which of the eight fighting pebbles is which check is not stated; the order of p. 453 is the likely one (claim thirty-board-groups; question 259).
