# The stack under ལ before ཚོར་བའི་དུས in the glossary's line 2 leans ྟ on a 6× crop, not settled

I5609 img. 526 (7500×1620 px, printed p. 518), line 2, the word before «ཚོར་བའི་དུས», read by eye on crops of 2.4×, 3.8× and 6× (x 2640–2900, y 500–700). The word is a ལ with a stack under it and then ས. Under the ལ the stack is a flat horizontal bar joined at its right end to a stroke that runs down and to the right, with no closed loop; that is the shape of a subjoined ཏ (ྟ), not a hooked, looped ྷ. The reading leans «ལྟས་ཚོར་», moderate: the bilevel cut fills the counters, so a small loop of ྷ could have closed. It is also the reading BDRC's exact phrase search gives for both copies. The other copy (I00KG07616 img. 518) was fetched but not enlarged in this reading.
