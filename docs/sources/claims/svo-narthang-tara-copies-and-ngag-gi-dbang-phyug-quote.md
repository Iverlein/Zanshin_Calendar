# The Narthang etext repeats the Svapnohana under four Tara works; a Sakya rite cites the Deceiving Death by Vagisvarakirti's name

Two finds of BDRC's exact phrase search on 2026-11-21 (inv-g), both etext snippets or etext text, not read on a scan.

1. The Narthang Tengyur's own etext (MW2KG5015) carries the Svapnohana's verses a second time under four Tārā works: «ral pa gcig ma dmar mo'i byin brlabs kyi cho ga» (MW2KG5015_1400, Shar phyogs nag po pa), «mchog gi sgrol ma ral pa gcig pa'i man ngag gi sgrub thabs» (_1401), «sgrol ma lha lnga'i sgrub thabs» (_1402, Rtag pa'i rdo rje) and «pad+ma dra ba 'byung ba'i sgrub thabs» (_1417, Dpal ye shes). The hits for «ཐུན་བཞི་ཟླ་བ་ཕྱེད་བར་དུ» and «དེ་ཡིས་དེ་དུས་སྙིང་སྡུག་འཐོབ» in them read exactly as the Narthang etext of the work itself (_1416): «thun bzhi zla ba phyed bar du» and, for the vulture clause, «gang gi gnyid sad na // de yis de dus snying sdug 'thob» ("snying sdug" with ག་). They are the same print's text repeated by the etext's page-spanning (the tail of one work's pages sits under the next), so they are one witness, not four; they add no reading from another print and no «པར་དུ» at the fourth watch.

2. The Sakya master Ngag dbang kun dga' legs pa'i 'byung gnas rgyal mtshan dpal bzang po's foot-rite for White Tārā (in the Derge Tengyur etext's volume VE23703_1531 at about char. 718359, p. 455, and in Zhu chen Tshul khrims rin chen's collected works, MW00EGS1016703, search-only) cites the work by «slob dpon ngag gi dbang phyug grags pas mdzad pa'i 'chi ba bslu ba'i man ngag las» (the teacher Ngag gi dbang phyug grags pa's Deceiving Death, from which he quotes the verses «chos spyod dbang po thul ba ni»…). A Tibetan author thus gives the maker of the Deceiving Death as Vāgīśvarakīrti (ངག་གི་དབང་ཕྱུག་གྲགས་པ, ngag gi dbang phyug grags pa), as the Derge etext's author field does; the quotation is not from the dream chapter and says nothing on the remedy's «ངག་དབང་».
