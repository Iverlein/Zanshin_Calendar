# The slash in the transcription of the 万代大雑書古今大成 table of undreamt days is the editor's sign for a line break in a two-line note

The transcription of the 万代大雑書古今大成 (Ito Takayuki, 翻刻『万代大雑書古今大成』（十五）, メディア・コミュニケーション研究 78, Hokkaido University, 2025-03-31, hdl.handle.net/2115/94454; the print of the Tenpō years, revised in the Meiji) prints the table 夢を見て語らざる日 (item ゆ六) with the slash inside brackets, and its 凡例 (editorial notes) states the sign: «割注は〈 〉で示した。割注中の改行箇所は／で示した» ("a warigaki, a note in two lines of small type, is shown in brackets; a line break inside a warigaki is shown by /"). So the slash of mdz:p11 is the transcriber's sign for the break between the two lines of a small double-line note, not a mark of the print; the print sets each month's cell as a two-line note and its only legend is the closing line 右の日の夢ハ善悪ともに語らず ("of the days above, the dreams, good or bad, are not told"). The same sign is used throughout the paper (for instance 【ミづのえ／ミづのと】 for a pair of stems in the 酒造る item). What the two lines of a cell are for (two groups of day branches) is not said by the text or the 凡例. This answers the question of a legend for the slash for this print: there is none in it, and the slash is the editor's. The 延宝2 大ざつしよ and the other prints are not looked at (the 星槎ラボ copy was not found by a web search).
