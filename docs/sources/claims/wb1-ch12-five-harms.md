# The five harms of the planets on the name mansion (WB pp. 97–98)

WB names five harms (*gnod pa lnga*) that the eight bodies (the sun, moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn and Rāhu) work on the mansion of a person's given name (pp. 97–98): the bite (*sol ba*), the embrace (*'khyud pa*), the smoke (*du ba*), the face set against (*gdong tshugs*) and the seen place (*mthong sa*); the mansion is found by the first letter of the name, in the table of the mansions' syllables (p. 97, quoted from O10e in day-letters.md). Fierce planets look to the right, gentle ones to the left; retrograde ones reverse them. By the text, a bite brings death, an embrace illness, smoke a purpose unfulfilled and the face set a terror; the sun's seen place wastes goods. Mars's and Rāhu's face set, Saturn's bite and embrace, and the sun's seen place are the worst; four fierce planets at once on the name mansion bring death or violent illness, two the loss of goods and kin, one empty hunger, the mixed epidemic and quarrel; a merit planet's smoke, bite and embrace do no harm, and no harm falls on a planet's own mansions (p. 98).
