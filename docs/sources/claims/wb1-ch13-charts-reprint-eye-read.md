# Ch. 13 charts pp. 136-147: what the reprint gives by eye beyond the etext

On p. 136's upper earth-lord chart the Gser thang reprint's top row can be read in part by eye on crops of 1.5x to 2x, and it shows that the 1996 etext misreads two names there: srid gshed (not gsher) and bla mkhyen (not bya mkhyen), the second being the label that RD195's first pass named as read. The cells name the earth lords of ch. 14 and the sme ba, but the chart's own key to what each cell means was not found on the page; the narrow inner ring cells and the captions of the woodcut stay unread. The strip charts of pp. 142-147 have wider cells with fewer entries and are the better ones to read next; on p. 142 only a few cells of each chart are filled, the rest empty, and the heading row of six cells above the first chart carries a name and one to three numerals each, which were not settled.
