# KD's digest: the ten sky doors in WB's order with a consequence each, and the thebs bzlog

The digest's fourteenth part (KD vol. 3, p. 741) gives the sky doors (གནམ་སྒོ) in WB's list and order, guest's, trade's, small child's, war's, kin's, fort's, bride's, grave's, funeral feast's (ཤིད) and the general door, and for each what is spoiled by doing its own work on the door's day: a guest's door ruins the host's house; the trade door breaks the profit; the small child's door brings obstacles to a birth or the first leading out; the war door a defeat and the loss repeated nine-fold; the kin door loss on a marriage or a feast; the fort door speedy loosening (or a flux); the bride door a bleeding disease; the grave door the ruin of the burying family in the end; the feast door a death at once; and the general door ruin from any work, above all a general counsel before the door. The list is the same as WB's and KP's (kp-sky-doors-same-pairs-as-wb.md); the digest adds a one-line consequence to each. The thirteenth part (thebs bzlog) adds that when the lineage strikes (སྡེ་བརྒྱུད་ཐེབས) the ransom and hail rites, hurled spells, burning and war are good, and avoided at the time of turning back; when the klu strike, rain, klu rites and sending to the klu are good, and avoided at the klu's turning back. Etext only.
