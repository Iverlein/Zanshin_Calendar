# 牛黃, 廨 欄 倉, 不將 in the Yongle day lines; the books of 雷英發 and 呂茂明 and their dates

In the Yongle dadian's day lines, 牛黃 is followed by 廨, 欄 or 倉囷 (the lines print 解 on 丙午 and 倉國 on 癸丑 as the transcription gives them) and then 不將. The three places are those of the row 牛黃殺 (avoid carrying out dung) in the Joseon 選擇要略's table of month spirits, which puts the spirit in a granary (倉), an office (廨) and a pen (欄) in the months of the hare, the goat and the boar; the Yongle day lines place it in the office on 辰 巳 午 未 days, the pen on 申 酉 戌 亥 days and the granary on 子 丑 寅 卯 days, the same three places in the same order. A 牛黃經 exists in the lore of oxen and their pens (玉匣記: lucky days for buying an ox and building a pen, and four lucky days of the Ox-yellow Scripture, 戊辰 戊午 己未 辛酉). So the words name where the ox-yellow spirit stands for the day, a thing of cattle pens and granaries, and are not part of the dream's reading. 不將 is the marriage-day term for stem and branch in agreement (陰陽不將); but the Yongle lines print it on every one of the eighteen days, and 選擇要略 says 壬子 and 癸丑 have no 不將, so the Yongle line's 不將 is another thing or another system; unsettled. The books: 雷英發 is the author of the Yuan woodblock 新編萬曆錦囊星家總括 (Commons, from the National Central Library, 1280-1368), so 萬曆 in his book titles is an almanac word and not the Wanli reign; the 萬曆錦囊拾遺備用 of the Yongle dadian is by its name of that family, not shown to be the same text. 呂茂明's 陰陽備用選擇成書 has no date or note anywhere found; a Song book 陰陽備用 in twelve rolls by 胡舜申 is catalogued by 陳振孫, a different book; the Yongle dadian is the terminus ante quem for 呂茂明's. Doubts: the Wikisource text is not checked against its images here; the readings of the three place words are the transcription's.
