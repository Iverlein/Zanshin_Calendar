# The bad dream signs of LGD and what they foretell

The bad dream signs of LGD (lgd:p968-969) are first the reverse of the good ones (darkness, dry forests, grain struck by hail, sun and moon setting or eclipsed, boat and bridge broken, running downhill), then imprisonment, a dark pit, wasting, hair cut or burnt, broken weapons, a landslide or chasm, bad clothes and blood, filth, being eaten by worms, bad food, chase by a beast, losing the road, the dead leading one off, a red whirlwind, a net, weapons piercing, lepers and beggars asking food, creditors, being led off by the disliked, a black donkey or a wild horse, goods of gossip, wandering alone. They foretell obstacles to life, attainments failing, debts of karma, sorcery, illness, gossip, wishes unmet, lawsuits, great obscuration, a bad rebirth; some are tricks of spirits, the demon of obstruction, protectors and their messengers, and are uncertain and to be examined. The red whirlwind chasing appears twice in the list on the scan.
