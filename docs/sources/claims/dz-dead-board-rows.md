# The Moonbeams' board of the living out of the dead: four rows by kin, five columns by age and sex

The *Moonbeams* lays its own board for taking the living out of the dead (dz:i061-063): on the life force of the month of death the dead's four aspects, each as own, mother, son, enemy and friend, in rows; then pairs exchanged between the aspects, the sons left in place. The four rows from the top are the places of the paternal kin (fathers, uncles, elder brothers), of the mother's kin and maternal uncles, of sons-in-law, grandchildren and widows, and of the *do bdag*; the five columns, own to friend, are the old, men in their prime, women in their prime, sons and grandsons, and servants. A white taken from three black or a black from three white "repeats" on the one whose year is of the three harmonious.

Inferred, not stated: in such a board the upper pebbles are the paternal line and the lower the women and in-laws, as WB's general verdict of the la pebbles reads the upper empty as the male line's decline and the lower as the female line's (wb.2:p52-54); the *Moonbeams*' board is not WB's twelve la places, and no manual read names WB's upper, lower and middle places.
