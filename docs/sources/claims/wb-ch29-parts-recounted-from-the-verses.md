# WB ch. 29's parts counted from their verses: nine close, three do not, none holds the 57

Counted from the verses of WB vol. 2, pp. 44–51 (the 1996 scan with Yigdzin-1 and MITRA agreeing in every figure used, the Gser thang reprint by eye at the key lines, the Taikhang print with Yigdzin-1, which has every figure of the twelfth, the fourteenth and the tenth as the 1996 print has them). The parts whose own pieces add to their stated count: the first (87, 338), the fourth's 16 (11 roots and 5 key points), the fifth (63 = 12 + 51, 155 = 7 + 148), the seventh (135 identifying, the blades' thirteen with the four they are gathered into counting seventeen), the eighth (180), the ninth (177), the eleventh (94 in all, the leftover pebble's 1 and 9 inside it), the thirteenth (202 identifying, 25 results = 11 roots + 13 verdicts + 1 deciding pebble, which Mipham's note p. 517 spells out), the fifteenth (54). The three that do not: the tenth's identifying pebbles, which add to 1,950 on the verses (the heaven-earth-between-mountain 72 counted, the verdict's 11 not; 1,961 with it) against 1,952; the twelfth, whose ten blocks add to 3,176 against the stated 3,186; the fourteenth, whose pieces add to 143 result and 2,768 identifying (2,774 by the earth lords' pieces as the verse lists them) against 136 and 2,784. In every case the pieces differ from the stated count by 16 or less, and by 10 or less in the whole of each part; none of them can hold a gap of 57. In the twelfth the day's 86 is the block's whole count (22 + 29 + 8 + 13 + 8 = 80 identifying and 6 result), Rahu's 133 likewise (3 + 3 + 4 + 30 + 16 + 23 + 6 + 3 + 5 + 9 + 10 + 9 = 121 and 12 result, the same on the 1996 and Taikhang prints), the year's 299 and the month's 229 are stated as block totals in the verse itself; Mipham's note (p. 513) reads the day's 86 and the hour's 74 as identifying with the deciding pebbles beside them. The earlier recount that the twelfth's identifying close exactly at 3,035 (RD95) holds only if the reckoner's la-stone's five pebbles are left out; the verse's own «བསྒྲིལ་བས་རྡེའུ་བཞི་བཅུ» (rolled together forty) is 5 + 26 + 6 identifying and 3 result, so with the five the identifying come to 3,040 on Mipham's reading and 3,018 on the block-total reading. The hour's block does not close on either (17 + 43 + 6 + 13 = 79 against 70 left of 74; «ཞེ་གསུམ», forty-three, read by eye on the reprint, the same words in the fourteenth). So the 57 pebbles of the opening's totals stay outside the sixteen parts' pieces: they are in the opening line's own total, not in any one part.
