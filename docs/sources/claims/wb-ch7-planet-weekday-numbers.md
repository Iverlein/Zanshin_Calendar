# The five planets' weekday figures in chapter seven

Chapter seven names the five planets by the weekday figures that the verse sets beside them: Mars (*dmar*) 3, Mercury (*lhag*) 4, Jupiter (*phur*) 5 and Venus (*sangs*) 6; Saturn (*spen pa*) follows, whose figure the machine did not give; on the page image at ordinary size it looked like "0", which would fit the week counted from the sun as 1 (the seventh day being 0), but it was not enlarged. The figures 3, 4, 5, 6 are the page's as read by the machine and seen at ordinary size on the scan, not checked at higher magnification.
