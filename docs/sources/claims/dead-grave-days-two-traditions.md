# The grave days in two traditions: the last months' 26th (WB's first, LT) or 16th (WB's second, Mipham, UT)

WB gives the grave-day strike (དུར་གྱི་འགྲམ, dur gyi 'gram, vol. 2, p. 88) in two accounts that set the 16th and the 26th differently, and the manuals divide between them. **Account 1** (WB's first, LT p. 59): the 6th of the first month of each season, the 16th of the middle months and the 26th of the last months are the grave days. **Account 2** (WB's "another account"): the sky grave of *khen* has the 6th of the first months, the earth grave of *khon* the 26th of the middle months, the middle grave of *zon* the 16th and the mountain grave of *gin* the 16th of the last months. Mipham's note (p. 506) reads the four graves as the first four's 6th (sky), the middle four's 16th (middle) and "the last's 16th" (the mountain, *tha chung*), and leaves the rest to the *Fruit Explanation* (འབྲས་བཤད, 'bras bshad): so the notes go with account 2 on the last months' 16th and on the middle grave's 16th. UT (p. 317) lists the grave executioners as the 6th of the first four, the 26th of the middle four and the 16th of the last, which is account 2's pair; LT is for account 1. Two living traditions, then, in the manuals as in WB; WB records both, and the notes follow the second for the last months. Which a user's almanac should follow is a choice of the owner, not a reading.
