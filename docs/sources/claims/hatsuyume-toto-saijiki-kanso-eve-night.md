# The 東都歳事記 second-day entry has no 初夢; the 栞草 puts the 初夢 on New Year's Eve, citing 紀事

The 東都歳事記 (1838, Ochanomizu copy, image 18) has an entry for the second day of the first month and it holds no 初夢 (mdz:i70618); the 歳時記栞草 (1851) gives the 初夢 as the dream from the night of New Year's Eve to the dawn of New Year's Day and cites it to a work labelled 紀事 (mdz:i7333115), which the page does not identify. So of the two Edo year-books looked at, neither puts the 初夢 on the night of 2 January.
