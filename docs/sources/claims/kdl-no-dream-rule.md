# The Klong rdol abridgement of Garga holds no dream and no watch of the night

Klong rdol bla ma's abridgement of Garga (kdl:p235-241) has no dream in it: its three parts are the rainbow, the great sound and various omens of animals, birds, trees, births and fluids, and its colophon-verse says only that whoever knows the omens' joys and sorrows should strive in good and turn from sin. The text gives no watch of the night (ཐུན, thun), no lunar day and no weekday for any omen; the only times are the parts of a day (sunrise, early morning, midday, sunset, dawn, midnight, waking) for a sound, and the night part and dusk for a rainbow (claims kdl-sound-by-part-of-day, kdl-rainbow-time). What the article KBS says of Klong rdol's dream method (claim kbs-three-thirds-of-the-night: the night in three thirds, the dawn third read) is therefore from another work of his, a text on examining the omens of dreams that is not this one and whose own text was not located.
