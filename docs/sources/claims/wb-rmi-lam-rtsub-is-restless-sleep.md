# WB ch. 25 and 27: «རྨི་ལམ་རྩུབ» and «རྨི་ལམ་ཟ་ཟི་མང་བ» are the sleep of the sick, not an image of ch. 30

In WB ch. 25 and ch. 27 the words «རྨི་ལམ་རྩུབ» (rough dreams, rmi lam rtsub) and «རྨི་ལམ་ཟ་ཟི་མང་བ» (many confused dreams, rmi lam za zi mang ba) name the sleep of the sick, not an image from the list of bad dream images of ch. 30 p. 144.

What the three places show, on the Gser thang reprint, the Zhol print and its Taikhang reproduction (the 1996 scan has no p. 428: its img. 438 is already p. 430, so p. 428 is read on the other three prints only):

- The word is «རྨི་ལམ» (dream, the sleep-dream), with an adjective or a count as predicate: «རྩུབ» (rough) on the ox day (vol. 1 p. 428) and under the sri of arrow and knife (vol. 2 p. 21), «མང་བ» (many) with «ཟ་ཟི» (confused) on the mewa five yellow (vol. 1 p. 431). No verb of dreaming or seeing, and no noun for what is dreamt, stands in any of the three lines.
- Each sits in a row of signs of an illness or of a class of years (liver and spleen unwell; vomiting blood, unease at evening; the sri of arrow and knife present), beside bodily and mental signs, never among images.
- Ch. 30 p. 144 does not use «རྨི་ལམ» at all: its list is headed «རྨི་ལྟས» (dream omens, rmi ltas; four times) and names each image by what is seen or done, with the verb «རྨི» once («ནག་པོ་རྨི», dreaming of black). The two vocabularies do not meet: WB's ch. 25 and 27 lines say that the sleep is bad, ch. 30 says what bad is seen.

So no image can be drawn from the three lines: a sick person's rough or many confused dreams are a sign of the illness (or of the class of years), and the bad images of ch. 30 are a different test, made of the asker before the reckoning. Mipham's notes (vol. 2, pp. 474-541) gloss none of the three places (the machine reading of all 64 images holds «རྨི་ལམ» only at p. 535, the preliminaries of ch. 30; «ཟ་ཟི» nowhere); his Chengdu etext is search-only in the pool and was not opened.

Open: what «ཟ་ཟི» means is not glossed in WB; «confused» is taken from the Four Tantras' parallel rows (claims st-dream-unripe-fever, st-dream-unripe-pestilence).
