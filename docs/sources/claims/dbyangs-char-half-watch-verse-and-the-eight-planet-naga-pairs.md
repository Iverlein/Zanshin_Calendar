# The Dbyangs 'char's half-watch verse in the 'Bri lugs bang mdzod and the eight planet-nāga pairs in the later books (RD326)

The Dbyangs 'char's half-watch verse is quoted in Tibetan in the first work of volume 39 of the 'Bri lugs bang mdzod (the Rnam dpyod blo gros spyan stong bkra ba, section 16, printed p. 17), as «from the text of the Dbyangs 'char (དབྱངས་འཆར་གཞུང, dbyangs 'char gzhung): the day-and-night's half-watches (ཐུན་ཕྱེད, thun phyed) are cut in eight; with the day's planet leading, in each half-watch a planet and a nāga (ཀླུ, klu) rise together». The pairs, in the order of the verse: Sun with Mtha' yas, Moon with Nor rgyas, Mars with 'Jog po, Mercury with Stobs kyi rgyu, Jupiter with Padma, Venus with Padma che, Saturn with Dung skyong, Rāhu with Rig ldan. Three later books give the same eight pairs in the same order (the Gces bsdus 'chi med nor bu'i phreng ba p152, the Gso rig gces btus rin chen phreng ba pp. 113–114, WB's verse at vol. 2 p. 403 as quoted in svo:p271s), and the search snippets of several ritual and tantra texts (a nāga offering, a Vajrabhairava sādhana, the Mkha' 'gro rgya mtsho commentary and others; snippets only, not opened) give the same eight names as a list of nāgas in that order. The verse itself says neither that the eight are equal nor how they are counted; the later books do (see the claim on the count). No text of the Dbyangs 'char itself that holds the verse was found: BDRC's exact-phrase searches of its words (2026-11-10) hit only WB's lines, the 'Bri lugs work, the Yul mdo khams stod anthology (vol. 10, search only) and the later books.
