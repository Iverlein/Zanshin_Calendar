# The five texts the Sngags rim chen mo cites for the dawn dream, found in the Kanjur and Tengyur (RD373)

The five texts the Sngags rim chen mo names at snr:p367-371 for the dawn dream and its remedies, and the Kanjur or Tengyur text each is, found by exact-phrase searches of the lines Tsong kha pa quotes (one run of six searches, then the volumes fetched; RD373, 2026-11-10):

| Name in the Sngags rim chen mo | The text | Where read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the Common Tantra (སྤྱི་རྒྱུད, spyi rgyud) | Toh 806, Sarvamaṇḍalasāmānyavidhiguhyatantra, "the secret tantra of the general rite of all mandalas", Derge Kanjur | etext VE4CZ5369_I1KG9222, pp. 295-297 (sct:p295-297) |
| the Garland (ཕྲེང་བ, phreng ba) | the Vajrāvalī, Abhayākaragupta's "rite of the mandala called the Vajra Garland", Peking Bstan 'gyur (not found as a Derge etext) | etext VEIE0OPIA3E40644_I1PD95884, pp. 229-231 (vjv:p230-231, an OCR text) |
| Mar me mdzad bzang po (མར་མེ་མཛད་བཟང་པོ) | Dīpaṃkarabhadra's rite of the Guhyasamāja mandala in 450 verses, Derge Bstan 'gyur | etext VE23703_1358, pp. 155-156 (mmb:p155-156) |
| Klu byang (ཀླུ་བྱང) | Klu'i byang chub's (Nāgabodhi's) rite of the Guhyasamāja mandala in twenty parts, Derge Bstan 'gyur | etext VE23703_1351, p. 274 (nbv:p273-274) |
| Dārikapa (དཱ་རིག་པ) | Dārika's Cakrasaṃvara mandala rite Entering Reality (Tattvāvatāra), Derge Bstan 'gyur | etext VE23703_1337, pp. 418-419 (trt:p418-419) |

Each text was found by a line of its own that Tsong kha pa quotes or follows nearly word for word, not by the name alone: the Common Tantra by the two lines on kusha and the east; the Garland by the sentence "the Great Vehicle itself greatly arising" and the order of the rite, which the print gives as Tsong kha pa gives it; the Dārika text by the whole verse of the teacher's seven acts; the other two by their colophons, which name the authors, and by what each prescribes for the dream. The identification of the Garland and of Klu byang rests on these matches and on the contents page and colophon; it was not confirmed by a commentary (the commentaries on the Sngags rim chen mo that BDRC lists were search-only). The Derge numbers (Toh) of the four Tengyur texts were not read in the fetched etexts.
