# Led by a throng of the dead; dancing with the dead; driven by beings of the lower worlds

Image group: body and mind. Read by 5 texts: Four Tantras (for a sick dreamer), Lama Gongdü (LGD), Svapna-īkṣā (SVI), ACIP method (RST), Klong rdol's verses as the article quotes them (KBS).
Offered to any dreamer; the Four Tantras' or WB ch. 25's rows join when the dreamer is ill.

RD274 (2026-11-09) after RD268 and RD271: Byang pa's etext reads water (ཆུ་) for beer and the fiends (སྲིན་པོ) for the dead in these lines; every other witness found has beer and the dead, as the Drathang does (gt-water-or-beer-fiends-or-the-dead). The weight of witnesses makes Byang pa's the less likely reading of the Four Tantras; the picker's row keeps the Drathang's. LGD's throng of the dead and RST's and KBS's drinking and dancing with the dead (among the dreams of death) agree with the Four Tantras' verdict; the red garland: dream-red-clothes-flowers; the beer: dream-beer-wine-blood-drunk.
