# The numbered list of ch. 34's works and the sixty-five examinations

WB's chapter 34 opens (vol. 2, p. 378) with a numbered list of sixty-one works and four small ones, then says that sixty-five earth examinations (ས་དཔྱད, sa dpyad) are shown. Read on the Gser thang reprint, the numbers run in order: 1 birth, 2 naming, 3 weaving cloth, 4 putting on, 5 gods' worship, 6 building forts and towns, 7 taking a household, 8 the hearth, 9 breaking the ground (and taming an elephant), 10 spectacles, 11 feasts, 12 setting out, 13 trade, 14 goods and wealth, 15 planting, 16 gathering grain, 17 hanging the «སྦ་ཕྱར», 18 a treasury, 19 raising a banner, 20 hunting, 21 prisoners, 22 ponds and wells, 23 sowing, 24 the «སྐྱས», 25 horse racing, 26 healing, 27 sounding, 28 taming horses, 29 lending, 30 dogs, 31 writing and arithmetic, 32 medicine, 33 lancet and cautery, 34 bathing, 35 washing hair and its divination, 36 entering the king's place, 37 weapons, 38 attendants, 39 taking a bride, 40 ornaments, 41 the dead's work, 42 raising supports of the three secrets, 43 examining sickness, 44 turning back, 45 burnt offering, 46 pressing down the sri, 47 ordination and teaching, 48 consecration, 49 «ཤྭ་རགས», 50 crafts, 51 war, 52 poetry, 53 ransom and fort, 54 sorcery, 55 rain, 56 a death, 57 councils, 58 the king's works, 59 works of the living, 60 auspiciousness, 61 life and wealth, then the four small ones: power (62), fierce (63), pacifying (64), enriching (65). This agrees with the inventory of white-beryl-ch34.md; the machine reading's doubled «༢༢», «༨༧», «༢༠༡», «༤༢» and «༥༥» are digit slips set right by the order.
