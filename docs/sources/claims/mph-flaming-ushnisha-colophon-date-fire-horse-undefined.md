# Mipham's Flaming Ushnisha colophon names the fire-horse year of the fifteenth rab byung, 1906, and no month or day

The colophon of Mipham's Flaming Uṣṇīṣa sādhana for turning back bad dreams reads, on the scan, «རབ་ཚེས་མེ་རྟ་ཟླ་ཚེས་ལ་མི་ཕམ་པས་བྲིས་པ་དགེ»: "written by Mi pham on the «rab tshes», fire-horse, «zla tshes»". «རབ་ཚེས» is the name of the fifteenth rab byung and «ཟླ་ཚེས» the short form of month-number and date-number (Tshig mdzod chen mo, Tshig gter rgya mtsho; tmz:box8), so the phrase is "the fire-horse [year] of the fifteenth rab byung, month and date" and the engine puts that year in 1906 (rabjung 15/40; the fire-horse of 1846 is in the fourteenth). No month and no day is written. The date is of the writing; the colophon names no night or hour for a bad dream (question q1138).
