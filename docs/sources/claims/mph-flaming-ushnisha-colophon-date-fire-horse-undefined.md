# Mipham's Flaming Ushnisha colophon gives fire-horse between two words and no number

The colophon of Mipham's Flaming Uṣṇīṣa sādhana for turning back bad dreams reads, on the scan, «རབ་ཚེས་མེ་རྟ་ཟླ་ཚེས་ལ་མི་ཕམ་པས་བྲིས་པ་དགེ»: "written by Mi pham on the «rab tshes», fire-horse, «zla tshes»". The two undefined words stand on either side of fire-horse; no year number, no month and no day number is written. A fire-horse year fell in his life in 1846 and 1906, and fire-horse is a day name in the sixty-day cycle, so the phrase does not date the work to a year without another source. It also names no night or hour for a bad dream; the date belongs to the writing.
