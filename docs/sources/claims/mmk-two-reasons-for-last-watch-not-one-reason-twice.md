# The Sakya reason (no dream comes) and Tsong kha pa's (not certain) are two reasons; neither uses the humours (RD377)

None of the explanations ties its reason to the phlegm, bile and wind of the root tantra's verse. The Sakya reason (rsk:p459-461) says of the second and third watches that no dream comes at all, while the root tantra describes a long list of dreams of bile for the second watch and of wind for the third, the signs of each (mmk.1:p399-400, mmk.1:p400-401), so the manual's «no dream comes» reads the verse's «not true» as «not dreamed»: this is the manual's reading, and the tantra's own page says only that the dreams of the first three arise from the humours and the fourth «comes true» (mmk.1:p396-397). Tsong kha pa's «not certain» keeps the dreams and says only that they are not sure to be true; the Drukpa and Phur pa manuals use that wording (dkb:p163-164, bpp:p236-237). The two reasons are therefore not one reason said twice: one says the first three watches give no usable dream, the other that what they give is uncertain.
