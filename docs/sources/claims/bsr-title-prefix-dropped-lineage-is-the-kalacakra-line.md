# Bu ston's Examination of Dreams: the title's dropped prefix and a lineage of the Kalacakra masters

On the scan the 2008 print's title line reads «རྨི་ལམ་རྟག་པ» at the resolution available, where the Delhi and Zhol par khang prints read «རྨི་ལམ་བརྟག་པ», so the title is "examination of dreams" with a dropped prefix letter; BDRC's title follows the other prints. The lineage given after the rite, Vikhyātadeva, Śākyaśrī, Dānaśrī, the teacher Jam gsar, the lama Rga lo, the adept Ze dmar ba Sangs rgyas ye shes, Bu ston, is spelt the same in all three prints for «འཇམ་གསར» and «རྒ་ལོ». BDRC's search places Ze dmar ba (Mnga' ris/Gyag sde Ze dmar ba Sangs rgyas ye shes) between the Gyag sde lo tsā ba Bsod nams bzang po's line and Bu ston in Zhu chen's list of teachers and as a pupil of Bro lung pa; Vikhyātadeva and Śākyaśrī stand at the head of that list; the Sa skya bka' 'bum has the teachers Jam gsar and Rga in a Kālacakra lineage, the Padminī commentary from the teacher Rga. So the lineage is the Kālacakra's: Jam gsar and Rga are masters of that line, not of a dream rite proper; the rite's attribution to this line rests on the colophon alone. No source found names the rite's tantra.
