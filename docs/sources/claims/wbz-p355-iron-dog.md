# The Lhasa blocks carve the iron dog in p. 355's fourth pair

The fourth pair of the best years of the three in concord (WB vol. 1, p. 355, l. 24), «ཕོ་ནི་ལྕགས་ཁྱི་མོ་ཆུ་སྤྲེལ», has the man's year as the iron dog (ལྕགས་ཁྱི, lcags khyi) on both Lhasa blocks, the Zhol print and the Taikhang reproduction: the letter has ཁ's hooked head, unlike the square བ of the wood rat in the first pair. So the iron dog is what WB prints; whether the list's pattern of pairs within one triad means an iron rat is not settled by the blocks, which do not read it.
