# Ch. 27: whose aspect is cherished for whom

At the close of ch. 27's reckonings in four (WB vol. 2, p. 25): for the king, the chief, mantrins, monks and the learned, the luck (ཀླུང་རྟ, klung rta) is the cherished aspect; for men the life force (སྲོག, srog); for children the body (ལུས, lus); for women the power (དབང་ཐང, dbang thang). The chapter's last reckonings, of the four cherished pebbles and the obstacle reckoning, come to seventy-five pebbles.
