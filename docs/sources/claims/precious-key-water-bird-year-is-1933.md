# The Precious Key's water bird year is 1933, by Prajñāmaṇi's dates

For question 659 (and q526). The colophon's year, the water bird (དཔལ་གནོད་ཆུ་བྱ, dpal gnod chu bya), falls in 1873 (fifteenth cycle) and 1933 (sixteenth); the cycle's number was not read on the scan (pk:i383-385). The colophon says the text was gathered when the presence of Prajñāmaṇi taught the Mentsikhang's pupils, and BDRC's person record gives Prajñāmaṇi as another name of Khyenrab Norbu, born 1883 (claim khyenrab-norbu-is-prajnamani). A teacher born in 1883 cannot have taught in 1873, so the year is 1933, the sixteenth cycle's water bird year, and not 1873. The 1987 print, carved anew from old blocks, rules out the next water bird year, 1993. The step rests on the identification of Prajñāmaṇi, which is by the name and not by a line of the colophon; the cycle's number itself stays unread. If the head teacher of the colophon's «འམ» is a second man, he is not dated by this, but the pupil's gathering was still at the presence of Prajñāmaṇi.
