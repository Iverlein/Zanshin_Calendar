# The Svapnohana: eating human flesh in a dream, by the part eaten

The *Svapnohana* measures eating human flesh in a dream, cooked or raw: the foot eaten, five hundred; the hand, a thousand; the head, a hundred thousand or a kingdom (p. 269). The text does not say of what the five hundred and the thousand are counted (goods, coins, followers); no unit is named.
