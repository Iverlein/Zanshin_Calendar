# The thirty: the Moonbeams joins each reading to its pair

The *Moonbeams* (DZ, ch. 2, img. 029–034) gives each of WB's thirty readings its pair of years. Matched by emblem and kind of person to WB's readings (vol. 1, pp. 454–471) and by relation to WB's pair list (p. 453):

| DZ | Kind of person, emblem | WB reading | DZ's pair (wood example, gloss) | WB's pair of the same place |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chinese 1–7 | king … women | 1–7 | wood rat-ox (own) … fire rat-ox (son) | Chinese 1–7 |
| Chinese 8 | house and household, garuḍa head on the roof | 15 | water dragon-snake (mother) | Chinese 15 |
| Chinese 9 | the ground | 16 | wood horse-sheep (own) | Tibetan 1 |
| Chinese 10 | armour, the jewel | 17 | fire monkey-bird (son) | Tibetan 2 |
| Chinese 11 | a child's tree, tree and spring | 18 | earth dog-pig (friend) | Tibetan 3 |
| Chinese 12 | the helping god, the owl | 19 | iron rat-ox (enemy) | Tibetan 4 |
| Chinese 13 | the harming demon, the raven | 20 | water tiger-hare (mother) | Tibetan 5 |
| Chinese 14 | the old, the deer | 21 | wood dragon-snake (own) | Tibetan 6 |
| Chinese 15 | the sick lying askew | 22 | fire horse-sheep (son) | Tibetan 7 |
| Tibetan 1–7 | small children … the bride's road | 8–14 | earth tiger-hare (friend) … iron tiger-hare (enemy) | Chinese 8–14 |
| Tibetan 8–12 | the corpse … men's life force | 23–27 | earth monkey-bird (friend) … fire dragon-snake (son) | Tibetan 8–12 |
| Tibetan 13 | small children's body, the wheel | 28 | iron bird-monkey (enemy) | Tibetan 14 |
| Tibetan 14 | women's power, the swastika | 29 | earth horse-sheep (friend) | Tibetan 13 |
| Tibetan 15 | great men's luck, the lotus | 30 | water dog-pig (mother) | Tibetan 15 |

So the *Moonbeams* states the join WB leaves unsaid (question 302): WB's reading n takes WB's pair n, the fifteen Chinese pairs for readings 1–15 and the fifteen Tibetan for 16–30, though it groups the readings into its own "Chinese" and "Tibetan" fifteen differently. The one exception: readings 28 (children's body) and 29 (women's power) take each other's pairs; KD's print has the same. Whether that is the *Moonbeams*' choice or a slip copied in both prints is not said. LT (lt:p65-67) numbers the thirty readings 1–30 in WB's order beside WB's pairs, without the join. The *Moonbeams* ranks below WB; it is followed here only for the join, where WB is silent.
