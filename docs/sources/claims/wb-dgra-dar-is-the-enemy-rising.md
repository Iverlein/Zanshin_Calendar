# p. 54: «དགྲ་དར» is WB's term for the enemy rising, used at p. 58 and in the counts

«དགྲ་དར» is not shown a misprint for «དགྲ་ལོ» at p. 54, l. 3. The phrase «དགྲ་དར», the enemy rising, is WB's own term in three other places: the second examination's «སྲོག་ལ་དགྲ་དར་ཤི་བ་ན» (p. 58, the life force's enemy rising at death), and twice in the tenth examination's count lines (the «དགྲ་དར་བཞི» of the strike lists, pp. 48–49). The half-and-half list of p. 54 names the enemy's place «དགྲ་ལོའི་མདུན་དུ»; the special verdict's «དགྲ་དར་མདུན་དུ་རྡེལ་ནག་བཏུད» then reads "before the enemy rising, black pebbles sunk", the enemy in the state of rise, with the dots the reprint prints under it. So the likelier reading is that the special verdict speaks of the enemy in rise, a different thing from the plain enemy year, and so «དར» is carried, not corrected. What the place is in the 13 la places is not settled by this; the verdict's own «ཁྲུས་ཀྱི་ས» ("the place of ablution") and the forefathers' grave set on an enemy's [ground] are the consequences it names.
