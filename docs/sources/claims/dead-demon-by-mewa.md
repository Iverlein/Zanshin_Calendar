# The mewa of death: the reading, the eating demon, the rite

Terms as named below: the dead called *bkra* (བཀྲ, *bkra*), the fierce spirits (བཙན, *btsan*), the water spirits, the lu (ཀླུ, *klu*), the *Nāgas' Hundred Thousand* (ཀླུ་འབུམ, *klu 'bum*), the lu-demons (ཀླུ་བདུད, *klu bdud*), the *ma mo* goddesses (མ་མོ, *ma mo*), roasted barley flour, *tsampa* (ཟན, *zan*).

The third examination of the dead (WB vol. 2, pp. 62–63) by the mewa (སྨེ་བ, sme ba) of death, with the time of day of the death each names and its rite.
