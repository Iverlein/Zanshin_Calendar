# WB ch. 10 sets out new and widely used table-making rules of the Phug school

The tenth chapter of WB (pp. 70–78) is a collection of rules for making and extending the little tables (*rē'u mig*, རེའུ་མིག) of the Phug school's reckoning, by the colophon the tenth chapter of the *Phug lugs rtsis kyi legs bshad mkhas pa'i mgul rgyan bai dūrya dkar po'i do shal* by Dpyod ldan Snying nor. Its own plan, given on p. 70: the widely used, faultless tables that scholars have extended, and some the author newly made, set down as his memorandum and for others' benefit; the expanded and abridged workings of the tables for the five planets are not repeated. The close (p. 78) offers the new tables to the learned as a feast-gift and says the lesser ones (hour, weight, breath, part) can be built by anyone who has understood these.
