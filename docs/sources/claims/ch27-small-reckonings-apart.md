# The small reckonings of ch. 27 kept apart

Replies 141 and 142 (ys:i498-499, ys:i499): the small reckonings (རྩིས་ཕྲན, rtsis phran) of ch. 27, such as the golden four legs (a result of the trigram in the obstacle reckoning) and "a girl born on a *khon thog* blocks the mother's door" (a branch of the birth reckoning), are kept apart from the *Gsal sgron*'s essentials so as not to scatter them, not because they are to be given up. Sprugtön's (སྤུག་སྟོན, spug ston) obstacle reckoning is given as one way among many, because it is well known as Sprug Khyung and unlike the corrupted others; it is not claimed as the tradition's own. Stated by the regent.
