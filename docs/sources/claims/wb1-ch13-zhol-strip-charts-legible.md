# Ch. 13 strip charts: the Zhol print's hand is the legible one, syllables still unsettled

The Zhol leaves img. 150-153 hold the twelve strip charts in a large clear hand (they had not been kept in the shared cache; img. 145-165 are there now). On the rat chart the cells' places, line counts, the woodcut and the repeating word se bdud can be read on crops of 3x to 4x, which the 1996 print's 673 px scan and the reprint's dotted face do not allow; most syllables are still not settled at that size, because the cursive hand joins stacked letters. The next reading should take the leaf at its full 3685 px width in strips of 6x, one cell at a time, and match each chart to its page of the 1996 etext (pp. 142-147) by its woodcut and word set.
