# ཁྱད་པར་དུ in the closing verse singles out the person's rise and decline, no ranking stated

«ཁྱད་པར་དུ» in ch. 34's closing verse (p. 428) is "above all", "in particular": the line «ས་བདག་བཟང་ངན་ཁྱད་པར་དུ། ལས་བརྩམས་གང་ཟག་དེའི་དར་གུད། དེ་ལ་གནོད་པའི་སྐྱོན་ལ་སོགས། ཞིབ་ཏུ་བརྟགས་ལ་ཁ་དམར་སྨྲ།» singles the person's own rise and decline and what harms him out as the member to examine with most care, then gives the instruction to examine closely and give the verdict. The verse states no weights for the other members, and neither the Bhutan digest nor Dpal bsdus's *Rmad byung* (its chapter on rise and decline, p. 317: the rise and decline of the time, the fortune and the verdict matched with each other, «ཕན་ཚུན་བསྟུན་ཏེ་ཁ་དམར་སྨྲ») restates it as a ranking. So what the scan allows: the person's rise and decline is marked as the especially examined item; that it outranks every other factor in the weighing is not said. Unsettled beyond that.
