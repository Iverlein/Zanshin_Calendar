# The funeral share by the power's and the element's deciding pebbles

Whether the dead obtains a funeral (འདུར, 'dur), and how large, is read on two deciding pebbles: the power's (its food count, ཟན་གྲངས, zan grangs) and the element's (wb.2:p68-70, the close of the fourth examination; the notes, wb.2:p500-501).

| Power and element | Funeral share |
| --- | --- |
| both white | a large share; the dead obtains the funeral rite |
| one white, one black | speckled: a middling share |
| both black | none; where the living are much attached, a secret rite instead, not shown |

- "Funeral" means the corpse's equipment, the grave site, the food offered and the like, not the merit made for the dead (the notes).
- A black share "the host's end is endless": the dead, though buried, does not enjoy the meal; little help to it.
- A white pebble also when an earlier dead's power raises this dead's power, and when the dead's life force raises the eldest living's life force and his power the dead's ("the living and the dead alike"); both together give the funeral in full.
- Timing: if the dead's life force cuts a living person's (the dead "destroys the living's year"), hold the funeral in the month that is the enemy of the dead's life force, and avoid directions harsh for the living. Never in the living's seventh, four-executioner or grave-entering months; put it off on the living's grave days (the notes).
- Remedy: even for those others call beyond help (butchers, smiths, parents of nine sons, lepers with bad karma) WB says to do great virtue and purification, "as water given strength against a great fire".
- Laying a white pebble where a share is not had is mere elaboration; the share actually won is what is done (the notes, wb.2:p503-504).
