# «ཀླུང་གི་དུར་ཕུགས»: the Zhol print reads «དུར་ལུགས»; the luck's tomb, not a stage of its rise

Narrowed 2026-11-01. P. 335's «ཀླུང་གི་དུར་ཕུགས་མ་ཤེས་ན། ཕན་གནོད་དམིགས་ནས་ཟིན་མི་འགྱུར» (the passage is quoted in verdicts.md, *The reckonings with each other*) reads «ཕུགས» on the 1996 print and on the Sakya Centre print (BDRC's search for «ཀླུང་གི་དུར་ཕུགས» finds exactly these two; the Gser thang reprint's page was not read here); the Zhol print's etext reads «ཀླུངས་ཀྱི་དུར་ལུགས» and the letter of the cursive block (image 435, line 5, at 3×) is nearer ལ than ཕ, not certain. If «དུར་ལུགས» is the reading the clause says: not knowing how the luck is "tombed" (the method of the luck's tomb), help and harm cannot be aimed at; «དུར» is WB's word for the tomb or grave (the grave year, the grave trigram, the notes' «དགྲ་དུར») and not a stage of rise and decline; and «ཕུགས» is "the inner, the end" (Hopkins, Rangjung Yeshe), so «དུར་ཕུགས» would be the tomb's far end. Either way the clause is not about the luck's rise and decline (the next lines name those: «དར་གུད་ཀླུང་དང་བསྟུན་པ་ནི། སྔ་མ་མི་འགྱུར་ཕྱི་མ་འགྱུར», the notes, vol. 2, p. 484: the former the birth luck, the latter the rise and decline of the reckoning). So the question's second alternative, the luck's tomb, is the likelier; no gloss states it. «ཀླུང་གི་དུར» occurs nowhere else in the etexts read (WB's two volumes, the notes, UT, the Bhutan collection, the G.ya' sel); elsewhere «དུར་ཕུགས» is found only in a rite-collection («དུར་ཕུགས་ནས་སྲོག་འདོན», "drawing the life from the tomb's end"), the grave's inside, by BDRC's search.

Corrected 2026-11-03 (RD129): the Zhol blocks themselves read «དུར་ཕུགས» (img. 435, l. 4, by eye at 3×, Yigdzin-1 and MITRA agreeing); «དུར་ལུགས» is the Zhol etext's OCR, so there is no variant between the three prints ([year-of-life.md](../year-of-life.md), RD129). The reading of the clause stands.
