# The Aṅgavijjā's ch. 53 (p. 210) has the bright beginning at forenoon like ch. 42; only ch. 41 has the afternoon (RD295)

RD295 read leaf n325 of Puṇyavijaya's print (p. 210, ch. 53, *Uppātaṇajjhāya*, line 10): the same six-fold list of the time of the day for a portent: the bright beginning, forenoon (*puvvaṇha*); the bright deep middle, midnight (*addharatta*); the bright ends, afternoon (*avaraṇha*); the dark beginning, evening; the dark middle, midnight; the dark ends, dawn. So two lists (chs. 42 and 53) give the forenoon for the bright beginning and the afternoon for the bright end, and the third (ch. 41, p. 185) has the afternoon for both. The lists differ in the bright middle (midday in ch. 42, midnight in ch. 53). The apparatus is silent on the word in ch. 41; the original reading is not settled by a manuscript, but the weight of the print falls on the forenoon.

RD312 (2026-11-10): the apparatus of the three lists has no note on the word (claim angavijja-ch41-afternoon-no-variant-in-apparatus).
