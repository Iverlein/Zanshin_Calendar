# The waking and sleeping instructions of Rtsa gsum gling pa vol. 27 images 81-82 and 131 are a writer's commentary on a treasure's root words, not the treasure's own text (RD402)

The waking and sleeping instructions at images 81-82 and 131 of the Rtsa gsum gling pa's Gter chos vol. 27 are in the section «Thun mong phyi yi sngon 'gro'i lag khrid gdab pa man ngag gnad kyi gzer bu» (printed pp. 71-126 by the volume's list), and its colophon (images 132-134, tsl.27:i132-134) says what the section is: a short commentary by its writer on the root vajra words of a treasure, the Sku gsum dgongs 'dus of 'Gro 'dul gling pa (an emanation of Gnubs chen Sangs rgyas ye shes, said to be taken from Padma bkod), written at the command of the master who gave that treasure's empowerment; the writer names himself with the name Orgyan gsang sngags bstan 'dzin, and says the younger brother Khenpo Ngag dbang dge legs mchog corrected it. The instructions are thus the writer's prose, guiding the practice of the root words; the root words in this section carry the treasure verse-mark «༔» (as at image 83, from «bla ma mkhyen no༔») and the instruction lines do not. They are not Rtsa gsum gling pa's own text, whose preliminaries are in another section of the same volume (the section for the Great Perfection Self-liberation of Samsara and Nirvana, from image 135). The volume's own compiler's note (images 4-7) lists the sections and says the compiler arranged the volume from what was found, with his own notes; whether the man who calls himself Orgyan gsang sngags bstan 'dzin of this colophon is the compiler Mkhan chen Dpal ldan shes rab is not stated on these pages. The root verses of the six bardos quoted at image 282 were not looked at again.
