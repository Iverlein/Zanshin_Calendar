# Ch. 19: carrying the reckoning forward, the carries of the planets, sun, Rahu, year-lord and the three days

Chapter 19 teaches how to carry a reckoning forward (rtsis 'phro spo ba) from a base year to another, and the wheel of the three days (wb.1:p213-216). The forward way begins at the first year of the circle of the empty entry (stong 'jug) and the great thousand (stong chen) of the Rab-byung: the sum of the past years up to the year to be moved is multiplied by 12 and set in two places, the lower multiplied by 2 and divided by 65; the wealth joined to the upper is the pure month of the year moved, the remainder (65) the carry for the corrected month (zla dag). From the corrected month, set in five places and multiplied by its constants (1, 20, 50, 0, 480, then 707, 6, 60, 7), the carry of the five-gathered planets' dhruva (gza' dhu ba) is made; the whole-part's by 12, 28 and the like; the sun's dhruva (nyi dhu ba) in five places by 2, 10, the elements, 17, 67, 6, 60 and the circle. An own-found way is given: the corrected month less 295,921 is the carry of the year reckoned and of the planets' dhruva, less 2,528 that of the whole-part, and 844 off the sun's. Rahu (sgra gcan) takes 460 or 220 off the corrected month; the year-lord (lo bdag), the king and the minister (rgyal blon) are moved the same way. In the Kar-rtsis the root-line (rtsa thig) and the year of the lord of men are counted from the Rab-byung as the Shakya year. The five planets' general and particular days (spyi zhag, sgos zhag) come from the corrected month by 30, 707 and 64, then 18,382 and 6,714,405. The numbers are as printed; several are machine readings only.
