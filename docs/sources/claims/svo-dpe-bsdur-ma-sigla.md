# The Dpe bsdur ma's note format and sigla: Peking, Narthang, Co ne, Derge

The Dpe bsdur ma (the collated Tengyur) prints its notes as entries without the word they replace: a siglum or a pair of sigla and the variant reading, the printed page number heading each group, a minus sign before words a print omits. The sigla are the first syllables of the column heads of the collation table that closes each work: «པེ་» Peking, «སྣར་» Narthang, «ཅོ་» Co ne, «སྡེ་» Derge (the base text is the Derge print). In vol. 14 the Peking is named about 10,500 times, the Narthang about 4,600, the Co ne about 120 and the Derge 6. The table's folio row for the Svapnohana reads 133b-135a for the Derge and the Co ne, 154b-157b for the Peking and 140b-142a for the Narthang; for the Svapnekṣā 130a-130b (Derge, Co ne), 154a-155b (Peking), 144a-145a (Narthang), read from the OCR. The volume's own explanation of the sigla is not in vol. 14.
