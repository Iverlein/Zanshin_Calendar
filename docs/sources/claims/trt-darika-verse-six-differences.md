# Dārikapa's verse stands in Dārika's Entering Reality with six differences from the Sngags rim chen mo's quotation (RD373)

Dārikapa's verse as the Sngags rim chen mo quotes it stands in Dārika's Cakrasaṃvara rite Entering Reality (Derge Bstan 'gyur etext VE23703_1337, [p418]-[p419]) line for line, with six differences of spelling or wording (trt:p418-419): (1) «ནང་པར་ང་ལ་རབ་ཏུ་ཟློས» in the Sngags rim chen mo, «ནང་པར་དེ་ལའང་རབ་ཏུ་ཟློས» in the Derge etext ("tell it to me" against "tell it to that one too"); (2) «ལྟས་དག་མཐོང་ན་སྤངས་ནས་ནི» against «ལངས་ནས་ནི» ("having given up (the work)" against "on rising"); (3) «བསྲུང་བའི་འཁོར་ལོ་ཀུན་ཏུ་སྤྱད» against «སྲུང་བའི་འཁོར་ལོས་ཀུན་དུ་སྤྱད»; (4) «བཟླས་བྱ་སྟེ» against «བཟླས་བྱས་ཏེ»; (5) «གསོལ་གདབ» against «གསོལ་བཏབ»; (6) «བརྩོན་པས» against «བརྩོན་པ». The rest is word for word.

The teacher's seven acts of the Sngags rim chen mo (torma to the field-guarding ḍākinīs, the hundred syllables, the guarding wheel, the hero-face mantra and the rest, the armour, the request with the ornaments and the pride, the fire rite) are this verse's; the pupil's remedy in the verse is the All-Doer (ལས་ཀུན་བྱེད་པ), which the Sngags rim chen mo's prose calls "all the rites" (ལས་ཐམས་ཅད་པ). The verse names no watch and no day. The Peking print's etext (VEIE0OPIA3E40644_I1PD95856, [p568]; OCR) reads the second difference as the Derge does, «ལྟས་དག་མཐོང་ན་ལངས་ནས་ནི», and the first as «ནང་པར་ངལ་འང» (the OCR's for «དེ་ལའང», a doubt), so both prints stand against the Sngags rim chen mo's «སྤངས་ནས» and «ང་ལ»; the other four were not compared in the Peking text. Whether the Sngags rim chen mo's copy differed or the etext of it has the slip was not decided.
