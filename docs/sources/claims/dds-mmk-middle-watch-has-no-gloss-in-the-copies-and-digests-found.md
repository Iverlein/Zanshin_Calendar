# The Mañjuśrī root tantra's «middle watch» has no gloss in the copies and the metrical digest found; its place is only the order of the lines (RD403, q1125)

RD403 (q1125). No gloss on the «middle watch» (བར་མའི་ཐུན) of the Mañjuśrī root tantra's earthquake omens was found. BDRC's full-text search (2026-11-11), the lines «བར་མའི་ཐུན་ནི་བྲི་གྱུར་ནས» and «ཐ་མར་ཉེ་ལ་འགུལ་བ་བྱུང་», gives three etexts for the first line, the Derge volume (VE4CZ5369_I1KG9214), KD vol. 2 (I4716, already known as a second copy) and a third volume (I1KG2753, not opened), and one for the second (Derge only); the bare words «བར་མའི་ཐུན» stand in six etexts, among them an astrological handbook (I3CN8749, dds.55:p213), which has the omens in a metrical digest: the same order, «middle of the night» (མཚན་དཀྱིལ) for the Derge «midnight», then «the middle watch lessened, it shakes near the end: peace, no disease», then «the end of midnight», and no word on what the middle watch is. The middle watch is therefore still only the stretch between «midnight» and «the end of midnight» by the order of the lines (the earlier claim), now in three copies of the lines and none that explains it. Of the other hits of the bare words, the volume I2PD18782 answers 403 to the etext call and was not read; those of I5743 and I1KG2753 were not opened.
