# The Mchog dga' puts the dream in the part of the night by the part of thirty that rises: six early night, ten a third, fifteen midnight, thirty dawn

The only passage read that ties a dream to the part of the night is in the Mchog dga' (མཆོག་དགའ, Mchog dga') on the Dbyangs 'char (དབྱངས་འཆར): the parts (ཆ་ཤས, cha shas) of the instant junction (ཏཏྟཱལ, tattāla) are of the nature of thirty; look at the part that rises in the place of the portion: if ten, the dream is dreamt in the third of the night (the clause is garbled in the etext); if fifteen, at midnight; if thirty, at dawn when one rises from sleep (ཐོ་རེངས་ནམ་ལངས་ཁར, tho rengs nam langs khar); if six, in the early part of the night, and so on; a skilled reckoner is to examine the details, and the hours (chu tshod) are needed for a very fine reckoning. So the night is cut in thirty, the part of the dream is the part that rises, and the reckoning is made at the time of the question. It is a Phug pa reckoning, not a rule that a dream of the dawn is true: the Kālacakra's own texts read here have no such division. Etext only, the loaned scans not opened; the part numbers (six, ten, fifteen, thirty) are the etext's and the clause for ten is garbled, so they are given as read, not checked on a scan.
