# The Narthang catalogue names the Deceiving Death's author Ngag dbang grags pa (img. 197) and has no Examination of Dreams entry where the Derge order would put it (img. 196–206) (RD365)

The Narthang Tengyur manuscript's catalogue (vol. 225, img. 197 line 4) lists, in its run of the works on Yamāntaka, "two condensed meanings of the Deceiving Death" by Ngag dbang grags pa and, beside them, an instruction (མན་ངག) by Ngag dbang grags pa with illustrations, in the translation of Dīpaṃkaraśrījñāna and Rin chen bzang po. The author's name is the short form «ངག་དབང་གྲགས་པ», as in the Jo nang catalogue, not the longer Ngag gi dbang phyug grags pa. The line names no number of verses. In images 196–206, where the Derge and Jo nang order would put the Examination of Dreams between the Deceiving Death and the Black Lord run, the Narthang catalogue has no entry for it (read by Yigdzin-1 on the images and on crops); the rest of the catalogue was not read page by page.
