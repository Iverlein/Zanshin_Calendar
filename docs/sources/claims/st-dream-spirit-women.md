# The dream of being led away by women marks a spirit, and its remedies (ch. 91)

The dream of being led away by women as the sign of a spirit (གདོན, gdon), and its remedies, ch. 91 of the Instruction Tantra.

In the closing part of the chapter on making the flavour (རོ་ཙ, ro tsa), the Four Tantras give the failing of the matter the chapter treats by cause. For cold wind of the kidneys there are medicines; for heat, pills with salt given with beer; for poison, the signs outer, inner and secret are identified, it is killed by its antidote and the body is guarded; for a spirit the sign is a dream (རྨི་ལམ, rmi lam) in which women lead the dreamer away. For the spirit the remedies are divination (མོ, mo), ཕྱུ (phyu), the rite of service (རིམ་གྲོ, rim gro) and the dependent link (རྟེན་འབྲེལ, rten 'brel), and the healing by mantra: a thread twisted by a clean boy from the wool of a sheep untouched by hands, seven vajra knots made in it, a mantra spoken a thousand times over it and the thread bound on; with it, milk of ba ra (བ་ར, unidentified) mixed with དྲི་ཆེན (dri chen) rubbed on the soles. The dream is thus a sign of the spirit as the cause among several, within a chapter on a particular matter, not a sign of a spirit in general. The mantra is not settled on the scan. The chapter is the ninety-first by its colophon, p. 493 of the print (img. 507) (st:p493).
