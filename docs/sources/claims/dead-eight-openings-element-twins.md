# The eight places that open death: twelve animals already placed, the three implied take their element-twin's

The root text (WB vol. 2, p. 90, read on the Gser thang reprint, img. 096, ll. 14-15) places the five trigrams' animals as: *li*, rat and pig; *khon*, tiger and hare; *dwa*, horse and snake; *kham*, the four slayers (ox, dragon, sheep, dog); *zin*, bird and monkey. That is 2 + 2 + 2 + 4 + 2 = twelve: every animal is already placed once. Mipham's note (p. 509, not p. 510; reprint img. 515, ll. 10-11) says the eight places are the five shown outright and *khen*, *gin* and *zon*, where death is also opened, shown by implication; the root text writes «བྱེ», the note «ཕྱེ». With no animal left over the three can only take animals again, and each of the three has the element of one of the five: *khen* iron like *dwa*, *gin* earth like *khon*, *zon* wood like *zin*. The animals would then be those of the twin: horse and snake, tiger and hare, bird and monkey. This is an inference from the structure, not a stated text; no source read names the animals.
