# The moon's light and the full moon meeting its mansion: the words of the notes at p. 484

The notes' gloss on two of the birth's children (p. 484, after the 1996 print's «ཟླ་བའི་གདོང» and the sun-and-moon pair) was read again on Mipham's own print and on the Chengdu typeset edition (2026-11-01):

- «ཉ་སྐར་དང་འཛོམས་པ» (question 182): the typeset prints «འཛོམས» with ཛ (the letter at 2.5× is the tailed tsa); Yigdzin-1 reads «འཛོམས» on Mipham's own print too, where the stack at 8× is not distinct to the eye. «འཇོམས» is the 1996 OCR's reading only; both prints that can be read say «འཛོམས», the full moon meeting its mansion (the fifteenth day, the "assembling").
- «ཟླ་བའི་སྣང» or «སྒང» (question 179): the typeset prints «སྣང» (the subscript at 6× is the closed ན-shaped foot of ྣ, not a ག's hook; so also the 1996 print at 1.5×). Yigdzin-1 reads «སྒང» on both prints, and «སྣ» for the sun's word in the same sentence, so its choice here is not evidence; Mipham's own print at 9× shows a boxy foot that fits ྣ and is not distinct. The notes' word is therefore «སྣང», the moon's light, glossed "the eleventh to the twentieth"; WB p. 339 and the three readings of it (སྒང) are a different spelling of the same sense (the month's middle, as p. 261's «ཟླ་བ་ཚེ་སྒང»).
