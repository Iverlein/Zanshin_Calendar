# The thirty: the fifteenth point, the garuḍa head on the temple's Chinese roof, for house and household, by the eight directions

The fifteenth of the thirty starting points (སྐོར་འགོ, skor 'go), from the mouth of the garuḍa head on the temple's Chinese roof (ལྷ་ཁང་རྒྱ་ཕུབས་ཀྱི་ཁྱུང་མགོ, lha khang rgya phubs kyi khyung mgo), is read for house and household, by the eight directions. Its eight pebbles are laid as for every point (claims thirty-count and thirty-eight-fighters), each white or black, and read so:

| Pebble | Asks | White | Black | Remedy for black |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | From the north: whether the turquoise bird has entered the silver road | Not entered; good for the house | The turquoise bird runs downward: very bad for the house and the household | — |
| 2 | From the north-east: whether a king's palace has been built | From the Lord's (དབང་ལྡན, dbang ldan) direction no king's palace is built, good | A king's palace is built, bad | Laying the king spirits' fort (རྒྱལ་པོ་རྫོང་འདེབས, rgyal po rdzong 'debs), the king thread-cross (མདོས, mdos) and the like |
| 3 | From the east: whether the temple is pressed by the garuḍa head | Not pressed, good | Pressed, bad | Strive at white service rites (རིམ་གྲོ, rim gro) |
| 4 | From the fire quarter, the south-east: whether the black demon wind has risen | Not risen, good | Risen, bad | Building a stupa or temple is good |
| 5 | From the south: whether the prosperity tree on the mountain has grown | Grown, good | Not grown, bad | Calling back prosperity (གཡང་ལོན, g.yang lon) and protecting prosperity (གཡང་སྐྱབས, g.yang skyabs) make it a little auspicious |
| 6 | From the south-west («སྲིབས», as printed): whether the sunny and shady sides (གདགས་སྲིབས, gdags sribs) are reversed | Not reversed, good | Reversed: bad for the local lord and the house owner | — |
| 7 | From the west, Varuṇa's (ཀླུ་དབང, klu dbang) quarter: whether the black road of the dead has opened | Not opened: the old live long and the young gain glory | The road of the dead from the west is open: bad abroad | Cutting the joined hands of the living and the dead (ལག་སྦྲེལ་བཅད, lag sbrel bcad) |
| 8 | From the north-west, the wind's quarter: whether the earth ogre Gnya' yan's grave store gathers | It does not, good | Lost to the earth ogre Gnya' yan: the grave's mouth opens | The *rgya ma* net (རྒྱ་མ, rgya ma; not identified), the *dong tse* (དོང་ཙེ, dong tse; not identified), the grave mouth's turning back of life; press down the earth ogre's leper skull (ས་སྲིན་མཛེ་ཐོད, sa srin mdze thod) |

Whose year is reckoned: The house owner's year (notes, p. 532).

Which of the eight fighting pebbles is which check is not stated; the order of p. 453 is the likely one (claim thirty-board-groups; question 259).
