# The month head gives the skipped and doubled dates of both the Phugpa and the Dpal ldan reckonings

The month head of the Lhasa almanac of a wood pig year (kd.1:p493b) gives, for the third month, the dates skipped and doubled in each of the two reckonings: Phugpa accomplished, the 6th doubled and the 14th skipped; Dpal ldan effect, a skipped date printed «༢ ༡» (the 21st, or the 2nd and the 1st; the etext's spacing does not decide) and nothing doubled; a month with no skip or double in either reckoning, all thirty days whole, is an auspicious month (བཀྲ་ཤིས་ཟླ་བ). So an almanac printing both reckonings lets the two differ in the dates they repeat and drop,. The skip and the double are fixed by the reckoning of the date's end (the date lasts its water-hours, kd.1:p496-498).
