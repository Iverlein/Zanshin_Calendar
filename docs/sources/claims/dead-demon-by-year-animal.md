# The dead's year animal: whom the eating demon leads, where it hides, the rite

Terms as named below: the priests, bande or bonpo (བན་བོན, *ban bon*), the fierce spirits (བཙན, *btsan*), the harmful spirit of the dead (སྲི, *sri*), Kong tse's turning rite (ཀོང་ཙེའི་གཏོ་བསྒྱུར, *kong tse*), a rite called *thang 'bum* (ཐང་འབུམ, *thang 'bum*), the *Nāgas' Hundred Thousand* (ཀླུ་འབུམ, *klu 'bum*), the collected dhāraṇīs (གཟུངས་བསྡུས, *gzungs bsdus*), the *Eight Appearances* (སྣང་བརྒྱད, *Snang brgyad*).

The third examination of the dead (WB vol. 2, pp. 58–60) by the dead's own year animal: for each of the twelve, whom the eating demon (ཟ་འདྲེ, za 'dre) "leads" next, where the eating demon or the executioner (གཤེད, gshed) hides, to whom it goes, the rite, and the time within which it returns if the rite is not done. Three priests (*ban bon*) kill the executioner in most entries; the text gives no pebble or verdict for this part.
