# The thirty's board read by its places, and "all speckled is good"

Reply 138 (ys:i496) reads the horse reckoning's checks by place: whether "in the place of the forefathers" the horse wealth looks toward the asker, whether "in the place of the last part of life" it grows, and, "in the middle, the present", harm by demons. That is the general board's grouping of WB (wb.1:p471-472) and the notes (wb.2:p532-533): upper, forebears; lower, oneself and the last part of life; middle, demons and wealth. Stated, for one point; that every point reads its checks so is an inference.

On the short commentary's "all the pebbles speckled is good" (wb.1:p471-472, «ཁྲ་ན་བཟང»): it does not mean four white and four black. All white and all black are bad, as in the obstacle reckoning; a mixed board is read by its base: on mostly black, each white pebble makes it better than plain black; on mostly white, one, two, three black pebbles are read in order as "very good" and down. Stated by the regent.
