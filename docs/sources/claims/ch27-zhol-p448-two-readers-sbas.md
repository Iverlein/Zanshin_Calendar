# Two machine readers of the Zhol scan read «གུད་སྦས» on p. 448, the etext «གུད་ཡས»

On the Zhol print (WBZ, I1KG1710 img. 1230, line 6) Yigdzin-1 and MITRA both read «རྟོགས་དཀའ་གུད་སྦས་ཀུན་བསལ་བའི»; the Zhol etext has «གུད་ཡས» and the reprint by eye «ཧྲུལ». The stack was not cut by eye, so the word is narrowed toward «སྦས» (lost, hidden) on the Zhol print, not settled.
