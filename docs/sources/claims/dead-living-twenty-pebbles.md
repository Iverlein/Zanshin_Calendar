# The living's twenty pebbles in the dead's reckoning: descended mewa and birth mewa

In the *Moonbeams*' first part of the reckoning for the dead, taking the dead out of the living, each living member of the household gets twenty fixed pebbles (dz:i055-057): the year, the four aspects fought against the sky year, the trigram, the descended mewa (བབས་སྨེ, babs sme), five of rise and decline, seven rough ones and the birth mewa (སྐྱེས་སྨེ). The living's mewa is laid twice, as descended and as at birth, the descended in the colour code of the mewa; the trigram is "whatever it is", not said to be the descended one. The notes lay the same twenty-one for the living with the descended trigram and mewa (wb.2:p499-500).

So for the living in the household the manuals read the person's descended mewa and trigram in the current year, the birth mewa beside it.
