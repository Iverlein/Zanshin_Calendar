# Hiroshige's Suidōbashi Surugadai: three carp streamers, all black

Seen on 2026-11-24 in the Tokyo National Museum's copy (ColBase, A-10569-7353, 名所江戸百景・水道橋駿河臺, Ansei 4 (1857), 5th month): one great black carp streamer fills the left half from top to bottom beside its pole; two smaller dark carp streamers fly farther off over the Surugadai houses; Mt Fuji on the horizon; no red carp. The British Museum's "three carp" and Wikipedia's "a single great black carp, the red not yet common" both stand: three carp, all black. Answers q1360.
