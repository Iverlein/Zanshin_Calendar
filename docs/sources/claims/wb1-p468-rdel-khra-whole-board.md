# WB vol. 1, p. 468: «རྡེལ་ཁྲ» is the speckled board, whose rite is the 26th's speckled rite

In the twenty-fifth starting point (WB vol. 1, p. 468, I3CN5028 img. 463, l. 23) the line is «རྡེལ་ཁྲ་ས་བདག་འཁྲུགས་གཏོ་བྱ། །རྡེའུ་ཐམས་ཅད་ནག་པ་ན། །རུ་ཏྲའི་གཏོ་ནག་མགོན་པོ་ཡི། །གཡུལ་བཟློག་དགྲ་འདུལ་དགྲ་ཟོར་བྱ»: the first word's last letter is a la, as the earlier reading has it («རྡེལ», pebbles; Yigdzin-1 «རྡེའུ»). It comes after the seventh check's black reading and before the eighth check, and sets a mixed board («ཁྲ») beside a wholly black one («རྡེའུ་ཐམས་ཅད་ནག་པ»), each with a rite. The rites are those of the twenty-sixth starting point, whose eight checks are named for the three kinds of rite, «དཀར་ནག་ཁྲ་གསུམ», white, black and speckled: its second check, «ཁྲ་བོས། ས་བདག་འཁྲུགས་སམ་མ་འཁྲུགས», is the disturbance of the earth lords, and its third, «ནག་པོས། རུ་ཏྲ…», Rudra's black rite, with the turning back of war and taming of enemies of the fourth. So the 25th's «རྡེལ་ཁྲ» takes its rite, the disturbed earth lords' rite (ས་བདག་འཁྲུགས་གཏོ), from the 26th's speckled kind, and reads the board of the eight checks as a whole: speckled, a mixture of white and black pebbles over the eight, against «རྡེའུ་ཐམས་ཅད་ནག» all of the pebbles black. It is not shown to be only the seventh check's pair (the living's pebble white and the dead's black), which the line before already gives its own rite («འཕུང་སྲི་སྐྱས་འདེབས»). The page says no more; no note of Mipham's (pp. 532-533, 535-538) speaks to it.
