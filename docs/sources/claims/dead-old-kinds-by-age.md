# The nine kinds of old men and of old women by age at death

WB names the old dead (བཀྲ, bkra) by sex and age at death, nine kinds each, every kind with its own garment, hiding place, funeral share and officiant (WB vol. 2, pp. 77–79). A man is old from sixty-one and a woman from sixty (p. 79). Men: 70 *bkra smun* (བཀྲ་སྨུན); 71–72 *bkra byin* (བཀྲ་བྱིན); 73–74 *shang dkar* (ཤང་དཀར); 75–76 *dga' rgyal* (དགའ་རྒྱལ); 77–78 *drod med* (དྲོད་མེད); 79–80 *bkra sri 'byams pa* (བཀྲ་སྲི་འབྱམས་པ); 81–82 *sems med* (སེམས་མེད); 84–85 *smyos bkra rkun bu* (སྨྱོས་བཀྲ་རྐུན་བུ); 86–87 *bkra nag mche ba can* (བཀྲ་ནག་མཆེ་བ་ཅན) (monks, Bon priests, physicians or brothers without descendants). The text gives no kind for 83 or for 61–69 in men. Women: 61 *za 'ug sman gcig ma* (ཟ་འུག་སྨན་གཅིག་མ); 62–63 *bkra sman byi ba* (བཀྲ་སྨན་བྱི་བ); 64–65 *gangs chung* (གངས་ཆུང); 67 *bkra sman rgyal mo* (བཀྲ་སྨན་རྒྱལ་མོ); 68–69 *sman gcig ma* (སྨན་གཅིག་མ); *srid sman* (སྲིད་སྨན) for one of the old's years without descendants; 72 *blo stor sems med* (བློ་སྟོར་སེམས་མེད); *mche ser ma* (མཆེ་སེར་མ) for one of 60–70 whose sons and husbands died; *bsrung med bkra* (བསྲུང་མེད་བཀྲ) in the seventies to ninety, and at 78 with many descendants *sems med*. Ages 66, 70–71 and 73–77 of women are not named one by one.
