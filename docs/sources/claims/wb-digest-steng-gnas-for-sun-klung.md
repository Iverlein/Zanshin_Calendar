# A digest of Langging's table has «སྟེང་གནས» where WB has the sun's klung

The digest 'Bras rtsis bar gsal li dwangs ma'i me long (MW1KG21827, 'Bro'i sbyin pa, Kan su'u mi rigs dpe skrun khang, 2006; search-only, snippets read 2026-10-28) carries Langging's table of the twelve year pairs word for word, under the lord's name «དམག་མི་ཝང་གིང» ("the soldier Wang-ging"), but where WB's next line is «ཉི་མའི་ཀླུང་དང་ལྷན་ཅིག་བསྐྱེད། །ལས་ཐབས་དམག་ཇག་རྩོད་པ་འཛུགས», the digest reads «སྟེང་གནས་དམག་ཇག་རྩོད་པ་འཛུགས» ("abiding above, it sets up war, raids and strife"). WB's phrase of the hal khyi, «སྔ་ལོའི་ཀླུང་རྟའི་སྟེང་དུ་གནས» ("stays on the previous year's wind-horse"), has the same «སྟེང་ … གནས», so the digest may be taking the sun's klung as the wind-horse the lord stays upon, and WB's «བསྐྱེད» ("is sent forth") as the same going-forth turned into a seat. This is an inference from the words of one search-only snippet; the sun's klung itself stays unglossed (question 432), and the reading «ཀླུང» of WB's prints is not in doubt. A conflict between two texts, not settled by either.
