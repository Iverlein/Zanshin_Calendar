# BDRC's full text finds WB's dream list only in WB's prints, and Klong rdol's verses only in copies of his own text; the Chöd collection of Ma gcig is not found as a text (RD360)

Exact-string searches on BDRC (2026-11-10): six of WB's lines (wb.2:p144l: «ཐོག་མར་རྨི་ལྟས་བསྟན་པ་ལ», «གཅེར་བུ་ཐུར་གཏིང་བབས་པ་དང་», «ནོར་རྙེད་གསོན་པོའི་གྲོགས་སྤུན་ཕྲད», «རང་ཉིད་ཤེས་རིག་ལྷག་པར་བྱིང», «བརྩི་བྱ་དེ་ལ་འཛེར་ཡོད་དོ», «དེ་དག་བསངས་དང་ཁྲུས་བྱས་ནས།») give one to four hits each, all in the White Beryl's own prints and the Phug lugs print and a reproduction of the book (one hit is in a noisy etext of the Do shal), none in another work. The phrase «མ་གཅིག་ཟབ་མོ་གཅོད་ཀྱི་དམིགས་འབུམ» and six further lines of Klong rdol's verses (the thirds of the night, the black woman without belly, the spirit classes) give seven hits: Klong rdol's text (RST), the modern article on it (KBS, two prints), the folk collection MDP, Padma rdo rje's book and two further modern books, which quote the same verses; no etext holds the verses outside these copies. «གཅོད་ཀྱི་དམིགས་འབུམ» as a title is found in Karma chags med's list of his sources («gcod kyi dmigs 'bum zab don thugs snying», drawn from Mkhas grub Shes rab ting 'dzin's sayings) and in the list of Kun dga' rin chen's works, but no Chöd collection of Ma gcig's with the dream verses, nor the Chöd text that Klong rdol quotes, was found. So the question whether WB's list comes from the stock of images that Klong rdol's verses draw on, or from another text, gains nothing: no third witness has turned up, and the likeness stays a likeness (claim dream-wb30-wording-shared-with-klong-rdol-and-rrt).
