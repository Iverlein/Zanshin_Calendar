# The second examination: the demon's cut by the time of death

WB vol. 2, p. 58: by the animal of the time of death, the demon cut the la, the life-spirit (བླ, bla), in the month that is its seventh, with a reason and a carrier. Mipham's notes (p. 503): the root text applies it to the year (died in a year, cut in the month that is its seventh); another reading takes the current year itself (died in a tiger year, cut in a monkey year) and the month, day and hour of death each against its own seventh before; examine the four and take the reason most in accord with what came before. WB: "speak by the one most in accord, above all by the one nearest in time".

| tiger | monkey | the king spirit of the east carried off the household demon (ཐེའུ་བྲང, the'u brang); bad traces after: suppress the harmful spirits of the dead (སྲི, sri) |
