# The seventy-two fixed earth lords by quarter and element

WB's list of the earth lords that never move (vol. 2, pp. 175–176, the end of its list of fixed lords): seventy-two that dwell, laid out by quarter. North: the pig's five above (བྱང་སྟོད་ཕག་ལྔ, byang stod phag lnga) and the rat's five below, with two parts of water between them; east: the tiger's and hare's five each, two parts of wood between; south: the horse's and snake's five each, two fire parts of earth between; west: the bird's and monkey's five each, two iron parts in the middle; at the four corners the four executioners (གཤེད་བཞི, gshed bzhi), five groups each; in the centre one earth part to each. The text counts the whole as seventy-two (གནས་པ་བདུན་ཅུ་རྩ་གཉིས, gnas pa bdun cu rtsa gnyis). The arrangement is by the five elements and the four quarters, not by days. The sums of the parts as printed do not obviously reach seventy-two (the text leaves the corner groups and the middle parts uncounted), so only the layout is stated here.
