# Peking img. 310 line 1, read again at 4×: the Svapnekṣā's last part of the night stays «བརྟགས་པ» or «བཏབ» (q961, RD401)

On the Peking print's leaf (vol. 46, I1KG13215 img. 310, line 1) the Svapnekṣā's last part of the night has «ཐ་མར་རྨི་ལམ» before the gap and «ལ་ཉལ» after it, plain at 4×. Between them stand a བ, a wide stack with a long tail, a small letter and a letter like པ: as wide as two syllables, so the earlier eye reading «བརྟགས་པ» (examined) stays the likelier and the Dpe bsdur ma's «བཏབ» (made, cast) stays the machine's. No sharper image of this leaf exists on BDRC (RD327 found one Peking instance only), and the 4× crop did not settle it; the question stays open as asked. The line's first four syllables and the following line's «མེ་ལོང་ལག་ཏུ་ཐོགས་པ་དང» (holding a mirror in the hand) are not in doubt.
