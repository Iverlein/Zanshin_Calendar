# KD's seven-member round: quarter-runs of eight, the three leftovers named 17, 18, 19

On KD's sheet of the household round for seven members (vol. 3, p. 726), read on the lent scan, the thirty-five pebbles are laid out in four runs of eight with three over, so the fold is by quarter-runs, not halves, and the three left over are written on the sheet with their names: the fold's («ཁུག་པ») is the 17th, one black pebble; the refold's («ཡང་ཁུག») is the 18th, two white; and a third, the secret refold's («གསང་བ་ཡང་ཁུག») is the 19th, which carries a colour word instead of a drawn pebble.

The runs are the gods' place (1 to 8), the people's place (35 down to 28), the house's place (20 up to 27) and the enemy's place (16 down to 9); the list of the three leftovers is a column of its own beside them under the label «རྡེལ་རོ», "the leftover". 35 = 4 × 8 + 3, and 17, 18 and 19 are exactly the numbers that stand in no run, so the three over are the three middle numbers of the thirty-five. Whether the folding order is the zigzag's is the business of claims/household-round-fold.md.

No pebble is drawn speckled (a «ཁྲ་བོ» as a drawn pebble): the cells hold white and black pebbles, white with black as a pair, and, in some cells, a colour word where a pebble would stand (the 2nd, 12th, 22nd, 29th, 32nd and 9th); on the upper table the colour words are the fallen mewas'. The line «ཕྲེང་འདི་དག་དོར་བར་བྱ» heads the first run's fourth to seventh cells and says these strings are cast away; which pebbles it casts away in the working is not stated by the sheet.

What is still open: the colour words (read as red and blue in the small print), the seven members' signs and numbers in the upper table, and the digits of its cells.
