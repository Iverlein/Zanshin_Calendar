# The examination of dreams in the Narthang manuscript and the Dpe bsdur ma notes

Narthang (W2KG5015 vol. 46 pp. 289-291, I2KG212330 img. 289-291) agrees with the Peking print at the title's Sanskrit «སྭ་པ་ནེ་ཀྵ», the jātaka garland «ཛ་ཏ་པ» and the swimming «རྐྱལ»; it has «བཏབ་ལ་ཉལ» where Derge has «བརྟགས་ལ་ཉལ» and the Peking print «བརྟགས་པ་ལ་ཉལ»; its snake is «སྦྲུལ» as in the Derge etext (Peking «སྤྲུལ»); its Amoghasiddhi line is three syllables «བྱ་ཤ…ཏེ» at the end of a line, the middle one unsettled by eye. The Dpe bsdur ma notes give Peking and Narthang together at most places, and «ཤིང་ཏེའུ» for Peking against «ཤང་ཏེའུ» for Narthang at the Amoghasiddhi line. W22704 (I3297 img. 291-293) is the same pages as a coarse bitmap, not a second witness.
