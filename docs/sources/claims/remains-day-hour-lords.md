# The day's and the hour's earth lords at the sending of the remains

The day's and the hour's earth lords at the sending of the remains (WB vol. 2, p. 97, read on the scan of the Gser thang reprint):

- The day's god is Sermdzö the yellow, staying wherever the day's count is.
- The day's earth lords, Libyi the black and the *khru lu*, sit by the day's animal: mouse, the beer jar; ox, the granary; tiger, the door; hare, the door bolt; dragon, the mill; snake, the rope; horse, the saddle; sheep, the trough; monkey, the axe; bird, the plates; dog, the threshold; pig, the hoe. Where they are met, a remedy is needed; the seats, the harms and the remedies are set out in full in ch. 31, p. 224 (earth-lords.md, O8).
- The hour's god (Yumdzö the blue) gathers at whatever the hour is; the earth lords who dwell in the place are asked of its old people.
