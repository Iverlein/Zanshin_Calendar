# The history of the four sheets has no colophon in any of four prints, and the prints differ in the title, in one sentence on the other three sheets and in the names of the chain (RD383)

RD383. The history of the four sheets («Rje btsun Lho brag pa'i khyad par gyi gdams pa snyan gyi shog dril bzhi'i lo rgyus gzhung lhan thabs dang bcas pa») stands in four prints read here (the Marpa gsung 'bum vol. 4, the Bka' brgyud sngags mdzod nag 'gros, the Shechen Gdams ngag mdzod and the Dpal spungs Gdams ngag mdzod vol. 8), and in none of them has it a colophon: no writer, place or date follows its last line, which is the oral instruction of «Cutting the bardo by mind» (dds.19:p131, dds.22:p1124-1125, dds.9:p181-182, dds.40:p234). What stands just before the title in the Bka' brgyud sngags mdzod is the colophon of the previous text, by Karma Ngag dbang yon tan rgya mtsho (Kong sprul) at Dpal spungs (dds.22:p1078-1080), and in the Dpal spungs volume the previous text's closing «Rgya guhya» (dds.40:p202-204); neither belongs to the history. The four prints differ in small things: the title, «Rje btsun Lho brag pa'i» in three and «Sgra sgyur Mar pa lo tsa'i» (the translator Marpa's) in the Shechen print (dds.9:p158-159); the sentence on the other three sheets, «gnang bar yod pa» (given) in the Marpa volume and the Bka' brgyud sngags mdzod and «ma gnang bar yod pa» (not given) in the Shechen print and the Dpal spungs volume, the second of which fits the story that the sheets were kept back (dds.19:p97-102); the names in the chain (dds.19:p97-102).
