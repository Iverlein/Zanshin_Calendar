# gnas skyug or gnas sgyug: what the search and the print show

«གནས་སྐྱུག» or «གནས་སྒྱུག» (question 356, and 211's last part): Mipham's own print at 5× (I2029 img. 720, l. 2) is not distinct (the machine reads a ka-based stack, «སྐྱུག», in both words). BDRC's full-text search (2026-11-01) finds «གནས་སྒྱུག» in the verse of WB itself as other works quote it (the etexts of the *Kun 'dus chen mo* and the *Do shal* give the verse "gnas sgyug spar kha 'thab na ngan"), five hits, and «གནས་སྐྱུག» only in the 1996 notes (vol. 2, p. 526) and in works that are not about it; so WB's own spelling is «སྒྱུག», the notes' etext spells «སྐྱུག», no print carved at a higher resolution has been read.
