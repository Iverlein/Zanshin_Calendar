# The twenty-six places not allowed even if clean

The twenty-six places where remains are not sent even if the ground is clean (གཙང་ཀྱང་མི་རུང, gtsang kyang mi rung), each with what follows if they are (WB vol. 2, p. 104, read on the scan; the numbers the 1996 editors'):

| # | Name | The place | What follows |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | the blue dragon | where a stream divides | abscesses and the *dmu chu* swelling |
| 2 | the red bird's beak | a corner of ground | harm from the *rbad 'dre* and great demons |
| 3 | the grey tiger | a crossroads | lameness and stiffness, blindness and limping |
| 4 | the black yak | a black bank, a black boulder | death by landslide or by a horse |
| 5 | the demon | a black rock | the line ends |
| 6 | the ogress's crotch | a ravine | death in childbirth and by suicide |
| 7 | under Meru | a stūpa's shadow | the great hostile |
| 8 | the ocean's floor | a pond or well | the lord bad and small (as printed) |
| 9 | under the turtle | an assembly hall, guest house or monastery | the family goes under another's hearth |
| 10 | the beast of prey's tooth | a rock edge | short lives |
| 11 | the weapon's point | a mountain's or house's corner, three-cornered ground | death by weapons |
| 12 | the nāgas' | a spring | leprosy |
| 13 | the earth lords' row | holes in a bank | many dull and mute |
| 14 | the *gnyan* | a boulder, an old bank | «རྨང་པོར» (sense not settled) |
| 15 | the *the'u brang*'s | a tree's foot | poverty |
| 16 | the corpse's swelling(?) | where smells are given off | bad for those after |
| 17 | the earth-corpse water hollow | sand | death abroad |
| 18 | the earth's work held | ash heaps, ridges | endless funeral costs |
| 19 | the earth snake's tongue | where three ravines meet | at last the line ends |
| 20 | the *btsan*'s house | a red rock | short life |
| 21 | the gods' and demons' street | where rock waters fight | many fevers |
| 22 | the god's upper part | the local god's place, a shrine's upper part cut | lawsuits and ruin |
| 23 | the gods' and demons' front | a mountain's narrow top | the "mouth of people" hostile |
| 24 | where many gather to talk | — | quarrels in the household |
| 25 | where many look | empty road heads, a forking road | good repaid with ill |
| 26 | where earth swellings gather | a ravine's foot | a woman's corpse brings many enemies |

Elsewhere a grave is made and the remains go to an auspicious place; the place is then repaired with fumigation, incense, medicines, grains, the five jewels, five silks, the three whites and sweets, a square stone drawn with the eight auspicious signs and substances, first offerings and words of truth, and first offerings again where the bones are taken.
