# The midnight line of the song reads «emanations of Māra» in most prints, «magical display of Māra» in Gtsug lag phreng ba's Life; no cause or remedy is added (RD397, q1114)

The line of the middle watch is not worded the same in every print. The Lives of Marpa and the Mgur mtsho have «at midnight it shows the emanations of Māra» (ནམ་གུང་ལ་བདུད་ཀྱི་སྤྲུལ་པ་སྟོན, nam gung la bdud kyi sprul pa ston); Dpa' bo Gtsug lag phreng ba's Life, in the Thrangu print, has «shows the magical display of Māra» (ནམ་གུང་བདུད་ཀྱི་ཆོ་འཕྲུལ་སྟོན, nam gung bdud kyi cho 'phrul ston). Both are one reading: a work of Māra, not to be trusted. The older words say nothing of the dream's content or of a spirit class, so the Karmapa's «power of spirits» (གདོན་དབང, gdon dbang) is further from them than Gtsug lag phreng ba's «magical display». Neither print adds a cause (a demon sending it or the sleeper's mind deceiving itself) or a remedy.
