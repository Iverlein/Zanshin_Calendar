# KD's digest of the 28 meetings: WB's lines, five of them in other words

Mkhyen rab nor bu's digest of WB's fifth section (KD vol. 3, pp. 731-732) gives the twenty-eight named meetings (Joy to Increase) a short reading each, in WB's order and names, and in most lines in WB's meaning (WB vol. 2, p. 333, [combinations.md](../combinations.md), *The text of pp. 331–333*). It differs in five lines, which are the digest's own wording or a copyist's:

- **People** (སྐྱེ་དགུ, skye dgu): the digest, "the people's ease"; WB, "the woman at home is happy".
- **Banner** (རྒྱལ་མཚན): the digest, "clothes and goods are got"; WB, "clothes and the like in plenty".
- **Lord of death** (འཆི་བདག): the digest, "fear of death begins"; WB, "death comes".
- **Pestle** (གཏུན་ཤིང, gtun shing): the digest, "a broken head, fear"; WB, «གཏུན་ལ་གཏུན་ཤིང་རིག་པར་བྱ», a line [combinations.md](../combinations.md) left out as having no meaning the verse gives. The digest gives the pestle a plain reading, which fits the verse's own «བཀྲ་ཤིས་ཟད་དང་མགོ་རྣམས་འཆག» ("heads break").
- **Increase**: the digest, "goods and enjoyments grow"; WB, "goods grow".

The name of the sixth-from-last meeting is spelt «སྟག་མྱོས» (tiger-drunk) in the digest and «རྟག་མྱོས» in WB; the digest's spelling agrees with the Tibetan name of the Sanskrit one. The digest omits WB's closing line for earth with wind ("some also say works of increasing goods are done") and prints «དགུ་བོ་དབྱུང་བའི་ལས» for water with wind where WB has «དགྲ་བོ་ཕྱུང་བའི་ལས» (the enemy driven out); «དགུ» is read as an OCR slip for «དགྲ», the etext alone. The ten element pairs and their results are WB's, and so is the weighing: three good, three sustaining, three bad and one cut-off. Etext only. WB stands above the digest in every line.
