# Ch. 32: digging in the part where Lto-'phye lies

WB vol. 2, ch. 32, p. 282 (img. 288, machine): digging in the part of the ground where the earth lord Lto-'phye (ལྟོ་འཕྱེ) lies is harmful, by the part dug. The ground is held as nine parts (དགུ་ཆ); digging in the part where Lto-'phye is kills the parents, sons and brothers, the wife and one's friends; digging at his back kills the digger himself or makes him move house and lose goods; at the tail the elephant and the mare are near, and cattle die and strength fails (reading of «མཇུག་མ་གླང་ཆེན་རྟ་མ་ཉེ། བ་ལང་འཆི་ཞིང་སྟོབས་ཉམས་འགྱུར» doubtful). Which part of the ground is the belly, the back and the tail follows the figure of p. 281 (the body ten *mtho*). No ground-rite is needed on a sage's site, in a cow's resting place, a cave, a hilltop, hard ground, an empty house, flagstones, twin stupas, falling-water islands and lakeshores. The ill of a high or low site is little there.
