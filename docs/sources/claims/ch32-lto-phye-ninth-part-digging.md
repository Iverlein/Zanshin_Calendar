# Ch. 32: digging in the part where Lto-'phye lies

WB vol. 2, ch. 32, p. 282 (img. 288; the lines on the parts, l. 17–19, read on the scan by RD91, 2026-10-28, on the Gser thang reprint and the 1996 print; the rest machine): digging in the part of the ground where the earth lord Lto-'phye (ལྟོ་འཕྱེ, lto 'phye) lies is harmful, by the part dug. The place is named «དགུ་ཆའི་ས», the ground of the nine parts or of the ninth part (དགུ་ཆ, dgu cha); digging in the part where Lto-'phye lies kills the parents, sons and brothers, the wife and one's friends; digging at his back kills the digger himself or makes him move house and lose goods; digging at the tail kills elephants, horses, water buffaloes (མ་ཧེ, ma he) and cattle, and strength declines («མཇུག་མ་གླང་ཆེན་རྟ་མ་ཧེ། །བ་ལང་འཆི་ཞིང་སྟོབས་ཉམས་འགྱུར», ha with e on both scans, where Yigdzin-1 read «ཉེ»). Whether «དགུ་ཆ» is the ninth small part or the nine small parts of one great part of p. 281's square is not stated. Which part of the ground is the belly, the back and the tail follows the figure of p. 281 (the body ten *mtho*). No ground-rite is needed on a sage's site, in a cow's resting place, a cave, a hilltop, hard ground, an empty house, flagstones, twin stupas, falling-water islands and lakeshores. The ill of a high or low site is little there.
