# Ch. 13 strip charts: pp. 145-146 read by place, the same names, many cells empty

On the Gser thang reprint the four strip charts of pp. 145-146 (monkey and bird, dog and pig) have the frame of p. 142's charts and can be read by place at 1.55x, half a chart at a time. The same small set of names fills the cells: the burial-taker (dur len), the bearer (bshan pa), the inner-demon (nang bdud), ba dan, the planet-head (gza' thog), the la-knower (bla mkhyen, probable), the vase-demon (bum bdud) and a word of two syllables at the top and right (not settled), with the nāga-planet (klu gza') and numerals as the strip's right caption and the Heruka words as its left caption. Many cells are drawn and empty, and the filled and empty cells differ from chart to chart (the bird chart's left column has two empty tiers, the pig chart's top row's third and bottom row's second cells are empty). The pig chart's left third tier probably reads the demons of the morning rising and the sun's rising (nam langs, nyi shar) of p. 142's heading row. The multi-line cells and numerals are mostly unsettled; what the cells stand for is not stated on the pages.
