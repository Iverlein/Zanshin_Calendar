# Ringed round by crows, hungry ghosts and men with a black birthmark

Image group: the dreamer ill. Read by 1 text: Four Tantras (for a sick dreamer).
Offered only when the dreamer is ill: every row is a reading for a sick dreamer (the Four Tantras above WB ch. 25).

| Text | Verdict (tone) | Passage | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Four Tantras (for a sick dreamer) | ringed round by crows, hungry ghosts (ཡི་དགས, yi dags) and the rme can (རྨེ་ཅན): a sign that the lord of death (འཆི་བདག, 'chi bdag) has seized the sick; dreamed constantly by the sick it means death, for one without illness it is not certain, and the rite of service (རིམ་གྲོ, rim gro) frees him (bad) | `gt:i42-43` | machine |

The word rme can is a person with a black birthmark or mole on the face, the sense of the medical dictionaries and the Four Tantras' own commentators; the dictionaries outside medicine give a base-born man, a butcher (RD271, gt-rme-can-a-man-with-a-black-birthmark; Byang pa's and the Drikung's rje can is a variant of it). Which sense the verse means is not settled, the first being the medical one; the verdict does not rest on it, so the image is not listed in dream-unsettled-words.

The verse is the Explanatory Tantra's (བཤད་རྒྱུད, bshad rgyud) ch. 7 on the Drathang print (RD268); the Serthang scan lacks the page. The rule on who dies, the cause and the six kinds of dream are in gt-dream-death-rule, the whole list in gt-dream-death-images, the one-syllable differences from the four commentaries in gt-dream-verse-variants. This image claim was added by RD274 (2026-11-09) so that the picker holds every image of the verse; the Drathang passage is a machine reading, the lines read by eye in part (gt:i42-43).
