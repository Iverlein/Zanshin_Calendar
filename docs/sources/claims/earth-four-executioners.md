# The four executioners and the four earth years

In UT's table of the clans' years the earth clan's power year is written «གཤེད་བཞི», the four executioners (ut:p56), where LT gives ox and sheep (lt:p26-28); for the earth clan all four earth animals (dragon, dog, ox, sheep) are its cut-offs and power years. For a wood clan the four earth animals are the great and small cut-off and the great and small friend year (wb.2:p499-500; lt:p26-28).

Inferred, not stated: the "four executioners' pebble door" of WB's la verdict (wb.2:p52-54) may be the four earth corners of the board; no manual read says so of the la board.

UT's table is read in BDRC's etext only, and its rows are garbled there; the scan has not been read.
