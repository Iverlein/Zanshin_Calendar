# WB ch. 13: the nine flying squares of the sme ba (pp. 126, 128)

Pages 126 and 128 of WB's chapter 13 show nine 3 by 3 squares, one for each digit 1 to 9 as the centre: p. 126 has the squares with centres 4, 9, 2, 3 and 5, p. 128 those with centres 7, 8, 1 and 6. Each is the flying square of its centre: the cells of the square with centre 5 (4 9 2 / 3 5 7 / 8 1 6) each moved on by the centre minus five, 9 passing to 1.

| centre | rows |
|---|---|
| 1 | 9 5 7 / 8 1 3 / 4 6 2 |
| 2 | 1 6 8 / 9 2 4 / 5 7 3 |
| 3 | 2 7 9 / 1 3 5 / 6 8 4 |
| 4 | 3 8 1 / 2 4 6 / 7 9 5 |
| 5 | 4 9 2 / 3 5 7 / 8 1 6 |
| 6 | 5 1 3 / 4 6 8 / 9 2 7 |
| 7 | 6 2 4 / 5 7 9 / 1 3 8 |
| 8 | 7 3 5 / 6 8 1 / 2 4 9 |
| 9 | 8 4 6 / 7 9 2 / 3 5 1 |

The pages carry no words, so how the squares are used with the sme ba of the year is not stated on them.
