# «mtshan brtas» in the Karmapa's «tho rangs mtshan brtas 'di ltar byung» announces the dawn dream that follows; its spelling has no second witness (q1086, RD401)

The third Karmapa's dream verses (rrd.5:p57-59) open: he prayed to the Lord at dusk, and «ཐོ་རངས་མཚན་བརྟས་འདི་ལྟར་བྱུང» (at dawn the «mtshan brtas» came thus); then, as a reason for writing it down, the rule «dusk's dream is habit, midnight's the power of spirits, whatever comes at dawn is true» in Marpa's name, and «a few dreams» set down in writing; the dream of dawn follows from p. 58 («ཐོ་རངས་གཉིད་ཀྱི་རྨི་ལམ་ན», in the dream of sleep at dawn). So «མཚན་བརྟས» stands where the dawn's dream is announced and what follows it is that dream: it is read as the night's sign, the dream that is then told, not as a separate omen, but the verse does not gloss it and this is the reading from the context. Whether the word is a spelling of «མཚན་བརྟགས» or «མཚན་རྟགས» is not shown: BDRC's exact-string search for the whole line «ཐོ་རངས་མཚན་བརྟས་འདི་ལྟར་བྱུང» finds one hit, this volume (VE30541_005, offset 85643), and for «ཐོ་རངས་གང་བྱུང་བདེན་ནོ» the same one; no second copy of the verses is on BDRC's full text, and the etext has no scan attached, so the spelling cannot be checked against a print.
