# No print or catalogue read names an author for the history of the four sheets or for the Breakthrough's companion method; BDRC credits Marpa only in the gsung 'bum record, and its work record in the Gdams ngag mdzod has no creator (RD383)

RD383. No print and no catalogue read names an author for the history of the four sheets or for the companion method of the Breakthrough (the death-sign teaching): no colophon in four prints (see dds-four-sheets-history-prints-differ), no entry for either text in the catalogue of Marpa's collected works (the catalogue lists nine items for the four sheets, root verses and commentaries or instructions, and no history, dds.44:p19-20), and in Kong sprul's and Tāranātha's notes the colophons name the writer of the catalogue and of the supplement only (dds.41:p547, dds.41:p116). BDRC's records: the work of the history inside Marpa's gsung 'bum (MW1KG14303_4AAEF5, work WA0XL4AAEF5910733) carries one creator, Marpa Lo tsa ba Chos kyi blo gros (P2636), role «main author» (R0ER0019); the work record of the same title in the Gdams ngag mdzod, the Bka' brgyud sngags mdzod and the Dpal spungs Gdams ngag mdzod (WA0XLF2F780077D61, instances MW23605_F2F780, MW20876_A2F06C, MW20877_313D01) carries no creator, and the collection records name Kong sprul as compiler of the Gdams ngag mdzod (P264, role «compiler», R0ER0015) and, with Dil mgo Mkhyen brtse, of the Bka' brgyud sngags mdzod. So Marpa's authorship of the history rests on BDRC's attribution of the gsung 'bum part, not on any text read, and the words of the history contradict it (the history speaks of Marpa in the third person and of a later «I»). The teaching on the death signs ends the transference text as «the companion method of the Breakthrough» and is attributed to no one by its own words (dds.19:p131).
