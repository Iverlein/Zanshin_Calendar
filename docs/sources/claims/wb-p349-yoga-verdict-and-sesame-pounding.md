# WB pp. 349–350: the yogas' verdict counts the 27; Sesame-pounding opens p. 350

The bad yogas of p. 349, "six, nine, ten, thirteen and eighteen", count the twenty-seven yogas in the order of the verses of p. 347 (not a shorter list): taken in that order the sixth is the one «རབ་སྟོངས» (works end badly), the ninth «གཟེར» (Whirl, great harm), the tenth «འབྲས» (Fruit, nāga illness), the thirteenth «མ་ཆེན» (Great, leprosy) and the eighteenth «ཡོངས་འཇོམས» (All-destroying, death in prison), all of them the bad ones by their own results, and they coincide with Atigaṇḍa, Śūla, Gaṇḍa, Vyāghāta and the like in the Indian list. This is a count of the machine's lines, not yet of the scan; the "good" list, with the six again, stays unread. Sesame-pounding (ཏིལ་བརྡུང་, til brdung) is not missing from the print: p. 349 ends with Virtue (དགེ་བ) and its woodcut; p. 350 (1996 img. 358, read at 2.4×) opens with the line «ཏིལ་བརྡུང་…» beside a woodcut of a man pounding in a mortar, then House-born (ཁྱིམ་སྐྱེས, «ཁྱིམ་སྐྱེས་ལ་ནི་ཞིང་ལས་དང་། ཁྱིམ་གསར་འཛིན་པ་ཀུན་ལ་བཟང་») and Merchant (ཚོང་པ); the machine dropped the line with the page break. The table of p. 351 lists the same order: Spreading, Wiping, Virtue, Sesame-pounding, House-born, Merchant, Bishti. The body of the Sesame-pounding verse was not read letter by letter.
