# The marriage wheels on the couple's bodies and signs

The marriage wheels of WB's marriage section (vol. 2, pp. 407–409). By letters: the husband's and wife's name letters are set against the five days Joyful, Good, Victorious, Empty and Complete (A, E, U, E, O), and a meeting brings illness and ruin. The wheel of ease: stars of the going day set three by three on the bodies of husband and wife; if the wife's name-star goes to the husband's heart they are long-lived and loving; a planet of merit on the husband's heart means victory; the surplus planet on the wife's heart means five sons; the wife's name-star in the husband's secret place no quarrel; the husband's in hers nine sons. The wheel of many children (four army leaders at the quarters, Jupiter, Moon, the surplus and Pasang counted seven stars each): the man powerful and many children, or both poor. The jackal wheel (the «ཡུགས་ས»): the husband's name-star in the three stars of the mouth, he dies in three months; in the middle, a year; at the end, three years; in the body, long illness. The snake wheel of the bride's illness: four sinful planets in the snake's body embracing the woman bring illness and suffering; those of merit, none; the Lord-of-the-Hood wheel by the three channels: one to four of the Sun, Mars, Saturn and Rāhu bring illness, death, loss of wealth and kin.
