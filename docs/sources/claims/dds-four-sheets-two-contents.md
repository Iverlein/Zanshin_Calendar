# The four sheets are given two contents: the Net-eyed wheel, the Breakthrough, Cutting the bardo and the Great Seal (the history, the Drukpa records, Kong sprul's catalogue), or the final meanings of Hevajra, Guhyasamāja, Mahāmāyā and the Four Seats (Tāranātha, the Khrid brgya) (RD383)

RD383. The four sheets are given two different contents in the sources read. (a) The history, Padma dkar po's record, the Rgyal sras biography and Kong sprul's catalogue: the Net-eyed wheel of channels and winds (Nāropa), the Breakthrough transference (Nāropa), Cutting the bardo by mind (Maitrī) and the Great Seal condensed in words (Maitrī) — the history says Nāropa gave the first and the transference, Maitrī the other two (dds.19:p97-102, dds.42:p165-166, dds.43:p138-139, dds.41:p496). (b) Tāranātha's supplement and the Khrid brgya's guidance text no. 89: four sheets each the final meaning of one tantra, Hevajra, Guhyasamāja, Mahāmāyā and the Four Seats, with Nāropa, Dpal Ye shes snying po, Kukuripa and the yoginī Chu shing snye ma can as the teachers named, given by Marpa to Mtshur ston Dbang gi rdo rje at four great offerings (dds.41:p106-108, dds.41:p304-306). Tāranātha adds that many old writings of the four sheets are kept and that a new piece with the name «four sheets» is now everywhere; the passage does not say which of the two contents the new piece has. Kong sprul's Shes bya mdzod calls the four sheets a right support in accord with Nāropa's Six Teachings, beside the Vajra Words of the Whispered Lineage (dds.45:p219). Zhu chen's lineage, which he gives for the Khrid brgya's no. 89 (content b), and Kong sprul's catalogue (content a) name nearly the same holders after Mtshur ston Dbang nge (dds.46:p104, dds.41:p496).
