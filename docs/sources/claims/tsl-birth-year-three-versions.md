# Rtsa gsum gling pa's birth: three years in the lineage's own sources, no month or day

Three dates are printed for the birth of Rtsa gsum gling pa (རྩ་གསུམ་གླིང་པ, rtsa gsum gling pa), none with a month or a day:

| Source | Year as printed | Passage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the modern editor's preface to TSL vol. 30 | the wood-dog year (ཤིང་ཁྱི་ལོ, shing khyi lo) of the twelfth sixty-year cycle (རབ་བྱུང, rab byung), given as 1694 | tsl.30:i4-5 |
| Jamyang Khyentse Wangpo's brief life, in the same volume | within the eleventh cycle; the sign of the year «does not appear clearly» | tsl.30:i565 |
| the introduction to KTD's translation of the autobiography on padmasambhava.org | 1685 | pbc:p1 |

KTD's translation of Khyentse's life (pbc:p2) repeats Khyentse: the eleventh cycle, the year unclear. Kongtrul's life (tsl.30:i575, birth sentence read in the etext) names no year. The autobiography itself (the Zhal gsung ma, tsl.30:i36-38 and the birth chapter at images 43-46, etext) tells the birth and the naming without a date.

The three do not agree and the pages give no reason for any of them. All three are writings of the lineage's holders or translations by them (TSL's modern edition, Khyentse as printed in it, KTD); which stands is for the owner to settle (rule of `.agents/rules/sources.md`: TSL's treasures rank with Mipham, the modern matter and KTD below). The years are given as printed and not converted.
