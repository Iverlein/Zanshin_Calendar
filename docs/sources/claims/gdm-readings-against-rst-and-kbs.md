# The manuscript, RST's etext and KBS's verses are one text; where they differ (the thirds of the night, the dawn third, what KBS leaves out)

Reading the manuscript's verses (machine reading; img. 88 lines 1-6 and img. 89 lines 1-2 by eye) against RST's etext and the KBS article's verses (the Yigdzin reading of kbs:p68-70, kbs:p72-78), the three are one text with these differences that carry a meaning or a spelling the others do not:

- the thirds of the night: the manuscript has «དེ་ཡང་མཚན་མོ་ཐུན་གྱི་སུམ་ཆར་བགོས» (seen on the scan); KBS has «མཚན་མོ་ཐུན་གྱི་སུམ་ཆར་བགོས» (the same «སུམ་ཆར»); RST has «ནམ་གྱི་མཚན་མོ་ཐུན་གྱི་གསུམ་ཆ་བགོས». BDRC's exact-phrase search for the manuscript's and KBS's «མཚན་མོ་ཐུན་གྱི་སུམ་ཆར་བགོས» finds only the KBS article's print: RST, MDP and Padma rdo rje's work do not read it so;
- the dawn third: the manuscript has «ཐོ་རངས་དུས་ཀྱི་ནམ་གྱི་གསུམ་ཆ་ལ། བཟང་ངན་འབྲས་བུ་འབྱུང་ཕྱིར་བརྟག་པ་གཅེས» (seen on the scan, img. 89 line 1); KBS has the same; RST has «ཐོ་རངས་ཁ་ཡིན་ནམ་གྱི་གསུམ་ཆ་ལ». BDRC's search for «ཐོ་རངས་དུས་ཀྱི་ནམ་གྱི་གསུམ་ཆ་ལ» finds only the second print of the KBS article (MW1CZ690_FBE63E, quoting Klong rdol bla ma by name); «ཐོ་རངས་ཁ་ཡིན་ནམ་གྱི་གསུམ་ཆ» finds only RST. The line of the dawn third as the clarifier, «ཐོ་རངས་གསུམ་ཆ་ཕྱིས་འབྱུང་གསལ་བྱེད་ཡིན», is the same in all;
- KBS and the manuscript agree against RST's etext in «སྙན་གྲགས་འབྱུང་ངོ་ཡོད» (RST «སྙན་གྲགས་ངོ་སོ་ཡོད»), «བརྡུངས» (RST «བརྡུང»), and «སྙན་གྲགས་འབྱུང» (RST «སྙན་གྲགས་འགྱུར»); the manuscript has also «རྒ་མེད» (machine; not settled) where RST and KBS have «སྒ་མེད» for the unsaddled donkey. RST and the manuscript read «ཁྱེར» in the weapon line where KBS has «འཁྱེར»; KBS and RST agree against the manuscript in «དགྲས» (manuscript «དགྲ») and «ཤིན་ཏུ» (manuscript «ཐིན་ཏུ», a spelling);
- the manuscript has what KBS leaves out: the couplet on former lives («ལ་ལ་ཚེ་རབས་སྔོན་མའི་བྱེད་པ་ཡིན། དེ་ལ་ངེས་བཟུང་མི་འགྲུབ་སྣ་ཚོགས་འབྱུང་»), the closing verses of the good signs (fine forms, savoury food, pleasant words, friends met, glad on waking: img. 89-90) and the whole passage for the one who meditates (a book found, flying, a jewelled sword, a boat, sun and moon rising: img. 92-93). RST has all three; KBS's four blocks quote the verses selectively;
- spellings of the machine reading only (not settled): «བནྡྷེ», «ལྭ», «སྤྲུལ», «བྲག་རོང», «བཟོ་དང་ཟམ་པ», «ཐལ་བ» where KBS has «བྲལ་བ» and RST «དྲལ་བ» in the first death omen.

So KBS's verses are nearer the manuscript than RST's etext is, in the two lines that matter for the thirds of the night; the three are not three works. Which of the manuscript or the KBS article's source is the older text of the two spellings the search cannot say.
