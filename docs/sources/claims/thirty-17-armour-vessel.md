# The thirty: the seventeenth point, the blazing jewel, for armour and the copper vessel

The seventeenth of the thirty starting points (སྐོར་འགོ, skor 'go), from the mouth of the blazing jewel (རིན་ཆེན་འོད་འབར་བ, rin chen 'od 'bar ba), is read for armour and the copper vessel (གོ་ཁྲབ་ཟངས་ཐེར, go khrab zangs ther). Its eight pebbles are laid as for every point (claims thirty-count and thirty-eight-fighters), each white or black, and read so:

| Pebble | Asks | White | Black | Remedy for black |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Whether the two treasuries are filled with gold, silver and wealth | Full, good | The treasury is empty: bad in the place of wealth | — |
| 2 | Whether the house is good | A house heaped with the light of jewels, good | In the place of the castle and of power there is no light of jewels, bad | Protecting prosperity (གཡང་སྐྱབས, g.yang skyabs) |
| 3 | Whether the silk throne is high | The silk-tiger throne's height is high, good | The throne is low, bad | Strive at service rites (རིམ་གྲོ, rim gro) |
| 4 | Whether the butter lake swirls | The ocean of butter swirls, good | The ocean of food and wealth does not swirl and dries, bad | — |
| 5 | Whether the cheese mountain is heaped | Heaped, good | The cheese mountain is not formed: bad in the place of dairy | — |
| 6 | Whether the meat mountain has collapsed | Not collapsed, heaped, good | Collapsed: bad in the place of livestock | — |
| 7 | Whether the beer pot's bottom is broken | Not broken, good | Broken: loss of utensils and enjoyments | — |
| 8 | Whether the dough is lost down the precipice | Not lost, good | Lost: bad for food and wealth in the later part of life | The hundred-and-four giving, protecting prosperity and calling back prosperity (གཡང་ལོན, g.yang lon) make it fairly good; gathering the first share of food and protecting the food's prosperity helps |

Whose year is reckoned: For the copper vessel, the year of the asker who buys it (notes, p. 532).

Which of the eight fighting pebbles is which check is not stated; the order of p. 453 is the likely one (claim thirty-board-groups; question 259).
