# WB ch. 35 pp. 447–448: star and black reckoning, its rank among the fields and the sources named for it

Pages 447–448 place star and black reckoning (སྐར་ནག་རྩིས་རིག, skar nag rtsis rig) among the fields: it belongs to the four Vedas, comes under the five great fields, is the chief of the five minor ones, and every sūtra from the Lalitavistara on is said to end in the path of reckoning. The sources the verse names for it are the Sūtra of the Great Peacock, the Sun Heart sūtras, the Mañjuśrī root tantra, the Kālacakra, the Ocean of Ḍākinīs, the Stag rna and Nāgārjuna's Prajñādaṇḍa. The author gives his reason for treating it: in the snow land many call themselves learned in reckoning and are lost in its ways. Its master is called a knower of the three times or a god-knower, and rests on the Wheel of Time (Kālacakra), whose outer form star reckoning teaches. Machine reading, MITRA agreeing; no rule of any day or reckoning is stated here.
