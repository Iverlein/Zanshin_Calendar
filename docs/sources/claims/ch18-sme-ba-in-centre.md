# The nine sme ba entering the centre: results (ch. 18)

When each of the nine *sme ba* (the numbered patches of the Chinese reckoning) enters the centre of the year, p. 212 gives: the one (white), epidemics, unrest and great water; the two (black), famine, unrest, piercing disease; the three (dark blue), swellings and dried fruit, bad for children; the four (green), bad for women, smallpox and plague; the five (yellow), bad for the wheel of the dharma and the virtuous friends, a bad year and unrest; the six (white), bad for the high-born, spirit sickness; the seven (red), bad for servants and livestock; the eight (white), a great red wind, drought and fighting, danger to children; the nine (red), bad for the old class of kings, children and livestock, and grain famine.
