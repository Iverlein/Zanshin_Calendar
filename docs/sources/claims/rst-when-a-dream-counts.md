# RST: when a dream counts and how its fruit is told

RST (the dream part, etext only): a dream with no root in habit and very wondrous, after which the mind is glad on waking, certainly bears good fruit; if the mind is not glad on waking, bad things come; if it is frightening and the dreamer wakes shaken, it is a demon's trick (བདུད, bdud). A marvel dreamed again and again is either the power of mantra or the trick of the eight classes of spirits (སྡེ་བརྒྱད, sde brgyad), and the two are told apart with wisdom by their mark. The images also depend on who appears: the same figures that give food, speak kindly, laugh and look kindly are a glad sign, and those that show a fearful form, scowl and rage are a bad one. For one in meditation practice the images are read as the practice striking home (finding a book, flying, climbing a tree, debate, gardens, music, a jewelled sword, fine clothes) or as signs of realisation (new lands and houses, children smiling and giving flowers, fruit, food and drink).
