# The black times' remedy at the door's right is «སྤྲ་ཁྱིམ»; the first letter of the stack is not settled

On p. 236 the black times (no. 11) are met by drawing, at the door's right, «སྤྲ་ཁྱིམ», at the left a swastika (གཡུང་དྲུང) and the sun and the moon. The stack at the head of the first word is blotted on the scan: the 1996 print's etext reads «སྦྲ», the Sakya Centre print's etext «སྤྱི»; the Zhol print's etext (I4CZ74285, p. 1160 of its etext, in a passage on setting up a door) has the same word as «སྤྲ་ཁྱིམ». What is drawn is not stated; no gloss found.
