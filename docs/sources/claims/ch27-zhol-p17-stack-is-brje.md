# The stack of p. 17, l. 6 is «བརྗེ» on the Zhol print, not an «ནྡ» stack

On the Zhol print (WBZ, I1KG1710 img. 647, the row «བོང་དྲེལ་བ[stack]ེའི་དཀྱིལ་འཁོར», a crop of x 1860–2060, y 420–540 at 8×) the stack after ba has a flat head with a dot, an e-vowel above and a long tail curving down to the right, the foot of ཇ, with no ན below it: the shape of the «བརྗེ» of the black verse on img. 659, so Yigdzin-1's «བནྡེ» there is the machine's lean to the common word. On img. 650 (the row that opens «ཕུངས་ན་[stack]ེའི་ཕུར་ཁ་ལངས», x 200–420, y 140–250 at 7×) the stack has the same tall form with e above and a long tail below, but its head is fused and the base letters cannot be cut; it agrees in shape, no more. So «བརྗེ» stands at two places read on the Zhol print (p. 23, l. 25 earlier; p. 17, l. 6 here), the third consistent in shape; the stack is not «བནྡེ» (a monk) there.
