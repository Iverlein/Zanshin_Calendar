# LGD's demon who comes alongside is the pair of the co-born god in other texts, kind not settled

The line of LGD p. 970 reads on the scan «རང་དང་ལྷན་ཅིག་སྐྱེས་པའི་ལྷ་དང༔ མཉམ་པོར་འོང་བའི་བདུད་དང༔ ཕྱི་རོལ་གྱི་ལྷ་འདྲེ་འཁྲུགས་པའི་ཆོ་འཕྲུལ»: the god born together with oneself, the demon who comes alongside (མཉམ་པོར་འོང་བའི་བདུད, mnyam por 'ong ba'i bdud), the tricks of outer gods and spirits in tumult (I1KG57 img. 989, last line; Yigdzin-1 and the eye agree, and the etext). The two are a stock pair in other texts, found by BDRC's exact-string search on 2026-11-10 (snippets only; no page opened). The phrase «མཉམ་པོར་འོང་བའི་བདུད» has 68 hits; in the Rin chen gter mdzod it stands in rites of dispatching an enemy and of subduing, in lines where one is to be parted from the one and joined to the other: in one snippet «the god who comes alongside» is set against «the demon born together» (a fanged beast turned the wrathful oath-bound's messenger), in another «meet the demon who comes alongside» follows «part from the god born together»; the same line occurs in Ngag dbang dpal bzang's collected works (a rite against an enemy). The co-born demon (ལྷན་ཅིག་སྐྱེས་པའི་བདུད, lhan cig skyes pa'i bdud; 88 hits) is in the Chöd teaching the demon of self-clinging, one's own ignorance in the form of dual appearance (a Gdams ngag mdzod snippet). So the pair "god born together / demon who comes alongside" is a god and a demon that each go with a person; LGD sets the second beside the first and before the outer spirits, which fits an attendant demon that is the first's opposite, not an outsider. Whether it is the Chöd demon of self-clinging or an attendant adversary of the person is not decided by the snippets, so the question stays open, narrowed.
