# The run of deaths at p. 104 on the Taikhang: the same ga-stack, held with doubt

On the Taikhang reproduction (I5878 img. 160, the Zhol print's img. 778; 8280 px, 2026-11-03) the stack of the run of deaths at the two places of WB vol. 2, p. 104, ll. 27-28 (the heading «ཤི་འགྲམས་སྒབ», and «སྤར་ཁའི་སྒབ་བཅད་ཐབས», on the Zhol blocks the third and fourth lines of that image) was cropped at 3-5x and has the same shape at both places as at the Zhol blocks' places read before: ས over a subjoined letter with an open bowl on the left, then བ. The impression is of ག, not of the crossed ཀ, but the print is blotted at the heading and the reading is held with doubt, not as read syllable by syllable. The remaining places (p. 105, l. 15; p. 106, ll. 2, 15; p. 108, ll. 11, 24, 26) were not read by eye in this reading: the Zhol etext is too garbled there to place them by its own text (it has «ཤིཀྐབཆོད» for p. 106, l. 2 and «ཤེསྒབ་བཅད» for p. 108, l. 11), and the Zhol scan's blocks are too faint at 1-1.6x for the stack. The question stays narrowed to the one word «ཤི་སྒབ» (sgab-one-word-everywhere), five places short of a reading on a sharp print.
