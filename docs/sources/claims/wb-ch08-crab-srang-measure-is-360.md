# WB ch. 8: the Crab's own srang measure is 360, not 340

The Crab's own srang measure in WB's ch. 8 is 360 («sky, time, fire», ནམ་མཁའ་དུས་མེ, nam mkha' dus me): the page's own bracket reads «time» (དུས, dus) as 6; the White Beryl's etexts read «(360)» at the place; the six measures 305, 290, 200, 270, 360, 375 total 1800, the page's own divisor, and their steps mirror; the p. 53 table's cell «340» is the same curled glyph misread as 4 (as also in «7740», likely 7760 by the water-srang pattern of steps of 70).
