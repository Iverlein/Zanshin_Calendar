# The 3rd to 6th heads' festivals beyond RD2's table

The heads of the 3rd to 6th months carry fixed festivals beyond RD2's table of dated avoidances (month-entries.md omits them), as the 1st head's wonders festival does: the 3rd, on the 15th, the great festival of the Kalacakra taught at the Heap of Rice ('bras spungs) as non-dual (gnyis med); the 4th, on the 7th the birth of the Powerful One of the Sages and on the 15th the festival of the enlightenment and passing beyond sorrow; the 6th, on the 4th the Sage's turning the wheel of the Dharma and on the 15th the festival of the entering of the mother's womb. The 2nd and 5th heads carry none, and the 2nd's avoidances (the 8th: Agni's seat, no cremation or sick-room purification; the 8th lords' caste, the 15th royal and common castes) and the 5th's (the 9th: no army, no selling of land) are the ones RD2 gave. The 3rd head (16th avoidances), the 4th (24th; the 9th excepted; the mantrin's rotten-mouth month) and the 6th (18th: the yos not spoken, no slaughtering horses, no butcher's or Bonpo's house) also agree with RD2's table.
