# The clan element's cut-off, power, mother, son, friend and enemy years for all five clans

LT states for every clan element which years are its great and small cut-off, power, mother, son, friend and enemy years (lt:p26-28):

| Clan element | Great cut-off | Small cut-off | Power | Mother | Son | Friend | Enemy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| earth | dragon | dog | ox, sheep | horse, snake | bird, monkey | rat, pig | tiger, hare |
| fire | dog | dragon | horse, snake | tiger, hare | ox, sheep | bird, monkey | rat, pig |
| wood | sheep | ox | tiger, hare | rat, pig | horse, snake | dog, dragon | bird, monkey |
| water | dragon | dog | rat, pig | bird, monkey | tiger, hare | horse, snake | ox, sheep |
| iron | ox | sheep | bird, monkey | dog, dragon | rat, pig | tiger, hare | horse, snake |

The great cut-off is the element's grave year in the twelve stages (wood the sheep, fire the dog, iron the ox, earth and water the dragon) and the small cut-off its seventh (lt:p12). UT's table agrees for water, wood and iron (ut:p56); its fire row is garbled in the etext. The wood row is the notes' chart for the la places (wb.2:p499-500), where the great and small of each pair follow the notes' rule (dragon and sheep great, dog and ox small; the male year great, the female small); for earth and water LT's great and small cut-off (dragon, dog) fit that rule, for fire (dog great) and iron (ox great) it does not, as LT writes them.

Inferred, not stated: the la board of the dead takes its twelve places from this table for clans other than wood, since the notes name the places by the same words, "the clan element's great cut-off …"; LT gives the table for the birth's special pebble.

UT's table is read in BDRC's etext only.
