# The dead's outer reason: enemy, son and inner meeting black, mother and friend white

The second examination's outer reason (WB vol. 2, p. 57: the life force, body, power and luck, and the year, month, day and hour): by the regent's reply 148 (ys:i506-508), its pebbles are laid as the rough pebbles of the dead are laid: an aspect with the enemy rising on it, or [empty, doubtful word], or in an inner meeting, black; meeting the mother or the friend, white. So the black that WB names "enemy" is black in general and holds three: the enemy meeting, the son meeting and the inner meeting; the white is mother and friend only. WB's later lines give the enemy meeting and the inner meeting results of their own. The same holds for life force, body, power and luck alike. In detail the pebbles are laid as in the obstacle reckoning, for the deciding; at the verdict a black place is called "enemy" and a white one is mainly mother and friend; where enemies and inner meetings are many, the reason of whichever is more is told.

The trigram's reason "or else an enemy and a sudden mishap" (p. 58) names the illnesses and demons outside the reckoning: of karma, of the body, of the enemy (the demon of the trigram's fighting enemy) and of a sudden mishap (reply 148, machine reading).
