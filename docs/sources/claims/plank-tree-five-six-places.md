# The plank-tree's fifteenth and sixteenth heads start at the sky year's down-fifth and down-sixth

In the *Moonbeams* (dz:i052-055) the fifteenth head counts from «གནམ་ལོའི་མར་གྱི་ལྔ་ཐུན» and the sixteenth from «གནམ་ལོའི་མར་གྱི་དྲུག་འབྲས»: the fifth and sixth places counted down from the sky year. WB's list of the year's places (vol. 1, p. 413, etext) names «བཞི་གཤེད་ལྔ་མཐུན་དྲུག་འབྲས་ཏེ་བདུན་ཟུར», and the notes (wb.2:p494, etext) speak of the sky year's «མར་ལྔ་མཐུན་གསུམ»: ལྔ་མཐུན (the five in harmony) is the down-fifth with its three harmony years, དྲུག་འབྲས (six fruit) the down-sixth; the scan's «ལྔ་ཐུན» is a spelling of «ལྔ་མཐུན» (KD) and «དྲུག་འབྲས» of KD's «དྲུག་འགྲུས». The WB places were read in BDRC's etext only.
