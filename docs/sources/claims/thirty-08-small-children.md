# The thirty: the eighth point, the five-pointed golden vajra, for small children

The eighth of the thirty starting points (སྐོར་འགོ, skor 'go), from the mouth of the five-pointed golden vajra (གསེར་གྱི་རྡོ་རྗེ་རྩེ་ལྔ་པ, gser gyi rdo rje rtse lnga pa), is read for small children. Its eight pebbles are laid as for every point (claims thirty-count and thirty-eight-fighters), each white or black, and read so:

| Pebble | Asks | White | Black | Remedy for black |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Whether the children's playground is empty | The tree of the life force grows; the playground is not empty | The playground is empty: the family's numbers dwindle | — |
| 2 | Whether the small la, the life-spirit (བླ, bla) has been carried off by spirits | Not carried off; sons and grandsons multiply | The small la is carried off by spirits | Release of life (ཚེ་ཐར, tshe thar), ransoming the la (བླ་བསླུ, bla bslu), the long-life rite (ཚེ་ཆོག, tshe chog) |
| 3 | Whether the toy bow and arrow (སེ་མདའ་སེ་གཞུ, se mda' se gzhu) are broken | Not broken | Burnt by fire | The *ming ston* (མིང་སྟོན; not identified), release from fright (དངངས་འགྲོལ, dngangs 'grol), striving at virtue |
| 4 | Whether the castle of life has collapsed | The castle of life stands | It has collapsed; good now, bad in the long run | The *rgya ma* net (རྒྱ་མ, rgya ma; not identified) of the family's numbers and freeing the narrow paths (འཕྲང་སྒྲོལ, 'phrang sgrol) |
| 5 | Whether the dagger of life (ཚེ་ཕུར, tshe phur) holds | Not broken: long life, no illness; offering to the gods (ལྷ་གསོལ, lha gsol) is auspicious | The dagger of life is broken: small children die off | Planting the la dagger and the life dagger |
| 6 | Whether the life-holding god has fallen to the demon (བདུད, bdud) | The leading god protects: long life, health and happiness | The life-holding god has fallen to the demons | Offering to the gods, ablution, fulfilment and confession; carry the protection of the life-holder, the gods' son |
| 7 | Whether a mother spirit (མ་འདྲེ, ma 'dre) contests the child's years of life | No contest; mother and child in harmony, good | Mother and spirit contest the years of life | The turning back (བཟློག, bzlog) of the living spirit and of the mamo (མ་མོ, ma mo) |
| 8 | Whether the small sri (ཆུང་སྲི, chung sri) has risen | Children easy to raise, happy and growing | The small sri rises | Press down the small sri and turn its face |

Whose year is reckoned: the person's own (the notes, vol. 2, p. 533: «གཞན་རྣམས་སོ་སོའི་ལོ་རྟ»).

Which of the eight fighting pebbles is which check is not stated; the order of p. 453 is the likely one (claim thirty-board-groups; question 259).
