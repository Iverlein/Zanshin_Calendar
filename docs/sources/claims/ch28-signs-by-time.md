# The observed signs read by the hour and by the day's link

WB vol. 2, pp. 32–37 (machine-read): the observed signs of the chapter that are keyed to something calculable. By the twelve times of day and night (dawn, sunrise, morning warmth, noon, afternoon, sun low, sunset, dusk, evening, midnight, after midnight, before dawn): a sneeze without a cold, the eyes twitching (right, left), the body's flesh twitching, a sudden itch, the magpie's call, the ears ringing [?] (right, left), each with a reading per time (pp. 34–35); the crow by seven daytime times and by the side it shows or the direction it faces (pp. 33–34). By the day's link (the almanac's link of the day): some fifteen signs (footsteps, a dog crying, clothes burned, mice gnawing, a crow, grease on clothes, an omen, the eyes, ears, throat, palate and crown, calf, feet, an itch), each with a reading for each of the twelve link days (pp. 35–37); only the day of ignorance has been set out so far.

The sign is observed; the time or the day it is read by is calculable.
