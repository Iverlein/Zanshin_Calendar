# The rebirth's deciding pebble and its twelve roots

The fifth examination reads the dead's next birth first in three realms, gods above, the Lord of Death's realm with the three lower realms below, and the uncertain places of the intermediate state among humans (wb.2:p70-72).

| Input | White / good | Speckled / middling | Black / bad |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| the rebirth's deciding pebble | a god | a human | the lower realms |
| the mewa of death, in general | the whites: a god | five yellow: a human; the reds and «yellow three»: wandering in the intermediate state | two black, green and three: the lower realms |
| the whole | life force, body, power, luck rising, obstacles small, cutting the year of death, planet and mansion good, a white mewa: higher realms, no wandering | all middling, a green or yellow mewa: a human | power and luck not wanting food, a black month, a red or black mewa, the first of a waning half near Bharaṇī: the lower realms |

The deciding pebble is laid on twelve root pebbles (the notes, wb.2:p500-501): the deciding pebbles of life force, power and element (3); the trigram and mewa of death, white if no harsh obstacle, black if any (2); year and month, white in the upper half and black in the lower (2); day white, night black (1); planet and mansion, white if good (2); the obstacles great or small (1); and the power and element taken together as in the funeral share (1). Counted white against black, they give the verdict. With the three deciding pebbles, the mewa's four classes are the seven identifying pebbles.
