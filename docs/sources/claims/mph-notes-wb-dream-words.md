# Mipham's notes on the White Beryl name dreams twice and gloss no dream sign

In Mipham's notes on the White Beryl, dreams are named twice and glossed nowhere. At the very start (wb.2:p474) dreams are a means, beside oral tradition, of finding the clan's token animal and so the clan, with no time and no reading. In section eight (wb.2:p535-537), on the reckoning of the moment of ch. 30, "examining dreams" is the first of the preliminaries that "are clear in the text" (གཞུང་ན་གསལ), and the notes add nothing. A search of the etext of the notes (vol. 2, etext pp. 482–549) for «རྨི» found only these two places (and the colophon list of ch. 30's contents on p. 552, which is WB's, not the notes'); «གཉིད» occurs twice on p. 502 and «ཉལ» on pp. 486 and 505, all in other glosses (a year table and the burial reckoning), none about sleep or dreams; the Yigdzin-1 pages of the notes (img. 482–549, 536–539 not in the folder) have no other dream word. So the notes do not read WB's dream list (ch. 30, the dream signs of the question) and give no source, day or time for a dream sign.
