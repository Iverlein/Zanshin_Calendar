# The sum of the earth lords of year, month, day and time is 474 and leaves out the 102 that never move

WB's «བཞི་བརྒྱ་དོན་བཞི» (p. 175) is 474, not 404: «དོན» is a number word, as in «གནས་པ་དོན་གཉིས» (72) on p. 174, where the same count is written «བདུན་ཅུ་རྩ་གཉིས» on p. 175. The notes (vol. 2, pp. 534–535) build the sum from its parts: 231 of the year, 124 of the month, 81 of the day, 37 of the hour and Rāhu as one, 231 + 124 + 81 + 37 + 1 = 474. The 102 that stay put whatever the year, month, day or hour (72 that dwell, 21 that never move, nine of the nine levels under the earth) are counted apart, before the sum. Read on the scan: the word and the line; the parts are the chapter's own counts (RD3, RD4) and the notes' paragraph read for the weighing.
