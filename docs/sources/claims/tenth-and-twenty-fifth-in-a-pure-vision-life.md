# A pure-vision life of the Guru lists the tenth, the twenty-fifth and all great occasions for the feast

In a modern anthology of lives of Padmasambhava (Bod ljongs mi dmangs, 2012) the item «Dag snang rin chen gter mdzod las: O rgyan rnam thar mu tig phreng rgyan» has, in ch. 16 (the Guru's testament to the keepers of the treasures), «ཚེས་བཅུ་ཉེར་ལྔ་དུས་ཆེན་ཐམས་ཅད་དུ། ཅི་འབྱོར་ཚོགས་ཀྱི་འཁོར་ལོར་མ་བསྐོར་ན། ཆོས་སྐྱོང་སྲུང་མའི་འཁུ་ལྡོག་བྱེད་ཉེན་ཡོད» (bkt:p272): if on the tenth, the twenty-fifth and all great occasions you do not circulate what you have in the wheel of the feast, the Dharma protectors may turn on you. It is the Padma bka' thang's ch. 102 sentence (padma-bka-thang-every-tenth-i-come-and-the-feast) enlarged: the same vows follow, the same «circulating» (བསྐོར), but the tenth is joined by the twenty-fifth and the feast is a duty with a threat.

Rank and state: the item is a pure-vision treasure (dag snang) whose revealer the anthology's OCR contents page does not give, in a modern edited volume: a witness, not a source for the app. BDRC's full-text search finds the line in no other work (1 hit for each of three phrases, 2026-11-21). The etext OCR is rough and the scans are a lending copy not read; the lines quoted read clean, the passage is etext only. It shows that a pure-vision tradition, not the old Padma bka' thang, pairs the tenth with the twenty-fifth for the feast; the pairing has no older witness among the Guru's texts read here.
