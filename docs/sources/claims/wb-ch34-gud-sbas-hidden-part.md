# «གུད་སྦས» is WB's word for the profound part it sets down apart, and ch. 34's colophon says it did so under each work

# «གུད་སྦས» is WB's word for the profound part it sets down apart, and the colophon of ch. 34 says it did so under each work

In ch. 34 WB twice says that the part of the teaching that is hard to find stated is set down separately and in small measure. At the foot of p. 379 (the Gser thang reprint, I3CN5029 img. 385, read by eye; the 1996 etext p. 387): «དེ་ཕྱིར་འདིར་ཡང་དེ་བཞིན་དུ། །ཅུང་ཟད་གུད་སྦས་ལོགས་སུ་བསྟན། །ཟླ་བའི་སྐར་མ་དག་ལ་བརྟག», "therefore here too, in the same way, a little of the hidden part (གུད་སྦས, *gud sbas*) is shown apart; examine the moon's mansions", after a line that says the profound instructions of the essence were unclear in the three commentaries. The colophon at the end of the chapter (p. 428, the same print, img. 434, rows 15–19 looked at by eye, the text machine-read, see wb.2:p428c) says the very profound kinds, «དམར་ནག་སོགས་ཆེས་ཟབ་རྣམས», red and black and the rest, said in the «ས་རོད་ཡའི་རྒྱུད» (the tantra's name written as the sounds *sa rod ya*, which is *svarodaya* by the same verse's «དབྱངས་འཆར», the *Svarodaya*), were put down under each work: «གུད་སྦས་ལས་སོ་སོའི་ཐད་དུ་བཀོད». So the chapter's own account of itself is that its lists of the day's results are the open part, and the hidden or profound part is given beside each work and not in the general lists; the closing verse names what is examined (wb.2:p428b), the colophon says where the profound kinds went.

The word elsewhere in WB's 1996 etext (read in context there, not on the scan): p. 98 «གུད་སྦས་ཚུལ་དུ་རྩ་འགྲེལ་དུ། །སྣང་མོད», "in a hidden manner it appears in the root text and the commentary", and p. 150 «ཡང་གུད་སྦས་མིན་ན», "and if it is not hidden". It always means what is not openly spelled out. The colophon's «ཁ་མཚོན་གཅོད་དཀའ», «མ་ནས» and «ཁུངས་གང་ཞིབ་བཅད» are not settled: the English of the colophon in the passage keeps them in square brackets.
