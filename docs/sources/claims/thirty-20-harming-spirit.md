# The thirty: the twentieth point, the black grave bird, for which harming spirit

The twentieth of the thirty starting points (སྐོར་འགོ, skor 'go), from the mouth of the black grave bird (དུར་བྱ་ནག་པོ, dur bya nag po), is read for which harming spirit. Its eight pebbles are laid as for every point (claims thirty-count and thirty-eight-fighters), each white or black, and read so:

| Pebble | Asks | White | Black | Remedy for black |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Whether the male demon (བདུད, bdud) has risen as a spirit | Not risen, good | Risen: bad for the men of the male line | Press down the *da pa* sri (ད་པ་སྲི; not identified) and the like |
| 2 | Whether the female demon has risen as a spirit | It does not rise as an enemy; the female line grows, good | Risen: bad for the female line | The turning back (བཟློག, bzlog) rite, turning the elements by rite, pressing down enemies |
| 3 | Whether the *thel 'gong* (ཐེལ་འགོང, thel 'gong; a spirit, not identified) steals in series | It does no harm; food and wealth grow | It steals | The laying-down rite (སྐྱས་འདེབས, skyas 'debs) against the stealing *'gong po* (འགོང་པོ, 'gong po) |
| 4 | Whether the thel 'gong has entered the womb door | Not entered; the human line grows, good | Entered: the human line does not grow, bad | The the'u brang spirit (ཐེའུ་བྲང, the'u brang)'s laying-down rite and the like |
| 5 | Whether the *gre bo* spirit (གྲེ་བོ, gre bo; not identified) attacks the life force (སྲོག, srog) | It does not attack: life is firm | It attacks: obstacles to life | — |
| 6 | Whether the *rgyal 'gong* (རྒྱལ་འགོང, rgyal 'gong; a spirit, not identified), a being, steals the la and life | It does not steal, good | It steals, bad | King spirits' *gzhi mgyogs* rite (རྒྱལ་པོ་གཞི་མགྱོགས, rgyal po gzhi mgyogs; not identified) |
| 7 | Whether the gre bo's strength (ཨ་ཐང, a thang) is cut | Not cut: good for oneself | Cut, bad | The rite of pressing down the sri of the prime of life (དར་སྲི, dar sri); recite the *Khro rol* (ཁྲོ་རོལ, khro rol) and the *Gtsug tor* (གཙུག་ཏོར, gtsug tor) |
| 8 | Whether the royal castle of the *dam sri* (དམ་སྲི, dam sri; a sri, not identified) is built | Not built in one's own place, good | Built, bad | The king's laying-down rite helps |

Whose year is reckoned: the person's own (the notes, vol. 2, p. 533: «གཞན་རྣམས་སོ་སོའི་ལོ་རྟ»).

Which of the eight fighting pebbles is which check is not stated; the order of p. 453 is the likely one (claim thirty-board-groups; question 259).
