# The day of the rite against a leaking roof

WB vol. 2, p. 37: the rite against the clay roof's leaking (སྐྱ་ཐིག, skya thig) is done on the day the sun stands in the mansion of the Pleiades (སྨིན་དྲུག, smin drug) and "has risen to fifty water-hours (ཆུ་ཚོད, chu tshod)": earth is spread on the roof and beaten dry with the words "our dripping ground is sealed", said so that one other person hears. Best when weekday planet, mansion, month, date and time also agree; done even when they do not. Another system does it when the moon's mansion is *skag* (སྐག, skag; Āśleṣā, no. 8). The first is Phug's own reckoning and accords with the siddhāntas.

The day is calculable from the sun's mansion; whether "fifty water-hours" means the sun's progress through the mansion (50 of its 60) or something else the text does not say.
