# Dream signs of practice in the treasure sādhana's verses; the compiled volume's ranks and the tenth day, facts only

Two verses of the treasure sādhana, quoted by the compiled volume as «གཏེར་སྒྲུབ་ལས» (from the treasure sādhana), name dreams as signs that a practice has taken. Of bodhicitta trained to its measure: even in dreams, at a torrent, a cliff-fall or other danger, one acts as another's refuge and helper (i152). Of the two accumulations being completed (by mandala offerings of a hundred, a thousand, ten thousand and so on): in dreams the sun and moon rise, one climbs upward, comes to a meadow of flowers and a garden, many women give food and drink, one gives the breast and looks into a mirror, and one gains a wheel-turning king's kingdom; "if of these actual experiences in dreams not even one of each comes, the work was begun with effort" (i168), the second half's sense not settled. The verses give kinds of dream, not a day or hour. Under the edition's own prose (facts only, volume 27 images 225-226, 154, 172 of vol. 29): the practitioners of the highest rank meet the deity in person, the middle rank in experience, the lowest "in dreams, meeting the face and receiving the prophecy"; and at a special time such as the tenth day of the month fortunate ones meet the face in pure vision, in experience or in dream (vol. 27 image 225; the same three ranks, vol. 29 image 154). The volume's own instruction on dream yoga (images 246-248, 283) names dawn, the pre-dawn and sunrise as the key times for recognising a dream and says to hold to it again in the late part of the night when some sleep remains.
