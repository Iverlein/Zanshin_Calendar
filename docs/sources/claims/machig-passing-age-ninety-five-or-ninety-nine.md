# Machig Labdrön passed at ninety-five (Blue Annals) or ninety-nine (Gzan skyur); no date

The age at which Machig Labdrön (མ་གཅིག་ལབ་སྒྲོན, ma gcig lab sgron) passed away differs between the two sources read, and neither gives a year, month or day:

- the Blue Annals: at ninety-five (དགུང་ལོ་དགུ་བཅུ་རྩ་ལྔ, dgung lo dgu bcu rtsa lnga) (dsn.2:i477-478);
- the *Phung po gzan skyur gyi rnam bshad*, ch. 2: at ninety-nine (དགུ་བཅུ་གོ་དགུ, dgu bcu go dgu), with an interlinear note that she never washed her hair from birth and it stayed black and glossy (pgz:p65; the 1992 edition the same without the note).

Both rank 3 in the registry; the Gzan skyur is the Chöd (གཅོད, gcod) lineage's own life of her, the Blue Annals a history from outside the lineage. No source read names the day of her passing or a day on which it is kept, except the monthly offering of Mindroling (claim machig-mindroling-twenty-first).
