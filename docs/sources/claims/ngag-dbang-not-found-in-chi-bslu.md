# Ngag dbang is not found in 'Deceiving Death' or its explanation; q796 stays open

Searches for the short name «ངག་དབང» of the Svapnohana's remedy (svo:p271) in the text of 'Deceiving Death' and its explanation: the whole etext of the Derge volume 28 (VE23703_1344) has «ངག་དབང» once, in the Svapnohana's line itself; the first five of the twenty-five etext volumes of Mkhyen brtse's collected works (IE0OPIC2BFA6FE, I1KG81113-81117; the other twenty were not fetched) have neither «ངག་དབང» nor «ངག་དབང་དགོངས» anywhere. In 'Deceiving Death' the author is named only in the full form «ངག་གི་དབང་ཕྱུག་གྲགས་པ» (cbl:p217, cbl:p238b, cbl:p278-279, cbs:p247-249, cbs:p267-268). Two parallels of diction exist: 'Deceiving Death' uses «ནང་པར» (early morning) for the time of practice (Derge pp. 255 and 264: "rising at «ནང་པར» recite a thousand ...") and the Svapnohana's remedy has «ག་ཡ་ཏྲི་ནི་སྟོང་ཕྲག་བཟླས» (the Gāyatrī recited a thousand times) and «ནང་པར་ཀློག་པ» (reading it at «ནང་པར»); the word is ordinary and the translators differ (Dīpaṃkaraśrījñāna with Rin chen bzang po, Vibhūticandra), so this shows no common author. The Svapnohana's dream signs and the 'Deceiving Death' verses share figures but no line (claim death-dreams-shared-figures-cbl-svo-klong-rdol), and the Svapnohana's remedy by fire offering and the Gāyatrī has no counterpart in 'Deceiving Death' (the Gāyatrī's name does not occur in the Derge pages 239-268). Result: the identification of «ངག་དབང» with Ngag gi dbang phyug grags pa is neither supported nor refuted by the texts; q796 stays open. Whether «ངག་དབང» can be a short form of the name, or stands for a god or a teacher of speech, no source read here says; the dictionaries (locate.py dict) were not consulted.
