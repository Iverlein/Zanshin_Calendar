# The thirteen gates of the General Tantra of Brahmā

WB vol. 2, pp. 170–171: the thirteen gates (རྩིས་སྒོ, rtsis sgo) of the *General Tantra of Brahmā*. The "sky year" is the asker's own year (the notes, p. 536; claim *moment-asker-year*). From the starting sign the years lived are counted, a male down (སྦུབ, sbub) and a female up (བཀེན, bken), and the year landed on is fought against the moment's sign; the harm named comes when the landed year cuts the moment inward (ཚུར་བཅད); cut outward, the reverse, good.

| Gate | Starts from | Then | Harm when cutting inward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 the king's command | the rat of the sky year's element, years lived down | the year eight up fought with the moment; and the year eight down | eight up: "the Chinese rope is cut"; eight down: "the gold-holder's leg is broken" |
| 2 the minister's command and wealth | male: the sky year's son tiger, down; female: its son monkey, up | the landed year fought | the king punishes the minister |
| 3 the fire ox leading change | the nearest year of one's own sex before oneself, in the son's element (wood rat: fire male tiger; wood ox: fire female hare), male down, female up | three up fought; three down fought | three up: bad has fallen; three down: the support collapses |
| 4 the sky | the sky year's son ox, its thirteenth below | male down, female up | bad |
| 5 the swastika's five rounds | the sky year's son monkey, male five down, female five up | then male down, female up | bad |
| 6 the sun gathering the army | the sky year's friend hare, five | male down, female up | "the sun gathers to war" |
| 7 the moment's demon-cutting | the sky year's enemy bird | male down, female up | the moment's demon-cutting |
| 8 gods and demons trading | the sky year's mother horse | male down, female up | gods and demons trade you away |
| 9 the thief leading the sheep | the sheep of the own element, at its seventh | male down, female up | the la-sheep led away |
| 10 the peacock with crossed necks | the own element's monkey, thirteen up | male down, female up | bad |
| 11 the sky's six wanderings | the own element's pig, twenty-five up | male down, female up | the sky dog's six wanderings |
| 12 the earth's six wanderings | the own element's pig, twenty-five down | male and female, down and up | (not named) |
| 13 the ruin-horse riding the drum | the own element's rat, thirteen down | male down, female up | the ruin-bird riding the drum |

"Son", "friend", "enemy" and "mother" are the element relations of the sky year; how "five" in gates 5 and 6 and the "own element" signs are fixed is not explained beyond the notes' example for gate 3 (p. 536: a water rat counts from the wood tiger, a water ox from the wood hare). No rite is named.
