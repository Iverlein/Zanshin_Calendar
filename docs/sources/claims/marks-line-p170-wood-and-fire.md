# The marks' line of p. 170 holds the wood and fire hours; the earth hour's hair

On p. 170 (reprint img. 176, ll. 5-6) the hair verse takes wood and fire together in one line («ཤིང་ལ་སྐྲ་ཕྲུག་མེ་རལ་ཅན»), then earth, iron, water. The marks' verse follows the same order: its first couplet, «ཤ་མཚན་ཤའི་ལྔ་ཅན། །དྲུག་གུ་མི་ལ་རྨེན་པའམ།», is the wood and the fire (a mole, «རྨེན་པ», is named), then earth a mole on the «སྐུར་པོ», iron a mole under the arm, water «ཤོ་རེ». No hour is lost; only the labels are left out. «ཤའི་ལྔ་ཅན», «དྲུག་གུ», «སྐུར་པོ» and «ཤོ་རེ» are not understood. The earth hour's hair is «བནྡེ» (the monk) in Yigdzin-1 and the etext; on the reprint the stack is a blot that the eye cannot tell from «བཛེ», which gives no word.
