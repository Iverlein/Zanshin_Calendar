# WB p. 303: the Phugpa way of the life-spirit sites is the uncle and nephew's, ཁུ་དབོན, not Khadbon

The way of the life-spirit sites on p. 303 is that held by «ཕུག་པ་ཁུ་དབོན», the uncle and nephew (ཁུ་དབོན, khu dbon) of Phug, the two who carry the Phugpa line, told "as in the Precious Garland (རིན་ཅེན་ཕྲེང་བ, rin cen phreng ba)". The u-sign is plain on the Gser thang reprint and the Zhol print (WBZ img. 1052) and stands in the 1996 etext; Yigdzin-1 alone had «ཁ», and the "Khadbon" of the earlier notes came from it. The page does not name the two.
