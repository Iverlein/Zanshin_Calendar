# The grave-day strike by date

The grave-day strike falls on dates (WB vol. 2, p. 88): the 6th of the first month of each season (ར་བཞི, ra bzhi), the 16th of the middle months (འབྲིང་བཞི, 'bring bzhi) and the 26th of the last months (ཐ་ཆུང་བཞི, tha chung bzhi). Another account ties four graves to trigrams and years: *khen* the sky grave (heart the iron dog, the four dogs, the eleven iron years, the first months' 6th), *khon* the earth grave (the earth sheep, the four sheep, eleven earth years, the middle months' 26th), *zon* the middle grave (the wood dragon, four dragons, eleven wood years, the 16th), *gin* the mountain grave (the earth ox, four oxen, eleven earth years, the last months' 16th). The two accounts set the 16th and 26th differently. The notes (p. 506, read on the scan 2026-10-23) count eighteen each, 72, and read the "eleven iron years" as the years whose power is iron.
