# The 2nd head's equinox and the 5th head's solstice

Two heads name a solar turning in the breath-taking and sgang phrases: the 2nd month's sgang reads "dpyid mnyam", the spring equinox, and the 5th month's breath-taking ends "the summer solstice: set it down after looking at the coarse and the fine" (dbyar nyi ldog pa rags zhib bltas la 'god). RD2 recorded the winter solstice in the 11th head; the autumn equinox would stand in the 8th (RD202). The 4th head's breath-taking has the cuckoo arriving and the wasp not yet working, the 3rd's the sky clear and trees' buds opening, the 6th's the hot sgang. The seasonal phrases are the model almanac's own signs and are not used elsewhere in these readings.
