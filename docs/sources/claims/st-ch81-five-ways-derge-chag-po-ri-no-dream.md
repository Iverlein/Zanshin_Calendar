# Ch. 81's five ways of entering carry no dream clause on the Derge and Chag-po-ri either: the second way's dreams are the commentators' sentence

The Serthang verse of ch. 81 on the five ways of entering (st:p352) has no clause on the dream in the second way's line, and the Derge and the Chag-po-ri prints read the same (RD325, 2026-11-10). On the Derge (img. 436, lines 2–3) and the Chag-po-ri (img. 428, lines 1–2), read by eye on crops, the second way is the single line «ལོངས་སྤྱོད་རེ་འདོད་ཚུལ་གྱིས་ཞུགས་པ་དང་», between the game and the unclean, and the five ways run on without a word on dreams, marks or remedies; nothing is added or lost between the prints but the spelling (དོ་སྡོ on the Derge by machine, དོ་སྔོ on the Chag-po-ri by machine, དོ་སྡེ on the Serthang scan). So the commentaries' «many confused dreams» of the second way (Blue Beryl, Khro ru, the Drikung, Skyem pa) is their own sentence on three prints of the Tantra, not the Tantra's text; the Drathang print was not read at this place (its ch. 81 page was not in the images opened). The dream list of the same chapter (Derge img. 434–435, Chag-po-ri img. 426) was read by Yigdzin-1 only and agrees with the Serthang's list in every word but the spellings; the letter after ཚོ in «ལྷས་ཚོར་དུས་ཏེ» (ར or ང) was not settled by eye on the Chag-po-ri crop (q893).
