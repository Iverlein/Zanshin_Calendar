# The Co ne Svapnekṣā: Yigdzin-1's windows give «བརྟགས་པ་ཉལ» for the night and «བྱ་ཤར་ཏེ་རུ» for the bird

Yigdzin-1 on windows of the Co ne frame (I1GS66080 img. 263, 2026-11-10, RD354):

- the last part of the night: in all 14 windows that reached the line the reading is «ཐ་མར་རྨི་ལམ་བརྟགས་པ་ཉལ», with པ and without the ལ of the Derge etext's «བརྟགས་ལ་ཉལ», and never «བཏབ»; RD276's two «བརྟགས་ལ་ཉལ» came from whole-frame runs and are outnumbered;
- the Amoghasiddhi line: of 15 windows, 11 give «བྱ་ཤར་ཏེ་རུ་ཕོ་ཉར», one «བྱ་གར་ཏེ་རུ», three a faint variant ending «ཏ་རུ» or «ཏ་ཅུ»; none gives «ཤང», «ཤིང» or «ཏེའུ».

The eye settled neither: the frame's lines run into each other. The Co ne is a machine witness to the Derge's four syllables «བྱ་ཤར་ཏེ་རུ» and, for the night, to a seventh syllable «པ» in the place of «ལ».
