# The constants of the three days' reckoning, word-numerals with their printed figures

The reckoning of the three days is written in word-numerals, each followed in brackets by its number, and the same names recur in every rule of chapter 17 and 19. As printed (machine readings where the two disagreed are settled by the word-numerals themselves or by the glyph count, see the doubts of the section): «མཁའ་རོ» sky-taste 60, «དུས» season 6, «འདོད» desire 13 (also «ལུས་མེད» the bodiless 13, «མྱོས་བྱེད» the intoxicator 13), «མདའ་རོ» arrow-taste 65, «དབང་མཚམས» 65, «ཆུ་རོ» water-taste 64, «རི་མཁའ་རི» and «ཐུབ་མཁའ་རི» 707, «རི་རོ» mountain-taste 67, «མ་ནུ་ཡིད» 1414, «མཁའ་དུས་མེ» 360, «རྒྱུ་སྐར་ཆུ་ཚོད» the stars' hours 1,620, «བུག་རོ་ཀླུ» 869, «བུག་མཁའ་མིག་བུག་ཡིད» 149,209, «མིག་ཀླུ་མེ་སྐྱོན» 18,382, «ཐུབ་རི་ཟླ་བ» 177, «འབྱུང་འདོད་དྲག་པོ» 11,135, «ཉི་མ་འདོད་ཟླ» 11,312, «མེ་ཚེས་ལག་པ» 2,153 (as printed on p. 196, read on the scan; the machine readings gave 2,152, and the words give 2,153), «ཀླུ་མིག་ཀླུ་ལག» 2,828, «མཁའ་མེ» 30, «ཟླ་ཡོན» 20. The mapping of the words to the digits is the text's own: a word-numeral is followed by its figure.
