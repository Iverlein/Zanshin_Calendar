# The constants of the three days' reckoning, word-numerals with their printed figures

The reckoning of the three days is written in word-numerals, each followed in brackets by its number, and the same names recur in every rule of chapter 17 and 19. As printed (machine readings where the two disagreed are settled by the word-numerals themselves or by the glyph count, see the doubts of the section): «མཁའ་རོ» sky-taste 60, «དུས» season 6, «འདོད» desire 13 (also «ལུས་མེད» the bodiless 13, «མྱོས་བྱེད» the intoxicator 13), «མདའ་རོ» arrow-taste 65, «དབང་མཚམས» 65, «ཆུ་རོ» water-taste 64, «རི་མཁའ་རི» and «ཐུབ་མཁའ་རི» 707, «རི་རོ» mountain-taste 67, «མ་ནུ་ཡིད» 1414, «མཁའ་དུས་མེ» 360, «རྒྱུ་སྐར་ཆུ་ཚོད» the stars' hours 1,620, «བུག་རོ་ཀླུ» 869, «བུག་མཁའ་མིག་བུག་ཡིད» 149,209, «མིག་ཀླུ་མེ་སྐྱོན» 18,382, «ཐུབ་རི་ཟླ་བ» 177, «འབྱུང་འདོད་དྲག་པོ» 11,135, «ཉི་མ་འདོད་ཟླ» 11,312, «མེ་ཚེས་ལག་པ» 2,153 (as printed on p. 196, read on the scan; the machine readings gave 2,152, and the words give 2,153), «ཀླུ་མིག་ཀླུ་ལག» 2,828, «མཁའ་མེ» 30, «ཟླ་ཡོན» 31 (was 30 as printed on p. 213: the words moon 1, yon 3, ch. 3 and 4 «ཟླ་ཡོན ༣༡» and the synodic month 29 days 31 dbyu gu 50 srang 0 breaths 480/707 = 29.53059 days all give 31, with 30 it would be 29.5278; the print's 30 is a blot or a misprint, q1347, 2026-11-24; the earlier 20 was not on the print). The stars' hours on p. 195 are 1,620 (was read 620 on the scan, a dropped ༡: the same constant stands at every other place of the chapter and is 27 mansions × 60 hours). The mapping of the words to the digits is the text's own: a word-numeral is followed by its figure.
