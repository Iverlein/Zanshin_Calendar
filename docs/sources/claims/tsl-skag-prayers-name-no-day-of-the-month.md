# The turning-back prayers of vol. 28 name the obstacles of year, month, day and time only as a kind, with no day

In the turning-back prayers of Pad rgyal gling pa in vol. 28 of the edition (etext pp. 612-637) the obstacles (སྐག, skag) are named four times, at pp. 612, 615, 616 and 637, each time as one item of a list of what is turned back: "the kinds of obstacle of year, month, day and time" (pp. 612, 615, 637) and "the obstacle conditions of year, month, day and time" (p. 616). A search of those pages in the etext for a date word, a number of a day or a month's name finds none: the prayers set no day of the month on which they are to be said and name no day as an obstacle day. Read in the etext only.
