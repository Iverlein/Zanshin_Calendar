# UT reads the dead's descended trigram

UT finds the trigram's grave-harm in the reckoning for the dead by matching the dead's life force with the descended trigram (བབ་སྤར, bab spar): wood with khon, fire with khen, iron with gin, earth and water with zon; falling there is the trigram's grave-eye (ut:p69-70). So in UT the trigram the dead's reckoning reads is the dead's descended trigram, the one of the year of death by age and sex.

Inferred for WB, not stated: the "dead's trigram" of the grave provisions and the "year of death's trigram and mewa" of the notes may be read so; UT does not speak of the provisions or of the third examination.

UT is read in BDRC's etext only; its scan has not been read.
