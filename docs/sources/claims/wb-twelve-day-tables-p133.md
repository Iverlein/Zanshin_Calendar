# WB vol. 1, p. 133: the table of the twelve days and the table of the twelve times of day

WB vol. 1, p. 133 has two tables of twelve cells each. The upper one is the twelve days: each cell gives the seat of the day animal's earth lord, what it is bad for, a remedy that is an act (scatter, send away, cut, boil, burn, wrap, dig; the rat's is «ཁ་ཡོ་ནས་འདྲེན»), the hearth god's side and a last name. The lower is the twelve times of the day, each with a la-knower and an animal, «ཁང་མཚོ», an earth king and an animal, and the same last name as the upper table's cell of that animal. The Bhutan table (I1KG11905 img. 571) copies the upper table's seat, harm and remedy under twelve direction headings with the times beneath. Read by Yigdzin-1 and MITRA on cropped cells and by eye; the doubtful cells (hare, sheep, monkey, bird, dog) are marked in the passages.
