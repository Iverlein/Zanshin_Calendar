# The dream signs of tsl.27:i203-205 are in the Secret Subduer's recitation instruction, whose colophon gives it as the treasure-revealer's own copy from the yellow scroll (q1041)

The dream signs of tsl.27:i203-205 stand inside one text that the compiled volume prints whole, from its title on etext p190 to its colophon on p211: «བཀའ་བརྒྱད་ཆེན་མོ་གསང་བ་ཟིལ་གནོན་ལས། བཟླས་པ་སྔགས་ཀྱི་པྲ་ཁྲིད» (Bka' brgyad chen mo Gsang ba zil gnon las, Bzlas pa sngags kyi pra khrid, "from the Secret Subduer of the Great Eight Pronouncements, the preliminary instruction on mantra recitation"). The text is not quoted inside the compiler's own prose: it opens with the treasure's homage and seal-verses, carries no sentence of the editor, and ends with the seal formulas of a treasure text («ས་མ་ཡ། རྒྱ་རྒྱ་རྒྱ། གཏེར་རྒྱ། སྦས་རྒྱ།…», "samaya, seal… seal of the treasure, seal of hiding") followed by «ཁོ་བོ་གཏེར་བདག་གླིང་པས་ལྕགས་བྱི་ལོར་སྦས་ཚལ་ནས་གདན་དྲངས་ཤིང་གསང་སྔགས་ཆོས་གླིང་དུ་ཤོག་སེར་ལས་ཞལ་བཤུས་སོ» — "I, Gter bdag gling pa, in the iron-mouse year had it brought from Sbas tshal and at Gsang sngags chos gling copied it from the yellow scroll". So the dream-sign list is part of Rtsa gsum gling pa's own treasure instruction, as he puts it, copied from the yellow scroll of the treasure, and not the compiler's verse; the compiler's own words are the volume's title and the preface on etext pp. 4-8.

What this does not settle: the list is prose in lines marked ༔ and the colophon's first lines speak in the voice of the Guru («the Padma 'byung gnas who is like me»), so «Rtsa gsum gling pa's own» means his as the treasure-revealer, with the Guru as the treasure's speaker; and the etext was not compared with the scan, so no syllable here is to be built on before it is read on the page.
