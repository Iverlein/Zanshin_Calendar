# «གཟའ་རྒོད་འདོམ»: a birth on the fierce planet's day, a day the calendar gives

The fifth of the middle seven children, the child of strength, is «གཟའ་རྒོད་འདོམ་སྐྱེས», "born at the meeting of the fierce planet" (wb.1:p339, on the reprint, printed page 339; the earlier section and the question give 340 after the 1996 etext's image numbers). «འདོམ» is "to meet, to mix" (Tshig mdzod chen mo: 'dres pa'am 'phrad pa; WB uses it so beside the nine-grid, «དགུ་མིག་འདོམ་ན», and the falling of the trigram, «བབས་འདོམ»). «གཟའ་རྒོད» is in the dictionaries the planet-ogre (གཟའ་སྒྲ་གཅན, Rāhu: Tshig mdzod chen mo, Great Tibetan–Chinese; the Rangjung Yeshe's glossary has Rāhu, Dan Martin Ketu), and it is also WB's own day-name: in ch. 31's list of the earth lords of the date, number 20 is «གཟའ་རྒོད་འབྱུང་བ་ཟས་འཚོལ», the fierce planet's arising, the time of seeking food, which "moves" on the 18th of the dragon and sheep months, the 16th and 22nd of the pig and snake months, the 13th of the horse month and the 9th and 22nd of the monkey month (1996 etext vol. 2, p. 238 = printed p. 230, etext only here; its works: bad for the wedding, for giving a bride, and for the birth of a child, a parent dying, the child's own undoing), and the same name stands among the days to avoid in ch. 33 (vol. 2, etext pp. 370, 393, 422, 431, 435, printed pp. 362, 385, 414, 423, 427). So the child is born when the date is one of these days, which the almanac gives; whether the "planets" (plural in the question's reading, singular in the print) are Rāhu and Ketu together is not said. An answer to the question as asked: it is a date the calendar gives; the dates are those above, to be read on the scan of ch. 31 (not done here).
