# WB ch. 8: ཏཏྐལ is tatkāla and the Crab is karkaṭa (ཀརྐ་ཊ)

The Sanskrit word printed ཏཏྐལ in WB's ch. 8 (Wylie «tat+kal», the second letter the stack ཏྐ) is tatkāla, «at that very time, instantaneous» (Monier-Williams), without the long ā; the Crab's name there is karkaṭa (karka Ta, ཀརྐ་ཊ, «crab, the sign Cancer»), the machine's ཀཎྜར, ཀརྚ་ཊ and ཀརྞ being one stacked letter ར over ཀ misread.
