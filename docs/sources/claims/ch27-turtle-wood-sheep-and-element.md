# Ch. 27: the turtle's years by element, and the wood sheep as the cow

In the golden turtle (WB vol. 2, p. 20, l. 14, read on the Gser thang reprint I3CN5029 img. 26 at 4 times) the line reads «ཤིང་གླང་མི་ལོ་ཤིང་ལུག་པ», the last syllable པ as the Zhol and Sakya etexts have it; no ba (བ) is on the scan.

A year "becomes" an element (གྱུར་པ) by its own element in the sixty-year cycle, the element in its name: each element has twelve of the sixty years. Every year the text names fits this: fire male monkey, fire female snake, fire female bird, fire male dragon are all fire years; iron male tiger, iron male monkey, iron female bird iron years; water rat, horse, ox, sheep water years; wood male rat, wood ox, wood sheep wood years. (Whether the "four earth years" of the turtle are the four of the dragon, dog, ox and sheep is not stated in the text.)

The wood sheep is the cow's year. Counting the years of each class as the text gives them: god 2 (wood rat, iron tiger), man 1, asura 2, animal 1, hungry ghost 1, hell 2, planet 8, re 'jam 9, garuda 4, klu 8, turtle 4, earth lord 8, *srin po* 9 and the cow, whose only year can be the wood sheep, 1: sixty. With the *srin po* "ten" as printed the count is sixty-one and the wood would have thirteen years; so «བཅུ་པོ» in the wood verse is read as a slip for nine, or the wood sheep is not a class year, and then the cow with her two white pebbles would have none. The first is the likelier; the printed ten is a misprint or a slip.
