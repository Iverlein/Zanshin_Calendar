# In the stage of dream the gift is empty; it is to be received in reality (TSL life)

In a dream the dakini holding him says: "now you are in the stage of dream (རྨི་ལམ་སྐབས, rmi lam skabs); even if you take it you have no power over it, it is empty; come in reality (དངོས་སུ, dngos su) and I will give" (i164-165). What is given in a dream is a promise, to be received later in the waking world; the treasure is then to be found with a "dependent arising companion" named in the dream (the emanation in the valley of Rta shod). The passage therefore ties the dream's gift to a later coming in reality, the same shape as the cave book of the ninth day (i113) and the valley of the tested dream (i170).
