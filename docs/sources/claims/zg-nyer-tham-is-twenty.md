# ZG's «ཉེར་ཐམ» is twenty: each of the meadow's three years is 120

«ཉེར་ཐམ» in Zhechen Gyaltsab's verse on the meadow's ornament leaves is twenty: the verse's metrical form of "twenty exactly", so «བརྒྱ་དང་ཉེར་ཐམ» is 120, and the three years' sums are 120 + 120 + 120 = 360 (zg:p300-301).

The scan, read by eye (img. 344, the gold year's sum «ཡོངས་དྲིལ་རྩ་གཅོད་བརྒྱ་དང་ཉེར་ཐམ་འབྱུང»; img. 345, l. 1, the conch year's «སྔར་བཞིན་འཐབ་པས་བརྒྱ་དང་ཉེར་ཐམ་བཅས»; img. 344 end, the turquoise year's «བརྒྱ་དང་ཉི་ཤུའོ»), prints «ཉེར་ཐམ» as the machine and BDRC's etext have it; nothing to correct. Four witnesses say what it is:

1. The sum. Each year is four groups of thirty (twenty-five pebbles and five verdicts), 4 × 30 = 120, and the three years are said to make 360, as WB's colophon (vol. 2, p. 22: «དྲུག་ཅུ་ཐམ་པ», the sixty "exactly" in «ཡོངས་དྲིལ་སུམ་བརྒྱ་དང་། དྲུག་ཅུ་ཐམ་པའི་རྡེའུ»). Only twenty fits, for the gold and the conch years as for the turquoise year's «ཉི་ཤུ».
2. The same leaves in the *Moonbeams* (dz:i050, ch. 5, f. 23b, on the scan) in nearly the same verse: the gold year «ཁྱོན་དྲིལ་བརྒྱ་དང་ཉི་ཤུའོ», the turquoise «རྡེའུ་བརྒྱ་དང་ཉི་ཤུའོ», the conch «བརྒྱ་ཉི་ཤུ», the whole «སུམ་བརྒྱ་དྲུག་ཅུ». Where Zhechen Gyaltsab's verse has «ཉེར་ཐམ», the *Moonbeams* has «ཉི་ཤུ» (twenty); the words «ཁ་དམར་ལྔ་བཅས་སུམ་ཅུ», «སྲོག་ལུས་དབང་ཀླུང་སྨེ་བ» and «ཁ་དམར་སྤྱི་བསྡོམས་ལྔ» are the same in both.
3. The word in other texts. «ཐམ་པ» after a number is "exactly, complete": WB's «དྲུག་ཅུ་ཐམ་པ» (sixty), the Life Reckoning's «ཉི་ཤུ་ཐམ་པ» (lt:p27-29, "twenty exactly"); «ཉེར» is the twenty of compounds (ཉེར་ལྔ, twenty-five), so «ཉེར་ཐམ» is «ཉི་ཤུ་ཐམ» pressed into a verse line of seven syllables. A grammar's root verses give the same word as a number to be checked by its sum: «རྐྱང་འཕུལ་ཞེ་བརྒྱད་རྩེག་འཕུལ་ནི། །ཉེར་ཐམ་འདོགས་འཕུལ་སོ་གཅིག་དང་། །སྦྲག་འཕུལ་དྲུག་བཅས་བརྒྱ་དང་ལྔ» ("the simple forms forty-eight, the stacked ones «ཉེར་ཐམ», the subscribed thirty-one, the compound six: a hundred and five"): 48 + 20 + 31 + 6 = 105 only if «ཉེར་ཐམ» is 20 (BDRC's etext of the collection *Bod kyi brda sprod phyogs bsgrigs*, MW3CN3425, image group I3CN7764, etext p. 290; the verse's lines just before write plain «ཉི་ཤུ», «འཕུལ་ཉི་ཤུ་འདོགས་འཕུལ་དགུ»; etext only, not on a scan).
4. BDRC's exact-phrase search for «ཉེར་ཐམ» (26 hits, 19 etexts; snippets read): the hits that are numbers read as twenty («སྟོང་ཕྲག་གཅིག་དང་ཉེར་ཐམ» after a count of a thousand; «ཚེས་ཉེར་ཐམ», the twentieth of the month, in a life of a master); none reads it as anything else.

What the search does not give: a dictionary entry or a gloss of «ཉེར་ཐམ» as a word (Steinert's dictionaries were not searched), so "metrical form of ཉི་ཤུ་ཐམ་པ" is our reading from the uses above, not a quoted definition.
