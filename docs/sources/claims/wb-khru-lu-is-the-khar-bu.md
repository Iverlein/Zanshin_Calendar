# «ཁྲུ་ལུ» of p. 97 is most likely the khar bu of p. 224

The Khar-bu of p. 224 is the earth lord whose seats by the day's animal p. 97 repeats, and the «ཁྲུ་ལུ» of p. 97 is most likely its other name. p. 224 says that Khar-bu, the twelve of the sun's harm (ཉི་མའི་གདུག་པ་བཅུ་གཉིས) and the sky's wheel-son (གནམ་གྱི་འཁོར་བུ) are one in essence; the Bhutan collection (I1KG11905, etext p. 570) sets «ས་བདག་ཁྲུ་ལུ» as the earth lord at the head of the very seats p. 224 lists, the mouse day at the beer jar first, with the same strained-beer lines. p. 97's own text in the 1996 etext (p. 105) is too corrupt to read («བདག་ཁྲུ་ལུ་གཉིས་པོ་ནི། རྫོར་གནས། … ཉི་མར་བོང་བར་གནས») and was not read on the scan; its «ཁྲུ་ལུ་གཉིས་པོ» (the two Khru-lu) is not explained. Narrows question 290: the same lord by the seats, the spelling not shown to be a misprint.
