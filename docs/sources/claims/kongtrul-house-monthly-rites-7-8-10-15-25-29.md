# A Rime foundation's monthly rites: the 10th guru and feast, the 25th Cakrasaṃvara, the 29th fulfilment, the 8th Tārā and long life

In the life of Kong sprul Yon tan rgya mtsho (ksr:p367-368), the rule of the monthly rites of his foundation, a Rime house of Kagyu and Nyingma lineages, is given by the day of the month:

- 7th: the offering to the image of Rje Padma Nyin byed and the Vajrasattva accomplishment offering.
- 8th: the long-life accomplishment of Guhyasamāja and the White Tārā accomplishment offering; the elders' hall does a Sangs gling gnas chog rite.
- 10th: the Lama Gongdü, a guru accomplishment and the feast offering of Vimalamitra.
- 15th: the Kālacakra offering rite.
- 25th: the Cakrasaṃvara accomplishment offering and the Khecarī (white and red) in two turns.
- 29th: the fulfilling-and-petition (སྐོང་གསོལ, skong gsol) with a long petition.
- Full and new moon: the monks' restoring and vow (གསོ་སྦྱོང, gso sbyong).
- For whom: the residents of the foundation; no benefit or fault named.
- Not named: the 9th, 11th, 24th, 26th and 30th, and no Medicine Buddha on the 8th (the 8th is Tārā and long life); the day of the Medicine Buddha's rite is not given here.

The tenth is the feast of the Guru-lama cycle, the twenty-fifth the dākinī's (Cakrasaṃvara and Khecarī) day, which agrees with the Guru's tenth and the twenty-fifth read elsewhere in the pool. Rank 3: a modern volume, facts only; the etext is all that is served.
