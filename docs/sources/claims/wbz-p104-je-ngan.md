# The eighth place not allowed is «je ngan je chung», and the fourteenth «rmang por», on the Zhol blocks

Among the twenty-six places a corpse is not to be sent (WB vol. 2, p. 104), the Zhol blocks carve the eighth's result «ཇེ་ངན་ཇེ་ཆུང», ever worse, ever smaller, with ཇ and no ར above (read with fair confidence), not «རྗེ», the lord; and the fourteenth's «རྨང་པོར་འགྲོ», ར over མ, as the 1996 print, not «དམངས་པོ».
