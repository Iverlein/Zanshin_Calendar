# ལྷས་ཚོར་ stands in the root text prints and most commentaries; ལྟས་ in the Blue Beryl's etexts, the Mongol glossary and one modern etext

Counted by BDRC's exact-phrase search on 2026-11-11 (etext snippets, no scan of any of them seen), the closing clause of ch. 81's dream list reads ལྷས་ཚོར་ in the Serthang, Derge, Chag-po-ri, Zhol, Drathang, Bhutan and 2005 prints of the root text (read on scans earlier), and in the commentaries and later writings that quote it: Khro ru, Blo bzang dbang rgyal's annotation of the Supplement, Blo bzang bstan 'dzin, Nyi zla lce nag tshang, the Gso ba rig pa collection, the Drang srong zhal lung commentary, Thub bstan phun tshogs, and a Gansu verse restatement (st:p350-351d). Only three groups show ལྟས་ཚོར་: the Blue Beryl's two chapter etexts (MW1PD3_ACDDC0, MW1PD3_B43539; the old I1PD412 etext agrees), the glossary of the Mongol Lung rigs bstan dar (two copies), and one modern Wylie etext (MW15925) whose editor corrects a ལྡས་ to ལྟས་. The weight is therefore ལྷས་ for the Tantra's text and the commentaries, ལྟས་ a minority that includes the Blue Beryl's own etext. Whether the Blue Beryl's printed page agrees remains unseen: its scans answer 401 and the chapter etexts' scans are archive.org loans (not reached). The meaning is not settled by the count: ལྷས་ would be "felt by the god", ལྟས་ "felt as an omen"; the Gansu versifier, who writes ལྷས་, gives no gloss. Search-only etexts (MW25122, MW1KG6160, MW21663, MW1PD95727, MW28849, MW1KG14616) were read as snippets only.
