# The Narthang catalogue lists the Examination of Dreams directly after the 360-verse Deceiving Death (img. 77), as Vibhūticandra's own translation, no maker named

Yigdzin-1 on the whole image 77 of I2KG212509 (vol. 225, printed folio 75), 2026-11-11, gives in one run: the Deceiving Death instruction in 360 verses (ཤླཽ་ཀ་སུམ་བརྒྱ་དྲུག་ཅུ་པ) made by Ngag dbang grags pa, translated by the Lord and Rin chen bzang po, with a note why it stands among the mother tantras; then «རྨི་ལམ་བརྟག་པ» (the Examination of Dreams), «བི་ཌཱུ་ཏི་ཙནྡྲའི་རང་འགྱུར» (Vibhūticandra's own translation), with no maker's name; then a practice of Padmadra by Dpal ye shes, translated by Sumanaśrī and Rin chen grub, most likely the next entry. So the Tibet House manuscript's catalogue has the same order as Phur lcog's catalogue (the Examination after the Deceiving Death, Vibhūticandra's translation, no author) and answers q1004 and the first part of q1143. The entry of img. 197 (svo:i197) is another entry of the same cycle ("two condensed meanings … with illustrations"); the 360-verse work is here, on img. 77, in the mother-tantra run, and the second question of RD365 is answered: the 360-verse work is listed, in a different place from the two condensed meanings.
