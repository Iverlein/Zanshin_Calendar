# WB ch. 9's colophon names earlier writers on eclipses and the author's use of them

The prose colophon (machine reading of pp. 67–69) says the author compared writers on eclipse tables: the translator Blo-gros-brtan-pa, Yag-sde Seng-ge-rgyal-mtshan, Bo-dong Phyogs-las-rnam-rgyal, Paṇchen Byams-pa-gling-pa, Mtshur-phu-ba Siddhārtha-raśmi and the works of the 'Bum-rams-pa derived from him; Dpal-mgon 'Phrin-las-pa's outer and inner roots; 'Dar-rnam and others; the reincarnate Dbang-drag-rdo-rje and Phu-klung dge-slong Blo-bzang-yon-tan, with the Phu-klung book of drawings from seven generations; Gtsang-chung Chos-grags-rgya-mtsho's notebook of eclipses; and the experiences recorded in the life of Chos-'byung. He finds none of them rooted in a good explanation of face and tail, turning back and not turning back, and says he compared them all with the moon's course watched for a year, and gave the title 'the Makara of the pure threefold analysis'. The names are given as printed, not identified.
