# No Chöd text found lists the causes of a confused dream; the pair of LGD p. 970 stays unglossed there (RD396, q908)

No Chöd or Machik text found gives a list of the causes of a confused dream. BDRC's exact phrases «རྨི་ལམ་འཁྲུལ་པའི་རྒྱུ», «རྨི་ལམ་གྱི་རྒྱུ་ལྔ», «རྨི་ལམ་འབྱུང་བའི་རྒྱུ», «རྨི་ལམ་གྱི་རྒྱུ་དང་» (RD396, 2026-11-11): the first is the Tshad ma grub pa'i le'u (Śākya mchog ldan, on the causes of mistaken dream-seeing, in Pramāṇa, not Chöd); the third (16 hits) are the Four Tantras' commentaries (Mes po'i zhal lung, the Rgyud bzhi'i rnam bshad, the Bdud rtsi snying po commentary: «རྨི་ལམ་འབྱུང་བའི་རྒྱུ་མཚན» the cause of the arising of a dream) and the Abhisamayālaṅkāra commentary; the fourth is Rang byung rdo rje and Shes rab sgron ma. The pair of LGD p. 970 «ལྷན་ཅིག་སྐྱེས་པའི་ལྷ་དང་མཉམ་པོར་འོང་བའི་བདུད» in its own words is found in two more places (2 hits): a Karma Kagyu prayer collection, in the offering to «the co-born god and the demons who come alongside, that attach to oneself» (རང་ལ་འགོ་བའི་ལྷན་ཅིག་སྐྱེས་པའི་ལྷ་དང་མཉམ་པོར་འོང་བའི་བདུད་རྣམས་ལ), and a modern prose, where they are things one has of one's own. So the pair is always two beings or forces that go with a person, never in the Chöd as a cause of dreams; the answer to q908 stays as in the claims lgd-mnyam-por-ong-ba-bdud-is-the-companion-demon-of-self-clinging and lgd-attendant-god-and-demon-swap-epithets-in-the-oath-bound-rite: LGD itself glosses neither. The phrase «ཕྱི་རོལ་གྱི་ལྷ་འདྲེ་འཁྲུགས་པ» of the same line (outer gods and spirits in tumult) has no hit.
