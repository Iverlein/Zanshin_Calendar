# Klong rdol, bad omens, for a practitioner and for others

In the verses the article quotes (p. 74), Klong rdol bla ma's bad omens in a dream are: for a practitioner in diligent yoga, falling again and again into a pit, going up and down slopes, entering a narrow crooked path, going towards sorrow and fear are the obscurations (སྒྲིབ་པ, sgrib pa) of the deeds that lead to the higher realms and liberation, as the Buddha's sutra says; for others, crossing a muddy bog and sinking, running to a foul place, wearing a filthy robe, eating or washing in filth (shade and impurity found); much gold dust enjoyed, riding a donkey without a voice, nakedness (the result of earlier plans); cloth without value, smoke, looking in a mirror without a hat, walking at noon (sorrow); old clothes, eating and drinking filth, creatures clinging to the body, held by a jackal or similar (illness); barley, peas and empty vessels in numbers, fragments of arrows and bows (quarrels soon); arrows and weapons piercing the body, an army chasing, flesh-light and fire-light, hail, going into a hole (harm by others' spite).
