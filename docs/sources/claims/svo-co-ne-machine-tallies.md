# The Co ne Svapnohana: tallies of the doubtful places by Yigdzin-1 on line strips

Yigdzin-1 on deskewed line strips of the Co ne's Svapnohana (472 runs, 2026-11-10; the eye settled no letter) gives, with the runs of RD273 and RD276 counted in:

- the second watch's first segment ends «བརྒྱད་པར» (with ར) in every run that reached it, neither the Narthang and Peking «བརྒྱད་པ་ལ» nor the Derge etext's «བརྒྱད་བར་ལ»; its last syllable is not settled;
- the third watch's line is the shorter «ཐུན་གཉིས་གསུམ་གྱི་བར», without «ཟླ་བ»;
- the fourth watch's line is there, «ཐུན་བཞི་ཟླ་བ་ཕྱེད་པར…», with «པར» and never «བར», its last syllable «དུ» (three runs), «བྱ» (two) or «ལ» (two), not settled;
- «བཟློག» against «བགྲོད» is two to two, «འགྲོན་དུ» against «འགྲོན་བུ» about fifteen to seven for «དུ», «བཅུའི་བར» with a vowel sign after «བཅུ» not decided, «རྟ་བླ་མོ» in nearly all runs and never a plain «ལ་མོ», and the Gāyatrī's first word is «གས་ཏྲི» of two syllables in all nine runs that reached it.

The Co ne is therefore a witness to the second watch's and fourth watch's «པར» and to the subjoined «བླ་མོ» of the Derge and the Dpe bsdur ma etext, by machine only.
