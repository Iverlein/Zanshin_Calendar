# The Blue Beryl's printed page of ch. 81's dream list is not served by BDRC: the etext's ལྟས་ stands unchecked

The Blue Beryl's printed page for ch. 81's dream list (the Dpe tshogs print, part 3, p. 1148; q910, q950) cannot be seen on BDRC (RD356, 2026-11-10): the manifests of I1PD411 and I1PD412 list 41 canvases each and the image service answers 401 for every page beyond, as RD303 and PLAN.md record; I8LS20838 img. 228 holds another work's page. The Blue Beryl's wording therefore rests on its etext (ལྟས་ in the closing clause once, ལྷས་ in its outline) and on the Khro ru gloss that copies it (ལྷས་, scan); the glossary to the Supplement (st-lhas-tshor-glossary-names-ltas-as-a-variant) shows that ལྟས་ was a variant in circulation.
