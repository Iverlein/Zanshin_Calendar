# The general board's «མི་ཚན» is the great man, «ཁོན» the Bon priest

# The thirty's general board: «མི་ཚན» is the great man (མི་ཆེན, mi chen), «ཁོན» the Bon priest (བོན, bon)

What WB vol. 1, p. 472, ll. 1–2 names when the whole board is read: «ཡོན་ཏན་མཁན་དང་མི་ཚན་སར … ཁོན་སར་ནག་ན་གསས་མཁར་བརྩིག» (questions 203 and 204).

- **The great man (question 204).** The 1996 print and its etext, and the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5028 img. 467, read again by eye at 1× and 2×, 2026-11-01), print «མི་ཚན» ("household, section"). WB's own point 30 is "for the great men, the learned and the craftsmen" and its first line names «མི་ཆེན་ཡོན་ཏན་མཁན» (p. 470, l. 1); the *Moonbeams* as KD prints it lists the fifteenth Tibetan point as «མི་ཆེན་ཡོན་ཏན་ཅན་དང་བཟོ་རིག་པ» (kd.3:p498-500); and the *Garland of Stars* (རྒྱུ་སྐར་ཕྲེང་བ, rgyu skar phreng ba) has the board's line as «ཡོན་ཏན་མཁན་དང་མི་ཆེན་སར», "the place of the learned and the great man" (kd.3:p545-546, etext). So «མི་ཚན» is a slip of ཚ for ཆ, and the line is the place of the learned and the great man, whose rite when black is to raise the luck, as point 30 is the luck's. A *mi tshan* is in the dictionaries a monastery's section of monks (Rangjung Yeshe, Ives Waldo, *Tshig mdzod chen mo*), which does not fit a board of laymen.
- **The Bon priest (question 203).** The reprint reads «ཁོན» at 3× (the first letter is ཁ to the eye; at 2113 px I do not call it certain). The *Garland* has «བོན་སར་ནག་ནི་གསས་མཁར་བརྩིག», "black in the Bon priest's place, build the gsas castle (གསས་མཁར, gsas mkhar)" (etext). The trigram khon (ཁོན, the earth, south-west trigram; dictionaries) has no place in a list of persons (learned, minister, women, boys, girls). WB's own fourth point is for the Bon priests (p. 456) and its first check's black reading is the same rite: "building the Bon god-castle makes it good", and the *Garland* has it too, «བོན་གྱི་གསས་མཁར་བརྩིགས» (printed p. 523, etext p. 543). So the line reads as the place of the Bon priest of point 4; «ཁོན» for «བོན» is likely, not proved: the *Garland* is read in the etext only (the lent scan not opened), the 1996 print and the reprint both read ཁོན, and the Zhol print's folio (WBZ img. 628) is too cursive at 3750 px for the one letter.
- The persons of the line then are the learned and great man (point 30, the luck), the Bon priest (point 4), the minister-uncle (ཞང་བློན, zhang blon; the *Garland* has «ཞང་བློན་སར»), women, boys and girls (points 27–29).
