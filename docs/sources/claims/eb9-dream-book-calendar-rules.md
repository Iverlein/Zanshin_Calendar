# The Ebara dream book's calendar content: the table of days a dream is not told (as in the 万代大雑書), one autumn entry, no 初夢

The 新版ゆめあはせ (夢占吉凶秘傳書), read through on the 国文研 copy of the Kaei 5 (1852) printing (eight leaves, ten images, the first and last being the covers), holds one rule that names a calendar term as such: the table of the days on which a dream is not told, twelve columns for the months 正月 to 十二月, each with day branches of the cycle. It repeats the table of the 万代大雑書's item ゆ六 cell for cell as far as the glyphs can be matched (the same count of branches in every month, the same equalities: 四月 = 五月, 八月 = 九月), so the two books give one table, not two.

Apart from it the book names a season once, in one entry (a sight seen "in the autumn" is better, eb9:i4), and two to three days as the time within which a dream shows (eb9:i9c). In the legible parts it has no preface, no table of the day's omens, no article on the 初夢, on the first night of the year, on 元日, 節分, 庚申 or a day of the cycle for dreaming, and none of the season-by-season rules of the 夢合延寿袋大成 (ymk:p30, ymk:p52). The book is a list of short entries, each a thing seen and "if seen, great good / bad", in the iroha order, in two text tiers with a picture between; the tiers were read by eye at crops of the 5616-px images, the cursive only in part decoded (the hentaigana of the table and of some entries are doubtful), looking for a month, season, day or New Year word; the picture panels' captions were not read. This is a claim of absence over the leaves seen, not a transcription of every entry.
