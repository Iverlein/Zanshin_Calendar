# Tāranātha's Sbyor drug gegs sel reads dreams of darkness, snakes, horses, dogs and women as a spirit's harm in the six yogas, with a remedy

In Tāranātha's (ཏཱ་ར་ནཱ་ཐ, Tā ra nā tha) Sbyor drug gegs sel, on the obstacles of the six yogas (སྦྱོར་བ་ཡན་ལག་དྲུག, sbyor ba yan lag drug) of the Kālacakra, the dream is a sign of what harms the practice: an upper spirit's harm brings darkness in dream, a lower one's dreams of sinful snakes and many animals, and the sign in dream comes with the sign in fact; a leaking of the drop by a spirit shows as dreams of horses, dogs and women, and the remedy is the common practice and recitation of one's deity, or for one practised in the fierce fire (གཏུམ་མོ, gtum mo) its blazing together with holding the wind, burning what came in the dream to ash. No hour, lunar day or planet is named. Etext only.
