# The household round's «མ་ཏྲི» ransom and the white offering at the «སྐྱེད་པུར»

The ransom is «བདུད་མདོས་མ་ཏྲི་ཁྲག་མདོས», the demon ransom, the *ma tri* and the blood ransom: Yigdzin-1 and the etext read «མ་ཏྲི», not «མ་ཏྲཾ» (MITRA «མ་ཊཾ»); the same run stands in Si tu dbon Karma's *Bum bzang* and, as «མ་ཏྲི་ཁྲག་མདོས», in a Nyingma list of rites (Stag sham Nus ldan rdo rje's receipt, beside the great ransom of the three realms and the cutting of the demon's snare), so the *ma tri* ransom is a ransom of its own, named with the blood ransom (a mother-goddess ransom, from the Sanskrit, is the likely sense; no dictionary has it). The offering is «ཁུ་དཀར་གསོལ», to offer white *khu* (the same words are used of offering to stupas in a biography; a white milk-food). The place is spelled «སྐྱེད་པུར» on the 1996 print, *skyed par* in the Gser thang etext and in KD, *sgyed pur* in the 1996 search text, «སྐྱེད་བུར» in Zhe chen rgyal tshab's restatement. In a Tsolho history the *skyed pu* is the hearth's stand in a tent, joined to the stove-front, which fits the hearth that the red pebble's remedy names on the same page but is shown by no text for this remedy; the Bon *skyed pur* of other texts is a planted ritual stake, another word. The Zhol print was read by eye on WBZ img. 506 by RD46 (2026-10-23) and gives «མ་ཏྲཾ» with doubt, so the prints may part there: «ཏྲི» is the 1996 print's and the other manuals', «ཏྲཾ» the Zhol print's doubtful reading; not reread here.
