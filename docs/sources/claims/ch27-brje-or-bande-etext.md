# Ch. 27: the etext reads the stack of «བརྗེ» as «བནྡེ», which would give the black verse its seventh syllable

BDRC's etext of the 1996 print reads the stack that the scans show as «བརྗེ» both ways. At p. 23, l. 22 it has «བརྗེའི་རྩིས་སྐོར་བཞི», at l. 25 «ནག་ནང་བནྡེ་གྲོང་སྤྲང་བྱེད» and at p. 17, l. 6 «སྤུངས་ན་བནྡེའི་ཕུར་ཁ་ལངས»; in the grave chapter it has «བནྡེ་མགོ་ནག» and «ཡང་ན་བརྗེའི་འགྲུལ་གྱིས་ལན», the two spellings in one verse. «བནྡེ» (ban de) read as two syllables gives the black verse of p. 23 its missing seventh syllable, and as the begging one it is the set's person, a monk; the set's content (vajra, discipline, begging) already pointed there (ch27-brje-set-is-the-monk).

Against this stands the eye: RD89 read both stacks at 9 to 10 times on the 1996 scan as a ra over a ja with e, stroke for stroke the same, and on the reprint at 4 times (RD98, p. 23, l. 25) the stack is a solid mass in which a na with a subjoined da is not excluded and a ra over a ja not shown. The etext is a machine reading that varies from line to line over one glyph, so it proves no spelling; it gives a reason for the dropped syllable that the scans do not exclude. Not settled: whether the block's stack is «བརྗེ» or «བནྡེ». Whether Zhechen Gyaltsab's spiritual friend for this set (ch27-brje-set-zg-spiritual-friend) and a monk can both be right is open.
