# The twelve earth lords by the day's animal and the corpse's path to shun

The twelve earth lords by the day's animal, each with the corpse's path to be shunned (WB vol. 2, p. 225; the list runs on to p. 226). Rat: Gnyan-tra (གཉན་ཁྲ, gnyan khra), the dead die thrice, no corpse out by the lower north. Ox: Green Gnyan, repeat deaths, no cemetery. Tiger: white garuda, no burning, no path by the upper east. Hare: earth tortoise, no wake path to the lower east. Dragon: the Yellow Banner, no path to the south-east. Snake: Tsangkun, good on the great road. Horse: the earth lord on the rat path, shun the grain, wake door and horse-top. Sheep: Besna (བེ་སྣ), the "Great Weapon", seven die, no path to the south-west. Monkey: the Tiger's Tail, no wake path to the upper west. Bird: Hethon (ཧེ་ཐོན), king, a wake brings loss of wealth, no path to the lower west. Dog: Thekhyim (ཐེ་ཁྱིམ), no path to the north-west. Pig: Gnyan-be (the line goes on p. 226). The old paths come from the same family of lords as the dwelling lords above; this page gives the day's courses.
