# The seven kinds of conduct of the sage's text: three told, four named

The chapter names seven kinds of conduct (སྤྱོད་པ་བདུན, *spyod pa bdun*) of the sage's text, of which it states only the first three with their mansions and results: the ordinary conduct (Svātī, Bharaṇī, Kṛttikā, Rohiṇī): no disease, heavy rain, a good year; the leading conduct (Ārdrā, Maghā, Āśleṣā); the summarised conduct (Punarvasu, Pūrvaphalgunī, Uttaraphalgunī), and, when the leading and the summarised meet, disease and a middling year. The other four (the sharp, the one from the end of the combination, the fierce sinful one, and the rest) are named without their lists. They are the seven courses of Mercury of the Bṛhat Saṃhitā 7.8 (Prākṛta, Vimiśra, Saṃkṣipta, Tīkṣṇa, Yogānta, Ghora, Pāpa), the last two folded into «དྲག་པོ་སྡིག་པ» and «སོགས»; the numbers one to six, seven to twelve, thirteen to twenty-four, twenty-five to thirty are the days of effect of Mercury's four motions (vikala 6, vakra 12, ativakra 24, ṛjvī 30; Bṛhat Saṃhitā 7.15).
