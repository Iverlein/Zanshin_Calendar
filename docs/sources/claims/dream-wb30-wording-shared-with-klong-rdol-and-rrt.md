# WB ch. 30's dream list shares wording with Klong rdol's verses and the 'Bri gung method, in no order of descent (RD319)

Set beside the lists of the pool, WB ch. 30's dream list (p. 144, ll. 11–24, [wb.2:p144l](../corpus/wb/ch30.md#wb-2-p144l)) shares words with two of them, Klong rdol's verses (RST, ACIP etext, `rst:p1-8`; KBS, the article's quotation, machine, `kbs:p72-78`) and the 'Bri gung extended method (RRT, etext, `rrt:p186-195`), and with the Sakya Paṇḍita method (SKP, machine, `skp:p40-50`) and the Lama Gongdü's Palri print (`lgd.42:p966-971b`, machine) in a few phrases. Not one of them carries WB's lines in WB's order, and WB's list is in seven-syllable lines of its own. The pairs, each phrase quoted from its passage:

**What this does and does not show.** The wording is closest to Klong rdol's verses: three images (G1, B5 with B6, G9) share a line's words, and the order of nakedness and bare riding is the same in all three texts. A shared stock of images and phrases behind the dream lists is likely; a borrowing in either direction is not proved. The pool has no text that says whence WB took its list, and no earlier text with WB's lines (dream-wb30-list-source-not-named). The verses of RST and KBS say themselves that they come from the Chöd visualisation collection of Ma gcig (rst-is-klong-rdols-dream-text), and SKP gives them under Vairocana's name, so they are a retelling too; RST is an etext and KBS a machine reading of an article, never read on a page image. WB's list is shorter than theirs, is cut into seven-syllable lines, puts good before bad, and judges for a reckoner (a defect in what is reckoned), where Klong rdol's verses judge for the dreamer.
