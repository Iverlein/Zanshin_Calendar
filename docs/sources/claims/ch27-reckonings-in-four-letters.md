# Ch. 27: «དཀར་པ་ན», «སྤ་འཁར» and «ལྕུག་ཕྲན» in the reckonings in four, read on the 1996 scan

Three words of the reckonings in four (WB vol. 2, pp. 23–24) that RD38 left in doubt or Yigdzin-1 misread are settled on the 1996 print's scan (I1KG12907 img. 31–32, crops of single lines at 8 to 12 times) beside the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5029 img. 29–30, 5 to 9 times):

- p. 23, l. 16, the king's life force white: «སྲོག་གི་རྡེའུ་དཀར་པ་ན», with pa, the same letter as the black verse's «ནག་པ་ན» on the same line; the reprint lost the pa's left stem and Yigdzin-1 reads «དཀར་བ་ན».
- p. 23, l. 16, the line's last syllable: «སྤ», so the white verse reads «སྤ་འཁར་ཕྱག་ནས་མ་ཤོར», the same «སྤ་འཁར» (the staff or sceptre) as the black verse's «སྲོག་གི་སྤ་འཁར་ཕྱག་ནས་ཤོར»; no u under it on either scan, against Yigdzin-1's «སྤུ».
- p. 24, l. 28, the child's body white: «ལུས་དཀར་ལྕུག་ཕྲན་ཟོར་མ་བཏབ», the lower letter of the stack closed in a round bowl, unlike the «ལྷ» of «ལྷ་ནད» on the same line; MITRA reads «ལྕུག», Yigdzin-1 «ཕྱུག». «ལྕུག་ཕྲན» is taken as the small shoot (ལྕུག, lcug), the body's image beside the life force's green rice-stalk «འབྲས་ལྗང»; the word was not looked up in a dictionary for this reading.

The minister's power verse (p. 24, l. 16) reads on both scans «གོས་ལེགས་ཚོང་ལེགས་ཟས་ནོར་འདུ», with no ra in «གོས» (clothes), while the white verse begins «དབང་དཀར་གྲོས་ཀྱི་མཚོ་མ་སྐམས» (the lake of counsel) and the black reads «ནག་ན་གྲོས་ཉེས་ཚོང་ཉེས་འབྱུང» (bad counsel): «གོས» stands as printed; whether it is a slip for «གྲོས» is not shown. The small ring over «བཟང» at the end of p. 23, l. 17 is on the reprint only.
