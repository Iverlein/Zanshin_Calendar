# Mipham's own practice: the feast on the tenth of both halves without a break, the Khro chu and protector offering on the 29th, Sarasvatī on the 15th

Dilgo Khyentse's short life of Mipham (mrs:p76-77) names what Mipham kept without a break, as a commitment he made before his family lord:

- Days: the tenth of the waxing and of the waning half, every month (the feast offering, ཚོགས་མཆོད, tshogs mchod); the twenty-ninth, "or the like days" (the reading of «ནམ་ཚེས་ལ» is not certain); the fifteenth and the day of Mars, "or the like dates".
- Practice: on the tenth, the feast offering; on the twenty-ninth, the poison torma of Khro chu and the offering and petition to the Dharma protectors; on the fifteenth and on a Tuesday, the offering to Sarasvatī (དབྱངས་ཅན་མ, dbyangs can ma) taught in the Sngags kyi be bum.
- For whom: Mipham himself; the text gives a commitment of one practitioner, not a rule for others.
- Year's end: seven days of the torma-averting rite of the Eight Pronouncements (བཀའ་བརྒྱད་བདེ་གཤེགས་འདུས་པ).
- Benefit or fault: none named.
- Tradition: Nyingma (Mipham, a Nyingma master). Rank 3: a modern author's account, facts only.

This gives the app's 10th (both halves), the 15th and the 29th a Nyingma witness for a master's own practice; it names no 8th, 25th or 30th.
