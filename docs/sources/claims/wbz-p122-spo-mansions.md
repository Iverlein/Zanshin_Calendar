# P. 122's dragon and snake mansions are «spo» and «spo smad» on the blocks

In the remedies by the dead's year (WB vol. 2, p. 122), the Zhol blocks carve the dragon's mansion as one stack with o, as the 1996 «སྤོ», and the snake's, printed «སྦ་སྨད» in 1996, with the vowel o as well: «སྤོ་སྨད» or «སྦོ་སྨད», the lower part of the same mansion name; the Taikhang carves the snake's o too; whether the stack's lower letter is པ or བ is not visible. The bird's «ལག་སོར» stands as printed. The page does not say which mansions these are.
