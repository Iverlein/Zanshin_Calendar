# The six times of the month in the Larung print's note: the 8th, 10th, 15th, 25th, 29th and the new moon

The Larung print of Longchenpa's commentary on the Guhyagarbha sets a note at the words "the six times" (དུས་དྲུག, dus drug) of the verse on the middling practitioner (lcb:p568): three times in the waxing half, the eighth, the tenth and the fifteenth, and three in the waning half, the twenty-fifth, the twenty-ninth and the new moon (གནམ་སྟོང, gnam stong). The verse's "four times" (དུས་བཞི, dus bzhi) has no note on the page.

- Days: the 8th, 10th, 15th, 25th, 29th and 30th of the lunar month.
- Practice: the recitation of mantra with the seals, kept without a gap by the middling practitioner for a month at these times; by the verse's last lines the feast and the torma follow the same measure.
- For whom: holders of the tantric commitments.
- Tradition: Nyingma (a note on Longchenpa's text; whose note, the print does not say on the page read).

The note is read on the page image. It is a gloss of unknown hand, so it ranks below Longchenpa's own words, which name only "the full moon, the new moon, the eighth and the others". The Dpal brtsegs print's etext (lcm) has no such note.
