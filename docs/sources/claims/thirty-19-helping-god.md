# The thirty: the nineteenth point, the divine owl, for which god helps

The nineteenth of the thirty starting points (སྐོར་འགོ, skor 'go), from the mouth of the divine owl (ལྷ་བྱ་གོང་མོ, lha bya gong mo), is read for which god helps. Its eight pebbles are laid as for every point (claims thirty-count and thirty-eight-fighters), each white or black, and read so:

| Pebble | Asks | White | Black | Remedy for black |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Whether the male god's (ཕོ་ལྷ, pho lha) row is broken | Not broken, good | Broken: recite the *lha mdo* (ལྷ་མདོ, lha mdo; not identified); the male god flees | Calling back what fled (འབྲོས་བསྙགས, 'bros bsnyags) and the mending rite (ཡོ་བཅོས, yo bcos) |
| 2 | Whether the maternal uncle's god (ཞང་ལྷ, zhang lha) wanders in the human realm | The uncle's god leads and does not wander, good | It wanders: sorrow on the uncle's side | The black three-headed nine thread-cross (དགུ་མདོས་ནག་པོ་མགོ་གསུམ, dgu mdos nag po mgo gsum) |
| 3 | Whether the war god (དགྲ་ལྷ, dgra lha) has fallen to the demons | The war god protects, good | The war god has fallen to the demons | Offer to the war god, press down the enemy sri (དགྲ་སྲི, dgra sri), the rite of expelling misfortune and raising glory (བྱུར་ཕུད་དཔལ་བསྐྱེད, byur phud dpal bskyed) |
| 4 | Whether the shoulder god (ཕྲག་ལྷ, phrag lha) is pressed by pollution | Not pressed; enjoyments good | Pressed by pollution | Ablution of the gods, offering to the gods (ལྷ་གསོལ, lha gsol), fumigation offering (ལྷ་བསངས, lha bsangs), calling back what fled and the like |
| 5 | Whether the local god is pressed by an enemy | Not pressed; it protects one | Pressed by an enemy | Offer to the local god, ransom from death, ransom the la, the life-spirit (བླ, bla), press down the enemy sri, the enemy pit (དགྲ་བྲུབ, dgra brub), the fumigation offering |
| 6 | Whether the god of the castle's peak (རྩེ་ལྷ, rtse lha) has been carried off by a lawsuit | Not pressed by a lawsuit, good | Pressed by a lawsuit | Offering and fumigation help |
| 7 | Whether the mother god (མ་ལྷ, ma lha) is struck by the pollution of widowhood | Not struck: good in the mother's place | Struck: somewhat bad for being married into another country | Offer to the mother god, protecting prosperity (གཡང་སྐྱབས, g.yang skyabs), calling back what fled |
| 8 | Whether the life god and the inner god (སྲོག་ལྷ་ནང་ལྷ, srog lha nang lha) lurk at a corpse | They do not, good | The life god disputes over a corpse, bad | Fumigation, the rite of removing the corpse (རོ་དབྱུང, ro dbyung) and the white rite of summoning life (ཚེ་འགུགས, tshe 'gugs) |

Whose year is reckoned: the person's own (the notes, vol. 2, p. 533: «གཞན་རྣམས་སོ་སོའི་ལོ་རྟ»).

Which of the eight fighting pebbles is which check is not stated; the order of p. 453 is the likely one (claim thirty-board-groups; question 259).
