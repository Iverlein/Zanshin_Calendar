# No commentary on WB glosses its eight watches; the wording comes from the Dbyangs 'char's half-watches (RD322, q939)

The commentaries on WB read say nothing of its eight watches (ཐུན་བརྒྱད, thun brgyad, vol. 2 p. 403; the half-watches ཐུན་ཕྱེད, thun phyed, vol. 1 p. 135): Mipham's notes (RD314), the regent's replies (G.ya' sel, poor OCR) and Dpal bsdus's manual hold no gloss of them. The wording has a source in the Dbyangs 'char verse, quoted in two modern collections: «the day-and-night's half-watches are divided in eight; from the day's planet each half-watch has a planet and a nāga rising together», so WB's eight equal parts are that verse's, called half-watches there and watches in WB's p. 403, and the Hevajra books' eight watches (four by day, four by night) are the same count under another name. This is a reading from the wording; no commentator says it.
