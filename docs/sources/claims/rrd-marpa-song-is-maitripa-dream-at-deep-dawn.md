# The song of the three watches is Marpa's report of Maitrīpa's teaching in the dream of the deep dawn, given only to Mi la, Mgo legs and Ngan rdzong ston pa

In the song the saying belongs to, the dream of the dawn is the one in which a teacher comes: the song itself was heard, it says, in the dream of the deep dawn (དགུང་ད་ནངས་གཉིད་ཀྱི་རྨི་ལམ, dgung da nangs gnyid kyi rmi lam) from the mouth of Maitrīpa, in the temple of Rin chen tshul in Nepal, and it was given in Tibet to Mi la and Mar pa Mgo legs and Ngan rdzong ston pa only. Its teaching is that the dream is illusion and the signs (a corpse, a dog, a child and the rest) point to mind; the three-part rule for the night serves only as the opening of the instruction, and no act is named for a midnight dream beyond not trusting it.
