# The wheels of WB ch. 12 in order: turtle, lion throne, parasol, great mutual, Brahma, the mansions' syllables

The twelfth chapter judges by a succession of named wheels (*'khor lo*), each a place for setting the planets over the twenty-eight mansions: the turtle wheel (*rus sbal 'khor lo*, p. 91: the region's good and ill by the head, the four limbs, the shoulders, heart and tail, the mansions three to each part from the Pleiades); the lion throne wheel (*seng gdan 'khor lo*, pp. 92–93: the kings of the centre, south and north, with its nine regalia); the parasol wheel (*gdugs 'khor*, p. 93: a serpent bearing parasols); the great mutual wheel (*phan tshun chen po'i 'khor lo*, p. 94: seven petals of four mansions, the planets' straight looks); the Brahma wheel for the questioner's state (*tshangs pa'i 'khor lo*, p. 95: nine sets of three mansions); then, at p. 96, a large drawing of the mansions' syllables with the hundred feet and the eighty-one wheel, whose text and results run on to p. 99. The chapter's own list of what it leaves out (p. 103) names the lion-throne, accomplishing Brahma and parasol wheels as the seventh, and the rain reckoning as the tenth.
