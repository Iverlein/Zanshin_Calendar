# The Narthang manuscript reads «བགྲོད་མིན» and «བཅུ་ཡི་བར»

On the Narthang manuscript (I2KG212312) the Svapnohana's clause reads «བགྲོད་མིན» (img. 284 line 5, 4–5×: བ, ག with ྲ and ོ, final ད) and not «བཟློག» (the Co ne machine runs' alternative of RD273); the following «མིན» has a head not told apart from ཤ (moderate). Beside it «བཅུ་ཡི་བར» reads with ཡི clear (img. 286 line 2, 6×), not «པི». So the Derge etext, the Peking print and the Narthang manuscript agree on both clauses by eye; the Co ne's readings are machine only. The clause's sense (q813) and the unit of «བཅུ» (q814) are not given by the line.
