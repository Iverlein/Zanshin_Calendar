# The elapsed days worked into weekday, animal, element, sme ba and trigram

WB turns the count of elapsed days into the day's five signs by working it in five places (vol. 1, p. 187): the first place, add 4 and throw away sevens, gives the weekday; the second, add 1 and divide by 12, gives the sun's animal (counting from the dragon; a rising *thig le* is taken as the hare); the third, add 5 and divide by the quarters, gives the element (1 and 2 fire, 3 and 4 earth, 5 and 6 iron, 7 and 8 water, 9 and 0 wood); the fourth, add 4 and divide by nine, gives the *sme ba*; the fifth, add the elements (5) and divide by eight, gives the trigram, 1 being *li* and 2 *khon*, and so on in order. The elapsed days themselves come from the corrected month times 30 with the date mixed in, over 1080 and the like, as the verse before it works them out; the count is not set down in a form that could be used without the passage.
