# Ch. 27, p. 17, l. 26: «འུར་ཞོགས», an alarm, on the reprint and the 1996 scan

On p. 17, l. 26, in the reckoning of weapons, the cut-inward verdict reads «ཞག་བཅུ་ཚུན་ལ་འུར་ཞོགས་དང་། །ར་མདའ་རིང་ཐུང་འབྱུང་བ་ཡིན», "within ten days an alarm (འུར་ཞོགས, 'ur zhogs) and long and short goat-horn arrows (ར་མདའ, ra mda') come".

Read on the scan, 2026-10-28 (RD90): on the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5029 img. 23, l. 26, at 4×) and on the 1996 print's own scan (I1KG12907 img. 25, at 5×) the first letter of the syllable before «ཞོགས» is open and rounded, the shape of the འ of «འབྱུང» later on the same line, and has neither the stem of ཞ nor the closed box and right upright of the line's «བ» («བ་ཡིན»). So the 1996 setting prints «འུར་ཞོགས», as RD51 and RD62 read the reprint and as the Zhol print and the Sakya etext give it; RD38's «ཞུར[?]» is withdrawn. «འུར་ཞོགས» is a dictionary headword, startling or alarming talk ([ch27-words-in-steinert-dictionaries](ch27-words-in-steinert-dictionaries.md)), so the verdict is an alarm, not a raid. The Taikhang's own first letter (wb.2:p17c) stays unread by eye (RD77).
