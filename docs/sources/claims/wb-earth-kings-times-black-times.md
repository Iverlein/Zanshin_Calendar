# The times of the earth kings and the black times

The times of the earth kings and the black times (WB vol. 2, p. 236). The eleven times of the earth kings (numbered 5 to 15 on the reprint) dwell above the four executioners in front; shun spectacles, the dead's wake and fort work; if violated the chief lord, the root and the village head are harmed. The seasonal table of the time gods: spring first month the snake's time on the upper south, the middle the horse's time upon the monkey, the last the sheep's upon the pig; summer: monkey on snake, bird on pig, rat on the upper north; autumn: pig on dragon, rat on the south-west, ox on dog; winter: tiger on the lower east, hare on the lower south, dragon on bird; the source is Piling Pharma. The black times (11) are reckoned from the same table: on rat and horse days the times of bird and hare are black; on such a day weighty works (grave, fort, feast, consecration, enthronement, taking land or a bride) are bad and fierce work is struck back; the remedy is the figures drawn at the door.
