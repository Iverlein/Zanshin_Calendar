# Rat+na gling pa's treasure: asking in a dream about the lifespan and the next birth

A treasure text (གཏེར་མ, gter ma) of Rat+na gling pa (RGD) teaches two ways of asking, in a dream (རྨི་ལམ, rmi lam), about the length of one's life (ཚེ་ཚད, tshe tshad) and the place of the next birth. The first is through Avalokiteśvara: a clean quiet place, no meat, beer, garlic or onion, an image and offerings, seven prayers, the mantra 108 or 1000 times over a powder snuffed into both nostrils, the lion's posture, a one-pointed sleep; the sign is a monk or a white man who prophesies in the dream, or a sign, or the form of the place of birth. The second is through Mañjuśrī: a thousand mantras at the evening watch over clean water, drunk before lying down; the sign is a clear voice or the appearance of the body of the place of birth. It is a rite for asking in a dream, like kcm's in the same volume, not a list of what dreams mean; the text says what to do when no sign comes: pray again and offer better. Read in the etext only.
