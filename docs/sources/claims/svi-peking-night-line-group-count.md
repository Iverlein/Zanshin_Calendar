# The Peking's last part of the night: six groups from བ to the shad, which fits «བཏབ་ལ་ཉལ» (RD354)

On the Peking print (I1KG13215 img. 310 line 1, 4x crop of x 3960–4390) the groups between the མ of «ལམ» and the shad are, from the left: a བ, three groups not told apart (a stack with a long tail, a flat-headed group, a looped group), a ལ-shaped group, a ཉ-shaped group with a long tail and ལ. That is six groups from བ to the shad. «བརྟགས་པ་ལ་ཉལ» (RD234, RD306) needs eight groups from བ, «བརྟགས་ལ་ཉལ» seven, «བཏབ་ལ་ཉལ» six; the count fits «བཏབ་ལ་ཉལ» as the Dpe bsdur ma's «དབཏབ» for the Peking and Narthang and Yigdzin-1's eight of eight, and goes against «བརྟགས་པ་ལ». Not settled: the long tail of the second group is not a ཏ's stroke, a stack and a ག can join in this hand, and the scan's JPEG blocks blur the joins; a sharper image or a reader of the hand decides. q961 stays open with the count recorded.
