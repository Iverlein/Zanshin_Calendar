# «སྐྱུར» in the second examination: a sour thing, beside the mourning place's food; unsettled

# «སྐྱུར» in the second examination: a sour thing, with the mourning place's food beside it; unsettled

WB vol. 2, p. 57, last lines of the elements' causes (1996 scan img. 65 l. 26; Zhol print img. 707 l. 4, read by Yigdzin-1 «ཡུགས་སའི་སྐྱུར»): «མེ་ལ་ཆུས་བཅད་ཤི་བ་ན། །བྱང་དུ་ཡུགས་སའི་སྐྱུར་གྱིས་ལན།», "died with fire cut by water: in the north, the «སྐྱུར» (skyur) of a mourning place (ཡུགས་ས, yugs sa) answers for it"; and p. 58, l. 25, the mewa's reasons: «ལྔ་སེར་སྐྱུར་རམ་དཀོར་འབགས་ལན།», "five yellow: «སྐྱུར» or the misuse of the offerings' goods answers for it". The Zhol print's cursive line at p. 57 is consistent with Yigdzin-1's reading, but a prefixed letter before the ska cannot be excluded at that size.

What the context shows:
- The causes of the same list are things done or met: grief (མྱ་ངན), relying on a red hill, feud, a red offering and the hearth. In the mewa's list the neighbours are foods and acts: for one white «ཡུགས་ས་མི་གཙང་ཟས་ཀྱིས་ལན» (the mourning place's impure food), for four green digging the soil (ཕྲུ་རློག), for six white impurity and a broken offering of the times. The same two words, the mourning place and a thing that answers, stand in both lists; so «ཡུགས་སའི་སྐྱུར» is of the kind of «ཡུགས་ས་མི་གཙང་ཟས», a food or drink of the mourning, which is why "sour" fits; Lha mo Tshul khrims's verse restatement keeps both lines as they are (her p. 57, MW1KG21818).
- WB uses «སྐྱུར་པོ» (skyur po) for the sour taste in the earth lords' lists: «མངར་མོ་བྱུང་ན་མི་སྒབ་རིང་། །སྐྱུར་པོ་བྱུང་ན་དཀོར་བདག་གནོད།», "if sweet comes ... long; if sour comes the treasure's owner harms" (1996 vol. 2, etext img. 157 = printed p. 149; two more in the etext, img. 168 and 177, «སྐྱུར་པོ་འཐུང་»); these are etext readings, not read on the scan.
- The verb "to cast" (བསྐྱུར, bskyur) is written with its prefix in every place the etext of vol. 2 has it (img. 71, 110, 111, 133, «ཆུ་ལ་བསྐྱུར»); the p. 57 and p. 58 word has none, which tells against "cast-off things". The etext's reading of the two words was checked on the scan only for p. 57 and p. 58.

So the likelier sense is "a sour [food or drink]", the mourning place's; the earlier sense "cast-off things of a mourning" (wb-ch29-words-in-the-dictionaries) is not excluded, since the dictionaries give both «སྐྱུར» as sour and as thing cast away. Not settled.
