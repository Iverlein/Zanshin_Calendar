# Padmasambhava's oath to come on the tenth of every month, and where it is stated

Padmasambhava's promise is that he comes on the tenth day (ཚེས་བཅུ, tshes bcu) of every month, and it is stated in treasure texts as his own oath. Mipham's commentary on the Seven-Line Prayer gathers the statements (mph.127:i302, i303-304, i305): from the Means of Urging on the Tenth Day (ཚེས་བཅུ་བསྐུལ་ཐབས, tshes bcu bskul thabs), a treasure of Ratna gling pa, «བོད་ཀྱི་རྒྱལ་བློན་བུ་སློབ་ལ༔ བྱུང་ངོ་ཟླ་བའི་ཚེས་བཅུ་ལ༔ ང་ཉིད་འབྱོན་པའི་དབུ་བསྙུང་མཛད༔», an oath to the king, ministers and disciples of Tibet to come himself on the tenth of each month that comes, and «ཟླ་བ་ཚེས་བཅུ་ཤར་རེས་ཀྱི༔ ང་ཡི་བརྗེད་རྡོ་སུས་འཛིན་པ༔ དེ་དང་ང་ལ་འདུ་འབྲལ་མེད༔», whoever keeps his reminder each time the tenth rises is never apart from him; from the Key Instruction on Accomplishing the Guru (བླ་མ་སྒྲུབ་པའི་གནད་ཡིག, bla ma sgrub pa'i gnad yig), «ཟླ་རེའི་ཚེས་བཅུ་བྱུང་ངོ་ཅོག་ལ་ཡང༔ སྤྲུལ་པས་བོད་ཁམས་ཁྱབ་པར་མི་འབྱོན་རེ༔ དམ་ཚིག་ཡིན་ནོ», emanations covering Tibet on every tenth, as a pledge. 'Jigs med gling pa's prayer gives the same as the Guru's word, «ཚེས་བཅུ་བྱུང་རེས་བོད་དུ་འབྱོན་ཞེས་གསུངས» (jgl.8:p739-742).

For whom: the oath is addressed to the king, ministers, sons and disciples of Tibet, and the benefit is promised to "whoever is devoted" («སུ་མོས»), without a restriction to monastics or to holders of tantric vows; the passages read name no vow as a condition. Tradition: the texts are Nyingma treasures and their commentators (Ratna gling pa, Mipham, 'Jigs med gling pa).

Not read: the chapter of O rgyan gling pa's Padma bka' thang on the tenth day; Mipham quotes that work here only for other verses. Whether each verse of mph.127:i303-304 is from Ratna gling pa's text is not said by the page.
