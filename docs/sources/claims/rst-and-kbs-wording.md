# RST and KBS's quotation of Klong rdol: where the verses differ

KBS's quotations of Klong rdol bla ma's verses (read by Yigdzin-1, not checked on the scan) and the etext of the whole text (rst:p1-8, etext) agree in sense and in nearly every line. The differences found by setting the first two KBS blocks beside the etext:

- the verses after the evening third have two more lines in the etext than KBS quotes: «ལ་ལ་ཚེ་རབས་སྔོན་མའི་བྱེད་པ་ཡིན། དེ་ལ་ངེས་བཟུང་མི་འགྲུབ་སྣ་ཚོགས་འབྱུང་», some of it is the deeds of former lives, nothing sure can be taken from it, and all sorts come; the claim kbs-three-thirds-of-the-night has the evening third without the former lives;
- the dawn third's last verse: the etext says «ཐོ་རངས་ཁ་ཡིན་ནམ་གྱི་གསུམ་ཆ་ལ», KBS «ཐོ་རངས་དུས་ཀྱི་ནམ་གྱི་གསུམ་ཆ་ལ»; whether «ཁ་ཡིན», "it is the mouth (the edge)", or «དུས་ཀྱི», "of the time", stands in Klong rdol's own writing is open, the etext being one ACIP copy and KBS a machine reading of an article;
- the verses of good signs end in the etext with a summing couplet, in short: lovely sights, tasty food, pleasant talk, meeting friends, good deeds, and glad on waking, give joy, and auspiciousness, long life, no illness and growing wealth (rst:p1-8), where KBS's block ends with drinking sweet water, long life;
- the etext opens with four lines KBS does not quote: that the verses follow the Chöd visualisation collection of the Mother, and that a dream while ill, distressed or with a disturbed mind is not examined (the claim rst-three-watches).

Spelling differences (རྨི and རྨིས, འཁྲུག and འཁྲུགས) are the machine's or the copyists' and decide nothing.
