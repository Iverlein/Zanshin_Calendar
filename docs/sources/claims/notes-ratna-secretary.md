# The learned secretary Ratna, read on Mipham's own print

The notes' postscript on Mipham's own print (I2029 img. 743, l. 2; Yigdzin-1, the stack read by eye at 12×) names «དྲུང་མཁས་པ་རཏྣའི་ཟིན་བྲིས», "the notes of the learned secretary Ratna": the head of the second-last stack is a ཏ over a small ྣ, not a ཀྵ, so the name is Ratna, as the corpus had it in brackets (wb.2:p538b, whose scan reading of the 1996 print and the Gser thang reprint gave «རཀྵ», the stack blotted there). Nobody is identified: BDRC's full-text search finds neither «དྲུང་མཁས་པ་རཏྣ» nor «དྲུང་མཁས་པ་རཀྵ» in any etext (2026-11-01); the notes call his writings, with some eight big commentaries on the White Beryl seen before and after, the documents "that can bear the weight" which they condense (question 201: answered as to the print, open as to the man).
