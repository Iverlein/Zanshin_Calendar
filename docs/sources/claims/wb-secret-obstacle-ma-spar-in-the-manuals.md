# The secret obstacle's «མ་སྤར»: the mother's trigram among the four relatives, in other manuals

Three manuals carry the verse «གཞན་ཡང་སྟག་ཡོས་རང་སྤར་ཟིན། མ་སྤར་ཁམ་ལ་བུ་སྤར་ལི། དགྲ་སྤར་དྭ་ལ་གྲོགས་མཚམས་བཞི། …མ་བུ་གྲོགས་ཐོག་འགྲོ་བ་བཟང་» (BDRC full text, search snippets: the Bon *Nag rtsis snang srid me long*, a Rgyal rong collection of reckonings, and *Sgo bzhi mdzod lnga'i yang bcud*): for a tiger or hare, whose own trigram is zin (wood), the mother's trigram (མ་སྤར, ma spar) is kham (water, which gives rise to wood), the child's trigram li (fire, which wood gives rise to), the enemy's dwa (metal), the friends the four intermediate directions. So «མ་སྤར» is a term of a set of four relatives of one's own trigram, 'mother' by the generating cycle, and WB's «མ་སྤར་ལི» and «མ་སྤར་ཁམ» use the same word with the same two trigrams. Whose own trigram has li for its mother (the earth ones, which fire gives rise to) and why it is the secret obstacle of a son and kham's of a daughter, no text says; Mipham's notes read it as the one reckoned falling on li or kham and tie the rite to the mother's luck. These manuals are witnesses only; the Bon one is no source.
