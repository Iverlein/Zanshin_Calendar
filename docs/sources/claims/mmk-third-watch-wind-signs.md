# Signs of the third watch's dream, of wind, and the three poisons (RD370)

Third watch, of wind: the signs listed are movement and speech: light all round in the quarters, going in the sky, leaping and jumping, climbing trees (thorny ones too), reciting treatises and mantra, speaking and prattling, eating sharp, hot and sour foods, beings dancing, oneself dancing, a restless mind; "such is the dream of the wind-sick". The text then ties the three humours to the three poisons (desire to phlegm, hatred to bile, delusion to wind) and says mixed dreams are seen by the mixed (mmk.1:p400-401).
