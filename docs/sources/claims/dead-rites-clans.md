# The five clans' strikes and slips by element

WB vol. 2, p. 114 (read line by line on the Gser thang reprint, 2026-10-23; each clan's slip mantra, OṂ MA MA with its own seed syllables, in the passage) gives the strikes of the clan (རུས, rus) for five clans named by syllable, and their slips; the slips' makers and cutters show which element each clan is:

| Clan | Element (by the slip) | What is laid on the corpse | Slip's maker; what cuts it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *keg* | wood | fire of several woods and royal charcoal | a carpenter; a piece of iron |
| *ji* | fire | a hearth stone in the armpit; seven ransoms and foods in seven holes; planet and mansion names pressed | a potter of the south; fire-crystal |
| *kungs* | earth | a smith's leftover ..., meteoric iron and a whetstone piece | an old woman of the south-west; gold |
| *shang* | iron | water of three valleys in a black pot; iron | a smith of the west; iron |
| *'u* | water | several woods pressed on it | a ferryman of the north; water-crystal |

A smooth closing uses substances that agree with the clan, a rough one cuts it with its enemy element. Mipham's notes say the sum of slips agrees when divided into the five clans (p. 517).
