# The thirty: the seventh point, the red copper ogress, for wives and women

The seventh of the thirty starting points (སྐོར་འགོ, skor 'go), from the mouth of the red copper ogress (ཟངས་ཀྱི་སྲིན་མོ་དམར, zangs kyi srin mo dmar), is read for wives and women (ཟ་མ་བུད་མེད, za ma bud med). Its eight pebbles are laid as for every point (claims thirty-count and thirty-eight-fighters), each white or black, and read so:

| Pebble | Asks | White | Black | Remedy for black |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Whether the sun of glory has set at the door | The sun of glory rises on her head | The sun of glory sets at the door: bad for both children and wealth | — |
| 2 | Whether the white silk skirt is frayed | Not frayed, good | The skirt frays at its edge: she looks at two or three men; bad for her lifelong companion; widowhood (ཡུགས, yugs) comes to her | — |
| 3 | Whether the cord of the lake turquoise is cut | The cord of the turquoise is not cut, good | The cord of the gold and turquoise is cut: many daughters and few sons | — |
| 4 | Whether the spring of gold and turquoise has dried | The spring of existence has not dried; good, with children and descendants | The spring has dried: sons and grandsons do not increase, deaths cut the line | — |
| 5 | Whether the son is lost from the mother's lap | Sons settle in her lap; no loss among sons and grandsons | The son is lost from her lap: children carried off by spirits | — |
| 6 | Whether a living spirit (གསོན་འདྲེ, gson 'dre) harms her continually | It does no harm | It harms continually; the children do not survive | Turning the living spirit's face (ཁ་བསྒྱུར, kha bsgyur) and pressing it down; then the children survive and grow |
| 7 | Whether the small sri (ཆུང་སྲི, chung sri) has risen | It has not | It has risen | Press down the small sri and turn its face |
| 8 | Whether the the'u brang spirit (ཐེའུ་བྲང, the'u brang), a being (འབྱུང་པོ, 'byung po), steals in series | It does no harm; children grow | The the'u brang steals in series | The the'u brang's *skyas* thread-cross (ཐེའུ་བྲང་སྐྱས་མདོས, the'u brang skyas mdos) |

Whose year is reckoned: the person's own (the notes, vol. 2, p. 533: «གཞན་རྣམས་སོ་སོའི་ལོ་རྟ»).

Which of the eight fighting pebbles is which check is not stated; the order of p. 453 is the likely one (claim thirty-board-groups; question 259).
