# Nor brang's elements of the harmful classes: gnyan wood, btsan fire, earth lord earth, king-spirit iron, nagas water

Nor brang O rgyan's account of the elements of the Black reckoning sorts the harmful spirits by element: the gnyan and the thebrang wood; the btsan and the gre bo fire; the earth lord (ས་བདག, sa bdag) and the land-demon (ཡུལ་འདྲེ, yul 'dre) earth; the king-spirit (རྒྱལ་པོ) and the treasure owner (དཀོར་བདག, dkor bdag) iron; the nāgas (ཀླུ) and the ma mo water. The same pages sort the classes of people (royal earth, lords fire, common water, brahmin wood, low iron), kinds of person (king wood, woman fire, monk earth, child iron, Bon-po water), kin and illnesses by the same five.

For the earth-lord work this is a modern author's table of the element of each class of harmful spirit, set beside WB's elements for the same classes in [year-month-earth-lords.md](../year-month-earth-lords.md) only after a check there; it names no date, direction or weight. The passage was read on the etext only; the kin line («ཕུ་ཁུ», «བུ་ཚ་མ་སྨད», «མ་ཕྱི་ཡུལ་མཁར») is rendered roughly.
