# Marpa's dream-practice verses: from evening to midnight the field of Padma mgon po; the display is the sign of holding the dream

A dream-practice text in Marpa's collected works gives the hours of the night as a field: from the evening until midnight (ནམ་ཕྱེད, nam phyed) is the field of the protector Padma (པདྨ་མགོན་པོ, padma mgon po); the practitioner lies on the left side with the head to the west, fixes awareness in the dream and a red letter on the western lotus, and a magical display such as being seized by a dog or chased by an enemy is the sign that the dream is being held, not to be seen as bad. The reading of the hour words (ས་ནི་སྲོས་ནས) and of the whole is from the etext only and unsettled. It is a rule of practice, not a rule of reading a dream as a sign.
