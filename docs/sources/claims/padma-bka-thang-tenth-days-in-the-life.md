# The Padma bka' thang dates five deeds of the Guru's life on a tenth

Orgyan gling pa's Padma bka' thang (108 chapters) names the tenth day (ཚེས་བཅུ, tshes bcu) as the date of five turns in the Guru's life; in each it is a date of an event, and no line calls the day one of practice.

- Ch. 26: the going forth, on the tenth after the eighth at the Asura's cave, in the monkey month of a wood-male horse year, with the sage Nyi ma'i gung (obk:p91). The twelve-tenths lists give this deed to the dragon month; the Bka' thang's month name is the Hor-style «sprel zla», not a count (see tenth-day-going-forth-under-ananda-or-prabhahasti).
- Ch. 40: the Guru comes through the sky to Zahor on the tenth of the pig month of a monkey year and shows himself to Mandāravā (obk:p133).
- Ch. 106: the farewell teaching at Samye on the tenth of the monkey month of the monkey year (obk:p352), the teaching on saṃsāra's faults and the guru yoga that ends «ང་ཡི་ཚེ་ལ་འདས་གྲོངས་ཡོད་མ་ཡིན» (in my life there is no passing away).
- Ch. 108: his departure on «the tenth when heroes and ḍākinīs gather» (obk:p374), into the sky like cloud or rainbow, leaving the life-force of the faithful.

The tenth of the monkey month of the monkey year thus carries the departure in this text, which fits the pool's claim that the monkey-year tenth is the Guru's own day (tenth-day-monkey-month-monkey-year); the lines of ch. 108 do not name the month and year, ch. 106 does. The text is a treasure of Orgyan gling pa (b. 1323), of the Nyingma treasure tradition, not a Bon text. State: etext of an excellent print; the lines of ch. 102 were looked at on the scan.
