# The 国文研 Ebara dream book's colophon: 京綾小路ふや丁西入, 墨屋吉兵衛 (RD398, q1045)

The colophon of the 国文研 print of the Ebara dream book (bid 200013909, image 9, native 5616 px, read at 3.5x) names its Kyoto bookseller's street as 京綾小路ふや丁西入, "Fuyacho, west of Ayanokoji" (麩屋町), and the bookseller 墨屋吉兵衛; the three glyphs after 綾小路 are ふ, や and 丁 (町), not ふやゝ町 and not 冨小路. The cut is 安永三午春新板 beside 嘉永五子春新板, the same pair of years as the other prints of the table. The Koizumi 雑書夢合／三世相小鑑 (mdz:i234427b) gives 京松原通冨小路 …屋吉右衛門, a different bookseller and street, so the two prints are by different Kyoto shops.
