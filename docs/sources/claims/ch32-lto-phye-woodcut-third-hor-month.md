# Ch. 32: the woodcut of Lto-'phye shows the fifteenth house-day of the third Hor month

WB vol. 2, ch. 32, p. 280 (img. 286 of the Gser thang reprint; the same cut on the Zhol print, img. 1020): the lower half of the page is a woodcut of the earth lord Lto-'phye (ལྟོ་འཕྱེ, lto 'phye) on the squared plot of p. 281, a man to the waist and a serpent below. Its caption says it shows his position at the fifteenth house-day (ཁྱིམ་ཞག, khyim zhag) of the third Hor month. The word for north stands before the caption and the head lies that way, which fits p. 281's head north in summer. The labels round the edges and in the circles on the body are not read: the caption was read at low size with the 1996 etext beside it.
