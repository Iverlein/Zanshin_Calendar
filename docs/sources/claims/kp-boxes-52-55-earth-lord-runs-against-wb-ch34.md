# KP boxes 52 to 55 end with earth-lord runs that WB's works 49 to 55 do not have

WB's verses for works 49 to 55 of ch. 34 (dikes, crafts and hair, war, poetics and grammar, thread-cross and torma, sorcery, rain; white-beryl-ch34.md, etext, machine grade) give only the zodiac houses that are good, middling and bad, with the standard «དུས་སྦྱོར» (the combination period) named in works 53, 55 and 57. They name no earth lord (ས་བདག) in these lines. KP's boxes 52 to 55 close their bad halves with runs of earth lords all the same: srang, the date's gnyan, the sun, the destroyer for box 52; the moon, wind, se byi, gnyan, khra, king, se lo and se shar and the devil's junctures for box 53; Viṣṭi, the se, se lo, dams shing, phung zor and the destroyer, with the gnyan of the grammar's times given apart, for box 54; the Balance, the destroyer, zin phung, dbul and nyi nag, with the reversal of the nāgas, for box 55 (kp:i55s). These runs are therefore KP's own addition to WB's houses, or come from another passage of WB not yet read for them; WB's p. 427b (destructive rites, the place facing the black year, month and day shunned) is the nearest place in the chapter. Box 55's last word, the reversal of the nāgas, corresponds to WB's own treatment of the nāga planet's harm (p. 415-416) only in kind. The two houses named bad for rain in WB (Aries, Libra, Scorpio) include the Balance, and KP's last line of box 55 names the Balance first: this agrees. Read at word level; the boxes' middles are not settled.
