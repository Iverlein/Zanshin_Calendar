# How the asker's year enters the reckoning of the moment

WB vol. 2, ch. 30 and Mipham's notes: in the reckoning of the moment the asker is "the one reckoned" (བརྩི་བྱ, brtsi bya), and his own year and its four aspects (life force, body, power and luck) are fought against the moment's: his year fixes where a count starts and how far it runs, and the moment is what the counted year fights; in the counted reckonings (pp. 170–172) the moment stands where ch. 27 puts the person's own year.

- **The eight places and the small examinations** (pp. 166–170): the *byed yor* (བྱེད་ཡོར) "here means the one reckoned", the *bya ra* (བྱ་ར) the moment's element (p. 166). The house is read by his life force against the moment, the field by his power, the grave by his luck, wealth by his body and power, beer by his body and power, the sick by the sick one's life force, trade by his power and luck, the casting's grain by the moment's life force against his power (the notes, p. 536: «མོ་ནས་དུས་སྲོག་གིས་བརྩི་བྱའི་དབང་ཐང་ལ་ཕར་འཐབ་པའོ»); whether food was eaten by the moment's power element on his year (p. 170); whether the drinking was finished by whether the moment and his year are both male, both female or mixed (p. 169).
- **The three mewa joined** (p. 165): "the mewa of the moment's year (དུས་ལོའི་སྨེ་བ) is made the one reckoned" and fought against the nine squares around the day's mewa.
- **The thirteen gates** (pp. 170–171): each counts from a sign fixed by the "sky year" (གནམ་ལོ); the notes (p. 536, read on the Gser thang reprint I3CN5029 img. 542): «ཚངས་པ་སྤྱི་རྒྱུད་ཀྱི་རྩིས་འདི་རིགས་གནམ་ལོ་ཞེས་པ་བརྩི་བྱའི་ལོ་ཡིན་གྱི་གནམ་ལོ་དངོས་མིན་ནོ», in reckonings of this kind from the *General Tantra of Brahmā* the "sky year" is the year of the one reckoned, not the actual year. The count runs over his years lived, male down and female up by his sex.
- **The fighting enemies** (p. 172): reckoned "making the time of reckoning the year of the one reckoned"; the notes (p. 537, img. 543): «ཡར་གསུམ་སོགས་ལ་སྲོག་ལུས་དབང་ཀླུང་བཞིས་འཐབ། དུས་ཀྱིས་བརྩི་བྱའི་ལོ་ཚབ་བྱའོ», for "three up" and the rest the four fight, the time takes the place of the year of the one reckoned: where ch. 27's like reckonings fight the counted year against the person's own year, here it is fought against the moment. RD58 (verdicts.md) read «ཡར་གསུམ་སོགས» as the three up, three down, eight up and eight down of p. 172's own counts, not ch. 26's board; whether the moment's aspects or the asker's fight the landed year is open (q424).

Inputs for a build: the asker's birth year (element, animal, sex), his years lived, his four aspects, and the moment's year, day mewa, hour and its element.
