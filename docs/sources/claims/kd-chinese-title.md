# KD's Chinese title is 藏传历算学大全, by a compilers' group; no 西藏天文历算总汇

KD's own Chinese title, printed on the Chinese colophon of its fifth volume (leaf 813 of the lent scan), is 藏传历算学大全, "Zang chuan li suan xue da quan", credited to a compilers' group (藏传历算学大全编写组), not to a person, and it is not 西藏天文历算总汇 (Xizang tianwen lisuan zonghui). So the Chinese title of KD in BDRC's record is right and is the only Chinese title the volumes carry; the register's 《西藏天文历算总汇》 (verdicts.md, *Kun dga' rig 'dzin, the writer of the sheets*) does not appear in KD under that name.

What the scans give for him: the volumes' Tibetan front matter names no writer (the title leaf is the work's title in calligraphy and a signature, the contents leaf lists the articles), the vol. 3 sheets are the only items that name him (the editor of the board of the Tibet Autonomous Region's Tibetan Medical College, vol. 3, img. 5, as read in RD28); the Chinese colophon lists the editor in charge (戴作民), the cover designers and five proof-readers (黄延静, 仁青措, 索朗次仁, 木兰扎桑, 多杰工布) and no author. Whether 《西藏天文历算总汇》 is another compilation of the same group or a title of the register's own making for KD is not answered by KD; only a second publication under that name would settle it.
