# KP's year ring, the Horse to Pig panels: WB's places for most lords, Se-lo, the dog-headed, Phe'u and Zin-phung exchanged

KP's year ring, read cell by cell for the Horse, Sheep, Monkey, Bird, Dog and Pig years ([kp:i89b](../corpus/kp/kp.md#kp-i89b), [kp:i90b](../corpus/kp/kp.md#kp-i90b)), puts most lords where WB 2, ch. 31 puts them for the year, and the lords that differ in the Rat, Ox, Tiger, Hare, Dragon and Snake panels (claim [kp-year-ring-offsets-against-wb](kp-year-ring-offsets-against-wb.md)) differ the same way here. The ring's places are the twelve animals, fixed (T1 the Dragon, T2 the Snake, T3 the Horse, T4 the Sheep, R1 the Monkey, R2 the Bird, B4 the Dog, B3 the Pig, B2 the Rat, B1 the Ox, L2 the Tiger, L1 the Hare); the cell of the year's own animal holds the king in all six panels (Horse T3, Sheep T4, Monkey R1, Bird R2, Dog B4, Pig B3).

**Answer to q1209: the offsets are not one offset, they are WB's.** A lord whose WB rule is a fixed count (the queen, the time-holder, Te-so on the wind-horse, the life-spirit knower of the se on the seventh) keeps its count; a lord whose WB rule is a list by triad or by year (the minister, the watchman, the general, the splitter, Hal-khyi's girl, the mount, the eleven earth kings, Pi-ling, Wang-ging) stands where WB's list puts it for that year, which is not the same count from the year in every panel. So the ring is not one drawing turned a place a year; each panel is drawn from WB's lists for its year.

In WB's places in the six panels read: the king group in the year's cell in all six; the queen Te-khyim two back from the king (Horse in the Dragon, Sheep in the Snake, Monkey in the Horse, Bird in the Sheep, Dog in the Monkey); Te-so on the year's wind-horse (Horse in the Monkey, Sheep in the Snake, Monkey in the Tiger, Bird in the Pig, Dog in the Monkey, Pig in the Snake), with Wang-ging in the Horse panel's Monkey and the Bird panel's Hare as WB 19; Hal-khyi's girl and her husband on the wind-horse of the previous year (Horse in the Pig, Sheep in the Monkey, Monkey in the Snake, Bird in the Tiger, Dog in the Pig, Pig in the Monkey); the time-holder on last year's animal (Horse in the Snake, Sheep in the Horse, Bird in the Monkey, Dog in the Bird); the life-spirit knower of the se on the seventh from the year (Horse in the Rat, Sheep in the Ox, Monkey in the Tiger, Bird in the Hare, Dog in the Dragon, Pig in the Snake); the minister (Horse in the Hare, Sheep in the Hare, Monkey in the Horse, Bird in the Horse, Dog in the Horse, Pig in the Bird); the mount and horse-leader (Horse in the Tiger, Bird in the Snake, Dog in the Horse, Pig in the Sheep; in the Sheep and Monkey panels no cell with them was found); the general (Horse in the Dragon, Sheep, Monkey and Bird in the Sheep, Dog and Pig in the Dog); the splitter (Horse in the Ox, Monkey in the Dragon, Bird in the Dragon, Pig in the Sheep; the Dog panel puts him in the Ox where WB has the sheep); Se-byi, the seventh from the watchman (Horse in the Dragon, Bird in the Sheep, Dog in the Dragon, Pig in the Ox); the watchman (Horse in the Dog, Monkey in the Dragon, Bird in the Ox); Pi-ling (Horse in the Dog, Sheep in the Tiger, Bird in the Ox, Dog in the Horse, Pig in the Dragon); the eleven earth kings (Horse in the Tiger, Sheep in the Monkey, Monkey in the Ox, Bird in the Horse, Pig in the Rat).

**The rule-like differences recur.** (1) Se-lo Sa-'phyag stands on the upward four-slayer, three places on from the year, where WB 15 puts the downward: Horse in the Bird, Sheep in the Dog, Monkey in the Pig, Bird in the Rat, Pig in the Tiger, and in the Dog panel probably in the Ox; with the Rat, Tiger, Hare and Snake panels of the other claim this makes it the chart's rule in nine panels read. (2) Tsang-kun the dog-headed (WB 22, on the upward four-slayer) stands with the minister's group on the downward four-slayer, three places back, the exact exchange of Se-lo's: Horse in the Hare, Sheep in the Dragon, Bird in the Horse (the Hare, Dragon and Horse being three back from the Horse, Sheep and Bird years). In the Rat panel the cell that [kp:i87b](../corpus/kp/kp.md#kp-i87b) gave the dog-headed (T2, with Hal-khyi's girl) was read again on the scan beside the Horse panel's B3 and shows the same last word as it, Zin-phung, not the dog-headed; the dog-headed in the Rat year stands, as in the others, with the minister (R2, three back from the Rat), where the second line of that cell is the one the first reading took for the queen. q1210's second lord is therefore settled as Se-lo's mirror, and the four differences of the Rat panel become two exchanged lords, Phe'u and Kungs-myul. (3) Phe'u keeps the other-way rule of the claim on the first six panels: in the middle years two places back (Horse in the Dragon, Bird in the Sheep), in the corner years two places on (Monkey in the Dog, Pig in the Ox) and in the last years on the seventh as WB 23 says (Sheep in the Ox, Dog in the Dragon). (4) Zin-phung (WB 24, on the year's sixth downward, five places back) stands five places on from the year in every panel read here but the Monkey's (Horse in the Pig with Hal-khyi's girl, Sheep in the Rat, Bird in the Tiger with her, Dog in the Hare, Pig in the Dragon; the Monkey panel's cell, the Ox, is doubtful) and in the Rat panel too (the Snake): five on and not five back, the sixth upward and not the sixth downward, as with Se-lo and the dog-headed the reverse of WB's direction; the Ox and Dragon panels of the other claim have him three on. (5) Kungs-myul (WB 14) has no fixed offset again: the Horse's Dragon with the queen, the Bird's Dog alone and, doubtfully, the Sheep, the Dog's Sheep, the Sheep panel's Dog and the Pig's Ox.

**The two life-spirit knowers.** Nang, the inner one, stands in the king's cell of the Horse (T3), Bird (R2), Dog (B4) panels, with the king, as it did in the Rat panel, and in cells of its own in the Sheep (the Hare, with the minister), Pig (the Hare, and the Rat with the eleven kings) and Dog panels; Gsang, the secret one, one place behind the year in the middle years (the Horse's Snake, the Bird's Monkey) and one place ahead in the corner years (the Monkey's Bird), which is the rule the other claim found (WB 6's secret place); in the last years (Sheep, Dog) no Gsang cell was found.

Not settled: the last line of the Horse's T3 (read «བྱི་ལམ») and of the Bird's R2, the second names in the Horse's L1 and the Bird's T3, the Sheep's T1, T3 and B4, the Monkey's T1, T2, T4 and B1, the Dog's T1 and T4, and the Pig's T1, R1, R2 and B4: the Tibetan of the passages marks each.
