# p. 281: the width's «do» reads clearly on the reprint

On the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5029 img. 287, line 8, 4×) the width of the earth lord's figure reads «ཞེང་ལ་རང་ཞལ་དོ་ཡི་ཚད» (zheng la rang zhal do yi tshad, in width the measure of his own face «do»): the letter before the genitive «ཡི» (yi) is དོ (do), a ད with the vowel sign ོ, the stroke clear. The letters are settled; the sense of «དོ» the page does not say, and p. 281's own example in great and small parts is the only count it gives.
