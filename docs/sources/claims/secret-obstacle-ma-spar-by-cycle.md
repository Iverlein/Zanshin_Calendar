# The secret obstacle's mother trigram: li for an earth own trigram, kham for a wood one, by the generating cycle

The own trigrams' mothers by the generating cycle give the answer to which own trigrams have li and which kham for their «མ་སྤར» (ma spar), if «མ་སྤར» is the mother in the sense of the manuals (wb-secret-obstacle-ma-spar-in-the-manuals): the eight trigrams are, by the verses of vol. 1, p. 256, li fire, khon earth, dwa iron, khen sky (metal), kham water, gin mountain (earth), zin wood and zon wind (wood). Fire gives rise to earth, so li is the mother's trigram of khon and gin; water gives rise to wood, so kham is the mother's trigram of zin and zon (the manuals' tiger and hare, whose own is zin). For the other four trigrams the mother is another: wood for li, earth for dwa and khen, metal for kham. So WB's two names (p. 390: «མ་སྤར་ལི» and «མ་སྤར་ཁམ») fit an own trigram that is earth or wood, and no other. This is an inference from the cycle, not stated by any text on this page. The line of p. 390 (1996 etext, not read on the scan) has three clauses: the thief secret obstacle of the own birth, «སྤར་སྟེང་སླེབ་དང་མ་སྤར་ལི» (arriving on the own trigram, and the mother's trigram li); for the son «བུ་ལ་གསང་ཀེག་མ་སྤར་ཁམ» (the mother's trigram kham); for the daughter «བུ་མོར་གསང་ཀེག་ཚོར་བ་དཀའ» (hard to feel). So li belongs to the first clause and kham to the son's, not li to the son's and kham to the daughter's as the question has it; why the first takes li and the son's kham, no text found says. Mipham's notes add nothing on the choice.
