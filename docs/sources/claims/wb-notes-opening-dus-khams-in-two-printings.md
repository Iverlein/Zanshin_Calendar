# Mipham's notes on WB, opening sentence: «དུས་ཁམས» in two typeset editions, «རུས་ཁམས» nowhere (q1025)

BDRC's exact-string search (2026-11-13) for the opening sentence of Mipham's notes on WB, «དུས་ཁམས་ནི་ངོས་ཟིན་ན» (dus khams ni ngos zin na), finds it in two etexts of the White Beryl, each time with «དུས»: the Beijing edition (Krung go'i bod kyi shes rig dpe skrun khang, 1996-1997, MW2CZ8040, vol. 2 etext char. 1066854) and the Gser thang edition (MW3CN5026, char. 1114046), both snippets reading «དུས་ཁམས་ནི་ངོས་ཟིན་ན་དེ་དང་མིན་ན་བླ་རྟགས་གཡག་སོགས་གང་ཡིན་ངག་རྒྱུན་དང་རྨི་ལམ་སོགས་ལས་བརྟག་པ་དང་། མི་གཤིས་སོགས་ཀྱིས་རུས་ཆེན་ལྔ་གང་ཡིན་ངོས་ཟིན་པར་བྱའོ». The variant «རུས་ཁམས་ནི་ངོས་ཟིན་ན» has no hit anywhere in BDRC's etexts. So the printing of wb.2:p474 is not a misprint of one print: two typeset editions agree, and the sentence's own next clause writes «རུས་ཆེན་ལྔ» (the five great clans) with «རུས». Either «དུས» is an error that both prints inherited from one earlier text, or the writer's term is «དུས་ཁམས» (the element of the time, that is of the birth's time) and the clause is meant as the element fixed by the time, then the clan by the bla-sign animal; the search cannot decide between them. Mipham's own print (I2029) and the Collected Works print (I1PD34647) were not opened at this sentence; the second's first leaves were looked at (img. 530, the notes' opening folio) and the word was not made out on them.

Answered 2026-11-14 (q1025): the Chengdu Collected Works print of Mipham's notes (I1PD34647, img. 530) reads «རུས་ཁམས་ནི་ངོས་འཛིན་ན» and «རུས་ཁམས་དང་ཕ་མེས»; «དུས་ཁམས» is the two typeset White Beryl editions' shared error (Yigdzin-1 on the scan, the word's first letter checked on a crop).
