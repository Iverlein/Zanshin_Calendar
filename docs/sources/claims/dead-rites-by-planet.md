# Death on each planet: the wheel's skull and place

WB vol. 2, pp. 118–119 (read line by line on the Gser thang reprint, 2026-10-23; the spells in full in the passage). For a death on a weekday's planet (གཟའ, gza') a figure is drawn above and something below, a short mantra said, and the wheel put into a skull and pressed at a place:

| Planet | Above / below | Skull; place |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sun | a water wheel / the moon's heart-syllable; nine fires and nine ladles in nine holes; nine black votive images; "nine repeat, nine are led" | horse, in horse hair; a river bank in the north |
| Moon | Saturn's figure / the earth's heart-syllable; five pea-and-clay men with owl feathers | sheep, in rat hair; a mountain hollow in the south-west |
| Mars (the war-chief) | the moon's figure and heart-syllable; a water wheel on the head; nine fires lit and put out at once | horse; a river bank in the north |
| Mercury | earths of five colours / Saturn's work | pig; a mountain in the south-west |
| Jupiter | Venus's figure / the element-executioner mantra; an iron man cut with axe and saw | bird; a white rock mountain in the west |
| Venus | Mars's figure / a fire of thorns | snake; the foot of a red rock in the south |
| Saturn | Jupiter's figure; five black-grain men in five holes; a barberry peg in the heart | hare; the foot of a tree in the east |
| Rāhu | the eight classes' heart-syllable at the neck; eclipse-wood above; the corpse chained, a jewel in its mouth | — |

Mipham's notes count eight planet remedies (p. 517).
