# White Tārā rite: the letters A, NU, TA, RA on the four mirrors and what each makes true

In the White Tārā rite each mirror's letter decides which kind of answer will come true: A (east), the appearances marked by A; NU (south), all omens (mtshan ma); TA (west), the appearances of omens, but they come true only if clear, otherwise they do not; RA (north), the appearances of the life-force (srog), and no others. The rite does not rank the hour of the dream; the dream asked for is whatever the wish was, and the answer is held by the mirror it was set in.
