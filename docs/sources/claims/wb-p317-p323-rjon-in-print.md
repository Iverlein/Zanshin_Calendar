# WB pp. 317, 323: the prints have རྗོན where the machine had རྔོན

Both prints have «རྗོན» in two places where the machine reading had «རྔོན»: in Puṣya's avoid list on p. 317, «རྗོན་རྐུ་ཞིང་འདེབས་མཁར་གྱི་ལས» (Gser thang reprint img. 323, Zhol print WBZ img. 1070), and as the deity of Mūla on p. 323, «ལྷ་ནི་རྗོན་པ་ཅན་དང་ནི» (reprint img. 329, WBZ img. 1078). The stack is the same at all four: ra above ja with the vowel o, and no nga.

The sense is not settled by this. Elsewhere in the chapter hunting is spelt «རི་དྭགས་རྔོན» (p. 360), so the earlier English, "hunting and theft" and "the one with hunters", may still be right with «རྗོན» as a spelling of hunting (རྔོན, rngon). That is a query, not a finding: no dictionary was consulted, and the deity has not been identified from any source.

The word on p. 323 is the only part of that passage read on the scan; the passage stays machine.
