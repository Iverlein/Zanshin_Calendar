# The household round: each member's four deciding pebbles, not the year's four meetings

What a member brings to the household round: WB and the notes against the *Moonbeams*.

- **WB** (vol. 1, p. 385) lays for each member the deciding verdict pebble of each of the four aspects, the "four classes gathered" of the line before; **the notes** (vol. 2, p. 490) say how: each member's twenty-four of cutting and tearing laid and gathered into four deciding pebbles.
- **The *Moonbeams*** (DZ f. 22b, img. 048) lays four pebbles of cutting and tearing made by the member's four against the current year's four alone, white or black "as in general".
- **The notes reject that way by name** (p. 491): some reckon five only, the four fought against the current year's life force and the rest with the mewa of the year, without the twenty-four's deciding pebbles; "this text does not do so".

WB stands, with its notes (rank: WB first, Mipham next; the *Moonbeams* a witness below them). For a build the member's four are the four deciding pebbles of that person's year of age, as the one-person reckoning lays them.
