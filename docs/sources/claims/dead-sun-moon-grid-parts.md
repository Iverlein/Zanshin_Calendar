# The five parts of each direction on the sun and moon grid: no gloss found

The sun and moon grid of the sky's road (WB vol. 2, p. 96): lines are drawn east-west and south-north; the upper east, the tiger, has five parts; "in each part the east's wood and two open parts"; the lower east, the hare, five parts each, and so for all the years; the year's earth lords' staying places are then counted from the winter's start at the east, and "the other four, the open ones" (གཞན་བཞི་ཡངས་སྟེང) can be sent on without fault. No commentary was found that lays out the parts: not Mipham's notes (pp. 504–512, 517–522, searched), nor LT, UT or ZG (searched for «ཡངས་ཆ», «ཐིག་བཏབ» and the lines' own words, 2026-11-01); BDRC's full-text search for «ཤར་སྟོད་སྟག་ལ་རྣམ་ལྔ», «ཡངས་ཆ་གཉིས» and «གཞན་བཞི་ཡངས་སྟེང» gives only WB's prints. The surrounding text gives no sense for the parts beyond the seventy-two-fold reckoning it starts next. A dead end.
