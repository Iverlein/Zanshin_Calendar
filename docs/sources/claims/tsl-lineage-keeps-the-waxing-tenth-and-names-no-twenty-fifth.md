# Rtsa gsum gling pa's lineage: the tenth of the waxing half and the fifteenth are named; no twenty-fifth, twenty-ninth or eighth found in vols. 27–30

What the texts of Rtsa gsum gling pa's lineage (rank 2) say of days of practice, from the etext of vols. 27–30 of the edition searched whole on 2026-11-16 and the lines read on the scans.

- The tenth of the waxing half. Pad rgyal gling pa's treasure sādhana of the guru is begun "on the tenth of the waxing half or at an auspicious conjunction" (tsl.28:i106-107, scan). At the family seat Gnam lcags ri khrod the offerings of the tenth were done by a guru sādhana addressed to Rtsa gsum gling pa himself (tsl.29:i109-110, etext, a note whose writer is not identified). In the life, the animals by the lakes of the Guru's mountain are those made the feast meat on the tenth (tsl.30:i279, scan). With the passages read before (sgc:i207-208, tsl.30:i265, tsl.30:i287) the tenth of the waxing half is the lineage's one named day of feast and guru practice.
- The fifteenth. The life sets a feast gathering before the opening of a treasure on the fifteenth of a month and calls the day a good occasion (tsl.30:i417, scan).
- The twenty-fifth. Not found as a day of practice. Every occurrence of the words for twenty-five in the four volumes is a count (the twenty-five disciples, twenty-five vase substances, chapter numbers) or a date of travel in the lives; the tenth of the waning half is nowhere named.
- The twenty-ninth, the eighth and the thirtieth. Not found as days of practice: the hits are dates in the lives and colophons. A torma-repelling manual asks for "the three nines or a date of the waning half" with rough planets and stars for its wrathful rite (tsl.28:i569, scan).
- The full and new moon and the eighth as days of owed offering stand in the Sgo chen liturgy collection only in confessions that are common Nyingma texts, not the lineage's own (sgc:i568, sgc:i589, sgc:i580, sgc:i590-591, etext).
- The vajra restoring rite names no day between its title and its closing verse (tsl.28:i212-213, etext; tsl.28:i232).

For whom: the sādhana's practitioner; in the life, the crowd of the faithful at the feast. Vols. 1–26 of the edition have no etext and were not searched, so the finding holds for vols. 27–30 and the Sgo chen collection.
