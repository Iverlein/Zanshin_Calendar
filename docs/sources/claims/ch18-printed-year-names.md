# Year-names printed on the scan otherwise than in ch. 13's list

Five names of the *Sūtra of the Secret*'s list are printed on the scan otherwise than in ch. 13's list of the sixty names (and the table of RD184): Aṅgiras as ཨདྒིར (*a dgir*) for ཨངྒི་ར (entry 9, p. 206); Joy-maker, དགའ་བྱེད (*dga' byed*), for Virtue-maker, དགེ་བྱེད (entry 39, p. 208); གདང་ངན (*gdang ngan*) for Bad-minded, བློ་ངན (*blo ngan*) (entry 58, p. 209); ཇ་ཆེན (*ja chen*) for Great Drum, རྔ་ཆེན (*rnga chen*) (entry 59, p. 209); ཁྲག་སྨྱུག་པ (*khrag smyug pa*) for Blood-vomiting, ཁྲག་སྐྱུག (*khrag skyug*) (entry 60, p. 209). The entries are in the right places of the cycle, so the names are the same years; the readings are the print's, not corrected. In the *Little Tiger-Ear* list the print writes the mouse as བྱིར (*byir*), the horse as ཏ (*ta*) and the snake as སྡུལ (*sdul*) in some entries (pp. 210–211). In the sūtra's list the snake is written སྤྲུལ (*sprul*) in two entries (p. 208).
