# The moment's eight places: house, field, grave and one's own place

WB vol. 2, pp. 166–168: the early and later signs. Three terms: the *bya ra* (བྱ་ར) is the element of the moment (the notes, p. 536: the moment's power), the *byed yor* (བྱེད་ཡོར) the one reckoned (the asker), the *thig sdod* (ཐིག་སྡོད) the life force of both. For each of the five elements there are three (cutting, rise and decline, verdict) and eight places: house, field, grave, one's own place, kin, wealth, illness and demon. Each place fights one aspect of the asker against the moment.

| Place | Aspect of the asker fought by the moment | Readings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| house (p. 166) | life force | the moment cuts it: a crack, earth lords harm people and wealth, no peace since moving in, moving elsewhere good; it cuts the moment: the house enlarged and reduced, doors moved, much moving; the moment generates the life force: rebuilt or repaired, unwanted wealth, danger of collapse; the moment generates the asker: a good house gathering people and wealth; same element: wood a corpse in the house (remove it), iron moving good, illness and empty wealth, fire an even share, water travel and gain, earth earth lords and oath-breaking sri, sores: water offerings, moving good |
| field (p. 167) | power | cut by the moment: a field cut at the waist, spurs thrust in, nāgas and earth lords: reconcile them; cut outward: a fortunate field; generated outward: a decayed hill or cave, tumours and boils unless cured; generated inward: the best field, keep it; same element by wood, fire, water, iron, earth, each with its shape, spirit, illness and rite (nāga offering; *Klu 'bum*; lake-sman thread-cross; Vajrapāṇi's mantra); rising, there is a field; declining, none |
| grave (pp. 167–168) | luck | by the moment's element and the pairs: the grave's place, who went into it, what the forefathers say; the forefathers' year cutting the moment, no fault, cut inward, a fault; the luck declining on the moment, a road, ravine or ground-enemy; the grave's back faces the direction of the moment's element (wood east) |
| one's own place (p. 168) | the moment's power element | its life force the paternal kin, its body the maternal uncle, its friend the maid, its luck the lord; kin and enemy present if among the good six of rise and decline, absent among the bad six |

Inputs: the moment's element, the asker's life force, power, body and luck, and the element relations (mother, son, friend, enemy, same).
