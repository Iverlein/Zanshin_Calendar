# The household round: the order of taking, for any number of members

The order in which the household's pebbles are taken into one row, the plain round (རྐྱང་པ, rkyang pa).

WB (vol. 1, p. 385): "the upper one's body and the lower one's element, in turn, until the remainder is complete, in a zigzag". The notes (vol. 2, p. 490): the eldest man's first pebble, the body; then the youngest woman's last, the mewa; then the first one's second, the last one's fourth, and so on. The notes' laying of five (p. 490, cell by cell) numbers the taking 1–25.

**The rule, as the laying of five bears out cell by cell.** Lay every member's column end to end in the order of the round (men eldest to youngest, then women), each column body, life force, power, luck, mewa: one line of 5 × N pebbles. Take alternately from its two ends, the front pebble first: 1 the first member's body, 2 the last member's mewa, 3 the first member's life force, 4 the last member's luck, and so on; when the outer pair is used up the next pair is reached without a break, and with an odd number of members the middle member's own column is taken from both its ends (body 21, mewa 22, life force 23, luck 24, power 25 in the laying of five). Every one of the twenty-five numbers of the notes' table comes out so. The rule is stated here in our own words from the table, not in the text; it does not depend on the count, so it runs the same for four, six or more members, where there is no middle member (open question 258).

**Other witnesses** (2026-10-25). LT takes the pebbles "the upper one's body and the lower one's mewa, the upper one's life force and the lower one's trigram, and so on in turn" (lt:p20-25), the same alternation from the two ends. The Bon *Mirror*'s diagram of three members (a witness only) numbers its columns exactly as the rule gives: the first member 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 down its column, the last 10, 8, 6, 4, 2, and the middle one life force 11, mewa 12, body 13, luck 14, power 15, taken from both its ends (sn:p70-71; its rows run life force, body, power, luck, mewa, without the notes' exchange of the first two). Kun dga' rig 'dzin's sheet of seven members (kd.3:p726, etext only) shows, in the numbers its etext keeps, the same order for seven (the mewa row 9, 19, 29, 32, 22, 12, 2). So the rule now has a stated alternation in LT and two worked layings, of three and of seven, beside the notes' five; the sheet of seven is still to be read on the scan.

The *Moonbeams* (DZ f. 23a, img. 049) gives the same words as WB, "from the upper one of the row its body and from the lower one its element … in a zigzag", and no example.
