# The seasons' marks counted from the solstices

WB vol. 2, pp. 27–28, the rough marks of the seasons, counted in days:

| From | After | Mark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| winter solstice | 1 month 7 days | crows build their nests |
| then | 1 month 8 days | geese arrive |
| then | 15 days | equinox, the earth warms; a bird («འོལ་བ») reaches Tibet |
| then | 21 days | the heron arrives, warmth comes |
| then | 7 days | time to sow |
| equinox | 3 months | summer solstice |
| then | 2 months | birds' calls cease |
| then | 30 days | autumn equinox |
| then | 21 days | "the tail of the year" (ནམ་མཇུག, nam mjug) is cut |

The text's months make the spring equinox 90 days after the winter solstice; the marks are counts from the solstices, not dates of the lunar calendar.
