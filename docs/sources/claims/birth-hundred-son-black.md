# The birth's hundred: one pebble a meeting, a son meeting black

How a son meeting is coloured where each meeting is one pebble, against where a meeting is weighed by its pebbles.

- **The birth's hundred: one pebble a meeting, the son black.** Mipham's notes (WB vol. 2, p. 475): mother and friend one white each, son and enemy one black each, a meeting with itself black for fire, iron and wood, white for earth and water. The *Moonbeams* says the same for the same hundred (DZ f. 16b, img. 036): «མ་གྲོགས་དཀར་ལ་དགྲ་བུ་ནག», with the same rule for a meeting with itself. WB's own count of the hundred (vol. 1, pp. 260–261) gives no colour rule of its own (searched in Yigdzin-1's reading of img. 270–271); WB's thirty (vol. 1, p. 454, on the scan) and its thirteen circles (vol. 2, p. 3, img. 11, «བུ་དགྲ་ཤིང་ལྕགས་མེ་གསུམ་པོ། །ནང་ཕྲད་ནག་པོ», by Yigdzin-1, not read on the scan here) lay the son black in the same one-pebble way.
- **Where a meeting is weighed by its pebbles** (the year of age's twenty-four and la pebbles, vol. 1, p. 380; the illness reckoning, p. 417): a mother three white, a friend two, a son a pair of white and black, an enemy two black. There the notes count a place once by its colour (p. 490), and the *Moonbeams* lays the son's meeting of the year of age as a half (ཕྱེད་མ, phyed ma; f. 22a, read by O18).

So the son black of p. 475 is the birth hundred's own rule in both the notes and the *Moonbeams*, not a shorthand for the pair (open question 197). A build lays the hundred one pebble a meeting, the son black, and keeps the pair and its count for the year of age and the illness reckoning.
