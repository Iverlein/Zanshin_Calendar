# «ཐེ་ཁྱིམ་བཙུན་མོ» is the earth lord te khyim, the te se's chief wife

# «ཐེ་ཁྱིམ་བཙུན་མོ» is the earth lord te khyim, the *te se*'s chief wife

WB vol. 1, p. 462, the bride's going, first check, black: «ཐེ་ཁྱིམ་བཙུན་མོའི་གདོང་ཐུག་ནས་བག་མ་འཆི་ལ་གཏོར་བསྒྱུར་བྱ» (question 391, in part).

- *Te khyim* (ཐེ་ཁྱིམ, the khyim) is in the dictionaries an earth lord of Chinese origin (sa bdag, ས་བདག; Ives Waldo, *Tshig mdzod chen mo*), and Valby glosses it "the principal wife of the se" (ཐེ་སེ, the se). WB's «བཙུན་མོ» (btsun mo, "lady, queen") is then the title, and the line reads: as the bride meets the face of the earth lord te khyim, the queen, she dies; turn the torma.
- The *Garland of Stars* (KD vol. 3) gives the same point the same words (p. 538, etext) and, in its list of what to avoid in sending a bride, the verse «ཐེ་སེ་ཕ་མིང་ཐེ་ཁྱིམ་བག»: the *te se* for the father's name, the *te khyim* for the bride (p. 644, etext). So the pair te se and te khyim are the earth lords of the father's line and of the bride, and the bride's going is read against them. That identifies the being; it does not say why the bride's power-mountain burning leads to meeting her.
