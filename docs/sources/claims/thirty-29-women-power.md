# The thirty: the twenty-ninth point, the coiled swastika, for wives and women, at the place of the power

The twenty-ninth of the thirty starting points (སྐོར་འགོ, skor 'go), from the mouth of the coiled swastika (གཡུང་དྲུང་འཁྱིལ་བ, g.yung drung 'khyil ba), is read for wives and women, at the place of the power (དབང་ཐང, dbang thang). Its eight pebbles are laid as for every point (claims thirty-count and thirty-eight-fighters), each white or black, and read so:

| Pebble | Asks | White | Black | Remedy for black |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Whether the lake of power has dried | Not dried: food, wealth and enjoyments gather | Dried | The rite of raising power |
| 2 | Whether the mountain of power is lost to fire | Not burnt | Burnt by fire: fire and water, the maternal luck harm | Raising rites and cures for power make it good |
| 3 | Whether sons are lost from the mother's lap | Sons grow: six, sons, grandsons, brothers and sisters, good | Bad in the place of the woman's sons and wealth | The auspicious glory thread-cross (བཀྲ་ཤིས་དཔལ་མདོས, bkra shis dpal mdos) helps |
| 4 | Whether the power is caught in the demon's net | Not caught, good | No harvest in the field, no curd; eating does not fill, giving does not please | Expel misfortune (བྱུར, byur), raise glory (དཔལ, dpal), turn the gossip (མི་ཁ, mi kha) |
| 5 | Whether the power's treasury is empty | Good | Bad in the father's line's place | — |
| 6 | Whether the power has entered the spirits' road | Not entered, good | Entered, bad | — |
| 7 | Whether the castle of power has collapsed | The enclosure of power is full; great enjoyment and eating fortune | Bad for enjoyments | — |
| 8 | Whether the power is good in the house | Grain, wealth and enjoyments grow; one father, a hundred sons | Bound by the demon's noose: good now but in the long run «hair oil turns to water», bad | Untie the demon's noose; offer clothes and whitewash to stupas; circumambulate, offer lamps, mend the roads |

Whose year is reckoned: the person's own (the notes, vol. 2, p. 533: «གཞན་རྣམས་སོ་སོའི་ལོ་རྟ»).

Which of the eight fighting pebbles is which check is not stated; the order of p. 453 is the likely one (claim thirty-board-groups; question 259).
