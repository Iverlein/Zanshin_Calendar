# KP §18: the 27 mansions in eight groups by compass side, the side towards which a skull is thrown

KP's §18 (BDRC labels 111–112) gives a remedy for the harm done to the earth lords of the moving mansions (རྒྱུ་སྐར, *rgyu skar*) by groups of mansions, not one by one, and each group has a compass side towards which an animal skull is thrown: east *smin drug, snar ma, mgo, lag, nabs so, rgyal*; south-east *skag*; south *mchu, gre, dbo, me bzhi, nag pa, sa ri*; south-west *sa ga*; west *lha mtshams, snron, chu stod, chu smad, gro bzhin*; north-west *byi bzhin*; north *mon gre, mon gru, khrums stod, khrums smad, nam gru, tha skar*; north-east *bra nye*. The eight groups cover the 27 mansions of the print's abbreviations exactly once (6+1+6+1+5+1+6+1 = 27), with *tha skar* in the north group, not at the head of the list. The lines were read on the scan at 1.6× in two halves and agree with the machine reading in their words; the skulls named for each group (a monkey, dog, fox for the east; a snake, mouse, bear, buffalo, tiger for the south; …) are the machine's and the counts do not always match the mansions (the east names five animals for six mansions), so the animals are not built on.

For the app: this is a table of **mansion to compass side** that the text uses only for where to throw a remedy. It names no good or bad day and no work, so it adds nothing to a day's reading. Whether the 27 abbreviations settle the doubtful ཆོད/ཆུཾད and ཁྲོད/ཁྲུཾད cases (mansions.md): the west group has «ཆུ་སྟོད་སྨད» (*chu stod smad*, both written out), the north «ཁྲུམས་སྟོད་སྨད» (*khrums stod smad*), so, as the machine reads them, the print here writes the pairs out with their two halves, not as the contractions of the boxes (not looked at on the scan at the size those two need).
