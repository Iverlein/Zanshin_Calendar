# The length of the day by house: 26;30 to 33;30 hours

The length of the day by house, as hours (*chu tshod*) and weights (*srang*, sixtieths of an hour), the figures stacked as «hours over srang» on the print: Pisces and Virgo 30 hours (day and night alike); Aries and Leo 31;10; Taurus and Cancer 32;20; Gemini («འཁྲིག་པ», 'khrig pa, the pair) 33;30; Aquarius and Libra 28;50; Capricorn and Scorpio 27;40; Sagittarius 26;30. Cleared from 60 each gives the night.

The assignment of the signs follows from the verse's pairs and the symmetry about Gemini and Sagittarius: Gemini 33;30 is the longest day and Sagittarius 26;30 the shortest; each pair of signs equally far from Gemini (Taurus and Cancer, Aries and Leo, Pisces and Virgo) shares a figure, and so does each pair equally far from Sagittarius (Scorpio and Capricorn, Libra and Aquarius). The steps are 1;10 each way, and the pairs sum to 60 hours (31;10 + 28;50, 32;20 + 27;40, 33;30 + 26;30). The page's sign before «ཀཊ» in the 32;20 verse reads «སྲང», which is Libra's name; by the symmetry the sign meant is Taurus (the verse has no other place for Taurus, and Libra is in the 28;50 pair «བུམ་སྲང»), so the line is a printing or reading fault for «གླང». The figures are the scan's (RD206).
