# Friends, foes, strength, kind and action of the planets, and the time-union (WB pp. 99-101)

Pages 99–101 give the relations and strengths of the planets, as the text states them: friends and foes (the sun's great friend is Jupiter; Rāhu, as king of the planets, has neither); the seven places a given house is seen from and the feet each sees (three and ten one foot, four and eight three, five and nine two, seven all); a rough order of strength from Saturn 1 to Rāhu 8; male, female and neuter planets and their castes; the strong courses (Mercury and Jupiter swift, Mars crooked, Venus and Saturn slow); house strengths and the days of the planets' houses; direction and day or night strength; the four actions (pacifying, increase, power, compelling and expelling, killing) and the four *rig byed* with their lords; the gods and planets of each time-union (*dus sbyor*) by house and their three facings; and the sessions of Rāhu. No table was compiled from them; each is a rule in verse, to be read on the scan before it is used.
