# The thirty: the third point, the poisonous snake of existence, for mantra-holders and vajra-holders

The third of the thirty starting points (སྐོར་འགོ, skor 'go), from the mouth of the poisonous snake of existence (སྲིད་པའི་དུག་སྦྲུལ, srid pa'i dug sbrul), is read for mantra-holders and vajra-holders (སྔགས་འཆང་རྡོ་རྗེ་འཛིན, sngags 'chang rdo rje 'dzin). Its eight pebbles are laid as for every point (claims thirty-count and thirty-eight-fighters), each white or black, and read so:

| Pebble | Asks | White | Black | Remedy for black |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Whether the golden vajra has gone up into the sky | One meets the Dharma, wishes are fulfilled; the line of mantrins is not cut (the words «བརྗེ[?]་སྔགས[?]་པ» doubtful on the scan) | The golden vajra goes up into the sky: one does not like the forefathers' Dharma, looks on it wrongly and delights in sin | — |
| 2 | Whether the silver bell is lost from the hand | The line of monks and mantrins (བན་སྔགས, ban sngags) is not cut; awareness is keen, knowledge good | The sweet-sounding bell slips away; mantra practice brings no result; works are not achieved | The mamo (མ་མོ, ma mo)'s thread-cross (མདོས, mdos) of turning back and the Protector's (མགོན་པོ, mgon po) turning back of war help |
| 3 | Whether the string of the commitment rosary is cut | Awareness keen, wishes fulfilled, merit grows | The string is cut, bad; obstacles come | Long-life practice (ཚེ་སྒྲུབ, tshe sgrub) |
| 4 | Whether the great canopy (ཞེང་ཆེན་བླ་བྲེ, zheng chen bla bre) is torn | Wishes fulfilled; the canopy spreads to its edges and the luck (ཀླུང་རྟ, klung rta) grows | The canopy leaks at its edges; monks and disciples rise as enemies | Strive at the torma turning back (གཏོར་བཟློག, gtor bzlog) and turning back enemies |
| 5 | Whether the great drum of existence is torn at its body | Fame gathers and people are kind | The great drum is torn | The mending of existence (སྲིད་པ་ཡོ་བཅོས, srid pa yo bcos) and the nine rounds (དགུ་སྐོར, dgu skor) |
| 6 | Whether the punishment of the ḍākinīs (མཁའ་འགྲོ, mkha' 'gro) has come | The mothers and ḍākinīs protect, very good | The punishment of the mothers and ḍākinīs comes | The ḍākinīs' welcoming and turning back (བསུ་བཟློག, bsu bzlog) and the pollution thread-cross (གྲིབ་མདོས, grib mdos) |
| 7 | Whether the knife, the iron and the dagger are broken | No obstacle to life; forefathers like kings, sons and grandsons like ministers | Obstacles come; for a young man a great risk of death | Press down the sri of the prime of life (དར་སྲི, dar sri) and tie on protection against the sri |
| 8 | Whether the dish of medicine, rakta and torma (སྨན་རག་གཏོར་མ, sman rag gtor ma) is empty | Forefathers like kings, sons and grandsons like ministers | A great risk that the eight lines (རིགས་བརྒྱད, rigs brgyad) are cut; good now, bad at the end | The general thread-cross of the four continents, the queen's crowd thread-cross, the rite of restoring the luck and the practice of medicine, rakta and torma |

Whose year is reckoned: the person's own (the notes, vol. 2, p. 533: «གཞན་རྣམས་སོ་སོའི་ལོ་རྟ»).

Which of the eight fighting pebbles is which check is not stated; the order of p. 453 is the likely one (claim thirty-board-groups; question 259).
