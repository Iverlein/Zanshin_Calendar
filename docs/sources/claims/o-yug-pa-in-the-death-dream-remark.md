# «འོ་ཡུག་པ» in the remark of the death-dream verse is a name, that of that of a Sakya teacher (or of a man of 'O yug), and the remark gives his saying (RD360)

In cbl:p237s the small-letter remark has two sayings, each closed by «གསུང་སྐད» ("so he said"): "the lama says: the sign that the time of death has come", and then «འོ་ཡུག་པ་གཤིན་རྗེ་དུས་མཚན་ནག་མོ་ཡིན་གསུང་སྐད». «འོ་ཡུག་པ» is not a common word of the verse or of the dream lists; BDRC's full-text search (2026-11-10, exact strings) finds it 44 times, and every place read in the snippets is a personal name in a Sakya lineage: «'o yug pa Bsod nams seng ge» (in the Sa skya seat-holder's pair with Shar pa Shes rab 'byung gnas, in the lineage lists of the Rdzong sar teaching-record and of another teaching-record after 'Phags pa and before Ri phug pa Shākya seng ge), «'o yug pa Rig pa'i seng ge» (in the verses of Sa skya Paṇḍita's life, among those who fled), «'o yug pa Rong ston Rdo rje'i sgra» (a lineage list in the Sa skya bka' 'bum etext), and «bla ma 'o yug pa» (in three histories of Tibet). The remark's second saying is therefore most naturally a saying of a teacher called 'O yug pa, reported as the lama's was; who he is (Bsod nams seng ge or Rig pa'i seng ge or another) the remark does not say. This is a reading of a name by its company in snippets, not a statement of the print.

What the saying says. «དུས་མཚན» (dus mtshan, the "time-sign") is the name of the sign in the verse and also of the black goddess «dus mtshan nag mo» of the Sakya protectors' praises (BDRC snippets from nine etexts: «dpal ldan lha mo re ma ti … dus mtshan nag mo khyod la bstod»). The saying thus equates the Lord of Death's "time-sign" with the black one, that is with her; the Dpe bsdur ma volume 46 reaches the same equation in the verse itself, «གཤིན་རྗེའི་ཆུང་མ་དུས་མཚན» (cbd:p1065-1066). Whether the remark is the old annotator's or Mkhyen brtse's own addition is not told: the editor's note (cbl:p278-279) says only that the note is old, supplemented in Mkhyen brtse's hand, copied from a margin whose annotator is not known; the same string, «འཆི་བའི་དུས་ལ་བབ་པའི་མཚན་མ་ཡིན་གསུང་སྐད», is found by BDRC in no other etext, and «འོ་ཡུག་པ་གཤིན་རྗེ» in no other.
