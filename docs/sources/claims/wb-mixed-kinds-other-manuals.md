# UT and the Bhutan marriage manual too give each aspect's kind and result, and no name for a mixed couple

Searched 2026-11-01 for a name for the couple whose four aspects fall into different kinds (question 209; the older claim [match-kinds-in-general](match-kinds-in-general.md)). UT's marriage digest (BDRC I4767, etext p. 79, printed p. 57) and the Bhutan marriage reckoning (I1KG11905, etext pp. 217–218) copy WB's verse (the man's life force and luck against the woman's, her body and power against his; each meeting as mother and son «ལས་ཀྱི་ཆུང་མ», as friends the kin's, as enemies the enemy's; inner meetings by element) and go on aspect by aspect with the remedy of each: the Bhutan manual (OCR, p. 218) gives each disagreeing aspect its own remedy and its own wheel: «སྲོག་མ་མཐུན་ན་སྲོག་བསླུ་དང་…», the life force not agreeing, a ransoming of life; the power not agreeing, a great giving; the luck not agreeing, the turning back of the enemy. The G.ya' sel's reply 113 (I5875 img. 469) reads the kinds by the same pairing and says of the inner meeting that earth and water go with the karma spouse and the other three with the kin («ནང་ཕྲད་ས་ཆུ་ནང་ཕྲད་བཟང་བ་སྟེ་རབ་ལས་ཀྱི་ཆུང་མར་ཤུགས་ཀྱིས་གོ་བ་དང་། ཤིང་ལྕགས་མེ་འབྲིང་པོ་སྟེ་གཉེན་གྱི་ཆུང་མར་ཤུགས་བསྟན»); Mipham's notes (p. 524) repeat that: «ནང་ཕྲད་ས་ཆུ་ལས་ཀྱི་ཆུང་མ་དང་གཞན་གསུམ་གཉེན་གྱི་ཆུང་མར་གཏོགས་ཞེས་གཡའ་སེལ་དུ་བཤད»; they too say "look at good and bad in general" and leave it there. None names the mixed couple: the couple is read aspect by aspect, each with its own result, as WB's notes lay it out. A dead end for a single name; the pointer for a build is [match-kinds-in-general](match-kinds-in-general.md).
