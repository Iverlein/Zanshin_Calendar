# WB ch. 35 p. 449: the parts of reckoning read as the parts of Mañjuśrī

Page 449's passage in the voice of Mañjuśrī reads the parts of reckoning as parts of the deity: the twelve year-spirits (སྲིད་པའི་ལོ་གཉན་བཅུ་གཉིས, srid pa'i lo gnyan bcu gnyis), the eight sage-magic-men, the nine palaces and great continents, the five elements, the planets with the twenty travelling mansions (རྒྱུ་སྐར་ཉི་ཤུ, rgyu skar nyi shu), the four gates of gathering, the three essences of reckoning and the fine and fierce "joining" (སྐྱོར་བྱེད, skyor byed) are each set against a part of his nature. It is a meditation on the parts, not a rule; it shows only that the year-spirits and the twenty mansions belong to the reckoning of the Phug tradition as the author knows it. Machine reading.
