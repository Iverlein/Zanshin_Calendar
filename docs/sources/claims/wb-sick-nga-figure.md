# Examining the sick by the nāga figure and the weekday

Examining the sick (WB vol. 2, pp. 415–416): a nāga figure is drawn with mouth, chest, belly, thigh, tail, neck, shoulder, navel and secret place, three stars each, from the moon-star of the hearing and the asking. The birth-name star of the sick on the neck: death; on the belly or thigh: a violent illness; on the tail or chest: long and bad; on the mouth, navel or shoulder: victory over the illness; in the secret place: bad. By the weekday of the asking: Sun, palpitation and delusion, short; Moon, lower-body pain, harm from the nāga offering and the dead; Mars, bile and head illness, soon healed, harm from tsen; Mercury, water and kidney illness; Jupiter, wind and bile, night wandering; Venus, lung and bone, the king's harm; Saturn, a gathered illness, danger of death from Yama, the earth lord and the mamo.
