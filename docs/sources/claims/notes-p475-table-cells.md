# The notes' worked table at p. 475: the first row and the marks, on two prints

The worked table of the notes at p. 475 (the birth's hundred, 150 in full): thirty cells under the life force's rise and decline with its verdicts, the body's under it, each cell a small circle or a cross; the deciding pebble of a column is the mark that is more among the five above it. Read cell by cell on the Chengdu typeset edition and on Mipham's own print (2026-11-01):

- The first row is labelled «སོ་སོའི་རང་རྡེལ», "each one's own pebble": the typeset prints it plainly, Mipham's print has it clear enough to read, so the blotted stack of the 1996 print and the Gser thang reprint is no variant (question 199).
- The cells are RD26's, with two differences between the prints: the typeset leaves two cells empty (the friend column in the son and friend rows) where Mipham's print has crosses, which the deciding cross of that column asks for (four crosses and one circle above it); and Mipham's print has a filled dot where the son column's deciding circle stands, which is ink (four circles and one cross above it).
- Which of the circle and the cross is white the page does not say: the sentence after the table, "the results of these are:", goes over to the next page and says no more on it in the lines read (question 198 stays open).
