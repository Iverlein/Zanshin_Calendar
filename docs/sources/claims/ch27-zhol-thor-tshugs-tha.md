# The feat of the body on p. 440 is «ཐོར་ཚུགས» on the Zhol print too

On the Zhol print (WBZ, I1KG1710 img. 1219, the line that carries «གཞན་ཡང་ཡི་གེ་བགྲང་རྩིས་དང་། །ཐོར་ཚུགས་གོམ་སྟབས་ལྕགས་ཀྱུས་བསྒྱུར།») the first letter of the feat's name, read in a 6x crop, has the open frame of a tha with the vowel sign o above it, followed by ra, so the word is «ཐོར་ཚུགས», not «ཟོར་ཚུགས». The Zhol blocks agree with the Gser thang reprint as RD84 read it, and Yigdzin-1 on the Zhol image and on the Zhol etext gives the same; three prints, two of them by eye. What feat the word names is not settled by any print.
