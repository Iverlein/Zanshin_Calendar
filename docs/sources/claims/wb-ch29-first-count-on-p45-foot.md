# The first examination's 87, 338 and 425 are WB's own line at the foot of p. 45, which Yigdzin-1 drops

The first examination's three figures are WB's own words, not the commentary's sum: they stand in the last two lines of p. 45, which Yigdzin-1 drops from its reading of the 1996 scan (img. 053) and MITRA keeps. Read by eye on the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5029 img. 051): the roots and result pebbles eighty-seven, the identifying three hundred and thirty-eight, the sum four hundred and twenty-five, and for the ten roots a hundred and eighty-five identifying pebbles. They close from the verses of p. 46: the result pebbles are 10 roots + 55 fighting + 22 small = 87, the identifying 185 + 10 + 143 = 338. So the table's first row (87, 338, 425) rests on a stated line and not on an addition, and the 87 of the first examination is stated in the same breath as its 425. Yigdzin-1 also leaves out the last lines of the 1996 img. 058 (p. 50) and img. 053; a count line that a machine reading lacks is looked for in MITRA's file and on the scan before it is called missing. Question 66 (the 87 is the first examination's) and question 65 (the first's figures are not among the gap).
