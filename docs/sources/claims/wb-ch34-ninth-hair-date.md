# The 9th hair date stands bad by ch. 33; its result is company of young women

In WB's list of the lunar dates for washing the hair (vol. 2, p. 404) the 9th is on the bad side because the list says so by reference: after the thirty results it states «གོང་གི་ཚེས་འབྲས་དང་བསྟུན་ན།», "following the date results above", and names the good and the bad dates by the date readings of ch. 33; the 9th is among the bad. Ch. 33's own 9th reads «སྐྲ་འཁྲུ་བྲན་བསྟེན་གཤིན་བག་ངན», washing the hair, keeping servants, funeral rites and marriage are bad on it (lunar-dates.md, row 9), so the bad standing is ch. 33's verdict, not a reading of the result «ན་ཆུང་མོད་འགྱུར». The result itself, "young women become many", is read by the witnesses as company or meeting: SN «ན་ཆུང་དང་འཕྲད», a Drigung haircut verse «དགུ་ལ་ན་ཆུང་འགྲོགས་», FPMT's "meeting youthful people", and in those lists it stands between long life (8th) and great power (10th), which are goods. So the question's second alternative holds: the date stands bad because of ch. 33, while the result is company with young women and WB says no more of it as a gain or a warning. Whether it is meant as a good (a bride, company) or a distraction is not decided by any source found.
