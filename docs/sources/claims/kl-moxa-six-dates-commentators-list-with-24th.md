# KL's six moxa dates: the commentators' list, in modern collections too, always with the 24th

# KL's six dates for moxa: the list is the Four Tantras' commentators', in modern Tibetan collections too, always with the 24th

For question 79. BDRC's full-text search, 2026-11-06, for the line that holds the dates: «ཚེས་གཅིག་དྲུག་བཅོ་བརྒྱད» finds five hits in four works, all the list known: the Blue Beryl on the Last Tantra's chapter on moxa (twice, in its chapter divisions, MW1PD3_254AC6 and MW1PD3_BED6AF) and its outline-only etext (MW2PD17386), with Acarya Phyag rdum named before it; Skal bzang chos kyi rgyal mtshan's works (MW1AC242); and a modern collection, *Gso ba rig pa*, vol. 3 in *Gangs ljongs rig bcu'i snying bcud chen mo* (MW1PD95727): «ཚེས་གཅིག་དྲུག་བཅོ་བརྒྱད་ཉེར་གཉིས་ཉེར་བཞི་ཉ་སྟོང་…ལ་གཏར་བསྲེག་གཉིས་ཀ་སྤང», the 1st, 6th, 18th, 22nd, 24th, full and new moon, no bloodletting or moxa. Every hit has the 24th; none has the six as KL gives them (1, 6, 15, 18, 22, 30) in numbers, and «ཚེས་གཅིག་དྲུག་བཅོ་ལྔ་བཅོ་བརྒྱད», «བཅོ་ལྔ་བཅོ་བརྒྱད་ཉེར་གཉིས་སུམ་ཅུ» and «གཏར་བསྲེག་ཚེས་གཅིག་དྲུག» have no hit. So KL's list is the commentators' with the 24th dropped and "full and new moon" written as the 15th and 30th; who dropped it, and why KL says "in agreement with the Tibetan reckoning", is not found in any text searched (the snippets only; the etext of the Blue Beryl is search-only).
