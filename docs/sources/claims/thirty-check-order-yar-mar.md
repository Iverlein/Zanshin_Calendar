# The thirty's check order and the pebbles counted up and down

WB's order of the eight fighting pebbles (vol. 1, p. 453) names the places by the words «ཡར» (up) and «མར» (down), one after the other: the fourth up (life force), the fourth down (body), the eleventh up (power), the eleventh down (luck's enemy), the seventh up (life force), the seventh down (body), then the progressed year itself. Pebbles 1, 3 and 5 are the «ཡར», 2, 4 and 6 the «མར».

The board's groups use the same words: the general board (vol. 1, p. 471; the Zhol print «ཡར་གསུམ», the 1996 print «ཡང་གསུམ») and the notes (vol. 2, p. 533: «དང་པོ་གསུམ་པ་ལྔ་པ་གསུམ་ལ་ཕ་མེས་དང་ཚེ་སྟོད་ཀྱི་ས་སྟེ་ཡར་གསུམ་ཞེས་བྱ། གཉིས་བཞི་དྲུག་ལ་མར་གསུམ། བདུན་བརྒྱད་ལ་བར་དུ་བརྩི») call checks 1, 3, 5 the "three up" (ཡར་གསུམ), 2, 4, 6 the "three down" (མར་གསུམ) and 7, 8 the middle. So the "up three" and "down three" of the board are by name the pebbles counted up and down on p. 453, and the check order is the order of the eight fighting pebbles. This is the one textual link between the two; before it only the order of the numbers was matched. The *Moonbeams* too lays a white pebble on the places counted up and a black on those counted down (claim thirty-dz-eight).

What it does not settle: the readings' contents do not all fall by the board's places. In points 1, 2 and 6 the first two checks both speak of the forefathers' or the father's and uncles' place, though check 2 is of the "lower three", oneself; and the aspect fought (life force at 1 and 5, power at 3, body at 2 and 6, luck at 4) is not what the checks name. Read as a grouping of the places, not check by check.
