# Tsong kha pa quotes Mar me mdzad bzang po's lines with «ཐབས་སྦྱོར» in the vows part and gives the Ender of Obstructors only in the dawn-dream part (q1059, RD401)

In the vows part of the Great Exposition of Secret Mantra (Tsong kha pa's gsung 'bum, Krung go edition, etext p. 360) Tsong kha pa quotes Mar me mdzad bzang po's lines «having asked about the dream, good and bad, destroy the non-virtue by the means of application (ཐབས་སྦྱོར, thabs sbyor); then have the well-guarded pupil take the vows» in exactly the words of the rite itself (mmb:p155-156), and uses them to say that the pupil takes the vows after the dream is examined and before the main part. He gives no gloss of «ཐབས་སྦྱོར» there and does not name the Ender of Obstructors. In the dawn-dream part of the same work (snr:p367-371) he says that Mar me mdzad bzang po has the Ender of Obstructors destroy the obstructors of a bad dream. So Tsong kha pa knows the rite's own wording and cites it with «ཐབས་སྦྱོར» in one place, and in the other gives a remedy the rite does not name: either he takes «ཐབས་སྦྱོར» to be the Ender, or the remedy comes from another work or another reading. The two places do not join; the text of neither says which. BDRC's exact-string search for the verse's first line (17 hits) shows the same quotation, with «ཐབས་སྦྱོར», in a Guhyasamāja practice text of the Derge Tengyur (Rin chen 'bar ba, MW23703_1251), in the Peking etext of the rite, in the collected works of Ngag dbang kun dga' bsod nams and in Karmapa Mi bskyod rdo rje's Dgongs gcig commentary, none glossing it as the Ender (snippets only; not opened).
