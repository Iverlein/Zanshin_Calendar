# The Svapnohana's fortunate images and their verdicts

Fortunate images of the *Svapnohana*, each with the text's own verdict (pages 268–271):
- Riding an ox or an elephant; a mansion; a mountain peak; a forest tree; sitting in a boat holding a lute; eating food; ploughing: goods are always gained (p. 268).
- A body struck with weapons, covered with small creatures, its dirt washed away; following excrement; blood flowing from the body: "in dreams, when it is not a road taken", auspicious (p. 268; the clause "not a road taken", «བགྲོད་མིན», is not settled, a doubt).
- A king, an elephant, an excellent horse, a traveller, oxen and cows seen at the end of sleep: kinsfolk many (pp. 268–269).
- Climbing alone a tree with milky fruit and waking on it: wealth soon (p. 269).
- Eating in a mansion; crossing the ocean: even one of low birth becomes a king (p. 269).
- Eating curd and milk porridge inside a great lotus petal in a pond: lordship of land (p. 269).
- A lamp, banner, chariot, parasol, girl, leaf, fruit, food seen at the end of sleep: the glory with nothing above (p. 269).
- Entrails winding a village: a petty king; winding a city: a king (p. 269).
- Shoes, parasol and sharp sword obtained, then waking: a journey on the road; crossing by boat even a broken one: a journey and quick return (p. 269).
- Sun's disc or moon seen: the sick are freed, the well become glorious (p. 270).
- A white snake seizing the right hand: property "within ten" (p. 270).
- A wife or kinsman speaking, or being seized and bound or lassoed by the hand: a son, or a great station (p. 270).
- Seizing a horse, a «བླ་མོ» (unclear), a snake or a water creature: victory or goods, or a son (p. 270).
- Drinking blood or beer: for a brahmin knowledge, for others wealth (p. 270).
- Drinking cow's milk: milk to drink, or a fire offering will be done; curd seen: comfort; a village seen: wealth; mustard: gain (p. 270).
- A seat, bed, mount, body, chariot or house burning at the waking: glory increases (p. 271).
- A sign worshipped: the wish is accomplished; a body seen specially: victory "through the manner"; gods, brahmins, the father or marked women saying the best in a dream: it is so; gods dancing or someone running on a terrace: a journey to a place (p. 271).
