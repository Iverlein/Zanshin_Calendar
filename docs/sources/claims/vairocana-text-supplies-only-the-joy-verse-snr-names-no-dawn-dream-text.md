# The Sngags rim chen mo takes only the joy verse from the Vairocanābhisambodhi; the dawn-dream steps come from other texts, and no Vairocana text reading the dawn dreams was found (q1012, RD401)

The Sngags rim chen mo (snr:p367-371) says of the verse that rouses joy: it is the Vairocanābhisambodhi's, but its last two lines «gang gis sems can» are not in the present Vairocanābhisambodhi, nor does the commentary explain them; they stand in many mandala rites. Everything after it in that part (the guarding, lying on kusha with the head to the east, the request «tell me at dawn», the reading of the dream by the night's four parts, the remedies) Tsong kha pa takes from other texts: the Common Tantra, Saraha, the Mañjuśrī root tantra, the Garland, Mar me mdzad bzang po, Klu byang and Dārikapa. So the pool's passages name no Vairocana-specific text that reads the pupils' dawn dreams; the Vairocana text supplies only the joy verse, which is the cause of the question's wording. A text of the Vairocana tradition that does read them was not found in the pool or by exact-string search.
