# The going forth on the dragon month's tenth: under Ānanda in Sgo chen's prayer, under Prabhāhasti in the other lists

The lists of the twelve tenths differ on the master under whom Padmasambhava went forth on the tenth of the fourth deed's month. The prayer in the liturgy collection of Sgo chen dgon has «འབྲུག་ཟླའི་ཚེས་བཅུར་ཀུན་དགར་རབ་ཏུ་བྱུང», he went forth under Kun dga' (Ānanda), in the dragon month (sgc:i208-214, read on the scan). 'Jigs med gling pa has «སློབ་དཔོན་པྲ་བྷ་ཧསྟིར་རབ་ཏུ་བྱུང», under the master Prabhāhasti, in the Dbo month (jgl.8:p739-742, scan), and Zhe chen rgyal tshab's summary of the treasure texts likewise «སློབ་དཔོན་པྲ་བྷ་ཧསྟིའི་དྲུང་དུ་རབ་ཏུ་བྱུང», in the dragon month (zgp.13:p537-540, machine reading). All three give him the name Śākya seng ge there. Padma gling pa's text names no master: "he relied on the knowledge-holder gurus of India at the time of the tenth" (pgl.1:p835-837).

The three texts are of one rank (3). The list printed in the lineage's own collection stands for the lineage, so a text for the day says Ānanda; the other two are recorded. The same lists differ on who burned him in Uḍḍiyāna on the horse (or Sa ga) month's tenth: the king with his consort beside him ('Jigs med gling pa), the king (Zhe chen rgyal tshab), the demon ministers (Sgo chen's prayer).
