# Mipham: a dream of the field and the Teacher is the lowest sign of progress toward Sukhāvatī

In Mipham's guide to purifying oneself for Sukhāvatī (mph.104:i236-237) a dream is a sign of progress, graded: the best sign is seeing Amitābha in person in this life with a prophecy, the middle a blessing felt as experience, the lowest "that one dreams the aspect of the field and of the Teacher in a dream" (རྨི་ལམ་དུ་ཞིང་དང་སྟོན་པའི་རྣམ་པ་རྨིས་པ). He explains why even that lowest dream counts: whatever shows in a dream, however poor, is the ripening of an imprint, so a small imprint of the wish is enough, by the power of the Buddha's vow. The practice is divided into "day and night" (ཉིན་མཚན): by day recollection of the field, at night lying down with the wish to see the Sugata, the remembrance unbroken ("sleeping and transference", ཉལ་འཕོ, whose methods he refers to the instructions of the old and new schools). No hour of the night, no day of the month and no source for the sign is named in this passage beyond "the Buddha's vow".
