# The copper vessel's sounding: a hundred pebbles and twenty-five readings

The copper vessel's sounding (ཟངས་ཀྱམ་སྒྲ་འབྱིན, zangs kyam sgra 'byin) is a hundred fighting pebbles with twenty-five deciding pebbles that carry the readings (WB vol. 2, pp. 90–91; the notes, p. 509, read on the scan). The fights: the dead's clan element (own, mother, son, enemy, friend) against the year's life force, body, power and luck; the dead's birth year's four aspects against the death year's; the birth month's life force against the death month's; birth day against death day; birth hour against death hour; five each. The pebble rule: mother or friend two white; son one white and one black; own one white for earth or water, black for fire, wood or iron; enemy cutting two black. The twenty-five places are grouped five by five: the chief or king (clan), the year's examiner holding the banner, the month's counsellor, the day's great hall, the hour's knife-holder. Each place has its image and reading: 1 black, appointed by karma; 2, the father's line's wealth no help; 3, a *srin mo* (སྲིན་མོ) ate the son's flesh, bad for sons-in-law and descendants; 4, quarrels; 5, the storehouse empty; 6, the mother's womb falls; 7, the paternal kin's share small; 8, sons-in-law's and sisters' share small; 9, the king's head empty, quarrels; 10, wealth and food struck; 11, illness without end; 12, few sons; 13, many illnesses; 14, no sons or sisters; 15 white, poison becomes medicine, fortune gathers (black the reverse); 16, bad for soldiers; 17, the paternal kin decline; 18, bad for wealth; 19, kin rise as enemies; 20 white, no enemy (black bad); 21, the stūpa without light; 22, the father's wealth no help to the son; 23, no sons; 24, a bad year, quarrels; 25, quarrels and loss of wealth. Unless said, black is the reading and white its reverse. The summary: two black, twenty-five demons; two white, twenty-five gods; one black, *'dre* and *srin* (འདྲེ་སྲིན) harm; one white, well-being; mixed, gods and demons as one. Without the birth month, day and hour, the clan element is reckoned. What "two black, one white" counts over (the four of a column, or all twenty-five) is a question.
