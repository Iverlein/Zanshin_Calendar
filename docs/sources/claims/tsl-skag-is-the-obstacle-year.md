# «སྐག» in Rtsa gsum gling pa's life is the obstacle (keg) of the own year: 13 and 25 are the own-year returns

In Rtsa gsum gling pa's life «སྐག» (skag) is a term of ill-fated years, the same as the «ཀེག» (keg, obstacle) of the year-of-age reckoning, so «བཅུ་གསུམ་རང་ལོ་སྐག་ཐོག་ལ» (i139) is a technical phrase and not a scribal mistake.

The vol. 30 etext uses the word four times in settled company. A dākinī warns: "in particular the skag of the twenty-fifth (year), and before the back of the year-skag (ལོ་སྐག, lo skag) has passed, if even a corner is seen by others, a great obstacle will come" (i193-194). A message from the tulku's house says: "this year the tulku's rough skag (རྩུབ་སྐག, rtsub skag) is great, so do a protective creation-stage rite" (i505). The volume's prayers against what befalls on a bad time name «ལོ་ཟླ་ཞག་དུས་སྐག་རིགས», the skag kinds of year, month, day and time (vol. 28 images 612, 615, 616, 637, read in the etext only). The year-of-age reckoning in Klong chen Chos dbyings stobs ldan rdo rje's *Mdo rgyud rin po che'i mdzod* (year-of-life.md, *The seven rough obstacles*) has the pair in other spelling: «རང་ལོ་གནམ་ལོར་ཤར་ན་རང་ཀེག་རྩུབ་པ» ("if one's own year goes onto the sky year, the own obstacle is rough"): «རྩུབ་སྐག» of i505 is the «རྩུབ་པ» of the seven rough obstacles, «སྐག» the «ཀེག». The twenty-fifth year in i193 and the thirteenth in i139 are the two first returns of one's own birth-animal year (13 and 25 in the Tibetan count), which fits «རང་ལོ» (own year); that arithmetic is my inference, not stated by the text. So the dākinī's «རང་ལོ་སྐག་ཐོག་ལ» says that at thirteen, when his own year's skag fell on him, the list and the sealed command were to be taken, and the demoness's shadow veiled it until fifteen.

Not settled: whether «ཐོག་ལ» means "on the day of" or "at the head of" the obstacle, and which of the sources' skag is the one calculated (own year against the sky year, or the return of the birth animal). On the scan (vol. 30 img. 139, printed p. 128, line 1, at 3.5x of a 1600-pixel image) the stack of «སྐག» is read as ས with ྐ and ག; the image is low in resolution.
