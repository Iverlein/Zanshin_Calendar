# WB ch. 29's unexplained words that the dictionaries do gloss

Steinert's 64 dictionaries (locate.py dict, 2026-11-01), headwords by Wylie:
- «ལྷོག་པ» (lhog pa, p. 55): a large ulcer or sore, a carbuncle (Rangjung Yeshe; Ives Waldo); the Tshig mdzod chen mo makes it an epidemic that falls on the flesh, and Dan Martin a malignant anthrax. In the trigram gin's entry, "the lhog pa part" is most likely the flesh of an animal that died of it, eaten. A reading by the dictionaries, not stated by WB.
- «ཡུགས་ས» (yugs sa, p. 57): mourning for a deceased spouse, with its impurity (Valby; Ives Waldo; Martin). So «ཡུགས་སའི་སྐྱུར» is "the skyur of a mourning place". «སྐྱུར» as a noun has the senses "thing cast away" (skyur ba, to throw out) and "sour"; which is meant, in the water-cut-fire reason and in the five-yellow mewa, is not settled; "cast-off things of a mourning" fits both places best.
- «ཁྲ་བོ» (khra bo): mottled, piebald; «བློ་ཐུབ» (blo thub): trustworthy, reliable (Ives Waldo; Tshig mdzod chen mo). No entry for the pair; as a verdict "speckled, to be trusted" is the literal sense.
- «འབྲང་རྒྱས» ('brang rgyas, pp. 68–69): a round torma shaped like a human heart (Tshig mdzod chen mo; Ives Waldo; Martin adds a ritual mound of barley flour at Ladakhi weddings). «བྱེ་ཐག» (sand-cord) is in no dictionary.
- «གཡེར་མ» (g.yer ma, p. 69): Zanthoxylum, Sichuan or "guinea" pepper (Valby; Tshig mdzod chen mo: a tree medicine, a pepper); so «གཡེར་མའི་ཆུ» is pepper water. Its use as corpse food is WB's own.
- «བསེ་རགས» (bse rags): «བསེ» is tanned or dyed leather, rhinoceros hide, «བསེ་ཁྲབ» a hide corselet (Rangjung; Valby); «རགས» has no entry; "a hide belt" fits.
- «ཡུ་མོའི་གསོབ» (yu mo'i gsob, p. 59): «ཡུ་མོ» a doe or hornless cow, «གསོབ» a stuffed, hollow dummy, such as a beast's stuffed hide (Tshig mdzod chen mo): a stuffed doe's hide.
- «འོག་རྟ» ('og rta, p. 61): a riding horse (བཞོན་རྟ, Tshig mdzod chen mo; Richardson after Martin).
- «ཕྱ་ར» (phya ra, pp. 67–68): a door curtain of yak-hair cloth.
- «གླང་མ» (glang ma, p. 65): a willow (two kinds, white and black; Valby, Tshig mdzod chen mo, Sgom sde), not a cow, and «བྱང་བུ» (byang bu) a tablet or label, Martin's "name-card used in death ritual": so «གླང་མའི་བྱང་བུ» are willow tablets, eighteen of them, written with the Pleiades' and the dead's names.
- «བུས་པ» (bus pa): a child (Old Tibetan), or the past of «འབུད་པ» ('bud pa, to send out); not placed in WB's context here.
Not in any of the dictionaries: «ཏིང་མཉྫི», «སླ་ང་རྩེས», «སྟེང་འཁོར», «ཟོ་བ་ཞབས་བརྡོལ», «སིག་ལྐོགས», «སྐྱར་མ», «སེལ་གོག», «བང་ས་དུ་རུ་ཅན», «ཕན་ངོན», «ཐལ་ཤལ», «གསང་ཤིད་ཟུ», «བསེ་རགས་བྱུར་ཤིད» as a whole, «ཕྱི་གུ».
