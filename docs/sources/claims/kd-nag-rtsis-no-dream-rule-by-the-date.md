# No dream line of the Nag rtsis books says what a lunar day's, weekday's or day animal's dream is of; BDRC's exact phrases give no such line

No dream line of the Nag rtsis (the Chinese reckoning) pages of the Kun 'dus chen mo vol. 3 (etext pp. 536-766) says what is dreamt on a lunar day, a weekday, a day animal or a month. Read in full: the seven places of the claim kd-nag-rtsis-dream-lines-seven-wb-lines, and the occurrences of «རྨི» on the pages of the book: the only others are the contents list of the Mchog dga' (p. 177), the Mchog dga's own dream section (pp. 197-200) and its two verse lines on Saturn (pp. 207, 335). The word «ལྟས་ངན» (bad omen) stands on eleven pages of the Nag rtsis part (511, 526, 559, 605, 635, 638-640, 652, 653, 690), none of them with a dream.

What ties to the date in these books is the element of the hour («དུས་ཚོད་དབང་ཐང་ཁམས»), reckoned from the day's power at dawn by WB p. 156 (wb.2:p157-161), and the day's animal for the sick (wb.1:p428-429). The dream lines that fall under them are one of the many signs the diviner tells the asker (the bed of last night; the sleep of the sick): they are not a rule for what a day's dream is of.

Searched by exact phrase in BDRC's full text (2026-11-10, from a headed Chromium): the seven lines themselves (3, 2, 2, 29, 2, 4 hits for the six that carry a dream word: every hit is a copy of WB, the Kun 'dus, a Nag rtsis commentary or, for «རྨི་ལམ་ཟ་ཟི་མང་བ», the medical texts' fever and spirit rows); «གཡང་ས་ཆེན་པོ་རྨི» (7 hits: WB, the Kun 'dus, the Gser thang and Taikhang copies, the Nag rtsis commentaries *Nag rtsis rdel 'grem 'bras bshad* MW1KG21818 and *Dpag bsam ljon shing gi 'bras bu* MW21984_2571E2, the *Bzo rtsis zlos gar*, and a Sa skya Lam 'bras meditation text where the great precipice is a meditator's experience, unrelated); «རྨི་ལམ་རྨི་བའི་ཉིན» (1 hit, a *Ri bo dwangs bsil* guide: a day on which one dreamt, a log); and no hit at all for «བྱི་བའི་ཉིན་རྨི་ལམ», «གླང་གི་ཉིན་རྨི་ལམ», «ཞག་གི་རྨི་ལམ་འབྲས་བུ», «ཚེས་ལ་རྨི་ལམ་རྨིས་ན», «གཟའ་ཉི་མའི་ཉིན་རྨི་ལམ», «ཤིང་གི་ཉིན་རྨི་ལམ», «ཉི་མའི་གཟའ་ལ་རྨི་ལམ». RD367's 126 date phrases found no rule either (claim dds-no-dream-verdict-by-date-in-bdrc-etext-search).
