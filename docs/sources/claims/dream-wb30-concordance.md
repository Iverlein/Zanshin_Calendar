# WB ch. 30's dream list item by item: each parallel's passage and verdict, and the items that match none (RD319)

WB ch. 30's list of dream omens (p. 144, 1996 print ll. 11–24, passage [wb.2:p144l](../corpus/wb/ch30.md#wb-2-p144l)) has thirty-four items, sixteen good (G1–G16) and eighteen bad (B1–B16, with B3 and B13 in two parts), numbered here as that passage numbers them, and two closing lines (C1 the verdict, C2 the remedy). This table sets each item beside the images of every other dream list in the pool, in WB's order, with the passage id of each parallel and its verdict. It adds nothing to the image claims' verdicts (the catalogue, dream-catalogue, holds them by image); it turns them the other way, so that a reader of WB's line finds what the others say of it, and what no other says.

**Texts and passages.** ST7 the Four Tantras' ch. 7 verse of the bad dreams of the sick, `gt:i42-43` (machine; every verdict there is for a sick dreamer, whom the lord of death has seized) and Byang pa's gloss from the *Moonbeams*, `bjp:p177-181` (etext); ST81 ch. 81, `st:p350-351` (scan); LGD, Lama Gongdü, the good `lgd:p967`, the bad `lgd:p968-969`, the mixed and the bad that looks good `lgd:p969`, `lgd:p969-970` (scan; the Palri print, `lgd.42:p966-971b`, machine, agrees in every verdict); SVO the Svapnohana, `svo:p268-271` (etext, machine for pp. 270–271); SVI the Svapna-īkṣā, `svi:p261-262` (etext); SKP, `skp:p40-50` (machine); RST Klong rdol's verses, ACIP etext, `rst:p1-8`; KBS the same verses quoted, `kbs:p72-78` (machine; only the bad lines and the death omens are copied in the pool's passage); RRT the 'Bri gung extended method, `rrt:p186-195` (etext). KCM (`kcm:p679-682`) and DTD (`dtd:p155-158`) are rites for asking in a dream and judge no image; the Four Tantras' ch. 2 (`st:p21`) names only the mother's dream of a man; the Klong rdol verses are RST and KBS. WB ch. 25's lines on the sick (`wb.1:p428-431`) are the sick's and read below the Four Tantras (dream-st-above-wb25). Tone letters as in the catalogue: G good, B bad, X mixed or turning, N a sign only. Each text speaks for its own reader: RST, KBS, SKP, SVO and SVI speak to any dreamer, RRT to a practitioner of a deity, ST7 to the sick; WB's verdict is that of a dream shown before a reckoning, "a defect in what is to be reckoned".

**How to read the columns.** *Same image, same tone*: the parallel names the same dream and judges it as WB does. *Same image, other tone, or only a near image*: the parallel names the image but judges it otherwise, names a neighbouring image, or judges for another reader. *Matches none*: nothing of the pool's other lists names the image, or only a word of it.

**Doubts.** (1) SKP p. 50's «ཁ་ནས་སོ་ནི་འབུད་གྱུར་ན» is read in the image claim dream-vomiting-thieves as vomiting; read letter by letter it is "if teeth («སོ») are thrown out of the mouth", which would be the one parallel to WB's «སོ་གོག» (B8). SKP is a machine reading and its spelling is loose («བཤྭ་», «སོག་» for «སོགས»), so the line is not built on. (2) G12 has no verb of its own on the line; its good verdict is the next line's «དགྲ་ཕམ་རང་རྒྱལ»; the catalogue reads the two as one winning and B10's «གཤགས་འཕམ» as its loss. (3) The fourth riding animal of G6 reads «རྟ», a horse, on three prints (RD294). (4) The parallels marked "not yet in the image claim" are found here in the passages already in the pool; the image claims and the catalogue's tables do not carry them.

**What the table says.** Of the thirty-four items, twenty-four have, in at least one other text, the same image with the same tone (G1, G3, G4, G6–G16; B1–B7, B9, B11 — twenty-four: fourteen good and ten bad). Nine have only a part of the image, a state of mind or a near image: G2 (snow, crystal and mirror together: a mirror in SVI, a crystal vessel and a snow peak in RRT), G5 (the clear and glad mind: RRT's states of a yogin, and RST's and KBS's rule of the waking feeling), B8 (teeth falling: no text read, SKP's line is doubtful; the blind and the mute have partial parallels), B10 (losing at contests, dice and debate: only the Four Tantras' lost fight, for the sick), B13a (the lawsuit: LGD names it as a fruit, its image is the creditors), B13b (the roof falling in: SKP's collapsing house), B14 (the sinking mind: RRT's sunken awareness of a practitioner), B15 (disturbed monks, nuns and *sman* women: only the nakedness and lost ornament of RRT) and B16 (obscured by a demon, "not seen, not clear": near images only). One item, B12 (the house's earth and dung swept out), has none; and the closing verdict C1 (the flaw in what is to be reckoned) is WB's own. RST and KBS (Klong rdol's verses) agree with WB's tone on almost every image they share, and differ only where they speak for a practitioner (the gold of G10, the mind of G5, the pit of B7, the sinking of B14). LGD agrees item for item except on dice (G12 and B10) and in the reverse images of adornment. SVO and SVI, which speak by deity-family signs or by gain, share fewer images with WB and differ on the burning house (B3b) and the body struck by weapons (B11). The Four Tantras' verse, for the sick, is bad on every image it shares with WB (gold G10, a fight G12 and B10, nakedness B5, riding B6, a pit B5 and B7, the limbs B9, and by Byang pa's gloss the sun and moon B1), so that for a sick dreamer WB's good G10 and G12 turn: the point the picker of block 73 needs (dream-treasure-wealth-found).
