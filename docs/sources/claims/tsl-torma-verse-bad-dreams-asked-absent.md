# A torma verse asks the guardians to make bad dreams and bad signs absent

In a sādhana's torma offering (volume 27, image 14, repeated on 17 and 36), the guardians of the place are asked, with untimely death, sickness, harmful spirits and obstructors, to make bad dreams (རྨི་ལམ་ངན) and bad signs (མཚན་མ་ངན) absent. This is a remedy by request to the guardians, in the verse's last lines, and it names neither a time nor a kind of dream. The same wish recurs in liturgies of the Sgo chen dgon collection (BDRC MW00EGS1016761, etext IE0OPI40212F00; pp. 413 and 417, and the benefits of a rite at pp. 550-551), so it is common liturgy and not a mark of Rtsa gsum gling pa's own teaching.
