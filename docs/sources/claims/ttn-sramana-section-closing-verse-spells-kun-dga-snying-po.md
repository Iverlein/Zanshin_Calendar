# Tāranātha's Śramaṇā section closes on a verse whose marked syllables spell his name; it is his own wording, not the be'u bum's text (q1130, q1013)

The closing verse of Tāranātha's Śramaṇā section (ttn:p137-138) is «ཟབ་མོ་ཡིན་ཞེས་ཀུན་ལ་གྲགས། རྒྱུད་ལས་གསུངས་ཕྱིར་མཁས་རྣམས་དགའ། ལུང་བསྟན་སྒྲུབ་པའི་སྙིང་པོ་འདི། གཞན་པས་ངོ་མཚར་ཆེ་བར་འཁུམ།» («known to all as deep; the wise are glad because the tantras say it; this heart of the prophecy practice, to others a great wonder»). In the etext four of its syllables carry the dot below (U+0F35): ཀུན༵ (kun), དག༵འ (dga'), སྙིང༵ (snying), པོ༵ (po). Read in order they spell Kun dga' snying po, Tāranātha's own name; the same device closes a text of the Khrid brgya, where the marked syllables give the compiler's name (dds.41:p304-306). So the closing verse is the author's mark, and the section is Tāranātha's own wording of the rite. It is not the text of the be'u bum: the be'u bum's set has no such verse, and the sentence «none like this instruction of Śramaṇā, an emanation of Tārā, the lama says, and it is established by experience» (tdr:p339) is not in Tāranātha's section, which instead has a seven-day preliminary and says «it is known to all as deep».

What this gives q1130 and q1013: the two texts are two wordings of one rite, in the same order; Tāranātha's is signed with his name and is a different text from the be'u bum's, whose wording is word for word that of the First Dalai Lama's collected works (claim brong-rtse-dream-set-no-author-sramana-in-dalai-lama-works). So Tāranātha's section does not come from the be'u bum's text, and nothing in either names the be'u bum's lama; whether the two share an earlier source is not shown. Etext only; the dots were not checked on the scan.
