# WB ch. 35 is the account of the fields of knowledge; star reckoning is one of five minor fields

Chapter 35 of the White Beryl is written in verse and is an account of the fields of knowledge (རིག་གནས, rig gnas), not a calendar chapter: it lists the eighteen fields by the Vinaya's count and by the Treasury's, reduces them to five great fields, and takes up grammar, then the five minor fields, then reasoning and crafts. Star reckoning (སྐར་རྩིས, skar rtsis) is the fifth of the five minor fields, after composition, poetry, synonyms and drama, and it is named in a few lines only. None of its pages (429–454) contains a rule of the calendar, a date table or an instruction for a day; the only dates are p. 432's days to beware of for grammar. Read on the scan (RD84, 2026-10-31).
