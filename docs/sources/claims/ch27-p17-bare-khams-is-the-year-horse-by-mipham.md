# Ch. 27: for p. 17 the notes' first part makes the bare «ཁམས» the element of the one reckoned's year, not the power's

For question 479. The notes' section seven (wb.2:p532-533) has two parts with two rules for the start. The first, on «རྒྱ་བོད་སྐོར་མགོ་སུམ་ཅུ», the thirty Chinese and Tibetan starting points, "the king and the others", says they are turned «བརྩི་ཡུལ་དེའི་ལོ་རྟ་དང་ཁམས་མཐུན་པའི་བྱི་གླང་སོགས་ནས», from the rat or ox and the rest whose element agrees with the year-horse (ལོ་རྟ, lo rta) of the one reckoned; the notes use «ལོ་རྟ» of a person's year throughout (the tenth start: "the year-horse of the lord who asks"; the eleventh: "the horse owner's year-horse"). The second, on the circles of Balmo, says «བརྩི་བྱའི་ཁམས་སྟེ་དབང་ཐང་གི་དགྲ», the element of the one reckoned, that is the enemy of the power (wb.2:p533b). The reckonings of p. 17 (wb.2:p17) are drawn from the limbs of the secret reckoning, not from the thirteen circles, and are of the first kind: starts for a great chief, ministers, soldiers and weapons, with the same tenth and eighteenth fights. So for them the notes' rule is the year's element: the bare «ཁམས་གཅིག» of the spiritual friend (l. 10) and «ཁམས་མཐུན» of the weapons (l. 24) are the element of the year of the one reckoned, whose own element is his life force's, and «དབང་ཁམས» of the ministers (l. 16) and «དབང་མ» of the soldiers (l. 21) are the power's, named because it is another element. That answers the question's second branch for p. 17 and leaves the circles' «ཁམས» as the power's (ch27-bare-khams-is-the-power-by-mipham). The step that p. 17 belongs to the notes' thirty is ours: the notes name no page, and the thirty are not listed one by one.
