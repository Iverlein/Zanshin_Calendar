# The dream death signs and the spans of life the texts give them (RD374)

RD374. The death-sign dream images and what the texts say they give, as read:

- Riding a donkey (a buffalo, horse, pig, rhinoceros in the medical list) naked and going south: the fourth month (the Ras chung Tika, dds.15:p119-121, p124); no span in the Sdom 'byung verse itself (dds.25:p580-582 and, as Tsong kha pa gives it, dds.16:p11-13: the donkey or monkey, the ant-hill, the south; the black woman, Time-Night, who takes him to Yama's, follows it); from six months (the Hevajra rite, dds.19:p53-55, the donkey rider smeared with red scent); the medical commentary names a disease (འཆི་བདག་གིས་གཅོང་ཆེན, the great gcong disease), not a time (dds.20:p213-220).
- Riding a red-nosed monkey and going east: at the early morning of the next day (Tilopa's Tika); the dawn's death sign (dds.15:p124); in the Hevajra rite, if he wakes, «his last night» (མཚན་མོ་ཐ་མ).
- Sand, ashes, temple, stupa climbed: «as before», which in the Kanjur's chapter 19 is the six months of the verse before it (dds.25:p580-582; the same in dds.16:p11-13); six months (Hevajra rite).
- Eaten by vultures, jackals, dogs, crows or foxes, bears, spirits: one year (Sdom 'byung, dds.25:p580-582; Hevajra rite).
- Red cloth and garland, smeared with sesame oil: not beyond six months (Sdom 'byung); eight months for red scent, garland or cloth found (Hevajra rite).
- The black woman: «Time-Night», death after seven days in the Hevajra rite; to Yama's place in the Sdom 'byung.
- The Gdams ngag mdzod's teaching gives the three images of the midnight dream with no span (dds.9:p173-174); only the inner (wind) signs have spans there, thirty-four or thirty-six days, turned back up to the half.

The Four Tantras (dds.21:p234-235) list the images and give no span to any; their rule is that continual bad dreams in a sick man mean death, in a healthy one an uncertain thing that treatment can turn.
