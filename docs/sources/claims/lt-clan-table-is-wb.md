# LT's clan table of cut-offs is WB's birth table and conflicts with the notes' corner rule for fire and iron

The five rows of LT (lt:p26-28) are WB vol. 1, p. 261 (img. 271, read on the scan): fire great cut-off dog, small dragon; iron great ox, small sheep; wood sheep and ox; earth and water dragon and dog. WB (vol. 1, p. 260; lt:p12) names the great cut-off as the clan element's grave stage and the small as its seventh. The notes (wb.2:p499-500), for the la board, give the four corner animals as dragon and sheep great, dog and ox small, with the wood clan as the one example, where all agree. For fire and iron the notes' rule would make the dragon and the sheep the great cut-off, against WB's table. Which holds on the la board for those clans no source read says; WB, the higher rank, has the grave-stage definition, and its la verse (wb.2:p45) says only great and small.
