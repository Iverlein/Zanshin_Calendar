# WB ch. 18 holds four lists of the sixty years' results, from four texts, side by side

WB's eighteenth chapter (vol. 1, pp. 198–212) gives the results of the years of the sixty-year cycle in **four separate lists**, drawn from different texts and not reconciled with one another: (1) the *Mirror of the Times' Teaching* (དུས་བསྟན་མེ་ལོང, *dus bstan me long*), a verse of two to four lines for each of the sixty named years (pp. 198–199); (2) a longer verse told by "the great sage, the lama", four to ten lines a year, with the crops, the animals, the diseases, the kings and the neighbouring peoples (pp. 202–206); (3) the *Sūtra of the Secret* (མདོ་སྡེ་གསང་བ, *mdo sde gsang ba*), by the year's name with its element, sex and animal, in a terse notation (pp. 206–209); (4) the Chinese *Little Tiger-Ear* (རྒྱ་ནག་སྟག་རྣ་ཆུང་བ, *rgya nag stag rna chung ba*), by element and animal alone (pp. 210–212). Between them the chapter sets what the lord of the year brings by planet and by mansion (pp. 199–200), the planets' courses and conjunctions (pp. 200–201), Saturn's harm by mansion (p. 202), the twelve animal years by the earth lord (pp. 209–210) and the nine *sme ba* entering the centre (p. 212). The closing note says that the sources differ among themselves in very many places, that the faults of the kind "the year is good of itself" were mostly left out, and that the incomplete years of the *Little Tiger-Ear* were filled in from the old chapter lists of Chos rje Bde legs Nyi ma and others.
