# The colours of the planets in chapter eight

The colours WB gives the planets in chapter eight, on pp. 54–55: Jupiter yellow, the sun a pale red, Mars red-black, Rāhu blue, the moon white and yellow, Saturn (*spen pa*) is black, Mercury (*lhag pa*) dwells as green and blue, Venus (*pa sangs*) has no fixed colour.
