# Machig Labdrön's empowerment by Tārā on the evening of the eighth of the last spring month, at forty-one

At forty-one, while she stayed at the retreat place Phug bzang of 'Chi phug, on the evening of the eighth day of the last spring month (དཔྱིད་ཟླ་ཐ་ཆུང་གི་ཚེས་བརྒྱད, dpyid zla tha chung gi tshes brgyad), Tārā (སྒྲོལ་མ, sgrol ma) came to Machig Labdrön (མ་གཅིག་ལབ་སྒྲོན, ma gcig lab sgron) with many ḍākinīs and gave her the four empowerments of each of the five Buddha families from the Udumvara tantra (pgz:p43). The month is named by its season (the last month of spring), not by number; the year only by her age. It is the one other dated event of her life found in ch. 1-2.
