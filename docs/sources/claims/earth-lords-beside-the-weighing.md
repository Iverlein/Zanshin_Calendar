# KP and SY close the weighing by sending the earth lords to screening and remedy

The *Kun phan me long* closes its digest of the weighing (the seven in order of strength and the combination period as chief) by turning to the earth lords as a separate matter: for the earth lords and the other bad kinds one applies, as the occasion comes, their screenings and remedies (སྒྲིབ་བཅོས, sgrib bcos), the general remedy standing for them (kp:i14). Nam mkha' seng ge's *Mirror* copies the weighing nearly word for word and likewise passes at once to the earth lords' general screening and remedy (sy:p389-392). In both the earth lords are not a term of the weighing and are not given a place in its order; they are met by remedy, beside it. Neither says whether a bad earth lord with no remedy at hand overrides a good result of the weighing.
