# Ch. 19: turning the reckoning back from a later year to an earlier

Chapter 19 gives the way back as well (yar slog, "turning back", wb.1:p215-216): from a later year, the years in between up to the year reckoned are multiplied by 12 and set in two places; the lower is multiplied by 2 and divided by 65 and the remainder is cleared from the later year's remainder of 65; if it cannot be cleared, 65 is added first. The corrected month is made as before from the later year to the year reckoned, and the dhruva of the planets and the sun are cleared from those of the later year, the carry freed. Rahu, the year-lord, the king and minister are turned back by the same pattern, each with its own multiplier and circle. The years between are cleared from the Shakya year of the later year, and when they cannot be cleared the Kar-rtsis empty-entry year-count (printed 5,764,801) is added. For the planets' days the general day is divided by its circle, 52, and the particular day cleared with 27 added when needed. The chapter closes with the things that the same reckoning can do beyond these (Rahu's «thebs», the interdependences, the earth diagrams, the height of a staff from its shadow) and with the author's disclaimer; the colophon follows on p. 217.
