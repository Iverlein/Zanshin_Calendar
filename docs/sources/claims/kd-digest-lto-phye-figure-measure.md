# KD's digest: Lto-'phye's figure ten mtho, nine small parts to a great part, 360 cells

The Lto-'phye sheet of the digest (KD vol. 3, pp. 742-745) describes the figure that WB vol. 2 p. 280 prints as a woodcut (wb-p280 woodcut caption claim: the fifteenth house-day of the third Hor month; the same caption stands over the digest's figure). What the digest says of the figure: the plot is a square cut into the four quarters east, south, west and north, each of ten great parts and each great part of nine small parts, ninety cells a side, equal to the days of three months; the cells round the rim are the 360 house-days. The figure is a man to the waist with a cobra's hood and a snake below, ten *mtho* long, in breadth the measure of his own face; his hood, face, heart, navel and secret place are one *mtho* each, and the snake's tail five. The head lies south in the three winter months, west in spring, north in summer, east in autumn; the count of house-days begins in the winter month of the Mouse-woman at the fire corner and goes round to the right, one small part to a house-day. So one *mtho* is one great part, a tenth of the figure's length, and "the ninth part" (དགུ་ཆ) of the digging verse (p. 282, quoted in the digest at p. 745) is the nine small parts of the great part that holds the member, as in the digest's own arrangement (not the nine cells of his breadth). The seasons' words «དབྱར», «དབྱར་འབྲིང», «སྟོན», «དཔྱིད» appear in the digest's figure, whose etext is garbled; the autumn east («སྟོན་ཤར») is the digest's own pairing of the season with the quarter. The finger-breadths of a *mtho* are not given. Etext only.
