# MW1LT2427 holds the Moonbeams, BDRC's page range six leaves late

# MW1LT2427 does hold the Moonbeams, in a part BDRC places six leaves too late

For questions 660 and 537. The Library of Tibet manuscript volume MW1LT2427 (image group I1LT2427, one cursive dbu med volume, outline O1LT2427 of 28 parts) holds the *Moonbeams*: its part 25 is headed in BDRC's outline «'byung rtsis man ngag zla ba'i 'od zer» (འབྱུང་རྩིས་མན་ངག་ཟླ་བའི་འོད་ཟེར), the title Dharmaśrī's work carries in the pool (dz:i029-034 and the colophon of the print on DZ). The outline gives the part pages 577–626 (BDRC's page count of the volume, queried 2026-11-07 through purl.bdrc.io; the neighbouring parts end at 576 and begin at 627), a different part, 13, being a siddhānta work also called «zla ba'i 'od zer».

Read on the scan (every image by Yigdzin-1, three runs, not checked syllable by syllable): the chapter closings stand at img. 572 (end of chapter 1, «བསྐོར་མགོ་སྤྱིའི་རྣམ་བཞག་བསྟན་པའི་ལེའུ་སྟེ་དང་པོ»), img. 575 (chapter 2, the thirty), img. 581 (chapter 3, the final reckoning), img. 588 (chapter 5, the year's circuit, with the verse on the five general totals), img. 591 (chapter 6, children and the sick), img. 593 (the dead's reckoning, chapter 7 by the order), and the colophon at img. 598, «…དྲུག་ཅུ་བསྐོར་བཅུ་གཉིས་མགོར་སྨྱོས་ཀྱི་བན་ཆུང་གི་སློབ་དཔོན་མཁས་པ་རྣམས་ཀྱི་དྲིན་ལས་ཅུང་ཟད་ངེས་པ་རྣམས་ཟིན་ཤུན་སུག་རིས་སུ་བགྱིས་པ་དགེ་ལེགས་སུ་གྱུར་ཅིག །ཤུ་བྷཾ། གཅིག་ཞུས།» (the same close as the other prints). Image 599 is blank. So the work stands at about img. 560–598 of I1LT2427, not at 577–626: the outline is some six leaves late, and the start of chapter 1 lies before img. 569 (not located; the leaves carry the printed folio numbers of the volume, 551 on img. 577, img. = folio + 26).

The 24-pebble count: img. 577, lines 1–2 (machine) carry the count of the deciding pebbles after the verse on the hundred and one pebbles: «དེ་ལྟར་ངེས་པར་བྱས་ནས་ཀྱང་། རྡེའུ་ཉེར་བཞིའི་གྲངས་གཅོད་དོ།», and img. 578 again «དེ་ཡི་གྲངས་གཅོད་ཉེར་གཅིག» and «གྲངས་གཅོད་རྡེའུ་ཉེར་ལྔར»; the machine reads the word «གྲངས་གཅོད» there, where the same run reads «སྟངས་གཅོད» at img. 588 and 593 («སྟངས་གཅོད་དྲིལ་བ», «སྟངས་གཅོད་གཅེས»). The two readings are one manuscript's machine output: the cursive stack «སྟ» against «གྲ» was not settled by eye at 2× on img. 577, so whether this copy writes «སྟངས་གཅོད» in the count is not decided; the answer to question 537 is still with the woodblock (I1NLM6115_001 img. 34, by eye).
