# The Svapnohana: a dream's fruit by the watch of the night

Vibhūticandra's *Svapnohana* gives one rule of time, and it runs by the watch (ཐུན, thun) of the night in which the dream is seen: first watch, fruit within a year; second, within eight months; third, within three months; fourth, within half a month, "seen without doubt"; a dream at the time dawn breaks (སྐྱ་རེངས, skya rengs), fruit within ten days (ཞག, zhag). The night is four watches here (in the Derge, Narthang and gser bris ma prints and the Dpe bsdur ma text; the Peking print omits the fourth watch's line, see svo-peking-fourth-watch-line), the dawn a fifth term of its own; the later the watch, the sooner the fruit. The text does not say how a watch is bounded (equal fourths of the night or the monastic watches) and gives no unit but those named. It does not say that the fruit of a good or a bad image differs in span; the span is the watch's whatever the image. No lunar day, weekday, month or season is named anywhere in the text (see the claim svo-time-ties-other-than-the-watch).
