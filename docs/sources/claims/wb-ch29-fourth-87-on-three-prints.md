# The fourth examination's 87 is on three prints and fits none of its figures

The fourth examination (the funeral share) closes «བསྒྲིལ་བས་རྡེའུ་བརྒྱུད་ཅུ་བདུན», "rolled together 87 pebbles" (with the spelling «བརྒྱུད» for eight), read by eye on the Gser thang reprint; the 1996 print and the Taikhang print (Yigdzin-1) read it so too. Its own figures are 11 roots + 5 key points = 16, 50 identifying, 66; the roots count out as 2 + 6 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 11. No sum or difference of 11, 5, 16, 50, 66 makes 87 (66 + 11 = 77, 66 + 21 = 87 has no 21 in the text). 87 is the first examination's root count (10 + 55 + 22). That the "rolling together" folds the fourth into the first's roots is possible but not said. So 87 is the work's own figure on three prints, not a slip of one; the fourth's total stays 66 in the sum of parts (question 66).
