# A glossary to the Supplement names ལྟས་ཚོར་ as what some read in ch. 81 and keeps ལྷས་ཚོར་ by the pair of times

The glossary to Sde srid's Supplement of the Four Tantras (BDRC I5609 img. 525-526, printed pp. 517-518; a second copy I00KG07616) is the one text found that treats ch. 81's «ལྷས་ཚོར་» as a possible textual error (RD356, 2026-11-10; Yigdzin-1 on the scans, the word's stack not settled by eye). It says that the letters lhas tshor occur in the commentary without an explanation, that some therefore say the meaning is "known through the omen (ལྟས) of the dream, so it is ལྟས་ཚོར", and answers that here the time of lhas tshor and the time the patient feels himself are the two taught, so there is no fault of letters. So ལྟས་ཚོར་ was read by some in the tradition, which fits the Blue Beryl's etext having ལྟས་ once beside ལྷས་ in its outline; the glossarist keeps ལྷས་ on the strength of the pair of times, which is what the Blue Beryl, Khro ru, Skyem pa and Dar mo say. No text glosses ལྷས itself: the sense "the time felt by the god (the spirit)" rests on the grammar of the idiom (st-lhas-tshor-felt-by-the-god) and on the pair, and the variant ལྟས་ ("the time of the omen") would give the same clause a different first term. Rank: the glossary's wording is moderate (machine on a bilevel print); the conclusion that both readings were in circulation is firm, which reading is the author's is not decided.
