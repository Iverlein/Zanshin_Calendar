# The year's and the month's earth lords the remains avoid

The year's and the month's earth lords whose directions the remains avoid (WB vol. 2, p. 96, read on the scan of the Gser thang reprint, the numbers the 1996 editors'):

- **The year's**: they stay seventy-two days each in turn from Tese on the wood tiger at the start of winter, the harmful one first on the iron tiger; on "the other four, the open ones" sending is free of fault. Of the many lists seventeen lords and ministers are counted: 1 Tese, 2 Tekhyim, 3 the queen Hangphenmo, 4 Tsangkun, 5 the seat-spreader, 6 the *se* life-spirit knower, 7 Sebyi, 8 Seshar, 9 the horse-leader, 10 the watchman, 11 Kungnyul, 12 the mount Rangta, 13 Sebya, 14 the son, 15 Karsham, 16 the son-in-law, 17 the hal dog; seven circle to the right, ten to the left. Each one's course by year is in ch. 31, pp. 177–189 (year-month-earth-lords.md, RD3).
- **The month's**: they stay six days each and move on in turn; eighteen are counted: 1 the dragon god, 2 Tegrum, 3 the inner god, 4 the month itself, 5 the month's luck lord, 6 the five gods, 7 the nine, 8 the earth king, 9 the earth army, 10 Tenma, 11 the old couple, 12 Dza zor, 13 the blue mouse road, 14 the wolf, 15 the belly-crawler, 16 the sky [dog], 17 the hal dog, 18 the yellow-black sky line. Their courses are in ch. 31, pp. 199–223 (RD4).

The remains go toward a clean direction where the year's, month's and day's earth lords are not.
