# In a recitation retreat the feast offering is done without fail on the tenth and the twenty-fifth

In a recitation retreat of the Union of the Jewels (དཀོན་མཆོག་སྤྱི་འདུས, dkon mchog spyi 'dus), Zhe chen rgyal tshab (zgt.12:p270) asks for a feast offering (ཚོགས་མཆོད, tshogs mchod) at the end of the late-afternoon or dusk session every day if it can be managed; if it cannot, the feast "must be done without fail" on the first and the last day of the retreat and on the tenth and the twenty-fifth.

- Days: the 10th and the 25th of the lunar month (and the retreat's first and last day).
- Practice: the feast offering, after the torma offerings of the session.
- For whom: the practitioner in a recitation retreat of this cycle (a holder of its empowerment), lay or ordained.
- Benefit or fault: none named; the wording is of obligation («ངེས་པར་བྱ་དགོས»).
- Tradition: Nyingma (the manual is Zhe chen rgyal tshab's; whose treasure the cycle is, the page read does not say).
