# The Śramaṇā rite of the be'u bum is also in Tāranātha's sādhana collection and is said to come from Śākyaśrī; no one names the be'u bum's lama

RD393 (2026-11-11). The Śramaṇā rite of the be'u bum is one version of a rite that other collections carry, and two of them say where it came from, neither naming the be'u bum's lama.
- Tāranātha's Śramaṇā section (ttn:p137-138, the dpe bsdur ma vol. 30, in the collection of sādhanas Rin chen 'byung gnas) has the same rite in the same order: a clean place, the vase on a four-sided platform with the wish-fulfilling tree, the blue goddess Śramaṇā on Mount Meru holding a mirror or a «རྔ་ཡབ» (rnga yab) and a branch of the tree, flowers offered into the vase, the mantra «oṃ śramaṇa piśaci (ni)muṇḍe svāhā», the sessions' tormas, the lying down, and the same remedy for a rite that does not come (defilement, grib, great: wash, confess, keep the boundaries, practise again), with seven days of preliminary where the be'u bum has 120,000 recitations and three to seven nights. It stands under the heading of a verse that calls it a dream-prophecy instruction "from the hero-alone practice" and closes as «ལྷ་མོ་ཤྲ་མ་ཎའི་སྒྲུབ་ཐབས་ཀྱི་ལེ་ཚན» ("the section of the sādhana of the goddess Śramaṇā"); the lion-faced dakini's sādhana follows it at [p139], the same pairing as the be'u bum's, where the lion-faced dakini's rite is the fourth section after the dream set. Which text is the older, or whether they share a source, is not shown.
- The Lcang lung manual of recitations (lcg:p26) lists the Śramaṇā mantra as that of the goddess "transmitted from the Paṇ chen Śākyaśrī" (པཎ་ཆེན་ཤཱཀྱ་ཤྲཱི་ནས་བརྒྱུད་བའི་ལྷ་མོ་ཤྲ་མ་ཎ), and puts the Sa lugs lion-faced dakini two places after her.
- Mipham's list (mph.128:i189-192) gives a Śramaṇā dream divination as "from the Realization of the Good Jewel" (Nor bu bzang po'i rtogs pa).
Taken together: the Śramaṇā dream rite was handed on in the Sakya and Jonang circles as a transmission from Śākyaśrī, with the lion-faced dakini beside it; the be'u bum's "lama" is a transmitter of this kind and is not named. These are three witnesses of the rite's spread, not an identification of the be'u bum's lama.
