# The thirty starting points' pairs of years

Each of the thirty starting points (སྐོར་འགོ, skor 'go) begins from a pair of year animals whose element stands in one relation to the element of the one reckoned (WB vol. 1, p. 453). "Own" is the same element; son, friend, enemy and mother are the other four of WB's five relations of the elements.

The pair's two years share the element, a male and a female year of it. That the commentary's thirty readings (pp. 454–471) take the pairs in this order, its first fifteen the Chinese and its next fifteen the Tibetan, is not said in so many words; the notes support it for the first: «རྒྱལ་པོ་སོགས་ཀྱི་རྩིས་མགོ་རྣམས་… བྱི་གླང་སོགས་ནས་བསྐོར་བ», the starting points of the king and the others are turned from the rat and ox and the rest (vol. 2, p. 532), and the king's is the first reading. Which element of the person is meant ("the element of the one reckoned"; the notes, vol. 2, p. 532: of the "year-horse", ལོ་རྟ, lo rta) is question 259.
