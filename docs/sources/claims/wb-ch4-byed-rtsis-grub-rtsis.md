# WB ch. 4: the accomplishing and the making reckoning, the five limbs

WB's chapter 4 sets out two reckonings of the new moon and of the planets: the accomplishing (གྲུབ་རྩིས, grub rtsis), which makes the month by the five places and the dhruvas of planet and sun (pp. 29–30, the Phug pa way of the *Zhal lung* with the constants as printed), and the making (བྱེད་རྩིས, byed rtsis), the reckoning that follows the Abridged Tantra (pp. 31–32). Both end in the five limbs of the day, named on p. 31: the planet or weekday (གཟའ), the date (ཚེས), the mansion (སྐར), the yoga (སྦྱོར) and the karaṇa (བྱེད་པ), "held as the five limbs". WB says of the making reckoning only that its fault, found by the *Zhal lung*, grows step by step from the first of the rab byung, and that the Zhal lung itself says there is no great need beyond doing it as customary (p. 32: «དཀྱུས་བཞིན་བྱ»). The chapter closes (pp. 32–33) with a note on the pure months that are not set in their places, the one general rule for the dhruvas of planet and sun, whole and fraction, that Stong 'jug's reckoners failed to make, and how the remainders, divided by the number of months, make the months come out pure (p. 33).
