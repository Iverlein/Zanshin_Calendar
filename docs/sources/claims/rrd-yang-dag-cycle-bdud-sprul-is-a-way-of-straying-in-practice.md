# A Yang dag treasure cycle lists the emanation of Māra among the ways of straying in the practice of wind and inner heat, cured by letting it free itself (RD397)

A Yang dag treasure cycle in the Rin chen gter mdzod (vol. 27) lists the emanation of Māra (bdud kyi sprul pa) among the very many ways of straying (gol sa) in the practice of wind and inner heat, with desire and grasping, the god realms above and below, and no perception; the visions of clarity taken for clairvoyance let Māra enter. The cure is the same for all: know everything as mind, and let whatever appears, straying or Māra, free itself in its own place, since it is the play of one's own awareness. This is a reading of Māra's emanation as one of the practitioner's own experiences in meditation, not as a dream; it does not mention the night or sleep.
