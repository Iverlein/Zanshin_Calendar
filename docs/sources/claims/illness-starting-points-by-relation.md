# The illness plank-tree's starting points take a relation of the one reckoned's element, not the element of the aspect examined

The starting points of the illness plank-tree take their element from a relation of the element of the one reckoned (WB vol. 1, pp. 417–419, with Lha mo tshul khrims's list, lt:p43b, as the key):

- 2 the king's seat: the tiger of the enemy element;
- 3 the felt hat under the dust (the father and forebears): the mother's tiger;
- 4 the ministers: the tiger of the element "that agrees in power" (WB); LT says the son's tiger; the Moonbeams' fixed sign for it is the wood tiger, the reckoned's own element, if the person is of wood;
- 5 the wolf's teeth (the spouse): the friend's tiger;
- 6 the demon's thief: the enemy's hare, counted up; 7 the karmic ogre: the friend's hare, counted down; 8 the sapling of the wish-granting tree (the children's life): the hare of the same element; 9 the treasury: the mother's or the son's hare;
- 12 the wind-storm: the son's tiger; 13 the star-house: the mother's monkey, counted up;
- 1, 10, 11, 14, 15 and 16 do not take an element: they start from the sky year's earth, its thirteenth above or below, its fifth above or below and its sixth below.

The Moonbeams' fixed signs (dz:i052-055: iron tiger, water tiger, wood tiger, earth tiger, iron, earth, wood and fire hares, fire tiger, water monkey) are these relations for a person of wood: iron overcomes wood (the enemy), water gives rise to it (the mother), wood is its own, earth is the friend, fire is the son. So the element is a relation of the one reckoned, not the element of the thing examined: the enemy's element begins both the king's seat and the demon's thief, the friend's both the spouse and the karmic ogre, the mother's both the forebears and the treasury. No text says that the element is chosen by the aspect examined, and the fourth point's relation differs between WB (the agreeing, that is the own) and LT (the son).
