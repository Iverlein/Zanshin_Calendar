# The son text and the hidden text as classes of the tradition's texts

WB's household round of the Chinese reckoning (question 397: LT says that with six pebbles a member "the son text" lays a trigram pebble) leaves the son text unnamed. The regent's replies name it as one of a class. Reply 136 (ys:i492-493 and the page after it, machine) says that some of these instructions "must be known from the hidden text (ལྐོག་ཡིག, lkog yig), the son text (བུ་ཡིག, bu yig) and the oral transmission", and reply 145 (img. 501, machine) speaks of "texts such as the hidden text or the accompanying son text" (ལྐོག་ཡིག་གམ་ལྷན་བྱ་བུ་ཡིག་ལྟར་གྱུར་པའི་ཡིག་རྒྱུན), from which the cut-and-drilled instructions come. Question 119 (img. 95) calls the place where the child's luck is shown «མན་ངག་བུ་ཡིག», the instruction son text. So the son text is a kind of accompanying text of the tradition, beside a hidden text, not a single named work that the pool can point to.
