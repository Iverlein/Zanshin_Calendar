# The families of tables: Fierce three, Peaceful two, slow, pure, quick-pure

The chapter names four families of tables by the planets' groups: the three Fierce (*drag gsum*) with the foot-holders (*rkang 'dzin*), the two Peaceful (*zhi gnyis*), and the "slow" and "pure" (*dal*, *dag*) tables; each in an extended (*rgyas pa*) and an abridged (*bsdu ba*) form, and a "quick-pure" (*myur dag*) table which pp. 73 and 77 say makes no difference in the three days (*zhag gsum*) against the pure root working, though the author works out the difference. The tables are made from the enjoyment of the day and night (*nyin zhag*) or of the lunar day, by division, then multiplication of the *bya ra* (row-headings) by the feet (*rkang*), and the "general wealth" (*spyi nor*) tables by a count of wealth and debt (*nor*, *bu lon*) given in order to the houses and feet (pp. 71–77). The figures of these steps are in the passages; they are in the print mostly as numerals beside code words and were not all read reliably (pp. 76–77).
