# Reading a failing rain from the planets over the elements' mansions (WB ch. 12)

For a rain that fails (pp. 94–95) WB's text divides the twenty-eight mansions by the four elements (water, earth, wind, fire mansions) and reads the rain by where the planets stand in the summer rains. Gentle planets and the moon in the water mansions in the bright half: rain falls and comes easily; the sun, Mars and Saturn gathered in the water mansions: hail; gathered over earth mansions: little rain, scattered by a wind storm; over wind mansions: a great wind storm; over fire mansions: drought; Venus, Jupiter and the moon over the water mansions: heavy rain, over an earth mansion a good year; with Rāhu present, wind or hail and violent rains; a gentle planet over a water mansion brings rain as soon as a fierce one arrives. For each district, begin the count at the Tha skar mansion up to the district's name mansion, add the moon's mansion for the house in passing, subtract the day's planet count, divide by the wheel, and read the water, earth, wind or fire mansion of the remainder: in the east of the district the rain is heavy for that house (water), little (earth), wind (wind), drought (fire). The rule is described in words; no table of the mansions' elements is given on these pages.
