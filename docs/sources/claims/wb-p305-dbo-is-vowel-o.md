# WB p. 305: «དབོ» ending the fifth meeting is the vowel O, as p. 407 spells out

In the White Beryl's two statements of the five fatal meetings of the day's letter (p. 305 and, repeated in the bride's reckoning, p. 407), the last meeting, the full date with Saturn at Bharaṇī, ends «བྲ་ཉེ་དབོ», and «དབོ» there is the vowel O, not a second mansion. The second statement closes the five with the words «འཛོམ་ན་ཁྱོ་ཤུག་མིང་ཡིག་ཐོག། རིམ་བཞིན་ཨ་ཨི་ཨུ་ཨེ་ཨོ» (when they meet, the sign «ཁྱོ་ཤུག», the vowel sign o, begins the name-letter; in order A, I, U, E, O), which puts O fifth, in the slot where the other four meetings have A, I, U and E; and the 1996 print's own scan (I1KG12907 img. 415, 5x) has the syllable «དབོ» plain before it. The fifth meeting is worded the same in both places («སྤེན་པ་བྲ་ཉེ་དབོ»), so the spelling is WB's own and no slip of one typing; the fourth differs (Revatī on p. 407, Mon dre on p. 305); the same print writes the mansion Uttaraphalgunī «དབོ» in its lists of the mansions, so the vowel's slot carries the mansion's spelling. That Saturn meets two mansions at once, Bharaṇī and Uttaraphalgunī, is not what the line says: it has the same four parts as the four lines before it.
