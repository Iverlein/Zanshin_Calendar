# The Narthang manuscript reads «ཐ་མར་རྨི་ལམ་བཏབ་ལ་ཉལ», not «བརྟགས»

On the Narthang manuscript (I2KG212330 img. 289, end of line 5 and start of line 6, 7×) the sign group after «རྨི་ལམ» is three letters, བ, ཏ, བ, with no ར, ག or ས: «བཏབ». Count: «བརྟགས་ལ» in the Derge etext, the Dpe bsdur ma text and (machine) the Co ne; «བརྟགས་པ་ལ» in the Peking print by eye (RD234); «བཏབ་ལ» in the Narthang manuscript by eye. The Dpe bsdur ma note «པེ» «སྣར» དབཏབ names Peking and Narthang together, which the eye confirms only for the Narthang. So the Peking's extra པ (q836) is not explained by the Narthang, which has a different word; which is the text stays open.
