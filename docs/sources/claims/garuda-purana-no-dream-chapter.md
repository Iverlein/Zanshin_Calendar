# The Garuḍapurāṇa has no chapter on dreams (RD265)

The *Garuḍapurāṇa* has no chapter on dreams and no verse on the time of a dream's fruit (gpu:p131). The English text of Dutt (1908) was searched whole for *dream*, and the Sanskrit and Hindi of the *Saroddhāra* and Sriram Sharma's editions for *svapna*, *prathame yāme* and *daśāhena*. The dream is named in the daily duties (bad dreams destroyed by the morning bath), in the signs of possession by Vināyaka, in the medicine chapters (the dreams of the three humours and of the sick), in the teaching on the three states of consciousness, and in the saying that the Lord's name uttered even in a dream destroys sin. RD265's row named the *Garuḍapurāṇa* among the Purāṇas with a dream chapter; of the three it has none, and the *Agni* and the *Matsya* have the chapter read in agp:p280-281 and mtp:p453-455.

Not searched: the *Garuḍapurāṇa*'s *Ācārakāṇḍa* in other recensions, and the Preta-kalpa for dreams of the dead (the Hindi layers give no *svapna* there).
