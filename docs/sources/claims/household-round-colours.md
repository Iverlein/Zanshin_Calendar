# The household round: the colours a member brings

Which colours the household's pebbles can have, by source.

- **WB** (vol. 1, p. 385): each member's four deciding verdict pebbles (བཏང་གཅོད, btang gcod) of life force, body, power and luck, and a pebble of the mewa's element. The readings of the places (pp. 385–387) name white, black, blue, green, yellow and red: blue, green, yellow and red can only come from the mewa pebbles, white and black from both.
- **The notes** (vol. 2, p. 490): the four are the deciding pebbles of the member's year of age, gathered from its twenty-four; the shortcut of five plain meetings with the year is named and rejected (p. 491). The same page lays a speckled (ཁྲ་བོ, khra bo) pebble where a place's white and black are even, but the household round's own lines say nothing of a speckled pebble.
- **Zhechen Gyaltsab**, Mipham's pupil, gathers each member's twenty-four into four verdicts as the notes do, after laying a speckled verdict where a year-of-age place's white and black are even, and then names no speckled pebble in the round (zg:p285-286).
- **The manuals** lay no speckled pebble in the round: LT makes each of the four a single plain meeting, white for a mother's or friend's meeting and an inner meeting of earth or water, black for a son's or enemy's meeting and an inner meeting of wood, fire or iron, and lays the trigram's and the mewa's pebbles in their own colours (lt:p20-25); the *Moonbeams* lays them "white or black as in general" (dz:i048-049); the Bon *Mirror*, a witness only, gathers the four into verdict pebbles and adds the mewa's pebble in its colour, with no tie rule (sn:p70-71).
- **The regent** (*Removing the Tarnish*, question and reply 123, ys:i096, ys:i478-479) defends the round as Phugpa's, drawn from the *Clear Lamp*, and says nothing of its colours.

So no source read on 2026-10-25 lays or reads a speckled pebble in the household round; the only round that can produce one is the notes', whose year-of-age deciding pebble may tie. How a speckled pebble is read in a place remains open (open question 318).
