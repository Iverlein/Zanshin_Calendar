# Commentaries and other WB etexts keep WB's Tibetan thirteenth and fourteenth pairs, not the Moonbeams' order

For question 651. The Tibetan commentaries and manuals that quote WB's list of the thirty's pairs give the Tibetan thirteenth and fourteenth as WB does, the friend's horse and sheep and then the enemy's bird and monkey («བཅུ་གསུམ་གྲོགས་ཀྱི་རྟ་ལུག་གཉིས། བཅུ་བཞི་དགྲ་ཡི་བྱ་སྤྲེལ་གཉིས»): the etext of Lha mo tshul khrims's explanation of the element reckoning (MW1KG21818, 2003, VEIE0OPI9C5B1B35_I1KG21886, p. 74) and the one of a Dge ldan manual of the same kind (MW21984_22CC1A, VEIE0OPIE709B49A_I4949, p. 97) have the verse word for word, as have BDRC's etexts of WB itself (the 1996 print, I1KG12906, and the Gser thang reprint, I3CN5028). BDRC's full-text search for the other order, «བཅུ་གསུམ་དགྲ་ཡི་བྱ་སྤྲེལ», finds nothing (2026-11-14). So no print of WB and no commentary found keeps the *Moonbeams*' order; the exchange is found only in the *Moonbeams*' own line pair, in the five printings of claim thirty-dz-28-29-exchange.

What the *Moonbeams* itself shows: its other thirteen Tibetan pairs follow the order of the animals' rotation as WB's list does (horse-sheep, bird-monkey, dog-pig, rat-ox, tiger-hare, dragon-snake, and again), and only the thirteenth and fourteenth are out of it, with the relation glosses «དགྲ» and «གྲོགས» kept on their animals. This points to a slip in the *Moonbeams* early in its transmission or to its author's own change; no text read says which. Not settled.
