# KP's «བར» in box 35 is the date lord Bar-gnam, abbreviated as in KD's almanac

KP's «བར» (bar) in the bad half of its box 35 (kp:i41s; boxes 42 and 46 carry it too) is the abbreviation of the Bar-gnam Hal-dog (བར་གནམ་ཧལ་ཁྱི, bar gnam hal khyi), a date earth lord, because the Digest's almanac gives the abbreviation in so many words.

KD vol. 1, p. 495 (kd.1:p495), in the day's entry of the model almanac, lists the day's other earth lords and adds «ངམ་ཞེས་ངམ་ཤིང་། བར་ཞེས་པ་བར་གནམ། ཀ་ཞེས་པ་ཀ་ཁྱུང་། དྲ་ཞེས་པ་དྲ་ཆེན་» ("«ngam» means Ngams-shing, «bar» means Bar-gnam, «ka» means Ka-khyung, «dra» means the Great Net"), and says that these are written as the Vaiḍūrya dkar po sets them. KP's box 42 writes «བར་གནམ» in full at the head of its run (kp-boxes-37-55-earth-lords-by-box). So «བར་འཕུང་བྱེད» in box 35 reads as the Bar-gnam and the destroyer (འཕུང་བྱེད, 'phung byed) as two earth lords, not one. The other half of q1248 stays: WB's work 33 (vol. 2, p. 412) has «ས་ཡི་ཕུང་བྱེད་རྒྱུ» and no Bar-gnam, and no text found says why KP adds it. BDRC's full-text search (2026-11-21) finds «བིཥྚི་བར་འཕུང་བྱེད» and «བར་འཕུང་བྱེད» 0 times, «བར་ཕུང་བྱེད» twice, both in a rite of the Rin chen gter mdzod and a protection against the 'gong po spirit («dpral bar phung byed e»), another word.
