# The thirty's 241: a commentary on the *Moonbeams* quotes WB's verse as it stands

Question 304, in part.

BDRC's full-text search finds WB's closing verse «དེ་ལྟར་རྡེལ་གྲངས་ཉིས་བརྒྱ་དང་། བཞི་བཅུ་རྩ་གཅིག་གཏན་འབེབས་ཡིན» followed by «ཞེས་གསུངས», "so he said", and the *Moonbeams*' lines on the base and the count, in two search-only texts: a text titled *'Byung rtsis man ngag zla ba'i 'od zer* in the collected works of Tshul khrims rgyal mtshan of Nalendra (MW1KG10375; whether his own commentary or an annotated copy is not told) and a collection of eastern Tibetan masters' teachings (MW1PD152297, Bod ljongs dpe rnying, 2012). Both keep 241 and add nothing on the one more pebble. The *Moonbeams* itself says 240 (kd.3:p498-500). The count is therefore settled in WB's prints alone as 241 and by every other text found as 240.
