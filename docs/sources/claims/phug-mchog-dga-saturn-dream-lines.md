# The Mchog dga' has two dream lines among the planet effects, both for Saturn, keen dreams in the eleventh place and bad dreams with Ketu

The Mchog dga' (མཆོག་དགའ, Mchog dga') on the Dbyangs 'char (དབྱངས་འཆར) gives, among long lists of what each planet brings in each place and each conjunction, only two lines with a dream: Saturn (སྤེན་པ, spen pa) in the eleventh place brings wealth of many kinds, keen dreams (རྨི་ལམ་རྣོ་བ, rmi lam rno ba) and the failure of enemies, and Saturn with Ketu brings bile and cold disease, strife with kin and friends, bad dreams and sorrow. Neither line names an hour, a lunar day or a season. Etext only.
