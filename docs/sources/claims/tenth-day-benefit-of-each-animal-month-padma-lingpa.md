# What keeping the tenth brings in each animal month, after Padma gling pa

Padma gling pa's Benefits of the Guru's Tenth Day (གུ་རུའི་ཚེས་བཅུའི་ཕན་ཡོན, gu ru'i tshes bcu'i phan yon; pgl.1:p835-837, read on the scan) gives one benefit for "keeping the tenth" (ཚེས་བཅུ་བྱས་པ, tshes bcu byas pa) in each animal month, in three groups of four:

| Month | Keeping the tenth in it brings |
| --- | --- |
| tiger | the spontaneously accomplished knowledge-holder |
| snake | the knowledge-holder with power over life |
| monkey | the knowledge-holder of the Great Seal |
| pig | the fully matured knowledge-holder |
| hare | the pacifying activity accomplished |
| horse | life and merit increased |
| bird | the three realms brought under power |
| mouse | enemies, obstructors and elemental spirits cut off |
| dragon | the misdeeds and obscurations of the body purified |
| sheep | those of speech purified |
| dog | those of mind purified |
| ox | the obscurations of the afflictions and of the knowable purified |

The first four are the four levels of knowledge-holder, the middle four the four activities, the last four the purification of body, speech, mind and the two obscurations. Zhe chen rgyal tshab's summary of the treasure texts of Mgon po rin chen and others gives the same benefit for every month, with worldly ones added (sickness and harmful spirits cleared, wealth, harvest and cattle, protection from the faults of year, month, day and time; zgp.13:p537-540, machine reading).

What is to be done is named only in general: on every tenth, "the king of times", white, virtuous deeds, "empowerment, instruction and the rest", by which life and merit become a hundred thousandfold; a torma (གཏོར་མ, gtor ma) is named in the closing verse of blessing. The text is addressed to "the lineage holders of Tibet's later generations"; it names no vow as a condition and no person excluded. The tenth of the monkey month of the monkey year is set above all as the date on which the Guru comes himself. It does not name the waxing or the waning half, the feast gathering by that word, the Seven-Line Prayer or a mantra to be counted.
