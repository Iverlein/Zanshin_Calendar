# Bad signs that are good for one practice, RRT (a practitioner's table)

RRT (etext) lists, for a practitioner of a deity, bad signs that are good for one practice only (naked, burning, vomiting, bleeding, bathing, eating one's own flesh, bowing to images and the lama, receiving empowerment: the supreme practice; no good weapon, a stūpa under the ground, sun and moon falling, many naked women: the Lord of Death; a red river crossed, a long precipice, sitting in blood: Hayagrīva; many grey-white ashes: Vajrakīla; a great red wind, a donkey, black darkness: the Mothers, with flower rain, dawn and the sun rising the reverse; a lion coming to eat one, a book read in the dark, hail: Vajrapāṇi; sleepiness: the hurled cake; a lone Ācārya, Mon people, monks: bad for the wisdom deity, good for the proud-spirit practice; and others). The images shared with the ordinary dreamer carry one RRT row each as a mixed row (bathing, burning, vomiting, bleeding, nakedness, eating one's own flesh); the rest are a practitioner's table and are not offered to the ordinary dreamer. RRT gives no time rule (claim rrt-no-time-rule).
