# The Derge and Jo nang catalogues and Bu ston: Vibhūticandra's own translation, no author; Padma rdo rje copies the colophon

The Derge Tengyur's catalogue volume (vol. 213, etext pp. 736 and 765), the Jo nang Tengyur catalogue (etext p. 64) and Bu ston's history (etext p. 409) give the Examination of Dreams as the eastern Indian paṇḍita Vibhūticandra's own translation (rang 'gyur) and name no author. The first two set it in the mother-tantra section directly after the Deceiving Death and before the Padma-net sādhana, with the Deceiving Death's note that its place there is the cataloguer's judgement, not the text's. Padma rdo rje's book prints the colophon sentence after the whole quotation (BDRC snippet), so his quotation names the same one man. The Derge catalogue's second «rmi lam brtag pa», a page later, is Advayavajra's own work. State: etext.
