# Palden Lhamo roams on the 9th, 11th, 24th and 26th: the verse's two halves and its note, in four prints of one text

In Karma chags med's retreat manual, Palden Lhamo (དཔལ་ལྡན་ལྷ་མོ, dpal ldan lha mo) "with her lord and servants" roams at the times of the ninth and the eleventh of both halves of the lunar month: the 9th, 11th, 24th and 26th (rcz:p286-288; rczp:i415).

- Days: the 9th, 11th, 24th, 26th. The verse line is «ཡར་མར་དགུ་དང་བཅུ་གཅིག་དུས», "waxing and waning, the ninth and the eleventh"; the note inside the line says «ཡར་གྱི་ཚེས་དགུ་དང་བཅུ་གཅིག་མར་གྱི་ཉེར་དྲུག་དང་ཉེར་བཞིའོ», the waxing ninth and eleventh, the waning twenty-sixth and twenty-fourth. This answers q1263: the note glosses the days as the four.
- What it names: that she roams (རྒྱུ, rgyu), as on the three nines all gods and demons roam; no offering, fast or vow is named for these days, and the verse names no authority for them. The next lines set a Protector's (མགོན་པོ, mgon po) offering on the 29th and Yama's kind roaming on the 28th (month-days-buddhas-and-deities).
- Witnesses: the Gser thang print (read on the scan, where the note comes after the verse's first word «ཡར» and so first looked like a gloss to a line on the waning half only), the Palampur print (read on the scan, img. 415), the Lhasa 2012 and Brag dkar etexts (the note in the same place). All are prints of one text; they are one witness for the day-list, not four.
- Tradition: Kamtsang Kagyu author of the seventeenth century.
- Not found: another text that gives these four days to her. BDRC exact phrases «ཚེས་བཅུ་གཅིག་ལ་ལྷ་མོ», «ཚེས་ཉེར་བཞི་ལ་ལྷ་མོ», «ཚེས་ཉེར་བཞི་ལ་དཔལ་ལྡན», «ཚེས་ཉེར་དྲུག་ལ་དཔལ་ལྡན», «ཉེར་དྲུག་ལ་ལྷ་མོ», «ཚེས་དགུ་ལ་ལྷ་མོ», «ཉེར་བཞི་དང་ཉེར་དྲུག» 0 hits; «ཚེས་བཅུ་གཅིག་དཔལ་ལྡན་ལྷ་མོ» 0; «ཚེས་དགུ་དང་བཅུ་གཅིག» finds this verse in these prints and the *Moonbeams* on the demons' days (a different matter), «ཚེས་བཅུ་གཅིག་ལ་དཔལ་ལྡན» is Drepung (Dpal ldan 'bras spungs) in lives. Of the schools' own monthly rounds read, none puts a day on her alone.
