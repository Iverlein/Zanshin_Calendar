# Ch. 17's colophon: its source in Dpal-'phrin and the errors it names

Chapter 17 ends with a statement of its own source and its faults (wb.1:p197). Its analysis of the three days rests on the Phugpa master Dpal-'phrin's «mkhas pa'i yid 'phrog» (Mind-Ravisher of the Learned), and the author says that error is possible there too: he names the house-circle of Jupiter and the constant «མེ་ཚེས་ལག་པ» (fire-tithi-hand, 2,153 as p. 196 prints it) taken from the whole as places where a stream of mistakes has run until now and been sent out as pure. The remaining difficult points and figures of the three days are told elsewhere and are not spread here; the chapter was written, he says, with a slack hand, to keep off sleep (at the tail of an illness, «tshad nad», sense not settled).
