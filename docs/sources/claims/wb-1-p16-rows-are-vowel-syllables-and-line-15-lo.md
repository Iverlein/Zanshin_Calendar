# WB vol. 1 p. 16 lines 15 and 22–28: ལོ in «ལོ་གཅིག་སུམ་བརྒྱ་དྲུག་ཅུ», the rows are syllables with vowel signs, the last row ས ཧ ཥ ཤ ཀྵ

Line 15 (the doubt ལོ against ཤོ): in the 1996 etexts «ལོ་གཅིག» stands 35 times (21 in vol. 1, 14 in vol. 2) and «ཤོ་གཅིག» not once; the first syllable is «ལོ» (one year of 360 days). The rows of lines 22–28, which the earlier reading took for rows of five single consonants, are verse lines whose syllables are a consonant with a vowel sign, following the two vowel rows of the verse before them (a i ṛ u ḷ; a e ar o al): «ཀ་ཁི་གྲྀ་གྷུ་བློ» and «ཀ་ཁེ་གར་གྷོ་དལ»; the 1996 etext's «ཀླུ་སྒྲ་གླུ», «དྨ་ཀཱ», «ལྷ» in place of the stored «ཀྵ་གྷ་གྷ», «རླ་ཀྵ», «གྷ» are OCR of the same stacks. Both witnesses agree on the shape of line 28's row, «ས་[ཝ|ཧྥ]་ཥ་ཤ་[རྐ|རྐུ]་ལྔ་པོ»: five letters, the sibilant and ha series; the second is ཧ (the etext's ཧྥ is ཧ with a clot, the stored ཝ a worse guess) and the fifth is ཀྵ (its stack has the shape of རྐ in both), so the row is «ས་ཧ་ཥ་ཤ་ཀྵ». The order within the row is the print's and was not told letter by letter; the vowel signs of lines 25–27 stay as stored.
