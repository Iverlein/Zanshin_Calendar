# Dudjom Rinpoche's name from his father's dream before the birth; birth on the tenth day (KTD)

In the biography of Dudjom Rinpoche (images 36-37) his name Ye shes rdo rje is explained from a dream of his father, the elder khenpo-tulku, before the child's birth: a ḍākinī, a beautiful woman, said a son would come and gave the name, and a clear mirror with a golden vajra on it was put in his shawl; mirror as wisdom, vajra set upright, hence the name. Birth is given on the tenth day, with the day named a great day of Padmasambhava; the dream is tied to the time before birth and to the dreamer's own household, as is Rtsa gsum gling pa's conception dreams (RD363 tsl-life-conception-dreams-read-as-sorcery-and-as-sign), here read as a sign by the father and at once accepted.
