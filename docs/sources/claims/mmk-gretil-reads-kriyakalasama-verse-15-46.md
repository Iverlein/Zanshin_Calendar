# The GRETIL Sanskrit of the Mañjuśrī root tantra's verse 15.46 reads kriyākālasamaś; the Tibetan rim pa is krama (RD-o, q1136)

The Sanskrit of the Mañjuśrīmūlakalpa as keyed in GRETIL from Gaṇapati Śāstrī's edition (mmk.2:p149b, verse 15.46) reads «svapnākhyaṃ sattvavarjitam / kriyākālasamaś caiva nirdiṣṭas tattvadarśibhiḥ»: the word is «samaś» (sama), not «kramaś». The earlier text-layer reading «समश्» was therefore right in its letters, and the earlier guess that it was krama with misread letters is not borne out for this verse. The Tibetan «རིམ་པ» (rim pa) renders krama in the lexica, so the Tibetan translators read or rendered the word otherwise than the edition; whether their manuscript had krama, or the edition's sama is a corruption of it, cannot be told without the Sanskrit manuscripts. The word krama itself stands in the same edition (22, «eṣa kālakramo yoge»). Etext of a modern edition, not a scan.
