# The prints of the Lama Gongdu's dream signs differ on the three words of the goods of gossip

The five prints of the Lama Gongdü's dream signs read the third item's three words differently: the Palri Parkhang typeset print (clear scan) «རམ་རུག་སྤང་གཡེང», the Kazi print (read by eye 2026-11-09, fourth syllable ends in ར) «རམ་རུག་སྤང་གཡེར», the Shechen etext (two places; the scan, vol. 33 img. 310 line 6, hand script, agrees with the machine on «སྦང་གཡེར» with the fourth syllable ending in ར by eye, the second syllable «རུག» or «རྒྱུག» not settled) «རམ་རུག་སྦང་གཡེར», the Ngodrup and Sherab Drimay hand script «རི་ཏུག་སྦར་ཁྱེར» (eye and machine) and the Sbas-yul manuscript (img. 148 line 3, machine; the cursive not settled by eye) «རྟ་ཇུག་སྦང་ཕྱེར». The first two syllables «རམ་རུག» are the same in Palri, Kazi and Shechen; the last pair is not settled. In the same volume of the Shechen set a second place lists «ཞབས་རམ་རུག» and «སྦང་མ» among the goods of gossip, which agrees on «རམ་རུག» and reads the second word «སྦང»; both are goods of gossip (མི་ཁ, mi kha) in both places. The words stay unclear in sense; the item is still among the bad signs only.

RD254 (2026-11-09), the four syllables at 3–12× on the scans, the readers on line crops: Palri, Kazi and the Zhe chen hand agree on the first two syllables «རམ་རུག» (the Zhe chen's second head not settled between «རུག», «དྲུག» and «ཟུག», but «རྒྱུག» is not seen, so the earlier doubt about it falls); the third is «སྤང» in the typeset Palri and Kazi prints (Kazi's final letter ང by comparison with the ང of «དབང» on the same page) and «སྦང» in the Zhe chen hand, the Sbas-yul hand and the Shechen etext; the fourth is «གཡེང» in Palri and «གཡེར» in Kazi (ར by comparison), the Zhe chen hand (ར, both readers) and the Shechen etext. The Sbas-yul manuscript and the Ngodrup and Sherab Drimay print go together against the rest: second syllable «ཏུག», third «སྦང»/«སྦར», fourth «ཁྱེར»/«ཕྱེར», first «རྟ»/«རི» (not settled). The second place of the Shechen etext (vol. 37, etext p. 36) lists «ཞབས་རམ་རུག» and «སྦང་མ» as two goods of gossip filled into a speckled woollen bag to turn a debt-child back, so «རམ་རུག» and «སྦང» are there two words, with «སྦང་མ» in place of «སྦང་གཡེར». No print or etext names what the goods are; the page image of that second place was not found.
