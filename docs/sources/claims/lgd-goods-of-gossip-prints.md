# The prints of the Lama Gongdu's dream signs differ on the three words of the goods of gossip

The five prints of the Lama Gongdü's dream signs read the third item's three words differently: the Palri Parkhang typeset print (clear scan) «རམ་རུག་སྤང་གཡེང», the Kazi print (machine only) «རམ་རུག་སྤང་གཡེར», the Shechen etext (two places) «རམ་རུག་སྦང་གཡེར», the Ngodrup and Sherab Drimay hand script «རི་ཏུག་སྦར་ཁྱེར» (eye and machine) and the Sbas-yul manuscript (machine only) «རྟ་ཇུག་སྦང་ཕྱེར». The first two syllables «རམ་རུག» are the same in Palri, Kazi and Shechen; the last pair is not settled. In the same volume of the Shechen set a second place lists «ཞབས་རམ་རུག» and «སྦང་མ» among the goods of gossip, which agrees on «རམ་རུག» and reads the second word «སྦང»; both are goods of gossip (མི་ཁ, mi kha) in both places. The words stay unclear in sense; the item is still among the bad signs only.
