# A Cakrasaṃvara commentary fixes the dream to the night: after sleep begins, a virtuous dream is looked for at midnight and in the session of the fifth chu tshod, then the early-morning session (RD386)

The Cakrasaṃvara commentary «Dpal de kho na nyid mkhas pa» (Derge vol. VE23703_1333 pp. 507–705, maker 'Jigs med dga' byed by the verse of its colophon, translator Rin chen grub) lays out the day of the generation-stage practice in sessions, and fixes the dream to the night: after the evening, one goes to sleep with the six limbs of Vairocana placed; at midnight (མཚན་མོ་ཕྱེད) one meditates again "and is to look at a virtuous dream" (རྨི་ལམ་དགེ་བ་བལྟ་བར་བྱའོ); the same in the session of the fifth chu tshod (ཆུ་ཚོད་ལྔ་པ); then the early-morning session (སྔ་དྲོའི་ཐུན་མཚམས) with Vajravārāhī. This is the only rite found in this reading that names a part of the night, midnight and a later session, for the dream; the aim is a virtuous dream, with no list of images, no meaning and no remedy. What the fifth chu tshod is, and whether it divides the night into six, the passage does not say (open); it is not shown to agree with the watches of the Svapnohana or the eight watches of the Kālacakra commentaries (RD314).
