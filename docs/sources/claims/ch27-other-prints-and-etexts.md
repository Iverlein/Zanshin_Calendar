# Ch. 27's unread words on the Sakya, Zhol, rare-texts etexts: none changes a reading

Ch. 27 read on every print other than the Gser thang reprint that has an etext, and found by exact search in BDRC's full-text etexts (`bdrc_search.py`, 2026-10-28; the etexts of the 1996 print, the Gser thang print, the Sakya print, the Zhol print and a fifth copy in the collection of rare Tibetan medical texts (བོད་ལུགས་གསོ་རིག་རྩ་ཆེའི་དཔེ་རྙིང་ཀུན་བཏུས, bod lugs gso rig rtsa che'i dpe rnying kun btus; MW2PD17386), whose etext is search-only: its snippets show romanised lines only):

- **The la's wandering (q363).** «བླ་ལྷོར་འཁྱམས» (rat, south) with «བླ་ནི་བྱང་གི་ཕྱོགས་སུ་འཁྱམས» (snake, north) is found in the 1996 etext, the Gser thang etext and the Zhol etext, each with ལྷོར; the Sakya etext (vol. 2 img. 33, machine) has the snake and the horse verses «བྱང་གི་ཕྱོགས་སུ་འཁྱམས» and the pig «ལྷོ་ཡི་ཕྱོགས་སུ་འཁྱམས», the rat's «བླ…ར་འཁྱམས» garbled in the first syllable pair; the Taikhang-copy etext (MW4CZ74207, poor) has the horse verse north. No print has any pair other than rat south, snake north, horse north, pig south. The slip, if one, is not one print's.
- **The golden turtle's wood sheep (q364).** «ཤིང་གླང་མི་ལོ་ཤིང་ལུག་པ། །ལྷག་མ་བཅུ་པོ་སྲིན་པོའི་ལོ» (ten) is found in the 1996 etext, the Gser thang etext, the Sakya etext (img. 38, with «ཤིང་ལུག་པ» as the Sakya etext has it, པ not བ), and the Zhol etext («བཅུ་པོ»); exact search of «ཤིང་ལུག་པ» gives 13 hits; those in WB's prints are this verse, the rest are other works with the words in another sense.
- **The doubtful letters (q366).** p. 9, l. 6: the Sakya etext (img. 22, machine) and the Zhol etext (img. 638) read «མག་སྐུད»; «མག་རྒུད» is the Taikhang's alone, read so by Yigdzin-1, eye and agy (RD57); the Sakya etext has «ཆུ་མིག» with the Taikhang there, the Zhol etext «ཆུམལག» (garbled). p. 17, l. 26: the Sakya etext (img. 35) reads «འུར་ཞོགས», as the Gser thang reprint and the Zhol print; «བུར» is the Taikhang's alone. p. 9, l. 16 «ན་ཟླ་སྟོན་མོ»: found in the 1996, Gser thang, Zhol and rare-texts-collection etexts, no other text. The first part of p. 17, l. 9 «ལྕོང་མོའི»: no etext holds the phrase with «རྔམས» (zero hits for «ལྕོང་མོའི་རྔམས», «ལྕོང་མོའི་རྔམས་རྒྱ་མཚོ་དཀྲུགས»), and none with a ཛ stack; the etexts' OCR does not reach the line.
- **The monk's set (q365).** «བརྗེའི» is spelled so in ch. 27's heading (the 1996 etext, the Gser thang etext, p. 23), and the rare-texts-collection print's snippet reads «brje'i rtsis skor bzhi» in the same line; and the regent's own summary in ch. 30 (vol. 2, p. 155, the list of what each reckoning is asked, 1996 etext img. 163) repeats it in the order king, «བརྗེ» (golden vajra, crystal rosary, silver maṇḍala, "whether discipline has declined or not"), mantrin (vajra dagger, skull rosary), Bon priest. The word is therefore not one slip of one block; no print spells བཙུན there. The Zhol print's own hand-written image (img. 658, last line) is too small to tell «བརྗེའི» from «རྗེའི», which Yigdzin-1 reads there.
- **The meadow's verdicts and the five totals (q216, q225).** Zhechen Gyaltsab, zg:p300-301, as in the claim [zg-meadow-five-totals](zg-meadow-five-totals.md).
