# The works WB chs. 3 to 5 name and rest on

The works WB's chapters 3 to 5 name. Chapter 3 rests on the *Pad dkar zhal lung* (the Lotus-white Oral Tradition), the view of Nor bzang rgya mtsho and Phug pa Lhun grub rgya mtsho, and on the *Stod 'grel* (the Upper Commentary), which has the earth-sheep year Don grub; its colophon (p. 28) names the *Chos 'byung*, the *Deb ther* and the *Rgyal rabs* as the documents with which the chronicle is supplied. Chapter 4 sets its accomplishing reckoning against the *Zhal lung* and names four reckoners who tried to make a general rule from the *Stong 'jug* (the Empty Entering): Sbyar chen Blo bzang bsam 'phel, Rnam gling Pan chen, Chang khyim Ngag dbang bde legs and Grub gling Blo bzang dngos grub (p. 32, the colophon note). Chapter 5 sends the reader for the detail of Rāhu and the other seven to the *Dbyangs 'char* tantra itself (p. 35) and names the Kar myang reckoning as that of the learned of Nepal (p. 36).
