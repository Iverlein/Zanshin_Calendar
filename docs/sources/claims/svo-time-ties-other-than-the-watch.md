# The Svapnohana's ties of a dream to a time other than the watch

The *Svapnohana* ties a dream to a time in a few other ways, none of them a calendar unit, and every one of them is in the images, not in the dream's own hour:
- The end of sleep (གཉིད་ཀྱི་མཐའ་མ, gnyid kyi mtha' ma): a king, an elephant, an excellent horse, a traveller, oxen and cows seen at the end of sleep make one's kinsfolk many; a lamp, a banner, a chariot, a parasol, a girl, leaves, fruit and food seen at the end of sleep give the glory with nothing above (pp. 268–269). The moment just before waking is thus named as the position where these images count; the text does not say the watch then matters.
- Waking in the image (གཉིད་སད་ན, gnyid sad na): climbing a milky fruit tree alone and waking on it, wealth soon; a pair of shoes, a parasol and a sharp sword obtained, then waking, a journey on the road; a house, a seat, a bed, a mount, a body or a chariot burning at the waking, glory increases; climbing a cart with a donkey and a she-camel alone and waking on it, quick death; a female vulture, a house-bird or a krauñca obtained and then waking, a heartfelt thing "at that time" (pp. 269, 270, 271).
- A time of the following nights: a woman in red clothes embraced in the dream, "he has no next night" (མཚན་མོ་ཕྱི་མ་མེད, mtshan mo phyi ma med), the one dream of dying (p. 270).
- A span with no unit: a white snake seizing the right hand, "within ten" (བཅུ་ཡི་བར, bcu yi bar), property is sure (p. 270); the text does not say days.
- Speeds: "soon", "quickly" (མྱུར་དུ, myur du) for wealth, for death and for return from a boat journey (p. 269).
No time of the lunar month, the weekday, the season or the year is named anywhere; the sun's disc and the moon are seen as images only (healing of the sick), and the moon of the first lunar day in Advayavajra's *Svapna-īkṣā* is an image too (svi:p261). Vibhūticandra's one ordering of time is the watch (claim svo-watches-and-spans).
