# The Vaidurya volume sets the moment's li on the year's tiger, horse and dog: year or month

The regent's second witness for the moment's start, quoted from the first translation of the *Speckled Elements* (ཁམས་ཁྲ, khams khra), the *Vaiḍūrya* volume (བཻ་ཌཱུརྻའི་གླེགས་བུ), reads «སྟག་རྟ་ཁྱི་གསུམ་ལོ་ལ་སྤར་ཁ་ལི་ཡིན་པས་ཟླ་བ་ཡང་ལི། ཚེས་ཀྱང་གཅིག་ལ་ལི། དུས་ཚོད་ཀྱང་ནམ་ལངས་ལི་ཡིན།»: "for the three, tiger, horse and dog, the year's (ལོ་ལ, lo la) trigram is li, so the month too is li, the first date li, the hour of dawn li." Here the animal triad is set on the year, and the month, the first date and the dawn follow as firsts of the year, month and day in turn.

This is a doubt, not a conflict: the same triad of animals is said of the month's animal by Mipham's notes (wb.2:p535-537) and left without a noun in the *Speckled Sheet* clause the regent quotes beside it. Read as the year's, the *Vaiḍūrya* line would put li at the first month's first date only in tiger, horse and dog years; read as the month's, as the build does, every tiger, horse and dog month begins li whatever the year. The reply does not say which, and does not say how the trigram runs from the first date through the month's later dates. The word «ལོ་ལ» was read on the scan (a running hand; Yigdzin-1 reads the same).
