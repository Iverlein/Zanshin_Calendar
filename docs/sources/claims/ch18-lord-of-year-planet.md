# What the planet that is lord of the year brings: the short and the long list of ch. 18

The chapter's short list (p. 199, from the *Mirror of the Times' Teaching*) gives, for the planet that is lord of the year: the Sun, little rain, bad crops, disease among creatures and livestock, the planets and the moon dim, the medicines weak; the Moon, heavy rain, plants and grain flourishing, riding animals flourishing, the merchant class (the *rje'u*) comfortable; Mars (the Red-eyed one), robbers flourish, great drought, many diseases, kings quarrel; Mercury, heavy rain, a good year but kidney disease, the learned increase; Jupiter, heavy rain, no disease among people and livestock, the learned, kings and brahmins comfortable; the White one (the planet before Saturn in the verse's order), fruit trees and crops flourish, the king and his subjects increase; Saturn, kings and petty kings quarrel, no rain, all crops fail, famine, the thieves comfortable. The longer list (p. 200) adds, planet by planet: the Sun parches the rain-free country, makes the central country restless by war, and makes the ascetic rich; the Moon gives rain, medicinal essences and milk; Mars grows the strength of fire, impoverishes people, multiplies robbers, and divides regions into those with too much rain and those with drought; Mercury and Jupiter bring the dharma, the learned and kings' strength; Venus (*pa sangs*) brings health and the royal clan's victory; Saturn brings spirits and obstructors, loss of livestock, no rain and children who are dull. The two lists agree in kind and differ in length.
