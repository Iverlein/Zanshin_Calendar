# WB p. 328: Aśvinī's numeral in the unstable-mansions class is 0 on the Zhol and Taikhang prints; the 4 of the 1996 print and the reprint is a shared misprint

Aśvinī's interlinear numeral stands in the White Beryl's class list of the unstable mansions (printed p. 328, «ཐ་སྐར ཞེས་ནི་བྱ་བ་སྟེ»), after Puṣya's 7, Hasta's 12 and Revatī's 26. Four prints, looked at on the scans: the Zhol print (WBZ img. 1084) and the Taikhang reproduction (I5878 img. 466) both have a small open ring over «ཐ», the zero «༠», which is Aśvinī's number in the series that counts from 0 and is what every other list of the same chapter has (the 1996 etext reads «ཐ་སྐར(༠)» in its later lists, printed p. 330 and on). The 1996 print (I1KG12907 img. 336, 703 px, legible at 7-14x) and the Gser thang reprint have instead a solid hooked sign here, the same shape the 1996 print gives Mṛgaśiras's 4 («མགོ(༤)», img. 338), and the 1996 etext transcribes it «༤». So «༤» is a misprint that both modern typesettings share and the two Lhasa-family prints do not have; the number is 0. The ring on p. 330 of the 1996 print (img. 338, «ཐ་སྐར(༠)») shows that the 1996 print itself can print the ring, so the p. 328 sign is a slip of that line.
