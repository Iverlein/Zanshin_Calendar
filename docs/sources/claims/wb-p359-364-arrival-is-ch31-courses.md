# The lords after the Black Moon on WB pp. 359-364 are the earth lords of ch. 31's courses, in the same order; the rule that brings each to a day is there

For question 550. The run of fierce lords that follows the Black Moon at the end of p. 359 (wb.2:p360-361, wb.2:p362-363) is not an unsourced list: it repeats, name for name and in the same order, the earth lords that move by date of ch. 31 (WB vol. 2, pp. 226-235, wb.2:p226-235), which give each lord its course, the months and dates, the animal days and the directions on which it walks. The order on pp. 360-364 and the numbers of the courses of ch. 31: the Black Sun (the great black day, 1), Little Black (2), Pi ling 'phar ma (p. 227), Zin phung (14), Phung po zor thogs (15), Ki kang (16), the Hal dog (17), the Sky Dog (18), Sky-joining (19), the Planet-ogre (20), the Poor-hand (21), the seven planets (22), Ngams shing (23), the Bar-gnam dog (24), the Garuda-leg Ki kang (25), the Great Net (26), Rāhu (27). So «ཚེས་ལ་རྒྱུ་བའི་ས་བདག», "the earth lords that move by the date", of p. 226 is the reckoning that brings each of them to a day, and ch. 33's verses only add what each spoils and the remedy (the pages 359-364 give works, outcomes and remedies, as the question says, and no rule of arrival, because the rule is a chapter before).

Where ch. 33 differs: the Black Moon opens the run on p. 359 but has no course of its own among ch. 31's lords; ch. 31 closes with the black days (p. 235), which say «གོང་དུ་ཟླ་ནག་བཤད་པ་ལྟར། འདི་ནི་ཟླ་བ་ཉི་མ་སྤྲད», "as the Black Moon was told above, this one applies the month to the day": the Black Moon is the month's rule of vol. 1, p. 183 (the four-slayer of the animal that rises, with the animals three either side of it), and the black day follows the same rule with the date's animal against the month's (earth-lord-courses.md, "The black days"). The nine and five gods' meetings of p. 361 have their courses in the month entries (vol. 1, p. 135; vol. 2, pp. 211-213; month-entries.md), not in ch. 31. And ch. 33's closing note (wb.2:p377) says that the forms and courses of the date's earth lords not in ch. 31 are kept by custom and their source is slight, which is the grade of every such lord outside ch. 31's chapter (wb-date-earth-lords-source-grade).
