# The Svapnohana: the embraced woman by the colour of her clothes, and white and black things

The *Svapnohana* gives the women by colour as a table: a woman in white clothes with a white garland and unguent, embraced in a dream, all glory; in black, glory grows; in yellow, kingship; in red, "no next night". It also says every white thing is manifest joy, except a skull, cotton, ash and bone, and that brahmins, cattle, elephants and gods are the good of the other things while the other black things are very bad (p. 270).
