# The Blue Beryl's gloss of the five ways of entering shares eight clauses in one order with the 'Bri gung dka' mchan but is not a copy of it, and the etexts do not say which it used (q949)

Compared on the etexts (state etext; Blue Beryl VEIE0OPICF5AF372_I1PD412 pp. 212-213, Drikung dka' mchan VEIE0OPI7984ABB9_I1KG24731 p. 609, both fetched whole 2026-11-14; passage st:p352b). The Drikung works its glosses into the verse's own lines, the Blue Beryl writes them as sentences. Clause by clause, in the order both have:

1. First way (ku re kyal ka, the game): Drikung «the mark comes now and not then; careful in food and conduct» (རྟགས་རེས་འོང་རེས་མི་འོང་བ་དང་ཟས་སྤྱོད་ལ་གཟང་), Blue Beryl the same words (ཟས་དང་སྤྱོད་ལམ་གཟབ), and adds «the spirit rite is needed» (གདོན་ཆོག་བྱ་དགོས).
2. Second way: both «comes suddenly» (གློ་བུར་དུ་འོང) and «many confused dreams» (རྨི་ལམ་ཟ་ཟི་མང་); the Blue Beryl adds the trembling of mouth and eye and shaking (ཁ་ཡོ་མིག་སླེ… འགུལ་འཕྲིག་) and «spirit remedy», both end «gather merit» (ཚོགས་གསོག / ཚོགས་བསག).
3. Third way: both «the awareness sinks and dulls, bathing and incense» (ཤེས་པ་བྱིང་འཐིབ, ཁྲུས་བསང་); the Blue Beryl adds the release of the nāga's spell (བྱད་འགྲོལ་བྱ་དགོས).
4. Fourth way: the two are word for word apart from the Blue Beryl's closing «བྱ་བ་དང»: «since it has seized the vital point, recitation-practice and the medicine 'sman ltag' are given» (གནད་ལ་ཟིན་པ་ཡིན་པས་བསྙེན་སྒྲུབ་དང་སྨན་ལྟག་སྤྲོད).
5. Fifth way: both «if the sign to be avoided, spoken of above, shows» (གོང་དུ་སྤངས་སའི་རྟགས་ཤར / གོང་བཤད་ཀྱི་སྤང་རྟགས་ཤར); the remedy differs: Drikung «urge to virtue» (དགེ་བ་ལ་བསྐུལ), Blue Beryl «work at gathering merit and purifying obscurations» (ཚོགས་བསགས་སྒྲིབ་སྦྱོང་ལ་འབད་དགོས).

What the root verse itself holds is only the five names (st:p352, st:p352c: no dream, no remedy), so every clause above is the commentators'. That two commentaries give the same eight clauses in the same order is a dependence of one on the other or both on a third. What tells against a plain copy: the Blue Beryl has four clauses the Drikung lacks (the spirit rite, the trembling and the release, and the spirit remedy at the second way) and a different remedy at the end; the Drikung's «ཤིས་པ་བྱིང» (for ཤེས་པ) and its «དགེ་བ་ལ་བསྐུལ» are not in the Blue Beryl. The later commentaries follow the Blue Beryl's wording (Khro ru, 2000, almost whole; the Gso rig rgyud bzhi'i dka' 'grel of Gser khang bkra shis, 1998, in a shorter form: «བསྙེན་སྒྲུབ་དང་སྨན་ལྟག་སྤྲོད་པ། ལས་ཀྱི་རྣམ་སྨིན། སྤང་རྟགས་ཤར་བ་སྟེ། ཚོགས་བསགས་སྒྲིབ་སྦྱོང་ལ་འབད་དགོས་པ»; BDRC search 2026-11-14), so the remedy «ཚོགས་བསགས་སྒྲིབ་སྦྱོང» is the Blue Beryl's line of tradition. Skyem pa has the clauses in a different order and wording. Neither the Blue Beryl nor the Drikung names the other or a source at this place; the Blue Beryl's vol. 2 etext (I1PD412) has no mention of the Drikung dka' mchan anywhere (no «འབྲི་གུང་», no «དཀའ་མཆན»).

So Sde srid may have built on the 'Bri gung gloss (older, 1595-1659 by the pool's claim st-blue-beryl-khro-ru-one-gloss) and rewritten its remedy, or both may draw on an older gloss of the Four Tantras that the pool does not have; the etexts cannot decide, and the scans were not compared (the Blue Beryl's page images are not served). A text older than both with these clauses was searched for on BDRC («བསྙེན་སྒྲུབ་དང་སྨན་ལྟག་སྤྲོད», «ཚོགས་བསགས་སྒྲིབ་སྦྱོང་ལ་འབད་དགོས», «ཀུ་རེ་ཀྱལ་ཀའི་ཚུལ་དུ་ཞུགས་པ») and not found: the six hits of the first are the Drikung, the Blue Beryl (two copies) and three modern commentaries, Khro ru, Gser khang bkra shis and the Drang srong zhal lung, which follow the Blue Beryl's wording.
