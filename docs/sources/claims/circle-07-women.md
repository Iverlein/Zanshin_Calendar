# The circles: the seventh, the red bird, for noble ladies

The seventh of ch. 27's thirteen circles (དཔྱད་ལྡིང, dpyad lding), from the red bird, is read for noble ladies and officials' wives. Its starting point: the rat of the mother element of the one reckoned (by the notes, the element of his power, claim circles-count), counted upward by the years he has lived; the year reached has its life force, body, power, luck and mewa fought by his own five, one pebble each, white or black by the rule of colour (claim circles-colour-rule).

What the five ask, in the text's words as we render them: drawn upward from the rat of the mother element of the one reckoned: whether the white silk fringe has frayed from its edge; whether the turquoise hanging-cord is cut; whether the sun of glory has set; whether the spring of existence has dried; whether a son has slipped from the lap.

| Pebble | White | Black | Remedy for black |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Life force | the white silk's cord not loosened: long life, great benefit to the line, all one does fulfilled as wished: good. | loosened: the mother's sri (མས་སྲི, mas sri) has risen; an obstacle to life feared, bad for the line. | restore the line's luck, fasten the wheel that holds life, plant the life-dagger, strive at the rites that raise the life force. |
| Body | the la-turquoise's hanging-cord with its lake of great light not cut: sons, wealth and enjoyments together; power over five or six descendants; no illness: good. | cut: bad for son, husband, mother, mother's brothers and siblings; illness comes, work begun is not achieved. | fasten the wheel that raises the body, recite the *White Uṣṇīṣa* (གཙུག་ཏོར་དཀར་མོ, gtsug tor dkar mo) much. |
| Power | the sun of glory not set behind the hill: the paternal name flourishes, a lake of glory swirls, dominion and enjoyments grow: good. | the sun set: great rise and decline for the paternal name, husband and maternal uncles; little help from relatives; great loss of wealth in the maternal uncles' place. | press down the *god sri* and fasten the raising of the power, give to the poor, the turning back and the rite of raising glory. |
| Luck | the spring of existence not dried: great fame, kind talk, wishes fulfilled. | dried: epidemics; a bad line of sons-in-law and in-laws («ཚད་ཟླ་མག་སྐུད», not settled); the home moves, hostile talk, blame without fault. | fasten the luck-raising, build a fortune enclosure (གཡང་ར, g.yang ra), strive at the ransom, turning back and the raising of the luck. |
| Mewa | no son slipped from the lap: descendants and line grow; new members come soon. | a son slipped from the lap: no descendants, or hard to raise, bad for the eldest; grief to the household within three generations («དྲུང་གསུམ»); a fear that a son will be carried off by a spirit. | set up the dhāraṇī of existence and ransom the la, done by a powerful mantrin or Bon priest. |

No verdict is laid over a circle as a whole, in the text or in the notes (vol. 2, p. 533).
