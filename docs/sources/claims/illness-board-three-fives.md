# The illness board's three five-rows: man, woman, children

The fifteen starting points without the pebble door are three five-rows (ལྔ་ཁྲམ, lnga khram): the first the man's, the middle the woman's, the last the children's. Black in the top row is bad for the father, in the middle for the mother, in the bottom for the children; the reckonings of the golden bridge, the turquoise juniper, the wish-tree and the houses of glory and misfortune read standing or broken in each row and between man and woman or mother and child (WB pp. 423–424; the notes, p. 495).
