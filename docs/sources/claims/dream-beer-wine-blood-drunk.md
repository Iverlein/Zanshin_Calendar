# Beer, wine or blood drunk

Image group: wealth and food. Read by 5 texts: Four Tantras (for a sick dreamer), Svapnohana (SVO), Method under Sakya Paṇḍita's name (SKP), ACIP method (RST), Klong rdol's verses as the article quotes them (KBS).
Offered to any dreamer; the Four Tantras' or WB ch. 25's rows join when the dreamer is ill.
