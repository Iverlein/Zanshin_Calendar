# The Mindroling rule-book's year: the root lamas' remembrance in Hor month 2, the Guru's tenth with dances in month 5, Bdud 'dul rab brtan's passing offering at the descent from the gods

The rule-book of the Mindroling assembly (MDB) sets three days of remembrance and the Guru's day into its year:

- Second Hor month: the yearly offering in remembrance (དུས་དྲན་ལོ་མཆོད, dus dran lo mchod) of "the two root lamas", from the 19th (rite of the ground) through eight days of the Sugatas Gathered of the Great Compassionate One from the 22nd, the offering dance on the 25th, the fire offering on the 29th (mdb:p172).
- Fifth month: rehearsals from the 2nd, on the 10th the rite of the Bla ma gsang 'dus with full music and the full dance in the afternoon (mdb:p177). Mindrolling Monastery's calendar of 2026 has a three-day ceremony from 22 June, 5/8 to 5/10 by the app's reckoning, the same three days (mlc:p1); it also has a great tenth-day ceremony with dances on 28 March 2026, 2/10, which the rule-book's year does not name.
- Ninth month, at the descent from the gods: the passing offering (དགོངས་རྫོགས, dgongs rdzogs) of the Lord Bdud 'dul rab brtan, moved there from the Saga month, from the 15th to the 24th (mdb:p179).

The months are Hor months by number; the fifth is Snron, the monkey month (claim monkey-month-is-the-fifth-hor-month-snron). The rule-book names neither the two root lamas nor who Bdud 'dul rab brtan is.
