# The 国文研 print of the Ebara dream book has no printed slash in the table of the days a dream is not told, only a space before the last group

On image 9 of the Kokubunken print 54-254 (5616 px) the twelve month columns each end in a lower group after a space and no stroke stands between; the slash of eb9:i9 and of mdz:p11 is a mark of the transcription or of the one print, and a legend for it appears in no print reached (RD371).
