# The thirty: the fourth point, the turquoise dragon, for the reckoning of the Bon priests

The fourth of the thirty starting points (སྐོར་འགོ, skor 'go), from the mouth of the turquoise dragon (གཡུ་འབྲུག, g.yu 'brug), is read for the reckoning of the Bon priests (གཤེན་རྒྱལ་བོན, gshen rgyal bon). Its eight pebbles are laid as for every point (claims thirty-count and thirty-eight-fighters), each white or black, and read so:

| Pebble | Asks | White | Black | Remedy for black |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Whether the sky-iron horn (གནམ་ལྕགས་བྱ་རུ, gnam lcags bya ru) has been carried off by the btsan spirit (བཙན, btsan) | The btsan has not entered it; very good | Carried off by the btsan | Building the Bon god-castle (གསས་མཁར, gsas mkhar) makes it good |
| 2 | Whether the red god's arrow is lost from the hand | The arrow is not broken, good | The arrow is broken, bad; a great rise and decline (དར་གུད, dar gud) for the Bon | Set up the earth lords' *gung phur* (ས་བདག་གུང་ཕུར, sa bdag gung phur; not identified) and the like |
| 3 | Whether gods and spirits have changed places | They have not | They have, bad | The mending of gods and spirits (ལྷ་འདྲེ་ཡོ་བཅོས, lha 'dre yo bcos) |
| 4 | Whether the swastika (གཡུང་དྲུང, g.yung drung) row of the gods has faded | The swastika's stripe has not faded; the eight lines are not cut | The Bon swastika's stripe has faded and the eight lines are cut | A rite of restoring |
| 5 | Whether the *ja gshang* (ཇ་གཤང, ja gshang; not identified) has fallen from its stand | It has not fallen | It has fallen: the male god (ཕོ་ལྷ, pho lha) flees | Calling back what fled (འབྲོས་བསྙགས, 'bros bsnyags), fulfilment and restoring, the *glong rgyas* (གློང་རྒྱས, glong rgyas; not identified) |
| 6 | Whether the six smiles of the tiger and leopard have faded | The stripes have not faded; the Bon's eight lines grow | The six smiles have faded; bad for the Bon lineages | The mending (ཡོ་བཅོས, yo bcos) of the ground's *gnyan* (ས་གཞི་གཉན, sa gzhi gnyan) |
| 7 | Whether the vulture's feather is broken at its middle | Not broken, very good | Broken, bad | Offering to the gods (ལྷ་གསོལ, lha gsol), calling back prosperity (གཡང་ལོན, g.yang lon), the long-life rite (ཚེ་ཆོག, tshe chog) |
| 8 | Whether the *sngo thul* (སྔོ་ཐུལ, sngo thul; not identified) leaks from its edge | The Bon gods lead and one eats the prosperity food | It leaks from its edge: the Bon gods are angry and in the end the generations are cut | The rite of spreading the human line, building the god-castle, the *g.yo 'khrug* (གཡོ་འཁྲུག; not identified) |

Whose year is reckoned: the person's own (the notes, vol. 2, p. 533: «གཞན་རྣམས་སོ་སོའི་ལོ་རྟ»).

Which of the eight fighting pebbles is which check is not stated; the order of p. 453 is the likely one (claim thirty-board-groups; question 259).
