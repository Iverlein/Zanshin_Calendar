# The fourth-watch line of the Svapnohana is «བར་དུ» in every etext; «པར་དུ» and «པར་བྱ» are machine readings of the Co ne alone

On 2026-11-10 BDRC's full-text search found the Svapnohana's fourth-watch line only as «ཐུན་བཞི་ཟླ་བ་ཕྱེད་བར་དུ» (eight hits: the Derge etext twice, four Narthang etext pages, Bya mdo Klu byams rgyal, Padma rdo rje); «ཕྱེད་པར་དུ», «ཟླ་ཕྱེད་པར་དུ» and «པར་བྱ» gave no hit. «པར་བྱ» is therefore only the machine's reading of the Co ne frame, whose letters cannot be told apart by eye (the frame is a bitonal 2055 px image and BDRC serves no larger one). The Peking omits the line (RD327), so the Co ne's own ending is still unseen. State: etext search snippets; the Co ne by eye, nothing read.
