# WB ch. 29's colophon: the sources of the reckoning for the dead

The colophon of WB ch. 29 (vol. 2, pp. 142–143, read on the scan) names the
sources of the reckoning for the dead:

- kept as it is for the results: the copper vessel's *Thirteen Points of
  Summary* (བསྡུས་དོན་བཅུ་གསུམ་མ) of Khyung nag Shāk dar (printed
  «ཆོམས་ཁྱུང་ནག་ཤཱཀ་དར»), corrected from two pure lines of transmission;
- the seven volumes on the dead in the hand copy of 'Chi med bde ba, of the
  Smon 'gro paṇ chen's text tradition, unlike those of Ü;
- for what was missing, some ten works: the *zangs kyam* of Zhu ru, the
  *zangs kyam* of Padma, the *Clear Lamp* (གསལ་སྒྲོན) root and commentary
  "exemplified by the twenty", the fifth *Mirror* of the copper vessel, *The
  Rat's Course*, the *Grave Box of Existence*, the copper vessel's *Magical
  Sounding* and the *Black Pebbles*, the earlier and later reckonings for the
  dead in the manner of «སྤྱུག[?]་སྟོན», and Nyang stod Nyi ma 'od zer's work
  that Ldum po added to;
- other remedies than the *mdos* for the added rites: the funeral assembly's
  examination of the ground, the search for the grave, opening the grave's
  door, the grave's account, cutting the joined hands, the torma to the earth
  lords' directions; the mewa's birthplace from a single transmission.

It rejects: reading the dead's verdict (ཁ་དམར, kha dmar) on a single obstacle
pebble ("one measure, six pebbles", "twenty", "forty-seven"); results of
trigram, mewa, planets and mansions set down roughly; the *Great Reckoning
for the Dead* of Mi reng dmar po circulating in Tsang; and Mi pham dge legs's
treatise of pairs as mistaken. Ldum po Don grub dbang rgyal is praised as
peerless in the black reckoning of the stars. The earth lords' places were
dropped as told in other chapters.
