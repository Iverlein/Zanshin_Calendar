# Ch. 13 strip charts: each chart's nine-corpses cell gives the month's date and time of vol. 2 p. 216, 12 of 12

The fierce date of nine corpses (tshes gnyan ro dgu, no. 1 of the scattered earth lords, vol. 2 p. 216) goes one date at one time of day to each month of the year: spring 1 the 8th in the evening, 2 the 18th at midnight, 3 the 28th at the morning's warmth, summer 1 the 5th at the morning's warmth, 2 the 15th in the evening, 3 the 25th in the late afternoon, autumn 1 the 1st at dawn, 2 the 11th at the morning's warmth, 3 the 21st in the evening, winter 1 the 9th after midnight, 2 the 19th at midnight, 3 the 29th at dawn.

The strip charts of pp. 142-147 each carry one cell that opens with the same words, «རོ་དགུ» followed by a date and a time of day, in the 1996 etext's machine text (cells in the OCR's order, so the place of the cell is not known; the figures and the time words as the OCR gives them): tiger «རོ་དགུ་བརྒྱད་ལ་སྤྱོད» (8, the time word cut), hare «བཅོ་བརྒྱུད་ནམ་བྱེད» (18, midnight), dragon «ཉེར་བརྒྱད་ཉི་དྲོས» (28, the morning's warmth), snake «ལྔའི་ཉི་དྲོས» (5, the morning's warmth), horse «བཅོ་ལྔའི་སྲིད» (15, evening), sheep «ཉེར་ལྔའི་སྒྱུར་སྨད» (25, late afternoon), monkey «གཅིག་གི་ཐོ་རེངས» (1, dawn), bird «བཅུ་གཅིག་ཉི་དྲོས» (11, the morning's warmth), dog «ཉེར་གཅིག་སྤྱོད» (21, evening), pig «དགུའི་ནམ་ཕྱེད་ཡོལ» (9, after midnight), rat «བཅུ་དགུའི་ནམ་བྱེད» (19, midnight), ox «ཉེར་དགུའི་ཐོ་རེངས» (29, dawn). The date and the time agree with the verse in 12 of 12 charts, in the order tiger to ox, each against the verse's month of that animal.

What this gives: (1) the order of the 1996 print's twelve charts, tiger first and ox last, is the verse's order of the months (spring 1 to winter 3), a second proof beside the nāga planet's captions (wb1-ch13-strip-charts-are-the-twelve-months), and the charts' woodcuts name months; (2) the rule by which a cell is filled: a strip chart holds, for its month, the scattered lords of vol. 2 pp. 216-219 with that month's own date, time of day or place in the ring, and not a figure of the year; (3) the cells' words such as «ཉི་དྲོས», «ནམ་ཕྱེད», «ཐོ་རེངས» are the times of that verse and the black destroyer's heading row's. Not checked: that the cell's place in the ring is fixed by anything for this lord (the verse gives it a date and a time, not an animal).

Evidence level: the verse's lines are the 1996 etext, the chart words the 1996 etext's OCR; no word was settled on the scan here, but the twelve figures and times each agree with an independent verse, which an OCR slip would not do twelve times.
