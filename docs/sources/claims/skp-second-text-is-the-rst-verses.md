# SKP, second text: the verses of the ACIP method under Vairocana's name

The second text of the Mongolian bundle (SKP pp. 50–55, machine reading) is, line for line in the parts read, the verse of the ACIP method (RST pp. 3–6): the same refusal to examine when ill, the same three thirds of the night, the same dawn third as the only one examined, the same images and the same figures of the spirits. They differ in who stands behind them: RST says it follows the Chöd visualisation collection of Ma gcig; SKP says it is as the third (chapter) of the adept Vairocana, and ends with «ལེགས་པར་བཤད་པ་པདྨ་དཀར». One verse text, two attributions; neither is known to be older. RST adds the meditator's images and the dreams of death, and the death signs, which SKP's second text does not carry.
