# The grave provisions, by the dead's trigram

The grave provisions of WB vol. 2, pp. 129–132, read on the scan, one block
per trigram, selected by the dead's trigram (Mipham's notes, p. 522: «གཤིན་སྤར་གང་ཡིན»).
Each block lays as the dead's share, so that the living's is not stolen:

The retinue in every block is a strong man whole in senses and limbs. The
direction of the stupa path is the trigram's own. The eleven entries are the
eleven identifying pebbles of the size (p. 51) and of the notes' list.
Which trigram of the dead is meant (the natal one or the trigram of the
year of death by age) is not stated in the passage; the notes' «གཤིན་སྤར»
leaves it to the reckoning's earlier use of the word.
