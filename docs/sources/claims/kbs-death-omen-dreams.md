# Klong rdol, dreams that foretell death

The article (pp. 77–78) quotes Klong rdol bla ma's dream signs of death: seeing a black woman walking without a belly; ash of a pyre, climbing a stupa; a camel's mouth turned south and a donkey's mouth east; a monkey turned north and ridden away; wearing red and gathering red flowers; walking alone on a plain, a skull-cup (ཐོད་ཅིང); drinking beer and dancing beside the dead (གཤིན, gshin). The verse closes: these are the examination of death-omen dreams. No time of fruit is given; this is part of the dawn dream's examination. Of the article's own remarks, the one fact of the text it adds is that the signs are drawn from daily life (a woman without a belly, starving) and from the character of animals, a reading of the author's.
