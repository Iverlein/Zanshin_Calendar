# A pregnant woman dreaming of a man

Image group: the dreamer ill (placed there by RD216; corrected by RD274). Read by 1 text: Four Tantras.
Offered only to a woman in the ninth month of pregnancy, as the sign of a boy; it is not a sign of illness and not for the sick (RD274, after the claim st-dream-pregnancy-boy: the dream is one sign among the rest, with lying on the right side, a light body and the right breast giving milk first, and no remedy).
The word that was unsettled, དག, is settled by three commentaries (RD229: Byang pa and Rin spungs pa, again and again; RD255: Khro ru, in numbers; a plural or frequency marker, none reads it "clear"); this image is no longer listed apart in dream-unsettled-words.
