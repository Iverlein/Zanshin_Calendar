# Ch. 34's closing verse: what is examined before a work's result is foretold, the earth lords among them

WB's own last word on foretelling a work's result (the closing verse of ch. 34, wb.2:p428b) lists what is to be examined: the date's results, the vowels and consonants, the planets' rise and decline and strength, the mansions, the concords, the yogas, the *tatkāla*, the trigram and mewa, the day signs, "the good and bad of the earth lords", and "above all" (ཁྱད་པར་དུ, khyad par du) the rise and decline of the person who begins the work and the faults that harm him; then the verdict is given. The list orders nothing but the person's own state, which it puts first.
