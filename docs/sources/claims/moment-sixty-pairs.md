# The ogre's, the demon's and the sixty-year counts cut by the moment

WB vol. 2, pp. 171–172: after the thirteen gates, further counts fought against the moment, each from a fixed point, male down and female up, the harm coming when the landed year cuts the moment inward. Read on the Gser thang reprint; the line Yigdzin-1 drops on p. 171 (the earth dragon and the earth sheep) is on the scan.

| Starting point | Harm |
| --- | --- |
| the moment's year, seven up (the ogre seeking a man) | found by the ogre |
| the moment, seven down (the demon recognising the man) | recognised by the demon |
| iron male dog | the demon's door opens |
| earth female sheep | the demon-man will not sell (?) |
| iron female bird | the crystal bird beats its wings |
| earth female pig | the grave pig «འཁྲུན» from the nine earth levels |

| fire female ox | the copper drum is beaten |
| water female snake | the blue water snake writhes |
| iron male horse | the wolf's teeth broken |
| water male rat | "the depth completed" |
| male: son tiger; female: mother monkey | male and female change |
| wood tiger (m), wood hare (f) | the end of men |
| fire horse (m), fire snake (f) | gods and demons rise |
| iron monkey (m), iron bird (f) | sudden misfortune |
| water rat (m), water pig (f) | faults in house, field and grave |
| earth dragon | defilement and quarrels from the maternal uncles |

Which year is counted from these fixed points is not stated here; by p. 172 and the notes (p. 537) the counted year is fought with the moment as the asker's stand-in. Iron male dog and earth female sheep each appear twice with different harms. No rite is named.
