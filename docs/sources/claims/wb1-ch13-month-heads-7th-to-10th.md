# The 7th to 10th month heads follow the fixed run; the twelve-link words 9 to 12

The heads of the 7th, 8th, 9th and 10th months keep the fixed run found in RD200: season and sign, Hor and Chinese month, the animal's great and small, the breath-taking and sgang, day and night lengths, a twelve-link word (the 7th grasping, len pa, 9; the 8th becoming, srid pa, 10; the 9th birth, skye ba, 11; the 10th old age and death, rga shi, 12, which continue the 'du byed 2 of the 12th and the rnam shes 3 of the 1st), the sun's arrival in the sign, the work (pacifying, increasing, powerful, drawing), the month's lord, the empty vase (7th east, 8th south, 9th west, 10th north), the dated entries, the five and nine gods' directions (7th east and west, 8th south, 9th west and east, 10th north) and the weekday line. The weekday lines as the machine reads them: the 8th dar 2 4 5, gud 0, skyod 1 3, zhud 6 (the same set as the 12th and 4th months); the 9th dar 3 1 0, gud 2 4, skyod 6, zhud 5; the 10th, with the last line seen at 3x, gud 6, skyod 0, zhud 2 4 and three stacked figures after dar not settled. The 8th and 9th complete the seven weekdays once each; the 7th and 10th are unsettled (the section's questions). Read mostly from the machine text; the 7th and 10th weekday lines were seen at 3x.
