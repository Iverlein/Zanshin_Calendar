# ལྷས་ཚོར་དུས་ཏེ and ལྡེར་བཟོ་འགུལ in ST ch. 81: what the other prints and the commentaries read

The two phrases of ch. 81's dream lines that the Serthang scan leaves unsettled, as the commentaries and the other prints read them (RD255, 2026-11-09; etext only, no scan opened).

**ལྷས་ཚོར་དུས་ཏེ (lhas tshor dus te).** No witness read in the etext has ལུས་ for ལྷས་: the Serthang print (`st:p350-351`), the Lhasa 1982 print (BDRC MW16778, a search snippet), the 'Bri gung *Dka' mchan* (`bdk:p580-581`), Sde srid's Supplement in both prints (`lth:p325`, `lhq:p509`), Khro ru (`ktr:p329-332`), the Bhutan print (`mnb:p267`) and the commentaries that follow Khro ru all keep ལྷས་, and BDRC's full-text search for «ལུས་ཚོར་དུས་ཏེ» found no hit. So the guess that ལྷས་ is a misprint for ལུས་ finds no support in these texts. The Supplement's 2005 print (`lth:p325`) and the Bhutan print (`mnb:p267`; OCR) read ངུས་ (ngus) for དུས་ (dus), a variant in two witnesses, against དུས in the Supplement's 1991 print (`lhq:p509`), the Serthang scan, the Lhasa 1982 print and every commentary; whether ངུས is the prints' own or the OCR's fault is open until the scans are read. No commentary says what ལྷས་ is. The 18th-century annotator of Sde srid's Supplement, Sman pa Blo bzang dbang rgyal (`mbl:p521-522`), has the gloss «ལྷས་ཚོར་བའི་དུས» (the time of the feeling) written into the line, and Khro ru's gloss is the same in nearly the same words. Khro ru takes the clause whole as «the time of the feeling» (ལྷས་ཚོར་བའི་དུས, with the nominal ཚོར་བ, "feeling"), the time in which the signs listed are the sign of a spirit striking, and says of the sister clause «རང་ཚོར་དུས་ཡིན་» that it is the time one feels oneself, so that one is sure to be struck by a spirit (`ktr:p329-332`, p. 332). The Drikung has the verse without a gloss.

**ལྡེར་བཟོ་འགུལ (lder bzo 'gul).** The spelling with བཟོ (bzo) is the Serthang scan's, the Lhasa 1982 print's (search snippet), the Drikung's (`bdk:p580-581`), Khro ru's (`ktr:p329-332`) and Sde srid's Supplement's in both prints (`lth:p325`, `lhq:p509`). The spelling with སོ (so) is the Bhutan print's (`mnb:p267`, OCR) and, as a machine reading, the Drathang print's (`st:p350-351`). Two spellings, then, and the weight of the witnesses read is on བཟོ. No commentary says what ལྡེར་ (lder) is: Khro ru glosses the three words together as «a temple shaking» (ལྷ་ཁང་ལྡེར་བཟོ་འགུལ་བ, with འགུལ་བ, "moves"), a sign of a male spirit (ཕོ་གདོན), and the Drikung puts the same gloss inside the line.
