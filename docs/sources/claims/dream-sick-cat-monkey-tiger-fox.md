# A cat, a monkey, a tiger or a fox seen by the sick

Image group: the dreamer ill. Read by 1 text: Four Tantras (for a sick dreamer).
Offered only when the dreamer is ill: every row is a reading for a sick dreamer (the Four Tantras above WB ch. 25).

| Text | Verdict (tone) | Passage | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Four Tantras (for a sick dreamer) | dreams of a cat (བྱི་ལ, byi la), a monkey, a tiger or a fox: a sign that the lord of death (འཆི་བདག, 'chi bdag) has seized the sick; dreamed constantly by the sick it means death, for one without illness it is not certain, and the rite of service (རིམ་གྲོ, rim gro) frees him (bad) | `gt:i42-43` | machine |

The commentaries add no illness to this line. The verse names the animals seen, not chasing; the Four Tantras' chasing by yaks, dzo and beasts of prey is a sign of a spirit in ch. 81 (dream-sick-naga-illness-dreams, dream-beasts-chasing). RST and KBS sort the cat and the monkey as figures of the rgyal po spirits and the fox of the 'gong po (dream-spirit-classes); that is a sort by spirit class, not a verdict, and the Four Tantras' verse does not use it. The tiger and the monkey of other texts: dream-beasts-chasing.

The verse is the Explanatory Tantra's (བཤད་རྒྱུད, bshad rgyud) ch. 7 on the Drathang print (RD268); the Serthang scan lacks the page. The rule on who dies, the cause and the six kinds of dream are in gt-dream-death-rule, the whole list in gt-dream-death-images, the one-syllable differences from the four commentaries in gt-dream-verse-variants. This image claim was added by RD274 (2026-11-09) so that the picker holds every image of the verse; the Drathang passage is a machine reading, the lines read by eye in part (gt:i42-43).
