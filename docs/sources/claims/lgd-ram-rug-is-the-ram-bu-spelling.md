# རམ་རུག in the goods of gossip: the slot of ram bu or ram pa, a spelling by ear

«རམ་རུག» (ram rug) of the Lama Gongdü's list of the goods of gossip (RD271, 2026-11-09; Steinert's collection, BDRC full-text search, etext windows, no scan opened): a spelling of the plant ram bu or ram pa that the rites' lists name; no dictionary has the spelling.

**The dictionaries.** རམ་རུག (ram rug) and its variants «རམ་རྒྱུག» have no headword in Steinert's 64 public dictionaries (the online Steinert dictionary, opened 2026-11-09, lists only ram bu and its compounds at this place). རམ (ram) is "or" (Rangjung Yeshe, Tshig mdzod chen mo), a clan, the fire seed-syllable; རུག (rug) is "to gather, to bend" (Ives Waldo, Dag tshig gsar bsgrigs: the look of many heads together). Neither makes sense with the other. རམ་བུ (ram bu): a many-leaved grass whose seed holds flour, a good fodder, its root usable as a medicine (Dag tshig gsar bsgrigs); a plant "polygonum viviparum" (Jim Valby); also "help" (a separate word); རམ་པ (ram pa, the Tshig mdzod, Jim Valby, Dan Martin): a grass like ran pa; a grass that may be used as tinder and a root that nomads gather; the Nyer mkho'i don gsal glossary calls ram pa another name of the dūrvā grass (དུར་ཝ, dur wa).

**In the lists.** BDRC's exact search puts the same slot of a list in three spellings: «རམ་རུག» (10 hits: Kongtrul's treasury, the Karma Chags med, Padmasambhava's life of Sog bzlog pa, the Lama Gongdü text on the way of the practice, Mgo log sprul sku, Yu khog rgya sprul), «རམ་བུ» (780 hits for the word) and «རམ་པ», «རམ་ཏུག», «རམ་བྲུག»: «ཚྭ་སྒོག་གཡེར་མ་རམ་པ» (salt, garlic, g.yer ma, ram pa), «ཚ་སྒོག་གཡེར་མ་རམ་བུ་ལ་སོགས་མི་ཁའི་རྫས» (hot garlic, g.yer ma, ram bu and the like, goods of gossip), «གཡེར་མ་རམ་བྲུག་དགུ» (nine g.yer ma, ram brug), «ཚྭ་སྒོག་གཡེར་མ་རམ༼ཏུག༽» (with the writer's own bracket giving the tug spelling), «ཞབས་རམ་རུག་དང་སྦང་མ» (the second text of the Lama Gongdü) and «ཚ་གཡེར་རམ་རུག་མཚུར»; in Karma Chags med's ransom, «འབྲུ་ནག་རམ་རུག་སྦྲང་མ». So «རམ་རུག» holds the place of «རམ་བུ» / «རམ་པ», with the ending a syllable written by ear (རུག, བྲུག, ཏུག).

**The sense.** The goods-of-gossip list's first item is the ram bu or ram pa plant (a grass or a root), by its spelling «ram rug» in this tradition; the identification of the plant (a dūrvā-like grass, a fodder grass) is the dictionaries'; it is not proved for the Lama Gongdü, where the word may be a lay spelling of the same item. The Sbas-yul manuscript's «རྟ་ཏུག» and the Ngodrup and Sherab Drimay print's «རི་ཏུག» (q841) are then the same item in an eastern hand: «རམ་ཏུག» (ram tug) with the first syllable mistaken for རྟ/རི by the machines, as the writer of the treasure's bracket «རམ(ཏུག)» writes it. The end «ཁྱེར»/«ཕྱེར»/«སྦར་ཁྱེར» is གཡེར (g.yer) written by ear (see lgd-sbang-g-yer-beer-mash-and-pepper). Rank: moderate for the slot, weak for the exact plant.
