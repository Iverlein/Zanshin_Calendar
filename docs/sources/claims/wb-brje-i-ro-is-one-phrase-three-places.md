# «བརྗེའི་རོ» on p. 54 is one phrase carved at three places of WB

The stack on p. 54, l. 27–28 (the yellow mewa's entry in the copper vessel: «སེར་པོ་མང་ན་བརྗེའི་རོ། །… ད་ལྟ་བརྗེ་མང་བར་འོང»), a ra over a letter that the reprint's blot does not tell as ཛ, ཇ or ད at 6×, is «བརྗེ» on the lexical side. Both BDRC etexts of the Gser thang reprint and of the 1996 print read «བརྗེ» at both places of p. 54 (reprint p. 60, 1996 p. 62), and the same phrase «བརྗེའི་རོ», "the corpse of the exchange", stands under yellow at two other places of the volume: «ལྔ་སེར་སྟེང་དུ་བརྗེའི་རོ» (the etexts' p. 103 and 105) and «ཉ་སྦལ་སེར་པོ བརྗེའི་རོ» (p. 201 and 203, with the bracketed 3). So the doubtful stack is one printed phrase repeated, and «བརྗེ» is what Steinert's dictionaries key (བརྗེ, brje, to exchange; no «བརྡེ» or «བརྫེ» headword, «བརྫེས» alone is a verb form of «རྫེ»). The mass on p. 54 does not exclude the other two letters; what is settled is the phrase, not the sense of "the corpse of the exchange", which the passage ties to a corpse left abroad.
