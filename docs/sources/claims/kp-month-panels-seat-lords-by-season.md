# KP's month panels: Ga-pa and Grum-bu stand on the side WB gives their season, south up, east left

KP's twelve month panels are set out by WB's seats: the dragon lord Ga-pa stands on the side of the panel that WB gives his season, and Thé-se Grum-bu where WB's counts put him, so the panel is the month's house with the lords' seats drawn in, south at the top and east at the left.

WB (vol. 2 ch. 31 nos. 1 and 2, pp. 199–200) seats Ga-pa in the house's east in spring, south in summer, west in autumn and north in winter, whatever the month of the season, and Thé-se Grum-bu by the month: the first month of each season at the cardinal point, the second at the corner animal (spring 2 at the dragon, summer 2 at the sheep, autumn 2 at the dog, winter 2 at the ox), the third at a place in the house (under the threshold, the hearth, the hand-mill, the house's edge). In KP's panels, in the machine's reading of the bands ([kp:i91-96b](../corpus/kp/kp.md#kp-i91-96b)), Ga-pa is found in the left column in the tiger, hare and dragon panels (spring), in the top row in the horse and sheep panels (summer), in the right column in the monkey, bird and dog panels (autumn) and in the bottom row in the pig panel (winter): east left, south top, west right, north bottom, the way a Tibetan chart is turned. Thé-se Grum-bu stands with him in the tiger and monkey panels (the first month of the season), in the top row in the hare panel (the dragon's corner is the south-east, the top left), at the bottom in the bird panel (the dog's corner is the north-west, the bottom right) and in the top row in the horse and bottom row in the pig panel; the dragon panel's left column has a phrase that reads like WB's «ཐེམ་འོག་གླང་བྲེས་བང» (the threshold's under; the words after «འོག» not settled in WB either). The time-of-day lines of WB's Wang-khyi (no. 49: the dog's time east in spring 3, the dragon's autumn 3 west, the sheep's winter 3 north) stand in the dragon, dog and ox panels.

So the panels follow WB and do not add a second system. What is not shown: the cells were located by the half-panel band of the machine's reading, not on the scan, so "top row" does not say left or right of it; Ga-pa was not found in the snake, rat and ox panels, Grum-bu not in the snake, sheep, dog, rat and ox panels, the sheep's and the dog's seat of the third month's house place was not looked for; and which cell is which animal's corner was not read. The next reader takes the cells at 3–4x with their compass sides, and checks the six gods of the yellow-black sky line (WB 23–28), whose seats WB gives by the month's animal pair, against the panels.
