# Ch. 27: how pp. 3, 9 and 17 name the element a start is counted from

How ch. 27 names the element that a starting point is counted from, on the three pages read line by line on both scans by RD90 (2026-10-28; Gser thang reprint I3CN5029 img. 9, 15, 23; the 1996 print I1KG12907 img. 11, 17, 25), each phrase clear on both:

| Page, line | Reckoning | Phrase as printed | Start |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| p. 3, l. 12–13 | first circle, the king | «བརྩི་བྱའི་ཁམས་ཀྱི་དགྲ་ཐོག་གི། །བྱ་ནས» | the bird on the enemy of the element of the one reckoned |
| p. 9, l. 11 | eighth circle, children | «བརྩི་བྱའི་དགྲ་སྟག་ནས» | the enemy tiger of the one reckoned (no word for element) |
| p. 17, l. 10 | a great spiritual friend | «བརྩི་བྱའི་ཁམས་གཅིག་བྱི་བ་ནས» | the rat of the same element |
| p. 17, l. 16 | ministers | «བརྩི་བྱའི་དབང་ཁམས་གྲོགས་སྟག་ནས» | the tiger of the friend of the power's element |
| p. 17, l. 21 | soldiers | «བརྩི་བྱའི་དབང་མའི་འབྲུག་ཐོག་ནས» | the dragon on the mother of the power |
| p. 17, l. 24 | weapons | «བརྩི་བྱའི་ཁམས་མཐུན་རྟ་ཐོག་ནས» | the horse of the agreeing element |

The four reckonings of p. 17 are one family, drawn "from the limbs of the secret reckoning" (ཡན་ལག་གབ་བརྩིས, yan lag gab brtsis) and counted the same way after the start (the eighteenth and tenth up and down against the four forces); two of them name the power (དབང, dbang, for དབང་ཐང, dbang thang) and two say only «ཁམས». Read as one family, «ཁམས» alone is the power's element, as the note of Mipham (མི་ཕམ, mi pham) on the circles says («བརྩི་བྱའི་ཁམས་སྟེ་དབང་ཐང་གི་དགྲ», wb.2:p533b) and the tenth circle's «དབང་གཅིག» (wb.2:p10-11) shows. That «ཁམས» in the circles of p. 3 and p. 9 means the same as in the reckonings of p. 17 is a reading of the text's wording, not a sentence of it; the scan settles only that the words are printed so. No page of the three names the life force's or the luck's element as a start.
