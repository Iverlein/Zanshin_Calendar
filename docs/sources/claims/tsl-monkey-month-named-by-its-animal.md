# The monkey month of the dream is the almanac's month named by its animal, the first of autumn by the Chinese count

In Rtsa gsum gling pa's life the dākinī's «སྤྲེལ་ཟླ» (sprel zla), "the monkey month", is a month named by its animal, not the year's animal and not the ninth month. WB's model almanac (wb.1:p163) heads the fifth Hor month (ཧོར་ཟླ་ལྔ་པ, hor zla lnga pa) with «ཧོར་ཟླ་ལྔ་པ་རྒྱ་རྩིས་སྟོན་ར། ཁམས་ལྟོས། སྤྲེལ་ཟླ་ཆེ་ཆུང་བལྟ།»: "Hor month five, by the Chinese reckoning the beginning of autumn (སྟོན་ར, ston ra); look at the elements; see the monkey month's great and small" (a Kālacakra mid-summer month, the sign Gemini). In the almanac the animal months run tiger (Hor 11), hare, dragon, snake, horse, sheep, monkey (Hor 5), bird, dog, pig, mouse, ox (tibetastromed.md, WB's months and seasons; the table of wb.1:p155-171). The colophons of vols. 28 and 30 write a month after its year as «ས་འབྲུག་ཟླ༨», «མེ་ཡོས་ཟླ་བ་༡༡» (year animal and the month's number) and «ས་ཕག་ལོར་སྟོན་རའི་ཚེས་བཅུའི་ཉིན» (i661: the tenth of the early autumn of the earth-pig year): a month named by its season («སྟོན་ར») stands in the colophons, one by its animal only in the dream. If the goddess-sister speaks in the almanac's terms, the monkey month is the first month of autumn by the Chinese reckoning, the "early autumn" of the colophon of i661.

Not settled: that the dream, told by the life's author in verse a generation after, speaks in WB's almanac count; the passage does not place the month in a year, and the Tibetan count of the Phugpa months (month 7 as first autumn month) would differ by two months from the almanac's («ཧོར་ཟླ» numbering and the Chinese reckoning, claim ch28-seasons-chinese-count). No source read gives the year of the dream.

Update 2026-11-14 (q1166): the Bod yig tshig gter rgya mtsho gives «སྤྲེལ་ཟླ» as the first autumn month, the seventh Tibetan month, which is WB's Hor 5 under the Tibetan numbering; the dream's age is not given by the life (the pool's old heading "at a year old" was wrong), and the early autumn tenth of tsl.30:i661 belongs to the life of Pad rgyal gling pa (printed pp. 565-750), not to Rtsa gsum gling pa's own.
