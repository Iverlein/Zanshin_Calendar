# The Amoghasiddhi bird line reads «བྱ་ཤར་ཏེ་རུ» on the Derge and the Co ne, «བྱ་ཤིང་ཏེའུ» on the Peking (read on the scan), «ཤང་ཏེའུ» on the Narthang by the note (RD309)

On the three prints the Amoghasiddhi sign reads, in the line "led to the work of peace; ... sends a messenger; burnt by fire in the forest": Derge (and the Co ne, by machine) «བྱ་ཤར་ཏེ་རུ»; Peking «བྱ་ཤིང་ཏེའུ», now located and read on the scan (img. 310 line 5, not img. 311); Narthang «ཤང་ཏེའུ» by the Dpe bsdur ma note only, the manuscript's own line still unsettled by eye (RD298). The Peking line is not a bird's variant of the Derge word by one letter: it has three syllables, བྱ, ཤིང, ཏེའུ, where the Derge has four, བྱ, ཤར, ཏེ, རུ, and ends the word in འུ (the sign of the diminutive and of bird names) where the Derge has རུ.
