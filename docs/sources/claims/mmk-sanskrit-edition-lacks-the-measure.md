# The Sanskrit edition has no counterpart of ch. 18's measure of time nor of its earthquake watches (RD378)

The Gaṇapati Śāstrī edition (part 1, 1920, in the Internet Archive's OCR) has no counterpart of the Derge's measure of time (se gol, rtsa rgyun, chu tshod, skad cig, yud tsam, the thirty yud tsam, the half-month, month, year and "great year" of twelve years) and none of the earthquake omens by watch: its chapters 18-21 (pp. 174-225), searched by pattern in the OCR, have the earthquake by asterism (about pp. 208-210), the eclipse in the 21st chapter from p. 219 directly after the omens chapter, and the divisions «yāma» and «caturbhāga» of the night only on pp. 175-176 and p. 199. The printed pages given in the row (179-181) hold the end of the 18th chapter and the opening of the 19th and have no unit of time. The two texts are therefore not the same recension in this part, or the OCR lost the lines; the question is the Sanskrit's, not the Tibetan's, and the edition's pages would have to be read on the scan (the leaves are in the Internet Archive's jp2 set, not opened here).
