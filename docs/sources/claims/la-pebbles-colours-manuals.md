# The la places' pebbles by number and colour in the manuals

The *Moonbeams* lays the la pebbles (བླ་རྡེལ) of the dead's reckoning either as the living's reckoning or with a fixed number and colour at each place (dz:i055-057): great cut-off three black, small cut-off two black, great mother year two large white, small mother year two small white, one's own year one white, son year half and half, enemy year black, friend year two white and one black; large and small pebbles at the great and small years. It mentions the root text's white at one's own year and black at its seventh, and says present practice does not lay them. The notes too refer to the root text's "three black for the great cut-off and so on" (wb.2:p499-500), and keep the white under each living person's year and the black on its seventh.

LT lays a similar but not identical set for the birth's special pebble (lt:p26-28): great cut-off three black, small two black, mother three white, power two white, son one white, friend half and half, enemy three black. The two manuals part at the son, friend and enemy places.
