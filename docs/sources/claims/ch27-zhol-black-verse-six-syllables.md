# The black verse of the «བརྗེ» set has six syllables on the Zhol print too

On the Zhol print (WBZ, I1KG1710 img. 659, line 1, a 7× crop of x 2560–2830) the black power verse of the «བརྗེ» set reads «ནག་ན་བརྗེ་གྲོང་སྤྲང་བྱེད»: the syllables ནག, ན, [stack with e], གྲོང, སྤྲང, བྱེད, six, as on the 1996 print and its reprint. The stack after ba has a flat head, an e above and the long curved tail of ཇ, with no ན below, so it is the «བརྗེ» of the other prints and not an «ནྡ» stack. The Zhol blocks therefore do not supply the seventh syllable; the short verse is the original's, not a slip of one setting. Second place read by eye on the same line, after the third run; the lost syllable is still not found.
