# No text read in RD292 bounds a watch of the night

No text read in RD292 bounds a watch (ཐུན). The Svapnohana's colophons and the colophon of the preceding work say nothing of the night; on the three prints the verse of the watches gives spans of fruit only (a year, eight months, three months, half a month; dawn, ten days), and the Peking, the Narthang manuscript and the Derge etext agree that the first line begins «དང་པོའི་ཐུན» and nothing follows to mark where a watch begins or ends. The earlier search (claims/night-watches.md) stands: WB counts four and four by day and night without bounds, no manual read fixes a watch by the clock, and the only gloss of the verse's dawn is «ཐོ་རངས» in the modern book of Bya mdo Klu byams rgyal. q798 stays open; the fallback (the night from sunset to sunrise in four parts) is not a reading of any text.
