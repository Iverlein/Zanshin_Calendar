# Householders pledge not to kill and to free lives in the month of miracles or on the eighth, fifteenth and new moon of every month

Thub bstan chos kyi grags pa's text on the benefits of freeing lives (tct:i351, read on the scan) gives householders (ཁྱིམ་པ, khyim pa), who cannot give up killing for long, a graded pledge: for as many years or months as they can; or for a whole month such as the month of miracles (ཆོ་འཕྲུལ་ཟླ་བ, cho 'phrul zla ba); or on its eighth and fifteenth; or on the eighth, the fifteenth and the new moon of every month («ཟླ་བ་བྱུང་ངོ་ཅོག་གི་ཚེས་བརྒྱད་དང་བཅོ་ལྔ་གནམ་སྟོང»). On each of these they pledge, in their own mind or before a lama, not to take life and to free lives (ཚེ་ཐར, tshe thar) and ransom life (སྲོག་བསླུ, srog bslu).

- Days: the month of miracles whole, or its 8th and 15th; the 8th, 15th and new moon of every month.
- Practice: not killing, and freeing animals bound for slaughter; the pledge may be silent, and is worth more made before a lama or a support.
- For whom: householders.
- Benefit: "beyond thought"; by motive, from long life and health here to the cause of awakening. An animal once freed is not worked, killed or sold again.
- Tradition: the author is the commentator on the Thirty-seven Practices printed in the same volume; BDRC lists the title among the works of D+hAr+ma kIr+ti Kun bzang bsod nams, "Thub bstan chos kyi grags pa or Mi nyag Kun bsod". His school and dates were not read in this session; he cites a long-life text written with the treasures' marks and a saying of the Precious One of Oḍḍiyāna (etext, img. 349 and 353).

Rank 3. This is the only passage found in this reading that ties not killing and the freeing of lives to the monthly days by name; Zhabs dkar's advice (zkt:p582) asks for no needless killing at all times.
