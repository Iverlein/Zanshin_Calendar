# WB vol. 1 p. 65: one mansion, or a difference of one, then subtract the fewer

# WB vol. 1 p. 65: the line is clear on the reprint and goes on with a subtraction

For question 666. On the Gser thang reprint of vol. 1 (I3CN5028 img. 72 = p. 65, 2046 px wide, the print sharp; read by eye 2026-11-07 and by Yigdzin-1 on a crop of lines 1–6) the lines that the machine text of wb.1:p64-65 gave in part read: «…གསུམ་གྱི་ཉི་མ་དག་པ་དང་། །སྒྲ་གཅན་གདོང་མཇུག་གང་ཉེ་གཉིས། །སྐར་མ་གཅིག་ལ་བབས་པ་འམ། །གཅིག་ཁྱད་འདུག་ཀྱང་ཉུང་གིས་མང་། །སྦྱངས་པའི་ལྷག་མ་བརྟག་གཞི་སྟེ། །གདོང་གིས་ཉི་སྦྱངས་སྐར་གནས་སྟོངས། །གོ་མ་ལོག་པའི་རི་[བོ]…» (the end of the third line, «ཀྱང», and the words after «སྟོངས་» are the machine's; the first four lines are clear to the eye, the rest of line 4 is read by Yigdzin-1 only).

The words after «ཉུང་གིས་མང» change how the line is read. They are «སྦྱངས་པའི་ལྷག་མ་བརྟག་གཞི་སྟེ», "the remainder of the subtracting is the ground of the test": «ཉུང་གིས་མང་[སྦྱངས་པ]» is an instruction to take the fewer from the more, and the remainder is what is then examined; the next line goes on to the head taking the sun away, the mansion's place being void. So the text says: the true sun (ཉི་མ་དག་པ) and the nearer of Rāhu's head and tail fall on one mansion, or, even with a difference of one, take the lesser from the greater and test the remainder. The part of the question that asks whether the two differ by one mansion at most is supported by «གཅིག་ཁྱད་འདུག་ཀྱང», "even if a difference of one is present", but the clause is a procedure on the sixtieths and not a bare "at most one": what is left after the subtraction is the test, and how large it may be is not in these lines. That the clause of p. 64 takes the sun of this test is the earlier reading by context, not shown by a sentence. The passage wb.1:p64-65 stays machine-read; the lines above are for it to be read again on the scan when it is next touched.
