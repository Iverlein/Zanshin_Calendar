# The Peking Svapnohana's Sanskrit title as BDRC records it, against the Derge colophon (q834)

BDRC's record of the Peking print's Svapnohana (MW1KG13126_2621) gives the Sanskrit title as «swap+na u hA nA» (སྭཔྣ་ཨུ་ཧཱ་ན), the Derge colophon «སྭབྣཱ་ཨུ་ཧཱ་ན». The two agree in the first «སྭ», the third «ཨུ», «ཧཱ» and «ན» and differ in the stack «པྣ»/«བྣཱ». The BDRC string is a cataloguer's transcription, not a reading of the print, so the Peking stack is not settled (q834 stays open).
