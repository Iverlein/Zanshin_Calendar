# The 9th hair date's result has no other witness than the haircut verses; the words judge nothing

Question 436 and old question 119: the 9th date's result «ན་ཆུང་མོད་འགྱུར» is written in the same words on every print and in every witness found: the 1996 etext (p. 412), SN (the Bon witness) and the Drigung haircut verse; no other manual read (the Zhol and second Zhol impression etexts, UT, LT, the Bhutan digest, SY's chapter) has the line at all. The dictionaries give «ན་ཆུང» as a young woman, a maiden, a teenager (Rangjung Yeshe, Ives Waldo; Mahāvyutpatti *yuvati*) and «མོད» as many, plentiful (*Tshig mdzod chen mo*, *Dag tshig gsar bsgrigs*): the words say that young women become many, and give no judgement of good or ill. WB's own verdict for the day, bad for washing the hair, is by reference to ch. 33's reading of the 9th (hair-dates.md, the claim wb-ch34-ninth-hair-date). No source found calls the result a warning or a gain; the question stays open as a question of the tradition, and a build may print the result in WB's words with the day's standing from ch. 33.
