# The thirty: the twenty-eighth point, the eight-spoked wheel, for small children, at the place of the body

The twenty-eighth of the thirty starting points (སྐོར་འགོ, skor 'go), from the mouth of the eight-spoked wheel (འཁོར་ལོ་རྩིབས་བརྒྱད, 'khor lo rtsibs brgyad), is read for small children, at the place of the body (ལུས, lus). Its eight pebbles are laid as for every point (claims thirty-count and thirty-eight-fighters), each white or black, and read so:

| Pebble | Asks | White | Black | Remedy for black |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Whether the body has entered the tomb | Not entered: no illness, happiness | Entered, bad: illness of body and suffering of mind | Changing the body and changing the name help |
| 2 | Whether the body's mountain has collapsed | Not collapsed: no illness | Illness comes | Doing the *yos ston* (ཡོས་སྟོན; not identified) for the child is the point |
| 3 | Whether the body's supporting god has parted | The body's mountain grows | Bad | Do the yos ston for the child |
| 4 | Whether the body is caught in the demon's net | Not caught | Bad | Entrust the child to the gods |
| 5 | Whether the demon's *zhu* (ཞུ, zhu; not identified) has fallen on the head | Not fallen: sons and grandsons increase | Bad | Recite the death's eye |
| 6 | Whether evil spirits have risen against the body | Not risen: no illness, one meets the gods | Black | Recite the long-life empowerment and the long-life dhāraṇī (ཚེ་དབང་ཚེ་གཟུངས, tshe dbang tshe gzungs) |
| 7 | Whether misfortune (བྱུར, byur) and lawsuits press | Not pressed | If a daughter died before, that bears it; if not, of the children half survive and half do not | — |
| 8 | Whether the demon's noose has struck | Not struck: children, wealth and livestock grow | Bad | Recite the dhāraṇī and ransom the la |

Whose year is reckoned: the person's own (the notes, vol. 2, p. 533: «གཞན་རྣམས་སོ་སོའི་ལོ་རྟ»).

Which of the eight fighting pebbles is which check is not stated; the order of p. 453 is the likely one (claim thirty-board-groups; question 259).
