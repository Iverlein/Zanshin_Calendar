# WB ch. 5: the twelve houses' numbers for the lord of the year's planet and stick

The lord of the making-year's planet and "stick" (དབྱུག) go to the twelve houses, as WB's chapter 5 prints them (p. 36, read from the machine text with the number words beside the figures): sheep, 11 and 57; ox, 44 and 52 («ཆུ ༤༤» and «མེ་དབང ༥༢»); twins, 11 and 17; crab, 4 and 53; lion, 1 and 22; girl, 4 and 24; balance, 6 and 51; scorpion, 1 and 47; bow, 3 and 16; sea-monster, 4 and 37; vase, 6 and 4; fish 0, 2, 52 and 1 mixed. Each house gets its "not error" added, ×60, and 7 is thrown away by the sage: the house that rises is the planet of the twelve houses. The page does not say what the pairs are; the passage is the one the roadmap calls the Lord's meeting (the rain sign "the meeting of the Lord of Power", p. 36).
