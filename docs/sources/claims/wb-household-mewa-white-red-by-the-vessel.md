# The household's white is the three whites, its red the seven red (p. 52 against the vessel)

The household's birth-mewa readings (p. 52) say «དཀར་པོ» and «དམར་པོ» where the numbered ones are «བཞི་ལྗང» and «ལྔ་སེར». The mewa's copper vessel (pp. 54–55) gives the same readings again and does number the red: «བདུན་དམར» (many on seven red: died by the blade, hidden between rock and water, the *btsan* spirits, the blade's mouth) and «དགུ་དམར» (nine red: the fortune has declined, calling the fortune). The red of p. 52 («མཚོན་གྲིར་ཤི་བ… བྲག་དང་ཆུ་འཐབ་བར་ལུས», the body and mind turned) is the same as the vessel's seven red, close in the blade and the rock-and-water, so the red of p. 52 is the seven red, and the nine red, the fortune's, has no row on p. 52. The white has no number in either place: the vessel says only «དཀར་པོ» (the gods displeased, king-spirit illness, madness) and so one reading serves the three whites, 1, 6 and 8. Nothing numbers them apart in WB or the notes.
