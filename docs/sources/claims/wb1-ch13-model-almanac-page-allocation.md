# Which almanac boxes stand on which page, pp. 165-171

The boxes run on across the page breaks without regard to the months, five to a row, and the heads interrupt them: p. 165 holds the end of a month (boxes 16-20), the head of the next and its boxes 1-10; p. 166 boxes 11-30 and the first lines of the following head; p. 167 the rest of that head and boxes 1-20; p. 168 boxes 21-30, a head and boxes 1-5; p. 169 boxes 6-30; p. 170 a head and boxes 1-15; p. 171 boxes 16-30 and a head. Each row starts at the class word joy (boxes 1, 6, 11, 16, 21, 26). The allocation for pp. 167-171 is from the 1996 print at low enlargement; the reprint (I3CN5028) has nothing between p. 166 and p. 175.
