# The vidyā-king of Chos kyi grags pa's dusk session is Nāgarakṣa; «dpa' bo rkyang sgrub» names the kind of practice, not a name (RD403, q1147)

In the long life (dds.49:p110-112) the first dusk session of the day after the pox lord's visit shows «རིག་པའི་རྒྱལ་པོ་དཔའ་བོ་རྐྱང་སྒྲུབ» (a vidyā-king, a hero's single-deity practice); the short account of the same night (dds.50:p6-8) shows «རིག་པའི་རྒྱལ་པོ་ནཱ་ག་རཀྵ ... པད་ལུགས་ཀུ་སཱ་ལི་རྐྱང་སྒྲུབ» (the vidyā-king Nāgarakṣa, with nine heads, sixteen arms and a serpent's tail, in the Padma manner a Kusali single-deity practice). Both accounts put the sight in the same session (the dusk session, སྲོད་ཐུན, srod thun, of the first of the month), follow it with the same prayer against the harm of earth spirits (ས་གདོན, sa gdon) and the same midnight dream of the nāga Gzi chen, and the short account then tells the Nāgarakṣa history at length, so the vidyā-king of the life is Nāgarakṣa. The words «དཔའ་བོ་རྐྱང་སྒྲུབ» are not a name: BDRC's full-text search (2026-11-13, 29 hits) returns them as a practice-type term in titles of the Bla ma dgongs 'dus treasure cycle («དཔའ་བོ་རྐྱང་སྒྲུབ་ཕྱི་ནང་གསང་གསུམ་གྱི་སྒྲུབ་པ་ནོར་བུའི་སྙིང་པོ», MW01CT0060_0E02DB, with the catalogue note «on the practice of the dpa' bo in his solitary form, outer, inner and secret»; MW23723_6A8B16 by Kong sprul) and in «Padma yang gsang khros pa'i dpa' bo rkyang sgrub kyi las byang» (MW1NLM2789_O1NLM2789_023), and the topic page T1GS118690 is entered as «dpa' bo rkyang sgrub». So the word is the kind of practice (a deity done alone, without retinue), as in the short account's «Kusali single-deity practice», and the life uses it where the short account names the deity. Catalogue entries only; none of the texts was read.
