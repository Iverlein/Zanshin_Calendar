# Zhe chen rgyal tshab's monthly round: the 29th's fulfilment, the guru on the 10th, yidam or dākinī on the 25th, offerings on the 15th, 30th and 8th

The life of Zhe chen rgyal tshab 'Gyur med pad+ma rnam rgyal (zgt.13:p441) records what he did every month in his later years: several rites for the dead whose names had been sent with offerings; the fulfilment offering of the twenty-ninth (ཉེར་དགུའི་བསྐང་གསོ, nyer dgu'i bskang gso); a guru sādhana on the tenth of the waxing half; a practice of the yidam or of the dākinī (ཡི་དམ་མཁའ་འགྲོ, yi dam mkha' 'gro), whichever fitted, on the tenth of the waning half; on the full moon, the new moon and the eighth, offerings as the occasion allowed, and in the evening the general torma of the oath-bound, the torma to the Dharma protectors, the giving of the body, the red burnt offering and the maṇi.

- Days and practices: 29th, fulfilment offering (the protectors are not named in the phrase itself); 10th, the guru; 25th, yidam or dākinī; 15th, 30th and 8th, offerings and the evening tormas to the protectors.
- For whom: one master's own round, a record, not a rule laid on others.
- Tradition: Nyingma (Zhe chen).

The pairing "the guru on the waxing tenth, the dākinī (or the yidam) on the waning tenth" is stated here in a Nyingma life read on the page image; no page read in this investigation calls the twenty-fifth "the day of the dākinīs" in a normative Nyingma text, and the passage sets the evening protector tormas on the full moon, new moon and eighth, the fulfilment on the twenty-ninth.
