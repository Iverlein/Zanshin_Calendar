# «ཁ་སྨྲང» is the account told; it is one word on pp. 50, 114, 166

«ཁ་སྨྲང» («དགོས་པའི་ཁ་སྨྲང་འདི་ཡིས་ཤོད», p. 50; the same shape on p. 114 and in the notes' p. 518) is, by the dictionaries read on 2026-10-18 (reckonings.md, *Open*, questions 67–68), the old word for speech, an account; «how large, small or middling the rite must be, this account tells». On p. 166 the same phrase stands in "name it" for the king-and-minister pebbles; read by the same reading, an account is told («ཁ་སྨྲང[?]» with the doubt of the stack), the place of the line in WB's verse being the closing of a list, where «ཤོད» tells it. p. 166's own page was not opened in this reading.
