# The history of the four sheets is anonymous in its words; BDRC credits Marpa, and the companion method names no author (RD374)

RD374. BDRC's records credit Marpa Chos kyi blo gros (1002/1100) as main author of «Lho brag pa's special instruction, the four sheets, the history with the texts and the companion methods» (the Marpa gsung 'bum part MW1KG14303_4AAEF5, WA0XL4AAEF5910733, creator P2636), and place the text in the Gdams ngag mdzod under Marpa's four sheets. Read in the etext, the history cannot be Marpa's words: it tells of Marpa in the third person («the lord Marpa Lho brag pa»), and a later holder speaks in the first person («བདག», 'I') of how he asked for the sheets and was given them alone, after the monk practitioner and others (dds.22:p1085-1114). The history is anonymous in its own words; no colophon naming its writer was found before the text's own end. The death-sign teaching comes after the root text of the Breakthrough and is given as its companion method (c: dds-midnight-dream-teaching-belongs-to-marpas-four-sheets): the author of the companion method is not named in any print read, and the attribution to Marpa is BDRC's, from the volume it stands in, not from the text.
