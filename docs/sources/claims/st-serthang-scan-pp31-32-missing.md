# The Serthang scan lacks printed pp. 31–32, the leaf of ch. 7's dream lines

The Serthang scan I3CN5038 does not hold printed pp. 31 and 32 of the Explanatory Tantra, the leaf on which ch. 7's dream verses and the lines on the dream by night lie. Printed folio numbers read at the foot of the images (RD267, 2026-11-09, strips of the foot at 2×): img. 42 is p. 29, img. 43 p. 30, img. 45 p. 33, img. 44 p. 34, img. 46 p. 34 again, img. 47 p. 35, img. 48 p. 36. So img. 44 and 45 stand in each other's places and p. 34 is there twice (img. 44 and 46, the two copies of the end of ch. 7), while pp. 31–32 are on no image near it.

The text agrees. Img. 43 ends in ch. 6 on the kinds of body («…ལུས་ཚོ་ཚེ་རིང་ནོར་མང་གཉིད་མཐུག་ཅིང་།», the last line, read on a 2× strip with Yigdzin-1) and img. 45 begins in ch. 7 at the signs of death by change of nature («རྨོངས་ཁྲོ་བ་སྔར་ལས་གོ་ལྡོག་ཅིང་། སྔར་སྤྱོད་སྐྱོན་ཡོན་རྣམ་པ་གཞན་འགྱུར་ན། མི་…», the first line); the Yu thog, Grwa thang and Peking etexts of the Explanatory Tantra have between the two points the rest of ch. 6, ch. 7's first section, the lucky and unlucky omens, the bad dreams, the rule of the dream by night and the good dreams, as much as two printed pages hold. The whole Serthang etext, which is the OCR of every image of the group, has none of these words (searched without regard to spaces for «མཚན་སྟོད», «བརྗེད་ངེས», «བརྗེད་ངས», «ཐོ་རངས་མཐོང», «ཕོ་ཉ་འོངས», «ཐ་མལ་རྣམ་འགྱུར», «རྒྱུན་དུ་རྨི»).

So the Serthang print has the lines, if its text is like the other editions', and the scan we have lacks the leaf; the line cannot be read on this scan, and a reading on it is not possible. Earlier readings of ch. 7 from this scan (st:p34) rest on img. 44, the true p. 34, and are not touched. The earlier rule of this scan, printed page = image − 13 up to img. 39 and − 12 from img. 59 (sources.yaml), holds for the images either side; between img. 43 and 47 the numbers move as above.
