# The seven kinds of dream examined in LGD

The examination of a dream has seven kinds in this order (LGD, p. 966): the sign (བརྡ, brda), the good (བཟང་པོ), the bad (ངན་པ), the mixed (སྤེལ་མ, spel ma, good then bad or bad then good), the bad that resembles good (བཟང་བཟང་འདྲ་ལ་ངན་པ), the good that resembles bad (ངན་ངན་འདྲ་ལ་བཟང་བ), and the not-declared (ལུང་མ་བསྟན, lung ma bstan), which the text leaves to be recognised since it is beyond thought. In the later paragraphs the text counts them as the first (the sign) to the seventh in the same order.
