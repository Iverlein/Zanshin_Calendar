# The sun or moon seen, their light touching the body, worn as ornaments

Image group: sky and light. Read by 4 texts: Svapnohana (SVO), Svapna-īkṣā (SVI), Method under Sakya Paṇḍita's name (SKP), Klong rdol's verses as the article quotes them (KBS).
Offered to any dreamer.
