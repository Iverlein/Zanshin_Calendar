# Women leading the dreamer away

Image group: the dreamer ill. Read by 1 text: Four Tantras (for a sick dreamer).
Offered only when the dreamer is ill: every row is a reading for a sick dreamer (the Four Tantras above WB ch. 25).
The mantra and བ་ར were read by RD227 (the mantra letter by letter, བ་ར as "cattle and goats"); the image is no longer listed apart in dream-unsettled-words.

| Four Tantras (for a sick dreamer) (for a sick dreamer) | a sign of a spirit (ch. 91): divination with its omen (མོ་ཕྱྭ, mo phywa, one compound), the rite of service and the dependent link, and a knotted thread (seven vajra knots, a mantra spoken a thousand times, bound on) are the remedies; the dream itself is in the verse; the thread and the milk of cattle and goats are bracketed notes of the commentary tradition worked into the Serthang text, not in the Drathang verse (bad) | `st:p493` | scan |

RD274 (2026-11-09) after RD255 and RD271: the commentaries (Khro ru, the Drikung) say how the dream marks the spirit: in it one is attached to women, kisses and embraces them, and seed is drawn out. Divination and its omen are the means of finding which spirit it is; the rite of service and the dependent link are done once it is known (st-dream-spirit-women, st-mo-phywa-divination-and-omen). The earlier reading of the pair as two remedies (ཕྱུ) is dropped: no dictionary or etext has ཕྱུ in this place. The mantra's gap in the Serthang print is not filled.
