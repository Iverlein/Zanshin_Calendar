# The thirty's «ban de sngags pha» and «lnga 'dra» on the Taikhang

On the Taikhang reproduction of the Lhasa blocks, the doubtful «བརྗེ་སྔགས་པ» of WB vol. 1, p. 455 reads «བནྡེ་སྔགས་ཕ་རྒྱུན», the line of monk (བནྡེ, ban de) and mantrin fathers, as the sense wanted (by eye, 1.4×); p. 457's «ཝ་འདྲ» reads «ལྔ་འདྲ». Yigdzin-1 on the same prints, not checked by eye, has the rest of question 305's words as the 1996 print but «ལུག་སྟར་ཐག» (p. 461, for «དྲ་སྟར») and «སྲིས་ཕྱེད» (p. 471, for «སྲིས་སྤྱད»).
