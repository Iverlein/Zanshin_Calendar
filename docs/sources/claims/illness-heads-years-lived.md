# The plank-tree's heads count the years lived from fixed signs

In the illness plank-tree of the *Moonbeams* (dz:i052-055) and LT (lt:p43-44) each head starts from a fixed sign, either a year of the sixty-year cycle (iron tiger, water tiger, wood tiger, earth male tiger, the iron, earth, wood and fire female hares, fire tiger, water male monkey) or a place fixed by the sky year (its 13th or 5th above or below, its sixth), and counts from it as many years as the one reckoned has lived (དུ་ལོན, du lon), down for most heads and up for the 11th, 13th, 15th and 16th; the landing year's four aspects are fought by the reckoned's four. The *Moonbeams* does not tell men and women apart here.

Inferred for WB's moment, not stated there: the sixty-year signs of WB pp. 171–172 (wb.2:p171-172) count the asker's years lived in the same way from each fixed year, as its thirteen gates do; the wolf's teeth broken, iron male horse in WB, are earth male tiger in the *Moonbeams*, so the fixed years are not shared between the two lists.
