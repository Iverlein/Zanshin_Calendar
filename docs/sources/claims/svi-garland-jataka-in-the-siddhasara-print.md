# The Siddhasāra volume's Svapnekṣā has the garland «ཛཱ་ཏ་ཀ» as the Derge; «ཛ་ཏ་པ» is in no etext

The Siddhasāra volume (BDRC MW1KG14614, Mi rigs dpe skrun khang 2008, etext I1KG15617) prints the Svapnekṣā in full (sds:p471-472), and its line on the garland reads «ཛཱ་ཏ་ཀ་ཡི་ཕྲེང་བ་བཅིང» (jātaka), as the Derge etext and the Derge-based Dpe bsdur ma text have it, and its bird line «བྱ་ཤར་ཏེ་རུ་ཕོ་ཉར་གཏོང» as the Derge. BDRC's exact search «ཛཱ་ཏ་ཀ་ཡི་ཕྲེང་བ» has two hits only, this volume and the Derge Tengyur etext; «ཛ་ཏ་པ་ཡི་ཕྲེང་བ» and the jasmine forms «ཛ་ཏི་ཡི་ཕྲེང་བ་བཅིང» and «ཛཱ་ཏི་ཡི་ཕྲེང་བ» have none. So «ཛ་ཏ་པ» is confined to the Peking and Narthang prints (which the Dpe bsdur ma note and the eye read the same), and «ཛཱ་ཏ་ཀ» stands in a second, modern typeset text: it is not an OCR fault of the Derge etext. Whether that text was set from the Derge or from another print is not told (the volume's preface is not read for it), so this is a second witness only if its base was not the Derge.

The dictionaries (Steinert's collection, `locate.py dict`) give «dza ti» / «dzA ti» as jasmine (Jasminum grandiflorum; the 84000 glossary: royal jasmine, Skt. jāti, synonyms «dza hi ka», «dza tI», «sna ma») and also nutmeg, and have no headword «dza ta ka» or «dza ta pa». A garland of jasmine flowers on the sleeper's pillow would suit the rite, but no source says «ཛཱ་ཏ་ཀ» is a spelling of «ཛཱ་ཏི»; the reading stays unproved.
