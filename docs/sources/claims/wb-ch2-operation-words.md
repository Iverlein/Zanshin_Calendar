# WB ch. 2 lists the words that mean add, subtract, multiply, divide, erase and find

Words of one meaning (machine reading of p. 21): add, ten words (bsre ba, ster ba, phul ba, sbyin pa, 'bul, khan pa, bsnan pa, bsnon pa, nor du 'gyur, ldan pa); subtract, ten (the words for purging, snatching, melting, taking at once, casting off, reducing, lessening, becoming debt, emptying; the first words 'phri, sbyang, 'phrog pa, zhu, 'phral, dor ba, 'brid pa, dman pa, bu lon 'gyur, dben pa); multiply, five (bsgyur, bstun or bsnun, spel ba, bsres, gsil); divide, four (bgo, phye, bsal ba, bcad pa); erase, four or more (byis pa or dbyi ba, bsubs pa, dor ba); find, three (rnyed pa, thob pa, nor). Two scans differ on a few of these words (bstun against bsnun; byis pa, dbyi ba).
