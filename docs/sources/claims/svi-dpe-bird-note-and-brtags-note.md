# The Dpe bsdur ma's notes on the Svapnekṣā's bird and on «བརྟགས་ལ་ཉལ»

The Dpe bsdur ma's note on the Svapnekṣā's Amoghasiddhi bird (printed p. 355, entry 4): Peking «ཤིང་ཏེའུ», Narthang «ཤང་ཏེའུ», against the base's «བྱ་ཤར་ཏེ་རུ» (vol. 25, etext p. 416, line break after «བྱ་ཤར་»). The Peking agrees with the Peking print read by eye (svi:p310b); the Narthang entry is «ཤང་ཏེའུ» and not «ཤང་ཤང་ཏེའུ», the form of q912's second option; the entry has no quoted lemma and does not say what the bird is. The note on «བརྟགས་ལ་ཉལ» (printed p. 354, entry 6) gives Peking and Narthang «བཏབ», which the Narthang manuscript confirms by eye and the Peking print read by eye (RD234, «བརྟགས་པ་ལ») does not.
