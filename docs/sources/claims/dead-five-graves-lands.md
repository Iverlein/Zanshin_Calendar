# The five graves' chants: land and wish

The five graves' chants (WB vol. 2, pp. 135–137, machine reading, the
etext's disagreements looked up on the scan) tie each kind of grave to a land
and a wish for those left behind:

| Grave | Land | Wish for the living |
| --- | --- | --- |
| the great (old) grave, bidden to rise | — | the year, month, day and hour are good now |
| mountain (རིར་སྐྱེལ) | Oḍḍiyāna | wealth spreading like a tree's leaves |
| water | Nepal | the line of descendants long as a river |
| fire, burning | India | merit blazing like fire |
| earth, hiding | Tibet | the qualities left behind good as gold |
| iron, the *bsgrub* of a new grave | China | the life of those left hard as iron |

Which grave the dead obtains is the eighth examination's finding
(pp. 74–75); this part only gives the words.
