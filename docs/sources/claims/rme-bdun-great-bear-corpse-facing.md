# «རྨེ་བདུན» in the corpse's facing is the Great Bear

In WB the seven stars of the Great Bear (རྨེ་བདུན, rme bdun) are a mark of direction: ch. 28 reads their sunset omens (wb.2:p31-32, machine) and ch. 32 sets the foundation stones to the four quarters by the rising of the Pleiades (smin drug), by Rishi and by «རྨེ་བདུན་འཆར་ནུབ་དཀྱིལ་དྲང་བྱང་» (the middle of its rising and setting is true north; wb.2:p283-285, machine; 1996 etext p. 291). The corpse's facing on p. 99 ends «རྨེ་བདུན་ཤར་བའི་གདོང་ལ་འཛེམ»: beware the face where the Great Bear rises. «རྨེ་བདུན» is therefore the Great Bear, not the mewa «སྨེ». The page's heading «སྤྲ་སྒོ» (the Zhol etext «སྨྲ་སྒོ») stays unexplained; its stacks are blotted on the reprint and unresolved on the Zhol scan (img. 771).
