# WB and the regent's replies give no rank between the earth lords and the day's factors

WB itself (ch. 31, ch. 33 with its weighing verse, ch. 34 with its closing verse, ch. 23's departure) and the regent's replies on ch. 31, 33 and 34 in *Removing the Tarnish* (YS, questions and replies 161–173 and 179–190) give no rank between an earth lord (ས་བདག, sa bdag) and the factors of the day (Rāhu, weekday, mansion, date, karaṇa, yoga, day sign) or the combination period. Where both appear, they stand side by side in one list: the things examined before any work's result is foretold (wb.2:p428b), the harms one general remedy wards off (wb.2:p369, from the etext only), the seventeen parts of a bride's departure (WB vol. 1, p. 365). No question to the regent asks for such a rank, and the questioner of 188 calls the order of what to take up and give up already clear (ys:i113-116). Any rank D4 takes is therefore the owner's choice, not a reading of WB or YS.
