# «སྒབ» (sgab) of p. 104 is spelled with ག: 79 places, none with ར or ཀ

In WB's 1996 etext «སྒབ» stands in 79 places of vol. 2 and 37 of vol. 1, «ཤི་སྒབ» in 23 and 11, the p. 104 line «ས་དཔྱད་བཅུ་གསུམ་ཤི་འགྲམས་སྒབ» among them; «སྒྲབ་བཅད», «སྐབ་བཅད» and «ཤི་སྐབ» are in none. The word under the Zhol print's grey wash at the end of line 3 of img. 778 is therefore «སྒབ» with ག; the Taikhang's stack was read ག-bodied by eye in earlier passes. The count is an etext check, the scan's last stroke not seen.
