# Ch. 81's dream list on the Derge and the Chag-po-ri prints, the commentary's use of ཟ་ཟི

RD290 (2026-11-10). Where the list is on the other prints. Derge (I00JW501163, a 7504 px strip a page, six lines): img. 434, the dream list in lines 5 and 6, so near 434 and not near 440 as proportion guessed. Chag-po-ri (I1KG13487, 2305 px, six lines): img. 426, lines 5 and 6, the same page break as on Derge (the chapter's first lines, «རྒྱུ་ནི་ལུས་ལ་གནས་པའི་ཆུ་སེར་ལ», stand at the head of Derge img. 434 and in Chag-po-ri img. 426). Yigdzin-1 over Derge img. 400–446 and Chag-po-ri img. 425–448, MITRA over Derge img. 434, the two compared with disagree.py (90% agreed, 291 of 322 syllables).

The list as the machines read it. Derge img. 434, Yigdzin-1: «རྨི་ལམ་ཆུ་དང་ཤིང་དང་ནིའུ་གསིང་དང་། །སྦལ་ལྗོང་ཉ་སྦྲུལ་འབུ་སྦྲང་སྡིག་སྡོམ་སོགས། །ལུས་ལ་འབྱར་དང་གསེབ་ན་འདུག་པ་དང་། །གཡག་མཛེ་གཅན་གཟན་རྣམས་ཀྱིས་དེད་པ་དང་། །མི་ཤོང་སྒོ་འཕྲང་འཛུལ་དང་གཡང་ལ་ལྷུང་། །ཉི་ནུབ་ས་སྲོས་སེར་བ་བབས་པ་དང་། །མདའ་དང་སྒོ་ང་ལྷ་ཁང་ལྡེར་བཟོ་འགུལ། །བུད་མེད་དག་གིས་ལུས་ཟུངས་འབྱིན་པ་རྨི། །ལྷས་ཚོར་དུས་ཏེ་གདོན་གྱིས་འདེབས་པའི་རྟགས།» MITRA agrees but for ནི་འུ (a split of ནིའུ) and ལྷས་ཚེར་. Chag-po-ri img. 426, Yigdzin-1: the same list with སྦལ་ལྗོང, ཉ་སྦྲུལ, ལྡེར་བཟོ་འགུལ, but གཡག་མཛོ་གཙན་གཟན (Derge: མཛེ་གཅན), མདའ་དང་སྒོང་ (Derge སྒོ་ང) and ལྷས་ཚོང་.

By eye. Derge img. 434, line 5 at about 2.2×: after སྦལ the next syllable is a ལྗ stack with ོ and ང and then the tsheg; no ས follows: སྦལ་ལྗོང, as the Serthang print has it, which answers q886 for this print (moderate: the scan is a soft rendering). Line 6 at the same size reads ལྷས་ཚོར་དུས་ཏེ་གདོན་གྱིས་འདེབས་པའི་རྟགས། with ོ over the ཚ and a ར closing the syllable (MITRA's ཚེར is a slip; moderate). Chag-po-ri img. 426, line 5 at 1.7×: སྦལ་ལྗོང་ཉ་སྦྲུལ་འབུ་སྦྲང་སྡིག་སྡོམ་ legible and, again, no ས after ལྗོང; line 6 at 4×: ལྷས་ཚོ then a last letter not settled between ར and ང (Yigdzin-1 reads ང; the Zhol, Derge and Serthang prints read ར): not settled on this print. The Zhol print (I5910 img. 483) was cropped at 3–4× and its bilevel glyphs are too crowded to tell the syllables apart: the Zhol's ལྗོངས remains the machine's reading only (q886 is half answered: Serthang, Derge and Chag-po-ri have no ས, the Zhol unconfirmed).

A parallel for the last clause. Soon after, the sister clause «རང་ཚོར་དུས་ཡིན་གདོན་གྱིས་ཐེབས་པར་ངེས།» stands in the signs of the mind: Derge img. 435 (Yigdzin-1) and Chag-po-ri img. 427 (Yigdzin-1), and the Drikung commentary (etext p. 607, printed p. 580) quotes both clauses with ཚོར and glosses neither. The sister clause names the time of one's own feeling (ཚོར) and then says that a spirit has struck for sure; «ལྷས་ཚོར» in the dream clause would then be a feeling by the lha, the spirit (ལྷ, lha, by the instrumental ས), at its time. The grammar is not proved by this and q885 stays open.

The word ཟ་ཟི (za zi) in the fever places. The Drikung commentary (etext VEIE0OPI7984ABB9_I1KG24731; printed page = etext page minus 27) quotes the lines of ch. 15 (etext p. 328) and ch. 23 (p. 378) with ཟ་ཟི unglossed, but uses the word some thirty times, which fixes its range and not a definition: with the mind (ཤེས་པ་ཟ་ཟི་, p. 771, 915), the eye (མིག་གིས་ཟ་ཟི་མཐོང་, p. 343, the eye sees za zi, a sight not there), speech (ཟ་ཟི་སྨྲ, p. 674, speaking za zi; ཟ་ཟི་ཉག་ཉོག་, p. 380), sleep (གཉིད་དུས་ཟ་ཟི་མང་, p. 343; ཟ་ཟི་མི་འོང་བར་, p. 324, no za zi coming in sleep; ཟ་ཟི་བླ་འཚོལ, p. 381, with the search for the bla) and once as the means of knowing (ཟ་ཟིས་ཤེས, p. 381). So the word is the wandering and confusion of the mind that shows in dream, sight and speech: the sense the older claims took from context, now with its range given, still from use and not from a gloss (q790). The Zhol, Derge, Chag-po-ri and Drathang pages of the three fever places (st:p183, st:p218, st:p241) were not located on the prints.
