# The twelve la pebbles: the board and how each living member is laid

The twelve la pebbles (བླ་རྡེལ་བཅུ་གཉིས, bla rdel bcu gnyis) reckon the living from the dead (WB vol. 2, p. 52); Mipham's notes lay them out (p. 500).

The board: twelve places, set out over the twelve year animals by the dead clan's element (རུས་ཁམས, rus khams): the great and small cut-off (ཆད་ཆེན, chad chen; ཆད་ཆུང, chad chung), and the great and small mother, power, son, enemy and friend years. Of the four corner animals dragon and sheep count great, dog and ox small; of the four directions the male year counts great and the female small. The notes' chart, for a clan of the wood element:

| Animal | Place |
| --- | --- |
| sheep | great cut-off |
| ox | small cut-off |
| rat | great mother year |
| pig | small mother year |
| tiger | great power year |
| hare | small power year |
| horse | great son year |
| snake | small son year |
| monkey | great enemy year |
| bird | small enemy year |
| dragon | great friend year |
| dog | small friend year |

The pebbles: under each living member's year animal a white pebble, and on its seventh (བདུན་ཟུར, bdun zur, the opposite animal) a black one; four fingers are left between pebbles for this. The notes' example: a fire sheep member of a wood household, one white under the great cut-off (sheep) and a black on the small cut-off (ox). "Fourteen pebbles" is said of one person. The notes also say the pebbles stand "three black for the great cut-off and so on" as WB lays them: the count is WB's own, on p. 45 (three black at the great cut-off, two black at the small, two white at each mother year, one white at each power year, a white and a black at each son year, one black at each enemy year, two white and one black at each friend year; [dead-la-count-wb-root](dead-la-count-wb-root.md)).

The readings then go by what lies "before" (མདུན་དུ, mdun du) each place: empty, black, white, or half black and half white ([dead-la-special-verdict](dead-la-special-verdict.md)), and by the board's whole shape ([dead-la-general-verdict](dead-la-general-verdict.md)).

The mother, power, son, enemy and friend places follow the five-element relations from the clan's element (for wood: water mother, wood power, fire son, iron enemy, earth friend), but the cut-offs and the friend share the four earth corners; how the two corner pairs are assigned for the other elements is not stated in the passages read.
