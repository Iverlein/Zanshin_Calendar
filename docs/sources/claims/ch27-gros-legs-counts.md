# Counts in WB's etexts for «གྲོས་ལེགས» and «གོས་ལེགས»

# «གྲོས་ལེགས» is not found in WB's etexts, «གོས་ལེགས» once, «གྲོས་ཉེས» three times

A count of the two volumes' etexts of the 1996 print (I1KG12906, I1KG12907), tsheg, shad and line breaks ignored: «གྲོས་ལེགས» no hit; «གོས་ལེགས» two hits, one the minister's set (vol. 2 printed p. 24, «དབང་དཀར་གྲོས་ཀྱི་མཚོ་སྐམས། གོས་ལེགས་ཚོང་ལེགས་ཟས་ནོར་འདུ») and one a different word (p. 451, «དགོས་ལེགས་ཉེས», "needs, good and bad"); «གྲོས་ཉེས» three hits (vol. 1 etext pp. 425 and 467, vol. 2 p. 24: the black verse «ནག་ན་གྲོས་ཉེས་ཚོང་ཉེས་འབྱུང»); «ཚོང་ལེགས» four hits (vol. 2 pp. 22, 24, 166, 168), «ཚོང་ཉེས» five. So WB has the phrase «གྲོས་ཉེས» of the black line elsewhere and never the white counterpart «གྲོས་ལེགས», and the white line's pair «གོས་ལེགས» of the same set has no second use either: the count neither confirms the ra nor refutes it, and the etext of the Zhol-block commentary that has «གྲོས་ལེགས་ཚོང་ལེགས» ([the claim ch27-gos-legs-is-gros-legs](ch27-gos-legs-is-gros-legs.md), q475) is a snippet. The count shows only that the paired black line is WB's own idiom, which the claim already used; the question stays narrowed to the eye (the ra's foot on the Zhol and Taikhang) and MITRA.
