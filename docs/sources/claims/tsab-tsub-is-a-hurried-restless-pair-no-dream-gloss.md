# «ཙབ་ཙུབ» is the hurried, unsettled pair of the lexica; no text glosses it as a dream term (RD396, q1081)

«ཙབ་ཙུབ» (tsab tsub) in «རྟག་ཏུ་ལྷ་མི་དགའ་བ་དང་། རྨི་ལམ་ཙབ་ཙུབ་ཤིན་ཏུ་མང་།» is the short form of «ཙབ་བེ་ཙུབ་བེ» (tsab be tsub be): the lexica give only "a mode of not letting the mind settle" (Tshig mdzod chen mo and Tshig gter rgya mtsho: «བློ་བག་མི་ཕེབས་པའི་རྣམ་འགྱུར»), "conduct that is not settled, steady" (Dag tshig gsar bsgrigs: «ལྷུན་ཆགས་པ་མ་ཡིན་པའི་བྱ་སྤྱོད»), "crude, restless, nervous" (Rangjung Yeshe), "in a hurry" (Valby; Dan Martin, from Das); the variants «འཚབ་འཚུབ» ('tshab 'tshub: "hurry, confusion, perplexity, fear", Rangjung Yeshe; «བརྡལ་ཟིང» "flurried", Tshig mdzod chen mo) and «རྩབ་རྩུབ» (rtsab rtsub: "weeds", "restless") are the same pair in other spellings (the Sgom sde tshig mdzod: «ཚབ་ཚུབ» is another spelling of «འཚབ་འཚུབ»). So the dreams of the line are many (ཤིན་ཏུ་མང) and of a hurried, restless, unsettled kind: jumbled, not clear; not a named kind of dream. The word occurs in no other dream text (RD396, BDRC's exact phrase «རྨི་ལམ་ཙབ་ཙུབ»: only the White Beryl, its Gser thang print and the Kun 'dus chen mo, the same line) and no lexicon, commentary or reckoner glosses it as a dream term; the sense above is the word's, not the dream's. The pool's «རྨི་ལམ་རྩུབ» (rough) and «ཟ་ཟི» (confused) do not gloss it, but «ཙབ་ཙུབ» sits nearest «འཚུབ» (troubled) of the hour entry's «རྨི་ལམ་འཚུབ» (kd.3:p588-589b), a word of the same shape with the sense "swirling, turbulent, restless".
