# The thirty: the twenty-second point, the sick lying askew, for the sick

The twenty-second of the thirty starting points (སྐོར་འགོ, skor 'go), from the mouth of the sick lying askew (ནད་པ་ཡོ་ཉལ, nad pa yo nyal), is read for the sick. Its eight pebbles are laid as for every point (claims thirty-count and thirty-eight-fighters), each white or black, and read so:

| Pebble | Asks | White | Black | Remedy for black |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Whether the lord of the illness is the *the'u gre* (ཐེའུ་གྲེ, the'u gre), the the'u brang spirit (ཐེའུ་བྲང, the'u brang) and the *gre bo* spirit (གྲེ་བོ, gre bo; not identified) | Not done by them, good | The lord of the illness is there: pains come above, in the male organ («ཕོ་མཚན་སྟོད», read so) | The mamo (མ་མོ, ma mo)'s turning back (བཟློག, bzlog) and the like |
| 2 | Whether fever has spread in the upper body | No fever above in oneself and the household, good | Fever spreads above and the upper corpse-door is open (སྟོད་རོ་ཆགས་སྒོ, stod ro chags sgo), take care | — |
| 3 | Whether cold disease has fallen into the lower body | Not fallen, good | It has fallen below, with nāga (ཀླུ, klu) disease and dropsy (དམུ་ཆུ, dmu chu) | Water torma (ཆབ་གཏོར, chab gtor), the nāga rite and the like |
| 4 | Whether there is madness or dizziness | None in oneself and the household, good | Madness and dizziness come | Strive at the king thread-cross (མདོས, mdos) and the blood thread-cross (ཁྲག་མདོས, khrag mdos) |
| 5 | Whether god disease and heaviness (བྱིངས་འཐིབས, byings 'thibs) come | They do not come, good | They come | Offering to the gods (ལྷ་གསོལ, lha gsol), fumigation offering (ལྷ་བསངས, lha bsangs) and ablution of the gods |
| 6 | Whether god-demons have risen as spirits | Not risen; no sign of illness, good | Risen, bad | The pure god thread-cross (གཙང་མ་ལྷ་མདོས, gtsang ma lha mdos) turns it back |
| 7 | Whether blood disease spreads its red | No sign of blood disease, good | Blood disease spreading red comes | The mamo's blood thread-cross, Dbang phyug's weapon thread-cross (ཟོར་མདོས, zor mdos) and the like |
| 8 | Whether tumours and dropsy come below | They do not come to oneself and the household, good | A risk of tumours and dropsy | The nāga rite and the many nāga cures («འབུང[?]», misprint of «འབད», strive?) |

Whose year is reckoned: the person's own (the notes, vol. 2, p. 533: «གཞན་རྣམས་སོ་སོའི་ལོ་རྟ»).

Which of the eight fighting pebbles is which check is not stated; the order of p. 453 is the likely one (claim thirty-board-groups; question 259).
