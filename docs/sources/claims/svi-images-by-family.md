# The Svapna-īkṣā's fortunate images by Buddha family

Advayavajra's fortunate images, sorted by Buddha family "in their own order and mixed" (pp. 261–262). Every one is simply a sign of the family; the text gives no further verdict image by image, it gives the rite for the whole list (see svi-remedy).
- Vairocana's: a mirror held in the hand; the moon of the first lunar day seen; riding a chariot wheel; white clothes and ornaments worn; an elephant subdued with a hook; acting as a caravan leader through compassion.
- Ratnasambhava's: finding gold; entering a bath; a vase set on the crown; mastery over all the other continents; riding a horse to the continent of jewels; being lifted by a crowd of people.
- Amitābha's: a lotus flower taken up; a book in the hand; a conch blown; a flower garland worn; a peacock caught in a snare; holding the lotus teaching with compassion.
- Amoghasiddhi's: winning a battle weapon in hand; becoming the king's retinue; the army surrounding the field; being set to peaceful work; a bird sent as messenger (the words «བྱ་ཤར་ཏེ་རུ» unclear); a forest burnt by fire.
- Akṣobhya's: swimming without sinking; going and soaring in the sky; wearing the sun and moon as ornaments; pressing down a lion throne; a white parasol raised and a banner hoisted; becoming worthy of all's worship.
- Vajrasattva's: blood and serum flowing; driving a mass of fire without fear; eating snakes and sin; holding vajra and bell; pressing down a mountain top; propping up a city with a finger.
- "Of the mind" (a seventh group, not a family): worshipping the guru and the chosen deity; asking for a book and raising a stūpa; going naked, singing and dancing; keeping company with an assembly of monks; fine clothes and tree-fruit eaten; the Tathāgata's seal; blessing words and prophecy; a great pond whirling; a high house built.
