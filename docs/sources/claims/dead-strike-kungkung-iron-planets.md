# The kung kung, iron-four fire-eight and planet strikes

Three more great strikes of repetition (WB vol. 2, pp. 88–89). The *kung kung* (ཀུང་ཀུང) after-strike: the dead of one of the four executioner years, the year or month executioner meeting, and the trigram of death on a corner: *khen*, the after-bad, bad for four; *gin*, the after-strike, bad for the male line (daughter-in-law or three sons lead the corpse); *khon*, grave-taking (the same lead); *zon*, the grave turned back (son, sister and father lead). Iron-four and fire-eight: the dead's year, the year and the progressed sign each on iron-four or fire-eight, the three meeting: a sudden condition comes, the dead "destroyed by the grave". Planets and mansions: dying on the weekday of the planet that is the enemy of the dead's element repeats, worse if that planet is in lawsuit or decline; Mercury, Jupiter and Venus rising do not strike, Sun, Moon, Mars and Saturn always do (rising milder). Rāhu's (ཁྱབ་འཇུག, khyab 'jug) and Kikang's (ཀི་ཀང, ki kang) courses, the food-seeker, messenger, the four first mansions, the enemy mansion, the demon day and black-day rising repeat swiftly. A rat, dragon or monkey dying on *li* or *kham* with Mars three times: people and wealth burnt; Saturn three times moves three graves, Mercury disturbs, Jupiter moves one grave. WB says Mipham Dge legs and others read this wrongly.
