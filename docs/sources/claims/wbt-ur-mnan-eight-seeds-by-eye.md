# The pressing-down wheel's first spoke row on the Taikhang: eight letters, the fifth plain

On the Taikhang (I5878 img. 172, folio 162, line 3; the Zhol print's img. 790), cropped at 3x and 8x (2026-11-03), the first spoke row of the pressing-down wheel is eight letters in a run before «ཅེས»: the first has the shape of ཧྲཱི without the dot of the anusvara, the second, third and fourth carry the dot, the fifth carries none and has no subjoined ར (a box-headed letter with a left bowl, a central triangular opening and a right stem, most like ཧཱ, not settled), and the sixth, seventh and eighth carry the dot again. So seven of the eight are hrī-like and the row is the one seed repeated, not eight different syllables; the first's missing dot and the fifth's identity are held with doubt. Question 593 is narrowed, not closed.
