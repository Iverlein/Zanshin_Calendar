# Śramaṇā rite: only the waxing half of the month is stated, no lunar day

The First Dalai Lama's instruction names no single lunar day. It says to begin "at a good time of the waxing half of the month" (yar ngo'i dus bzang po), in a clean secluded place, and gives no date, no mansion, no hour for the start. The rite runs night after night until the answer comes, and success is promised in 3, 5 or 7 nights (best, middle, least). The evening is the time of special effort ("srod kyi dus su lhag par 'bad"): the sleep itself follows the evening practice.
