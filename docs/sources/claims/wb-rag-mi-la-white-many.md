# «རག་མི་ལ» in the white sum of the four aspects: a reading from the line's pair

Narrowed 2026-11-01, not settled. The verse (1996 etext p. 348 = printed p. 338, the sum of the four aspects together): «སྲོག་ལུས་དབང་ཀླུང་བཞི་པོ་ཡི། །རྡེལ་དཀར་མང་བྱུང་མིས་ཁུར་འོང་། །བྱ་བ་གྲུབ་ཅིང་རག་མི་ལ། །མ་ལུས་རང་གིས་རྟོགས་པ་ཡིན།» («མི་ལ» «རག» as printed on the 1996 print and the Gser thang reprint; BDRC's search for the whole line finds only these). The black half answers it: «རྡེལ་ནག་མང་ཤར་བསོད་ནམས་ཆུང་། །ཆོས་ལ་རྒྱབ་བརྟེན་བྱ་བར་བཟང་། །མི་ཡི་བྲན་བྱེད་དགོས་པ་ཡིན།», "many black: little merit; good to rely on the dharma; one must serve others". The dictionaries give «རག» as "to depend on, to be obtained" (Rangjung Yeshe, Ives Waldo). The pair makes «རག་མི་ལ» most likely "[the work] depending on people" read against «མིས་ཁུར», "carried by others" in the line before: the white sum is that others carry the load, works are accomplished and rest on people, all is understood by oneself, where the black is serving others. A reading from the verse's own antithesis; no gloss in the notes or any other etext found (the string «རག་མི་ལ» in BDRC's search: twenty hits, none of them an astrology text apart from WB's prints, the others a prayer-line «བསེ་རག་མི་ལ» and ritual texts).
