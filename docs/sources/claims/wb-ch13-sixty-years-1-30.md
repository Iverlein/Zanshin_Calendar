# WB ch. 13: the table of the sixty years, years 1–30 (names and the three elements)

WB's chapter 13 opens with a table of the sixty years of the cycle (རབ་བྱུང, rab byung), fourteen columns across a double page, one row to a year, with no number column. Pages 104, 106 and 108 hold the left seven columns of years 1 to 8, 9 to 19 and 20 to 30; the facing pages 105, 107 and 109 hold the right seven. The rest of the sixty is on the pages after p. 109 and is not in this claim.

The left half gives, per year: the Sanskrit name in Tibetan script (not read), the Tibetan name, the Chinese stem-and-branch name in Tibetan script (not settled to the letter), the name by element and animal, and three elements: the life element (སྲོག་གི་ཁམས, srog gi khams), the body element (ལུས་ཁམས, lus khams) and the power element (དབང་ཐང་ཁམས, dbang thang khams). The life element of each row is the element of the row's animal (rat and pig water, ox, dragon, sheep and dog earth, tiger and rabbit wood, snake and horse fire, monkey and bird iron), so it is fixed by column 4; the body and power elements are the table's own entries.

The first year is the fire-rabbit year, named རབ་བྱུང; the cycle then runs in the usual order of element pairs and the twelve animals, so the third is the earth-snake year (ས་སྦྲུལ) and the thirtieth the fire-monkey year (མེ་སྤྲེལ). The Sanskrit names of column 1 were not transcribed.
