# The Zhol and Taikhang prints read «དུར་གྱི་སྟེང» for the light-clearer and name no animal for the grave

On the Zhol print (I1KG1710 img. 935, read on the scan at 4x) and the Taikhang reproduction (I5878 img. 317, folio 307, read on the scan; Yigdzin-1 dropped the lines beside the woodcuts), the light-clearer's line is «ར་བ་བཞི་ལ་དུར་གྱི་སྟེང། འབྲིང་པོར་མར་གྱི་ཁྱིམ་མཚེས་ལ། ཐ་ཆུང་ཡར་གྱི་གཤེད་ལ་གནས།»: the word is «སྟེང» ("on top of") and not «རྟིང», as at the Gser thang reprint and the 1996 print. All four editions read the same, and none gives the animal of the grave for the tiger, snake, monkey and pig months: whether it follows the element of the month's animal or the triads of ch. 23 (q444) is not answered by any print.
