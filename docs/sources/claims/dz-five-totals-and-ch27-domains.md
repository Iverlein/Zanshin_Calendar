# The Moonbeams' five general totals and ch. 27's five domains: same pairing, third domain differs

The *Moonbeams*' five general totals of the year of age (DZ f. 24a) are the same five pairings of aspect and domain as the results WB ch. 27 gives the meadow's ornament's verdicts (vol. 2, p. 22), with one domain differing. The *Moonbeams*: the verdicts examined "for life, illness, food, merit and demons and curses by the life force, body, power, luck and mewa" gathered into five general totals. WB: "for the life force the adversities of life; for the body illness; for the power wealth; for the luck merit; for the mewa gods and demons, a crowd's curse (ཁྲོམ་བྱད) and the like". Life, illness and merit stand for the same aspects in both; the mewa's «གདོན་བྱད», demons and curses, is WB's «ལྷ་འདྲེའི་ཁྲོམ་བྱད»; the third pair is «ཟ་ཐང», food, for the power in the *Moonbeams* and «དབང་ནོར», power and wealth, in WB.

The two are not the same reading in what they gather. WB's meadow's ornament lays sixty verdicts (four groups of five for each of three years) and tells the results "as the white and black verdicts fall", each by its domain; the *Moonbeams* gathers a whole year's verdicts into five totals, one for each aspect, to decide by one result of the kind. So the *Moonbeams*' shortcut uses the same domains as WB's reading by the verdicts of ch. 27, but WB nowhere names a gathering into five totals for ch. 24, the year of age, nor says its verdicts of ch. 27 are totals. The domains may stand in WB ch. 24 by the *Moonbeams*' warrant, with the third as WB's power and wealth against the *Moonbeams*' food; that is a judgement left open, not a finding of the text.
