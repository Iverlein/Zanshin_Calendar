# The sky's road of the twelfth examination: bird road in the sizes and the notes, mouse road on p. 96

The sky's road (གནམ་གྱི་བྱ་ལམ་/བྱི་ལམ, gnam gyi bya lam / byi lam) of the twelfth examination is written with «བྱ» (bird) in the verse of the sizes (WB vol. 2, p. 50, etext p. 58, read the same in the Gser thang, Sakya and Zhol etexts) and in Mipham's note on the examination (p. 512), and with «བྱི» (mouse) on p. 96 in both the 1996 print and the Gser thang reprint, where the next line, the earth's road, is «ས་ཡི་བྱི་ལམ» with «བྱི» throughout, so that the copy on p. 96 has probably taken over the earth's road's spelling. The "road" is the day's five-fold count of the lunar day, planets and mansions (Mipham's note lists what it gathers: the thirty lunar days, their five rise-and-decline, the twelve suns, the clan's and life force's grave days, the eight trigrams and nine mewa of the lunar day, and its earth lords, then planets, vowels, consonants and mansions); the note does not explain why it is called "bird". The spelling with «བྱ» is the witness of two places and of the commentator; the one on p. 96 stands as printed. Narrowed: probably «བྱ་ལམ», "bird road", with «བྱི་ལམ» on p. 96 an echo of the earth's road.
