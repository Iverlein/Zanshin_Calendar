# The thirty: the sixteenth point, the eight golden continents, the ground, for merit and the means of work: the ground, the trees, the site of a castle

The sixteenth of the thirty starting points (སྐོར་འགོ, skor 'go), from the mouth of the eight golden continents, the ground (གླིང་བརྒྱད་སེར་གྱི་ས་གཞི, gling brgyad ser gyi sa gzhi), is read for merit and the means of work: the ground, the trees, the site of a castle. Its eight pebbles are laid as for every point (claims thirty-count and thirty-eight-fighters), each white or black, and read so:

| Pebble | Asks | White | Black | Remedy for black |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Whether the ground is stable and good | Stable; merit grows, good | The ground is not stable, bad | The rite of raising merit |
| 2 | Whether the trees on the mountain have grown | On the mountain of merit the trees of food and wealth grow, good | The tree of enjoyments has dried: bad in the place of merit and enjoyment | — |
| 3 | Whether the site for a castle or house is good | Good; merit and wealth grow | The site is bad: give the earth lord (ས་བདག, sa bdag) the chanted rite (གྱེར་གཏོ, gyer gto) and the like | — |
| 4 | Whether the grain grows well | The offerings and grain grow well | The five grains grow weakly: there is a fault | — |
| 5 | Whether the mountain's lamp and the rock's lamp fight | They do not fight, good | They fight: merit somewhat less | Building a stupa helps |
| 6 | Whether the sun's road from the east is cut by a mountain | Not cut, good | Cut: old women rise as enemies, loss comes | — |
| 7 | Whether the valley lane (སྲང་ལུང, srang lung) has been struck by a broom | Not struck from the valley mouth, good | Struck: bad in the place of food and wealth | — |
| 8 | Whether the sunny and shady sides are reversed | Not reversed, good | Reversed, bad: face the door toward one's own and the mother, or toward the clan's mother («རང་དང་མ», «རུས་ཀྱི་མ»; read: the elements so named) | — |

Whose year is reckoned: the person's own (the notes, vol. 2, p. 533: «གཞན་རྣམས་སོ་སོའི་ལོ་རྟ»).

Which of the eight fighting pebbles is which check is not stated; the order of p. 453 is the likely one (claim thirty-board-groups; question 259).
