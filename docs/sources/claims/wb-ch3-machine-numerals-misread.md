# Both machine readings misread the figures of ch. 3; the chain and the number words correct them

Both machine readings of WB's vol. 1 pp. 22–28 misread the figures. Yigdzin-1 gives the numerals without their brackets and runs two figures together (p. 33 «༢༥༦༩༡» for 2566), drops a digit (p. 34 «༩༣» for 936) and confuses ༡ with ༤, ༢ with ༩ and ༥ with ༤; BDRC's etext drops whole half-lines and brackets and misreads the same digits in other places. Where the two agree on a figure that the chain of reductions (the previous year less the reduction written in number words, claim wb-ch3-years-passed-chain) contradicts, the print's own figure is probably the chain's: a reading of this chronology takes the figure from the chain and the number words, and uses a machine reading only to see which digit is in doubt. The same holds for chs. 4 and 5, where each figure stands beside number words (p. 30: «དུས་མིག» is 26, «དུས་མཚོ» 46, «འཁོར་ལོ» 27; p. 29: «དུས་ཉིས» 126, not 129): Yigdzin-1 read these as ༢༩, ༤༩, ༢༢ and ༡༢༩.
