# The living household: each living member's twenty-one pebbles

Before the dead is reckoned, Mipham's notes lay the living household (གསོན་ཁོག, gson khog) on a white cloth beside the dead's: the men of the house by seniority, then the women from the eldest, each with a partition, each marked with year, power (དབང་ཐང, dbang thang) and mewa (སྨེ་བ, sme ba) (vol. 2, p. 499).

For each living member, in short form, twenty-one pebbles, all against the current year:

| Pebbles | What | Colour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | life force (སྲོག, srog), body, power, luck each fought against the current year | as the obstacle reckoning lays them ("three white for the mother" and so on) |
| 5 | rise and decline (དར་གུད, dar gud) of life force, body, power, luck and the descended mewa against the current year | good six white, bad six black, as in general |
| 2 | falling in the nine-eye (དགུ་མིག, dgu mig) and in the grave-eye (དུར་མིག, dur mig) | black if fallen in, white if not |
| 1 | the seven rough ones (རྩུབ་བདུན, rtsub bdun) | black if fallen on, white if not |
| 9 | identifying pebbles: the descended trigram and mewa and the like | (identifying, no colour reading) |

The notes add that the year, life force, power, trigram and mewa are only identifying and "easier written in letters", and that reckoning each member so shows the household's obstacles as a whole. The full form is the forty-seven-pebble obstacle reckoning for each. The page's chart is a living member of the fire sheep year, mewa nine red, aged 37, trigram kham (ཁམ, kham). The split of the nine identifying pebbles beyond trigram and mewa is not stated.
