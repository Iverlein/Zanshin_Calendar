# The stacked figures of the 11th and 7th heads: the page's 1, 2 and 3 told apart

RD202, 2026-11-18, Gser thang reprint img. 161 (p. 154, the 11th month head) and img. 173 (p. 166, the 7th month head's lines 1-4), stacks in crops at 8x to 10x, each figure set beside the same page's weekday-line and box numerals at the same enlargement. The page's own forms, which tell the digits apart where the earlier passes took every hooked figure for 1: the 1 is a thin hook whose tail curves to the lower left (weekday line, "zhud 1"); the 2 has a closed ring head with a hole and a tail to the lower right ("skyod 2"); the 3 has a heavy slab head with a beak to the left and a tail to the lower right ("zhud 3"); the 0 is a ring or dot; the 6 a loop with a flag; the 7 a curled loop with a rising tail; the 8 a wedge-headed stroke with a long tail to the lower right; the 5 like a 4 with a hooked head. The heavy hooks of the day and night stacks, which RD201 and RD200 took for 1, have the 3's slab head and beaked left edge, not the 1's thin hook; 11th head: the tiger's great and small (end of line 2) upper 17 (a thin 1 and a 7), lower two figures, a ring-headed one (2) and an 8; the stack of line 3 before "the eleventh's sgang": upper a thin 1 and an 8, lower a heavy 3 and a 6; day length 36 over 30 over 0 (3 and 6; 3 and 0; 0); night length 33 over 30 over 0; ignorance, the first link: upper a thin 1 and a figure read 7 or 8, under it a dot. In the 7th head the day is 37 over 10 over 0 and the night, with an unsettled upper tier, over 50 over 0. Check: the middle tiers add to 60 in both heads (11th: 30 + 30; 7th: 10 + 50) and the lower tiers to 0, so the stacks are probably chu tshod over chu srang over a third unit, day plus night making the day-and-night; the upper tiers do not add in the 11th head (36 + 33 = 69), so a figure of the upper tier of one of the two is misread (a 2 for a 3 would make 26 + 34 only if the night's second figure were a 4, which its shape is not), and no length is settled there. The weekday-line figures of the heads agree with RD2's.
