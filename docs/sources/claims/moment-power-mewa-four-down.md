# The notes make the fighter of the three mewa the moment's power-mewa, four down

WB vol. 2, p. 165 sets the day's mewa in the centre of the nine squares and says the mewa «དུས་ལོའི་སྨེ་བ» is made the one reckoned and fights the squares (read on the Gser thang reprint, img. 171, l. 11: «ལོའི», not «དུས་སྨེ»). Mipham's notes (p. 536) name the fighter «དུས་ཀྱི་དབང་སྨེ», the moment's power-mewa, and give it a derivation: «མར་བཞི», four down from the moment's own mewa, so from the example's 5 yellow the fighter is 8 white. The same notes count the power-mewa of a person four down from the body-mewa (the year's own mewa) in the year-of-life and marriage reckonings, so the notes' fighter is not the year's mewa itself but its four-down; where WB says «of the moment's year» the notes' reading is the year's mewa taken as the body-mewa and the power-mewa derived from it. The example's elements agree with this: 8 white (iron, WB vol. 1, p. 255) fights the centre's 4 green (wood) as a friend, the east's 2 black (water) as a son, the north-east's 7 red (fire) as an enemy. Narrowed, not settled: WB's «དུས་ལོའི» may be the year's mewa itself (as built) or, by the notes, its four-down; the example does not name the moment's year, so the 5 yellow cannot be tied to a year. The centre: the notes' «བརྩི་ཉིན་ཉི་མའི་དབང་ཐང་གི་སྨེ་བ» «following the year in the mewa chain» is the day's mewa of reckoning; the notes' example has 4 green there.
