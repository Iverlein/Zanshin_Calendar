# The tenth of the monkey month of the monkey year: the Guru in person

The tenth day (ཚེས་བཅུ, tshes bcu) of the monkey month of the monkey year (སྤྲེལ་ལོ་སྤྲེལ་ཟླ, sprel lo sprel zla) is set above the other tenths in two texts Mipham quotes. The text he names Bla ma gsang 'dus them med (བླ་མ་གསང་འདུས་ཐེམ་མེད, bla ma gsang 'dus them med) calls it "the great power" («དབང་ཆེན་སྤྲེལ་ལོ་སྤྲེལ་ཟླའི་ཚེས་བཅུ») beside "all the tenths, the king of times" («དུས་ཀྱི་རྒྱལ་པོ་ཚེས་བཅུ་ཐམས་ཅད»), on which the Guru sends emanations over Jambudvīpa and gives accomplishments (mph.127:i302). The Key Instruction on Accomplishing the Guru (བླ་མ་སྒྲུབ་པའི་གནད་ཡིག, bla ma sgrub pa'i gnad yig) has him come himself, "certainly", to all Tibet on that day, and by emanations on every other month's tenth (mph.127:i305): the monkey-year tenth is the Guru in person, the monthly tenth his emanations.

Neither passage says which month of the year the monkey month is, nor what happened on that day; 'Jigs med gling pa's list of the twelve tenths (jgl.8:p739-742) does not name the monkey month and puts the birth on the tenth of the sixth Hor month. The pool's q1166 gives the monkey month as Tibetan 7 = Hor 5 from the almanac count; whether the birth-month of the prayer and the monkey month of these verses are meant as one month is not settled by the pages read.
