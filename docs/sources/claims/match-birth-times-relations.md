# Point 10: the spouses' birth months, days and hours, four relations

The tenth point of the match (WB vol. 1, p. 360): "the birth month, day
and hour without enemy and grave (དགྲ་དུར, dgra dur), seventh (བདུན་ཟུར,
bdun zur) and four-executioner (བཞི་གཤེད, bzhi gshed): agreeing, great
merit and a long companionship; disagreeing, parted from many children;
for separation read the rite and the *Ratnakūṭa*." Mipham's notes (vol. 2,
p. 525) make the relations mutual, between the man's month and the
woman's, his day and hers, his hour and hers: "good if they do not meet
each other as enemy, seventh and the like".

**«དགྲ་དུར» is two relations, enemy and grave.** The notes gloss the same
compound in WB's household reckoning, «[དབང་]ཐང་དགྲ་དུར་སྐྱེས་པ་མང»
(vol. 1, p. 306), by both parts (vol. 2, p. 478): the power with many
enemies in the hundred pebbles, and births on the grave year and grave
trigram of the life force and power. WB pairs «དགྲ» with a second
relation the same way in «དགྲ་བདུད», enemy and the demon's cut, which the
same note glosses as enemies and the grave year.

So point 10 tries each of the three pairs, the two months, the two days,
the two hours, taken as animals, for four relations, each defined
elsewhere in WB (as O16 read them for open question 165):
**enemy**, one's life-force element overcoming the other's; **grave**, one
falling on the grave of the other's life-force element (wood's the sheep,
fire's the dog, iron's the ox, earth's and water's the dragon; p. 258);
**seventh**, the opposite animal, six apart; **four-executioner**, three
apart either way (p. 235). None of them, agreeing; any of them, the
point's bad result and its rite. The text does not weigh one relation
against another or one of the three times against the others.
