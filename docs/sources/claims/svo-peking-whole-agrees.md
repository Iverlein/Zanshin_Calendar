# The Peking print of the Svapnohana agrees with the Derge text but for the fourth watch

Read whole by eye on the Peking print (img. 315–318, letters about 40 px), the Svapnohana follows the Derge text verse by verse in the order of its images, its watches (but for the fourth, see svo-peking-fourth-watch-line), the good and bad images, the women by colour, the sun and moon, the snake, the bird, the black and white things, the drinks, the fire, the signs worshipped, the remedy and the colophon; no image, verdict or remedy is added or lacking. The places that RD207 and RD218 could not settle on the Derge scan are read on this print: «བགྲོད་མིན», «འགྲོན་བུ», «བཅུ་ཡི་བར», «རྟ་ལ་མོ», «ག་ཡ་ཏྲི» (ཏྲི), «ཨུཉྩི» (moderate), «སྦྲུལ་གྱིས», and the verbs and particles of the women's verses are as the etext (གང་ལ་འཁྱུད, དེ་ཡིས). Reading was at 1.25× with the Derge text in mind: a one-syllable variant in an unremarkable word may have passed; the places named were read at 2×. The Sanskrit title, the last syllable of «བློ་མེད་…» and the start of the last line are not settled (svo:p271d).
