# Which corpses each of the five graves refuses, and when each is good

For each of the five graves, the corpses that may not be given it and the signs that make it good (wb.2:p74-75). The notes (wb.2:p503-504) apply the signs to the trigram and mewa of death and the planet and mansion of the time of death, say not all need come together but the more do the worse, and count the pebbles: not to burn 31, good to burn 9 (10 with the deciding pebble), not to hide 19 (21 with the deciding), not *sgrub* 24, not water 25, not mountain 21, each grave's good ones 10 with the deciding.

| Grave | Not given to | Good when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| burning (fire) | a bird or monkey year; iron element; *dwa* trigram; white mewa; Friday; the six western mansions; three generations of monkeys; leprosy, dropsy, cramp, tumour, stomach swelling, seized by the planet, itch, *bad*, *dreg*, goitre; a kin-slayer; power iron; died in autumn; one under sixty with spouse alive and no child dead; one of 70–80 widowed and bereaved yet never in want; a child of five to ten; long wounds, wasting, deafness, paralysis (*klu* illnesses) | the year of death and the dead on the four executioners or horse and snake; trigram and mewa fire or earth; many of the living mice and pigs |
| hiding (earth) | a *btsad po*, a spiritual teacher, a mantra practitioner; a mouse or pig year; Monday or Wednesday; water element; black or blue mewa; the six northern mansions; three generations of mice and pigs; *kham* trigram; cold illness; choked by flour, food, the breast, spittle or smoke; died in winter; power water | the year of death and the dead bird or monkey; the living of tiger and hare years on the four executioners (so the line runs, «གཤེད་བཞི་གསོན་པོ་སྟག་ཡོས་ལོ»); trigram and mewa iron or earth |
| *sgrub* (iron) | a tiger or hare year; Thursday; *zin* trigram; four green mewa; the six eastern mansions; liver illness, blindness, leprosy, goitre, stomach swelling, cramp, the blade; a kin-slayer, a Bon priest, a Mon, a black mantra practitioner; one who sent curses or died of a cutting illness; power wood; died in spring; wood element, three generations of tigers | the year of death and the dead mouse or pig; trigram and mewa water or iron; the living horse and snake |
| water | a horse or snake year, fire; Sunday or Tuesday; *li* trigram; the two red mewa; the southern mansions; heart illness, burns and hot illnesses; a barbarian, dropsy, kidney illness; a son killed by a blade, stomach swelling, the old and infants, a *thon bu*, one killed by water, a widow; mouse, boar, the dull; died in summer; fire element, three generations of horses and snakes; power fire | the year of death and the dead tiger or hare; trigram and mewa water or wood; the living on the four executioners |
| the mountain (wood) | an ox, sheep, dog or dragon year, earth; the four corner trigrams; five yellow mewa; Saturday; the four corner mansions; the last month of each season; the blocking blade, spleen illness, killed by a bird, cast into a pit, caused by a tiger, donkey or wild ass; a madman, the old and infants; earth element, three generations on the four executioners; power earth | the year of death and the dead horse or snake; trigram and mewa fire or wood; the living bird and monkey |
