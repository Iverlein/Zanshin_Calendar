# Signs of the first watch's dream, of phlegm (RD370)

First watch, of phlegm: the root tantra lists as signs of a dream of the first watch what is white, cool or moist: water, the sea, ponds and floods, crystal, pearls and snow, white parasols, ornaments, elephants and bulls, white clothes, cotton, silver, salt, eating rice, sesame, beans and sweet-fat foods, and dwellings, beds, carriages and mounts; he who sees such a dream "belongs to phlegm", and the same dreams show the phlegm-natured's conduct (mmk.1:p397-399).
