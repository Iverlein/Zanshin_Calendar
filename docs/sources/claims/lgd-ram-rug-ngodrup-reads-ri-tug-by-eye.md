# Ngodrup and Sherab Drimay's goods of gossip, «རི་ཏུག་སྦར་ཁྱེར», settled by eye; no text says what the first word is

On the Ngodrup and Sherab Drimay print's p. 309 (I1867 img. 309, line 4 of the page, the hand script cut in a block, 4-fold by eye) the goods of gossip read «རི་ཏུག་སྦར་ཁྱེར་ལ་སོགས་པ་མི་ཁའི་རྫས་ལ་སྤྱད་པ» (RD355, 2026-11-10): the first syllable has the ི on a ར, «རི»; the second a ཏ with ུ and ག, «ཏུག», not «རུག»; the third a stack ས over བ ending in ར, «སྦར»; the fourth ཁྱ with ེ and ར, «ཁྱེར». Yigdzin-1 on the page gives the same four syllables. So this print writes the second syllable with ta, «ཏུག» (tug), which is the spelling «རམ་ཏུག» that RD271 found elsewhere, and the first syllable not as «རམ» (ram) but «རི» (ri): either the scribe's ear for the same word or an unlike first word of the list. The Sbas-yul cursive (I1LT3208 img. 148, line 3, 2-fold) gives a ཟ-like first glyph and, by Yigdzin-1, «རྟ་ཇུག་སྦང་ཕྱེར», not settled by eye. The two eastern hands thus agree with the Palri, Kazi and Zhe chen readings on the second syllable's shape (a final ག and ུ) and on the third and fourth syllables as sbang and g.yer, and part from them on the first syllable. No text read says what the first word is: BDRC's exact-string search (2026-11-10) for «རམ་རུག་སྦང་གཡེར» and «རམ་རུག་སྦང» finds it only in the Shechen etext (vol. 37) and, for the pair «རམ་རུག» with «སྦང», in Karma Chags med's ransoms (I2PD20754); «སྦང་གཡེར» is found elsewhere only in Bon medical texts (witnesses to usage, not sources: a wound-treatment line «རྨ་སར་སྦང་གཡེར་ཚ་བབས་མར་ནག་ཕྱེ» in the Bon Kangyur, the 'Bum nag and the Bon medical collection 'Bum bzhi), where it is a pair of substances in a list of medicines, as in the lists the earlier claims name. The "three words" are items of a list; the first stays unsettled.
