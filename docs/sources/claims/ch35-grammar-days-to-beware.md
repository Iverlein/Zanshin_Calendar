# WB p. 432: the dates to beware of for grammar

On p. 432 the verse gives the days to beware of for grammar (སྒྲ་རིག་སྤྱི་ཡི་དུས་གཉན, sgra rig spyi yi dus gnyan) by lunar date: for the general grammar the 8th, 14th, 16th, 23rd, 29th and the full and new moon days; Pāṇini's tradition adds the 13th; the Dbyangs can tradition the 28th and the 12th; and on the 27th one avoids explaining and hearing. The dates are spelled in words in the print («བརྒྱད་དང་བཅུ་བཞི་བཅུ་དྲུག་དང་། །ཉེར་གསུམ་ཉེར་དགུ་ཉ་སྟོང་རྣམས།», «པཱ་ཎིར་དེ་སྟེང་བཅུ་གསུམ་དང་། །ཉེར་བརྒྱད་དབྱངས་ཅན་བཅུ་གཉིས་དང་། །ཉེར་བདུན་འཛེམ་པས་བཤད་ཉན་སྤང་།»), and the page was read line by line on the scan for RD84 (2026-10-31); the 1st of the month is named before them as the general date to beware of for the Indian fields. Which of the last two lines' dates belong to which tradition rests on the verse's grouping, which the print does not punctuate further.
