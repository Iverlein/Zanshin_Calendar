# WB p. 432: the dates to beware of for grammar (machine)

On p. 432 the verse gives the days to beware of for grammar (སྒྲ་རིག་སྤྱི་ཡི་དུས་གཉན, sgra rig spyi yi dus gnyan) by lunar date: for the general grammar the 8th, 14th, 16th, 23rd, 29th and the full and new moon days; Pāṇini's tradition adds the 13th; the Dbyangs can tradition the 28th and the 12th; and on the 27th one avoids explaining and hearing. The numerals are from the machine reading and are not checked on the scan; the count of dates here is the machine's, so the page is to be read on the scan before it is built on (the neighbouring list on the same page is the one white-beryl-ch34.md read for work 52).
