# The benefits of the fasting practice of Avalokiteśvara by the number of times done (Zhe chen rgyal tshab)

Zhe chen rgyal tshab sums up the benefits of the fasting practice (སྨྱུང་གནས, smyung gnas) of Avalokiteśvara from a text he calls the Great Benefits of the Fasting Practice and from the bodhisattva the text names in short Zla rgyal (ཟླ་རྒྱལ, zla rgyal; zgt.1:p446-450, etext only):

| Times done | Benefit |
| --- | --- |
| once | the evil of forty thousand aeons cleansed; the best birth among gods and men; a man is born in Sukhāvatī, a woman gains the qualities of the first stage |
| 8 | like a stream-enterer; birth in Sukhāvatī |
| 25 | like a once-returner; eighty thousand aeons cleansed |
| 50 | like a non-returner; eighty thousand aeons cleansed |
| 108 | equal to an arhat; a thousand million aeons of evil and veils cleansed; sure birth in Sukhāvatī before Amitāyus |

The body's fasting cleanses the evils of the body and shuts the birth of a hungry ghost; silence cleanses those of speech and shuts birth as an animal; the mind held in concentration cleanses those of mind and shuts birth in hell. Feeding one person who is fasting counts as feeding a bodhisattva of the eighth stage on the day of the single seat (གདན་གཅིག, gdan gcig), five hundred pratyekabuddhas after the fast.

- Days: none. The passage ties the fasting practice to no lunar day or month; it is given as what makes the one-day vow of the great days (the four festivals; the full moon, the new moon and the eighth) of measureless benefit when joined to it. Zhabs dkar names the days: the full moon, the new moon and the eighth (zkt:p582, etext).
- For whom: "a son or daughter of good family"; said in a passage on householders.
- Tradition: Nyingma author; the practice itself is common to the schools. Karma chags med holds that silence and the fasting practice are the action tantra's way and are not needed in the conduct tantra's daily restoring (rcz:p150-151, etext).

Rank 3. A rite text that states the fourth month, its first half, or the pairs of days was not read: the manuals BDRC's search shows (MW3CN5420, MW1KG13001, MW3CN4918, MW3CN5417) were not opened.
