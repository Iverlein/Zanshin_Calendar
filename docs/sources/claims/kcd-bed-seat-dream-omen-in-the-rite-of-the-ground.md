# The one dream omen in the Kālacakra texts read: the master sleeps on a blessed bed in the south, head west, and sees good, middling or bad signs; no hour, day, planet or season

The one dream omen found in the Kālacakra texts read is in the initiation chapter's rite of the ground (the Vimalaprabhā, དྲི་མེད་འོད, Dri med 'od, and the Padminī pañjikā, པདྨ་ཅན, Pad ma can, in verse). After the pegs and the protection wheel the master has the bed-seat blessed in the south, then lies with the head to the west and the feet to the east, and by the power of his meditation or by dream looks for signs «to know good and bad» (བཟང་ངན་ཤེས་པའི་ཕྱིར, bzang ngan shes pa'i phyir; the verse's wording). Good: Buddhas, bodhisattvas and goddesses with their emblems (the flute and the ḍāru drum are named in the verse), or vajra ḍākinīs and lords of wrath destroying the māras and obstructors on this side of the rim of the protection wheel. Middling: nothing seen, or a little. Bad: the hungry, thirst-tormented māra kind with dried-up bodies and knives, who delight in harming good people (the verse adds: who mock the wise). On a bad sign the master leaves the bed-seat, meditates the protection wheel in the east of the maṇḍala and gives the great food offering. The rite is the master's, and the disciple's if he sees it; no hour of the night (the text says only that the master lies down), no lunar day, no planet and no season is named.
