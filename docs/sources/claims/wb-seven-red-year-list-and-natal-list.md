# The seven-red: the year's list and the two-black-born's list

WB's seven-red (བདུན་དམར, bdun dmar) is laid twice. P. 403 (the year's mewa, the list for whoever arrives on it) says to beware of both bloodletting and moxa (གཏར་སྲེག་འཛེམ, gtar sreg 'dzem). P. 405 opens with «རང་སྐྱེས་སྨེ་བའི་བྱེ་བྲག་དང་། བསྟན་པའི་བབས་འབྲས་འདི་ལྟར་བརྗོད», the particulars of the natal mewa and the results of the arrival taught, and under it those born on the two black (ནག་སྐྱེས, nag skyes) who arrive on the seven-red are told: moxa good, bloodletting bad. So the second list is introduced as the natal mewa's particulars of the first; the print states no rule between them. The reading that fits its own heading, worked out here and not stated: the two-black-born take p. 405's line (moxa allowed, bloodletting not), everyone else p. 403's (neither). The line's head «ནག་སྐྱེས་བདུན་དམར་བབས་པ་ན» is read by Yigdzin-1 on the 1996 print only; the etext and MITRA drop it, and the Gser thang scan was not read at that line.
