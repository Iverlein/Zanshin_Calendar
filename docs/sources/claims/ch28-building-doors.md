# Building: the doors of sky and earth and their remedies

WB vol. 2, p. 42: building a house or fort. Avoid: the sky years dog and dragon and the pig and snake years (black years); for the owner, a year with obstacles, the progressed year (ལོག་མེན, log men) on a door of sky or earth, mewa 2–6, the trigrams *khon* and *kham*; the months dog, dragon, ox, sheep, pig, snake, black and *gnyan* months; black days «ཉི་ནག་དྲུག་གསུམ» [?], the earth lords' *gnyan*, bad digging days; the site's own obstacle and facing direction; bad hours.

The bad earth lords are the demon's house (འདྲེ་ཁྱིམ, 'dre khyim). The sky door harms men and elders, the earth door women and the young; by the year, the household is ruined; month, the master dies; day, the house falls; hour, children and servants; trigram, inauspicious; mewa, food, wealth and livestock; demon's house, illness and disputes. Remedies: for the year the *Hundred Thousand*; month, the *Eight Appearances*; day, the collected dhāraṇīs and the *Ornament of the Stem*; hour, the *Recitation of the Names* and the *Summary*; trigram, the *Avataṃsaka*; mewa, summoning fortune and the *Stream of Wealth*; demon's house, the *Crown Protuberance* reversal; sky door, Ākāśagarbha's hundred names; earth door, Kṣitigarbha's. Weigh gain against danger: no side is free of all bad.

For ploughing and sowing: a year without obstacle or executioner, no feud of the earth (ས་འཁོན, sa 'khon), power and luck rising.
