# Wangkhyi turns his face away at the day-time of the animal's day in each last month of a season

On p. 210 Wangkhyi is bad toward the south in summer's last month on the ox's day at its time, north in winter on the sheep's day-time, west in autumn on the dragon's, east in spring on the dog's; the short «ཉི་དུས» of the last three seasons is the «ཉི་མ་དུས་ཚོད» of the first, so the animal names both the day and the hour: the animal's day, and on it the animal's double-hour. It is not an ox hour of every day of the month. The page closes in three steps: in general the days of the four last months, in particular the grave days (དུར་ཉི), most of all the day-time of where he stays (གནས་པའི་ཉི་དུས); the four animals (ox, sheep, dragon, dog) are the four grave animals.
