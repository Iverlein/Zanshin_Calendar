# The rat day's short form «ཁ་ཡོ་ནས་འདྲེན» is WB's own, and the verb of the line is «strain»

The rat day's remedy of WB vol. 2, p. 224 has a short form in WB's own table of the twelve days (vol. 1, p. 133): «ཆང་རྫ་ལ་གནས། ཆང་མ་ལ་ངན། ཁ་ཡོ་ནས་འདྲེན།», on the Gser thang reprint read on the scan at 3x, the line breaking between «ཁ་ཡོ» and «ནས་འདྲེན»; the Bhutan collection (Thimphu 1981) copies the table twice. So the «ཁ» before «ཡོ་ནས» is WB's own wording and not a copyist's addition. The verb of «ཐེམ་བུར་མི་འཚག» is «འཚག» ('tshag), "to strain", not "to lay": the verse's first line has the beer strained for the dead at the jar («ཆང་བཙགས»), and the remedy says where the beer is not strained (the threshold) and, after it, what is done with it.
