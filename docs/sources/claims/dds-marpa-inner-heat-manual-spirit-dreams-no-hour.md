# Marpa's inner-heat manual calls a dream of a horse, dog or woman that loses the drop a spirit's work, with three antidotes and no hour (RD387)

In Marpa's collected works (vol. 4, the manual on the faults of the inner heat, `dds.19:p187-189`, etext) a dream of a horse, a dog, an ugly old woman or a woman with desire acted out, after which the drop is lost, is called the work of a spirit (གདོན, gdon). The cure has three antidotes (compassion and the gift of the body to the spirits against hatred, the unlovely skeleton against desire, emptiness against dullness), done three times by day and by night, and at bedtime asafoetida on four places and a white thread at the waist. The text names no hour of the night and does not use the words «midnight» (ནམ་ཕྱེད) or «power of spirits» (གདོན་དབང).
