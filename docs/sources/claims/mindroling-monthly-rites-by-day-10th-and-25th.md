# Mindroling's monthly order: the Guru's tenth on the 10th and the waning tenth feast on the 25th, the protectors on the 8th, 15th, 29th

The rule-book of the Mindroling assembly (mdb:p163-166, a Nyingma house, printed in the collected works of Gter bdag gling pa 'Gyur med rdo rje) lists what the assembly does within each month, by the day:

- 3rd: from dawn the smoke offering (bsang mchod) with the horn and pipe.
- 4th: the commemoration of the Omniscient Holder of Sutra and Mantra, with the recitation of the two root tantras (Guhyagarbha and Kabgyé Deshek Düpa).
- 8th: the smoke offering; the long-life rite of the Deathless Heart-essence Kundü.
- 10th: the offering of the tenth day by way of the peaceful and wrathful Guru (Gu ru zhi drag), with the extended supplication and the fulfilment-and-offering of the protectors.
- 12th: the commemoration of Khri lcam, the two recitations.
- 14th: the Phurba knife rite and the fulfilment of the planets and Vaiśravaṇa.
- 15th: the smoke offering; the seasonal offering of the Mother (Yum) by way of a painted scroll (Vajrasattva accumulation-offering), the common torma; a monthly memorial tea-day; the Prayer of Good Conduct; the waxing half's restoring and vow (gso sbyong); the full-moon day (gnam gang) the Lord of Life rite.
- 18th: the commemoration of the Omniscient Great One and his brother, the Eight Pronouncements.
- 21st: the seasonal offering of Ma gcig, the Deshek Kundü accumulation-offering by way of a scroll.
- 22nd: the commemoration of the Lord Lama, the peaceful and wrathful accumulation-offering by way of a scroll.
- 24th: the two recitations as before.
- 25th: the feast offering of the tenth of the waning half (mar ngo'i tshes bcu tshogs mchod), by way of Lama Sangdü, with the extended supplication and the fulfilment of the Ma ning.
- 29th: the Yama the Slayer rite with the torma of the Lord of Death, the common torma, and the averting of the nine.
- 8th, 15th and 29th: five monks make an extra seasonal torma of the Dharma protectors in the protectors' chapel; the 8th of both halves an extra torma of the keeper; the waning half's restoring and vow is kept without a break.

So in this house the Guru's tenth-day offering is on the 10th, and the feast offering of the waning half's tenth (mar ngo'i tshes bcu tshogs mchod) is on the 25th; the passage names no 11th or 26th. The numbers are the days of the month as the assembly counts them; the passage does not say how a skipped or doubled lunar day is reckoned.

Rank 3 (a Nyingma house's rule); it is the rule of one assembly, not a general rule. The passage is the etext only: the monthly list stands on pages whose scans (img. 163-167) were not served, and the OCR has garbled letters (the day before «gcig la» on p. 165 is lost; the «yig» before the 29th on p. 166). Other months (pp. 170-196) list their festivals and the winter and summer assemblies; the fifth month (p. 179) has a ninth-day offering «like the waxing tenth» and a tenth-day Lama Sangdü rite (p. 180), not read in full.
