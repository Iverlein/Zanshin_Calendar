# A 大雑書 with a dream section by image only (万代大雑書古今大成)

A 大雑書 can hold a dream section. The 万代大雑書古今大成 (Tenpō era, revised in Meiji) has one, headed 夢はんじ (dream judgments), 132 articles, each a plain "if one sees X, Y" (sky, sun and moon, thunder, snow, rain and the like in its first articles), with a preface naming the dream-diviners of old China and a charm of the Night-Ruling God (主夜神) to be written and fixed over the bed against bad dreams. The articles quoted (the first twelve) name no date, day of the cycle or season: the dream is read by its image alone. The rest of the 132 were not read here (they are in the other parts of the transcription).
