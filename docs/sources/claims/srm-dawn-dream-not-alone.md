# Śramaṇā rite: the dawn dream alone does not count; the answer is graded by its manner

In the Śramaṇā rite the dawn dream alone does not count. The text sets no hour of the night at which the answer must come; the practitioner lies down in the evening after the recitation, holding one matter in mind, and whatever answer comes in any of the nights of the rite is taken. The answer is graded by its manner, not by its hour: the best is a prophecy in person or an appearance in meditation experience (nyams snang), the middle a voice sounding in the sky, the lowest a dream (rmi lam). So a dream is the weakest way of answering here, and it is read together with the other two kinds.
