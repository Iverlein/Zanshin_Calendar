# The boasters' reckoning is the «phyi rtsis», and the fifth and sixth years fight the life force on the blocks

On p. 42 of WB vol. 2 the reckoning some boastful reckoners keep secret is the outer reckoning (ཕྱི་རྩིས, phyi rtsis) on both Lhasa blocks, not «བྱི་རྩིས». The fifth and sixth of the 360 starting points' years are carved as the 1996 print has them, both against the life force (སྲོག, srog): the blocks do not carry the grave and the executioner's grave in those places. The market simile is milk diluted («འོ་བཞིན་བསླད»).
