# KP's month panels: ++ follows the good sky-line gods, ×× the bad lords (read on one panel)

On KP's first month panel (the tiger month, BDRC I4CZ65599 img. 93, pool img. 91, left panel) the signs after the cells are plain crosses and pluses printed with the cell's words: «××» after the cells at the top left and the bottom right, «++» after the cell at the top right (whose word reads «ཤེལ་ཁང» (shel khang, the Crystal House) at 4×) and the bottom left. The Crystal House is one of the six gods of WB's yellow-black sky line (WB vol. 2, ch. 31, nos. 23-28, year-month-earth-lords.md): the god-house (གསས་ཁང, gsas khang), the golden power (གསེར་དབང, gser dbang), the sky treasury (གནམ་མཛོད, gnam mdzod), the Crystal House, Vaiśravaṇa (རྣམ་ཐོས, rnam thos) and the lord of life (ཚེ་དབང, tshe dbang), of which WB says that all works are good when they stand ("གསོན་གཤིན་ལས་རྣམས་ཐམས་ཅད་བཟང་"). In the machine's cell lists of the twelve panels (kp:i91-96b) «++» or «+» follows, every time, one of these six names (gsas khang, rnam thos, shel khang, gser dbang, tshe dbang), and «×» stands after «ཧལ་ཁྱི» (Hal-dog), a bad lord. The signs therefore read as good (++) and bad (××) marks of the cells' lords; the first wide cell's head word is then plausibly «ཚེ་དབང» (the lord of life), the sixth of WB's list, not «ཆོད་དབང». The reading is from one panel on the scan and the machine's lists elsewhere; no KP text states what the signs mean. Not settled.
