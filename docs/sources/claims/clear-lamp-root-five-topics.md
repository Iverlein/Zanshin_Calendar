# The five topics of the Clear Lamp's root are its five roots, earth-examination the fourth

For question 656. The root of the Clear Lamp's reckoning, «རྩ་བ་སྐབས་དོན་ལྔ་པ» (rtsa ba skabs don lnga pa), "the root in five topics", is the first of the six treatises Duhar Nagpo made. Nor brang O rgyan lists its five, called roots, in his history in KD vol. 1 (kd.1:p643-646): the general starting points (སྐོར་འགོ་སྤྱི, skor 'go spyi), the reckoning of the bright and the dark (དར་གུད, dar gud), the corpse-treasury reckoning (དུར་མཛོད, dur mdzod), earth-examination, good and bad (ས་དཔྱད, sa dpyad) and the evil means that kill the enemy (ངན་ཐབས་དགྲ་གཤེད, ngan thabs dgra gshed). Tshul khrims rgyal mtshan's history in the same volume (etext p. 526, VEIE0OPI2F246A26_I4715) agrees that earth-examination is the fourth of the five («རྩ་བ་སྐབས་དོན་ལྔ་པ་ལས་བཞི་པ་ས་དཔྱད་ཀྱི་རྩ་བ»), as it is fourth in Nor brang's list.

So the five topics of WB's verse (wb.1:p249-250) are these five, and not the "five things of the elements" (nature, etymology, divisions, definition, function) that the root's own opening lists (q656's first narrowing). The two historians do not say which three of the five were divided into four: Nor brang repeats WB's verse in prose (a starting point of the root dropped, three topics split into four, the two earth-examination roots bound into one) and names none. Read from the etext only (state etext); the root's own pages stay unread by eye.
