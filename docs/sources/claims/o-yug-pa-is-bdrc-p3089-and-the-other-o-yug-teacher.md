# «འོ་ཡུག་པ» of the death-dream remark: BDRC's P3089 'U yug pa Bsod nams seng ge (died 1253) fits the Sakya company; no Sakya record gives the remark's saying

BDRC's person record P3089 gives «'u yug pa Bsod nams seng ge» (also «'u yug pa Rig pa'i seng ge», «Rig pa'i seng ge»): a Sakya master of logic, chiefly a disciple of Sa skya Paṇḍita, died 1253, teacher of six persons in the record. The record has the two names for one man, so the two readings of the earlier claim (Bsod nams seng ge or Rig pa'i seng ge) may be one. In the snippets of BDRC's full-text search for «འོ་ཡུག་པ» (2026-11-21, 44 hits) the same man stands as seat-holder of Sa skya's monastery with Shar pa Shes rab 'byung gnas while Sa paṇ was in Mongolia (Sde pa Rin spungs pa's «རྒྱུད་པའི་སྒྲོལ་ཁ་»: «gdan sa bo kha 'dzin la 'o yug pa zhes pa»).

A different man of the same place is «'o yug gi dge bshes Don pa» (Ngag dbang kun dga' bsod nams's and Shes rab rin chen's collected works): asked about the experiences of Ma gcig Ri ma, he judges them («rgyal 'gong gcig gnod pa 'dra / brgyad stong pa gcig thon zer», "like a harm from a king-spirit; one from the Eight Thousand will come out"), so a Sakya record does give sayings of an 'O yug teacher on how to judge signs, but this saying is not the dream remark's and he is a dge bshes, not the «'O yug pa» of the seat-holders.
