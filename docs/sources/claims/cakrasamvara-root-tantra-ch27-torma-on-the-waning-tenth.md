# The Cakrasamvara root tantra verse on the tenth stands in ch. 27 of Toh 368, at the end of the chapter on conduct, vow, offering and torma

The torma verse that Padma dkar po, Zhe chen rgyal tshab, Ngag dbang kun dga' bsod nams and Dpal sprul give as «the Root Tantra of Cakrasaṃvara» stands in the Derge Kanjur's Smaller Śaṃvara (Toh 368) at its twenty-seventh chapter, «the rite of conduct, vow, offering and torma» (spyod pa dang brtul zhugs dang mchod pa dang gtor ma'i cho ga), at the end of the chapter, Derge p. 467 (lsv:p467). The Derge wording: «མར་གྱི་ངོ་ཡི་ཚེས་བཅུ་དང་། །ཟླ་བ་ཡར་ངོའི་གང་གྱུར་ལ། །མྱུར་དུ་འབད་པས་མཆོད་བྱ་སྟེ།», "on the tenth of the waning half and on whatever occurs of the waxing half, strive quickly to offer". So the waning tenth is named in the tantra itself; for the waxing half the tantra says «gang gyur» (what occurs), and the tenth is the reading of the quoters (Dpal sprul «ཟླ་བ་ཡར་ངོའི་ཚེས་བཅུ་ལ»). What is offered is the torma, «according to one's wish and work, by the rite», with the intoxicating flesh of the Vajra Sow, to the glorious Heruka, who grants the supreme when pleased; at night the vow-holder gives it with loosened hair, naked, and the Mothers are pleased. The chapter says nothing of a feast (tshogs) or of the hours of the day; the waxing tenth by day and the waning tenth by night is Dpal sprul's and the quoters' arrangement, of which the chapter has only the night torma.
