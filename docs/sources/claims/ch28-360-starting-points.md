# The 360 starting points done rightly

WB vol. 2, pp. 42–43: the 360 starting points (སྐོར་འགོ, skor 'go) "done rightly": the life force is sought from the enemy element, the body from one's own, the power from the son, the la from the mother, the grave from one's own, the executioner's grave from the enemy; where life force and power are both complete and in accord; male years from a male year, female from a female. From that year the person counts his age downward; the years from where it arrives fight, in turn, his life force, body, power, la, life force (as printed), and the sixth meets the life force. The pebble of each: white, no attachment; black, an attachment: of death (life force), illness (body), wealth (power), enemies (la and luck); one's own and the enemy's executioner likewise. When black, the rites aimed at it.

Calculable from the person's year, element and age.
