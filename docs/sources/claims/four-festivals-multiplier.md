# The four great festivals' multiplier: a hundred koṭi or a hundred million

The texts agree that virtue done on the Buddha's four great festivals (དུས་ཆེན་བཞི, dus chen bzhi) is multiplied beyond that of the month's good days, and differ on the number.

- RCZ (rcz:p286-288, scan): a hundred koṭi-fold (བྱེ་བ་བརྒྱ་འགྱུར, bye ba brgya 'gyur), that is a thousand million; "many sūtras agree", none named; one torma given is a hundred koṭi of tormas.
- DTN (dtn:i316, scan): a hundred million (ས་ཡ་ཕྲག་བརྒྱ, sa ya phrag brgya), in a verse quoted without its source, with the sum worked out: one handful of barley bears 833,333 loads, 6 measures and 4 handfuls, which is a hundred million handfuls exactly.
- DGK (dgk:i491, etext only): "countless koṭis", no number.

The two numbers differ tenfold and both texts are rank 3; none is chosen. Both numbers are given on the authority of the same two scriptures, the minor section and the Nirvāṇa, and DTN's "hundred million" is the wording of a verse that other texts credit to the great paṇḍita Śākyaśrī: [shakyashri-verse-on-the-festivals-and-eclipses.md](shakyashri-verse-on-the-festivals-and-eclipses.md) (second wave, 2026-11-16). The scripture's own sentence was not found.

The festivals' days in RCZ: the 1st to the 15th of the month of mchu (མཆུ, mchu), the miracles; the 15th of sa ga (ས་ག, sa ga), the entry into the womb, the going forth and the awakening; the 4th of chu stod (ཆུ་སྟོད, chu stod), the turning of the wheel; the 15th of tha skar (ཐ་སྐར, tha skar), the consent to descend, and the 22nd, the descent, counted as one. RCZ says that India and Tibet disagreed on the months until the Kashmiri paṇḍita Śākyaśrī wrote a treatise identifying them. DGK names the same four by their months, with the descent in the month of dbyug pa (དབྱུག་པ, dbyug pa). RCZ's Saga full moon does not name the parinirvāṇa, which the app's reading after Edition Rabten does.

Neither text speaks of the whole first month or the whole fourth month as multiplied; the first fortnight of the month of mchu is the miracles' own time in RCZ.
