# The Mchog dga' says a dream is clear only when sun and moon stand seven houses apart, the question's hour

The Mchog dga' (མཆོག་དགའ, Mchog dga') on the Dbyangs 'char (དབྱངས་འཆར) says there is not a dream at every time: counted from the house of the sun the seventh holds the moon, or counted from the moon's house the seventh holds the sun, and then a question about a dream finds a clear dream; otherwise not. Some say: when the other luminary is between the first and the seventh a dream is clear, and when sun and moon stand reversed or as one there is none. The author holds that one takes whichever agrees with experience, the former giving few dreaming times and the latter many. This is a rule about when a question is answered with a dream, in terms of the moon's and the sun's houses at the hour of the question (counting the first house as one, the seventh from the sun's sign is the sign opposite it, so the first rule reads as: the moon in the sign opposite the sun, about the full moon; that count is my reading, the text does not say it), not a rule that a dream on a given lunar day is true.
