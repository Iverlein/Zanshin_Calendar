# The commentaries' lists of dream images by constituent overlap the Mañjuśrī root tantra's for phlegm and bile and differ for wind (RD384)

The lists of dream images by constituent in the commentaries on the Zab mo nang don overlap the Mañjuśrī root tantra's lists for phlegm and bile and differ for wind (RD384, 2026-11-10, etext only).

The commentaries (rrd.13:p286-289, rrd.14:p232-234, rrd.15:p235-236, rrd.17:p352-355) give for phlegm: water, snow, pearls, white earth or quarters, white clothes, a woman, an elephant, silver; for bile: blazing fire, red and yellow clothes, red and yellow earth, gold, monks in red-yellow; for wind: blue substances such as ne'u sing and blue clothes, black clothes, a black crow, flying in the sky, riding a horse, a blue jewel, clear awareness, or all things unstable. The root tantra (as RD370 read it, mmk.1:p397-399, p399-400, p400-401) lists for the first watch, phlegm: water, the sea, crystal, pearls and snow, white clothes, elephants and bulls, silver; for the second, bile: blazing fire, the ruby, a golden sky and earth, gold, yellow clothes; for the third, wind: light all round, going in the sky, leaping, climbing trees, reciting, speaking, sharp, hot and sour food, dancing, a restless mind. So phlegm and bile agree in kind, and in several of the same objects; for wind the commentaries share only the going in the sky. The commentaries give no watch to the lists: they hang them on the constituent, and put the times in the three-part verse (claim rrd-verse-of-dusk-midnight-dawn-is-a-commentators-addition).
