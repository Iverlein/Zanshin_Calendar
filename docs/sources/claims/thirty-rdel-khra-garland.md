# «རྡེལ་ཁྲ» between the seventh and eighth checks, in WB and in the Garland of Stars

# «རྡེལ་ཁྲ» in the grave site's verse: the *Garland* has it between the seventh and eighth checks, as WB does

The *Garland of Stars* (རྒྱུ་སྐར་ཕྲེང་བ, rgyu skar phreng ba; KD vol. 3, p. 541, etext) has the grave site's closing lines in WB's order and almost its words: after the seventh check (the living's pebble white and the dead's black, the ruin sri (འཕུང་སྲི, 'phung sri) harms), «རྡེལ་ཁྲ་ས་བདག་འཁྲུག་གཏོ་བྱ། ཐམས་ཅད་ནག་ན་རུ་ཌ་ཡི། གཏོ་ནག་མགོན་པོ་གཡུལ་བཟློག་བྱ།», then the eighth (question 215).

What this adds to RD54's reading (claim wb1-p468-rdel-khra-whole-board):

- The verse is not WB's invention: it stands in a second work, with «རྡེལ་ཁྲ» and «ཐམས་ཅད་ནག» spelled as WB spells them, and its next point, the three-headed earth lord's, makes the same two rites the rites of its speckled and its black reading: the speckled asks "whether the earth lord is disturbed or not" and is mended by the great disturbance rite, the black asks "whether Rudra is slain" (kd.3:p541). So the speckled and the black here are the colours of the rites that the twenty-sixth point goes on to lay, which fits a whole-board reading (speckled = mixed, all black = all eight black), as RD54 judged.
- Against it: in both texts the two lines come between the seventh and the eighth checks, not after the eighth. A verdict on the whole board would be expected after all eight checks; placed so, it can as well be the seventh check's two pebbles (the living's and the dead's) read as speckled when they differ and as "all black" when both are black. The *Garland* does not say which; neither reading is excluded, and the whole board stays the likelier by the twenty-sixth point's colours.
- The same word is used elsewhere for a pebble made of two: the *Garland* (printed p. 508, the *Moonbeams*' fifth chapter, the year's round) lays a speckled pebble taken "from the upper rank's body and the lower's element"; that is a rule of another reckoning and is not shown to be this one.
