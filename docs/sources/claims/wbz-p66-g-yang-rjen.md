# U. Phalgunī's entry: one stack with o, and «g.yang rjen», on the blocks

In WB vol. 2, pp. 65–66 the mansion where U. Phalgunī (དབོ, dbo) stands is one stack with the vowel o on both Lhasa blocks, ས over a lower letter not legible at these resolutions; it is not «དབོ» written as two letters. The word after «གཡང» in U. Phalgunī's entry is «རྗེན», ར over ཇ with e, then ན, on both blocks; Yigdzin-1's «རྟེན» on the Taikhang is a misreading. What «ཚགས་བྱ» does to it is not stated.
