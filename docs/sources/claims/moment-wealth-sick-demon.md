# Wealth, the sick and the demon by the moment's element

WB vol. 2, pp. 168–170: wealth, the sick and the demon by the moment's element, with the test of whether each is present.

| Moment's element | Wealth | Illness and its source | Demon and rite |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| wood | dry goods, wooden ware in the east of the store; donkeys, goats, sheep, a blue dog | wind, sinews, stomach and liver, eyes, right ribs; from east or west a smith kinsman or maternal uncle carpenter; vegetables, right rib meat, tsampa-mash or beer | theurang, oath-breaking sri or nāgas following wooden ware; turn them back from the east |
| fire | copper ware, red cloven-hoofed animals, a horse, a chestnut | heart, intestines, blood, tongue, chest, neck, fevers; from south or north a sister, widow or nephew; neck meat, beer, cool drinks | tsen, mu, rock sen-mo following copper, horses, red things; tsen thread-cross |
| earth | yellow dry goods, a good field, a yellow dog, greys | stomach and spleen, flesh, sores, boils, blocked nose, water illness, colds, left hand; from east or south-west an old kinswoman, grandchild or guest; fried pastry, fat meat, building work | earth lords, *dge bsnyen*, oath-breaking gongpo from south-west and east, following yellow animals; cure the earth lords |
| iron | iron or silver ware, white goods, sheep, dogs, white cloth, five or more | lungs, tumours, bones, ears, lung fever; from south and west a maternal uncle, sibling, potter, smith or defiled person; porridge, head and upper meat, left rib, butter and white food | a demon or king spirit; their thread-crosses |
| water | baskets, black things in the north of the store; mares, black horses, yaks, cattle, dogs, about three | water illness, kidneys and waist, knees, legs, teeth, ears; a northern kinswoman, widow or black woman of the father's house | demon, oath-breaking sri or *byur 'dre* from the north, following a widow's goods; lake-sman thread-cross |

Present or not: wealth by the asker's body and power, rising or declining on the moment; the *bya ra* cutting body and power, none (p. 168). The demon: the moment cutting the *byed yor*, a harmful demon; cutting outward, no harm; generating inward, guardian gods; generating outward, harm; same element, that element's demon (p. 169). The sick: the sick one's life force rising on the moment, no death; declining, reckon the cutting too; middling, a long illness (p. 169). Where the illness is: the asker's organ whose element declines on the moment (p. 170).
