# WB pp. 303-304: the Kalacakra seed-letter sites and the sites of the Phug uncle and nephew differ on most dates; both climb from the foot to the head and come back

Read 2026-11-01 on the 1996 scan (I1KG12907 img. 311-312, 2.6x, by eye for layout and the doubtful vowel signs only) against the two machine readings (Yigdzin-1 and MITRA, which agree on the body places of all thirty dates). The two lists of the life-spirit sites (bla gnas) are two systems, not one. The Kalacakra list sets a seed letter on a limb joint: toe joints 1-3, ankle, knee, thigh joint (dates 1-6), finger joints 1-3, wrist, elbow, shoulder head (7-12), throat, chin, forehead (13-15), the crown between the 15th and 16th, then nape, heart and the arm and leg joints back down to the first toe joint on the 30th; the waning half mirrors the waxing one, the date d meeting the date 31-d on the same joint. The way of the uncle and nephew of Phug (ཕུག་པ་ཁུ་དབོན, phug pa khu dbon; "Khadbon" in the first notes was a machine error, RD157), given "as in the Precious Garland", names parts of a man by date, each with the horse's part beside it: sole and toes (1), heel joint (2), calf (3), waist (4), groin hollow (5), thigh (6), knee (7), above the kidneys (8), ribs (9), shoulder top (10), forearm top (11), palm (12), neck (13), behind the ears and cheek (14), the whole body (15), then back through nape, neck, palm, forearm, shoulder, ribs, kidneys, knee, thigh, groin, above the knee, calf, ankle (16-28), the face of the forearm (29) and the whole body (30). So the lists agree only in their course (foot at the start, head about the 15th, foot again at the end, the waning half running back) and where two places touch (the 1st on the foot, the 6th at the thigh); on the other dates the place differs by one or more joints (the 3rd: toe joint against calf; the 4th: ankle against waist; the 5th: knee against groin; the 7th: finger against knee). The Phugpa waning half repeats its waxing places by the mirror date 30-d (18 palm = 12, 19 = 11, 20 = 10, 21 = 9, 22 = 8, 23 = 7, 24 = 6, 25 = 5, 27 = 3, 28 = 2), not by 31-d as the Kalacakra list does. Since RD157 (2026-11-05) every place and letter is read on the scan (wb.2:p303-304), with the vowel signs of the 1st, 3rd, 5th, 9th, 22nd, 26th and 28th still doubtful at its foot; the comparison with ch. 21 of WB and with Kalacakra's own text was not made.
