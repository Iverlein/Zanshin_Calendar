# The household's general pebbles: black the more, and whose element is dear

WB p. 415's «བཟའ་ཚང་སྤྱི་རྡེལ» (bza' tshang spyi rdel) stands in the closing section of the chapter, after the dead's years and their rites, and is followed at once by what is dear to each: men the life force, young children the body, women the power, the high-born and the virtuous the luck, and «དེ་ལྟར་རྡེལ་ནག་ནན་གྱིས་བཅོས», so remedy the black pebbles firmly. The line «དཀར་ནག་གཉིས་ལས་ནག་པོ་མང་» is therefore a condition (where of white and black the black are the more), and the order of remedies (རིམ་གྲོ, rim gro) follows it, each member's dearest of the four being remedied first. The general pebbles are the household's pebbles taken together, as in the household round and the household's mewa verdicts of pp. 407–408; no text counts them otherwise. The verse is copied word for word in KD vol. 3 (printed p. 679), and p. 416 goes on to say that only the results of the pebbles' own rites are shown in brief, more being taught in the tantras and rite-texts.
