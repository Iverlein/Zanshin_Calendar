# The second examination: the inner reason by the trigram of death

WB vol. 2, p. 58: the trigram's (སྤར་ཁ, spar kha) reason, in general the living reckoning's protections neglected and an illness or demons (གདོན, gdon) striking, otherwise an enemy or a sudden mishap, read with the third general point; Mipham's notes: as the obstacle reckoning's trigram part, "a fault of the time", or without it a sudden mishap (p. 503).
