# P.3908 has twenty-three chapters, the day chapters at 19-21, no ten-stem chapter; the booklet ends without a colophon

P.3908 has twenty-three chapters, in order: the heavens (1), the earth (2), mountain and plants (3), water, fire and thieves (4), rank and brothers (5, doubtful), the body, comb and mirror (6), food (7), Buddha, the Way and music (8), estate and house (9), clothing (10), animals (11), dragons and snakes (12), swords and bows (13), husband and wife (14), towers, household things and money (15), boats, carts and markets (16), life, death and sickness (17), tombs (18); then the three chapters of the day, the branch (19), the double-hour (20) and the day officer (21); then the taboos of bad dreams (22) and the driving off of a bad dream (23). The preface says "more than twenty chapters" and the booklet ends at 23, with the Heart Sutra copied after it and no colophon; there is no chapter of the ten stems. The chapter numbered 21 is the day officers, as the page says (廿一); so the manuscript does not give a ten-stem chapter and the numbering of the other reference list (stems 21, officers 22) is not the manuscript's. Whether other copies of the book have a ten-stem chapter is not settled by this copy (q1053 stays open as to them). Doubts: chapter headings 5, 11 and 14 are partly read; the bodies of chapters 3 to 18 were not copied.
