# The mixed kind's last clause: Palri «མ་བྱེད», the etexts' «མ་ཤེས», the other prints' «མ་ཕྱེད»

In the mixed kind's last clause the Palri Parkhang print (img. 542, by eye at 3×) reads «མ་བྱེད» with a plain ཡ-subjoined stack, e vowel and ད; its own sense is "if one does not do it". The Rin chen gter mdzod and Shechen etexts have «མ་ཤེས» ("is not recognised"). On the other prints the machine (Yigdzin-1) reads «མ་ཕྱེད» (phyed, to separate): Kazi (typeset, img. 559), Zhe chen and Ngodrup (hand), all by machine, and Sbas-yul «མ་ཆེད». On Kazi the stack after «མ» looks boxed like ཕ, not settled by eye at this resolution; the hands were not read by eye. So the better-supported wording of the clause is «མ་ཕྱེད» (not told apart), which agrees in sense with the etexts' «མ་ཤེས» (not recognised): a mixed dream that cannot be sorted into good or bad is to be taken as a trick. Palri's «བྱེད» is the outlier and reads most likely as a slip of བ for ཕ; if taken as written, "if one does not make it [out]" gives the same practical sense. In neither reading does the clause say that the mixed dream is not to be followed. The ཕ/བ reading of Kazi and the hands is open.
