# Birth by the weekday

Birth by the weekday (WB vol. 2, pp. 379–380): the text gives for each of the seven days a short life-and-character verse for a boy and a girl. Sun: long-lived if he lives seven days or months; girl rich. Moon: boy rich and strong, girl beautiful. Mars: sinful, angry, hard to rear; girl dies. Mercury: sickly, boy bad to his mother, girl brings poverty to the parents though herself powerful in food. Jupiter: clear-minded, learned, dharma; girl long-lived. Venus: boy long-lived, rich; girl sick yet glorious. Saturn: handsome, little merit. The results are those of the verses as read; Mercury's name is «ལྷག་པ» in the Gser thang reading (the machine reading has «ཉག་པར»).
