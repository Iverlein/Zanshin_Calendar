# Peking and Narthang read rta la mo where the Derge text has rta bla mo

Peking and Narthang agree against the Derge etext and the Dpe bsdur ma text (whose note records that the two prints read «ལ» for its «བླ») on the word in "a horse, la mo, a pot-belly, a water creature seized": they read «རྟ་ལ་མོ», without the subjoined la of «བླ». Peking by eye at 3× on a 40-pixel scan, Narthang by eye at 8×. The Derge scan cannot tell the two spellings apart, so the Derge reading stands only on its etext and on the Dpe base text, which follows the Derge. The sense of «ལ་མོ» is not known and the verdict (victory or goods, or a son) is not changed by the spelling.
