# The doubtful syllables of WB vol. 2, pp. 53–55 on the Taikhang and Zhol blocks

On the Lhasa blocks: p. 53's «དྭའུ» is carved as printed (ད with wa-zur, then འུ), «ཁྱོ» has ya-btags, not ra; «ན» in «བྲག་དམར་ན་ལ» is Yigdzin-1's reading of the Taikhang (WBZ worn there). P. 54's stack «བརྫེ» is ར over ཇ or ཛ, not ད (so «བརྫེ» or «བརྗེ»; the flag of ཛ not seen). P. 55: «རྒན» a ར-headed stack before ན, «ཡོ» plain, «སྡེར» as printed; «སྦོས» is ས over a square-headed letter with o, བ or ཀ not told apart.
