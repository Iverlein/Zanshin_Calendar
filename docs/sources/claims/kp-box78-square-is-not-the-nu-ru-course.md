# KP's square on img. 78 matches neither the general nor the detailed course by direction

The square of KP's img. 78 (the Bhutan print's clean setting: east 2, 4, 19, 27; north-east 9, 22; north 11, 17, 24, 26; north-west 6, 16, 21, 29; west 5, 18; south-west 1, 8, 23; south 10, 12, 14, 25, 28; south-east 3, 7, 13, 15, 20, 30) was tested date by date against WB's general nu ru course (rahu.md, *The general course*): the square's direction is the course's starting direction on 9 dates (1, 2, 9, 10, 11, 12, 20, 24, 25) and its direction of ending on 3 (4, 5, 28), since the course's end is always opposite its start, the square is not that course either way (about 7 would fit by chance). The detailed course's entries (4, 6, 8, 11, 12, 14, 15, 17, 18, 21, 22, 24, 25, 27, 29, 1) fit no better (4 and 18 end where the square puts them, 12 and 25 likewise, 15, 22 and 29 do not). So the square counts another reckoning of Rāhu's place by date; the middle cell «སྤྱི་བརྒྱུད། ཚེས་གྲངས» is "general lineage, the dates", not the nu ru course by name. Narrows question 148: the course is not the nu ru course nor the detailed one by direction of setting out or of ending; which one is open, perhaps the inner course by date and mansion of p. 238, whose text was not found in a table.
