# WB ch. 35 p. 448: the order in which the calculation is to be learned

Page 448 gives the order in which the author would have a student learn the calculation: first the fixed rule for the year and its argument; then the five limbs, Rāhu (སྒྲ་གཅན, sgra gcan), the five planets of the seers and the six places, with the pith of the combinations of time; then the three kinds of days (ཞག་པོ་གསུམ, zhag po gsum) and the use of each; then how days, months and years turn, by the "emptiness-entering" steps (སྟོང་འཇུག, stong 'jug), the half-pulling (ཕྱེད་འགུགས, phyed 'gugs) diagrams, the four seasons, the sun's turn and the 360 junctions of the days; then the exact steps and the works of Nor bzang rgya mtsho, and last the White Beryl. The number of conversion points is given as twenty-five (འགྱུར་འགོ, 'gyur 'go) and the commentary to follow as Padma dkar po's. The page names the topics only and gives no figure of the calculation.
