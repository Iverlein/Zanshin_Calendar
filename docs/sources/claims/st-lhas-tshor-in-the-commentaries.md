# ལྷས་ཚོར་དུས་ཏེ in the commentaries that reach ch. 81: the time of the feeling, the dream the start of the harm

RD303 (2026-11-10), etext. For the clause «ལྷས་ཚོར་དུས་ཏེ» the commentaries give no gloss of ལྷས་ itself. The Blue Beryl and Khro ru gloss the clause as «ལྷས་ཚོར་བའི་དུས», the time of the feeling, and the sister clause «རང་ཚོར་དུས་ཡིན» as «རང་གིས་ཚོར་བའི་དུས་ཡིན», the time one feels oneself. In their outline the Blue Beryl, Skyem pa and Dar mo name the dream «ལྷས་ཚོར་གྱི་དུས» and the marks of body, speech and mind «རང་ཚོར་གྱི་དུས»; Skyem pa and Dar mo add that the dream is the time the spirit's harm begins and the marks of the three doors are the harm already struck. A glossary to Sde srid's Supplement (snippet only) has «ལྷས་ཚོར་ནི་ལྷ་འདྲེའི་རྟགས་ཀྱིས་ཚོར་བ». All of this fits ལྷས as the agent of the god or spirit, as st-lhas-tshor-felt-by-the-god reads it, and none is a gloss of the word; no commentary has ལུས. The Blue Beryl's etext once has ལྟས་ཚོར་བའི་དུས་; the page is not open. The last letter of ལྷས་ཚོར་ on the Chag-po-ri print (q893) was not settled again.
