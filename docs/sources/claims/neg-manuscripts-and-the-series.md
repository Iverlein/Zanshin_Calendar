# Negelein's manuscripts a–w and which of them carry the Svapnohana's five-span series (RD272)

Negelein's letters (Introduction pp. XIX–XX, read on the page images by RD269 and RD272) name 20 dream texts; which carry a series of spans, and which series, by what he prints in his notes to 1.15–17 (neg:p14-16) and what the *Śabdakalpadruma* has (skd:p470-471):

| Letter | What it is | Series printed | The Svapnohana's (year, 8 months, 3 months, half a month, 10 days)? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| a | Oxford Bodleian MS Sansk. d 215 (the Bodleian's record, seen as a search summary: paper, A.D. 1845, a short treatise on dreams) | year, 8 months, 3 months, **a month**; garbled dawn line | no: a month for the fourth watch |
| b | *Svapnādhyāya* of the *Brahmavaivartapurāṇa*: the *Śabdakalpadruma* vol. 5 pp. 470–1, the Calcutta Śaka 1812 print, Leipzig MS 1179 as base | year, 8, 3, **half a month**, 10 days | **yes** (b 5–6); the *Śabdakalpadruma* lines read by RD272 are the same |
| c, d, e | the same Purāṇa, other chapters or the manuscript Leipzig 1179 against the printed 2.82 | not printed in the notes to 1.15–17 | not shown |
| f | Leipzig MS 1180, called the *Svapnādhyāya* of the *Matsya* | year, 8 (or 6), 3, a month, 10 days at dawn | no |
| h | *Agnipurāṇa* 229 (as the *Adbhutasāgara* quotes) | year, 6, 3, a month | no |
| i | Leipzig MS 1178, "*bṛhaspatiproktaṃ svapnādhyāyam*" (neg:p32) | year, 8 months, three fortnights, a month, 10 days | no (three fortnights, a month) |
| m, n, q | Leipzig MSS 1176, 1177 and the Ācāramayūkha's *Svapnādhyāya* | like f | no |
| s | *Matsyapurāṇa* 241.2ff. (Calcutta 1876) | year, 6, 3, a month | no |
| r | Chambers 326 (*Parāśarāmaprakāśa*, *Nidrāmayūkha*) | the half-verse of dawn ascribed to Bṛhaspati (fol. 151a); quotes the *Gaṇeśakhaṇḍa* ch. 33 as the *Śabdakalpadruma* does | not printed |
| g, k, o, p, u, v, w | *Bhaviṣyapurāṇa*, Suśruta 1.29, Chambers 608, 793 o, Tod 51, Vāgbhaṭa in the *Śabdakalpadruma*, *Agnipurāṇa* 231 | not printed in the notes to 1.15–17 | not shown |

So **only the Brahmavaivarta's text carries the five-span series** among the texts Negelein collated; the Bṛhaspati *Svapnādhyāya* (i) is near it (eight months, ten days at dawn) but gives three fortnights and a month, and a manuscript (a), "cf. AP 68.2.58f.", gives the eight months with a month. Only a part of the Brahmavaivarta's chapters is shown (the *Gaṇeśakhaṇḍa* chapter 33 is Paraśurāma's bad dreams, not this series). The Śaunaka *Kārikā* Negelein cites by folios (69a–74a, a manuscript he does not list among his letters, with śānti rites and a dream section at 73a–b) is read in the pages cited (neg:p17-19 and the text layer) and has no span.

Dates: Negelein's own Jagaddeva manuscripts are C (Saṃvat 1687, 29 leaves) and B (undated, "fairly young"); the Bodleian dates a to A.D. 1845; the others are undated in the pages read.

RD277 (2026-11-09) adds: the Bṛhaspati *Svapnādhyāya* of the Penn manuscript Item 1871, of Item 673 (dated 1824), of the Srinagar volume and of the Ramtek M 856 does not read the "three fortnights" of i: all four read year, eight months, three months, a month, ten days, the series of Negelein's a and f; the "three fortnights" of i is found in none of the open copies. The Penn Item 2730 and the Leipzig 1178 were not read. See penn-odia-svapnadhyaya-series.md.
