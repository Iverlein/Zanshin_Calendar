# Birth by the zodiac house

Birth by the zodiac house (WB vol. 2, pp. 382–383): twelve verses, each with an age at which the child must live through, a lifespan, the month, date and weekday of a deadly time and the cause. For example, the sheep's house (Aries, with Taṭkāla): ten days, ten months, ten, eighteen, twenty or thirty years; if he does not die he lives long; a Mars weekday at the thirteenth of Smindrug month, death at midnight by fire. The ox's house: twenty days, twenty months; ninety years; winter or Mars weekday, a lotus-bile illness at dusk. The twelve houses are given with Aries, Taurus, Gemini («འཁྲིག་པ»), Cancer («ཀརྞ»), Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius, Capricorn, Aquarius and Pisces. The dates of deadly times are those of the verses and are read once on the scan only in part; they are not yet fit to build on.
