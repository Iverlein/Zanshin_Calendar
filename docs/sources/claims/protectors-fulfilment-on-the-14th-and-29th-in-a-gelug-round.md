# The Dharma protectors' fulfilment on the 14th and the 29th in a Gelug monastery's round; the 29th in the customaries of every school

The Dharma protectors' fulfilment (ཆོས་སྲུང་གི་བསྐང་བ, chos srung gi bskang ba) falls on the 14th and the 29th in the round of the month's days that the eighth Dalai Lama gives for the monastery Tshe mchog gling (ygr:i374, scan): the 2nd Guhyasamāja, the 8th the Medicine Buddha, the 10th Cakrasaṃvara, the 14th the protectors' fulfilment, the 15th Vijaya and the Elders, the 25th Bhairava, the 27th Guhyasamāja with the names, the 29th the protectors' fulfilment, the new moon Vijaya and the Elders.

- Days: the 14th and the 29th (protectors); nothing on the 9th, 11th, 24th or 26th. The 10th and the 25th stand for Cakrasaṃvara and Bhairava, not for the guru and the ḍākinīs as in the Nyingma lists.
- For whom: the monks of one Gelug monastery, a rule of the house; the 29th's rite is the same one as the 14th's.
- The 29th as the protectors' day recurs in the monastic customaries that BDRC's full-text search returns as snippets (read as snippets only, the etexts being search-only or open and not opened here; the passages are not recorded): the Sku 'bum succession (the 29th the fulfilment, "bskang gso"), Dbyangs can dga' ba'i blo gros's commentary on the *Snyan me long* (the 8th Mi 'khrugs pa, the 15th Kun rig, the 25th Gsang 'dus, the 29th the fulfilment), the Do shal history of Gser smyug (the 8th Medicine Buddha, the 15th Kun rig, the 29th the fulfilment-petition), Mnga' ris dgon sde lists (the 25th gcod tshogs and Phag mo, the 29th the extended fulfilment-petition), an autobiography (the empowerments of the 28th Mahākāla Gur, the 29th Mahākāla Gri gug with Jñāna), and a book of monastic rules (the 29th the four-armed Protector's fulfilment with the lords of the ground, Rgyal and Btsan). Houses of several schools (the authors named are Gelug, Drikung, Karma Kagyu and Nyingma by the pool's sources) keep the 29th; the 14th is the Gelug round's second day.
- Together with fulfilment-offering-on-the-twenty-ninth (Zhe chen rgyal tshab, the Yang ri rule): the 29th, the day before the new moon, is the one day of the lunar month on which every school read gives the protectors a rite; the 9th, 11th, 24th and 26th are Palden Lhamo's in Karma chags med alone.
