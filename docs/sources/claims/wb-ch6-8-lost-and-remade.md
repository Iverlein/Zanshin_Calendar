# Chapters 7 and 8 say of themselves that their methods were lost and remade

Chapter seven says of itself, in its colophon (p. 50), that its method was lost in the middle (*bar skabs su nub*) and that it needs a correction every sixty years (*lo drug cu re'r skyon 'don dgos pa*); it names the practice at Rtses thang Bsam gtan gling that depends on the downward shadow (*thur grib*), and says the *Log rtog gsal sgron* spells out the faults of the tithi-conduct in fine detail. Chapter eight's colophon (p. 55) says in the same way that the reckoning of the union of times' elapsed day was lost in the middle after Rdar pa Lo tsā ba's note and was newly made by the author.
