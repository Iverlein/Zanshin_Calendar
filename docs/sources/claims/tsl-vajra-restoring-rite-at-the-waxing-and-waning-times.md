# Pad rgyal gling pa's treasure: the vajra restoring rite is to be done at the waxing and waning times and continually

A short treasure text of Pad rgyal gling pa in vol. 28 of the edition of Rtsa gsum gling pa's treasures (the successor's treasure, in the lineage; image 232, read on the scan) calls itself the vajra restoring rite (རྡོ་རྗེ་གསོ་སྦྱོང, rdo rje gso sbyong) of the vajra Vinaya, a recitation of Vajrasattva that gathers the tantras of confession. Its closing verse says: striven at "at the times of the waxing and the waning" (ཡར་མར་དུས, yar mar dus) and continually, it cleanses every stain of impaired commitments, and the supreme and common accomplishments come in this life without hardship; without it a life of practice is like a traveller with a broken leg.

For whom: holders of tantric commitments. The day: the verse names the two halves of the month, not a day's number; that the times meant are the full and the new moon, the days of the monastic restoring rite, is likely from the rite's name but is not said on the page. The text's opening pages, where a day might be named, were not read.
