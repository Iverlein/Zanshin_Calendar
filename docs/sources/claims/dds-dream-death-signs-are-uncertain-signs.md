# The dream death signs are the uncertain ones in every text read (RD374)

RD374. In each of the death-sign texts read, the dream signs are the uncertain ones: «these are uncertain death signs (མ་ངེས་པའི་འཆི་ལྟས), so any one of body, speech or virtuous practice clears them» (dds.15:p124; Tilopa's Tika: «all bad dreams are turned back by any one of virtuous practice», dds.15:p119-121); Tsong kha pa: without making the inner wind signs certain, the outer signs alone give no certainty of dying (dds.16:p11-13); the Four Tantras: the same dream seen by a healthy person is uncertain and treatment frees him (dds.21:p234-235, dds.20:p213-220); the Hevajra rite gives means of turning them back (dds.19:p53-55). The Gdams ngag mdzod's teaching likewise puts the inner wind signs, not the dream, in the first place for the span of life (dds.9:p173-174). This is the same rule as RD370's: the first three signs are uncertain, and Tsong kha pa is the one who states the rule.
