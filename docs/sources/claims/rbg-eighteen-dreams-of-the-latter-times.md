# RBG: the eighteen dreams of the latter times are King Kṛki's, not a method

The RBG entry "the eighteen dream signs of the latter times" is not a dream manual: its source in the Derge Tengyur (Ska ba dpal brtsegs, the *Chos kyi rnam grangs kyi brjed byang*, read as a witness, since RBG's own pages could not be reached) tells the eighteen dreams of King Kṛki, each an image (an elephant's tail caught in a window, a thirsty man driven off from the well, sandalwood sold as firewood, a lion's body eaten by its own worms, monkeys crowning one of them king) explained as a sign of the decline of monastic conduct and the Dharma. It asks nothing of the dreamer, gives no watch or day, and no remedy; the dreams are the king's, not a rule for anyone's reading of his own. For a dream reading by the person it is no source of verdicts; at most a list of famous dreams.
