# The notes' worked example: the six of the 360, and the count through the years lived

The notes' worked example (WB vol. 2, pp. 536–538, read on the Gser thang reprint at its last lines and on the 1996 print by Yigdzin-1) is written for the thirteen gates' count and shows, in five landings, how the counts of ch. 30 run. Checked here by the sixty-year cycle (wood rat first, two years to an element):

- **How far the count runs.** The one reckoned is a water rat of fourteen; the sky year, the wood ox, is the year he has reached (a water rat plus thirteen). Every landing of the example is thirteen years on from its starting year: earth dog to iron pig, wood tiger to fire hare, water dog to wood pig. So the count runs through the years lived with the starting year counted as the first, and "down" (སྦུབ) goes forward in the sixty-year order; a female is "counted up" (བཀེན) from her starting sign (the notes' female water ox, from the wood hare). This is the gates' count; the sixty pairs' own lines use the same words (male down, female up) and give no number.
- **Who fights.** In the example the one reckoned's own aspects fight the landed years («བརྩི་བྱའི་སྲོག་གིས་འཐབ»): his life force against the iron pig's, his body against the fire hare's, and so on; the moment gives no aspect of its own to the count, only the element sought (the earth for the sky year's enemy). And the notes say in the same place that in a reckoning of this kind «གནམ་ལོ» is the year of the one reckoned, not the real year of the moment; the example's two years coincide, which is why it cannot tell the readings apart for the moment's reckoning.
- **The six of the 360.** The example's landings are, in order, life force (srog), body (lus), power (dbang), la (bla), own grave (rang dur) and slayer's grave (gshed dur): six, each with its own sixty, which is the 360 of the close. The luck (klung) is not among them, and the printed list «སྲོག་ལུས་དབང་ཀླུང་དབང་བླ་རང་དུར་གཤེད་དུར» carries two words more than the example (luck and a second power), so the six are the example's; the second «དབང» and the luck are not counted in the six.

Not settled by the example: whether the moment's four fight the landed year of the counts of p. 172 (the lines «བརྩི་བྱའི་ལོ་བྱས») or the one reckoned's four do.
