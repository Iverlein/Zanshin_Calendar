# One's own mind clear and glad; the feeling on waking

Image group: mind. Read by 3 texts: WB ch. 30, ACIP method (RST), Klong rdol's verses as the article quotes them (KBS).
Offered to any dreamer.

| Text | Verdict (tone) | Passage | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WB ch. 30 | the dreamer's own mind clear and glad: a good omen (good) | `wb.2:p144` | scan |
| ACIP method (RST) | a dream with no root in habit and very wondrous that leaves the mind glad on waking bears good fruit; if the mind is not glad, bad things come (mixed or turning) | `rst:p1-8` | etext |
| Klong rdol's verses as the article quotes them (KBS) | a dream leaving the dreamer glad on waking brings good fruit, one leaving him uneasy brings bad (mixed or turning) | `kbs:p72-78` | machine |
