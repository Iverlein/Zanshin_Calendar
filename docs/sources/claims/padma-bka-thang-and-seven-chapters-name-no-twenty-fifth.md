# Neither the Padma bka' thang nor the Seven Chapters names the twenty-fifth or a dakini day

Searched whole on 2026-11-21 in the etexts: the Padma bka' thang (299 pages, from the Ser gtsug print) and the Seven Chapters (pp. 1-34 of its text in the Rin chen gter mdzod, vol. 5). Every «ཚེས» in the Padma bka' thang is a date of the life: the first of the first spring month (the lotus opens, etext p. 19), the fifteenth of the first summer month (a king's offering, p. 67), the eighth and tenth of ch. 26 (pp. 105), the eighth of the Buddha's death (p. 110), the tenth of ch. 40, the first of the ox month (the new year, p. 332), the tenth of chs. 102, 106 and 108, and the eighth of the colophon (pp. 391, 396); none is a day of practice except the tenth of ch. 102. «ཉེར་ལྔ» occurs only as a count (chapter numbers, twenty-five disciples, twenty-five hidden treasures, the twenty-five empowerments). «བཅུ་ཚོགས» and «ཚོགས་འཁོར» as a day: no. The Seven Chapters have the single «ཚེས» of the waxing tenth.

So the ḍākinī gathering that «heroes and ḍākinīs gather» on the tenth (obk:p374) is on the tenth itself; the Padma bka' thang gives no twenty-fifth. A pure-vision life of the Guru in a modern anthology does name «the tenth, the twenty-fifth and all the great occasions» (tenth-and-twenty-fifth-in-a-pure-vision-life) and the pool's other pair (feast-without-fail-on-the-tenth-and-twenty-fifth-in-retreat) comes from Zhe chen rgyal tshab. The Seven Chapters and the Padma bka' thang are not their source. The 11th and 26th, which the owner left open, are not named in either.
