# «རུས་ནག» of the tenth circle is the north's black turtle

The «རུས་ནག» ("black bone") on which the tenth circle's pig, sheep and hare are counted (WB vol. 2, p. 10, l. 25; question 367) is the northern guardian of the four-direction set, the black turtle (རུས་སྦལ་ནག་པོ, rus sbal nag po) with its name cut short, and the circle's four "unchanging nails" are that set.

- The circle's nails (wb.2:p10-11): the tiger, the unchanging nail of the east; the turquoise dragon, of the south; the red bird, of the west; the «རུས་ནག», of the north. The pig, sheep and hare stand on the north nail, the rat, dragon and monkey on the south, the tiger, horse and dog on the east, the bird, ox and snake on the west; the centre has no nail.
- WB's own grave chapter names the same set twice. In the prayer to the earth's lords (wb.2:p133-135): the grey (སྐྱ་བོ) tiger of the east, the blue dragon of the south, the red bird of the west, «བྱང་གི་རུས་སྦལ་ནག་པོ», the black turtle of the north, and the yellow monkey of the centre. At the chamber (wb.2:p138-139): «ཤར་སྟག་སྐྱ། ལྷོ་འབྲུག་སྔོན་པོ་ནུབ་བྱ་དམར། བྱང་གི་རུས་ནག་དབུས་སྤྲེལ་སེར», with «རུས་ནག» in the same place of the verse as «རུས་སྦལ་ནག་པོ» in the first, the two forms of one name (p. 139 read on the Gser thang reprint, I3CN5029 img. 145, l. 16, enlarged 2 times: «བྱང་གི་རུས་ནག་དབུས་སྤྲེལ་སེར»).
- Other writers give the same five in the same order, as BDRC's search shows them (snippets of etexts, not read on a scan): Ngag dbang dpal bzang's and Thub bstan chos kyi glang po's collected works («ཤར་གྱི་སྟག་སྐྱ་བོ། ལྷོའི་གཡུ་འབྲུག་སྔོན་མོ། ནུབ་ཀྱི་བྱ་དམར་པོ། བྱང་གི་རུས་སྦལ་ནག་པོ། དབུས་ཀྱི་སྤྲེའུ་སེར་པོ», in romanisation in the snippets), the Karma chags med collection, and Pawo Tsuglag Trengwa's *Feast of the Learned* (the Lhasa sites of the four guardians, the turtle's with the Pha bong kha).

So the black bone is the north's black turtle; "bone" is the first syllable of the turtle's name (རུས་སྦལ, rus sbal, "bone-frog", the turtle). The set's animals are the four directions' and the circle's nails fix a year's start by the direction of its triad. Why the turtle is called a bone, no text read says. The translation "black turtle" in the grave chapter's passage rests on this match.
