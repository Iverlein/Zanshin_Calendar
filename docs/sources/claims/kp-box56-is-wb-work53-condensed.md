# KP box 56 is WB's work 53 condensed: same earth-lord run, three omissions, no addition

KP's box 56 (thread-cross and torma rites) is WB's work 53 (vol. 2, p. 422) in a short form. Its good half has WB's list of the mdos kinds by the numbers of the sme ba (2 black: demons and ma gshin; 3 and 4: nāga mdos; 5 yellow: victory mdos; 7 red: the planet-btsan and dmu mdos) and the sky gate and earth gate by khen and khon. Its bad half's run of earth lords is WB's, name for name and in the order pi ling, se bya, kungs myul, tsang kun, nāga planet, Rahu's course, but with three differences on the scan: (1) KP puts «དབང་མོ་ཁྲག་སྒོར་ཙ་མུནྡི» (the Lady, Khrag sgor, Tsa mundi) before the course of Vishnu («ཁྱབ་འཇུག»), WB after it with «ཁྲག་ཟོར་ཙཱ་མུཀྟྲི» (WB's text is machine reading, so the two spellings are not a settled difference); (2) KP has no direction for the Dog and the Bird and Monkey days («ཁྱི་ལ་ནུབ་བྱང་», «བྱ་སྤྲེལ་ཟླ་བ་ནུབ་ཕྱོགས») and no «བྱོལ་མདོས», and gives the Palmo the course times without WB's north-east; (3) KP's «གནམ་ཟས་འཚོལ» lacks WB's dog («གནམ་ཁྱི་»). It adds no work, day or direction that WB lacks; it only drops some. The only direction KP keeps is the Seer's, west-north.
