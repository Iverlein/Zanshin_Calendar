# The planets-and-mansions round: 1,874 in WB closes the twelfth, 1,828 is Mipham's

WB's own verse gives the planets-and-mansions round (the sky's bird path) of the twelfth examination 78 result and 1,874 identifying pebbles (p. 50, read by eye, «སྟོང་བརྒྱུད་བརྒྱ། བདུན་བཞི»), and the twelfth's total of 3,035 identifying pebbles closes exactly with 1,874 among its pieces and would be 46 short with 1,828. Mipham's note (p. 513) says «ངོས་འཛིན་སྟོང་བརྒྱད་བརྒྱ་ཉེར་བརྒྱད» (1,828) is the figure "counted" for the round "by the general sum" and calls it mere elaboration; he gives no reason for the 46 fewer, and his own other figures of that section (287, 121, 5, 26, 6) are WB's. The fourteenth examination's round of the same planets and mansions is given 88 and 1,936 (p. 51), a third figure for a related, not the same, count. By the rule that WB stands, 1,874 is WB's figure and 1,828 Mipham's recounting; the difference of 46 is unexplained (question 200).
