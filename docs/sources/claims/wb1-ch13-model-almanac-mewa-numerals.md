# The model almanac's mewa numerals rise one a date and a month starts three higher: p. 156 read, p. 154 box 1 is 1

In the model almanac of WB vol. 1 (pp. 154-171) the small numeral under the trigram letter of a day box is the date's mewa (སྨེ་བ, sme ba) and it rises by one a date, 1 after 9, with no break at the month's end. Read on the scan (RD196, 2026-11-24, wb.1:p156e): the 12th month's boxes 1-25 (p. 156) carry 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 1; the digits whose shape is clear (4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 1) stand in the boxes the run puts them in, three times each, nine boxes apart, and the page's own box-head numerals give the key to the hand.

The month starts three higher than the one before: the 11th month's box 1 (p. 154) carries the hook that is a 1 on p. 156 (so the 11th starts on 1, its boxes 4 and 5 being the heavy 4-wedge and the 5-sign), and the 12th starts on 4. This is the rule of the almanac's front matter (a first month 1, the middle one 4, the last 7; thirty dates are three nines and three over) and agrees with the working in reckonings.md. It makes the earlier "7" of box 1 on p. 154 doubtful: the glyph there is the 1-hook; boxes 2 and 3 of p. 154 were not told apart by shape. Held against the rule on the scan for every other box of pp. 154-166 (215 boxes, wb.1:p154-166m, RD196 2026-11-24): the digits of clear shape and the small hooks agree with ((3m + k - 1) mod 9) + 1 (m = 0 for the 11th month, k the date) in all of them but two. P. 154 box 11 has no trigram letter and no numeral at all; p. 161 box 19 (3rd month) carries the round 6 where the rule gives 4, its neighbours being a small hook and the 5-sign: a misprint or a mis-cut block, not a skipped number (the next box is 5, the run goes on). The month starts therefore run 1, 4, 7, 1, 4, 7, 1, 4 from the 11th month to the 6th Hor month, as the front-matter rule says. The pages after p. 166 are not in the reprint. Not read: the small hooks' shapes (2, 3, 7) are told apart only by their place, so a swap among them would not show.
