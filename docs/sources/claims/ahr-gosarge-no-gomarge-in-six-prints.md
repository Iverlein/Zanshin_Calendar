# The Aṣṭāṅgahṛdaya's cows' line reads gosarge in the Nirṇayasāgara apparatus and in five further prints, and the Aṣṭāṅgasaṅgraha has gosarge sadyaḥ (RD312)

The cows' line of the *Aṣṭāṅgahṛdaya* (Śārīrasthāna 6.63) reads *gosarge* in the Nirṇayasāgara edition of 1939 (Paraḍkar; p. 436) with no variant noted at the word, in a page whose apparatus records the variants of the manuscripts at other words (notes 11 to 17); the commentary printed there glosses it "at the time of the cows' release, that very day". The text layers of five further editions of the *Hṛdaya* (Jīvānanda Vidyāsāgara, Calcutta 1860; Navare–Śarmā; Sengupta's *Sarvāṅgasundarī*; Kashi Sanskrit Series; Atrideva Gupta's *Vidyotinī*) read the line with the *sa* of *gosarge* (their text is OCR, not read on the images); none of the six prints read has *gomārge* or *gomarge*. The elder Vāgbhaṭa's *Aṣṭāṅgasaṅgraha* (Śārīrasthāna 12, read on the image) has the same dream as *gosarge mahat sadyaś ca*, "at the cows' release great and at once". The *Śabdakalpadruma*'s *gomarge* (skd) is a misprint of the dictionary's type, where *ma* and *sa* are alike (RD295), not a variant of the *Hṛdaya*'s manuscripts.

The older manuscripts themselves, behind these prints, were not seen.
