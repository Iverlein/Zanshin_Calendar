# ཟ་ཟི (za zi) in «རྨི་ལམ་ཟ་ཟི་མང» is a word of its own: unclear, confused, troubled

The word ཟ་ཟི (za zi) in «རྨི་ལམ་ཟ་ཟི་མང་» is a word with a sense of its own, unclear, confused, troubled, and not a doubled sound for "many". Every lexicon of Steinert's clone that has the word gives it that sense, and five give «རྨི་ལམ་ཟ་ཟི» "troubled dream" as the example; Jäschke (p. 485) cites exactly the medical line («rmi-lam za-zi man», Med.) and reads it "troubled dreams", Das (p. 1089) "a confused or troubled dream" under the sense «= ཟང་ཟིང, རབ་རིབ», where he also puts «སྣང་བ་ཟ་ཟི», an obscure dim light. The Four Tantras use it for the mind (ཤེས་པ), the eye, speech, sleep and the search for the bla, and pair it with the doubled words ཉག་ཉོག (muddled talk) and ཆལ་ཆོལ (disorder); two physicians gloss it so (ཟ་ཟི་ནི་ཉག་ཉོག, in the glossary of the Lhan thabs's life-wind chapter, and «ཟ་ཟི་ནི་ཆལ་ཆོལ་མང་། ཤེས་པ་གཡུང་།…», the mind clouded and its awareness unclear, in a sman yig). The commentaries (Drikung, Khro ru, Blo bzang dbang rgyal) paraphrase it as a sign of the mind and never define it. So the dream row reads "many confused (troubled) dreams", «མང» being the count and «ཟ་ཟི» what the dreams are; "restless" and "repeated" are in no entry.

What stays open: a gloss in Mipham's notes on WB (none found, the Chengdu etext is search-only), the Blue Beryl and Zur mkhar ba (not opened for this word), whether the spirit-entering line of the Instruction Tantra («རྨི་ལམ་ཟ་ཟི་མང་»: Khro ru adds a rite there) means the dream as the sick one's symptom or the spirit's sign, and what ཟ་ཟི་ནི་ཉག་ཉོག is in the Lhan thabs's own line.
