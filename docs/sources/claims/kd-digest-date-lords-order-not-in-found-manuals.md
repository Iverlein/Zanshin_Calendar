# The Digest's order of the date's earth lords is in no other etext found; the Do shal and a 2006 'bras rtsis keep WB's order

BDRC's full-text search (2026-11-21) finds the verse run of the White Beryl's date earth lords (WB vol. 2, pp. 362-363, wb.2:p362-363) in other etexts in WB's own order, and the lines of the Digest (KD vol. 3, pp. 738-740, kd.3:p738-740) in no other text.

The run's first lines, «ངམས་ཤིང་དགེ་བཤེས་ཆེ་འདོན་གྱི། ཁྲི་བརྩིག་བསྟོད་བཀུར་བྱ་བ་དང་། བཤད་གསར་མག་པ་བུ་སྲིང་ངན། … བར་གནམ་ཧལ་ཁྱི་རྒྱུ་བའི་དུས།» (Ngams-shing first, then the Bar-gnam Hal-dog's course), stand in WB's two prints (4 hits for «གཟའ་རྒོད་འབྱུང་བ་ཟས་འཚོལ་དུས» among them), in the Do shal of the Vaiḍūrya dkar po by Sde srid Sangs rgyas rgya mtsho, a Phug lugs reckoning handbook, in the Rebkong anthology's copy of the Do shal (MW2PD17386 in the pool's notes), and in 'Bro'i sbyin pa's 'Bras rtsis bar gsal li dwangs ma'i me long (MW1KG21827, Kan su 2006, snippet only), whose snippet has «king kang nyid dang 'bras bu mtshungs / ngam shing / ngam shing dge bshes che 'don gyi … bar gnam / bar gnam hal khyi rgyu ba'i dus»: Ki kang, then Ngams-shing, then the Bar-gnam Hal-dog, as WB prints them on p. 363. All carry the lines in WB's order; none carries KD's order.

KD's own lines, «ཁྲི་བརྩིག་བཤད་གསར་དམག་ལས་སྤང» (1 hit) and «དབུལ་ལགོ་མཁར་ལས་མགོ་འཛུགས་དང» (1 hit), are in KD alone (MW28845, Bod kyi rtsis rig kun 'dus chen mo, Si khron 1998); «ཉི་ནག་ངན་བྱེད་དྲག་ལས་ལ» (1 hit) and «ཟླ་བཤད་ནང་བཞིན་འཛེམ་པ་གཅེས» (1 hit) likewise. «པི་ལིང་འཕར་མ་ཞེས་བྱ་བ» (the Pi-ling 'Phar-ma, 5 hits) is in WB's prints, KD and two further copies. So the grouping by kind of work, and the Poor-hand's own verse after Ngams-shing, is KD's rewriting of WB's lines, not an earlier manual's order. Snippet and etext evidence only, no scan: the claim says what the search finds, not that no earlier manual exists.
