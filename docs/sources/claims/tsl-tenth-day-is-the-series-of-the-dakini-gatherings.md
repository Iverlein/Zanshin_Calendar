# The tenth day of the monkey month belongs to the tenth-day series of the treasure's lineage

In the tradition of the treasure the tenth day (ཚེས་བཅུ, tshes bcu) is the special time on which the dākinīs, the guru and the visions come, and the life's dream on the tenth day is tied to it, not only the date of one dream. A verse of the treasure in vol. 27 on the day's course says «དངོས་སུ་ལུང་བསྟན་ཚེས་བཅུར་དག་སྣང་མཇལ», "actual prophecy: on the tenth, meet the pure vision (དག་སྣང, dag snang)" (i221), and the editor's prose on it says that for the fortunate, at a time of special quality, the tenth and the like, pure vision in person, or in experience (ཉམས, nyams) and dream (རྨི་ལམ, rmi lam) alike, meeting the face, and accomplishments come (i225). In the life, dākinīs say they come to the gathering "at the tenth and other good times" (i287), arrive at their monastery "on the tenth" (i240), and that he will meet his father "in experience and on the tenth continually" (i277); the text also records that his forefathers' seat made the tenth-day offerings (vol. 29 etext p. 109, a sentence about a forefathers' seat, not recorded as a passage: the offerings «ཚེས་བཅུ་དུས་མཆོད») and a treasure drawn out at "the tenth of the early autumn" of the earth-pig year (i661). The goddess-sister's tenth of the monkey month (i87) falls in that series and, if the monkey month is the first autumn month (claim tsl-monkey-month-named-by-its-animal), the same calendar day, the tenth of the first autumn month, as that of i661's treasure (a later year).

The ninth day of the Heruka dream (i113) and the fifteenth of the tested dream (i170) are not in the tenth's series; the ninth is given by the speaker as tomorrow's day for finding the book, and the life sets no rule for it.

The pages read do not call the tenth "Guru's day" (the name of the guru, གུ་རུ, gu ru, Padmasambhava, does not stand beside it); they name it only as the time of the gathering and of the vision, so a tie to a Guru-day series is not shown by them. No sentence read says a dream seen on the tenth is truer than one on another night.
