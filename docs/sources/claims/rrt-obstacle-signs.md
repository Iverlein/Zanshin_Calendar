# RRT: the signs of an obstacle, the king spirit and the dam sri

RRT pp. 191–193 (etext). The kinds of gods and spirits cannot be fully counted, but two obstructers are given with their signs. The king spirit (རྒྱལ་འགོང, rgyal 'gong) shows as a temple, a monk, a rough monk, a cat, a monkey, many white birds, a proud horse, a donkey, a child, a dog or a fox. The dam sri (དམ་སྲི, dam sri) shows as a woman, a child, a puppy, a pig, a black hen, a leprous woman with water running from her mouth; and in the practitioner as fear, a restless mind, joylessness, quarrels with friends, aimless wandering at dusk, sleepiness, a dry throat, little strength, hunger though fed, a sunken or drifting mind, and the longing for whatever he wishes given a twist as a spirit's trick. A heavy obstacle comes with no sign at all. Signs common to fruit and obstacle: light and sparks from images and the drum, the skull moving, the sacrificial blood boiling, the chosen deity's face seen, accomplishment, prophecy, sharp dreams, clairvoyance.
