# The Narthang and Peking prints agree against the Derge etext at the dream verses: fire, not «absent», and the red things' dream as «rmi ba»

At the planets of the dream verses the prints read alike where the Derge etext differs. The Sun's line reads «རྒྱལ་པོ་དང་ནི་མེ་དང་ཁྲག» (a king, fire and blood) in the Narthang and Peking scans read by eye and in the dpe bsdur ma etext, not «མེད་དང་ཁྲག» as the Derge etext prints it. The red things end «རྨི་བ་ཡིན» («are dreamt») in the Narthang and Peking scans by eye, in the dpe bsdur ma etext and in Yigdzin-1's reading of the Derge scan, not «རྨི་ལམ་ཡིན» as the Derge etext has it. The flowers are «མེ་ཏོག་གོས» in the Narthang and Peking scans, «མེ་ཏོག་གི» in both etexts. Venus's last word is «འཁྲིག་པ» in the Narthang and Peking scans, «འཁྲུག་པ» in both etexts. So the Derge etext stands alone at «མེད» and «རྨི་ལམ་ཡིན», and what the Derge scan itself has there is not settled by eye. In the lines that carry the rule the prints change the grammar and not the sense: «བདུན་ལ» (Narthang, Peking) for «བདུན་ན» (Derge and dpe bsdur ma etexts), «དེའི་ཚེ» for «དེ་ཚེ», «གཞན་ལ་འདྲི་བ་ནི» for «གཞན་པ་འདྲི་བ་ལ», «ཆ་ཤས» for the Derge etext's first «ཆ་ཤེས», «ཞིབ་དུ» for «ཞིབ་ཏུ». The Narthang, Peking and Gser bris ma scans also read «དུས་སྦྱོར་འཆར» («the junction rises») at the verses' first line where both etexts read «དུས་སྦྱོར་རམ» («the junction, or»); the Narthang, Peking and dpe bsdur ma etext read «ཡོད་པ་ནི» where the Derge etext reads «ཡོད་པ་ན».
