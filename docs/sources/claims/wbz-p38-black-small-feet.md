# The blocks carve the black small feet's month, links and time as the 1996 print

In the twelve guards of the black small feet (WB vol. 2, pp. 38–39), the Zhol and Taikhang blocks carve the ox's link «ཀ་ཤི», the tiger's month «དཔྱིད་ཐ» (last spring), the hare's time «རྡེལ་འགོ་དྲོས» and the snake's link «རེག» (contact) exactly as the 1996 print. None of the four is a slip of the 1996 typesetting; if they are errors, they are in the blocks.
