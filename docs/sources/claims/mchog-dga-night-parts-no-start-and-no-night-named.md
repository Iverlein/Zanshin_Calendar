# The Mchog dga' counts the night in thirty parts but names no start for them, no reason for reading a junction's part as the night's, and no night for the question (RD394, q1042)

The Mchog dga' (མཆོག་དགའ, Mchog dga') names no starting point of the night for its thirty parts, gives no reason for reading the part of the junction (དུས་སྦྱོར, dus sbyor) as a part of the night, and does not say which night a question about a dream means. Its third head is introduced as «showing the time of the dream» (རྨི་ལམ་རྨི་བའི་དུས་བསྟན་པ), and it asks «when a dream comes, in how much of the night» (རྨི་ལམ་འོང་བའི་ཚེ་མཚན་མོའི་ཆ་ཇི་ཙམ་ལ་འོང་བ). The answer is one sentence: the parts of the five places that the instant junction (ཏཏྟཱལ, tattāla) holds are «of the nature of thirty» (སུམ་ཅུ་ཆའི་རང་བཞིན), so look at the number that stands in the parts place; ten is a third of the night, fifteen midnight, thirty the dawn of rising from sleep, six the early part of the night (kd.3:p177-179). Read again on pp. 179–180: the close of the chapter returns to the same junction only to say that for the dream and the thief the parts of the five places and the thirty-part chart fall on one line at the boundaries, so no contradiction arises, and that what does not fall on a boundary is to be examined closely (kd.3:p179-180). Neither page uses a word for dusk, sunset, the first watch or any start («དགོང», «སྲོད», «ཉི་མ་ནུབ»), and neither says whether the asker is asking about last night's dream or the coming one: the verbs are «whether a dream comes» (འོང་མི་འོང, 'ong mi 'ong) and «if asked at that time the dream is clearly there» (འདྲི་ན་རྨི་ལམ་གསལ་པོར་ཡོད), which fix the reckoning at the moment of the question and nothing else.

What the page does give is a scale: since six, ten, fifteen and thirty are placed as fifths, a third, a half and the whole of the night, the count must run from the night's start to dawn; dusk is the only start that fits «dawn» for thirty, but that is our inference from the numbers, not the text's word. The two questions that stay unanswered by this commentary are therefore the start of the night and the night meant; the part of a junction as a part of the night is simply asserted.
