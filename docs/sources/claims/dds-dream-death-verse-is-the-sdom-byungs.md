# The dream death-sign verse «gang zhig gis ni rmi lam mthar» is the Saṃvarodaya's (Sdom 'byung, Derge Kanjur chapter 19); «as before» is six months (RD374)

RD374. The dream death-sign verse «gang zhig gis ni rmi lam mthar / bye ma'am thal ba'i phung po'am / lha khang mchod sdong la 'dzegs nyid / de ni snga ma bzhin du 'chi» is in the Śrī Mahāsaṃvarodaya Tantrarāja (བདེ་མཆོག་འབྱུང་བ, bde mchog 'byung ba, the Sdom 'byung), chapter 19, «Looking at the signs of certain death and the yoga of transference», in the Derge Kanjur etext (dds.25:p580-582, img. 581-582 of I1KG9204): «gang zhig gi ni rmi lam mthar / bye ma'am thal ba'i phung po'am / lha khang ngam ni mchod sdong 'dzeg / de ni sngon bzhin 'chi bar 'gyur». Tsong kha pa quotes the verses as «from the Sdom 'byung», the dream signs «from the same» (dds.16:p11-13), and the Rgyud sde kun btus quotes them «Bde mchog Sdom 'byung las» (dds.17:p392-393); the Hevajra rite printed under Marpa's name has them in a longer form with the months (dds.19:p53-55). The launcher's wording «བྱེ་མ་འམ་ཐལ་བའི་ཕུང་པོ» is not found on BDRC as one string; the Kanjur has «བྱེ་མའམ་ཐལ་བའི་ཕུང་པོ་འམ» and Tsong kha pa «བྱེ་མའམ་ཐལ་བའི་ཕུང་པོའམ».

«As before» (སྔོན་བཞིན, sngon bzhin) refers to the verse just before it in the chapter, whose last line is «death within six months» (ཟླ་དྲུག་ནང་དུ་འཆི་བར་འགྱུར): so the climber of sand, ashes, temple or stupa dies within six months. The verse of the donkey or monkey, the ant-hill and the south gives no span of its own: the black woman, «Time-Night» (དུས་མཚན, dus mtshan), follows it and «he will go to Yama's belly»; the eaters (vultures, jackals, dogs, crows, bears, flesh-eating spirits): within a year; red clothes, red garland, sesame oil: not alive in six months. The Kālacakra has none of this (RD361). The verse is the tantra's own, so Tsong kha pa is its quoter, not its author; the Indian words were not looked at.
