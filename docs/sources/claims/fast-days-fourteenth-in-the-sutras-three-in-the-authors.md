# The fast days: the sūtra verses keep the fourteenth, the Tibetan authors count three; the six days are in no author found

The sources now read give these lists of the days of the lay fast, and they do not agree on the fourteenth.

| Source | Days | State |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lokaprajñapti (ljz:i41-47) | the 8th, 14th and 15th of each half-month, and the half-month of miracles | etext |
| Maitreya's prophecy as Zhe chen rgyal tshab quotes it (zgt.1:p442-443) | the 14th, the 15th and the 8th of the half-month, and the half-month of miracles | etext |
| the Sūtra Requested by a God and the Avadāna, as Zhe chen rgyal tshab quotes them (zgt.1:p445-446) | the 14th, the 15th, the 8th and the new moon | scan |
| Lo chen Dharma shrI (dpn:i55, dpn:i58), Zhe chen rgyal tshab in his own words (zgt.1:p445-446), Zhabs dkar (zkt:p582) | the full moon, the new moon and the eighth: "the three" | scan, scan, etext |
| Co ne Grags pa bshad sgrub (dtn:i316) | the 8th, the 15th and the new moon, "the three good days of every month"; the half-moon of the waning half is not counted | etext |

So the sūtra verses the Tibetan authors quote keep the fourteenth, and one of them has four days; the same authors, speaking for themselves, give three days and no fourteenth. Zhe chen rgyal tshab sets one verse with the fourteenth two pages before his own "three" and the other on the page after it, and remarks on no difference. No Tibetan author was found who lists the six days 8, 14, 15, 23, 29 and 30 together: BDRC's phrase search has no hit for the runs «བཅུ་བཞི་བཅོ་ལྔ་ཉེར་གསུམ་ཉེར་དགུ», «ཉེར་གསུམ་ཉེར་དགུ་གནམ་སྟོང», «ཉེར་གསུམ་དང་ཉེར་དགུ་དང་གནམ་སྟོང» or «བཅུ་བཞི་དང་བཅོ་ལྔ་དང་ཉི་ཤུ་གསུམ». The six follow only from reading the Lokaprajñapti's "of the half-month" for both halves, and Karma chags med's verse on who roams (rcz:p286-288: the 8th and 23rd, the 14th and 29th, the full and the new moon) is the one Tibetan text read that names all six, as days when deeds multiply, not as fast days.

All rank 3. The app's three days (SPEC §5.8: the 8th, 15th and 30th) are the three of Lo chen, Zhe chen rgyal tshab, Zhabs dkar and Co ne Grags pa bshad sgrub. The fourteenth is recorded as the sūtras' day, not built. The White Beryl and Mipham were not read for this in this pass.
