# KP §18: the sky doors are WB's pairs, each with a remedy; the two headings the machine read differently agree with WB on the scan

KP's §18 on the sky doors (གནམ་སྒོ, *gnam sgo*) (BDRC labels 116–118) heads each door by a numbered pair and gives for each, «if it is covered» (སྒྲིབ་པ་ན), what to do: the heading words read in the print are «བཞི་གསུམ» four-three (war: the officer's name and image held, a mantra that kills the war-men), «གཉེན་སྒོ» the kin door, «བདུན་གསུམ་བག་སྒོ» seven-three (the bride), «བརྒྱད་གསུམ་དུར་གྱི་གནམ་སྒོ» eight-three (the charnel), and before them «ཚོང་གི་གནམ་སྒོ» the trade door and «བུ་ཆུང་གནམ་སྒོ» the small child's door, with «མགྲོན་གྱི་གནམ་སྒོ» the guest's door first; after eight-three come «ཤིད་སྒོ» (the funeral-feast door; the machine's «ཤིང་སྒོ» corrected on the scan) and «སྤྱི་ཡི་གནམ་སྒོ» the general door. This is the list of WB pp. 365–368 (wb-p365-368-sky-doors-riders-skulls.md): the first number rising, the second always three, the guest's door first, the general door last. Two points the machine read differently from WB were settled on the scan at 6–12× (2026-11-14, scan-g): the six-three door is «མཁར་སྒོ» (the fortress door, as in WB; the final of the second syllable is the angular hooked stroke with a pointed diagonal foot, not the round bowl of «འ»; the remedy KP gives, pressing the enemy's name into the wall's foundation or the herd's pen, is a fortress's), and the nine-three door is «ཤིད་སྒོ» (the funeral-feast door, as WB's feast door; the final of the first syllable is the flagged «ད», not the rounder «ང»). KP agrees with WB on both; the machine's «མཁའ» and «ཤིང» were its misreadings.

For the app: the dates of the doors are the n-th, n+10-th and n+20-th of the lunar month (the claim named above), already in the app. KP adds the remedy for each door and nothing about its dates; so KP confirms the door's works (war, kin, bride, charnel) and adds no date.
