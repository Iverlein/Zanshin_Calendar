# KP §18: the sky doors are WB's pairs, each with a remedy; two headings differ in the machine reading

KP's §18 on the sky doors (གནམ་སྒོ, *gnam sgo*) (BDRC labels 116–118) heads each door by a numbered pair and gives for each, «if it is covered» (སྒྲིབ་པ་ན), what to do: the heading words read in the print are «བཞི་གསུམ» four-three (war: the officer's name and image held, a mantra that kills the war-men), «གཉེན་སྒོ» the kin door, «བདུན་གསུམ་བག་སྒོ» seven-three (the bride), «བརྒྱད་གསུམ་དུར་གྱི་གནམ་སྒོ» eight-three (the charnel), and before them «ཚོང་གི་གནམ་སྒོ» the trade door and «བུ་ཆུང་གནམ་སྒོ» the small child's door, with «མགྲོན་གྱི་གནམ་སྒོ» the guest's door first; after eight-three come «ཤིང་སྒོ» (as the machine reads it) and «སྤྱི་ཡི་གནམ་སྒོ» the general door. This is the list of WB pp. 365–368 (wb-p365-368-sky-doors-riders-skulls.md): the first number rising, the second always three, the guest's door first, the general door last. Two differences from WB's list are in the machine reading and not settled on the scan: the six-three door is «མཁའ་སྒོ» (the sky door) where WB has «མཁར་སྒོ» (the fortress door; the remedy KP gives, pressing the enemy's name into the wall's foundation or the herd's pen, is a fortress's), and the nine-three door, WB's feast door, is «ཤིང་སྒོ» (the wood door) where a *shid* (funeral feast) door is expected.

For the app: the dates of the doors are the n-th, n+10-th and n+20-th of the lunar month (the claim named above), already in the app. KP adds the remedy for each door and nothing about its dates; so KP confirms the door's works (war, kin, bride, charnel) and adds no date.
