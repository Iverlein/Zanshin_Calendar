# The notes' «སྔོ་བགྲང»: the speckled pebble has no count of its own

The notes' «ཁྲ་བོ་ལ་སྔོ་བགྲང་མེད» (vol. 2, p. 495, ll. 22–25) is a different word from p. 419's «བསྔོ»: it is «སྔོ» with no prefix on the reprint's scan (wb.2:p495c; the 1996 etext also reads «སྔོ», the reprint's own etext «རྟོགས», which the scan does not bear), and «བགྲང» is "count". The notes themselves say what the line means in the sentences that follow it: a speckled pebble (ཁྲ་བོ, khra bo) "is counted nowhere, outside or in, but if it comes it is somewhat weaker" (ཁྲ་བོ་ནི་ཕྱི་ནང་གང་དུའང་བགྲང་བ་མེད་ཀྱང་བྱུང་ན་ཅུང་ཞན, wb.2:p495-496, machine), and the line itself goes on «རགས་པར་འགྱེལ་ལ་བརྩི», roughly it is counted as fallen. So «སྔོ་བགྲང་མེད» reads as "no count of its own" for the speckled in the squares of four (the houses of glory and misfortune), an inference from the notes' paraphrase: it counts for neither the white nor the black four-square.

The word «སྔོ་བགྲང» stands in other texts as «སྔོ་བགྲང་བྱེད» (BDRC full-text search, 70 hits for «སྔོ་བགྲང», snippets read 2026-10-25, in Wylie «sngo bgrang byed»): in a Gelug commentary "to make a count (of the faults, of the items) oneself" («རང་གིས་སྔོ་བགྲང་བྱེད་པ», of the number of rules to be named, and «ཉེས་པ་རྣམས་སྔོ་བགྲང་བྱེད་པར་དགོངས», reckoning up the faults), in a Nyingma text «དམ་ཚིག་ལ་སྔོ་བགྲང་བྱེད» (counting off the pledges one by one). These say "count off, enumerate", which agrees with the notes' paraphrase. It is not the «བསྔོ་བགྲང» of the Dākinī collection's «ཆོས་གང་ལ་ཡང་བསྔོ་བགྲང་མེད་པར» (BDRC search, a search-only etext), which has a prefix and means something else; it has no bearing on p. 419's «བསྔོ». No text found glosses the notes' line beyond their own next sentence (BDRC full-text search for «ཁྲ་བོ་ལ་བསྔོ་བགྲང», «ཁྲ་བོ་ལ་སྔོན་བགྲང» and «ཁྲ་བོ་བགྲང་བ་མེད»: no hits); the Sakya print of WB (WB2, vol. 2) has no such lines in its etext.
