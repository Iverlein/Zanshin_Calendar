# Ch. 32: the order of a family graveyard up the hill

WB vol. 2, ch. 32, p. 276 (img. 282 of the Gser thang reprint, read by eye, RD156): the order of a family graveyard, going up the hill. The grandfather and grandmother (མེས་ཕོ་ཕྱི་མོ, mes pho phyi mo) make the foundation; three paces up, the father and mother; three paces on, the male line in a row on the right and the female line on the left, in three tiers, those of the middle generation in the centre; three paces on, the two «སྨན» (sman) to right and left. A man or a woman of incest (ཕོ་ནལ, pho nal; མོ་ནལ, mo nal) lies apart, below the right and the left corner. Machine reading had «ཁྱི་མོ», a bitch, for the grandmother.
