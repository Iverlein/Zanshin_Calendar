# RD314: the Svapnohana's four watches are unbounded; the two four-watch reckonings agree on quarters of the night

What RD314 settles for q798: no text read bounds the Svapnohana's four watches, and none names the Svapnohana beside a reckoning. Three Tibetan reckonings of watches exist and can be set beside it: eight equal watches of the whole day-and-night, four of the night (the tantric breath reckoning); four of the night, each a quarter of that night's own length by season (a meditation hand-book); three watches of the night (the Vinaya and bodhisattva-vow scheme) with the dawn placed in or after the last. The Svapnohana's four, with a fifth term of dawn (སྐྱ་རེངས) after the fourth, fit the first two, not the three-watch scheme, which has no fourth watch.

On this the fallback of the earlier claim (the night from sunset to dawn cut into four) stands as the likely meaning: quarters of the night's own length. It is not a reading of the Svapnohana, which states no bound; it is the reading the two four-watch reckonings agree on.
