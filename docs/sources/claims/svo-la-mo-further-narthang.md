# The two further Narthang prints read rta la mo, as Peking and the first Narthang print do

The Narthang print of W22704 (I3279) and the gser bris ma (W23702, I1PD31365) read «རྟ་ལ་མོ» in the line of the horse, la mo, pot-belly, water creature: by eye on enlargements a plain «ལ» after «རྟ», without «བ» over a subjoined «ལ»; Yigdzin-1 reads it the same. With Peking and the first Narthang print (svo:p271b) that makes four prints against the Derge etext and the Dpe bsdur ma base text, which have «བླ་མོ»; the conflict stands, the spelling of the four agreeing. The sense of the word is still open. The prints' other doubtful words (bgrod min, 'gron bu, bcu yi bar, ga ya tri) were read by Yigdzin-1 only, and agree with Peking.
