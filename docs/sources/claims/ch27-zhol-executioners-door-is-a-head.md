# The four executioners' door of p. 53 is a stanza head in the Zhol and Taikhang run

The door line «གཤེད་བཞིའི་རྡེལ་སྒོ་སྟོང་པ་ན» stands on the Zhol print at img. 700, line 2, and on the Taikhang at img. 82, line 2 (Zhol minus 618), as the head of a stanza of its own. On the Zhol print the heads of the stanzas round it are marked in the machine reading and read in order: the power year's front (དབང་ལོའི་མདུན་དུ), the small place (ས་ལ་ཆ་ཆུང), the four executioners (གཤེད་བཞི), the small cut-off (ཆད་ཆུང), the enemy's door (དགྲ་སྨར), «གོགས་སྨར», the great and the small wine (ཆང་ཆེན, ཆང་ཆུང). So the executioners' door is a head in the run of the list, not a clause under the great cut-off; the verse's own words name no owner. The final ས of «སྟོངས» was not decided on either print (the Taikhang crop at 2.8x shows «སྟོང» and «པ» close; the Zhol letters are small).
