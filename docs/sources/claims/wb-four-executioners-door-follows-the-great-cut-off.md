# p. 53: the four executioners' door has the great cut-off place's consequence and position

«གཤེད་བཞིའི་རྡེལ་སྒོ་སྟོངས་པ་ན། །ཕ་མེས་ཡང་མེས་རིང་དག་ལ། །མི་མགོ་ནག་པོ་ལྟོ་བར་བཅུག» (p. 53, the 1996 etext p. 61): when the four executioners' pebble door is empty, on the fathers, forefathers and the long line of ancestors the black man's head is put into the belly. The special verdict's «ཆད་ཆེན་མདུན་དུ་རྡེལ་ནག་པ… ཕ་དང་མེས་པོའི་རིང་དག་ལ། མི་མགོ་ལྟོར་བཅུག» (the great cut-off's front, black pebbles: "the place of the grave", the fathers and the ancestors' line, a man's head put into the belly) has the same consequence in nearly the same words, and the line sits between the power year's place and the small cut-off's place, where the great cut-off's entry would be. So by the consequence, by position and by the executioners' being the grave's slayers, the four executioners' door is the door of the great cut-off place. This is inference, no manual says it; UT's hint (the four executioners as the earth's power year) fits the power place before it and is not taken.
