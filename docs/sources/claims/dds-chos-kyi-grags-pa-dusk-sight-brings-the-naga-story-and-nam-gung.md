# Rig 'dzin Chos kyi grags pa: the «earlier dream of dusk» is the dusk-session sight of the vidyā-king (Nāgarakṣa in the short account), and nam gung is the hour in sleep after it (RD403, q1117)

RD403 (q1117). The life (dds.49:p110-112) says the nāga story came to the author's mind «by the circumstance of that earlier dream of dusk» (སྔར་སྲོད་ཀྱི་རྨི་ལམ་དེའི་རྐྱེན་གྱིས). The only dusk event the life narrates before it is the sight of the vidyā-king in the first dusk session (སྲོད་ཐུན), with the prayer against the harm of earth lords and nāgas, so the words point to that sight and name no other dusk dream. A shorter account of the same night in another text of the same volume (dds.50:p6-8) makes it plain: in the first dusk session he sees clearly a vidyā-king, Nāgarakṣa with nine heads, sixteen arms and a serpent's tail, and prays against earth-spirit harm; he then falls asleep, and at about midnight (ནམ་གུང་ཙམ) the blue man, the nāga Gzi chen, comes in a dream with the same request about Ka tshal; on waking, led by it, he remembers the history of Nāgarakṣa and the prince freed from earth sickness. There the sight at dusk is of the very deity the history is about, which is why it brings the history to mind. So the answer to the first half of the question is the dusk-session sight, with the short account as its witness (etext only).

«Nam gung» in both tellings is a time of the night after the dusk session: in the shorter account the words are «after going to sleep, at about nam gung, in a dream», in the life «at nam gung» (the nāga is not said there to be dreamed, but the hour is the same, and the life elsewhere uses nam gung for a session, p114). The word is never defined; the midnight session is the reading both tellings allow and nothing in them contradicts. A doubt on the name in the life: its «Pawo Kyangdrub» (དཔའ་བོ་རྐྱང་སྒྲུབ) may not be a name, since the shorter account has «Kusali single-deity practice» (ཀུ་སཱ་ལི་རྐྱང་སྒྲུབ) for the same sight; the line in dds.49 reads equally «a hero's single practice».
