# Saturn in each mansion: the peoples and trades it harms

Page 202 lists, for Saturn (*spen pa*) moving in each of the mansions from Kṛttikā to Revatī, the peoples and trades it harms: Kṛttikā, gold-smiths and the great men of the country; Aśvinī, makers of verse, scholars and ministers; Bharaṇī, singers and dancers and the eaters of human flesh; Rohiṇī, the people of Kosala and others; Mṛgaśiras, makers of offerings and the people of the central country; Punarvasu, Saurāṣṭra and the border peoples; Puṣya, bell-makers, nomads and Tajiks; Āśleṣā, snakes and water-dwellers; Maghā, the Chinese, horse-keepers, doctors and merchants; Pūrvaphalgunī, women who sell themselves and girls; Uttaraphalgunī, monks, molasses and salt; Citrā, merchants and the Yavanas and Kaliṅgas; Svātī and Viśākhā, water-dwellers and farmers; Anurādhā, doctors and astrologers; Jyeṣṭhā and Mūla, Saurāṣṭra and the mountain-valley people; Pūrvāṣāḍhā, Kashmiris and Nepalis; Uttarāṣāḍhā, Tibet, translators and those on the sea shore; Śravaṇa, brahmins and the Kaliṅgas; Dhaniṣṭhā, the Mon and Zahor; Revatī, the king's retinue and the Tajiks' autumn crops. Rāhu in the eastern mansions harms the king; in the middle it gives comfort and a good year.
