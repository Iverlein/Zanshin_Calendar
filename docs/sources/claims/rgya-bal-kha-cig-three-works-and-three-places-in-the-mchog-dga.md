# «རྒྱ་བལ་ཁ་ཅིག» is found in three works only and stands for a reckoning of vowels and planets by direction, set beside the Kālacakra (RD396, q1074)

BDRC's exact phrase «རྒྱ་བལ་ཁ་ཅིག» (3 hits, 2026-11-11) is found only in the Kun 'dus chen mo (MW28845), the Bzo rtsis zlos gar vol. 2 (MW1PD95727, search only) and the commentary Dpyod ldan snying nor (MW2PD17386_D19206, no scan): that is, the Mchog dga' and two works that draw on it or the Phugpa commentaries; no Kālacakra text, no Tibetan history, no Sanskrit work is found. In the Mchog dga' itself the phrase stands beside «the Sbas pa gsal byed» (the Kālacakra commentary) and the Kālacakra at printed p. 71 (kd.3:p71, «མོན་གྲེ» spelt as «some of the Chinese and Nepalese» join it), with «the commentators and their followers» at p. 77 (kd.3:p77, five long vowels in the directions and five short ones in the corners) and with «some of the earlier ones» at p. 87 (kd.3:p87, their verse on the planets' directions, which the Mchog dga' says does not fit the root text but agrees with the bird-wheel). So they are a group of authorities on the vowel and planet reckonings (the Kālacakra's vowel scheme and its planets by direction), set against the earlier Tibetan commentators; the Mchog dga' does not name a book or person, and the dream lines of Saturn (kd.3:p177-179) are one more place of the series. Who they are is not told: whether it names a reckoning that came by way of China and Nepal, or two lineages of commentators, no text found says.
