# The Rnam dpyod blo gros spyan stong bkra ba was written by Orgyan bstan 'dzin, a Gsho thang mantrin (RD326, q947)

The Rnam dpyod blo gros spyan stong bkra ba (the work in the 'Bri lugs bang mdzod that quotes the Dbyangs 'char verse) names its writer in the colophon (printed pp. 75–76): «Blo gros kyi 'jug sgo yangs pa Orgyan bstan 'dzin» (བློ་གྲོས་ཀྱི་འཇུག་སྒོ་ཡངས་པ་ཨོ་རྒྱན་བསྟན་འཛིན), who calls himself in its verses a Gsho thang mantrin and corpse-carrier (གཤོ་ཐང་སྔགས་པ་རོ་ཁུར་མཁན), says that a lord ༧ Lho rje Drung pa Dkon mchog bstan 'dzin phrin las mthar phyin mi 'gyur mchog gi snang ba ye shes mchog grub rgyal po called him Dpal bzang po, and gives the occasion: his own forty-ninth year, a fire-bird year, between the sessions of the awareness-holder Padma 'od 'bar, at Theg chen Yang rtse kun gzigs pho brang, the lama's residence of Thub bstan bshad sgrub gling in Mdo khams. It is a compilation chiefly from the words of earlier holders («yongs 'dzin mkhas dbang»), made from a very corrupt hand copy. BDRC's record of the work names no creator. Open: whether Dpal bzang po and Orgyan bstan 'dzin are one man; the year (no element-and-animal year more exact than «fire-bird, forty-ninth year» is given, and the birth year is not known from the text).
