# What a day box of the model almanac holds, and what was read

A day box of the model almanac holds, from the top: the date's numeral and five-fold class word, then the Kalacakra vowel, consonant, element and object; a scalloped oval with the vowel-arising set (vowel, consonant, element, object); a line with the stage word, an animal-like word, a trigram letter over a numeral and the earth lords' courses; and the sky-door words, some in a frame. Read by eye on the reprint: boxes 1-5 and 11 of the 11th month (p. 154) and boxes 1-5 of a later month (p. 165). In both the first two lines are identical for the same date: they depend on the date's place, not the month's. The 11th month's boxes agree with the table of day-letters.md. Not read: the third line and the sky-door words of every other box, all of month 12 to month 10 except the five boxes of p. 165, and boxes 6-10 and 12-15 of p. 154 beyond their class words.
