# «བསྔོ་རུ་མ་བཏུབ» and «བསྔོ་མ་བཏུབ»: the plank-tree's white and black mixed

WB says it five times, and no text found glosses it. In the white-and-black verdicts of the plank-trees and the four views of ch. 25 the words are «དཀར་ནག་བསྔོ་རུ་མ་བཏུབ» (p. 419, the four plank-trees' rule; p. 425, «བུ་ཕྲད་དཀར་ནག་སྣ་ཚོགས་པ། བསྔོ་རུ་མ་བཏུབ་གྱུར་པ་ན», the son meeting of mixed colours) and «བསྔོ་མ་བཏུབ» (p. 423, the juniper's «དཀར་ནག་འདྲེས་མ»; p. 423-424, the wish-tree's «དཀར་ནག་འདྲེས་ནས»; p. 424, Tsugtu's «སྣ་ཚོགས་རྡེའུ»), in each place the verdict on pebbles of both colours mixed. After the p. 419 and p. 425 lines the verse goes on «ཐང་ཤིང་མ་བབས་ཕྱི་ཆགས་ཡོད» (the plank-tree has not come down, a later fault), after the juniper's «མནའ་ཟོས་ཡང་ན་རྦད་འདྲེ་གནོད» (an oath broken or the *rbad* spirit harms), after the wish-tree's «གདོན་བསྒྲིབས་ཐང་ཤིང་མ་བབས་ཏེ། འབྱུང་འཁྲུགས་ནད་ཡིན་གསོ་བ་དཀའ» (spirits veil it, the plank-tree has not come down; an illness of the elements' disorder, hard to cure) and after Tsugtu's «ནད་པ་ཐེ་ཚོམ་ཅན་དུ་འགྱུར» (the sick person becomes uncertain).

Spelling, print by print. The first half is «བསྔོ་རུ» on p. 419 and p. 425 and «བསྔོ་མ» on p. 423-424 in the Gser thang reprint (its scan, at 3×, and its etext), the 1996 print's etext on p. 419, 424 and 425, the Zhol print (the p. 419 line by eye on the blocks and on the Taikhang reproduction, I5877 img. 570; its other lines only in a poor etext, p. 559-562, where the words come out as «བསྡོམ་བཏུབ» and the like), KD vol. 3 (I4717 etext, pp. 706, 710-712) and the manuals that copy WB: the Gyalrong collection (MW1KG16672), *Sgo bzhi mdzod lnga'i yang bcud* 29 and Gnyer ston Stag snang rgyal's *Rtsis rig shes bya kun gsal* (MW1KG4867), found by BDRC's full-text search (snippets only, 2026-10-25). Only two differ: the 1996 etext's «བསྡོ་མ» on p. 423 (an OCR reading of the same letters the reprint's scan carves «བསྔོ», read RD41) and KD's «བསྔོར་མ» for «བསྔོ་མ» on pp. 710-711 (its digest of the p. 423-424 lines; the same «བསྔོར» stands in the middle of its p. 706's «བསྔོ་རུ», so this is a second spelling or a slip of the digest, unsettled). The last syllable is «བཏུབ» everywhere, «བཏུབ་ན» in the rule and «བཏུབ་གྱུར་པ་ན» on p. 425.

What the notes say: only that «ཐང་ཤིང་མ་བབ» is told of "a reckoning that has not come to a decision" (wb.2:p495-496), the clause after the words, not the words. The same print has «བསྔོ་རུ» once elsewhere, in the verse on the life force and the luck cut apart (vol. 1, p. 282, 1996 etext p. 292 and the Gser thang reprint's p. 277 etext): «ཟས་ནོར་ནམ་མཁའི་འཇའ་ཚོན་འདྲ། །བསྔོ་རུ་ཡོད་ལ་གྲངས་སུ་མེད», food and wealth like a rainbow in the sky, «བསྔོ་རུ» there, not counted; and BDRC's search shows «བསྔོ་རུ་མེད» in a Nyingma commentary on the *Kun byed rgyal po* («མཆོད་པ་བསྔོ་རུ་མེད» and «སྨོན་ལམ་བསྔོ་རུ་མེད», an offering and a prayer with nothing to be dedicated (བསྔོ, bsngo) in them, snippets only). These show «བསྔོ་རུ» as a noun phrase from the verb «བསྔོ» (to dedicate, assign), "what is to be dedicated or assigned", and «བཏུབ» as "can". A rendering "white and black cannot be assigned [to one side]" fits the notes' gloss of the following clause (the reckoning that comes to no decision) but is an inference from those uses, not a gloss, and nothing glosses the line: none of these has the words, searched in their etexts (2026-10-25): the regent's replies (I5875's etext; I5876 not searched), WB2's second volume (the Sakya print, whose etext holds none of the notes' lines searched), Lha mo tshul khrims (LT), the *Dpal bsdus* (UT), KD vol. 3's *Lhan thabs rgyu skar phreng ba* (its pp. 706-712 only copy the verse) and the Bhutan collection (I1KG11905). health.md's "will not take the offering" has no source and is not kept.
