# Gnam chos: the Great Compassionate One's dream rite, mantra water and the wheel at the pillow

The Great Compassionate One's dream examination in the Gnam chos begins on printed p. 558 with a rite of mantra water and a mantra over the ashes of a dead woman, smeared on five places of the body; a second, "new prophecy" text opens with the instruction to draw a wheel at one's pillow (a lotus from the middle of a lake at its centre, a mantra in its ten spokes). The two pages read (machine reading only, unchecked on the scan) state no lunar day, hour of the night or count of nights; the prophecies are dated only by the night they were spoken (the 21st of a Fire-Dragon month, the 14th of a Fire-Monkey month). The rest of the text (pp. 559-561) and the Mtsho rgyal text were not read.
