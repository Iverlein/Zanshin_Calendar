# The Mchog dga' gives a dream's kind by the planet that is lord of the field at the instant or nine-part junction

The Phug pa commentary Mchog dga' (མཆོག་དགའ, Mchog dga') on the Dbyangs 'char (དབྱངས་འཆར) in its twelfth section, the reckoning of dreams (རྨི་ལམ་གྱི་རྩིས, rmi lam gyi rtsis), takes the dream's content from the planet that is lord of the field (ཞིང་བདག, zhing bdag) at the instant junction (ཏཏྟཱལ, tattāla) or the nine-part junction (དགུ་ཆ, dgu cha): the sun gives a king walking, sitting, fire, blood, red things; the moon women, white flowers and jewels, the moon rising; Mars coral and flying in the sky; a fourth planet (the etext's «གཟའ་ལག») teaching and hearing the Dharma; Jupiter writing, reading, horses, children at play, scripture, protector gods, kin; Venus crossing water, dancing, a young woman; Saturn, absent in the root text, keeping bad company and climbing hills and cliffs according to some Chinese-Nepalese. A planet that is not the house's own lord gives mixed or unremembered dreams, an enemy of the lord bad ones. The kind of the dream is given by the planet at the time of the question; no single image is judged good or bad here.
