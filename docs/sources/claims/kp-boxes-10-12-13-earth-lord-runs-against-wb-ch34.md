# KP boxes 10, 12 and 13 close with earth-lord runs that match WB ch. 34's only in part

WB's chapter 34 names earth lords in its verse for three of the works whose boxes KP closes with a run of them, and KP's runs differ from WB's in places.

Work 10 (feasts, household goods). WB's run is «གནམ་ཁྱི་ཟས་འཚོལ་ཀི་ཀང་དང་། དབུལ་ལག་འཆར་དང་བར་གནམ་དུས། གཟའ་བདུན་ཧལ་ཁྱི་ཟས་འཚོལ་བ། པི་ལིང་ལྷ་དགུ་ཚོགས་པའི་ས། བཙུན་མོ་ཧང་ཕན་གནས་ཕྱོགས་འཛེམ།»: the sky dog's food-seeking, ki kang, dbul lag, the mid-sky times, the planets' hal khyi, pi ling, the earth of the nine gods, the queen Hang phan (the one consort, avoided by place and direction). KP's box 10 (kp:i26) names instead the se, tsang kun, hang phan, se byi, se shar, kungs myul, the earth king, the dragon god, the five gods, the great net and Rahu's course times; only ki kang (as «ཀིང་ཟོར»), dbul, the hal khyi, pi ling, gnam and hang phan are in both. WB speaks of Hang phan as a queen consort, KP's box names the name alone.

Work 12 (setting out). WB: «འཇིག་ཉི་ཀུན་བརྡུངས་ཕུང་ཟོར་ཉི། པི་ལིང་ས་ཕུང་ནག་པོ་རྒྱུ། ཉི་ནག་བདུད་གཅོད་ལ་སོགས་ངན།». KP (kp:i27) ends «ཕུང་ཟོར་ པི་ལིང་ སའི་འཕུང་བྱེད་ཉི་ནུབ། འགྲོ་མིའི་བདུད་གཅོད་སོགས།». The same run: phung zor, pi ling, the demon-cutting. Where WB has «ས་ཕུང་ནག་པོ་རྒྱུ» (the black earth-heap's course) and «ཉི་ནག» (the great black day), KP has «སའི་འཕུང་བྱེད» (the earth's destroyer) and «ཉི་ནུབ» (the sunset); WB's last line says «ངན» (bad), KP's «the demon-cutting of travellers, and the like».

Work 13 (a child's first outing). The two agree in the names (the se, hang bu, se zhig, the year's and month's earth lords, the hal khyi that goes by direction, the head-on meeting of the first and the eighth, the chung sri and the child-eaters avoided). WB writes «ཧལ་ཁྱི་མེད། … གདོང་མ་ཐུག» (no hal khyi; not meeting head on) where KP has a dotted gap and «གདོང་ཐུག» (kp:i29); the sense of the negative is open.

The differences are read from WB's etext (ch. 34, white-beryl-ch34.md) against KP's scan; WB's wording was not re-read on its scan here. WB stands above KP (SPEC §8); nothing here changes what the app builds, since KP's run names no work for a lord.
