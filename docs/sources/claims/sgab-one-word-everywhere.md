# «ཤི་སྒབ» is one word at every place of the thirteenth examination

On the Gser thang reprint (I3CN5029 img. 110, 111, 112, 114, p. 104, l. 27 and p. 105, l. 15, p. 106, ll. 2 and 15, p. 108, ll. 11, 24, 26, read in line crops at 1.3×, 2026-11-01) the term for the run of deaths is one and the same stack at all seven places: ས over a subjoined letter, then བ, with no place where it differs. At that size the subjoined letter's head is not enough to tell ka from ga, so the reprint does not decide between «སྐབ» and «སྒབ». The Zhol blocks carve «སྒབ» (wbz-shi-sgab), the grave chapter's «ན་སྒབ་ཤི་སྒབ་འགྲམས་སྒབ་ཆོད» (p. 135, l. 17; read on the same reprint, img. 141) is printed with the same stack, and the heading's «ཤི་འགྲམས་སྒབ» in Yigdzin-1 agrees. So the one word is «ཤི་སྒབ», the run of deaths, and the print's «སྐབ» of the 1996 setting at pp. 104–108 is the same word spelt with the other letter, not a second word. The same spelling question stands at vol. 1, p. 331 («བོང་བུ་སྐབ་མི་འཕེལ» against «སྒབ་འཕེལ» of p. 330 and ch. 27, p. 13, «སྒབ་འཕེལ་ཆེ་སྟེ་མི་ཟད»): the word «སྒབ་འཕེལ», increase of the run (of livestock or luck), is spelt with ga where the print is clear, so «སྐབ» there is the same slip of one print; p. 331 itself was not opened again in this reading.
