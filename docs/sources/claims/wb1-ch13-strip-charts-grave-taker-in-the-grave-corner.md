# Ch. 13 strip charts: the grave-taker stands in the corner of the grave animal the verse of p. 217 names, 8 of 8 placeable charts

The grave-taker (dur len, no. 5 of the scattered lords, vol. 2 p. 217) has its head, by the verse, on the sheep in the horse, sheep and monkey months, on the dog in the bird, dog and pig months, on the ox in the mouse, ox and tiger months and on the dragon in the hare, dragon and snake months: always one of the four grave animals, ox, sheep, dog, dragon, and the four corners of the strip charts' ring are those four places (dragon top-left, sheep top-right, dog bottom-right, ox bottom-left, in KP's ring order of kp:i87c).

The reprint readings by eye (wb.1:p142e to p146e, 1.5x to 2.2x, cells counted from the corners, doubtful words as the passages mark them) set dur len in the corner the verse names in these charts: tiger bottom-left (ox), hare the left column's upper cell with gshed po (dragon, top-left), dragon the left column's top cell (top-left), snake the same (top-left), horse the right column's top cell beside snang sel (top-right), sheep the right column's top cell, ending a four-line cell (top-right), monkey the right column's top cell (top-right) and pig the right column's last cell (bottom-right, the dog). That is 8 of the 8 charts in which the cell can be placed; the bird's (dog, bottom-right: a «ཀླུ་ལེན» doubtful in the last right cell) is probable; the dog, rat and ox charts' cells were not settled. The older passages count the cells of a column in a different way from the ring of wb.1:p142-147r, so this agreement rests on reading "the column's top cell" as the corner; it is a count by place, by eye at low enlargement, not a block match.

So the verse's four-way placing of dur len is the placing of the charts: the cell stands on the animal's place the verse names, as with ba dan and bya khyung. The light-clearer (snang sel) is set against the same corners and is not set on the same animal as the grave-taker in the first and last months of a season: see the answer of q1364.
