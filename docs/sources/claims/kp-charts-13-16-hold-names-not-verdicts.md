# KP §13-§16: the charts of the year's, month's, day's and hour's earth lords are names by cell, with no work and no verdict

KP's §13–§17 (pool img. 87–104, BDRC labels 89–106) are charts, and no cell holds a verdict. §13 (img. 87–90): twelve panels, three to a page, each a year animal's woodcut (Rat, Ox, Tiger on label 89; the next pages hold the other nine; the pictures were seen on labels 89 and 91: a rodent, a bull, a tiger, a horse, a sheep, a monkey) with the names of the year's lords in cells round it. §14 (img. 91–96): twelve panels, two to a page, each a month, with a framed woodcut of the month's lord (an animal-headed man) in the middle and cells for its sides, with «བབ» («descends») and the signs «++» and «××» in the cells; its names include the *gser dbang*, *rnam thos*, *srid gshed*, *bla rtsang*, *zla dngos*, *sa khyi*, *zla rlungs*, *pi ling*, *'phar ba*, *bstan ma*, *rnga zor*, *sa rgyal*, *bye lam*, *be sna*, *shel khang*. §15 (img. 97–100): the nine *nag mo*, the *ltogs 'phye*, the *gza' thog*, *dur len*, *snang sel*, *sde brgyad*, the *klu* and *gza'* by Hor date and hour (the *ro dgu* are named by date: 15, 11, 14, 19, 21, 25, 29, with «ཉི་མྱུར», «ནམ་ཕྱེད», «ཐོ་རེངས», «སྲོད» as the hours). §16 (img. 101–102): the day's hearth god's seats and the persons harmed (kp:i101) and the hours (kp:i102). The machine reads the names but loses which cell and which side each stands on; a few cells (the year panels' first row, the month panel of label 93) were read by eye at 2.4× and show the pattern: lord's name above, a short phrase below, the side named in the cell next to the woodcut.

What the charts do not carry: no work, no weekday, no mansion and no good or bad day is named. They are where the lords stand, which §18 then mends. So for the app (SPEC §5): nothing in §13–§15 changes a day's reading; §16's day chart (kp:i101) names who the hearth god's seat harms, a corpse-bearer, a hunter, the owner of a dead body, a digger of earth, a beer-maker, which is the same sort of «bad for…» persons as the day animals' list in WB and may be set against it when the hearth god's place is next worked (ROADMAP T2.28), but it is machine-read and rough.
