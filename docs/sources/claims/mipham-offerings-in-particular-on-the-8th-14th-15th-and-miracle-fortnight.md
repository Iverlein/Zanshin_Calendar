# Mipham's kriyā and caryā mantra manual: offerings in particular on the 8th, 14th and 15th and in the fortnight of miracles

In his short manual on accomplishing the mantras of action and conduct tantra (mgz.23:p7-8, read on the scan) Mipham (rank 2) says that the offering to the deity is made at the end of each session and is excellent whenever it can be made, and quotes "the tantra": on the eighth, the fourteenth and the fifteenth, and in the half-month of the miracles, offerings are to be made in particular.

- Days: the 8th, 14th and 15th of the month; the fortnight of the Buddha's miracles (the first to the fifteenth of the first month in the White Beryl's almanac, wb.1:p157).
- Practice: offerings before the maṇḍala or image of one's deity (torma, flowers, incense, food, lamps), beyond the daily ones.
- For whom: one who recites a kriyā or caryā mantra, lay or ordained; the text does not single out lay people.
- Benefit: none stated in the lines; the tantra is not named.

It is Mipham's one plain instruction found that ties ordinary days of the month to an added practice. The 10th, the 25th, the 29th and the 30th are not in it.
