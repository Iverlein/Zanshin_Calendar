# The Narthang manuscript reads «ཛ་ཏ་པ», the third sign པ

On the Narthang manuscript (img. 289 line 5, 8×) the third sign of the jātaka garland is a པ (a closed triangular body with a leg and no left upright stroke), not the ཀ of «ཀུ་ཤའི» in the line above in the same hand (a left upright and a hooked arm). So the Narthang manuscript and the Peking print (RD234, a doubt there) agree on «ཛ་ཏ་པ» against the Derge etext's «ཛཱ་ཏ་ཀ»; the Dpe bsdur ma note «པེ» «སྣར» ཛ་ཏ་པ says the same (moderate). Which is the original reading is not given by the print count.
