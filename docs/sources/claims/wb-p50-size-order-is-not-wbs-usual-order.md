# p. 50's «ཆེ་ཆུང་འབྲིང» is not WB's usual order of the sizes, and no colour is paired to it

In WB's own verses the three sizes of a rite are mostly written great, middling, small: «ཆེ་འབྲིང་ཆུང» (che 'bring chung) in vol. 1 ch. 22 (img. 338, about p. 328, twice: the bad omens and the year's faults) and in the notes (img. 490, about p. 482: the danger when a mewa of the life force, body, power or luck falls on black, blue, red or yellow). The line of p. 50, «གཏོ་རིམ་ཆེ་ཆུང་འབྲིང་ཇི་ལྟར། དགོས་པའི་ཁ་སྨྲང་འདི་ཡིས་ཤོད།» (read by eye on the reprint, last line of the page; MITRA has it too, Yigdzin-1 stops before it), has the other order, great, small, middling; it comes after «many white, white; many black, black; even, the white-black pair», and the colours in that order (white, black, pair) would pair with the sizes in this order as white great, black small, pair middling, which no text says and the sense forbids. So the order of the three sizes is the line's own and is not a pairing; the verse form (a seven-syllable line) is a likelier reason for the unusual order than a rule. The pairing white small, black great, mixed middling comes from the LTWA manuscript, not from WB (RD29 and the claim wb-p50-order-of-rites-is-a-list). The notes' p. 517 on the thirteenth examination counts its groups of remedies (202 identifying, 11 roots, 13 verdicts, 25 results) and does not size the rites by the colour in the lines read; no text of this reading does. Question 212 stays narrowed.
