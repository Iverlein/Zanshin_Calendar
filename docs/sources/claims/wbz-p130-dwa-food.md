# Dwa's food on the Taikhang: «bya sha ju rtse skyur/sgur rtsib g.yon»

Dwa's grave food (WB vol. 2, p. 130) reads on the Taikhang reproduction «བྱ་ཤ་ཇུ་རྩེ་སྐྱུར/སྒུར་རྩིབ་གཡོན», seven syllables as the metre wants: bird meat, «ju rtse», a third syllable under ས with u (སྐྱུར or སྒུར), and the left ribs. The four syllables of Yigdzin-1's 1996 reading («ཇོ་ཇུ་ལྕེ་སྐུར») are not on the blocks; what «ju rtse» names is not stated.
