# The seventh house from the sun's house, where the moon makes a dream clear, is counted with the first house as one, so it is the opposite sign (q1021)

The Dbyangs 'char (དབྱངས་འཆར) says the moon in the seventh from the sun's place, or the sun in the seventh from the moon's, makes the dream clear. The Mchog dga' (མཆོག་དགའ, Mchog dga') never states its counting rule at that line, but everywhere else in the same work houses are counted from a starting house with the starting house as the first: from Aries the fifth is Leo and the ninth Sagittarius (the places of the triangle), and the tenth is Capricorn, whose field-lord is Saturn (kd.3:p125-126, p128, in the order of the houses of p84-85, Aries first); the fourth, seventh and tenth are likewise the pillars of the square beside the first. On that count the seventh from a house is the sixth after it, the house opposite. So the clear dream is the sun and the moon in opposite signs, about the full moon; the other opinion the Mchog dga' mentions (the moon between the sun and the seventh) is wider and takes in most other cases but a conjunction. That the seventh is counted with the first as one is shown by the book's own use, not said at this line, and no scan was read. Etext only.
