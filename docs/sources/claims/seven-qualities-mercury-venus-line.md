# The Seven Special Qualities' Mercury and Venus line, read on KD vol. 2's scan: one mansion, the true sun, a smaller part

The *Seven Special Qualities* (KD vol. 2, p. 441, read now on the lent scan) restates WB's Mercury and Venus clause as one condition and one result: when the true sun (ཉི་དག) and either Mercury or Venus have come to stand in one mansion, the part seized becomes smaller than reckoned above (འཛིན་ཆ་གོང་ལས་ཆུང་བར་འགྱུར). The clause stands in the verse as the third of the lines that follow «ཉི་མ་རིལ་འཛིན་ཤར་ན་ཡང་། ཟླ་བ་བཞིན་དུ་སྒྲིབ་མི་ནུས།», "even if the sun's total seizing comes out it cannot cover as the moon covers": the planets are given as the reason the sun's total seizing is not total in sight, and the next lines go on to the side of the seizing.

What this settles of questions 116 to 118, beyond the etext reading of 2026-10-12: (116) the line is read, in the one other text that restates it, as one condition: "one mansion" for the sun and the planet, with no time clause and no 135, so the restating text takes «གང་རུང་ཅིག་དང་སྐར་མ་གཅིག» as "either of the two, in one mansion". (117) The sun compared is the true sun (ཉི་དག), the true reckoning's, not the abridged one; the planet is not given a place beyond the name, so which place of Mercury or Venus is meant is still not said. (118) The result is stated as "smaller than above", not as "cannot be seized"; it is not glossed as a reading of WB's «བཟུང་མི་སྲིད» (the text does not name WB), so whether it is a reading of it or a rule from another source stays open. The Sakya print of the same text (printed pp. 166–167) is stream-only on archive.org and cannot be lent: not read.
