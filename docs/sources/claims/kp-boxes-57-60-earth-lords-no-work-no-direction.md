# KP boxes 57 to 60 name the earth lords as in WB, with no work, day or direction; boxes 61 to 64 are in another order

The bad halves of KP's boxes 57 to 60 name earth lords as a short run, in the same names as WB's matching works (the councils, 57; lasting works, 59; auspicious works, 60): phung zor and dbul lag («དབུལ་ལག» one name) in 57 to 60, pi ling, hal khyi, ki kang, Rahu and the Great Net in 60 (WB's councils has the same), the harsh earth lords as a class in 59. Differences: box 60 has «ཉི་ནུབ» (the sun's setting) before Rahu, which WB's councils lacks; box 57 has «དབུལ་ཉི་» where WB has «དབུལ་པོ་ལག་». No box gives a day, a direction or a work for a lord. KP's four boxes after 60 are in the order fierce, control, pacify, life and wealth by their titles, not WB's order; the earth-lord runs of the four are not settled by eye (worn impression), and KP2 (photographed, img. 285 = KP img. 62, img. 288 = KP img. 63 in a different layout; img. 286-287 blank versos) was too dim at 4.5× to settle them.
