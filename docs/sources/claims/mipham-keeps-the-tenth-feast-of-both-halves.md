# Mipham kept the feast of the tenth of both halves without break, the wrathful poison torma on the 29th

Dil mgo Mkhyen brtse's short life of Mipham (a modern author, facts only) reports of Mipham's daily practice that he kept the feast (ཚོགས་མཆོད, tshogs mchod) of the tenth of both the waxing and the waning half without break, the poison torma of the wrathful water (ཁྲོ་ཆུ, khro chu) on the twenty-ninth, a regular offering to the Dharma protectors, and the offering to Sarasvatī on the fifteenth and on Mars days (dkm:p76). The twenty-fifth is not named. This is a biographer's report, not Mipham's own word, and it is evidence for the practice of the one who ranks next to the White Beryl, as the pool's claim that Mipham gives the Guru's promise (tenth-day-padmasambhava-promise-to-come) is evidence for the text. Together they make the tenth of both halves, the 29th and the 15th the days Mipham's own life and writings name; the 25th is not among them.
