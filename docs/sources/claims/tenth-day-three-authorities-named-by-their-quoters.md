# The three authorities for the tenth-day feast as older authors quote them: Cakrasamvara, Yoginisancara, Longchenpa

Dpal sprul's three authorities (dpk.1:p380-381) are quoted by name by older authors, each with the same lines: for the Root Tantra of Cakrasaṃvara (དཔལ་འཁོར་ལོ་སྡོམ་པ་རྩ་བའི་རྒྱུད, dpal 'khor lo sdom pa rtsa ba'i rgyud) the verse «རང་འདོད་ལས་ལ་རྣལ་འབྱོར་པས། །ཆོ་ག་ཇི་བཞིན་སྦྱིན་པར་བྱ། །མར་གྱི་ངོ་ཡི་ཚེས་བཅུ་དང་། །ཟླ་བ་ཡར་ངོར་གྱུར་པ་ལ། །མྱུར་དུ་འབད་པས་མཆོད་བྱ་སྟེ།» (BDRC etexts, 2026-11-21: Padma dkar po's Sgrub thabs rin po che kun las btus pa, the Sgrub thabs kun btus, Zhe chen rgyal tshab's collected works, Ngag dbang kun dga' bsod nams; snippets, the quotations only: «དཔལ་འཁོར་ལོ་སྡོམ་པ་རྩ་བའི་རྒྱུད་ལས» and Padma dkar po «རྩ་རྒྱུད་ལས»). In that verse the offering is the torma «as one wishes, according to the rite» (not the feast), so the tenth is named as the time of the torma for the Root Tantra. For the Yoginīsañcāra, Tsong kha pa, Zhe chen and the Bde mchog drug cu ma's commentary give «རྣལ་འབྱོར་མ་ཀུན་སྤྱོད་ལས། དགུན་འབྲིང་ལ་སོགས་ཟླ་བ་ཡི། །བཅུ་པ་ལ་ནི་གུས་པས་མཆོད» (Tsong kha pa and the Bde mchog drug cu ma's commentary read «བཅུ་པ་ལ་ནི་གུས་པས་མཆོད», "honour with reverence"; Zhe chen has «རབ་ཏུ་མཆོད» as Dpal sprul has). The Longchenpa verse is quoted by Zhe chen rgyal tshab and by the Rdo rje'i dam tshig gi rnam bzhag text with a different first line from Dpal sprul's: «ཉ་དང་གནམ་སྟོང་ཚེས་བརྒྱད་ཉི་ཤུ་དགུ། །ཚེས་བཅུ་ཡར་ངོ་མར་ངོའི་ཉིན་དང་མཚན། །བསྙེན་བསྒྲུབ་ཚོགས་མཆོད་བསྐང་བཤགས་འབད་པར་བྱ།» ("the full moon and the new moon, the eighth, the twenty-ninth, the tenth of the waxing and the waning half, day and night: strive in approach and accomplishment, the feast, fulfilment and confession"), «ཀུན་མཁྱེན་ཆེན་པོས» without the title; BDRC's exact search of the lines finds only these quoters, so the work of Klong chen pa is not named. The chapter of the root tantra, of the Yoginīsañcāra and the work of Longchenpa were not located.

Located 2026-11-21 (rd-h): the root tantra's verse is Toh 368 ch. 27 (lsv:p467), the Yoginīsañcāra line Toh 375 ch. 10 (ysn:p79-80) and Longchenpa's lines the root verses of ch. 9 of his Sems nyid ngal gso with the Shing rta chen po (nsg:p57-58, nsc:p271-272); see the three claims cakrasamvara-root-tantra-ch27-torma-on-the-waning-tenth, yoginisancara-ch10-offering-on-the-tenth and longchenpa-ngal-gso-ch9-days-of-feast-and-confession.
