# The household's general verdicts when many share a mewa of the year

The household's general verdicts on the mewa of the year (WB vol. 1, pp. 407–408): read when many members of one house have their mewa of the year on the same number.

| Many on | Reading | Remedy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 white | a corpse comes though no one is due to die; gods' illness and epidemics | beware of impurity |
| the three whites (1, 6, 8) | the male god goes up to the sky; (second list) a god comes from the place of the gods' punishment: gods' illness and epidemics | flight and climbing rites, purifying washing; the gods' rite, poison washing, offering, fulfilment and confession |
| black (2) | the *ya bdud* stirs: illness, harm to servants, horses, dogs and cattle, separation | keep the man's and woman's country apart, move house and hearth, white earth and *tsha tsha*, recite the Prajñāpāramitā in three lengths, teaching and alms |
| 3 blue | the earth lords offended: the hearth falls, heads break, enemies rise, grief | the Uṣṇīṣa white and black, the *Gzungs bsdus*, the black three-headed one |
| 4 green | earth lords and nāgas offended: nāga illness, *dmu chu*, tumours | nāga offerings, the lake goddesses' thread-cross, the *ma mo* |
| 5 yellow | *rgyal 'gong*, loss, bile illness, *rbad 'dre* | the king's and blood thread-crosses, the hundred *bse*; do not make Bon practitioners enemies |
| 7 red | enemies and *sri* rise; wounds and blood, bad for one's horses | the *btsan*'s release, the *zor*, the red repelling |
| 9 red | life and fortune decline | the fortune rites, summoning life, the fortune door and peg |
| two black and dark blue (second list) | a corpse is wanted; illness, grief | the māra thread-cross, the ninefold repelling of grief |
| the life force's enemy rising, the power's enemy rising, the mewa red | bad for livestock | — |
| the power's enemy rising and the body red "mouth upward" | *sha bred*, bad for livestock | — |
| many black, one white | "the wolf rises from the hill-side" | each one's own mewa rites |
| many white, one black | "a bone in the butter" | each one's own mewa rites |

Each kind of person's own "two black", the mewa on which a fall is bad for that person: 1 white, small boys (children); 2 black, everyone, the crux for men in their prime; 3 blue, children (the *shar po*); 4 green, widows (*yugs sa*); 5 yellow, *brje*; 6 white, Bon (this pair not settled); 7 red, women; 8 white, ministers and monks; 9 red, the elders. To fall there is bad: raise the rites.
