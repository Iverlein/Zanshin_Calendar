# Dream images whose verdict rests on an unsettled word

The images below carry at least one verdict that rests on a word or a clause not settled; each is listed here apart and each image claim says so in its opening lines. The picker offers none of them until the word is settled (as the moment's signs are, SPEC §5.9.5). Not on this list, because the verdict does not rest on the word: WB ch. 30's fourth riding beast (read horse on an ink-filled stack; the verdict of the line holds for lion, elephant and buffalo), and the article KBS's gloss of «སྒ་མེད» as "without a voice", which the Tibetan makes "without a saddle" as in WB and RST (see dream-riding-donkey-bare).

| Word or clause | What rests on it | Images | Passage (state) |

| SVO «བགྲོད་མིན», "when it is not a road taken" | The five images the Svapnohana's second verse calls auspicious (the body struck with weapons, covered with small creatures, its dirt washed away, following excrement, blood flowing) rest on this clause: whether it limits the verdict ("not when it is a road taken") or says something else is not settled; the verse stands against every other text on four of the five (struck by weapons, creatures on the body, filth, blood: bad in LGD, SVI, RST, KBS). | dream-struck-by-weapons, dream-creatures-on-body, dream-filth, dream-blood-flowing, dream-bathing | svo:p268-271 (etext) |
| SVO «འགྲོན་བུ» | Read "traveller, guest" in ordinary Tibetan; the earlier summary "cowries" is not supported; the verdict (kinsfolk many) is for the whole run of king, elephant, horse, traveller, oxen and cows at the end of sleep. | dream-king-elephant-horse-traveller | svo:p268-271 (etext), svo:p269 (etext) |
| SVO «བླ་མོ» and «བཅུ་ཡི་བར» | An unclear word in "a horse, a (བླ་མོ) and a snake, a water creature seized: victory or goods, or a son"; and "within ten" (བཅུ་ཡི་བར) with no unit named, which the verdict of the white snake needs. | dream-snake-frog-water-creature | svo:p270 (machine) |
| SVO p. 270, the terse run | "A village: wealth; a barley fire offering to be known; with mustard, gain; flowers, flowers; fruit": the verdicts of the last members are not told; the run is not offered as rows. | dream-grain-harvest-fruit (the SVO row only) | svo:p270 (machine) |
| SVI «བྱ་ཤར་ཏེ་རུ» | The words of Amoghasiddhi's sign "a bird … sent as messenger" are unclear; the line is a family sign without a verdict of its own. | dream-birds | svi:p261-262 (etext) |
| LGD «རམ་རུག་སྦང་གཡེར» | "Using things such as … , goods of gossip": the first words are not understood; the item stands among the bad signs. | dream-goods-of-gossip | lgd:p968-969 (scan) |
