# Dream images whose verdict rests on an unsettled word

The images below carry at least one verdict that rests on a word or a clause not settled; each is listed here apart and each image claim says so in its opening lines. Settled since this claim was written (each reading named; the picker may offer these images): RD227 (2026-11-09, read on the scan and set against the Drathang print and the dictionaries) the creature names of ST ch. 81 (dream-sick-naga-illness-dreams: a meadow, tadpoles and frogs, fish and snakes, insects and bees, a scorpion and a spider; the etext's ལྕོལ is a misreading of ཉ་སྦྲུལ), the thread's mantra and བ་ར of ST ch. 91 (dream-sick-women-lead-away: the mantra letter by letter, བ་ར cattle and goats) and ST ch. 2's «ཡང» (light); RD229 and RD255 (the commentaries) དག of ST ch. 2, a plural or frequency marker in Byang pa, Rin spungs pa and Khro ru (dream-pregnant-man-seen), and the two doubtful pairs of ch. 81 narrowed (the sorting of the list by spirit, st-dream-naga-illness); RD264 (the scans of the Bhutan and 2005 prints) ལྷས་ཚོར་དུས་ཏེ and ལྡེར་བཟོ་འགུལ read on three prints, the ངུས of two etexts an OCR fault; RD271 (the dictionaries and BDRC's etexts, no scan) the ch. 7 verse's རྔ་མོ (the camel), རྨེ་ཅན (a man with a black birthmark; the sense of the verse stays between this and a base-born man), བྲོ་བའི་ཁྲལ་འདེད (a levy; which bro is not told) and བག་མ་ལན་པ (taking a bride), ch. 81's ལྡེར་བཟོ་འགུལ (the temple's clay statues move), ལྷས་ཚོར་དུས་ཏེ (the time felt by the god), གཏད་དུ་ཚུད་པ (within the aim of a harm sent, not a pledge) and རྟ་བླ (the centipede), ch. 91's མོ་ཕྱྭ (divination and its omen, one compound) and the Svapnohana's རྟ་བླ་མོ (a centipede: the line names creatures, not a horse); RD228 and RD231 (the Co ne machine reading of RD273, not read by eye, varies in «བཟློག་མིན», «འགྲོན་དུ», «བཅུ་པི་བར» and «གས་ཏྲི» and is not set against them) the spellings of the Svapnohana's «བཅུ་ཡི་བར», «བགྲོད་མིན» and «འགྲོན་བུ» (Peking, Narthang and the Dpe bsdur ma text agree; the sense of «བགྲོད་མིན» and the unit of the ten stay open; the traveller is settled and dream-king-elephant-horse-traveller leaves this list). Not on this list, because the verdict does not rest on the word: WB ch. 30's fourth riding beast (read horse on an ink-filled stack; the verdict of the line holds for lion, elephant and buffalo), the article KBS's gloss of «སྒ་མེད» as "without a voice", which the Tibetan makes "without a saddle" as in WB and RST (see dream-riding-donkey-bare), and the ch. 7 verse's word choices that change no verdict (the head broken or cut, the beer or the water, rme can or rje can: gt-dream-verse-variants). The picker offers none of the images below until the word is settled (as the moment's signs are, SPEC §5.9.5).
