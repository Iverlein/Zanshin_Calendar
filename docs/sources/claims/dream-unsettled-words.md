# Dream images whose verdict rests on an unsettled word

The images below carry at least one verdict that rests on a word or a clause not settled; each is listed here apart and each image claim says so in its opening lines. Struck by RD227 (2026-11-09), read on the scan and set against the Drathang print and the dictionaries: the creature names of ST ch. 81 (dream-sick-naga-illness-dreams: a meadow, tadpoles and frogs, fish and snakes, insects and bees, a scorpion and a spider; the etext's ལྕོལ is a misreading of ཉ་སྦྲུལ) and the thread's mantra and བ་ར of ST ch. 91 (dream-sick-women-lead-away: the mantra letter by letter, བ་ར cattle and goats); ST ch. 2's «ཡང» (light). The picker offers none of them until the word is settled (as the moment's signs are, SPEC §5.9.5). Not on this list, because the verdict does not rest on the word: WB ch. 30's fourth riding beast (read horse on an ink-filled stack; the verdict of the line holds for lion, elephant and buffalo), and the article KBS's gloss of «སྒ་མེད» as "without a voice", which the Tibetan makes "without a saddle" as in WB and RST (see dream-riding-donkey-bare).
