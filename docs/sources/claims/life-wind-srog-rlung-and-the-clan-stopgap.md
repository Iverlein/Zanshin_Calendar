# The birth's life-wind as one term, and the clan element as a stopgap (replies 134)

In question 134 (ys:i099, I5875 img. 99, machine; its clauses read on the scan by an earlier reading) the questioner writes «བཙས་པ་སྲོག་རླུང་གི་འབྱུང་བ», "the element of the birth's life-wind", and asks whether this life-wind and the rough clan element are one. Three things in the pool favour a single term, the life-wind (སྲོག་རླུང, srog rlung), over the pair of the life force and the wind-horse (སྲོག, རླུང་རྟ):

- The reply (ys:i491-492, scan) ends with «སྲོག་རླུང་ལ་བརྗོད་པ་ལས», "beyond what is said of the life-wind", as one thing.
- The question's own next clause speaks of the parents' «རླུང་རྒྱུ», their wind-course, as what must be one for a clan, not of a life force.
- In question 106 (ys:i092, machine) «སྲོག་རླུང» stands as one factor beside year, month, day and hour, the five from which "all results are shown"; and the questioner writes the birth's luck, elsewhere, as «བཙས་ཀླུངས» (question 114, ys:i094b, machine), a different word.

So «བཙས་པ་སྲོག་རླུང» is the birth's life-wind, a term of its own beside the birth's luck («བཙས་ཀླུང», the *klungs*), not a loose spelling of it; the notes' gloss of the kind of person as the birth luck (wb.2:p497) is a different reading of the same line and is not settled by this. The word «དཔྱད་འགོག་བྱེད་དུ» of reply 134 (ys:i491-492), asked as question 400, stands in the sentence "since every reckoning wants a clan, as a stopgap (དཔྱད་འགོག) some take the trigram as the clan's reckoning and identify the clan element with the birth trigram": read here as a stopgap, a means of blocking the lack of an examinable clan; no other occurrence in the replies was found.
