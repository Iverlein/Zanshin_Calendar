# Digging figure p. 387: a mtho is twelve finger-breadths, so the navel's six are half and the figure's five add up

The dictionaries give the span (མཐོ, mtho) a size apart from the plot: Tshig mdzod chen mo and Bod yig tshig gter rgya mtsho say it is the measure of twelve finger-breadths (also thumb tip to middle fingertip), and Negi's Sanskrit dictionary gives vitasti, twelve aṅgulas. Read so, p. 387's numbers fit the Bhutan digest's total of five: the stake half a mtho (མཐོ་ཕྱེད), the face one, the chest one, the navel one and six finger-breadths (one and a half), and, if the secret place takes one as in ch. 32's list, five in all. The six finger-breadths are then half a mtho. This is arithmetic on the passage's own numbers, not a sentence of a source, and ch. 34 does not give the secret place's share. Ch. 32's whole mtho for the stake stays the other count; a mtho as a tenth of the plot (p. 281) and as twelve finger-breadths are not shown to be one length. Not read again on the scan.
