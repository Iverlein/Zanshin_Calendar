# The sage's and the pig's seven days fixed by the true mean sun

WB fixes the sage's seven days (*drang srong*, «རི་ཏི» in the print) and the pig's seven days (*phag zhag*) in the true reckoning, against the earlier scholars who fixed them in the making (*byed rtsis*) only: the sage's days begin when the mean sun reaches mansion 10, 43 *chu tshod*, 30 *chu srang*; the pig's when it reaches 5, 46 and 30 (the number words «སྐར་ཕྱོགས་མེ་མཚོ་མཁའ་མེ་སྲང» and «མདའ་དང་དུས་མཚོ་མཁའ་མེ་སྲང»). The sage's seven days are those in which the water has eight qualities; the pig's, from the story of the *Mdzangs blun* of the trees about Meru, are seven days when the rain is poison and the grain's essence fails, and milk and butter are not sent out, water work and medical treatment are avoided. The author gives the rule that Mkhas dbang Lhun grub rgya mtsho, who is never in error, fixed the sage's rising for the eight (*rab brgyad*) with the *drag po* free of the foot.
