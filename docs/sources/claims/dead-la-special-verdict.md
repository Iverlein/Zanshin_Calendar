# The la pebbles' special verdict: black or half before each place

WB's third verdict on the twelve la pebbles (ཁྱད་པར་ཁ་དམར, khyad par kha dmar; vol. 2, pp. 53–54): a black pebble set before a place (input: a living member's seventh falling there), each place named and read; half black and half white (ཕྱེད་མ, phyed ma) read again; "if white pebbles are set, the black verdict reverses".

| Place | Its name | Black before it | Half before it | Remedy named |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| great cut-off | the place of the grave | forefathers' crimes and broken oaths, the lama's reproof; a blade-killed or wasting illness (གཅོང, gcong) corpse in the grave, the grave's protector turned; short lives from the head, the kin cut in two or three | the male line wanders abroad, elder brides die of *gcong*, the elders fall ill of impurity; ruin within three generations | — |
| small cut-off | the place of the brides | oaths and the gods' cave defiled, women take the men's rank; elder brides die by knives; younger brides with two husbands [?]; a husband kills a wife, a bride dies of *gcong* | separated women taken as brides, power and wealth eaten by others | — |
| mother year | the mother who bore, the great Chinese tiger (རྒྱ་སྟག་ཆེན་མོ, rgya stag chen mo) | men and wealth given away from the fortune, fortune spent; leaders ate oaths and left; men in their prime die young | kindness from the mother's side, offering-wealth; wealth runs empty | suppress the harmful spirits of the dead (སྲི, sri), invoke the gods, call the fortune (half) |
| son year | the son who bore, the sky's foot, son-in-law and grandson | enmity with a nāga *btsan* spirit (ཀླུ་བཙན, klu btsan) at the red rock; blades around; bad prospects for the brides | the grave met by wolves [?] or fortune lost; good first half of life, faulty descendants after | — |
| power year | the place of producing, of the power year | years, months and corpses mistaken; thriving then declining, maimed descendants, the grave dug up or fortune lost; a mishap within seven years at the root place | oaths eaten, the male line cut off; of a hundred men two will help in the end | a thousand offerings (half) |
| friend year | the place of the speckled black servant | a bastard lost and killed, brides with two husbands, the house to ruin; the male god lost, children maimed | many men and horses came or come; without a wife, food and wealth halved with others | ablution, clearing, fleeing and chasing; a nāga pledge bound on the fields (black) |
| enemy year (printed «དགྲ་དར») | the place of ablution | the grave on an enemy's hill or the skulls given to nāgas; in three generations a hundred enemy fathers killed and a hundred sons rising; otherwise one's own line great | ever disputes with enemies | turning the mind to religion (half) |

Where several pebbles are "set" (བསྟུད), the notes count two or three gathered as set, laid from the eldest down; a great friend year white with a small one black counts as speckled (p. 502).
