# Narthang catalogue images 208–240: no entry for the Examination of Dreams; the only dream title is Nāgārjuna's Wish-fulfilling Jewel Talk

Yigdzin-1 over images 208–240 of I2KG212509 (vol. 225; 33 images, whole images, on 2026-11-11) finds no «རྨི་ལམ་བརྟག་པ» and no Svapnohana entry. The word «རྨི་ལམ» occurs once, in img. 239 line 3: «རྨི་ལམ་ཡིད་བཞིན་ནོར་བུའི་གཏམ» made by Nāgārjuna, a work of another kind (svo:i239). The images 208–216 carry the last tantra entries (a Vibhūticandra translation of a secret practice of Nāgārjuna on img. 209, the Cakrasaṃvara non-dual practice on img. 215) and from about img. 219 the Prajñāpāramitā and Madhyamaka commentaries, so the tantra runs of the catalogue end before img. 218; the Examination of Dreams, a tantra-section work, is then to be looked for in images 3–140, which stay unread. The even-numbered images of this stretch (208, 210, 214, 216, 218, 220, 226, 230, 232, 234, 236, 238) came back empty or near-empty from the model on whole images and were not read; images 241–288 were not read.
