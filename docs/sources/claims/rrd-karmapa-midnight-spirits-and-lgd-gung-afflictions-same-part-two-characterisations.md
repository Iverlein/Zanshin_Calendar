# The Karmapa's midnight dream of spirits and LGD's «gung» dream of the afflictions: the same part of the night in his usage, two characterisations, no text reads one as the other (RD384, q1007)

For the question of the Karmapa's midnight dream of spirits and the Lama Gongdü's «གུང» dream of the afflictions: the two words name the same part of the night in the Karmapa's own use, the two characterisations differ, and no text read says that one reads the other (RD384, 2026-11-10, etext only).

(1) Same part: in the dream verses the middle part is «ནམ་ཕྱེད» (rrd.5:p57-59) and in his counsel for retreat «གུང» is the session between dusk and dawn (rrd.5:p111-112), the one for the clear light of sleep; LGD's «གུང» stands between dusk and early dawn (lgd:p966-967), and the Rin chen gter mdzod's bardo instruction writes «ནམ་གུང» (lgd-three-parts-of-night-named-in-the-karmapa-and-the-rin-chen-gter-mdzod). So «གུང» is the middle of the night in the Karmapa's hand as in LGD, and the Karmapa's «ནམ་ཕྱེད» is that part, if the two words of his are one.
(2) Two characterisations: the Karmapa gives Marpa's «power of spirits (གདོན་དབང)»; LGD, «dreams of the afflictions (ཉོན་མོངས་པ)»; the commentators on his Zab mo nang don give the midnight to bile, a third characterisation, in neither. Klong rdol bla ma Ngag dbang blo bzang's verse (st:p31-32c) gives the midnight third «གུང» to «the play of demons» (བདུད་སྲིན་ཆོ་འཕྲུལ), which is nearer the Karmapa's «spirits» than LGD's wording.
(3) A doubt, not a finding: the Mañjuśrī root tantra ties the three constituents to the three poisons, desire to phlegm, hatred to bile, delusion to wind (mmk.1:p400-401); if midnight is bile and bile is hatred, then «afflictions» might be a short word for what the root tantra's second part holds; no text read says this.
