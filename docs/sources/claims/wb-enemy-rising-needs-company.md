# The enemy rising makes an aspect bad only with company: the grammar of «སྲོག་མ་མ་གུད་པ»

Narrowed 2026-11-01 (the line read on the Gser thang scan, I3CN5029 img. 489, the last line of p. 483, and BDRC's search finds the same string on the reprint's etext only). «སྲོག་དགྲ་དར་ཀྱང་སྲོག་མ་མ་གུད་པ་དང་སྤར་སྨེ་ངན་པ་མ་འཛོམ་ན་དེ་ཙམ་མི་སྐྱོན། འཛོམ་ན་ངན་ནོ།» is read as follows. The doubled «མ་མ» can be «སྲོག་མ» + «མ་གུད་པ», "the life force's mother, not declined" (no. A), or the second མ a dittography of «སྲོག་མ་གུད་པ», "the life force's mother in decline" (no. B). In A the sentence is "even if the life force's enemy rises, when the mother is not in decline and the bad trigram and mewa do not come together it is not so faulty; if (they) come together, bad", where «འཛོམ» then refers to the faults that fail the two conditions; in B "if the mother's decline and a bad trigram and mewa do not come together with it, it is not so faulty; if they do, bad". The notes' next page puts the rule in the other direction too (p. 484, etext: «དགྲ་དར་ཞེས་ངན་དྲུག་ལ་བབ་པའོ», "the enemy rising" falls on the six bad ones, not glossed there). Either way the answer is the same for a build: the enemy's rise alone is not a bar; it is bad when the aspect's mother is in decline and a bad trigram and mewa meet it. The grammar of the doubled མ is not settled by the scan; an editor's dittography is the likelier.
