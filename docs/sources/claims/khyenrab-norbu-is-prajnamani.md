# Prajñāmaṇi (པྲཛྙཱ་མ་ཎི) is Khyenrab Norbu's Sanskrit name, by BDRC's person record

For questions 672 and 659. BDRC's person record P227 (fetched 2026-11-13 from purl.bdrc.io/resource/P227.json) is Khyenrab Norbu of Tsetang (རྩེད་ཐང་མཁྱེན་རབ་ནོར་བུ, rtsed thang mkhyen rab nor bu), 1883–1962, noted as a famed medical practitioner and scholar connected with the Chagpori in Lhasa. Its other name, entered in Wylie, is «pradz+nyA ma Ni», that is པྲཛྙཱ་མ་ཎི (Prajñāmaṇi), beside the primary name «mkhyen rab nor bu». So Prajñāmaṇi is the Sanskrit form of Khyenrab Norbu's name, by the catalogue: prajñā is «mkhyen rab» and maṇi is «nor bu».

BDRC's full-text search for «པྲཛྙཱ་མ་ཎི» (6 hits, 2026-11-13) finds the name used for him in his own and his pupils' writings: as the author's name closing his short works in the anthology *Bdud rtsi sman gyi mdo chog* (MW2DB4624; «pradz+nyA ma Ni» after the titles of the Medicine Buddha empowerment and the wish-prayer for the medical teaching; the record gives Khyenrab Norbu among its authors), and in the collected works of A mdo Dge bshes 'Jam dpal rol pa'i blo gros (MW28936), where the snippet reads «sman rtsis spyi 'doms pradz+nyA ma Ni mchog gis», "the venerable Prajñāmaṇi, the head of medicine and reckoning". Search-only etexts: the snippets are all that is seen, the scans were not opened.

The colophon of *The Precious Key* (pk:i383-385) says the pupil gathered the text when "the presence of Prajñāmaṇi" (པྲཛྙཱ་མ་ཎིའི་ཞལ་སྔ, pradz+nyA ma Ni'i zhal snga) taught the Mentsikhang's pupils. That this is Khyenrab Norbu rests on the name's use for him in the works above and on BDRC's record, not on a line of the colophon, which gives no father, place or title for Prajñāmaṇi beyond the teaching of medicine and reckoning; no other person of the name was met in the six hits.
