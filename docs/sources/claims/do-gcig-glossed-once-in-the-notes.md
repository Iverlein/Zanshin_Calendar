# The do gcig is glossed once, in Mipham's note on the birth reckoning's results

Mipham glosses the *do gcig* (དོ་གཅིག) once, in his note on the birth reckoning's results (wb.2:p480): of the two kinds of result of the four planets, the roof-cover (phub) and the *do gcig*, "the count from the mother's year-trigram the text states plainly; the count of the inner planet's *kag* he has identified with the trigram that is fallen on, on top of the born trigram, being reached"; in meaning, one born on something like the three planets' joining who comes again upon that joining is rough. No text defines the word where the trigram reckonings use it (vol. 1, p. 317 and p. 390; vol. 2, p. 89 and the spirits' speeches of p. 164): the note is the one gloss, and the regent's reply 152 reads the word as what falls onto the born trigram. The question repeats questions 350 and 385; it is narrowed by the same note and no further.
