# The Mchog dga's Saturn lines (a keen dream in the eleventh place) and its Ketu line (bad dreams and sorrow) are in no text of the root that was searched

The Mchog dga's lines on Saturn in the eleventh place («རྨི་ལམ་རྣོ་བ», keen dreams; kd.3:p187), on Saturn with Ketu («རྨི་ལམ་ངན་དང་མྱ་ངན་འོང», bad dreams and sorrow; kd.3:p315) and the Saturn-and-hills lines of kd.3:p177-179 are in no text of the Dbyangs 'char that was searched. The Derge etext of the whole volume (VE23703_1521, 552 pages) has the dream planets only on pp. 121-122, without Saturn, Rahu or Ketu, and has no line «སྤེན་པ་ཡི་ནི་ཀེ་ཏུ་ལ» or «མཁྲིས་ནད་ཚད་ནད་གྲང་ནད»; the Narthang, Peking and Gser bris ma scans and their interlinear glosses, as far as the machine reading goes, have no «སྤེན་པ» between the dream planets and no «འཛེག». BDRC's exact-phrase search finds «སྤེན་པ་ཡི་ནི་ཀེ་ཏུ་ལ» and «མཁྲིས་ནད་ཚད་ནད་གྲང་ནད་འོང» only in two Kun 'dus chen mo etexts (VEIE0OPI2F246A26_I4717 and VEIE0OPI51EA6051_I2PD16001); «རྨི་ལམ་རྣོ་བ་དང་དགྲ་ཉམས» finds nothing. The lines are therefore the Mchog dga's own, or come from a source other than the tantra's Tengyur translation; the root's rule gives Saturn, Rahu and Ketu no dream. Where the commentary takes them from is not answered.
