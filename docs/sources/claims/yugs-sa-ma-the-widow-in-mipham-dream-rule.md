# «ཡུགས་ས་མ» in Mipham's dream-rite rule is a widow

# «ཡུགས་ས་མ» in Mipham's be bum rule is the widow (yugs sa ma), the unclean one of mourning (q1022)

In Mipham's rule «འདི་སྒྲུབ་དུས་མི་གཙང་ཁྱི་དང་ཁྱད་པར་ཡུགས་ས་མ་ལ་འཛེམ» (mph.128:i189-192: while practising this, keep from the unclean, from dogs, and especially from «ཡུགས་ས་མ») the word is a widow. The White Beryl's death trigram (wb.2:p60-62, read on the scan) has the same spelling in the entry of the trigram Zon, «a widow's (ཡུགས་ས་མ, yugs sa ma) black traces that followed ... cut the life», and in the entry of Kham «ཡུགས», a widow, in the same place; the mewa's lines of the same chapter name «ཡུགས་ས» the mourning place and its impure food as a cause of death (wb.2:p57-58 is quoted in the claim dead-skyur-sour-thing-of-mourning). The project's earlier glosses give the word the same way: «ཡུགས་ས», the mourning for a dead spouse (Jim Valby, Ives Waldo), and «yug za ma», «yugs sa mo», a widow (Dan Martin), in the readings of KP's box 37 (kp-activities.md) and of WB's ch. 34 (the Ox day on which taking a widow is good). So the rule names three things to keep from while the dream rite is done: the unclean, dogs, and above all a widow, a person in the pollution of a death. The be bum's own rules (tdr:p337-342e) were not re-read for the same word here. The etext's spelling is Mipham's as the machine reads it; «ས་མ» for «ས་མོ» is the etext's, not checked on the scan.
