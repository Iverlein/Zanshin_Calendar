# The strike of wrong deeds, and who the strikes reach

The eighth great strike, of wrong deeds and sudden conditions (WB vol. 2, pp. 89–90), and the small strikes under it. Mishandling the corpse or the cutting of the death-gap, a badly laid former grave, a great epidemic, curses and demons: each examined and treated. Dying on the executioner's kin by time: on the father of the year executioner (the clan element's grave year risen in the year) bad for fathers, uncles and elders; on the mother of the month executioner, grandmothers and elder women; on the son of the day executioner, young men; on the grandson of the hour executioner, small children. Dying on the enemy executioner, enemy mansion, four first mansions or la mansion repeats; the trigrams *dwa*, *zin*, *khon*, *kham* repeat. By the mewa of death, who is struck: two-black the beer-maid, widows and household lord; three-blue men without protector, sons-in-law, nephews, grinders; four-green daughters-in-law, sisters, water fetchers, servants; five-yellow the old one's sisters and carriers; seven-red butchers and fire carriers; nine-red household lord and maternal uncle; a dead with black mewa and life force in decline: "the king's gate blocked", a second corpse comes, and the seven-day burning, dough offering, feast and red feast are forbidden. By the element that is many among the dead's year, month, day, hour, life force, body, power, luck, trigram, mewa and progressed sign: wood, grandmothers and the homeland; fire, maternal uncles, kin, wealth and friends; earth, the roads and four borders; iron, father and uncles; water, the corpse's owner and household. The eight places opening death: rat or pig dying on *li*, tiger or hare on *khon*, horse or snake on *dwa*, the four executioners on *kham*, bird or monkey on *zin* (with *khen*, *gin*, *zon* implied, the notes): a corpse companion is wanted.
