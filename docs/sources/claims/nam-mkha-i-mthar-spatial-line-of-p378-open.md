# «ནམ་མཁའི་མཐར» is spatial in the volume; the line «at the end of the sky the dream is examined» stays open (RD395, q1102)

In the Mahākāla commentary of Dpal rta dbyangs (Derge Tengyur vol. 28, pp. 317–429; the same text in the Dpe bsdur ma), the line «ནམ་མཁའི་མཐར་ནི་རྨི་ལམ་བརྟགས» ("at the end of the sky the dream is examined") stands among the preliminaries of the mandala: the disciples are given the ten-syllable mantra to recite, and the dream is examined before the vajra master makes the cord-line. The phrase «ནམ་མཁའི་མཐར» and «ནམ་མཁའི་མཐའ» is in the same volume a spatial one: "the ten directions to the end of the sky" (p. 108), "the form that reaches to the end of the sky" (p. 274), "equal to the edge of the sky" (p. 326), and the lexica give «nam mkha'i mtha'» only as the limit of space. In no place read does it stand for a time of the night.

The sense of the line is open: the dawn, a point of the visualisation, or the end of a meditation on space cannot be told apart from the text. The print has no other reading; no Sanskrit of the commentary was found.
