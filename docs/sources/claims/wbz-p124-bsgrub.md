# The five graves' «bsgrub» is carved so on the blocks

The fifth of the five graves on p. 124 of WB vol. 2, «སྲེག་སྐྱེལ་སྦ་ཞིང་བསྒྲུབ», is «བསྒྲུབ» on the Zhol blocks too (ས, then ག with ra-btags and u), so the reading «བསྣུབ» of p. 74 is not what the blocks carve here; p. 125's «བསྒྲུབ་པ་ན» was seen the same at a lower scale.
