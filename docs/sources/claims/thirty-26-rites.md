# The thirty: the twenty-sixth point, the three-headed earth lord, for the rites, white, black and speckled

The twenty-sixth of the thirty starting points (སྐོར་འགོ, skor 'go), from the mouth of the three-headed earth lord (ས་བདག་མགོ་གསུམ, sa bdag mgo gsum), is read for the rites, white, black and speckled. Its eight pebbles are laid as for every point (claims thirty-count and thirty-eight-fighters), each white or black, and read so:

| Pebble | Asks | White | Black | Remedy for black |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | By the white rite, whether the earth lord (ས་བདག, sa bdag) have been offered to | The earth lord king is offered to | The king is not offered to | Offer to the earth lord king and take care |
| 2 | By the speckled, whether the earth lords are disturbed | Not disturbed, good | Disturbed | The great rite for the disturbed is precious |
| 3 | By the black, whether Rudra has been liberated | Liberated, good | Not liberated | The black rites that turn back enemies |
| 4 | Whether the enemy has been tamed by the name-sound (མཚན་སྒྲ, mtshan sgra) | Tamed, good | Not tamed | Press down the enemy sri (དགྲ་སྲི, dgra sri) |
| 5 | Whether the rite for gossip (མི་ཁ, mi kha) has been sent | It has been sent; no fault, one has met the gods | It has not been sent | Turn and turn back the crowd's gossip and do the rite of the fifth *sla nga* (སླ་ང; not understood) |
| 6 | Whether the male line's turning back of the crowd has been done | Done, good | Not done | Strive to turn back for the male line |
| 7 | Whether the female line's turning back («ཐུན་བཟློག», l. 1; «མཐུན་བཟློག», l. 8) has been done | Done, good | Not done | Strive at the kinds of turning back for the female line |
| 8 | Whether the earth lords' path has been mended | Mended | Not mended | Strive at the rites |

Whose year is reckoned: the person's own (the notes, vol. 2, p. 533: «གཞན་རྣམས་སོ་སོའི་ལོ་རྟ»).

Which of the eight fighting pebbles is which check is not stated; the order of p. 453 is the likely one (claim thirty-board-groups; question 259).
