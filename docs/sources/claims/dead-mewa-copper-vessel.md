# The mewa's copper vessel: the household's birth mewa

"This is the mewa's copper vessel" (སྨེ་བའི་ཟངས་ཀྱམ, sme ba'i zangs kyam; WB vol. 2, pp. 54–55): over one paternal kin, when many of the living were born on one mewa, a cause and a rite. Mipham's notes: "in the mewa's copper vessel, the living's birth mewa many seven red and so on" (p. 502). Input: the count of living members per birth mewa.

| Many born on | Reading | Remedy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7 red | a death by the blade hidden between rock and water; many die by the blade | tame the harm of the *btsan* spirits (བཙན, btsan), bind the blade's mouth |
| 2 black | water and blades, water illnesses and dropsy (དམུ་ཆུ, dmu chu); bad for widows and widowers | turning back the assembly, turning the ritual weapon (ཟོར, zor), fulfilment rites; or the four continents' thread-cross (གླིང་བཞིའི་སྤྱི་མདོས, gling bzhi'i spyi mdos) |
| 5 yellow | a corpse of a … [?] left abroad, many … [?] now | five hundred clay votive (སཙྪ, tsha tsha) stūpas |
| 4 green | if nāgas carried someone off in the kin, bearable; if not, nāga illness always | the nāgas' offerings [?], release from spells |
| 3 blue | a forefather's plague corpse lay or was hidden at a stone's foot [?] | ox rites, «ལྷོག་པ» (lhog pa, not understood), incense [?]; the lake goddesses' (མཚོ་སྨན, mtsho sman), female *bsen* spirits (བསེན་མོ, bsen mo) and nāga rites |
| white | the gods displeased; king-spirit illness (རྒྱལ་ནད, rgyal nad), madness, stupor | beware of impurity, hearth smoke and feud; restore the male god, exorcise the king spirit |
| 9 red | the fortune declined | call the fortune, bind a fortune pledge (གཡང་གཏའ, g.yang gta') |

WB writes the colours: «བདུན་དམར», «ནག་པོ», «སེར་པོ», «ལྗང་གུ», «སྔོན», «དཀར་པོ», «དགུ་དམར»; the numbers for black, yellow, green and blue follow the mewa's colours, and "white" is not numbered (1, 6 or 8). The syllable in the yellow row (p. 54) is not settled on the scan.
