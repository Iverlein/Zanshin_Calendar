# Longchenpa's passing offering is kept on the 18th of the twelfth month

The day of Klong chen rab 'byams pa (Longchen Rabjam) is the 18th of the twelfth Tibetan month in every source read, Tibetan and English, Nyingma houses of Amdo, Central Tibet and exile alike:

- the Nyingma monasteries of Reb gong: the offering of the time (དུས་མཆོད, dus mchod) of Klong chen rab 'byams pa on the 18th of the twelfth month, with the self-empowerment and feast of the guru accomplishment Thig le rgya can (rgn:p1);
- Ge smad: his passing offering (འདས་མཆོད, 'das mchod) on the 18th of the twelfth winter month (gmd:p1);
- Smyo shul mkhan po's history: a master passed «on the 18th of the Rgyal month, the day of Rgyal dbang Klong chen pa's passing offering» (nkc:p1); the Rgyal month is the twelfth Hor month in the White Beryl's model almanac (wb.1:p154-171);
- a monastery in exile: his passing offering in Tibetan month 12, no day (gtb:p1); Kong sprul Yon tan rgya mtsho's foundation: the Nyingthig offering to the lama on his festival in the twelfth month (ksr:p367-368);
- the Rigpa students' calendar and Jeff Watt's list: 12/18 (tbc:p1, har:p1); Mindrolling Monastery: 25 January 2027, which is 12/18 by the app's reckoning (mlc:p1).

What is kept: an offering to the lama of the Heart Essence (snying thig) lineage, with a feast; the Reb gong house adds the self-empowerment of the Thig le rgya can. All of it rank 3 (search snippets and web pages); the year and day of his death in his own lives were not read.
