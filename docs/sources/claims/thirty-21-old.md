# The thirty: the twenty-first point, the great deer, for the old

The twenty-first of the thirty starting points (སྐོར་འགོ, skor 'go), from the mouth of the great deer (ཤ་བ་རྒྱ་བོ, sha ba rgya bo), is read for the old, the *bkra*, the reckoning of the aged (བཀྲ, bkra). Its eight pebbles are laid as for every point (claims thirty-count and thirty-eight-fighters), each white or black, and read so:

| Pebble | Asks | White | Black | Remedy for black |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Whether the white bkra sees the great-grandchildren | It sees them, good | It does not: bad for the human line | The bkra's calling back prosperity (གཡང་ལོན, g.yang lon) and the like |
| 2 | Whether the speckled bkra sees the grandsons | It sees them, very good | It does not: bad | The rite of calling back the bkra's prosperity |
| 3 | Whether the black bkra sees the granddaughters | It does not see daughters, good | It sees daughters: bad for the human line | A turning back (བཟློག, bzlog) rite |
| 4 | Whether the black bkra sri presses | Not pressed: good for the human line | Pressed, bad | As remedy rite (གཏོ, gto), summon the bkra's prosperity to grow |
| 5 | Whether the bkra king has come forth to the royal place | Come forth, very good | Not come forth, bad | Summoning the bkra's prosperity and other rites of good fortune |
| 6 | Whether the auspicious castle has been built | Built, very good | Not built, bad | The chanted rite (གྱེར་གཏོ, gyer gto), empowerment, recite the *Heaped Good Fortune* (བཀྲ་ཤིས་བརྩེགས་པ, bkra shis brtsegs pa) |
| 7 | Whether the black bkra cuts the generations | Not cut: sons and grandsons are seen, good | The generations are cut | The rite of increasing the auspicious human line |
| 8 | Whether auspicious sons and grandsons are gathered | Gathered | Not gathered, bad | Summoning the bkra's prosperity |

Whose year is reckoned: For one who reached old age and died, the old person's year and the year of death, with the age (notes, p. 532).

Which of the eight fighting pebbles is which check is not stated; the order of p. 453 is the likely one (claim thirty-board-groups; question 259).
