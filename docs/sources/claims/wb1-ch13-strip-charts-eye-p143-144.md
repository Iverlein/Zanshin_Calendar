# Ch. 13 strip charts: pp. 143-144 read by place, the same few names in the cells

On pp. 143 and 144 of the Gser thang reprint (img. 150-151) the four strip charts (dragon, snake, horse, sheep) were read by eye on 1.6x crops, left and right half. Single-word cells read with fair confidence: nang bdud, dur len, bus bdud, ba dan, bshan pa, se bdud; snang sel, bla mkhyen, gshed and bya khyung are doubtful. A cell holds one of this set or is left empty, and the same names recur in the same cells of neighbouring charts (bus bdud as the third cell of the left column of the horse and the sheep charts). P. 143 carries a heading row of six cells whose first reads nyi nub (the sun's setting) and whose others carry day-part names that were not settled; p. 144 has none. The multi-line cells, the numerals and the strip captions (a left line beginning he ru ka, a right line beginning klu gza') were not settled. The page does not say what the cells stand for.
