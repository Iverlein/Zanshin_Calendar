# What RD56 adds to and settles in the topic files

What RD56 adds to and settles in the topic files. (1) white-beryl-ch34.md left the digging rite before work (6) «not written out» (pp. 386–388, the earth lord's body): p. 387 is now written out whole in wb.2:p387, with the figure's colours, the measures, the nine parts and the signs of what is dug. (2) The numbered list on p. 378 is read on the reprint and agrees with the inventory of white-beryl-ch34.md (42 raising supports, 43 sickness, 44 turning back); the machine reading's duplicate «༤༢» and the unnumbered sickness are its slips. (3) The machine readings of p. 381's foot dropped lines (the Bre-born's character and a half-line of the Khrums); supplied from the scan. (4) earth-lords.md's pig day for the hearth god, «ལྷོ», is confirmed against the machine reading «ལྟོ». (5) year-month-earth-lords.md says only that WB lists the lords that do not move (pp. 175–177, «not checked line by line»): their layout by quarter and element is now written (wb.2:p175-176), and the numbers of the lords of the corners and quarters, 7 to 15, are read on the reprint. (6) pp. 236–237's Rāhu course, the eleven earth kings' times and the black times, and p. 240's closing note, which none of the topic files quote, are in wb.2:p236-237 and wb.2:p240. No contradiction of the topic files was found.
