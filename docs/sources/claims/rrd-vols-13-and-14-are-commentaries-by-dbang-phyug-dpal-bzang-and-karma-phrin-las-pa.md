# Vols. 13 and 14 of the Karmapa's collected-works etext hold commentaries on his Zab mo nang gi don by Dbang phyug dpal bzang po and by Karma Phrin las pa, not his own text (RD384)

The two commentaries in vols. 13 and 14 of the third Karmapa's collected-works etext (BDRC MW30541) are not his: vol. 13 is Dbang phyug dpal bzang po's, vol. 14 Shar Dwags po paṇḍita Karma Phrin las pa's, both on his Zab mo nang gi don (RD384, 2026-11-10, etext only).

The colophon of vol. 13 (p. 483-485; rrd.13:p484) names the monk of lower Mdo khams Īśvaraśrībhadra, that is Dbang phyug dpal bzang po, who "gathered it as the omniscient Rang byung rdo rje spoke" at Mtshur phu, and the editors' note, the work of the abbot of Sgam po, Chos rje Shākya bzang po, which had it printed and checked. The colophon of vol. 14 (p. 385-387; rrd.14:p385) names Karma Phrin las pa (the third Shar Dwags po paṇḍita), who asked the treatise of his lama Chos grags rgya mtsho, and says the commentary is "a legacy of Rang byung rdo rje"; the commentary quotes the root verses by their opening words and the Karmapa's autocommentary as «the commentary says» (འགྲེལ་པར་གསུངས). Both sit in the collection of the Karmapa's works as the BDRC set has it (the page headings read «the collected works of the third Karmapa Rang byung rdo rje»), so BDRC's full-text search lists the commentaries' dream passages under «gsung 'bum/ rang byung rdo rje» although they are not his words; only the places read in vol. 5 (rrd.5:p57-59, p64, p111-112) are.
