# The head of the set on Zhol img. 658, last line, carries the stack of «བརྗེ» with the ཇ tail

On the Zhol print (WBZ, I1KG1710 img. 658, the last line, «དེ་བཞིན་བ[stack]ེའི་རྩིས་སྐོར་བཞི», a crop of x 2600–2820, y 405–495 at 9×) the stack after ba has an e-vowel over it and a long tail curving down to the right under the line, the foot of ཇ, as the black verse's stack on img. 659 and the stack of p. 17; Yigdzin-1 reads «བ་ནྡྷེའི» there, a machine lean to the common monk's word. The crop is cut on the left and the base letters are not told apart, so this is agreement in shape, not a letter-by-letter reading. The head names the set, as the heads of the ngakpa's, the bonpo's and the minister's sets that follow it do, so the set's person is written with the same stack at its head, in its black verse (img. 659) and at p. 17 (img. 647), all three with the ཇ tail.
