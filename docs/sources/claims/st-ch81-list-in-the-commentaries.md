# Ch. 81's dream list in the commentaries: which reach it, what they quote and gloss, spelled as it stands

RD303 (2026-11-10), etext, and the scan of Khro ru's folios. Of the seven commentaries named, five reach ch. 81 (Blue Beryl part 3, Khro ru, the Drikung, Skyem pa Tshe dbang, and Dar mo sman rams pa in the lower half of the Mes po'i zhal lung set); Byang pa, Rin spungs pa and Zur mkhar ba end before the Instruction Tantra.

Only the Blue Beryl, Khro ru and the Drikung quote or gloss the list itself; Skyem pa and Dar mo have the outline only. The tadpole is ལྕོང་མོ (lcong mo) in the glosses of the Blue Beryl and Khro ru, which read ལྕོང on the verse (Khro ru's scan, moderate), and the Drikung keeps ལྗོང (ljong) as the Serthang does, so ལྗོང and ལྕོང are one word spelt two ways; the etext's ལྕོལ is nowhere. The glosses split སྡིག་སྡོམ into two creatures, སྡིག་པ and སྡོམ (scorpion and spider by the dictionaries; no commentary names the animals), and split the line into frog, tadpole, fish, snake, worm, bee-grub, the two, and, beyond the verse, རྟ་བླ and the ant. ནེའུ་གསིང is the spelling of the Blue Beryl, Khro ru and the Drikung's editor's variant; the Drikung's print reads ནིའུ་གསིང as the Serthang does; nobody glosses it beyond placing it among the signs of the harm sent.

Khro ru's creature gloss is the Blue Beryl's with spelling changes only. The Blue Beryl's etext has ལྟས་ཚོར་བའི་དུས in the closing clause; its page is not open to check.
