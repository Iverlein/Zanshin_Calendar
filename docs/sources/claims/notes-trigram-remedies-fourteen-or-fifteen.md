# WB counts the trigram's remedies fourteen, the notes fifteen, and no commentary counts them

The fourteen of WB and the fifteen of the notes stand as the two counts of the trigram's remedies: WB's line of the remedy pebbles (vol. 2, p. 50–51) says «སྤར་བཅོས་ལ་བཅུ་བཞི», fourteen; Mipham's note (wb.2:p517) lists, for the eight identifying pebbles, six for whichever trigram it is: its general remedy, its sum of slips, the white, black and speckled rites one each, and the smith's paper-roll with its remedy, and says «བཅོ་ལྔ». The six items sum to fourteen with the eight; the fifteenth appears only if the paper-roll and its remedy are two (seven items), which the clause's «དྲུག་རེ» ("six each") does not allow. No commentary, LT, UT, KD, the Bhutan digest or the *Lotus Pond* (which sends the trigram remedies to the table), has the count. The notes' «བཅོ་ལྔའོ» is therefore either the notes' own slip for fourteen or counts the paper-roll's remedy apart; not decided.
