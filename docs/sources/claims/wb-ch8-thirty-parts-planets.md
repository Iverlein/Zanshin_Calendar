# The thirty parts of the union of times: the planets' stretches in unequal and equal signs

The thirty parts (*sum cu cha*) of the union of times: the common *dhruva* divided by 60; the quantity found, 1 to 30, falls to a planet by stretch. In the unequal signs (Aries, Gemini, Leo, Libra, Sagittarius, Aquarius) Mars 1-5, Saturn 6-10, Jupiter 11-18, Mercury 19-25, Venus 26-30; in the equal signs (Taurus, Cancer, Virgo, Scorpio, Capricorn, Pisces) Venus 1-5, Mercury 6-12, Jupiter 13-20, Saturn 21-25, Mars 26-30. The stretches are 5, 5, 8, 7, 5 and 5, 7, 8, 5, 5 parts, each list sums to 30. This is the Indian *triṃśāṃśa* rule: Bṛhat Parāśara Horā Śāstra 6.27, «triṃśāṃśeśāśca viṣame kujarkījyajñabhārgavāḥ, pañcapañcāṣṭasaptākṣabhāgānāṃ vyatyayāt same»: in the odd signs Mars, Saturn, Jupiter, Mercury, Venus with 5, 5, 8, 7, 5 degrees, in the even signs the order reversed (enjoylearningsanskrit.com/scriptures/parashara/chapter-6, read 2026-11-13; not a pool source). The scan's order of planets and stretches agrees with it in both lists; the machine reading that gave the first stretch to the lama was wrong.
