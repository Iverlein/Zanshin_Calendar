# «ལ་ཉེ», «ཐེབས», «འབྱམས»: the road omens of p. 140

«ལ་ཉེ» (la nye) is a sign or omen (རྟགས་མཚན, Great Tibetan Dictionary, Ives-Waldo; Mahāvyutpatti śakuna, the science of omens); «ཐེབས» (thebs) is to hit, strike, arrive at, be on the mark (Ives-Waldo, Valby, Dag tshig «ལན་ཐེབས་སོང», the answer hit); «འབྱམས» ('byams) is to spread, drift, be diffused without obstruction. So on p. 140 «ཐེབས» is the sign hitting the mark: meeting a speaker of another clan, or a man and woman of a cut line, means the cutting takes hold; the turning-back omen (horse, donkey, widow, driven sheep) means it turns back; meeting none, it drifts, unsettled. The lines say nothing of what is done after each; likely, not proved.
