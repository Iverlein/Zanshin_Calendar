# Ch. 13, pp. 142-147: the strip charts are the twelve months, their captions the nāga planet's turning dates

The twelve strip charts of pp. 142-147 are the twelve months, tiger first, and not the twelve years. The caption at the right end of each strip is the nāga planet (klu gza') with the dates on which the black nāga planet turns in that month, and the twelve captions agree with the verse of vol. 2, p. 222 month by month: the first spring month (tiger) 15, the second (hare) 18 and 12, the third (dragon) all dates but the 25th, the first summer month (snake) 25, the second (horse) 15, the third (sheep) 11, the first autumn month (monkey) 15 and 16, the second (bird) 6, the third (dog) 9, 15 and 19, the first winter month (pig) 19, the second (rat) 6 and 27, the third (ox) none. Read on the scan: the tiger, snake, horse, sheep, monkey, bird, pig and dragon lines and the rat's long line as far as its shapes allow; not settled as figures or syllables: the hare's two lines (read 18 and 12 by their shapes and the verse), the dog's upper and lower tiers, and the ox's short line (the verse says none).

This settles the order of the twelve (the woodcut's animal is the month's animal, the order tiger, hare, dragon, snake, horse, sheep, monkey, bird, dog, pig, rat, ox) for the 1996 print and the Gser thang reprint. The Zhol print's set, which begins with the rat, was not read caption by caption. The headings above the first two charts (pp. 142-143) are a different table, the black earth-destroyer's dates by time of day ([wb1-ch13-heading-rows-black-destroyer](wb1-ch13-heading-rows-black-destroyer.md)).
