# The 3rd head's stacked figures, read in part

RD201, 2026-11-08, p. 160 (Gser thang reprint img. 167): the figures of the 3rd head crossed with crops at 5x. They are stacks of two or three tiers, the lowest tier a bare zero circle. After the horse's great and small (line 3): a two-tier stack, top 26, bottom 25 or 27 (the last glyph doubtful). The day-length stack: tiers 17 (the 7 doubtful), 10, 0; the night-length stack: tiers 28 (doubtful), 40, 0 (the 4 of 40 is close to a 5). The two lengths do not sum to a whole day on this reading, so the digits are held as doubts, not data. The other stacks of the head and every stack of the 2nd, 4th, 5th and 6th heads were not read digit by digit. The first six lines of the 3rd head, held against 2.5x crops, agree with the machine reading.
