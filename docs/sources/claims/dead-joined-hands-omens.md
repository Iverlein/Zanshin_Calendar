# The road omens after cutting the joined hands

The rite of cutting the joined hands of the living and the dead (WB vol. 2,
pp. 139–140) closes with omens met afterwards (ལ་ཉེ, la nye; Mipham's notes,
p. 522, examine them "when setting out on the road"), read on the scan:

- meeting a person of another clan talking, or a man and a woman whose line
  was cut: "it takes hold" («ཐེབས»);
- meeting a horse or a donkey, a widow, or a driven sheep: "the omen of
  turning back" («ལྡོག་པའི་ལ་ཉེ»);
- meeting none of these: "it is wandering" («འབྱམས»).

The text does not say what takes hold, turns back or wanders, nor what is
done after each.
