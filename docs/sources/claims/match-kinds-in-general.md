# A match of mixed kinds is judged in general; no text counts the kinds into one

The third point gives the kind of spouse aspect by aspect (WB vol. 1,
p. 355): of the four aspects, the man's life force and luck against the
woman's, her body and power against his, each meeting as mother or son
is a spouse of karma (ལས་ཀྱི་ཆུང་མ, las kyi chung ma), as friends a spouse
of kin (གཉེན, gnyen), as enemies a spouse of enmity (དགྲ, dgra); of an
element meeting itself, earth and water are good and fire, iron and wood
middling. WB names a whole only for all four of karma ("all four meeting
as mother and son").

Mipham's notes (vol. 2, p. 524) give the order of work and nothing more:
join each aspect to its kind first, "the life force a spouse of karma and
so on", then "look at good and bad in general" (སྤྱིར་བཟང་ངན་བལྟ), and
apply each aspect's results ("if the life force's element agrees" and the
rest). The same page reckons the bad years "in general" too, one deciding
pebble for the couple (black if both meet them, speckled if one, white if
neither), not pair by pair.

So for a match of mixed kinds a build can show each aspect's kind and its
result, and the bad years' one pebble; neither WB, the notes nor Sangye
Gyatso's reply 113 (read by RD21) turns four kinds into one name for the
couple.
