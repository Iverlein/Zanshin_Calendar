# The la board's count is WB's own verse, which the Moonbeams follows; LT's count is the birth's

WB vol. 2, p. 45 (img. 053, read on the 1996 scan) lays the la pebbles by its own verse: great cut-off three black, small cut-off two black, great and small mother year two white each, power year one white each, son year «དཀར་ནག་ཟུང» (a white and a black), enemy year one black each, friend year two white and one black each, four fingers between pebbles, and one white under the living's own year and a black on its seventh, fourteen pebbles. The *Moonbeams* (dz:i055-057) repeats it as its fixed count, saying a half for the son year where WB says the white-and-black pair, and own year for WB's power year. The notes' three black for the great cut-off and so on (wb.2:p499-500) points at this verse. LT's count (mother three white, power two white, son one white, friend half, enemy three black; lt:p26-28) is the verse of WB vol. 1, p. 260 on the birth's key pebble (སྒོས་རྡེལ), a different reckoning, not a rival count for the la board. Answers question 371.
