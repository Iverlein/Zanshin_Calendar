# The karma reckoning's six words on p. 526 and the power on p. 478, read by eye

Read by eye (RD143, the Gser thang reprint I3CN5029 img. 532, ll. 13-15 at 1.5×, and img. 484, ll. 3-6): the notes' sentence on p. 526 gives each of WB's words its aspect in the order «ཚེ་ཟད་པ་སྟེ་སྲོག་ངན་པ། ཟུང་ཟད་པ་སྟེ་ལུས་ཀྱི་རྩིས་ངན་པ། གཅོང་ཅན་ཏེ་ལོ་སྐྱོན་སོགས་ཡོད་པ། དབུལ་པོ་སྟེ་ལས་རྩིས་ཀྱི་དབང་ཐང་ཆུང་བ། ཁས་ཉེན་ཏེ་ཀླུང་རྟ་ངན་པ། ལས་འབྲས་ཏེ་རྩིས་སྤྱིར་ངན་པ», all as the machine reads it. The luck is the wind-horse («ཀླུང་རྟ», klung rta) bad. On p. 478 (ll. 3-6) the notes use the same list's «དབང་ཐང» twice for a person's power: «དབང་ཐང་དགྲ་བདུད་མང་» is a power with many enemies and demons, said of a household whose members meet as enemy and friend, and «ལས་རྩིས་བརྒྱ་རྡེལ་གྱི་དབང་ཐང་ལ་དགྲ་མང་བ» the power of the karma reckoning's hundred pebbles with many enemies. So the notes themselves see the power's block of the hundred as carrying enemy pebbles that can run many; nothing on either page says that the pebbles' colour decides the power's badness. The question's list (the deciding pebbles of the block, rise and decline at birth, something else) gets no new member from the two pages: the narrowing of 2026-10-25 stands, an aspect bad when its block's verdict is black, as a reading.
