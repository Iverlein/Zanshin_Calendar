# The moxa list with the 24th is quoted from Phyag rdum by Gtsang sman and 'Bri gung; only KL lacks it (q667)

BDRC's full-text snippets (2026-11-21, exact phrase «ཉེར་བཞི་ཉ་སྟོང་», 10 hits) show the moxa list with the 24th quoted from Acārya Phyag rdum (ཨ་ཙ་ར་ཕྱག་རྡུམ, a tsa ra phyag rdum) in Gtsang sman Ye shes bzang po's Zhal gdams man ngag gnad 'gag do shal mdzes rgyan (MW7616, MW1PD53272): «ཚེས་གཅིག་དྲུག་དང་བཅོ་བརྒྱད་ཉེར་གཉིས་དང༌། ཉེར་བཞི་ཉ་སྟོང་རྣམས་ལ་གཏར་མེ་སྤངས» (the 1st, 6th, 18th, 22nd, 24th, full and new moon: no bloodletting or moxa), and in 'Bri gung Chos kyi grags pa's Dka' mchan on the Four Tantras (MW1KG24729), the Arura textbook (MW8LS18556) and two more works. So the tradition keeps the 24th wherever it quotes the list; KL's six (1, 6, 15, 18, 22, 30) lack it, and the dropping is KL's or its source's. Snippets only (etext search); not read on a scan.
