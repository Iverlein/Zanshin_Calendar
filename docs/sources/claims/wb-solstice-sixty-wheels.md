# The sixty wheels of twelve petals: the wheel and the cell from the years elapsed

The earlier, longer form of the same wheel counts sixty wheels of twelve petals and gives, from the elapsed rab byung years, the wheel and the cell: the years elapsed times 480 (the number word «མཁའ་ནོར་ཆུ་བོ») divided by 13 («མེ་གཟུགས»); the quotient is the number of wheels elapsed, the remainder the cell of this year in that wheel. The upper of the cell's figures is India's solstice, the lower Tibet's. For the black month the measure of the winter solstice at the author's place is given as the date 18, the desired part 7 and the fifth part 4, counted on from the east round the petals (the printed figures for the date after it are 11, for the desired part 1).
