# A lama's attendants chosen by the elements of their years

WB vol. 2, p. 39 (machine-read): choosing a great lama's attendants by the elements of their years. Best, no enemy, grave, four executioners, bad year-star or rough luck between them. Otherwise by office: the chamberlain matched to the lama's body (for a male-wood lama, an earth-monkey year); the cook and steward to his power (water monkey or pig); bodyguards and officers to life force and luck (water bird, water snake, water pig); the guest-master to luck's mother and friend (for a male-wood-rat lama, water pig, earth snake, water snake, earth pig, earth or water bird).
