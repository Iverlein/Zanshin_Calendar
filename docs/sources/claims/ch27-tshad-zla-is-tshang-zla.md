# Ch. 27: «ཚད་ཟླ» on p. 9 is a slip for «ཚང་ཟླ», a term of Zhechen Gyaltsab and of WB's marriage chapter

«ཚད་ཟླ» (WB vol. 2, p. 9, l. 6, clear on both scans with a final da) has a twin that is a defined term: Zhechen Gyaltsab uses «ཚང་ཟླ» for those of one house who share the life force or the power ("the «ཚང་ཟླ» of one life force", etext pp. 326 and 337, printed pp. 282 and 293), and his verse for this very line reads «ནག་ན་ཚང་ཟླ་མག་སྐུད་བུ་ཚར་ངན» (etext p. 352). WB's own marriage reckoning has «ཚང་ཟླའི་སྤུན་གྲངས» (1996 etext p. 533, printed p. 525). A word «ཚད་ཟླ» with da is found only once in WB (RD90) and in Valby's dictionary as "brother". So «ཚད་ཟླ» is a slip of the block for «ཚང་ཟླ», house-mates, kin of the one house: two witnesses use the word «ཚང་ཟླ» for exactly this place, none uses «ཚད་ཟླ». The Taikhang and the Zhol print were not looked at for this letter.
