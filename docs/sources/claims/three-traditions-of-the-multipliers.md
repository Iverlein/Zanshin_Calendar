# Three traditions of the multiplied merit in a star-reckoning manual: Atiśa's, the Nirvāṇa and the minor section's, the Root Tantra of Mañjuśrī's

An undated manual of star reckoning (skl:i210, etext only; its author is not named in BDRC's record) sets three traditions of the multiplied merit side by side, each with its authority:

| Occasion | "What Dīpaṃkara composed" (Atiśa) | The *Myang 'das* and the *Sil bu* | The Root Tantra of Mañjuśrī (འཇམ་དཔལ་རྩ་བའི་རྒྱུད, 'jam dpal rtsa ba'i rgyud) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| the full moon, the new moon, the eighth | a hundredfold | — | a koṭi |
| a lunar eclipse | a thousandfold | — | a thousand koṭi |
| a solar eclipse | ten thousandfold | — | a hundred thousand koṭi |
| the four great festivals (དུས་ཆེན་བཞི, dus chen bzhi) | a hundred thousandfold | a hundred million | — |

The first column agrees with the saying of "the Lord and his disciples" in ckg.13:p19 (a hundred thousandfold on any of the four festivals). The second is the verse credited to Śākyaśrī. None of the three gives RCZ's hundred koṭi for a lunar eclipse or its hundred thousandfold for the month's good days (rcz:p286-288); the White Beryl's hundredfold for the ordinary good days (wb.1:p62) is the first column's, its hundred thousand koṭi for the sun the third column's and Śākyaśrī's.

The manual adds what is done at those times: virtuous works in general, training in the mind of awakening, the compounding of medicine and Mañjuśrī's practice, and for a practitioner of the two stages fire offering, the taking of essences and the vajra recitation.

- Days: the 8th, 15th and 30th of every month; eclipses; the four great festivals.
- For whom: anyone for the general works; holders of the tantric commitments for the last.
- Tradition: not established (the manual's school and date are unknown); a witness only.

The tantra and Atiśa's text were not opened; the numbers are the manual's report of them.
