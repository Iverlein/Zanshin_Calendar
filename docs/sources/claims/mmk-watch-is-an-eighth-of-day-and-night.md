# A watch (ཐུན) is one eighth of the day-and-night: four chu tshod or four yud tsam; four watches by day and four by night (RD372)

In the root tantra's own words a watch (ཐུན, thun) is one eighth of the day-and-night, four by day and four by night. The 18th chapter (Derge etext [p438]-[p439]) says: of a hundred «སེ་གོལ» (se gol, an eye's swift motion) one «རྩ་རྒྱུན» (rtsa rgyun); four rtsa rgyun are a «ཆུ་ཚོད» (chu tshod); "for four chu tshod it is called a watch; four watches are the daytime, and the nights are shown likewise; thus, being eight, day and night is reckoned"; and, a few lines on, "for four «ཡུད་ཙམ» (yud tsam) one watch, the mantra-knowers reckon". Two doubts: the two equations (four chu tshod, four yud tsam) are not reconciled on the page, and the page earlier says the yud tsam are thirty to the day-and-night, which does not fit eight watches of four. The same lines set "the time of the fire offering and of recitation" beside "the time of sleeping and of not sleeping". So the four watches of the dream verse (mmk.1:p397) are, in this tantra's own measure, quarters of the night; whether they are equal quarters of the night as it runs from dusk to dawn is not said.
