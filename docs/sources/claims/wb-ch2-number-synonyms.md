# WB ch. 2 lists the number-words that stand for the numbers one to thirty-two, and calls the list no measure

Number-words as p. 20 gives them (machine reading): ornament and 'good' six; precious, earth-holder, Sage, mountain, seer, planet, horse seven; auspicious, god, nāga, jewel-hooded, existence, belly-goer eight; root, flesh-eater, hole, treasures nine; wrathful, wealthy, one who goes to the quarters, strength, finger ten; maker of bliss, arising of bliss, mighty, fierce eleven; sun, dependent arising, the twelve houses twelve; desire, bodiless, maddener, layers of the parasol, the varied thirteen; desire-mind, Manu, Śeda's sons fourteen; the lunar days and day-night fifteen; lord of men, king sixteen; faults and elements eighteen; the Victor and the land twenty-four, the same twenty-five; wheel and asterisms twenty-seven; teeth and the twice-born thirty-two; void and sky the dot, zero (ཐིག). The author says the list was set down as a way of hiding the meaning of the Vajra words, so it cannot be taken as a measure, though the famous and needed ones are only these. Not every word is read with certainty.
