# KCM: a rite for asking in a dream, with its time

Karma chags med's *Magic Mirror* (KCM, pp. 679–682, machine reading) is a rite for obtaining a clear dream in answer to a question, not an examination of the dream's images. What is asked: any matter the practitioner wishes shown tonight; the two girls, daughters of gods and nāgas, are asked to show it. The time: the tenth day of the waxing or waning moon and similar good times are best, but in any case three days' protector offering with fulfilment and confession come first; the night's own part is not named, only «the proper time» (a doubtful reading). The dream counts when a prophecy in words or some sequence of signs appears; the dreamer examines and knows by it. A bad dream is not met: the text has no remedy and no verdict list. Not to be given to a bad vessel.
