# The old one sri'u by the mewa and the body that died black, yellow or red

The old one *sri'u* by the mewa and the corpse of an old one who died black, yellow or red (WB vol. 2, p. 80, ll. 3–11; passage wb.2:p77-80): Mipham's note on the provisions says that the old one whose body (ལུས, lus) "died black" or "died yellow" is not found in the commentary but is dealt with by whichever mewa the old one's body has, that is, the colours are the mewa of the dead's body (one of the four of life force, body, power and luck), and the root's rites then follow: the corpse of the black body is hidden in five small holes toward the descendants; that of the yellow body is treated with the *g.yen* victory rite and may be sent at once with the funeral; that of the red body, as a strong *g.yen*, with the cutting of the *btsan* and demons. «དབུས་ནག» is read as the black at the centre of the nine places of the mewa and not explained; the power in «དབང་ཐང་ཅན» is the dead's power element. Narrowed: what the colours mean (the body's mewa); not found: the exact count behind «དབུས་ནག» and why the one-white and four-green are named with it.
