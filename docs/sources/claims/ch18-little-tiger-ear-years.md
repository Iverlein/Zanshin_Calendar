# The Chinese *Little Tiger-Ear*'s list of the years, wood mouse to fire sheep (WB ch. 18, pp. 210–211)

The *Little Tiger-Ear* list is the fourth list of the chapter (claim `ch18-four-lists-of-the-years`). It names the years by element and animal alone, from wood mouse (1), in the order of the cycle. Entries 1–44, wood mouse to fire sheep, are on pp. 210–211; the list goes on on p. 212 (fire sheep to water pig). The notation is that of the key (claim `ch18-tiger-ear-key`). Entry 10 runs over the page break (pp. 210–211).

1. Wood mouse: the black and the white only middling; the mountain field wins, *gshongs* and is defeated; in early summer grain is held to be scarce.
2. Wood ox: the year is good, early sowing good; spring rain and heavy hail, drought at midsummer (*gung*, not settled); unrest comes in spring.
3. Fire tiger: strong drought; the white and livestock harmed; the black good and barley is said to be scarce.
4. Fire hare: *so* and *zar* and the white harmed; *nam stod* dry, *nam smad zhod*; many omens, the year good and *skya* wins too.
5. Earth dragon: the white and goods and gold many; riding animals good, disease of livestock, the realm in unrest.
6. Earth snake: heavy rain, the sky [season] middling; wheat and barley locked up; unrest comes.
7. Iron horse: *so* and *zar* good; heavy rain, and the realm is made peaceful.
8. Iron sheep: the year good, early drought; later *zhod* is said to come.
9. Water monkey: *khre* wins, *ju tse* is defeated; the white and the black, the year good, and what is not virtuous comes.
10. Water bird: heavy rain, disease of livestock comes [the entry goes on at the head of p. 211].
11. Wood dog: wins from the head of the year; disease of people and livestock comes; the sky [season] bad.
12. Wood pig: *nam stod* dry and *nam smad zhod*; people and grain decline and famine comes.
13. Fire mouse: the mountain field wins, *gshongs* and is defeated, and drought comes; it comes out well in the end.
14. Fire ox: little rain, grain and food scarce; famine in the north, "the sea without limit"; livestock come to harm.
15. Earth tiger: the black good, strong hail; the white harmed, the sky bad, the year middling.
16. Earth hare: the black good, the white and the trees harmed; weapons spread, rule in unrest.
17. Iron dragon: flourishing from the head of the year; heavy rain, *so* and *zar* good; *gshongs* and the black is defeated.
18. Iron snake: the sky middling, wheat and barley locked up; "*btsa'* comes" (བཙའ་འོང, not settled), peas are harmed.
19. Water horse: *nam stod* dry, *nam smad zhod*, *so* and *zar* good; wheat and barley bad and strong hail in spring.
20. Water sheep: the year good, strong early drought; *gshongs* and defeated and later *zhod* comes; female livestock only middling.
21. Wood monkey: *nyung ma* flourishes; disease of livestock many, wheat and barley middling; great profit in trade comes.
22. Wood bird: wheat and barley die; the black harmed, *zhod* in autumn, livestock middling; fear and trembling of many kinds come to all.
23. Fire dog: the black and livestock harmed; *skya rgyal*; dry goods dear.
24. Fire pig: *nam stod* dry, *nam smad zhod*; unrest in the west and north and the kings few; the kings are few.
25. Earth mouse: great drought in the year; bad sky, the year middling, grass and fodder scarce; livestock do not [thrive: མི་བྲིན, not settled] and people die by weapons.
26. Earth ox: unrest in spring, the year good.
27. Iron tiger: the white harmed, the black good; disease of the eyes in the fields, fodder scarce; war and *mug* rise and livestock are harmed.
28. Iron hare: disease of the eyes in the year; barley locked up, *nam stod* dry, *nam smad zhod*; nevertheless heavy rain comes.
29. Water dragon: heavy rain and a good sky; the black bad, lord and minister stir; wheat and barley locked up and unrest in the west and north.
30. Water snake: *skya rgyal*, epidemics come; goods cheap and the arrivals only middling.
31. Wood horse: the sky good, the realm in unrest; *nam smad zhod* and livestock harmed.
32. Wood sheep: the year good for people and livestock; disease and unrest come; *skya rgyal*.
33. Fire monkey: sky-army and strong drought; unrest comes, a bad sky, livestock middling; the king kills the minister and famine rises; the rich go hungry and the hungry become rich.
34. Fire bird: the subjects make themselves king; *khre* wins, the year's livestock and wood-work scarce; later sowing good and for women grain declines, *skya rgyal* and dry goods many.
35. Earth dog: the earth lord harms people; early drought and unrest within.
36. Earth pig: the plain fields harmed; thieves rowdy, the end good.
37. Iron mouse: many people die by weapons; in the garden mice eat and livestock are harmed; the white and the black good and *skya* wins too.
38. Iron ox: the year good, *skya rgyal*; the sky middling, dry goods high in price; girls are sold to feed boys; female livestock come out well.
39. Water tiger: war and famine rise; in summer and autumn drought and the fish of the plain are drawn out (ཉ་ཐང་འཐུ, not settled); unrest rises in the north.
40. Water hare: father, son and brothers in unrest within; *nam stod* dry, *nam smad zhod*; the commons' pride and unrest grow; yet the year comes out good.
41. Wood dragon: *skya rgyal*, many with "turned noses" (སྣ་ལོག, not settled); drought early, *zhod* late, and *sad* comes too; unrest, and the king of "the mouth-danger" wins (ཁ་ཉེན་རྒྱལ, not settled); *skya rgyal*, dry goods scarce.
42. Wood snake: *nam stod* dry, *nam smad zhod*; the year middling and illness from summer to early autumn; Ti se and Ma pham bad (ཏི་སེ་མ་ཕམ་ངན, the sense not settled); something not fit comes and then it turns to good fortune.
43. Fire horse: strong drought; people and grain decline; the sky and the year's livestock are good.
44. Fire sheep: *gshongs* and good, early [drought; the entry goes on on p. 212].
