# The ninth place of the sounding, 'phung sri dza mo or rdza mo: no gloss found

The ninth place of the copper vessel's sounding (WB vol. 2, p. 91): the Gser thang reprint prints «འཕུང་སྲི་ཛ་མོ» (read so by eye on that scan), the etext of the 1996 print reads «རྫ་མོ»; the 1996 scan is too small to settle the letter. The sentence is "black: the *'phung sri dza mo*: the king's head empty, quarrels (ཁ་སྨྲས)", and the *'phung sri* (འཕུང་སྲི) are a class of harmful beings that WB's own recitation at the funeral calls down ("let all the *'phung sri* go down", vol. 2, p. 139, etext only). «ཛ་མོ» and «རྫ་མོ» are in none of Steinert's dictionaries; BDRC's full-text search finds «འཕུང་སྲི་རྫ་མོ» once (WB's 1996 etext, at the sounding) and «འཕུང་སྲི་ཛ་མོ» nowhere, so the Gser thang etext's OCR reads neither. A dead end: both spellings stand, no gloss is found for the being or for the word.
