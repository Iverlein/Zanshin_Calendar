# The 8th, 9th and 11th heads by eye: words and weekday lines agree with the machine text and RD2

Read by eye on the scans (RD200, 2026-11-12), the 8th head (p. 168, 1996 print img. 178) and the 9th head (p. 170, img. 180) agree word for word with the machine text kept in wb.1:p168 and wb.1:p170 and with RD2's table. The weekday lines are confirmed: 8th dar 2 4 5, gud 0, skyod 1 3, zhud 6; 9th dar 3 1 0, gud 2 4, skyod 6, zhud 5, each of 0 to 6 once. The print spells the third word skyod (སྐྱོད), where the machine wrote gyod. The 11th head's weekday line (p. 154, reprint img. 161) read by eye as RD2 has it: dar 0 6, skyod 2 4, zhud 1 3, gud 5. The 11th head's line 5 names the first link, ignorance, the sun's arrival at Sagittarius, expelling works good and the empty vase in the east. The stacked figures after the day and night words, and those between the lines, are two or three small figures one over the other and were not read digit by digit on any of the three pages.
