# WB p. 328: Revatī's minor star is «ལས་སྣ་…ལོད», with ལ, in three places of WB

Revatī's minor star on p. 328 reads «ལས་སྣ་དྲོ་ལོད» with the letter ལ, not ཡ. WB gives the same star in three places of the two 1996 etext volumes, «ལས་སྣ་གྲོ་ལོད» in vol. 1 (p. 258, beside the star numbered 26), «ལས་སྣ་དྲོ་ལོད» in p. 328's verse and «ལས་སྣ་གྲོ་ལོད» in the list of the mansions' fortunes by days at printed p. 425 (etext p. 433), always with «ལོད» and never «ཡོད»; the 1996 print's own scans (I1KG12906 img. 268 and I1KG12907 img. 336, 3-8x) show a ལ with a closed loop in both. The Gser thang reprint's letter, read ཡ by eye at 9x, is a blot of that typesetting against these repeats. The word is the one of the name Dorje Drolö (རྡོ་རྗེ་གྲོ་ལོད, in a prayer of the pool, sgc), where «ལོད» is the spelling too. The first syllable is «གྲོ» in two of the three WB places and in that name, «དྲོ» in p. 328's etext; the scans were not told apart at 700 px, so p. 328's «དྲོ» is the etext's.
