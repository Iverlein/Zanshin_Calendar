# The table 夢を見て語らざる日 stands in three prints with the same twelve cells; the slash is a gap before the last group

The table of the days on which a dream is not told stands in three prints read cell by cell: the 万代大雑書古今大成 (mdz:p11), the 雑書夢合／三世相小鑑 of 1774/1801 (mdz:i234427b) and the 萬歳大雑書日用寳 of 1867 (mdz:i183121; the same leaf in a second copy of 1866, Shunan Kano). The twelve cells are the same in all three, differing only in spelling. Each cell is two or three day branches, then after a gap or slash a last group of one or two branches. In the two prints without the slash the gap stands in the same place, so the slash only marks the break before the last group; no print says what the two groups mean. The 万代 and the 1867 print carry a closing line (dreams of those days, good or bad, are not told); the 1774/1801 print has none on the leaf.
