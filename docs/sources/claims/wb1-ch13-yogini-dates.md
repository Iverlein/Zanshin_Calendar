# The eight yoginīs by tithi and direction (WB vol. 1, p. 134)

Kālacakra's eight yoginīs go round the eight directions by tithi in four fixed sets of dates, each set a direction (WB vol. 1, p. 134): the Brahmā-goddess east on tithis 1, 9, 16, 24; the fierce one north on 2, 10, 17, 25; the youth south-east on 3, 11, 18, 26; the Vishnu-goddess south-west on 4, 12, 19, 27; the sow south on 5, 13, 20, 28; the powerful one west on 6, 14, 21, 29; Cāmuṇḍā north-west on 7, 15, 22 with the first half of the thirtieth; the glorious one north-east on 8, 23 with its later half. The watch of each is the eighth part of the tithi in the order of the list.
