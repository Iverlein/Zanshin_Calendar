# The light-clearer stands on top of the grave («དུར་གྱི་སྟེང»), not behind it

The word after «དུར་གྱི» on p. 219 is «སྟེང» (on top of), not the «རྟིང» (behind) of the earlier claim: its vowel is the arch of «ེ» (not a gigu), the subjoined «ྟ» has its tail, and every etext that BDRC's search shows reads «steng»; the head letter, sa or ra, is blotted. So in the four first months the light-clearer (no. 15) stands «on top of the grave», in the middle ones on the lower neighbour, in the last ones on the upper slayer. The same idiom stands in the nine black ones: «དུར་གྱི་ཐོག» for the disease-demon head-cutter and «མར་གྱི་བཞི་གཤེད་ཐོག» for the tiger-leopard (1996 etext, machine reading), so the grave, the lower neighbour and the slayer are named places that a lord stands on. Which animal the grave is stays as question 28 narrowed it (the grave animal of the month's animal; the count of ch. 31 not said). Replaces the reading of wb-light-clearer-behind-the-grave.
