# The five planets' days from the common day, without the special day

Chapter six closes with the author's own account of what is new in it. The days of the five planets are got from the common day (*spyi zhag*), the tithi-mixed day-count, without the "special day" (*sgos zhag*) that the earlier workings of the day-count needed; the author says nobody before made a method like it, that it compares to the new rough correction of the *Lnga bsdus* (the "Five-condensed") of the accomplished reckoning, and that Dpal mgon 'phrin las pa's notes say the same of the house-days for the slow pace and the courses for the swift pace. The colophon is on p. 41; the opening of the chapter (p. 37) says the same in its praises.
