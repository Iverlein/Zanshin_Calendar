# Derge images of the Svapnohana and the Svapnekṣā: a second Yigdzin-1 run agrees with the etext but for spelling, and the 8-pixel frames still give no scan state (RD218)

A second run of Yigdzin-1 over the Derge frames of the Svapnohana (I1344 images 268–269) and of Advayavajra's examination (I1365 images 261–262) agrees with the Derge etext syllable for syllable except in spelling: 98 % on image 269 by syllable alignment, and on the Svapnekṣā's two images the differences are «དང་པ་ཡིས»/«དང་བ་ཡིས», «སེམས་པའི་ལ»/«སེམས་དཔའ་ལ», «ཕམ»/«ཕ་མ», «པར»/«བར», «དགས»/«དྭགས», all of them p/b, wa-zur or tsheg slips of a reader that cannot see 8-pixel letters. On the Svapnohana the run adds nothing to RD207's machine readings: the title's Sanskrit, «འགྲོ་ན་བུ»/«འགྲོན་བུ», «འོ་མ་འམ»/«འོ་མའམ» and «གྲུལ»/«གྲུ་ལ» differ only in letters or tsheg places the frame cannot show.

The scan state is not reached. The frames BDRC serves for these images are the cached ones (about 2000 × 320 px; a request for width 4000 returns no image), the letters about 8 px high, so la against ba, the tsheg's place and the stacked letters of the Sanskrit words cannot be read by eye even at 8× (a crop of image 269 line 6 shows the line's shape and the shads, not the letters). The doubts of RD207 stay doubts on this print: gang la/gang ba, «འོ་མ་འམ», the Gāyatrī's spelling, «བླ་མོ», «འགྲོན་བུ», «བགྲོད་མིན». The spellings that were settled were settled on other prints (the Peking and the Narthang, RD233, RD310, RD353, RD354), not on the Derge scan.
