# Dreams in Rtsa gsum gling pa's life name the tenth, the ninth and the fifteenth day, each checked after (TSL)

Three dreams in Rtsa gsum gling pa's life name a day of the month, always in the dream speaker's own words, and each is followed by something checkable. (1) At about a year old, at night: the goddess-sister Bde ba mtsho says "from today, the tenth day (ཚེས་བཅུའི་ཉིན) of the Monkey month (སྤྲེལ་ཟླ), set up the crown of the holy Dharma" (i87); the next thing told is that he woke "as from a dream" and told his mother, who said not to talk of it and that learning letters is good dependent arising (i88). (2) Dream of the Heruka-clad Hūṃ nag pa: "tomorrow's time is the day of the ninth" (སང་དུས་ཚེས་དགུའི་ཉི་མ, i113); the next day he found, in a rock cave, a dust-covered book without covers, the *Yang zab dkon mchog spyi 'dus* (i113). (3) The tested dream (see tsl-life-dream-tested-by-date): the fifteenth day and the waxing half (i170). The "Monkey month" is not placed in the year on these pages (the etext «སྤྲེལ་ཟླ»). These are day-of-the-month ties made by the dream's speaker; none is a rule that dreams on that day are true.
