# The Chinese *Little Tiger-Ear*'s list of the years, fire sheep to water pig (WB ch. 18, p. 212)

The *Little Tiger-Ear* list (claim `ch18-little-tiger-ear-years`) ends on p. 212: its first words close entry 44, the fire sheep, and sixteen entries follow, earth monkey (45) to water pig (60), so the list is complete in sixty. All are in the key's notation (claim `ch18-tiger-ear-key`). The English is by entry in the passage `wb.1:p212`; in short:

44. Fire sheep: early drought again and later *zhod*; the year's earlier livestock turn bad.
45. Earth monkey: *nam stod* dry, *nam smad zhod*; livestock and goods dear, much discomfort.
46. Earth bird: early bad, later sowing good.
47. Iron dog: heavy rain, wins from the head of the year; *so* and *zar* good; *skya rgyal* comes.
48. Iron pig: early drought, later *zhod*; the plain field harmed, *skya* defeated.
49. Water mouse: heavy rain, a good sky.
50. Water ox: heavy rain, unrest in spring, strong *ltags*, much famine.
51. Wood tiger: the white harmed, the black good; robbers rowdy, drought in summer and autumn.
52. Wood hare: early drought, later *zhod*; the white and the fruit of trees good.
53. Fire dragon: the year bad, famine and fear of death from hunger; the state in unrest, then happy again.
54. Fire snake: drought, the sky good; famine from the east.
55. Earth horse: the realm in unrest, livestock harmed; the year middling, hunger.
56. Earth sheep: early drought, later *zhod*; the year good, epidemics come.
57. Iron monkey: the year good, *ju tse* wins; war and *mug* arise; *gshongs* and defeated, unrest in the west.
58. Iron bird: *khre* wins, peas locked up; early bad, later good; early drought, later *zhod*.
59. Water dog: the black harmed; for pregnant women grain declines (line not settled).
60. Water pig: early drought, later *zhod*; unrest and rowdiness, robbers rise, the east not at ease.

The closing note of the chapter says that the incomplete years of the *Little Tiger* were filled in from the old chapter lists of Chos rje Bde legs Nyi ma and others, since the whole could not be found in the examples at hand.
