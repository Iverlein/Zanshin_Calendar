# The sets of p. 338 are read on the hundred's deciding pebbles

WB vol. 1, p. 338 closes the hundred and the grades of rise and decline
with sets read across the four aspects: the four mothers white, black or
half and half; many white or many black among the life force, body,
destiny and luck; the four sons and the four friends white, black or
mixed; the life force's own rising with its enemy declining good, the
reverse bad, destiny and the luck alike, the life force declining with
its enemy rising ending by the blade. The text names pebbles and their
colours («མ་གཞི་དཀར་པོར་བྱུང», «རྡེལ་དཀར་མང་བྱུང», «བུ་བཞི་དཀར་པོ་བྱུང»), not stages.

Mipham's notes take the four mothers as the mothers of the life force,
body, destiny and luck (vol. 2, p. 484), and in the five doors of bad
luck, which use the same words, read "decline" as the hundred's own
pebble black and "the enemy rising" as the enemy pebble white (p. 484).
Lha mo tshul khrims restates such sets wholly in pebble colours, «སྲོག་གི་རང་རྡེལ་དང་མ་དང་བུ་རྡེལ་དཀར་ཞིང་དགྲ་ནག»
and the like (LT, printed pp. 162–164, etext pp. 170–172).

So the app reads the sets on the twenty deciding pebbles of the hundred
(each the greater of its five, [birth-hundred-board](birth-hundred-board.md)):
rising is the own pebble white, the enemy rising its pebble white. Where
WB gives three cases (white, black, half and half for the mothers) a
three-to-one split gives no reading; for the sons and friends any mix is
"mixed". This is an application of the notes' gloss, not a rule WB states
for these lines.
