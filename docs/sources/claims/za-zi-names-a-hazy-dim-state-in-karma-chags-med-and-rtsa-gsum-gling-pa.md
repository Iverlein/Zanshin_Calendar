# ཟ་ཟི (za zi): a hazy, dim state of sleep or sight, in Karma chags med and Rtsa gsum gling pa's life and vol. 27 (RD402)

In the three texts read the word ཟ་ཟི (za zi) always goes with a state of being not clear: in Karma chags med (rrd.19:p302-303) a sleep so thick that one does not dream even a dim dream (rmi lam za zi tsam yang mi rmis); in Rtsa gsum gling pa's verse life a dream seen in a brief doze is called an experience-appearance dream, za zi (tsl.30:i102-103), and a dream or experience-appearance 'za zi' recurs at image 98, and 'the border of sleep and za zi' at image 47 (the night, with sparks and the sound of Om Ah Hum); in vol. 27 (etext p246, 'gnyid za zi tsam la') the falling into sleep and the dissolution of the elements ('shes pa za zi', image 294) are described by it. It names the dimness or haze of the sight or of the awareness, never by itself the half-asleep condition, but the dim, hazy half-awake dream that comes just before or in place of a clear one; the phrase 'the border of sleep and za zi' in the life shows it can stand as one end of the border. The text of the verse life at image 47 is not a definition, and no dictionary or commentary was opened; the reading is from the contexts only.
