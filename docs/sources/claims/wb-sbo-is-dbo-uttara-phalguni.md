# «སྦོ» on p. 65 is U. Phalgunī's second spelling, not a misprint

The Tshig mdzod chen mo gives «སྦོ» (sbo) as the second name of «དབོ» (dbo), Uttara Phalgunī (also called khra, nyi ma'i lha ldan, phyi mo); Rangjung Yeshe has «དབོ» for it and Valby "11th mansion". The 1996 etext reads the entry of p. 65, last line «སྦོ་ལ་ཤི་ན་གཟའ་བདུད་ཁྱེར»; on the Gser thang reprint the stack is one ས-headed stack with the vowel o and two printed dots below the line, whose lower letter (བ, པ or ྲ) is not told apart at 4×. So «སྤྲོ»/«སྦྲོ» is not a misprint for «དབོ»: the entry is «སྦོ», a recognised spelling of the mansion, and the Lhasa blocks agree that it is one stack with o (wbz-p66-g-yang-rjen). The answer holds for every page of WB where «སྦོ» stands for Uttara Phalgunī.
