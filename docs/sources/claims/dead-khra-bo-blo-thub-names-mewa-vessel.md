# «ཁྲ་བོ་བློ་ཐུབ» names the mewa's copper vessel that follows it

WB vol. 2, p. 54, 1996 scan img. 62 ll. 24–25 (read on the Gser thang reprint img. 060):

> རང་ལ་ཁམས་ཀྱིས་བརྒྱབ་པ་ན། །དགྲ་ལ་གུས་པ་ཕུལ་ན་བཟང་། །ཁྲ་བོ་བློ་ཐུབ་ཅེས་བྱ་བ། །འདི་ནི་སྨེ་བའི་ཟངས་ཀྱམ་ཡིན།

The two last lines make one sentence: "What is called «ཁྲ་བོ་བློ་ཐུབ» (khra bo blo thub), this is the mewa's copper vessel (སྨེ་བའི་ཟངས་ཀྱམ, sme ba'i zangs kyam)". The phrase is therefore the name of the section that follows (the living's birth mewa, many white, black, yellow, green, blue, seven red and nine red), not a verdict laid on the household's years before it. Three things support it:

- Mipham's note (p. 502, wb.2:p502) quotes it so: «ཁྲ་བོ་བློ་ཐུབ་སྨེ་བའི་ཟང་ཀྱམ་ལ། གསོན་པོ་སྐྱེས་སྨེ་བདུན་དམར་མང་བ་སོགས་སོ།», "'speckled, the mind can bear it,' in the mewa's copper vessel: the living's birth mewa, many seven red and so on".
- The second examination names the mewa's reason «ཁྲ་བོ་བར་གྱི་ལེ་ལན» (khra bo bar gyi le lan), the speckled middle reason (p. 57, wb.2:p57-58): the outer reason by the years is the first, the inner by the trigram the second, the speckled middle by the mewa the third.
- The same note defines the speckled (ཁྲ་བོ, khra bo) as the mixed colour: "a great friend year white and a small black and the like belongs to the speckled" (p. 502).

So «ཁྲ་བོ» is speckled, white and black mixed, between the good and the bad, and «བློ་ཐུབ» (blo thub) is what the mind can bear: the middle reading, to be borne. The dictionaries give "mottled" and "trustworthy" separately (wb-ch29-words-in-the-dictionaries); the pair has no entry. BDRC's full-text search for the exact phrase finds one hit in all etexts, the notes of WB itself (2026-11-03).

Why the copper vessel is called the speckled one is not said. Inferred, not stated: it is read from many colours at once, white, black and the rest.
