# Milarepa's and Marpa's days: 1/14 and 1/15 in the calendars, the other way round in one Kagyu house, a third month in two others

Three accounts of the days of Mi la ras pa (Milarepa) and Mar pa lo tsā ba (Marpa):

1. **Milarepa on the 14th and Marpa on the 15th of the first month**: the Rigpa students' calendar and Jeff Watt's list (tbc:p1, har:p1), and the 2015 almanac of the Tsurphu reckoning, «as the Palpung religious calendar has it» (mtl:p1, search snippet). The Fifth Dalai Lama's college for Marpa's lineage kept the same pair every month, Milarepa's offering of the time on the 14th and Marpa's memorial offering on the 15th (dl5:p305), which is not a day of the year.
2. **Marpa on the 14th and Milarepa on the 15th of the first month**, the month of miracles: a Kagyu house in Rin chen dbang rgyal's memoir, with the prayers of the Kagyu forefathers' lives on the evening of Marpa's day and Milarepa's guru accomplishment, the Ye shes dpal 'bar, on the evening of his (rcw:p1, search snippets).
3. **Milarepa in the third month**: a monastery in exile holds his passing offering in Tibetan month 3 (gtb:p3), and Rta rna dgon in Nang chen held it in the horse month between the 12th and the 15th, with the retreat masters' cotton-cloth rite (rtd:p604); the horse month is the third Hor month in the White Beryl's model almanac (wb.1:p154-171).

The Karmapa's conference of 2023 was called because these dates are not settled: it set none, and held that the calendar of the forefathers' time must be found first (kgo:p1). No source read gives the day from a life of Milarepa or Marpa. Which stands is open; all sources are rank 3.
