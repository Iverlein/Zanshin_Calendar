# The purified month is multiplied by thirty

After the purified month (*zla ba rnam par dag pa*) WB multiplies it by «dot, quality» (*thig yon*), the figure beside the number-words being thirty (30; dot is 0, *yon*, the quality, is 3, written from the lowest digit up); then the tithi being reckoned is mixed in and the result set in three places. The figure is read on the scan of p. 37 (Gser thang reprint, img. 44), and its two digits agree with the two number-words. The machine texts of RD188 gave «207», which does not fit the number-words beside it.
