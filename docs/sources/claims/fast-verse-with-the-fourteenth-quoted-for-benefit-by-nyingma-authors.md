# The sutra verse with the fourteenth is quoted for the benefit of the one-day vow, also by Nyingma authors

The verse of Zhe chen rgyal tshab's «ལྷས་ཞུས་པའི་མདོ» and «རྟོགས་བརྗོད» (zgt.1:p445-446) stands in at least ten BDRC etexts of fasting (བསྙེན་གནས/སྨྱུང་གནས) literature, always as one of the scriptural proofs of the benefit of the one-day vow, and none of the snippets (BDRC phrase search «ཚེས་བརྒྱད་དང་ནི་གནམ་སྟོང་ལ» 10 hits, 2026-11-21) gives it as a rule of the author's own. The Nyingma authors among them: Kaḥ thog Rig 'dzin Tshe dbang nor bu (1698-1755), in his liturgy of the eleven-faced Avalokiteśvara's fast (MW1GS45274_79F04E, written 1753), quotes it as spoken to Śāriputra in the Avadāna («རྫོགས་པའི་སངས་རྒྱས་ཀྱི་རྟོགས་བརྗོད་དུ། བཅུ་བཞི་དང་ནི་བཅོ་ལྔ་དང་། ཚེས་བརྒྱད་དང་ནི་གནམ་སྟོང་ལ། བསྙེན་གནས་ཡན་ལག་བརྒྱད་པ་བསྲུངས། ངའི་ཉིད་ཇི་འདྲ་དེ་འདྲར་འགྱུར།»), and the fast manuals MW3CN5417 (Kun dga' dbang phyug), MW3CN4918 and MW3CN7845 carry the same lines (snippets). A longer text (etext VEIE0OPI636AA6B4_I1GS66284, read in a window of 3000 characters; its author and title were not identified from the window) sets Maitreya's prophecy (14, 15, the 8th and the miracle month) and the Avadāna beside the lay one-day vow («ཁྱིམ་པ་རྣམས་ཀྱི་སོ་ཐར་གྱི་གསོ་སྦྱོངས་བསྙེན་གནས་འབའ་ཞིག་ཉིན་ཞག་གཅིག་གི་མཐའ་ཅན་བསྲུང་བ», "the lay one-day vow of the individual liberation, kept for just one day and night") for the benefit, again without a fourth fixed day for the laity. No Nyingma author who tells lay people to keep the fourteenth as a fourth day was found: neither the snippets nor the one page read say so. Only the snippets of the other nine were read.
