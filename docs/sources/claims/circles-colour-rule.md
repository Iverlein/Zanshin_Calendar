# The circles: the rule of colour, the son black

In ch. 27 a pebble is white when the two aspects that meet stand as mother or friend, or meet as earth with earth or water with water; black when they stand as son or enemy, or meet as wood with wood, iron with iron or fire with fire; and of these the enemy meeting, which cuts, is the gravest (WB vol. 2, p. 3: «བུ་དགྲ་ཤིང་ལྕགས་མེ་གསུམ་པོ། །ནང་ཕྲད་ནག་པོ»).

So in this chapter a son meeting is black. The rule has no speckled pebble. Mipham's notes, on the reckoning of the moment (vol. 2, p. 536), report another usage ("they say"): son and the earth and water inner meetings speckled; that is said of ch. 30, not of the circles.
