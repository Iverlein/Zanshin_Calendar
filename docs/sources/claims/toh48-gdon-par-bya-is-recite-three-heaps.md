# The Showing of Dreams sūtra's «གདོན་པར་བྱ» is «to be recited»: the Three Heaps are recited, the years stand before the verb (RD395, q1089)

In the Kanjur Showing of Dreams sūtra (Toh 48), «གདོན་པར་བྱ» is "is to be recited", the infinitive of «འདོན་པ» ('don pa), not a word for a length of time. The remedy it names for a bodhisattva's dream is the recitation of the Dharma-teaching of the Three Heaps (phung po gsum pa) three times by day and three times by night, and the length is the noun that goes before the verb: seven years for one who stands on the first stage, six for the second, five for the third, four for the fourth, three for the fifth, two for the sixth, one for the seventh.

The lexica give the verb: Rangjung Yeshe lists «gdon pa» among the forms of 'don pa, "to chant, recite"; the Tshig mdzod chen mo calls it the future of 'don pa; Negi gives for «gdon pa» the Sanskrit pāṭha, bhāṣaṇa, pravartana, and for «gdon par bya» pravartayet, with the very formula of the sūtra, «nyin dang mtshan mo lan gsum du / phung po gsum pa gdon bya zhing» (Bodhicaryāvatāra, bo.a. 5.98 in Negi's reference). The earlier claim that the sūtra's stages give years "of which the verb is not understood" is answered.
