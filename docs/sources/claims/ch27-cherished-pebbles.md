# Ch. 27: the reckonings in four, by kind of person

Ch. 27's reckonings in four (WB vol. 2, pp. 23–25): four pebbles, the reckoned person's life force, body, power and luck, each fought by the year reached from a group of five years of one animal, counted down or up by the progressed sign (ལོག་མེན, log men) joined to the mother and son elements (the counting is not spelled out further). Which five, for whom:

| For | From the five | Life force asks | Body asks | Power asks | Luck asks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| the lord-king, emperor and two queens (the four cherished pebbles, གཅེས་རྡེལ, gces rdel) | monkeys | the sceptre | the proud helmet | the treasury | the palace |
| monks («བརྗེ», as printed) | rats | the golden vajra | the crystal of the heart-practice | the silver maṇḍala | the vase of discipline |
| mantrins | dragons | the vajra dagger | the skull-rosary's cord | robe and cloak | the bell's voice |
| Bon priests | sheep | the hand-drum | the thread-cross's cord | fortune | the felt cap |
| young men | tigers | the arrow's shaft | the bow | the three wheels | the spear of the prime |
| uncles and ministers | dogs | the wolf-skin | the ministers' row | the lake of counsel | the back-support |
| noble ladies | birds | the turquoise rosary | the mirror | the golden ladder | the magic wheel |
| common women | monkeys | the sun of glory | the robe's skirt | fortune | the castle of existence |
| children | birds | the green rice-stalk | the small «ལྷུག[?]» | the *ha lo* flower | the golden eye |

Each pebble white is good for its domain, black bad: the readings are in the passage. No remedies are named in this part.
