# p. 70: «དམར་གཉིས་སེར་གསུམ» is one of four classes of mewa, as Mipham's note counts them

«སྨེ་བ་དཀར་གསུམ་ལྷ་རུ་སྐྱེ། །ནག་གཉིས་ལྗང་གསུམ་ངན་སོང་སྐྱེ། །དམར་གཉིས་སེར་གསུམ་བར་དོར་འཁྱམ། །ལྔ་སེར་ལ་ཤི་མི་ལུས་ཐོབ» (p. 70) gives four classes of mewa, and Mipham's note reads them so: the notes (p. 507 of the 1996 and Gser thang etexts) count the deciding pebble's seven identifying pebbles as «བཏང་གཅོད་གསུམ» (the three just explained) and four of the mewas, «སྨེ་བ་དཀར་གསུམ་དང་དམར་གཉིས་སེར་གསུམ་དང་ནག་གཉིས་…གསུམ་སྟེ་བཞི»: the white three, the red-two-and-yellow-three, the black-two-and-green-three, with the single five-yellow the fourth; the Gser thang etext's «ནག་གཉིས་ལྡང་གསུམ» for the verse's «ལྗང་» and the notes' «སྣང» are the OCR's. So «སེར་གསུམ» is not a mewa by itself: «དམར་གཉིས་སེར་གསུམ» is one class label, and «སེར་གསུམ» is not shown to name a mewa that the next line's «ལྔ་སེར» (human) contradicts. The nine mewas in four classes are the three whites, five yellow, and the remaining 2, 3, 4, 7, 9 between the lower realms and the intermediate state; which of them take the label's two numbers each the notes do not say, and the detail section (pp. 71–72) puts the black 2, blue 3, green 4 and the reds 7 and 9 all in lower births.
