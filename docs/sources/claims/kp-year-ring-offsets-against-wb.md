# KP's year ring: the lords keep WB's offsets from the year in six panels; Se-lo, Phe'u and Kungs-myul do not

KP's year ring is the zodiac circle with the animals in fixed places, and the lords of the Ox, Tiger, Hare, Dragon and Snake panels stand at WB's offsets from the year animal for most lords, as the Rat panel's do; where they differ, they differ the same way in every panel.

The ring's places are the same in every panel: T1 the Dragon, T2 the Snake, T3 the Horse, T4 the Sheep, R1 the Monkey, R2 the Bird, B4 the Dog, B3 the Pig, B2 the Rat, B1 the Ox, L2 the Tiger, L1 the Hare, clockwise from the top left, the order of the twelve animals going on year by year ([kp:i87c](../corpus/kp/kp.md#kp-i87c), [kp:i88c](../corpus/kp/kp.md#kp-i88c), the Rat panel in [kp:i87b](../corpus/kp/kp.md#kp-i87b)). What a panel changes from year to year is which lords stand in which cell, and a lord's cell is its offset from the year animal in WB 2, ch. 31. Read cell by cell in the Rat, Ox, Tiger, Hare, Dragon and Snake panels, these lords keep WB's offset in every panel they were found in:

- The king Te-se (WB 1), his consort Hang-ne (3) and the seat-spreader (13) in the cell of the year's own animal: Ox B1, Tiger L2, Hare L1, Dragon T1, Snake T2.
- The queen Te-khyim (2), two places back from the king (one year left between them): the Dog in the Rat year, the Pig in the Ox year, the Rat in the Tiger year, the Ox in the Hare year, the Tiger in the Dragon year, the Hare in the Snake year. She stands with the minister Tsang-kun (5) in the Ox, Tiger and Snake panels, and the minister's own WB places hold in the Tiger panel (the mouse) and the Snake panel (the hare); in the Ox panel the minister stands in the Pig cell where WB puts him on the bird.
- The king's son Te-so (4) on the year's wind-horse: the Tiger year's Monkey, the Hare year's Snake, the Dragon year's Tiger, the Snake year's Pig.
- Hal-khyi's girl and her husband (17, 18) on the wind-horse of the previous year: the Ox year's Tiger, the Tiger year's Pig, the Hare year's Monkey, the Dragon year's Snake, the Snake year's Tiger.
- The time-holder Se-bya (12) on last year's animal: the Ox year's Rat, the Tiger year's Ox, the Hare year's Tiger, the Dragon year's Hare.
- The mount Se'u Rang-rta and the horse-leader (10, 11), the ninth from the king: the Tiger year's Dog, the Hare year's Pig, the Dragon year's Rat, the Snake year's Ox.
- The watchman (7), the general (20) and Tsang-kun the splitter (21) at WB's places in the Tiger (Dog), Hare (Sheep, Dog), Dragon and Snake (Ox) panels where read; the splitter in the Dog in the Ox panel.
- Se-byi (8), the seventh from the watchman: the Tiger year's Dragon, the Hare year's Ox, the Dragon year's Dog, the Ox year's Sheep.
- Se-shar (9) on the tomb of the year's wind-horse: the Dragon in the Ox and Snake years, the Ox in the Tiger year, the Dog in the Hare year, the Sheep in the Dragon year.
- The life-spirit knower of the se (6, outer place) on the seventh from the year: the Ox year's Sheep, the Tiger year's Monkey, the Hare year's Bird, the Dragon year's Dog, the Snake year's Pig.
- Wang-ging the red (19): the Ox year's Sheep, the Tiger year's Hare.
- Pi-ling (25): the Hare year's Sheep, the Dragon year's Bird, the Snake year's Pig.
- The eleven earth kings (26): the Ox year's Hare, the Tiger year's Sheep, the Hare year's Bird, the Dragon year's Pig, the Snake year's Dog.

Where KP differs from WB, the difference is the same in every panel. (1) Se-lo Sa-'phyag (WB 15, the king's downward four-slayer) stands on the upward four-slayer, three places on from the year, in four panels: the Rat year's Hare, the Tiger year's Snake, the Hare year's Horse, the Snake year's Monkey; the Rat panel's difference is therefore KP's rule and not a slip of one cell. (2) The life-spirit knower Phe'u (23), which WB puts on the third upward in the middle years and on the third downward in the corner years, stands in the opposite direction in both: in the middle years (Rat, Hare) two places back (the Dog, the Ox), in the corner years (Tiger, Snake) two places on (the Dragon, the Sheep), and in the last years (Ox, Dragon) on the seventh as WB says (the Sheep, the Dog). The swap of «yar» and «mar» is then KP's, or the sense of WB's direction words is the reverse of what the notes here took. (3) Kungs-myul (14) is the one lord whose place changes without a rule: the Rat year's Dog, the Ox year's Horse, the Tiger year's Ox, the Hare year's Dragon, the Dragon year's Ox, the Snake year's Sheep; neither WB's slayer years nor a fixed offset gives these. (4) Zin-phung (24), read in three panels (the Ox year's Dragon, the Tiger year's Sheep, the Dragon year's Sheep), is not at WB's sixth downward in any. (5) In the Ox panel the minister Tsang-kun stands in the Pig where WB has the bird; the Ox R2 cell is not resolved and may hold him.

The life-spirit knowers whom WB lists once as «of the se» appear in KP as «Nang» and «Gsang» in cells of their own. «Gsang» stands one place behind the year in the middle and last years (the Rat year's Pig, the Ox year's Rat, the Hare year's Tiger, and the Dragon year's Ox read in part) and one place ahead in the corner years (the Tiger year's Hare, the Snake year's Horse), which is WB 6's secret place: behind the middle years, in front of the corner years. «Nang» stands in the Tiger year's Horse, the middle of the tiger, horse and dog triad, which is WB 6's inner place; in the Dragon (Rat) and Snake (Bird) panels it stands in the second and the next place of the triad, and in the Ox and Hare panels in places not settled by the cells read.

Not read: the second lines of a few cells (marked in the passages), the Horse to Pig panels (img. 89–90, another reader) and the month charts of img. 91–100.
