# The circles' mewa: Zhechen Gyaltsab's verse puts the years in triads, each with its own mewa sequence

Zhechen Gyaltsab's general table verse (the *Garland*'s volume, I4PD2444 etext p. 308 = printed p. 264, img. 308, the scan looked at at page size, the lines quoted not read letter by letter; machine state, the etext's own OCR) states the mewa of a person: the mewa run from the centre «གནམ་ལྕགས་རི་མེ་ཆུ། ས་ཤིང་རླུང་»; the fourth upward from the centre is the life force's, the fourth downward the power's, the birth's the body mewa (གར་སྐྱེས་ལུས་སྨེ); the three agreeing years' luck-mewa goes with the luck; and the years are set in triads, tiger-horse-dog, pig-sheep-hare and bird-ox-snake named in the verse, each with a further year (the iron monkey, a fire year, the water pig) and each "to be joined with its own mewa sequence" (རང་རང་སྨེ་ཕྲེང་དང་སྦྱར་གཙོ); the etext's lines for the triads are garbled by OCR.

The triads of this verse are three of the four groups of the tenth circle's fine start (tiger-horse-dog, rat-dragon-monkey, pig-sheep-hare, bird-ox-snake). So the notes' "mewa sequence that agrees" (wb.2:p533b; question 368) may be the sequence that the person's year's triad takes, the starting point's mewa being the one in that sequence. That is a reading of the verse together with the notes, not stated by either; it adds a step to claim mewa-sequence-agreeing and does not replace it. Not settled; no manual read lays the two mewas out as one rule.
