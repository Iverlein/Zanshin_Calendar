# «ཀླུང» in WB's reckoning for the dead is the klung-horse year, an animal

In WB's own reckoning for the dead «ཀླུང» is the person's klung-horse year, an animal: p. 99 lists «དུར་མིག་ཐོག་དང་ཀླུང་ལམ» among what to avoid for the dead (the grave-eye's top and the klung road), and Mipham's note (p. 515) explains the klung road as «རང་རང་གི་ཀླུང་རྟའི་ལོ་ཐོག», "on the year of each one's own klung-horse". A modern dictionary of Steinert's collection (no. 62) defines the klung-horse as the helping fortune tied to the life force and power (klung "the gathering", rta "the helper"). So the sun's klung of p. 186 is most likely an animal, a klung-horse counted by the sun; which animal and how it is counted for Langging's «together with» stays unsettled, as in RD65's claim.
