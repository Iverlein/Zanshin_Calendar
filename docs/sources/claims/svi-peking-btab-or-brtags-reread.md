# On the Peking the eye keeps «བརྟགས་པ་ལ», Yigdzin-1 gives «བཏབ་ལ» eight of eight, the Dpe entry is «བཏབ»: unresolved (RD327)

On the Peking print (I1KG13215 img. 310 line 1) the eye holds «ཐ་མར་རྨི་ལམ་བརྟགས་པ་ལ་ཉལ» with an extra པ (the cluster between «ལམ» and «ལ» is as wide as two syllables, not the three letters of «བཏབ»; moderate, the print is faint grey), while Yigdzin-1 reads «བཏབ་ལ་ཉལ» in eight of eight runs on strips of 1× to 2×, as the Dpe bsdur ma's Peking and Narthang entry does. The conflict between the eye and the editors' entry is not resolved by a second scan, because there is none; it stays as a conflict of the machine and the Dpe bsdur ma on one side and three eye readings on the other.
