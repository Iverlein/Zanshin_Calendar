# The thirty: the thirteenth point, the golden vase, for husband and wife together

The thirteenth of the thirty starting points (སྐོར་འགོ, skor 'go), from the mouth of the golden vase (གསེར་གྱི་བུམ་པ, gser gyi bum pa), is read for husband and wife together (བཟའ་མི་ཟུང, bza' mi zung). Its eight pebbles are laid as for every point (claims thirty-count and thirty-eight-fighters), each white or black, and read so:

| Pebble | Asks | White | Black | Remedy for black |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Whether the vase of life is lost from the couple's hands | Not lost, good | It is lost: sudden obstacles to life, above all the *rol po kha nag gnyan* (རོལ་པོ་ཁ་ནག་གཉན, rol po kha nag gnyan; not identified) | Long-life practice (ཚེ་སྒྲུབ, tshe sgrub) and the like |
| 2 | Whether the river of life is cut at its middle | Not cut, good | Cut at its middle: the couple's time together is short | Clearing the narrow paths (ལམ་འཕྲང, lam 'phrang) by sweeping and the *nye 'don* (ཉེ་འདོན; not identified) help |
| 3 | Whether hatred has entered within | Not entered; the couple agree and love each other | Hatred has entered: separation comes; if it came before, it is borne; if not, take great care | — |
| 4 | Whether gossip (མི་ཁ, mi kha) parts them | No talk of emptiness, good | The many mouths talk of emptiness, bad | The queen's turning of the crowd (རྒྱལ་མོ་ཁྲོམ་བསྒྱུར, rgyal mo khrom bsgyur) and turning the elephant's nose (གླང་ཆེན་སྣ་བསྒྱུར, glang chen sna bsgyur) |
| 5 | Whether wrong food has gone inside | No wrong food; no trouble («ཁ་ལན», «དྲ་སྟར», not understood) | Food and medicine stirred with poison, bad | — |
| 6 | Whether the mountain of misery is climbed | Not climbed; no one bringing harm comes | The mountain of misery is climbed: sorrow, separation and tearing of hair | As service rites, recite the *'Bum*, the Hundred Thousand (འབུམ, 'bum), take empowerment and stamp tsha tsha (སཱཙྪ, sAtstsha) |
| 7 | Whether the net of turning back has been cast | No turning back on the road, good | One goes the wrong way and a separation comes; if forefathers and the like went abroad, that bears it | — |
| 8 | Whether the small sri (ཆུང་སྲི, chung sri) has entered the womb door | It has not; the small sri does not rise and children grow | It has entered the womb: children wither and die off | Strive to separate and press down the small sri |

Whose year is reckoned: the person's own (the notes, vol. 2, p. 533: «གཞན་རྣམས་སོ་སོའི་ལོ་རྟ»).

Which of the eight fighting pebbles is which check is not stated; the order of p. 453 is the likely one (claim thirty-board-groups; question 259).
