# The purified month, the first step of the planets' chapters

For the five planets' chapters WB sets out the month first, in one fixed way. Take the years elapsed from the fire-hare year of the Rabjung (the sixty-year cycle), multiply by twelve, add the elapsed months, and put the result in two places. The lower is multiplied by two and fifteen is added; the sum is divided by sixty-five and the quotient is thrown up above: this is the purified month (*zla ba rnam par dag pa*). Chapter 7 begins with the same steps. The figures twelve, two, fifteen and sixty-five are read on the scan of p. 37 (the bracketed figures beside the number-words «eye, form», «pair», «arrow, form», «element, taste»); the arithmetic beyond that (what is done with the purified month) is not worked here.
