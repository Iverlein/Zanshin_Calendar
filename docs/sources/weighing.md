# Weighing the factors of a day

Sigla and the rules for quoting are in [README.md](README.md). How the
factors of a day are weighed against each other, from KP's opening prose
(box 1, the "errors of the calendar-makers"), img. 13–14. Read 2026-10-04:
Yigdzin-1's reading, every line checked on the scan (2550 px); the corrections
of the machine reading are noted after the text. The app's weighing (SPEC
§5.12) is built from this file.

## What it says

1. **Weights.** The date counts once, the planet (the weekday) four times,
   the mansion eight times, the combination period (*dus sbyor*) ten million
   times (བྱེ་བ).
2. **Two that disagree.** Planet against mansion: the planet leads. Mansion
   against date: the mansion leads. Yoga against karaṇa: the very dangerous
   karaṇa leads. The day (*nyi ma*) against the hour (*dus tshod*): the hour,
   "a sharp weapon", leads.
3. **All mixed.** When good and bad are mixed among them all, the
   combination (*'phrod*) leads; where there is no special case (*dmigs
   bsal*), the side that has more (*phyogs sdebs*); where they are not
   alike at all, weigh their strength, and the stronger counts.
4. **Ranking.** Rāhu, the weekday (*res gza'*), the mansion (*rgyu skar*),
   the date, the karaṇa, the yoga, the *nyi ma*: each is stronger than the
   one after it.
5. **Above all.** The special case in which all their powers are complete
   in one combination period (*tatkāla dus sbyor*) leads; the work of the
   planet, mansion, karaṇa and yoga is complete in the house of that period,
   so it is held highest of all.

## Notes for the app

- The *dus sbyor* is a period within the day, computed from the hours by
  sign (KP's second part §9, img. 78–80), and so is the hour of rule 2:
  neither is a factor of the whole day. A day reading has no hours; the
  rules that remain are 2 (without the hour), 3 and 4.
- Rāhu's course by date and time is KP §7–8 (img. 76–77); the app does not
  compute it.
- The weights of rule 1 put the mansion above the planet, rules 2 and 4 the
  planet above the mansion. Where two factors disagree, rules 2 and 4 are
  the ones that decide; rule 1 says how much a good factor is worth, not
  which wins.
- The *'phrod* of rule 3 is the combination of weekday and mansion: the ten
  element pairs (*'byung 'phrod*, the app's element pair) and the 28 named
  combinations (*'phrod chen*, KP §3, WB pp. 331–333). WB says more than
  rule 3: even where the planet and the mansion are each good, a bad
  combination decides (p. 333); and of the special days of weekday and
  mansion, which may be the *dmigs bsal* of rule 3, "the individual results
  of planet and mansion are the main thing" (p. 337). Both are in
  [combinations.md](combinations.md); the app's ranking (SPEC §5.12) puts
  the combinations above the weekday and the mansion, and the special days
  after them.
- The *nyi ma* is the day's animal sign, the lunar date's animal (open question 9, answered 2026-10-04).
  The trigram and the sme ba are not among the seven.
