## *Dmigs bsal* and *phyogs sdebs*

*Dmigs bsal* is the particular case, set against the general (*spyi*,
*spyir btang*); *phyogs sdebs* is the general grouping of factors or works
by kind, good and bad among them. Neither word names a factor of its own.

**In WB.** The other 29 places of *dmigs bsal* in the 1996 etext (both
volumes) have that sense, the particular against the general: a special
month, year or place, an exception to a rule. In the mansion verses, the
works a mansion names one by one against its classes of work:

**Tshul khrims rgyal mtshan**, abbot of Nalendra, *Bod kyi lo tho'i 'phel
rim dang nang don ngo sprod mdor bsdus* (*Bod kyi rtsis rig kun 'dus chen
mo*, vol. 1, pp. 498–499, etext; again in his collected works,
MW1KG10375), restates KP's rules in prose and then glosses them:

> …ཞེས་དང་། བླ་སྲོག་དབང་སྐར་ཅི་ཡང་བཟང་། །ཀེག་བདུད་གཤེད་ཀྱི་སྐར་མར་ངན། །ཞེས་པས་མཚོན་སྤྱི་ལ་གང་ལྟར་ཡང་བྱེ་བྲག་རང་ལ་དམིགས་བསལ་རྩི་དགོས་པ་རྣམས་གཙོ་བོར་གཟུང་བ་གལ་ཆེ་སྟེ། ཇི་སྐད་དུ། སྤྱིར་བཏང་བསྟན་བཅོས་ངེས་པ་ལ། །བྱེ་བྲག་སྟོབས་དང་ལྡན་པར་འགྱུར། །ཞེས་གསུངས་པས་སོ།

"…as these show, whatever the general result, it matters to hold first
what has to be reckoned as a special case for the particular one; as is
said, over a treatise's general rule the particular is strong." His
examples are a person's own weekdays and mansions: «བླ་གཟའ་དར་དུས་བྱ་ལས་འགྲུབ། །སྲོག་གཟའ་དགེ་ལ་གཤེད་གཟར་བྱོལ།»
and the *bla*, *srog* and *dbang* mansions good for anything, the *keg*,
*bdud* and *gshed* mansions bad (the first verse is in the Bhutanese
anthology *Dkar rtsis dang nag rtsis kyi gzhung*, Thimphu 1981, BDRC
MW26041; neither is in WB). For the tie he writes strength alone, as
above.

## What it says

1. **Weights,** the Kashmiri paṇḍita's (WB): the date once, the planet
   (the weekday) four times, the mansion eight times, the combination
   period ten million times (བྱེ་བ). WB sets them aside for the Phugpa order
   of strength.
2. **Two that disagree.** Planet against mansion: the planet leads. Mansion
   against date: the mansion leads. Yoga against karaṇa: the very dangerous
   karaṇa leads. The day (*nyi ma*) against the hour (*dus tshod*): the hour,
   "a sharp weapon", leads.
3. **All mixed.** When good and bad are mixed among them all, the
   combination (*'phrod*) leads; where a particular case (*dmigs bsal*)
   occurs, it leads; where there is none, the general grouping (*phyogs
   sdebs*) leads; where good and bad still do not come out alike, count the
   difference of greater and lesser strength, and the stronger counts.
4. **Ranking.** Rāhu, the weekday (*res gza'*), the mansion (*rgyu skar*),
   the date, the karaṇa, the yoga, the *nyi ma*: each is stronger than the
   one after it.
5. **Above all.** The powers of all are complete in the combination period
   (*tatkāla dus sbyor*). The reasons: a particular case leads as a
   craftsman's tool fits its job; the concord leads as two who agree in
   counsel; the combination period leads because the work of planet,
   mansion, karaṇa and yoga is complete in its house, so it is held
   highest of all.

## Notes for the app

- The *dus sbyor* is a period within the day, computed from the hours by
  sign (KP's second part §9, img. 78–80), and so is the hour of rule 2:
  neither is a factor of the whole day. A day reading has no hours; the
  rules that remain are 2 (without the hour), 3 and 4.
- Rāhu's course by date and time is KP §7–8 (img. 76–77). The app weighs
  the White Beryl's detailed course on the sixteen dates it names, first
  of the seven ([rahu.md](rahu.md), SPEC §5.13); the course by the hour is
  not built.
- The weights of rule 1 put the mansion above the planet, rules 2 and 4 the
  planet above the mansion. WB settles it: the weights are the Kashmiri
  paṇḍita's, and the Phugpa tradition takes the order of strength instead.
  No sum by the weights (open question 12, answered 2026-10-06).
- The *'phrod* of rule 3 is the combination of weekday and mansion: the ten
  element pairs (*'byung 'phrod*, the app's element pair) and the 28 named
  combinations (*'phrod chen*, KP §3, WB pp. 331–333). WB says more than
  rule 3: even where the planet and the mansion are each good, a bad
  combination decides (p. 333). The app therefore makes the combination,
  where its two parts agree, the result of the day and of every work.
- The *dmigs bsal* of rule 3 is the particular case (open question 13,
  answered 2026-10-06): for a work, a reading that names that work, as
  against the class of work it falls in (WB ch. 34, the mansion verses'
  «དམིགས་བསལ་མ་གཏོགས»); for a person, their own weekdays and mansions
  (Tshul khrims rgyal mtshan). It is neither the combination period nor
  the special days of weekday and mansion. The *phyogs sdebs* is the
  general grouping, a factor's place among the good or the bad (Mkhyen rab
  nor bu: nine yogas bad, eighteen good). Neither word, nor *mang nyung*,
  asks for a count of the factors on each side: where the particular and
  the general leave good and bad mixed, the stronger factor decides.
- What this means for the app (built 2026-10-06, SPEC §5.12): no count of
  sides. A work is decided by the factors whose lists name it (its *dmigs
  bsal*), the strongest of them where they disagree; the day's tone, which
  names no work, by the factors' own good or bad (their *phyogs sdebs*),
  the strongest where they disagree. Until then the app counted sides
  where the combination did not decide: on 2000–2049 that decided the
  tone of 7,314 of 18,263 days, and the strongest voice gives 1,533 of
  them the other tone and turns 5.6 % of the works' sides. The person's
  own weekday and mansion, a *dmigs bsal* for Tshul khrims rgyal mtshan,
  are not weighed: neither text says where they stand against the
  combination.
- The special days of weekday and mansion are none of these terms; WB puts
  them below planet and mansion, "the individual results of planet and
  mansion are the main thing" (p. 337, [combinations.md](combinations.md)),
  and the app counts them as one voice in that rank.
- The *nyi ma* is the day's animal sign, the lunar date's animal (open question 9, answered 2026-10-04).
  The trigram and the sme ba are not among the seven.
