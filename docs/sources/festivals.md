# Festivals and monthly observances

Sigla and the rules for quoting are in [README.md](README.md). What the
festivals' and the monthly observances' readings rest on (SPEC §5.7,
§5.8; ROADMAP X5), read 2026-10-16. Rabten's and Henning's wording is
theirs: the readings state its facts in the project's own words.

## Edition Rabten, *Tibetan Calendar 2026*

The front matter (rabten.eu/downloads/calendarEN.pdf, 23 pages; read in
its text layer), in substance:

- The 8th, the full moon, the new moon and eclipse days are special days
  for any wholesome action; lay practitioners are especially recommended
  to take the Eight Vows on them (the readings: "the eight precepts").
- The 10th and the 25th are days for the *ganacakra* or tsok rites of
  tantra (ཚོགས, tshogs), especially recommended for guru pūjās.
- Sojong (Upoṣadha) days, twice a month, are for the confession of
  monastic communities (the full and the new moon in the calendar's
  pages).
- *Dharma festivals*: Chötrul Düchen, the day of the great miracles; Saga
  Dawa, the fourth month, on whose full moon the Buddha took birth,
  attained enlightenment and passed into parinirvāṇa; Chökhor Düchen, the
  first turning of the wheel; Lhabab Düchen, the return from the realm of
  the gods; Gaden Ngamchö, Tsongkhapa's parinirvāṇa; the masters'
  parinirvāṇa days and birthdays (not kept by the app). Of them: very
  special days for practice, on which any positive or negative action has
  a strongly multiplied effect.
- *Tibetan festivals*: Losar, the first day of the year, celebrated in
  every auspicious and joyous way; Zamling Chisang, the smoke offering
  (བསང, bsang) to all the protectors; Sangpo Chuzom, the ten good omens
  (not kept, SPEC §5.7).
- In the month pages: "Thanksgiving to Dharma-Protectors" on the 29th of
  the twelfth month (16 February 2026), the day before Losar.

## Henning, the Phugpa calendar for 2026

Henning's computed calendar (kalacakra.org/calendar/tdata/pl_2026.txt,
read in the browser) gives each festival a line under its date and no
reading: 1/1 "From 1st to 15th, Demonstration of Miracles"; 3/15
"Revelation of the Kalacakra Tantra"; 4/7 "Birth of the Buddha"; 4/15
"Enlightenment and Parinirvana of the Buddha"; 6/4 "Turning of the Wheel
of the Dharma"; 6/15 "The Buddha's entry into the womb of his mother";
9/22 "Descent of the Buddha from the realm of the gods". His list page
(tiblist.htm) only links the years.

## The White Beryl's month heading

WB vol. 1, p. 176 ([almanac-page.md](almanac-page.md), *The month's
heading*): «ཐུབ་དབང་ལྷུམས་ཞུགས་བལྟམས་པ་དང་། །ཆོས་འཁོར་ཆོ་འཕྲུལ་མྱ་ངན་འདས། །ལ་སོགས་དུས་ཆེན་བཅས་པ་རྣམས། །རང་ལུགས་གང་བཤད་བཞིན་འདྲི», the Sage's
entry into the womb, birth, turning of the wheel, miracles and passing,
and the other great occasions (དུས་ཆེན, dus chen), are written at the head
of their month as one's own tradition gives them. The festivals' family
note says this. WB's ch. 9 merit, «དུས་བཟང་དཀྱུས་རྣམས་བརྒྱ་འགྱུར» (p. 62), a
hundredfold on the ordinary good occasions, does not name the festivals,
so no reading uses it for them.
