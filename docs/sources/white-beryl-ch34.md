Sigla, numbering and quoting rules are in [README.md](README.md). WB vol. 2,
1996 Beijing edition, pp. 378–428 (img. 386–436; printed page = image
number − 8): «ལེའུ་སོ་བཞི་པ། བྱ་བ་གལ་ཆེའི་རིགས་སོ་སོ་སྒོས་སུ་འབྲས་བུ་བཤད་པ»,
the results of the important kinds of work one by one. Read on 2026-10-08:
the whole chapter in BDRC's etext for orientation, and every list the app
uses on the scans (I1KG12907, img. 386–436, enlarged 2.4–4×). Where a
quotation is given below, it was read on the scan.

## What the chapter is

Its opening verse (p. 378) lists the works it treats, numbered by the
editors 1–65: birth, naming, cutting and wearing clothes, offerings, building,
a new home, the hearth, opening the soil … sorcery, rain, a death, councils,
disputes, lasting works for the living, auspicious works, life and wealth,
and the four kinds of rite. Each work then has its own section, opened by
its number. Most sections give, in verse, the weekdays, the mansions (each
with the editors' bracketed number), the rising signs, the day animals
(*nyi ma*), the dates, the trigrams and the *sme ba*, and the Chinese
almanac's days to avoid; some add wheels counted from the Sun's or the
Moon's mansion, which are moments, not days.

The *kun phan me long*'s activity boxes follow it in the same order, box
for box, and carry the same lists ([kun-phan-me-long.md](kun-phan-me-long.md),
[kp-activities.md](kp-activities.md)): KP's boxes are the chapter's digest,
as its title says («བཻ་དཀར་དགོངས་དོན»). Where they part, the cause is most
often an abbreviation or a numeral: the two khrums (ཁྲོད, ཁྲུཾད), ༤ and ༦,
one mansion for its neighbour.

The chapter closes (p. 428) saying that the results of the works are
numberless, so it follows the authoritative ones, and keeps the deepest of
the tantras' kinds apart. It says nothing of its rank against the other
factors of the day. Its lists are the works' particular cases (*dmigs
bsal*), and each entry stands where its factor stands (SPEC §5.12).

## How it is built

`core/.../tibetan/WhiteBerylWorks.kt`, joined to the boxes in
`Electional.ACTIVITIES` (SPEC §5.10). The chapter's rules are KP's
([kp-activities.md](kp-activities.md)): what it names only with a
qualifier (a kind of the work, a direction, a person), on both sides, as
middling or acceptable, or in a way the print leaves in doubt, counts for
neither; "otherwise as work N" takes N's lists in the kinds it names
nothing in; rising signs, links, *sme ba*, Abhijit and the Chinese
almanac's days are not calculated. Then:

- what the chapter names good is added to the box's good and taken from
  its bad, and the other way round;
- what the box names and the chapter does not keeps the box's reading
  (KP has its own entries, and some of its divergences may be readings);
- works with no box are lists of their own, under wordings the catalog
  already had.

## Left out, and why

- Birth (1), prisoners (21), the sick (43) and a death (56) forecast what
  follows from a day; they are not works chosen for it.
- Disputes, petitioning and judging (58): every entry names a sub-act,
  and KP's three boxes already carry them.
- «བྱ་མཇུག(༢༡)» and «བྱ་གཞུག(༢༡)» (hearth, treating horses): the
  editors give Śravaṇa and Abhijit the same bracket, and in birth (p. 382)
  bya gzhug is ༢༢; which mansion the name is, is not settled.
- The 9th for marriage, the 1st and 6th (or 16th) for learning, and Friday
  for life and wealth («ལྷག་པ(༤) བཟང་ཞིང་པ་སངས(༦) དང༌། །ཕུར་བུ(༥) ཟླ་བ(༢) འབྲིང»,
  good or middling): the verse can be read two ways.
- KP box 28 is headed རྟ་རྒྱགས་བྱེད་པ, feeding up horses; the chapter has
  རྟ་རྒྱུག, racing, in its list of works (p. 378) and its heading (p. 401),
  with the same lists. The app keeps the box's wording.

does not change on any day; the days' works grow from 1,627,039 to
1,649,384 (23,616 added, 1,271 gone where the chapter takes a factor to

days whose lists run against their tone fall from 2,140 to 2,085.
